Vas'talos, previously known as Prefectia under the Imperium, is a Sept of the Tau Empire.

瓦斯'塔罗斯，以前在帝国之下被称为总督星，是钛帝国的家门。

While it was still ruled by the Imperium, Prefectia was a Fortress World known as the gateway to the strategically important Dovar System. During the Prefectia Campaign, the planet was conquered by the Tau under Shadowsun and declared a new Sept. The Tau have characterized the capture of Prefectia to be their greatest victory yet, but in truth the Imperium only regards it as a minor loss.

当它仍由帝国统治时，总督星是一个堡垒世界，被称为通往具有重要战略意义的多瓦尔星系的门户。在总督星战役期间，该行星被影阳旗下的钛征服，并宣称了一个新的家门。钛认为占领总督星是他们迄今为止最大的胜利，但事实上，帝国只认为这是一个小小的损失。
