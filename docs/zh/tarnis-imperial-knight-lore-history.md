Tarnis was once the home world of the Imperial Knights of House Drakkus, before they were destroyed in an attack by an invading Khorne Warband, lead by the FallenZufiel. The Warband was just the first of the forces to invade Tarnis, as the Waaagh! of WarbossNekkruncha made planetfall as well. With two invading forces, all hope looked lost for Tarnis, until a Dark Angels strike force led by MasterTigraine soon arrived to save it. However, the Dark Angels did not realize that the Khorne Warband, had a dark purpose invading Tarnis, as it is the location where they planned to complete a Chaos ritual; that allowing them to free the mighty BloodthirsterFelfurion from the Warp. The last surviving Knight of House Drakkus, Vortigan, had managed to survive the attack on Fort Drakkus and assisted the Dark Angels with repelling the Ork and Chaos forces. They were further aided by the Iron Warden of Tarnis: Altorus. The combined forces successfully repelled the Waaagh by killing Nekkruncha, and defeating the Warband even after they had managed to summon Felfurion. While Altorus fell during the battle to retake Fort Drakkus, Vortigan took up his mantle as the new Iron Warden of Tarnis.

塔尔尼斯曾经是帝国骑士德拉库斯家族的家园世界，在他们被堕天使祖菲尔领导的入侵的恐虐战帮摧毁之前。战帮只是第一个入侵塔尔尼斯的部队，就像战争头目奈克努恰的Waaagh！一样也导致了行星陷落。随着两支入侵部队的出现，塔尔尼斯似乎失去了所有的希望，直到提格兰大师率领的黑暗天使打击部队很快赶来拯救它。然而，黑暗天使并没有意识到恐虐战帮入侵塔尔尼斯的目的是黑暗的，因为这是他们计划完成混沌仪式的地方；这使他们能够将强大的嗜血狂魔法里奥恩从亚空间中解放出来。德拉库斯家族最后一位幸存的骑士沃蒂根在对德拉库斯堡垒的袭击中幸存下来，并协助黑暗天使击退了欧克和混沌部队。他们得到了塔尔尼斯的钢铁守望：阿尔托鲁斯的进一步帮助。联合部队通过杀死奈克努恰成功击退了Waaagh！并在成功召唤法里奥恩后还是击败了战帮。当阿尔托鲁斯在夺回德拉库斯堡垒的战斗中倒下时，沃蒂根接过了他的衣钵，成为了塔尔尼斯的新钢铁守望。
