Mancora is a Knight World and the Homeworld of House Trainor. It is also the Homeworld of the Howling Griffons Space Marine Chapter and their primary recruiting world. It is also the location of their Fortress-Monastery The Proud Eyrie.

曼科拉是一个骑士世界，也是特雷纳家族的家园世界。它也是咆哮狮鹫星际战士战团的家园世界和他们的主要募兵世界。这也是他们的堡垒修道院骄傲鹰巢的所在地。

The planet has been kept as an active pre-industrialised world with a near constant state of war between feudal areas and city-states in order to maintain a heavy martial focus to the world and provide a strong base for Chapter recruitment.

这个行星一直是一个活跃的工业化前世界，封建地区和城邦之间几乎一直处于战争状态，以保持对世界的高度军事集中，并为战团募兵提供坚实的基础。

Mancora has a higher-than-average birth rate of Psykers, which is carefully policed by the Howling Griffons and has led to them having a higher number of powerful battle-psykers within their ranks.

曼科拉的灵能者出生率高于平均水平，这是由咆哮狮鹫精心监督的，导致他们的队伍中有更多强大的战斗灵能者。
