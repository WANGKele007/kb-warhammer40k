A refractor field generator is a small device which projects a protective energy field around the user. Its effect is to disperse the energy of an incoming shot or blow over the total area of the field. These devices are small enough to be concealed within jewellery.

折射立场发生器是一种小型设备，可以在使用者周围投射保护性能量立场。它的作用是将进入的射击或打击的能量分散到整个立场。这些装置足够小，可以隐藏在珠宝中。

Refractor Field Generator 折射立场发生器

## Overview 概述

The field, although less reliable than the superior but otherwise similar conversion field, can stop even lascannon and plasma hits. A noted effect is the complete inability to maintain stealth, as there is a bright flash when the device is triggered. The glow of an active refractor field is also more noticable in low-light conditions.

该立场虽然不如高级但其他方面类似的转化立场可靠，但甚至可以阻止激光炮和等离子击中。一个值得注意的效果是完全无法保持隐形，因为当设备被触发时会有一个明亮的闪光。在低光照条件下，活跃的折射立场的发光也更明显。

Refractor Field device hidden within a piece of jewellery 折射立场装置隐藏在一件珠宝中

Refractor fields are uncommon, owing to the resources and arcane lore required to construct them, and within the Imperial Guard they tend to only be used by officers and enginseers. The White Scars are also known to use them - the *Souldrinkers* (a unique close-combat unit) are equipped with them. They are common within the armoury of Legio Custodes.

折射立场并不常见，因为制造它们需要资源和神秘学识，在帝国卫队内部，它们往往只供军官和工造士使用。众所周知，白色伤疤也会使用它们 — 饮魂者（一种独特的近战部队）配备了它们。它们在禁军军团的军械库中很常见。

A much rarer variation of this technology is the Reductor Field, which, rather than trying to stop projectiles, tries to cushion their impact to a point where they are of no danger to the wearer. Commissar Yarrick is known to wear one.

这种技术的一个更罕见的变体是减速立场，它不是试图阻止弹药，而是试图将它们的冲击缓冲到对佩戴者没有危险的程度。众所周知，亚瑞克政委戴着一个。
