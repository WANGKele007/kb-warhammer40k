The Pit of Raukos was a minor Warp Rift. The Imperium and Adeptus Mechanicus designation for it was 108/Beta-Kalapus 9.2.

劳科斯深坑是一个较小的亚空间裂隙。它在帝国和机械神教的名称是108/贝塔-卡拉普斯9.2。

The Pit of Raukos was an old, isolated Warp Rift within the Galaxy, not one of the newer such anomalies created in the aftermath of the Great Rift. The Rift dominated the dying star system orbiting the sun 108/Kalapus-9. The Pit of Raukos was in a region of space never claimed by Humanity, it was a deep wound in the Materium through which Daemons and Chaos Space Marine forces launched raids into Imperial territory. During the Indomitus Crusade, Roboute Guilliman fought a major battle at the Pit of Raukos to crush Chaos forces.

劳科斯深坑是银河系内一个古老而孤立的亚空间裂隙，不是大裂隙后产生的较新异常之一。裂隙支配了围绕太阳108/卡拉普斯-9运行的垂死恒星系统。劳科斯深坑位于一个人类从未宣称过的太空区域，它是物质界的一个深深的伤口，通过它，恶魔和混沌星际战士对帝国领土发动了袭击。不屈远征期间，罗伯特·基里曼在劳科斯深坑进行了一场重大战役，以粉碎混沌部队。

Guilliman and ArchmagosDominusBelisarius Cawl have agreed to use the Pit of Raukos to test Cawl's experimental pylon system's ability to close warp rifts. The pylons would be successfully used to seal the Pit of Raukos, ending the warp rift and allowing for passge into the Attilan Gate.

基里曼和统御大贤者贝利撒留·考尔已同意使用劳科斯深坑来测试考尔的实验方尖碑系统闭合亚空间裂隙的能力。方尖碑成功用于关闭劳科斯深坑，终结亚空间裂隙，并允许进入阿提兰之门。
