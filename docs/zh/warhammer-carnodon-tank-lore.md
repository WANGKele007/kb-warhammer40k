The Carnodon was a type of tank used by the Imperial Army during the Great Crusade and Horus Heresy.

食肉兽是帝国军队在大远征和荷鲁斯叛乱期间使用的一种坦克。

Carnodon Tank 食肉兽坦克

## Overview 概述

The Carnodon is a versatile medium battle tank which acted as the linchpin for many of Imperial Army victories during the Great Crusade. As a premier mobile fire platform, it was regularly adapted to combat a wide variety of foes and respond to any number of battlefield conditions. During the early years of the Great Crusade, it rapidly became a byword for conquest amongst the Expeditionary fleets. As the Great Crusade moved ever outwards and supply of the Aurox chassis design peaked, it is thought that many Carnodon tanks entered a strategic reserve and were distributed to Crusade mustering worlds behind the front lines; with over two hundred thousand examples thought to have been stored on Tallarn alone. During the Horus Heresy they often saw service with the second-line Imperialis Militia.

食肉兽是一种多功能中型战斗坦克，在大远征期间，它是帝国军队取得许多胜利的关键。作为一个成功的移动火力平台，它经常被用来对抗各种各样的敌人，并对任何数量的战场条件做出反应。在大远征的早期，它迅速成为远征舰队征服的代名词。随着大远征不断向外推进，金牛底盘设计的供应达到顶峰，人们认为许多食肉兽坦克进入了战略储备，并被分发到前线后面的远征集结世界；据信，仅在塔兰就储存了20多万辆实例。在荷鲁斯叛乱期间，它们经常被看到为二线帝国民兵服务。

Those few Carnodons that have survived the passage of the millennia are revered as links to the former incarnation of the Astra Militarum — that mainstay of the Great Crusade, the Imperial Army. With the hour so dark, and the need for trusted war assets greater than ever, the Priesthood of Mars has reinstated the STC for the Carnodon on several forge worlds, and under the light of the Great Rift the ancient tank has trundled forth from their manufactorums once more.

那些在数千年的时间里幸存下来的少数食肉兽被尊为与星界军的前身 — 大远征的中流砥柱 — 帝国军队的联系。由于时刻如此黑暗，对可信赖的战争资源的需求比以往任何时候都大，火星祭司在几个铸造世界恢复了食肉兽的STC，在大裂隙之光下，古老的坦克再次从他们的制造厂里驶出。

The Carnodon was armed with either twin Lascannons, Multi-lasers, or Autocannons or a single Volkite Culverin on its turret as well as sponson-mounted Autocannons, Lascannons, Multi-lasers, Heavy Bolters, Heavy Flamers, or Volkite Calivers.

食肉兽在其炮塔上配备了双联激光炮、多管激光或自动炮，或一门爆燃长炮，以及安装在舷侧炮台上的自动炮、激光炮、多管激光、重型爆矢枪、重型火焰枪或爆燃火铳。
