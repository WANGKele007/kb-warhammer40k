The Marauder Bomber (or just Marauder) is the standard Imperial Navy heavy bomber and can be used both in space and within atmospheres. It is used for a number of different missions, but especially excels in the air interdiction role.

掠夺者轰炸机（或简称掠夺者）是帝国海军的标准重型轰炸机，既可在太空中使用，也可在大气层内使用。它用于许多不同的任务，但特别擅长空中封锁作用。

Imperial Marauder Bomber 帝国掠夺者轰炸机

## History 历史

The Marauder was developed during the Scouring, after the shortcomings of the Thunderbolt in combatting heavy tanks and Titans became evident due to its lack of heavy weapons. After unsuccessfully trying to modify the Thunderbolt, the Adeptus Mechanicus used newly discovered knowledge to modify an obsolete STC fuselage, resulting in the Marauder Bomber.

掠夺者是在大清洗期间开发的，此前霹雳在对抗重型坦克和泰坦方面的缺点因缺乏重型武器而变得明显。在尝试改装霹雳失败后，机械神教利用新发现的知识改装了一架过时的STC机身，最终研制出了掠夺者轰炸机。

## Roles 角色

Marauder Bombers, with their long range and good maximum payload for their size, are the workhorses of the Imperial Navy. They are often based on orbiting spacecraft or, if in a prolonged campaign, on naval airbases established on the ground. Thanks to their capacious fuel tanks and powerful engines, these bases are located far from the frontline, even on another continent.

掠夺者轰炸机因其长距离、最大有效载荷大而成为帝国海军的主力。它们通常基于轨道空舰，或者在长期战役中，基于在地面建立的海军空军基地。由于其大容量油箱和强大的引擎，这些基地远离前线，甚至位于另一个大陆。

Marauder Bomber leader desperately trying to break away from Ork aircraft in the sky of Varestus Prime 掠夺者轰炸机领导在瓦雷斯图斯一号的天空中拼命试图摆脱欧克飞机

While capable of fulfilling a variety of roles, they are often used for one of three mission types:

虽然能够履行各种角色，但它们通常用于三种任务类型之一：

 - 

Strategic bombing: long-range attacks on enemy cities and institutions while operating at high altitudes beyond most anti-aircraft weapons ranges and with Thunderbolt escort. While successful in this role, the Marauder is considered to have too small of a payload to be a truly effective strategic bomber.

 - 

战略轰炸：在大多数防空武器射程之外的高空，在霹雳护卫下，对敌方城市和机构进行长程攻击。虽然在这个角色中取得了成功，但掠夺者被认为有效载荷太小，无法成为真正有效的战略轰炸机。

 - 

Free range interdiction: squadrons seek targets of opportunity such as supply dumps and convoys, which have been identified by aerial or orbital reconnaissance, before ex-filtrating under cover from friendly fighters. While these missions are not performed in concert with ground troops, they are seen as the most effective use of Marauder bombers.

 - 

自由范围封锁：中队在从友军战斗机掩护下撤离之前，会寻找通过空中或轨道侦察确定补给站和车队等机会目标。虽然这些任务不是与地面部队协同执行的，但它们被视为掠夺者轰炸机的最有效使用。

 - 

Dedicated ground attack and support: Very close assistance of friendly ground forces by engaging specific targets on the front lines. These are seen as the riskiest type of mission, as the bombers must fly at very low altitudes and risk friendly fire. Though adequate, the Marauder is considered to lack the appropriate weaponry for a dedicated ground attack role.

 - 

专门的地面攻击和支援：通过与前线的特定目标交战，为友军地面部队提供非常密切的协助。这些被视为最危险的任务类型，因为轰炸机必须在非常低的高度飞行，并面临友军火力的风险。虽然足够，但掠夺者被认为缺乏专门用于地面攻击的适当武器。

Close support missions have the added difficulty of identifying targets in a constantly-changing battlefield situation, which Imperial Guard commanders solve in a variety of ways. Some regiments use previously-assigned visual markers, such as weapons fire or coloured smoke, to designate enemy targets. Others incorporate forward air controllers to guide aircraft onto the targets. These Imperial Navy personnel, often experienced pilots themselves, will ride in communications vehicles that allow them to talk directly to the pilots overhead. A third method involves a coordinated withdrawal of ground forces prior to an air strike, decreasing the chance of any friendly fire. This is however a dangerous tactic as it requires disengaging from the enemy.

近距离支援任务增加了在不断变化的战场环境中识别目标的难度，帝国卫队指挥官通过多种方式解决了这一问题。一些兵团使用以前分配的视觉标记，如武器射击或彩色烟雾，来指定敌方目标。另一种则是配备前线空中管制员，以引导飞机飞向目标。这些帝国海军人员通常是经验丰富的飞行员，他们将乘坐通信载具，直接与头顶的飞行员通话。第三种方法是在空袭前协调撤出地面部队，减少友军火力的可能性。然而，这是一种危险的策略，因为它需要与敌人停止交战。

## Technical Specifications 技术规格

Within its heavy armoured superstructure the Marauder Bomber carries an internal bomb bay which can carry up to 6,000 pounds of explosives. At maximum capacity it can carry up to twelve bombs or six heavy bombs, while four wing mounted pylons allow it to carry up to twelve additional Bombs. For additional firepower it also carries a pair of twin-linkedlascannons in the nose section, while two sets of twin-linked turret-mounted heavy bolters, one in the tail section and the other atop the fuselage, serve as defensive armament. Instead of regular bombs the internal bomb bay may carry two Hellstorm Bombs, large incendiary devices which convert an area into a destructive inferno. Alternatively, the Marauder may deploy chaff consisting of feathery nickel filaments used to blind enemy sensor equipment, illumination-mines or fuel-air explosives.

在其重型装甲上层结构内，掠夺者轰炸机携带一个内部炸弹舱，最多可携带6000磅炸药。在最大容量下，它最多可以携带12枚炸弹或6枚重型炸弹，而四个安装在机翼上的挂架允许它携带多达12枚额外的炸弹。为了获得额外的火力，它还在机头部分配备了一对双联激光炮，而两套双联炮塔安装的重型爆矢枪，一套在机尾，另一套在机身顶部，用作防御武器。内部炸弹舱可以携带两枚地狱风暴炸弹，而不是普通炸弹，这是一种大型燃烧炸弹，可以将一个地区变成毁灭性的炼狱。或者，掠夺者可以部署由羽毛状镍丝组成的干扰物，用于致盲敌方探测装备、照明地雷或燃料空气炸药。

Imperial Marauder Bomber 帝国掠夺者轰炸机

On worlds with extensive oceans, the Marauder may replace its standard bombs with a single torpedo. The Marauder can also serve as a nuclear bomber by carrying a single Atlas bomb. To deal with enemy tanks, the Marauder can also carry missile racks under its wings and a stripped-down version of the battle cannon as main armament, with it occupying most of the forward fuselage area. By reducing its crew to two and removing every weapon system except the nose turret, slaving it to the pilot, a Marauder can also serve as transporter for Drop-Troopers, carrying them in the emptied bomb bay. The Drop-Troopers deploy by side hatch.

在拥有广阔海洋的世界上，掠夺者可能会用一枚鱼雷取代其标准炸弹。掠夺者也可以通过携带一枚阿特拉斯炸弹作为核轰炸机。为了对付敌人的坦克，掠夺者还可以在机翼下携带导弹架和作为主要武器的精简版战斗炮，它占据了机身前部的大部分区域。通过将乘员人数减少到两人，并拆除除前炮塔外的所有武器系统，将其交给飞行员，掠夺者还可以作为空降部队的运输机，将他们带到空荡荡的炸弹舱中。空降部队通过侧舱门部署。

The Marauder is equipped with a terrain-scanning auspex with a range of a hundred kilometres, a vox, able to transmit between individual crew-members, to other aircrafts and to flight operations, a bombardier foresight reticule that uses a viewscreen to project a digitally constructed target image based on data collected from the bomber's forward sensors and cameras, chaff and heat-flare launchers, afterburners, a target-lock alarm warning, and a modar. It also has vector-nozzles, allowing the bomber to switch from forward flight to vertical in order to land.

掠夺者配备了一个范围为100公里的地形扫描鸟卜仪、一个能够在机组人员之间、其他飞机和飞行操作中传输信息的音阵、一个显示屏根据从轰炸机前方传感器和摄像头收集的基于数据的数字构建的目标图像投影的投弹手远视十字线、干扰物和热闪发射器、加力燃烧室、目标锁定警报器和分子多普勒雷达。它还配备了矢量喷口，使轰炸机能够从前进飞行切换到垂直飞行，以便着陆。

Marauders can also be equipped with fuel drop tanks to extend range and, when operating over oceans, with a life raft, that has two paddles, life vests, emergency rations for 10 days, water rations for 2 days, 5 flares and an emergency transponder with batteries for 24 hours of operation.

掠夺者还可以配备油箱以扩大距离，在海上行动时，还可以配备救生筏，救生筏有两个桨、救生衣、10天的应急口粮、2天的水配给、5个信号弹和一个带电池的紧急应答器，可供24小时运行。

Like other Imperial aircraft, Marauder bombers can also receive further upgrades.

与其他帝国飞机一样，掠夺者轰炸机也可以进行进一步升级。

## Variants 变体

Though principally a bomber, Marauders can also be modified to fulfil other roles.

虽然主要是轰炸机，但掠夺者也可以被改装以发挥其他作用。

### Marauder Colossus 掠夺者巨像

The Marauder Colossus is a variant of the standard Marauder Bomber designed to deliver a single gigantic 10,000kg Colossus Bomb. The Colossus has been modified to save weight and space, with its dorsal turret removed.

掠夺者巨像是标准掠夺者轰炸机的变体，旨在运送一枚重达10000公斤的巨型巨像炸弹。巨像经过改装以节省重量和空间，其背部炮塔被拆除。

Marauder Colossus 掠夺者巨像

The Colossus Bomb is guided by the bombardier onboard the Marauder Colossus via sophisticated guidance systems. The Marauder Colossus finds its usage most often for strategic strikes against ground targets resistant to conventional firepower.

巨像炸弹由掠夺者巨像上的轰炸机通过复杂的制导系统进行制导。掠夺者巨像最常用于对抵抗常规火力的地面目标进行战略打击。

### Marauder Destroyer 掠夺者毁灭者

One particular Marauder variant is the Marauder Destroyer, a dedicated ground-attack craft which is better suited to close support missions.

一种特殊的掠夺者变体是掠夺者毁灭者，这是一种专用的地面攻击机，更适合执行近距离支援任务。

Marauder Destroyer 掠夺者毁灭者

### Marauder Pathfinder 掠夺者探路者

The Marauder Pathfinder is a electronic warfare and squadron support variant of the Marauder. Equipped with an array of sensory probes, the Pathfinder is used to scout ahead of a squadron and keep an eye on enemy activity.

掠夺者探路者是掠夺者的电子战和中队支援变体。探路者配备了一个传感探测阵列，用于在中队前方侦察并监视敌人的行动。

Marauder Pathfinder 掠夺者探路者

Replacing the bomb bay and dorsal turret, the Pathfinder features an array of advanced augurs and tracking systems, supplemented by auspex banks, multi-spectrum surveyors and high-powered vox-arrays. It functions as a high-altitude, long-range reconnaissance aircraft, operating both before and after an attack has taken place. An experienced augur operator is able to relay the data stream to an entire squadron in rapid fashion, allowing for quick tactical changes to counter enemy movements.

探路者替换了炸弹舱和背部炮塔，配备了一系列先进的占卜仪和跟踪系统，并辅以鸟卜仪阵列、多光谱测量仪和高功率音阵阵列。它的功能是作为一架高空长程侦查机，在攻击发生前后都进行操作。经验丰富的占卜仪操作者能够快速地将数据流传递给整个中队，从而可以快速进行战术更改以对抗敌人的移动。

### Marauder Vigilant 掠夺者警戒

The Marauder Vigilant is a variant of the standard Marauder Bomber designed to conduct reconnaissance operations and also perform as a command and control aircraft. Modifications include replacing its Lascannons with Heavy Bolters and removing the dorsal turret. The bomb bay is also converted to carry heavy surveillance equipment.

掠夺者警戒是标准掠夺者轰炸机的变体，旨在进行侦查行动，也可作为指挥和控制飞机。修改包括用重型爆矢枪替换其激光炮，并拆除背部炮塔。炸弹舱也被改装为携带重型监控设备。

Marauder Vigilant 掠夺者警戒

The Vigilant's main mission is to fly high-altitude, long range reconnaissance and surveillance missions. This happens both before an attack to identify enemy targets and after, to conduct battle damage assessment. Most bomber squadron include at least one Vigilant.

警戒的主要任务是执行高空、长程侦查和监视任务。这既发生在攻击之前，以识别敌方目标，也发生在攻击之后，以进行战斗损伤评估。大多数轰炸机中队至少包括一架警戒。

It features an advanced augur system incorporating banks of scanners, multi-spectrum surveyors and high-powered communications equipment. The Vigilant can also track enemy targets and relay the information to other nearby aircrafts.

它配备了一个先进的占卜系统，该系统集成了扫描仪阵列、多光谱测量仪和高功率通信设备。警戒还可以跟踪敌方目标，并将信息传递给附近的其他飞机。

Marauder Nightstalker 掠夺者午夜追猎者

The Marauder Nightstalker is a variant of the Marauder Vigilant, not yet fully adopted into regular service by the time of the Sabbat Worlds Crusade. Operating as a dedicated nightfighter, the Marauder Nighstalker is stripped of nearly everything, boasting only light armour and a nose equipped with whisker antennae. It is unpressured and is operated by a two-personnel crew in full flight-armour, one pilot and one auspex operator who is responsible for the high-gain auspex equipment.

掠夺者午夜追猎者是掠夺者警戒的变体，在安息日世界远征时尚未完全投入主力服役。掠夺者午夜追猎者是一架专门的午夜战斗机，几乎被剥去了一切，只拥有轻装甲和配备须状天线的头部。它没有压力，由两名全副飞行盔甲的机组人员操作，一名飞行员和一名负责高增益鸟卜仪设备的鸟卜仪操作者。

During combat operations, the Marauder Nightstalker drops behind an enemy bomber swarm, approaching until it achieves hard target-lock. Its only armament, a modified Volcano Cannon mounted on the nose between the antennae array, has enough power for six shots and can down a Tormentor, Harbinger or Apocalyptor in a single hit.

在作战行动中，掠夺者午夜追猎者会落在敌方轰炸机群后面，一直接近，直到达到硬目标锁定。它唯一的武器是安装在天线阵列之间的机头上的改进型火山炮，其能量足以发射六次，并且可以一次击落折磨者、预兆或天启者。

### Other variants 其他变体

Voidborne variant 承虚者变体

The Marauder Bomber has a variant that is used in void-combat, being stationed on Navy vessels. This variant is far bigger than its terrestrial counterpart, being 260 feet long, with a wingspan of 360 feet and weighing 190 tons unladen. In planetary operations mode, it has a maximum range of 4000 ikm, extended to 6500 ikm with auxiliary fuel supply, and a maximum atmospheric thrust of 286.75^2. During space-borne operations, the Marauder variant has a maximum interstellar thrust of 502.7^3 and a flight time of 9.3 hours, extended to 14 hours with auxiliary air tanks.

掠夺者轰炸机有一个变体，用于虚空战，驻扎在海军战舰上。这种变体比陆地上的变体大得多，长260英尺，翼展360英尺，空载重量190吨。在行星运行模式下，它的最大航程为4000ikm，在辅助燃料供应的情况下可扩展到6500 ikm，最大大气推力为286.75^2。在太空作战中，掠夺者变体的最大星际推力为502.7^3，飞行时间为9.3小时，使用辅助气罐延长至14小时。

It is armed with nose-mounted Vulcan pattern lascannons, two dorsal-mounted twin autocannons and tail-mounted heavy bolters. It carries a payload of six wing-slung Flail missiles and 14,500 ilbs of explosives or incendiaries. Its crew consists of six persons: a flight commander, a bombardier, a Mechanicustech-adept and three gunners, one first class and two second class.

它配备了安装在机头上的火神型激光炮、两门背装双联自动炮和尾装的重型爆矢枪。它携带六枚翼挂式链枷导弹和14500ilbs炸药或燃烧弹。它的乘员由六人组成：一名飞行指挥官、一名投弹手、一名机械教技术专家和三名炮手，一名一等和两名二等。

The Marauder variant operates in squadrons of five crafts, with another one as back up. The squadron consists of thirty crew members, with ten additionally as standby reserves, and is serviced by a ground crew of fifty, including tech-priests and servitors.

掠夺者变体以五架为一个中队，另一架作为备用。该中队由30名机组人员组成，另外还有10名后备人员，由50名地勤人员提供服务，其中包括技术神甫和机仆。

Hephaestus 赫菲斯托斯

Hephaestus was a Marauder belonging to the 2208th Expeditionary Air Wing. During the air war on Bacchus it was modified by Flight SergeantPetre Plient to carry a housing for a Seer-Skull, to allow Propagandist Kile Simlex to create picts of an active warzone. The modifications were technically sanctioned, though Plient doubted they would pass inspection if an Enginseer was to properly inspect them. The Seer-Skull Mizar was integrated into the bomber's systems, and was able to film from both its own mount as well as all the gunners' sights.

赫菲斯托斯是属于第2208远征军航空联队的掠夺者。在巴屈斯上的空战中，飞行军士佩特·普莱恩特对其进行了改装，为先知颅骨携带了一个外壳，使宣传员基尔·西姆莱克斯能够创造一个活跃战区的图片。这些修改在技术上得到了认可，尽管普莱恩特怀疑如果工造士对其进行适当的检查，它们是否会通过检查。先知颅骨北斗六被整合到轰炸机的系统中，能够从自己的支架和所有炮手的瞄准器进行拍摄。
