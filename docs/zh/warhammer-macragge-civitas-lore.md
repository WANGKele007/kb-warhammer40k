Magna Macragge Civitas, also known as Macragge City, is the capital city of the Imperial planet Macragge.

宏伟马库拉格城邦，也被称为马库拉格城，是帝国行星马库拉格的首都。

Magna Macragge Civitas during the Horus Heresy 荷鲁斯叛乱时期的宏伟马库拉格城邦

Regarded as the greatest city in the entire eastern Imperium, Magna Macragge Civitas lies in a coastal cleft between the Hera's Crown Mountains to the north and the Gulf of Lycum to the south. The city lies south of a region of Macragge known as Illyrium.

宏伟马库拉格城邦被视为整个帝国东部最伟大的城市，位于北部赫拉皇冠山脉和南部莱库姆湾之间的沿海裂谷中。这座城市位于马库拉格被称为伊利瑞姆的地区以南。
