The Stormhammer is an Imperial GuardSuper heavy tank.

风暴锤是帝国卫队的超重型坦克。

Stormhammer 风暴锤

### Overview 概述

The Stormhammer is constructed by Forge Worlds of the Adeptus Mechanicus. The massive vehicle is armed with an impressive array of weaponry configured to serve at the vanguard of an expedition into areas considered hostile because of both enemy forces and environmental factors. Due to their survival and exploration systems they can tread the surface of worlds with skies of acid, seas of lava, and toxic winds. In battle, the tank was likened to a Destroyer of the Imperial Navy, for it is capable of extended independent operations as well as holding a key part in a coordinated battle plan. The Stormhammer could also act as a command vehicle with the proper upgrades.

风暴锤是由机械神教铸造世界制造的。这辆巨大的载具配备了一系列令人印象深刻的武器，在远征队中充当先锋，进入因敌军和环境因素而被视为敌对的地区。由于它们的生存和探索系统，它们可以在酸性天空、熔岩海洋和有毒风的世界表面前进。在战斗中，该坦克被比作帝国海军的驱逐舰，因为它能够进行长时间的独立作战，并在协调的战斗计划中发挥关键作用。经过适当的升级后，风暴锤也可以作为指挥载具。

The massive array of a Stormhammer's weaponry included a turret-mounted Stormhammer Cannon, hull-mounted dual Battle Cannons, co-axial Multi-Laser, six sponson Multi-Lasers, a hull-mounted Lascannon, and a Smoke Launcher. Other potential weapons included Heavy Flamers, Heavy Bolters, and four Hunter-Killer Missiles.

风暴锤的大量武器包括一门安装在炮塔上的风暴锤加农炮、一门装在车体上的双战斗炮，同轴多管激光，六个舷侧多管激光、一个装在车体的激光炮和一个烟雾发射器。其他潜在的武器包括重型火焰枪、重型爆矢枪和四枚猎人杀手导弹。

Stormhammer 风暴锤

During the Great Crusade and Horus Heresy, the Stormhammer was the preeminent super-heavy tank of the Solar Auxilia, often used as the vanguard of the Imperium's invasion and pacification forces. During this time, the cohorts of the Solar Auxilia could field entire Companies of the vehicles. The Stormhammer still gets deployed in the 41st Millennium, even serving as a command vehicle if required.

在大远征和荷鲁斯叛乱期间，风暴锤是太阳辅助军最卓越的超重型坦克，经常被用作帝国入侵和平定部队的先锋。在此期间，太阳辅助军的大队可以部署整个连队的载具。风暴锤在第41个千年仍被部署，甚至在需要时充当指挥载具。
