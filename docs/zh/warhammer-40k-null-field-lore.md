Null-fields, also called contra-empyric fields, are an effect that disrupts, negates, or completely blocks Warp and psychic effects within an area. The size, strength, and overall effect of a null-field can vary drastically. Null-fields can function similarly to force fields, in a way, but pertain to blocking or disrupting warp energy specifically.

虚无立场，也称为反至高天立场，是一种破坏、否定或完全阻断区域内亚空间和灵能效果的影响。虚无立场的大小、强度和整体效果可能会有很大差异。在某种程度上，虚无立场的作用类似于力场，但专门用于阻挡或破坏亚空间能量。

Null-fields are utilised by humanity and other xenos species via congenital abilities or technology.

人类和其他异型物种通过先天能力或技术利用虚无立场。
