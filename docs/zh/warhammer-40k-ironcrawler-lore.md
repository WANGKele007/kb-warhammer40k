Chronos Pattern Ironcrawlers are massive Imperial all-terrain vehicles, that are used on Necromunda and their large tracks can drive over anything on the Hive World's surface.

克洛诺斯型钢铁爬行者是巨大的帝国全地形车，用于涅克罗蒙达，它们的大型履带可以驶过巢都世界表面的任何东西。

Ironcrawler 钢铁爬行者

## Description 描述

Sealed cabs keep the Ironcrawlers' drivers safe from radioactive dust storms and attackers alike, while a transport cage behind the cab, allows a gunner to take advantage of numerous fire points. When compared to the Cargo-8, Ironcrawlers has more armour on their sides and are overall more durable as well. The vehicles can also be further customized with reinforced armour, powered steering, and booby-trapped fuel tanks

密封的驾驶室使钢铁爬行者的驾驶员免受放射性沙尘暴和袭击者的伤害，而驾驶室后面的运输笼则允许炮手利用多个火力点。与货运-8相比，钢铁爬行者的侧面有更多的盔甲，整体上也更耐用。这些载具还可以进一步定制加强装甲、动力转向和陷阱油箱
