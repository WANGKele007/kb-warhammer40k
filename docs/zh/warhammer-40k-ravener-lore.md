A Ravener is a Tyranid species adapted for fast assaults, surprise raids and swift pursuits.

掠食虫是一种泰伦物种，适合快速攻击、突袭和快速追击。

Ravener 蛇虫

## Overview 概述

Raveners are capable of burrowing under the ground for long distances and moving over all manner of terrain at alarming speeds. They have spade-like Scything Talons for digging, snake-like bodies for agile movement, and thorax-mounted biomorphs such as Devourers and Deathspitters. They also bear some resemblance to the Tyranid Warrior species.

蛇虫能够在地下长距离挖洞，并以惊人的速度在各种地形上移动。它们有铲状的镰爪用于挖掘，有蛇状的身体用于敏捷运动，还有胸置的生物形态，如吞噬者和死亡喷吐者。它们也与泰伦战士虫物种有一些相似之处。

Raveners have highly sensitive auditory and visual senses that can detect a broad range of vibrations and energy bands, allowing them to hear small tremors on a planet's surface and also giving them the unique ability to "see" energy-forms emitted by Vox transmissions, sensor-stealth technology and even Teleport Homers. They can effectively hunt prey by detecting an unguarded signal and tracking it back to its origin. Thus the enemy's technology is turned against them, and any concealed outposts or infiltrators are not hidden from a Ravener.

蛇虫具有高度敏感的听觉和视觉感官，可以检测到大范围的振动和能量等级，使它们能够听到行星表面的小震动，并赋予它们独特的能力，可以“看到”音阵传输、传感隐形技术甚至传送信标发射的能量形式。它们可以通过检测无警戒的信号并将其追踪到源头来有效地狩猎猎物。因此，敌人的技术被用来对付他们，任何隐藏的前哨或渗透者都不会对蛇虫隐藏。

Raveners, like many Tyranid organisms, are essentially unthinking beasts when not under the influence of the Hive Mind. They lose all sense of priority, reverting to the animalistic joy of chasing down a panicked and fleeing foe rather than attacking closer stationary targets.

与许多泰伦生物一样，蛇虫在不受虫巢思维影响的情况下，本质上是不思考的野兽。他们失去了所有的优先理性，回到了追逐惊慌失措的逃跑敌人的动物般的快乐，而不是攻击更近的静止目标。

Ravener 蛇虫

Variations 变体

Reports have emerged of "elite" broods of Raveners operating on worlds far beyond the reach of the Hive Mind for extended periods. These Raveners carry a number of unusual adaptations and Biomorphs unseen in previous examples. Tentatively designated "Kill Teams", they are amongst the most efficient and effective predator organisms used by the Tyranids. Such Raveners can lay dormant on worlds for potentially millennia until they are awakened or needed.

有报告称，蛇虫的“精英”群体在虫巢思维无法触及的世界上长期活动。这些蛇虫携带了许多在前面的例子中看不到的不寻常的适应和生物形态。暂时被指定为“杀戮小队”，它们是泰伦使用的最高效和最有效的捕食者之一。这样的蛇虫可以在世界上潜伏数千年，直到他们被唤醒或被需要。
