The Cerebore is a large Tyranid assault beast capable of rallying other Tyranid organisms around it. It was deployed by Hive Fleet Scarabus during the Coronis Campaign.

脑钻虫是一种大型的泰伦突击野兽，能够将其他泰伦生物聚集在它周围。它是在科洛尼斯战役期间由虫巢舰队斯卡拉布斯部署的。
