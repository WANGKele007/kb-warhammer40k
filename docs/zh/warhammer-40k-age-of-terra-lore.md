The Age of Terra is the earliest period of human history, lasting from roughly the beginning of recorded history to the 15th Millennium or 18th Millennium. This was an age of adventure and hope. Glimpses and echoes are all that survive.

泰拉时代是人类历史上最早的时期，大致从有记载的历史开始一直持续到第15个千年或第18个千年。这是一个充满冒险和希望的时代。仅存的是些许掠影与回响。

During much of this time, mankind was limited to the confines of Terra and ancient civilisations came and went. But eventually, mankind developed space travel and colonised most of the Solar System, though no records remain with details. Most notable of these colonies were Mars, and the moons of Jupiter, Saturn, and Neptune.

在这段时间的大部分里，人类被限制在泰拉的范围内，古代文明来来往往。但最终，人类发展了太空航行，并在太阳系的大部分地区建立了殖民地，尽管没有关于细节的记录。这些殖民地中最著名的是火星，以及木星、土星和海王星的卫星。

Although no records remain of Humanity's first steps into interstellar travel, it began during this this age. Some claim that the 13 Appollians were the ones who lead Mankind into the stars, and their landing on Luna was only the first small step. Interstellar travel started as small and faltering steps, which became strides, and then bounding leaps through the void of space. The first encounters between human and alien races would also occur during this time, but encounters aren't directly detailed. Fragments suggest that accords were struck with some, while wars were waged against others, mostly notably the Orks. Some postulate that Orks were probably the first alien civilisation humans met during their interstellar travels, having been at war with each other ever since then.

虽然没有人类进入星际航行的第一步的记录，但它始于这个时代。有人声称，13名阿波罗成员是带领人类进入群星的人，他们登陆月球只是第一小步。星际航行最初是缓慢而蹒跚的步伐，后来变成了大步，然后在太空中跳跃。人类和异型种族之间的第一次相遇也在这段时间内发生，但相遇没有直接详细说明。碎片表明，双方达成了协议，而对其他发动了战争，尤其是欧克。有人推测，欧克可能是人类在星际航行中遇到的第一个异型文明，从那时起就一直处于战争状态。

The Age of Terra was succeeded by the Dark Age of Technology as humanity began to colonise worlds outside of the Solar System.

随着人类开始在太阳系以外的世界殖民，泰拉时代被黑暗技术时代所取代。

 - 

The Perpetuals emerge as an evolution of Homo Sapien on Terra.

 - 

永生者是智人在泰拉上的进化。

 - 

Birth of the Emperor in the 8th Millennium B.C.

 - 

公元前8世纪帝皇诞生。

 - 

Birth of the Chaos Gods Khorne, Nurgle, and Tzeentch during the medieval period of human history.

 - 

混沌之神恐虐、纳垢和奸奇在人类历史的中世纪时期诞生。

 - 

The mysterious Perpetual known as The Emperor forms a kingdom and battles the proto-Cognitae alongside his Warmaster, Ollanius Persson. The two part ways after Persson destroys a Warp-generating tower erected by the Chaos Cultists in defiance of the Emperor's orders.

 - 

被称为帝皇的神秘永生者形成了一个王国，并与他的战帅欧兰尼奥斯·佩松一起与原初闻道学派作战。佩松违抗帝皇的命令，摧毁了一座混沌邪教徒建造的亚空间生成塔后，两人分道扬镳。

 - 

Some Ancient Eldar of the Eldar Empire took a fleeting interest in nascent human culture, out of intellectual curiosity.

 - 

出于求知欲，灵族帝国的一些古代灵族对新兴的人类文化产生了短暂的兴趣。

 - 

Cities such as Atlantys and Nova Yoruk are cited as being the most legendary and ancient cities of Terra. Nations known as Jermani, Merica, Britania are said to have prospered and wilted during this time.

 - 

亚特兰蒂斯和新约鲁克等城市被认为是泰拉最具传奇色彩和最古老的城市。被称为杰马尼、梅里卡、不列颠尼亚的国家据说在这段时间里繁荣和衰落。

 - 

Mars is the first world terraformed by humanity and became a bastion of technological innovation and scientific learning.

 - 

火星是人类改造的第一个世界，成为技术创新和科学学习的堡垒。

 - 

The Sol System is fully colonised by the end of M15.

 - 

到M15末，太阳系已经完全殖民化。

 - 

The Men of Gold appear at some point, watching and learning from Humanity. In time, they create the Men of Stone for greater space colonisation.

 - 

金人在某个时刻出现，观察并向人类学习。随着时间的推移，他们创造了石人，以进行更大的太空殖民。
