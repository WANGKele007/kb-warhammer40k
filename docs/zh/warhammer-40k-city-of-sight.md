The City of Sight is a region of Terra close to the Himalazia mountains and is part of the vast Imperial Palace complex.

视界之城是泰拉靠近喜马拉雅山脉的一个地区，是庞大的帝国皇宫建筑群的一部分。

During the Great Crusade, the City of Sight served as the headquarters of the Adeptus Astra Telepathica and was where many Sanctioned Psykers and Astropaths were housed and trained. During the Horus Heresy, the City of Sight was devastated by the psychic fallout caused by Magnus' attempted warning of the Emperor regarding Horus' betrayal.

在大远征期间，视界之城是星语庭的总部，也是许多合法灵能者和星语者的住所和训练地。在荷鲁斯叛乱期间，视界之城被马格努斯试图就荷鲁斯的背叛向帝皇发出警告所造成的灵能余波所摧毁。

In M42, the City of Sight and its operations continue to persist. Many promising psykers are sent there to be trained.

在M42，视界之城及其运转继续存在。许多有前途的灵能者被派往那里接受训练。
