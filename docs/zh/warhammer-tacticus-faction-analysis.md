Warhammer 40,000: Tacticus is a 3D turn based strategy video game, that is developed and published by Snowprint Studios. It was released for Android and iOS on 15th August 2022 and desktop in February 2025.

《战锤40000：战术》是一款3D回合制策略电子游戏，由Snowprint Studios开发和发行。它于2022年8月15日在安卓和iOS上发布，并于2025年2月在桌面发布。

Tacticus has since inspired a board game, announced in July 2026. In the game, a team of loyalist Astartes confront the Death Guard, the wretched servants of the Plague God.

战术启发了一款桌游，于2026年7月发布。在游戏中，一队忠诚的阿斯塔特与死亡守卫对峙，后者是瘟疫之神的可怜仆人。

## Description 描述

The first campaign introduced to the game takes place in the Pariah Nexus, where the Indomitus Crusade is fighting the growing Necron threat. Among the earliest factions to join the game were the Ultramarines, Adepta Sororitas, Imperial Guard and the Black Legion.

 游戏中引入的第一个战役发生在驱灵死域，不屈远征正在那里与日益增长的死灵威胁作斗争。最早加入游戏的派系包括极限战士、修女会、帝国卫队和黑色军团。
