火蜥蜴战团，也被称为“火裔”，其内部存在一种不同寻常的矛盾。除去他们华丽且精雕细琢的装饰，其外貌只能被描述为恶魔般的。所有火蜥蜴战士都拥有煤炭般漆黑的皮肤，上面通常布满了众多荣耀印记。他们的肉体散发出热量，周围空气弥漫着灰烬的气息，呼吸间吐出的尽是煤渣。他们那“燃烧般”的红色眼睛——不仅仅是虹膜，就连整个巩膜都是红色的——使这种令人畏惧的外貌更加可怕。不过，瞳孔依然可以辨认，所谓的“火焰之眼”是一种审美效果，并非字面意义上的火焰。这种奇特效果是由他们家乡星球的低水平辐射造成的，导致了黑色素变异。当这种外貌与火裔们常见的安静、强烈的气质相结合时，这种气质让他们显得沉着冷静、内向深沉，给人的印象令人不安。

There is an unusual contradiction at the heart of the Salamanders Chapter, known also as the Fireborn. Their outward appearance, beyond their ornate and finely wrought trappings, can only be described as diabolic.Coal-black skin is ubiqui- tous amongst all Salamanders and often inscribed with multitudinous branding scars. Heat radiates from their flesh and the air around them is redolent of ash, and their breath of cinder. This forbidding appearance is made more fearsome by their burning' red eyes - not just the iris, but the entire sclera." The pupils, though, are still discernible and the notion of flaming eyes is an aesthetic one, and non-literal. The unusual effect is caused by low-level radiation from their home world, resulting in a melanin mutation. When allied to the quiet inten- sity common to many of the Fireborn, who have a stoic, introspective nature,the impression is disquieting.

尽管他们外表令人畏惧，但这些战士对人类有着深厚的敬意。毫不夸张地说，在所有初创团中，火蜥蜴最重视人类生命的神圣性，将其视为他们的军事信条的核心。这并不是说其他星际战士不将自己视为人类的守护者，也不按照这一信念行事，只是没有火蜥蜴走得如此之远，甚至愿意牺牲自己的生命，乃至他们的任务，以保护无辜者。

Despite their intimidating appearance, these warriors have a deep sense of regard for mankind. It is not overstatement to say that of all the First Found-ing Chapters, the Salamanders hold the sanctity of human life highest in their martial credo. That is not to say that other Space Marines do not see themselves as defenders of humanity and act in accordance with that belief, but none go as far as the Salamanders do to preserve human life, even to the extent that they would willingly sacrifice their own lives, and indeed their mission, if it would mean protecting innocents.

这种自我牺牲的理念，连同自力更生，是火蜥蜴信仰的核心信条之一，被称为普罗米修斯信条或普罗米修斯教派——这是帝国教义中几种被认可的本土信仰体系之一。其历史可以追溯到大叛乱战争（大约M31时期），当时火蜥蜴还是一支军团。正是他们的原体——锤之持有者、锻造大师伏尔甘，教导了他们这种方式。

This notion of self-sacrifice, along with self-reliance, is one of the core tenets of the Salamanders' beliefs, called the Promethean Creed or Promethean Cult-one of several sanctioned indigenous belief systems of the Imperial Creed. 夜曲

Nocturne

夜曲位于极限星域，沐浴在卡尔达斯特的光芒下。卡尔达斯特有时也被称为德雷肯加兹【1】或褶皱守望者【2】，是夜曲的主星。夜曲是帝国圣疆的一部分，是夜曲星系中主要且唯一的可居住星球。该星系的其他行星包括作为远程“瞭望塔”的克霍尔瓦和切拉什，以及无法维持生命的枯竭星球，如芬扎尔和被称为“龙的喉结”的星团，这些地方被用作星际战士候选人的致命试炼场

Situated in the Ultima Segmentum and bathed in the light of Caldaster,² its primary star, Nocturne lies firmly within the Imperium Sanctus and is the major and only habitable world of the Nocturne System. Other planets in the system include Chor'va and Cherash, which both operate as remote 'watchtowers', as well as husk worlds such as Fenzar and the cluster known as 'the Dragon's Goitre' that can no longer sustain life but are used as lethal proving grounds for the Chapter aspirants.

夜曲的太阳有时也被称为德雷肯加兹或皱守望者，它既无情又赋予生命。

Also sometimes called Drakengaaz or the Rugose Watcher, Nocturne's sun is both pitiless and yet also life-giving.

火蜥蜴与他们的母星保持着紧密的联系，与其他许多星际战士战团不同，他们与当地原住民生活在一起，作为榜样、守护者和保护者。按照帝国的分类标准，夜曲是一个阿尔法级死亡世界，按照任何标准都不适合人类居住，就像其较小的卫星一样。靠近夜曲的巨大月球天体普罗米修斯，导致周期性的地壳和地震活动，以巨大的地震和火山爆发的形式摧毁行星表面。夜曲的大部分地理环境由易变的山脉、灰烬沙漠和酸性海洋主导。这种类似近灭绝级事件的动荡时期大约每十五年（泰拉标准）发生一次，被称为试炼时刻。战团的大部分文化和其母星的文化都基于“杀不死你的会让你更强大”的观念：几乎是一种字面上的火中淬炼，其象征意义贯穿于战团的许多仪式和意识形态中。

Salamanders maintain strong ties to their home world and, unlike many Space Marine Chapters, live amongst the indigenous population as exemplars, custodians and protectors. According to Imperial taxonomical designation, Nocturne is an alpha-class death world that by any barometer should be inimi-cal to human habitation, much like its lesser planetoids. Proximity to Nocturne's oversized lunar celestial body, Prometheus, causes periodic tectonic and seis-mic upheaval that ravages the planet's surface in the form of titanic earthquakes and volcanic eruptions. Much of Nocturne's geography is dominated by volatile mountain chains, ash deserts and acidic oceans. The periods of upheaval, akin to a near-extinction-level event, occur approximately every fifteen years (Terran standard) and are known as the Time of Trial. Much of the Chapter's culture and that of their home world is predicated on the notion of what does not kill you makes you stronger: an almost literal tempering by fire, the semiological reso-nance of which carries through into the many rituals and ideological mindset of the Chapter.

这一毁灭性的自然现象只有通过圣城才能生存下来，圣城是基于原始夜曲大宅的七个重要定居点。

Survival of this devastating natural phenomenon is only made possible through the sanctuary-cities, seven vital settlements based on the original Great Houses of Nocturne.

阿伊顿被称为“火之尖刺”，它在拥有官方名称之外还有一个荣誉称号，这绝非独一无二。它之所以被称为此，是因为有一根巨大的火成岩长矛刺穿了城市本身的心脏，这是众多地质奇观之一。克吕墨涅是“商人蔓延区”，有时被称为“蔓延区”，是一个贸易枢纽和港口，小型登陆舰可以在此停靠，许多与机械神教的交易也在此进行。夜曲唯一的沿海定居点是位于艾瑟比安海的厄庇墨透斯，这是一个广阔的、充满硫磺的海域，有疏浚船和猎人在其中航行。它被称为“珠宝之城”，以其宝石和华丽的主塔而闻名。

Aethonion is referred to colloquially as the Fire Spike and it is by no means unique in terms of attracting an honorific in addition to its official designation. It is so called for the immense spear of igneous rock that impales the heart of the city itself, one of many geological wonders. Clymene is the Merchant Sprawl, sometimes called 'the Sprawl', a trade hub and port via which smaller landers can be docked and where many of the dealings with the Adeptus Mechanicus are conducted. Nocturne's only ocean-bound settlement, Epimethus, is situated in the Acerbian Sea, a vast, sulphurous expanse plied by dredgers and hunters. 夜曲的七个圣城是在夜曲最早期殖民者的时代建立的，当时他们还处于游牧和部落文化阶段，由古老的所谓大地萨满【3】通过神秘且迷信的方式，预示出地质最稳定的区域。于是，永久定居点得以建立，并延续进入了不屈纪元。从其更原始的起源开始，这些城市已经通过帝国技术得到了改进和加固，作为对在浩劫中发现的稀有矿物和宝石矿藏的“报酬”。通过火星机械教创造的强大虚空护盾、地震缓冲器和其他稳定奇迹，夜曲的居民得以继续生存。在最动荡的时期，更具游牧性的夜曲人会组成长长的难民队伍，前往圣城寻求安全。火蜥蜴认为保护和守护这些人民进行危险的迁徙是他们的庄严职责。这种无私精神是深植于内心的正直和同情心的体现，在其他星际战士中很少能见到如此程度的。

The seven sanctuary-cities were settled in the days of Nocturne's earliest colo-nists, then a nomadic and tribal culture, and founded by the ancient so-called earth shaman who divined,through esoteric and superstitious means, the areas of greatest geological stability. Thus permanent settlements were raisedand endure into the Era Indomitus. Since their more rustic origins, the cities have been improved and fortified with Imperial technologies in'payment'for caches of rare minerals and gemstones unearthed during the cataclysms.Via power-fulvoidshielding,seismicbuffers andotherstabilisingmarvels wrought bythe Martian Adeptus Mechanicus,Nocturne's citizens continue to persist. During the greatest periods of upheaval,the more nomadic Nocturneans willventure in long refugee trains to sek safety within the sanctuaries. 死亡火山和较小的火山链

Mount Deathfire and the Lesser Volcanic Chains 

夜曲最大的火山是死亡火山，它几乎横跨整个大陆，是一个充满冒烟的喷气孔、沸腾的火山口和火成岩尖峰的巨大火山。这座山在文化和精神上的意义重大，是神圣仪式的场所，所谓世界的“心血”以及夜曲深岩龙【4】最后的栖息地。它的巨大地下洞穴从未被完全绘制出来【5】但它们是广泛的。一个名为火葬台的房间位于山的物理最低点。这是一个神圣的地方，战团的已故英雄们在这里被火化，并被送入大地，人们相信他们将重生于山的岩石之中，他们的灵魂或精神回归大地。

较小的山脉贯穿了整个世界，虽然没有死亡火山那么具有毁灭性，但仍然令人敬畏、极具破坏性且数量众多，因为在每次浩劫中都会诞生许多新的山脉。确实，夜曲人有一句俗语：“给每座火山命名就像给每粒尘土或每片灰烬命名一样。【6】”另外两条较小但古老且仍然相当大的火山链是龙脊和蛇牙，夜曲人以他们相信在世界核心沉睡的神话生物来命名。

古老仪式

Ancient Rites

夜曲人对仪式主义有着极大的热情，他们通过拥抱自身的精神性和内在自我，以获得对宇宙和自身的更深刻理解。这催生了多种不同寻常的仪式，例如“燃烧之行”。曾经，年老体衰、已过巅峰的部落酋长们会踏上这一旅程，前往沙漠度过生命的最后时光，“在火焰中”走完生命的最后一程。如今，这一仪式已被战团采纳，将其作为一项精神净化仪式。就像在古老时代一样，一位“火蜥蜴”将踏上一段旅程，无论是身体上的还是精神上的，试图治愈精神上的萎靡，并重塑自我。这是一项危险的尝试，几乎没有“火裔”能够从这一仪式中归来。

Nocturneans cling to ritualism with a great fervour, embracing their spirituality and inner self in order to attain greater understanding of the universe and themselves. It has given rise to several unusual rites, such as the Burning Walk. Once undertaken by old tribal chieftains beyond the prime of their years and in decline, whereby the aged warrior would venture into the desert to live out his last days 'in fire', it has since been adopted by the Chapter, who now use the Burning Walk as a spiritual purifica-tion ritual. As in ancient days, a Salamander will go on a journey, either physical or spiritual, in an attempt to cure a malaise of the spirit, and to forge himself anew. It is a perilous undertaking and one from which almost no Fireborn has ever returned.

夜曲人更为尚武的一种习俗是“黑手之仪”。就像史前猎人会将木矛的尖端在火焰中烧硬一样，夜曲战士也会将自己武器的刃口染黑，并将双手在烟灰中涂抹，以此来祈求好运，或是让自己为即将到来的战斗做好准备。作为历史教训的忠实追随者，一些“火裔”会将这一仪式进行到底，他们会将自己身着重甲的护手置于火焰之中，直到手指被烧黑，这既是一种团结仪式，也是对古老传统的致敬。

One of the more martial customs of the ancient Nocturneans concerns the Rite of the Blackened Hand. In the same way that prehistoric hunters would harden the tips of their wooden spears in a fire, so too did Nocturnean warriors blacken the edges of their own blades and rub their hands in the soot afterwards as a way to garner favour or steel themselves for the fight ahead. Keen adherents to the lessons of their history, there are Fireborn who conduct this rite by placing their armoured gaunt-lets in a flame until the fingers blacken, as both a bonding ritual and to honour the old traditions.

普罗米修斯

Prometheus

火蜥蜴战团 的堡垒修道院同时也是该战团的主要太空港和抵御外来侵略的第一道防线。它驻扎着第一连“火龙”，他们是除了新兵之外唯一不与夜曲星民众共同生活的火裔。这些战士是火蜥蜴的精英，受训使用战术无畏动力甲，并且擅长极端近战。这些火裔中的佼佼者拥有丰富的武器库，重型火焰喷射器和雷锤与风暴盾的组合十分常见。他们是独一无二的战士，乍一看可能显得鲁莽，但实际上比常人更愿意为了保护弱者而承受和牺牲。他们是火蜥蜴勇气和人性的典范，但同时也有着一种内敛的坚忍，近乎孤僻。

The fortress-monastery of the Salamanders is also the Chapter's major space port and principal first line of defence against foreign aggressors. It is garrisoned by the First Company 'Firedrakes', the only Fireborn (aside from raw recruits before their induction) who do not live amongst the Nocturnean populace. These warri-ors are the Salamanders elite, trained in the use of Tactical Dreadnought armour and specialists in extreme close-quarters fighting. An extensive arsenal is made available to these first amongst Fireborn; heavy flamers and a thunder hammer and storm shield combination are common. They are singular fighters, who at first glance might appear reckless but actually demonstrate an even greater will-ingness to endure and sacrifice in the name of defending those weaker than themselves. They are exemplars of Salamander courage and humanity but also possess a reserve, an inner stoicism bordering on introversion.

除了作为战团舰队的港湾，舰队包括令人畏惧的战列舰、打击巡洋舰以及各种监视舰和哨舰，以保卫核心世界外，月球堡垒还设有万神殿议事厅。这是一个庄严神圣的地方，火蜥蜴的重大决策在此由连长兄弟和其他高级军官组成的议会商议。这里也保存着《火焰大典》【7】，原体伏尔甘的教诲集。这种长老议会的传统源自他们家乡的古老习俗。这种由长老组成的议会的概念可以追溯到他们家乡的古老习俗。从这个意义上说，火蜥蜴与信奉政治协商制度的极限战士并不那么不同，尽管这里的集会是由部落酋长而非参议员组成。

As well as providing a harbour for the Chapter's fleet, which comprises formi-dable battle-barges, strike cruisers and various other monitors and sentries as pickets in defence of the core world, the lunar fortress also contains the Pantheon Chamber. This is a solemn and sacred place where the great decisions that fall to the Salamanders are deliberated by a council of brother-captains and other high-ranking officers. It is also where the sacred Tome of Fire, the collected teach-ings of the primarch Vulkan, are kept. This notion of a council of elders ties back to the ancient customs of their home world. In this way, the Salamanders are not so dissimilar to the Ultramarines, who believe in a system of political quorum,although here the assembly is one of tribal chieftains rather than senators.

尽管普罗米修斯本身已经是一个强大的堡垒，拥有舰队和驻军，但伏尔甘之眼和锻造舰“火焰圣杯”使其更加坚固。后者无异于一台巨大的引擎，一艘神话般的铁砧船，生产出数量惊人的武器和战争物资。无论是它还是伏尔甘之眼，这是一门具有毁灭性威力的巨型防御激光炮，都是由伏尔甘亲手打造的，因此受到战团专家的极度崇敬。

As formidable a bastion as Prometheus already is, with its fleet and garrison, it is made more so by the Eye of Vulkan and the forgeship Chalice of Fire. The latter is nothing less than a great engine, a mythical anvil-ship that produces weapons and war materiel in staggering abundance. Both it and the Eye, a monumental defence laser of devastating potency, were forged by Vulkan's hand and are there-fore regarded with extreme reverence by the Chapter adepts.

夜曲星的末日

The 'Dooms' of Nocturne

最早是卡里曼记述了夜曲星的最后毁灭。在其史诗诗篇中，这位哲学家描述了这座火山星球的数种预言中的灭亡方式。这些灭绝传说大多与它的地质脆弱性或古老居民有关。

It was Kalliman who originally wrote of the last destruction of Nocturne. In his epic poems, the philosopher described several prophesied deaths of the volcanic world. Most of these extinction myths related to its tectonic fragility or its ancient denizens.

第一个所谓的“末日”描绘了乌德拉库勒【8】的崛起。据说它沉睡在世界之心周围，乌德拉库勒是所有深岩中最古老、最大的一条。它的名字意为“燃烧之手”或“爪”，曾被用来尊崇武器和其他战争装备。在世界终结之时，这头巨兽将苏醒，可怕的哀嚎将响彻全球。大地将颤抖，群山喷火，因为乌德拉库勒庞大的蛇形身躯缓缓从夜曲星的地基上解开缠绕，直到最终穿透地壳，倾洒出它的心血，将土地和人民淹没在无底的岩浆之海中。

The first so-called 'doom' depicted the Rising of Urdrakule. Said to slumber wrapped around the heart of the world, Urdrakule is the oldest and largest of all the deep drakes. Its name means 'burning hand' or 'claw', and has been used to honour weap-ons and other trappings of war. At the time of ending, the beast would wake and a terrible keening would ring throughout the world. The earth would tremble and the mountains bleed fire as Urdrakule's vast serpentine body slowly uncoiled around the foundations of Nocturne itself, until at last it broke through the crust of the world to spill its heartblood, drowning its lands and peoples in a fathomless lava sea.

大分裂是第二个末日。卡里曼的诗句中提到“空气中充满了灰烬和煤烟，无尽的黑暗”，接着是“世界结构被拆解时的巨大震动”。随后大地将被撕裂，沿着断层线发生一系列毁灭性的地震。在高潮部分，夜曲星赤道上将出现一条巨大的裂缝，深到足以将世界撕裂，其两半将毫无生机地在虚空中翻滚。

The Great Fragmentation is the second of the dooms. Kalliman's verse speaks of an 'endless darkness as the air is filled with ash and soot', and then of 'great tremors as the fabric of the world is unstitched'.And the earth would then be rent, splitting along its fault lines in a chain of devastating earthquakes. At the climax, a great chasm would open across Nocturne's equator so deep that the world would be sheared apart, its two halves left to tumble lifeless and cold through the void.

第三个灭绝事件提到了“火之剑”，这与末日的预兆者有关，是一位毁灭者和火焰审判之剑，将带来夜曲星的最终毁灭。

A third extinction event refers to the Ferro Ignis or 'fire sword', and this relates to a harbinger of the end days, a destroyer and fiery blade of judgement that will bring about Nocturne's final destruction.

其他关于灭绝的传说中，还包括预言中的“地狱之时”或“火焰之时”。虽然这类传说数量众多，但每一个都是关于被野兽、大地或火焰杀死的微妙变体。

Other extinction myths include the prophesied Tempus Infernus or Time of Fire'. There are a great many, although each is a subtle variation on the themes of death by beast, earth or flame.

招募

Recruitment

对有意加入战团的潜在候选人施加的严苛考验极为残酷，而夜曲堪称绝佳的试炼场，能够淘汰那些缺乏成为火裔所需的内在力量与坚韧的人。在这些试炼中失败往往意味着死亡，这似乎与普罗米修斯信条中关于保护生命的原则相悖，但火蜥蜴也相信熔炉的概念以及在其中对新兵的淬炼。那些进入的人是自愿的，而那些死去的人只是回归大地，成为夜曲死亡与重生循环的一部分【9】。

The rigours meted out upon potential aspirants to the Chapter are harsh, and Nocturne makes for the perfect training ground to weed out those who do not possess the inner strength and fortitude to become one of the Fireborn. Failure in these trials often means death, which might seem at odds with the tenets of the Promethean Creed that relate to the preservation of life, but the Salaman- ders also believe in the idea of a crucible and the tempering of initiates within it. Those who enter do so willingly, and those who perish simply return to the earth, as part of the cycle of Nocturnean death and rebirth.?

在烈日下，跨越灰烬荒地和沙漠平原的艰苦行军，只是任何潜在新兵即将面临的试炼的一个前奏。当地的动物也提供了严峻的挑战，试炼内容从狩猎到躲避不等。无论是穿越火葬沙漠被一群萨'尔克蜥追赶，还是在广袤的阿瑞迪恩平原上与一头巢狮兽一对一较量，都有众多生物能够从一群候选人中筛选出强者。这提供了一种高度多样化且不断变化的训练计划

Gruelling marches across ash wastes and desert plains under a burning sun are merely a foretaste of the trials ahead for any potential recruit. The native fauna also provide a staunch challenge, and tests vary from hunting to evasion. Whether trekking the Pyre Desert pursued by a pack of sa'hrk or pitted one-on-one against a leo'nid out in the vastness of the Arridian Plain, there are creatures aplenty to root out the strong amongst any cohort of aspirants. What this provides is a highly varied and changeable training regimen.

招募通常基于至少两个元素原则，其中大多数是大地和火的变体。燃烧沙地、流血之石【10】、炽热火焰和凶猛野兽的试炼，都在某个时候被用来淬炼新兵。

Recruitment is generally based on at least two elemental principles, the majority of these being variations of earth and fire. The trials of Burning Sands, of Bleed-ing Stone, of Searing Fire and of Savage Beasts have all, during one occasion or another, been used to temper aspirant intakes.

有一个独特的试炼是图腾之路，它是为那些显示出一定灵能潜力的候选人，或者偶尔是发展较晚的战斗兄弟，这种潜力由战团智库之一，甚至是首席智库长亲自判断。这是一种灵能幻象之旅，一名编修员（智库的最低等级）必须前往死亡火山之下。与所有招募和训练工作一样，并非每个候选人都被期望存活。

One unique trial is that of the Totem Path, which is reserved for those aspir-ants or, on occasion, late-developing battle-brothers, who display a measure of psychic potential as divined by one of the Chapter Librarians, or indeed, the Chief Librarian himself. It is a form of psychic vision journey', whereby a Lexicanium (the lowest rank of Librarian) must venture beneath Mount Deathfire. As with all recruitment and training endeavours, not every aspirant is expected to survive.

标记与徽记

Markings and Heraldry

 图片

作为《阿斯塔特圣典》的拥护者，火蜥蜴使用与其他许多**星际战士**战团相同的队标与番号。然而，他们战甲上的纹饰却与他们的世界和传承息息相关。火焰或燎原之火的图案常常以各种形式出现在各个连队甚至个别小队的装备上。在图像上的共性确保了部队的凝聚力，防止单位标记变得杂乱无章，但一定程度的个性化表达是被鼓励的。自然，龙头形象随处可见。通常以侧面的龙头形象呈现，但并非绝对，这是该战团使用最为广泛的徽记。其颜色和位置因连队番号而异，可能会被印在肩甲或其他地方。燃烧的余烬和铁匠的锤子是比较少见的符号，但仍被使用，火盆和铁砧也是如此。火蜥蜴的军官或老兵倾向于使用无番号的鳞甲肩甲，以此表达对地球深处的龙的崇敬 。

As adherents to the Codex Astartes, the Salamanders utilise squad markings and designations common to many Space Marine Chapters. However, the iconography of their war plate is dedicated to imagery and motifs associated with their world and heritage. The flame or conflagration is often deployed, in various forms, across companies and even individual squads. Commonalities in imagery ensure cohesion and prevent unit markings becoming ad hoc, but a certain degree of expression is encouraged. Natu-rally, the drake is readily in evidence. Usually depicted as the head of the beast and in profile, though not exclusively, it is the most ubiquitous piece of heraldry deployed by the Chapter. Its colour and placement vary according to company designation, and it may be emblazoned on the shoulder guard or elsewhere. The burning embers and the forgesmith's hammer are less common sigils, though still in use, as are the brazier and anvil. Salamanders officers or veterans tend to favour a scaled pauldron, bereft of designation but denoting their reverence for the drakes of the deep earth.

在肩甲或膝盖护甲的主要装置的右侧，可能会添加一些小的装饰，形式多样，用以纪念参加过某些战役或圣战，或是纪念英勇行为，亦或是为阵亡兄弟设立更为庄重的纪念。手套或动力拳套及其变体也是火裔徽记展示的绝佳画布 。

Minor affectations may be added to the major device on a shoulder guard or knee plate, in both cases to the right-hand side, and come in many forms to honour serving in certain campaigns or crusades, or to commemorate deeds of valour or more solemn memorials for slain brothers. Gauntlets or power gloves, and their variants, are also fertile ground for Fireborn heraldry to find its canvas.

火蜥蜴披风

The Salamander Mantle

在火蜥蜴的所有战备装备中，没有什么比被称为“披风”的装备更具精神意义。简单来说，这是一种用火蜥蜴皮制成的斗篷，是每个有抱负的火蜥蜴必须经历的入团仪式的一部分。虽然并非所有战斗兄弟都会在战斗中穿戴他们的披风，这通常属于高阶军官或专家的特权，但每个火蜥蜴战士都拥有一件，每件战袍都有其主人赋予的名字。

Of all the fine trappings that a Salamander will bear in war, none hold as much spiritual significance as what is simply called 'the mantle'. To describe it some-what reductively, it is a drakehide cloak that is part ofevery aspiring Salamander's rite of passage into the Chapter【11】." Though not every battle-brother wears their mantle into battle, for this tends to be the province of higher-ranking officers or specialists, every Salamander has one and every mantle has a name given by its bearer.

披风是火裔训练和评估潜在新兵的最终仪式的一部分。对于已经成功通过此前挑战的候选人来说，最后的考验是狩猎、杀死并最终剥皮一种致命的火龙，这种本土爬行动物分布在火葬沙漠、斯科里亚平原和塞米安灰脊等地。这是一项具有象征意义且极为庄重的行为，绝非无情的猎杀。在这里，候选人被视为猎物，必须战胜更致命的对手。一旦被制服，火龙的皮被剥下、处理并加固，成为披风的基础。无论军衔高低，每位火蜥蜴战士都有责任保护和尊重它，以及它所来自的生物的记忆。

The cloak is part of the culminating ritual by which the Fireborn train and assess their potential recruits. For the aspirant who has successfully navigated the challenges to this point, a final trial remains: that of the hunting, killing and ultimately skinning of a deadly firedrake, one of the indigenous saurians found in the Pyre Desert, Scorian Plain and Themian Ash Ridges amongst other places.It is a symbolic and profoundly solemn deed, and not the callous slaughter of prey. It is the aspirant who is regarded as the prey and who must overcome the deadlier foe. Once bested, the drake's hide is stripped, cured and fortified. It becomes the basis of the mantle and it is every Salamander's duty, regardless of rank, to preserve and respect it as well as the memory of the creature from which it was taken.

仪式伤疤

Ritual Scarring

烙印仪式象征着忍耐，同时也是记录火裔功绩与誓言的方式。这一仪式如同许多火蜥蜴战团的传统一样，源自最早定居夜曲星的先民。以火焰或在此处以烙印来缔结契约，具有重大的意义和文化价值。

The act of scarification is a symbol of endurance but also serves as a record of the deeds and vows of the Fireborn. It is a ritual, like many of the Chapter, that dates back to the earliest Nocturnean settlers. The notion of sealing a pact by fire or, in this case, via a brand, holds great meaning and cultural significance.

与火蜥蜴战团的许多其他传统不同，仪式烙印通常由一名人类仆从，即烙印祭司执行。这一职位在其他凡人中备受尊崇，每名火蜥蜴战士都有自己的烙印祭司，他们对这些祭司充满敬意并给予最高的尊重。仪式烙印的艺术深深植根于传统，需要多年才能精通。新入伍的火蜥蜴战士在痛苦仪式中获得的第一个烙印伤疤，可以有多种形式，如火龙的鳞片或獠牙。这些伤疤通常被烙印在身体上，因为只有资深的火蜥蜴战士才会在脸上留下烙印，而且只有在身体其他部位都已布满伤疤后才会这样做。在其他伤疤上再次烙印，形成更复杂的伤疤图案，并非闻所未闻，这暗示着更深层的意义。

Unlike many other practices of the Salamanders, ritual scarring is usually conducted by a human bondsman called a brander-priest. This is regarded as an estemed position amongst other mortals, and every Salamander has their own attendant, whom they treat with respect and the highest regard. The art of ritual branding is heavily rooted in tradition and it takes years to develop profi-ciency. The first branding scar a newly inducted initiate will gain is given during the Rite of Pain, and can take many forms, such as the drake scale or fang. It is often seared into the body, for only veteran Salamanders tend to receive brand-ing scars on the face, and only then once the rest of their flesh is covered. It is not unknown for brands to be seared atop other brands, thus creating more complex patterns of scarification, hinting at even further meaning.

每个烙印都基于夜曲星符号方言的视觉语言，可以有多种形式，包括但不限于：铁砧，象征忍耐或从重伤中幸存；火焰，象征勇气行为；漩涡，有多种解读，但最常见的是授予那些拯救生命的人；长矛，象征狩猎和精准射击；龙眼，象征智慧和耐心；剑，象征战斗击杀；火山口，授予那些取得伟大成就的人。火龙是一种特殊符号，只授予在第一连火龙服役的老兵。

Each brand is based within the visual language of Nocturnean sigil-dialect and can take all manner of different forms, including but not limited to: the anvil, signifying endurance or the survival of a serious injury; the flame, to signify an act of courage; the gyre, which has a variety of interpretations but most commonly is given to someone who has saved a life; the spear, for hunting and marksman-ship; the dragon's eye, meaning wisdom and patience; the sword, for battle kills; and the caldera, awarded to those who have achieved a great feat. The drake is a special symbol, for it is only given to veterans who have served in the First Company Firedrakes.

这份清单远非全面：还有其他不太明显、更具特色的符号，可能与某个大氏族（Great House）或特定的连长或战役有关。在任何情况下，所有这些伤疤都带着荣誉，是火裔在战团中威望的标志。

This list is not nearly comprehensive: there are other less obviously distinct sigils that can be related to one of the Great Houses, or to a specific captain or campaign. In any case, all such scars are borne with honour and are marks of a Fireborn's prestige in the Chapter.

冶金与锻造

Metallurgy and Forging

火蜥蜴战团的自力更生信条也体现在武器和盔甲的锻造与维护上。所有火蜥蜴战士都是技艺精湛的工匠，他们在金属的装饰和锻造中注重美感与艺术性。“黑铁匠”或“金属塑形者”是夜曲人用来称呼锻造师和金属工人的术语。就像他们文化中许多部分一样，尽管经历了数千年岁月的磨砺，这些俗语依然完整地保留下来，它们源自夜曲星最古老的时代，甚至早于原体被发现。

The creed of self-reliance embodied by the Chapter also extends to the forging and maintaining of their weapons and armour. All Fireborn are expert craftsmen and value beauty and artistry in the ornamentation and forging of metal. Black-smiter' or 'metal-shaper' are terms Nocturneans use to refer to forgesmiths and metalworkers. Like much of their culture, which has survived intact despite the rigours of the millennia, these colloquialisms are drawn from the oldest days of Nocturne and predate the discovery of the primarch himself.

检视一名火蜥蜴战士的盔甲和战斗装备，就如同目睹一件手工精湛、制作精良的作品。在星际战士中，很少有人能像火裔这样技艺高超，更少有人会自己锻造刀剑和盔甲板块，而这些任务通常会交给盔甲匠或战团工匠。火蜥蜴战士为自己能够打造出既华丽又耐用的作品而深感自豪，这种将形式与功能完美结合的技艺，是帝国众多顶尖制造者梦寐以求的境界。这并非出于虚荣，火裔这样做是为了向他们的文化和历史致敬，他们乐于享受实现这种卓越所需的耐心和精确性。

To examine a Salamander's armour and wargear is to behold something arti-sanal and superbly crafted. Few amongst Space Marines are as accomplished as the Fireborn and fewer still fashion and forge their own blades and armour plate,a task routinely given to an armourer or Chapter artisan. Salamanders take great pride in their ability to create the ornate and the enduring, a union of form and function many of the Imperium's foremost fabricators would weep to attain. This is not an act of vanity; the Fireborn do it to honour their culture and history, and they revel in the patience and exactitude required to achieve such excellence.

 图片

即使是最低微的火蜥蜴战斗兄弟，也会锻造属于自己的刀刃和爆弹枪。这些武器可能镀金、银饰，甚至使用更稀有的金属，这些在战团的军械库中十分常见。星际战士通常会维护自己的武器库，火蜥蜴也不例外。他们备受赞誉的连长和战团的杰出人物拥有令人惊叹的武器阵列，包括刀刃、长矛、奇异的枪械，以及象征着他们战士信条核心的锤子。

Even the humblest Salamanders battle-brother will forge his own blade and his own bolter. Gilded, silver-chased or utilising even rarer metals, all are common-place amongst the Chapter's armoury. Space Marines will often maintain an arsenal of weapons, and Salamanders are no exception. Their vaunted captains and Chapter luminaries possess an incredible array of blades, spears, esoteric guns and the symbolic hammer around which much of their warrior creed revolves.

手工打造的战甲配有龙面头盔、华丽的胸甲、鳞片护胫和其他精美装饰，其中一些细节如此微妙，肉眼难以察觉。

Artisanal war plate is rendered up with dragon-faced helms, ornate chest guards,scaled greaves and other finery, some so subtle it is difficult to detect with the naked eye.

锻造与打造技艺——因仪式过程中的高温和缺氧而常人难以忍受——只是火蜥蜴与锻造文化关系的一部分。冶金，其研究与理解，是战团成为锻造大师的关键所在。通过简单的触摸甚至气味，火蜥蜴就能察觉任何金属的强弱。他们能在大地中寻找金属，精准判断金属的淬火极限，以迅捷手法将其折叠至近乎不可摧毁的状态，并巧妙地将不同元素完美合金化，增强其柔韧性和强度，提升锋利度与密度。如此深刻的洞察力是与生俱来的，这是一种传承千年的传统，源自他们的原体伏尔甘，在他面前，金属与大地的秘密无处遁形。

Practical knowledge of forging and crafting upon the anvil - unendurable for a normal mortal due to the intensity of heat and scarcity of air during this ritua-listic process - is only part of the Salamanders' relationship to this aspect of their culture. Metallurgy, its study and understanding, is an essential part of what makes the Chapter such accomplished masters of the forge. By the merest touch or even smell, a Salamander can detect the strength and weaknesses of any metal. He can root it out in the earth, he can gauge exactly how far a metal can be tempered, he can fold it with such alacrity as to be nigh-on unbreakable, and he can apply the fuller's touch to perfectly alloy one element to another to create flexibility and strength, and enhance sharpness and density. Such profound acumen is unteachable; it is innate, a tradition that goes back millennia to their forefather, Vulkan, for whom no secret of metal or earth was unknown.

火蜥蜴战团中技艺最为精湛的金属锻造者是铸造大师，他们在战团中占据着重要且受人尊敬的地位。铸造大师不仅要负责维护火蜥蜴战团强大的星际战士军械库，包括其载具、救赎者无畏和战术无畏盔甲，还是古老铁匠知识的守护者。通过传承这些知识，部分原体的智慧与技艺得以保留。这个团体所创造的伟大奇迹被视为战团的圣物，体现了伏尔甘在精神和冶金方面的教导。

Most learned of the Salamanders' metal-shaping brethren are the Forgemasters,who occupy a position of some prominence and esteem in the Chapter. As well as being responsible for maintaining the Salamanders' formidable Space Marine armoury, including their vehicles, Dreadnoughts and Tactical Dreadnought armour, it is the Forgemaster who is the custodian of the ancient knowledge of the earliest blacksmiters, and through this pasing on of knowledge is some of the primarch's wisdom and talent preserved. The great marvels wrought by this fellowship are considered nothing less than relics of the Chapter and embody Vulkan's spiritual and metallurgical teachings.

火蜥蜴战团的锻造工艺大多精巧隐蔽，以确保战团外观的统一性与威严。除了使用特种合金和独特的金属元素，如坚不可摧的奥杜莱特，火蜥蜴战团在锻造常见的金属如精金和陶钢时也达到了更高的技艺水平。在天赋异禀的铸造大师手中，精金可以变得更加多用途且轻便，同时不损失其防护性能。陶钢被锻造得极为坚韧，其耐火特性得到增强，整体强度也有所提升。

Most of this forging art is subtle or cleverly concealed so as to ensure the aesthetic cohesion and reinforce the hegemonic appearance of the Chapter. As well as utilising special alloys and unique metal elements such as obdurite, the Salamanders are also adept at forging with more commonly known metals such as adamantine and ceramite, though to a greater level of mastery. In the hands of a gifted Forgemaster, adamantine can be made more versatile and lighter, without losing any of its protective qualities. Ceramite is fashioned to be hyper-resilient, its fire-resistant traits enhanced and its overall toughness improved. 

尽管在形式和功能上有一些共同点，例如普罗米修斯板甲、火龙战刃和所谓的龙怒爆弹枪【12】，火蜥蜴战团的每一件装备，无论是专用的还是普通的，都是独一无二的，它们根据穿戴者和制造者的不同而有无数细微的定制。

Whilst there are some commonalities to form and function, such as Promethean plate, drakeblades and so-called Dragonrage bolts,1° every piece of Salamanders wargear, specialised or otherwise, is unique to its wearer and maker and has myriad subtle customisations.

战团征战

The Chapter at War

火蜥蜴战团有一句格言，体现了他们偏好的作战方式：“眼对眼，牙对牙”。当然，如同所有星际战士战团一样，他们能在任何战场、任何条件下，针对任何敌人，以最高效的方式赢得胜利。但不可否认，火蜥蜴倾向于近战。这并非一定意味着肉搏战——尽管战团成员的力量和耐力使肉搏战成为一种极具可行性的战术——而是通过使用火焰枪和热熔枪等火山碎屑武器。这些武器的射程较短，迫使火蜥蜴必须将自己置于敌人核心位置的战斗教义中。

There is a maxim amongst the Fireborn that speaks to their preferred way of war:'eye to eye, tooth to tooth'. Though, as with any Adeptus Astartes Chapter, they can fight in any theatre, under any conditions and against any foe in the most efficient method to grant victory, there is no denying the Salamanders' proclivity for close-quarters combat. That is not necessarily to say hand-to-hand - although the strength and durability of its battle-brothers makes this a highly viable strat-egy for the Chapter -but rather through the use of pyroclastic weapons such as flamers and meltaguns, whose shorter range necessitate a battle doctrine that puts the Salamanders right at the heart of their enemy.

在拉维库斯的清洗行动中，火蜥蜴部署了大量重型装甲和近战战士，以消灭潜伏的绿皮部落在装备了喷火拳套的百夫长组成先锋，缓慢推进的根除者方阵和手持喷火器的突击星际战士冲入敌群。 “救赎者” 型兰德掠袭者巩固了侧翼，分别在战线两侧锚定阵地，同样装备了毁灭性的喷火武器。步兵稳步推进，无视迎面而来的武器雨，直到与兽人短兵相接。随后，地狱般的火焰被释放出来，如同某位诗人笔下的史诗或叙事诗一般。到处都是火焰，一片无尽的火海，明亮地燃烧在大片黑烟之中。火蜥蜴在其中稳步前行，有条不紊且精准无比，他们的侧翼逐渐延伸并包围，火焰之墙将兽人赶成一团，使其被彻底消灭。留下的只有焦土，这是他们经过后唯一剩下的东西。

At the Culling of Ravikus, the Salamanders deployed mass heavy armour and close-quarters warriors to wipe out an embedded greenskin horde. Led by a spear-head of Centurions with flame gauntlets, a slow-moving phalanx of Eradicators and flamer-bearing Assault Marines crashed into the enemy horde. Land Raid-ers in the 'Redeemer' pattern shored up the flanks, anchoring the battleline on either side and similarly armed with devastating incendiary weaponry. The infan-try advanced implacably, ignoring the hail of weapons flung against them until they closed on the orks. Then came the unleashing of hell, as in some poet's saga or epic verse. Everywhere was fire, a great unending sea of it, burning brightly amidst swathes of black smoke. The Salamanders drove on through it, method-ical and exacting, their flanks gradually extending and encircling, the wall of flame herding the greenskins into a mass where they could be destroyed utterly. Scorched earth remained; it was all that was left in their wake.

Only then, scarred but unbowed, did the Salamanders sally from vodan's ramparts and sweep away the ragged enemy horde, grinding out victory from apparent certain defeat

It was the Salamanders, who, at the inception of Lord Guilliman's grand crusade, first volunteered to lead and protect the inaugural torchbearer fleets. The Primaris technologies have been a revelation to the Salamanders, who accepted the gift gratefully, their ranks buoyed by an induction of warriors. Still, the traditions of the Chapter have not been lost. Even those battle-brothers new to Nocturne soon learned of its ways and adopted their generic heritage so that they look no different to their Firstborn brothers. 战团英雄

Heroes of the Chapter

该战团历史与火蜥蜴一样悠久，其伟大的战士和领袖名单漫长且显赫。作为战团长兼普罗米修斯摄政王的图杉，在不屈纪元的英雄中首屈一指，但还有其他杰出人物，例如索尔 巴肯，他是招募新兵的长官，出生在忒弥斯的地狱深渊，其身材即使在星际战士中也令人望而生畏；还有哈宗 达克尔，一个出身卑微的神秘人物，曾是一名中士，后来成为智库成员，并在夜曲之战中神秘失踪。药剂师菲吉斯也值得一提，他是唯一一位经历过古老行仪式燃烧之行并存活下来的火蜥蜴，被认为是一位睿智而克制的战士。

With a history as venerable as the Salamanders', its line of great warriors and leaders is long and distinguished. Tu'Shan, as Chapter Master and Regent of Prometheus, is pre-eminent amongst the heroes of the Era Indomitus, but there are others such as Sol Ba'ken, Master of Recruits, who was born in the hell-pits of Themis and has a physical stature daunting even for one of the Astartes; or Hazon Da'kir, an enigmatic figure of humble origins, a former sergeant who became a member of the Librarius and disappeared mysteriously during the Battle of Nocturne. Apothecary Fugis is also worthy of note, being the only Sala-mander ever to survive the ancient rite of the Burning Walk, and was said to be a wise and temperate warrior.

阿德拉克斯·阿加通

Adrax Agatone

阿德拉克斯·阿加通是第三连“火山碎屑”的连长，他弥合了长子星际战士和原铸星际战士之间的文化隔阂。他是战团中第一个跨越原铸界限的人，不是像其他战团的入伍者那样因重伤而被迫做出选择，而是为了促进兄弟间的团结。阿加通的战斗记录漫长且卓越。他曾经是一名战术中士，在继承前任连长恩科恩的职位后，他在**杰维奥克斯**指挥第三连。他参加了夜曲之战，当时家园星球被混沌叛军战团入侵，后来又参加了雷纳亚的净化行动，消灭了一个根深蒂固的基因窃取者教。他是一个冷静的战士，也是一个彻头彻尾的实用主义者。他出生于阿瑞迪恩平原，是忒弥斯人，他的飞龙皮披风来自那片荒芜之地的野兽，因此他是一名出色的猎手。尽管阿加通坚定地遵循普罗米修斯教义，但他也是一个无情且凶猛的战士，其焦土政策为他在同僚中赢得了凶悍的名声。

Bridging the cultural divide between Primaris and Firstborn Salamanders is captain of the Third Company 'Pyroclasts', Adrax Agatone. It was the Master of the Arsenal who was the first of the Chapter to cross the Rubicon Primaris, not as means to save his life from grievous injury - as inductees from other Chapters have been forced to do - but to promote unity amongst his brethren. Agatone's war record is long and distinguished. A former tactical sergeant, he succeeded the previous captain, N'keln, and commanded the Third Company at Geviox. He fought at the Battle of Nocturne, when the home world was invaded by a Chaos renegade war host, and latterly at the Purging of Renaya, where he eradicated a pervasive genestealer cult.He is a stoic fighter and an arch pragmatist. Born of the Arridian Plain and a native to Themis, he took his drakehide mantle from a beast of that desolate land and as such is a consummate hunter. Though a staunch adherent to the Promethean Creed, Agatone is also a merciless and ferocious warrior whose scorched-earth mentality has garnered him a fierce reputation even amongst his peers.

Chapter Relics and Weaponry

作为技艺精湛的工匠，火蜥蜴战团拥有众多珍贵的战团遗物和备受尊崇的文物。这些是传奇武器、工具和奇迹，曾由战团的杰出成员使用，其中许多已有数千年历史，由原体亲手锻造，或在其指导下打造而成。火蜥蜴战团赋予这些遗物特殊的神圣意义，视为他们作为工艺大师的杰出技艺的巅峰。与一些战团不同，火蜥蜴战团并不崇拜旗帜或布料，而是倾向于那些历经岁月考验、代表着战团精华的遗物。

As expert craftsmen, the Salamanders have a great many Chapter relics and honoured artefacts. These are the legendary weapons, tools and marvels wielded by vaunted Chapter luminaries, many of which date back thousands of years and were forged by the primarch himself, or under his expert guidance. Salamanders endow their relics with particular sacred importance, consid-ered the apex of their esteemed and exemplary skill as master craftsmen. Unlike some Chapters, who might revere a banner or piece of cloth and see it preserved from the ravages of entropy behind a flickering stasis field, the Salamanders tend towards artefacts that have endured and come to represent the best of their Chapter.

Final Thoughts

火蜥蜴战团以及他们继承了部分遗产的夜曲人，无疑是深邃且富有精神的人，他们倾向于内省和自我剖析，但同时又无比勇敢、充满人道主义精神

。战团中的每一位战士都是他们锻造的盔甲和武器中的艺术品，是一个充满熔炉般的炽热与灰烬般的余温的活生生的火山口。他们是人类捍卫者的真正典范：完全无私，却又令人望而生畏。与他们相处，学习他们的生活方式和事迹，见证他们作战与和平的方式，会让人产生一种奇怪的认知失调感：看到一个身披巨龙般动力盔甲、却有着高尚且富有同情心的灵魂的恶魔

。

Salamanders, and the Nocturneans to which they owe part of their heritage, are undoubtedly a deep and spiritual people, prone to introspection and self-analysis but also courageous and unstintingly humane. Each of the Chapter's warriors is a work of art in their forged armour plate and arms, and a living, breathing caldera of furnace heat and ashen redolence. They are true exemplars of what it means to be a defender of mankind: utterly selfless and yet terrifyingly infer-nal to behold. To spend time amongst them, learning of their ways and deeds, witnesing their manner of war and of peace, is to experience the strange cogni-tive dissonance of seeing a devil clad in draconic power armour but with a noble,compassionate soul.
