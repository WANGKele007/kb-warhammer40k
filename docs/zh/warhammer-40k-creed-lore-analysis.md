*"What do I ask of my officers? Merely that they do their duty with fire in their bellies and a prayer on their lips" *— Ursarkar E. Creed.

*“我对我的军官们有什么要求？仅仅是要求他们怀着怒火和嘴唇上的祈祷来履行职责”* — 乌撒卡·E·克里德。

Lord Castellan Ursarkar E. Creed (URR-sar-kar kreed) was a Lord General of the Astra Militarum and Lord Castellan of Cadia during the Thirteenth Black Crusade. He was the most renowned of all Cadian Shock Troopers and led Imperial forces through the most important battles that the Cadians have ever faced, from raids by the Eldar to the periodic Black Crusades led by the forces of Chaos.

领主堡主乌萨卡·E·克里德（乌-萨-卡·克里德）是第十三次黑色远征期间星界军的领主将军和卡迪亚的领主堡主。他是所有卡迪亚突击军中最著名的，带领帝国部队经历了卡迪亚有史以来最重要的战斗，从灵族的突袭到混沌部队领导的周期性黑色远征。

Ursarkar Creed & Colour Sergeant Kell 乌萨卡·克里德&上士雅兰·凯尔

Jarran Kell, Creed's loyal Colour Sergeant, was always found by his side and has fought through countless battles with him.

雅兰·凯尔，克里德忠诚的上士，总是在他身边，并与他一起进行了无数次战斗。

## Biography 传记

### Early life 早期生活

Creed was born in 959.M41 on Cadia. His father, formerly a Cadian Shock Trooper, had a sheiling in Kasr Gallan. When he was eight, cultists attacked Kasr Gallan and killed his father, his mother and his sister. He remained under a bed with the corpse of his sister, trying to hide himself, when a Space Marine, dressed in gleaming black power armour and swathed in cream-coloured robes that hid his face, lifted the bed and gave him a laspistol before speaking with a voice that was deep, ancient and lonely.

克里德于959.M41出生于卡迪亚。他的父亲曾是一名卡迪亚突击军，在加利安堡有一个牧舍。八岁时，邪教徒袭击了加利安堡，杀害了他的父亲、母亲和妹妹。他和妹妹的尸体一起躺在床下，试图躲起来，这时一名身穿闪闪发光的黑色动力盔甲、裹着米色长袍的星际战士掀开床，递给他一把激光手枪，然后用深沉、古老而孤独的声音说话。

What happened after that is unknown, but then Creed was found by Orsani Rudvald of the Cadian 8th in the ruins of the city of Kasr Gallan. In one hand was clutched that old laspistol, and in the other a tattered copy of De Gloria Macharius. Creed refused to speak of the atrocities he witnessed, or how long he was forced to survive on his own. The Cadian 8th were impressed by this boy's courage and determination and adopted him. When he was old enough, he joined the ranks. He quickly became an excellent soldier and was drafted into the Whiteshields, the elite legion for the young of Cadia. This is where he met his future Colour Sergeant Jarran Kell.

之后发生了什么尚不清楚，但后来克里德被卡迪亚第八兵团的奥尔萨尼·鲁德瓦尔德在加利安堡的废墟中发现。一只手抓着那把旧激光手枪，另一只手拿着《荣耀的马卡里乌斯》的破旧副本。克里德拒绝谈论他目睹的暴行，也拒绝谈论他被迫独自生存了多久。卡迪亚第八兵团对这个男孩的勇气和决心印象深刻，并收养了他。当他长大后，他加入了队伍。他很快成为一名优秀的士兵，并被征召进入白盾，这是卡迪亚年轻人的精英军团。在这里，他遇到了他未来的上士雅兰·凯尔。

### Whiteshield 白盾

Soon after becoming a whiteshield Creed would face the constant war and death expected of a Cadian, as he was thrust into the Drussite Crusade. By the time the Cadian forces were victorious, Creed had been promoted to Captain and Jarran Kell was made his Colour Sergeant. From this position, he often led from the front lines, earning him the respect of the men under his command. In official matters Creed was as disciplined as any Cadian officer, but he knew when to turn a blind eye, and as such is disliked by many Commissars.

成为白盾后不久，克里德将面临卡迪安人预期的持续战争和死亡，因为他被投入了德鲁斯特远征。当卡迪亚部队获胜时，克里德已被提升为连长，雅兰·凯尔被任命为他的上士。从这个位置，他经常从前线领导，为他赢得了部下的尊重。在官方事务中，克里德和任何卡迪亚军官一样有纪律，但他知道什么时候该视而不见，因此许多政委都不喜欢他。

### Officer 军官

*"**[Creed]** is the finest of the hunting hawks." *— AtillanKhan Harumann.

*“**[克里德]**是猎鹰中最优秀的。”* — 阿提拉人汗·哈鲁曼。

Creed rose quickly through the officer corps, proving to be a masterful strategist, adept at coordinating both defence and attack. He had a natural genius for commanding any unit of men, whether it was a platoon or an entire army, and his tactical genius was of a kind not seen in the Imperium for generations. In particular, he is legendary for his ability to infiltrate troops, even Super-Heavy War Machines behind enemy lines, and many commanders emulate his strategies in order to rapidly redeploy their own forces.

克里德在军官团中迅速崛起，被证明是一位技艺精湛的战略家，擅长协调防御和进攻。他天生就有指挥任何部队的天赋，无论是排还是整个军队，他的战术天赋是帝国几代人都没有的。特别是，他以渗透部队，甚至是敌方战线后超重型战争机器的能力而闻名，许多指挥官效仿他的策略，以便迅速重新部署自己的部队。

Creed discusses the defense of Cadia with Sven Bloodhowl 克里德与斯文·血嚎讨论保卫卡迪亚的问题

In 977.M41 Creed led the 8th Cadian on the world of Amistel, alongside ColonelSackville Slade from the 219th Albitern Grenadiers and Khan Harumann of an unnamed Attilan unit. Creed's willingness to not follow protocol, and his lowborn status, caused a row between the Cadian and Albitern officers (though the Attilan officer gained immense respect for the Cadian). After Creed gained favour with Field MarshalKassel, Colonel Slade was determined to *"teach Creed a lesson"*. He gathered twenty men from his own regiment, but his plans were foiled before they could begin. As punishment Colonel Slade was transferred to a penal unit.

977.M41，克里德率领卡迪亚第八兵团前往阿米斯泰尔世界，与第219阿尔比特恩掷弹兵的萨克维尔·斯莱德上校和一支无名的阿提拉部队的汗·哈鲁曼并肩作战。克里德不遵守礼仪的意愿，以及他出身低微的身份，导致了卡迪亚和阿尔比特恩军官之间的争吵（尽管阿提拉军官对卡迪亚人非常尊重）。克里德获得卡塞尔元帅的青睐后，斯莱德上校决心“给克里德一个教训”。他从自己的兵团里召集了二十个人，但他的计划在开始前就被挫败了。作为惩罚，斯莱德上校被转移到一个刑罚部队。

During the Hrud migration of 983-985.M41, Creed fought with Kell at his side throughout, and was commander of the forces that attacked and killed the Chaos LordBrule on Treconondal. He was then promoted to Lord General of Cadia, and his first victory was over the UlthwéEldarraid on Aurent in 992.M41. He was now the most successful living commander of Cadia and only his lowly birth held him back from holding the highest command positions. In 996.M41, Creed led the Cadian 8th on Besana against the Chaos cultists of Luciver Anckor, a faction called Anckorites.

在983-985.M41的赫鲁德迁徙期间，克里德始终与凯尔并肩作战，并担任在特雷科农达尔袭击并杀死混沌领主布鲁尔的部队指挥官。随后，他被提升为卡迪亚的领主将军，他的第一场胜利是在992.M41期间击败了奥伦特上的乌斯维灵族突袭。他现在是卡迪亚最成功的在世指挥官，只有出身卑微才让他无法担任最高指挥职务。996.M41，克里德率领卡迪亚第八兵团在贝萨纳对抗路西弗·安科尔的混沌邪教徒，一个名为安科尔派的派系。

### Battle of Tyrok Fields 蒂洛克领域之战

*"As is ever the case with the foul tricks of the heretic, their deeds draw forth greater resolve and courage from the faithful and few are more loyal in their faith than the General, Ursarkar E. Creed." *— LieutenantOrsani Ruvald, Archivist, 8th Cadian Regiment.

“就像异端的肮脏伎俩一样，他们的行为激发了忠诚者们更大的决心和勇气，很少有人比乌萨卡·E克里德将军更忠诚于他们的信仰。” — 卡迪亚第八兵团档案员奥尔萨尼·鲁德瓦尔德副官。

In 999.M41, right before the beginning of the 13th Black Crusade, units of the Volscani turned traitor on the Tyrok Fields, near Kasr Tyrok, during a mustering of Cadian, Volscani, and Mordian regiments. The exact cause is not fully known, some within the Inquisition suspected the Volscani feared to face the forces of Chaos again while others suspect they had fallen to the ruinous powers. Creed and Kell had been en route to the mustering in an 8th Cadian ornithopter when the Volscani turned on their fellow regiments.

999.M41，就在第13次黑色远征开始之前，沃斯卡尼的部队在蒂洛克堡附近的蒂洛克领域上叛变，当时卡迪亚、沃斯卡尼和莫迪安兵团正在集结。确切的原因尚不完全清楚，审判庭内部的一些人怀疑沃斯卡尼害怕再次面对混沌部队，而另一些人则怀疑他们已经落入毁灭力量之手。克里德和凯尔乘坐卡迪亚第8兵团的扑翼机前往集合的途中，沃斯卡尼向他们的同伴兵团开火。

Creed leads his men during the fall of Cadia in the 13th Black Crusade 克里德在第13次黑色远征的卡迪亚陷落期间领导他的部下

Creed's ornithopter landed, and he joined his command squad, taking personal command of his forces on the ground. Kell unfurled the banner of the 8th Cadian and begun the Imperium Gloriam chant among the regiment. Creed took a knee, and made the sign of the aquila across his chest. Some veteran guardsmen would later claim Creed was sensing the vibrations in the ground to know the correct time to launch and attack. Creed stood and then gave the order to affix bayonets and charge. Following the costly victory over the Volscani, Creed found the desecrated remains of Governor PrimusMarus Porelska. Creed wrapped the body in the banner of the 8th, and personally carried it from the battlefield.

克里德的扑翼机降落了，他加入了他的指挥组，亲自指挥地面部队。凯尔展开卡迪亚第八兵团的旗帜，开始在兵团中吟唱帝国荣光圣歌。克里德跪下，在胸前做了个天鹰手势。一些经验丰富的卫兵后来声称，克里德能够感知到地面的震动，从而知道正确的发动和攻击时间。克里德站了起来，然后下令挂上刺刀并冲锋。在对沃斯卡尼取得了代价高昂的胜利后，克里德发现了首席总督马鲁斯·波雷尔斯卡被亵渎的遗体。克里德把尸体裹在第八兵团的旗帜里，亲自从战场上抬了出来。

In times of emergency the special rank of Lord Castellan can be bestowed, either for life or until the crisis is over. Whilst initially there were opponents to Creed being given the title of Lord Castellan, eventually all stood down for the sake of unity. Gradually his potential political enemies stepped back and allowed Creed to be named Lord Castellan of Cadia. Creed refused the title three times, but realised the rank and file Cadians would only accept him as the Lord Castellan. This was met with jubilation among the rank and file Cadians.

在紧急情况下，可以授予领主堡主的特殊军衔，终身或直到危机结束。虽然最初有人反对克里德被授予领主堡主的称号，但最终所有人都为了团结而离开。渐渐地，他的潜在政敌后退了一步，允许克里德被任命为卡迪亚的领主堡主。克里德三次拒绝了这个头衔，但意识到普通的卡迪亚人只会接受他为领主堡主。这在普通的卡迪亚人中引起了欢呼。

### 13th Black Crusade 第十三次黑色远征

*"I am not averse to facing the enemy myself. Why should I be? In my right hand I hold the finest regiment of soldiers from a world of soldiers. In my left hand I hold the will and favour of the divine *Emperor of Mankind*." *— Ursarkar E. Creed

*“我自己并不反对面对敌人。我为什么要反对呢？我的右手握着士兵世界中最优秀的士兵团。我的左手握着神圣人类帝皇的意志和恩惠。”* — 乌萨卡·E·克里德

Ultimately, Creed had to fight off Abaddon's 13th Black Crusade, the most devastating Chaos crusade yet. With Jarran Kell at his side, he managed to co-ordinate the forces surrounding Cadia to prevent the dark gods from gaining a hold in the material universe, but only at the cost of many lives and even one of the planets of the Cadian system.

最终，克里德不得不击退阿巴顿的第13次黑色远征，这是迄今为止最具破坏性的混沌远征。在雅兰·凯尔的陪伴下，他设法协调了卡迪亚周围的部队，以防止黑暗诸神在物质宇宙中占据一席之地，但代价是许多生命，甚至是卡迪亚星系的一颗行星。

Creed meets with Cawl on Cadia during the 13th Black Crusade 克里德在第13次黑色远征期间在卡迪亚与考尔会面

During the final fighting on Cadia, Kell was killed to buy time for Creed to escape from the Bringers of Despair and he linked up with various other Imperial commanders including Belisarius Cawl, Saint Celestine, Orven Highfell, Greyfax, and Marius Amalrich. In the final battle beneath the surface of Cadia, Creed and his allies sought to delay Abaddon's advance until the Pylons of Cadia could be fully activated. Avenging Kell, Creed was able to save Saint Celestine and Greyfax during their duel against Abaddon, but lost an arm in the process. As Abaddon was about to finish off his hated adversary, he was impaled from behind by Celestine.

在卡迪亚的最后一场战斗中，凯尔被杀是为了给克里德争取时间，让他逃离绝望的边缘，他与其他帝国指挥官联合，包括贝利萨留·考尔、圣塞勒斯汀、奥尔文·高落、格雷法克斯和马吕斯·阿马尔里奇。在卡迪亚地表下的最后一场战斗中，克里德和他的盟友试图推迟阿巴顿的进攻，直到卡地亚的方尖碑完全被激活。克里德为凯尔报仇，在与阿巴顿的决斗中拯救了圣塞勒斯汀和格雷法克斯，但在此过程中失去了一只手臂。当阿巴顿正要干掉他讨厌的对手时，他被塞勒斯汀从后面刺穿了。

Creed watches the invasion of Cadia 克里德目睹卡迪亚入侵

However, Abaddon was ultimately able to destroy Cadia by redirecting remnants of the Blackstone FortressWill of Eternity into its surface, destabilizing its pylons and pulling the planet into the Warp. Creed and his Cadian 8th bought time for the Imperial forces on Cadia to evacuate, holding back endless hordes of Daemons. As Cadia died around him, Creed accepted his fate but was confronted by a giant in scaled armour. The giant laughed and said that Creed's time need not to end here. The giant proved to be the Necron OverlordTrazyn the Infinite, who put Creed in a gallery on Solemnace, labelled the Last Stand of Ursarkar Creed.

然而，阿巴顿最终能够摧毁卡迪亚，将黑石要塞永恒意志号的残余重新定向到其地表，破坏其方尖碑的稳定，并将行星拖入亚空间。克里德和他的卡迪亚第八兵团为卡迪亚上的帝国部队的撤离争取了时间，阻止了无尽的恶魔大军。当卡迪亚在他身边死去时，克里德接受了自己的命运，但却遇到了一个身穿鳞甲的巨人。巨人笑着说，克里德的时代不必就此结束。这位巨人被证明是死灵霸主无限者塔拉辛，他将克里德放在索勒姆纳斯的长廊里，被标记为乌萨卡·克里德的最后一战。

### Legacy 遗产

Ursarkar was succeeded as Castellan of Cadia by his daughter with an Imperial chamberlain, Ursula J. Creed. Creed left behind a data-crystal in his personal vault on Cadia shortly before his death regarding a contingency for his people should Cadia be destroyed in the coming war with Abaddon. Ursula has been successful in recovering this crystal, and afterwards her father's cloak was mysteriously discovered within her personal chambers.

乌萨卡的女儿乌苏拉·J·克里德和一名帝国管家接替了他的职务，成为了卡迪亚的堡主。克里德在他牺牲前不久在卡迪亚的个人秘库里留下了一个数据水晶，关于卡地亚在即将到来的与阿巴顿的战争中被摧毁时，他的人民的应急措施。乌苏拉成功地找到了这块水晶，后来她父亲的斗篷在她的私人房间里神秘地被发现了。

## Equipment 装备

Before the Fall of Cadia, Creed used two laspistols for a very long time. One of them was an old Mars-pattern model, battered, scratched and with a faded serial number stencilled in small white letters across one side; probably the same pistol he was found with in Kasr Gallan. The other was a more ornate piece, designed for an officer, with brass fittings to the handle and an aquila on the barrel.

在卡迪亚陷落之前，克里德长时间使用两把激光手枪。其中一个是旧的火星型设计，破旧不堪，有划痕，一侧用白色小字母印着褪色的序列号；可能就是他在加利安堡被发现时的那把手枪。另一件是为军官设计的更华丽的一个，手柄上有黄铜配件，枪管上有一个天鹰。

## Trivia 琐事

As part of his debut in Codex: Imperial Guard (5th Edition), Ursarkar Creed came with the special rule *Tactical Genius*, which could designate a single infantry or vehicle model to have the Scouts special rule, allowing said unit the potential to infiltrate behind enemy lines. As the rule on publication did not specify *what kind* of vehicle or make any exclusions, players were allegedly using it to infiltrate Super-Heavy Vehicles and Imperial Titans leading to various jokes among players and humorous memes on the internet about Creed and his "tactical genius". This would seemingly be indirectly acknowledged by Games Workshop itself in a Warhammer Community article in relation to the "Gifted Commander" rule in 9th edition. This rule allowed for Super-Heavy Vehicles to be infiltrated, but not Titans.

作为他在圣典：帝国卫队（第5版）中首次亮相的一部分，乌萨卡·克里德附带了特殊规则战术天才，该规则可以指定一个步兵或载具模型具有侦察特殊规则，使该部队有可能渗透到敌后。由于发布规则没有具体说明哪种载具或做出任何排除，据称玩家正在使用它渗透超重型载具和帝国泰坦，导致玩家之间出现各种笑话，互联网上出现了关于克里德和他的“战术天才”的幽默模因。这似乎是GW本身在战锤社区在第9版关于“天才指挥官”规则的文章中间接承认的。这条规则允许超重型载具被渗透，但不允许泰坦。

### Conflicting sources 来源冲突

The novel Cadia Stands states in Chapter Two that Creed is currently forty years old. In Chapter One it states that He had only just become a General at age forty and then states he has been a General for at least two years, with a longer career hinted at. White Dwarf 283 (AU) states Creed had been a General by 977.M41.

小说《卡迪亚屹立不倒》在第二章中指出，克里德目前已经四十岁了。在第一章中，它指出他在四十岁时才成为将军，然后说他已经当了至少两年的将军，并暗示了更长的职业生涯。白矮人第283期（AU）说克里德在977.M41之前已经成为将军了。
