The Aurox Armoured Transport was an armoured personnel carrier used by the Solar Auxilia, Imperialis Militia and Space Marine Legions, during the Great Crusade and Horus Heresy.

金牛装甲运输车是太阳辅助军、帝国民兵和星际战士军团在大远征和荷鲁斯叛乱期间使用的装甲运兵车。

Aurox Armoured Transport 金牛装甲运输车

### Overview 概述

Similar in design to that of the Rhino Armoured Carrier used by the Space Marine Legions, the Aurox was made to be mass-manufactured, reproducible and easily serviced by units in the field. In order to better protect its crew, the Aurox was given a rear section that was even bulkier and more heavily armoured than mainline battle tanks, and its modular design allowed for additional armour and weapons configurations. One such design was the Saturnyne Pattern Aurox, which was re-fitted for extreme environmental conditions and was used by the Solar Auxilia.

金牛的设计与星际战士军团使用的犀牛装甲运输车相似，可大规模制造，可重复使用，易于现场维修。为了更好地保护其乘员，金牛被赋予了一个比主流战斗坦克更庞大、装甲更重的后部，其模块化设计允许额外的装甲和武器配置。其中一种设计是土星型金牛，它被重新定做以适应极端环境条件，并被太阳辅助军使用。

In battle the Aurox's main function was to get grenadier units into position to bombard their foes, or to give Imperial Commanders protection as they planned their next strike.

在战斗中，金牛的主要功能是让掷弹兵单位就位轰炸敌人，或者在帝国指挥官计划下一次打击时为其提供保护。
