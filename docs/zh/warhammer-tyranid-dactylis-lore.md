A Dactylis is a large Tyranid creature designed to launch Spore Mines from long range with machine-like precision. Its forelimbs are long, multi-jointed and covered in corded tendon and muscle that make a distinctive cracking sound when used to hurl its payload.

指虫是一种大型的泰伦生物，旨在以机器般的精度从远距离发射孢子雷。它的前肢很长，多关节，覆盖着绷紧的肌腱和肌肉，在投掷有效载荷时会发出独特的爆裂声。

Dactylis 指虫

When the Imperium initially encountered the Dactylis, its ammunition was a parasitic creature known as a Bile Pod which fed off glands at the rear of the Dactylis's cranium. In effect they were similar to a Poison Spore Mine. In more recent encounters the Dactylis has displayed spore-generating colonies of bacteria on its cranial glands which are now able to provide a variety of ammunition.

当帝国最初遇到指虫时，它的弹药是一种被称为胆汁囊的寄生生物，它以指虫颅骨后部的腺体为食。实际上，它们类似于毒素孢子雷。在最近的遭遇中，指虫在其颅骨腺体上显示出产生孢子的细菌菌落，这些菌落现在能够提供各种弹药。
