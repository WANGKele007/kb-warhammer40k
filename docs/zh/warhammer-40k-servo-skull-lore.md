Servo-skulls are drone-like familiar devices fashioned from human skulls – often those of pious Imperial servants, low-ranking tech-adepts, tech-priests with unfinished work, or loyal adepts of the Adeptus Terra – and then given a rudimentary machine spirit, support systems, and anti-gravity engine to allow them to hover and drift bodiless through the air. A favoured servant, loyal functionary or other servant of the Imperium might see this as is a great honour, being able to continue their work even after death.

伺服颅骨是由人类颅骨制成的类似无人机的常见设备 — 通常是虔诚的帝国之仆、低级技术专家、未完成工作的技术神甫或中央政务部的忠诚专家 — 然后被赋予基本的机魂、支援系统和反重力引擎，使它们能够在空中无身盘旋和漂移。一名受宠的仆人、忠诚的官员或帝国的其他仆人可能会认为这是一种巨大的荣誉，即使在死后也能继续工作。

Servo-skull 伺服颅骨

## Overview 概述

When the Adeptus Mechanicus creates a servo-skull, they reverently prepare the removed cranium with oils and salves, then implant it with grav-drives and powercells. The residual blobs of grey matter are preserved and sealed within, then threaded through with biowire and psycho-reactive crystals.

当机械神教创造一个伺服颅骨时，他们虔诚地用油和软膏准备被移除的颅骨，然后植入反重力驱动和动力电池。残留的灰质斑点被保存并密封在里面，然后用生物丝和精神反应晶体穿过。

Servo-skulls are most strongly associated with the Adeptus Mechanicus, but are used throughout the organisations of the Imperium to perform specific tasks for which they are installed with the basic equipment required, usually a pair of simple manipulator pincers, a weapon, a pict recorder, a projector, surgical implants, stab-light, loudhailer, or various other devices for the many roles that exist. Though they typically take the form of skulls with various mechanical appendages, other designs have also been utilised. The lords of House Draconis for example, utilised set of five servo-skulls crafted to resemble various mythical creatures, the Pegassus, the Chimaer, the Minot, the Wyvornne and the Dracon in reference to heraldic icons of each Knight House of Adrastapol.

伺服颅骨与机械神教最密切相关，但在帝国的各个组织中都被用来执行特定的任务，它们安装了所需的基本设备，通常是一对简单的操纵钳、武器、录像机、投影仪、手术植入物、刺灯、扩音器或各种其他设备，用于现有的许多角色。虽然它们通常采用带有各种机械附件的头骨形态，但也使用了其他设计。例如，德拉科尼斯家族的领主们使用了一套五个伺服颅骨，这些颅骨被精心制作成各种神话生物的样子，如天马、奇美拉、米诺陶、飞龙和巨龙，以纪念阿德拉斯塔波尔的每个骑士家族的纹章图标。

The posthumous honour of becoming a Servo-skull is only reserved for the most pious and devoted Imperial servants 成为伺服颅骨的死后荣誉只留给最虔诚和最忠诚的帝国之仆

The roles they fulfill include Librarium functionaries, medical assistants, lumen-bearers, proclaimers or scroll-carriers. Some are designed for military roles, and among these some are built with enhanced optical sensors to allow them to fulfill a scouting role.

他们所扮演的角色包括图书馆工作人员、医疗助理、流明持有者、宣传员或卷轴携带者。有些是为军事角色设计的，其中一些是用增强的光学传感器制造的，使其能够发挥侦察作用。

They are also often used by Inquisitors as Familiars. In this case they are often mentally linked to the Inquisitor, allowing him to control them and see and hear through their senses. In general, Servo-skulls are useful for their ability to enter otherwise inaccessible or hazardous areas because of their small size and great manoeuvrability.

他们也经常被审判官用作使魔。在这种情况下，他们往往在精神上与审判官联系在一起，让他能够控制它们，通过它们的感官去看和听。一般来说，伺服颅骨因其体积小、机动性强，能够进入其他人无法进入或危险的区域，因此非常有用。

Strategus Pattern Servo Skull equipped with a compact las-weapon 战略型伺服颅骨，配备紧凑激光武器

Servo-skulls will traverse Imperial Navy vessels, and other voidships, through special passageways through walls and other tight crawlspaces, allowing them to move between levels and other areas like a Ratling. However, a servo-skull's rigidity and various attachments often don't allow them to squeeze through as tight of gaps as the small Abhumans. When not in use, servo-skulls might be found docked in charging roosts.

伺服颅骨将穿过帝国海军战舰和其他虚空舰，穿过穿越墙壁和其他狭窄爬行空间的特殊通道，使它们能够像老鼠一样在层和其他区域之间移动。然而，伺服颅骨的刚性和各种附件通常不允许它们像小型亚人那样挤过狭窄的缝隙。不使用时，伺服颅骨可能会停靠在充能舱中。

Servo-Skulls can be dangerous, even without any overt weaponry attached. Mundane tools and implements, such as a plasma torch, can be weaponised to varying degrees. The cortical tail of a servo-skull can be used as a garrotte and strangle the life from a baseline human. Such a response could be due to a preprogrammed response, such as to a security violation, or due to the servo-skull being remotely operated by another individual, such as a tech-priest. A servo-skull could also serve as a lure, guiding perceived threats into traps or environmental hazards. These actions could be taken due to programmed defensive protocols of the Mechanicus, or a reaction from some remnants of lingering consciousness, or perhaps a combination of the two.

即使没有任何公开的武器，伺服颅骨也可能是危险的。世俗的工具和器具，如等离子手电筒，可以在不同程度上被武器化。伺服颅骨的皮质尾部可以用作绞索，扼杀基础人类的生命。这种反应可能是由于预编程的反应，例如违反安全规定，也可能是由于伺服颅骨被另一个人（如技术神甫）远程操作。伺服颅骨也可以作为诱饵，将感知到的威胁引导到陷阱或环境危害中。这些行动可能是由于机械教的程序化防御协议，也可能是由于挥之不去的意识残余的反应，或者可能是两者的结合。

The Magi Psykana with more heretical leanings say that although servitors and servo-skulls have no minds, they still retain their souls, and these suffer terribly.

具有更多异端倾向的灵秘圣贤士说，虽然机仆和伺服颅骨没有思维，但他们仍然保留着自己的灵魂，这些灵魂遭受着极大的痛苦。

## Servo-skull patterns 伺服颅骨型号

Aggression Enhancer Servo-skull 侵略性增强伺服颅骨

Aggression Enhancer Servo-skulls are used by the Adeptus Mechanicus and are equipped with binharic speakers. They constantly blurt out the Omnissiah's benedictions to smite the unworthy, and use Beta-wave generators that stimulate the aggression centres of biological and cybernetic minds alike.

侵略性增强伺服颅骨由机械神教使用，并配备了双声道扬声器。他们不断地脱口而出欧姆尼赛亚的祝福来打击不值得的人，并使用贝塔波发生器来刺激生物和智控思维的侵略性中心。

Data Skull 数据颅骨

Data Skulls contain a data screen set into their backs. This makes them the perfect item for relaying important targeting data, recording the findings of Inquisitorial investigations and many other uses.

数据颅骨的背部装有一个数据屏幕。这使得它们成为传递重要目标数据、记录调查结果和许多其他用途的完美物品。

Data Skull 数据颅骨

Gheistskull 鬼魂颅骨

Gheistskulls are made from the skulls of deceased Imperial Navy Breachers and carry compact explosives. They accompany Breacher teams during boarding attacks.

鬼魂颅骨由已故帝国海军破坏者的颅骨制成，携带紧凑型炸药。他们在跳帮攻击时陪伴破坏者小队。

Gheistskull 鬼魂颅骨

Guardian-Skull 守护颅骨

The Guardian-Skull serves as an uncommon but effective personal bodyguard. It has a somewhat reinforced structure and mounts a single weapon system guided by a targeting selection program. They are also equipped with small arms and additional weapons. Few servo-skulls are aggressive, being typically reserved for clerical, data retrieval, or data gathering tasks. As such, they are often overlooked and few perceive them as a threat to be destroyed. Servo-skulls can often assail a target unnoticed, acting as a small and fast delivery method for poisons, tracking devices, or extreme violence.

守护颅骨是一个不常见但有效的个人护卫。它有一个稍微加固的结构，并安装了一个由目标选择程序引导的单一武器系统。他们还配备了小型手臂和其他武器。很少有伺服颅骨具有攻击性，通常用于文书、数据检索或数据收集任务。因此，它们经常被忽视，很少有人认为它们是一种需要被摧毁的威胁。伺服颅骨通常可以在不被注意的情况下攻击目标，作为毒药、追踪装置或极端暴力的小型快速传递方法。

Guardian-Skulls are further subdivided into two different types, depending on weapons configuration.

根据武器配置，守护颅骨进一步细分为两种不同类型。

Combat- or Dissector-Skull 战斗或解剖颅骨

The Combat- or Dissector-Skull mounts several articulated limbs fitted with mono-edged blades or, rarer, with electro-flails or chain knives. It is activated by voice command. These dissector-skulls can also aid in surgical assistance on biological matter with their mono-bladed scalpel.

战斗或解剖颅骨安装了几个铰接的肢体，这些肢体装有单刃刀片，或者更罕见的是，装有电链枷或链锯刃。它由语音命令激活。这些解剖颅骨也可以用它们的单刃手术刀在生物物质上提供手术辅助。

Gun-Skull 枪炮颅骨

The Gun-Skull can mount a single firearm from a selection of pistols or other compact weapons; usually equipped with a laspistol and able to aim with machine accuracy. It can also be equipped with an autocarbine or lascarbine. Alongside this it also carries a red-dot laser sight.

枪炮颅骨可以从一系列手枪或其他紧凑型武器中安装一支枪；通常配备激光手枪，能够以机器精度瞄准。它还可以配备自动卡宾枪或激光卡宾枪。除此之外，它还携带了一个红点激光瞄准器。

Infoslave Skulls 信息奴隶颅骨

Mono-task Infoslave Skulls make ideal lookouts for the Adeptus Mechanicus, to watch against the sudden deployment of enemy troops. Infoslave skulls are outfitted with the most advanced data recording known and constantly gather data on environment, enemies, the effects of enemy weapons on friendly troops across their broad-spectrum optics. They constantly back up their own data and transmit it to the nearest Adeptus Mechanicus open frequency. These skulls are often assigned to senior Skitarii, other times they are sent ahead of battles to obtain vital tactical data.

单任务信息奴隶颅骨是机械神教的理想观察员，可以监视敌军的突然部署。信息奴隶颅骨配备了已知最先进的数据记录，并通过其广谱光学系统不断收集有关环境、敌人、敌人武器对友军影响的数据。他们不断备份自己的数据，并将其传输到最近的机械神教开放频率。这些颅骨通常被分配给高级护教军，有时它们会在战斗前被发射以获取重要的战术数据。

Monitor Skulls 监控颅骨

Monitor Skulls are used by Alecto'sVarangantuan Enforcers and patrol with the Security Forces' squads. Their red ocular sensorsare equipped with facial recognition systems, so the Servo-skulls can scan the Hive World's citizens for wanted fugitives and their spinal columns end in a viciously curved barb the Monitors can use as a weapon. They also patrol the Enforcers' Bastion headquarters and give vocal alarms of *"Unauthorised weapon detected."* to summon the Enforcers, should their scanners discover a civilian has entered the buildings with weapons.

阿莱克托的瓦朗甘图安执法者使用监控头骨，并与安全部队的小队一起巡逻。他们的红色视觉传感器配备了面部识别系统，因此伺服颅骨可以扫描巢都世界的公民，寻找被通缉的逃犯，他们的脊柱末端有一个恶毒的弯曲倒钩，监恐者可以将其用作武器。他们还巡逻执法者堡垒总部，并发出“检测到未经授权的武器”的声音警报。如果他们的扫描仪发现一名平民携带武器进入大楼，他们可以召集执法者。

Monotask-Skull 单任务颅骨

The Monotask-Skull is the most common form of servo-skull. It specialises in a single task and is fitted with the required equipment. Most Monotask-Skulls also mount a basic set of extendable manipulator pincers, onboard navigation and a pict-relay to record or to transmit visuals back to its owner or to a linked cogitator.

单任务颅骨是伺服颅骨最常见的形态。它专门从事单一任务，并配备了所需的设备。大多数单任务颅骨还安装了一套基本的可扩展操纵钳、机载导航和图片中继，用于记录或将视觉效果传输回其主人或链接的沉思者。

Auger-Skull 占卜颅骨

The Auger-Skull mounts an auspex and a vox-data system to transmit information.

占卜颅骨安装了一个鸟卜仪和一个音阵数据系统来传输信息。

Corpus Mymir 智慧文集

The Corpus Mymirs were Great Crusade and Horus Heresy-era Adeptus Mechanicus Servo-Skulls, that contained the brains of Psykers.

智慧文集是大远征和荷鲁斯叛乱时代的机械伺服颅骨，其中包含了灵能者的大脑。

Courier-Skull 信使颅骨

The Courier-Skull functions as a messenger and mounts a specialised identity scanner. The message it relays can be a scroll, a data-slate or a holo-recording. It is also preprogrammed with extensive maps of the local area.

信使骷髅充当信使，并安装了专门的身份扫描仪。它传递的信息可以是卷轴、数据板或全息记录。它还预先编程了当地广泛的地图。

Data Interrogator 数据查询者

Data Interrogators (also called Override Skulls, Skeleton Keys and Skulls of the Omnissian Martyr) are often made from high-ranking Magi, enginseers, and esteemed experts in Lingua-technis. They are adept at single-mindely extracting data or bypassing cogitative locks or barriers. Once locked into place, their mechadendrites burrow into cogitator interfaces and automatically begin overriding barriers. These pieces of technology are divisive among Tech-Priests, as they afford the uninitiated access to a fraction of the Adeptus Mechanicus' esoteric knowledge.

数据查询者（也称为覆盖颅骨、骷髅钥匙和欧姆尼赛亚殉教者颅骨）通常由高级圣贤士、工造士和受人尊敬的语言技术专家制成。它们擅长一心一意地提取数据或绕过思维锁或障碍。一旦锁定到位，它们的机械附肢就会钻入沉思者接口，并自动开始越过障碍。这些技术在技术神甫中引起了分歧，因为它们为外人提供了一小部分机械神教的秘传知识。

A Data Interrogator is programmed with a single directive before deployment, which can range from overriding servitors or cogitators in buildings and machinery, to harvesting information from datavaults. The programming rarely translates to reliability in the field.

数据查询者在部署前使用单个指令进行编程，其范围可以从超控建筑物和机器中的机仆或沉思者，到从数据库中收集信息。编程很少转化为现场的可靠性。

Dominator Beacon 统御信标

Dominator Beacons are custom-built Iron Hands Servo-skulls, that the Chapter use as mobile sentries for vital strategic sites.

统御信标是定制的钢铁之手伺服颅骨，战团将其用作重要战略地点的移动哨兵。

Illuminator-Skull 照明颅骨

The Illuminator-Skull is used for illumination and mounts a lamp-pack, a glow-globe or just a burning brazier of coals, especially if the skull is operated by the Ecclesiarchy.

照明颅骨用于照明，并安装了一个灯组、一个发光球或一个燃烧的煤盆，特别是如果颅骨是由国教操纵的。

Loud Hailer-Skull 高呼颅骨

The Loud Hailer-Skull mounts a powerful public address system to relay messages, sounds or even just the owner’s voice.

高呼颅骨安装了一个强大的公共广播系统，可以传递信息、声音，甚至只是主人的声音。

Medicae-Skull 医疗颅骨

The Medical-Skull features a built-in medi-kit and a medical scanner to administer first aid. It is also known as a Medi Skull on Necromunda.

医疗颅骨配备了一个内置的医疗工具包和一个医疗扫描仪，用于进行急救。它在涅克罗蒙达上也被称为治疗颅骨。

Necromundan Medi Skull 涅克罗蒙达治疗颅骨

Motion-Augur Skull 运动占卜颅骨

A Motion-Augur Skull (also called a Deadrover, Tracer Skull or Vigil Pattern Sensor Skull) has various forms of alarms and klaxons. Their primary purpose is to deter intruders and thieves, but can also be specially designed to deafen or cause mental unrest in trespassers. Often times these skulls are keyed to notify the appropriate authorities when triggered. These roving motion detectors are more subtle and tirelessly diligent than any human guard.

运动占卜头骨（也称为死亡漫游者、示踪颅骨或警戒型感知颅骨）具有各种形态的警报器和电喇叭。它们的主要目的是威慑入侵者和小偷，但也可以专门设计为使入侵者耳聋或引起精神不安。通常情况下，这些颅骨会被键入，以便在触发时通知有关当局。这些移动的运动探测器比任何人类警卫都更敏锐、更不知疲倦。

Utility-Skull 多功能颅骨

The Utility-Skull mounts a multi-tool to perform simple preprogrammed maintenance tasks. It can also be remotely-operated by its owner to perform functions in otherwise inaccessible areas.

多功能颅骨安装了一件多工具，可以执行简单的预编程维护任务。它也可以由其所有者远程操作，在其他无法进入的区域执行功能。

Hydro-Skull 水能颅骨

Hydro-Skulls are equipped to put out fires and spray out water from their spines. They are used on the Imperium's spaceships.

水能颅骨装备有灭火和从脊椎喷水的能力。它们被用于帝国的宇宙飞船上。

Scryerskull 占星颅骨

One of them are wired into Belisarius Cawl's armoured carapace. The relic servo-skull Scryerskull Perspicatus reads the battlefield as a cascade of binary code giving its users a information about treasures of the battlefield and the weaknesses of enemy vehicles found wanting in the Perspicatus’ sight.

其中一个被连接到贝利撒留·考尔的装甲甲壳中。圣物伺服颅骨占星颅骨洞察将战场视为一系列二进制代码，为使用者提供有关战场宝藏和洞察视线中发现的敌方载具弱点的信息。

Servo Commissar-skull 伺服政委颅骨

伺服政委颅骨预先编程了祷文和训诫，由星界军政委使用。

Servo-Designators 伺服指示者

Servo-Designators are used by the Astra Militarum. Released as swarms from modified Valkyries, these high altitude servo-skulls analyse targeting data from ongoing combat operations to provide updated firing solutions to the troops operating in the theatre.

星界军使用伺服指示者。这些高空伺服颅骨是从经过改装的女武神发射的，它们分析正在进行的战斗行动的目标数据，为在战区作战的部队提供最新的射击解决方案。

Servo-scribe 伺服抄写员

Servo-scribes are pairs of Servo-skulls that function as Scribes. They can be attached to Astra MilitarumCommand Squads.

伺服抄写员是一对伺服颅骨，起着抄写员的作用。他们可以隶属星界军指挥组。

Skull Probe 颅骨探测仪

The Skull Probe is a military device utilized by Space Marine Chapters for reconnaissance. It is capable of moving by stealth, and can fly faster than most units can move on foot. The Skull Probe is also capable of detecting other units concealed by stealth technology. Though unarmed, they can generate an electromagnetic pulse that temporarily neutralises an enemy structure, though the Skull Probe will be destroyed in the process.

颅骨探测仪是星际战士用于侦察的军事设备。它能够隐形移动，飞行速度比大多数单位步行快。颅骨探测仪还能够探测到被隐形技术隐藏的其他单位。虽然没有武器，但它们可以产生电磁脉冲，暂时无效敌方结构，尽管颅骨探测仪在此过程中会被摧毁。

Shroud-Skulls 寿衣颅骨

Shroud-Skulls are specialized Servo-skulls that are equipped with auspex dampers, vox-stummers and electroshrouding incense. Small flocks are used by Phobos Strike Teams to surround an encampment, before the Shroud-Skulls use their equipment to conceal it from inquisitive foes.

寿衣颅骨是一种专门的伺服颅骨，配备了鸟卜仪抑制器、音阵沉默者和电遮蔽熏香。火卫一打击小队使用小群人包围营地，然后寿衣颅骨使用他们的装备向好奇的敌人隐藏营地。

Spider Cyber-Familiars 蜘蛛智控使魔

Spider Cyber-Familiars were Servo-skulls that crawled on the ground with six bladed legs and had two clawed arms on their fronts. They acted as Familiars for Legionaries during the Great Crusade and Horus Heresy, while also being able to follow orders and operate independently, far from their owners, if need be.

蜘蛛智控使魔是伺服颅骨，用六条带刃的腿在地上爬行，前肢有两只带爪的手臂。在大远征和荷鲁斯叛乱期间，他们充当了军团士兵的使魔，同时也能够听从命令并独立行动，如果需要的话，远离他们的主人。

Spider Cyber-Familiar of Gerr Lomand 格尔·洛曼德的蜘蛛智控使魔

Spider Skulls 蜘蛛颅骨

Spider Skulls are one of the more gruesome servo skulls inhabiting a hive, using two human hands to scurry about instead of a suspensor system. They typically lurk in the air-ducts and try to drop onto targets in order to inject their venom from hypodermic ‘fangs’.

蜘蛛颅骨是栖息在巢都中较为可怕的伺服颅骨之一，它用两只人手而不是悬浮系统四处乱跑。它们通常潜伏在空气管道中，试图降落到目标上，以便从皮下“尖牙”注射毒液。

Tome-Skull 典籍颅骨

Tome-Skulls are Servo-skulls that carry Imperial tomes.

典籍颅骨是携带帝国典籍的伺服颅骨。

Vigil-skull 警戒颅骨

Vigil-skulls are used by the Astra Militarum to spy on their foes. The Servo-skulls are deployed in groups and are equipped with vox-thieves and pict-feeds. These are then transmitted to those operating the Vigil-skulls and the pict-feeds are displayed on a Hololithic.

警戒颅骨被星界军用来监视他们的敌人。伺服颅骨被分组部署，并配备了音阵窃取者和图片供给。然后，这些信息会被传输给那些操作警戒颅骨的人，图片供给会显示在全息上。

Vox Skull 音阵颅骨

Vox Skulls (also called Messenger Skulls or Messenger-martyrs) are Monotask Servo-skulls dedicated as messenger tools. They are equipped with vox-replay technologies. The most honoured skulls are blessed with Pict Recorders and small hololithic projectors to display a subject's likeness.

音阵颅骨（也称为信使颅骨或信使殉道者）是专用于信使工具的单任务伺服颅骨。它们配备了语音回放技术。最受尊敬的颅骨配有图片记录仪和小型全息投影仪，可以显示主题的画像。

Vox Skull 音阵颅骨

Messenger Skulls can record messages spanning hours, and their cogitators possess enough navigational and biometric data to seek out the message's intended recipient within a limited sphere, such as a level of a hive city, small downship, or the confines of a voidship. Although the skull will search tirelessly to find the intended recipient, it is typically given a location to begin its search.

信使颅骨可以记录跨越数小时的消息，它们的沉思者拥有足够的导航和生物识别数据，可以在有限的范围内（如巢都城市、小型下船或虚空舰的范围内）找到消息的预期接收者。尽管颅骨会不知疲倦地寻找预期的接受者，但通常会给出一个开始搜索的位置。

Watch-skull 观察颅骨

Watch-skulls are Servo-skulls that help their owners search out foes in the dark.

观察颅骨是伺服颅骨，可以帮助主人在黑暗中寻找敌人。
