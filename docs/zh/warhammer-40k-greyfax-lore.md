Katarinya Greyfax (rosette number 7134/S3) is a Radical (though she was initially a Puritan) Inquisitor of the Ordo Hereticus.

卡塔琳娅·格雷法克斯（玫瑰结编号7134/S3）是讨逆修会的激进派（尽管她最初是纯洁派）审判官。

Katarinya Greyfax 卡塔琳娅·格雷法克斯

### Biography 传记

Greyfax was imprisoned within a Tesseract Vault by the Necron OverlordTrazyn the Infinite for centuries until she was released, along with his other prisoners, to aid the Imperium's forces on Cadia during the 13th Black Crusade. Despite being a powerful Psyker herself, when Greyfax emerged she was shocked at the sight of Imperial psykers using their powers without sanction and to see the Space WolvesChapter'sWulfen running rampant on the battlefield. What left her utterly appalled, though, was seeing how the Imperial defenders of Cadia worshiped the so-called Living Saint, Celestine, as it was nothing short of idolatry in Greyfax's mind. These sights, however, have not shaken her unwavering faith in the Emperor and Greyfax is committed to aiding the Imperium in defending Cadia. Greyfax ultimately worked alongside these psychic abominations and mutants in battling Abaddon personally.

格雷法克斯被死灵霸主无尽者塔拉辛囚禁在超立方体墓穴中长达几个世纪，直到她和他的其他囚犯一起被释放，以在第13次黑色远征期间协助帝国在卡迪亚的部队。尽管格雷法克斯本人是一名强大的灵能者，但当格雷法克斯出现时，她看到帝国灵能者在没有约束的情况下使用他们的力量，并看到太空野狼的狼人在战场上肆虐，感到震惊。然而，让她完全震惊的是，看到卡迪亚的帝国守军是如何崇拜所谓的活圣人塞勒斯汀的，因为在格雷法克斯看来，这简直是偶像崇拜。然而，这些景象并没有动摇她对帝皇的坚定信仰，格雷法克斯致力于帮助帝国保卫卡迪亚。格雷法克斯最终与这些精神上的可憎之物和变种人并肩作战，亲自对抗阿巴顿。

Ultimately, however, Cadia fell and Greyfax fled to the outer system with several of her allies, such as Saint Celestine and Belisarius Cawl. Hounded by the Black Legion, they were guided into the Webway on Kalisus by Eldrad Ulthran.

然而，最终，卡迪亚陷落，格雷法克斯和她的几名盟友，如圣塞勒斯汀和贝利萨留·考尔，逃到了外围星系。在黑色军团的追击下，他们被埃尔德拉德·乌斯兰引导进入卡利苏斯的网道。

Inquisitor Greyfax during the Escape from Klaisus 卡利苏斯撤离期间的审判官格雷法克斯

Greyfax accompanied Celestine during the subsequent Ultramar Campaign and fought in the Fortress of Hera as Roboute Guilliman was reborn. She took part in the resulting Terran Crusade and was one of the few of the Imperial expedition to reach Terra. During the voyage she finally put her suspicion toward unnatural entities like Celestine past her, and was freed from Trazyn's Mindshackle Scarab by Belisarius Cawl. Once on Terra, Celestine and Greyfax announced their intent to spread their blessings across Terra and report to their masters in the Ordo Hereticus.

格雷法克斯在随后的奥特拉玛战役中陪伴塞勒斯汀，并在赫拉要塞作战，因为罗伯特·基里曼重生了。她参加了由此产生的泰拉远征，是为数不多到达泰拉的帝国远征队之一。在航行中，她最终将对塞勒斯汀等非自然实体的怀疑抛诸脑后，并被贝利萨留·考尔从特拉辛的锁心圣甲虫中解救出来。一旦踏上泰拉，塞勒斯汀和格雷法克斯宣布他们打算在泰拉上传播他们的祝福，并向讨逆修会的大师报告。

Later, Inquisitors Greyfax and Horst and a squad of Grey Knights were sent by Roboute Guilliman to recover the Eye of Night. The two inquisitors entered the Eye of Terror, seeking the location of the Eye of Night from Moriana, who revealed that Horst knew the location but that he was suffering from amnesia. She gave them a daemonic guide who led them to a daemon prince who held Horst's body and the Eye of Night. Greyfax killed the daemon (and Horst in the process), recovering the Eye of Night from within its body.

后来，审判官格雷法克斯和霍斯特以及一队灰骑士被罗伯特·基里曼派去取回午夜之眼。两名审判官进入恐惧之眼，从莫里亚纳那里寻找午夜之眼的位置，莫里亚纳透露霍斯特知道这个位置，但他有记忆缺失。她给了他们一个恶魔般的指引，带领他们找到了一个恶魔王子，他抓着霍斯特的身体和午夜之眼。格雷法克斯杀死了恶魔（以及霍斯特在此过程中），从其体内恢复了午夜之眼。

Greyfax has been active in combating the enemies of mankind since her return, such as working with the White Scars to set a trap of Deathstrike Missile Launchers that obliterated the Death Guard on the world of Dortwyr. The Inquisitor has found recent prominence due to her fanatical purges of Necron forces localized around Ultramar.

格雷法克斯自回归以来一直积极与人类之敌作战，例如与白色伤疤合作，设置了一个死亡打击导弹发射器的陷阱，摧毁了多特韦尔世界的死亡守卫。这位审判官最近因对奥特拉玛周围的死灵部队的狂热净化而声名鹊起。

Inquisitor Greyfax 审判官格雷法克斯

She later became embroiled in the affairs of EcclesiarchDecius XXIII, who was attempting to repeal the Decree Passive. She saved the Ecclesiarch from several assassination attempts alongside Saint Celestine. The two servants of the Emperor bonded during the journey to Ophelia VII to protect the Ecclesiarch. In the end, it was revealed the entire affair was a plan by the Tyrant of Blueflame to manifest into the Materium by taking Celestine's body. After the Tyrant was defeated, the two parted ways.

后来，她卷入了试图废除被动法令的教宗德西乌斯二十三世的事务。她与圣塞勒斯汀一起，从几次暗杀企图中拯救了教宗。帝皇的两个仆人在前往奥菲利亚七号的途中结成了同盟，以保护这位教宗。最后，人们发现整个事件是蓝焰暴君的一个计划，通过夺取塞勒斯汀的身体来显现到物质界中。暴君战败后，两人分道扬镳。

By the several year mark of the Indomitus Crusade, Greyfax had taken control of the Inquisitorial Conclave based on Luna and oversaw the fortress there. She spoke to her subordinate Rostov on the matter of the Hand of Abaddon seeking to reconstruct the Anathame.

到不屈远征的几年大关时，格雷法克斯已经控制了以月球为基地的审判庭秘会，并监督了那里的堡垒。她与下属罗斯托夫就阿巴顿之手寻求修复仪式之刃的问题进行了交谈。

At some point, Inquisitor Greyfax executed the Ordo Xenos Inquisitor Helynna Valeria, who, like Greyfax, was once discovered to be under the influence of Trazyn's Mindshackle Scarabs.

在某个时刻，审判官格雷法克斯处决了攘外修会审判官赫琳娜·瓦莱里亚，她和格雷法克斯一样，曾被发现受到塔拉辛的锁心圣甲虫的影响。

Later, having learned of the many and varied threats to the Imperium, she adopted the Radical mindset. In the "new" galaxy, all means are good - as long as they serve the good of Humanity itself.

后来，在了解了帝国面临的许多不同威胁后，她采取了激进的观念。在“新”宇宙中，一切手段都是好的 - 只要它们为人类自身的利益服务。

### Weapon and Equipment 武器和装备

Katarinya Greyfax is armed with a master-crafted power sword, a master-crafted condemnor boltgun, and a psyocculum – the *Eye of the Emperor*.

卡塔琳娅·格雷法克斯配备了一把大师级动力剑、一把大师级宣判者爆矢枪和一件灵能观测器 — 帝皇之眼。
