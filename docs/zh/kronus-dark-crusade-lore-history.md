 Kronus alternatively Cronus is an Imperial planet and a Tomb World where the Dark Crusade took place. It was also part of the Shadow Crusade during Horus Heresy.

 克洛诺斯是一个帝国行星，也是一个发生黑暗远征的墓穴世界。这也是荷鲁斯叛乱时期暗影远征的一部分。

## **History 历史**

 Kronus is a Tomb World, the Necrons went in to stasis on Kronus after their battle with the Old Ones over 60 million years ago.

 克罗诺斯是一个墓穴世界，在6000多万年前与古圣作战后，死灵在克罗诺斯进入了静滞状态。

 During Horus Heresy, commanded by Eliphas the Inheritor, Word Bearers alongside World Eaters invaded the Deimos Peninsula area of the planet, desecrating it and butchering most of the occupants. This prompted a counter-attack from Ultramarines. It turned out to be a trap however, and the small loyalist force was slaughtered.

 在荷鲁斯叛乱期间，由继承者艾里法斯指挥，怀言与吞世者一起入侵了行星的火卫二半岛地区，亵渎了该地区并屠杀了大多数居住者。这引发了极限战士的反击。然而，事实证明这是一个陷阱，小规模的忠诚部队被屠杀了。

 Tech-priests are known to have brought the Hellstorm Cannon of the Imperator-class Emperor Titan Aquila Ignis of the heretical Legio Mortis to Kronus after the Horus Heresy. A Daemon of Nurgle has manifested itself in the Hellstorm Cannon due to the fact that the Aquila Ignis crew, Princep Rufus Aquila and the Moderati under his command gave themselves to Nurgle during the Horus Heresy.

 众所周知，在荷鲁斯叛乱之后，技术神甫将异端死亡军团的帝皇泰坦火鹰号的地狱风暴炮带到了克罗诺斯。纳垢恶魔出现在地狱风暴炮中，因为火鹰号首席和他指挥的节制者在荷鲁斯叛乱期间将自己交给了纳垢。

 Magos Paladius was dispatched by the Fabricator General to Kronus, shortly after the Horus Heresy, to oversee the study of the Hellstorm Cannon of the Imperial-class Emperor Titan Aquila Ignis. Magos Paladius is known to have established mining complexes in the Hyperion Peaks to aid with the construction of secondary research facilities. Magos Paladius is also known to have explored Kronus.

 在荷鲁斯叛乱之后不久，铸造将军派遣贤者帕拉迪乌斯前往克罗诺斯，监督帝国级帝皇泰坦天鹰号的地狱风暴炮的研究。众所周知，帕拉迪乌斯贤者在亥伯龙峰建立了采矿建筑群，以帮助建设二级研究设施。帕拉迪乌斯贤者也曾探索过克罗诺斯。

 Abandoned by the Imperium of Man, the planet eventually came under the sway of the emergent Tau Empire. It was a largely peaceful colony, until an Imperial Guard Regiment under the leadership of General Lukas Alexander arrived upon the planet, in pursuit of the Eldar Farseer Taldeer, due to her actions during the war on Lorn V. Taldeer, in turn, had arrived upon Kronus to battle the recently emerged Necrons. The Necrons had woken up after an Imperial archeological expedition accidentally opened their crypt.

 被人类帝国抛弃后，这个行星最终受到了新兴的钛帝国的影响。这是一个基本上和平的殖民地，直到卢卡斯·亚历山大将军领导的一个帝国卫队兵团抵达这个行星，追捕灵族先知塔迪尔，由于她在洛恩五号战争期间的行动，塔迪尔又抵达克罗诺斯，与最近出现的死灵作战。在帝国考古探索者意外打开他们的墓穴后，死灵苏醒了过来。

 The Ork Warboss Gorgutz 'Ead 'Unter had also made his way to Kronus, following the collapse of his Waaagh! on Lorn V. After the Ork Warboss had "landed" his Rok on the Green Coast, he quickly set about subjugating the Feral Orks on the planet, in an attempt to rebuild his Waaagh!

 欧克战争头目猎头者戈古兹也在他的Waaagh!崩溃后前往克罗诺斯，在欧克战争头目将他的巨石“降落”在绿色海岸后，他迅速着手征服行星上的凶猛的欧克，试图重建他的Waaagh！

 The Word Bearers, under the leadership of the Dark Apostle Eliphas the Inheritor, had come to Kronus in order to avenge their defeat at the hand of the Ultramarines during the Horus Heresy. Opening a Warp Gate in the Deimos Peninsula, they sought to turn the planet into a Daemon World.

 在黑暗使徒继承者艾里法斯的领导下，怀言者来到克罗诺斯，为他们在荷鲁斯叛乱期间在极限战士手中的失败报仇。他们在火卫二半岛打开了一个亚空间门，试图将这个行星变成一个恶魔世界。

 The Blood Ravens Space Marines, led by young Brother-Captain Davian Thule, arrived on Kronus in search of artefacts left behind by their forebears. Striking from their Castellum Incorruptus in North Vandea, they sought to eliminate all other factions on the planet, including the Imperial Guard soldiers under General Alexander.

 由年轻的兄弟连长戴维安·图勒领导的血鸦星际战士抵达克罗诺斯，寻找他们祖先留下的造物。他们从北万代亚的不坏城堡出发，试图消灭行星上所有其他派系，包括亚历山大将军领导下的帝国卫队士兵。

 The Ethereal Caste, the leaders of the Tau Empire, could not, and would not, allow one of their colonies to fall without putting up a fight. Thus, they sent Aun'El Shi'Ores and Shas'O Kais to the planet to reconquer it in the name of the Greater Good.

 作为钛帝国的领导者，以太阶级不能也不会允许他们的一个殖民地在不进行战斗的情况下沦陷。因此，他们派遣了奥'埃尔·希'奥尔和沙斯'欧·凯斯前往这个行星，以上上善道的名义重新征服它。

 The conflict waged was a brutal one, with each faction engaging in heavy fighting with one another. In the end however, the indomitable command of Davian Thule saw victory for the Imperium, despite the decimation of Imperial Guard forces under General Alexander. Davian Thule struck hard and fast from his bulwark in North Vandea. He broke the back of each and every enemy force on Kronus; Farseer Taldeer fell to her death in the cold snow before his forces as her battered and broken warriors fled the world. The Necron Lord and deathly manifestation of the Night Bringer were defeated beneath the Thur'Abis Plateau, Davian Thule and his warriors personally delivering an explosive to the heart of those catacombs which burried that place in rubble. Gorgutz saw yet another of his Waaagh!'s crushed, but in his typical notorious fashion fled the world. Eliphas too suffered defeat at the hands of Thule's Blood Ravens and the Force Commander personally witnessed that Dark Apostle torn to bloody ribbons by his dark masters. Aun'El Shi'Ores was slain by the stoic advance of the Space Marines too and his body recovered by the hastily retreating Tau forces, an emotionally broken Shas'O Kais leading his defeated survivors off the world.

 这场冲突是残酷的，每个派别都相互进行了激烈的战斗。然而，最终，尽管亚历山大将军率领的帝国卫队遭到重创，但戴维安·图勒的顽强指挥为帝国带来了胜利。戴维安·图勒从北万代亚的堡垒猛烈而快速地攻击。他击溃了克罗诺斯的每一支敌军；先知塔迪尔在她的部队之前在寒冷的雪地中死亡，她那些满身伤痕的战士逃离了世界。死灵领主和拥夜者的死亡化身在瑟'阿比斯高原下被击败，戴维安·图勒和他的战士们亲自将炸药运送到埋在废墟中的地下墓穴的中心。戈古兹又看到了他的Waaagh！被压垮了，但以他典型的著名方式逃离了世界。艾里法斯也在图勒的血鸦手中战败，部队指挥官亲自目睹了黑暗使徒被他的黑暗主人撕成血丝。奥'埃尔·希'奥尔也被星际战士的坚忍不拔的推进所杀，他的尸体被匆忙撤退的钛部队发现，情绪崩溃的沙斯'欧·凯斯带领他被击败的幸存者离开了世界。

## **Places of Note 注意地点**

Headquarters Regions 总部区域

 - 

Deimos Peninsula: Once the most beautiful place on Kronus, the Deimos Peninsula was turned into a pure hellscape after the arrival of the Word Bearers on the planet. It now houses the Warp portal responsible for bringing more of the Chaos Marines onto the planet.

 - 

火卫二半岛：曾经是克罗诺斯上最美丽的地方，在怀言者到达行星后，火卫二半岛变成了一个纯粹的地狱景观。它现在拥有亚空间门户，负责将更多的混沌星际战士带到这个行星上。

 - 

Thur'Abis Plateau: Sitting in the middle of the great desert on Kronus, the plateau was actually a catacomb of Necron origin, and this is where the Necron emerged to destroy the living once again.

 - 

瑟'阿比斯高原：坐落在克罗诺斯大沙漠的中央，该高原实际上是死灵起源的地下墓穴，这是死灵再次毁灭生者的地方。

 - 

Green Coast: Situated in the South Western part of the continent, the Green Coast is the main habitat for the Orks living on the planet. After having "landed" his Rok in the area, Warboss Gorgutz 'Ead 'Unter has made this his base of operations.

 - 

绿色海岸：位于大陆的西南部，绿色海岸是生活在行星上的欧克的主要栖息地。在将巨石“降落”到该地区后，战争头目猎头者戈古兹将其作为行动基地。

 - 

North Vandea: Situated on the north eastern coast, the region of North Vandea houses the Blood Ravens Castellum Incorruptus, and serves as their main base of operations on Kronus.

 - 

北万代亚：位于东北海岸，北万代亚地区拥有血鸦城堡，是他们在克罗诺斯的主要行动基地。

 - 

Victory Bay: Originally known as "Ironworks Bay", the city was renamed after the forces led by Governor-Militant Lukas Alexander discovered the ancient Hellstorm cannon, taken from an Imperator Titan, in the middle of the city. It has since been named the Imperial capitol on the planet.

 - 

胜利湾：最初被称为“钢铁厂湾”，在军务总督卢卡斯·亚历山大领导的部队在城市中心发现了从帝皇泰坦手中拿走的古代地狱风暴炮后，该城更名。此后，它被命名为行星上的帝国首都。

 - 

Or'es Tash'n: Known as Asharis during the Imperial era, Tash'n is the Tau Empire capital on Kronus, and houses a large number of Tau and Kroot inhabitants. It is situated slightly to the north-west of the Thur'Abis Plateau.

 - 

奥'雷斯·塔什'恩：在帝国时代被称为阿沙里斯，塔什'恩是克罗诺斯的钛帝国首都，居住着大量的钛和克鲁特居民。它位于瑟'阿比斯高原西北部。

 - 

Tyrea: Located on the far northwestern corner of the embattled continent, Tyrea is the site of Taldeer's primary base of operations on Kronus. A large gateway connects the base to her wraithship on the edge of the Kronus system.

 - 

蒂雷亚：位于危机四伏的大陆的西北角，蒂里亚是塔迪尔在克罗诺斯的主要作战基地。一个巨大的门户将基地连接到克罗诺斯星系边缘的她的幽冥舰。

Other Important Regions 其他重要地区

 - 

Pavonis: A great city located roughly in the centre of the embattled regions, to the north of the Thur'Abis Plateau, Pavonis is the planet's primary spaceport. The region is home to an Adeptus Mechanicus tracking system combined with Tau Air Caste technology, which would allow those who controlled it to avoid enemy fire while making deep strikes from orbit, making attacks anywhere on the planet (with the exception of the headquarters regions) possible.

 - 

帕沃尼斯：帕沃尼斯是一座伟大的城市，大致位于危机四伏地区的中心，位于瑟'阿比斯高原以北，是行星的主要太空港。该地区拥有与钛空氏族技术相结合的机械神教跟踪系统，这将使控制它的人能够避开敌人的火力，同时从轨道上进行深入打击，从而可以在行星上的任何地方（总部地区除外）进行攻击。

 - 

Vandean Coast: The planet's primary manufacturing facilities are located in the Vandean Coast. Though dormant, it can be reactivated by an attacking force, allowing more production of troops and vehicles for the war effort.

 - 

万代恩海岸：该行星的主要制造设施位于万代恩海岸。虽然处于休眠状态，但它可以被进攻部队重新激活，从而为战争生产更多的部队和载具。

 - 

Eres Badlands: Located west of the Deimos Peninsula, the Eres Badlands is the location of a powerful Daemon of Khorne imprisoned beneath the surface, seeking the blood of mortals to feed its power. In return for providing the souls demanded, the Daemon would grant any who could control it the power to make multiple attacks.

 - 

厄里斯恶地：位于地狱火半岛以西，厄里斯恶地是一个强大的恐虐恶魔的所在地，他被囚禁在地表之下，寻求凡人的鲜血来滋养自己的力量。作为提供所需灵魂的回报，恶魔将授予任何能够控制它的人进行多次攻击的力量。

 - 

Hyperion Peaks: Located north of the Green Coast. With its extremely rich minerals and Manifactorias in almost perfect condition, the owner of this land can deploy forward bases on his/her campaign, which is a devastating advantage in battle. But it's not easy to capture it, due to it is in a middle of one of Kronus greatest deserts, the commander have to face a heavily dug in enemy, no support lines on his/her own, he/she have to send in a single strike team to purge any resistance before the dig in could be established.

 - 

亥伯龙峰：位于绿色海岸以北。凭借其极其丰富的矿产和近乎完美的铸造厂，这片土地的所有者可以在他/她的战役中部署前沿基地，这在战斗中是一个毁灭性的优势。但要占领它并不容易，因为它位于克罗诺斯最大的沙漠之一的中部，指挥官必须面对一个深挖的敌人，自己没有支援线，他/她必须派遣一个打击小队在深挖之前清除任何抵抗。

 - 

The Aceria Forests: Located north of the Deimos Peninsula. The owner of this province gains increased manpower that allows for greater squad and vehicle capacity.

 - 

阿塞里亚森林：位于迪莫斯半岛北部。该行省的所有者获得了更多的人力，从而增加了小队和载具的容量。
