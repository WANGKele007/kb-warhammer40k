The Fall of the Lords of Gardinaal is a famous battle of the Iron Hands which took place during the Great Crusade.

加迪纳尔领主之陨是大远征期间发生的一场著名的钢铁之手的战斗。

## Overview 概述

The invasion itself was focused on the human civilization of Gardinaal, whose rulers were trying to ready themselves for an inevitable Imperial attack by stalling with protracted negotiations. When the Thousand Sons detachment with Imperial emissaries sensed that the Gardinaal diplomats were attempting to use psychic abilities to influence talks, the Astartes massacred them and gave the Imperium the reason it finally needed for war. It fell to the 413rd Expeditionary Fleet under Ulan Cicerus of the XVth Chapter of the UltramarinesSpace Marine Legion to conduct the campaign to subdue Gardinaal. The fleet included Marnean Armsmen and Serranic Peltasts of the Imperial Army as well as the Legio AtarusTitan Legion.

入侵本身的重点是加迪纳尔的人类文明，其统治者试图通过拖延谈判来为不可避免的帝国袭击做好准备。当千子分遣队与帝国特使意识到加迪纳尔外交官试图利用灵能能力影响谈判时，阿斯塔特屠杀了他们，并给了帝国战争最终所需的理由。它落在了极限战士星际战士军团第十五战团的乌兰·西塞勒斯率领的第413远征舰队手中，负责指挥征服加迪纳尔的战役。舰队包括帝国军队的马尔南武装军和塞拉尼克轻盾军，以及炎纹军团泰坦军团。

After swiftly breaking the Gardinaal orbital defences, Imperial forces began to land on the surface of the planet. However the Imperial Army troops were pushed back due to Gardinaal's formidable aerial fleet and air defence artillery. The Gardinaal forces then used STC based Rhinos and Predators armed with particle weaponry to besiege whatever bridgeheads the Imperial Army was able to establish. Cicerus responded by deploying his Ultramarines and Titans directly onto the Gardinaal flanks, but this too was met with defeat when the Gardinaal forces responded with nuclear weapons. After the atomic bombardment that decimated both sides, Gardinaal scout-titan sized quadrupedal heavy walkers appeared and pushed the Marines and Imperial Titans back. Half of the Firebrand Legions demi-legio, significant numbers of Ultramarines, and 500,000 Imperial Army troops were lost in the failed attack.

在迅速突破加迪纳尔的轨道防御后，帝国部队开始在行星表面登陆。然而，由于加迪纳尔强大的空中舰队和防空火炮，帝国军队部队被击退。加迪纳尔部队随后使用基于STC的犀牛和掠食者，配备粒子武器，围攻帝国军队能够建立的任何桥头堡。西塞勒斯的回应是直接将他的极限战士和泰坦部署到加迪纳尔侧翼，但当加迪纳尔部队用核武器回应时，这也遭到了失败。在摧毁双方的原子弹轰炸之后，加迪纳尔侦察泰坦大小的四足重型步行者出现了，并将星际战士和帝国泰坦击退。在这次失败的袭击中，一半的炎纹军团、大量的极限战士和50万帝国军队士兵损失。

With its invading forces bled, the 413rd Expeditionary Fleet could only maintain a blockade of the Gardinaal system. It was then that Ferrus Manus leading a force of Iron Hands and Emperor's Children arrived from the Warp as reinforcements. Taking command of the operation from Cicerus, Manus began by launching a deadly retribution against Gardinaal. The worlds primary cities were wiped out in brutal orbital bombardments. Meanwhile, the Emperor's Children contingent with the Iron Hands sowed panic and death with lightning-fast raids. Shortly thereafter, the Iron Hands began their own landing operation.

随着入侵部队的流血，第413远征舰队只能维持对加迪纳尔星系的封锁。就在那时，费鲁斯·马努斯率领钢铁之手和帝皇之子作为援军从亚空间赶来。马努斯从西塞勒斯手中接过行动的指挥权，开始对加迪纳尔进行致命的报复。世界上的主要城市在残酷的轨道轰炸中被摧毁。与此同时，钢铁之手和帝皇之子用闪电般的突袭播下了恐慌和死亡的种子。不久之后，钢铁之手开始了自己的登陆行动。

After rapidly capturing their initial objectives, the Iron Hands dug in and established a defensive network of automated defences (such as the Tarantula) and Gun Servitors. This freed up the Iron Hands themselves for the next phase of Ferrus' plan, using their Land Raiders to brutally advance on Gardinaal positions and wither them down with unrelenting firepower in conjunction with Emperor's Children swift raids. Seeing defeat near, the Lords of Gardinaal at last caved in. Desperately, they tried to sue for peace but their pleas were ignored. Anarchy broke out among Gardinaal's forces as soldiers and civilians alike attempted to flee the Imperial onslaught. However the Iron Hands and accompanying Imperial titans cut them down with a vengeful wrath. After refusing Gardinaal's unconditional surrender, Ferrus Manus devastated the planet's capital city with artillery and Titans. The Emperor's Children soon landed on the cities bastions to destroy whatever defences remained.

在迅速占领了最初的目标后，钢铁之手开始挖掘并建立了一个由自动防御（如狼蛛）和枪炮机仆组成的防御网络。这释放了钢铁之手自己，为费鲁斯计划的下一阶段做好了准备，他们利用他们的兰德掠袭者残酷地向加迪纳尔阵地推进，并与帝皇之子的快速突袭相结合，用无情的火力将其消灭。眼看战败在望，加迪纳尔领主最终屈服了。他们绝望地试图寻求和平，但他们的请求被忽视了。当士兵和平民都试图逃离帝国的猛攻时，加迪纳尔的部队爆发了混乱。然而，钢铁之手和随行的帝国泰坦以复仇的愤怒将他们击倒。在拒绝了加迪纳尔的无条件投降后，费鲁斯·马努斯用火炮和泰坦摧毁了这个行星的首都。帝皇之子很快登陆了城市的堡垒，摧毁了剩下的防御工事。

The Ultramarines were given the honour of being the first into the breach, having been the first Astartes to set foot on the planet. The Ultramarines faced a desperate and spirited counter-attack by surviving Gardinaal forces which proved to inflict surprisingly high casualties on the Astartes. Soon the Lords of Gardinaal themselves rode into battle on their great walking tanks, particle cannons blazing. Beneath these vehicles advanced waves of Rhinos and Predators modified with explosives to become suicide vehicles. Behind them came the last of the Lords remaining forces, carapace armoured retainers pumped full of combat drugs and enforcement walkers rigged with flechette cannons and hydraulic claws. Cicerus himself was cut down before this final desperate attack.

极限战士有幸成为第一个进入缺口的，他们是第一个踏上行星的阿斯塔特。极限战士面临着幸存的加迪纳尔部队的绝望和激烈的反击，事实证明，这给阿斯塔特造成了惊人的高伤亡。不久，加迪纳尔领主自己骑着他们巨大的步行坦克，粒子炮熊熊燃烧，投入战斗。在这些载具下方，前方的犀牛和掠食者用炸药改装成自杀式载具。在他们身后是最后一批领主的残余部队、用尽战斗药物的装甲仆人和装有箭弹炮和液压爪的执行步行者。西塞勒斯本人在这次最后的绝望袭击之前被击倒了。

The Lords of Gardinaal declared triumph at this turn of events, but before them now strode Ferrus Manus himself leading a contingent of Iron Hands Terminators. Unleashing a wave of precise Lascannon fire, the Lords and their walkers were cut down. Emperor's Children aircraft meanwhile circled above, making sport of cutting down Gardinaal defenders. Manus himself then leaped into the fray, breaking the last Lords of Gardinaal. The few enemies who remained fled to wherever they could, but to the surprise of all involved Ferrus did not order to exterminate the survivors. Instead he simply uttered *"It is done"* and left the planet to Imperial authorities.

加迪纳尔领主在这一转折点上宣布胜利，但在他们面前，费鲁斯·马努斯本人率领一支钢铁之手终结者分遣队大步前进。释放出一波精确的激光炮火力，领主和他们的步行者被击倒了。与此同时，帝皇之子的飞行器在上空盘旋，以击倒加迪纳尔的守军为乐。马努斯自己也加入了战斗，击倒了最后的加迪纳尔领主。剩下的少数敌人逃到了他们能逃到的地方，但令所有相关人员惊讶的是，费鲁斯没有下令消灭幸存者。相反，他只是简单地说了一句“结束了”，然后把这个行星留给了帝国当局。
