The Thunderbolt heavy fighter is the workhorse of the Imperial Navy. A rugged and reliable design, with good firepower and manoeuvrability, the Thunderbolt has been in service for centuries and remains well-liked by its crews.

霹雳重型战斗机是帝国海军的主力。坚固可靠的设计，良好的火力和机动性，霹雳已经服役了许多个世纪，仍然受到其乘员的喜爱。

Thunderbolt Fighter 霹雳战斗机

## Overview 概述

The Thunderbolt primarily serves as an air superiority fighter, given the task of hunting down enemy bombers or engaging enemy fighters in order to establish air superiority over the battlefield. However one of the chief advantages of the design is its versatility, allowing it to fulfil other types of missions. Thunderbolt fighters frequently serve as high altitude escort fighters for flights of Marauder Bombers. They can also be adapted to serve as low level fighter-bombers, night fighters and reconnaissance aircraft. Typically, Imperial Navy Thunderbolt Wings operate thirty aircraft.

霹雳主要用作空中优势战斗机，其任务是狩猎敌方轰炸机或与敌方战斗机交战，以在战场上建立空中优势。然而，该设计的主要优点之一是其多功能性，使其能够完成其他类型的任务。霹雳战斗机经常作为掠夺者轰炸机航行的高空护航战斗机。它们也可以被改装为低空战斗轰炸机、夜间战斗机和侦察机。通常，帝国海军霹雳联队运营着30架飞机。

Thunderbolt cockpit 霹雳驾驶舱

Attrition among Thunderbolt pilots is enormous, with few surviving for very long once deployed and generally only 14% of pilots making it three past combat zones before meeting their end. Those that do survive may become deadly fighter aces remembered as heroes of the Imperium. Besides the Navy, the Thunderbolt is also operated by the Tech-Guard of the Adeptus Mechanicus.

霹雳飞行员的损耗是巨大的，一旦部署，很少有人能存活很长时间，通常只有14%的飞行员在经过三个战区后到达终点。那些幸存下来的人可能会成为致命的王牌战士，被人们铭记为帝国的英雄。除了海军，霹雳还由机械神教的技术卫队操作。

## History 历史

The Thunderbolt has been in Imperial service for over ten millennia, thanks to its versatility, having been used in battles as great as the defence of the Imperial Palace to even prior during the Great Crusade and the Horus Heresy, with the Thunderbolt formed the bulk of the intra-atmospheric fighter aircrafts of the Imperialis Armada and was used to support the landing operations and battlefield advances of the Solar Auxilia. During this period of service, the Thunderbolt has only been modified in minor ways for the myriad of different environments it has served in.

霹雳已经在帝国服役了一万多年，这要归功于它的多功能性，甚至在大远征和荷鲁斯叛乱之前，它就已经被用于像帝国皇宫防御这样伟大的战斗中，霹雳是帝国无敌舰队大气层内战斗机的主要组成部分，用于支援太阳辅助军的着陆行动和战场推进。在此服役期间，霹雳仅针对其服役过的无数不同环境进行了细微的修改。

During the Scouring various alternative weapon fits to better combat heavy tanks and Titans were trialed, but none were very successful, throwing the Thunderbolt off-balance and affecting the whole aircraft. These trials were finally ended with the development of the Marauder.

在大清洗期间，各种适合更好地对抗重型坦克和泰坦的替代武器被试用，但都没有非常成功，使霹雳失去平衡并影响了整架飞机。这些试验最终随着掠夺者的发展而结束。

## Technical Specifications 技术规格

The Thunderbolt is a tough aircraft to destroy, built around an armoured superstructure and cockpit, made from armorplas and ceramite.

霹雳是一种很难摧毁的飞机，围绕上层结构装甲和驾驶舱建造，由装甲塑料和陶钢制成。

Thunderbolt heavy fighter 霹雳重型战斗机

Twin plasma ramjets or twin F122v afterburning turbofans gives the Thunderbolt a good top speed in atmospheric flight, while a rocket booster engine allows it to operate in the vacuum of space and for fast take-offs when mounted on ground-based hydraulic ramps. The Thunderbolt should not be confused with a true "star-fighter" like the Fury Interceptor though, as its space capabilities are used primarily when deploying from an orbiting spacecraft into atmospheric sub-orbital descents or likewise returning by engaging the rocket booster engine after reaching the Thunderbolt's atmospheric ceiling.

双等离子冲压发动机或双F122v后燃涡扇使霹雳在大气飞行中具有良好的最高速度，而火箭助推引擎使其能够在太空的真空中运行，并在安置在地面液压坡道上时快速起飞。不过，霹雳不应与像狂怒拦截机这样的真正的“星际战斗机”混淆，因为它的太空能力主要用于从轨道空舰部署到大气层亚轨道下降时，或者在到达霹雳的大气层上限后通过启动火箭助推器引擎返回时。

The Thunderbolt incorporates aircraft systems controls that feature life support, tactical and targeting readouts for all weapons as well as a damage diagnosis system. All these information can be directly accessed by the pilot through the tactical display of the Ryza pattern type IV flight helmet. These vital systems have backups in case of a malfunction in the helmet.

霹雳融合了飞机系统控制，具有生命支持、所有武器的战术和瞄准读数以及损伤诊断系统。飞行员可以通过瑞扎型IV型飞行头盔的战术显示器直接访问所有这些信息。这些重要系统有备份，以防头盔发生故障。

The Thunderbolt is equipped with a cogitator, a heads-up display, incorporating a gunsight allowing for target lock-on, an auspex that includes a lock-on warning and collision warning system, electric autoloaders for the autocannons, a gun-cam, a vox linked to other aircrafts and mission control, a rear pict unit, afterburners and flare countermeasure-launchers. Thunderbolts used by the Solar Auxilia further mounted Flare Shields and Simulacra repair systems.

霹雳配备了一个沉思者、一个平视显示器，其中包含一个允许锁定目标的瞄准器、一个包括锁定警告和碰撞警告系统的鸟卜仪、一个用于自动炮的电动自动装弹机、一个枪炮摄像头、一个与其他飞机和任务控制的音阵链接、一台后置拍照单元、加力燃烧室和闪光对策发射器。太阳辅助军使用的霹雳进一步安装了闪光防护和拟像维修系统。

The Thunderbolt also has vector-nozzles, allowing it to move in a non ballistic way by shifting sideways, nearly turning end-on-end, reversing thrust and pulling a near one-eighty turn, braking up- or downwards during flight, coming almost to a standstill, or turning forward momentum into a gentle hover. The vector thrust system also allows the Thunderbolt to perform vertical take-off and landing-operations both downwards and upwards by swivelling the nozzles.

霹雳还配备了矢量喷口，使其能够以非弹道方式移动，包括侧向移动、几乎完全翻转、反向推进和将近180度转弯、在飞行过程中向上或向下制动、几乎静止或将前进动量变成温和的悬停。矢量推力系统还允许霹雳通过旋转喷嘴向下和向上执行垂直起飞和着陆操作。

The Thunderbolt can be modified for better night-fighting by mounting black, antler-like antennae arrays on the nose. It can also be modified, if necessary, to have its weapons controlled by a voice-activator, allowing for weapons switching and firing by voice-command.

霹雳可以通过在机头安装黑色鹿角状天线阵列进行改装，以获得更好的夜间战斗效果。如有必要，还可以对其进行修改，使其武器由语音激活器控制，允许通过语音命令切换武器和射击。

Quad nose-mounted Autocannons serve as the primary dogfighting and anti-infantry weapons, while a pair of twin-linkedLascannons are normally used to engage enemy ground vehicles. Four pylons, two mounted underneath each wing, allow the fighter to carry up to four extra fuel tanks, Bombs, Hellstrike Missiles or, more rarely, air-to-air missiles. The Thunderbolts of the Solar Auxilia were able to carry four Kinetic Piercer Missiles or Sunfury Heavy Missiles. It can also mount additional equipment.

四门前置自动炮是主要的狗斗和反步兵武器，而一对双联激光炮通常用于与敌方地面载具交战。四个塔架，每个机翼下安装两个，允许战斗机携带多达四个额外的油箱、炸弹、狱击导弹，或者更罕见的空对空导弹。太阳辅助军的霹雳能够携带四枚动能穿孔导弹或日怒重型导弹。它还可以安装其他装备。

### Thunderbolt Fury 霹雳狂怒

The Thunderbolt Fury is one of the most common variants of the standard Thunderbolt fighter. This variant replaces the fighter's Autocannons with a pair of twin-linked Avenger Bolt Cannons. The combination of mass-reactive Bolt shells and the high rate of fire makes for a dogfighting aircraft of punishing repute. Many squadrons see a Fury or two amongst their ranks, though few consist of Furies alone as they are known to eat through ammunition at an astonishing rate.

霹雳狂怒是标准霹雳战斗机最常见的变体之一。这种变体用一对双联复仇者爆矢炮取代了战斗机的自动炮。质量反应爆矢弹和高射速的结合使一架狗斗飞机名声大振。许多中队在他们的队伍中看到一两架狂怒，尽管很少有单独由狂怒组成，因为众所周知，他们会以惊人的速度消耗弹药。
