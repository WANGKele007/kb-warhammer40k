Ignatius Cardinal (also known simply as Ignatius) is a world of the Imperium.

伊格纳修斯枢机（也简称伊格纳修斯）是一个帝国世界。

## Overview 概述

The planet hosts a Schola Progenium facility, known as the Schola Progenium Prime of Ignatius. The Schola was located within visible distance of Ignatius (the world's capital city) and the main Ecclesiarchy Temple on the planet, whose towers stretched over two kilometres into the air. The world was noted to be cold, wet and dark.

这颗行星上有一个忠嗣学院设施，被称为伊格纳修斯第一忠嗣学院。学院位于伊格纳修斯（世界的首都城市）和行星上主要的国教圣殿的可见距离内，其塔楼高达两公里。世界以寒冷、潮湿和黑暗而闻名。

Notable alumni include Colonel-CommissarIbram Gaunt and CommissarVaynom Blenner.

著名的校友包括兵团政委伊布拉姆·冈特和政委维诺姆·布伦纳。
