The Svenotar Scout Trike is a model of three-wheeled Trike that is used in Necromunda's Ash Wastes by the Kin of the Ironhead Squat Prospectors.

斯维诺塔尔侦查三轮车是三轮的摩托车设计，由铁首矮人勘探者的族裔在涅克罗蒙达的灰烬荒地中使用。

Svenotar Scout Trike 斯维诺塔尔侦查三轮车

## Description 描述

They are among the fastest vehicles used by the Ironheads and range out from Laager camps to root out ore veins or rumours of lost archaeotech. These tasks can be carried out quickly due to the Svenotar's speed and rugged reliability. The vehicles are also equipped with twin-linked hardpoints which can be armed with an Ironhead Autogun, Ironhead Boltgun, or Meltagun. Various upgrades are available for the Svenotar as well, including a Crash Cage, Ablative Armour, booby-trapped fuel tanks, archaeotech reactors and a control unit for a floating Techmite Occuli. These drones act as scouts for their Svenotar riders and are usually used to transmit their owners with the targeting data of entrenched enemies' positions.

他们是铁首使用的最快的载具之一，从车阵营地到铲除矿脉或失落考古技术的谣言。由于斯维诺塔尔的速度和坚固的可靠性，这些任务可以快速完成。这些载具还配备了双联外挂点，可以配备铁首自动枪、铁首爆矢枪或热熔枪。斯维诺塔尔也有各种升级，包括防撞笼、烧蚀装甲、诱杀燃料箱、考古技术反应堆和漂浮技术螨全视控制单元。这些无人机充当斯维诺塔尔骑手的侦察兵，通常用于向其拥有者传输牢固的敌人位置的目标数据。
