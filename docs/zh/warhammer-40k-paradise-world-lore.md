A paradise world (also known as pleasure world and garden world) is a class of planet characterised by its outstanding natural beauty. Its natural conditions are perfect for human habitation. They have little, if any, industry and low populations. Instead of populating or industrializing these rare planets, some are retained in their natural state and used as recreational bases for important Imperial servants. On these planets, warriors may train their bodies and minds for war, studying arcane battle-philosophy and practicing martial arts. They are known to be havens for Imperial Nobles who possess wealth beyond measure. Sometimes the nature of these worlds makes them rife with temptation and heresy.

天堂世界（也称为快乐世界和花园世界）是一类以其卓越的自然美景为特征的行星。它的自然条件非常适合人类居住。他们几乎没有工业，人口也很少。这些罕见的行星并没有被填充或工业化，而是保留了它们的自然状态，并被用作重要的帝国之仆的消遣基地。在这些行星上，战士们可以训练自己的身体和思想，学习神秘的战斗哲学，练习武艺。众所周知，它们是拥有不可估量财富的帝国贵族的避风港。有时，这些世界的性质使它们充满了诱惑和异端。

The garden world is a sub-classification of civilised class.

花园世界是文明类别的一个子分类。
