The Black Legion is the current largest warband of Chaos Space Marines. Formed after the defeat of the Sons of Horus in the Horus Heresy, the Legion was founded by former Sons of Horus First CaptainEzekyle Abaddon near the conclusion of the Legion Wars. It includes a substantial portion of the former Sons of Horus alongside Traitor Astartes from all walks of life. The Black Legion sees itself not as the successor of Horus and his Legion, but as a new army to continue the Long War and succeed where he failed.

黑色军团是目前最大的混沌星际战士战帮。军团是在荷鲁斯叛乱中荷鲁斯之子被击败后成立的，由前荷鲁斯之子第一连长以泽凯尔·阿巴顿在军团战争接近尾声时创立。它包括大量的前荷鲁斯之子以及各行各业的叛徒阿斯塔特。黑色军团并不认为自己是荷鲁斯和他的军团的继承者，而是一支新的军队，继续进行万古长战，并在他失败的地方取得成功。

The Black Legion can be found operating in countless small warbands across the galaxy, except when they are called together under Abaddon's banner to spearhead one of his notorious Black Crusades.

黑色军团可以在银河系以无数的小战帮运行，除非他们在阿巴顿的旗帜下被召集起来，领导他臭名昭著的黑色远征。

## History 历史

### Origins 起源

The Black Legion origins lay with the XVI Legion and following the First Pacification of Luna, the Luna Wolves. They were the legion of WarmasterHorus himself, said to be the most favored of the Emperor's sons. In honour of Horus's great achievements during the Great Crusade, the Emperor decreed that the legion should be renamed the Sons of Horus. However, Horus was later corrupted by Chaos and rebelled against the Emperor, instigating the devastating Horus Heresy. At the Siege of Terra, Horus was slain by the Emperor and his rebellion defeated.

黑色军团起源于第十六军团，在第一次月球平定之后，是影月苍狼。他们是战帅荷鲁斯自己的军团，据说是帝皇最宠爱的子嗣。为了纪念荷鲁斯在大远征期间的伟大成就，帝皇下令将军团更名为荷鲁斯之子。然而，荷鲁斯后来被混沌腐化，反抗帝皇，煽动了毁灭性的荷鲁斯叛乱。在泰拉围城中，荷鲁斯被帝皇杀死，他的叛乱被击败。

The Eye of Horus, the symbol of the Sons of Horus 荷鲁斯之眼，荷鲁斯之子的标志

Horus's death broke the Sons of Horus's morale, and they were the first of the Traitor Legions to retreat from Terra, an act that would earn them the hatred of all the other Traitor Legions. The Legion re-grouped on the world of Maeleum inside the Eye of Terror. There they built a fortress-tomb for the safe-keeping of the Warmaster's corpse and even in death still revered him as their commander. Nobody was appointed in his place and the Captains of the Legion would offer sacrifices and pray for guidance in his shrine.

荷鲁斯的死摧毁了荷鲁斯之子的士气，他们是第一个从泰拉撤退的叛徒军团，这一行为使他们赢得所有其他叛徒军团的仇恨。军团在恐惧之眼的马勒姆世界重新集结。在那里，他们建造了一座堡垒坟墓，用于安全保管战帅的尸体，即使在死亡时，他们仍然尊他为指挥官。没有人被任命接替他的位置，军团的连长会在他的圣地献祭并祈祷指引。

The Sons of Horus were initially the most aggressive Legion against the Imperium, as if to atone for their previous cowardice on Terra or perhaps to attempt to 're-prove' that they were still the best Legion. The Sons dedicated themselves not to one single Chaos Power but constantly shifted their allegiance to whatever god suited them at the time. Marines willingly became possessed by the Chaos Gods' daemons; with every change in loyalty, the daemons of the rejected god abandoned the hosts, leaving them lifeless husks. The once great Legion constantly dwindled in number, until they neared extinction. Eventually the desperate experimentation and research by the Legion's Sorcerer-Librarians uncovered a method of possession that did not destroy the mortal host, saving the Legion.

荷鲁斯之子最初是对抗帝国最具侵略性的军团，似乎是为了弥补他们之前在泰拉上的懦弱，或者试图“重新证明”他们仍然是最好的军团。荷鲁斯之子没有献身于单一混沌力量，而是不断地将他们的忠诚转移到当时适合他们的任何神身上。战士心甘情愿地被混沌诸神的恶魔附身；随着忠诚的每一次改变，被拒绝的神的恶魔都抛弃了宿主，留下了没有生命的外壳。曾经伟大的军团数量不断减少，直到他们濒临消亡。最终，军团的巫师智库进行了绝望的实验和研究，发现了一种不会摧毁凡人宿主的占据方法，从而拯救了军团。

The Black Legion symbol, derived from the Eye of Horus 黑色军团的标志，源自荷鲁斯之眼

The Traitor Legions, along with the restored but still numerically inferior Sons of Horus, then became embroiled in a series of internecine wars triggered by the Emperor's Children legion, culminating in the destruction of the Sons of Horus' fortress on Maleum. To the disgust of the Sons of Horus, the Warmaster's corpse was taken by the Emperor's Children and several clones were created by their self-styled 'Primogenitor', Fabius Bile.

叛徒军团，以及恢复但数量仍然较低的荷鲁斯之子，随后卷入了由帝皇之子军团引发的一系列自相残杀的战争，最终摧毁了荷鲁斯之子在马勒姆的堡垒。令荷鲁斯之子们厌恶的是，战帅的尸体被帝皇之子带走了，他们自封的“始祖”法比乌斯·拜尔创造了几个克隆体。

### Rebirth - The Black Legion 重生 - 黑色军团

It was at this point that the old Sons of Horus Justaerin Captain Falkus Kibre was able to track down the Vengeful Spirit and Abaddon deep inside the Eye of Terror. Abaddon revealed his intent to destroy the clone of Horus and reform the Sons of Horus into a Legion to finish the war Horus had begun. After smashing the Emperor's Children's fortress and killing the clone of Horus, Abaddon declared himself the rightful successor of the Warmaster. Painting their armour black, Abaddon renamed his forces the Black Legion, rejecting Horus' name and all the failure that went with it. Abaddon managed to reunify the bulk of the old Sons of Horus under his banner, crushing all who resisted. The founders and inner circle of his newly formed Black Legion became known as the Ezekarion. The newly christened Black Legion faced its first serious threat in the form of the Death Guard warlord Thagus Daravek, who was a chief rival to Abaddon in his rise to power and another potential Warmaster of Chaos. However, Daravek was eventually defeated, and Abaddon's power grew exponentially as he gathered many allies across the Eye of Terror, most of the Daemon Primarchs included. Using Chaos rather than being enslaved by it, Abaddon has waged the Long War on the Imperium ever since, most notably during his thirteen Black Crusades.

正是在这一点上，荷鲁斯之子加斯塔林连长法库斯·凯博能够在恐惧之眼深处追踪到复仇之魂号和阿巴顿。阿巴顿透露，他打算摧毁荷鲁斯的克隆体，并将荷鲁斯之子改造成一个军团，以结束荷鲁斯开始的战争。在摧毁了帝皇之子的堡垒并杀死了荷鲁斯的克隆体后，阿巴顿宣布自己是战帅的合法继承者。阿巴顿将他们的盔甲涂成黑色，将他的部队重新命名为黑色军团，拒绝了荷鲁斯的名字以及随之而来的所有失败。阿巴顿设法将荷鲁斯的大部分旧子嗣统一在他的旗帜下，粉碎了所有抵抗者。他新成立的黑色军团的创始人和核心圈子被称为伊泽凯里昂。新命名的黑色军团面临的第一个严重威胁是死亡守卫军阀塔格斯·达拉维克，他是阿巴顿崛起的主要对手，也是另一位潜在的混沌战帅。然而，达拉维克最终被击败，阿巴顿的力量呈指数级增长，因为他在恐惧之眼聚集了许多盟友，包括大多数恶魔原体。从那时起，阿巴顿就利用混沌而不是被混沌奴役，对帝国发动了万古长战，最著名的是在他的十三次黑色远征期间。

Black Legionnaire 黑色军团战士

### The Black Crusades 黑色远征

Since their formation, the Black Legion has engaged in thirteen infamous Black Crusades against the Imperium. While seemingly just major attacks from the Eye of Terror, the Black Crusaders of Abaddon have a secret and even more sinister purpose. Throughout the Millennia he has identified ancient monoliths holding back the tides of the Warp. Using his Black Crusades to sometimes conceal his true objective, Abaddon has targeted and destroyed many of these structures as part of a plan known as the Crimson Path. The ultimate objective of Abaddon's Black Crusades is to expand the Eye of Terror to envelop Terra itself.

自成立以来，黑色军团已经参与了13次臭名昭著的对抗帝国的黑色远征。虽然看起来只是恐惧之眼的重大袭击，但阿巴顿的黑色远征有一个秘密，甚至更险恶的目的。在整个千年中，他发现了阻碍亚空间潮汐的古代巨石。阿巴顿有时会利用他的黑色远征来掩盖他的真实目标，作为深红之路计划的一部分，他瞄准并摧毁了许多这些建筑。阿巴顿的黑色远征的最终目标是扩大恐惧之眼，以包住泰拉本身。

At the culmination of the 13th Black Crusade Abaddon succeeded in destroying Cadia. This set off a chain reaction that allowed for the formation of the Great Rift, effectively dividing the Imperium in half between Imperium Sanctus and Imperium Nihilus. Abaddon now seeks to further isolate the Imperium with his Invasion of Vigilus and through his attempts to use Blackstone to cut off Imperial Warp Routes. Months later, Abaddon entered into an alliance with the Daemon Vashtorr to find the fragments of a mysterious weapon known as The Key, beginning the Arks of Omen Campaign.

在第13次黑色远征的高潮，阿巴顿成功摧毁了卡迪亚。这引发了一场连锁反应，导致了大裂隙的形成，有效地将帝国一分为二，分别是帝国圣疆和帝国暗面。阿巴顿现在试图通过入侵警戒星，并试图利用黑石切断帝国的亚空间航线，进一步孤立帝国。几个月后，阿巴顿与恶魔瓦什托尔结盟，寻找一种名为钥匙的神秘武器的碎片，开始了噩兆方舟战役。

## Gene-seed 基因种子

The Black Legion consists of a large portion of former Luna Wolves Gene-seed alongside those from absorbed warbands or captured from the Imperium. Though the Luna Wolves Gene-Seed was historically considered reliably pure, the regular practice of seeking Daemonic possession has accelerated its mutation. However, such mutations in the reborn Legion are seen as a mark of favour from the Gods of Chaos and are generally displayed with pride.

黑色军团由很大一部分前影月苍狼基因种子组成，还有来自被吸收的战帮或从帝国俘虏的基因种子。尽管影月苍狼基因种子在历史上被认为是可靠纯结，但寻求恶魔占据的常规做法加速了它的突变。然而，重生的军团中的这种突变被视为混沌诸神的青睐的标志，通常会自豪地展示出来。

## Culture and Organisation 文化与组织

The defeat and exile of the Traitor Astartes was a crushing blow to the collective ego of Abaddon and his initial followers, and they broke down and fragmented easily without a purpose or powerful leader to act as the point of their spear. These elements suffered significantly during the early years of exile when it was leaderless, though since Abaddon's leadership it has regained a sense of discipline and purpose. Horus' favoured doctrine of 'tearing the throat out of the enemy' by eliminating their high command in a swift strike remains a well-used tactic.

叛徒阿斯塔特的失败和流亡对阿巴顿及其最初追随者的集体自尊心是一个沉重的打击，他们很容易崩溃和分裂，没有目的或强大的领导者来充当他们的长矛。这些人在流亡的早期遭受了重大损失，当时它没有领导人，尽管自阿巴顿领导以来，它已经重新获得了纪律和目标感。荷鲁斯所推崇的通过在快速打击中消灭敌人的最高指挥部来“撕裂敌人的喉咙”的学说仍然是一种被广泛使用的战术。

The Black Legion battles on Vigilus 在警戒星上战斗的黑色军团

All Black Legion leaders, from the remaining veterans of the Horus Heresy, to the more recently raised, are both cunning and ferocious. The Legion's tactics have been shaped by its lack of numerical strength compared to other Legions, and commanders are adept at using their troops to the best effect.

所有黑色军团的领袖，从荷鲁斯叛乱的剩余老兵到最近提升的人，都既狡猾又凶猛。与其他军团相比，军团的战术是由其缺乏数量力量而形成的，指挥官们善于将他们的部队发挥到最佳效果。

After the death of Horus, proper structure within the squads and companies disintegrated, and their later dispersal in various spacecraft further fragmented the Legion. Warbands of virtually any size and composition can be found following Black Legion Champions - ranking officers from older times or newly emerged leaders who have won favour through their violent deeds. At times, such warbands rally together under the banner of a greater Champion or even Abaddon himself, for a major raid or incursion into the hated Imperium. However, loyalty to differing Chaos Gods often leads to internal politics and conflict. Possession by daemons is still considered highly favourable, and many members of the Legion strive for the honour of being hosts.

荷鲁斯死后，小队和连队内部的适当结构瓦解，他们后来分散在各个星舰中，进一步分裂了军团。几乎任何规模和组成的战帮都可以在黑色军团冠军身后找到，这些冠军是旧时代的高级军官或通过暴力行为赢得青睐的新兴领导者。有时，这些战帮会在更大的冠军甚至阿巴顿本人的旗帜下团结起来，对令人憎恨的帝国进行重大突袭或入侵。然而，对不同混沌诸神的忠诚往往导致内部政斗和冲突。恶魔的占据仍然被认为是非常有利的，许多军团成员都在为成为宿主而努力。

Two known leadership structures exist within the Black Legion. These are the Ezekarion, which are Abaddon's closest lieutenants and comrades and the Chosen of Abaddon, which are his four greatest military commanders. However there may be some overlap between the two bodies, as Iskandar Khayon has claimed to have both been a member of the Ezekarion and the Chosen of Abaddon Sorcerer Ygethmor. Khayon goes on to say that the title of Ygethmor and Lord Vigilator of the Chosen of Abaddon has been held by several individuals. Another important subsect within the Black Legion is the Hand of Abaddon, which includes numbers of non-Astartes and acts as the hidden arm of the Warmaster.

黑色军团内部存在两种已知的领导结构。这些是伊泽凯里昂，他们是阿巴顿最亲密的副手和战友，以及阿巴顿亲选，他们是他的四位最伟大的军事指挥官。然而，这两个机构之间可能存在一些重叠，因为伊斯坎达尔·卡扬声称他同时是伊泽凯里昂的成员和阿巴顿亲选巫师伊戈斯莫。卡扬接着说，阿巴顿亲选的伊戈斯莫和警戒者领主的头衔已被几个人持有。黑色军团中的另一个重要分支是阿巴顿之手，其中包括许多非阿斯塔特，并充当战帅的隐藏臂膀。

The Black Legion invades 黑色军团入侵

Perhaps unique amongst the Traitor Legions, the Black Legion seems to have a significant number of non-Space Marines in relatively high positions. This is seen in the likes of Moriana (Abaddon's seer), Ceraxia (a Dark MechanicusTech-Priest who is Mistress of the Arsenal), and the Hand of Abaddon, which has a variety of Chaos humans, daemons, and mutants in its ranks.

也许在叛徒军团中是独一无二的，黑色军团似乎有相当多的非星际战士成员担任相对较高的职位。这可以在莫里亚娜（阿巴顿的先知）、塞拉克西亚（作为军械库女主人的黑暗机械教技术神甫）和阿巴顿之手中看到，阿巴顿之手中有各种各样的混沌人类、恶魔和变种人。

Rather than having a single homeworld, the Black Legion operates from a variety of strongholds throughout the Eye of Terror, perhaps most notably the Pyris Nebula.

黑色军团没有一个单一的家园世界，而是在恐惧之眼的各个据点运作，也许最著名的是派瑞斯星云。

### Known Warbands 已知战帮

Though the Black Legion is the largest and most organised of the Traitor Legions, it still operates a number of warbands that work in isolation and pursue their own agendas until they are called upon by their master. Warbands of the Black Legion are incredibly diverse, capable of drawing across an immense breadth of skills and specializations. They consist of a mix of veterans of the millennias-old Long War as well as more recent recruits from post-Heresy loyalist Space Marine Chapters. Legion, Chaos God (or lack thereof), or past affiliation does not matter to Abaddon, only skill and loyalty. Thus, the Black Legion consists of massive amounts of warriors not drawn from the original Sons of Horus.

尽管黑色军团是叛徒军团中规模最大、组织最严密的，但它仍然有着许多独立运营的战帮，他们追求自己的议程，直到被主人召唤。黑色军团的战帮非常多样化，能够跨越广泛的技能和专长。他们由数千年前万古长战的老兵以及叛乱后忠诚派星际战士的新兵组成。军团、混沌诸神（或缺乏）或过去的从属关系对阿巴顿来说并不重要，重要的是技能和忠诚。因此，黑色军团由大量非荷鲁斯之子的战士组成。

## Recruitment 招募

Though the Luna Wolves were once considered a large legion, the Black Legion were long considered numerically inferior to other Chaos Space Marine legions since the inter-Legionary wars. While the number of original Legionaries remaining is unknown, and it is unknown how or even if the Black Legion recruit new implantation candidates, it is known that they recruit Marines from other Legions and renegade Chapters.

虽然影月苍狼曾经被认为是一个庞大的军团，但自军团内战以来，黑色军团长期以来一直被认为在数量上不如其他混沌星际战士军团。虽然剩下的原初军团士兵人数未知，也不知道黑色军团是如何甚至是否招募新的植入候选人的，但众所周知，他们从其他军团和变节战团招募战士。

In spite of these difficulties, by M41 the Black Legion was the largest of the Traitor Legions, having ten times the numbers of the Word Bearers. This is aided by their policy of absorbing other Chaos Space Marines and their warbands into their own ranks, regardless of which God of Chaos they may follow, if any.

尽管存在这些困难，但到M41时，黑色军团是叛徒军团中最大的，其人数是怀言者的十倍。这得益于他们的政策，即吸收其他混沌星际战士和他们的战帮加入自己的队伍，无论他们可能追随哪位混沌之神（如果有的话）。

## Fleet 舰队

At the time of the 13th Black Crusade, the fleet of the Black Legion had grown enormously, comparable to Imperial NavyBattlefleets in size. These included seven Battleships, thirteen Heavy Cruisers, twenty-three Cruiser squadrons, and thirty Escort squadrons. The Black Legion fleet was also in possession of a number of superweapons, such as the Planet Killer and two Blackstone Fortress, until Abaddon gave one Blackstone Fortress to Huron Blackheart in the wake of the Badab War and the other was destroyed when Cadia fell. The flagship of the Black Legion's fleet and of Abaddon himself remains the Battle BargeVengeful Spirit.

在第13次黑色远征时，黑色军团的舰队规模已经大幅增长，与帝国海军的战斗舰队规模相当。其中包括7艘战列舰、13艘重型巡洋舰、23个巡洋舰中队和30个护卫舰中队。黑色军团舰队还拥有许多超级武器，如行星杀手和两座黑石要塞，直到阿巴顿在巴达布战争后将一座黑石要塞交给休伦·黑心，另一座在卡迪亚陷落时被摧毁。黑色军团舰队和阿巴顿本人的旗舰仍然是战斗驳船复仇之魂号。
