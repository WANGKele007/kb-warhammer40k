The Leman Russ is the main Battle Tank of the Imperial Guard. This rugged and dependable tank has been used to equip the Guard for many centuries, and while fundamental changes in construction and appearance have occurred, its capabilities have changed very little. Able to operate in a variety of environments and withstand enemy fire while delivering powerful tank-killing blows. It was named after Leman Russ, the Primarch of the Space Wolves.

黎曼鲁斯是帝国卫队的主战坦克。这种坚固可靠的坦克已经被用来装备卫队许多世纪了，虽然结构和外观发生了根本性的变化，但它的能力变化很小。能够在各种环境中作战，承受敌人的火力，同时提供强大的坦克杀伤打击。它以太空野狼的原体黎曼·鲁斯的名字命名。

## History 历史

During the Great Crusade the STC of the Leman Russ Battle Tank was discovered on Nova Borilia. The world had been part of the Noman civilization, which had enslaved a portion of humanity for millennia. The Noman were crushed by the Space Wolves, who recovered the STC and named its fruit in the honour of their Primarch.

在大远征期间，黎曼鲁斯战斗坦克的STC在新星博瑞利亚被发现。世界曾是诺曼文明的一部分，其奴役了一部分人类数千年。诺曼被太空野狼击败，他们夺回了STC，并为其成果命名，以纪念他们的首领。

## Design 设计

### Overview 概述

Ever since, it has been the main battle tank of the Imperial Army (and later the Imperial Guard) and the mainstay of armoured regiments across the Imperium, the Leman Russ is produced in the millions on Forge Worlds and in Hive factories across the Imperium. While slow in comparison to other Guard vehicles and lacking in advanced technology, its ruggedness and reliability are the tank's defining attribute. Emphasizing heavy firepower and armour over mobility, it is rare for any Imperial Guard force to engage an enemy army without the support of Leman Russ tanks. The Leman Russ has been in service for millennia, a testament to its simple but efficient and easily producible design, making it arguably the most successful tank in the galaxy. It has been estimated that if all Leman Russ in service would be lined up, the line would stretch from the Mechanicus workshops on Mars to the gates of the Imperial Palace on Terra. Design changes are only introduced to accommodate different materials, power plants and repair facilities, the basic design capable of withstanding a myriad of environments, from blistering acid waste to chilling methane swamps. The tank itself weighs 60 or 61 tons.

从那以后，它一直是帝国军队（后来是帝国卫队）的主战坦克和帝国各地装甲兵团的中流砥柱，黎曼鲁斯在铸造世界和帝国各地的巢都工厂生产了数百万辆。虽然与其他卫队载具相比速度较慢，缺乏先进技术，但它的坚固性和可靠性是坦克的决定性特征。强调重火力和装甲而非机动性，任何帝国卫队部队在没有黎曼鲁斯坦克支援的情况下与敌军交战都是罕见的。黎曼鲁斯已经服役了许多个千年，这证明了其简单但高效且易于生产的设计，使其可以说是银河系中最成功的坦克。据估计，如果所有服役的黎曼鲁斯都排成一队，这条队伍将从火星上的机械教工坊延伸到泰拉上的帝国皇宫大门。设计变更仅用于适应不同的材料、动力装置和维修设施，基本设计能够承受从起泡的酸性废料到令人恐惧的甲烷沼泽的无数环境。坦克本身重60或61吨。

### Construction, naming and modifications 构造、命名和修改

In the manufactorums of an Industrial World, ceaseless production lines produce thousands of Leman Russ tanks as well as Chimeras every day. For example, the world of Armageddon alone manufactures ten million new Battle Tanks per year. In addition, the Leman Russ can be constructed on automated Forge World assembly lines that are powered by fusion reactors, moving along on conveyor belts while pneumatic hammers shape plates into armour. The whole process is supported by servitors and overseen by tech-priests. After finishing the assembly process, the newly constructed tanks are transported in batches of 10,000 units by servitor-driven trains to star ports, where they are transferred off-world by bulk haulers. If necessary, for example due to enemy invasion forcing the servitors to be conscripted, the assembly process can be manually finished by the tech-priests.

在工业世界的制造厂里，不间断的生产线每天生产数千辆黎曼鲁斯坦克和奇美拉。例如，仅阿玛吉多顿就每年生产1000万辆新的战斗坦克。此外，黎曼鲁斯可以在由聚变反应堆驱动的自动化铸造世界装配线上制造，在传送带上移动，同时气动锤将板材塑形为装甲。整个过程由机仆支持，并由技术神甫监督。在完成组装过程后，新建造的坦克由机仆驾驶的火车分批运送到星港，在那里由大体积运输机运往世界之外。如有必要，例如由于敌人的入侵迫使机仆被征召，装配过程可以由技术神甫手动完成。

Section of a Leman Russ manufactorum 黎曼鲁斯制造厂的一部分

On Forge Worlds, every Leman Russ is named by an automated process, generating a designation not based upon a particular battle or triumph but simply by a scrap of concept-code run by an old cogitator.

在铸造世界上，每辆黎曼鲁斯都是通过一个自动化过程命名的，生成的名称不是基于特定的战斗或胜利，而只是由一个旧的沉思者运行的一段概念代码。

Already during the construction process, tiny flaws, errors of manufacture and minuscule modifications will be incorporated into the tank's design over time. Later, in Guard service, a Leman Russ will be modified by its crew in dozens of tiny, different but essential ways, at least until the tank is returned to a Mechanicus facility for refit, where most of the modifications will be reverted unless overlooked.

在制造过程中，随着时间的推移，微小的缺陷、制造错误和微小的修改将被纳入坦克的设计中。后来，在卫队服役中，黎曼鲁斯将由其乘员以数十种微小、不同但必不可少的方式进行改装，至少在坦克被送回机械教工厂进行整修之前是这样，除非被忽视，否则大部分改装都将被恢复。

### Armament 军备

The main gun of the Leman Russ is its turret-mounted Battle Cannon, a 120mm smoothbore gun, with an elevation from -10° to +32°, which can equally decimate enemy infantry and tanks, its mechanism dominating the cramped turret interior. The turret itself rotates with the help of gears on a turret ring, allowing for a 360° field of fire. A water-cooled jacket surrounds the cannon while gyrostabilisers ensure accurate targeting while on the move. It also has a bore evacuator to dispel combustion gasses and a muzzle recoil brake.

黎曼鲁斯的主炮是其安装在炮塔上的战斗炮，这是一种120毫米滑膛炮，仰角为-10°至+32°，可以同样的摧毁敌方步兵和坦克，其机械装置控制着狭窄的炮塔内部。炮塔本身在炮塔环上的齿轮的帮助下旋转，允许360°的火力范围。水冷护套环绕着大炮，而陀螺仪稳定器确保了在移动中的精确瞄准。它还配备了一个膛内排气装置，用于消除燃烧气体和炮口后坐力制动器。

Leman Russ from the 3rd Company of the 124th Cadian Armoured Regiment 第124卡迪亚装甲兵团第3连队的黎曼鲁斯

The cannon is fed by powered feed-lines and can, when firing forward, lift the front of the tank with the power of its recoil. If the turret is rotated to the side and the Battle Cannon fired, the tank skews to the opposite direction without stopping in forward motion. The Leman Russ carries 36 or 40 shells, selectable by fire selectors, in a flash protected magazine, with storage capacities for three shell types, including but not limited to anti-tank and conqueror shells. Other ammunition includes acid shells that burn biological creatures like tyranids from the inside. Shells are automatically transferred from the magazine to the loading rack where they are primed and shunted forward, allowing for a rate of fire of a shell every few seconds. Aiming the weapon is done through a dedicated periscope with a 45° field of vision with assistance from Targeter gear such as commander's cupola lenses and laser-rangefinders. Auspex guidance such as landscape readers and crosswind indicators provide additional information to assist in locating and targeting the enemy.

加农炮由能量馈线供能，向前射击时，后坐力会抬起坦克的前部。如果炮塔向侧面旋转，战斗炮开火，坦克会向相反方向倾斜，而不会停止前进。黎曼鲁斯携带36或40发炮弹，由开火转换器选择，装在一个防闪光弹仓中，可储存三种炮弹类型，包括但不限于反坦克和征服者炮弹。其他弹药包括从内部燃烧泰拉等生物的酸弹。炮弹会自动从弹仓转移到装载架，在那里它们会被装填并向前转轨，从而允许每几秒钟发射一发炮弹。武器的瞄准是通过一个具有45°视野的专用潜望镜完成的，并得到了指挥官的穹顶透镜和激光测距仪等目标装置的帮助。鸟卜仪制导，如地形阅读器和侧风指示器，提供了额外的信息来帮助定位和瞄准敌人。

Secondary armament will generally consist of a Lascannon in the forward hull mount with a 90° field of fire, powered by a multiphasic generator, though other options include a Heavy Bolter or Heavy Flamer. The side sponson mounts are an optional part of the tank's design, but when mounted they can be fitted with a variety of heavy weapons for close-in defence including Heavy Bolters, Heavy Flamers, Multi-meltas or Plasma Cannons. These are manned by dedicated gunners and capable of a 90° field of fire to the front and sides of the vehicle. Many Leman Russes also incorporate a co-axial autocannon mounted on the turret to engage infantry and light armour.

次要武器通常包括位于车体前部安装的激光炮，其具有90°的射界，由多相发电机提供能量，但其他选择包括重型爆矢枪或重型火焰枪。侧舷支架是坦克设计的可选部分，但安装后可以安装各种重型武器，用于近距防御，包括重型爆矢枪、重型火焰枪、多管热熔或等离子炮。这些由专门的炮手操作，能够对载具的前部和侧面进行90°的射击。许多黎曼鲁斯还配备了安装在炮塔上的同轴自动炮，以对抗步兵和轻装甲。

### Armour 装甲

The Leman Russ' armour is not the most sophisticated but is practical and rugged, allowing it to weather the harshest environments or enemy fire. Due to the tanks simple and functional design it requires minimal training for new crews to become familiar with the tank's operations. A reinforced cast plasteel hull and turret with ferro-steel armour plating combine to make it proof against all but the most deadly attacks. Even impacts powerful enough to lurch the tank several meters sideways will fail to penetrate through its tough hide, though such attacks can damage secondary systems such as the forward scopes and leave the crew stunned. Impacts on the frontal glacial plate, also lurching the tank three metres backwards, don’t penetrate the armour. In the event the tank is dropped two hundred metres and lands upside-down, most of the tank remains intact, with only external gear like the barrel of the Battle Cannon and the sponson-mounted weapons being damaged. This armour is thickest on the tank's front, with decreasing thickness along the sides and rear, to prevent the engine from overheating or over-straining of the transmission. The armour is up to 200mm thick.

黎曼鲁斯的装甲不是最复杂的，但实用且坚固，使其能够抵御最恶劣的环境或敌人的火力。由于坦克的设计简单实用，新乘员只需接受最少的培训即可熟悉坦克的操作。加固的铸塑钢车体和镀有钢铁装甲的炮塔相结合，使其能够抵御除最致命的攻击外的所有攻击。即使强大到足以将坦克侧向倾斜几米的冲击力也无法穿透其坚硬的遮盖，尽管这种攻击可能会损坏前瞄准镜等辅助系统，并使乘员昏迷。对正面冰川板的撞击，也只会使坦克向后倾斜三米，不会穿透装甲。如果坦克摔落200米并倒置，坦克的大部分仍然完好无损，只有外部装置，如战斗炮的炮管和舷侧武器受损。这种装甲在坦克的前部最厚，沿侧面和后部厚度减小，以防止引擎过热或传动装置过载。装甲的厚度可达200毫米。

### Engine 引擎

The sturdy, efficient engine, a HL232 V12 Multi-Fuel or a Lucius 1500 turbine engine, which powers the Leman Russ can run on practically any type of fuel and keep the vehicle moving even in the most hellish conditions; secondary systems such as life support and electrics are powered by internal generators. While a slow vehicle the tank's track arrangement means it exerts a low amount of ground pressure which, combined with fine regenerative steering, allows it to perform almost balletic maneuvers. A Leman Russ is capable of speeds of more than 30kph, both on-road or off-road on a high-grav world, or even 70kph on rocky terrain, shiftable by five gears. Suspensions and hard-wound steel spring shock-absorbers provide added stability on the move. The tracks are pin-locked, all-terrain units, reinforced by tension-screws.

坚固、高效的引擎，HL232 V12多燃料或卢修斯1500涡轮引擎，为黎曼鲁斯提供动力，几乎可以使用任何类型的燃料，即使在最恶劣的条件下也能保持载具行驶；生命支持和电力等辅助系统由内部发电机供能。虽然是一辆慢速载具，但坦克的履带布置意味着它施加的地面压力很低，再加上精细的再造转向，使其能够进行几乎芭蕾舞般的机动。黎曼鲁斯的速度可以超过30公里/小时，无论是在公路上还是在高海拔地区的越野中，甚至在岩石地形上可以达到70公里/小时的速度，可以换五个档位。悬架和硬缠钢弹簧减震器在移动中提供了额外的稳定性。履带采用钉锁，全地形单元，由张力螺钉加固。

### Equipment 装备

Other standard gear include Smoke Launchers, a Searchlight, an air filtration fan and a Comm-link tied into the wider comm-net, allowing for direct communication with supporting forces like the artillery. Also equipped are heating systems, keeping the inside of the tank summerly warm even in icy conditions. Another notable feature is the usage of padded command helmets for the crew. These helmets are fitted with direct links to the weapon's targeters, allowing the gunners to see through the linked targeters, including in thermal vision. The helmet also includes haptic feedback, making the linked weapon directly controllable, responding like the gunner's own arm, and protects against impacts if the tank is shaken. Some Leman Russ tanks are further upgraded with Camo Netting, a Dozer Blade, Extra Armour, a Hunter-Killer Missile and/or a pintle-mountedHeavy Stubber/Storm Bolter.

其他标准装备包括烟雾发射器、探照灯、空气过滤风扇和连接到更宽通信网的通信链路，允许与炮兵等支援部队直接通信。还配备了加热系统，即使在结冰的情况下，也能保持坦克内部夏季般的温暖。另一个值得注意的特点是为乘员使用带衬垫的指挥头盔。这些头盔与武器的瞄准器直接相连，使炮手能够看透相连的瞄准器，包括热成像。头盔还包括触觉反馈，使连接的武器可以直接控制，像炮手自己的手臂一样做出反应，并在坦克摇晃时防止撞击。一些黎曼鲁斯坦克进一步升级了迷彩网、推土铲刀、额外装甲、猎人杀手导弹和/或枢轴式重型伐木枪/风暴爆矢枪。

### Crew 乘员

A minimum of four crew are required to operate the Leman Russ, with two more needed to man the sponson weapons if mounted, though they must do so without comfort in a cramped, hot and noisy environment. Both the loader and the gunner are found in the turret, next to each other. Behind the gunner, the commander is seated, within easy reach of maps, comms equipment, small arms and other needed equipment. The sponson gunners are the most junior members of the crew, responsible for manning the cramped sponson weapons and otherwise aiding the other members in any tasks. Above them is the loader, responsible for loading the main gun and, as needed, manning the hull-mounted weapon. Outside of combat his job will be to keep track of the tank's logistical needs and ensure it has been re-armed and refueled prior to battle.

操作黎曼鲁斯至少需要四名乘员，如果安装了舷侧武器，还需要两名乘员来操作，尽管他们必须在狭窄、炎热和嘈杂的环境中不舒服地操作。装弹手和炮手都位于炮塔内，彼此相邻。在炮手身后，指挥官坐在地图、通信设备、轻武器和其他所需装备的触手可及之处。舷侧炮手是乘员中资历最浅的成员，负责操作狭窄的舷侧武器，并在任何任务中协助其他成员。在他们上方是装弹手，负责为主炮装弹，并根据需要为车载武器配备人员。在战斗之外，他的工作将是跟踪坦克的后勤需求，并确保在战斗前对其进行重新武装和加油。

The driver is next in seniority, a highly technical job responsible for not only driving the tank but also overseeing basic maintenance of the tank's running gear. When stationary the driver can man the hull-mounted weapon, though this practice is discouraged by most since it leaves the tank immobile. The gunner is tasked with firing the tank's primary weapon, along with any co-axial weapons, and also functions as the tank's second-in-command. Between battles his responsibility is to clean and maintain the tank's weapons. The tank commander directs the other crewmembers and is responsible for maintaining an overall picture of the battle, keeping in contact with other tanks through external communication equipment, and manning any pintle-mounted weapons.

驾驶员的资历排在第二位，这是一项技术性很强的工作，不仅负责驾驶坦克，还负责监督坦克传动装置的基本维护。当载具静止时，驾驶员可以操控安装在车体上的武器，尽管大多数人不鼓励这种做法，因为它会使坦克静止不动。炮手的任务是发射坦克的主要武器，以及任何同轴武器，同时也担任坦克的副指挥官。在战斗间隙，他的职责是清洁和维护坦克的武器。坦克指挥官指挥其他乘员，负责维持战斗的整体情况，通过外部通信设备与其他坦克保持联系，并为任何枢轴武器配备人员。

## Known patterns 已知型号

### Mars Pattern 火星型

The Mars Pattern is the most common type of Leman Russ. It is divided into numerous sub-patterns.

火星型是黎曼鲁斯最常见的类型。它分为许多子型号。

Mars-Alpha Pattern 火星-阿尔法型

The Mars-Alpha Pattern hull, whilst originating from Mars, is produced on many Forge Worlds across the Imperium. There are many alterations to the standard pattern hull, the main being that the hull is elongated giving the tank crew more room to operate in. These tanks are often used as a squadron command vehicle for armoured companies, with extra space given to sensor equipment and communication systems.

火星-阿尔法型车体虽然源自火星，但在帝国的许多铸造世界中都有生产。标准型号的车体有很多改动，主要是车体加长，为坦克乘员提供了更多的操作空间。这些坦克通常用作装甲连队的中队指挥载具，并为传感设备和通信系统提供了额外的空间。

Leman Russ with Mars Alpha Pattern hull 火星阿尔法型车体的黎曼鲁斯

The engine compartment is of an older pattern, seen more commonly on Destroyers and Thunderers, easily recognizable by its two horizontal exhausts. The position of the hull’s weapon is reconfigured to take a ball mounted lascannon or heavy bolter, with the additional small vision periscope above for the gunner.

引擎舱是一种较旧的型号，在毁灭者和雷鸣上更为常见，通过其两个水平排气口很容易识别。车体武器的位置被重新配置为携带球装激光炮或重型爆矢枪，并在上方为炮手配备了额外的小型视觉潜望镜。

The hulls of the Mars Alpha Pattern are constructed differently from the standard. Most Leman Russes are cast with plasteel, whilst this pattern is constructed from individual riveted plates. How this does not seem to have any detrimental effect on the tanks to resist enemy fire is unknown, and any additional data on how this is archived is jealously guarded by the Adeptus Mechanicus. The side armour of a Mars Alpha Pattern consists of 150mm of solid plasteel.

火星阿尔法型的车体结构与标准不同。大多数黎曼鲁斯是用塑钢铸造的，而这种型号是由单独的铆接板构成的。这似乎对坦克抵抗敌人火力没有任何不利影响，目前尚不清楚，关于如何这样的存档的任何其他数据都由机械神教小心保管。火星阿尔法型的侧装甲由150毫米厚的实心塑钢制成。

Mars-Solar Pattern 火星-太阳型

The Mars-Solar Pattern Leman Russ (often simply referred to as the Solar Pattern Leman Russ) was commonly fielded by the Solar Auxilia during the Great Crusade and Horus Heresy. It was fully sealed against the vacuum of space and hostile environments, possessing radiological shielding and life support systems. Furthermore, the Mars-Solar Pattern Leman Russ was both faster and more sophisticated than its more common counterparts.

在大远征和荷鲁斯叛乱期间，太阳辅助军通常会部署火星-太阳型黎曼鲁斯（通常简称为太阳型黎曼鲁斯）。它完全密封，不受太空真空和恶劣环境的影响，拥有放射性防护和生命支持系统。此外，火星-太阳型黎曼鲁斯比其更常见的对应更快、更复杂。

Mars-Solar pattern Leman Russ 火星-太阳型黎曼鲁斯

Solar Pattern Leman Russ Tanks came in two primary variants:

太阳型黎曼鲁斯坦克有两种主要变体：

黎曼鲁斯突击坦克是那些配置用于直接攻击防御工事或近距离支援步兵的坦克，特别是那些安装在天龙座运输车内的坦克。它们通常配备有粉碎者炮、等离子毁灭炮或爆燃宏隼炮。

### Goliath-pattern 歌利亚型

The Goliath-pattern is a very rare pattern of Leman Russ. Only three were made during the civil war on Krieg. It is larger, heavier, and more armoured than other patterns. Of the three made, one was destroyed, one is in service with the Krieg 76th and the other's fate is unknown. The plans for how to construct the Goliath-pattern were lost.

歌利亚型是黎曼鲁斯非常罕见的型号。在克里格内战期间，只制作了三辆。它比其他型号更大、更重、更坚固。在制造的三辆中，一辆被摧毁，一辆在克里格第76团服役，另一辆的命运未知。如何构建歌利亚型的规划已经丢失。

Notable design features are the exposed hull weapon, where the gunner must be out of his hatch to fire the weapon, and the co-axial storm bolter.

值得注意的设计特征是暴露的车体武器，炮手必须从舱门中出来才能发射武器，以及同轴风暴爆矢枪。

### Phaeton-pattern 法厄同型

The Phaeton-pattern (Type XV), while still using the standard retrofitted sponsons mounts, energy rechargers and ammunition supply feeds found on the Type XII-XIII, differs from previous designs in its usage of only a single crystal battery generation unit, which fulfils all ancillary energy requirements. The power unit is reconstructed in accordance with the more combat-effective designs used during the storming of Vrech, thanks to a newly rediscovered STC on Gravis 5.

法厄同型（XV型）虽然仍使用XII-XIII型上的标准改装后的侧舷、能量充能器和弹药供应装置，但与之前的设计不同，它只使用了一个单晶电池发电单元，满足了所有辅助能源需求。得益于格拉维斯五号上新发现的STC，动力装置根据在弗雷赫突袭期间使用的更具战斗力的设计进行了重制。

Energy storage and expulsion is much more efficient thanks to the improvements in the thaumic-resonance of crystal orientations and a development in the growth pattern of Diaquartzoid matter. Furthermore, the standard photolatic collectors have been replaced by much more sensitive panels that allow for the accumulation and storage of energy down to fifty picocandela per second at standard solar luminosity. Still, the bulk of required power is provided by a fifty square kilo-cubits Adaptable Thermic Combustor engine, requiring a stricter measure concerning the usage of non-dried vegetative-substances and foodstuffs as fuel.

由于晶体定向的奇迹共振的改善和双石英类物质生长模式的发展，能量的储存和放出效率要高得多。此外，标准的光传收集器已被更灵敏的面板所取代，这些面板允许在标准太阳光度下积累和储存低至每秒50皮坎德拉的能量。尽管如此，所需的大部分动力是由一台50平方千尺的适应性热燃烧引擎提供的，这需要对使用非干燥的植物物质和食品作为燃料采取更严格的措施。
