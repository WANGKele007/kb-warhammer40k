A Chaos Dreadnought is a Dreadnought in the service of the Chaos Space Marines. Under the influence of Chaos, many Chaos Dreadnoughts have degenerated into insane monsters, known as Helbrutes. However exemplars of ancient Dreadnought patterns still exist, having become dark reflections of their imperial counterparts.

混沌无畏是为混沌星际战士服役的无畏。在混沌的影响下，许多混沌无畏已经退化成疯狂的怪物，被称为地狱兽。然而，古代无畏型号的范例仍然存在，已经成为帝国型号的黑暗反映。

Chaos Dreadnought 混沌无畏

## History 历史

Over the course of the Horus Heresy, the Legiones Astartes that had sided with Horus were gradually corrupted by the influence of Chaos. The war saw the emergence of unique Dreadnought variants among the Traitor Legions that reflected this growing change. These included the Mhara Gal Tainted Dreadnought of the Word Bearers and the "berserker" Dreadnought of the World Eaters. The Mhara Ghal was the first to be utterly tainted by Chaos, its occupant a possessed of the Gal Vorbak who had fused with the chassis.

在荷鲁斯叛乱的过程中，支持荷鲁斯的阿斯塔特军团逐渐被混沌的影响所腐化。战争中，叛徒军团中出现了独特的无畏变体，反映了这一日益增长的变化。其中包括怀言者的受祝污染无畏和吞世者的“狂战士”无畏。受祝是第一个被混沌完全污染的，它的使用者被受祝之子附身，受祝之子与底盘融为一体。

Helbrute 地狱兽

After the Heresy, knowledge behind the creation of the old Dreadnought patterns declined among the traitors. With the exception of the prized Contemptor chassis, little effort was made to maintain and replicate the Legion's more sophisticated wargear. Without the technology to protect them from the ravages of time, such as stasis tombs, and exposed to the malignance that had spread among the legionnaires, internment within a Dreadnought's sarcophagus became a living hell. Under the sway of Chaos, many fell prey to insanity and mutation. This fate is also shared by the Dreadnoughts of Renegade Chapters that have succumbed to Chaos in the years after the Heresy, such as the Crimson Slaughter.

叛乱之后，叛徒对创造旧无畏型号背后的知识有所衰退。除了珍贵的蔑视者底盘外，几乎没有做出任何努力来维护和复制军团更复杂的战争装备。没有技术来保护他们免受时间的蹂躏，如淤滞墓，并暴露在军团中传播的恶意之下，那么在无畏石棺内的安置就变成了人间地狱。在混沌的影响下，许多人沦为疯狂和突变的牺牲品。在叛乱之后的许多年，如深红屠杀者，屈服于混沌的变节战团也面临着同样的命运。

Iron Warriors Dreadnought 钢铁勇士无畏

For Chaos Space Marines, incarceration within a Dreadnought is seen as a fate worse than death. Unsurprisingly, it is used as a means of punishment by their Lords.

对于混沌星际战士来说，被困在无畏内被视为比死亡更糟糕的命运。不出所料，它被他们的领主用作惩罚手段。

## Known types of Chaos Dreadnoughts 已知混沌星际战士类别

### Helbrute 地狱兽

Helbrutes are the most common type of Chaos Dreadnought.

地狱兽是最常见的混沌无畏。

They are created when the body of the warrior within a Dreadnought melds with the chassis until the whole machine appears to be made of daemonic flesh.

当无畏中的战士身体与底盘融合，直到整个机器看起来像是由恶魔血肉制成时，它们就被创造出来了。

Helbrute 地狱兽

Unlike other Chaos Dreadnought variants, new armours are being built, incorporating pseudo-parasitic sarcophagi that cause the traumatizing transformation into a Helbrute. As a result of this process, Helbrutes are completely insane.

与其他混沌无畏变体不同，新的装甲正在建造中，其中包含了伪寄生石棺，这些石棺会导致地狱兽的精神创伤转变。由于这个过程，地狱兽完全疯了。

### Ferrum Infernus Chaos Dreadnought 铁狱混沌无畏

Ferrum Infernus is a designation for Chaos Dreadnoughts of the Castra Ferrum pattern that have not mutated into Helbrutes.

铁狱是铸铁型中尚未变异为地狱兽的混沌无畏的名称。

Since the knowledge of creating new Dreadnoughts has been lost, the Ferrum Infernus chassis is a relic, hailing from the days of the Horus Heresy.

由于创造新无畏的知识已经丢失，铁狱底盘是荷鲁斯叛乱时代的遗物。

Ferrum Infernus Dreadnought of The Faithless 无信者的铁狱无畏

The warriors interred within the chassis vary strongly in demeanour. Some have become highly experienced veterans, while others have descended into madness.

埋葬在底盘内的战士举止各异。有些人已经成为经验丰富的老兵，而另一些人则陷入了疯狂。

Sonic Dreadnought 音波无畏

The Sonic Dreadnought is a Ferrum Infernus variant deployed by the Emperor's Children and other warbands dedicated to Slaanesh.

音波无畏是一种铁狱变体，由帝皇之子和其他献身于色孽的战士部署。

These Chaos Dreadnoughts employ larger versions of the sonic weaponry used by Noise Marines, namely the Blastmaster, Sonic Blaster and Doom Siren.

这些混沌无畏采用了噪音战士使用的更大版本的声波武器，即爆破大师、音波爆破炮和末日塞壬。

Sonic Dreadnought 音波无畏

Only Noise Marines who are willing to give up all physical sensation upon interment can be trusted to pilot a Sonic Dreadnought without growing unstable.

只有愿意在埋葬时放弃所有身体感知的噪音战士才能被信任驾驶音波无畏而不会变得不稳定。

### Chaos Contemptor Dreadnought 混沌蔑视者无畏

The Chaos Contemptor Dreadnought is the rarest and most powerful Chaos Dreadnought.

混沌蔑视者无畏是最稀有、最强大的混沌无畏。

It is the only variant to still be held in reverence by Chaos Space Marines and occupancy is usually reserved for powerful Chaos Lords.

它是唯一一个仍然受到混沌星际战士尊敬的变体，通常只留给强大的混沌领主。

Dharkallon, Chaos Contemptor of the World Eaters 达尔卡隆，吞世者的混沌蔑视者

Due to efforts to keep these war machines functional, those interred do not risk falling into madness like the occupants of lesser patterns. However, only few well-versed machine-adepts and Warpsmiths understand the complex technology behind the Contemptor.

由于努力保持这些战争机器的机能，那些被埋葬的人不会像较小型号的使用者那样陷入疯狂。然而，只有少数精通的机械专家和次元铁匠了解蔑视者背后的复杂技术。

### Mhara Gal Dreadnought 受祝无畏

The Mhara Gal Dreadnought is a twisted possessed-variant of the Chaos Dreadnought first used by the Word Bearers during the Horus Heresy.

受祝无畏是混沌无畏的扭曲变体，最早由荷鲁斯叛乱时期的怀言者使用。

Mhara Gal Dreadnought 受祝无畏

### Berserker Dreadnought 狂战士无畏

A Berserker Dreadnought is a Chaos Dreadnought variant that is utilized by the followers of Khorne.

狂战士无畏是恐虐追随者使用的混沌无畏变体。

Berserker Dreadnought 狂战士无畏

### Hellforged Leviathan Dreadnought 狱铸利维坦无畏

The Hellforged Leviathan Dreadnought is a Chaos-corrupted version of the Leviathan Dreadnought used by the Traitor Legions.

狱铸利维坦无畏是叛徒军团使用的利维坦无畏的混沌腐化版本。

Hellforged Leviathan Dreadnought 狱铸利维坦无畏
