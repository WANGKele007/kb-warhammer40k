A privacy field is a technology that deadens all noise coming in or out of a small area. There are various privacy fields that are both opaque and soundproof, while also allowing for external sounds and visuals outside of the privacy field to be sensed.

隐私立场是一种技术，可以消除进入或离开小区域的所有噪音。有各种不透明和隔音的隐私立场，同时也允许感知隐私立场之外的外部声音和视觉。

## Overview 概述

This technology for a privacy field can easily be contained within a dataslate. These typically consist of a basic conversation scrambler and vox-thief blocker, which is usually enough to keep the curious ignorant of the conversation being had within the field. It's often used to have private conversations in crowded or otherwise bustling public spaces.

这种用于隐私立场的技术可以很容易地包含在数据板中。这些通常包括一个基础交谈扰码器和音阵窃取阻断器，这通常足以让好奇的人对现场正在进行的对话一无所知。它通常用于在拥挤或繁忙的公共场所进行私密交谈。

Some privacy fields also created a blackened, one-way shroud around the area, allowing those inside to be hidden by a black veil and still view outside of the privacy field. These might be seen used in the upper-class box-seating of Imperial theatre houses. The opaque veils and auditory deafening features keep the political scheming and other antics those Nobles and ruling elite seated inside private.

一些隐私立场还在该区域周围创建了一个变黑的单向覆盖，允许里面的人被黑色帷幕隐藏，仍然可以看到隐私立场之外的东西。这些可以在帝国剧院的上层包厢中看到。不透明的帷幕和听不见的听觉特征使坐在里面的贵族和统治精英们的政治阴谋和其他荒唐行为保持私密。

Auditory privacy fields are sometimes used by ArchmagosDominusBelisarius Cawl and his helpmeet Qvo-87, who are both equipped with this technology on their persons.

统御大贤者贝利撒留·考尔和他的助手邱弗-87有时会使用听觉隐私领域，他们都在身上配备了这项技术。
