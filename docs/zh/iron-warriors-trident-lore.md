The Trident was a formation within the Iron WarriorsLegion during the Horus Heresy. It consisted of three Triarchs, senior Legionaries (usually Warsmiths) who served as Perturabo's advisers in military matters and as part of his retinue. Triarchs served until they either died or lost Perturabo's confidence.

三叉戟是荷鲁斯叛乱时期钢铁勇士军团中的一种编队。它由三名三叉戟组成，他们是资深的军团士兵（通常是战争铁匠），在军事事务中担任佩图拉博的顾问，也是他的随从的一部分。三叉戟要么死了，要么失去了佩图拉博的信任。

## Known Triarchs 已知三叉戟

 - 

Kydomor Forrix - First Captain of the Iron Warriors.

 - 

凯多莫·弗里克斯 - 钢铁勇士的第一连长。

 - 

Erasmus Golg - Warsmith of the 11th Grand Company. Killed in the Battle of Phall.

 - 

伊拉斯谟·戈尔 - 第十一大连的战争铁匠。在法尔之战中阵亡。

 - 

Harkor - Warsmith of the 23rd Grand Company. Demoted following the Siege of the Cadmean Citadel after disobeying Perturabo's orders.

 - 

哈克尔 - 第23大连的战争铁匠。在卡德曼城堡围攻中违抗佩图拉博的命令后被降职。

 - 

Kroeger - Warsmith of the 23rd Grand Company. Promoted after Harkor's dismissal.

 - 

克罗格尔 - 第23大连的战争铁匠。在哈克尔被撤职后晋升。

 - 

Barban Falk - Warsmith of the 126th Grand Company.

 - 

巴尔班·法尔克 — 第126大连的战争铁匠。
