*With the Imperium in a constant state of war, some worlds are designated as Fortress worlds to act as bastions against the enemies of the God-Emperor. You are part of that defence, keeping the evil outside the gates from breaking through. This has made you part of the glory of the Imperium's mighty forces of war.*

随着帝国处于持续的战争状态，一些世界被指定为堡垒世界，作为对抗神皇之敌的堡垒。你是防御的一部分，防止门外的邪恶突破。这使你成为帝国强大战争部队荣耀的一部分。

A Fortress World is a category of Imperial planet where the entire populace is immersed in warfare. They are created as bastions against some threat. The most well known fortress world was Cadia, which was guarding the Cadian Gate at the entrance to the Eye of Terror. In bureaucratic matters classifications of Research Stations and fortress worlds overlap, both being ρ-class.

堡垒世界是帝国行星的一个类别，全体平民都沉浸在战争中。它们是为了抵御某种威胁而建造的堡垒。最著名的堡垒世界是卡迪亚，它守卫着恐惧之眼入口处的卡迪亚之门。在官僚事务中，研究站和堡垒世界的分类重叠，都是ρ级。

Since the establishment of the Indomitus Crusade, many Fortress Worlds are being converted into Hub-Fortresses to support the war effort.

自不屈远征建立以来，许多堡垒世界正在被改造成枢纽堡垒，以支援战争。
