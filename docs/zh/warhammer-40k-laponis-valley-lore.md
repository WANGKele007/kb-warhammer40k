The Valley of Laponis is a valley on Macragge. Little more than a cleft in the mountain when Roboute Guilliman arrived on the planet. Within a decade of his arrival it would be thousands of kilometers-long valley which housed the Fortress of Hera, the fortress-monastery of the Ultramarines.

拉波尼斯山谷是马库拉格的一个山谷。当罗伯特·基里曼抵达行星时，这只不过是山上的一个裂缝。在他抵达后的十年内，这里将成为数千公里长的山谷，极限战士的堡垒修道院赫拉堡垒坐落在这里。
