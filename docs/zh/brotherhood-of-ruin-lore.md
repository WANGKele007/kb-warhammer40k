The Brotherhood of Ruin was an Abhuman empire operating around the Maelstrom during the Great Crusade period. Commanding many thousands of small ships and having a force of Ork mercenaries, the warlords of the Brotherhood unleashed their cyborg and Ork legions against the Adeptus Mechanicus world of Sarum. However they were annihilated by the World Eaters in the Golgothan Slaughter.

毁灭兄弟会是一个在大远征时期围绕大漩涡运作的亚人帝国。兄弟会的军阀们指挥着数千艘小舰，并拥有一支欧克雇佣兵部队，他们释放了他们的半机械人和欧克军团，对抗萨勒姆机械教世界。然而，他们在戈尔戈坦大屠杀中被吞世者消灭了。
