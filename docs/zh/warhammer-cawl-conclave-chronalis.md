The Conclave Chronalis was a meeting of several Forge Worlds called together by Archmagos DominusBelisarius Cawl, Prime Conduit of the Omnissiah. This meeting took place on Pontus Avernes; it was a presentation and request for substantial resources and aid to expedite Cawl's great work of closing the Cicatrix Maledictum.

时间秘密会议是由欧姆尼赛亚的主导管统御大贤者贝利撒留·考尔召集几个铸造世界的会议。这次会议是在庞图斯·阿维尔涅斯举行的；这是一次演讲，并要求提供大量资源和援助，以加快考尔关闭诅咒瘢痕的伟大工作。

## Overview 概述

Lector DogmisAlixia Kalisperis suggested to Cawl that Pontus Avernes be the location of this gathering.

教条讲师艾莉克西亚·卡利斯佩里斯建议考尔将庞图斯·阿维尔涅斯作为这次聚会的地点。

MagosFrenk was allowed to record the meeting and impromptu trial of Belisarius Cawl to bring back to his master, InquisitorCehen-qui, one of many enemies Cawl has made in the Imperium over his long life.

贤者弗兰克被允许记录贝利撒留·考尔的会议和即兴审判，以带回给他的主人，审判官塞亨-奎，考尔在他漫长的一生中在帝国中制造的许多敌人之一。

In this meeting Cawl presented his plan. The means to achieving his goals on the world present their own issues and heresy against the currently prevailing beliefs held by the majority of the Cult Mechanicus. Cawl's means proposed using time-altering technology, xenos tech, and consorting with a NecronCryptek in order to steal and then use Necron technology to aid in the construction and control of Cawl's prototype pylon matrix.

在这次会议上，考尔提出了他的计划。实现他在世界上的目标的手段提出了他们自己的问题和异端对抗大多数机械教派目前持有的主流信仰。考尔提出的方法是使用时间改变技术、异型技术，并与死灵墓穴技师厮混，以窃取并使用死灵技术来帮助构建和控制考尔的原型方尖碑矩阵。

In orbit of a black hole named Calligan’s Maw in the Astra Cartographica is the ancient Necron world Irtanathep. The world's sun was killed and turned into this black hole during the War in Heaven. Irtanathep was a world with some kind of pylon control system, and Cawl speculated it was attacked during the war because of this.

在星图会中一个名为卡利根之喉的黑洞的轨道上，是古老的死灵世界伊尔塔纳瑟普。世界上的太阳在天堂战争中被杀死，变成了这个黑洞。伊尔塔纳瑟普是一个有某种方尖碑控制系统的世界，考尔推测它在战争期间因此受到攻击。

Irtanathep was caught on the cusp of the black hole's event horizon. Due to time slowing under conditions of extreme gravity, the world is experiencing extreme temporal dilation. The world is doomed to be locked in time, a near-perpetual free fall into the black hole's event horizon for uncountable millennia. Cawl wished to go here to access the pylon network control centre. That is why he called so many magi who are hierophants in the ars chronometrica.

伊尔塔纳瑟普被捕获在黑洞事件视界的交点。由于在极端重力条件下时间减慢，世界正在经历极端的时间膨胀。世界注定要被锁定在时间中，在无数个千年的时间里，近乎永久地自由落入黑洞的事件视界。考尔想去这里访问方尖碑网络控制中心。这就是为什么他召集了那么多在时间测量技艺中担任导师的圣贤士。

This open proposal to defy the nature of time resulted in uproar amongst the magi, for Chronaxic manipulation is one of the prime bans. Amongst the crowd, some temporal experts insisted that no fundamental laws of either the Cult or of material science would be broken, as they were dealing with a natural effect. A minority said it did not matter, because what Cawl proposed was de facto voyaging through time, no matter how cleverly it was dressed up, and that was clearly forbidden.

这一公然挑战时间本质的提议在圣贤士中引起了轩然大波，因为操纵时间轴是主要禁令之一。在人群中，一些时间专家坚持认为，无论是教派还是材料科学的基本定律都不会被打破，因为它们是在处理自然效应。少数人表示这并不重要，因为考尔提出的建议实际上是穿越时间的航行，无论它打扮得多么巧妙，这显然是被禁止的。

A tunnel through time and space to this frame of existence would require great effort to stabilise frame shifting across several chronic time zones, both to get in and in order to extract within real-time relative parameters. Maintaining the same vector for entry and exit would be essential to not reemerge thousands of years later, if at all. Archmagos Logis Magnacomptroller Covarix Sestertius estimated the energy cost of this theoretical endeavour would be equal to the total energy output of a sun, a gargantuan outlay in resources.

穿越时间和空间到达这一存在框架的隧道需要付出巨大的努力来稳定跨越几个慢性时区的帧偏移，为了在实时范围内获取和提取相对参数。保持相同的出入境媒介对于不在数千年后出现至关重要。逻辑大贤者大审计官科瓦里克斯·塞斯特提乌斯估计，这一理论研究的能源成本将相当于太阳的总能量输出，这是一笔巨大的资源支出。

The Forge Worlds of Accatran and Tigrus would choose to aid in Cawl's scheme. Forge World Metalica declined, stating they would request that the Synod of Mars ex-communicate of the 2 Forge Worlds from the Cult Mechanicus.

阿卡特兰和提格里斯铸造世界选择帮助考尔的计划。梅塔利卡铸造世界拒绝了，表示他们将要求火星议会关于2个铸造世界从机械教派的前通信。
