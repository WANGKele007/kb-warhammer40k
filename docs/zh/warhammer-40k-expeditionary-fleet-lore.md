An Expedition or Expeditionary Fleet was the standard Imperial exploratory spacefaring force during the Great Crusade.

远征队或远征舰队是大远征期间标准的帝国探索航天部队。

## Overview 概述

Originating from Terra, the Imperial Expeditionary Fleets dispersed all over the galaxy, carrying out the Emperor's vision of uniting known space under the banner of Humanity. Expedition Fleets had a wide remit. Their primary mission was to find lost human colony worlds and bring them into the bosom of the Imperium. Secondary missions involved the exploration of space, the cataloging of everything found, the claiming of resources in the name of the Emperor and the elimination of any alien threats.

帝国远征舰队起源于泰拉，分散在整个银河系，在人类的旗帜下实现了帝皇统一已知空域的愿景。远征舰队的职权范围很广。他们的主要任务是找到失落的人类殖民地世界，并将其带入帝国的怀抱。次要任务涉及太空探索、对发现的一切进行编目、以帝皇的名义索取资源以及歼灭任何异型威胁。

Each of the great Expeditionary Fleets was composed of hundreds of thousands of spaceships, ranging from singular examples of recovered technology wonders to mass-produced classes of various ranges and sizes, including warp runners, freighters, galleasses, piquet frigates, cruisers, battleships and even star forts.

每一支大远征舰队都由数十万艘宇宙飞船组成，从回收技术奇迹的单一例子到各种范围和尺寸的大规模生产，包括亚空间穿梭机、货船、加莱赛战船、哨兵护卫舰、巡洋舰、战列舰，甚至星堡。

The unification of human worlds did not always proceed without incident, as not all lost worlds showed immediate interest in making themselves subservient to the Imperium. In these cases, compliance with Imperial directives was enacted with the use of military force. These situations, as well as the ever-present alien threat, resulted in Expedition Fleets having a strong armed contingent, and were usually under the command of a military officer of the Imperial Army, a Lord Commander. In addition to a Lord Commander, each Expedition had a Master of the Fleet who was in charge of the Imperial Navy ships in the Expedition. Each of the Primarchs was able to, and often did, command an Expeditionary Fleet, attaching to it the majority of their Legion.

人类世界的统一并非总是一帆风顺，因为并非所有失落的世界都表现出立即屈从于帝国的兴趣。在这些情况下，通过使用武力来遵守帝国指令。这些情况，以及始终存在的异型威胁，导致远征舰队拥有强大的武装分遣队，通常由帝国军队的一名军官，即领主指挥官指挥。除了一位领主指挥官外，每一个远征队都有一位舰队司令，负责远征队中的帝国海军舰船。每一位原体都能够，而且经常这样做，指挥一支远征舰队，并将其大部分军团附属于该舰队。

The Imperium still makes use of Expedition Fleets in its recent history.

帝国在其近期历史上仍然使用远征舰队。
