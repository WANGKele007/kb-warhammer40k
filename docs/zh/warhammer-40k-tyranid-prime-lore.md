The Tyranid Prime is a highly evolved Tyranid Warrior, considered to be the apex of the Warrior strain.

首席战士是一位高度进化的泰伦战士虫，被认为是战士虫品系的巅峰。

Tyranid Prime 首席战士

### Overview 概述

Tyranid Primes are highly intelligent, more so than their common counterparts. When they lead a brood of Tyranid Warriors, the brood acts as one entity using tactics and precision that military commanders find hard to match. When in the presence of more powerful Tyranid creatures like a Hive Tyrant, a Tyranid Prime will defer synaptic control to it, but when alone a Prime is easily capable of turning a group of Tyranid creatures into an effective fighting force.

首席战士非常聪明，比普通的对应更聪明。当他们带领一群泰伦战士虫时，这些战士虫就像一个实体，使用军事指挥官难以匹敌的战术和精准度。当有更强大的泰伦生物（如虫巢暴君）存在时，首席战士会推迟对它的突触控制，但当单独存在时，首席很容易将一群泰伦生物变成一支有效的战斗部队。

Tyranid Prime with a Termagant 首席战士和枪虫

It is only recently that Tyranid Primes have been recognized as a separate species from regular Warriors, though this is probably due to them blending in with the rest of the brood.

直到最近，首席战士才被认为是与普通战士虫不同的一个物种，尽管这可能是由于它们与其他族群融合在一起。
