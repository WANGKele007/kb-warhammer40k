The Hellbore is a burrowing vehicle used by the Adeptus Mechanicus, Imperial Guard, and Adeptus Astartes.

地狱钻是机械神教、帝国卫队和阿斯塔特修会使用的一种挖掘载具。

Hellbore Burrowing Transport 地狱钻挖掘运输车

Larger then both the Termite and Mole, it uses a phase-field generator to burrow a drill filled with troops into the ground, allowing them to surface behind or on the flanks of enemy fortifications and positions. It has the capacity to transport up to one of the following: 12 Squads of Space Marines, 12 Imperial Guard Squads, 8 Dreadnoughts, 4 light vehicles, or 2 Rhinos (with troops inside them to their normal carrying capacity). The troops embark and disembark by an assault ramp which folds into the Hellbore's body when not in use. The Hellbore drilling-rig is armed with a multi-melta and heavy bolters while the transport vehicle is armed with heavy bolters and defended by two power fields.

它比白蚁和鼹鼠都大，它使用相位立场发生器将装满部队的钻头钻入地下，使他们能够在敌人防御工事和阵地的后方或侧翼出现。它最多可以运输以下其中一种：12个星际战士小队、12个帝国卫队小队、8名无畏、4辆轻型载具或2辆犀牛（内部部队达到正常承载能力）。部队通过一个突击坡道上下，该坡道在不使用时可以折叠到地狱钻的身体里。地狱钻的钻机配备了多管热熔和重型爆矢枪，而运输载具则配备了重型爆矢枪并由两个能量立场进行防御。

The drilling-rig portion of a Hellbore is as long as a Stormbird while being wider in beam and more heavily armoured.

地狱钻的钻机部分与风暴鸟一样长，但梁更宽，装甲更重。

Hellbores also get used by the forces of Chaos like the Iron Warriors.

地狱钻也被钢铁勇士等混沌部队所使用。

Hellbores remain in active service within the Imperium as of the Era Indomitus, with the Cadian 101st still making use of them.

地狱钻在不屈时代仍在帝国内服役，卡迪亚第101团仍在使用。
