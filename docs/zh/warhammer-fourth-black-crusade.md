## Overview 概述

The Fourth Black Crusade would prove to be highly costly to the Imperium, seeing the destruction of the Citadel of Kromarch and the loss of many lives. The attack began in 001.M34 and was led by Abaddon the Despoiler. Abaddon had learnt well from his last two Black Crusades and besieged the walls of Cadia while taking the bulk of his forces out into the wilds of the Segmentum Obscurus.

第四次黑色远征对帝国来说代价高昂，克罗马奇城堡被毁，许多人丧生。袭击始于001.M34，由掠夺者阿巴顿领导。阿巴顿从他最近的两次黑色远征中吸取了教训，他围攻了卡迪亚之墙，同时将他的大部分部队带到了朦胧星域的偏远地区中。

On his drive to Terra, Abaddon landed his forces on El'Phanor and this was where his attack would end. Abaddon led his forces personally against the defenders, his followers and warriors dropping all around him in the attack on the great citadel of the Kromarch, until the walls crumbled and his attack was pressed home thanks to his Daemon Sword Drach'nyen sundering the gates. Once inside the Citadel, Abaddon extinquished the ancient line of Kromarch and his kin and destroyed the ancient monolith within.

在前往泰拉的途中，阿巴顿将部队降落在埃尔'法诺，这就是他的攻击将结束的地方。阿巴顿亲自率领部队对抗守军、他的追随者和战士，在对克罗马奇大城堡的袭击中，他们在他周围倒下，直到城墙倒塌，由于他的恶魔剑德拉科'尼恩劈开了城门，进攻被逼退了。一进入城堡，阿巴顿就消灭了克罗马奇及其血亲的古老血脉，并摧毁了城堡内的古代巨石。

Abaddon then gave his troops free reign over the city and no man, woman or child was free from the assaults of Chaos. This move doomed Abaddon however, as it gave the Imperial forces time to regroup and plan revenge on Chaos. The Imperial forces fell on the tide of Chaos and wiped it from the face of the planet, few escaping back to the Eye of Terror, signaling the end of the Black Crusade.

阿巴顿随后让他的军队自由统治这座城市，没有一个男人、女人或孩子能逃脱混沌的攻击。然而，这一举动注定了阿巴顿的失败，因为它给了帝国部队时间重新集结并计划对混沌进行复仇。帝国部队在混沌浪潮中倒下，将其从行星上抹去，很少有人逃回恐惧之眼，这标志着黑色远征的结束。

Sometime during the 4th Black Crusade, the Despoiler bombarded the world of Eriad VI with the goal of destroying the Necronpylons built on the world, in which he succeeded.

在第四次黑色远征的某个时候，掠夺者轰炸了伊利亚德六号世界，目的是摧毁建立在世界上的死灵方尖碑，他成功了。
