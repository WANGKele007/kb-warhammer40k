The Cawl Inferiors are a pair of machines that imitate a limited simulacrum of Archmagos DominusBelisarius Cawl. They are used as the input and output points for the exchange of clear astropathic messages relayed between the two inferiors, and used by Archmagos Cawl and the UltramarinesprimarchRoboute Guilliman. Each Cawl Inferior interfaces with, and are kept within, their respective astropathic devices located deep within the Macragge's Honour and the Zar Quaesitor.

次级考尔是一对仿效统御大贤者贝利撒留·考尔有限模拟物的机器。它们被用作输入和输出点，用于在两个次级之间交换清晰的星语信息，并被大贤者考尔和极限战士原体罗伯特·基里曼使用。每个次级考尔都与位于马库拉格之耀号和探索者之王号各自深处的星语装置连接并保存在其中。

The Lord Regent Guilliman also uses the Cawl Inferior as a counselor, of sorts, when unable to communicate with the real Archmagos in person. Cawl *claims* that these simulacrums are not Artificial Intelligences.

当无法亲自与真正的大贤者沟通时，领主摄政基里曼也会使用次级考尔作为顾问。考尔声称这些模拟物不是人工智能。

## Description 描述

Located deep within the *Macragge's Honour*, the entrance to the area containing the Cawl Inferior starts in the personal quarters of Roboute Guilliman. In a hidden space within the quarters is a gene-locked elevator with built-in weapons primed to kill anyone, besides Guilliman, who attempted to access it. A simple retinal scan allows him in into the elevator, but once inside, the security protocols are more extensive. Arcane devices on armatures extend from the walls to test Guilliman's body, mental state, and spiritual aura. Once confirmed, the arms withdraw into their recesses one by one. The elevator then descends two hundred decks. Once at its only destination, its rear doors opens onto a chamber lit by red artificial lighting so dim that a normal man would be effectively blind. The chamber is hot, loud with the chatter of hidden machinery. On the far side of the chamber are a set of complex, three interleaving sets of doors made of metre-thick bonded hexsteel, protected by another set of security tests. The doors open into a second and much larger circular space, lit with the same colour lighting. The inner chamber’s spherical exterior walls were similarly armoured to the doors.

位于马库拉格之耀号的深处，次级考尔所在区域的入口始于罗伯特·基里曼的个人宿舍。在宿舍内的一个隐蔽空间里，有一个基因锁定的电梯，里面装有内置武器，随时准备杀死任何人，除了试图进入电梯的基里曼。简单的视网膜扫描可以让他进入电梯，但一旦进入电梯，安全协议就更加广泛。电枢上的神秘装置从墙上延伸出来，测试基里曼的身体、心理状态和精神氛围。一旦确认，这些武器就会一个接一个地收回到凹槽中。然后电梯下降200层。一旦到达唯一的目的地，它的后门就会打开一个由红色人工照明照亮的房间，这种照明非常昏暗，普通人实际上会失明。房间里很热，有隐藏的机器的嗡嗡声。在房间的另一边是一组复杂的、三组交错的门，由一米厚的嵌合六角钢制成，并受到另一组安全测试的保护。门打开进入第二个更大的圆形空间，用相同颜色的灯光照亮。内室的球形外墙与门的装甲相似。

The inner chamber is twenty metres across and the air smells of ozone, sanctified oil, curdled milk, and old blood. A grilled floor is suspended over a pit full of humming machines, dividing the room in two. The majority of the red light comes through the floor grille. A small door that leads to the quarters of Astropath PrimeGuidus Losenti is directly across the room from the main gate. This door is also composed of three massively thick doors that open in sequence. Pipes bound tightly by metal staples hang in from the ceiling. The chamber is absent the gothic decorations normal of the 41st millennium, except the Machina Opus emblem on a roundel set into the domed ceiling. At eye level, closed panels line the walls between the two doors, ten on the left and ten on the right.

内室有二十米宽，空气中弥漫着臭氧、圣油、凝乳和旧血的味道。一块被炙烤的地板悬挂在一个满是嗡嗡作响的机器的坑上，将房间一分为二。大部分红灯都穿过地板格栅。正门正对面有一扇通往首席星语者吉杜斯·洛森蒂宿舍的小门。这扇门也由三扇依次打开的厚重门组成。用金属钉紧紧束缚的管道从天花板上垂下来。这个房间没有第41个千年的哥特式装饰，除了圆顶天花板上圆形的机械教齿轮徽章。在视线高度，两扇门之间的墙壁上排列着封闭的面板，左边十个，右边十个。

Behind each panel are the Cawl Inferior's central components: twenty severed human heads in illuminated armour glass tanks, bathed in clear yellow nutrient fluids. The heads are too individual to have been vat-born slaves, and there is no sign of the penal coding tattoos worn by servitors made from criminals. The neck of each is capped by metal. Small bundles of tidy cables and pipes are linked to these plates, curl downwards under the heads, then up into the machines above them. The majority of the device's psychic circuitry workings are hidden behind the walls, floor, and ceiling of the room.

每个面板后面都是次级考尔的核心组件：20个被砍下的人头，放在发光的装甲玻璃罐里，沐浴在清澈的黄色营养液中。这些头太个体化了，不可能是缸裔奴隶，也没有罪犯身上佩戴的刑码纹身的迹象。每个的颈部都用金属盖住。小捆整齐的电缆和管道连接到这些板上，在头部下方向下卷曲，然后向上进入上方的机器。该设备的大部分灵能回路运作都隐藏在房间的墙壁、地板和天花板后面。

The Cawl Inferior may be enacting his own agenda, particularly wishing to become Fabricator-General of Mars, a position the real Cawl has little interest in acquiring.

次级考尔可能正在制定自己的议程，特别是希望成为火星铸造将军，而真正的考尔对获得这一职位兴趣不大。

## Capabilities 能力

The Cawl Inferior is primarily programmed to emulate the voice and mannerisms of its creator: Belisarius Cawl. The Cawl Inferior functions via transmitting and receiving hexidecimal codes that activate and/or modify pre-programmed responses to its operator. These codes are immune to decryption as they are incomplete. Belisarius programmed in multiple likely scenarios that he mathematically extrapolated. The linked brains and the logic engines in the greater portion of the chamber contain all eventualities relevant to the tasks undertaken by Roboute Guilliman and the Archmagos. Among these pre-programmed responses are status reports and updates to Archmagos Cawl's various projects. The Cawl Inferior also contains the sum total of its creator's knowledge, put there to help better emulate Belisarius and as a back up in case the Archmagos were to die.

次级考尔主要被编程为模仿其创造者贝利撒留·考尔的声音和举止。次级考尔通过发送和接收十六进制代码来激活和/或修改对其操作者的预编程响应。这些代码是不完整的，因此不会被解密。贝利撒留在多个可能的场景中编程，并通过数学推断。房间大部分的连接大脑和逻辑引擎包含了与罗伯特·基里曼和大贤者执行的任务相关的所有可能性。这些预先编程的响应包括大贤者考尔各个项目的状态报告和更新。次级考尔还包含了其创造者的知识总和，用于帮助更好地模仿贝利撒留，并作为大贤者死亡情况的备份。

While the machine emulates its creator well enough that Guilliman often forgets that it is not the Archmagos himself, it often strongly asserts that it is not. Its responses are so well performed, even seemingly emotional at times, that the primarch doubts it is as simple a device as it claims to be, suspecting that the Cawl Inferior is a true Abominable Intelligence. Guilliman wonders if the device is capable of, and has been, lying to him to conceal its (and its creator's) intentions. When Guilliman stated his intention to meet with Archmagos Cawl himself, the Cawl Inferior claimed that such a meeting may not be possible, that it did not know its creator's current location, and shut itself down.

虽然这台机器很好地模仿了它的创造者，以至于基里曼经常忘记它不是大贤者本人，但它经常强烈断言它不是。它的反应非常好，有时甚至看起来很情绪化，以至于原体怀疑它是否像它声称的那样简单，怀疑次级考尔是一个真正的憎恶智能。基里曼怀疑该设备是否能够并且一直在欺骗他以掩盖其（及其创造者）的意图。当基里曼表示他打算亲自与大贤者考尔会面时，次级考尔声称这样的会面可能不可能，它不知道其创造者的当前位置，并关闭了自己。

Cawl quite enjoys talking with his Cawl Inferior and could do so endlessly, if he permitted himself to. The inferior being a mirror to Cawl himself, they often share back and forth conversations of self-adulation and wit.

考尔很喜欢和他的次级考尔交谈，如果他允许的话，他可以无休止地这样做。次级考尔是他自己的镜子，他们经常来回地谈论自我吹捧和智慧。

## Operation 操作

All interactions with the Cawl Inferior are performed via voice commands. Activation of the Cawl Inferior requires both the presence of Roboute Guilliman, and Guidus Losenti. The process begins when Losenti commands the initiation sequence (the voice command being: ‘By the will of the Omnissiah, engage initiation sequence.’). The device requests the identity of the users, first Losenti then Guilliman. After both have stated their identities, the device performs a gene scan on both, via a band of green light that passes over them. Once gene scan is confirmed, the device requests a code from Losenti, and projects a sound dampener over Guilliman to prevent him from hearing Losenti's code. If Guilliman attempts to read the Astropath's lips, the device temporarily blinds him with a las-strobe. Once Losenti has stated his code, the sound dampener is lifted from Guilliman and he states his own code (which is a sequence of nonsensical words that change depending on the date). Once the machine confirms the codes, the main series activation sequence begins.

与次级考尔的所有交互都是通过语音命令进行的。激活次级考尔需要罗伯特·基里曼和吉杜斯·洛森蒂在场。当洛森蒂命令启动初始序列时，该进程开始（语音命令是：“根据欧姆尼赛亚的意志，启动初始序列。”）。该设备请求使用者的身份，首先是洛森蒂，然后是基里曼。在两人都表明了自己的身份后，该设备通过一条穿过他们的绿光带对两人进行基因扫描。一旦基因扫描得到确认，该设备就会向洛森蒂请求一个代码，并在基里曼上方投射一个消声器，以防止他听到洛森蒂的代码。如果基里曼试图读取星语者的嘴唇，该设备会用激光闪光暂时让他失明。一旦洛森蒂说出了他的代码，消声器就会从基里曼身上移开，他说出了自己的代码（这是一系列根据日期而变化的无意义的单词序列）。机器确认代码后，主系列激活序列开始。

The panels in the walls retract to reveal the twenty servitor heads. The device requests an activation code and does not deploy another sound dampener. The activation code is sent astropathically to Losenti by Cawl, and is different for every activation of the Cawl Inferior (an example of this code is: ‘Charnibel crow, white crow, white crow, white crow, charnibel crow, black crow’). The device repeats that the code is accepted three times and begins the Cawl Inferior awakening rite. Thin lines in the metal walls, begin to glow with golden energies, circuitry engraved into the tanks’ glass glimmer similarly, and the faces of the heads start to twitch. As the activation process continues, the psychic energies in the room become more intense, paining those who are sensitive to them. Losenti leaves to avoid these energy and to provide Guilliman with privacy.

墙上的镶板缩回，露出了二十个机仆的头。该设备请求激活代码，并且不部署另一个消声器。激活码由考尔以星语的方式发送给洛森蒂，每次激活次级考尔都是不同的（例如：“死亡乌鸦、白乌鸦、白乌鸦、白乌鸦、死亡鸦、黑乌鸦”）。该设备重复接受代码三次，并开始次级考尔苏醒仪式。金属墙上的细线开始闪烁着金色的能量，刻在水箱玻璃上的回路也同样闪烁着光芒，头部的脸开始抽搐。随着激活过程的继续，房间里的灵能能量变得更加强烈，让那些对它们敏感的人感到痛苦。洛森蒂离开是为了避免这些能量，并为基里曼提供隐私。

The bonded astopath working the terminal aboard *Macragge's Honour* isn't strictly necessary for the functioning of the Inferior, but Cawl found that Guilliman responded better to a human, so played to his sympathies. Interfacing with the Inferior shortens a psyker's lifespan. As such, Cawl utilises an Astropath's brain in a jar for his Cawl Inferior aboard the Zar-Quaesitor, because this as a component lasted longer.

在马库拉格之耀号上的终端工作的束缚星语者对于次级考尔的运作并不是严格必要的，但考尔发现基里曼对人类的反应更好，因此迎合了他的同情。与次级考尔建立接口会缩短灵能者的寿命。因此，考尔在探索者之王号上为他的次级考尔使用了一个缸中星语者大脑，因为这是一个持续时间更长的组件。

Energy runs more freely around the channels engraved into the walls, extending its web outwards from the heads until the whole room is a traced with a golden glow. As the energy network expands, it hits upon hidden sigils in the fabric of the room: warding runes in the secret techno-arcana tongue of Magos Psykana and others of Xenos origin start to glow as well. The twitches of the servitor heads intensify into a frenzy of silent screams, as the fluid in the tanks bubble rapidly. Eventually the process completes and the machine calms.

能量在雕刻在墙上的通道周围更自由地流动，从头部向外延伸，直到整个房间都被金色的光芒所描绘。随着能量网络的扩展，它在房间的结构中发现了隐藏的标志：灵秘贤者和其他异型起源的秘密技术奥秘语言中的守护符文也开始发光。当水箱里的液体迅速冒泡时，机仆的头部抽搐加剧成了一阵疯狂的无声尖叫。最终，该过程完成，机器平静下来。

The Cawl Inferior greets Guilliman with a preprogrammed greeting. Interaction with the Cawl Inferior from this point resembles that of a normal conversation. The servitor heads silently word everything said by the machine. After delivering requested reports, the machine asks for any orders and updates that need to be sent back to Cawl. Upon receiving orders from Guilliman, it transmits these as codes back to its counterpart aboard Archmagos Cawl's Ark ship. After determining that the conversation is over, the Cawl Inferior automatically deactivates, the golden glow fades, the servitor heads go slack and the panels close infront of them, and the psychic pressure in the room lowers to pre-activation levels.

次级考尔用预设的问候语问候基里曼。从这一点上讲，与次级考尔的互动类似于正常的对话。机仆默默地说出机器说的每一句话。在交付所需的报告后，机器会要求将任何命令和更新发送回考尔。在收到基里曼的命令后，它将这些作为代码发送回大贤者考尔的方舟舰上的对应。在确定对话结束后，次级考尔会自动停用，金色的光芒会消失，机仆的头会松弛，面板会在他们面前闭合，房间里的灵能压力会降低到激活前的水平。

The Cawl Inferior is also installed with a master shut down switch which can be operated by the user.

次级考尔还安装了一个可由使用者操作的主关闭开关。

Cawl has an identical "Cawl Inferior" aboard his own flagship. Cawl's Cawl Inferior on his flagship is sequestered deep within its bowels. To further aid in assuring his privacy, Cawl tends to wear a Blackstone Amulet around his neck to prevent any psychic eavesdropping or tampering.

考尔在自己的旗舰上有一个相同的“次级考尔”。他的旗舰上的考尔的次级考尔被深埋在它的内部最深处。为了进一步帮助确保他的隐私，考尔倾向于在脖子上佩戴黑石护身符，以防止任何灵能窃听或篡改。
