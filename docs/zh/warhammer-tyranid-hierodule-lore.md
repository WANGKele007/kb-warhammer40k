The Hierodule is a large Tyranid Bio-construct, around the size of an ImperialSuper Heavy Tank. Like many Tyranid creatures the Hierodule seems to be a mutable breed, able to be created in different forms depending on the needs of the Hive Fleet. There have been two different forms of Hierodule observed by Imperial forces so far, namely the "Scythed" and the "Barbed" variants. In addition, the Hierodule has a subspecies in the Praefactor.

圣奴虫是一种大型泰伦生物构造体，大约有一辆帝国超重型坦克的大小。像许多泰伦生物一样，圣奴虫似乎是一个可变的品系，能够根据虫巢舰队的需要以不同的形态创造。到目前为止，帝国部队观察到了两种不同形态的圣奴虫，即“镰”和“刺”变体。此外，圣奴虫在总督星中有一个亚种。

Tyranid Scythed Hierodule 泰伦镰圣奴虫

## Barbed Hierodule 刺圣奴虫

### History 历史

After losing Harmman's World to Hive Fleet Kraken, the Imperial forces reinforced the Moran System. The assault, by Hive Fleet Kraken, was soon manifested. While the Imperium had learned from its losses, the Tyranid had evolved. The Hierodule had evolved into the Barbed Hierodule with a powerful ranged attack. It was quite a shock to the Imperial forces when it appeared on Maxima Moran.

在将哈曼世界输给虫巢舰队克拉肯后，帝国部队加强了莫兰星系。虫巢舰队克拉肯的袭击很快就显现出来了。当帝国从损失中吸取教训时，泰伦已经进化了。圣奴虫已经演变成了具有强大远程攻击能力的刺圣奴虫。当它出现在莫兰时，帝国部队感到非常震惊。

Tyranid Barbed Hierodule 泰伦刺圣奴虫

### Observations 观察

The Barbed Hierodule replaces two of the Scythed Hierodule's claws with a pair of massive Barbed Strangler bio-cannons. Unlike the Scythed variant, it has no symbiote acid spray but instead has thicker armour plates, capable of withstanding many weapon types. Even those that penetrate the reinforced eco-skeleton cause little damage due to the Barbed Hierodule's sheer bulk.

刺圣奴虫用一对巨大的尖刺扼杀者生物炮取代了镰圣奴虫的两只爪。与镰变种不同，它没有共生体酸喷雾，而是有更厚的装甲板，能够承载多种武器类型。由于刺圣奴虫的庞大体积，即使是那些穿透强化生态骨架的武器也几乎不会造成破坏。

## Scythed Hierodule 镰圣奴虫

### History 历史

The Scythed Hierodule was first encountered on the Imperial planet Hamman's World. In the assimilation of Hamman's World, the Scythed Hierodule was in the Vanguard of the Tyranid forces beside masses of Gaunts. They were especially effective against prepared defenses, such as bunkers.

镰圣奴虫首次在帝国行星哈曼世界上被发现。在哈曼世界的同化过程中，镰圣奴虫与兵虫并肩，成为了泰伦部队的先锋。它们对掩体等有准备的防御尤其有效。

Tyranid Scythed Hierodule 泰伦镰圣奴虫

The Scythed Hierodule has only been encountered in a single tendril of Hive Fleet Kraken and there infrequently but their appearance surely will grow with time. Xenos Biologists have tried to capture a live sample, but have, so far, failed because every Xenos Hunter team, sent on the quest, have failed to return. The finding of a non-alive sample is also a problem because every one of the extracted Hierodules was almost totally destroyed by the heavy weapons fire.

镰圣奴虫只在虫巢舰队克拉肯的一个卷须中遇到过，而且很少出现，但它们的出现肯定会随着时间的推移而增长。异型生物学家试图捕获活体样本，但到目前为止，由于被派往任务的每个异型猎手小队都没有返回，因此失败了。非活体样本的发现也是一个问题，因为每一个获取的圣奴虫都几乎完全被重型武器摧毁。

### Observations 观察

The Scythed Hierodule is armed with four huge razor sharp claws, each capable of slicing through a battle tank, and heavily protected by chitin plates, in places up to 30 cm thick. A Hierodule is far larger than a Carnifex. As well as its claws, the Hierodule also bears a symbiote creature which appears to live in its back, growing out between the armour plates. This toxic creature helps defend its host from attack, squirting a jet of potent mutagenic acid against any enemy that gets too close.

镰圣奴虫配备了四个巨大的剃刀般锋利的爪子，每个爪子都能切开一辆战斗坦克，并受到部分地方厚达30厘米几丁质板的严密保护。圣奴虫比刽子手大得多。除了爪，圣奴虫还带有一种共生生物，这种生物似乎生活在它的背部，生长在装甲板之间。这种有毒生物有助于保护其宿主免受攻击，向任何靠得太近的敌人喷射一股强效诱变酸。

## Trivia 琐事

The original epic scale Hierodule model produced in the mid 1990s depicted the creature to be larger than its more modern Forge World counterpart. In this incarnation it was only slightly smaller than the Hierophant, therefore closer in size to a Titan compared to the current creature which is closer in size to a Super Heavy Tank.

20世纪90年代中期生产的原初Epic规模圣奴虫模型将该生物描述为比更现代的FW对应更大。在这个化身中，它只比教皇小一点，因此与目前体型更接近超重型坦克的生物相比，它的体型更接近泰坦。
