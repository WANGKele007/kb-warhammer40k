## Overview 概述

Space Marine Power Armour is fully sealed, isolating the wearer from the outside environment and protecting him from gas weapons and harsh atmospheres. It is considered as having built-in void suit capabilities. Should a penetration occur, resulting in a leak of the environmental seal and possible exposure to the vacuum of space, a quick application of repair cement is sufficient to restore its sealed environment (at least temporarily), though more advanced patterns such as artificer armour may have automated fibre re-weaving or similar systems. It also commonly includes numerous auxiliary systems such as communicators and auto-senses. The backpack contains the main power plant, environmental system and additional stabilizers. The primary power source is a fusion generator which is capable of lasting forever with the proper care. This is important, as should a Space Marine's power armours motive system completely fail due to critical damage, the armour is very heavy and becomes a hindrance slowing the marine down making it difficult so much as to move around in unpowered armour. The backpack also houses a small Stacked Atomic Chain battery, powering the suit's life-support, air recycling, fluid recovery and various automated medical functions. Still, should a marine require it, power packs also contain spare energy cells and a solar converter to recharge them. All armour may further be augmented by various artefacts, relics and enhancements such as pauldrons and greaves. The most important part of the armour scheme of Power Armour are its ceramite armour plates. These are individually up to an inch thick and are honeycombed to help dissipate energy and localise damage suffered. The entire battleplate is over 5 centimeters thick. Thanks to its ceramite and adamantium exterior, Power Armour is almost invisible to heat detection.

星际战士动力盔甲完全密封，将穿戴者与外界环境隔离开来，保护他免受毒气武器和恶劣环境的伤害。它被认为具有内置的虚空服功能。如果发生渗透，导致环境密封泄漏并可能暴露在空域真空中，速用修复胶合剂足以恢复其密封环境（至少是暂时的），尽管更先进的型号，如精工盔甲，可能有自动纤维复织或类似系统。它通常还包括许多辅助系统，如通信器和自动感应。背包内装有主动力设备、环境系统和额外的稳定装置。主能源是一个聚变发生器，在适当的照顾下能够永远持续下去。这一点很重要，因为如果星际战士的动力盔甲动力系统因严重损坏而完全失效，盔甲会非常重，成为阻碍和令战士减速的障碍，这使得穿着无动力的盔甲四处走动变得非常困难。背包还装有一个小型堆叠式原子链电池，为套装的生命支持、空气循环、液体循环和各种自动化医疗功能提供动力。尽管如此，如果战士需要，动力背包还包含备用能量电池和太阳能转换器来为其充能。所有盔甲都可以通过各种造物、圣物和增强（如肩甲和胫甲）进一步增强。动力盔甲的盔甲方案中最重要的部分是其陶钢装甲护板。这些单独的厚度可达一英寸，呈蜂窝状，有助于分散能量并局部化所受的损伤。整个战斗护板的厚度超过5厘米。得益于其陶钢和精金的外部，动力盔甲几乎对热探测不可见。

Each individual suit of power armour has a unique history of battles it was worn in and are often comprised of the remnants taken from other suits having their own respective history. Due to this, the machine spirits of power armour tend to develop their own personalities over time.

每一套动力盔甲都有其独特的战斗历史，通常由其他具有各自历史的盔甲的残余组成。因此，随着时间的推移，动力盔甲的机魂往往会发展出自己的个性。

Power armour has been in use since before the Age of the Imperium, from the techno-barbarians of Earth to the original Space Marines created by the Emperor. Over the history of the Imperium, power armour has developed into many different forms. Need, circumstances and recovery of new materials and technology has shaped the armour's evolution.

从地球上的科技蛮人到帝皇创造的最初的星际战士，动力盔甲早在帝国时代之前就开始使用了。在帝国的历史上，动力盔甲已经发展成许多不同的形态。需求、环境以及新材料和技术的回收影响了盔甲的演变。

Space Marine Power Armour is an extraordinarily sophisticated defensive system which combines huge resistance to physical damage with a sensory array and sealed environment which protects its wearer from the ravages of the void and alien atmospheres. Integrated with the armour are networks of electro-motivated fibre bundles which mimic and augment the muscular strength of the wearer, so long as it remains powered. The true genius of the design, however, lies in its close integration with the already superhuman physiology, senses and reflexes of the Space Marine within. Working in concert, armour and Astartes together become a weapon without equal. Space Marine Power Armour can withstand las-fire, bolt rounds, 10cm long hypervelocity flechettes, and even cannon shots, but is significantly weaker at resisting power swords, which can cut through it with ease. Power Amour reduces the damage taken from small arms somewhere between 50 to 85 percent, even offering at least some protection against all but the most devastating weapons deployed on the battlefield. The ceramite plating of power armour can contain small capsules that, when ruptured by damage, produce a sealing foam that closes off wounds and armour breaches.

星际战士动力盔甲是一种非常复杂的防御系统，它结合了对物理损伤的巨大抵抗、传感阵列和密封环境，保护穿戴者免受虚空和异星大气的破坏。与盔甲融为一体的是电动纤维束网络，只要它保持动力，就会模仿和增强穿戴者的肌肉力量。然而，该设计的真正天才在于它与星际战士已经超人的生理、感官和反射的紧密结合。盔甲和阿斯塔特协同工作，成为无与伦比的武器。星际战士动力盔甲可以承受激光射击、爆矢弹、10厘米长的超高速箭弹，甚至加农炮的射击，但在抵抗动力剑方面明显较弱，可以轻易地穿透它。动力盔甲可以将小型武器造成的伤害降低50%至85%，甚至可以对战场上部署的除最具毁灭性的武器外的所有武器提供至少一些保护。动力盔甲的陶钢护板可以包含小胶囊，当因损伤而破裂时，会产生密封泡沫，封闭伤口和装甲缺口。

### Production and Installation 生产与安装

Each suit of Space Marine Power Armour is a work of supreme artisanship, and although it may be one of many, each is a unique artefact whose spirit and history is of deep spiritual significance to both the Chapter to which it belongs and the Space Marine who wears it. It can take decades to complete just one suit of the new Mk VII power armour, so it is often easier to make spare parts for an existing suit than it is to make a completely new one.

每一套星际战士动力盔甲都是最高工艺的杰作，尽管它可能是许多作品中的一件，但每一套都是一件独特的工艺品，其精神和历史对它所属的战团和穿着它的星际战士都具有深刻的精神意义。完成一套新型Mk VII动力盔甲可能需要几十年的时间，因此为现有盔甲制造备件通常比制造一套全新盔甲更容易。

There are numerous different Marks of Power Armour, most of which date back in terms of design and application many thousands of years ago to the time of the Great Crusade and the Horus Heresy. Each has its own lore and genesis in place and time. Even in the 41st Millennium some intact suits from this near-mythical age survive in active service as war-relics of the Space Marines. Countless others have been produced or reacquired since, and different Chapters often have entirely different panoplies they can call upon to armour their forces. It is also far from uncommon for more recently fashioned armour to incorporate slivers or whole components from far older suits as revered connections to the past.

动力盔甲有许多不同的型号，其中大多数的设计和应用可以追溯到数千年前的大远征和荷鲁斯叛乱时期。每个都有自己的传说和起源。即使在第41个千年，这个近乎神话时代的一些完好无损的套装仍然作为星际战士的战争圣物在现役中幸存下来。自那以后，无数其他的被生产或重新获得，不同的战团通常有完全不同的盔甲，可以用来武装他们的部队。对于更新式的盔甲来说，将来自更古老的盔甲的碎片或整个部件作为与过去的神圣联系也并不罕见。

Space Marines go through an arduous process where they receive various implants culminating with the implantation of the Black Carapace, which allows the Marine a direct and instinctual interface with the armour, making it in effect a second skin. This grants the feeling of both touch and temperature to the wearer even while armoured, allowed by internal sensors that pass on these sensations through the Black Carapace. The advanced systems of Space Marine power armour also monitor the Marine's biological functions and suit integrity, feeding the medical information to the Marine and alerting him to critical failures within his armour. A vox-datalink is also present, which allows for information, such as geo-locational data, to be relayed to an Astartes.

星际战士经历了一个艰巨的过程，他们接受了各种植入物，最终植入了黑色甲壳，这使战士与盔甲有了直接和本能的接触，使其实际上成为了第二层皮肤。即使穿着盔甲，这也会给穿戴者带来触觉和温度的感觉，这是由内部传感器通过黑色甲壳传递这些感觉所允许的。先进的星际战士动力盔甲系统还可以监测战士的生物机能和套装的完整性，向战士提供医疗信息，并在盔甲出现严重故障时提醒他。还存在一个音阵数据链路，它允许将地理位置数据等信息中继到阿斯塔特。

## Patterns 型号

### Mark I "Thunder Armour" 一型“雷霆盔甲”

Mark I plate was developed by the techno-barbarian factions that dominated Terra prior to the Emperor's ascendancy. When the Emperor began his conquest of Earth, the "Thunder Warriors", the proto-Space Marines which formed part of his retinue, were armoured in the same way as the soldiers of the other warlords. A thunderbolt and lightning symbol was displayed on the breastplate of warriors loyal to the Emperor - this was the personal badge of the Emperor in those days, predating the Imperial Aquila which only became the symbol of the Imperium much later. The emblem gives the suit its common name – "Thunder Armour."

Mark I型是由在帝皇统治之前统治泰拉的技术蛮人派系开发的。当帝皇开始征服地球时，“雷霆战士”，即他随从中的原初星际战士，与其他军阀的士兵一样装备了盔甲。忠于帝皇的战士的胸甲上展示着闪电符号 — 这是当时帝皇的个人徽章，早于后来才成为帝国象征的帝国天鹰。徽章赋予了这套盔甲一个共同的名字 — “雷霆盔甲”

Mark I "Thunder Armour" 一型“雷霆盔甲”

Thunder Armour was not an enclosing suit and offered no life support functions, being unnecessary while the fighting was confined to Terra and the similar environments of Luna and Mars. This early armour was produced on a completely local basis and as such individual suits varied heavily and their exact designs were often a matter of personal taste.

雷霆盔甲不是一套封闭的盔甲，也没有生命支持功能，在战斗仅限于泰拉以及月球和火星的类似环境时是不必要的。这种早期的盔甲完全是在当地生产的，因此个人盔甲差异很大，他们的确切设计往往取决于个人品味。

The main part of the armour is the massive powered torso which encloses the chest and arms. Coiled energy cables beneath the armour plating transmit power from the power pack on the back to the arms, greatly increasing physical strength by a factor of between 3 and 4 times. Since fighting on Terra during this period was primarily close-quarters, the strength of a warrior's chest and arms was of paramount importance. The legs of this armour Mark are typically not power-armoured at all, but are enclosed in tough padded breeches. The best-equipped warriors sometimes wore armoured greaves and armoured boots, and such additional protection was common amongst the Thunder Warriors.

盔甲的主要部分是包裹胸部和手臂的巨大动力躯干。盔甲护板下的线圈能量缆线将能量从背面的动力背包传输到手臂，使力量大大提高了3到4倍。由于这一时期在泰拉上的战斗主要是近距离的，战士的胸部和手臂的力量至关重要。这种型号盔甲的腿通常根本没有动力盔甲，而是被包裹在坚硬的衬垫马裤中。装备最好的战士有时会穿装甲胫甲和装甲靴，这种额外的保护在雷霆战士中很常见。

As with all powered armour, the power supply is contained with a notably bulky back-mounted unit, most of which is taken up by cooling systems designed to prevent the entire armour design from overheating.

与所有动力盔甲一样，能源包含在一个特别笨重的后置单元中，其中大部分由冷却系统占据，旨在防止整个盔甲设计过热。

Although it is very rarely used in the 41st Millennium, some suits of Thunder Armour are maintained for ceremonial purposes.

虽然它在第41个千年中很少使用，但一些雷霆盔甲是为了仪式目的而保留的。

This ancient style of armour is noticeably noisier than later Marks.

这种古老的盔甲风格明显比后来的型号更吵。

### Imperial Pattern 帝国型

A class of power armour referenced in but a handful of sources, "Imperial Pattern" power armour is described as a highly idiosyncratic design notable for a helmet not unlike that of the much later Mark VI, for a distinctly archaic arrangement of vents and joints, and for lacking the flared grieves so distinctive of the known classes. In these sources, the wearers of this class also bear a singular weapon thought to be related to the almost obsolete volkite, some manner of disintegration beamer. It was used by at least some Prototype Legionaires. It was made available between Mark II and Mark III.

“帝国型”动力盔甲是少数资料中提到的一类动力盔甲，被描述为一种高度独特的设计，其头盔与后来的六型头盔没有什么不同，通风口和接口处的布置明显过时，也没有已知类别中如此独特的喇叭式胫甲。在这些资料中，这一类的穿戴者还携带了一种被认为与几乎淘汰的爆燃有关的独特武器，这是一种某种形式的瓦解光束枪。它至少被一些原型军团士兵使用过。它是在Mark II和Mark III之间提供的。

### Mark II "Crusade Armour" 二型“远征盔甲”

After the conquest of the main planets of the Sol system, the factories on Mars were set to work constructing a new type of armour for the Emperor's elite Space Marines. The first armour based upon recovered STC information, this armoured suit is commonly referred to as "Crusade Armour," since it was designed with the planned Great Crusade in mind. Its production led to the Thunder Armour being retroactively designated as the Mark I and quickly retired. It was the first fully enclosed power armour, with life-sustaining capabilities, making it suitable for combat on toxic alien planets and the depths of space. The first suit to interface with the Black Carapace which allowed for maximum synchronization, the Mk.II Pattern was standard for the Legiones Astartes during most of the Great Crusade, with millions produced, and its helmet in particular became a symbol for the Legiones Astartes throughout the Imperium. By the ending stages of the Crusade, Mk.II Armour had been phased out for the Mk.IV but many Legions still possessed them in great numbers. In particular, Legions not favoured by Warmaster Horus or had many units spread out on the frontiers of the Imperium retained the largest amounts of Mk.II Armour. Some Space Marines simply favoured the Mk.II over the Mk.IV for its reliability and history of service.

在征服了太阳系的主要行星后，火星上的工厂将为帝皇的精英星际战士制造一种新型盔甲。这是基于恢复的STC信息的第一种盔甲，这种盔甲套装通常被称为“远征盔甲”，因为它的设计考虑到了计划中的大远征。它的生产导致雷霆盔甲被追溯指定为Mark I并迅速退役。这是第一个全封闭的动力盔甲，具有维持生命的能力，使其适合在有毒的异星和太空深处作战。Mk.II型是第一套与黑色甲壳接口的盔甲，它允许最大程度的同步，在大远征的大部分时间里，Mk.II型是阿斯塔特军团的标准，生产了数百万套，尤其是它的头盔成为整个帝国阿斯塔特军团的象征。到大远征的最后阶段，Mk.II盔甲已被Mk.IV逐步淘汰，但许多军团仍大量拥有它们。特别是，不受战帅荷鲁斯青睐或在帝国边境部署了许多部队的军团保留了最大数量的Mk.II盔甲。一些星际战士只是因为其可靠性和服役历史而更喜欢Mk.II而不是Mk.IV。

### Mark III "Iron Armour" 三型“钢铁盔甲”

This model dates from the wars of the Great Crusade, when Astartes Legions were forced into deadly boarding actions and tunnel combat. This armour was ideal when cover was minimal and combat was a matter of frontal assault. The most visually brutal and imposing of all the widely produced armour marks, the Iron Armour was a heavy, modified Mark II design (itself the first mass-produced Power Armour of the Crusade era). The Iron Armour's structure has augmented frontal defence and incorporates additional ablative plates, and is intended foremost for use in boarding actions, tunnel assaults and void warfare, although it is considerably heavier and more power hungry than its contemporaries. The new helmet design is a heavily armoured model with sloping plates designed to deflect shots to the left and right, and inspired the Mark IV and VI helmet designs.

这种型号可以追溯到大远征的战争，当时阿斯塔特军团被迫采取致命的跳帮行动和地道战。这种盔甲在掩护很少、战斗需要正面攻击的情况下是理想的。钢铁盔甲是所有广泛生产的盔甲型号中视觉上最残酷、最令人印象深刻的，它是一种重型的、经过修改的Mark II设计（本身就是大远征时代第一个大规模生产的动力盔甲）。钢铁盔甲的结构增强了正面防御，并加入了额外的烧蚀护板，主要用于跳帮行动、地道攻击和虚空战，尽管它比同时代的重得多，耗能也更大。新的头盔设计是一种重型装甲设计，带有倾斜护板，旨在将子弹向左和向右偏转，并启发了Mark IV和VI头盔的设计。

### Mark IV "Maximus Armour" 四型“终极盔甲”

At the end of the Great Crusade the Space Marines found their armour wearing out. While some Legions chose to continue local production and maintenance, the Mechanicum of Mars started to design a new variant for centralised issue, which was to be designated the Mark IV or *Imperial Maximus* suit.

在大远征结束时，星际战士发现他们的盔甲磨损了。虽然一些军团选择继续在当地生产和维护，但火星机械教开始为集中发行设计一种新的变体，该变体被指定为Mark IV或帝国终极盔甲。

### Mark V "Heresy Armour" 五型“叛乱盔甲”

The equipping of the legions was only partially complete when the Horus Heresy broke out. 当荷鲁斯叛乱爆发时，军团的装备只完成了部分。许多刚刚完全装备了Mark IV盔甲的军团转而对抗帝国，而许多忠诚的军团则被故意剥夺了进入帝国的机会，并继续使用较旧的Mark II和Mark III盔甲作战。此外，即使是配备Mark IV盔甲的军团也很快发现自己的备件不足，无法制造替换单元。由于战争破坏和机动作战，为受损装备提供补给变得困难或不可能。星际战士工匠和技术军士不得不使用旧型号的老式装备来保持军团的战斗力，以及从被杀的敌人和他们自己的非正统创造中抢救。

This ad-hoc assemblage of various armour mark segments (including new or unofficial design elements) being created by a multitude of legions resulted in an entirely new mark of armour being 'accidentally' created; these previously non-standard, emergency/stopgap designs were retroactively termed as the Mark V once production of the Mark IV was halted and the design for the subsequent Corvus Armour mark (Mark VI) was finalised. Some form of standardisation across Mark V suits is notable, despite their apparently random creation and individual varied origins, due to the dissemination of molecular bonding techniques across the legions. These techniques were developed due to battlefield experience in fighting other Astartes; Astartes plate needed to be proof against their own penetrator weapons, and it was discovered that power armour would benefit from additional reinforcing in this regard. All sorts of improvised additional armour can be seen on Mark V suits, but the most common technique was affix additional layers of armour with the aforementioned molecular bonding studs. This relatively quick and simple technique effectively added another ablative layer to marine armour suits. The extra weight this generated would prove considerable, however, which placed a strain on power supplies. As the addition of extra power generation sources and cabling would just require more armour plating, catching the design in an eternal circle, the wearers of the suit was forced to either deal with the additional weight, or turn up the power output of his backpack but suffer extreme heat output, which not only would prove uncomfortable and even compromising in certain situations, and in many cases older, heavy power cabling was used, which created a weak point on the chest.

由众多军团创建的各种盔甲型号部分（包括新的或非官方的设计元素）的临时组合导致了一种全新的盔甲型号被“意外”创造；一旦Mark IV的生产停止，随后渡鸦盔甲型号（Mark VI）的设计最终确定，这些以前非标准的紧急/权宜之计设计被追溯称为Mark V。由于分子键合技术在整个军团中的传播，尽管Mark V盔甲的创造明显是随机的，起源也各不相同，但其某种形式的标准化是值得注意的。这些技术是由于与其他阿斯塔特作战的战场经验而发展起来的；阿斯塔特护板需要能够抵御他们自己的穿透武器，人们发现，在这方面，加强动力盔甲将受益匪浅。Mark V盔甲上可以看到各种各样的临时附加装甲，但最常见的技术是用上述分子键合嵌钉附加附加装甲层。这种相对快速简单的技术有效地为战士的盔甲增加了另一层烧蚀层。然而，这将产生相当大的额外重量，给能源带来压力。由于增加额外的能量发生器和缆线只需要更多的铠装，使设计陷入一个永恒的循环，穿着这套盔甲的人被迫要么应对额外的重量，要么提高背包的功率输出，但却承受着极端的热量输出，这不仅会让人感到不舒服，甚至在某些情况下会有失体面，而且在许多情况下，会使用更旧、更重的能量缆线，这会在胸部造成弱点。

Production Mark V 制造五型

Production Mark V is the most widely recognisable form of Mark V, and consists of elements of power armour that most Legions were able to manufacture in their own forges, or otherwise obtain themselves, falling back on common designs for parts that had specific cross-compatibilities, often deriving from Mark IV, V, VI and later Mark VII.

制造Mark V是Mark V最广为人知的形式，由大多数军团能够在自己的制造厂中制造或以其他方式获得的动力盔甲元素组成，这些元素依赖于具有特定交叉兼容性的部件的通用设计，通常来自Mark IV、V、VI和后来的Mark VII。

Non-production Mark V 非制造五型

Non-production Mark V is a genuine amalgamation of field modifications combining several different marks of armour in ways no Tech-Priest would ever think to sanction. In general, Mark II and III components can be combined without issue, as can those of Marks IV, VI and the much later Mark VII. Parts outside these two distinct groups can only be combined with one another with great difficulty, however, and so this was often only seen in the more technically adept Legions such as the Iron Hands and Salamanders - in particular those elements of the Shattered Legions that were cut off from ready sources of resupply in the wake of the treachery at Istvaan V.

非制造Mark V是战场改装的真正融合，以技术神甫从未想过的方式结合了几种不同的盔甲型号。一般来说，Mark II和III的组件可以毫无问题地组合在一起，Mark IV、VI和更晚的Mark VII的组件也是如此。然而，这两个不同群体之外的部分只能非常困难地相互结合，因此这通常只出现在技术上更熟练的军团中，如钢铁之手和火蜥蜴军团，尤其是在伊斯塔万五号叛乱后被切断现成补给来源的破碎军团。

### Mark VI "Corvus Armour" 六型“渡鸦盔甲”

At the end of the production of Mark IV armour, a long term research project began to replace it. As this remained a theoretical endeavour for some time, it lead to the production of the Corvus Suit as a stopgap measure, using a mixture of new and old features. The Mk.VI has become infamous for its use amongst those associated with stealth and infiltration, such as the Raven Guard and Alpha Legion.

在Mark IV盔甲生产结束时，一项长期研究项目开始取代它。由于这在一段时间内仍然是一项理论工作，因此它导致了渡鸦盔甲的生产，作为一种权宜之计，使用新旧功能的混合。Mk.VI因其在暗鸦守卫和阿尔法军团等隐形和渗透部队中的使用而著名。

Raven Guard in Mark VI Corvus Armour 暗鸦守卫Mark VI渡鸦盔甲

The first Mark VI prototype design (which bore the provisional designation of Mk V at the time) was field-tested during the Great Crusade's Scalland Campaign by Legionaries of the Raven Guard, whose experiences and comments resulted in improved design features in the finalised armour mark. Prototype Mk.VI was already in circulation amongst many Legions by the time of the Heresy, but it proved controversial for some of these, with the Iron Warriors flatly rejecting the design entirely in favour of heavier armour. Meanwhile, the Alpha Legion was able to acquire small numbers of prototype Mk.VI Armour and modified it according to their Legion's own needs. Built in secret, the "Corvus-Alpha" pattern of Corvus Armour first saw use openly during the Horus Heresy. Production and mass issuing of the Mk.VI was interrupted by the outbreak of the Heresy and subsequent Schism of Mars, with many suits being evacuated off Mars as the world fell to Dark Mechanicum forces.

第一个Mark VI原型设计（当时暂定为Mk V）在大远征的斯卡兰战役期间由暗鸦守卫的军团士兵进行了实地测试，他们的经验和意见使最终的盔甲型号的设计特征得到了改进。到叛乱时代，原型Mk.VI已经在许多军团中流通，但其中一些军团对此存在争议，钢铁勇士断然拒绝了该设计，完全赞成更重的盔甲。与此同时，阿尔法军团能够获得少量原型Mk.VI盔甲，并根据军团自身的需求对其进行了改装。渡鸦盔甲的“渡鸦-阿尔法”型是秘密制造的，在荷鲁斯叛乱时期首次公开使用。Mk.VI的生产和大规模发行因叛乱爆发和随后的火星分裂而中断，随着世界落入黑暗机械教部队之手，许多盔甲被撤离火星。

Dark AngelsFirewing warrior in Mark VI with enhanced auto-senses Mark VI中的黑暗天使火翼战士，具有增强的自动感应

The finalised armour mark was issued just as the Horus Heresy began. It was named Corvus Armour in honour of Corvus Corax, Primarch of the Raven Guard, and the first sets of it shipped to the Raven Guard Legion ahead of all other loyalist legions, in recognition not only of their role in the design process, but of the sacrifices made by the Raven Guard in the Drop Site Massacre and their determination to rebuild and rejoin the fight against Horus the Great Betrayer. By the time of the Siege of Terra the Mk.VI was the most widely issued variant in loyalist service, with entire chapters of the Blood Angels and Imperial Fists being equipped with the pattern.

最终的盔甲型号是在荷鲁斯叛乱开始时发布的。它被命名为渡鸦盔甲，以纪念暗鸦守卫的原体科沃斯·科拉克斯，第一套盔甲比其他所有忠诚派军团都先运往暗鸦守卫军团，这不仅是为了表彰他们在设计过程中的作用，也是为了表彰暗鸦守卫在登陆点大屠杀中所做的牺牲，以及他们重建和重新加入与大背叛者荷鲁斯作战的决心。到泰拉围城时，Mk.VI是忠诚派服役中发行最广泛的变体，圣血天使和帝国之拳的整个战团都配备了这种型号。

Sons of Horus with Cthonian Pattern "aggressor" helmet 荷鲁斯之子配科索尼亚型“侵略者”头盔

Meanwhile, as the war ground on, the Dark Mechanicum was able to hack the data vaults of those still loyal to the Emperor to provide the designs to Horus and his forces. With countless Forge Worlds under the control of the Warmaster, production of Mark VI armour continued apace for the Traitors, and the Emperor's Children in particular used it en masse during the Siege of Terra. The predominance of the Corvus armour would continue well into The Scouring and the present age.

与此同时，随着战争的进行，黑暗机械教能够破解那些仍然忠于帝皇的人的数据库，为荷鲁斯和他的部队提供设计。随着无数铸造世界处于战帅的控制之下，叛徒们继续快速生产Mark VI盔甲，尤其是帝皇之子在泰拉围城期间大规模使用了它。渡鸦装甲的优势一直持续到大清洗和当代。

Alpha Legion Mark VI "Corvus-Alpha" variant 阿尔法军团Mark VI“渡鸦-阿尔法”变体

Mark VI armour offered no better protection-levels than the Mark IV, but incorporated more efficient internal systems where power-routing and auto-senses were concerned. Power systems were given back-up redundancies with no increase in weight, while both olfactory and auditory sensors were improved. Additionally, the armour featured the best stealth capabilities of all power armour marks so far designed, as well as a new method of compartmentalising components that allowed them to be swapped out with pieces from previous marks with ease. It is the lightest variant of power armour and featured dual technology circuits that allowed the replacement of rare or sophisticated parts with common or basic alternatives. It also included fail-safes such as duplicate power cabling.

Mark VI盔甲的防护等级并不比Mark IV好，但在能量布线和自动感应方面采用了更高效的内部系统。能量系统被赋予了备用冗余，而重量没有增加，同时嗅觉和听觉传感器都得到了改善。此外，该盔甲具有迄今为止设计的所有动力盔甲型号中最好的隐形能力，以及一种新的部件分隔方法，使其能够轻松地与以前型号中的部件互换。它是动力盔甲的最轻变体，具有双技术回路，允许用常见或基本的替代品替换稀有或复杂的零件。它还包括故障保护，如重复能量布线。

Corvus Armour is also distinctive for its conical nose – designed to house additional systems including advanced auto-senses. According to some, the studded left shoulder pad, taken from the previous MkV pattern, commemorates the glory of those heroes who stood at the Eternity Gate alongside the Emperor himself. Others said that studded shoulder pads shows the parts where additional ceramite armour was bonded to the armour - due to the need for quick field repairs.

渡鸦盔甲还因其锥形头部而与众不同，该头部设计用于容纳包括先进自动感应在内的其他系统。据一些人说，左肩甲上的嵌钉取自之前的Mk.V型号，纪念那些与帝皇并肩站在永恒之门的英雄的荣耀。其他人说，嵌钉肩甲显示了由于需要快速现场维修而将额外的陶钢装甲粘合到盔甲上的部分。

The Mark VI features clean, sleek lines, due to the routing of power cables under the exterior armour plate (abdominal covering of these was one of the suggestions made by the Raven Guard) and the increased sloping of the helmet design. The left shoulder armour features the tell-tale molecular bonding studs of Mark V armour. The initial suits did indeed use the ablative technique pioneered in Mark V suits, as paucity of supplies during the Heresy meant that some sacrifices still had to be made, even with a new armour design. In later years, the left shoulder was armoured to the same level as the right, but the studs were still affixed in a deliberate continuation of the honoured design that had won the Horus Heresy for the Imperials.

Mark VI具有干净、光滑的线条，这是由于外部装甲护板下的能量缆线布线（腹部覆盖这些是暗鸦守卫提出的建议之一）和头盔设计的倾斜度增加。左肩甲以Mark V盔甲的分子键合嵌钉为特色。最初的盔甲确实使用了Mark V盔甲中开创的烧蚀技术，因为叛乱时期物资匮乏意味着即使有新的盔甲设计，也必须做出一些牺牲。在后来的几年里，左肩被武装到与右肩相同的水平，但嵌钉仍然被有意地延续了为帝国赢得荷鲁斯叛乱的光荣设计。

Most such sets of battleplate in Dark Angels hands had been part of the original prototype shipments, and many had seen either extensive battlefield modification or featured experimental gear not present on later production issue MkVI battle plate. Indeed, many of these modifications would be replicated on standard issue MkVI armour captured from the Night Lords or acquired from Compliant Forge Worlds during the long years of the Thramas Crusade.

黑暗天使手中的大多数此类战斗护板都是原初原型装运的一部分，许多人要么看到了广泛的战场改装，要么看到了后来生产的Mk.VI战斗护板上没有的特色实验战备。事实上，在萨拉玛斯远征的漫长岁月里，从午夜领主手中缴获或从平定铸造世界获得的标准Mk.VI盔甲上复制了许多这些修改。

Chaos Space Marines can be seen in this armour despite its association with Imperial Space Marines; they captured a significant quantity of the suits on Mars during the Heresy, as well as Mechanicum production facilities, and were therefore able to produce their own Mark VI suits.

尽管与帝国星际战士有联系，但在这种盔甲中可以看到混沌星际战士；他们在叛乱期间在火星上缴获了大量的盔甲，以及机械教生产设施，因此能够生产自己的Mark VI盔甲。

### Mark VII "Aquila Armour" 七型“天鹰盔甲”

Mark VII armour, also known as the “Aquila,” “Imperator”, “Eagle” armour or “Armorum Impetor”, was developed during the Horus Heresy by loyalist Fabricator-LocumZagreus Kane, and remained in use as one of the most common form of power armour ten thousand years later. It represents the final major development of Mark VI armour.

Mark VII盔甲，也被称为“天鹰”、“帝皇”、“雄鹰”盔甲或“帝皇盔甲”，是在荷鲁斯叛乱期间由忠诚派铸造代理扎格瑞斯·凯恩开发的，并在一万年后仍被用作最常见的动力盔甲之一。它代表了Mark VI盔甲的最终重大发展。

Star Phantom in Mark VII Aquila Armour 星辰幻影Mark VII天鹰盔甲

While the final battle for Mars was underway, the Imperium realised the planet would eventually be lost. Armour development teams were transferred to Earth to continue, incorporating their latest work into the armour type that would become Mark VII. As Mars fell to Horus, the new armour began reaching the Space Marines on Terra and Luna. The Mk.VII was first issued in large numbers to the loyalist Legions during the Great Scouring.

当争夺火星的最后一战正在进行时，帝国意识到这颗行星最终会陷落。盔甲开发团队被转移到地球继续工作，将他们的最新工作融入到后来成为Mark VII的盔甲类型中。随着火星落入荷鲁斯之手，新的盔甲开始到达泰拉和月球上的太星际战士。Mk.VII在大清洗期间首次大量发行给忠诚派军团。

Mark VII's main improvement is the newly designed chest plastron which covers the chest and arm cabling. The chest bears the Imperial Eagle, giving the armour its common name of *Armorum Imperator*, or Eagle Armour. This mark abandons the studded shoulder armour plate, and replaces the helmet. As Mark VII is a development of Mark VI, both have a great deal in common, and parts from one mark are readily interchangeable with parts from the other, like the helmet.

Mark VII的主要改进是新设计的胸甲，它覆盖了胸部和手臂的缆线。胸前刻有帝国天鹰，因此盔甲的通用名称为帝皇盔甲或天鹰盔甲。这个型号放弃了嵌钉的肩甲护板，取而代之的是头盔。由于Mark VII是Mark VI的发展，两者有很多共同点，一个型号的零件很容易与另一个型号中的零件互换，比如头盔。

Mark VII contains its own back-up power supply and a solar array to recharge this, so the suit can operate even without its backpack though only a short time.

Mark VII包含自己的备用能源和一个太阳能充能阵列来为其充能，因此即使没有背包，该盔甲也可以运行，尽管时间很短。

Company Champions wear armours specifically crafted for them. This pattern of armour is known informally as the Curadh variant and is based loosely on Aquila-pattern battleplate. It features additional reinforcement upon the shoulder guards, designed to showcase the Champion's many honours and provide better protection. It also comes with a reinforced breastplate emblazoned with a winged shield, where a Champion is allowed to display his personal heraldry. As for the helm it is deliberately fashioned to resemble knightly helms of Terra's ancient past.

连队冠军穿着专门为他们制作的盔甲。这种盔甲型号被非正式地称为库拉德变体，它基础松散地基于天鹰型的战斗护板。它在肩甲上增加了额外的加固，旨在展示冠军的许多荣誉，并提供更好的保护。它还配有一个饰有翼盾的加固胸甲，冠军可以在那里展示他的个人纹章。至于头盔，它被精心打造成类似于泰拉古代的骑士头盔。

### Mark VIII "Errant Armour" 八型“游侠盔甲”

The suit itself is a highly modified Mark VII, with additional fixed armour plates on the torso that now enclose all of the suit's cables, which were vulnerable to weapons fire, a new armoured collar protects the helmet's respirator, and also addressed the vulnerability issues of the neck joint, which had been known to act as a "shell-trap", (a round could hit the chest armour and be deflected up into the neck joint), and augmented poldron armoured sections. The new collar required a new helmet design, which caused problems for backward compatibility with earlier helmet types.

该盔甲本身是经过高度改装的Mark VII，躯干上有额外的固定装甲护板，现在包围了盔甲的所有缆线，这些缆线容易受到武器射击的影响，一个新的盔甲护颈保护了头盔的呼吸器，还解决了颈关节的脆弱性问题，颈关节被称为“子弹陷阱”（一发子弹可能会击中胸甲并向上偏转到颈关节），并增加了肩甲部分。新的护颈需要新的头盔设计，这导致了与早期头盔类型的向后兼容性问题。

到目前为止，Mark VIII盔甲的产量有限，在第41个千年的战场上出现的数量也有限。它通常被老兵星际战士穿戴，作为军衔的象征，在死亡守望和米诺陶的队伍中也很常见。

### Mark X Power Armour 十型动力盔甲

Compared to the earlier marks of Power Armour, which were standardised with a number and a single name, the Mark X Power Armour is actually a versatile modular system of ceramite armour pieces that can be assembled into different variants. The default variant of Mk X Armour is the Tacticus pattern, which is worn by the majority of Primaris Space Marines. Other variants include the lighter Phobos pattern with scouting and stealth capabilities and the heavier Gravis pattern worn by Aggressor Squads.

与早期的动力盔甲型号相比，Mark X动力盔甲实际上是一个多功能的模块化系统，可以组装成不同的变体。Mk X盔甲的默认变体是战术型，大多数原铸星际战士都穿着它。其他变体包括具有侦察和隐形能力的较轻的火卫一型，以及侵略者小队穿戴的较重的格拉维斯型。

## Other Designs 其他设计

### Runic Armour 符文盔甲

The Space Wolves grant valiant members of their chapter specially crafted suits of power armour inscribed with runes of protection by the Rune Priests. These provide the wearer with a defence against psychic attacks. This armour is created by the Iron Priests, and at least some examples are known to be codified as the Mark XII Lupio pattern.

太空野狼为其战团的英勇成员提供了特制的动力盔甲，上面刻有符文牧师的保护符文。这些为穿戴者提供了抵御灵能攻击的防御。这种盔甲是由钢铁牧师创造的，至少有一些实例被编纂为Mark XII野狼型。

### Aegis Armour 神盾盔甲

Grey Knights wear Aegis Armour, power armour that is incredibly well crafted; anointed and inscribed with prayers and wards, ritually consecrated and psychically charged. The suits are generally based on components from Mark VI, VII and VIII power armour designs, with a modified helmet design with a tapered, knight-like visor.

灰骑士身穿神盾盔甲，这是一种制作精良的动力盔甲；受膏并刻有祷文和保护，在仪式上被圣化，在灵能上被填充。这些盔甲通常基于Mark VI、VII和VIII动力盔甲设计的组件，采用改进的头盔设计，带有锥形，骑士般的面甲。

### Terminator Armour 终结者盔甲

Like power armour, Terminator suits have an outer shell of ceramite-bonded plates powered by electrically-motivated fibre bundles. Plates of heavy plasteel further armour the ceramite sections, especially on the front of the suit. This extra armouring provides a level of protection that is far superior to normal Marine armour; not even a Krak missile will penetrate the suit's breastplate.

像动力盔甲一样，终结者盔甲有一个由陶钢结合护板组成的外壳，由电动纤维束提供动力。厚塑钢板进一步保护了陶钢部分，尤其是在盔甲的前部。这种额外的装甲提供了远远优于普通战士盔甲的保护水平；即使是穿甲导弹也无法穿透盔甲的胸甲。

## Additional Designs 额外设计

A number of variant components for Space Marine power armour are known to exist. Although designs and components are subject to variation, especially in the case of Artificer Armour, more standardized spin-off designs have been developed over the course of the Imperium's history. These include the Sarum and Phoenician pattern helmets (the latter of which was exclusively used by the Emperor's Children), and the Praetor pattern power armour, which was locally produced at Veridia Forge by the Ultramarines in the later years of the Great Crusade.

已知存在许多用于星际战士动力盔甲的变体组件。尽管设计和组件可能会发生变化，特别是在精工盔甲的情况下，但在帝国的历史过程中，已经开发出了更标准的衍生设计。其中包括萨勒姆和凤凰型的头盔（后者仅供帝皇之子使用），以及在大远征后期由极限战士在维瑞迪亚铸造世界当地生产的执政官型的动力盔甲。

In addition, a number of Space Marine backpack units have been identified. Although the M.35, M.36, and M.37 designs are usually used by Imperial Space Marines in the 41st Millennium , a number of designs associated with older Marks of power armour, such as the Mars and Phaeton pattern backpack units, are still used to some extent. Additionally, the Anvilus pattern backpack is a Crusade-era Imperial prototype that incorporated advanced thruster vents for use in void operations, but at the cost of decreased radiation shielding.

此外，还发现了一些数量的星际战士背包单元。尽管帝国星际战士在第41个千年通常使用M.35、M.36和M.37设计，但一些与旧动力盔甲型号相关的设计，如火星和法厄同型背包单元，在一定程度上仍在使用。此外，安维鲁斯型背包是大远征时代的帝国原型，它采用了先进的推进喷口，用于虚空作战，但以减少辐射屏蔽为代价。

### Conflicting sources 来源冲突

 - 

The MkII armour is claimed in Deathwatch: Rites of Battle to not be able to move its helmet, with the Astartes still being able to rotate his head around inside said helmet. This conflicts with Forge World's depictions of the MkII armour set and the novel Horus Rising, where an Astartes is seen in MKII Power Armour with the helmet turned toward several of his brethren. According to Games Workshop, Forge World intentionally redesigned the armour's helmet and neck joint in this way to enable more modelling options.

 - 

《死亡守望：战斗仪式》中声称MkII盔甲不能移动头盔，阿斯塔特仍然可以在头盔内旋转头部。这与Forge World对MkII盔甲设定和小说《荷鲁斯崛起》的描述相冲突，在小说中，一名阿斯塔特穿着MkII动力盔甲，头盔转向他的几个兄弟。据Games Workshop所示，Forge World有意以这种方式重新设计了盔甲的头盔和颈部接口，以提供更多的建模选项。
