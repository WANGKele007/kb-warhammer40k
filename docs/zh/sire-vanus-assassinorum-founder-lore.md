Sire Vanus was the founder and leader of the Vanus Temple of the Officio Assassinorum during the Great Crusade and Horus Heresy. Sire Vanus was a calm and calculating director, often simply providing data and information to his colleagues instead of engaging in debate.

在大远征和荷鲁斯叛乱期间，赛尔·瓦努斯是刺客庭瓦努斯神庙的创始人和领导者。赛尔·瓦努斯是一位冷静而精明的理事，他经常只是向同僚提供数据和信息，而不是参与辩论。

In the aftermath of the Siege of Terra he allied with Master of the Chosen of Malcador, Khalid Hassan, and sought to have him act as the new Grand Master of Assassins and rebuild the organization, which by then had its agents scattered all across the galaxy. He urged Hassan to take a more decisive stand against the remaining Primarchs in order to prevent another Heresy.

在泰拉围城之后，他与马卡多亲选的导师哈立德·哈桑结盟，并试图让他担任新的刺客大导师，重建组织，当时该组织的特工分散在整个银河系。他敦促哈桑对剩下的原体采取更果断的立场，以防止另一次叛乱。
