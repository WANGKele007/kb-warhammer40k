Harpax *"Swarmer"* Scouts were a class of cyber-drones of ancient origin used by the traitor Mechanicum during the Horus Heresy.

掠夺者“虫群”侦察机是荷鲁斯叛乱期间叛徒机械教使用的一类古代智控无人机。

Harpax swarm 掠夺者虫群

## Description 描述

They were usually used as front-line troop or vanguard swarms, ranging ahead of larger Stalker Cohorts.

它们通常被用作前线部队或先锋虫群，先于更大的追猎者大队。

In smaller groups, Harpax would harass the enemy's forward units to clear a path for their master's army. In larger numbers, they would overwhelm the opposition, capable of dismantling armoured convoys through sheer force of their numbers.

在较小的组中，掠夺者会骚扰敌人的前方部队，为他们的主人的军队扫清道路。如果人数更多，他们将压倒对手，能够凭借其庞大的数量拆除装甲车队。

Barely larger than a human, they are armed with Exo-Planar Repeaters for anti-infantry or Lascutters for anti-armour roles, overwhelming their enemies through sheer weight of numbers.

他们的体型几乎不比人类大，装备有用于反步兵的异维连发枪或用于反装甲的激光切割者，通过庞大的数量压倒敌人。

Harpax were encountered by the Dark Angels on Galatia during the Thramas Crusade.

在萨拉玛斯远征期间，黑暗天使在加拉提亚遇到了掠夺者。
