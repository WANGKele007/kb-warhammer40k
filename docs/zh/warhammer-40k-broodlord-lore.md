Broodlords are a Tyranid leader organism regarded as the epitome of the Genestealer breed.

巢主是一种被视为基因窃取者品系典型的泰伦领袖生物。

Broodlord 巢主

## Overview 概述

Broodlords are even more powerful than the standard Genestealer, and are specifically adept at infiltration to get closer to the enemy. They are very adaptable and highly intelligent as well as being a synapse creature, allowing it and its pack to remain in touch with the Hive Mind. Their powerful talons can cut through most materials with ease.

巢主甚至比标准的基因窃取者更强大，并且特别擅长渗透以接近敌人。它们具有很强的适应性和高度的智能，并且是一种突触生物，使它和它的群体能够与虫巢思维保持联系。它们强大的爪子可以轻松地刺穿大多数材料。

Broodlord 巢主

The Broodlord is always the first genestealer to make planetfall when the Tyranids first infest a world, and leads genestealers in bloody ambushes.

当泰伦首次侵扰一个世界时，巢主总是第一个让行星陷落的基因窃取者，并带领基因窃取者进行血腥的伏击。

When a Tyranid hive fleet enters an infested system, the Hive Mind asserts its synaptic dominion over the Broodlord and its clan.

当一支泰伦虫巢舰队进入一个被侵扰的星系时，虫巢思维会宣称它对巢主及其氏族的突触统治。
