The Attilan Gate is a stable Warp travel route that allows Imperial vessels to move from Imperium Sanctus to Imperium Nihilus.

阿提兰之门是一条稳定的亚空间航行路线，允许帝国战舰从帝国圣疆移动到帝国暗面。

Located near Attila, it is the second known such stable path through the Great Rift after the Nachmund Gauntlet. The passage was discovered by the Rogue TraderKatla Helvintr. Thought large and capable of accommodating many ships at once, it is still considered somewhat unstable. Belisarius Cawl utilized NecronPylon technology in an attempt to stabilize the gate and make it more passable. Following the Last Battle of the Pit, the nearby Warp Rift dubbed the Pit of Raukos was sealed by Cawl and the Gate was stabilized.

它位于阿提拉附近，是继纳克蒙德走廊之后第二条已知的穿过大裂隙的稳定路径。这个通路是由行商浪人卡特拉·赫尔文特发现的。虽然它很大，可以同时容纳许多船只，但仍然被认为有点不稳定。贝利撒留·考尔利用死灵方尖碑技术试图稳定大门，使其更容易通行。在深坑终战之后，附近被称为劳科斯深坑的亚空间裂隙被考尔封锁，大门也被稳定下来。
