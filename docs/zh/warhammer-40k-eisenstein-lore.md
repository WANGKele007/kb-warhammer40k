The *Eisenstein* was an Imperial warship that played a crucial role at the outbreak of the Horus Heresy, carrying loyalistSpace Marines back to Terra from the Isstvan System, to warn the Emperor of Horus' treachery.

爱森斯坦号是一艘帝国战舰，在荷鲁斯叛乱爆发时发挥了至关重要的作用，将忠诚派星际战士从伊斯塔万星系带回泰拉，警告帝皇荷鲁斯的背叛。

The loyalist legionaries were referred to as The Seventy.

忠诚派军团士兵被称为“七十人”。

## History 历史

The Eisenstein was considered an old craft even by the time of the Heresy. At over 2 kilometers long and resembling a Gladius Class Frigate, the *Eisenstein* was in service to the Death Guard by the events on Isstvan III. According to the *Eisenstein*s captain, in an ancient Earth language, "Eisenstein" means "iron-stone", and was the name of two noted men from the Age of Terra - one a remembrancer, the other a scientist.

即使在叛乱时代，爱森斯坦号也被认为是一艘古老的船。爱森斯坦号长2公里，形似短剑级护卫舰，在伊斯塔万三号事件中为死亡守卫服役。据爱森斯坦号的船长介绍，在古代地球语中，“爱森斯坦”的意思是“铁-石”，是泰拉时代两位著名人物的名字，一位是记述者，另一位是科学家。

Shortly before the massacre at Isstvan III, Nathaniel Garro and his Company were assigned to the *Eisenstein*. Because of an injury suffered in a recent skirmish, Garro was prevented from joining the surface attack with the other Death Guards. First CaptainCalas Typhon stationed Ignatius Grulgor's company aboard the *Eisenstein* to deal with Garro and his men when the time came. As was typical of Astartes ships, day-to-day operations were overseen by a Human crew, in the *Eisenstein's* case this was under Shipmaster Baryk Carya. Before the bombardment started, Saul Tarvitz transmitted a warning to the *Eisenstein*, before flying down to the planet's surface to attempt to warn the other loyalists. Acting on this warning, Garro and his men investigated the rest of the ship and found Grulgor and his men preparing to bombard the surface with virus bombs. Garro attempted to stop it, and one of the bombs ruptured, killing Grulgor and his men.

在伊斯塔万三号大屠杀发生前不久，纳撒尼尔·伽罗和他的连队被分配到爱森斯坦号。由于在最近的一次小规模冲突中受伤，伽罗被阻止与其他死亡守卫一起参加地面进攻。第一连长卡拉斯·提丰将伊格内修斯·格鲁戈尔的连队驻扎在爱森斯坦号上，在时机成熟时处理伽罗和他的部下。与阿斯塔特舰船的典型情况一样，日常运营由人类船员监督，在爱森斯坦号的情况下，这是由舰长巴里克·卡里亚监督的。轰炸开始前，索尔·塔维茨向爱森斯坦号发出警告，然后飞到行星表面试图警告其他忠诚者。根据这一警告，伽罗和他的部下调查了船的其余部分，发现格鲁戈尔和他的部下准备用病毒炸弹轰炸地表。伽罗试图阻止它，其中一枚炸弹破裂，导致格鲁戈尔和他的部下死亡。

Shortly before taking the *Eisenstein* to warp, to travel to Terra and warn the Emperor, Garro was joined by Iacton Qruze and a group of civilians escaping from Horus's flagship, at the direction of Garviel Loken. The *Eisenstein* narrowly evaded fire from Typhon's ship, the Terminus Est, before escaping the system.

在将爱森斯坦号送入亚空间，前往泰拉并警告帝皇之前不久，伽罗与伊克顿·克鲁兹和一群从荷鲁斯的旗舰逃离的平民一起前往加维尔·洛肯的方向。爱森斯坦号在逃离星系之前，勉强躲过了提丰的舰船终焉号的火力。

During its voyage, the ship's Geller field failed briefly, allowing the power of Nurgle to re-animate the corpses of the dead Marines, who were unstoppable until Garro ordered the ship to make an emergency reversion to the Materium. The tainted Marines disappeared, but the stress of the reversion was too much for the ship's Navigator. The *Eisenstein* was stranded, until Garro ordered the ship to jettison its warp engines and detonate them, in the hope of signalling a passing ship. They were rescued by the Imperial Fists' mobile fortress monastery, the Phalanx, and the *Eisenstein* was scuttled.

在航行过程中，该舰船的盖勒立场短暂失效，让纳垢的力量重新激活了死亡战士的尸体，他们势不可挡，直到伽罗命令该舰紧急返回物质界。被污染的战士消失了，但这场回归的压力对舰上的导航者来说太大了。爱森斯坦号滞留了，直到伽罗命令该舰抛弃亚空间引擎并引爆，希望以此向过往舰船发出信号。他们被帝国之拳的移动堡垒修道院山阵号救出，爱森斯坦号被凿沉。

Since the Heresy, the *Eisenstein* has garnered a mysterious reputation and its status remains unclear. Sometime after the formation of the Great Rift, many saw the vessel reappearing on multiple occasions. On the seventh such appearance, it is said that Mortarion himself will appear and wreak destruction.

自叛乱以来，爱森斯坦号获得了神秘的名声，其状态尚不清楚。在大裂隙形成后的某个时候，许多人看到这艘战舰多次出现。据说，在第七次出现时，莫塔里安本人将出现并造成破坏。

## Previous Editions 以前版本

The *Eisenstein* was originally created for the 1988 Realm of Chaos "Slaves to Darkness" book, published for the First Edition of Warhammer 40,000 in order to introduce the Horus Heresy. The one-paragraph mention describes how "loyal Adeptus Astartes officers and troops" took over a frigate named *Eisenstein* after they discovered Horus's Chaos taint at the time of Isstvan III being bioweapon bombarded. The ship was reported to flee to warn others as Horus moved to Isstvan V.

爱森斯坦号最初是为1988年出版的《混沌领域：黑暗之奴》一书而创作的，该书为《战锤40000》第一版出版，旨在介绍荷鲁斯叛乱。其中一段描述了“忠诚者阿斯塔特修会军官和部队”在伊斯塔万三号遭到生物武器轰炸时发现了荷鲁斯的混沌污染后，如何接管了一艘名为爱森斯坦号的护卫舰。据报告，当荷鲁斯驶向伊斯塔万五号时，该舰逃离以警告其他人。

The 1989 game "Space Marine", precursor to the Epic game system and takes place during the Heresy, describes the *Eisenstein* in a story box through the conversation of Captains Tarvitz, Garro, Varren, and unnamed others. Their topic was to either "Betray Horus or betray the Emperor," and in it we are told that Horus had five "Chapters", incorporated warrior-lodges from Davin within them, and the Codex command structures were disregarded. Additionally, the "bulk" of the legionaries under Horus were now loyal to "feral world deities" and to Horus. Istvaan (alternate spelling in early texts) was destroyed without order from Terra, and the virus bombing was not justified.

1989年的游戏《星际战士》是Epic游戏系统的前身，发生在叛乱时期，通过连长塔维茨、伽罗、瓦伦和其他未具名的人的对话，在故事箱中描述了爱森斯坦号。他们的主题是“背叛荷鲁斯或背叛帝皇”，其中我们被告知荷鲁斯有五个“战团”，其中包含了来自戴文的战士结社，而圣典指挥结构被忽视了。此外，荷鲁斯领导下的“大部分”军团现在忠于“蛮荒世界神”和荷鲁斯。伊斯特万（早期文本中的另一种拼写）在没有来自泰拉的命令的情况下被摧毁，病毒轰炸是非正当的。

There is no mention of loyalists being killed. Captain Varren says that he has 15 men of the World Eaters, Captain Garro says 12, an unnamed voice says 10 from the Luna Wolves, and another unnamed voice says 20 from the Thousand Sons. Tarvitz says that there are 9 Emperor's Children beyond himself. In total, it represented 70. They agree that Varren will take the frigate *Eisenstein* because it is able to warp jump, and Tarvitz mentions that it was falling behind because of mechanical problems. The others would slow their own ships and then take control of the five ships. Four of the ships would then defend the Eisenstein and clear a path for it to flee. The World Eaters under Varren would then take the message.

没有提到忠诚者被杀。瓦伦连长说他有15名吞世者，伽罗连长说12名，一个未具名的声音说影月苍狼有10名，另一个未具名的声音说千子有20名。塔维茨说，除了他自己，还有9个帝皇之子。总共有70人。他们一致认为，瓦伦将乘坐爱森斯坦号护卫舰，因为它能够亚空间跳跃，塔维茨提到，由于机械问题，它落后了。其他人将放慢自己的舰船，然后控制五艘船。然后，四艘船将保卫爱森斯坦号，并为它的逃跑开辟道路。瓦伦领导下的吞世者随后会接受这一消息。

## Canon Conflicts 标准冲突

In one source, the exact number of Marines from each Legion that escaped to warn the Imperium were:

在一个消息来源中，每个军团逃离以警告帝国的战士的确切人数是：

 - 

13 Death Guard (Led by Garro)

 - 

13名死亡守卫（由伽罗领导）

 - 

14 World Eaters (Possibly including Varren)

 - 

14名吞世者（可能包括瓦伦）

 - 

11 Sons of Horus 11名荷鲁斯之子

 - 

21 Thousand Sons 21名千子

 - 

11 Emperor's Children 11名帝皇之子

Other sources state that the 70 marines were lead by Captain Varren of the World Eaters, and escaped in Thunderhawk transports. Still another source suggests that Captain Saul Tarvitz of the Emperor's Children led the group. According to this account, Varren's World Eaters were supposed to seize control of the *Eisenstein* at the same time that the other Marines would take control of four separate ships (presumably their own transports/frigates) and use these vessels to defend the *Eisenstein* so it could jump to warp space. This does not mesh with later sources, which seem to imply that all 70 Marines were aboard the *Eisenstein* on its voyage to Terra.

其他消息来源称，70名战士由吞世者的瓦伦连长率领，乘坐雷鹰运输机逃跑。还有一个消息来源表明，帝皇之子的索尔·塔维茨连长领导了该组织。根据这一说法，瓦伦的吞世者应该在其他战士控制四艘单独的舰船（可能是他们自己的运输舰/护卫舰）的同时夺取爱森斯坦号的控制权，并使用这些船只保卫爱森斯坦号，使其能够跳到亚空间。这与后来的消息来源不符，后者似乎暗示所有70名战士都在爱森斯坦号前往泰拉的航行中。

This account also presents a slightly different number or Marines per Legion:

此叙述还显示了每个军团的战士人数略有不同：

 - 

16 World Eaters (Including Varren)

 - 

16名吞世者（包括瓦伦）

 - 

13 Death Guard (Including Garro)

 - 

13名死亡守卫（包括伽罗）

 - 

10 Luna Wolves 10名影月苍狼

 - 

20 Thousand Sons 20名千子

 - 

10 Emperor's Children (Including Tarvitz)

 - 

10名帝皇之子（包括塔维茨）

Unfortunately this adds up to only 69 men. It is possible that one of the unnamed officers of either the Luna Wolves/Sons of Horus or Thousand Sons included themselves in the count and the other didn't, since the numbers appear as direct quotes from the officers in question. Of course, there is no way Varren, who remarks in the source that the total of seventy Marines is equal to the sum of the five Legions' Numbers, could know this. It is thus probably a simple mistake in the text.

不幸的是，这加起来只有69人。可能是影月苍狼/荷鲁斯之子或千子的一名未具名军官将自己纳入了计数，而另一个没有，因为这些数字似乎是直接引用了有关军官的话。当然，瓦伦在消息来源中表示，70名战士的总数等于5个军团的人数的总和，他不可能知道这一点。因此，这可能是文本中的一个简单错误。

Each of these accounts cause problems. The original account introduces the Inquisition at the time of the Emperor. Gav Thorpe has since changed the creation of the Inquisition to after the Emperor's Ascension for the Inquisitor game.

这些叙述中的每一个都会造成问题。原初叙述介绍了帝皇时代的审判庭。此后，加夫·索普在审判官游戏中将审判庭的创建改为帝皇升天后。

According to *The Flight of the Eisenstein*, all the Marines aboard the *Eisenstein* are Death Guards, except for Iacton Qruze; Varren does not appear, while Tarvitz attempts to reach the planet's surface rather than escaping aboard the ship.

据《爱森斯坦号的逃亡》，爱森斯坦号上的所有战士都是死亡守卫，除了伊克顿·克鲁兹；瓦伦没有出现，而塔维茨试图到达行星表面，而不是登船逃离。

In the audio drama Garro: Sword of Truth, loyalist elements of the Emperor's Children and the World Eaters (including Varren) flee the Isstvan system at the same time, and reach Terra shortly after Garro returns from his mission to Calth.

在广播剧《伽罗：真理之剑》中，帝皇之子和吞世者（包括瓦伦）的忠诚成员同时逃离了伊斯塔万星系，并在伽罗结束对考斯的任务返回后不久到达泰拉。
