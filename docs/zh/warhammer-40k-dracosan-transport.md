The Dracosan Armoured Transport was an armoured personnel carrier used by the Imperial Army during the Great Crusade and Horus Heresy, employed almost exclusively by forces following the Solar Auxilia order of battle or similar formations, like the household troops of certain Rogue Trader Militants or the elite retainers of high status Imperial Commanders.

天龙座装甲运输车是帝国军队在大远征和荷鲁斯叛乱期间使用的一种装甲运兵车，几乎完全由遵循太阳辅助军战斗顺序或类似编队的部队使用，如某些战斗派行商浪人的家族部队或高阶帝国指挥官的精英仆人。

## Overview 概述

The Dracosan Armoured Transport was manufactured on several dozen forge worlds across the imperium to standards only usually required of war machines destined for service with the Legiones Astartes.

天龙座装甲运输车是在帝国的几十个铸造世界上制造的，其标准通常只适用于为阿斯塔特军团服务的战争机器。

The Dracosan was large enough to accommodate a full-strength Infantry Section of twenty troops, and heavily armoured enough to protect them from intense enemy fire.Its armour was greater than the ubiquitous Rhino and included an inherent rad/alchem protection as well as a life support system of greater endurance.

天龙座足够大，可以容纳一个由20名士兵组成的全兵力步兵部，并且装甲足够重，可以保护他们免受敌人的猛烈火力。它的装甲比无处不在的犀牛更强，包括固有的防辐射/防化学武器保护以及更持久的生命支持系统。

Dracosan Armoured Transport 天龙座装甲运输车

Equipped with a fearsome array of weapons of its own, including either a powerful twin-linked lascannon or a mighty demolisher cannon at the expense of some of its transport capacity, the Dracosan could steadfastly defend itself against enemy counter-attack and pound an adversary’s emplacements to dust before those on board disembarked to storm enemy positions. In addition, the Dracosan was equipped with a searchlight, extra armour and a smoke launcher. It could also be further outfitted with a flare shield, a dozer blade, an auxiliary drive and armed with a pintle-mountedmulti-laser or heavy flamer and up to two hunter-killer missiles.

配备了一系列可怕的武器，包括强大的双联激光炮或以牺牲部分运输能力为代价的强大粉碎者炮，天龙座可以坚定地防御敌人的反击，并在车上人员登陆敌人阵地之前将敌人的阵地夷为平地。此外，天龙座还配备了探照灯、额外装甲和烟雾发射器。它还可以进一步配备一个闪光盾、一个推土铲刀、一个辅助驱动，并配备一个安装在枢轴上的多管激光或重型火焰枪以及最多两枚猎人杀手导弹。

A cargo transport variant of the Dracosan is known as the Brontosan.

天龙座的一种货运变体被称为雷龙。
