**History 历史**

Solemnus is believed to have been first settled by mankind during the millennia-distant Dark Age of Technology. A thousand years before the Black Templars, under Marshal Emrik, came to Solemnus to quash an insidious genestealer cult that had held almost the entire planet in its filthy talons. After cleansing the planet of the genestealer taint, the Black Templars claimed Solemnus and used it as a recruiting world. The chapter maintained a monastery-fortress on the planet, which they used to resupply and gather suitable recruits for their chapter between crusades; consisting of a permanent population of a hundred battle-brothers, as well as the neophytes, serfs, adepts and many other minions who saw to the needs of the Black Templars and the fortress itself.

人们认为，在千年前的黑暗技术时代，人类首次定居了庄严星。一千年前，在埃姆里克元帅的领导下，黑色圣堂来到庄严星，镇压了一个潜伏的基因窃取者教派，这个教派几乎把整个行星都控制在肮脏的爪子里。在清除了行星上的基因窃取者的污染后，黑色圣堂宣称拥有庄严星，并将其用作募兵世界。该战团在行星上维护着一座修道院堡垒，他们在远征之间用它来为战团补给和招募合适的新兵；由一百名战斗兄弟组成的常驻人口，以及满足黑色圣堂和堡垒本身需求的新进者、侍从、专家和许多其他下级官员。

A world troubled by storms, its populace sustained themselves by sheep-farming, forestry, the raising of livestock, basic mining and quarrying. Those same people maintained their own method of government via a system of petty kingdoms and vassal lords. But it was the castellan of the monastery-fortress who was the true overlord, and effectively governor, of the planet, who represented Solemnus to the greater Imperium.

一个饱受风暴困扰的世界，其民众依靠养羊、林业、畜牧业、基本采矿和采石来维持生计。这些人通过小王国和附庸领主制度维持着自己的政府方式。但只有修道院要塞的堡主才是这个行星真正的霸主，也是有效的统治者，他代表庄严星进入了更大的帝国。

Right before the Black Templars fleet, under Marshal Brant, were expected to return to the planet, from the Apollo Subsector, and the site of the Liberation of Lugnasad, Solemnus was attacked by an armada of the Blood Scar Ork tribe, under Warboss Morkrull Grimskar. The Ork armada targeted the monastery-fortress with an orbital bombardment, which the fortress' shield generators absorbed, before finally sending a Rok crashing down near the fortress. The rok fired its weapons at point blank range and the force of the impact caused the fortress' shields to overload and collapse. A section of its walls were destroyed during the attack, which allowed a large force of Orks to invade the fortress and massacre all inside.

就在布兰特元帅率领的黑色圣堂舰队预计将从阿波罗分区和卢格纳萨德解放的地点返回行星之前，庄严星遭到了战争头目莫尔克鲁尔·格里姆斯卡率领的血疤欧克部落舰队的袭击。欧克舰队对修道院堡垒进行了轨道轰炸，堡垒的护盾发生机吸收了轰炸，最终一个巨石被击落在堡垒附近。巨石在近距离发射了武器，冲击力导致堡垒的护盾过载并崩溃。它的一部分城墙在袭击中被摧毁，这使得一支庞大的欧克部队入侵了堡垒，并在里面进行了大屠杀。

When Marshal Brant's fleet, they found Solemnus under assault. The Black Templars fleet attacked the Ork armada, which began to retreat under fire, before sending drop pods to the planet. A fierce battle commenced, with the Black Templars fighting the Orks within the ruins of the fortress. Soon the battle was finished, with all the Orks on Solemnus killed. The Black Templars searched for any survivors amid the ruins, only finding Brother Jarold, nearly dead, atop a pile of Ork corpses. His wounds were so great that the only way to save him was to inter him within a Dreadnought.

当布兰特元帅的舰队发现庄严星遭到袭击时。黑色圣堂舰队袭击了欧克舰队，欧克舰队在炮火下开始撤退，然后向行星发射空投舱。一场激烈的战斗开始了，黑色圣堂在堡垒的废墟中与欧克作战。战斗很快就结束了，庄严星上的所有欧克都被杀死。黑色圣堂在废墟中寻找幸存者，只发现几乎死亡的贾罗德兄弟，躺在一堆欧克尸体上。他的伤口太严重了，唯一能救他的办法就是把他送入无畏里。

The Black Templars quit Solemnus, soon after the battle and destroyed the ruins of the fortress. It is no longer a recruiting world for the Black Templars. Or indeed any Chapter, thanks to the warning beacon left in orbit by the departing Space Marines. Now it was left as a memorial to those brethren who had lost their lives to the Blood Scar tribe's assault on the planet, making it no more, and no less, than another relic of the Black Templars Chapter.

战斗结束后不久，黑色圣堂离开了庄严星，摧毁了堡垒的废墟。这不再是一个黑色圣堂的招募世界。或者说，任何战团，都要归功于即将离开的星际战士在轨道上留下的警告信标。现在，它被留下来纪念那些在血痕部落对行星的袭击中丧生的兄弟们，使其不比黑色圣堂的另一个遗物多，也不少。

Marshal Brant's remaining forces departed in pursuit of the Orks. Renamed the Solemnus Crusade, they vowed to avenge their departed brethren against the hordes that followed Warboss Morkrull Grimskar.

布兰特元帅的剩余部队出发追击欧克。更名为庄严远征，他们发誓要为死去的兄弟们报仇，对抗跟随战争头目莫尔克鲁尔·格里姆斯卡的部落。
