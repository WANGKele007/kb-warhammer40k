Marius Amalrich was a Marshal in the Black TemplarsChapter.

马吕斯·阿马尔里奇是黑色圣堂战团的元帅。

Marius Amalrich 马吕斯·阿马尔里奇

## History 历史

Commanding the Cruxis Crusade, he came to the aid of Imperial defenders on Cadia during the 13th Black Crusade. During the final actions of the battle for the Cadian System, Amalrich was among those seeking to protect Belisarius Cawl's artefact from the Black Legion. This team of defenders, including Saint Celestine and InquisitorGreyfax, were ultimately saved by Eldar and guided into the Webway on Kalisus.

在第13次黑色远征期间，他指挥克鲁西斯远征，帮助了卡迪亚的帝国守军。在卡迪亚星系之战的最后行动中，阿马尔里奇是寻求保护贝利萨留·考尔的造物免受黑色军团伤害的人之一。这支防御小队，包括圣塞勒斯汀和审判官格雷法克斯，最终被灵能救下，并被引导进入卡利苏斯的网道。

Amalrich was distrustful of his new Eldar "allies" but nonetheless was forced to accompany them to Macragge, where he aided in the fight to resurrect Roboute Guilliman in the Ultramar Campaign. Amalrich was the sole survivor of his Crusade after the battle at the Fortress of Hera, and took up the Black Sword in the aftermath. Later during the Terran Crusade Amalrich was captured by Chaos forces along with the rest of the Imperial expedition, but freed by Cypher and his Harlequin allies. The escapees came under renewed Chaos assault, and Amalrich gave his life to impale Skarbrand through the chest with the black sword. Guilliman was later able to exploit this wound to defeat the Bloodthirster.

阿马尔里奇不信任他的新灵能“盟友”，但仍被迫陪同他们前往马库拉格，在那里他在奥特拉玛战役中协助复活罗伯特·基里曼。阿马尔里奇是赫拉要塞之战后远征的唯一幸存者，并在结束后拿起了黑剑。后来，在泰拉远征期间，阿马尔里奇与帝国远征队的其他成员一起被混沌部队俘虏，但被赛弗和他的丑角盟友释放。逃亡者再次受到混沌的攻击，阿马尔里奇献出了自己的生命，用黑剑刺穿了斯卡布兰德的胸膛。基里曼后来能够利用这一伤口击败嗜血狂魔。
