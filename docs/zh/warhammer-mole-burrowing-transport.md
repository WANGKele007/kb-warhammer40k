The Mole is a burrowing vehicle used by the Adeptus Mechanicus and Imperial Guard.

鼹鼠是机械神教和帝国卫队使用的一种挖掘载具。

Mole Burrowing Transport 鼹鼠挖掘运输车

Larger than the Termite but smaller than the Hellbore, it uses a phase-field generator to burrow a drill filled with troops into the ground, allowing them to surface behind or on the flanks of enemy fortifications and positions.

它比白蚁大，但比地狱钻小，它使用相位立场发生器将装满部队的钻头钻入地下，使他们能够在敌人防御工事和阵地的侧翼或后方出现。
