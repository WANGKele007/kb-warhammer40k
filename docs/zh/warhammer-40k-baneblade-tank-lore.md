The Baneblade is the primary super-heavy tank used by the Imperial Guard and is one of the largest and oldest tanks used by the Imperium. These massive machines often serve as the command vehicles for entire regiments or spearhead armoured attacks, organised into their own super-heavy companies. Such is the power of a Baneblade that nothing short of another war engine, such as a Titan, would dare to face one in single combat. Captured Baneblades are also used by the forces of Chaos.

毒刃是帝国卫队使用的主要超重型坦克，也是帝国使用的最大、最古老的坦克之一。这些大型机器经常作为整个兵团的指挥载具或先锋装甲攻击，组织成自己的超重型连队。毒刃的力量如此之大，以至于除了另一台战争引擎，比如泰坦，任何人都不敢在一场战斗中面对它。被缴获的毒刃也被混沌部队使用。

Baneblades in combat 战斗中的毒刃

## History 历史

The Baneblade is among the oldest armoured vehicles still in use by the Imperial Guard, based on rare Standard Template Construct technology dating back to the Dark Age of Technology. After its discovery early in the Great Crusade it became the backbone of Super-Heavy tank units of the Imperial Army as well as becoming the template for the Fellblade for the Legiones Astartes.

毒刃是帝国卫队仍在使用的最古老的装甲载具之一，基于可追溯到黑暗技术时代的罕见标准模板构造技术。在大远征早期发现后，它成为帝国军队超重型坦克单位的中坚力量，也成为阿斯塔特军团残暴刃的模板。

It is believed that entire divisions of Baneblades once served the Emperor of Mankind in his Great Crusade, but by the 41st Millennium an Imperial Guard regiment is considered fortunate if it has a single company of Baneblades, and many must make due with only one. The STC data necessary to create an original Baneblade, including the many advanced systems unique to its construction, is a closely-guarded secret limited to Mars — from which most true Baneblades originate - and a few other favoured Forge Worlds. Baneblades constructed on these worlds will be logged and registered with Mars, with their own identity number and name, and all information pertaining to their whereabouts and crew are regularly reported to their place of origination. As well, due to the nature of such a prime asset, the Adeptus Mechanicus shares responsibility with the Imperial Guard for selecting the crewmembers of a Baneblade.

据信，整个的毒刃师都曾在人类的大远征中为人类帝皇服务，但到了第41个千年，如果一个帝国卫队兵团有一个单独的毒刃连队，那么它就被认为是幸运的，许多人只能得到一辆。制造原初毒刃所需的STC数据，包括其构造所特有的许多先进系统，是一个严格守卫的秘密，仅限于火星 — 大多数真正的毒刃都起源于火星 — 以及其他一些受欢迎的铸造世界。在这些行星上制造的毒刃将在火星上记录和注册，并带有自己的身份号码和名称，所有与它们的行踪和乘员有关的信息都会定期报告给它们的起源地。此外，由于这种重要资产的性质，机械神教与帝国卫队共同负责选择毒刃的乘员。

Solar Auxilia Baneblade 太阳辅助军毒刃

The main Forge Worlds that produce Baneblades are — Mars, Lucius, Estaban VII and Ryza.

生产毒刃的主要铸造世界是火星、卢修斯、埃斯塔班七号和瑞扎。

## Design 射击

### Armament 军备

The super-heavy tank's primary weapon is its turret-mounted Baneblade Cannon, a massive ten-metre-long battle cannon capable of launching rocket-assisted shells at targets many kilometers away. Such is the power of this cannon that its tremendous recoil can rock the massive tank back on its tracks and produces a deafening roar inside the tank in spite of the layers of armour and acoustic dampening material. The cannon's double-sleeve construction allows for venting ports around its muzzle for exhaust gases to escape, preventing a dangerous build-up which could damage the weapon. The co-axial Autocannon provides additional firepower but is more often used to fire magnesium ranging rounds for the Baneblade's main cannon. The first gunner, stationed in the gunnery deck inside the turret, controls both weapons and aims through the secondary scope located next to the primary scopes atop the turret. The first loader is also stationed inside the turret, loading each of the metre-long shells into the breach and manning any pintle-mounted weapons. A small locker within the turret contains up to half a dozen shells while an enclosed shell elevator supplies additional shots from the magazine.

这种超重型坦克的主要武器是安装在炮塔上的毒刃炮，这是一种巨大的10米长的战斗炮，能够向数公里外的目标发射火箭助推炮弹。这门炮的威力如此之大，以至于它巨大的后坐力可以使巨大的坦克倒退，并在坦克内产生震耳欲聋的轰鸣声，尽管有多层装甲和隔音材料。炮的双套筒结构允许其炮口周围的排气口排出废气，防止可能损坏武器的危险积聚。同轴自动炮提供了额外的火力，但更常用于为毒刃的主炮发射镁测距弹。第一炮手驻扎在炮塔内的炮台上，通过位于炮塔顶部主瞄准镜旁边的辅助瞄准镜控制武器和瞄准。第一装弹手也驻扎在炮塔内，将每枚一米长的炮弹装入缺口，并为任何枢轴式武器配备人员。炮塔内的一个小弹药架最多可容纳六发炮弹，而一个封闭的炮弹升降机则从弹仓中提供额外的弹药。

The Baneblade's secondary weapon is its hull-mounted demolisher cannon. Of sturdier construction with a shorter, wider-mouthed barrel, the demolisher also fires rocket-assisted shells and uses the same method as the main cannon to vent exhaust gases. A reinforced mount allows the Baneblade to advance while firing the Demolisher. Demolisher shells are located in a fast-loaded shell magazine. They can easily shatter ferrocrete and even liquify soft targets with their shockwaves alone. The second gunner, located in the tank's forward compartment, is in charge of aiming and firing the demolisher cannon through auger sights while a rail sled from the magazine feeds shells into the weapon.

毒刃的次要武器是其安装在车体上的粉碎者炮。粉碎者的结构更坚固，炮管更短、更宽，也发射火箭助推炮弹，并使用与主炮相同的方法排放废气。加固的底座使毒刃在发射粉碎者的同时能够前进。粉碎者炮弹位于快速装载的炮弹库中。它们可以很容易地粉碎钢筋混凝土，甚至仅凭冲击波就可以液化软目标。第二炮手位于坦克的前舱，负责通过螺旋瞄准器瞄准和发射粉碎者炮，同时弹仓中的轨道滑车将炮弹送入武器。

The Baneblade's tertiary weapons consist of hull-mounted twin-linkedheavy bolters, which can be fired by the driver when necessary, and two side sponsons, each one with a twin-linked heavy bolter and a lascannon. Each of the side-mounted heavy bolters has a limited 90° angle of fire though the lascannons, mounted on turrets atop the sponsons, have a larger range of 270°. The third gunner remotely controls these weapons from his station on the command deck, assisted by the third loader, but indeed any of the Baneblade's many weapons can be remotely aimed and fired from this station. Ammunition for the heavy bolters are supplied by autoloaders which draw from 4,000-round hoppers each. If the autoloaders jam or malfunction, the third loader has to clear out and refill the entire system by means of the sponson access hatch located outside the tank, a dangerous task in the middle of combat. The lascannons draw energy directly from the powerplant, but if their shunts burn out from overuse those too must be replaced, though the cabling for these are located in the lower deck. The heavy bolters can be replaced with heavy flamers while the whole sponson assembly can mounted at the front, centre or rear of the Baneblade's side armour. Other Baneblades of lesser patterns from more primitive worlds or those that have been modified to circumvent complex, battle-damaged components forgo remote-operated sponsons and machine spirit-aided weapons guidance for manual operation, requiring a crew of 11 or 12 tankers.

毒刃的第三级武器包括安装在车体上的双联重型爆矢枪，必要时可由驾驶员发射，以及两个侧舷，每个侧舷都有一个双联重型爆矢枪和一个激光炮。每个侧装式重型爆矢枪的射击角度都是有限的90°，尽管安装在舷侧顶部炮塔上的激光炮的射角更大，为270°。第三炮手在第三装弹手的协助下，从位置上的指挥面板远程控制这些武器，但事实上，毒刃的许多武器中的任何一个都可以从这个位置远程瞄准和发射。重型爆矢枪的弹药由自动装弹机提供，每个自动装弹机从4000发弹药的料斗中取出。如果自动装弹机堵塞或发生故障，第三装弹手必须通过位于坦克外部的舷侧检修口清理并重装整个系统，这在战斗中是一项危险的任务。激光炮直接从动力装置中获取能量，但如果它们的分流器因过度使用而烧坏，也必须更换，尽管这些分流器的电缆位于下层甲板。重型爆矢枪可以用重型火焰枪代替，而整个舷侧装配可以安装在毒刃侧甲的前部、中部或后部。其他来自更原始世界的较小型号的毒刃，或者那些经过修改以绕过复杂的、战斗受损的组件的毒刃，放弃了远程操作的舷侧和机魂辅助的武器制导，转而进行手动操作，需要11或12名坦克乘员。

Variations on this standard include some Baneblades with a second set of side sponsons, while others may have them removed in place of even more side armour plating. As a final addition some Baneblades will include a pintle-mountedHeavy Stubber or Storm Bolter and/or a Hunter-Killer Missile.

该标准的变体包括一些带有第二组侧舷的毒刃，而另一些则可能将其拆除，代替以更多的侧装甲板。作为最后的补充，一些毒刃将包括一个枢轴式重型伐木枪或风暴爆矢枪和/或猎人杀手导弹。

### Armour 装甲

Wrapped in Durasteel and layers of Ceramite and Plasteel the Baneblade is among the most well-protected vehicles employed by the Imperial Guard; indeed it can survive countless weapon impacts which would reduce most other tanks to molten slag. Even the tracks are armoured in adamantine. Not even solid slugs fired by the feared Railguns mounted on T'auHammerhead skimmer-tanks will penetrate the Baneblade's frontal glacis, though a trio of simultaneous impacts can tear off one of the side compartments and cause spalling fragments within the interior. The frontal armour is even tough enough to withstand several seconds of fire from a Turbo Laser before the beam melts its way through as well as shells fired by an enormous cannon capable of making a crater a hundred metres in diameter and lifting the front of the Baneblade a metre into the air on impact. Against some weapons though the Baneblade is no match, as even a single shot from the Shadowsword's Volcano Cannon is capable of atomising this massive tank. When necessary the Baneblade is also equipped with Smoke Launchers to produce a cloud of blinding smoke, by firing five tetra-density rating smoke grenades per minute.

毒刃由耐用钢和多层陶钢和塑钢覆盖，是帝国卫队使用的防护最好的载具之一；事实上，它可以在无数次武器撞击中幸存下来，这将使大多数其他坦克变成熔渣。就连履带都是用精金装甲的。即使是安装在钛锤头鲨悬浮坦克上的令人恐惧的磁轨炮发射的固体弹丸也不会穿透毒刃的正面斜甲，尽管三次同时发生的撞击会撕裂其中一个侧舱，并在内部造成碎片剥落。正面装甲甚至足够坚固，可以承受涡轮激光在光束熔化之前的几秒钟射击，以及由一门巨大的炮发射的炮弹，该炮能够形成直径100米的弹坑，并在撞击时将毒刃的前部抬到空中一米。尽管毒刃无法对抗某些武器，因为即使是影剑火山炮的一炮也能使这辆巨大的坦克雾化。必要时，毒刃还配备了烟雾发射器，通过每分钟发射五枚四密度等级的烟雾弹来产生致盲烟雾云。

### Command and Control 指挥和控制

Many and varied are the ancient technologies which go into producing a true Baneblade, making it more advanced than most other Imperial Guard vehicles. Besides its Searchlight the tank is packed with auspex gear, such as seismic sensors, with a multitude of augur lenses ringing the tank's outer hull. Targeting arrays and two periscopes atop the tank's turret allow the commander a clearer view of the battlefield thanks to their prodigious height. In addition the commander can look out through any of his gunner's scopes and designate targets for them, assisted by the tank's logic engines in locating an enemy's weak points. Short- and long-range vox equipment allows for communication anywhere up to orbiting satellites on multi-channels, while special pulse-laser comms systems allows the Baneblade to share data with other super-heavy tanks to form a logic engine network; regular vehicles retrofitted with these systems can also join in. All of the information the Baneblade receives from these many sources are combined and displayed on the three-dimensional map chart located behind the commander's station. Here an accurate map of the tank's surroundings as well as all friendly and enemy contacts are displayed, allowing the commander to plan out his operations.

制造真正的毒刃的古老技术多种多样，使其比大多数其他帝国卫队载具更先进。除了探照灯，该坦克还装有鸟卜仪装备，如地震传感器，坦克外壳上还装有许多预兆透镜。坦克炮塔顶部的瞄准阵列和两个潜望镜由于其惊人的高度，使指挥官能够更清楚地看到战场。此外，指挥官可以通过炮手的任何瞄准镜向外看，并在坦克逻辑引擎的协助下为他们指定目标，以定位敌人的弱点。短程和远程音阵设备允许在多信道与轨道卫星进行通信，而特殊的脉冲激光通信系统允许毒刃与其他超重型坦克共享数据，形成逻辑引擎网络；改装了这些系统的常规载具也可以加入。毒刃从这些来源接收到的所有信息都被组合在一起，并显示在指挥官站后面的三维地图上。这里显示了坦克周围环境的精确地图以及所有友军和敌军的联系，使指挥官能够规划他的行动。

### Engine 引擎

The Baneblade is powered by a massive multi-fuel powerplant, located in the rear of the tank, which produces the enormous amount of energy necessary for the tank to function. Like powerplants found in the Leman Russ or Chimera vehicles it is capable of running on any type of fuel. When the tank is stopped the powerplant is normally left running and ready to go, however it also must undergo full maintenance and service check every six months during which it is completely shut down. The frequency of these stops increases depending on the type of fuel used or the battlefield conditions, and it is quite common to undergo one prior to a major engagement or long patrol. In cases when the powerplant is cold a battery supply allows for limited functionality of the tank's systems: for example the heavy bolters can still be used as normal while the lascannons will be good for only a few shots. The main power plant is an adaptable thermic combustor, producing an impressive 110 kilo-cubits^2 of raw power, fuelled by four supplementary fuel tanks, giving the Baneblade an operational range of 230 miles from a base of operations.

毒刃由位于坦克后部的大型多燃料动力装置提供动力，该动力装置产生坦克运行所需的大量能量。与黎曼鲁斯或奇美拉载具中的动力装置一样，它能够使用任何类型的燃料运行。当坦克停止时，动力装置通常保持运行并准备就绪，但在完全关闭期间，它还必须每六个月进行一次全面的维护和服务检查。这些停止的频率会根据所使用的燃料类型或战场条件而增加，在大规模交战或长期巡逻之前进行停止是很常见的。在动力装置冷却的情况下，电池供给允许坦克系统的功能有限：例如，重型爆矢枪仍可正常使用，而激光炮仅适用于几次射击。主动力装置是一个适应性热燃烧器，由四个额外燃料箱提供燃料，产生令人印象深刻的110平方千尺的原始力量，使毒刃的作战距离达到230英里。

### Interior Layout 内部布局

The Baneblade is primarily divided into three separate decks. The first is the gunnery deck located in the tank's turret, where the first gunner and first loader are stationed. Entrance into the turret is provided by two hatches on top - one jutting out of the turret's side to form an observation cupola, the other ringed by vision blocks - which as with all hatches on the Baneblade can be opened only through fingerprint scanners. In the center of the turret is the shell elevator and turret ladder well to access the command deck while the rear of the turret is taken up by a secondary command station for use by the tank's commander when necessary.

毒刃主要分为三个独立的甲板。第一个是位于坦克炮塔内的炮台，第一炮手和第一装弹手驻扎在那里。炮塔的入口由顶部的两个舱口提供 — 一个从炮塔侧面伸出，形成一个观察穹顶，另一个被视觉块环绕 — 与毒刃上的所有舱口一样，只能通过指纹扫描仪打开。炮塔的中心是炮弹升降机和炮塔梯井，可以进入指挥甲板，而炮塔的后部则由一个第二指挥站占据，供坦克指挥官在必要时使用。

The command deck is the next level of the Baneblade and is located in the upper half of the tank's body. This deck forms an irregular octagon with armaglass viewing cupolas set into each facet, while the track units running under the extreme edges of the deck form shelves along either side. Along the front of the tank is the command suite, a high-backed chair with multiple tac-displays in front and the three-dimensional chart display behind it. The commander's station is connected to the comms-suite running along the right-hand side of the deck, forming a continuous L-shape. The comms-suite consists of banks of monitors, logic engines and augur readouts while the comms-operator's chair is set upon rails, allowing the tank's second-in-command to easily move along his post. The gunnery station for the third gunner and loader is located behind and to the left of the command suite, while the tech station for the Baneblade's Tech-adept is in the rear. Here the floor of the command deck steps up, forcing the tech-adept to slide into his seat, which nearly touches the ceiling. The command deck can be accessed by the centrally-located turret ladder well or by an emergency access hatch in the tank's main body located above the tech station.

指挥甲板是毒刃的下一层，位于坦克车身的上半部分。这个甲板形成了一个不规则的八边形，每个面上都有装甲玻璃观察穹顶，而在甲板最边缘下方运行的轨道单元则沿两侧形成架子。坦克前方是指挥室，一把高背椅，前面有多个战术显示器，后面有三维地图显示器。指挥官站与甲板右侧的通信室相连，形成一个连续的L形。通信室由一排监视器、逻辑引擎和预兆读数组成，而通信操作员的椅子放在轨道上，使坦克的副指挥官可以轻松地沿着他的岗位移动。第三炮手和装弹手的炮台位于指挥室的左侧后方，而毒刃的技术专家的技术站位于后方。在这里，指挥甲板的地板上升了，迫使这位技术专家在他的座位中滑动，座位几乎碰到了天花板。指挥甲板可以通过位于中心的炮塔梯井或位于技术站上方的坦克主体上的紧急通道舱口进入。

The lower deck is accessed through a kidney-shaped slot in the front-left of the command deck, and is notable for allowing an average person to almost stand up straight. The lower deck is broken up into compartments connected by a narrow gangway running along the left-hand side of the tank. The forward compartment sits forward of the command deck and consists of the driver's and second gunner's stations, which can also be accessed by a hatch in the hull of the tank. Here the driver views the outside world through their main auspex scope as well as numerous smaller screens and augur lenses and drives the tank, while the second gunner does likewise for the Demolisher cannon. The driver also has access to a rear-view monitor to prevent accidents when reversing the tank. Next is the magazine, the most heavily-armoured part of the Baneblade, where ammunition for the tank's ballistic weapons is stored. Here the second loader feeds shells to the turret-mounted cannon through the centrally-placed shell elevator and for the Demolisher via rail sled. Towards the rear the gangway turns into a small alcove from which the tank's tech-adept can access the powerplant, while all along its length are lockers for the crew, a trio of enclosed bunk beds and the "wall of honour" where the tank's glorious history is recorded.

下层甲板可通过指挥甲板左前方的肾形槽进入，值得注意的是，它允许普通人几乎站直。下层甲板被分成几个隔间，由一条沿着坦克左侧延伸的狭窄步桥连接。前舱位于指挥甲板前方，由驾驶员和第二炮手站组成，也可以通过坦克车体上的舱口进入。在这里，驾驶员通过他们的主要鸟卜仪瞄准镜以及许多较小的屏幕和预兆透镜来观察外面的世界，并驾驶坦克，而第二炮手对粉碎者炮也这样做。驾驶员还可以使用后视监视器，以防止在倒车时发生事故。接下来是弹药库，毒刃装甲最重的部分，用于储存坦克弹道武器的弹药。在这里，第二装弹手通过中央放置的炮弹升降机将炮弹送入炮塔安装的炮，并通过轨道滑车将炮弹送入粉碎者。在后方，步桥变成了一个小壁龛，坦克的技术专家可以从这里进入动力装置，而整个步桥上都是乘员的储物柜、三张封闭的双层床和记录坦克辉煌历史的“荣誉之墙”。

### Patterns 型号

The Lucius-pattern Baneblade only slightly varies from the Mars-pattern. Due to increased frontal armour thickness, the Lucius Baneblade weighs more, because of the generally inferior quality of ore supplied to the forges of Lucius compared with that supplied to Mars.

卢修斯型毒刃与火星型仅略有不同。由于正面装甲厚度增加，卢修斯毒刃的重量更重，因为供应给卢修斯铸造厂的矿石质量通常不如供应给火星的矿石质量。

The extra weight led to increased strain on the engine and resulted in fires by overheating and more frequent engine failures. The Tech-Adepts of Lucius over the years managed to increase the engine capacity of Lucius-pattern Baneblades to take this strain. The most obvious exterior evidence of this are the twin exhausts, better internal cooling and transmission to the drive wheels which aided in raising the performance of engine. It is believed that these changes give the Lucius-pattern Baneblade approximately the same performance as its slightly lighter Mars variant, though comparative field tests between these two patterns have never been allowed.

额外的重量导致引擎压力增加，并导致过热和更频繁的引擎故障引发火灾。卢修斯的技术专家多年来设法提高了卢修斯型毒刃的引擎负载，以应对这种压力。最明显的外部证据是双排气、更好的内部冷却和驱动轮的传动装置，这有助于提高引擎的性能。据信，这些变化使卢修斯型毒刃的性能与其稍轻的火星变体大致相同，尽管这两种型号之间的比较现场测试从未被允许。

Other differences include adding a muzzle brake to the main battle cannon, which aids in flash suppression and reducing recoil.

其他区别包括在主战斗炮上增加炮口制退器，这有助于抑制闪光和减少后坐力。

### 'Counterfeit' Baneblades “伪造”毒刃

The monopoly Mars and a select few Forge Worlds have on the Baneblade's STC data, combined with the never-ending need for these behemoth machines, has led to the development of 'counterfeit' Baneblades. Officially categorized by the Adeptus Mechanicus as second-generation Baneblades, these are produced by Forge Worlds eager to secure large military contracts but who do not have the complete STC data necessary to produce a true Baneblade. Such Baneblades might use normal Battle Cannon ammunition in place of special rocket-assist rounds, or have a normal Battle Cannon in place of the Demolisher. They might also have inferior armour bracing or engine performance or less advanced comms arrays and logic engines.

火星和少数铸造世界对毒刃STC数据的垄断，再加上对这些庞然大物的永无止境的需求，导致了“伪造”毒刃的发展。这些被机械神教正式归类为二代毒刃，由铸造世界生产，他们渴望获得大型军事合同，但没有生产真正毒刃所需的完整STC数据。这种毒刃可能会使用普通的战斗炮弹药来代替特殊的火箭助推炮弹，或者用普通的战斗炮来代替粉碎者。它们也可能具有较差的装甲支持或引擎性能，或者不太先进的通信阵列和逻辑引擎。
