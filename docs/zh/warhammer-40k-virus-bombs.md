Virus bombs are potent weapons of mass destruction, and were commonly used to carry out Exterminatus in pre-Heresy times. In this role they have largely been superseded by Cyclonic Torpedoes, however virus bombs are still retained by some Imperial arsenals.

病毒炸弹是一种强大的大规模杀伤性武器，在叛乱时代之前通常用于实施灭绝令。在这个作用上，它们在很大程度上被旋风鱼雷所取代，但一些帝国武库仍保留着病毒炸弹。

## Strategic use 战略使用

In order to kill all organisms on a planet with virus bombs, a fleet of ships must all bombard the world in unison with many air-bursting warheads for sufficient levels of virus saturation to occur. Virus bombs were used on Isstvan III at the start of the Horus Heresy by Horus to cull the Space Marines of the present Legions that were judged likely to remain loyal to the Emperor. Virus bombs were used to execute exterminatus as late as 273.M41 on the hive worldStalinvast. The viral payload of a virus bomb can be keyed to specific gene-codes, specifically targeting aliens, mutants or even rebelling humans with a predominant hair colour.

为了用病毒炸弹杀死行星上的所有生物，一支舰队必须用许多空爆弹头一齐轰炸世界，以使病毒达到足够的饱和水平。在荷鲁斯叛乱开始时，荷鲁斯在伊斯塔万三号上使用了病毒炸弹，以消灭目前被认为可能忠于帝皇的军团的星际战士。早在273.M41，病毒炸弹就被用来在巢都世界斯塔林瓦斯特执行灭绝令。病毒炸弹的有效载荷病毒可以与特定的基因编码相关联，特别是针对异型、变种人，甚至是头发颜色占主导地位的叛逆人类。

The Life-Eater Virus, as used on Isstvan III and Stalinvast, is terrifyingly voracious, capable of spreading across the entire surface of a planet in a matter of minutes. Airbursts of viral clades consume airborne bacteria blooming into a roving black cloud. The hyper-fast mutative change and near exponential growth rates allow the virus to leap species in seconds, but all resulting in near instant putrefaction regardless of being an animal, plant, microbe. Jungles and forests quickly rot into lakes of sludge. The rapid breakdown of organic matter releases tremendous amounts of flammable gas. The virus can penetrate the smallest chinks in power armour and enter rebreathers. The virus quickly rots and breaks down anything of biological origin, reducing it to sludge. The gas eventually ignites, either on its own or with the intentional insertion of an incendiary device, into an apocalyptic, planet-wide firestorm, searing the planet's entire surface to bare rock, as well as burning the atmosphere of all oxygen.

伊斯塔万三号和斯塔林瓦斯特上使用的生命吞食者病毒非常贪婪，能够在几分钟内传播到整个行星的表面。病毒分株的空爆会消耗空气中的细菌，形成一团四处游荡的黑云。超快的突变变化和近乎指数级的增长率使病毒能够在几秒钟内跨越物种，无论是动物、植物还是微生物，都会导致近乎瞬间的腐烂。丛林和森林很快就会腐烂成淤泥湖。有机物的快速分解会释放出大量的易燃气体。病毒可以穿透动力盔甲的最小缝隙，进入呼吸器。病毒迅速腐烂，分解任何生物来源的东西，将其变成污泥。这种气体最终会自行或通过故意插入燃烧装置点燃，形成一场世界末日般的全球性火焰风暴，将行星的整个表面烧成裸露的岩石，并燃烧掉大气中的所有氧气。

The Emperor was said to have strongly disliked using virus bombs during the Great Crusade.

据说帝皇在大远征期间非常不喜欢使用病毒炸弹。

## Tactical use 战术使用

Virus bombs have also been used on a tactical scale, such as during the Second War for Armageddon. Von Strab had a secret stash of them, put there by his family when the planet was colonized. They wreaked a terrible havoc on the invading Orks but had lost their potency over time and did not have exactly the desired effect. The virus also has a tendency to blow with the winds, and during the Armageddon deployment, it washed back over the defending Imperial Guard, killing as many defenders as attackers.

病毒炸弹也被用于战术规模，例如在第二次阿玛吉多顿战争期间。冯·斯特拉布有一个病毒炸弹的秘密贮藏，是他的家人在行星被殖民时放在那里的。它们对入侵的欧克造成了可怕的浩劫，但随着时间的推移，它们已经失去了效力，并没有达到预期的效果。病毒也有随风而动的趋势，在阿玛吉多顿部署期间，它冲过了防御的帝国卫队，杀死了与攻击者一样多的防御者。

## Recovery 取回

The lifeless rocks left after a viral bombing may still contain valuable non-organic resources after the virus itself has died off.

病毒轰炸后留下的无生命岩石在病毒本身死亡后可能仍含有宝贵的非有机资源。
