The Hierophant biotitan is an immense, hideous creature, towering over the battlefield and bristling with spines, tendrils and symbiote weapons. They are amongst the largest bio-creatures yet encountered amongst the swarms of the Tyranidhive fleets, comparable in size and power to an ImperialTitan.

圣师生物泰坦是一种巨大而可怕的生物，高耸在战场上，长满了刺、卷须和共生武器。它们是泰伦虫巢舰队的虫群中迄今为止遇到的最大的生物之一，其大小和力量与帝国泰坦相当。

Tyranid Hierophant 泰伦圣师虫

## Overview 概述

A brood of Hierophants are likely to be at the forefront of a major Tyranid attack. They are armed with massive Bio-cannons, multiple claws and chitin blades, and armoured with a thick chitinous hide that exudes poisonous spores from rends in the plates. In addition, the creature's vulnerable belly region is protected by spiny tendrils which lash out at any enemy who gets too close. These tendrils are capable of impaling or strangling with incredible force. As a last defence, the Hierophant is equipped with poisonous sacks that exude a gas lethal to non-Tyranid creatures from vents in its armour plates. Thanks to its psychic abilities, a Hierophant is further protected by a crackling warp shield and is able to reknit damaged tissue. Its legs are high tensile popliseal tendons.

一群圣师虫很可能站在泰伦大规模进攻的最前沿。它们装备着巨大的生物炮、多个爪和几丁质刀刃，并披着厚厚的几丁质覆盖，从板的缝隙中散发出有毒的孢子。此外，这种生物脆弱的腹部区域受到多刺卷须的保护，这些卷须会猛烈攻击任何靠得太近的敌人。这些卷须能够以令人难以置信的力量刺穿或扼杀。作为最后的防御，圣师虫装备了毒袋，从其装甲板的通风口散发出对非泰伦生物致命的气体。由于其灵能能力，圣师虫可以进一步受到爆裂声亚空间护盾的保护，并能够修复受损组织。它的腿是高强度的腘腱。

Hierophant 圣师虫

Like all Tyranid creatures, the bio-titan seems to be able to mutate rapidly, evolving new weapons and defences. Other variants of the biotitan have been identified with huge crushing claws or long scything blades, cluster spines and other bio-weapons, like scything talons or lash whips.

像所有泰伦生物一样，生物泰坦似乎能够迅速变异，进化出新的武器和防御。圣师虫的其他变种已被发现具有巨大的粉碎爪或长镰刃、簇刺和其他生物武器，如镰爪或猛击鞭。
