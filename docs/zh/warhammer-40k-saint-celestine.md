*"She was an angel, pure as righteous destruction. She laid low the twisted and lifted the hearts of the righteous. At her passing, a million voices cried out her name. Shall we ever again see her like? No, not now, nor ever again..." - *Saint Patricia on the disappearance of St. Celestine

*“她是一位天使，纯洁如正义的毁灭。她击败了扭曲，唤醒了正义之心。在她逝世时，一百万个声音呼喊着她的名字。我们还能再见到她吗？不，现在不行，再也不能……” *- 圣帕特里夏谈到圣塞勒斯汀的失踪

Saint Celestine is a legendary member of the Adepta Sororitas. Now a Living Saint, she has been described as a grand design of the Emperor unfulfilled. She has lived multiple lives in the Emperor's service, and each time she is slain is reborn once more.

圣塞勒斯汀是修女会的传奇成员。现在，她是一位活圣人，被描述为帝皇未实现的宏伟设计。她为帝皇服务了好几世，每次被杀都会重生。

Saint Celestine 圣塞勒斯汀

## History 历史

Little is known of her life and battles before she was proclaimed a Living Saint — presumably they were suppressed. In her earliest known form she was a citizen of Terra during the Horus Heresy. She was killed during the orbital bombardment of Terra during the Siege of the Emperor's Palace.

在她被宣布为活圣人之前，人们对她的生活和战斗知之甚少 — 大概是被压制了。在她最早的已知形态中，她是荷鲁斯叛乱时期的泰拉公民。她在帝皇宫殿围城期间在对泰拉的轨道轰炸中丧生。

Saint Celestine 圣塞勒斯汀

In a later life she was a Sister Repentia of the Order of Our Martyred Lady and that she was but one warrior fighting at the prolonged Palatine Schism, against thousands of heretics. Celestine was thought to have been killed in battle before the capital city of the planet Eurytion, and was said to have killed over one hundred schismatics before she fell. Her body was retrieved by her sisters, and was found to have life still in it. Believing her touched by the Emperor himself, the Sisters cleansed her body and found her to be free of wounds. The next day, the assault resumed with Celestine at its head. The battle lasted mere hours. Celestine became the figurehead of the Palatine Crusade, leading the forces of the Imperium to countless victories. At last, the Crusade's commander, Lord Ansgar wanted to assault the sector's capital world, but Celestine instead ventured to a backwater world called Sanctus Lys, which was home to an ancient shrine once visited by Saint Katherine. There she entered a hidden crypt, alone, forbidding any present to follow.

在后来的生命中，她是我们的殉教女士修会的赎罪修女，她只是一名在长久的帕拉庭分裂中与数千名异端作战的战士。塞勒斯汀被认为在欧律提翁行星的首都之前在战斗中丧生，据说在她倒下之前已经杀死了100多名分裂主义者。她的尸体被她的姐妹们取回，发现里面还有生命。修女们相信她被帝皇亲自触碰了，于是清洗了她的尸体，发现她没有伤口。第二天，以塞勒斯汀为首的进攻重新开始。战斗只持续了几个小时。塞勒斯汀成为了帕拉庭远征的饰像，带领帝国部队取得了无数胜利。最后，远征的指挥官安斯加尔领主想袭击该星区的首都世界，但塞勒斯汀却冒险前往一个名为圣光星的死水世界，那里有一座圣凯瑟琳曾拜访过的古代圣地。在那里，她独自走进一个隐蔽的地下室，禁止任何存在跟随。

At the first light of dawn, Celestine rose from the crypt changed. Clad in the Armour of Saint Katherine and wielding The Ardent Blade, she had become a vessel of the Emperor's divine radiance; she was declared a Living Saint by Lord Ansgar and his Thorian compatriots. Hailed as the 'Hieromartyr of the Palatine Crusade', Saint Celestine led the warriors of the Imperium to victory in a multitude of Wars of Faith, dispatching tens of thousands of heretics and enemies of the Emperor. The Adepta Sororitas venerated her, and her sisters would have followed her into the Eye of Terror, had she asked them to.

拂晓时分，截然不同的塞勒斯汀从地下室里出来。她身披圣凯瑟琳的盔甲，挥舞着热忱之刃，成为了帝皇神圣光辉的容器；她被安斯加尔领主和他的托尔派同袍宣布为活圣人。圣塞勒斯汀被誉为“帕拉庭远征的希罗马提尔”，她带领帝国的战士在众多信仰战争中取得了胜利，处理了数万名异端和帝皇之敌。修女会崇拜她，如果她要求，她的姐妹们会跟着她进入恐惧之眼。

Saint Celestine was lost on Forrax, as the treacherous Warmaster detonated an atomic failsafe in his fortress, annihilating everything for miles across. When news of her death reached Holy Terra, the Tower of Heroes tolled once to mourn her loss, but the Sororitas in their halls in Convent Prioris were comforted with the thought that Celestine would be at the side of the Emperor.

圣塞勒斯汀在弗拉克斯上失踪，因为叛徒战帅在他的堡垒里引爆了一个原子故障保护，摧毁了数英里内的一切。当她死亡的消息传到神圣泰拉时，英雄之塔曾鸣钟哀悼她的逝世，但首席修道院大厅里的修女想到塞勒斯汀会站在帝皇身边，感到安慰。

She appeared unexpectedly in 980.M41 at a crucial moment of the Promethean War on Heletine, where the Order of the Ebon Chalice and the SalamandersSpace Marines were engaged with forces of the Black Legion. There she turned the tide against the Chaos Space Marines and drove a spearhead directly to the Daemon PrinceLord Gralastyx, and ultimately slew him. When the dust of the victory had settled, however, there was no sign of her. It remains unclear if this happened during her life or after she had been declared dead on Forrax.

980.M41，她出人意料地出现在赫利廷的普罗米修斯战争的关键时刻，当时黑檀圣杯修会和火蜥蜴星际战士与黑色军团的部队交战。在那里，她扭转了混沌星际战士的趋势，将矛头直指恶魔王子领主格拉拉斯蒂克斯，并最终杀死了他。然而，当胜利的尘埃落定时，却没有她的踪迹。目前尚不清楚这是在她生前发生的，还是在她被宣布死于弗拉克斯之后发生的。

Saint Celestine 圣塞勒斯汀

During the 13th Black Crusade, Saint Celestine once again manifested at the host of thousands of Sisters of Battle which had been thought lost in the Warp for centuries. She proved decisive in the battle and became one of the key Imperial commanders, personally battling Abaddon the Despoiler. She resurrected two faithful Canoness' during the war, Eleanor and Genevieve, who became her champions known as the Geminae Superia. However despite her best efforts Cadia ultimately fell and Celestine guided many of the survivors including Belisarius Cawl, InquisitorGreyfax, and MarshalMarius Amalrich to Kalisus at the behest of a psychic vision. There, they found a Webway Portal and Eldar allies who saved them from the pursuing Black Legion.

在第13次黑色远征期间，圣塞勒斯汀再次在数千名被认为在战争中迷失了几个世纪的战斗修女中现身。她在战斗中表现出了果断，并成为帝国的关键指挥官之一，亲自与掠夺者阿巴顿作战。战争期间，她复活了两个忠实的大修女，埃莉诺和吉纳维芙，他们成为了她的冠军，被称为超凡双子。然而，尽管她尽了最大的努力，卡迪亚最终还是陷落了，塞勒斯汀在灵能幻象的命令下，引导了许多幸存者，包括贝利萨留·考尔、审判官格雷法克斯和元帅马吕斯·阿马尔里奇前往卡利苏斯。在那里，他们找到了一个网道传送门和灵族盟友，他们将他们从追击的黑色军团中拯救出来。

Saint Celestine continued to lead her survivors, now known simply as the Celestin, on a Crusade to Macragge. There Cawl was able to resurrect Roboute Guilliman as Ultramar was invaded by Chaos forces. Afterwards Celestine accompanied the reborn Primarch on the Terran Crusade, and was one of the few to successfully reach Terra.

圣塞勒斯汀继续带领她的幸存者，现在简称为塞勒斯汀，向马库拉格发起远征。在那里，考尔能够在奥特拉玛被混沌部队入侵时复活罗伯特·基里曼。之后，塞勒斯汀陪同重生的原体参加了泰拉远征，是少数成功到达泰拉的人之一。

Shortly after the Terran Crusade, Celestine became embroiled alongside InquisitorGreyfax in the attempts by EcclesiarchDecius XXIII to repeal the Decree Passive. Ultimately after fighting on San Leor and Ophelia VII, the Tyrant of Blueflame revealed himself and his plan to use Celestine as a vessel to manifest into reality. As she became possessed, Longinus was forced to kill her. However much to everyone's shock she was instantly reborn. Tracing the Tyrant of Blueflame's corruption back to the Ecclesiarch, Celestine later slew him.

在泰拉远征后不久，塞勒斯汀与审判官格雷法克斯一起卷入了教宗德西乌斯二十三世废除被动法令的企图。最终，在圣莱奥尔和奥菲利亚七号的战斗中，蓝焰暴君透露了自己和他使用塞勒斯汀作为容器来实现的计划。当她被附身时，朗基努斯被迫杀死了她。然而，令所有人震惊的是，她立刻重生了。塞勒斯汀将蓝焰暴君的腐化追溯到了教宗，后来杀死了他。

Celestine in battle 战斗中的塞勒斯汀

Sometime later on Tsadrekha, Saint Celestine appeared to help the Imperial Fists and local army against a Chaos invasion. After a long battle against Chaos forces, the Saint entrusted the psyker who acted as an astropathic beacon on that planet to Kassar of the Alpha Legion and prepared to fight against Khârn. In the end, Celestine died at hands of the World Eater.

在赛德瑞卡的一段时间后，圣塞勒斯汀似乎帮助帝国之拳和当地军队对抗混沌入侵。在与混沌部队进行了长时间的战斗后，圣人将在那个行星上充当星域灯塔的灵能者托付给阿尔法军团的卡萨尔，并准备与卡恩作战。最后，塞勒斯汀死于吞世者之手。

Celestine woke up in the dimension of the Warp, on top of a huge mountain made of skulls, bones and broken and rusted armors. All around her neck there was a deep scar that soon faded away. Celestine had lost her memory, but she soon realized who she was and that she was experiencing one of her many deaths. She walked in a nightmarish landscape full of daemons, and facing various trials, regaining her memory, armour and sword. She found two other ladies, who were revealed to be parts of her own soul: Faith and Duty, and a young girl who was her Hope. After the trials she was able to return once more to life.

塞勒斯汀在亚空间的维度中醒来，在一座由颅骨、骨头和破碎生锈的盔甲组成的大山之上。她脖子上有一道很深的疤痕，很快就消失了。塞勒斯汀失去了记忆，但她很快意识到自己是谁，她正在经历许多次死亡中的一次。她走在一片充满恶魔的噩梦般的风景中，面临着各种考验，重新获得了记忆、盔甲和剑。她找到了另外两位女士，她们被揭示为她自己灵魂的一部分：信仰和责任，以及一个作为她的希望的年轻女孩。经过种种考验，她又活了过来。

She appeared on the planet of Kophyr, where she lead the few available Astra Militarum forces against War Engine, the leader of a Blood cult of Khorne that had taken control of the planet. However, while the campaign was successful, she was betrayed by an Inquisitor who fell under the sway of Khorne. The Inquisitor used his psychic powers to turn half the Imperial Guardsmen against Celestine. While the mutiny was defeated, Celestine was killed by a close range melta gun shot. Upon dying, she awoke once more a top the mountain of bones, with no memory of who she was.

她出现在科菲尔行星上，在那里她带领少数可用的星界军部队对抗战争引擎，战争引擎是控制该行星的恐虐鲜血教派的领袖。然而，尽管这场战役取得了成功，但她被一位受恐虐支配的审判官背叛了。审判官利用他的灵能能力，让一半的帝国卫队成员对抗塞勒斯汀。当叛乱被击败时，塞勒斯汀被近距离的热熔枪击中身亡。死后，她在一座骨山的顶部再次醒来，不记得自己是谁了。

At Vigilus during the War of Nightmares, Celestine appeared with her Battle Sisters to rally the defenders and help turn the tide. At Hive Mortwald, Celestine and her Sisters rallied the defenders and aided InquisitorCartavolnus. Facing them was the Black LegionSorcererXagramothis, who summoned a trio of giant Daemonhosts. In the ensuing battle Celestine led her Sisters as well as Space Marines battle the creatures and the hordes of lesser Daemons that accompany them. As Cartavolnus escapes via dropship he witnesses Celestine destroy all three Daemonhosts, but the battlefield is swept away in an empyric fire and the Living Saint went missing thereafter.

噩梦战争期间，塞勒斯汀和她的战斗修女出现在警戒星，召集守军，帮助扭转局势。在莫特瓦尔德巢都，塞勒斯汀和她的姐妹们召集了防御者，并帮助了审判官卡塔沃努斯。面对他们的是黑色军团巫师沙格拉莫西斯，他召唤了三名巨大的恶魔宿主。在随后的战斗中，塞勒斯汀带领她的姐妹们以及星际战士与生物和陪伴他们的低级恶魔部落作战。当卡塔沃努斯通过空降船逃脱时，他目睹了塞勒斯汀摧毁了所有三个恶魔宿主，但战场被一场大火冲刷，活圣人此后失踪了。

Saint Celestine 圣塞勒斯汀

During the Nachmund Rift War, Saint Celestine reappeared on Sangua Terra to rally the defenders and attempt to hold back the Chaos hordes under Haarken Worldclaimer. In the end the situation became hopeless, and a badly wounded Celestine helped initiate an evacuation from Urbanosprawl Alpha.

在纳克蒙德裂隙战争期间，圣塞勒斯汀再次出现在赤地星，召集守军，试图阻止夺世者哈肯领导下的混沌部落。最后，情况变得毫无希望，重伤的塞勒斯汀帮助信徒从乌尔巴诺扩区阿尔法撤离。
