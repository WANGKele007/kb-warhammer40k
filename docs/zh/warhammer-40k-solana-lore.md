Solana is a founding member of the Logos Historica Verita, alongside Deven Mudire, Fabian Guelphrain and Theodore Viablo.

索拉娜是历史真理部的创始成员，与德文·穆迪尔、法比安·盖尔弗兰和西奥多·维亚布洛一起。

## Biography 传记

### Becoming a Historitor 成为一名历史学家

Solana is a Martian who Lord RegentRoboute Guilliman selected to join the Logos Historica Verita; though frequently assumed by the uninitiated, Solana is not a Tech-Priest, as she often points out . The Martian and Deven Mudire were among the first selected to join the Logos Historica Verita, alongside a third historitor named Thiennes. Thiennes would however be removed for displeasing Guilliman and his fall from grace greatly impacted both Solana and Deven. The two would later be joined by Fabian Guelphrain, a TerranAdept, who was from a lower social strata. While Solana treated Fabian with kindness and answered any questions he had, the aristocrat Mudire treated the commoner Adept with disdain. Guilliman later brought the three of them to meet with Archmagos DominusBelisarius Cawl aboard his Ark MechanicusZar-Quaesitor. This was Fabian's first off-world experience and Solana took the time to ease his worry. As they talked, the Martian told Fabian that she was excited to meet Cawl, whose work was an inspiration to her, despite the fact that many within the Adeptus Mechanicus decried him as a Heretek. On the Zar-Quaesitor, the three historitors would witness Cawl's unveiling of the Primaris Space Marines.

索拉娜是一名火星人，摄政王罗伯特·基里曼选择她加入真理历史部；尽管索拉娜经常被外行假设，但她并不是一位技术神甫，正如她经常指出的那样。火星人和德文·穆迪尔是首批挑选加入历史真理部的人之一，还有一位名叫蒂恩斯的第三位历史学家。然而，蒂恩斯因惹恼基里曼而被免职，他的失宠对索拉娜和德文都产生了巨大的影响。后来，来自较低社会阶层的泰拉专家盖尔弗兰加入了这两个人的行列。索拉娜对法比安很友善，回答了他所有的问题，而贵族穆迪尔则对平民专家不屑一顾。基里曼后来带着他们三人在机械方舟探索者之王号上与统御大贤者贝利撒留·考尔会面。这是法比安的第一次离开世界的经历，索拉娜花了一些时间来缓解他的担忧。当他们交谈时，火星人告诉法比安，她很高兴见到考尔，考尔的作品给了她灵感，尽管机械神教的许多人都谴责他是技术异端。在探索者之王号上，三位历史学家将见证考尔展示原铸星际战士。

Soon afterwards, the Indomitus Crusade began and the three historitors were assigned to Lord CommanderGuilliman's Fleet Primus, as it prepared to leave Terra. There, they were joined by a fourth historitor, Theodore Viablo. The four would be given tasks to ascertain their talent by compiling historical facts in order to establish a true history of the Imperium. If found to be of sufficient quality, their works would be given to Roboute Guilliman's Fleetmasters to read. Solana's work would be accepted by the Primarch.

不久之后，不屈远征开始，三名历史学家被分配给领主指挥官基里曼的第一舰队，当它准备离开泰拉。在那里，第四位历史学家西奥多·维亚布洛加入了他们。这四个人将被赋予任务，通过汇编历史事实来确定他们的才能，以建立帝国的真实历史。如果发现质量足够高，他们的作品将交给罗伯特·基里曼的舰队司令阅读。索拉娜的作品将被原体接受。

### Separate Ways 各奔前程

The four would then serve beside each for several years in Fleet Primus, training many other members of the Logos Historica Verita and collating a history of the Imperium since the end of the Horus Heresy. However after witnessing the Founding ceremony of the Void Sabres, the 104th Ultima Founding they had witnessed together, the founding members were finally separated. On the orders of Roboute Guilliman, they were given new duties that sent them away from Fleet Primus - all save for Viablo who was to remain as Guilliman's personal chronicler. Solana herself was charged with traveling to the Adeptus Mechanicus' Forge Worlds, where she would serve as a herald for the Logos Historica Verita. It would then be several years, before the four founding members were reunited again.

随后，这四人将在第一舰队并肩服役数年，培训真理历史部的许多其他成员，并整理自荷鲁斯叛乱结束以来的帝国历史。然而，在共同见证了他们看到的第104个极限建军，虚空军刀的建军仪式后，创始成员最终各奔前程。在罗伯特·基里曼的命令下，他们被赋予了新的职责，这些职责使他们离开了第一舰队 — 除了维亚布洛，他将继续担任基里曼个人的编年史家。索拉娜本人被命令前往机械神教的铸造世界，在那里她将担任真理历史部的传令官。再过几年，四个创始成员才再次团聚。

### Checking on Cawl 检查考尔

Solana was later dispatched by Guilliman to the Zar-Quaesitor, where she and MagosEremenitas were instructed to observe Belisarius Cawl and report on his progress in stabilizing the Attilan Gate. Cawl revealed that he was using Necron technology derived from Pylons to attempt to stabilise the gate, experimenting on a Tomb World frozen in time at the edge of a Black Hole in order to achieve this. Solana, alongside Cawl, Alpha Primus, and Qvo journeyed to the Necron "War World", revealed to be a World Engine, which was frozen to a time during the War in Heaven. She accompanied Cawl throughout the adventure on the World Engine, the Battle of Ulvheim, and Cawl's journey into the stabilised Attilan Gate.

索拉娜后来被基里曼派往探索者之王号，在那里她和贤者埃雷梅尼塔斯奉命观察贝利撒留·考尔，并报告他在稳定阿提兰之门方面的进展。考尔透露，他正在使用源自方尖碑的死灵技术来稳定大门，为了实现这一目标，他在黑洞边缘的冻结墓穴世界上进行了实验。索拉娜与考尔、原铸之首和邱弗一起前往死灵“战争世界”，发现这是一台世界引擎，在天堂战争期间被冻结。她全程陪伴考尔在世界引擎上冒险，乌尔夫海姆之战，以及考尔进入稳定的阿提兰之门的旅程。
