Zarhulash the Potentate is a C'tan.

统御者扎胡拉什是名星神。

Having since been enslaved by the Necrons, a shard of Zarhulash was used to power the Pharos on Sotha. In its conversations with Belisarius Cawl, Zarhulash considers the C'tan the only true gods of the Galaxy. It considers the Chaos Gods mere etheric disturbances, the Machine God to be a lie, and the Emperor to be a weapon. Zarhulash considers the Necrons to be treacherous children who the C'tan will one day take revenge against.

自被死灵俘获以后，扎胡拉什的一块碎片被用来为索萨的灯塔供能。在与贝利撒留·考尔的对话中，扎胡拉什认为星神是银河系唯一真正的神。它认为混沌诸神只是以太的紊乱，万机神是谎言，帝皇是武器。扎胡拉什认为死灵是背信弃义的孩子，星神总有一天会报复他们。

During Belisarius Cawl's experimentations upon the Pharos, Zarhulash came into contact with the Archmagos and agreed to aid him in removing the heart of the Pharos. In exchange, Cawl agreed to remove his shackles of Living Metal and create a portal with the Pharos to allow him off Sotha. Cawl seemingly agreed, but this was a ruse, as he teleported Zarhulash to the far end of the galaxy, surrounded by hostile Necron tomb worlds. Zarhulash has sworn revenge on Cawl for the act.

在贝利撒留·考尔对灯塔进行实验期间，扎胡拉什与大贤者接触并同意帮助他移除灯塔的心脏。作为交换，考尔同意解除他的活金属枷锁，并通过灯塔创造一个传送门，让他离开索萨。考尔似乎同意了，但这是一个诡计，因为他将扎胡拉什传送到银河系的远端，周围是敌对的死灵墓穴世界。扎胡拉什发誓要为考尔的行为复仇。
