Keritraeus is an UltramarinesPrimarisLibrarian who served in Lord CommanderGuilliman's Indomitus Crusade.

凯里特劳斯是一名极限战士原铸智库，曾在领主指挥官基里曼的不屈远征中服役。

## Biography 传记

In his service to the Crusade, Keritraeus was part of LieutenantCassian's Ultramarines strike force, which destroyed the flesh-cults that had overthrown the Imperium's rule on Knossa.

在远征服役期间，凯里特劳斯是卡西安副官的极限战士打击部队的一员，该部队摧毁了推翻帝国对克诺萨统治的血肉教派。

Afterwards, they left the world to rejoin the main forces of the Indomitus Crusade, but disaster struck when their Strike Cruiser, Primarch's Sword, was hit by a Warpstorm. Though the Strike Cruiser eventually escaped from the storm, it was left heavily damaged and the Ultramarines found themselves stranded in the Kalides System.

之后，他们离开了世界，重新加入了不屈远征的主力部队，但当他们的打击巡洋舰原体之剑号被一场亚空间风暴击中时，灾难降临了。尽管打击巡洋舰最终从风暴中逃脱，但它严重受损，极限战士发现自己被困在卡利德斯星系中。

With the *Primarch's Sword* unable to enter the Warp, Cassian consulted with Keritraeus and ChaplainDematris on their best course of action. After much consideration, it was decided they would go to the nearby Imperium world Kalides Prime, so they could repair the *Primarch's Sword* and rejoin the Indomitus Crusade. Cassian also agreed with Chaplain Dematris that they might face a world in the grips of heresy and the Lieutenant ordered his forces to prepare for battle, as they began to travel to Kalides Prime.

由于原体之剑号无法进入亚空间，卡西安就最佳行动方案咨询了凯里特劳斯和德梅特里斯牧师。经过深思熟虑，他们决定去附近的帝国世界卡利德斯一号，这样他们就可以修理原体之剑号，重新加入不屈远征。卡西安还同意德梅特里斯牧师的意见，即他们可能会面临一个被异端控制的世界，副官命令他的部队准备战斗，因为他们开始前往卡利德斯一号。
