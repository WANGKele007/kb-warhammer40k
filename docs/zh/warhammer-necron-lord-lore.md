Necron Lords or Nomarchs are leaders of Necron armies that serve under Overlords and rule over individual Tomb Worlds.

死灵领主或总督是死灵军队的领导者，他们在霸主手下服役，统治着各个墓穴世界。

## Overview 概述

Taking orders from their Overlord, Necron Lords direct battles and oversee the functions of the Tomb World as part of the Royal Court. Often seen wearing crumbling vestments and wielding ancient and arcane staffs, they are a chilling sight on the battlefield, filling their foes with dread. Though age mars and chips at their once-perfectly silvered forms, time has not diminished their malice. To further unnerve the enemy, they direct their forces to attack in eerie silence and have glittering arcs of energy surrounding the soulless fires that burn in their empty eye sockets.

根据霸主的命令，死灵领主指挥战斗，并作为皇家王庭的一部分监督墓穴世界的运作。他们经常穿着破旧的法衣，挥舞着古老而神秘的权杖，在战场上令人不寒而栗，让敌人充满恐惧。尽管损伤和碎裂在它们曾经完美的银色形体上，但时间并没有减少它们的恶意。为了进一步让敌人感到不安，他们指挥部队在可怕的沉默中进攻，并在空洞的眼窝里燃烧着没有灵魂的火焰，周围环绕闪耀的能量弧线。

The basic armament for a Necron Lord is a Staff of Light, but this is sometimes upgraded to a Warscythe, Hyperphase Sword, or Voidblade. Necron Lords are also known to go into battle with Resurrection Orbs.

死灵领主的基本武器是光之权杖，但有时会升级为战镰、超相位剑或虚空刃。众所周知，死灵领主也在战斗中配备复活天球。

Necron Lord with Resurrection Orb 配备复活天球的死灵领主

Necron Lords serve in the courts of powerful Necron Overlords or Phaerons, often ruling over individual Tomb Worlds on the behalf of greater Necron Dynasties. A Necron Overlord can sometimes have many Necron Lords at his command. Despite this, however, a Necron Lord is a fearsome opponent in his own right and they themselves command vast legions of Necron forces. Less sophisticated Lords are relegated as Helmsmen, piloting Necron vehicles such as Ghost Arks.

死灵领主在强大的死灵霸主或法皇的王庭上任职，经常代表更大的死灵王朝统治各个墓穴世界。一个死灵霸主有时可以指挥许多死灵领主。然而，尽管如此，死灵领主本身就是一个可怕的对手，他们自己指挥着庞大的死灵部队军团。不太老练的领主会被降级为舵手，驾驶幽灵方舟等死灵载具。
