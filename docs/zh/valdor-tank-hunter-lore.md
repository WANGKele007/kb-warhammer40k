The Valdor Tank Hunter is an ancient variant of the Malcador heavy tank used by the Imperial Guard. It is one of the largest and oldest Tank Hunters used by the Imperium, but is perhaps most notable for its use of arcane knowledge dating back to the Dark Age of Technology.

瓦尔多坦克歼击车是帝国卫队使用的马卡多重型坦克的古老变体。它是帝国使用的最大、最古老的坦克歼击车之一，但最引人注目的可能是它使用了可以追溯到黑暗技术时代的神秘知识。

## History 历史

The Valdor Tank Hunter is said to be named after the great hero Constantin Valdor, leader of the Emperor's legendary Legio Custodes during the Horus Heresy. Cryptic data fragments held by the on Mordia date it to sometime after the Heresy during the bitter wars of the so-called Scouring. It is said to have been the answer to the Great Enemy's numerical superiority of Land Raiders following their capture of Anvilus 9 earlier in the Heresy, and was most likely used by loyal Adeptus Mechanicus forces and those Imperial Army units with close ties to the Machine Cult.

瓦尔多坦克歼击车据说是以伟大的英雄康斯坦丁·瓦尔多的名字命名的，他是荷鲁斯叛乱时期帝皇的传奇禁军军团的领袖。莫迪亚上的战斗学院持有的神秘数据片段将其追溯到叛乱之后在所谓的大清洗的激烈战争期间的某个时候。据说，这是对大敌在叛乱早期占领安维鲁斯九号后兰德掠袭者的数量优势的回应，最有可能被忠诚派机械神教部队和与机械教派关系密切的帝国军队单位使用。

In the 41st Millennium the Valdor remains associated with the Adeptus Mechanicus, with small numbers found on Mars itself and certain far-flung strongholds such as Lathe-het and Cryaxus II but, despite similarities it shares with the Malcador, its unique and highly-advanced systems are beyond the abilities of most Forge Worlds to construct or maintain. Among the Imperial Guard a handful of these tanks are maintained as treasured relics by only the most venerable of armoured regiments; the Cadian 1st Armoured Regiment is reported to have five Valdors within its motorpool.

在第41个千年，瓦尔多仍然与机械神教有联系，在火星本身和某些遥远的据点，如拉斯-赫特和克赖克索斯二号上发现了少量，但尽管它与马卡多有相似之处，但其独特而高度先进的系统超出了大多数铸造世界制造或维护的能力。在帝国卫队中，只有最受尊敬的装甲兵团将其中少数坦克作为珍贵的圣物进行维护；据报告，卡迪亚第一装甲兵团在其载具库内有五辆瓦尔多。

During the Siege of Vraks Valdors were detected only as part of the renegade forces and only im the later stages of the conflict, though they managed to have an effect rather disproportionate to their numbers. It is believed by the 88th Army's intelligence officers that in Vraks' stores there were no more than 20-30 of these tanks and the renegades of Vraks didn't have the knowledge to operate them, so the situation changed only after the arrival of Dark Magi and the warband of the Steel BrethrenChaos Space Marine Warband. The evil Marines managed to revive the Valdors and in payment took the precious data-core of their design and several Valdors with them - which raises the possibility that these powerful tanks will be encountered in future conflicts with the arch-enemy's forces.

在弗拉克斯围城期间，瓦尔多只被发现是变节者部队的一部分，而且只是在冲突的后期阶段，尽管它们设法产生了与其数量不成比例的影响。第88集团军的情报军官认为，在弗拉克斯的仓库里，这些坦克的数量不超过20-30辆，而弗拉克斯变节者不知道如何操作它们，所以直到黑暗魔士和钢铁兄弟会混沌星际战士战帮到达后，情况才发生了变化。邪恶的战士设法重启了瓦尔多，并作为回报，带走了它们宝贵的数据核心设计和几辆瓦尔多 — 这增加了这些强大的坦克在未来与大敌的部队的冲突中遭遇的可能性。

## Technical Information 技术信息

The system which sets the Valdor apart from other tank hunters is its primary weapon, the Neutron Laser Projector. The projector and its neutronic-coil arc-reactor, while more compact and less energy-intensive than comparable weapons such as the Shadowsword's Volcano Cannon, still consumes much of the Valdor's internal space. It is also risky to operate due to the relative lack of reactor shielding, especially if the containment coils are ruptured, but is nevertheless a powerful weapon most deadly against smaller tanks who lack the thick shielding and protective mass of large super-heavy vehicles.

使瓦尔多与其他坦克歼击车区别开来的系统是它的主要武器，中子激光投射器。投射器及其中子线圈电弧反应堆虽然比影剑的火山炮等同类武器更紧凑、能耗更低，但仍然消耗了瓦尔多的大部分内部空间。由于相对缺乏反应堆防护，特别是在抑制线圈破裂的情况下，操作也有风险，但对于缺乏大型超重型载具厚防护和保护质量的小型坦克来说，这仍然是一种最致命的强大武器。

Solar Auxilia Valdor 太阳辅助军瓦尔多

A sponson-mounted Heavy Stubber serves as the Valdor's secondary weapon, although it can be swapped out for an Autocannon, Heavy Bolter, Heavy Flamer or Lascannon, and it is equipped with a Searchlight and Smoke Launchers as standard. Other upgrades include a Dozer Blade, Extra Armour, a Hunter-Killer Missile and a pintle-mounted Heavy Stubber or Storm Bolter.

安装在舷侧的重型伐木枪是瓦尔多的次要武器，尽管它可以换成自动炮、重型爆矢枪、重型火焰枪或激光炮，并且标配探照灯和烟雾发射器。其他升级包括推土铲刀、额外装甲、猎人杀手导弹和枢轴式重型伐木枪或风暴爆矢枪。
