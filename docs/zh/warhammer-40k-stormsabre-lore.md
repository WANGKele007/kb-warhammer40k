The Stormsabre is a larger and more heavily armoured variant of the Banebladesuper-heavy vehicle used by the Imperial Guard.

风暴军刀是帝国卫队使用的毒刃超重型载具的一种更大、装甲更重的变体。

Stormsabre 风暴军刀

## Overview 概述

It is the equivalent to ancient Terran battering rams and the Stormsabre's single giant cannon, which is larger than a volcano cannon, would not be out of place among the Imperium's space fleets. In battle, the Stormsabre will make short work of any fortifications unlucky enough to find themselves in front of its hulking frame.

它相当于古代泰拉的猛击攻城锤，风暴军刀的一门巨炮比火山炮还大，在帝国的太空舰队中也不会显得格格不入。在战斗中，风暴军刀会迅速摧毁任何不幸地发现自己站在其庞大框架前的防御工事。

## Trivia 琐事

风暴军刀由皮特·哈里森创造，并曾出现在战锤社区的一篇文章中。
