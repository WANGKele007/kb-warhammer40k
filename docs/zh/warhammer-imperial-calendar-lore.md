The original Imperial Calendar was created by the Emperor of Mankind sometime before the end of the Great Crusade. By the 42nd millenium at least five "non-heretical" variations of the original calendar existed. The Imperial Calendar bases its years on the Anno Domini system, beginning at 1AD/CE. A typical year, as Imperial scholars write it, would look something like 456.M41. When an Imperial date is written using only year, it is acceptable to write the year with a backslash, for example; 456/M41. These specify the year on Terra. Only the year is used by Imperial Citizens at large, with most systems using their own calanders for local events, for example the Vigilus Template.

最初的帝国历法是由人类帝皇在大远征结束前的某个时候创建的。到了42个千年，原初历法至少有五种“非异端”变体。帝国历法以公元/公元后1年开始的公元系统为基础。正如帝国学者所写，一个典型的年份像456.M41。当帝国日期仅使用年书写时，可以使用反斜杠书写年份，例如；456/M41。这些指定了泰拉上的年份。一般来说，帝国公民只使用年，大多数星系都使用自己的历法来处理当地事件，例如警戒星样板。

### Calculating an Imperial Date 计算帝国日期

When dates are used for Imperial Administrative purposes, the date is further expanded into four parts; the Check Number (or Source Class), the Year Fraction, the Year, and the Millenium.

当日期用于帝国行政目的时，日期会被进一步扩展为四个部分：校验数（或来源类别）、千分年、年和千年。

Thus a full Imperial Date would be written 0.123.456.M41 or 0123456.M41. To calculate an Imperial date the following method is used:

因此，完整的帝国日期应写为0.123.456.M41或0123456.M41。要推测帝国日期，请使用以下方法：

Check Number 校验数

The check number refers to the accuracy in the date as compared to time on Terra, with 0 being the most accurate and 9 being the least. These numbers are based on the Class of the source of the information. Check numbers are vital based on the temporal distortions the warp can cause on real space.

校验数是指与泰拉上的时间相比，日期的准确性，0表示最准确，9表示最不准确。这些数字基于信息来源的类别。基于亚空间可能对现实空间造成的时间扭曲，校验数至关重要。

 - 

0 - Earth Standard Date - The event took place on Terra.

 - 

0 - 地球标准日期 - 事件发生在泰拉上。

 - 

1 - Earth Standard Date - The event took place within the Sol System.

 - 

1 - 地球标准日期 - 事件发生在太阳系内。

 - 

2 - Direct - The source was in direct psychic contact with Terra or the Sol system.

 - 

2 - 直接 - 源头与泰拉或太阳系有直接的灵能联系。

 - 

3 - Indirect - The source was in direct psychic contact with a Class 2 source, but not Terra.

 - 

3 - 间接 - 来源与2级来源有直接的灵能联系，但与泰拉没有。

 - 

4 - Corroborated - The source was in direct psychic contact with a Class 3 source.

 - 

4 - 证实 - 消息来源与3级消息来源有直接的灵能联系。

 - 

5 - Sub-corroborated - The source was in direct psychic contact with a Class 4 source.

 - 

5 - 次证实 - 消息来源与4级消息来源有直接的灵能联系。

 - 

6 - Non-referenced - 1 Year - The source was not in psychic contact with a Class 0-5 source. However it can be linked to a sequence of dates, where one was made in contact with a Class 1-5 source. The maximum period of time seperating this date and the Class 1-5 date is no greater than 1 Year.

 -

 - 

9 - Approximated - Class 9 differs from classes 0-8. It is used when reporting from a source that doesn't use the Imperial Dating System, or when using a historical date approximated from a non-Imperial source.

 - 

9 - 近似值 - 9级不同于0-8级。它用于从不使用帝国日期系统的来源进行报告，或者使用非帝国来源的近似历史日期。

Year Fraction 千分年

For record-keeping, each year is divided into 1000 equal parts, numbered 000-999. Assuming Julian years (365.25 days each), this makes each part 8 hours, 45 minutes, 57.6 seconds long.

为了记录保存，每年被分为1000个相等的部分，编号为000-999。假设儒略年（每个365.25天），这使得每个部分长8小时45分钟57.6秒。

Year 年

This is the year within the millennium. Years in the millenium start at 001 and run through to 000 or from 000 to 999.

这是千年内的年。千年内的年份从001开始一直到000，或从000到999。

Millennium 千年

This is the millennium in which the event occurred. These begin at the first millenium AD/CE. They are written down with the letter M, followed by the millenial number. For example the years between 2001 and 3000 would become M3.

这是事件发生的千年。这些始于公元/公元后第一个千年。它们以字母M开头，后跟千年数字。例如，2001年至3000年之间的年份将为M3。

Example 示例

Thus an event taking place on Terra at 12:00am January 1st 1998 would be written as 0.000.998.M2. Alternatively the year 1987 could be written simply as 987/M2.

因此，1998年1月1日凌晨12:00在泰拉上发生的事件将被写成0.000.998.M2。或者，1987年可以简单地写成987/M2。

### The Chronostrife 时间冲突

Between the end of the Great Crusade and the formation of the Great Rift in the ending stages of the 41st Millennium, a conflict had arisen within the Ordo Chronos on the what the correct date was, with the main five competing factional interpretations ranging in date by around a thousand years and some lesser, more heretical tendencies going further. Roboute Guilliman upon learning of this begun to formulate ideas on how to remedy this.

在大远征结束和第41个千年末期大裂隙形成之间，时间修会内部就正确的日期发生了冲突，主要的五种相互竞争的派系说明的日期相差约一千年，一些较小的、更异端的极端派系则更进一步。罗伯特·基里曼在得知这一点后，开始就如何补救这一点提出想法。

### Trivia 琐事

 - 

The Anno Domini system used as a base for the Imperial Calendar is never specified. It could be either the Julian or Gregorian calanders. The source refers to "our year 1998" and the "calander we are familiar with", so it is probable that it is referring to the Gregorian calander.

 - 

从未指定用作帝国历法的基础的公元系统。它可能是儒略历或公历。该来源提到了“我们1998年”和“我们熟悉的历法”，因此很可能是指公历。

 - 

An exact method for breaking the year fraction into 1000 equal parts is not given by in-universe sources, however there are some methods avaliable. All give a year fraction as lasting approximately 8.5 Terran hours.

 - 

宇宙中的资料没有给出将年分解为1000个相等部分的确切方法，但有一些方法是可用的。所有这些都给出了千分年，大约持续8.5个泰拉时。

 - 

DakkaDakka forum user Makr stated in 2009 that the year fraction could be worked out as follows :

 - 

DakkaDakka论坛用户Makr在2009年表示，千分年可以计算如下：

Figure out what day of the year an event is. (For Example 200)

 - 

找出一个事件是一年中的哪一天。（例如200）

 - 

Multiple that by 24. (For Example 200 x 24 = 4800)

 - 

24倍。（例如200 x 24=4800）

 - 

Add the number of hours an event is to that number. (For Example if the event was at 4pm. 4800 + 16 = 4816)

 - 

将事件的小时数加到该数字上。（例如，如果事件发生在下午4点。4800+16=4816）

 - 

Multiple this by the constant 0.11407955263862231501532129004257 (Giving us 549.407 from our example above)

 - 

将其乘以常数0.11407955263862231501532129004257（从上面的例子中得出549.407）

 - 

Round this to the closest full number (549). Thus giving you the year fraction.

 - 

四舍五入到最接近的整数（549）。从而提供千分年。

 - 

Additional to this Lexicanum user User:307th Artemian noted on 17 February 2015 that Makr's calculation added 24 hours to the number, and thus that you should use the date from 1 day before, then add the hour in the current day.

 - 

除此之外，Lexicanum用户User:307th Artemian在2015年2月17日指出，Makr的计算将24小时添加到了这个数字上，因此你应该使用前一天的日期，然后在当天添加小时。

 - 

Another Lexicanum user noted on 25 January 2025 that the Constant used by Makr does not account for leap years when converting from the Gregorian calander. 0.11376786 should be used as the constant instead if the year is a leap year.

 - 

另一位Lexicanum用户在2025年1月25日指出，Makr使用的常量在从公历转换时不考虑闰年。如果年份是闰年，则应使用0.11376786作为常数。

Conflicting Sources 来源冲突

 - 

The Warhammer 40,000 5th Edition Rulebook disagrees with all other sources on whether the years in a millenium run from 000 to 999 or from 001 to 000. All state that they are based upon the Anno Domini system which runs 001-000.

 - 

《战锤40k第五版规则书》与所有其他来源不一致，千年中的年是从000到999还是从001到000。所有这些都表示它们基于运行001-000的公元系统。
