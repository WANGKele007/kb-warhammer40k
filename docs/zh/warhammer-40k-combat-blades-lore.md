Combat Blades are a common back-up close combat weapon, used by warriors across the Imperium. Knives are used by a variety of human forces, such as the Imperial Guard and Space Marines, as well as their Traitor Guardsmen and Chaos Space Marine counterparts.

战斗刀是一种常见的近战后备武器，供帝国各地的战士使用。刀被各种各样的人类部队使用，如帝国卫队和星际战士，以及他们的叛徒卫队和混沌星际战士对应。

## Astartes Combat Blades 阿斯塔特战斗刀

The Astartes Combat Knife is a close combat weapon used by all Space Marines and Chaos Space Marines.

阿斯塔泰斯战斗刀是所有星际战士和混沌星际战士使用的近战武器。

Space Marine Combat Knife 星际战士战斗刀

Only the great Astartes warriors would call this formidable weapon a mere knife - if it were not for the too-wide grip, humans could use these impressive blades as swords. Tough and thick, these knives are designed for in-close fighting and are often the last weapon a Space Marine will use when all others have run out of ammunition or power. The blade of a combat knife is monomolecular, its edges made out of Hypersteel.

只有巨大的阿斯塔特战士才会把这种可怕的武器称为一把刀 - 如果不是因为握把太宽，人类可以把这些令人印象深刻的刀刃当作剑。这些刀又硬又厚，专为近战而设计，通常是星际战士在所有其他武器都耗尽弹药或能量时使用的最后一种武器。战斗刀的刀刃是单分子的，其边缘由超钢制成。

Flesh Tearers Combat Knife 撕肉者战斗刀

Prior to 775.M41, an STC for a Astartes Combat Blade was discovered by two Imperial Guard Scouts that was "sharper and lighter and tougher." They were hailed as heroes for this discovery, and received a planet each. The knife was adopted by 30 chapters of the Adeptus Astartes.

在775.M41之前，两名帝国卫队侦察兵发现了一种“更锋利、更轻便、更坚固”的阿斯塔特战斗刀STC。他们被誉为这一发现的英雄，并各自获得了一颗行星。这把刀被阿斯塔特修会的30个战团所采用。

## Imperial Guard Combat Knife 帝国卫队战斗刀

Used by the Imperial Guard. Also referred to as Survival Knives, these edged weapons sometimes also double as attachable Lasgunbayonets.

帝国卫队使用。这些锋利的武器也被称为生存刀，有时也可以兼作可附加的激光枪刺刀。
