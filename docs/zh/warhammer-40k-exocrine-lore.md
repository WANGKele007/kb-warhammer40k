An Exocrine is a large Tyranid creature bred for long range fire support.

腺体虫是一种大型的泰伦生物，用于远程火力支援。

Exocrine 腺体虫

## Overview 概述

Little more than a living artillery platform, the Exocrine is controlled by the brain of its Bio-Plasmic CannonBiomorph, a giant weapon able to unleash a vast ball of plasma, or several focused streams of fire from its multiple barrels. The Exocrine itself is purely a means of transportation for the bio-plasmic cannon nested into its flesh. Only when the larger beast remains still can the symbiote focus all of its mental resources into targeting and destroying its prey. Because of their ability to deliver powerful long-ranged firepower, Exocrines are perhaps the most feared gun-beast in the Tyranid swarms.

腺体虫只不过是一个活的火炮平台，由其生物离子炮生物形态的大脑控制，这是一种能够从其多个炮管中释放出巨大等离子球或多个聚焦火焰流的巨型武器。腺体虫本身纯粹是嵌套在其血肉中的生物离子炮的运输工具。只有当较大的野兽仍然存在时，共生体才能将所有的精神资源集中在瞄准和摧毁猎物上。由于他们能够提供强大的远程火力，腺体虫可能是泰伦虫群中最可怕的枪炮野兽。

Exocrine 腺体虫

While the Exocrine itself is largely slaved to the will of its parasitic cannon biomorph and acts simply as a means of transportation for it, they are nonetheless powerful creatures and can smash and tear enemies apart that get too close.

虽然腺体虫本身在很大程度上受制于其寄生炮生物形态的意志，只是作为它的运输工具，但它们仍然是强大的生物，可以粉碎和撕裂靠得太近的敌人。
