原文:“It is a realm of thought and emotion that exists outside of the physical realm. “在大漩涡边缘的恶魔世界“地狱之牙”，人们发现了奥米尼科佩亚的线索。奥米尼科佩亚是一件神秘装置，收纳了所有附带灵能组件的STC模板，其价值无法估量。数十亿护教军被调派前往地狱之牙；倘若这件传说中的奥米尼科佩亚神器被寻获，人类终将迎来灵能层面的救赎。”

原文:"Clues to the location of the Omniscopia are uncovered upon Hell’s Teeth, a Daemon world on the edge of the Maelstrom. The Omniscopia, an arcane device that contains every STC with a psychic component, is of immeasurable value. Many billions of Skitarii are dispatched to Hell’s Teeth, should the legendary Omniscopia be found, they would finally have Humanity’s psychic salvation."
