Malstrain Genestealers are a type of Genestealer variant found on Necromunda.

恶系基因窃取者是在涅克罗蒙达上发现的一种基因窃取者变体。

Malstrain Genestealer 恶系基因窃取者

## Description 描述

A Malstrain Genestealer is a unique Genestealer subspecies that was created on Necromunda due to the world's heavy radiation and generations of inbreeding. Malstrains are heavily mutated from baseline Genestealers, and as a result, the Tyranids' Hive Mind refuses to answer their psychic call. Malstrain Genestealers have a twisted and heavily mutated appearance. They commonly sport sloughed skin and exposed organs, and have asymmetrical features not seen in pure Tyranid species.

恶系基因窃取者是一种独特的基因窃取者亚种，由于世界强烈的辐射和几代近亲繁殖而在涅克罗蒙达上产生。恶系从基础基因窃取者上严重变异，因此，泰伦的虫巢思维拒绝回应他们的灵能呼唤。恶系基因窃取者具有扭曲和严重变异的外观。它们通常有脱落的皮肤和暴露的器官，并且具有在纯粹泰伦物种中看不到的不对称特征。

Malstrain Genestealers with Malstrain Tyramites 恶系基因窃取者和恶系泰伦螨虫

Malstrain Genestealers are often accompanied by familiars dubbed Malstrain Tyramites. These creatures are not Tyranids, but rather local tick-like insectoids that have fed on the dead and evolved. These creatures are nonetheless directed by the Malstrain Patriarch.

恶系基因窃取者经常伴随着被称为恶系泰伦螨虫的家人。这些生物不是泰伦，而是当地的蜱类昆虫，它们以死者为食并进化而来。尽管如此，这些生物仍由恶系族长指挥。

## Origins 起源

Hermiatus, a Tech-Priest of Necromunda, came to believe that the Genestealer plague was not just genetic manipulation but a psychic infection. In his obsessive quest for answers, he connected his mind to a Purestrain Genestealer via a neural interface, despite warnings. The experiment ended with Hermiatus releasing the creature, which infected his assistants and ultimately himself. Hermiatus merged Genestealer DNA with his own, becoming an ally to the xenos, and helped establish the Cult of the Second Son within Hive Secundus.

涅克罗蒙达的技术神甫赫米亚托斯开始相信基因窃取者瘟疫不仅仅是基因操纵，而是一种精神感染。在他对答案的痴迷追求中，尽管有警告，他还是通过神经接口将自己的思维与纯血基因窃取者链接起来。实验以赫米亚托斯释放该生物而结束，该生物感染了他的助手，最终也感染了他自己。赫米亚托斯将基因窃取者的DNA与自己的DNA合并，成为异型的盟友，并帮助在第二巢都内建立了二子教派。

Malstrain Alpha 恶系阿尔法

During the war for Hive Secundus, Inquisitor Vestrix identified the unique strain of the Genestealer Curse, dubbed the "Malstrain", which exhibited unpredictable and aberrant genetic traits. Unlike traditional Genestealer Purestrains, the Malstrain showed no regular generational cycle, with each new generation often producing horrific mutations. This was likely a result of genetic tampering by Hermiatus and the effects of inbreeding over time.

在第二巢都战争中，审判官维斯崔克斯鉴定出了被称为“恶系”的基因窃取者诅咒的独特品系，该品系表现出不可预测和异常的遗传特征。与传统的基因窃取者纯血不同，恶系没有规律的世代循环，每一代都经常产生可怕的突变。这可能是赫米亚托斯的基因干预和近亲繁殖随时间变化的结果。

## Spread on Necromunda 涅克罗蒙达上的传播

It is suspected that the Malstrain escaped Hive Secundus before its destruction. Evidence of mutated Genestealers has since been discovered in other hives on Necromunda, though such cases are officially dismissed as typical Genestealer genetic diversity. In reality, the Palanite Scrutinators have gathered substantial evidence of the Malstrain's spread, but under orders from Lord Helmawr, the information remains concealed. Should the Adeptus Terra learn of the Malstrain's proliferation, Necromunda could face devastating retribution from the Imperium.

怀疑恶系在被摧毁之前逃离了第二巢都。此后，在涅克罗蒙达的其他巢都中发现了变异的基因窃取者的证据，尽管这些实例被正式认定为典型的基因窃取者遗传多样性。事实上，帕拉尼特审查者已经收集了恶系传播的大量证据，但在赫尔马沃领主的命令下，这些信息仍然被隐瞒。如果中央政务部得知恶系的扩散，涅克罗蒙达可能会面临帝国的毁灭性惩罚。
