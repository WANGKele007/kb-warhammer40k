The Hades Breaching Drill is a specialized vehicle utilized by Imperial Guard Engineers, including Death Korps Engineers.

哈迪斯突破钻是帝国卫队工兵（包括死亡军团工兵）使用的一种专用载具。

Hades Breaching Drill 哈迪斯突破钻

## Overview 概述

The vehicle's primary mission is to either tunnel below enemy positions or breach fortifications. Its powerful cutting-head is equipped with a melta-cutter for blasting rocks into liquid slag and also has four large diamantine-tipped powercutters to quickly smash and pound rock into rubble, which is then excavated via conveyor belt through the chassis. Typically engineers will have spent weeks or months digging a sap tunnel into position ahead of a carefully planned attack, and after being secretly moved into place the Hades is then used to quickly smash through the last few yards of rock to the target. Engineer squads emerge from the tunnel in its wake, assaulting the enemy position in a surprise assault.

该载具的主要任务是在敌方阵地下方挖掘隧道或突破防御工事。其强大的切割头部配备了一个热熔切割机，用于将岩石炸成液态熔渣，还配备了四个大直径的驱动切割机，可以快速将岩石粉碎并捣碎成碎石，然后通过底盘的传送带进行挖掘。通常，在精心策划的攻击之前，工兵会花数周或数月的时间挖掘一条挖掘隧道，在秘密移动到位后，哈迪斯会被用来快速粉碎最后几码的岩石到达目标。工兵小队紧随隧道而出，突袭敌人阵地。

Although slow and cumbersome, once on the surface the Hades drill can still be an improvised weapon and is capable of cutting through just about any material. These include bunkers, defensive buildings, walls, vehicles, or even troops. However the Hades is not really a battlefield weapon but a piece of engineering equipment. In the past it has been pressed into service on the surface when attacking heavily fortified positions or fortress walls, but high rates of loss means it is a weapon of last resort and considered a poor replacement for dedicated siege platforms such as Leman Russ Demolishers.

虽然缓慢而笨重，但一旦浮出地表，哈迪斯钻仍然可以是一种临时武器，能够切割几乎任何材料。这些包括掩体、防御建筑、墙壁、载具，甚至部队。然而，哈迪斯并不是真正的战场武器，而是一件工程设备。过去，当攻击加强的阵地或堡垒墙壁时，它在地表投入使用，但高损失率意味着它是最后的武器，被认为是黎曼鲁斯破坏者等专用攻城平台的糟糕替代。

The Hades differs greatly from other specialised subterranean transport vehicles like the Adeptus MechanicusTermite, Mole, and Hellbore, which uses a phase-field generator to dig under the earth rather than the Hades' brute-force method.

哈迪斯与其他专业的地下运输载具有很大不同，如机械神教的白蚁、鼹鼠和地狱钻，它们使用相位立场发生器在地下挖掘，而不是哈迪斯的蛮力方法。
