*They came to us as gods, and we, like fools, took them at their word. Mephet'ran the Deceiver, Aza'gorod the Nightbringer, Iash'uddra the Endless Swarm; I curse their names, and the names of all their malevolent brethren *— From the Chronicle ofSzarekh, Last of the Silent Kings

*他们像神一样来到我们这里，我们却像傻瓜一样，相信他们的话。欺骗者墨菲特兰，拥夜者阿扎'格罗德，无尽虫群伊阿什'乌德拉；我诅咒他们的名字，诅咒他们所有恶毒兄弟的名字* — 摘自斯扎拉克，最后的寂静王的记录

The C'tan (Aeldari*Yngir*) are beings of pure energy that form a fundamental constant of the universe. Said to be the first beings in existence, they led the Necrons in their war against against the Old Ones before their followers turned on them and shattered most of them into shards to be used as weapons of war.

星神（灵族语因吉尔）是构成宇宙基本常数的纯能量生物。据说他们是现存的第一批生物，在他们的追随者背叛他们并将他们中的大多数粉碎成碎片用作战争武器之前，他们带领死灵与古圣作战。

Three of the C'tan, the Void Dragon, Nightbringer, and Deceiver 三名星神，虚空龙，拥夜者和欺骗者

## History 历史

### Origins 起源

It is said that the C'tan were born during the creation of the universe itself, formed of the insensate energies unleashed from the churning mass of primordial forces. Long before the first planets formed and cooled, these earliest self-aware entities emerged from the seas of plasma and solar flares. The entities, who would later be known as the C'tan, bore little resemblance during their early stages to the terrifying entities they would eventually become. In their initial phase of evolution they were merely titanic parasites suckling upon the stars that bore them, shortening the lives of their solar hosts by uncounted millennia. In time, these star vampires learned to fly through the cosmos upon diaphanous wings of magnetic flux, leaving their birthplaces and drifting to new feeding grounds. The energies required to sustain them resulted in the C'tan paying no heed to solid matter, for even the internal fires and massive electromagnetism of new-born planets were insufficient to register on their monstrous hunger.

据说，星神诞生于宇宙本身的创造过程中，由原始力量的搅动释放出的无感知能量形成。早在第一颗行星形成和冷却之前，这些最早的自我意识实体就出现在等离子体和太阳耀斑的海洋中。这些后来被称为星神的实体在早期阶段与它们最终成为的可怕实体几乎没有相似之处。在它们进化的初始阶段，它们只是巨大的寄生虫，吮吸着孕育它们的恒星，使它们的太阳宿主的寿命缩短了数千年。随着时间的推移，这些恒星吸血鬼学会了借助磁通量的透明翅膀在宇宙中飞行，离开他们的诞生地，漂流到新的觅食地。维持它们所需的能量导致星神不理会固体物质，因为即使是新生行星的内燃和巨大的电磁力也不足以记录他们巨大的饥饿。

C'tan Star God, as understood by the Necrontyr 惧亡者所理解的西'坦星神

### C'tan Incarnate 星神化身

These beings continued their benign existence with little conception of the universe until the intervention of the Necrontyr. Since the earliest times the Necrontyr had studied the stars to understand their baleful energies, seeking some power to unleash upon their bitter enemies, the Old Ones, during the War in Heaven and find a cure for their short lifespans. The Necrontyr, having been defeated by the Old Ones and scattered to the halo stars. had fallen into civil war, the second such War of Secession. It was during this period that the Necrontyr under their Silent KingSzarekh had perceived anomalies in the oldest dying stars. They had discovered a sentience more ancient than any corporeal lifeform in creation; beings of pure energy in the complex skeins of etheric energy. These beings found feeding upon the solar flares and magnetic storms of bloated red giants were perceived as the progeny of the Necrontyr death-god, who would act as weapons of retribution to cast down the Old Ones. These beings would come to be known as the C'tan, Star Gods in the Necrontyr language.

这些生物继续他们的良性存在，对宇宙几乎没有理解，直到惧亡者的干预。从最早的时候起，惧亡者就研究了恒星，以了解它们的有害能量，在天堂战争期间寻求一些力量来攻击它们的宿敌，古圣，并为它们的短命找到治疗方法。惧亡者被古圣击败，分散到光环群星上。已经陷入内战，这是第二次这样的分裂战争。正是在这一时期，在寂静王斯扎拉克的带领下，惧亡者发现了最古老的垂死恒星的异常。他们发现了一种比造物中任何有形生命形态都更古老的感知；以太能量复杂骨架中的纯能量生物。这些以膨胀的红巨星的太阳耀斑和磁暴为食的生物被视为惧亡者死神的后代，将作为报复武器，推翻古圣。这些生物被称为西'坦，用惧亡者语来说是星神。

C'tan shard 星神碎片

There are various conflicting accounts about the circumstances of the discovery of the C'tan. The archives of Solemnace claim their discovery was accidental, a chance discovery made by a stellar probe during the investigation of a dying star. The Aeldari account within the Book of Mournful Night claims that the raw hatred the Necrontyr felt for the Old Ones sang out across space and served as a beacon for the C'tan. Regardless of the circumstances prior to the first contact, contact was first established with a C'tan known the Aza'gorod the Nightbringer, which was born upon the very star which the Necrontyr first lived their brief, morbid lives. Upon manifesting across the incorperial starlight bridge, the Nightbringer was incarnated within a body of living metal (necrodermis) and brought with it the curse of death. Having fed upon the flavourless power of a star, the Nightbringer found delight in the Necrontyr's awe and fear, and had begun slaughtering those which had brought it into being, feeding upon their terror and suffering.

关于星神的发现情况，有各种相互矛盾的说法。索勒姆纳斯的档案声称他们的发现是偶然的，是恒星探测器在调查一颗垂死恒星时偶然发现的。《悲夜之书》中的艾尔达里描述称，惧亡者对古圣的原始仇恨在太空中歌唱，并成为星神的灯塔。不管第一次接触之前的情况如何，第一次接触是与一位名叫阿扎'格罗德的C星神建立的，他诞生在惧亡者第一个过着短暂而病态生活的行星上。在穿过无形星光之桥显现后，拥夜者化身到一具活金属（死灵金属）体内，并带来了死亡的诅咒。在汲取了恒星的无味力量后，拥夜者在惧亡者的敬畏和恐惧中找到了乐趣，并开始屠杀那些创造它的人，以他们的恐惧和痛苦为食。

Only by the desperate pledges of servitude were the Necrontyr able to convince the Nightbringer that there other species beyond number for it to destroy. Soon after, the Necrontyr awakened many other star-gods, the Necrontyr becoming their willing servants in their war against the Old Ones. Soon after, the Necrontyr awoke numerous other C'tan, which like the Nightbringer craved worshippers and slaves, although such servants soon descended into murderous insanity brought on by the presence of the Star Gods. One C'tan in particular outstripped all others in accumulating followers and devotees: Mephet'ran the Messenger. Being one of the weaker C'tan, it instead gained power through guile and persuasion to secure its future, even sending its slaves to serve other C'tan to curry favour.

只有通过绝望的奴役承诺，惧亡者才能说服拥夜者，让他相信还有无数其他种族可以毁灭。不久之后，惧亡者唤醒了许多其他的星神，惧亡者成为了他们与古圣的战争中心甘情愿的仆人。不久之后，惧亡者唤醒了更多其他星神，他们和拥夜者一样渴望崇拜者和奴隶，尽管这些仆人很快就因星神的存在而陷入疯狂的杀戮。一个星神在积累追随者和奉献者方面尤其超过了所有其他星神：信使墨菲特'兰。作为较弱的星神之一，它通过诡计和说服获得了力量，以确保自己的未来，甚至派奴隶为其他星神服务以讨好。

### Fall of the Necrontyr 惧亡者之陨

While to the Necrontyr, Mephet'ran was known as the Messenger, amongst its own kind it was known as the Deceiver. With honeyed words, the Deceiver spoke of an ancient war between the Old Ones and the C'tan long before the birth of Necrontyr, a war the C'tan lost. The Deceiver and its brethren hid themselves away from the vengeance of the Old Ones and the C'tan bided their time until they could find allies against them. To that end, the Deceiver offered the Triarch council two things the Necrontyr desperately sought: unity and immortality, which would be implemented in the form of biotransference. For one year since the Deceiver's proposition the Triarch and the nobles of the Royal Court debated the matter with Orikan, the court astrologer, being the only dissenting voice. Regardless of the dissent, the Triarch ultimately agreed to the Deceiver's offer.

而对于惧亡者来说，墨菲特'兰被称为信使，在同类中，它被称为欺骗者。欺骗者用甜言蜜语讲述了早在惧亡者诞生之前，古圣和星神之间的一场古老战争，星神输掉了这场战争。欺骗者和它的兄弟们躲起来躲避古圣的复仇，星神等待时机，直到他们能找到对抗他们的盟友。为此，欺骗者向三圣议会提供了惧亡者拼命寻求的两件事：统一和不朽，这将以生体转化的形式实现。在欺骗者提出这一主张后的一年里，三圣和皇家王庭的贵族就此事进行了辩论，王庭占星家欧瑞坎是唯一的反对者。尽管有异议，三圣最终还是同意了欺骗者的提议。

The Necrontyr nobility would be first to enter soul forges of biotransference and those who resisted would have their limbs broken and be carried to the soul forges by Ghost Arks. A notable organised resistance was formed by Nephreth the Untouched, the last natural Phaeron of the Ammunos Dynasty, who took war to the C'tan themselves. Nephreth had given hope to the Necrontyr as he alone was free of the cancers that blighted the entire species. However, this was yet another deception of Mephet'ran. The supposedly disease-free Nephreth was in fact a cast-off C'tan Shard of the Deceiver, who plotted to rally opponents of biotransference under a single flag in order to totally crush resistance. The real Nephreth was switched out in his infancy by the Deceiver.

惧亡者贵族是第一批进入生体转化灵魂锻炉的人，而那些抵抗的人将被打断四肢，被幽灵方舟带到灵魂锻炉。一个著名的有组织的抵抗是由阿穆诺斯王朝的最后一位正常法老 — 不可触碰者奈芙瑞斯组成的，他自己向星神宣战。奈弗雷斯给了惧亡者希望，因为只有他一个人没有患上困扰整个种族的癌症。然而，这是墨菲特'兰的又一次欺骗。被认为没有疾病的奈芙瑞斯实际上是欺骗者被抛弃的星神碎片，他密谋将生体转化的反对者团结在一面旗帜下，以彻底粉碎抵抗。真正的奈芙瑞斯在婴儿时期就被欺骗者换掉了。

As the furnaces of cyclopean machines of biotransference clamoured, the C'tan swarmed about the sites drinking in the torrents of cast-off life energy and gaining strength. This life energy was spirit-matter, a metaphysical energy savoured by the C'tan. Only as the Silent KingSzarekh watched the C'tan feast upon the life essence of his people did he realise his terrible mistake.

当巨大的生体转化机器的熔炉发出喧闹声时，星神蜂拥而至，饮着被抛弃的生命能量的洪流并获得力量。这种生命能量是精神物质，一种被星神享受的形而上学能量。只有当寂静王斯扎拉克看着他的人民的生命精华成为星神的盛宴时，他才意识到自己犯下了可怕的错误。

### War in Heaven 天堂战争

The Necrontyr, now become the soulless Necrons, and the C'tan now fought as one, thus dooming the Old Ones to eventual defeat. Planets were razed, suns were extinguished, and whole systems were devoured by black holes summoned by the reality-bending powers of the star gods. However, as the C'tan feasted upon the lives of countless species, the Deceiver convinced the C'tan that they themselves were the greatest feast to be had, and the C'tan fell into civil strife as the greatest amongst them consumed the weaker ones. With this reprieve, the Old Ones nurturing of younger races: the Aeldari, Rashan, K'nib and many other, had finally borne fruit and were unleashed upon the forces of the C'tan and Necrons. During this time of great infighting amongst the Star Gods, the sentient warp weapons that would become the Aeldari gods likewise fell into infighting. Pacts were made and broken and the line between the Aethyr Gods and Star Gods blurred.

惧亡者现在变成了没有灵魂的死灵，而星神现在作为一个整体战斗，从而注定了古圣最终的失败。行星被夷为平地，太阳被熄灭，整个星系被星神扭曲现实的力量召唤的黑洞吞噬。然而，当星神享用无数种族的生命时，欺骗者说服星神，他们自己就是最丰盛的盛宴，星神陷入了内乱，因为他们中最强的吃掉了较弱的。有了这一延缓，古圣对年轻种族的培育：灵族、拉尚、克'尼卜和其他许多种族，终于结出了果实，并加入到对星神和死灵部队的攻击中。在这段星神之间的大混战中，将成为灵族诸神的有感知亚空间武器也陷入了混战。契约被制作和打破，以太神和星神之间的界限变得模糊。

The War in Heaven 天堂战争

As the hot-blooded young races nurtured by the Old Ones spread across the Galaxy, their warp-spawned magicks battling the arcane science of the Necrons, the empire of the C'tan was driven back, as the Warp was anathema to them, and the C'tan found themselves driven back by the relentless advance. The C'tan unified for the first time in millions of years with a C'tan known as Mag'ladroth, the Void Dragon developing a plan to seal the Warp from the material universe, using a network of blackstone warp-suppression pylons such as the ones upon Cadia and within the Cerberus Shroud. Likewise, the C'tan had sown a terrible crop within the genes of the small tree-beasts that would one day become humans. These terrible genes would one day manifest within humanity in the form of Pariahs, which could be utilised in the form of Necron Pariahs. The C'tan were also finally able to breach the Old Ones' galactic warp tunnel network thanks to a C'tan known as Nyadra'zatha the Burning One using a series of living stone portals known as Dolmen Gates.

随着古圣培育的热血种年轻种族在银河系中扩散，他们的亚空间催生了与死灵的神秘科学作战的魔法，星神的帝国被击退了，因为亚空间对他们来说是可憎的，星神发现自己被持续的推进所驱退了。星神在数百万年来首次被称为玛格'拉德罗斯的星神统一，虚空龙制定了一项计划，利用一系列黑石亚空间抑制方尖碑，如卡迪亚和刻耳柏洛斯寿衣内的，将亚空间从物质宇宙密封起来。同样，星神在那些有朝一日会变成人类的小型树野兽的基因中播下了可怕的种子。这些可怕的基因总有一天会以被遗弃者的形式在人类中显现出来，这可以以死灵流放者的形式被利用。星神最终也能够突破古圣的银河系亚空间隧道网络，这要归功于一个被称为燃烧者尼亚德拉'扎撒的星神使用了一系列被称为墓石之门的活石传送门。

### Fall 陨落

With the Webway breached and beset by warp-spawned perils, the Old Ones had finally perished. With the C'tan have spent much of their energy to achieve this victory, they soon began to fall upon one another in personal wars inflamed by The Deceiver. The began to ignore their slave race and as the Star Gods battled one another millions died as entire star systems were consumed.

随着网道被破坏并被亚空间衍生的危险所困扰，古圣最终灭亡了。由于星神已经花费了大量精力来取得这场胜利，他们很快就开始在欺骗者煽动的个人战争中相互攻击。他们开始忽视他们的奴隶种族，随着星神之间的斗争，数百万人在整个恒星系统被消耗时死亡。

It was then that Silent KingSzarekh had chosen this moment to finally turn upon the C'tan. In the case of Llandu'gor the Flayer it was destroyed, whereafter it cast the Flayer Virus upon the Necrons. In most cases however, the C'tan were assailed with arcane weapons such as Hypercannons and fragmented into shards and held within multi-dimensional prisons known as Tesseract Labyrinths to later be used as unwilling weapons by the Necrons Other C'tan may have simply entered stasis due to the psyker swarm unleashed by the Old Ones depriving the C'tan of their much-needed sustenance, awaiting for the day that life flourishes throughout the Galaxy. However, according to the Book of Mournful Night, a C'tan known as Tsara'noga the Outsider may in fact remain the sole intact C'tan, having become too terrible in its madness to be defeated. Others claim that it may have also rent itself asunder.

就在那时，寂静王斯扎拉克选择了这一刻，最终转向星神。在剥皮者兰度'戈尔的实例下，它被摧毁了，之后它将剥皮者病毒投射到了死灵上。然而，在大多数情况下，星神受到超加农炮等神秘武器的攻击，并被分割成碎片，被关押在被称为超立方体迷宫的多维监狱中，后来被死灵用作不情愿的武器。其他星神可能只是因为古圣释放的灵能者群剥夺了星神急需的食物而进入静滞状态，等待整个银河系生命繁荣的那一天。然而，根据《悲夜之书》，一个被称为局外者萨拉'诺迦的星神实际上可能仍然是唯一完整的星神，因为它的疯狂已经变得太可怕了，无法被击败。其他人则声称，它也可能已经分崩离析。

### Recent History 近期历史

Since the Great Awakening, the C'tan have appeared as bound shards used as unwilling weapons of war. However, there are isolated reports of C’tan shards across the Galaxy that have broken their chains, intent on revenge. Various free shards of the Deceiver are noted to have begun actively working to various ends. One shard of the Deceiver manipulated members of the Alpha Legion to construct a series of anti-warp pylons within the galactic core before it was trapped in a Tesseract Labyrinth.

自大苏醒以来，星神就以被束缚的碎片的形式出现，被用作不情愿的战争武器。然而，有孤立的报告称，银河系各地的星神碎片已经打破了它们的锁链，意图复仇。欺骗者的各个自由的碎片已经开始积极地工作达到不同的目的。欺骗者的一块碎片操纵阿尔法军团的成员在银河系核心内建造了一系列反亚空间方尖碑，然后被困在超立方体迷宫中。

Another shard instigated the Gothic War, which saw the destruction of four Talismans of Vaul, ancient weapons of the Old Ones notably used to battle the Void Dragon. Other independent shards of the Deceiver have appeared upon Naogeddon and Gladius leading Necrons and even gathering new slaves in the form of Pariahs and followers amongst the ranks of the Adeptus Mechanicus.

另一块碎片引发了哥特战争，摧毁了四个瓦尔护身符，这是古圣用来对抗虚空龙的古代武器。其他独立的欺骗者碎片出现在纳奥吉顿和格拉迪乌斯上领导死灵，甚至以被遗弃者和机械神教队伍中的追随者的形式收集新的奴隶。

The armies of the Imperium are known to guard the Gates of Varl from the quiescent perils of the C'tan. Some members of the Thokt Dynasty know that feral infant C'tan still dwell within stars.

众所周知，帝国的军队会保护瓦尔之门免受星神的寂静危险。托赫特王朝的一些成员知道，野生的婴儿星神仍然住在恒星里。

## Transcendent C'tan 超然星神

Transcendent C'tan's are C'tans bursting with pure elemental energy. Each Transcendent C'tan is a collection of anywhere between a dozen and a hundred lesser shards, and its power far surpasses the sum of its parts.

超然星神是充满纯粹元素能量的星神。每个超然星神都是十几到一百个较小碎片的集合，其力量远远超过其各部分的总和。

Transcendent C'tan 超然星神

Shards of these mighty creatures are used by the Necrons as weapons by entombing them in Tesseract Vaults and controlling them with a Canoptek Sentinel and energy shackles designed by Svarokh. Should the situation become dangerous enough, the Necrons may release the Transcendent C'tan from its prison to rampage across the battlefield. This however is often as dangerous to the Necrons as is it to their foes.

这些强大生物的碎片被死灵用作武器，将它们埋在超立方体墓穴中，并用冥工哨兵和斯瓦罗赫设计的能量枷锁控制它们。如果情况变得足够危险，死灵可能会将超然星神从监狱中释放出来，在战场上横冲直撞。然而，这对死灵来说往往和他们的敌人一样危险。

## Relationship with the Warp 与亚空间的关系

The major weakness of the C'tan is their inability to comprehend the warp. It is speculated that they find it impossible to survive in it and are particularly susceptible to warp-spawned magicks and psykers. They had a plan to block off the warp forever in an attempt to remove their single greatest threat.

星神的主要弱点是他们无法理解亚空间。据推测，他们发现不可能在其中生存，并且特别容易受到亚空间产生的魔法和灵能者的影响。他们计划永远封锁亚空间，试图消除他们最大的威胁。

## Other instances of the C'tan 星神的其他实例

During the Great Crusade, it is known that the Imperium encountered a suspected C'tan device known as the Vortex field generator which would later be relocated to Jericho fort, making that site invulnerable to orbital bomardment. The armies of the Imperium are known to guard the Gates of Varl from the quiescent perils of the Ctan.

在大远征期间，众所周知，帝国遇到了一个被称为涡旋立场发生器的可疑星神装置，该装置后来被重新安置在杰里科堡垒，使该地点不受轨道撞击的影响。众所周知，帝国的军队会保护瓦尔之门免受星神的寂静危险。

Members of the Imperial Assassin Callidus Temple use a weapon called the C'tan Phase Sword. It is unknown what specific relation the weapon has with the C'tan themselves, but when it comes into contact with a C'tan's body it is absorbed as if it is a piece of its body returning home.

帝国刺客卡利都司神庙的成员使用一种名为星神相位剑的武器。目前尚不清楚这种武器与星神本身有什么具体关系，但当它接触到星神的身体时，它会被吸收，就像它身体的一部分回家一样。

The Imperium launched two expeditions to discover the mythical C'tan device known as the breath of the Gods. The device was capable of syphoning the energy of stars throughout time, giving the C'tan essentially an unlimited source of power. The first under Vettius Telok was thought lost, but in truth Telok was driven mad and attempted to use the time-manipulating device to destroy the Imperium. The second, the Kotov Crusade, discovered Telok with the device and managed to stop the mad Magos and destroy it.

帝国发起了两次探索，以发现被称为诸神之息的神秘星神装置。该装置能够随着时间的推移虹吸恒星的能量，为星神提供了无限的能量来源。韦提乌斯·特洛克领导下的第一个被认为失败了，但事实上，特洛克陷入疯狂，并试图使用时间操纵装置摧毁帝国。第二次是科托夫远征，在这个装置发现了特洛克，并设法阻止了疯狂的贤者并摧毁了它。
