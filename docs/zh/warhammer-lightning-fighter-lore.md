The Lightning fighter craft is mainly used by the Imperial Navy for establishing air superiority due to its increased maneuverability over the Thunderbolt. It is often seen and heard plummeting from orbit or being scrambled from naval bases. The Lightning is also used by the forces of Chaos.

闪电战斗机主要被帝国海军用于建立空中优势，因为它比霹雳战斗机的机动性更强。人们经常看到和听到它从轨道上速降或从海军基地被紧急起飞。闪电也被混沌部队所使用。

## History 历史

Lost during the Scouring, the STC data for the Lightning was rediscovered during the Saint-Saen Crusade, which liberated twelve worlds in Segmentum Obscurus. They were found on Karnak II by an Adeptus MechanicusExplorator team and the designs immediately given to the Mechanicus Lords of Cypra Mundi. It would take over five hundred years of research and trials, including readings of the Emperor's Tarot and cross-referencing with the archives of Mars, before the designs were finally approved for production in supplying fleet carriers and cruisers of Battlefleet Obscurus. Since then the designs have slowly been disseminated to the Navy worlds of Bakka and Hydraphur and Lightnings are now commonly seen alongside the Thunderbolt in both Segmentum Tempestus and Segmentum Pacificus.

闪电的STC数据在大清洗中丢失，在圣塞恩远征期间被重新发现，解放了朦胧星域的十二个世界。它们是由一个机械神教探索者小队在卡纳克二号上发现的，这些设计立即交给了凯普拉·蒙迪的机械教领主。这需要五百多年的研究和试验，包括阅读帝皇塔罗牌和与火星档案的交叉引用，然后这些设计最终被批准用于为么朦胧星域战斗舰队的航母和巡洋舰提供产品。从那时起，这些设计慢慢传播到巴卡和海德拉弗海军世界，闪电现在通常与霹雳一起出现在暴风星域和太平星域中。

Lightning squadrons are not fixed in size, but most feature more than two dozen aircrafts at the start of hostilities. They will inevitably suffer heavy attrition, only to be reinforced by fresh pilots.

闪电中队的规模并不固定，但在战争行动开始时，大多数中队都有二十多架飞机。他们将不可避免地遭受严重的减员，只会得到新飞行员的增援。

## Tactical Role 战术角色

The main role of the aircraft is to engage enemy craft and establish air-superiority over a battlefield, with a secondary mission of providing air escort for Marauder Bombers. For this reason they are often based on orbiting ships or forward air bases, where their rocket booster allows them to quickly take off and engage enemy air forces. Ground-based Lightnings can also benefit from hydraulic "fast-launch" ramps which, in combination with their rocket booster engine, provides better acceleration and faster take-off time than conventional VTOL craft. Standard practice has three to four Lightnings so poised in a constant state of readiness. Although not their primary function, Lightnings may also act as a ground attack aircraft, with free license to attack targets of opportunity. This role however is better suited to the Lightning Strike.

该飞机的主要角色是与敌方飞机交战，在战场上建立空中优势，次要任务是为掠夺者轰炸机提供空中护航。因此，它们通常基于轨道舰艇或前线空军基地，在那里，它们的火箭助推器使它们能够快速起飞并与敌方空军交战。地面基地的闪电还可以受益于液压“速射”坡道，该坡道与火箭助推引擎相结合，比传统垂直起降飞行器提供更好的加速度和更快的起飞时间。标准做法是有三到四架闪电处于持续的准备状态。虽然不是其主要功能，但它也可以作为地面攻击机，自由攻击机会目标。然而，这个角色更适合闪电。

## Technical Specifications 技术规格

The Lightning uses a swept-wing design and twin F100-XB afterburning turbofans to give it superior speed and better rate of climb and dive compared to the Thunderbolt, although it is lighter, less rugged and more demanding on maintenance crews. Like the Thunderbolt, it also features a rocket booster engine which allows for quick take-offs when using a "fast-launch" ramp and for limited operational capability in space, although it is not a true "starfighter" like the Fury Interceptor. Otherwise, the Lightning can use its rocket engine to perform a conventional fast take-off using runways, reaching an altitude of thousands of feet in less than five seconds. Additionally, the Lightning is vector thrusted, allowing it to 'jink', 'twist' and 'dance' through the air and perform vertical take-off and landing.

闪电采用后掠翼设计和双F100-XB后燃涡扇引擎，与霹雳相比，它具有更高的速度和更好的爬升率和俯冲率，尽管它更轻，不那么坚固，对维护人员的要求更高。与霹雳一样，它还配备了火箭助推引擎，在使用“速射”坡道时可以快速起飞，在太空中的作战能力有限，尽管它不是像狂怒截击机那样的真正“星际战斗机”。除此以外，闪电可以使用其火箭引擎使用跑道进行传统的快速起飞，在不到五秒钟的时间内达到数千英尺的高度。此外，闪电是矢量推进的，使其能够在空中“急转”、“旋转”和“闪动”，并进行垂直起降。

Standard armament for the Lightning is a long-barrelled, ventral-mounted Autocannon and twin-linkedLascannons on the wing tips. Four wing hardpoints allows it to carry up to four Hellstrike Missiles, Hellfury Missiles, Bombs or six Skystrike Missiles. Other standard systems includes a pair of Parallax tracking augers located in the nose section, while many will mount additional equipment. The autocannon fires 127 high-velocity armour piercings rounds per second.

闪电的标准武器是一门长管、腹部安装的自动炮和翼尖上的双联激光炮。四个机翼挂载点使其最多可携带四枚狱击导弹、狱怒导弹、炸弹或六枚天击导弹。其他标准系统包括位于机头部分的一对视察跟踪鸟卜仪，而许多将安装额外的设备。自动炮每秒发射127发高速穿甲弹。

The Lightning is also equipped with a missile-lock warning system, chaff and flare launchers, a multi-frequency vox, a navigational cogitator for route plotting and a heads-up display linked to the weapons systems allowing for target lock-on.

闪电还配备了导弹锁定警告系统、箔条和闪光发射器、多频音阵、用于路线绘制的导航定位器以及与武器系统相连的平视显示器，允许锁定目标。

Lightnings can operate as recon planes by mounting photo-equipment. Lightnings can also operate as void fighters, carried onboard spaceships on launch decks and launched by mag-catapults. These versions are armed with a cannon and underwing missiles linked to a heads-up display and are equipped with grav-seats and pattern-recognition systems that can detect hostile spaceships, even identifying xenos vessels like the Raven.

通过安装摄影设备，闪电可以作为侦察机使用。闪电也可以作为虚空战斗机，载于的星舰上的发射甲板，由弹射器发射。这些版本配备了加农炮和与平视显示器相连的翼下导弹，并配备了重力座椅和模式识别系统，可以检测敌方星舰，甚至可以识别像渡鸦这样的异型战舰。

## Variants 变体

### Voss Pattern Lightning Strike Fighter 沃斯型闪电打击战斗机

Also called the Primaris Lightning Strike Fighter, its design dates back to the Great Crusade where it was created by Archmagos-ArbiterSykosk Thule. Originating from the Forge World of Voss Prime, the 'Strike' variant of the Lightning is designed for a fighter-bomber role. It is normally equipped with a potent armament of twin-linked Lascannons and six Hellstrike Missiles or Rad Missiles mounted on three dual hardpoints. Each hardpoint mount can also individually carry a twin-linkedautocannon, a twin-linked multi-laser, a twin-linked missile launcher equipped with frag or krak warheads, two Sunfury heavy missiles, two Kraken penetrator warheads, two electromagnetic storm charges or a Phosphex bomb cluster.

它也被称为首要闪电战斗机，其设计可以追溯到大远征，由仲裁大贤者赛科斯克·图勒创造。闪电的“打击”变体源自沃斯一号铸造世界，专为战斗轰炸机角色而设计。它通常配备有双联激光炮和安装在三个双外挂点上的六枚狱击导弹或辐射导弹的强大武器。每个外挂点安装架还可以单独携带一门双联自动炮、一门双连多管激光、一个配备破片或穿甲弹头的双联导弹发射器、两枚日怒重型导弹、两枚克拉肯穿透弹头、两枚电磁风暴炸药或一枚磷化集束炸弹。

Voss Pattern Lightning Strike Fighter 沃斯型闪电打击战斗机

The Primaris Lightning was a common type of fighter used by the Solar Auxilia and the Space Marine Legions during the Great Crusade and Horus Heresy. It was equipped with a chaff launcher and an armoured cockpit and could be further modified by including ground-tracking auguries and a ramjet diffraction grid. It could also replace its human pilot with a hard-wired servitor and a dedicated cogitator targeting array to better identify and target enemy vehicle weak spots.

首要闪电是太阳辅助军和星际战士在大远征和荷鲁斯叛乱期间使用的一种常见战斗机。它配备了一个箔条发射器和一个装甲驾驶舱，可以通过包括地面跟踪鸟卜仪和冲压发动机衍射格栅进行进一步修改。它还可以用一个硬线机仆和一个专用的沉思者瞄准阵列代替其人类飞行员，以更好地识别和瞄准敌方载具的弱点。

The Primaris Lightning could fly at at least three times the speed of sound.

首要闪电的飞行速度至少是音速的三倍。

At the time of its introduction, the aircraft represented a new paradigm in aerial warfare for the Imperium of Man, being powered by a forced-plasma ramjet drive and optimized for the most sophisticated munitions available. However the pattern proved extremely costly to produce and could only be handled by the most experienced pilots. A degraded yet more easily operated and maintained version was rumoured to be under consideration just prior to the outbreak of the Horus Heresy, but was not introduced during the conflict.

在推出时，该飞机代表了人类帝国空战的新范式，由强制等离子体冲压发动机驱动，并针对最先进的弹药进行了优化。然而，事实证明，这种型号的生产成本极高，只能由最有经验的飞行员来操纵。据传，在荷鲁斯叛乱爆发之前，正在考虑一个降级但更容易操作和维护的版本，但在冲突期间没有引入。
