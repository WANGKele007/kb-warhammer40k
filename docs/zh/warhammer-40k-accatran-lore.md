Accatran is an ImperialForge World situated on the edge of the Ork Empire of Charadon. It is home to the Legio Destructor (Beasts of Steel).

阿卡特兰是一个帝国铸造世界，位于查拉顿的欧克帝国边缘。它是破坏者军团（钢铁野兽）的家园世界。

## Overview 概述

A heavily polluted world, the Mechanicum rulers of Accatran live deep underground, close to the planet's core. Highly hierarchical, the closer one lives to Accatran's core, the higher their status. While the ruling Magi live closest to the core, only the mad or barbaric tech-scavengers live on its hellscape of a surface. Visitors to Accatran are flown down into the planet's crust via landing tunnels and make planetfall at a location befitting of their position.

这是一个污染严重的世界，阿卡特兰的机械教统治者生活在地下深处，靠近行星的核心。等级森严，越接近阿卡特兰的核心，他们的地位就越高。虽然统治的圣贤士生活在最接近核心的地方，但只有疯狂或野蛮的技术拾荒者生活在其地狱般的地表。前往阿卡特兰的游客通过着陆隧道飞入地壳，并在适合他们位置的地点降落。

Although the Archmagi of Accatran proclaim themselves as close allies of Ultramar, the world is truthfully nothing more than a dependency of Roboute Guilliman's realm, and in the eyes of Mars Accatran is considered to be of an inferior grade when compared to other forge-worlds, such as Tigrus. Yet its devotion to Ultramar has also made Accatran staunchly loyal to the Imperium throughout history.

尽管阿卡特兰的大贤者宣称自己是奥特拉玛的亲密盟友，但这个世界实际上只不过是罗伯特·基里曼的领域的附属，在火星看来，阿卡特兰与提格里斯等其他铸造世界相比被认为是较差的。然而，阿卡特兰对奥特拉玛的忠诚也使其在历史上始终坚定地忠于帝国。

The inhabitants of Accatran wear predominantly black coloured robes.

阿卡特兰的居民主要穿着黑色长袍。

## History 历史

The founder of Accatran was Xerxes Immortalis, a great general and warrior-magos whom, legend insists, had held the planet free of darkness for a full millennium and more during the Age of Strife. When it joined the Imperium, Accatran became much more loyal to Terra than Mars, and devoted its fighting forces (organised as Taghmata "Xerxes") to the Great Crusade.

阿卡特兰的发现者是薛西斯·不朽，一位伟大的将军和战士贤者，传说中他在纷争时代将行星从黑暗中解放了整整一千年甚至更长时间。当它加入帝国时，阿卡特兰变得比火星更忠于泰拉，并将其战斗部队（组织为禁卫“薛西斯”）奉献给了大远征。

As a result of its loyalist stance, Accatran and its forces were covertly marked for destruction by the Traitors at the Horus Heresy's start. A large portion of Taghmata "Xerxes" was thus part of the Calth muster and targeted during the Battle of Calth. Accatran's main army on Calth was entirely wiped out by Traitors during the Betrayal at Ithraca, though it also inflicted horrific losses on its opponents in the process.

由于其忠诚的立场，阿卡特兰及其部队在荷鲁斯叛乱开始时被叛徒秘密标记为毁灭。因此，禁卫“薛西斯”的很大一部分是考斯集结的一部分，并在考斯之战中成为目标。在伊萨卡背叛中，阿卡特兰在考斯的主要军队被叛徒彻底消灭，尽管在此过程中也给对手造成了可怕的损失。
