Noctilith, commonly called Blackstone, is a strange metallo-organic compound superficially similar to obsidian. It has the mysterious capability to strengthen or weaken warp influence. Blackstone is found across the Galaxy. Once thought to be of little worth by Humanity, control of this resource could potentially win or lose the war against Chaos in a galaxy split in two by the Great Rift's opening.

夜石，通常被称为黑石，是一种奇怪的金属有机化合物，表面上与黑曜石相似。它具有增强或削弱亚空间影响的神秘能力。黑石遍布银河系。曾经被人类认为没有什么价值，对这种资源的控制可能会在大裂隙打开后一分为二的银河系中赢得或输掉与混沌的战争。

## Overview 概述

Noctilith is the substance which the Necrons use to create their star-spanning tetrahedral zones of stable space and is known to be highly resistant to the powers of the Warp. Noctilith is known to hold back Empyric energies, limiting both Psychic abilities and the manifestation of Daemons. Found in abundance on Tomb Worlds, the Adeptus Mechanicus under Belisarius Cawl in particular has sought out deposits for blackstone. Conversely, Noctilith can also enhance the effects of psychic powers, acting as a locus for warp energy. Noctilith can be tuned by specialized devices to either amplify or nullify the Warp.

夜石是死灵用来创建稳定空间的跨恒星四面体区域的物质，已知对亚空间的力量具有高度抵抗力。众所周知，夜石会抑制至高天的能量，限制灵能能力和恶魔的显现。贝利撒留·考尔手下的机械神教在墓穴世界中大量发现，他们专门寻找黑石矿床。相反，夜石也可以增强灵能力量的效果，作为亚空间能量的中心。夜石可以通过专门的设备进行调节，以放大或消除亚空间。

Tech-Priest acquiring Blackstone from a Necron structure 技术神甫从死灵构造获得黑石

The Imperium covets Noctilith, which some call "Pariah stone" due to its similar effect. Cawl and more ambitious members of the Mechanicum wish to mine enough Noctilith and empower it in order to create a shield against Chaos, perhaps subverting the growth of the Great Rift. The Necrons have been able to use Noctilith to create entire regions of space free from the touch of the Warp, such as the Pariah Nexus.Szarekh hopes to create this array on a galactic scale.

帝国觊觎夜石，由于其类似的效果，一些人称之为“被遗弃者石”。考尔和更野心勃勃的机械教成员希望开采足够的夜石并赋予它能量，以创造一个对抗混沌的盾牌，也许会颠覆大裂隙的成长。死灵已经能够使用夜石创造出整个不受亚空间影响的空间区域，如驱灵死域。斯扎拉克希望在银河系范围内创建这个阵列。

Further effects of Noctilith have been observed. Any living creature within the area of influence of Noctilith will find themselves afflicted with a numbing despair that only worsens with time until they become little more than a mindless husk - a perfect vessel for Necrons' experimentation on the reversal of biotransference.

已经观察到夜石的进一步影响。夜石影响范围内的任何生物都会发现自己陷入了麻木的绝望之中，这种绝望只会随着时间的推移而恶化，直到他们变成一个无脑的躯壳 — 这是死灵逆转生体转化实验的完美容器。

Blackstone fragment 黑石碎片

It is said that the Chaos Gods have their own plans for Noctilith. During the aftermath of the 13th Black Crusade, Black Legion forces used Blackstone shards of the Cadian Pylons to cut off Warp routes to Terra.

据说混沌诸神对夜石有自己的计划。在第13次黑色远征之后，黑色军团部队使用卡迪亚方尖碑的黑石碎片切断了通往泰拉的亚空间航线。

Noctilith has a variety of uses, fragments and shard are able to be repurposed in a variety of augmented armaments or psychic defences. A Tech-Priest could alter their weapon of choice, whether it be a simple data-wand, or an Omnissian Staff or Axe, it could be augmented to have both give or leech motive force from organic, mechanical, or even supernatural entities.

夜石有多种用途，破片和碎片可以重新用于各种军备增强或灵能防御。技术神甫可以改变他们选择的武器，无论是简单的数据杖，还是欧姆尼赛亚之杖或斧，它都可以被增强，以具有来自有机、机械甚至超自然实体的给予或吸取动力。

## History 历史

In the distant past of the galaxy, ancient races understood the workings of blackstone. Something more advanced than both the Aeldari and Necrons created the Blackstone Fortresses long, long ago. Despite this, only the Necrons would carry the secrets of blackstone into the future. Millions of years ago, the ancient Necrons used Noctilith to build a variety of Warp-dampening Pylons across the Galaxy.

在银河系的遥远过去，古代种族了解黑石的运作。很久很久以前，比灵族和死灵更先进的东西创造了黑石要塞。尽管如此，只有死灵会把黑石的秘密带到未来。数百万年前，古代死灵使用夜石在银河系建造了各种亚空间抑制方尖碑。

The Cadian Pylons, which were made of Blackstone 由黑石建造的卡迪亚方尖碑

In the time of the Great Crusade, the Custodian Guard constructed a prison on Terra made of Blackstone salvaged from Cadia to hold their most dangerous captives.

在大远征时期，禁军卫队在泰拉上建造了一座监狱，用从卡迪亚打捞来的黑石建造，用来关押他们最危险的俘虏。

Some time before 338.M41, the radicalInquisitorQuixos developed an interest in the warp-altering properties of the Cadian Pylons, in the hope of permanently closing the Eye of Terror. Controlled through Quixos' enslaved daemonhost, Cherubael, a cult calling itself the "Sons of Bael" studied the pylons. These studies bore fruit, and Quixos duplicated the pylons on other worlds using corrupted merchant cartels and personal supplies of Blackstone.

在338.M41之前的一段时间，激进派审判官奎克索斯对卡迪亚方尖碑的亚空间改变特性产生了兴趣，希望永久关闭恐惧之眼。通过奎克索斯被奴役的恶魔宿主切鲁贝尔控制，来自一个自称“贝尔之子”的研究方尖碑的教派。这些研究取得了成果，奎克索斯利用腐败的商业企业联盟和黑石的个人用品在其他世界复制了方尖碑。

Unfortunately these plans never came to fruition as he was executed by an Inquisitorial taskforce led by Eisenhorn. It is unknown if Quixos' plan would have succeeded regardless - he was deeply addled by warp corruption and it was well within reason that his experiment could have instead torn the Eye of Terror asunder, which would have resulted in the Noctis Aeterna's devastation and the formation of the Great Rift almost six centuries early.

不幸的是，这些计划从未实现，因为他被艾森霍恩领导的审判庭特遣部队处决。目前尚不清楚奎克索斯的计划是否会成功 - 他深陷亚空间的腐化，他的实验本可以撕裂恐惧之眼，这将导致永恒之夜的毁灭和大裂隙的形成，这几乎提前了六个世纪。

During the ten thousand years of the Long WarAbaddon launched strategic strikes on worlds of the Galaxy rich in Blackstone, allowing for the expansion of the Great Rift following the destruction of the Cadia during the Thirteenth Black Crusade. The destruction of Cadia and its Pylons resulted in increased interest in the study of Blackstone by the Adeptus Mechanicus. In truth Mechanicus had been mining raw Blackstone throughout the Segmentum Solar for well over five centuries. The secretive nature of Mechanicus’ gathering of Blackstone has drawn the eyes of the Inquisition towards their operations.

在一万年的万古长战中，阿巴顿对富含黑石的银河系世界发动了战略打击，在第十三次黑色远征期间摧毁卡迪亚后，大裂隙得以扩张。卡迪亚及其方尖碑的破坏导致机械神教对黑石的研究兴趣增加。事实上，五个多世纪以来，机械教一直在太阳星域开采黑石原矿。机械教收集黑石的秘密性质吸引了审判庭对其行动的关注。
