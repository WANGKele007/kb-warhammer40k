## Overview 概述

Tyranid Warriors are large creatures, although significantly smaller than the massive Hive Tyrants. They are fast and powerful, with the capability to be strong at ranged combat or in close quarters in a similar fashion to the Hive Tyrant. Tyranid Warriors are the most adaptable of all the Hive Mind’s bioforms. They are creatures from the blackest of nightmares, unstoppable killing machines with pulsing ichor for blood, needle-sharp teeth and darkly gleaming eyes that reveal a terrible intelligence at work. A Tyranid Warrior stands twice the height of a man, its carapace protected by a thick chitin. One might expect such a creature to be slow in its actions, but a Tyranid Warrior is lithe, with reactions as swift as a whip. Tyranid Warriors have the mental flexibility to employ a wide variety of bio-weapon symbiotes. As such, on the battlefield, they can be found leading all areas of a Tyranid swarm: fighting in close quarters with claws, boneswords and lash whips, or at longer ranges with devourers, deathspitters or even heavier bio-cannons. Whatever weaponry it wields, a Tyranid Warrior is a dangerous and unforgiving foe, able to identify and exploit the weaknesses of its targets with innate shrewdness. Worse, with its alien consciousness permanently bonded to the ageless Hive Mind, a Tyranid Warrior can instantly draw upon a reservoir of knowledge and experience that spans epochs, should its own prove insufficient to the task at hand.

泰伦战士虫是大型生物，尽管比巨大的虫巢暴君小得多。它们快速而强大，有能力在远程战斗或近距离战斗中以类似于虫巢暴君的方式变得强大。泰伦战士虫是所有虫巢思维生物形态中适应性最强的。它们是来自最黑暗噩梦的生物，是不可阻挡的杀戮机器，有脉动的血液，锋利的牙齿和闪闪发光的眼睛，显示出可怕的智慧在起作用。一个泰伦战士虫的身高是一个人的两倍，它的甲壳被厚厚的几丁质保护着。人们可能会认为这样的生物行动迟缓，但泰伦战士虫很轻盈，反应像鞭子一样迅速。泰伦战士虫有精神灵活性，可以使用各种各样的生物武器共生体。因此，在战场上，他们可以领导泰伦虫群的所有领域：用爪子、骨刀和猛击鞭近距离作战，或者用吞噬者、死亡喷吐者甚至更重的生物炮远距离作战。无论使用何种武器，泰伦战士虫都是一个危险而无情的敌人，能够以天生的精明识别和利用目标的弱点。更糟糕的是，由于它的异型意识永久地与永恒的虫巢思维联系在一起，如果它自己的知识和经验不足以完成手头的任务，一个泰伦战士虫可以立即利用跨越时代的知识和经历库。

Tyranid Warriors 泰伦战士虫

Though they are formidable fighters in their own right, it is the Tyranid Warriors’ role as the synaptic lynchpins of the swarm that makes them truly deadly. Tyranid Warriors are psychic resonators for the unwavering will of the Hive Mind and some of the more common conduits used to exert control over the less receptive creatures of the hive fleets. As such, Tyranid Warriors form a vital link in the Tyranid swarm, acting as relays and amplifiers through which Hive Tyrants issue their commands. So crucial is this role to a hive fleet’s efforts to defeat a prey world’s defenders that each Hive Tyrant is invariably accompanied by several broods of Tyranid Warriors cultured from its very own flesh – the better to enhance the psychic link throughout the swarm. This is not to imply that Tyranid Warriors are merely drones, for each is instinctively capable of assessing local battlefield situations. They can then, if the need arises, direct those Tyranid creatures near them, like an officer marshaling their forces, to exploit any tactical weakness that may appear in the enemy’s defences.

尽管它们本身就是强大的战士，但正是泰伦战士虫作为虫群突触关键的角色使它们真正致命。泰伦战士虫是虫巢思维坚定不移意志的灵能共振者，也是用来控制虫巢舰队中不太容易接受的生物的一些更常见的渠道。因此，泰伦战士虫在泰伦虫群中形成了一个至关重要的环节，作为中继器和放大器，虫巢暴君通过它们发出命令。这个角色对于虫巢舰队击败猎物世界的守军的努力至关重要，每个虫巢暴君总是伴随着几群从自己的血肉中培养出来的泰伦战士虫 — 这有助于增强整个虫巢的灵能链接。这并不意味着泰伦战士虫只是无人机，因为每个战士虫都本能地能够评估当地的战场情况。然后，如果需要，他们可以指挥附近的泰伦生物，就像一名军官指挥他们的部队一样，利用敌人防御中可能出现的任何战术弱点。

Tyranid Warrior with Bonesword and Lashwhip 装备骨剑和猛击鞭的泰伦战士虫

The Tyranid Warrior (*gladius*) is a species of the Warrior (*tyranicus tyranicii*) genus. The *gladius* species itself is highly adaptable to any battle role, with over 212 known variants - combining flying, walking, leaping biomorphs, with biological ranged weapons or assault symbiotes. The basic Biomorph array for Tyranid Warriors is a Devourer and a pair of Scything Talons. However a great other combination of weaponry has been observed, including Rending Claws, Boneswords, a Lash-Whip, a Barbed Strangler, a Venom Cannon, a Deathspitter, and Spinefists.

泰伦战士虫（短剑）是战士（泰伦暴君）属的一个物种。短剑物种本身对任何战斗角色都有很强的适应性，有超过212种已知的变体，结合了飞行、行走、跳跃的生物形态，以及生物远程武器或攻击共生体。泰伦战士虫的基本生物形态阵列是一个吞噬者和一对镰爪。然而，人们还观察到了其他武器的组合，包括撕裂爪、骨舰、猛击鞭、尖刺扼杀者、毒液炮、死亡喷吐者和尖刺拳。

## Tyranid Warrior Classifications 泰伦战士虫分类

 - 

Tyranid New-Spawn are Warriors produced from brand new biomass absorbed by the Hive Ship, not implanted with the learned experience of a previous organism.

 - 

泰伦新衍是由虫巢舰吸收的全新生物质制成的战士虫，没有植入以前生物的学习经验。

 - 

Tyranid Alphas are experienced Warriors that have been spawned many times with their prior knowledge intact, allowing them to be a more deadly foe. When no Prime is present, an Alpha will take charge of a group of Warriors.

 - 

战士虫阿尔法是经验丰富的战士，他们的先前知识完好无损地被多次培养出来，使他们成为更致命的敌人。当没有首席在场时，阿尔法将负责一队战士虫。
