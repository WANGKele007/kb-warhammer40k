A Battlewagon is a common type of large Ork vehicle.

战斗卡车是一种常见的大型欧克载具。

## Overview 概述

Designed to carry a whole fighting mob of Orks, plus any Gretchin, Snotlings, Squigs or shiny things that may belong to the mob, Battlewagons are typically large, heavy and noisy. Ownership of a Battlewagon marks an Ork out as particularly prestigious or 'flash'. A typical Battlewagon owner is most likely a Nob of some description, though various Oddboyz may have access to Battlewagons at certain times. Warbosses often mount themselves and their immediate retinue in Battlewagons when travelling to battle, and may have more than one if their retinue is of sufficient size.

战车的设计是为了携带一整群欧克战斗暴徒，以及任何可能属于暴徒的屁精、鼻涕精、史古格或闪亮的东西，战斗卡车通常又大又重，而且很吵。拥有战车标志欧克特别有声望或“闪光”。一个典型的战斗卡车的车主很可能是某种类型的老大，尽管各种古怪小子在某些时候可能会有使用战斗卡车的机会。战争头目们在前往战斗时，经常自己和他们的亲卫乘坐战斗卡车，如果他们的随从有足够的规模，可能会有不止一个。

Ork Battlewagon 欧克战斗卡车

Like all Ork vehicles, Battlewagons do not conform to a set standard, although the Mekboyz of the Ork clans do tend to lean towards building their Battlewagons based around an initial chassis design that is somehow known to them almost instinctively. Past this initial point however, Battlewagons will tend to look rather unique as various Meks and decoratively-minded owners get their hands and hammers on the vehicle. Most Battlewagons come with flagpoles bolted on somewhere so the owner can display his personal or clan banner in a prominent place, as well as thick armour plate in most locations - though many Battlewagons are open-topped.

与所有欧克载具一样，战斗卡车不遵守设定标准，尽管欧克氏族的技师小子确实倾向于根据他们几乎本能地知道的初始底盘设计来建造他们的战斗卡车。然而，经过这一初始阶段，随着各个技师和有装饰意识的车主把手和锤子放在战斗卡车上，它往往看起来相当独特。大多数战斗卡车都配有栓在某处的旗杆，这样车主就可以在显眼的地方展示他的个人或氏族旗帜，在大多数地方还可以装上厚厚的装甲板 — 尽管许多战斗卡车都是敞篷的。

A Kannon, Zzap Gun, Lobba, or a Lifta-Droppa along with an array of Big Shootas and Rokkit launchas, Deffrollas, Stikkbomb Chukkas, and Grabbin' Claws are popular weapons choices for a Battlewagon. They are not particularly fast and so not all that popular with Mekboyz, who prefer their own rides to be significantly more speedy, not to mention kustomized. The chief advantages of Battlewagons is that they are sturdy and well-armoured, offering the passengers good protection (until they stick their heads out from behind the armour plate to see what's going on) and can transport a large number of Orks and their equipment directly into battle.

欧克加农、吱吱炮、投掷炮或升降炮，以及一系列大号欧克枪和火箭发射器、死亡滚轮、棍子炸弹发射器和抓爪，都是战斗卡车的热门武器选择。它们不是特别快，所以不太受技师小子的欢迎，他们更喜欢自己的骑行速度要快得多，更不用说定制了。战斗卡车的主要优点是它们坚固耐用，装甲精良，为乘员提供了良好的保护（直到他们从装甲板后面探出头来看看发生了什么），并且可以将大量的欧克及其装备直接运送到战斗中。

## Variants 变体

### Krushas 碾碎者

Krushas are a popular subtype of Ork Battlewagon which includes Bonebreakas, Bonecrushas, Spleenrippas, and Gitmashers. These combine armoured transport capabilities with an arsenal of heavy firepower and an enormous spiked Deff Rolla to create an devastating assault vehicle used to spearhead assaults.

碾碎者是欧克战斗卡车的一种流行亚型，包括破骨者、碾骨者、裂脾者和碎敌者。这些将装甲运输能力与重型火力军械和巨大的尖刺死亡滚轮相结合，创造了一种用于矛头攻击的毁灭性突击载具。

Bonebreaka 破骨者

### Gunwagon 炮车

Gunwagons are another popular subtype of Ork Battlwagon, that forego a substantial portion of their transport capability in order to mount heavy artillery. Little more than armour-plated mobile gun platforms, these vehicles advance towards the enemy at a steady crawl, hammering firepower at the enemy ceaselessly. Inside their cabins, the tanks' spotters peer through kustom lookin'-tubes and tele-gubbinz, calling out fresh targets to their gun crews. The only real drawback to these designs are the devastating explosions that result should a gunwagon be hit with enemy fire. The Orks, being Orks, see this less of a flaw and more of a golden opportunity to take the enemy with them.

炮车是欧克战斗卡车的另一种流行亚型，为了装载重型火炮而放弃了大部分运输能力。这些载具只不过是装甲移动炮台，以稳定的速度向敌人前进，不断向敌人发射火力。在他们的座舱里，坦克的观察员透过定制观察管和远程零件向外张望，向他们的炮手发出新的目标。这些设计的唯一真正缺点是，如果炮车被敌人的炮火击中，会导致毁灭性的爆炸。作为欧克，欧克认为这与其说是一个缺陷，不如说是一次干掉敌人的绝佳机会。

Gunwagon 炮车

### Lifta Wagon 抬升卡车

Lifta Wagons are Ork Battlewagons equipped with a Lifta-Droppa to engage in heavy lifting and salvaging. Operated by an Mekboy gunner sitting as far away from the muzzle of the Lifta-Droppa as possible, the weapon scoops up enemy tanks and either smashes it up-side down or throws it back into the enemies ranks. The Lifta-Droppa is more suitable to be mounted on a Stompa or a Gargant than the smaller, unshielded vehicle, giving the Lifta-Wagon often short lived lifespan on the battlefield. The Lifta Wagon and its weapon are powered by a temperamental atom-smasha unit.

抬升卡车是配备升降炮的欧克战斗卡车，用于重型起重和打捞。由一名坐在离升降炮的炮口尽可能远的技师小子炮手操作，该武器抬起敌人的坦克，要么将其侧面朝下粉碎，要么将它扔回敌人的行列。升降炮比较小的无护盾载具更适合安装在古巨圾或加冈特上，这使得升降炮在战场上的寿命往往很短。抬升卡车及其武器由一个反复无常的原子粉碎装置提供动力。

Lifta Battlewagon 抬升战斗卡车
