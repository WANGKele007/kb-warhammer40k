The Galaspar Cluster is a region of space made up of eleven primary star systems. The main system of the cluster is the Galaspar System.

加拉斯帕星群是由11个主恒星系组成的太空区域。该星群的主要星系是加拉斯帕星系。

It was brought into compliance during the Great Crusade by the Death Guard when they crushed The Order, the former rulers of the region.

在大远征期间，死亡守卫镇压了该地区的前统治者秩序，使其平定。
