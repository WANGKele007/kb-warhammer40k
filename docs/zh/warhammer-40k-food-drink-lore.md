The Imperium is a massive empire spanning the galaxy and as such its numerous subjects make use of all manner of fauna and flora for foodstuffs. As well as food, Imperial worlds produce a variety of drinks.

帝国是一个横跨银河系的庞大帝国，因此其众多臣民利用各种动植物作为食物。除了食物，帝国世界还生产各种饮料。

## Food 食物

Within the Imperium, entire worlds are devoted to agriculture and food, providing the necessary sustenance for populations elsewhere in the galaxy. These agri worlds can grow a variety of food in a variety of ways, from the ill-tempered grox that provide meat to simple wheat for bread, with entire continents being given over to the growth of foodstuffs. The most standard type of food grown on Agri-Worlds and subsequently distributed to feed the innumerable masses is processed corn.

在帝国内部，整个世界都致力于农业和粮食，为银河系其他地方的人口提供必要的食物。这些农业世界可以以各种方式种植各种食物，从提供肉类的脾气暴躁的格洛克斯到谷物到面包用小麦，整个大陆都被用于种植食物。在农业世界种植并随后分发给无数平民的最标准的食物类型是加工玉米。

This variety can produce confusion when Imperial cultures mix; in one recorded instance, Tona Criid, a native of Vervunhive on Verghast, mistook camouflage paint for a type of food-paste supplied to the off-world Tanith Firstregiment.

当帝国文化混合时，这种多样性会产生混乱；在一个有记录的例子中，维尔加斯特上维尔文巢都的当地人托娜·克里德将迷彩漆误认为是供应给世界之外的塔尼斯第一兵团的一种食物膏。

Space Marines have been known to subsist on triglyceride gel and amino-porridge, as well as local wildlife hunted during their firing exercises.

众所周知，星际战士以甘油三酯凝胶和氨基粥为生，也以在射击练习中猎杀的当地野生动物为生。

The Dark Angels are known to consume nutrigruel, carboloafs and vitamead.

众所周知，黑暗天使会食用营养粥、碳块和维生素 。

## Drink 饮料

Amasec and Rotgut are two forms of popular alcoholic drink in the Imperium. Wildsnake, a form of alcohol that contains an actual venomous snake, is a famed beverage of Hive Primus on Necromunda. The Tanith drink a now field-brewed drink known as Sacra, a particularly strong brew. Doomberry Juice has tranquilising effects, but causes nightmares.

阿玛赛克和劣等酒是帝国时期流行的两种酒精饮料。野蛇是一种含有真正的毒蛇的酒，是涅克罗蒙达上第一巢都的著名饮料。塔尼斯人喝一种现在被称为萨克拉的田间酿造的饮料，这是一种特别浓的饮料。末日浆果果汁具有镇静作用，但会引起噩梦。

## Non-Imperial Foodstuffs 非帝国食品

It is known that some non-Imperial foods are edible by humans, or at least Astartes. Eldar food is known to have been consumed by the Marines of the Emperor's ChildrenSpace Marine Legion. This may indicate compatibility of the human body with Eldar food, just Astartes physiology (being enhanced as it is), or that the Eldar simply knew what humans found palatable as food.

众所周知，一些非帝国食物可以供人类食用，或者至少是阿斯塔特。众所周知，灵族食物曾被帝皇之子星际战士军团的战士食用。这可能表明人体与灵族食物的相容性，只是阿斯塔特生理（尽管它是增强的），或者灵族只是知道人类认为什么是美味的食物。

Xenos-cultured wheat is also not unknown, though illegal.

异型种植的小麦也并不陌生，尽管是非法的。

The Tau have been known to use nim ko’nai as rations.

众所周知，钛人使用尼姆·科'奈作为口粮。

Orks are known to eat other Orkoid Fungus-based lifeforms such as Squigs but they also drink fungus-beer and other liquors made by ork Brewboyz.

众所周知，欧克会吃其他以欧克真菌为基础的生命形式，如史古格，但它们也会喝真菌啤酒和其他由欧克酿酒小子酿造的酒。
