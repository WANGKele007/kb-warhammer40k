The Interex were a highly advanced Human civilisation during the time of the Great Crusade.

在大远征时期，英特雷斯是一个高度发达的人类文明。

## Biology 生理

Interex were tall humans with only one genetically engineered difference - large bat-like ears that helped them use their unique language based on music and sounds. Interex still used language - an evolved human dialect of Terra - but very rarely.

英特雷斯是高大的人类，只有一个转基因的区别 — 蝙蝠般的大耳朵，帮助他们使用基于音乐和声音的独特语言。英特雷斯仍然使用语言 — 泰拉的一种进化的人类方言 — 但很少使用。

## Overview 概述

The Interex were humans originating from Terra, that created their own civilization. At some point they made contact with the Eldar, who told them about the dangers of Chaos and the threat that the inhabitants of the warp pose to humanity. Later, the Interex made contact with the Kinebrach, who were defeated as a result of a long war and subsequently entered their society, becoming an inextricable part. After that, the Interex encountered, defeated and localized the aggressive alien Megarachnids on the planet Murder (Urisarach in the language of the Interex, meaning Spiderland). The Megarachnids had begun to attack the frontier planets of the Interex, infesting eight Interex systems at the start of the conflict. During the Great Crusade the Interex were encountered by the Luna Wolves and at that time their civilization included 30 systems. The technology of the Interex was in many ways more advanced than that of the Imperium, but not as strongly focused on warfare.

英特雷斯是起源于泰拉的人类，他们创造了自己的文明。在某个时刻，他们与灵族取得了联系，灵族告诉他们混沌的危险以及亚空间居民对人类构成的威胁。后来，英特雷斯与基尼布拉赫接触，基尼布拉赫因长期战争而战败，随后进入他们的社会，成为不可分割的一部分。在那之后，英特雷斯遇到、击败并定位了谋杀行星上的侵略性异型巨蛛怪（英特雷斯语言中的乌里萨拉，意为蜘蛛之地）。巨蛛怪已经开始攻击英特雷斯的边界行星，在冲突开始时侵扰了八个英特雷斯星系。在大远征期间，影月苍狼遇到了英特雷斯，当时他们的文明包含30个星系。英特雷斯的技术在很多方面都比帝国更先进，但并不像帝国那样专注于战争。

Devoted to fighting Chaos, (they referred to it as 'Kaos'), they regarded the 63rd expedition skeptically, as they thought they might be tainted by it. They were in talks with Horus and his peers when somebody (later discovered to be First Chaplain Erebus of the Word Bearers) infiltrated the Hall of Devices, the Interex museum of artefacts and weapons, sabotaged the building to explode, and stole a valuable and dangerous Kinebrach sword known as an Anathame. Fighting broke out between Space Marines and Interex forces until Warmaster Horus was successfully evacuated off planet. The stolen Anathame, secretly taken by Erebus, would be the weapon that later mortally wounded Horus. The Interex are confirmed to have been destroyed in an Imperial campaign before the beginning of the war on the Auretian Technocracy.

致力于对抗混乱（他们称之为“卡奥斯”），他们怀疑第63远征队，因为他们认为他们可能受到了它的污染。他们正在与荷鲁斯和他的同伴交谈，这时有人（后来被发现是怀言者的第一牧师艾瑞巴斯）渗透到英特雷斯造物和武器博物馆的设备大厅，破坏了建筑并导致爆炸，并偷走了一把被称为仪式之刃的珍贵而危险的基尼布拉赫剑。星际战士和英特雷斯部队之间爆发了战斗，直到战帅荷鲁斯成功撤离行星。艾瑞巴斯秘密夺取的被盗仪式之刃，将成为后来致命伤荷鲁斯的武器。英特雷斯被证实在与奥瑞提安技术联盟的战争开始前的一场帝国战役中被摧毁。

## Technology 技术

The Interex had incredibly advanced technology available to them. At first glance, many of Horus' warriors believed the Interex to be aliens in the shape of centaurs. Later it was found the centaur base was a form of mobile fighting platforms for Interex warriors. They would step into the base, and form a centaur-looking warrior with increased mobility. They also used a type of bow or crossbow that the warriors of Horus wrote off as decorative parade weapons, until a skirmish revealed that they could emit bolts of light fully capable of punching through Space Marine armour.

英特雷斯拥有令人难以置信的先进技术。乍一看，荷鲁斯的许多战士认为英特雷斯是半人马形状的异型。后来人们发现半人马基底是英特雷斯战士的一种移动战斗平台。他们将进入基底，形成一个看起来像半人马的战士，机动性更强。他们还使用了一种弓或弩，荷鲁斯的战士将其视为装饰性的阅兵武器，直到一场小规模冲突表明，他们可以发射出完全能够穿透星际战士装甲的闪电箭。

Another example of Interex weaponry is the Interex mauler. A lightweight pistol-like beam weapon. The weapon has no kick and is absolutely accurate. It utilizes Kinebrach filament photonics. When fired it discharges collimated beam of powerful energy.

英特雷斯武器的另一个例子是英特雷斯猛击枪。一种类似手枪的轻型光束武器。这把武器没有后坐力，绝对精准。它利用基尼布拉赫细丝光子。发射时，它会射出精准而笔直的高能光束。

The Interex also had automated drones throughout their society that were akin to Advanced AI, as opposed to the Imperium's more barbaric-like use of servitors.

英特雷斯在整个社会中也有类似于高级人工智能的自动无人机，而不是帝国更野蛮地使用机仆。
