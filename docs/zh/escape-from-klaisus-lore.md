Klaisus, known to the Eldar as Ulthanash's Rest, is an ice moon and the furthest body from the sun in the Cadian System.

卡莱苏斯，被灵族称为乌萨纳什之息，是一个冰雪卫星，也是卡迪亚星系中离太阳最远的天体。

## History 历史

This moon was once an outpost of the ancient Eldar empire. During this time it was used as a waystation that lead to the Psychedelta convergence and the Eastern Fringe of the galaxy, the area that would later become known as the Realm of Ultramar.

这个卫星曾经是古代灵族帝国的前哨。在此期间，它被用作通往灵能三角洲融合和银河系东部边缘的中转站，该地区后来被称为奥特拉玛领域。

### Fall of Cadia 卡迪亚陷落

During the Thirteenth Black Crusade the planet became a refuge for Imperial survivors of the destruction of Cadia under the guidance of Saint Celestine. The Black Legion pursued the refugees to Kalisus in pursuit of the artifact of Belisarius Cawl, but were forced to retreat by the Eldar. The Eldar opened up a large Webway Portal at the heart of the Crone's Claw mountain range and guided the Imperial survivors into it.

在第十三次黑色远征期间，在圣塞勒斯汀的指引下，这个行星成为了卡迪亚毁灭后帝国幸存者的避难所。黑色军团追捕逃亡者到卡利苏斯，寻找贝利萨留·考尔的造物，但被灵族迫使撤退。灵族在老妪之爪山脉的中心开辟了一个大型的网道传送门，并引导帝国幸存者进入其中。
