Planet 7/2 was a world of the Galaxy within the Nephilim Sector.

行星7/2是位于拿非利星区的银河系世界。

Existing at the fringes of the Sector and far from any nearby Imperial worlds, Planet 7/2 was believed to be uninhabited. However in truth it was home to a massive Necron complex which coordinated the Pariah Nexus. This made it an ideal target for a raid by Belisarius Cawl and the White Consuls under Captain Messinius, who attacked it during the Pariah Crusade as Fleet Primus and Fleet Tertius launched diversionary strikes at Mesmoch and Paradyce.

存在于星区的边缘，远离任何附近的帝国世界，行星7/2被认为是无人居住的。然而，事实上，它是一个庞大的死灵建筑群的所在地，该建筑群协调了驱灵死域。这使它成为贝利撒留·考尔和梅西尼乌斯连长领导的白色执政官突袭的理想目标，他们在帕里亚远征期间袭击了它，因为第一舰队和第三舰队对梅斯莫赫和帕拉迪斯发动了转移注意力的袭击。

In the subsequent raid the Space Marines fought through waves of Necron constructs before reaching a central command hub, which Cawl interfaced with. Cawl was able to disrupt the local Pylon network, learn much about the true nature of the Pariah Nexus, and unleash dataphages within the complex's control systems. After completing their mission, Cawl and the survivors were able to teleport back to their orbiting Strike Cruiser with a Pharos device discovered on the world.

在随后的突袭中，星际战士在到达考尔连接的中央指挥中心之前，经历了死灵构造体的浪潮。考尔能够破坏当地的方尖碑网络，了解驱灵死域的真实性质，并在建筑群的控制系统中释放数据噬菌体。在完成任务后，考尔和幸存者能够用世界上发现的灯塔装置传送回他们轨道上的打击巡洋舰。
