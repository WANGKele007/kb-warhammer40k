Hastur Sejanus was the Captain of the 4th Company of the Luna WolvesSpace Marine Legion and a member of the Mournival. A senior commander and particularly close friend to his primarch, Horus, he was murdered during compliance negotiations upon Sixty-Three-Nineteen. His appearance was later used by Erebus in an attempt to trick and coerce Horus into following the path of Chaos.

哈斯塔·塞扬努斯是影月苍狼星际战士军团第四连队的连长，也是四王议会的成员。他是一名高级指挥官，也是其原体荷鲁斯的特别亲密的朋友，在63-19的的平定谈判中被谋杀。他的出现后来被艾瑞巴斯用来欺骗和强迫荷鲁斯走上混沌之路。

### History 历史

Captain Sejanus served as praetor of the Fourth Company and stood at Horus's side during the Luna Wolves' joint undertaking with the Blood Angels to subjugate the Nephilim species on Melchior. He fought with 8,000 legionaries, and he was wounded by a shreikpulse weapon during the campaign.

塞扬努斯连长担任第四连队的裁判官，在影月苍狼与圣血天使联合征服梅尔基奥尔的拿非利种族时，他站在荷鲁斯身边。他与8000名军团士兵并肩作战，在战役中被一件尖啸脉冲武器击伤。

Considered a senior lieutenant to Horus since at least the time the Luna Wolves waged war against the Keylekid species, Sejanus notably became a close comrade to Phosis T'Kar of the Thousand Sons during T'Kar's secondment to his legion. Their bond of brotherhood was renewed during the Ullanor Triumph, which both captains attended as part of their respective primarch's inner circles.

塞扬努斯被认为是荷鲁斯的高级副官，至少在影月苍狼与凯莱基德种族作战的时候，塞扬努斯在塔'卡被借调到他的军团期间，成为了千子弗西斯·塔'卡的亲密伙伴。他们的兄弟情谊在乌兰诺大捷期间得到了重新开始，两位连长都作为各自原体核心集团的一部分参加了这次胜利。

In the wake of the great Triumph, Sejanus commissioned the making of four rings, for himself and the three other members of the Mournival, from a silversmith remembrancer attached to the battle bargeVengeful Spirit. Unfortunately, Sejanus's ring was destroyed, and the other three presumably lost, when he rushed into the remembrancer's shop to aid the smith, whose throat had been fatally crushed by Captain Targost.

在大捷之后，塞扬努斯委托一位在复仇之魂号上的记述者银匠为自己和四王议会的其他三名成员制作了四枚戒指。不幸的是，当塞扬努斯冲进记述者的商店帮助银匠时，他的戒指被摧毁了，其他三枚戒指可能也丢失了，因为银匠的喉咙被塔戈斯特连长致命地压碎了。

A little over a year after the Ullanor Triumph, in the 203rd year of the Great Crusade, Captain Sejanus was given the honour of making the first face-to-face contact with the ruler of the planet later known as Sixty-Three-Nineteen. Unfortunately, a mis-step in diplomacy resulted in the murder of Sejanus and his retinue. The legion considered his death a tragedy, and the manner of it (ambushed and hacked down during a diplomatic treaty) caused particular outrage. Warmaster Horus, who was said to have loved Sejanus like a son and to have campaigned with him across one hundred worlds, ordered that Sejanus's murderers, the Invisibles, be decimated. Sejanus's position in the Mournival was later taken by Captain Garviel Loken.

在乌兰诺大捷一年多后，在大远征的第203年，塞扬努斯连长有幸与后来被称为63/19的行星的统治者进行了第一次面对面的接触。不幸的是，外交上的一个失误导致了塞扬努斯和他的随从被谋杀。军团认为他的死是一场悲剧，其方式（在外交条约期间遭到伏击和击倒）引起了特别的愤怒。据说战帅荷鲁斯像爱儿子一样爱着塞扬努斯，并与他一起在一百个世界里参加战役，他下令杀死杀害塞扬努斯的凶手，无形者。塞扬努斯在四王议会的位置后来被加维尔·洛肯连长取走。

Word BearersChaplain Erebus assumed Hastur Sejanus's form during his coercion of Horus to Chaos during the Warmaster's treatment in the Serpent Lodge on Davin, in an attempt to beguile the Warmaster by presenting his guide in the spirit realm as the shade of a close friend.

在戴文蛇屋对战帅进行治疗期间，怀言者牧师艾瑞巴斯在强迫荷鲁斯堕入混沌的过程中使用了哈斯塔·塞扬努斯的形态，试图通过将他在灵魂领域的引导作为亲密朋友的阴影来欺骗战帅。

### Appearance and Abilities 外貌与能力

Noted as the commander's favourite and a perfect captain, Sejanus was a popular figure in the Luna Wolves. Both an accomplished diplomat and warrior, with a martial record second only to that of First Captain Abaddon's, Sejanus was described as being a particularly handsome and noble man of balanced humours, with Captain Loken going so far as to say that there was no finer figure in Mk. IV plate. He had widely set eyes that glittered silver, and a firm straight nose that mirrored that of the Luna Wolves Primarch Horus. He had very dark skin and a short comb of grey hair running down the center of his scalp.

作为指挥官最喜爱的人和完美连长，塞扬努斯是影月苍狼中一个受欢迎的人物。塞扬努斯既是一位有成就的外交官，也是一位战士，其军事记录仅次于连长阿巴顿，他被描述为一个特别英俊、高贵、体液平衡的人，洛肯连长甚至说，在Mk.IV盔甲中没有比他更优秀的人物了。他那双银色的眼睛炯炯有神，还有一个挺拔笔直的鼻子，和影月苍狼原体荷鲁斯的鼻子一模一样。他的皮肤很暗，头皮中央有一束灰色的短冠。

### Trivia 琐事

 - 

Hastur is the name of a senior, malignant entity in H.P. Lovecraft's Cthulhu mythos whose form is not to be trusted;

 - 

哈斯塔是H.P.洛夫克拉夫特的克苏鲁神话中一个高级、恶意实体的名字，其形态不可信；

 - 

There have been several historical persons named Sejanus, with the preeminent one being Lucius Aelius Sejanus, a close friend of the Emperor Tiberius and commander of his Praetorian Guard.

 - 

历史上有几位名叫塞扬努斯的人，其中最杰出的是卢基乌斯·埃利乌斯·塞扬努斯，他是提比略皇帝的密友，也是他的禁卫军指挥官。

 - 

The name Hastur is most likely also a reference to Hathor, who is the Egyptian Horus' wife.

 - 

哈斯图尔这个名字很可能也是对埃及荷鲁斯的妻子哈索尔的引用。
