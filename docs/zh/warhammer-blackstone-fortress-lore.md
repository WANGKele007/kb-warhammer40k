Blackstone Fortresses (known as Talismans of Vaul by the Eldar) are massive starships created in the distant past to protect against the return of the C'tan. Their exact origins are unclear, though it is suggested they were built by the Old Ones. Six were found by the Imperium in the Gothic Sector around M31, and were used as naval bases, until Mephet'ran the Deceiver discovered their location, and manipulated Abaddon into capturing two, and destroying the other four, during the Gothic War.One of these was given to Huron Blackheart, and the other was used to destroy Cadia during the 13th Black Crusade. Several more Blackstone Fortresses exist across the Galaxy, including one recently discovered in the Western Reaches.

黑石要塞（被灵族称为瓦尔护身符）是在遥远的过去建造的大型星舰，用于防止星神的回归。它们的确切起源尚不清楚，但有人认为它们是由古圣建造的。帝国在M31左右的哥特星区发现了六个，并将其用作海军基地，直到欺骗者墨菲特'兰发现了它们的位置，并在哥特战争期间暗中操纵阿巴顿俘获了两个，摧毁了另外四个。其中一个是送给休伦·黑心的，另一个是在第13次黑色远征期间用来摧毁卡迪亚的。银河系中还有几座黑石要塞，包括最近在西部河段发现的一座。

## Origins 起源

It is believed that the Blackstone Fortresses were created by the Old Ones to combat the C'tan. Though other sources state that they were created by the Eldar before their Fall, in order to destroy the C'tan should they ever return.Eldar Mythology holds that they were created by Vaul, the Eldar smith god, in order to end the War in Heaven and that they contain the souls of Eldar Seers who perished in the war. The Talismans are said to contain Eyes of the Witch, created by the Crone Goddess Morai-Heg, which would allow the slain heroes to channel unfathomable amounts of energy out of the Immaterium to forever banish the Yngir.

据信，黑石要塞是由古圣为对抗星神而建造的。尽管其他消息来源称，他们是灵族在陨落之前创造的，目的是在星神回归的时候摧毁它们。灵族神话认为，它们是由灵族铁匠之神瓦尔创造的，目的是结束天堂战争，它们包含了在战争中丧生的灵族先知的灵魂。据说护身符包含由老妪女神莫莱-赫格创造的女巫之眼，这将使被杀的英雄能够将难以理解的能量从非物质界中引导出来，永远驱逐因吉尔。

Blackstone Fortress 黑石要塞

It is said that during the Necron Revolt, the Blackstone Fortresses were used to shatter the C'tan known as The Void Dragon.

据说在死灵反抗期间，黑石要塞被用来粉碎被称为虚空龙的星神。

## Technology 技术

Built primarily of Noctilith (commonly known as Blackstone), Blackstone Fortresses are planetoid-sized crafts, which generate immense amounts of power even while dormant. Their technology goes far beyond the current knowledge of any species in the galaxy.

黑石堡垒主要由夜石（俗称黑石）建造，是小行星大小的造物，即使在休眠状态下也能产生巨大的能量。他们的技术远远超出了银河系中任何种族的现有知识。

The Blackstone Fortress Will of Eternity fires upon Cadia 黑石要塞永恒意志号向卡迪亚开火

They are equipped extremely large and powerful Warp Cannons known as the Eyes of the Witch. Once fully activated, a Blackstone Fortress is capable of breaking the barrier between realspace and warpspace, creating a beam of pure immaterium to destroy everything in its path. This power can be linked with other Fortresses to create an even more powerful beam, as was evident when Abaddon the Despoiler used two Fortresses to destroy the planet Fularis II, and three to cause the Tarantis star to go supernova. Abaddon was able to remotely control the Blackstone Fortresses with the alien artifacts Hand of Darkness and Eye of Night. Huron Blackheart's Blackstone Fortress Corsair's Keep has a Webway gate at its heart, that the Masque of the Shattered Mirage were able to use. It is later confirmed by the Imperium that it seems each Blackstone Fortress has Webway Gates within it, some large enough to accommodate warships on their own.

他们装备了被称为女巫之眼的巨大而强大的亚空间炮。一旦完全激活，黑石要塞能够打破现实空间和亚空间之间的屏障，创造出一束纯粹的非物质界光束，摧毁其路径上的一切。这种力量可以与其他要塞联系起来，创造出更强大的光束，正如掠夺者阿巴顿使用两座要塞摧毁弗拉利斯二号行星，三座要塞使塔兰蒂斯变成超新星时所证明的那样。阿巴顿能够用异型造物黑暗之手和午夜之眼远程控制黑石要塞。休伦·黑心的黑石要塞海盗堡号的核心有一个网道门户，破碎幻影假面舞会可以使用。帝国后来证实，似乎每个黑石要塞都有网道门户，其中一些足够大，可以单独容纳战舰。

Blackstone Fortresses are powered by a core, the workings of which are not easily understood even to the HeretekTech-Priests of the Dark Mechanicum. At the center of a Blackstone Fortress is a fiery core which resembles a miniature sun, which serves to power the station. Blackstone Fortresses seem to have some sentience of their own, and will help those it deems as worthy allies while defending itself against hostile invaders. When defending itself a Blackstone Fortress can impale opponents with jagged shards that rise up from the floor and walls, rearrange passageways, swallow up intruders in pits, and set groups of Spindle Drones and Ur-Ghuls upon foes.

黑石要塞由一个核心提供动力，即使是黑暗机械教的技术异端技术神甫也不容易理解其工作原理。黑石要塞的中心是一个炽热的核心，就像一个微型太阳，为空间站供能。黑石要塞似乎有自己的感知，会帮助那些它认为值得为盟的人，同时保护自己免受敌对入侵者的攻击。在自卫时，黑石要塞可以用从地板和墙壁上升起的锯齿状碎片刺穿对手，重新安排通道，吞噬坑中的入侵者，并将纺锤无人机和乌尔-古尔斯群设置在敌人身上。

## History 历史

### Imperial Discovery 帝国的发现

Six Blackstone Fortresses were discovered by the Imperium in the Gothic Sector around late M31. Initial studies dated the Fortresses between 17,000 and 300,000 years old, however their exact construction process is unknown. Most of the Fortresses' systems were offline aside from the main power systems. The Adeptus Mechanicus retrofitted their own weapons (the weapons batteries they were given far surpassed the firepower of any ship in the Imperium) and life-support systems to the main power grid, as well as a number of hangars for smaller craft, turning each Fortress into a primary naval base for Battlefleet Gothic.

帝国在M31晚期左右在哥特星区发现了六座黑石要塞。最初的研究表明，这些要塞的历史在17000年至300000年之间，但它们的确切建造过程尚不清楚。除了主能源系统外，要塞的大多数系统都处于离线状态。机械神教改装了他们自己的武器（他们获得的武器阵列远远超过了帝国中任何船只的火力）和主电网的生命支持系统，以及一些用于小型飞行器的机库，将每个要塞变成了哥特战斗舰队的主要海军基地。

Blackstone Fortress 黑石要塞

### Captured by Chaos 被混沌俘获

Through the subtle manipulation of the C'tanMephet'ran the Deceiver, some time in M40, Abaddon the Despoiler learned of the Fortresses, where he was guided by the visions of Zaraphiston to an EldarCrone World. There, he read an ancient prophecy revealing their existence.

在M40的一段时间里，通过星神欺骗者墨菲特'兰的微妙操纵，掠夺者阿巴顿了解了要塞，在那里他被扎拉菲斯顿的幻象引导到了一个灵族老妪世界。在那里，他读到了一个古老的预言，揭示了它们的存在。

The Gothic War was Abaddon's Black Crusade to capture and activate the six Blackstone Fortresses. He succeeded in taking three, but was defeated by the combined might of the Imperium and the Eldar. The Imperial Navy and Angels of Redemption tried to recapture one of the Fortresses, however it self-destructed, along with the three still in the Imperium's hands. The remaining two retreated with Abaddon back into the Eye of Terror.

哥特战争是阿巴顿为夺取并激活六座黑石要塞而发起的黑色远征。他成功地夺取了三个，但被帝国和灵族的联合力量击败了。帝国海军和救赎天使试图夺回其中一座要塞，但它与帝国手中的三座要塞一起自毁。剩下的两座撤退，阿巴顿回到了恐惧之眼。

### 13th Black Crusade 第十三次黑色远征

The two Chaos fortresses would reappear, 800 years later, supporting Typhus and his Plaguefleet in Abaddon's 13th Black Crusade. They would later split up, each sent to reinforce the attacks on Cadia and Kasr Partox. The Blackstone assaulting Cadia, the Will of Eternity, nearly destroyed the planet but was itself crippled by the Phalanx after a Space Wolves boarding party disabled its shields. The debris of the *Will of Eternity* was later redirected into Cadia by Abaddon, which shattered the planet.

800年后，这两座混沌要塞再次出现，在阿巴顿的第13次黑色远征中支援泰丰斯和他的瘟疫舰队。他们后来分道扬镳，各自被派去增援对卡迪亚和帕托克斯要塞的袭击。攻击卡迪亚的黑色要塞，永恒意志号，几乎摧毁了这个行星，但在太空野狼跳帮队摧毁了它的盾牌后，它自己也被山阵号瘫痪了。永恒意志号的碎片后来被阿巴顿重新导向卡迪亚，摧毁了这个行星。

The Will of Eternity falls to Cadia 永恒意志号坠落卡迪亚

### Huron Blackheart 休伦·黑心

In the wake of the Badab War, Abaddon gave one of the two Fortresses as a gift to Huron Blackheart, to secure the support of the Red Corsairs for his plans, and as a demonstration of his power. In truth, this Blackstone Fortress, later known as Corsair's Keep, had been rendered dangerous as Abaddon's attempts to control it via Daemonic possession drove the equivalent of a Machine Spirit insane. This Fortress was later used to house the reborn Primarch Roboute Guilliman and his followers when they were captured during the Terran Crusade. It became the site of fierce fighting when Cypher and Sylandri Veilwalker freed Guilliman and his men.

巴达布战争后，阿巴顿将两座要塞中的一座作为礼物送给休伦·黑心，以确保红海盗对他的计划的支持，并展示他的力量。事实上，这座黑石要塞，后来被称为海盗堡，已经变得危险，因为阿巴顿试图通过恶魔的占据来控制它，这相当于一个疯狂的机魂。这座堡垒后来被用来安置重生的原体罗伯特·基里曼和他的追随者，当时他们在泰拉远征期间被俘。当塞弗和西兰德里·帷幕行者释放基里曼和他的部下时，这里成为了激烈战斗的场所。

Later, Harlequins of the Masque of the Shattered Mirage launched a surprise attack on the Red Corsairs Blackstone Fortress, aided by a rogue faction from Yme-Loc. The Eldar seek to capture and control this ancient "Talisman of Vaul".

后来，在耶姆-洛克的一个离群派系的帮助下，破碎幻影假面舞会的丑角对红海盗黑石要塞发动了突然袭击。灵族试图夺取并控制这个古老的“瓦尔护身符”。

### Indomitus Crusade 不屈远征

During the Indomitus Crusade, a Blackstone Fortress was used as the base of operations for the mysterious Black Legion organisation dubbed the Hand of Abaddon. During the Battle of the Stygius Gilt, Imperial forces led by InquisitorRostov and LieutenantFerren Areios boarded the Fortress. During the battle, its core was destroyed by Tharador Yheng to prevent its operational capture by the Imperials. It is currently being salvaged by Imperial-allied elements of the Leagues of Votann.

不屈远征期间，黑石要塞被用作神秘的黑色军团组织阿巴顿之手的行动基地。在镀金冥河之战中，由审判官罗斯托夫和副官费伦·阿瑞奥斯率领的帝国部队跳帮了要塞。在战斗中，塔拉多·伊亨摧毁了其核心，以防止其被帝国俘获。目前，它正被帝国盟友的沃坦联盟成员抢救。

History 历史

In the 3rd edition lore, the Blackstone Fortresses were created by the Eldar to guard against the C'tan. However, after 5th Edition, the C'tan were relegated to being shattered by the Necrons before the Great Sleep and it is said they used Blackstone Fortresses to defeat the likes of the Void Dragon. Games Workshop has since suggested they were made by the Old Ones.

在第三版传说中，黑石要塞是由灵族建造的，用来防御星神。然而，在第五版之后，星神在大休眠之前被死灵击碎，据说他们用黑石要塞击败了虚空龙像这样的。Games Workshop后来表示，它们是由古圣制作的。
