Irtanathep is an ancient Necron world. It is trapped on the cusp of the event horizon of a black hole named Calligan's Maw that used to be the planet's local star.

伊尔塔纳瑟普是一个古老的死灵世界。它被困在一个名为卡利根之喉的黑洞事件视界的交点，该黑洞曾经是这颗行星的本地恒星。

## History 历史

During the War in Heaven, the system's star was collapsed by weapons of near unimaginable power by the enemies of the Necrons. The black hole wasn't created naturally; the star was killed. This is what created the black hole that would be named Calligan's Maw aeons later. The world was of high significance to the Necrons due to it being a pylon control nexus. ArchmagosDominusBelisarius Cawl speculates that it was attacked at some point in order to stop its operation, and in the process the star was destroyed.

在天堂战争期间，该星系的恒星被死灵的敌人以近乎难以想象的力量摧毁。黑洞不是自然形成的；恒星被杀了。这创造了后来被命名为卡利根之喉的黑洞。这个世界对死灵来说意义重大，因为它是一个方尖碑控制枢纽。统御大贤者贝利撒留·考尔推测，它在某个时候遭到了攻击，以阻止其运作，在这个过程中，这颗恒星被摧毁了。

Due to the time dilation caused by the gravity, a second lasts an aeon on the world. Irtanathep's inhabitants have been trapped in the moment of their star's destruction for uncounted aeons and will be continue to be trapped in that moment for uncountable more. The pylon network control centre is also still working, untouched by time.

由于重力引起的时间膨胀，一秒在世界上持续了一个世纪。伊尔塔纳瑟普的居民已经被困在他们的恒星毁灭的那一刻无数年了，并将继续被困在那一刻。方尖碑网络控制中心仍在工作，没有受到时间的影响。
