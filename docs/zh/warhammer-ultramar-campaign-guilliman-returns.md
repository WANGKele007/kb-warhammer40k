### Overview 概述

Shortly after the destruction of Cadia in the Thirteenth Black Crusade, ChaosWarmasterAbaddon the Despoiler received a disturbing prophecy from his Sorcerers, who informed him that the Tech-PriestBelisarius Cawl had a device of profound importance that could turn the tide of the war. The Sorcerers also informed Abaddon that Cawl was among the Imperial refugees from Cadia, and had been escorted into the Webway by the Eldar with Macragge as their destination. Hoping to halt Cawl's plan, Abaddon sent a fleet of Chaos Space Marines to Ultramar.

在第十三次黑色远征中摧毁卡迪亚后不久，混沌战帅掠夺者阿巴顿收到了他的巫师的一个令人不安的预言，他们告诉他，技术神甫贝利萨留·考尔有一个非常重要的装置，可以扭转战争的局势。巫师还告诉阿巴顿，考尔是来自卡迪亚的帝国逃亡者之一，被灵族护送入网道，目的地是马库拉格。为了阻止考尔的计划，阿巴顿向奥特拉玛派遣了一支混沌星际战士舰队。

The Imperial survivors of the 13th Black Crusade, led by Saint Celestine, MarshalAmalrich, and InquisitorGreyfax, first emerged from the Webway along with their Eldar allies on the Shrine World of Laphis in the Macragge System. Despite the uneasy alliance between the Imperials and Eldar, necessity saw them fight together effectively and soon the area around the Webway Portal was secured from the Black Legion, which was taken by surprise. The newly arrived forces then moved on a besieged Ultramarines fortress. After relieving the Ultramarine garrison within the fortress from Heldrakes, Saint Celestine successfully convinced the Astartes to allow them passage to Macragge itself as part of Cawl's plan. However, all of the Ynnari besides Yvraine and the Visarch went back into the Webway, their mission accomplished for now.

由圣塞勒斯汀、元帅阿马尔里奇和审判官格雷法克斯领导的第13次黑色远征的帝国幸存者，首先与他们的灵族盟友一起从网道出现在马库拉格星系中的拉皮斯圣地世界。尽管帝国人和灵族之间的联盟并不稳定，但必要性使他们有效地并肩作战，很快网道传送门周围的地区就被黑色军团控制住了，这让人大吃一惊。新抵达的部队随后向被围困的极限战士要塞进发。圣塞勒斯汀从地狱飞龙手中解救了要塞内的极限战士驻军后，成功地说服了阿斯塔特允许他们前往马库拉格，这是考尔计划的一部分。然而，除了伊芙蕾妮和维萨奇之外，所有的死神军都回到了网道，他们的任务暂时完成了。

Arriving at Macragge via Strike Cruiser, the Imperial forces found the capital of Ultramar under full-scale Chaos invasion. The Ultramar Defence Fleet was desperately engaging in an orbital battle against the Black Legion, Iron Warriors, The Purge, and Night Lords. After fighting their way through the Chaos blockade, the Imperial forces landed on Macragge and moved on the Fortress of Hera, which was now besieged. At Hera they found Ultramarines Chapter MasterMarneus Calgar and the other senior officers of the Chapter. Cawl revealed that he needed to see the comatose body of PrimarchRoboute Guilliman, provoking outrage among the Ultramarines. However, Cawl revealed that he was in fact over 10,000 years old and has been working on a way to bring back Guilliman on the orders of the Primarch himself shortly before his wounding by Fulgrim. Cawl revealed a new suit of Power Armour for Guilliman that could regenerate his wounds, as well as a new weapon for the Primarch. With Yvraine at his side, it was now possible for Cawl to fully restore Guilliman. With the backing of LibrarianTigurius the Ultramarines eventually relented and allowed Cawl and his entourage into the Shrine of Guilliman.

帝国部队通过打击巡洋舰抵达马库拉格，在全面的混沌入侵下找到了奥特拉玛的首都。奥特拉玛防御舰队正在拼命地与黑色军团、钢铁勇士、净世疫军和午夜领主进行轨道战。在穿越混沌封锁后，帝国部队在马库拉格登陆，向赫拉要塞进发，该要塞现已被围困。在赫拉要塞，他们找到了极限战士战团长马涅乌斯·卡尔加和战团的其他高级军官。考尔透露，他需要看到原体罗伯特·基里曼昏迷的身体，这引起了极限战士的愤怒。然而，考尔透露，他实际上已经超过10000岁了，在基里曼被福格瑞姆击伤前不久，他一直在努力按照原体本人的命令将其带回。考尔展示了一套新的为基里曼的动力盔甲，可以再生他的伤口，以及为原体提供的一种新武器。在伊芙蕾妮的陪伴下，考尔现在有可能完全恢复基里曼。在智库狄格里斯的支持下，极限战士最终让步，允许考尔和他的随行人员进入基里曼圣地。

As Cawl began his work, the Black Legion made one final desperate assault on the Shrine to try and prevent Guilliman's return. Calgar, Saint Celestine, and the Grey Knights3rd Brotherhood led by Grand MasterVoldus led the defence and bought Cawl the time he needed. Ten Dreadclaws smashed through the shrine, disgorging a hundred Black Legion Chaos Space Marines. Soon enough the Imperial defenders were all overwhelmed by this force, but as all seemed lost Cawl and the others were successful in their work and the restored Roboute Guilliman entered the fray.

当考尔开始工作时，黑色军团对圣地进行了最后一次不顾一切的攻击，试图阻止基里曼的回归。卡尔加、圣塞勒斯汀和大导师沃尔达斯领导的灰骑士第三兄弟会领导了防御，并为考尔争取到了他需要的时间。十个恐惧爪砸向圣地，倾泻了一百名黑色军团混沌星际战士。很快，帝国的守军被这支部队压倒了，但当一切似乎都失去了时，考尔和其他人都成功地完成了他们的工作，恢复的罗伯特·基里曼加入了战斗。

Faced with the onslaught of the Primarch, the Chaos invaders were completely broken while the Imperial defenders were reinvigorated by his presence. Devastated by the Primarch's onslaught, the Black Legion was forced into a retreat. Enraged at the desecration of his homeworld, Guilliman made sure none of the traitorous Marines left the Shrine alive.

面对原体的猛攻，混沌入侵者被彻底击败，而帝国守军则因他的存在而重新振作。黑色军团被原体的猛攻摧毁，被迫撤退。基里曼对自己的家园世界被亵渎感到愤怒，他确保没有一名叛徒战士活着离开圣地。

### Aftermath 余波

In the aftermath of the battle Roboute Guilliman led a general counterattack across Ultramar, devastating the Chaos invaders. With their mission now a failure, the remaining Chaos fleet fled Ultramar. Once at peace Guilliman adjusted to the reality of his father's Imperium 10,000 years later, now an empire of decay and superstition. After being fully briefed by his Ultramarines Guilliman declared that he will journey to Terra, beginning the Terran Crusade.

战斗结束后，罗伯特·基里曼领导了奥特拉玛的全面反击，摧毁了混沌入侵者。由于任务失败，剩余的混沌舰队逃离了奥特拉玛。在1万年后，基里曼安静地适应了他父亲的帝国的现实，现在这个帝国已经腐朽和迷信。在听取了他的极限战士的全面简报后，基里曼宣布他将前往泰拉，开始泰拉远征。

Shortly after the defeat of the Black Legion in Ultramar, the realm again would come under Chaos invasion in the Plague Wars.

在奥特拉玛内击败黑色军团后不久，该领域将再次在瘟疫战争中受到混沌入侵。
