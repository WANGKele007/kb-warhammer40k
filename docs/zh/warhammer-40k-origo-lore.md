Origo was an Imperium world.

欧瑞格是一个帝国世界。

It was blockaded at the direct request of its Segmentum High Command when a war engulfed its system in 701.M36. The blockade, composed of a mixed force of Space MarinesChapters formed around the RaptorsFifth Company, stood firm until the war was won by the Imperium a decade later. However, when the blockade was finally lifted, it was revealed that Origo had been scoured clean of life and no evidence of its once teeming population ever existing could be found.

701.M36，一场战争吞噬了它的星系，应其星域至高指挥部的直接要求，它被封锁了。由围绕猛禽第五连队组建的星际战士战团的混合部队组成的封锁一直持续到十年后帝国赢得战争。然而，当封锁最终解除时，据透露，欧瑞格已经被彻底清除了生命，没有证据表明曾经有大量人口存在。

What befell Origo remains a mystery to this day and several dozen highly ranked Lords of the Inquisition, in particular those of the Ordo Xenos, have vanished attempting to determine the truth.

欧瑞格的遭遇至今仍是一个谜，几十位审判庭的高级领主，特别是攘外修会的，在试图确定真相时消失了。
