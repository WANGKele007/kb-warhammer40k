A Sentinel is a bipedal, all-terrain walker used by the Imperial Guard for a number of missions including reconnaissance and search-and-destroy.

哨兵是一种双足全地形步行者，被帝国卫队用于侦察、搜索和摧毁等多项任务。

Sentinel firing a Lascannon 哨兵发射激光炮

## History 历史

When the nascent Imperium reunited with the Knight Worlds during the Great Crusade, they found that, in addition to the Knights used in battle by the nobility, there were also lesser unarmed frames used by Drovers – the non-noble herdsmen who tended the fields and estates. One such popular and tested frame, the Drover suit, would be armed and adapted into the Sentinel widely used by the Imperial Army and Imperial Guard.

当新生的帝国在大远征期间与骑士世界重逢时，他们发现，除了贵族在战斗中使用的骑士外，农奴（照料田地和庄园的非贵族牧民）也使用了较少的非武装构架。其中一种流行且经过测试的构架，牧人机甲，被武装并改装成帝国军队和帝国卫队广泛使用的哨兵。

## Design 设计

The Sentinel achieves movement with a pair of articulated legs, which allow for both maneuverability in dense environments and bursts of speed on open terrain. The walker is even capable of spinning around on one leg. Sentinels can vary in height, though are usually between seventeen and eighteen feet tall. They are fitted with advanced gyro-stabilizers to improve their balance, and their powerplants include noise-reduction technology for stealthy operation. Though it cannot move with the speed of the wheeled vehicle, the Sentinel can run, much like a man. By condensing its leg pistons and lowering the cockpit, the Sentinel is even able to jump while moving at full speed, continuing to fire its weapon system in the process. In case the walker drops ten feet, the fall is absorbed by the Sentinel's hydromechanics.

哨兵通过一对铰接腿实现运动，这既允许在密集环境中的机动性，也允许在开阔地形上的爆发速度。步行者甚至能够单腿旋转。哨兵的高度可以变化，但通常在17到18英尺之间。它们配备了先进的陀螺仪稳定装置以提高平衡性，其动力装置包括用于隐形操作的降噪技术。尽管哨兵无法以轮式载具的速度移动，但它可以像人一样奔跑。通过压缩腿部活塞并降低驾驶舱，哨兵甚至可以在全速行驶时跳跃，并在此过程中继续发射武器系统。如果步行者落下十英尺，下落会被哨兵的流体力学吸收。

Visually, Sentinels have been observed crouching to lower their height, or kneeling entirely for ease of transport, as well as hyper-extending their legs and increasing their height:

在视觉上，观察到哨兵蹲下以降低高度，或完全跪下以便于运输，以及过度伸展双腿和增加身高：

Besides the addition of more armour or different weaponry, Sentinels can be modified in a multitude of ways. The most common include adding Camo Netting, a searchlight, smoke launchers, and/or a Hunter-Killer Missile. Other adaptations can be made depending on where the Sentinel operates, such as servo-driven claw spikes for a better grip when walking over glacial planes.

除了增加更多的盔甲或不同的武器外，哨兵还可以通过多种方式进行修改。最常见的包括添加迷彩网、探照灯、烟雾发射器和/或猎人杀手导弹。根据哨兵的操作地点，可以进行其他调整，例如伺服驱动的爪钉，以便在冰川平面上行走时更好地抓握。

## Battlefield Role 战场角色

The bipedal locomotion afforded by the Sentinel gives it a unique ability to advance into terrain that other armoured vehicles with tracks could not possibly hope to achieve. This allows it to stalk through dense jungles or urban ruins to locate and wipe out pockets of enemy resistance, though their light armour and exposed crew compartment leaves them vulnerable to even small-arms fire. Armoured Sentinels which have an enclosed cockpit and heavier weapons however are used more as roving hunter-killer units, seeking out and destroying enemy tanks where they can find them.

哨兵提供的双足运动使其具有独特的能力，可以进入其他有履带的装甲载具不可能到达的地形。这使得它能够在茂密的丛林或城市废墟中潜进，定位并消灭敌人的抵抗，尽管它们的轻甲和暴露的乘员舱使它们甚至容易受到轻武器的攻击。然而，装甲哨兵有一个封闭的驾驶舱和更重的武器，更多地被用作巡回猎人杀手单位，寻找并摧毁敌方坦克。

Sentinels in action 行动中的哨兵

Because Sentinels typically operate far ahead of the main Imperial force, they tend to operate autonomously in squadrons of one to three Sentinels or as part of a larger recon troop. As such, pilots often use their own initiative with regard to their orders. Many pilots have a history as insubordinate mavericks, although due to their invaluable support in scouting this is usually overlooked. When they are not used in a scouting role they are deployed on the flanks of the army to harry enemy formations or act as mobile anti-armour squadrons.

由于哨兵通常在帝国主力部队之前行动，他们倾向于在一到三个哨兵的中队中自主行动，或者作为更大侦察部队的一部分。因此，驾驶员经常主动执行命令。许多驾驶员都有作为反抗异见者的历史，尽管由于他们在侦察中的宝贵支援，这通常会被忽视。当它们不用于侦察任务时，它们被部署在军队的侧翼，以骚扰敌方编队或充当机动反装甲中队。

The Sentinel is the workhorse of virtually every Imperial Guard formation. It is even the exception to the rule that all Armoured Companies include only tanks.

哨兵几乎是每个帝国卫队编队的耐用机器。这甚至是所有装甲连队只包括坦克的规则的例外。

## Armament & Variations 军备&变体

The weaponry of the Sentinel varies greatly depending on variation and role, but typical armament includes a single heavy weapon that is most often a Multi-Laser. These can be replaced with an Autocannon, Missile Launcher, Plasma Cannon, or Heavy Flamers. In addition to this weapon, some Sentinels also sport a Hunter-Killer Missile and a large Sentinel Chainsaw.

哨兵的武器因变化和角色而异，但典型的武器包括一种重型武器，通常是多管激光。可以用自动炮、导弹发射器、等离子炮或重型火焰枪代替。除了这种武器，一些哨兵还携带猎人杀手导弹和大型哨兵链锯。

Guardsmen and Sentinel pilot enjoying a hot beverage 卫兵和哨兵驾驶员正在享用热饮

Generally speaking, two significant categories of Sentinel are the Scout Sentinel, characterized by its open top configuration. and the Armoured Sentinel, characterized by its fully enclosed cockpit. Beyond this, there are many different planetary specific patterns - many of which are commonly associated with a weapons configuration. The ubiquitous Mars Pattern is armed with a multi-laser, the Catachan Pattern is known for its heavy flamer, the Cadian Pattern for its autocannon, and the Armageddon Pattern for its lascannon and fully enclosed cockpit.

一般来说，哨兵的两个重要类别是侦察哨兵，其特征是顶部开放的结构。以及装甲哨兵，其特点是驾驶舱完全封闭。除此之外，还有许多不同的行星特定型号，其中许多通常与武器配置有关。无处不在的火星型配备了多管激光，卡塔昌型以其重型火焰枪而闻名，卡迪亚型以其自动炮而闻名，阿玛吉多顿型以其激光炮和全封闭驾驶舱而闻名。

### Scout Sentinels 侦查哨兵

The Scout Sentinel is characterised by its open-topped configuration its default armament being a Multi-laser which it may switch out for a Heavy Flamer, Autocannon, Lascannon, Missile Launcher or Multiple Rocket Pod. Open cockpits and augur devices enable the pilots of Scout Sentinels to survey the enemy with ease, while their lighter armour and enhanced gyro-stabilisers allow them to swiftly traverse the roughest terrain and bring heavy weapons to bear from unanticipated angles. Many examples of them surround the drivers compartment with a roll cage Should the multi-laser be damaged or destroyed, the driver is able to fire a sidearm such as a laspistol instead. Veterans become known as Long-Range Scouts and have a reputation for being taciturn loners or insubordinate mavericks. However, the skills they use to successfully infiltrate enemy territory, with their often-camouflaged Sentinels, are second to none:

侦察哨兵的特点是其顶部开放的配置，其默认武器是多管激光，它可以切换为重型火焰枪、自动炮、激光炮、导弹发射器或多管火箭舱。开放式驾驶舱和鸟卜仪装置使侦察哨兵的驾驶员能够轻松地侦察敌人，而他们较轻的装甲和增强的陀螺仪稳定装置使他们能够迅速穿越最崎岖的地形，并从意想不到的角度携带重型武器。其中许多例子都是用防滚架围绕驾驶室。如果多管激光损坏或毁坏，驾驶员可以发射随身携带的激光手枪。老兵被称为远程侦察兵，并以沉默寡言的孤独者或不服从命令的特立独行者而闻名。然而，他们用伪装的哨兵成功渗透敌方领土的技能是首屈一指的：

Cadian Sentinel 卡迪亚哨兵

The Cadian Pattern Sentinel produced on Cadia is armed with an Autocannon, making it ideal for destroying armoured infantry and light-to-medium vehicles. It is upgraded with extra armour in order to better protect the pilot. Visibly, based on the vehicle's appearance, it is distinct by its partially surrounded cockpit than the more typical roll-cage seen on other scout sentinels, however it is still open-topped unlike an Armoured Sentinel.

在卡迪亚生产的卡迪亚型哨兵配备了自动炮，使其成为摧毁装甲步兵和轻型至中型载具的理想选择。它升级了额外的装甲，以更好地保护驾驶员。显然，根据载具的外观，它的驾驶舱部分被包围，与其他侦察哨兵上看到的更典型的防滚架不同，但它仍然是敞篷的，不像装甲哨兵。

Catachan Sentinel 卡塔昌哨兵

The Catachan Pattern Sentinel, also called Death World Sentinels originated on Catachan and is often employed by other regiments native to Death Worlds. They are equipped with a Heavy Flamer and are built with Rough Terrain Modification upgrades as standard, with the most common type of these being a Sentinel Chainsaw attachment. The crews of these Deathworld Sentinels quickly learn how useful the chainsaw can be in close combat. However, they are not built as scouts, and do not provide the same sort of scouting capability as normal Scout Sentinels.

卡塔昌型哨兵，也称为死亡世界哨兵，起源于卡塔昌，经常被死亡世界本土的其他兵团使用。它们配备了重型火焰枪，并标配了崎岖地形改造升级，其中最常见的类型是哨兵链锯附件。这些死亡世界哨兵的乘员们很快就了解到链锯在近战中是多么有用。然而，它们不是作为侦察兵建造的，也不能提供与普通侦察哨兵相同的侦察能力。

Catachan Sentinel 卡塔昌哨兵

Due to the impassable terrain they fight in, Death World Veterans use few to no other vehicles than this pattern of Sentinel, using them as hunter-killer units. They are often seen with Camo Netting modifications as well.

由于他们在无法通行的地形中作战，死亡世界老兵除了这种哨兵型号外，几乎不使用其他载具，而是将其用作猎人杀手单位。他们也经常看到迷彩网的修改。

Mars Pattern Sentinel 火星型哨兵

The Mars Pattern Sentinel carries a Multi-Laser, making it effective against infantry and light vehicles. It is a ubiquitous pattern throughout the Imperium.

火星型哨兵携带多管激光，使其对步兵和轻型载具有效。这是整个帝国中无处不在的型号。

Tallarn Sentinel 塔兰哨兵

Tallarn Pattern Sentinels as used by Tallarn Desert Raiders and similar regiments is ideal for fighting over open desert terrain. Its typical Multi-laser weapon system is ideal for fighting infantry and light vehicles. It has wider based feet which allow it to spread its weight over a larger area, preventing it from sinking into the sand, and includes sand filters to prevent the power-plant from being clogged by fine particles. When operating in desert conditions, some Sentinel pilots make their own modifications, from simple adjustments like adding a Tarpaulin roof to the cockpit to shade the driver from the sun. While these improve the Sentinels performance in a desert environment, they do little to affect its battlefield performance.

塔兰沙漠突袭者和类似兵团使用的塔兰型哨兵非常适合在开阔的沙漠地形上作战。其典型的多管激光武器系统非常适合与步兵和轻型载具作战。它有更宽的基脚，可以将重量分散在更大的区域，防止它沉入沙子中，并包括沙尘过滤器，以防止能量发生器被细颗粒堵塞。在沙漠条件下操作时，一些哨兵驾驶员会进行自己的修改，从简单的调整，比如在驾驶舱上增加防水布顶，为驾驶员遮挡阳光。虽然这些可以提高哨兵在沙漠环境中的性能，但对其战场性能几乎没有影响。

Support Sentinel 支援哨兵

The Support Sentinel is a modification of the standard Sentinel, carrying heavy supporting firepower for Sentinel Companies as well as Infantry Companies and Infantry Platoons. They provide immediate, front line fire power that does not not have the command and control restrictions of typical Imperial heavy artillery.

支援哨兵是标准哨兵的改进型，为哨兵连队以及步兵连队和步兵排提供重型支援火力。它们提供即时的前线火力，不受典型帝国重型火炮的指挥和控制限制。

The most common variant is armed with a Multiple Rocket Pod. It is used for suppressing fire and anti-infantry saturation fire, the pod firing multiple small frag rockets, highly effective against massed enemy infantry such as Orkmobz or swarms of TyranidGaunts. The second type of Support Sentinel is armed with a missile launcher, and is equipped with specialized targeting equipment, allowing it to fire indirectly as mobile light artillery.

最常见的变体配备了多管火箭舱。它用于压制火力和反步兵饱和火力，吊舱发射多枚小型破片火箭，对欧克暴徒或泰伦兵虫等密集的敌方步兵非常有效。第二种类型的支援哨兵配备了导弹发射器，并配备了专门的瞄准设备，使其能够作为移动轻型火炮间接射击。

Support Sentinel with Missile Launcher 装备导弹发射器的支援哨兵

The Support Sentinel is not intended as a scout vehicle, and does not provide the same scouting capabilities as a proper Scout Sentinel. Support Sentinels are not common, and each Sentinel Company is lucky if it contains one or two squadrons of this variant. Sentinel commanders value them highly for the extra firepower they give. As wella s changes to the weapons, the Support Sentinel includes many other modifications. The pilot is protected from the missile's jet-wash by an armoured screen around his cockpit. It is also equipped with extra range-finder and target acquisition systems, mounted under the hull where typically the standard Sentinel's weapons would be.

支援哨兵不是侦察载具，也不能提供与正式侦察哨兵相同的侦察能力。支援哨兵并不常见，如果每个哨兵连队都有一两个这种变种的中队，那就很幸运了。哨兵指挥官高度重视他们提供的额外火力。除了武器的变化，支援哨兵还包括许多其他修改。驾驶舱周围的装甲隔板保护驾驶员免受导弹的喷流的冲刷。它还配备了额外的测距仪和目标捕获系统，安装在车体下方，通常是标准哨兵武器的所在地。

Elysian Sentinel 艾丽西亚哨兵

The Elysian Pattern Sentinel or Drop Sentinel is a variant of the Standard Sentinel, equipped with either a Heavy Bolter or Multi-Melta, and will have modifications made to its power plant and cab to allow it to fit inside a Valkyrie once its legs are folded away. The Drop Sentinel can be launched from the Valkyrie and landed via Grav-chutes alongside the rest of the drop troops. They provide the Elysian Drop Troops, who lack many of the Imperial Guard's heavier weapons, with vital mobile fire support and anti-tank firepower. The Elysians will utilized them in direct fire roles to destroy heavy opposition during an assault.

艾丽西亚型哨兵或空降哨兵是标准哨兵的变体，配备重型爆矢枪或多管热熔，并对其动力装置和驾驶室进行修改，使其在腿折叠后能够安置在女武神内。空降哨兵可以从女武神发射，并与其他空降部队一起通过重力伞降落。他们为缺乏帝国卫队许多重型武器的艾丽西亚空降部队提供了重要的机动火力支援和反坦克火力。艾丽西亚人利用它们进行直接射击，在进攻中摧毁重型对手。

Elysian Sentinel 艾丽西亚哨兵

Unlike other patterns, Drop Sentinels are generally not used as scouts, instead filling the role of mobile heavy support for drop regiments that otherwise lack ground-based armoured vehicles or heavy weapons. ### Armoured Sentinels 装甲哨兵

Armoured Sentinels are modified with additional protective panelling and plating for durability, being built with extra armour by default. This makes them excellent front-line hunters. Armoured Sentinels have recoil compensators in place of the additional gyro-stabilizers, allowing them to them to fire heavier weapons. Sensor arrays and auspex systems are replaced with additional power cells and cooling systems, allowing the Armoured Sentinel to fulfill its role ass a mobile heavy weapons platform. Armoured Sentinels are used as roving hunter-killer units - search and destroy teams which stalk enemy battle tanks and eliminate them with practiced efficiency. They are able to carry heavy weapons into war zones too dense for battle tanks, and excel in destroying vehicles, fortified bunkers, and xenos monstrosities as mobile hunter-killers. They are typically fielded in squadrons of one to three. The default weapon of an Armoured Sentinel is a multi-laser, which can be switched out for a variety of other weapons including heavy flamers, autocannons, lascannons, Missile Launchers, Multiple Rocket Pods, and Plasma cannons.

装甲哨兵经过修改，增加了额外的嵌板和镀层，以提高耐用性，默认情况下会使用额外装甲。这使他们成为优秀的前线猎手。装甲哨兵有反冲补偿器来代替额外的陀螺仪稳定器，使他们能够发射更重的武器。传感器阵列和鸟卜仪系统被额外的电池和冷却系统所取代，使装甲哨兵能够发挥其作为移动重型武器平台的作用。装甲哨兵被用作巡回的猎人杀手单位 — 搜索和摧毁小队，跟踪敌方战斗坦克，并以熟练的效率将其消灭。他们能够将重型武器携带到对战斗坦克来说过于密集的战区，并擅长作为移动猎人杀手摧毁载具、强化掩体和异型怪物。它们通常以一到三个中队的形式部署。装甲哨兵的默认武器是多管激光，可以切换为各种其他武器，包括重型火焰枪、自动炮、激光炮、导弹发射器、多管火箭舱和等离子炮。

### Miniature Development History 微缩模型发展历史

 - 

The very first Sentinel would be released in 1991, designed by Michael Perry alongside rules and lore written by Graeme Davies for use in Warhammer 40,000: Rogue Trader with a painted example by Darren Mathews. It was armed with a multi-laser and both the model's design and its lore/rules would specify that this was mounted on the vehicle in a swivel. It would see use throughout 1st Edition and entering into 2nd Edition.

 - 

第一个哨兵于1991年发布，由迈克尔·佩里设计，与格雷姆·戴维斯编写的规则和知识一起用于《战锤40000：行商浪人》，并附有达伦·马修斯的涂装示例。它配备了多管激光，模型的设计和知识/规则都规定它以旋转的方式安装在载具上。它在第1版和第2版中使用。

 - 

Codex: Imperial Guard (2nd Edition) would be released in 1995, along with datafax rules for a Sentinel armed with an Assault Cannon, rather than the Multi-Laser of 1st Edition However, no new model was released at this time, necessitating that players continued to use their 1st Edition models (or other proxies). In 1998, White Dwarf 222 (UK) would unveil a new Sentinel Model, designed by Norman Swales and Michael Perry alongside an article written by Rick Priestley The Praetorian Guard would obtain their own specific blister pack of this model, with the pilot piece being a Praetorian soldier. These examples would be used primarily in 2nd Edition, though its run would be relatively short lived.

 - 

《圣典：帝国卫队》（第2版）将1995年发布，同时发布的还有配备突击炮的哨兵的数据传真规则，而不是第1版的多管激光。然而，当时没有发布新型号，玩家必须继续使用第1版型号（或其他代替）。1998年，白矮人第222期（英国）推出一款新的哨兵模型，该模型由诺曼·斯沃斯和迈克尔·佩里设计，并附有里克·普里斯特利撰写的一篇文章。忠诚禁卫获得他们自己的这种模型的特定泡罩包装，驾驶员是一名禁卫士兵。这些实例主要用于第二版，尽管其使用时间相对较短。

 - 

On October 1999, White Dwarf 239 (UK) would announce the release of the 3rd Edition Imperial Guard Codex. The 3rd Edition IG codex would arrive with newer sentinel artwork drawn by Karl Kopinski and rules for a Sentinel, armed once again with a multi-laser. This model would be unveiled in March 2000 in White Dwarf 243 (UK) with a detailed article in April of White Dwarf 244 (UK) titled Making a Sentinel - showcasing the design team and process for creating the newer Sentinel Model. Concept art for the newer model was drawn by Jes Goodwin, while the final product included involvement with Tim Adcock, Michael Perry, Alan Perry, and Colin Dixon. Michael and Alan did the pilot/driver model. September 2000 would also see the unveiling of a new Steel Legion/Armorued Sentinel designed by Tim Adcock in White Dwarf 249 (UK).
