Mycetic Spores are large, living shells used to transport Tyranid creatures from Hive Ships to a planet's surface. They could be considered the Tyranid equivalent of an Imperialdrop pod.

菌荚孢子是一种大型的活外壳，用于将泰伦生物从虫巢舰运送到行星表面。它们可以被认为是泰伦的帝国空投舱。

## Description 描述

Mycetic spores are designed to survive the stresses of orbital insertion and to protect their cargo from impact with the planet's surface. Once through the upper atmosphere, the spores alter their shape to slow descent. Long trailing tendrils are used to create drag, while liquid filled pustules will cushion the shock of impact. The spores themselves perish soon after impact, having safely released a brood of Tyranid creatures, most commonly Hormagaunts or Genestealers but a spore can contain anything from small Rippers to a hulking Carnifex.

菌荚孢子的设计是为了在轨道空投的压力下存活下来，并保护它们的货物免受行星表面的撞击。一旦穿过高层大气，孢子就会改变形状，缓慢下降。长拖尾卷须用于产生阻力，而充满液体的脓疱将缓冲冲击的震荡。孢子本身在撞击后很快就会死亡，安全地释放出一群泰伦生物，最常见的是刀虫或基因窃取者，但孢子中可以包含从小型撕裂者到笨重刽子手的任何东西。

Mycetic Spore 菌荚孢子

In the initial stages of a Tyranid planetary assault, waves containing thousands of mycetic spores are launched into the planet's atmosphere by orbiting Hive Ships. They are also used to reinforce key areas of the battlefield, or to protect creatures from incoming fire until they reach point-blank range with the enemy. Larger organisms are transported at a later stage of the invasion when the ships have entered a low orbit and are mopping up any resistance. Mycetic spores are also used as boarding vessels as well.

在泰伦行星攻击的初始阶段，含有数千个菌荚孢子的浪潮通过绕轨道运行的虫巢舰发射到行星的大气层中。它们还用于加强战场的关键区域，或保护生物免受来袭火力的攻击，直到它们与敌人近距离接触。在入侵的后期，当船只进入低轨并清除任何抵抗时，较大的生物体会被运输。菌荚孢子也被用作跳帮容器。

Mycetic spores possess only a rudimentary intelligence and will simply lash out at everything nearby. They are armed with Ripper Tentacles, thick ropes of tendon and muscle that can smash, crush, or ensnare and drag in enemies. They also have fanged maws, and often a powerful ranged bio-weapon.

菌荚孢子只具有基本的智能，只会攻击附近的一切。他们装备了撕裂者触须，这是一种由肌腱和肌肉组成的粗绳，可以粉碎、压碎或诱捕和拖拽敌人。它们也有尖牙喉，和通常一种强大的远程生物武器。

Mycetic Spore 菌荚孢子

The Tyranid Hive Mind is becoming more adept at its use of mycetic spores as the Tyranids push deeper into the Galaxy. Recent strains of mycetic spores have evolved sensory nodes, shielded with ablative chitin that burns off during orbital insertion, to allow the spore to make a more accurate landing by homing in on pheromone trails released by Lictors. Other strains of mycetic spores are actually two bioforms fused together: when a spore of this type makes impact with the planet's surface, its outer shell dies away and peels apart to reveal another symbiotic organism inside that takes sustenance from the outer shell to fuel a powerful bio-weapon.

随着泰伦向银河系深处推进，泰伦的虫巢思维越来越善于利用菌荚孢子。最近的菌荚孢子品系已经进化出感知节点，用在轨道空投过程中燃烧掉的烧蚀几丁质防护，使孢子能够通过归航于利卡特释放的信息素轨迹而更准确地着陆。其他品系的菌荚孢子实际上是两种融合在一起的生物形态：当这种类型的孢子与行星表面碰撞时，它的外壳会死亡并剥落，露出内部的另一种共生生物，它从外壳中获取营养，为强大的生物武器提供燃料。
