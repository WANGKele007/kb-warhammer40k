## History 历史

During the Great Crusade and Horus Heresy, the 4th Chapter of the UltramarinesLegion was known as the Aurorans. They possessed five times as many armoured vehicles as a standard Chapter. The 4th Chapter also used non-standard iconography, using the color green to mark veterans and marking its members with the so-called "Auroran Ribbon", a sign which later became the Aurora Chapter's symbol.

在大远征和荷鲁斯叛乱期间，极限战士军团的第四战团被称为极光。他们拥有的装甲载具数量是标准战团的五倍。第四战团还使用了非标准的图像，用绿色标记老兵，并用所谓的“极光缎带”标记其成员，这个标志后来成为极光战团的象征。

Aurora Chapter Firstborn 极光战团长子

In 007.M31, the 4th Chapter was among the Ultramarines units mustered on Calth. It was mainly deployed on the planet's continent of Ithraca. There, it was drawn into the apocalyptic Betrayal of Ithraca, fighting alongside the 24th Ultramarines Chapter, Legio Praesagius, and other loyalists against the Word Bearers, Legio Suturvora, and other Chaos followers. The 4th Chapter suffered massive losses at Ithraca City, but rallied under Sub-CaptainMantargo and helped to hold back the traitors before being forced to retreat due to the mass emergence of daemons.

007.M31，第四战团是集结在考斯的极限战士单位之一。它主要被部署在行星的伊萨卡大陆。在那里，它被卷入了末日般的伊萨卡背叛，与极限战士第24战团，厄兆军团和其他忠诚派一起对抗怀言者，苏图沃拉军团和其他混沌追随者。第四战团在伊萨卡城遭受了巨大的损失，但在曼塔戈副连长的带领下团结起来，帮助击退了叛徒，然后由于恶魔的大规模出现而被迫撤退。

The Aurora Chapter was officially set up in the Second Founding, and since then operates largely in the Ultima Segmentum. The chapter is well known for their armoured assaults; the Chapter possesses three times the typical number of Land Raiders and Predators, all of which are manufactured on their homeworld, Firestorm.

极光战团于二次建军时正式成立，此后主要在极限星域运营。该战团以他们的装甲突击而闻名；该战团拥有三倍于平常数量的兰德掠袭者和掠食者，所有这些都是在他们的家园世界火风暴上制造的。

After defeating the Alpha Legion in the Redemption Rebellion alongside the Knights of the Raven in 744.M41, the Aurora Chapter blamed the Knights for their heavy losses and have been in a bitter feud with them since.

在744.M41与渡鸦骑士一起在救赎叛乱中击败阿尔法军团后，极光战团指责渡鸦骑士损失惨重，并从此与他们发生了激烈的争执。

The Aurora Chapter are bitter rivals with the 66th Armoured Company, a World Eaters warband that also specializes in armoured warfare.

极光战团与第66装甲连队是死敌，第66装甲连队是一支专门从事装甲战争的吞世者战帮。

## Heraldry 纹章

The Aurora Chapter wear green armour with a golden aquila. Company affiliation is shown by the colour of a Marine's pauldron trim, as per the Codex Astartes.

极光战团穿着绿色盔甲，上面有金色的天鹰。根据阿斯塔特圣典，连队隶属关系由战士的肩甲边缘颜色表示。

Sergeants are marked with a red skull upon their green helmets, as opposed to an entirely red helmet.

军士的绿色头盔上有一个红色的颅骨，而不是一个完全红色的头盔。

The Chapter's Chaplains wear Rosarii gifted to them by the Ecclesiarchy.

该战团的牧师佩戴着国教赠送给他们的玫瑰念珠。

The Aurora Chapter has a stylised ribbon as its chapter symbol, an icon already used by the 4th Ultramarines Chapter in the Horus Heresy.

极光战团有一个风格化的缎带作为战团象征，这是荷鲁斯叛乱中极限战士第四战团已经使用的图标。
