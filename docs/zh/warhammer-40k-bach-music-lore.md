Bach was an ancient human musician from the earliest years of human civilisation.

巴赫是人类文明早期的一位古代音乐家。

Their baroque solemnities feature rising crescendos of quickening music, as well as the mathematical progression of tones. Many millennia later, their music is still found enjoyable to Humans of the Imperium and Adeptus Mechanicus, as well as Xenos species like the Necrons and Aeldari. Humans tend to spell his name incorrectly as Bacque or Bak.

他们巴洛克式的庄严气氛以音乐节奏加快为特征，以及音调的数学序列。数千年后，他们的音乐仍然被帝国的人类和机械神教，以及像死灵和灵族这样的异型种族所喜爱。人类往往将他的名字拼写为巴屈或巴克。

Individuals who have stated that they enjoy said music include ArchmagosDominusBelisarius Cawl, CryptekAsanethAyu,InquisitorErasmus Crowl, and a DrukhariHaemonculi.

表示喜欢上述的音乐的人包括统御大贤者贝利撒留考尔、墓穴技师阿萨奈斯阿尤、审判官伊拉斯谟·克劳和黑暗灵族血伶人。
