The Reign of the Astartes Unfettered was an era the Imperium would have entered, if WarmasterHorus had been victorious in the Siege of Terra.

如果战帅荷鲁斯在泰拉围城中获胜，帝国将进入一个无束阿斯塔特统治时代。

## Description 描述

According to the Iron WarriorOrtag Theokon, the era would see the Imperium reconstructed, so that it was free of the Emperor's influence. Thus allowing the Imperium to be maintained without lies or compromsies. However, the Reign of the Astartes Unfettered did not come to pass, after WarmasterHorus was killed and the Emperor emerged victorious in the Siege of Terra.

根据钢铁勇士奥尔塔格·西奥康的说法，这个时代将看到帝国的重建，使其不受帝皇的影响。因此，帝国得以在没有谎言或妥协的情况下得以维持。然而，在战帅荷鲁斯被杀，帝皇在泰拉围城中获胜后，无束阿斯塔特统治没有实现。
