Transonic Weapons are a type of weapon used by Adeptus Mechanicus forces, most infamously Sicarian Ruststalkers.

跨音武器是机械神教部队使用的一种武器，最著名的是怀刃锈蚀追猎者。

Transonic blade and razor 跨音速刃和剃刀

These weapons emit a low insistent buzz that makes stomachs turn. When they strike armour, these weapons will adjust their hostile sonic field to match the material's own resonant frequency, quickly slicing through it. This principle of dissonance makes transonic weapons highly effective at slicing through the toughest of armour with ease. Transonic weapons make a low buzz almost inaudible to unaugmented hearing. Although most are unable to hear it consciously, those in the weapons' presence without correct adjustments can feel vibrations through their entire body, from their guts to their eyes.

这些武器会发出低沉的嗡嗡声，让腹部翻腾。当它们攻击装甲时，这些武器会调整其敌对声场，以匹配材料自身的共振频率，从而快速穿透它。这种不协原理使跨音武器在轻松穿透最坚硬的装甲方面非常有效。跨音武器发出的嗡嗡声很低，未经听觉增强的人几乎听不见。尽管大多数人无法有意识地听到它，但那些在武器面前没有正确调整的人可以感受到从内脏到眼睛的全身振动。

## Sub-Types 子类

 - 

Chordclaw — A battle claw that can turn muscle, bone, and fat to jelly. Sicarian Ruststalkers typically pair a chordclaw with a Transonic Razor, however, a wielder with at least 3 arms might wield a chordclaw in addition to a pair of Transonic Blades. Chordclaws are fundamentally designed differently from transonic "cutting" weapons (blades and razors) and so also behave differently on the battlefield.

 - 

弦爪 — 一种可以将肌肉、骨骼和脂肪变成果冻的战斗爪。怀刃锈蚀追猎者通常将弦爪与跨音剃刀配对，然而，除了一对跨音刃外，至少有3只手臂的使用者可能还会挥舞弦爪。弦爪的设计从根本上不同于跨音“切割”武器（刃和剃刀），因此在战场上的表现也不同。

 - 

Transonic Blade — Longer sword; often seen wielded in pairs by Sicarian Ruststalkers.

 - 

跨音刃 — 长剑；经常被怀刃锈蚀追猎者成对挥舞。

 - 

Transonic Razor — Shorter sword; Sicarian Ruststalkers often wield a transonic razor alongside a chordclaw.

 - 

跨音剃刀 — 较短的剑；怀刃锈蚀追猎者经常在弦爪旁边挥舞跨音剃刀。

 - 

Transonic Cannon - A short-range "sound gun" that blasts transonic frequencies in a specific direction.

 - 

跨音炮 - 一种短程“声炮”，在特定方向发射跨音频率。
