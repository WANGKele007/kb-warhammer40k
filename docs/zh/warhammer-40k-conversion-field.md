Conversion Fields, also known as Power Fields, are employed by protective devices like the Rosarius and the Iron Halo. They are designed to generate a personal energy shield to protect the wearer, with the field's effect converting the energy of an impact into light; thus, a blinding flash is produced when the field stops a shot. The field is capable of rendering even plasma gun shots harmless. Such is the versatility of a power field that it can also be used to protect equipment, doorways, and vehicles.

转换力场，也称为能量立场，被玫瑰念珠和铁光环等保护装置所采用。它们的设计目的是产生一个个人能量盾牌来保护佩戴者，立场的效应将冲击的能量转化为光；因此，当立场阻挡射击时，会产生刺眼的闪光。该立场甚至能够使等离子枪射击无害。能量立场的多功能性使其也可用于保护装备、门道和载具。
