The Auretian Technocracy was the government of the human colony Aureus in a binary cluster designated Drakonis Three Eleven. The 63rd Expeditionary Fleet and 203rd Expedition destroyed it at the end of the Great Crusade.

奥瑞提安技术联盟是一个名为德拉科尼斯3·11的二元星群中人类殖民地奥里斯的政府。第63远征舰队和第203远征队在大远征结束时摧毁了它。

The Technocracy used knowledge from at least one STC template to develop their civilisation. The Technocracy military unit called "The Brotherhood" utilized power armour similar to that employed by the Adeptus Astartes.

技术联盟利用至少一个STC模板中的知识来发展他们的文明。被称为“兄弟会”的技术联盟军事部队使用了类似于阿斯塔特修会所使用的动力盔甲。

Upon encountering the Imperium, the ruler of the Technocracy (Emory Salignac, Fabricator Consul) and his honour guard met with WarmasterHorus on the Vengeful Spirit. The Consul mentioned to the Warmaster that his government had an STC in its possession; Horus (having since secretly pledged to overthrow the Emperor) drew his pistol, killed the Consul on the spot, and ordered the Sons of Horus to exterminate the Consul's Brotherhood guards. Official records at the time stated that the Fabricator Consul's staff contained a weapon which would have assassinated the Warmaster, prompting the war against the Technocracy. However, Garviel Loken, one of the Astartes present at the incident, claims this was a lie used to justify a war against the Auretians.

在遇到帝国时，技术联盟的统治者（埃默里·萨利尼亚克，铸造领事）和他的荣誉卫队在复仇之魂号上会见了战帅荷鲁斯。领事向战帅提到，他的政府拥有一个STC；荷鲁斯（此后秘密发誓推翻帝皇）拔出手枪，当场杀死了领事，并命令荷鲁斯之子消灭领事的兄弟会守卫。当时的官方记录显示，铸造领事的权杖携带了一件可以暗杀战帅的武器，引发了对技术联盟的战争。然而，事件发生时在场的阿斯塔特之一的加维尔·洛肯声称，这是一个谎言，用来作为对奥瑞提安发动战争的辩护。

After the brutal Auretian Technocracy War, the civilisation was destroyed.

在残酷的奥瑞提安技术联盟战争之后，文明被摧毁了。
