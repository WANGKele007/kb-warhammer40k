Helstalkers are scuttling Daemon Engines that serve as steeds for Lords Discordant, who ride them into battle.

地狱追猎者是作为坐骑服务于不协领主的破坏恶魔引擎，领主骑着它们投入战斗。

Helstalker with Lord Discordant Rider 地狱追猎者和不协领主骑手

These nefarious Daemon Engines are able to parasitically devour the Motive Force of their mechanical prey, be it Imperial or Xenos in origin, with their Hypno-Armour Syringes that inject Scrapcode directly into its victim. As it does so, its own metallic frame swells with ingested code and subroutines, and the Lord Discordant siphons off the pained spirit of the dying technology. This harvested energy is then used to reinvigorate other Daemon Engines, or is released as beams to infect the systems of other vehicles.

这些邪恶的恶魔引擎能够寄生吞噬其机械猎物的源力，无论是帝国还是异型，它们的催眠装甲注射器直接将废码注入受害者体内。当它这样做时，它自己的金属框架会随着摄入的代码和子程序而膨胀，不协领主会吸走痛苦的垂死技魂。然后，这些收集到的能量被用来重振其他恶魔引擎，或者作为光束释放出来，感染其他载具的系统。

Besides their own parasitic abilities Helstalkers are well-armed for war, with either an autocannon or baleflamer.

除了他们自己的寄生能力外，地狱追猎者还为战争做好了充分的准备，无论是自动炮还是灾焰炮。
