Tigrus, also known as Tigris, is a Forge World of the Imperium, that was formerly conquered by the orks. It was home to the Titan LegionLegio Solaria.

提格鲁斯，也被称为提格里斯，是帝国的铸造世界，以前曾被欧克征服。这里是泰坦军团太阳军团的所在地。

## Overview 概述

Prior to the ork invasion, Tigrus was known as the site of the original development of the Vanquisher cannon and FellbladeAccelerator Cannon. Additionally, it was the only production site of Hunter Shells for the Leman RussBattle Cannon. Tigrus also produced Boltguns and Bolt Pistols.

在欧克入侵之前，提格鲁斯被称为征服者加农炮和残暴刃加速加农炮的原初开发地点。此外，它是黎曼鲁斯战斗炮唯一的猎手炮弹生产基地。提格鲁斯还生产爆矢枪和爆矢手枪。

Tigrus also developed the Graviton Imploder, a crude and power hungry substitute solution to the Mechanicum’s difficulties in replicating the ancient patterns of Graviton Weapons. Although considered a blasphemous perversion of older patterns by some Magi, in the aftermath of the Horus Heresy its design would serve as the genesis for future weaponry, replacing the more arcane patterns after the Mechanicum lost the ability to produce them during the civil war.

提格鲁斯还开发了重力内爆者，这是一种粗糙而耗能的替代解决方案，可以解决机械教在复制重力武器古代型号方面的困难。尽管一些圣贤士认为这是对旧型号的亵渎性歪曲，但在荷鲁斯叛乱之后，它的设计将成为未来武器的起源，在机械教在内战期间失去生产能力后取代了更神秘的型号。

Presently Tigrus is considered to be a close ally of Ultramar.

目前，提格鲁斯被认为是奥特拉玛的亲密盟友。

The inhabitants of Tigrus wear orange and dark steel hued robes.

提格鲁斯的居民穿着橙色和暗钢色的长袍。

## History 历史

Situated between Ultramar and the Eastern Fringe, Tigrus had a reputation of being a stout supporter of the Great Crusade.

提格鲁斯位于奥特拉玛和东部边缘之间，以坚定支持大远征而闻名。

During the Shadow Crusade the Word Bearers and World Eaters sought to isolate and destroy Tigrus before it could move its support to the Ultramarines, but due to the Battle of Calth only a small traitor force managed to arrive. The ruling Magi of Tigrus were able to scatter the traitor host and then subsequently launched a counteroffensive into the greater Eastern Fringe.

在暗影远征期间，怀言者和吞世者试图在提格鲁斯能够向极限战士提供支援之前孤立并摧毁提格鲁斯，但由于考斯之战，只有一支小规模的叛徒部队设法到达。提格鲁斯的执政贤者能够驱散叛徒，随后向更大的东部边缘内发动反攻。

Tigrus was overrun by the orks of the WarlordArrgard the Defiler in M35. Even into late M41, it was still in Ork hands.

提格鲁斯在M35被军阀亵渎者阿加德的欧克占领。即使到了M41晚期，它仍然掌握在欧克手中。

It is recorded that in M39.365, an Explorator Fleet originating from Tigrus and operating under Martian licence discovered the Artificial World of Pontus Avernes. Exploitation rights for the site were disputed and ultimately awarded to Mars following arbitration by the Synod of Mars.

据记载，在365.M39中，一支来自提格鲁斯并在火星许可下运营的探索者舰队发现了庞图斯·阿维尔涅斯人造世界。该地点的开发权存在争议，最终在火星议会仲裁后被授予火星。

By the year 747.M41, Forge World Tigrus is noted for its involvement in the War of Neotech, during which Adeptus Mechanicus forces conducted a campaign against the T'au Sept of Vesh'yo. Three Skitarii Legions were deployed against the T'au defenders, with substantial reinforcements committed before victory was secured. As a result of the conflict, six star freighters laden with captured T'au war materiel and technology were transported to Tigrus.

到747.M41，铸造世界提格鲁斯以参与新技术战争而闻名，在此期间，机械神教部队对维斯'约的钛家门发动了一场战役。三支护教军团被部署起来对抗钛守军，在胜利之前投入了大量增援。冲突的结果是，六艘载有缴获的钛战争物资和技术的星际货船被运往提格鲁斯。

By M42 Tigrus was considered to be of a higher grade in eyes of Mars when compared to other Forge Worlds, such as Accatran. In the early days of the Indomitus Crusade, the world was attacked by the NecronSautekh Dynasty.

到M42年，与阿卡特兰等其他铸造世界相比，提格鲁斯在火星眼中被认为具有更高的等级。在不屈远征的早期，世界遭到了死灵索特克王朝的袭击。

The Forge-World would attend the Conclave Chronalis hosted by Belisarius Cawl, and would agree to join his effort to close the Great Rift.

铸造世界参加贝利萨留·考尔主持的时间秘密会议，并同意加入他关闭大裂隙的努力。

## Trivia 琐事

### Conflicting sources 来源冲突

 - 

Tigrus's status and reclamation are unclear. In Imperial Armour Volume One - Imperial Guard and Imperial Navy, it is stated that Forge World Tigrus remained under ork occupation during M41. However, in the novel Genefather, an Explorator Fleet originating from Tigrus is recorded as operating in M39, implying that elements associated with the Forge World were active significantly earlier.

 - 

提格鲁斯的地位和开垦情况尚不清楚。在《帝国装甲第一卷 — 帝国卫队和帝国海军》中，有记载称，在M41期间，铸造世界提格鲁斯仍被占领。然而，在小说《基因之父》中，一支来自提格鲁斯的探索者舰队被记录为在M39中运行，这意味着与铸造世界相关的成员在更早的时候就已经活跃起来。

 - 

The novel Titandeath and The Horus Heresy: Age of Darkness 2nd Edition Rulebook spells the world's name as Tigris.

 - 

小说《泰坦之死》和《荷鲁斯叛乱：黑暗时代第二版规则书》将世界的名字拼写为提格里斯。
