Ymgarl Genestealers are a mutated form of Genestealers originally thought to be native to the moons of planet Ymgarl, notable in that they are among the first contacts mankind has ever had with the Tyranid race.

伊姆加尔基因窃取者是基因窃取者的一种变异形态，最初被认为是伊姆加尔行星卫星的原生生物，值得注意的是，它们是人类与泰伦种族的第一次接触。

Ymgarl Genestealer 伊姆加尔基因窃取者

### Overview 概述

The Ymgarl Genestealers descend from Hive FleetTiamet, probably around M35 when they appeared on the moons of Ymgarl. Having found no intelligent races there, the early Ymgarl Genestealers infected the local giant leeches, shaping themselves into a leech-like appearance.

伊姆加尔基因窃取者来自虫巢舰队提亚马特，可能是在M35左右出现在伊姆加尔的卫星上。由于在那里没有发现智慧种族，早期的伊姆加尔基因窃取者感染了当地的巨型水蛭，将自己塑造成水蛭般的外观。

Ymgarl Genestealer as it first appeared 首次出现的伊姆加尔基因窃取者

Ymgarl Genestealers were first encountered on the moons of Ymgarl long before the arrival of Hive FleetBehemoth, wherefrom they occasionally spread on cargo barges to other worlds. They were thought to be autochtonous to the moons until they were encountered among the swarms and were revealed to be a Tyranid organism from the start. Lying dormant in hiding, they consume anything that disturbs their hibernation. This is due to their unique ability of being able to alter their physical characteristics via a limited form of shapeshifting, the drawback being a greatly quickened metabolism which forces them to either feed almost constantly, hibernate in hiding or starve to death. They possess feeding tentacles instead of a maw, resembling those on the Lictor, which they use to suck their prey dry of blood, their only source of nourishment.

早在虫巢舰队贝希摩斯抵达之前，伊姆加尔基因窃取者就首次在伊姆加尔的卫星上被发现，他们偶尔会从那里通过货船传播到其他世界。它们被认为是卫星的土著生物，直到它们在虫群中被发现，并被发现从一开始就是一种泰伦生物体。它们在躲藏中处于休眠状态，会吃掉任何扰乱蛰伏的东西。这是由于它们能够通过有限的变形形态改变其身体特征的独特能力，缺点是新陈代谢大大加快，迫使它们几乎不停地进食、躲藏蛰伏或饿死。它们拥有进食触手而不是嘴巴，类似于利卡特上的触手，用来吸干猎物的血液，这是它们唯一的营养来源。

Ymgarl Genestealer 伊姆加尔基因窃取者

Unlike any other Tyranid creature, the Ymgarl Genestealers are outcasts. The Hive Mind has no wish to reabsorb their genetic material in fear that the uncontrollable genetic instabilities that allow the Ymgarl Genestealers their formidable flesh-shaping abilities might contaminate the Tyranid gene-pool. Instead, they are abandoned on whatever planet they happen to be on, being forced to search for contact with the Hive Mind on other planets after the Hive Fleet has fed. They infiltrate the ships of any explorers and investigators unlucky enough to land on the prey planet after the Fleet's passing and spread in their instinctual need of being absorbed back to the Fleets, only to be abandoned again.

与任何其他泰伦生物不同，伊姆加尔基因窃取者是被抛弃的。虫巢思维不希望重新吸收他们的遗传物质，因为他们担心无法控制的遗传不稳定性，使伊姆加尔基因窃取者拥有强大的血肉塑形能力，可能会污染泰伦基因库。相反，他们被遗弃在他们碰巧所在的任何行星上，在虫巢舰队进食后，他们被迫在其他行星上寻找与虫巢思维的联系。他们渗透到任何不幸在舰队经过后降落在猎物行星上的探索者和调查者的舰船中，并在本能上需要被吸收回舰队的情况下扩散，结果又被遗弃了。
