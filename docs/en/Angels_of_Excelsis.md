The Angels of Excelsis are a Loyalist Space Marine Successor Chapter of the Blood Angels, created during an unknown Founding. 

Chapter History

Notable Campaigns

Mytosia II (Unknown Date.M42) - The Angels of Excelsis saw action against the forces of the Genestealer Cult known as the Gilded Claw. Captain Lysis Valanir slew the Genestealer Patriarch of the cult, though the cult's fate remains unknown.

Notable Angels of Excelsis

Captain Lysis Valanir - Captain Valanir was involved in the attack on the Gilded Claw Genestealer Cult of Mytosia II. During the battle, he fell to the Black Rage and was deemed lost. The Blood Angels' High Chaplain Astorath the Grim fought alongside Valanir until they reached the Genestealer Patriarch of the Gilded Claw. Astorath watched as the captain killed the Genestealer Patriarch, but Lysis lost his own life during the fight.
