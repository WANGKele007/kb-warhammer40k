A hypnomat is a specialised chamber used to subject a Space Marine to hypnotic sights and sounds in order to train him to use his newly-implanted gene-seed organs and control his metabolism. The hypnotherapy begins during Phase 6 of the Adeptus Astartes' initiation protocols.

The hypnomat can also be used to implant memories and reshape an Astartes' personality to meet the intense psychological demands and stresses of constant warfare. Its use is a key stage in the creation of a Space Marine.

The device can be modified to carry out other functions, such as allowing a Space Marine to relive their memories, though the user's wits will be briefly dulled when they emerge from the simulation. 

Hypnomats are also used for brief, hololithic-like individual training exercises, such as the teaching of a specialised combat technique.

A hypnomat is a cylindrical chamber with input cables emerging from within that are plugged into a Space Marine's Black Carapace neural ports in the arms and neck. The chamber's functions are controlled by an external control panel outfitted with a number of toggles and buttons. The internal chamber of the hypnomat glows when in use. Trolleys are used to move the machines around where needed and despite a hypnomat chamber's bulk, a single baseline Human, usually a Chapter serf, is still capable of moving them.
