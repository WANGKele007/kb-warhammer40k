The Thunderstrike Assault Gun, usually simply called a Thunderstrike, was a heavy, mobile artillery piece preferentially deployed by the Iron Warriors Space Marine Legion during the battles of the Great Crusade and the Horus Heresy. 

A Thunderstrike was the preferred artillery piece of the Iron Warriors' elite Stor-Bezashk siege units.
