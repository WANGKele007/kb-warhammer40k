The Balefortress is a watch fortress of the Deathwatch Chapter located in the Thresnia Sector. The Balefortress is perhaps the last Imperial bulwark left that can stop the Ork WAAAGH! Nakkaslash from capsizing the sector.

It is commanded by Watch Master Alathresis, who was seconded from the Novamarines Chapter.
