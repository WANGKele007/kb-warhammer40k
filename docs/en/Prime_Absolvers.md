The Prime Absolvers are a Loyalist Space Marine Chapter and a successor of the Dark Angels, created solely from Primaris Space Marines during the Ultima Founding of ca. 999.M41.

Chapter History

Chapter Master Dagiel Harn of the Prime Absolvers first learned of the existence of the Fallen from Supreme Grand Master Azrael himself. A headstrong warrior, he saw no reason for his Chapter brethren to feel the responsibility borne by many of the other Unforgiven successors.

To Dagiel Harn, the Fallen are no different than other Traitors. Though he established an Inner Circle for his Chapter and specialised companies (reminiscent of the Dark Angels' Deathwing and Ravenwing), he sees his Chapter's mission as hunting all the Emperor's foes equally.

Notable Prime Absolvers

Chapter Master Dagiel Harn - Harn is the first and current Chapter Master of the Prime Absolvers, who learned of the existence of the Fallen Angels from Supreme Grand Master Azrael of the Dark Angels himself. However, Harn disagreed with the Unforgiven's willingness to subordinate all of their other duties to the Imperium in pursuit of the elimination of this singular source of shame. Though Harn created an Inner Circle for his Chapter who would be told the truth of the Dark Angels Legion's ancient shame and duplicated the tactical functions of the Dark Angels' Deathwing and Ravenwing companies in his own Chapter, he does not see the Fallen as more deserving of his Astartes' retribution than any other foe of the Emperor.

Chapter Appearance

Chapter Colours

The Prime Absolvers primarily wear blue power armour with deep red on their helm, shoulder pauldron insets, elbow guards, vambraces and gauntlets. Their pauldron trim is grey. The Aquila or Imperialis worn on the chest plate is also deep red.

The white squad tactical specialty symbol -- battleline, fire support, close support, Veteran or command -- is indicated on the right shoulder pauldron.

A black High Gothic numeral prominently displayed upon it indicates a battle-brother's assigned squad. The left shoulder pauldron displays the Chapter's iconography.

It is unknown how the Chapter indicates a battle-brother's company assignment.

Chapter Badge

The Prime Absolvers' Chapter badge consists of a white, twelve-spoked wheel, which resembles a "Breaking Wheel," an ancient torture device utilised for public execution by breaking the bones of a criminal or bludgeoning them to death.
