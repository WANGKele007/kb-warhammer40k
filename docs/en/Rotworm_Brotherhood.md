The Rotworm Brotherhood is a splinter warband or vectorium of Plague Marines that has become independent of the Death Guard Traitor Legion's central command. This warband is led by a conclave of Death Guard Sorcerers who rebelled against the authority of Mortarion. 

The Rotworm Brotherhood work to raise Chaos Cults of mortal worshippers of the Plague God across the Imperium, their task to multiply like bacilli and spread the worship of Nurgle, undermining civilisation before the Rotworm Brotherhood descends to despoil all.

Warband Appearance

Warband Colours

The Rotworm Brotherhood's power armour is the same as that of the Death Guard, composed of decayed and cracked plates of ceramite in light grey with dark green trim. The Rotworm Brotherhood's Chaos Space Marines, like those of the Death Guard, often wear grotesquely warped suits of antique Mark III Iron Power Armour.

Warband Badge

The Rotworm Brotherhood's warband badge is usually a modified version of the standard Mark of Nurgle, similar to that used by the Heretic Astartes of the Death Guard.
