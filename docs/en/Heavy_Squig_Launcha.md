The Heavy Squig Launcha is an Ork vehicle weapon normally deployed aboard the Rukkatrukk Squigbuggy. As its name suggests, it fires various breeds of the vicious Orkoid organisms known as Squigs at the foe. It is larger and capable of longer range than its smaller counterpart, the Ork-portable Squig Launcha.

The Heavy Squig Launcha is bolted securely to the Rukkatrukk's chassis, and is operated by the vehicle's leering gunner. Three types of "ammunition" are favoured for use with a Heavy Squig Launcha -- Bitey Squigs, Bile Squigs, and Boom Squigs.
