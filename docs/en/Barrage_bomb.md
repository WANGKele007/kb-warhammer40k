A barrage bomb is a type of Imperial voidship weapon used for orbital bombardments of planetary targets. It is essentially the same as an Imperial torpedo which has been modified for the destruction of ground-based targets rather than another voidship in ship-to-ship combat. Barrage bombs are used by the warships of the Navis Imperialis and the Punisher-class Strike Cruiser of the Adeptus Arbites.
