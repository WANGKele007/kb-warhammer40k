Gorgon Pattern Terminator Armour, also called just Gorgon Terminator Armour, and officially named Gorgon Pattern Tactical Dreadnought Armour, was a rare and fearsome sub-variant of Terminator Armour employed exclusively by the Space Marines of the Iron Hands Legion at the dawn of the Horus Heresy. A grim and brutal marvel of cybernetic warfare, Gorgon Terminator Armour was wrrought in the forges of the Iron Hands homeworld of Medusa under the direct oversight of Primarch Ferrus Manus and his cadre of Iron Fathers, as the Gorgon Pattern represented the Iron Hands' relentless pursuit of martial perfection through brutal technological innovation. 

Gorgon Pattern Terminator Armour stood as one of several classified attempts to surpass the standardised Tactical Dreadnought Armour used by the Legiones Astartes -- yet it would come to embody the dark extremes of that ambition. Derived from the venerable Indomitus Pattern Terminator Armour, the Gorgon prototype pushed the limits of both machine and flesh. Far from merely a suit of armour, it was a system of integration that demanded the wearer submit wholly to the doctrine of the flesh's weakness. The Gorgon Pattern could not simply be worn -- it had to be bonded to its user through bionic surgery. 

To endure its interface protocols, an Astartes warrior was subjected to invasive cybernetic augmentation, including spinal sheathing, neural interfacing, and redundant organ bypass systems. Pain was not a deterrent, but a threshold to be crossed; a mark of worthiness. Among the Iron Hands, such a sacrifice was not only accepted, it was coveted. Volunteers came in droves. 

For those who had already shed much of their mortal forms, the Gorgon Pattern offered not just enhanced protection and firepower, but a final, purifying transcendence -- a true unity of man and machine. These warriors were then formed into Gorgon Terminator Squads, each consisting of four elite Veterans clad in the heavy, impassive ceramite shells of the Gorgon warplate, their number led by a grim figure known as a "Hammerbearer" -- a squad leader armed with a deadly Thunder Hammer and the authority of the Gorgon himself.

When Gorgon Terminators advanced, the enemy did not retreat, they broke. And yet, for all its power, the Gorgon Pattern was still only a prototype -- a pattern of warplate caught mid-refinement, its potential never fully realised. This brutal lineage was cut short at Isstvan V, beneath the ash-blown skies of the Drop Site Massacre. There, among the shattered corpses of the Loyalists and the betrayed hopes of the Great Crusade, Ferrus Manus was struck down by his once-brother, Fulgrim of the Emperor's Children. With his death, the refinement of the Gorgon Pattern Tactical Dreadnought Armour ended. Those suits that remained were fought in until destruction, their wearers dying in silence as the dreams of the Iron Hands hardened into bitterness and steel. 

In the 41st Millennium, few examples of the Gorgon Terminator Armour survive -- most are interred within the reliquaries of the Iron Hands or lost to the flames of war. Yet in its creation, the Gorgon Armour laid bare a truth the sons of Medusa have never forgotten: evolution through suffering, strength through sacrifice, and purity through the machine.

History

Gorgon Pattern Terminator Armour was one of a number of Tactical Dreadnought Armour sub-classes found within the ranks of the Iron Hands Legion during the Great Crusade and Horus Heresy. This pattern was one of a number of ongoing attempts by the primarch Ferrus Manus and his cadre of Iron Fathers to refine and augment the various patterns of Terminator Armour employed by the forces of the Legiones Astartes. 

This unique prototype of Indomitus Pattern Tactical Dreadnought Armour was devised by the genius of Ferrus Manus and his Iron Fathers at the very outset of the Horus Heresy to supplement the Iron Hands Legion's combat-depleted stocks of Indomitus Terminator Armour. The design replaced the field generators imbedded in the armour with experimental systems that converted incoming electromagnetic and kinetic energy into bursts of blinding light, able to incapacitate and maim nearby foes.

The heat and electrochemical toxin bleed from the armour's systems limited the armour's agility, and its negative side effects required a high level of cybernetic rebuild for its wearer to endure. However, volunteers for this painful modification were plentiful in the Legion's ranks, many deeming it an honour to serve as part of this great endeavour.

It is an interesting case in point that while several thousand suits of Terminator Armour in the most common Cataphractii, Tartaros and Indomitus Patterns were in service with the X Legion (many of which were concentrated in the hands of the doomed warriors of the Avernii Clan and lost at Isstvan V), the primarch himself was known to not be entirely satisfied with the performance of any of these designs and so a full issue to his Legion was never approved. 

One of the most extreme technological examples of the Iron Hands' attempts at innovation, Gorgon Terminator Armour required a direct cybernetic bond with its wearer, much like a Dreadnought, though Ferrus Manus would not live long enough to refine the pattern to its full potential.
