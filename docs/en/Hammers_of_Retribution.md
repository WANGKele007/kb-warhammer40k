The Hammers of Retribution is a Loyalist Space Marine Chapter of unknown Founding and origin. Almost nothing is known about this Chapter in Imperial records.

Notable Hammers of Retribution

Sergeant Agamorr - Agamorr is just short of two Terran centuries in age, though the few beings he meets without his helm on would never guess this by his appearance alone. Agamorr has served in the Deathwatch for just over three decades; his standard original 10 year tour of duty was extended when it was discovered that he had a knack for eliminating unusual xenoforms. Agamorr's black Power Armour is clearly ancient, with carefully maintained and ornately inlaid plates. One of his shoulder plates bears the skull icon of the Ordo Xenos Deathwatch; the other is marked with the badge of his Chapter. When preparing to go into battle, he adds an ancient, well-worn Thunder Hammer to the Bolt Pistol that he normally carries at his side. Agamorr is straightforward in both his words and deeds. He seldom hesitates once he has chosen a course of action and his speech is often direct and to the point. In combat situations, Sergeant Agamorr expects to be fully obeyed. He feels no fear -- the emotion is, to Agamorr, akin to a distantly remembered dream of childhood.

Chapter Colours

Chapter Badge

The Hammers of Retribution's Chapter badge is a pair of ornate crossed Thunder Hammers.
