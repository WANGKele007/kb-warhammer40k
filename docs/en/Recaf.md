Recaf is a popular hot beverage that is made from crushed and brewed leaves and is drunk on Imperial worlds across the galaxy. The composition can vary from planet to planet, but most blends incorporate some form of stimulant such as caffeine or detoxified pharamoxine compounds.

It is the perfect companion for, say, a sentry's long watch at night or when one is about to start another day of grueling work on a manufactorum assembly line. Recaf is essentially a synthetic form of the ancient Terran drink known as coffee.
