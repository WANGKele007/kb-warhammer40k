Light of a Crystal Sun by Josh Reynolds is a Warhammer 40,000 short story published in 2018 and collected in the anthologies Nexus & Other Stories and Warriors and Warlords. 

Synopsis

To be a Heretic Astartes is to be the most reviled of all Humanity's foes. Once proud and majestic transhuman warriors, these Chaos Space Marines have turned from the Emperor's light. They are now foul champions to the Dark Gods, and would see the Imperium bathed in blood. 

Desperate to stop his degeneration, the galaxy's most dangerous scientist seeks to discover the secrets of life and death.
