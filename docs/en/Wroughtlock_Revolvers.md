Wroughtlock Revolvers are a type of powerful ballistic weapons employed by the warriors of the Leagues of Votann, particularly by the Bombasts of their Hernkyn Yaegir teams. A Bombast's primary tactical role is to draw attention away from their fellow Yaegir's operations and force the enemy into reckless action. 

To this end they are usually equipped with twin attention-grabbing Wroughtlock Revolvers that produce particularly loud reports when they are fired.
