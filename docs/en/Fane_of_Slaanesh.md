A Fane of Slaanesh is a focus of worship for the servants of the Chaos God Slaanesh. The blasphemous icon of the fane is wrought in precious metal. Around it are laid offerings to the Dark Prince, but in truth it is one of his most exalted Greater Daemons who peers through from the Realm of Chaos.

Those whose offerings meet with the entity's approval are rewards highly -- but those who offend are justly punished. A Fane of Slaanesh can be used to channel psychic power to the Dark Prince's sorcerers or increase the martial prowess of his warriors, mortal or daemon alike.
