A Celestial Shield is a psychically-activated force field technology that is sometimes used by Craftworld Aeldari Guardians as an emergency personal defence shield. Once activated, the psychic shield allows the Guardian to deflect kinetic and directed energy attacks that would otherwise have slain or badly injured them at a key moment during the battle.
