House Durbach is an Imperial Knight house that is known to control an entire world by itself, thus making it one of the so-called "Great Houses." House Durbach is of the Questor Mechanicus and gives its direct fealty to the Adeptus Mechanicus.

Its house motto is "Strength in Faith, Strength in Steel."
