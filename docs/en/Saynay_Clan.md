The Saynay Clan is a cannibalistic Chaos Cult operating in the Koronus Expanse of the Segmentum Obscurus. The Saynay are a dynasty of corrupt monsters that fled the fetid world of Dusk in the Calixis Sector after the corruption of their blood was discovered by agents of the Inquisition. In time they made their way to the Koronus Expanse, and here they exist still in disparate voidcraft whose insides are like slaughter houses hung with the dismembered corpses of those taken in the clan's raids on shipping and isolated outposts. 

The Saynay and its vassals feed on the dead, daub foul Heretical runes on the hulls of their ships, and perform terrible sacrificial rites to summon Daemons into the defiled flesh of the dead. 

Above the slaughter preside the Human-flesh-gorged matriarchs and patriarchs of the clan, their finery and rotting wigs cracked with dry gore. Such is the reputation of the Saynay Clan that even other pirates and raiders shrink from them and cower at the threat of their attention.

History

Once, the Imperial noble family of Lothar Saynay ruled extensive holdings in the darkest fens of the Feral World of Dusk in the Malfian Sub-sector of the Calixis Sector. This loathsome clan had interbred with mutants, witches, and worse, and they performed many unspeakable acts upon their vile estates in their cannibalistic worship of the Daemon Mord'dagan, the Eater of the Dead. Eventually, an Inquisitorial purge was ordered upon the head of the Saynay Clan and his depraved family, an act that exposed many Chaos Cults and much darker patterns across the Calixis Sector in which the Saynay dynasty had been involved. 

The patriarch of the Saynay Clan was slain by the Inquisition, but much of his family escaped, passing beyond the Maw into the Koronus Expanse in an array of converted pleasure yachts, battered armed merchant ships, and a handful of pirate frigates. Others stowed away aboard pilgrim ships and bulk freighters, and there are many whispers that the Saynay Clan purchased a safe passage through the Warp with the bloody sacrifice of their fellow travellers. Ever since, they have lingered within the Koronus Expanse like a disease, preying upon any outlying colonies and isolated Rogue Trader voidships they can find.

Dark stories of the Saynay Clan circulate constantly among the lower decks of Port Wander and in the halls of Footfall; it is said that the clan extensively practices cannibalism and has suffered many mutations amongst their ranks. Sorcery is also commonly associated with the Saynay Clan, and many Rogue Traders have reported encountering Saynay ships guided by Warp-craft or ramshackle pirate vessels teeming with witches and Daemons. Some have even claimed that entire voidships have been possessed by Warp entities summoned into realspace and bound into the hull by a sorcerer's will. However, sorcerers are kept on a short leash within the clan; they are only allowed to advise, never to lead.

It is fortunate for the denizens of the Koronus Expanse that the Saynay Clan's resources are limited; most of their voidships are escorts and frigates, many of them in poor repair and few with any form of advanced weaponry. Often, the Saynay Clan will attack from surprise, attempting to board and overwhelm an enemy vessel in a tide of bloodthirsty mutants, Rogue Psykers, and Chaos-corrupted cannibal madmen. The clan seeks wealth and additional voidships for their fleet, but they are also intent on expanding their depraved family by acquiring captives and slaves. For this reason, many of their raids are often confused with those perpetrated by Aeldari pirates on the fringe of the Expanse. 

The clan's flotilla attacks isolated voidships and Human colonies in the Koronus Expanse; the Saynay then bring survivors back aboard their flotilla's ships, where they are sacrificed to the Saynay's Daemonic patron, Mord'dagan, the Eater of the Dead, in rituals where the victims are bled, butchered and devoured while still alive. These sacrifices served to summon further Daemons, some of which are then bound into the hulls of the ships themselves by Saynay sorcerers, while others are bound into their captives to create Daemonhosts, while yet others (particularly the minor Daemons known as Blade-things) are left unbound and help in the abduction of fresh victims (or not, as is their whim).

Undoubtedly, however, the greatest threat of the Saynay Clan is their allegiance formed in 717.M41 with the Renegade Navigator-turned-pirate, Ember Nostromo. In her warship, the pre-Angevin Crusade Monarch of Whispers, the Saynay have access to something they lacked previously -- a true warship. By the early 9th century of the 41st Millennium, the Monarch of Whispers had been leading the Saynay Clan's flotilla for over a standard century.

In the early 9th century of the 41st Millennium, the Saynay Clan was one of several groups investigating the accursed astropaths known as the Hexicars.
