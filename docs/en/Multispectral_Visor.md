A Multispectral Visor is a scanning technology employed by the Brôkhyr engineers and artificers of the Leagues of Votann. As the name implies, the technology allows an individual Brôkhyr to view any substance or environment in multiple spectral wavelengths. While originally designed to aid fabrication of new technologies at the Forge, some Brôkhyr Iron-masters also make use of their visors while carrying out their duty of maintaining and repairing damaged Kin war engines on the battlefield. Their work can give those vehicles added tactical potency following a battlefield refit.
