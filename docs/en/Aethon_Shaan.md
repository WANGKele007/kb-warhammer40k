Aethon Shaan is the current Master of Shadows (Chapter Master) of the Raven Guard Chapter of the Adeptus Astartes. Before his elevation to his current position, he served as the Shadow Captain of the Raven Guard's 1st and 4th Companies.

History

Origins

Aethon Shaan has served in the Raven Guard Chapter for hundreds of standard years, and over his long career proceeded to serve in the 10th, 8th, 4th and 1st Companies, respectively, before becoming the Shadow Captain of the 4th Company.

Invasion of Ultramar

When the Chaos army known as the Bloodborn assaulted Ultramar in 854.999.M41 during what was known as the Invasion of Ultramar, the Raven Guard's Master of Shadows sent Shaan and one of the 4th Company's elite squads to assist the Ultramarines in the defence of their homeworlds. Their entry into Chapter Master Marneus Calgar's chambers inside the Fortress of Hera was so stealthy that even the legendary Scout Sergeant Torias Telion did not detect their presence until they announced themselves. Their specific mission was to capture Ardaric Vaanes, a Renegade Raven Guard who had been seen travelling in the retinue of the Iron Warriors' Warsmith Honsou, the mastermind of the invasion, and return him for judgement to Deliverance.

Shaan and his squad infiltrated the Chaos-held city of Axum on Tarentus and neutralised the sentries, allowing an Ultramarines strike force to enter and demolish the Chaos Space Marines' defences. However, Axum was a trap laid by Honsou for Ultramarines Captain Uriel Ventris, who only just managed to evacuate his troops before the city was levelled from orbit. During the evacuation, Shaan saved Scout Sergeant Issam's life by casually beheading a persistent Khorne Berserker clinging to their escaping Thunderhawk.

Shaan and his squad next joined Ventris and his Ultramarines 4th Company in defending the world of Calth from the assault of the Iron Warriors, and aided Inquisitor Namira Suzaku in her interrogation of Ardaric Vaanes after he was captured during the fight at Guilliman's Gate.

Shaan and his warriors proved in turning the tide of battle on Calth, as most notably Shaan and his Veterans destroyed a monolithic battle-fortress of the arch-enemy known as the Black Basilica. A towering monstrosity of enormous power, Shaan and his warriors managed to infiltrate the massive mobile fortress and plant explosives in the Basilica's main store of munitions. The destruction of the Basilica was the turning point in the defence of Calth, as its destruction also ended the life of the Dark Mechanicum Adept Cycerin, who had plagued the defenders with scrap-code attacks throughout the invasion and sought to take control of the Praetorian Servitors fighting beside the Ultramarines. 

Shaan and his warriors then accompanied Captain Uriel Ventris into the heart of Calth to track down the Renegades, led by Ardaric Vaanes, who had allowed himself to be captured in a last bid for redemption. Despite his misgivings, Shaan fought alongside the Ultramarines and the Renegade against the Iron Warriors. In the climax of the battle for Calth, Shaan accompanied Ventris and his Command Squad through the caverns to the underground tomb of Remus Ventanus, the saviour of Calth during the Horus Heresy. Shaan lost several Astartes in the fight with Honsou's warriors, but survived the fight, and the collapsing of the cave by Honsou. yet the Raven Guard's sacrifices were not in vain, for the Traitors failed in their mission and were purged from Ultramar with bolter and blade. 

Afterwards, Shaan was bewildered by the actions of Vaanes, who had fought for their side in the final battle. Shaan had been ordered to take Vaanes back to the Ravenspire for punishment, but did not know what he would say now. Uriel Ventris suggested that Shaan tell the truth: that Vaanes had given his life fighting against the Ruinous Powers. Shaan agreed, and asked the Ultramarines Apothecary Selenus to extract Vaanes's gene-seed, for return to the Raven Guard's fortress-monastery on Deliverance.

Shaan and his surviving warriors also accompanied the Ultramarines 2nd and 4th Companies to Talassar to relieve the besieged 1st Company led by Chapter Master Marneus Calgar, taking part in the jump pack assault against the Daemon Prince M'kar's Daemonic army. Shaan was given permission to attend the honourary funeral service in memory of the 347 Ultramarines and 10 Raven Guard Astartes who had died to protect the Imperium on Calth. Shaan returned to Deliverance with the gene-seed of the fallen, including that of Captain Vaanes, who had earned the redemption he had sought. 

During the mission to Ultramar Captain Shaan and his warriors frequently proved the superiority of the Raven Guard in matters of stealth, managing to remain hidden under the very scrutiny of the legendary Ultramarines Scout Sergeant Torias Telion and the 10th Company's Captain Antilochus. Without the skills of the Raven Guard the battle for Calth, and perhaps all of Ultramar, might well have been lost to the forces of Chaos.

First Captain

Shaan eventually became Shadow Captain of the Raven Guard's 1st Company and was given the honorific title of "Lord of Deliverance". In this capacity he oversaw the defence of the Raven Guard's homeworld while also gaining de facto control of the Chapter's logistical, manufacturing and information network, much of which was based on Deliverance's parent world of Kiavahr. Shaan thus came to specialise in the Rite of Shadow known as the Spraakfys, which deals with the nature of realities and falsehoods. 

He also came to master the Trifold Path of Shadow. But while the Raven Guard's Master of Shadow Kayvaan Shrike and his predecessor Corvin Severax had both leaned too closely into the Path of Ambush, Shaan had managed to master a true balance between the three primary tactical principles of Corvus Corax.

Master of Shadows

During the Indomitus Crusade, Shaan oversaw victories against the Genestealer Cults of Gelvia, the Iron Warriors at the Strigneth System and the Orks at Aescia II. All three victories had been won with minimal loss of life and exemplified the proper exercise of the Trifold Path of Shadow. The Raven Guard's Master of Shadows Kayvaan Shrike, having long looked for a successor since he considered himself unworthy of his position, knew that Shaan exemplified the tactics originally embodied by their primarch. Shrike saw that he and his predecessor Corvin Severax's over-reliance on the Path of Ambush had weakened the Raven Guard, and saw in Shaan someone who better exemplified the totality of the way of war taught by the primarch as embodied in the Trifold Path of Shadow. 

Shaan was summoned back to the Ravenspire and quietly and unceremoniously appointed the Chapter's new Master of Shadows while Shrike resigned from the position to continue leading Raven Guard forces in the Bastior Sub-sector during the Fourth Tyrannic War. Befitting his new title and role commanding the Chapter, Shaan prefers to lead from the shadows and wage campaigns of subterfuge and misdirection. He emphasises the value of good intelligence and dividing the Raven Guard into highly autonomous independent strike forces. 

To that end, he rarely appears openly in battle and maintains a network of spies, info thralls, and Servo-automata to constantly collect information on potential threats. On the rare occasion that Shaan commits himself to the fight, his presence heralds doom for the enemy. Usually, he arrives at the culmination of the Raven Guard's clandestine efforts, descending to land the killing blow upon an already weakened foe.

Wargear

Claws of Severax - The Claws of Severax are a pair of Lightning Claws wielded by Aethon Shaan and once belonged to one of his predecessors as the Raven Guard's Master of Shadows, Corvin Severax, until his death in the Prefectia Campaign. Both of these Chapter relics were recovered by Shadow Captain Kayvaan Shrike, who later became Severax's successor. But the two artefacts remained within the Raven Guard's Ravenspire fortress-monastery, until Shrike abdicated his position to Aethon Shaan during the Era Indomitus. Upon becoming the Chapter's Master of Shadows, Shaan chose to wield the Claws of Severax and the Blackwing Mantle. 

Blackwing Mantle - The Blackwing Mantle is a relic Raven Guard Jump Pack that emits black flames. It once belonged to one of Aethon Shaan's predecessors as the Raven Guard's Master of Shadows, Corvin Severax, until his death in the Prefectia Campaign. Both of these Chapter relics were recovered by Shadow Captain Kayvaan Shrike, who later became Severax's successor. But the two artefacts remained within the Raven Guard's Ravenspire fortress-monastery, until Shrike abdicated his position to Aethon Shaan during the Era Indomitus. Upon becoming the Chapter's Master of Shadows, Shaan chose to wield the Claws of Severax and the Blackwing Mantle. 

Servo-automata - These drones brought from the Ravenspire aid Shaan on the field of battle.
