Sister Rachayel of the Orders Hospitaller was serving on the Feral World of Aurum in the Jericho Reach during the Achilus Crusade's efforts to bring the planet into Imperial Compliance. 

Murdered by the Genestealer infestation present on the planet, her death precipitated the deployment of a Deathwatch Kill-team to Aurum in the hopes of both determining what had happened and bringing still-independent Aurum officially into the Imperium of Man.

History

Alongside the Ecclesiarchy's chief missionary, Father Marius, Sister Rachayel operated from the Word of Faith mission in Haistand -- the planetary capital. Around 817.M41, however, Raychayel was found dead, the native Aurans claiming it was suicide.

Suspecting foul play, the Imperium eventually ordered a Deathwatch Kill-team to undertake a joint mission to Aurum to both investigate the Sororitas' death and convince the world's reigning monarch, Caele Rylus Darkscourge, to bring his world into the Imperium of Man.

The investigation led the Kill-team to the Black Depths, a location on the northern edge of Haistand. Behind the facade of a promethium siphoning industry and a criminal cartel, something far worse resided. Something the Aurans had strove so hard to bury, yet the Caele himself did not yet know: a breeding ground for Genestealers and infected warriors.

In the Black Depths, some caverns were so flooded with crude promethium that they were unsafe to drain, filled almost to the ceiling with black liquid and noxious fumes.

It was here that the infected warriors and Genestealers hid, venturing out at night to hunt and multiply. Sister Rachayel had discovered the xenos den during one of these nightly attacks.

Unfortunately the xenos also discovered that she was tracking them. They did not kill her, but rather attempted to implant her with their corrupted seed. Refusing to become an instrument of the unclean, Sister Rachayel fell upon her own sword to prevent the loss of her genetic purity.

Knowing that her disappearance would cause questions, Karthas, an infected native and one of the leaders of the Genestealer Cult, brought her body back to Haistand and hoped it would be dismissed as a suicide.

Though it took a while for the suspicious nature of the sister's death to reach the high command of the Achilus Crusade, the Deathwatch ultimately responded and proceeded to face the Genestealer Cult of Aurum.
