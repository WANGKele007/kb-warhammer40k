A Good Man is a short story published in the Gaunt's Ghosts series that was originally part of an anthology. A Good Man was published as part of the Sabbat Worlds anthology in October 2010, and was later re-published as a stand-alone e-book.

Synopsis

The Sabbat Worlds Crusade has moved on from Verghast, leaving a shattered world in its wake. An influx of Imperial servants come to rebuild the infrastructure, Zale Linder among them. But something is wrong -- the person who summoned him to Verghast doesn't seem to exist. As Linder investigates, he is drawn into a web of secrets and lies where nothing is as it seems and no-one can be trusted.
