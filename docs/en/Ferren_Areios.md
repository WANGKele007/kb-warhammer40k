Ferren Areios is the current captain of the Ultramarines Chapter's 6th Company and its Master of the Rites.

The newly promoted captain of the 6th Company, Ferren Areios, was one of the first wave of recruits to be transformed into a Primaris Space Marine during the Ultima Founding. Formally awakened after over ten thousand standard years in stasis during the earliest days of the Indomitus Crusade, he has since forged a reputation as an incredibly effective battlefield commander.

History

Origins

Originally known only by his forename, Ferren was born and raised in the bowels of an ancient hive city called Hive Daner Fifty and was one of thousands of youths abducted by the agents of Archmagos Dominus Belisarius Cawl following the end of the Horus Heresy to form the first generation of Primaris Space Marines deep in the vaults he had created to hold them beneath the surface of Mars. Later, Ferran was awakened on Mars and then for many standard millennia confined to the Ark Mechanicus Zar-Quaesitor, subjected to endless experimentation, training and thousands of standard years of enforced Sus-an Membrane hibernation. 

Upon Roboute Guilliman's resurrection during the Ultramar Campaign of the 13th Black Crusade in ca. 999.M41, Ferren was among the Primaris recruits designated the "Unnumbered Sons" intended to serve in those Space Marine Legion-sized forces drawn from the genetic legacies of all nine Loyalist First Founding Space Marine Legions. It was known that Ferren had been implanted with Guilliman's gene-seed, yet it had not been decided whether he would join the ranks of the Ultramarines or one of their many successors. 

Ferren's training record came to the attention of the primarch, who bestowed upon him the rank of lieutenant before he had even been awakened for the final time. Ferren would serve as an adjutant to Captain Vitrian Messinius of the White Consuls, and benefited greatly from this veteran officer's experience.

Awakenings

Captain and Lord Lieutenant Vitrian Messinius was present when Ferren was brought out of hibernation and he told the new Primaris Marine what was now expected of him. After Ferren absorbed this, and swore his service to the Emperor, Messinius asked him if he wanted to change his name. As his old life was gone and he would serve under Messinius, Ferren asked the Lord Lieutenant to choose a new name for him. And so it was Messinius who gave Ferren Areios the second part of his name, taken from an ancient Martian myth concerning the god of war for which the Red Planet had originally been named. 

Ferran joined Messinius as he sought to check on the progress of the mishap-prone Indomitus Crusade Fleet Quintus. It had been chosen to be the first of the Indomitus Crusade's ten fleets to launch, but a series of accidents, sabotage and sickness, had delayed Fleet Quintus' readiness. This led Roboute Guilliman to despatch Messinius to determine if it was still possible for Fleet Quintus to launch on time. Messinius had been informed that a plague was also spreading amongst Fleet Quintus; vessels and when he and Ferren arrived on the cruiser Ideos, they witnessed the disease first hand. They soon learned that the sickness was a supernatural, Warp-born plague and those stricken with it caused a number of minor Nurglish Daemons to materialise when they died. After aiding in dispatching an outbreak of these Daemons on Ideos, the two reported to Guilliman on how catastrophic this plague truly was.

Alongside Messinius, Ferren fought with distinction as part of Fleet Quintus once it finally launched, participating in the Battle of Machorta Sound, in which he personally slew a Daemon Prince of Khorne. Leading a boarding party of fresh Primaris Marines aboard the Chaos command ship Blood King, Areios and his warriors fought their way to the bridge. There, they encountered a strange statue that began to move and revealed itself as a Daemon Prince of the Blood God. Ferran's entire squad was wiped out by the foul entity, and Ferren himself was brought to his knees by the Daemon's savage strength in melee combat. However, before the Daemon could deliver the fatal blow, a squad of Aggressors arrived as reinforcements, battering the enemy with enough heavy weapon firepower to allow Ferren to plunge his power sword into the Daemon Prince's chest, banishing it back to the Warp.

As the standard years passed during the first stage of the Indomitus Crusade to stabilise the Imperium Sanctus, Messinius came to rely more heavily upon Ferren, granting him ever greater command responsibility, which he repaid to the Lord Lieutenant with effective service. 

Battle Group Iolus

Roughly 5 standard years into the Indomitus Crusade, Ferren had been named captain of the 1st Company of the 1st Battalion of the 1st Division of Battle Group Iolus of Indomitus Crusade Fleet Quintus. While planning an attack on Tiantin City on the planet Suladen, Messinius overruled Ferren's slower and more careful strategy in favour of speed. Ferren and his company later participated in the attack using the revised plans. After reaching the enemy's heavy artillery they were attacked by a Rogue Psyker. Ferren had his warriors distract the psyker, and then killed the Heretic with a grenade. Shortly after, one of Ferren's sergeants informed him that several Traitoris Militarum troops had surrendered, and were willing to exchange their lives for useful intelligence on the forces of the Archenemy. Despite this, Ferren ordered these prisoners executed as an unnecessary drain on Imperial resources, largely because he believed they had nothing of worth to tell him.

At the Battle of Srinagar, fought against the Word Bearers, Ferren suffered grave injuries at the hands of Dark Apostle Pridor Vrakon, who was disgusted by the ease with which he had defeated one of the Emperor's vaunted new breed of Space Marines despite Ferren's many standard centuries of simulated training. Ferren was saved from death only by the action of his Belisarian Furnace, which allowed him to be recovered by Chapter serfs and nursed back to health.

Hand of Abaddon

Upon recovering, Ferren learned that in the wake of the Battle of Raukos which had ended the first phase of the Indomitus Crusade approximately a dizen standard years after it had begun, the Unnumbered Sons were to be dissolved into separate Primaris Marine Chapters or Primaris reinforcements for existing Firstborn Chapters. Ferren hoped he would remain under the command of Vitrian Messinius and join the White Consuls. Roboute Guilliman, however, had different plans. Instead, Lieutenant Areios was inducted into the 6th Company of the Ultramarines and immediately dispatched to participate in the ongoing fighting on the world of Garrovire.

Within solar days of Ferren's arrival on that world, Captain Maximus Epathus of the 6th Company, along with Lieutenant Varos, were slain during a mission to investigate a strange psychic obfuscation discovered within the ruins of a purged city on Garrovire, where they encountered a Chaos-tainted statue. Deciding to destroy the idol as the probable source of the psychic emanations in the region, the Ultramarines were attacked by a Daemon-possessed member of their squad, leaving Vero and Epathus dead, and the Librarian Maendaius suspected of Chaos corruption.

As the last surviving senior officer of the company, Areios assumed de facto command of the company. As commander of the depleted 6th Company for the remainder of the campaign on Garrovire, Areios proved himself beyond all expectations. Following a vision from the Librarian, the 6th Company's assigned strike cruiser joined up with Inquisitor Leonid Rostov to search for the base of the Black Legion champions known as the Hand of Abaddon.

With aid from the the Leagues of Votann's Omrigar Kindred, and Rostov's small Imperial fleet, the 6th Company, 84th Mordian Iron Guard Regiment, and a platoon of Inquisitorial Stormtroopers were able to assault the captured Blackstone Fortress the Hand of Abaddon used as its headquarters. Ferren oversaw the defeat of the fortress's complement of Heretic Astartes and Chaos Cultists and personally slew the Butcher King, a member of the Hand of Abaddon, but not before five of the eight other members of the Hand had escaped.

Captain of the 6th

When he finally returned to Macragge, Ferren Areios met with Lord Commander of the Imperium Roboute Guilliman and was officially recognised as rightful captain of the Ultramarines' 6th Company and Master of the Rites. Before his meeting with Guilliman, Ferren travelled to the Ultramarines' Hall of Solemnity in the Fortress of Hera to see the resting place of his predecessor, Maximus Epathus. While looking for Epathus' grave he ran into Cato Sicarius, who offered Ferren advice on dealing with the death of Epathus, and shared the lessons he had learned over the tumultuous years of his own service to the Emperor. While they talked Sicarius led Ferren to Epathus's resting place. 

Thereafter, Ferren joined Captains Uriel Ventris of the 4th Company and Mikael Fabian of the 3rd Company in mopping-up operations on worlds of Ultramar affected by the recent Plague Wars. It is this duty that brought Ferren Areios and his company to the very border of the Five Hundred Worlds of old, isolated in what has now become part of the Plague God Nurgle's domain, known as the Scourge Stars. How Ferren Areios will handle being pushed to the limits of his Chapter's creed by the Death Guard Heretic Astartes who control this region remains to be seen.
