A Sonic Shrieker is an ancient psycho-sonic augmentation for Heretic Astartes power armour helmets, in essence a diminutive version of a Doom Siren, rumoured to have been adapted from xenos technology in the days of the Great Crusade. They were first seen sparingly as attempts to emulate technologically the genetic enhancement experiments undertaken by Emperor's Children Legion Apothecaries like Fabius, such as the ability to unleash a sonic scream granted to Lord Commander Eidolon. 

The Sonic Shrieker can be used to unleash a brief but potent sonic barrage upon an opponent at close quarters, disorienting him, and leaving him open to a killing blow. A Sonic Shrieker can only be fitted to a power armour helmet, for it requires a connection to the miniature fusion reactor in the armour's backpack in order to fire, and even then, the Sonic Shrieker needs the better part of a solar hour to build up enough energy to fire once more when discharged.
