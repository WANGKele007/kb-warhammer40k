A Magna-Coil Autocannon is an automatic, self-loading, ballistic autocannon deployed on the Magna-Coil Grav Bikes used by the Hernkyn Pioneers of the Leagues of Votann that fires a high velocity hail of solid shells.

However, Kin weapons like Magna-Coil Autocannons are superior in almost every respect to those patterns of autocannon wielded by the agents and military of the Imperium, since the Kin employ superlative materials and methods in their construction and have no fear of further innovating on an ancient STC design. As a result, the Magna-Coil Autocannon and its variants often have longer ranges and more stopping power than any of their Imperial equivalents.
