The neural whip is an Imperial melee weapon in the form of a whip that may be manufactured as either a shock or Power Weapon.

The powered variant is primarily utilised by the Sisters of Battle, particularly the mistresses of the Sisters Repentia. The flailing, psycho-conductive neural whips are as much symbols of the rank of Sisters Repentia mistress as they are vicious weapons that lash out at the enemies of the Emperor before they are able to strike back.

The shock variant of the neural whip is more common, and used by a wide range of individuals from cruel overseers of Imperial labourers to murderous Heretics. While the shock neural whip has a variety of designs, the weapon is typically one-handed, thicker than a normal whip, with metallic lines providing the whip's electricity and each cord of the whip ending in a heavy-impact weight. 

At low settings neural whips are often used to "encourage" workers or Navis Imperialis and merchant fleet ratings to increase their productivity. Higher settings can stun an enemy or drop them dead in a single strike. Even unpowered, each blow from the whip can rip through flesh and primitive types of armour, causing painful and often deadly lacerations. Most neural whips of either the shock or powered variety are able to strike foes up to three metres away.
