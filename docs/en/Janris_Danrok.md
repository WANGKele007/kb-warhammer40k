Janris Danrok is the high factotum of the von Valancius Rogue Trader dynasty of the Koronus Expanse. Danrok is a native of the Civilised World of Dargonus that serves as the capital world of the von Valancius holdings in the region. High Factotum Danrok is a master of the mechanisms of interstellar trade: negotiations, compacts, endless records, bribes, threats, and the filling and emptying of a starship's great-holds. He's also responsible for maintaining the crew of the dynasty's flagship at strength and obtaining all needed supplies for the voidship.

Danrok is also in charge of all diplomatic relations between House von Valancius and the other factions of the Expanse, particularly other merchant organisations, and carries out all of the preliminary negotiations with other parties for the dynasty.

High Factotum Janris Danrok can also help to recruit mercenaries to fill out his Rogue Trader's retinue and arrange for training in a host of different martial and technical specialties so that the Rogue Trader can improve their skills and those of their retinue. 

Finally, Danrok maintains contact with the governments of all of the worlds in the von Valancius Protectorate in the Koronus Expanse, and is charged with the duty of briefing the Rogue Trader and their retinue on any events that occur across their realm. It is Danrock who provides his Lord Captain with options for new economic and infrastructure projects the Rogue Trader might choose to invest their dynasty's fortune in.
