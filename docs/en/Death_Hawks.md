The Death Hawks is a Loyalist Space Marine Chapter of unknown Founding and origin. A detachment from this Chapter participated with the Space Wolves in the failed defence of the world of Parenxes from the Red Corsairs.

Chapter History

Notable Campaigns

The Tyrant and the Wolf (995.999.M41) - The Chaos Lord Huron Blackheart staged a counterassault upon Parenxes, a world defended by detachments of the Death Hawks and the Space Wolves Space Marine Chapters. During the engagement, a series of Red Corsairs strike forces managed to board, disable and capture the Space Wolf Strike Cruiser, Wolf of Fenris after slaying Wolf Lord Gnyrll Bluetooth. The combined Loyalist Astartes strike force was ultimately destroyed by the piratical Heretic Astartes.
