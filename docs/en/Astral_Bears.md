The Astral Bears are a Loyalist Space Marine Chapter of the Ultima Founding composed entirely of Primaris Space Marines. 

Despite being a relatively new Chapter, they have already won many victories during the Era Indomitus that bring great honour to the memory of their primarch Jaghatai Khan.

Unlike the White Scars, the Astral Bears value resilience and durability over speed in battle, and are known to field multiple strike forces or even entire companies clad entirely in Mark X Gravis Power Armour when they go to war. 

Notable Astral Bears

Chapter Master Arcus - Arcus, master of the Astral Bears, is known to favour Mark X Gravis Power Armour over all the other Mark X variants and never goes to war clad in anything else.
