The Silver Drakes is a Loyalist Space Marine Chapter comprised entirely of Primaris Space Marines, created from an unknown genetic lineage and raised during the Ultima Founding of ca. 999.M41. 

After being raised, the Silver Drakes were tasked by the Imperium with turning back Hive Fleet Ouroboris in the Thracian Sector. Their Inceptor Squads cut a swathe through the hive fleet's sky-swarms, hunting the immense winged monsters at their heart. 

Though the Silver Drakes' tactics are effective, their numbers are still few. Isolated and outnumbered, the Chapter has dug in to fight a long, bloody, running war.

As they battled the hive fleet, the Chapter dispatched a request for aid which was answered by Commander Dante of the Blood Angels, the Regent of the Imperium Nihilus, who has sent the Charnel Guard, Blood Dragons and Sable Knights Chapters to aid the Silver Drakes in their fight. In addition, Craftworld Aeldari emissaries have also arrived in the Thracian Sector and offered their aid against Ouroboris.
