A force commander is the unofficial title given to an officer or non-commissioned officer of the Adeptus Astartes who commands a non-standard size force of Space Marines.

Astartes forces are usually led by their overall Chapter Master or a captain who commands one of the Chapter's companies.

However, when the forces of several different Chapters, or forces from more than one company of a Chapter, are engaged in battle, the overall command of the action goes to the most capable officer or non-commissioned officer on the field. 

For the length of the engagement, this individual is usually referred to as the "force commander", regardless of his actual formal rank.

Notable Force Commanders

Sergeant (later Captain) Aramus - Aramus was the force commander of various Blood Ravens Chapter strike forces during the Aurelian Crusades of the 41st Millennium.

Captain Indrick Boreale - Indrick Boreale served as the force commander of the 5 companies of Blood Ravens Space Marines deployed during the catastrophic Kaurava Campaign.
