The Intolerant was a warship of the Dark Angels Legion during the Great Crusade and Horus Heresy. A capital ship in service with the Dark Angels' Dreadwing, the Intolerant was outfitted with arcane weapons and technology dating back to the Age of Strife, including its primary weapon, the starship-sized Vortex Weapon known as an Annihilator Cannon.

During the Horus Heresy, the Intolerant used its Annihilator Cannon to unleash a devastating orbital bombardment against Traitor forces of the World Eaters and Word Bearers Legions at the Battle of Zepath.
