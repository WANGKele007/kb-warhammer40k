The Doom Court was one of the eight cohorts of the daemonic Blood Legion of Khorne commanded by the Bloodthirster Khulzhar. Khulzhar is a Bloodthirster of Unfettered Fury, one of eight Bloodthirsters who serve Ax'akhan, who himself is one of eight Bloodthirsters beneath Ghalh'kra the Infernus. 

Khulzhar led one of the many Blood Legions that took part in the Blood Crusade, and during the war for the Attila System, took personal command of the Herald of Khorne Un'grath the Undefeated's cohort -- the feared Doom Court. Below is the composition of the eight packs that comprised the Doom Court as it entered battle upon the Fortress World of Alexandrum.

Packs of the Doom Court

Boreblades - Comprised of Bloodletters.
Blackskulls - Comprised of Bloodletters.
Al'zharx's Slaughterpack - Comprised of Bloodletters.
The Horned Ones - Comprised of Bloodletters.
Dzarr's Red Fury - Comprised of Bloodletters.
Brass Thunder - Comprised of Bloodcrushers.
Hunters of Blood - Comprised of Flesh Hounds.
Black Claws - Comprised of Flesh Hounds.
