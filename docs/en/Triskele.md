A Triskele is a deadly Craftworld Aeldari ranged weapon composed of three blades that can be thrown in a great, coruscating arc that slices through anything in its path before returning to its owner. Only Exarchs of the Howling Banshees Aspect Shrine wield them. 

The weapon can also be used as a Power Weapon in close combat and is highly effective against even the most heavily-armoured foes. 

Jainas Mor

A unique version of this weapon, the Jainas Mor ("Silent Death" in the Aeldari Lexicon), is utilised by the Howling Banshees' Phoenix Lord, Jain Zar. The Jainas Mor is far more powerful than a standard Triskele.
