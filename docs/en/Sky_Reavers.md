Sky Reavers are a species of avian reptiles that hunt in large flocks. They can be found on the Feral World of Aurum within the Jericho Reach.

A single Sky Reaver is not a great threat. However, in large numbers they descend from the sky like a bladed storm. Indeed, their malleable cloud of avians easily disperses and reforms around incoming blows or weapon fire.
