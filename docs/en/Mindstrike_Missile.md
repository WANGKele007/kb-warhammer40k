A Mindstrike Missile is an anti-psyker weapon often utilised by the forces of the Grey Knights and the Inquisition.

A Mindstrike Missile contains clusters of psi-reactive munitions. They are effective against hordes of light troops, and particularly deadly against psykers.

Sources

Codex: Grey Knights (5th Edition), pg. 57
