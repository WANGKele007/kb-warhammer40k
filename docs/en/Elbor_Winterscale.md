Elbor Winterscale was a Rogue Trader of House Winterscale active in the Koronus Expanse in the mid-41st Millennium.

In 540.M41, travelling from the far reaches of Winterscale's Realm, Elbor reported being stalked by a "ghostly xenos ship with tattered sails." Though unknown to Elbor and his crew, it is believed this is the first recorded appearance of the Asuryani phantom ship Whisper of Anaris, the only survivor of the Chaos-corrupted craftworld of Lu'Nasad.
