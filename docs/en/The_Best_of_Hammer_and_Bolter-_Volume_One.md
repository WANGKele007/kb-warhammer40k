The Best of Hammer and Bolter: Volume One is a 2012 anthology edited by Christian Dunn. 

Synopsis

Hammer and Bolter is Black Library's digital magazine, bringing the best in Warhammer and Warhammer 40,000 short fiction every month. Now, for the first time in print, the editors are proud to present the best stories from the first twelve issues, with contributions from Black Library veterans like Dan Abnett, Aaron Dembski-Bowden, and Gav Thorpe, through to some rising stars and a host of new talent. Featuring every major faction and race from both universes, The Best of Hammer and Bolter: Volume One is the ultimate collection of sci-fi and fantasy short stories from the dark vaults of the Black Library.
