House Dunstan is a Loyalist Imperial Knight house of the Questor Mechanicus that has sworn itself to the service of the Adeptus Mechanicus. 

House History

Notable Campaigns

Prosperan Rift War (Unknown Date.M42) - House Dunstan sent 5 Imperial Knights to reinforce the Imperial troops stationed at the Harlecrypt System of the Prosperan Rift in their defence against the forces of the Thousand Sons Traitor Legion.

Notable Knights

None listed in current Imperial records.

Notable Personnel

None listed in current Imperial records.

House Appearance

House Colours

The colours of House Dunstan are blue and yellow.

House Arms

The arms of House Dunstan are not listed in current Imperial records.

Canon Conflict

This Knight house appears in the outdated 126th issue of the UK edition of White Dwarf magazine, and the image presented here does not mesh with more recent canon. 

The Knight shown above is presented as a Knight Lancer, but this type of Knight in its current incarnation does not use such a chassis.
