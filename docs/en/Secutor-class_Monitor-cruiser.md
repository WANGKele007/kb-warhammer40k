The Secutor-class Monitor-cruiser is a modified Lathe-class Monitor-cruiser used in the Calixis Sector by the Imperial Navy.

In an attempt to supplement a lack of larger cruisers within the sector, these monitor-cruisers are better-armed than other light cruisers, but less manoeuvrable.

Armament

Secutor-class Monitor-cruisers are bristling with many different starship-grade weapon types on its port, starboard, prow and dorsal sections.

Specifications

Dimensions - 4.3 kilometres long, 0.5 kilometres abeam approx.
Mass - 24 megatonnes
Crew - 50,000 approx.
Acceleration - 3.9 gravities maximum sustainable acceleration
