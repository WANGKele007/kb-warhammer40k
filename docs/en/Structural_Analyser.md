The Structural Analyser is a unique, prototype T'au signature support system only used by the Pathfinder known as Darkstrider. 

This device, worn over the eye, utilises a newly-developed form of sensor located in the backpack of Darkstrider's armour that scans for the critical weak points in enemy armour, before feeding this information back into the targeting computers of Darkstrider and the Pathfinder Team he leads.
