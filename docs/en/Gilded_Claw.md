The Gilded Claw is a Genestealer Cult that had infested the Imperial world of Mytosia II during the Era Indomitus. The world was assaulted by the Angels of Excelsis Chapter, a successor of the Blood Angels, in an attempt to clear the infestation. During the battle, Captain Lysis Valanir fell to the Black Rage and was deemed lost. The Blood Angels' High Chaplain Astorath the Grim fought alongside Valanir until they reached the Genestealer Patriarch of the Gilded Claw. Astorath watched as the captain killed the Genestealer Patriarch, but Lysis lost his own life during the fight.
