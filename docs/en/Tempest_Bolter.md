A Tempest Bolter is a long-range, rapid-fire bolter weapon with four barrels often deployed on Space Marine vehicles like the Gladiator Reaper grav tank that are specifically designed for an anti-infantry role.

Sources

Codex: Space Marines (9th Edition), pp. 182, 199
