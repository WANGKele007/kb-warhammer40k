An Eclipse Shield was a protective energy field technology that was a prototype variant of the Flare Shield based on the technology of the Selenite Cults of Luna. It was deployed on the aircraft used by the Legio Custodes during the Unification Wars, the Great Crusade and the Horus Heresy, such as the Ares Gunship and the Orion Assault Dropship.

Not only does the Eclipse Shield retard kinetic force and directed energy streams, but it also absorbs photons. Thus, when activated by heavy incoming fire, the shield appears as a midnight black energy field, completely obscuring the Legio Custodes vehicles it protects as they screams towards their drop zone or target, thus earning its name.
