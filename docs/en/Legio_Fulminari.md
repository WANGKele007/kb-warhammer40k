The Legio Fulminari is a Loyalist Titan Legion of the Collegia Titanica, one of the military arms of the Adeptus Mechanicus. 

Legion History

The Legio Fulminari came to the defence of the Realm of Ultramar during the Ultramar Campaign of the 13th Black Crusade in ca. 999.M41 when the forces of Abaddon the Despoiler sought unsuccessfully to prevent the resurrection of the Primarch Roboute Guilliman.

Following Guilliman's awakening and his renewed command over Imperial forces in Ultramar, he ordered the Legio Fulminari and elements of the Dark Angels Chapter of Space Marines to liberate the Ultramaran Civilised World of Ischara from the forces of Chaos.
