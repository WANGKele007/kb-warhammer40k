The Stone Hearts is a Loyalist Space Marine Chapter of unknown Founding and origin.

Almost nothing is known about this Chapter in Imperial records, except that their 4th Company is part of the retinue of Rogue Trader Tyrel "Destroyer" Cathek.

As such, they're likely to have taken part in the many purges ordered by their psychopathic master.

Notable Stone Hearts

Kelleth - Kelleth was a battle-brother captured by Fabius Bile and imprisoned at Dynikas V. He died trying to escape.

Chapter Appearance

Stone Hearts battle-brothers are known for marking their bodies with intricate tattoos that denote each battle that they have fought in.

Chapter Colours

The Stone Hearts' Chapter colours are not listed in current Imperial records.

Chapter Badge

The Stone Hearts' Chapter badge is not listed in current Imperial records.
