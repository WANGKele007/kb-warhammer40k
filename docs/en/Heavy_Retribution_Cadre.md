A Heavy Retribution Cadre is a T'au Empire Fire Caste cadre intended to break the most difficult concentrations of enemy force by infiltrating behind enemy lines under the most difficult of battlefield conditions. To this end, they are comprised of two KV128 Stormsurges and a team of
XV95 Ghostkeel Battlesuits, which makes this perhaps one of the most powerful types of cadre deployed by the Fire Caste in terms of sheer firepower.
