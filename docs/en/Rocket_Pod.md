A Rocket Pod, also called a Multi-Rocket Pod, Multiple-Rocket Pod or Missile Launcher Pod, is a dumb-fire missile launcher deployed on Imperial aircraft and vehicles that provide infantry and armour fire support, including Navis Imperialis Valkyrie and Vulture gunships and as external weapons deployed on Astra Militarum Support Sentinels.

They work by firing large numbers of small fragmentation rockets, which cover a large area in lethal shrapnel when they explode. They are primarily an anti-infantry weapon and are particularly effective against dense concentrations of poorly-armoured footsoldiers.

Any Sentinel outfitted with missile weapons will make use of both Frag and Krak Missiles for effectiveness in both anti-personnel and anti-armour roles when deployed with a Rocket Pod to provide fire support.
