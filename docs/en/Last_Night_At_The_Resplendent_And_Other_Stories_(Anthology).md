Last Night at the Resplendent and Other Stories is a 2021 store exclusive anthology. 

Synopsis

Contains the following short stories:

Embers of Extinction by Brandon Easton
The Dead Hours by David Guymer
Da Big Mouf by Danie Ware
Five Candles by Lora Gray
A Coin for the Carrion Thieves by John French
Last Night at the Resplendent by Sandy Mitchell
