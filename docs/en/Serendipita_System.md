The Serendipita System is a minor Imperial star system located in the Damocles Gulf region of the Lithesh Sector of the Segmentum Ultima, notable for hosting a massive brown dwarf orbited by multiple Human-habitable moons. This is the system where the Reclaimers Chapter tracked down and took possession of the space hulk Spawn of Damnation.

History

In 928.M41, the space hulk Spawn of Damnation emerged out of the Warp in the Serendipita System's outer halo of debris and entered a cometary orbit around its star. Shortly afterward, the strike cruiser Revenant of the Reclaimers Chapter of the Adeptus Astartes followed in pursuit, and swiftly located the hulk. The local System Defence Force mobilised to destroy the hulk and the Genestealer population that claimed it, but the Reclaimers forbade it and initiated salvage operations instead. The Serendipita SDF reluctantly helped the Space Marines blockade the hulk, so that the SDF's vessels could proceed to destroy Spawn of Damnation and the Revenant before they could infest the settled zone of the system if the Genestealers were triumphant.

The Spawn of Damnation was also home to an Ork warband which had boarded the hulk at its previous location, and the Greenskins were busily building an invasion force to launch against Serendipita. Fortunately, the Reclaimers' boarding party was accompanied by Commissar Ciaphas Cain, who managed to steer the two xenos species aboard the hulk into open warfare with each other. The conflict reduced the enemy numbers to the point that the Reclaimers were able to finish the job and claim the hulk for their own.

After the cleansing of the ancient vessel, the Reclaimers and their Adeptus Mechanicus allies salvaged archeotech and whatever else of strategic value could be found aboard the space hulk, taking the spoils to Fecundia, the nearest Forge World. The salvage operation continued until the hulk drifted out of the Serendipita System in 948.M41, at which point the Reclaimers abandoned it in preparation for its inevitable return to the Warp. A small contingent of Reclaimers volunteered to stay aboard the hulk as it disappeared into the Immaterium, in hopes of maintaining control over the vessel and resuming the valuable salvage operation when the Spawn of Damnation next returned to realspace.

Notable Worlds

Protostar - The Serendipita System's Sol-like primary star is orbited by a massive gas giant, a brown dwarf large enough to radiate appreciable heat in its own right as a "protostar," which sits in the Serendipita System's habitable zone. All of the system's habitable planets are actually moons orbiting the protostar, making them unusually convenient for interplanetary travel. The protostar is also orbited by an immense ring system, and is home to six other minor Civilised Worlds and Agri-worlds among the gas giant's satellites.

Serendipita - Serendipita, for which the system is named, is a pleasantly temperate, lightly populated Civilised World, with abundant oceans and small polar ice caps. The first of the system's planets to be settled by Humans, Serendipita is largely spared the necessity of heavy industry or farming by the existence of the other settled planets in the system, making it an attractive tourist destination.

Tarwen- Tarwen is a grimy and dour Industrial World, which provides most of the system's manufacturing needs.
