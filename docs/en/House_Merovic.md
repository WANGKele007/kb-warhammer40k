House Merovic was a Loyalist Imperial Knight House of the Imperium. There is very little information in Imperial records about this Knight House, apart from their valiant last stand on the Fortress World of Praesidium Primus, where they fought against the impossible numbers of the Tyranids of Hive Fleet Leviathan and the countless mobs of the Orks under the leadership of the Overfiend of Octarius.

House History

Notable Campaigns

The Battle of Praesidium Primus (Date Unknown) - The combined might of the Imperial armed forces, supported by a few companies of the Deathwatch Chapter of the Space Marines and the full might of the Knight House of Merovic, fought the hordes of Tyranids and Orks that had spilled from the Octarius Sector and into the Kurbynola System. Though House Merovic fought valiantly, they were totally annihilated to the last member.

Notable Knights

None listed in current Imperial records.

Notable Personnel

Patriarch Balas - Balas was the Patriarch of the Knight House of Merovic. He, like the rest of his House, fell on Praesidium Primus, while trying to hold off the countless hordes of Tyranids and Orks that were assaulting the planet.
