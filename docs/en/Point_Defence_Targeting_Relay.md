A Point Defence Targeting Relay is a T'au vehicle support system that aids a T'au skimmer's forward cupola-mounted secondary weapon systems. 

During battle, a Point Defence Relay will automatically target and engage enemies that try to assault the vehicle and/or nearby friendly troops, thus providing superior covering fire for nearby Fire Caste units.
