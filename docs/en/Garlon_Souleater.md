Garlon Souleater is a Heretic Astartes Chaos Sorcerer of the piratical Red Corsairs warband. The cadaverous and cruel Souleater is the Chaos Lord Huron Blackheart's most powerful sorcerer within the Red Corsairs. 

Souleater was once a Space Marine of the Mantis Warriors before betraying the Emperor and joining the Astral Claws, the Red Corsairs' predecessors, during the Badab War. It is said that he never removes his helmet because he is hideously mutated beneath it. 

History

Quest for the Ebon Talon

Garlon Souleater aligned himself with the up-and-coming warlord Verngar the Apostate during the Red Corsairs' hunt for the Tzeentchian Chaos relic known as the Ebon Talon in the Era Indomitus. Verngar, a rival for the Chaos Lord Huron Blackheart's control of the Red Corsairs, hoped to use recovery of the artefact as a way to fully control the powers of the Blackstone Fortress that Abaddon the Despoiler had given to the Red Corsairs before the Terran Crusade. In this way, Verngar believed he would cement his right to challenge Blackheart for leadership of the warband. 

After the recovery of the relic, during Verngar's assault on the Loyalist Invaders Chapter's homeworld of Ogrys, Souleater used his abilities to help Verngar control the power of the Ebon Soul, which required an immense amount of willpower to master. Even Blackheart was surprised that Verngar had gained enough mastery to unleash the relic's power, though both Souleater and the Warpsmith Turazan had aided Verngar in learning to control the artefact. 

When Verngar eventually lost control of the artefact as it dominated his psyche, Blackheart took the opportunity to end his rival's challenge, cutting off the errant warlord's hand to claim the relic for his own. Souleater managed to maintain his position within the Red Corsairs despite the failure of Verngar's challenge to Blackheart.
