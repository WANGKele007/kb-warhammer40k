An Eradicator, usually a member of an Eradicator Cadre, were veteran warriors of the Sisters of Silence's Chamber of Vigilance who were considered exemplars of their order. These sisters are called upon to form the vanguard of larger military actions and meet with psyker incursions at the leading edge of a greater Imperial military force using their battle-won experience to spearhead assaults.

Equipped with tactically flexible weaponry, Eradicator cadres are capable of adapting to any foe with equal effectiveness and are relied upon to provide fire support to more specialised cadres of the Silent Sisterhood in open warfare.
