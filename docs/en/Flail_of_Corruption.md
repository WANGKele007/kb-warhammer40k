A Flail of Corruption is a type of Power Weapon wielded by the Blightlord Terminators and Plague Marines of the Death Guard Traitor Legion. Like all weapons blessed by the Plague Lord Nurgle, a Flail of Corruption can infect any foe struck by it with a variety of deadly and extremely fast-acting pathogens.
