The Kindled Spirits was a thrallband of Heretic Astartes of the Thousand Sons Traitor Legion that was lost to an assault by the Drukhari and their Harelquin allies in the Webway during the event known as the First War in the Webway in 579.M37.

Warband History

Notable Campaigns

First War in the Webway (579.M37) - A coven of sorcerers from the Kindled Spirits thrallband conducted a great ritual in the Webway, hoping to gain access to the scream-filled city of Commorragh, the home of the Drukhari. Before their ritual was complete, hundreds of Drukhari, led by troupes of Harlequins, poured from an invisible portal and launched themselves at the Rubricae defending the coven. With their spell sundered, the Kindled Spirits counterattacked, their blasts of Warp-flame eventually breaching the fabric of the Webway itself. As the arterial walls of the Webway buckled and collapsed outwards, the backlash stranded the combatants in a shattered pocket-reality with no way out. There are whispers among the servants of the Ruinous Powers that the fighting has continued ever since, and that each warrior is fated to die and be reborn in an endless cycle for the rest of time.
