The Kapellan League is a minor league of the Leagues of Votann located in the galactic core. Though it is an ancient and well-established Kin confederacy, it is an ill-fated league, which has declined considerably over the Terran centuries in both power and influence.

History

The Kapellan League's ongoing decline greatly accelerated when, less than a Terran millennium ago, the Fane of Kôrynn's Kindred achieved self-awareness. This newly ascended Votann exhibited an aggressive drive for knowledge and a demanding appetite for raw materials, the better for Kôrynn's Kindred to enhance its physical shell and arm its Kin in its defence. Prior to this, Kôrynn's Kindred had been a relatively minor member of the faltering Kapellan League.

Blessed as they now were with their own Votann, they broke away to start the aggressive new league known as the Kronus Hegemony and took several other former Kapellan Kindreds with them.
