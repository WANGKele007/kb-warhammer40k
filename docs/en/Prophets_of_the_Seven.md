The Prophets of the Seven are a warband or vectorium of Heretic Astartes of the Death Guard Traitor Legion's 4th Plague Company. They are commanded by a Death Guard Sorcerer and are one of the few Death Guard warbands which likes to enter battle amidst living carpets of Nurglings, the Lesser Daemons of the Plague God Nurgle. 

Warband History

Nachtmund Rift War (Unknown Date.M42) - The Nachmund Rift War is an ongoing conflict being waged by the Imperium in the Nachmund Sub-sector. Closely connected to the War of Beasts and War of Nightmares fought earlier that kept the strategically-placed Sentinel World of Vigilus largely in Imperial hands so that it could continue to serve as one terminus of the Nachtmund Gauntlet through the Great Rift, the greater Nachmund Gauntlet corridor itself came under siege by the forces of Chaos. Abaddon the Despoiler remained determined to close off the Nachmund Ganutlet to Imperial forces to prevent Terra from reinforcing the isolated Imperial worlds in the Imperium Nihilus. The Prophets of the Seven were among the forces that the Death Guard committed to the combined Chaos effort in this campaign.

Warband Appearance

Warband Colours

The Prophets of the Seven's power armour is the same as that of the Death Guard, composed of decayed and cracked plates of ceramite discoloured in sickly greens and browns. Prophets of the Seven Chaos Space Marines, like those of the Death Guard, often wear grotesquely warped suits of the antique Mark III Iron Power Armour.

Warband Badge

The Prophets of the Seven's warband badge is usually a modified version of the standard Mark of Nurgle, similar to that used by the rest of the Death Guard.
