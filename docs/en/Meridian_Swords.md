Meridian Swords are twin Power Swords wielded by some members of the Adeptus Custodes. Custodians who use these weapons in close combat are highly-skilled in their use, and use them in such a way that they appear to be dancing through battle. 

Meridian Swords are secondary weapons for Custodians who get caught up in melee combat. They are only used until their Armour Thralls have had the time to reload the Bolt Casters built into the Guardian Spears and Sentinel Blades which serve as their primary ranged weapons.
