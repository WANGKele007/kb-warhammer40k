Note: This page is about the Loyalist Space Marine Chapter. For the World Eaters warband of Chaos Space Marines, please see Ravagers.

The Ravagers is a Codex Astartes-compliant Loyalist Space Marine Chapter of unknown Founding and origin. Almost nothing is known about this Chapter in Imperial records. Its fortress-monastery is a space station that orbits the world of Vindict V.

Chapter History

The Ravagers' only notable action occurred in the early 39th Millennium, when their entire Chapter was called upon by the Inquisition to perform Exterminatus upon the Genestealer-infested planet of Stalinvast.
