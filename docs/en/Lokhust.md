The Lokhust are a subsect of the insane Necron Destroyer Cult that seeks to destroy all organic life in the galaxy. These maddened Necrons have modified their necrodermis to utilise anti-gravity Destroyer platform bodies to dramatically enhance their mobility on the battlefield and reap all organic life in one of their "Red Harvests" through the use of heavy anti-armour and anti-infantry fire support weapons. 

The Lokhust have been observed to come in three basic types of units: a Lokhust Lord who often commands an entire Destroyer Cult, a Lokhust Destroyer, and a Lokhust Heavy Destroyer.
