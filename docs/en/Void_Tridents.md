The Void Tridents is a Loyalist Space Marine Chapter comprised entirely of Primaris Space Marines created from the lineage of the Ultramarines and raised during the Ultima Founding of ca. 999.M41.

Recruited from the Ocean World of Talassar in the Realm of Ultramar. The Chapter's warrior culture is strongly tied to the naval traditions of that world, which translates in combat-doctrine terms to a mastery of void-ship to void-ship warfare, boarding actions against orbital fortifications, low-gravity warfare and drop invasions of enemy-held worlds. 

However, under the command of Lord Commodore Theodro Vethrus, the Void Tridents have also proven themselves to be expert at planetary assaults.

The Void Tridents are one of the Chapters who have been assigned to protect the enlarged Realm of Ultramar in the wake of the Plague Wars by Lord Commander of the Imperium and Imperial Regent Roboute Guilliman.

Chapter History

Notable Campaigns

Indomitus Crusade (ca. 999.M41-012.M42) - Under its Chapter Master, Lord Commodore Theodro Vethrus, the Void Tridents were the first Chapter to cross the Nachmund Gauntlet from the world of Sangua Terra to Vigilus. The Void Tridents outran the Daemon fleet of Warpsmith Tzungdan as they cut a path past Omis-Prion. Had it not been for the distress signals their Librarian picked up regarding Vigilus, they would have likely been able to outmanoeuvre Tzungdan and destroy his base. In the end, they diverted to Vigilus and fought a series of board-and-slay missions against the Ork cordon there instead.

War of Beasts (Unknown Date.M42) - The Void Tridents sent three companies and one demi-company to help defend the strategically vital Sentinel World of Vigilus from xenos and Chaos assaults.

Nachmund Rift War - Siege of Dharrovar (Unknown Date.M42) - The Nachmund Rift War is an ongoing conflict being waged by the Imperium in the Nachmund Sub-sector in the Era Indomitus. While the Fortress World of Vigilus itself continues to remain largely in Imperial hands in the wake of the ongoing War of Beasts and War of Nightmares on Vigilus, the greater Nachmund Gauntlet finds itself besieged by the forces of Chaos to claim that incredibly important strategic prize which unites the Imperium Nihilus and the Imperium Sanctus through the Great Rift. More than ninety battle-brothers of the Void Tridents under the command of Captain Julianis Mikon of the 5th Company participated in the failed Imperial assault on the Chaos Knight World of Dharrovar.

Notable Void Tridents

Lord Commodore Theodro Vethrus - Theodro Vethrus is the first and current Chapter Master, or "Lord Commodore," of the Void Tridents. The lord commodore was originally a noble-born native of Talassar who now serves as the ducal regent of Talassar due to the frequent absences of Captain Cato Sicarius of the Ultramarines, who serves as the Grand Duke of Talassar, from the world in service to the Emperor and Roboute Guilliman. With the Void Tridents maintaining an indefinite military presence on this world, Theodro Vethrus spends much of his time on his homeworld dealing with matters of state as much as those of his Chapter.

Captain Julianis Mikon - Mikon is the captain of the Void Tridents' 5th Company and commanded them and other Void Tridents elements in the Siege of Dharrovar during the Nachmund Rift War of the Era Indomitus.

Chaplain Larus Ennekis - Ennekis was attached to the 5th Company under the command of Captain Mikon during the Siege of Dharrovar of the Nachmund Rift War.

Epistolary Caius Vibion - Vibion was attached to the 5th Company under the command of Captain Mikon during the Siege of Dharrovar of the Nachmund Rift War.

Archab - Archab in an Intercessor who served in the 2nd Squad (Battleline) of the 4th Company.

Chapter Fleet

Spear of Glaudor (Strike Cruiser) - During the Nachmund Rift War, the Spear of Glaudor was part of the force that the Void Tridents committed to the Siege of Dharrovar.

Chapter Appearance

Chapter Colours

The Void Tridents wear the dark blue of the southern Talassarian oceans echoed in their Mark X Power Armour. The Aquila or Imperialis on the chest is copper, as is the shoulder pauldron trim and the helm. The inset of the right shoulder pauldron is white.

The Chapter's colours are reminiscent of the strong naval traditions of its homeworld of Talassar -- the blue represents the sea, while the white and copper are both maritime colours, the white of a ship's sails and the copper of its fittings.

A sergeant is designated by a white stripe on the helmet. A lieutenant is designated by a red and white stripe on the helmet.

Chapter markings and unit designations are red. The red squad specialty symbol -- battleline, close support, fire support, Veteran or command -- is displayed on the right shoulder pauldron, while the inset of the left shoulder pauldron is red and proudly displays the Chapter's iconography.

The company number is displayed as a red High Gothic numeral on the left knee plate and the squad number as a black High Gothic numeral placed in the centre of the squad specialty symbol on the right shoulder pauldron.

Chapter Badge

The Void Tridents' Chapter badge is a red trident pointed upward, centred on a field of white.
