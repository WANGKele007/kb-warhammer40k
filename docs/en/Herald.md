A Herald was a Space Marine who possessed the bygone specialist rank of consul in the ancient Space Marine Legions who served within both the Loyalist and Traitor Legions as well as the Blackshields -- Space Marine outcasts and marauders of uncertain allegiances or origin -- during the Horus Heresy. They carried the standards and other sacred totems of their Legions, chapters, companies and other organisations. 

As the wars of the Heresy spread to consume the galaxy, billions fought and died under the banners of warlords they had never seen or heard firsthand, and even amongst those such as the Legiones Astartes, near-religious fervour became common for those artefacts touched by primarch, Warmaster or Regent, and given to a chosen emissary as a sign of authority and favour. 

The Blackshields, having obscured their heraldry or cast aside former masters were no different, their strange sigils or blackened flags becoming totems of destruction and the foresworn. Amongst the Blackshields, these emissaries were known as "Dark Heralds."
