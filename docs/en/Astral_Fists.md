The Astral Fists is a Loyalist Space Marine Chapter of unknown Founding and origin that operates within the Ultima Segmentum. The Astral Fists have yet to leave their mark in Imperial history, so very little is currently known about this Chapter.

Both the Astral Fists and the Angels Eradicant sent small contingents to investigate the last distress call of the now-extinct Marines Mordant Chapter on their homeworld of Vitrea Mundi in the Maelstrom Zone after that Chapter came under assault by the insidious forces of the Alpha Legion.

The Chapter's only notable engagement was when it was ordered to engage the Tyranids Echidna Splinter Fleet sometime during the 41st Millennium.

Chapter Appearance

Chapter Colours

The Astral Fists' Chapter colours are not listed in current Imperial records.

Chapter Badge

The Astral Fists' Chapter badge is a clenched silver fist.
