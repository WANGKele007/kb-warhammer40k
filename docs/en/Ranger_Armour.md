Ranger Armour is the light personal protection worn by Craftworld Aeldari Rangers on the Path of the Outcast. The armour worn by Rangers is a special mesh suit with wraithbone carapace plates attached to it to provide enhanced protection, while still maximising ease of movement and the ability to move quietly and undetected. As a result, Ranger Armour is very flexible, which allows Rangers to move more freely. However, this flexibility comes at the expense of increased protection, as this armour is useless against bolter fire or powerful directed energy weapons.
