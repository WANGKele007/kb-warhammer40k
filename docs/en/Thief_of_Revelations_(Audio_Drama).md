Thief of Revelations is the sixteenth audio drama for the Horus Heresy Series that was not originally released as part of an anthology or other release. Thief of Revelations was published online in November 2013 by the Black Library as an audio CD that included the audio drama Hunter's Moon. The audio drama was later republished in prose format as an e-book and as a stand-alone audio CD. The title was also published as part of the Legacies of Betrayal main series anthology novel and again as a part of The Razing of Prospero omnibus.

Official Synopsis

The Thousand Sons -- a Legion whose destiny was irrevocably altered at Prospero, and yet who now seem to dance only to fate's tune. Ahzek Ahriman and Magnus the Red cast their psychic sight over the galaxy, seeking any clue as to what the future might hold and where their true allegiance should ultimately be placed.
