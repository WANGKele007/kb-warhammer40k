The Wheels of Excruciation are autonomous Warp constructs dedicated to the service of Slaanesh that can be called into realspace by the servants of the Dark Prince to attack their foes.

The Wheels of Excruciation whirl around the minarets of the Dark Prince's palace in the Realm of Chaos as flocks of crows might whirl around a temple's spires.

They can be called into reality by a devotee of Slaanesh to lacerate and even decapitate their enemies, hunting down flesh untainted by the touch of Chaos corruption and delivering the agony of uncounted cuts.
