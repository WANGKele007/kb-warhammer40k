Carleon was a Firstborn sergeant of the Blood Angels' 8th Company. In the aftermath of the Devastation of Baal in the Era Indomitus, he was tasked with purging Baal Secundus, one of the moons of the Blood Angels' Chapter planet of Baal, of the remaining Tyranids of Hive Fleet Leviathan. 

At the same time, he was also ordered to assess the performance of the Chapter's newly-integrated Primaris Space Marines, who had been delivered to the Blood Angels by Primarch Roboute Guilliman's Indomitus Crusade fleet in the final days of the Battle of Baal. 

Carleon was subsequently promoted to lieutenant during the Purge of Baal Secundus.
