A Graviton Blast Cannon is a man-portable Grav Weapon used by the military forces of the Leagues of Votann. It is a favoured weapon among the squads of the Brôkhyr Thunderkyn. It unleashes a directed energy particle beam of gravitons towards a target that dramatically increases the local gravity, crushing the foe under its own mass. 

However, Kin weapons like Graviton Blast Cannons are superior in almost every respect to those patterns of Grav Weapons wielded by the agents and military of the Imperium, since the Kin employ superlative materials and methods in their construction and have no fear of further innovating on an ancient STC design. As a result, the Grav Blast Cannon and its variants often have longer ranges and more stopping power than any of their Imperial equivalents.
