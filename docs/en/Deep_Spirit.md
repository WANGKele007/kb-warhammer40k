A Deep Spirit, also called a Master Program or a Tomb Mind, is a form of artificially intelligent command and control cogitator program employed by the Necrons. These intelligent master control systems manage the affairs of a Tomb World's complexes and ensure the well-being of the slumbering Necrons within their subterranean stasis-crypts while they are still locked within the Great Sleep. Deep Spirits are primarily responsible for commanding and coordinating the swarms of robotic Canoptek constructs and the other automated defences of their Tomb World's stasis-crypts and they react violently to any unwanted intrusions.
