The Hyperactive Nymune Organ is an organ present in the anatomy of the Kroot species that stores great amounts of biochemical energy, which activates in certain dire, emergency situations such as combat to increase the speed of the Kroot's metabolism, improving their raw muscle speed, reflexes, and reaction time.

It is not only genetic baseline Kroots but also other organisms who originally evolved from baseline Kroots like a Great Knarloc or a Kroot Hound that can possess this organ and its metabolic benefits.
