The Games Day Anthology 2012/13 is an anthology book published by the Black Library. The book was first released exclusively at Games Day Australia and Games Day Birmingham in 2012. The book contains several short stories from different Games Workshop properties. The short stories contained within were later released separately.

Synopsis

No official synopsis has been released.

Contents

The Games Day Anthology 2012/13 contains the following short stories:

Horus Heresy

Distant Echoes of Old Night by Rob Sanders

Warhammer 40,000

Extinction by Aaron Dembski-Bowden
The Blessing of Iron by Anthony Reynolds
The Memory of Flesh by Matthew Farrer
Perihelion by Dan Abnett

Warhammer Fantasy

Master of Mourkain by Dan Abnett
