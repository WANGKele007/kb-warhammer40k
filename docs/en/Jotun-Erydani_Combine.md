The Jotun-Erydani Combine is a league of the Leagues of Votann located in the galactic core. The Kindreds of the Jotun-Erydani Combine are renowned for their craftsmanship and the technologies they forge.

Trivia

The name "Jotun" is a play on jötunn (plural: jötnar), a type of supernatural being in much Germanic mythology. In Norse mythology, they are often contrasted with the gods (Æsir and Vanir) and other non-human figures, such as dwarfs and elves, although the groupings are not always mutually exclusive. The jötnar typically dwell across boundaries from the gods and humans in lands such as Jötunheimr. Although the term "giant" is sometimes used to gloss the word "jötnar" and its apparent synonyms in some translations and academic texts, jötnar are not necessarily notably large.

The use of the name here accords with the general reliance upon Norse imagery and mythology used in the fictional culture of the Leagues of Votann.
