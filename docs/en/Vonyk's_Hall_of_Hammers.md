Vônyk's Hall of Hammerers is a hold of the Ymyr Conglomerate of the Leagues of Votann located in the galactic core. Its Forge is famed across Kin space for the skill of its Brôkhyr, who produce machines and other advanced technologies of sublime might.

Codex: Leagues of Votann (9th Edition), pg. 30
