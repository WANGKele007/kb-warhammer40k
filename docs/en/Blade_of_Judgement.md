A Blade of Judgement is a relic power weapon of the Blood Angels Chapter that can take the form of a greatsword, axe or glaive, and was especially designed to kill other Space Marines.

History

The Blades of Judgement were originally crafted for the ancient Ofanim of the Blood Angels Legion who were tasked with ensuring that those of the bloodline of the primarch Sanguinius who fell to the genetic curse of the Red Thirst were swiftly executed. Later, the Ofanim were deployed during the Horus Heresy as elite troops who could execute Heretic Astartes with the same skill and efficiency.

To this end, the Blades of Judgement wielded by the Ofanim were said to be as much symbols of the wielder's grim office as they were weapons, assembled from blanks forged by an anonymous maker, for their intended purpose was too despicable for any artisan to assume credit. 

The true number of these arms is uncertain, but there are known to exist weapons of this type in the form of greatswords, axes and glaives each united in the sole purpose of executing those of Space Marine transhuman physiology that were used by the Ofanim.
