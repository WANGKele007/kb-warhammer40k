A Unified Targeting Matrix is a T'au Battlesuit Support System used to coordinate special formations of XV88 Broadside Battlesuits known as Broadside Destructor Phalanxes. 

Only issued to the formation's attached T'au Commander with the rank of Shas'el, a Unified Targeting Matrix allows for the combined fire of the formation's Heavy Rail Rifles to demolish even the most heavily armoured target with ease.
