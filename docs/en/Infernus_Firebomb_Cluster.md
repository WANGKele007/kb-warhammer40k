An Infernus Firebomb Cluster was a series of incendiary munitions that could be dropped by the Legio Custodes Ares Gunship on entrenched enemy positions. 

The Infernus Firebombs unleashed a blazing inferno that could boil the bodily fluids and crisp the flesh of the crews of armoured vehicles and static fortifications alike. The cluster was carried under the Ares' ventral hull surface.
