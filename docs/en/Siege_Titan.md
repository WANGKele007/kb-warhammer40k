A Siege Titan was a Titan of the Collegia Titanica deployed during the Great Crusade and the Horus Heresy. It was among the largest grades of Titan, standing taller and possessing more tonnage than even a Warlord-class Battle Titan.

A Siege Titan, as the name implies, was designed specifically to assault and destroy enemy fixed fortifications. The Siege Titan's armament reflected this tactical role, as it was armed with a Disruption Field-wreathed wrecking ball on one arm and a multi-headed siege drill on the other.

Siege Titans were deployed at the Battle of Beta-Garmon, remembered as the "Titandeath," during the Horus Heresy.

Notable Siege Titans

Atranican (Siege Titan) - Atranican was a Siege Titan of an unknown Titan Legion that fought at the Battle of Beta-Garmon during the Horus Heresy and was destroyed during the combat on Beta-Garmon II.
