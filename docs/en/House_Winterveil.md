House Winterveil is a Loyalist Imperial Knight House of the Questor Imperialis, directly aligned with the Imperium of Man. 

There is very little information in Imperial records about this Knight House, and it is unknown from which Knight World it hails. 

However, the ebon Knights of House Winterveil have thwarted dozens of Drukhari raids in the systems surrounding their home world, earning bitter enmity from the scions of Commorragh.
