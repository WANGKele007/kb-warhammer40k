Redemption On Dal'yth by Phil Kelly is a Warhammer 40,000 short story published in 2020 and collected in the anthology Nexus & Other Stories. 

Synopsis

The T'au are a technologically advanced xenos race whose rapidly expanding empire and lethal battlesuit technology threatens the Imperium. Divided into four castes and guided by the enigmatic Ethereals, the T'au work tirelessly in furtherance of their all-consuming philosophy: the Greater Good. 

On Dal'yth, a pair of living statues stand in stasis. Commander Sha'kanthas, renowned battlesuit pilot, is locked in a duel with his opponent, Space Marine Chapter Master Durian Raevor who attempted to conquer the planet centuries earlier. Were the stasis field to suddenly falter, the two warriors could wreak untold havoc upon the T'au's peaceful training facility...
