The Blood Hounds of Gryrut were a Blackshield Titan Legion.

Legion History

The Blood Hounds of Gryrut were a Blackshield Titan Legion who were active during the Horus Heresy. Comprising a single maniple of Warhound-class Titans, they carved out a stellar empire for themselves within the Segmentum Obscurus as the Heresy raged around them.
