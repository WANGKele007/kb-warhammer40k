A Reclamation Pool is a Tyranid biomechanical technology that is used to break down the biomass a hive fleet is seeking to strip from one of its prey planets. 

All of the biomass collected by the invading Tyranids bioforms is placed into the digestive enzymes within a Reclamation Pool where they are broken down into a putrid slurry of amino acids, carbohydrates, fatty acids, nucleic acids, various minerals and all the other basic molecular building blocks of organic life. 

This biomass slurry is then pumped by the plant-like bioconstructs called Capillary Towers that begin to spring up all over a world infested by Tyranids into low orbit where it is devoured by the hive fleet's Hive Ships.

Role

Every Tyranid infestation of a new world proceeds through the same stages. First comes the identification and infiltration of a prey planet, when Mycetic Spores land vanguard reconnaissance bioforms such as Genestealers and Lictors. These creatures become active, probing the local population's defences, if any, and assessing the world's ability to resist an assault. 

Next comes the subjugation phase, when the planet's defenders are assaulted and overwhelmed by swarms of Tyranid warrior bioforms. After all major resistance has largely been overcome, the consumption phase of the planetary biosphere begins as the world's atmosphere and native flora and fauna is altered by the growth of the biomechanical constructs known as Spore Chimneys and Capillary Towers. 

Even as the more militant warrior bioforms continue to battle the planet's defenders to eliminate all remaining resistance, other, more utilitarian bioforms begin sweeping up behind them, collecting all of the available biomass whether in its natural state or altered by the introduction of Tyranid microorganisms. Some of the substrates collected are used to generate additional Tyranid bioforms by the Hive Mind to complete the conquest.

In this manner, the prey planet's natural resources are turned against it, providing additional tools for the swarm. The majority of the collected biomass, however, is set aside to feed the Hive Ships orbiting the planet. Those massive biomechanical organisms generally delay their feeding until after the planet has been completely secured.

Near the battlefront, untold thousands of maggot-like Rippers and Malanthropes are deployed from spores dropped from orbit to form massive, tightly packed swarms. With fang-filled jaws, they devour every organic substance they can find -- living or dead.

Once these abominations have eaten all they can, they slowly drag their bloated bodies towards the digestive Reclamation Pools formed by the actions of the Capillary Towers and Spore Chimneys. Rather than spewing the devoured biomass into the pools, the Rippers simply hurl themselves in, to be digested along with all that they devoured.

New Rippers are continuously created to propagate this cycle of gluttony. As the planet's surface is depleted, similar bioforms deplete all of the life in the oceans and within the planet's crust. Eel-like Rippers swarm through the oceans in massive swarms devouring all of the native sealife. Burrowing variants expand the tunnels dug by Trygons and Raveners, consuming subterranean fungus, root systems, and any burrowing fauna. Mineral reserves are also depleted, particularly rare ores that may be incorporated into the exoskeletons of the various weapon biomorphs used by the warrior bioforms.

The Reclamation Pools are then harvested by the hive ships which enter low orbit and pump the gene-rich biological soup on board using the Capillary Towers, whose tips now stretch into the upper atmosphere.
