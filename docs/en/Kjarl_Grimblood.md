Kjarl Grimblood is a Wolf Lord of the Space Wolves Chapter, one of twelve legendary warriors who lead the Great Companies into battle. Kjarl Grimblood is a fierce rival of Sven Bloodhowl. He and his Great Company, called the Grimbloods, bear the sigil of the Fire Wolf. In Fenrisian myth the Fire Wolf burns without being consumed, and it is his voice of flame that melts the snows and thaws the glaciers before each season.

They say that Kjarl's foresight is so supernaturally acute that it is whispered that he possessed the Gift, and that he is able to see the future in the flames, though none say it in his presence. His Great Company favours a great many flame weapons, boasting no fewer than twelve Land Raider Redeemers.

So it is that the Grey Hunters of the Grimbloods have a special rite of passage -- once a Grey Hunter has killed his prey with flame, they have earned the right to paint their face with blood before each battle, the crimson flames upon their countenance marking them as a Red Hunter for all to see.

History

Kjarl Grimblood fought with the Draxian Hegemony in the Battle of Medes 841.

In the 41st Millennium, during the Hunt for the Wulfen, Kjarl and his warriors defeated a host of Daemons led by the Blue Scribes in the Hades Reach and recovered a handful of Wulfen. 

During the Siege of the Fenris System in 999.M41, Kjarl and his Grimbloods fought with the Daemons of Tzeentch in the ancient heartwoods of Oshva Weald on Fenris itself. Kjarl divided his forces in two parts and managed to catch the Daemons in a pincer formation. Caught between the two jaws of Grimblood's trap, the Flamers of Tzeentch were annihilated.

After the battle Kjarl had a vision where The Fang was engulfed in magical flames, so the Wolf Lord shared this information with the only person he could trust -- Rune Priest Svangthir Ashbeard, as Kjarl feared for others to learn that he was prone to strange "visions". The wise Rune Priest listened to the Wolf Lord and agreed with the vision because his own divinations had revealed to him the same thing -- soon The Fang would be attacked by Chaos forces.

A mighty ward against enemy attacks, these powerful girdles incorporate potent gravitic force fields that protect the wearer.
