The Exo-armour Grenade Launcher is a grenade launcher mounted on the shoulder of the exo-armour suit worn by the Leagues of Votann Einhyr Hearthguard. It can provide some medium-ranged explosive firepower for the Einhyr.

Sources

Codex: Leagues of Votann (9th Edition), pp. 94, 101
