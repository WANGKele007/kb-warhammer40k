A Voidscale Cloak was an advanced protective garment worn by the Sisters of Silence during the Great Crusade and Horus Heresy.

Though they were similar in design to the sisters' Voidsheen Cloaks, Voidscale Cloaks utilised a denser and more efficient vitreous mesh. The larger, more refractive scales the cloaks employed were also capable of deflecting far greater kinetic force and absorbing even the most destructive forms of directed energy and radiation.
