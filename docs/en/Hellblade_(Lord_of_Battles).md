A Hellblade is a massive Chain Weapon that is used by the greatest of all Daemon Engines, the Lord of Battles. 

The Lord of Battles is a Daemon Engine of the Chaos God of war and bloodshed, Khorne, and as such it is armed with some of the most devastating close combat weapons of any Daemon Engine. 

The Hellblade takes the form of a Dreadnought Chainfist that has been greatly enlarged and is easily the size of a Leman Russ Battle Tank.

The weapon is usually mounted on the Lord of Battles' right arm, and is used in conjunction with the massive Death Storm Cannon mounted on the war machine's left arm. 

The Hellblade is fully capable of tearing enemy vehicles to pieces, and is also able to be used effectively against smaller Titans, particularly the Warhound-class Scout Titan and the Reaver-class Battle Titan. It is unknown if the Hellblade is used by any other Chaos units, such as Chaos Titans.
