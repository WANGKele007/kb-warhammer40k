Echoes of Imperium is the second audio drama anthology for The Horus Heresy series. Echoes of Imperium was first released as an audio disk in September 2016, and contains three original audio dramas and a dramatised version of The Shadowmasters.

Official Synopsis

Mankind is divided, the galaxy wracked by great and impenetrable Warp Storms. War rages from the far-flung reaches of the Ultima Segmentum, through the wilderness of the void, all the way to ancient Terra. And where once there was a single, united Imperium, now there are two...

Contents

Echoes of Imperium contains the following Horus Heresy Series audio dramas:

The Herald of Sanguinius by Andy Smillie
Stratagem by Nick Kyme
The Watcher by CZ Dunn
The Shadowmasters by Gav Thorpe
