Pharos is the thirty-forth volume in the Horus Heresy series of novels.

Synopsis

Pharos is part of the Imperium Secundus story arc, and is the direct sequel to Vulkan Lives (Book 26), Unremembered Empire (Book 27), The Heart of Pharos (Audio Drama), and A Safe and Sheltered Place (Short Story). With the noble Emperor Sanguinius ruling from Macragge, Imperium Secundus stands as a lone beacon of hope even as the Warmaster’s forces continue to ravage the rest of the galaxy. Roboute Guilliman, still Master of Ultramar, has convinced his brother that Terra has fallen and that the mysterious Mount Pharos on Sotha now holds the key to mankind’s future. But the Night Lords, those cruel and pitiless sons of Konrad Curze, have been watching from the shadows, and make ready to launch their long-planned attack on the Pharos itself…
