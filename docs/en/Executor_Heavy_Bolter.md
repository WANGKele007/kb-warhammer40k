An Executor Heavy Bolter is a variant of Heavy Bolter utilised primarily by Primaris Marine Heavy Intercessors. It has a longer range, does more damage and is capable of greater armour penetration than the standard Astartes pattern Heavy Bolter. It possesses an elongated, box-like magazine for the bolt ammunition which is embossed with a skull and bones symbol.
