The Black Angel, also called the Angel of Darkness, is a psychic entity that exists in the Warp and takes on the form of an angel cloaked in black armour. The Black Angel, like his counterpart the Golden Angel or Sanguinor, is closely related to the Blood Angels Chapter of the Adeptus Astartes and the people of their homeworld of Baal.

Transcending the linear passage of time in the ageless existence of the Immaterium, the Black Angel engages in an eternal battle in the Warp against the Sanguinor. Just as the Sanguinor seems to be an embodiment of the best part of the psyche of the slain primarch Sanguinius and his gene-sons among the Blood Angels and their successors in the Sanguinary Brotherhood, so is the Dark Angel a psychic entity that embodies the Flaws of Sanguinius' and the Blood Angels' nature such as the Black Rage. The Sanguinor is strengthened every time one of the sons of Baal protects the life of an innocent, but the Black Angel grows in power every time one of the scions of Sanguinius falls to the Black Rage.

The xenos civilisation that called Baal home before the coming of Humanity to those worlds in the Age of Technology as well as intelligent species that will come to Baal after Mankind has left have all been influenced by the eternal struggle of these strange entities.

Sometime after the Devastation of Baal in the Era Indomitus an apparent avatar of Sanguinius appeared before Chief Librarian Mephiston of the Blood Angels and revealed that he is in fact the vessel that will become the Angel of Darkness, but since time does not pass in a linear fashion within the Warp he has in fact always existed. The entity stated that if Mephiston became a vessel for the Black Angel, he could use his body as a prison and save the Blood Angels from their ultimate fate of falling to the Black Rage for a time at the cost of his own soul. Mephiston apparently accepted the bargain, though the truth of the matter was unclear.
