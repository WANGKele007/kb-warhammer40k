A Lightning Lock is a directed energy weapon deployed on the Armiger Pattern Knight Moirax. Moirax Knights have the ability to overcharge their own, unstable Rad Furnace reactor cores in order to supplement the power of their ionising locks, unleashing the surge of power in sustained electromagnetic blasts of energy over short ranges.
