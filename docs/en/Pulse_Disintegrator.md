The Pulse Disintegrator is a particle beam weapon deployed by the Drukhari on many of their more powerful vehicles, such as the Tantalus assault skimmer.

These weapons fire rapid pulses of unstable subatomic matter, vaporising their targets. Unlike the crude, directed-energy Plasma Weapons of more primitive species like Humanity, Drukhari Pulse Disintegrators are able to maintain a high rate of fire without overheating.
