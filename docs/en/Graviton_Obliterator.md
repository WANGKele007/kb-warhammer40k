A Graviton Obliterator is a Titan-grade Grav Weapon deployed during the Great Crusade and Horus Heresy on Reaver-class Titans that unleashes a directed energy particle beam of gravitons towards a target that dramatically increases the local gravity, crushing the foe under its own mass. 

The Graviton Obliterator possesses a longer range and more stopping power than its smaller Titan-grade counterpart, the Graviton Destructor. It is also more effective against heavily armoured enemies. It can destroy a structure and pulverise any foes sheltering within simultaneously.
