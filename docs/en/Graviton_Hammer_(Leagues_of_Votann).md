A Graviton Hammer is a melee Graviton Weapon in the form of a single-handed warhammer used by the military forces of the Leagues of Votann. Like all Graviton Weapons the Graviton Hammer works by dramatically increasing the local gravity, crushing the foe under its own mass after a bone-breaking strike from the hammer.

A Graviton Hammer is the standard melee weapon used by Brôkhyr Iron-masters when they are operating in the field to maintain and repair their Oathband's vehicles and wargear.
