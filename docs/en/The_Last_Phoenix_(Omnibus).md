The Last Phoenix is the second omnibus novel of the The Horus Heresy series.

Synopsis

Only in perfection can evil flourish. With the Space Marine Legions falling upon one another in open battle, the most flawed of the mighty primarchs began to pursue their own agendas, seeking out whatever destinies they imagined for themselves under Horus' new order. Fulgrim, the Phoenician lord of the Emperor's Children Legion, had always striven to outshine his brothers -- but even he could never have guessed the depths of betrayal to which he would eventually sink in pursuit of greater power. In strength, there is weakness. In beauty, horror.
