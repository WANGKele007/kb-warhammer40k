Primus Nullificator was an officer rank of the Space Marine Legions created during the Horus Heresy who were tasked with the esoteric duties of combating Daemons and other Warp entities. Primus Nullificator was one of several specialty ranks that fell under the generalised banner of the mid-tier Legion officer rank of consul, formally known as a Legiones Consularis in High Gothic, which was awarded to an individual Astartes who was recognised for their unique talents and hard-won skills.

With the increase of uncontrollable occurrences of Daemonic incursion across the galaxy during the Horus Heresy, many officers within the Legiones Astartes on both sides of the conflict resorted to arcane and superstitious means to counter the little-understood threat presented by the Warp entities. 

These pioneers sanctioned research into esoteric artefacts and weapons and openly sought prohibited lore in the hope that it would provide wisdom capable of combatting the galaxy's intangible horrors. These Space Marine officers, who were first among the Nullificators, forged an example and encouraged those serving under them to also seek out the answers to the arcane threat they now faced by any means necessary.

Role

Only as the Traitor forces of Warmaster Horus revealed the true corruption of their hearts by the Ruinous Powers did the Primus Nullificators begin to appear in the order of battle of the Legiones Astartes. The first such units were ad-hoc assemblies of veteran warriors wielding hastily manufactured weaponry and poorly understood superstition in a brave attempt to stem the tide of Chaos madness Horus had unleashed upon the Imperium. 

Few of these valiant warriors would survive, but the wisdom bought with their lives earned their successors all the better. By the final years of the Horus Heresy, the Nullificators had become a highly disciplined arm of the Space Marine Legions, forged in the crucible of desperation to counter the threat of the foulest xenos breeds and the Daemonic incursions that had become all too frequent.

Unit Composition

1 Primus Nullificator

Wargear

Cataphractii Pattern Terminator Armour 

Hexagrammic Wards - Varying widely in form and efficacy based on both the origin and experience of the unit, wards either etched into or worn upon the armour of Nullificators quickly became a standard feature of their panoply of war as the terrors of the Horus Heresy veered further into the realms of madness. By the end of the Scouring, such measures were codified as a sub-surface pattern of etched warding runes, formed of rare alloys and mineral compounds, but during the earliest years of the Horus Heresy often amounted to little more than half-understood scrawlings and pagan charms.

Aether-Shock Maul - Based on a similar technology to the Volkite Weapons developed on Mars, these vicious Shock Mauls discharged pulses of stored actinic energy when they struck a foe, which could prove particularly damaging to the physical corpus of Daemons materialised in realspace.
