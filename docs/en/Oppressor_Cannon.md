An Oppressor Cannon is a powerful ballistic weapon deployed as a main turret weapon on the Astra Militarum's Rogal Dorn Battle Tank. 

This enormous artillery piece is a devastating anti-armour powerhouse, capable of knocking out almost anything up to a Chaos Knight in a single salvo -- and it still has room for a co-axial Autocannon to the side.
