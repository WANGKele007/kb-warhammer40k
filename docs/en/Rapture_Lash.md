A Rapture Lash is a Power Weapon in the form of a barbed metallic whip that ends in a vicious hook. In the hands of a skilled wielder, it can be used to entangle, scourge and eviscerate or disembowel an opponent. 

It is a favoured close combat weapon of many Emperor's Children Legion Heretic Astartes who enjoy the sensations of terrible pain and anguish unleashed when they use the weapon upon their foes. This includes the Obsessionists who command squads of melee-focused Infractors and range-focused Tormentors, and of the Lords Exultant.
