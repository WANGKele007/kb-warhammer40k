Selicia is a canoness of the Order of Our Martyred Lady, an Order Militant of the Adepta Sororitas. She led a force of the Sisters of Battle to the Ice World of Ashenfell to defend it from an Ork WAAAGH! during the Chalnath Expanse Campaign.

History

Selicia was commanded by her superiors in the Order of Our Martyred Lady to take a strike force of the Sisters of Battle to the Chalnath Expanse world of Ashenfell, a frigid former Industrial World that had been assaulted by an Ork WAAAGH!. She was tasked with reinforcing the Imperial defenders, despite being heavily outnumbered by the Greenskins, and reclaiming Ashenfell for the Emperor.
