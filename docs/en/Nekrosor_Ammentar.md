Nekrosor Ammentar is a relatively lucid, unqiue and extremely powerful Necron Destroyer Lord, who is believed by many Necrons to be the first victim and the source of the Destroyer Curse. Others consider Ammentar to be entirely mythological -- a parable of guilt and betrayal that hearkens back to the Necrons' shattering of the C'tan. Ammentar commanded the vast force known as the Cursed Legion which is comprised entirely of Necrons who have been subsumed into the Destroyer Cult.

Ammentar is very real, however, and labours with surprising lucidity for a victim of the Destroyer Curse on its crusade to eradicate all organic life from the galaxy in a so-called "Red Harvest". It harbours an extreme affinity for the C'tan known as the Nightbringer, and aims to reunite its scattered C'tan Shards to one day revive the ancient Star God in full. This is a plan opposed by most of the starfaring species of the galaxy, including and perhaps most aggressively by the Necrons themselves, who have no wish to see their ancient masters restored to full power.

On the battlefield, Ammentar is a whirlwind of death festooned with weapons, from barbed claws and its strange Unmaker Gauntlet to Enmitic Disintegrators and a whip-like, bladed tail. Its infectious Destroyer Madness radiates from the Nekrosor like religious fervour, driving its Destroyer Cult allies to greater heights of murderous ferocity, who cut down the few poor souls left alive after the Nekrosor's blinding speed sliced through their allies before their weapons were even raised.

Yet for all its violent rampages and the ever-present cloud of insanity that follows it, Nekrosor Ammentar is no simple brute, and more than anything it is the Destroyer Lord's predatory intelligence and the occasional bouts of lucidity it displays that other Necrons fear the most. Ammentar is smart enough that its abhorrent crusade might actually succeed, and this is what the Necrons fear the most, for then their dream of resurrecting their own lost souls would be denied forever. 

"Nekrosor" is a title used among the Necrons of an unknown origin, though it is said that it could only have been granted by the Triarch themselves. With the original meaning of the title in the Necron Lexicon lost, it has become fused with the personal name of the only being in the galaxy still carrying it, Nekrosor Ammentar, and the Destroyer Lord is usually referred to by both as if it were a proper name rather than a title.

History

Strangely, all memories of Ammentar's origin appear to have been forcibly removed from the collective Necron psyche, though only Szarekh, the Silent King, possesses the command protocols to make such sweeping modifications to his species' memory engrams. Regardless of its true origin, Ammentar's mere presence is enough to corrupt the minds of other Necrons -- from the humblest Necron Warrior to the noblest Necron Overlord -- and it commands vast hordes of enthralled and undying soldiers in its crusade to eradicate all life. 

Nekrosor Ammentar is afflicted by the same maddening techno-virus that warps all of the afflicted Necrons of the Destroyer Cult into single-minded killing machines, but unlike all others afflicted with the Destroyer Curse, its advanced mind is not held entirely in thrall to its basest desires. 

What all Necrons learn, through dread reputation or traumatic experience, is that a pall of psychosis follows Ammentar and twists the minds of those it passes near, turning every Necron from the lowest Tomb World orderly to the highest Overlord into raging beasts hungry for death on a galactic scale. Few Necrons even realise their command protocols have been completely overwritten until it's too late and they are strapping Hyperphase Swords onto their arms with cold glee. Legend has it that Ammentar has even corrupted the Master Programs of entire stasis-crypt complexes.

While embodying the Destroyer Cult, Ammentar is far from a mindless savage and is not held in thrall to its base destructive desires. Ammentar has gathered a large army of the Destroyer Cult called the Cursed Legion by other Necrons that has also been dubbed the "Nekrosor's Crusaders". Those Necrons that do not willingly join them are captured and converted by the Legion's captive Crypteks. Ammentar's followers are so numerous it cannot lead them all directly, so Ammentar often delegates control to the Oblivion Court, a high command that consists of its most capable generals and devotees. While scattered throughout the galaxy, they are united in carrying out Ammentar's goal of reforming the Nightbringer.

Whether through the slow degradation of time or a catastrophic injury it received in the distant past, the Nekrosor began to harbour an almost religious fascination with the C'tan called the Nightbringer -- deadliest of all the Necrons' ancient Star Gods -- and has since taken up its chosen master's mantle to shepherd the eradication of all organic life. Its obsession with one of the Star Gods is repugnant to its fellow Necrons, though few can stop its Cursed Legion's crusade to obtain every last scattered C'tan Shard of Aza'gorod and unleash the Nightbringer anew upon the galaxy in its full, unholy glory.

In the Era Indomitus Nekrosor Ammentar discovered that a C'tan Shard of the Nightbringer was hidden among the worlds of the Realm of Ultramar and its forces moved to claim the prize, resulting in a clash with the Ultramarines' 2nd Company commanded by Captain Demetrian Titus on the Vespator Front of the Ultramarian Reclamation. Ammentar took its Cursed Legion to the Hive World of Novamagnor, once an ancient Necron Tomb World of the Sautekh Dynasty called Uzshemek. The world had quickly negotiated its reentry into the Realm of Ultramar after the arrival of Ultramarian forces, but was consumed by conflict once the Cursed Legion assaulted the planet.

During the subsequent War for Novamagnor, most of the planet fell to the Destroyer Cult and Flayed One Necrons of the Cursed Legion, in part thanks to the treachery of the xenophilic Archmagos Wytbor Oct. Ammentar was successful in overrunning the capital hive city sprawl of Firstbrand as well as acquiring the C'tan Shard of the Nightbringer, though thanks to the efforts of Captain Titus the Imperials continued to retain control over various regions of the world.

Wargear

Nekrosor Ammentar seems to be a Destroyer Lord who embodies all the different strains of the Destroyer Cult. Its serpentine lower body echoes the shape of Ophydian Destroyers, while its Enmitic weaponry is similar to that employed by the Hexmark Destroyer. Its bladed limbs bear the hallmarks of Skorpekh Destroyers.

Due to extensive physical modifications, Ammentar is capable of tunnelling through both planetary surfaces and various sub-dimensions, striking from beyond the veil of reality itself to reach its organic prey and exterminate them.

Enmitic Disintegrators

Unmaker Gauntlet - The Unmaker Gauntlet is a potent and unique melee weapon large enough to hold a Primaris Space Marine in its grip that when combined with Nekrosor Ammentar's relentless fury, leaves nothing but ruin in its wake.

Blade tail and whip coils - Ammentar possesses large claws for tearing through flesh, and razor-sharp blades on its serpentine tail and tendrils.

Nullstone Field Generator - This technology prevents any Necron allies near Ammentar from feeling any pain even when struck by potentially mortal wounds or unyielding psychic attacks.
