A Prism Rifle is a powerful Aeldari Laser Weapon that is favoured ranged weapon of the Craftworld Aeldari Aspect Warriors known as the Shadow Spectres.

Rarely encountered by the forces of the Imperium before the campaign on Betalis III, the ritual weapon of the Shadow Spectre Warrior Aspect is the Prism Rifle. This weapon relies upon the same Aeldari weapons technology as that found in the far larger Prism Cannon, a vehicle-mounted anti-tank weapon carried by the Fire Prism gravity tank. Prism Weapons are considered a marvel of design even by the sophisticated technological standards of the Asuryani.

Each Prism Rifle is connected to a single targeting matrix available to all of the Shadow Spectres on the battlefield, known as a Ghostlight. The Ghostlight combines each individual shot from a squad of Shadow Spectres into a single, searing blast of energy with a greatly magnified range that allows the Aspect Warriors to destroy an enemy's armoured support from an unseen location far across the battlefield.

Instead of firing their Prism Rifles (or the Exarch's Prism Blaster) individually the squad may take a single Ghostlight shot of greatly enhanced power which is particularly useful when used against the most powerful armoured targets like Space Marine Land Raiders.

Prism Blaster

A Prism Blaster, also sometimes referred to as a Prism Lance, is a ranged weapon similar in design and mechanism to the Prism Rifle but with a longer barrel that further focuses and refines the weapon's beam to provide even greater range with a more powerful blast. The Prims Blaster is the favoured weapon of a Shadow Spectres Exarch.
