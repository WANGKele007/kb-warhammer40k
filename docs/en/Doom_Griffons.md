The Doom Griffons is a Loyalist Space Marine Chapter of unknown Founding and origin. 

Almost nothing is known about this Chapter in the Imperial records, save that it participated in the Siege of the Fenris System led by the Dark Angels in 999.M41.

Notable Doom Griffons

Captain Bohemund - Bohemund is the captain of the Doom Griffons' 4th Company that participated in cleansing the daemonic taint that had taken over the Fenris System.
