The Ghulo Industrial Complex is a highly mercantile and expansionist minor league of the Leagues of Votann located in the galactic core. The Ghulo are considered unmatched among the Kin in the fields of void mining and terraforming of new worlds.

League History

The Ghulo Industrial Complex controls a broad belt of territories that curls counter-spinward towards the southern heartlands of the galactic core. This League of Votann is forever seeking to add new star systems and planets to their already impressive holdings and are skilled in transforming even the most barren worlds into productive and valuable assets.

The Ghulo Industrial Complex is a league whose Kindreds pour almost all of their time and energy into maintaining trade routes, and keeping their convoys of heavy haulers and escort ships moving along them. This league contains several extremely large and powerful Cthonian Mining Guilds, whose influence in the Hearthspakes of its Kindreds is such that they are virtually the de facto rulers of the Ghulo Industrial Complex as a whole.

League Worlds

Delvegard (Hold World) - Delvegard is an important world for the Ghulo Industrial Complex, and sits at the heart of a rich centre of trade routes and mining centres that serve the needs of its commerce-minded league.

League Colours

The colours of the Ghulo Industrial Compolex are unknown.

League Insignia

The insignia of the Ghulo Industrial Complex is white Kin script spelling out the word "Ghulo" where the "o" takes the form of a cog wheel.
