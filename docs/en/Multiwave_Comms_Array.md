A Multiwave Comms Array is a radio-wave communications device similar to the Imperial vox used by the military forces of the Leagues of Votann. It is able to broadcast and receive on multiple frequency carrier waves. 

The Multiwave Comms Array is often employed by a single member of a squad of Hearthkyn Warriors who has been designated to serve as a communications trooper who coordinates tactical actions between their squad and the rest of their Oathband.
