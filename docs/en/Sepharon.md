Sepharon, called the "Warden of the Gates," serves as the Master of the Logisticiam for the Dark Angels Chapter of Space Marines.

The Master of the Logisticiam is responsible for carrying out all of the administrative duties required to keep the Chapter functioning. 

He directs all of the Servitors, Chapter serfs and other administrative support personnel of the Chapter in the performance of their duties.
