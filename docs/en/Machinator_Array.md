The Machinator Array is a complex and intricate system of manipulators, augmetic body replacements, built-in tools, sensors, and energy projectors utilised by the most able magi of the ancient Mechanicum and the Adeptus Mechanicus of the 41st Millennium. Each array is unique to the magos who bears it and is a vital part of their cybernetically-augmented bodies.
