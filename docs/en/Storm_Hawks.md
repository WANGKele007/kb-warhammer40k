The Storm Hawks is a Loyalist Codex Astartes-compliant Space Marine Chapter and a rare Successor Chapter of the Raven Guard. The Chapter's exact date of creation is unknown, but the Storm Hawks are thought to be related to the Raven Guard through a later Founding. Their Chapter homeworld is named Zephyr.

Chapter Appearance

Chapter Colours

The Storm Hawks primarily wear white power armour. The Aquila or Imperialis on the chest guard is red. 

The red squad tactical specialty symbol on the right shoulder guard designates operational specialty -- battleline, close support, fire support, Veteran or command. 

A white High Gothic numeral is stenciled in the centre of the squad tactical specialty symbol which indicates squad number. 

The colour of the left knee guard indicates company number in accordance with the dictates of the Codex Astartes -- i.e. white (1st Company), yellow (2nd Company), red (3rd Company), green (4th Company), etc.

Chapter Badge

The Storm Hawks' Chapter badge is a red hawk's head with a red lightning bolt protruding from its open beak, centred on a field of white.
