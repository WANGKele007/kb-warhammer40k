Malin Vistario was a Chaos Sorcerer of the Thousand Sons Legion during the Great Crusade and Horus Heresy. 

Vistario aided the battle of the Emperor's Children Venerable Dreadnought Rylanor against his former primarch, the Daemon Prince Fulgrim, some ten thousand standard years after the Isstvan III Atrocity. 

History

Formerly a member of the prophetic Corvidae Cult of the Thousand Sons, Vistario was despatched by the Thousand Sons' Daemon Primarch Magnus the Red from Sortiarius, the so-called Planet of the Sorcerers, to investigate a distress signal that came from the long-dead world of Isstvan III, the place where the Horus Heresy began. 

The message repeated itself over and over, with a simple phrase, ambiguous, yet portentous: "The Ancient awaits."

By this time, the Thousand Sons had already long turned to Chaos and had reestablished themselves on the Planet of the Sorcerers. 

After discovering the Emperor's Children Loyalist Venerable Dreadnought Ancient Rylanor still alive in ancient subterranean catacombs upon the planet, Malin and his fellow sorcerers -- Akhtar of the Raptora and Murshid of the Athanaeans -- briefly contained the virus bomb unleashed by Rylanor as he faced his fallen primarch, Fulgrim, who had also responded to the transmission. 

Finding nothing worthy of respect in what Fulgrim had become, Vistario ultimately shot and killed Akhtar to break his psychic control and set the virus bomb loose, killing all in the process and dealing a grievous blow to Fulgrim's ego -- the only real wound the Daemon Primarch was still capable of feeling.

Trivia

Vistario and the short story he appears in are the only sources of information on the Seventh Enumeration of the Thousand Sons Legion. 

While conversing with his companions, Vistario noted that "all whispers eventually return to Sortiarius," implying that Magnus the Red would eventually learn of the events that had transpired at Isstvan III. 

Whilst the story takes place long after the Horus Heresy, Vistario and his companions only mention their pre-Heresy Legion cults, implying that all three of them were members of the Thousand Sons prior to the Battle of Prospero.
