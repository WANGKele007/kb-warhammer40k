A Galatus Warblade is a huge Dreadnought Close Combat Weapon in the form of a Power Sword wielded by the Legio Custodes Contemptor-Galatus Dreadnoughts. The Galatus Warblade is wielded by the Custodes Dreadnought as easily as a Sentinel Guard might their own sword.

The Galatus' signature weapon is able to shear through ranks of armoured troops with a single pass, while its built-in, twin-linked Infernus Incinerator units are equally designed to swiftly purge a numerically superior enemy.
