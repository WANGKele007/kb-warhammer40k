The Umbral Knights is a Loyalist Space Marine Chapter comprised entirely of Primaris Space Marines, created from an unknown lineage and raised during the Ultima Founding of ca. 999.M41.

Chapter History

The Umbral Knights played a role in the Indomitus Crusade under Lord Commander of the Imperium Roboute Guilliman's orders until they were tasked with staying behind after one of the initial conflicts of the Indomitus Crusade was won so that they could consolidate the crusade's gains among the liberated worlds.
