The Crimson Axes were a Loyalist Space Marines Chapter that was a Successor Chapter of the brutal Executioners Chapter and was created during an unknown Founding. There is very little information in official Imperial records about this obscure Chapter other than it was reported lost circa 092.M39. Circumstances unknown.
