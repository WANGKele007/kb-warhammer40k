Echoes of the Long War is the sixth volume in The Beast Arises series of novels.

Synopsis

Paralysed by the continued Ork invasion of Imperial space, the rulers of Terra continue to fight among themselves. Finally losing patience with the High Council, Koorland -- the last surviving Imperial Fist -- ousts the Lord Commander of the Imperium and seizes control. Elsewhere, the Fists Exemplar are forced by circumstance to fight alongside the Iron Warriors. Where will such an alliance lead -- can a Traitor Legion ever be trusted?
