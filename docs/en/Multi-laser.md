The Multi-laser is a rapid-fire heavy Laser Weapon, ideal for destroying lightly-armoured vehicles or large infantry formations. Due to its power requirements and weight, it is usually mounted on Imperial vehicles such as Chimeras or Sentinels, or platform-mounted and operated by a Techmarine where it is known as a Rapier. 

Multiple reinforced barrels and enhanced phase capacitors allow for a standard laser discharge to be fired in a series of rapid "bolts". While losing some of the penetrative and stopping power of a standard lascannon, the resulting high rate of fire of up to 100 "bolts" a solar second improves the weapon's ability to engage a series of targets in rapid succession.

While the standard lascannon is a semi-automatic weapon (one squeeze of the trigger fires one shot), the Multi-laser is fully automatic; it fires laser beam after laser beam as long as the trigger is held down. The Multi-laser can thus be considered a bigger and more powerful variant of the Hellgun.

Multi-laser Patterns

Gravis Multi-laser Array - A pattern of Multi-laser used by the troops of the Imperialis Militia of the Imperial Army during the Great Crusade and Horus Heresy.
