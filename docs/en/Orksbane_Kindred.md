The Orksbane Kindred is a Kindred of the Ymyr Conglomerate of the Leagues of Votann. The Orksbane Kindred built their hold upon the heavily-irradiated Hold World of Tamâkh. Known as Brôkhfyre, the hold is dug deep into Tamâkh's planetary mantle -- insulated against the exotic and deadly energy spectra that pour from the world's nearby star. 

What violent apotheosis the tortured star is undergoing, none can say, but for three Terran millennia now the Kin of the Orksbane Kindred have captured the energies of its raging solar flares using kilometres-high stellar vanes. These energies power their hold's Forge, which is amongst the greatest in all the Leagues of Votann, renowned for the amazing weapons of war it can produce.
