Kjarg Iron-oath, originally named Gaius, is a Primaris Marine of the Ultima Founding of the genetic lineage of Leman Russ who served in the Indomitus Crusade under the name of Sergeant Gaius in the ranks of the Unnumbered Sons for three standard years.

Gaius was the squad leader of an Intercessor Squad dubbed "Lupus Six" that were sent along with a company of other Unnumbered Sons of Space Wolves genetic lineage in a Torchbearer fleet to bring the first Primaris Marine reinforcements to the Space Wolves. Before arriving at Fenris the Torchbearers relieved the Space Wolves' Drakeslayers Great Company who were fighting Orks, which led Gaius and his squad to join it to reinforce its ranks. Gaius and his squad were therefore the first Space Wolf Primaris Marines to set foot on Fenris when the Drakeslayers returned to their homeworld.

Gaius and his squadmates had a diffcult time adopting to and being accepted into the Chapter by the Firstborn Space Wolves because of their non-Fenrisian births. Frustrated and angry, Gaius submitted himself to an impromptu Test of Morkai, where he earned his new Fenrisian name "Kjarg", which was the name of the largest wolf in Fenrisian legend, said to have faced down Morkai himself, and the by-name "Iron-oath" for keeping his oath to survive the Trial of Morkai. He is currently the pack leader of the Space Wolves Primaris Marine pack called the "Firstwolves".
