Envelopers are a species of insectoids native to the Death World of Yanth in the Askellon Sector. 

They have two sets of wings for flight, but their highly adhesive spray makes them truly deadly. Their webbing shoots forth from openings behind their forward mandibles, and can hit targets at far distances. 

Natives have nicknamed them "stickyflies," and immediately attempt to shoot down any they see before the insectoids can fire their entangling webs. Once trapped, heavy-duty solvents and acids are often required to free anyone webbed.
