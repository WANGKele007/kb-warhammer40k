Indomitus Point is a watch fortress of the Deathwatch Chapter located in the Segmentum Ultima. 

In the Era Indomitus, in the wake of the Ultimaris Decree providing the Primaris Marines to the Deathwatch, many watch captains prefer to field Hellblasters as Roboute Guilliman intended, lacing small squads of them in amongst more varied kill-teams to provide reliable close-range fire support. Indeed, the watch fortresses of Doombreak, Furor Shield and Indomitus Point have taken to using Hellblasters much like lighter, more manoeuvrable Devastator Squads in order to deal with the hulking Tyranid and Greenskin monstrosities they face.
