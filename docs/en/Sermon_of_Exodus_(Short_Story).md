Sermon of Exodus is a short story published in the The Horus Heresy series. Sermon of Exodus was originally published as a part of the event-only anthology novella Sedition's Gate. It is now also included in the War Without End anthology. The cover image used is that of War Without End as Sermon of Exodus has yet to be released separately. Sermon of Exodus is prequel to the events of Book 30, The Damnation of Pythos.

Synopsis

In the wake of Horus' miraculous recovery on Davin, the Davinites begin an exodus to the stars, joining with the Traitor Legions to whom they will offer the power of the Dark Gods.
