The Imperial Talons is a Loyalist, Codex Astartes-compliant Space Marine Chapter. It is suspected that it is one of the rare Successor Chapters of the Raven Guard. Its exact Founding date is unknown. 

Chapter Appearance

Chapter Colours

The Imperial Talons primarily wear red power armour. The Aquila or Imperialis on the chest plate is white. 

The white squad specialty symbol -- battleline, close support, fire support, Veteran or command -- is indicated on the right shoulder guard. 

A white High Gothic numeral located on the inside of the shoulder plate, next to the squad specialty symbol, indicates squad number. 

The colour of the shoulder plate trim indicates company number in accordance to the Codex Astartes -- i.e. white (1st Company), yellow (2nd Company), red (3rd Company), green (4th Company), etc.

Chapter Badge

The Imperial Talons' Chapter badge is a large, stylised white talon of a bird of prey, centred on a field of red.
