A Drill Torpedo is a Leagues of Votann voidship-grade weapon used by the vessels of Leagues of Votann Fleets which is a form of modified mining equipment. A Drill Torpedo, as its name implies, can drill deep into an enemy voidship's hull before exploding just as easily as it can be used to drill into a mineral-rich asteroid to blast off portions of ore-rich rock.

Sources

Codex: Leagues of Votann (9th Edition), pp. 16-17, 28
