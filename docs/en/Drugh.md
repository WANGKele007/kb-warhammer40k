The Drugh are a large, worm-like, intelligent xenos species that lived on the world of Pyrus I and was known to possess potent psychic abilities.

Their habitat was subterranean and it is believed by Imperial xenologists that their original homeworld was destroyed by the forces of the Imperium of Man near the end of the Great Crusade.

An Imperial transmission dated 0435.991.M41 lists the White Scars Chapter homeworld of Chogoris as under full-scale infestation by a subterranean colony of the Drugh.

An examination of Drugh "mouthparts" by Magos Biologis Sharle Darvus revealed that this part of the species's anatomy served a dual function as both a source of defecation and the primary means of consumption.
