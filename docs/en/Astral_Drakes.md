The Astral Drakes was a Loyalist Space Marine Chapter of unknown Founding and origin. Almost nothing is known about this Chapter in Imperial records, save that it was annihilated to the last Astartes by the Ork Warlord Ghazghkull Mag Uruk Thraka.
