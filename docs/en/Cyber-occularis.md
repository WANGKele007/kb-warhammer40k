The Cyber-occularis is a type of servo-automata employed by the tech-priests of the ancient Mechanicum that is an extension of its master's senses on the wider battlefield, being more robustly constructed then a standard cyber-Familiar. These drones often resemble artificial raptors or strange hybrid creatures of organic corpse and machine.
