The Iron Shades are a Loyalist Space Marine Chapter of the 7th Founding and unknown genetic origin. They recruit aspirants from the Death World of Shoba. The philosophy of the Iron Shades holds that perfection is achieved through conflict. Hailing from a perpetual world of darkness and preferring to work in the shadows with little fanfare, they nonetheless are unusually well-disposed towards average Imperial citizens compared to most Adeptus Astartes.

The Iron Shades' 5th Battle Company are specialists in void warfare.

Chapter History

13th Black Crusade (ca. 999.M41) 
Battle of the Agripinaa System - The Iron Shades battled the Death Guard warband known as the Lords of Silence at Forge World Agripinaa in the opening stages of the 13th Black Crusade. A strike cruiser and contingent of at least 42 Iron Shades was displaced alongside the Lords of Silence during the battle due a Warp-Drive accident; this group was subsequently wiped out by the Death Guard forces.

Indomitus Crusade - Road of Martyrs (Unknown Date.M42) - The Road of Martyrs was the name given to an early battle of the Indomitus Crusade. The campaign was initiated by Indomitus Crusade Fleet Secundus, which was led by Sisters of Battle forces from the Order of the Ebon Chalice and Order of Our Martyred Lady who fought their way towards the Eye of Terror in the Segmentum Obscurus. This dangerous and open-ended assignment to roll back Chaos advances in the region became known as the "Road of Martyrs".

Chapter Appearance

Chapter Colours

The Iron Shades wear deep black power armour with midnight blue trim and silver Chapter iconography. Individual Iron Shades are known to possess honour tattoos, a practice inherited from the people of Shoba.

Chapter Badge

The Iron Shades' Chapter badge is a shrouded skull bisected by a bone white sword.
