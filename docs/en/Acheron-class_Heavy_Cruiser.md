The Acheron-class Heavy Cruiser used by the forces of Chaos is an unusual starship in many ways. It was originally designed by the Navis Imperialis to serve as a test bed for a new series of weapons based on the analysis of ancient -- and possibly xenos -- weaponry designs uncovered by the Adeptus Mechanicus in Sector 51, also the home of the ill-fated Portis Cthulhus. 

Whether the vessel's subsequent defection to the service of Chaos during the Gothic War is related to this unconventional origin is still debated by Imperial naval scholars. 

The project was evidently a success, producing a starship with a formidable array of long-ranged weapons batteries and even longer-ranged Lances that would ultimately become the basis for the design of the Overlord-class Battlecruiser. 

No longer needing a test subject, only one Acheron-class Heavy Cruiser, the unnamed BF/67-A, was ever built by the Imperium. Unfortunately, the powerful vessel eventually fell to Chaos during the Gothic War and finally earned its name, dubbed the Chaos Eternus by Imperial Admiral Grove.

Notable Acheron-class Heavy Cruisers

Chaos Eternus - Originally unnamed and only designated BF/67-A, the solitary and only known Acheron-class Heavy Cruiser turned to Chaos during the Gothic War. It would earn its name after escaping Imperial forces for the fourth time under Admiral Grove during the Scharnhorst Conflict. Fleet Admiral Mourndark ordered the Gothic-class Cruiser Emperor's Wrath and the Tyrant-class Cruiser Zealous to catch the Chaos Eternus near the world of Numitor.

Canon Conflict

While other sources claim that only one vessel of this class was ever built, another Acheron-class Cruiser type vessel did exist at the time of the Great Crusade and Horus Heresy. At least one of this vessel type served in the fleet of the XIIth Legion, transporting the World Eaters' 17th Company commanded by Captain Argus Brond.

It was destroyed in orbit over Terra during the Siege of Terra. It is not known if this class of cruiser was identical to the future Acheron Heavy Cruiser Chaos Eternus or the Cardinal-class Heavy Cruisers that preceded it.
