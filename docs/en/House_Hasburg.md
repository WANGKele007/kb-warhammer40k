House Hasburg is a Loyalist Imperial Knight House of the Imperium. There is very little information in Imperial records about this Knight House, or from what Knight World it originally hails from. They are a hidebound knightly house, whose days are almost wholly consumed tending to the obscure and esoteric tenets of tradition. At the dawn of every day the entire court must gather to hear the reading of the names and deeds of every ruler of the house, dating back to its foundation millennia before; there are hundreds of names, and the process takes up most of the day.
