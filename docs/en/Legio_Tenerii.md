The Legio Tenerii is a Loyalist Titan Legion of the Collegia Titanica. This Titan Legion originally hailed from the notable Forge World of Mars. 

Soon after the signing of the Treaty of Mars, both the Legio Tenerii and Legio Suturvora were ordered to quit Mars and enter the service of the Great Crusade in perpetuity until its work of liberation was complete.
