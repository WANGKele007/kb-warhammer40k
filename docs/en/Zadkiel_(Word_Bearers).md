Zadkiel was a Heretic Astartes and a naval fleet-captain of the Word Bearers Legion during the Great Crusade and Horus Heresy. He was named the commanding officer of the Abyss-class Battleship Furious Abyss, a mammoth battleship constructed in relative secrecy in the Thule orbital shipyards of Jupiter in the Sol System by the traitorus fabricator-general Kelbor-Hal's Dark Mechanicum faction. 

The Furious Abyss was intended to play an instrumental role in the Battle of Calth by launching a massive assault on the Ultramarines' homeworld of Macragge and weakening the XIII Legion enough to make the survivors easy prey for the Word Bearers attacking Calth.

The great battleship was ultimately destroyed by a small team of Astartes deployed from multiple Loyalist Legions -- including the Ultramarines, Space Wolves and World Eaters -- commanded by Captain Lysimachus Cestus of the Ultramarines who set the warship's Plasma Reactor to overload. The great warship was consumed by a massive explosion before it was ever able to put its plans against Macragge into motion.

As such, shortly after the Furious Abyss began its maiden voyage towards Calth, Fleet-Captain Zadkiel lost not only his vessel but his own life when he was slain by Captain Cestus during the Loyalists' assault on the Fursious Abyss though it also cost the Ultramarine officer his own life. Zadkiel and Cestus were the final Astartes to die onboard the great warship.

The destruction of the Furious Abyss forced the Word Bearers to face a surprised but still militarily potent Ultramarines Legion on Calth, and resulted in the pyrrhic victory for the sons of Primarch Roboute Guilliman at the Battle of Calth.
