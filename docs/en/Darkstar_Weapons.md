Darkstar Weapons are a class of close combat weapons wielded by the military forces of the Leagues of Votann that are crafted from the unique ore known as darkstar. Melee weapons forged from darkstar are considered the most lethal in the armouries of the Kin.

Perhaps the most feared of Kin melee weapons are those that employ darkstar ore, mined from the fringes of the Dead Zones of the galactic core. This inimical material emits a universal damping field through unknown means that shuts down organic metabolic and even electro-mechanical functions on contact. 

It is worked into Kin weaponry with the greatest of care by the Brôkhyr who specialise in crafting it and wielded with equal gravitas, for the slightest cut from a darkstar weapon's blade can end the victim's life as if a switch had been pulled.

Darkstar Axe

A Darkstar Axe is a darkstar melee weapon that has been fashioned into the shape of the razor-edged battleaxes favoured by many Kin warriors. Darkstar Axes are a favoured weapon of Einhyr Champions.

Darkstar Sword

A Darkstar Sword, also called a Darkstar Blade, is a darkstar melee weapon that has been fashioned into the shape of a razor-edged double-blade sword in the Kin style. Darkstar Swords are usually wielded only by the elite among the Oathbands, including Einhyr Champions and kâhls.

Darkstar Glaive

A Darkstar Glaive is a darkstar melee weapon fashioned into the shape of a glaive crafted in the Kin style. The most famed Darkstar Glaive is the unique weapon named Bane, wielded by the ferocious Kin warrior Buri Aegnirssen.

Notable Darkstar Weapons

Bane - Bane is a Leagues of Votann Darkstar Glaive of prodigious power that is wielded by Buri Aegnirssen, the last survivor of the Kin's Emberg-Aegnir Bloc. This weapon was created for Aegnirssen by the most gifted artisans of the Ymyr Conglomerate in gratitude for his aid against the Tyranids. It is fitted with an extendable haft and is used by Aegnirssen to keep Tyranid bioforms at a distance.
