The Hellstorm Bolt Rifle is a variant of the Mark II Cawl Pattern Bolt Rifle primarily used by Heavy Intercessors. It possesses a drum-shaped, rotary magazine and is capable of a higher rate of fire than the standard Bolt Rifle. 

It looks similar in appearance to the Heavy Bolt Rifle and the Executor Bolt Rifle save for its differently shaped magazine.
