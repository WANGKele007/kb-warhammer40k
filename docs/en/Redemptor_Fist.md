A Redemptor Fist is a massive, articulated Dreadnought-sized Power Fist of advanced design used by Primaris Space Marine Redemptor Dreadnoughts as a primary melee weapon on one of their two weapon arms. With this pistoned gauntlet, the Redemptor can tear even Tyranid bio-beasts and Daemon Engines apart at close quarters.

Sources

Codex Adeptus Astartes - Space Marines (8th Edition), pp. 83, 166
