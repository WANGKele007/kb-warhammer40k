The Breath of the Gods, also known to the Craftworld Aeldari in the Aeldari Lexicon as the Caoineag, was an ancient C'tan device of incredible power designed to supply the C'tan with an essentially unlimited source of power to serve as sustenance. It did this by syphoning energy from stars simultaneously in the past, present and future. This energy and its temporal manipulation could also be used to create planets, entire solar systems or potentially even entire galaxies. 

Unfortunately, this temporal manipulation was also extremely dangerous and if used improperly could erase the entire timeline, and essentially destroy the universe. When activated improperly the device could also unleash space-time anomalies and cause stars in the past or future to die before their time. 

When the Breath of the Gods was active the Aeldari Farseer Bilenna Faerelle of Biel-Tan used her psychic abilities to observe it drawing in the threads of fate and unravelling them as it ripped space and time apart, leading to a premature end state or "Omega Point" of the universe.

The device was used by the insane Adeptus Mechanicus explorator Vettius Telok to create his personal Forge World of Exniliho, which also became the temporal equivalent of a black hole, a singularity into which time itself was drawn and destroyed. Although this effect was initially confined to the planet's depths, Farseer Bilenna knew that its temporal shock waves would slowly spread out across the galaxy and then the wider universe, resulting in its slow and inevitable death.

The Breath of the Gods is also thought to be responsible for the creation of the Warp disturbance in the southern reaches of the Segmentum Pacificus known as the Halo Scar. Warp travel through this region was virtually impossible and it was where the planet Exniliho was located.

History

The Breath of the Gods was originally created by the Necrons during the War in Heaven to feed the enormous appetite of their ruling C'tan Star Gods for stellar energy. 

Millions of Terran years later an Adeptus Mechanicus expedition led by Explorator Vettius Telok was launched in an attempt to locate the alien technology remembered in ancient legends as the Breath of the Gods but was ultimately declared lost. In the 41st Millennium, Archmagos Lexell Kotov initiated the Kotov Crusade to seek out Telok's expedition and complete his quest to recover the Breath of the Gods. The Kotov Crusade eventually discovered Telok and the Breath of the Gods on the artificial world of Exniliho among the Halo Stars that Telok had created using the power of the alien device. Although originally designed to be powered by a C'tan Shard, Telok had discovered that the entropic fields generated by the xenos species known as the Hrud could act as an alternate power source for the artefact. Harnessing a colony of captured Hrud within the confines of the device as living generators, Telok powered the Breath of the Gods and had used its control over space-time to create the planet Exniliho which he transformed into a new Mechanicus Forge World. 

Unfortunately, it was soon apparent to Kotov and his allies that Telok had gone insane, as his plan was to transport the Breath of the Gods to the Noctis Labyrinth on Mars using Kotov's Ark Mechanicus Speranza and capture the legendary Void Dragon said to live there inside it to enhance its power generation. He then intended to use the relic's vast powers to destroy Terra and Mars and overthrow the Emperor of Mankind. Once his plans were discovered by the new arrivals, the contingent of Black Templars Space Marines that had accompanied the Kotov Crusade into the Halo Stars moved violently against Telok and his minions. 

Unfortunately the Kotov Crusade had to initially retreat when faced with Telok's crystalline warriors. Meanwhile the Tech-priest Galatea, who had been rescued from the Adeptus Mechanicus research station called the Valette Manifold earlier in the crusade, and was a former subordinate of Vettius Telok who had previously dabbled in tech-heresy, betrayed Kotov by absorbing the mind of the Tech-priest Linya Tychon and seeking to meet with Telok. 

The crusade was fortunately reinforced by the arrival of Farseer Bilenna Faerelle and the forces of Craftworld Biel-Tan under her command. The Farseer had foreseen that Telok's plans for the Breath of the Gods could result in the destruction of the entire space-time continuum. After Telok and his crystal warriors seized the Speranza, determined to return to the Sol System and carry out his mad plan, Kotov and his surviving allies escaped from the surface of Exniliho back to the Ark Mechanicus were they engaged Telok in a psycho-techno battle within the cyberspace of the ship's computer systems. 

The Speranza's sapient Machine Spirit (artificial general intelligence) ultimately sided with Kotov and rejected Telok's control, allowing Kotov to delete Telok's data-core and kill him. With Telok's death the Breath of the Gods tore itself apart once there was no one to control it. Its captive Hrud colony also escaped, wreaking further havoc. 

The Speranza fired blasts from the whole host of its arcane weaponry dating back to the Age of Technology -- including chronometric cannons, anti-matter projectors and hypometric weapons which possessed the power to delete entire regions of space-time from existence -- into the debris of the xenos device, vaporising it utterly and ending its threat to the universe. 

With the destruction of the Breath of the Gods all the energy it had stolen from stars in the past and future was returned to its point of origin, allowing those stars to burn brightly across a normal life cycle once more.
