A Voidsheen Cloak was a protective garment worn by the Oblivion Knights of the Sisters of Silence. It was made from micro-vitrious mesh designed to diffract and absorb kinetic and directed energy.

Enhanced Voidsheen Cloak

The Enhanced Voidsheen Cloaks worn by the commanders of the Silent Sisterhood like Jenetia Krole and the Oblivion Knights-Centura are further reinforced by refractor generators as a mark of their rank and office.
