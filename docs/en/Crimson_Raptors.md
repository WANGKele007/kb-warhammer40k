The Crimson Raptors are a Loyalist Space Marine Chapter of an unknown Founding and genetic origin. Their homeworld is named Bloodpeak.

Th Crimson Raptors have been active in many of the conflicts of the Era Indomitus.

Chapter History

Notable Campaigns

War of the Prosperan Rift - Chancyll Suppression (Unknown Date.M42) - After the opening of the Great Rift, the Prosperan Rift became rife with war. Across multiple star systems, forces of Chaos loyal to the Daemon Primarch Magnus the Red and his divine master Tzeentch fought to expand Magnus' psychic haven in realspace centred on Sortiarius which had been moved through the use of a sorcerous ritual from the Eye of Terror to a position above his lost homeworld of Prospero. Populations rose up against their Imperial overlords and the beleaguered soldiery of the Imperium desperately fought back. At the world of Chancyll, the Crimson Raptors deployed 4 demi-companies to support the Adeptus Astartes of the Dragonspears and Knights of Doom, the Sisters of Battle of the Order of the Bloody Rose and the Order of the Blessed Martyr, the Astra Militarum troops of the Chancyllian Cannoneers, Chancyllian Musketry, Chancyllian Lightfoot and Praetorian Hussars regiments, and the Imperial Knights of House Brahmica and House Hawkwood.

Pariah Nexus (Unknown Date.M42) - The Crimson Raptors are known to have participated in this campaign.

Octarius War (Unknown Date.M42) - The Crimson Raptors were confirmed to have been present in this warzone, although due to the Adeptus Astartes' total operational autonomy it's unclear how long they stayed and how many forces they committed.

Chapter Appearance

Chapter Colours

The Crimson Raptors' Chapter colours are not listed in current Imperial records.

Chapter Badge

The Crimson Raptors' Chapter badge is an eagle's head.
