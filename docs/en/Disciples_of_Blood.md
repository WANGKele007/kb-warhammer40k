The Disciples of Blood are a Loyalist Space Marine Successor Chapter of the Blood Angels, created during an unknown Founding. 

Created as it was from the Blood Angels' gene-seed, its Astartes carry the genetic flaws inherited from Sanguinius, including the Red Thirst and the Black Rage.

Chapter History

Notable Campaigns

Devastation of Baal (ca. 999.M41) - The Disciples of Blood were among the forces deployed by the Blood Angels Successor Chapters to defend their parent Chapter's homeworld of Baal from an assault by the Tyranid Hive Fleet Leviathan.
