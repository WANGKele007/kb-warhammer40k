Note: This page refers to the White Scars Legion khan who served during the Horus Heresy; for the White Scars Chapter Master of the same name in the 41st Millennium, see Jubal Khan. 

Jubal Khan, remembered by his brothers as the "Lord of the Summer Lightning" and "Death That Comes with Summer Laughter", was a Terran-born khan (captain) of the White Scars Legion during the Great Crusade and Horus Heresy. 

A warrior of exceptional speed and charisma, he embodied the martial joy and free spirit of the Vth Legion more keenly than many born on Chogoris itself.

History

Born as Luthian on Terra in the late 30th Millennium, Jubal first drew the attention of his primarch Jaghatai Khan during a sparring match on Chogoris. His speech, bearing, and spirit were so steeped in Chogorian custom that he seemed native to the steppe. At that time, he served in the Brotherhood of the Summer Lightning, the very horde he would later rise to lead as khan.

By the time of the Chondax Campaign in the last days of the Great Crusade, Jubal had taken command of the brotherhood, though he was often detached from the main body of the Legion, leading an ambitious Ikhan ("hunt") against the elusive Mjordhainn xenos beyond the galactic rim.

Following the events of the Second Battle of Prospero, Jaghatai Khan summoned Jubal back to the core of the Legion. In the wake of Qin Xa's death, the primarch offered Jubal the prestigious position of Master of the Keshig, the primarch'd own elite honour guard. Jubal declined the role, instead accepting the newly created title of Master of the Hunt -- a position that suited his untamed and ranging nature. 

During the climactic battles of the Solar War during the Horus Heresy, Jubal led White Scars elements aboard the battleship Lance of Heaven, engaging the fleets of the Sons of Horus First Captain Ezekyle Abaddon and the Word Bearers' Zardu Layak above the ecliptic plane of the Sol System. 

In the brutal boarding actions that followed, Jubal fought Abaddon directly in a void-bound duel, wielding his power guandao (Chogorian glaive) with blistering fury. Despite his prowess, Jubal was ultimately slain by the first captain of the Sons of Horus, and the Lance of Heaven was lost with him, a fittingly glorious end for one of the White Scars' most radiant sons.
