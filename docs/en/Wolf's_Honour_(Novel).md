Wolf's Honour by Lee Lightner is the sixth volume in the Space Wolf series of novels and is collected in Space Wolf The Second Omnibus. 

Synopsis

In the continuing saga of Ragnar Blackmane, the Space Wolves come under attack from all sides by the Thousand Sons Chaos Space Marines. In a last-ditch attempt to stop their ancient enemy, Ragnar and his battle-brothers launch a lightning strike counterattack on the Thousand Son's base. Will the Space Wolves triumph, and can Ragnar retrieve the Spear of Russ from his nemesis, the Chaos Space Marine Madox?
