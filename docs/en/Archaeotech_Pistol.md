An Archaeotech Pistol is an ancient sidearm dating back to the Age of Technology. This type of pistol was capable of firing a variety of projectiles, be they micro-atomic munitions, highly efficient las beams or searing, directed energy kill-rays that drew power from a planet's magnetosphere. Some of these pistols were observed firing searing bolts of emerald energy. 

Archaeotech Pistols often served as the sidearms of high-ranking Imperial nobles and Imperial Army and Space Marine commanders during the Great Crusade and Horus Heresy eras. In the Era Indomitus, Adeptus Mechanicus Explorators have been known to wield Archaeotech Pistols.

Kinetic Destroyer

The Kinetic Destroyer is a type of master-crafted Archaeotech Pistol wielded by the Adeptus Custodes' Custodian Venatari.

The weapon's design is of ancient and rare origin, and all extant examples were originally unearthed in the stasis vault of a crashed voidship discovered deep below the earth of what was once one of Old Earth's oceans before they dried up in later millennia. Its design is believed by the Adeptus Mechanicus to date back to the earliest ages of Human expansion into the stars.

When fired, the weapon releases a huge amount of kinetic force through the expenditure of energy shells, each of which is an irreplaceable munition sourced from the same original cache as the weapons themselves. Though tens of thousands of these shells were discovered with the wreck, even by the time of Great Crusade in the 30th Millennium their stock was running low and would one day become exhausted, rendering all of the remaining weapons in the hands of the Legio Custodes useless.
