The Legio Kaurthos is a Loyalist Titan Legion of the Collegia Titanica, one of the military arms of the Adeptus Mechanicus. It is based on the Forge World of Avachrus in the Gilead System of the Imperium Nihilus, which was cut off from the rest of the Imperium in the Era Indomitus by the birth of the Great Rift and the myriad Warp Storms that accompanied its birth.

Most of the Titans of the Legio had been dispersed to battlefields across the galaxy before the massive Warp rift tore across the heavens. As a result, little of the Legio's complement remains behind at its fortress of Kaurdar on Avachrus at present. 

Legion History

The monolithic fortress of Kaurdar is the only structure on the surface of Avachrus, located deep within the crystalline wastelands of the planet's dark side, as the world is tidally-locked with its star. This gargantuan citadel was once the home of the Titan Legio Kaurthos, led by the Gaius Vastatio; a god-machine towering over 20 metres tall and arrayed with a panoply of devastating weaponry.

The fabricator-general of Avachrus is Ultima Gol Bessor, who abdicated his position as leader of the Forge World to sail with the Legio Kaurthos's forces on a confidential assignment over a standard century ago -- some suspect to the same fate as the Absolvers Chapter's absent Chapter Command. A power vacuum erupted in Bessor's absence, with individual forge cities left to dictate their own policies. This heralded the rise of the Belaxia Foundry, and the subsequent ascension of its leader as the new regent of Avachrus, Archdomina Aexekra Vakuul.

Kauradar is almost abandoned now; though void-shielded against storms and outfitted with turret defences, the Titanworks lay quiet. Those vigilant and faithful continue to defend the machinery that remains, awaiting the return of their noble fabricator-general.
