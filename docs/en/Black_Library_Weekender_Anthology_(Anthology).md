Black Library Weekender Anthology is a two-volume anthology of short stories, released exclusively at Black Library's Weekender event on November 3 and 4, 2012. The book contains several short stories from different Games Workshop properties. The short stories contained within were later released separately.

Synopsis

No official synopsis is given. 

Contents

The Black Library Weekender Anthology contains the following short stories:

Horus Heresy

Lost Sons by James Swallow
The Divine Word by Gav Thorpe

Warhammer 40,000

Know Thyself by Andy Smillie
Crucible by John French
The Smallest Detail by Sandy Mitchell
Old Scars by George Mann
Signal to Noise by C.Z. Dunn
Shadow Play by Rob Sanders

Warhammer Fantasy

Gods of Flesh and Blood by Graham McNeill
Berthold's Beard by Josh Reynolds
The Great Maw by Laurie Goulding
Bloodwalker by C.L. Werner
