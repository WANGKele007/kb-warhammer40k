The Legio Gigantes was a Loyalist Titan Legion of the Collegia Titanica. 

The Legio Gigantes remained loyal during the Horus Heresy, but tragically, while in transit through the Scellis-Trevelya System, Warp Storms unleashed by the Tenebrae Pylon Array caused the Legion to be lost to the Warp, and presumably destroyed.
