The Baleflamer is a daemonic Flamer Weapon that is employed by some Daemon Engines. The Baleflamer takes the shape of a large projector muzzle that is usually located within the Daemon Engine's mouth. 

The only current recorded use of the Baleflamer is by the Daemon Engine in the service of some Chaos Space Marine warbands known as a Heldrake. 

The Heldrake usually is armed with either a Baleflamer or a Hades Autocannon in its mouth. The weapon is fuelled by the dark Warp fires that burn within the beast's chest, and when unleashed the burning ichor that flows forth and rains down upon the creature's target ignites flesh and soul alike.
