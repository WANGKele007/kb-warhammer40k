In Remembrance is the first stand-alone short story in the Gaunt's Ghosts series of novels by Dan Abnett. In Remembrance was first published in the Black Library's Inferno! magazine. The story was later re-published as part of The Founding omnibus and later as a stand-alone e-book.

Synopsis

On the world of Verghast, the Imperium is triumphant. The forces of the Astra Militarum -- aided by the brave men and women of Vervunhive -- have defeated the Chaos-tainted soldiers of rival Ferrozoica Hive. The Traitor leader is dead, killed in single combat by Colonel-Commissar Ibram Gaunt. Now, in the aftermath, a sculptor seeks to commemorate Gaunt's men, the heroes of Vervunhive -- the Tanith First-and-Only.
