House Octobus was a Loyalist Imperial Knight House of the Questor Imperialis that served the Imperium of Man directly. There is little information on this knightly house in official Imperial records or what Knight World they originally hailed from. All that is known, is that it has long since been forgotten. The only known survivor of this once great knightly house is the Freeblade Blood Lord, piloted by Sir Marcel.
