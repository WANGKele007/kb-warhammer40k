A Helfather is one of the custodians of the Iron Council that governs the Iron Hands Chapter of Space Marines.

Helfathers are heavily augmented Iron Hands in Terminator Armour. The armour is completely black without trim or clan markings. They are rarely seen leaving Meduson on the Iron Hands homeworld of Medusa.

Iron Hands Battle-Brothers are unsure of the process of becoming a Helfather as they never speak and it is unknown if they even have names.

Helfathers are in fact created from fallen Iron Hands resurrected by the Keys of Hel. Their bodies are almost completely augmetic in nature.

Notable Helfathers

First Sergeant Telarrch - Telarrch was an Iron Hands Sergeant who served under the command of Iron Father Kristos. He had been given the cerebro-conditioning of Clan Raukaan to make him loyal only to Kristos and rid him of memories of his weakness. He was slain when 72% of his brain matter was destroyed in a single moment by a Melta-type weapon. Kristos had stolen and originally intended to use Venerable Augustine Sangreal Galvarro's Dreadnought sarcophagus for Telarrch, but instead Telarrch was later resurrected using the Keys of Hel and had what flesh remained to him replaced by augmetics. He took up new duties as one of the Helfathers who protected the Chapter's Iron Council.
