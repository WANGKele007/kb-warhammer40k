Kaggeran is a Frontier World found within the Arkalas Cluster of the Jericho Reach. Once prosperous, the planet had fallen to xenos-heresy long before the Achilus Crusade arrived, becoming a weapon supplier for both sides of the ensuing war between the Imperium and the T'au Empire.

History

Like the worlds of Argoth and Rheelas, Kaggeran was colonised during the early years of the 32nd Millennium, under the expansionist mandate of a succession of reigning lords sector. Little in the fragmentary records of that era remains, but there is no evidence to suggest that any of the worlds in what was then known as the Arkalas Cluster were anything more than a collection of backwater colonies adjacent to a well-travelled Warp route.

This changed in the latter years of the 8th century of the 41st Millennium, less than a solar decade after the conflict known as the Damocles Crusade. Expanding outwards from their homeworld, and having reached the coreward edge of the Jericho Sector, the alien T'au began to establish a strong presence within the region, variously conquering, treating with or simply colonising the worlds they came across. The trio of worlds were a distant goal at the time, many solar months travel for even their fastest ships, but all three were resource-bearing worlds, and thus a prize to be coveted.

Swift messenger ships carrying Water Caste dignitaries followed the intelligence-gathering drone-scouts, conveying greetings on behalf of the T'au Empire and expressing a desire for peaceful cooperation, endeavouring to appeal to "The Unity" as like-minded individuals in a hostile galaxy.

The T'au could attempt little more than this opening preamble before the Imperium arrived to reclaim their worlds. Unwilling and unable to fully muster a defence against the crusaders, the T'au fell back from Argoth, Rheelas and Kaggeran, while gathering a force to contest the Imperium on Wrath, closer to the worlds the T'au had already claimed.

Achilus Crusade

Kaggeran became the second T'au Sept World to fall during Operation Hammerfall, with the first having been Rheelas. Within solar hours, the planet was garrisoned and declared Compliant by the Achilus Crusade, having found only minimal signs of contact with the T'au.

When the Adeptus Astartes led an assault on Ravacene, however, a rebellion surged across multiple Compliant worlds, including Kaggeran. It was only through the expert coordination of Deathwatch kill-teams that the Unity seditionist group on Argoth, Kaggeran and Rheelas was put down. This destabilised and demoralised the T'au forces enough that the Astra Militarum could round up and execute the leaderless and scattered rebellion.

Quarantine

Within solar days of his promotion to command of the Canis Salient, Lord Commander Sebiascor Ebongrave gave his first general order -- the quarantine of Argoth, Rheelas and Kaggeran, the three worlds that had suffered the worst of the T'au-sympathiser rebellions.
