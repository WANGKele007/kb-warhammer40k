The Angels Excelsis was a Loyalist Space Marine Chapter and a Successor Chapter of the Blood Angels, created during an unknown Founding of the 36th Millennium. 

Most of the original three hundred founders of the Chapter had died within a standard year of the Chapter's creation, due to the dangers they faced on their first mission. 

Created as they were from the Blood Angels' gene-seed, the Angels Excelsis carried the genetic Flaw inherited from Sanguinius.

The Angels Excelsis were destroyed to the last Astartes during the Devastation of Baal in ca. 999.M41. 

Chapter History

Notable Campaigns

Devastation of Baal (ca. 999.M41) - The Chapter took part in the defence of Baal along with several other successors of the Blood Angels during the Devastation of Baal but was ultimately destroyed by the Tyranids of Hive Fleet Leviathan.

Notable Angels Excelsis

Lord Follordark - Lord Follordark, called the "Void Sword" and "Master of Utrech," was the last known Chapter Master of the Angels Excelsis Chapter.

Captain Erwin - Captain Erwin was the commander of the Chapter's 2nd Company prior to the Devastation of Baal and was the commander of the strike cruiser Splendid Pinion. He and the rest of his Chapter were killed during the void battle phase of the Devastation of Baal.

Chapter Fleet

Essence Eternal (Battle Barge) - The Essence Eternal was a battle barge of the Angels Excelsis that served as the flagship of the Chapter. During the Devastation of Baal it was destroyed by the bio-ships of Hive Fleet Leviathan.

Splendid Pinion (Strike Cruiser) - The Splendid Pinion was the strike cruiser assigned to the Angels Excelsis' 2nd Company and was part of the force sent to assist the Blood Angels during the Devastation of Baal. The Splendid Pinion was destroyed during the void battle that engulfed Baal.

Angelic (Unknown Class) - The Angelic was a warship of the Angels Excelsis. During the Devastation of Baal it was destroyed by the bio-ships of Hive Fleet Leviathan.

Chapter Appearance

Chapter Colours

The Angels Excelsis had a two-tone colour scheme with deep crimson covering the torso of their power armour, while the arms, legs and backpack are marble white. 

The captains of the Angels Excelcis wore golden helmets.

Chapter Badge

The Angels Excelsis' Chapter badge was a winged axe.
