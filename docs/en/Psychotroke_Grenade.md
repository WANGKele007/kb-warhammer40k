A Psychotroke Grenade is a special type of grenade utilised by the Grey Knights and the agents of the Inquisition to induce hallucinations in and disorient a target.

Psychotroke Grenades explode in a cloud of sweet-smelling nerve gas that induces violent, and often debilitating, visions in the enemy. 

This gas is incredibly psycho-reactive and mere proximity is enough to trigger its effects, rendering even sealed armour useless.

Effects

The following mental effects have been noticed in targets of Psychotroke Grenade assaults. Note that the descriptions included below have been based
on the recorded responses of Human subjects -- xenos races will, of course, react in their own, inimitable fashion.
What's this? Smells a bit odd. - Some targets are capable of resisting the effects of the nerve gas.
Suddenly, it's all so clear. The fighting, your allies, that mortal wound- everything's just a hugecosmic joke! Best to sit down and have a good laugh about it. - The target either ceases fighting or finds their combat effectiveness dramatically reduced.
Hah! Those enemies aren't real, they're just an illusion -- you're not falling for that! - The target does not act to defend themselves.
They're horrible! Your darkest fears have been given form and are coming to kill you! - The target's morale is dramatically reduced as they confront their own inner fears made real.
The world is spinning - everything is all jumbled up. It's all you can do to keep your feet. - The target's combat effectiveness is dramatically reduced by a sudden loss of balance.
They say they're your friends, but they're notthey're out to get you. Best get them first! - Overcome by sudden extreme paranoia, the target may turn to attack their own allies.
