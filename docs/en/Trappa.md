A Trappa is a Feral Ork who is a specialist at gathering meat and furs from their homeworld's native fauna for the rest of the tribe.

They are excellent at sneaking up on their enemies and set traps for them many solar days in advance. They use three different basic yet often deadly traps, known as Bang Traps, Fire Bombs and Punji Pits.
