The Resilient is a battle barge of the Ultramarines' Chapter fleet that serves the 2nd Company. It served as the 2nd Company's flagship, under the command of Captain Sevastus Acheran, during the Recidious Campaign of the Fourth Tyrannic War. The 2nd Company's objective was to save the Recidious System of the Bastior Sub-sector from a splinter fleet of Hive Fleet Leviathan and an assault by the forces of the Thousand Sons Sorcerer Lord Imurah who were in pursuit of the Warp-based weapon developed by the Adeptus Mechanicus known as Project Aurora.

During the campaign the Resilient carried 90+ battle-brothers of the 2nd Company, all of whom were deployed to the various theatres as Primaris Marine Intercessor Squads via Thunderhawk gunships.
