An Enmitic Disintegrator Pistol is the pistol form of a Necron Enmitic Weapon.

Enmitic Weapons are directed energy weapons that unleash pulses which cause the victim's atoms to violently repel each other. Enmitic Weapon ammunition is not a physical material or power source, but rather information. They cast quantum-commanded hekatic decrees written in the basal language of reality itself, which re-orders the molecules of their targets in such a way as to dissolve their atomic bonds. Enmitic Disintegrator Pistols are often favoured weapons of Necron Hexmark Destroyers.
