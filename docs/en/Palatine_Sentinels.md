The Palatine Sentinels are one of several psycho-conditioned Astra Militarum regiments who guard the Imperial Palace's outer walls on Terra.

Unlike the Lucifer Blacks, who are famed within the Imperium at large, the white-robed Palatine Sentinels are not well-known outside of Terra itself.

Notable Palatine Sentinels Regiments

156th Palatine Sentinels Regiment, "Tramman Standards" - The Palatine Sentinels' 156th Regiment defended the Imperial Palace during the 13th Black Crusade. They fought to protect the palace's walls from a horde of daemons during the chaotic aftermath of the Great Rift's creation.
