Rahz Obek, also known as "Firebearer" among his brother Legiones Astartes, was a captain of the Salamanders Space Marine Legion during the Great Crusade and the Horus Heresy, serving as commander of the "Firedrakes", the elite Terminator-armored Veteran Astartes and personal honour guard of their primarch Vulkan. As one of the most senior officers of the XVIIIth Legion, Obek was entrusted with his gene-father's protection and the command of the Legion's most dangerous assault operations.

Known for his stern temperament and unwavering adherence to the Salamanders' Promethean Cult, Obek embodied the endurance and self-sacrifice for which the Salamanders were renowned. He fought during the Drop Site Massacre on Isstvan V and survived the destruction of much of his Legion, remaining steadfast in his belief that Vulkan endured beyond the fires of their betrayal. Obek survived the massacre, though what befell him afterwards is unknown.

History

After reunion with his Legion on Nocturne and the reformation of the XVIIIth Legion, the primarch Vulkan created the "Firedrakes", an elite unit within the Legion, comprised of the finest Veteran warriors, chosen not simply for their martial skill, but also because of their mental resilience, discipline and capacity for self-sacrifice -- such abilities as the Lord of Drakes himself was famous for. Rahz Obek was among them. As the Imperium expanded across the galaxy, he eventually achieved the rank of captain of the Firedrakes and stood among the most senior and respected officers among his battle-brothers, serving as one of the leading commanders of the Salamanders Legion during the latter solar decades of the Great Crusade. 

The warriors of the Firedrakes were equipped with Assault Terminator armour and deployed in circumstances where extreme resilience and shock assault capability were required. They frequently formed the spearhead of the Salamanders' planetary assaults, executed precision strikes against heavily fortified positions, and acted as a strategic reserve committed only when a battle's outcome hung in the balance. 

Obek maintained strict discipline within the formation, holding his warriors to the highest standards of conduct and endurance. Under his command, the Firedrakes became renowned for their implacable advance and refusal to yield ground once committed. Unlike some of his battle-brothers who expressed their primarch's humanitarian values through overt compassion towards mortals, Obek was known for his stern and uncompromising interpretation of those same teachings. He believed that true protection of the weak required unbreakable strength and absolute devotion to duty. Obek's proximity to his gene-father during campaigns reinforced both his authority and his conviction, and he was regarded as one of the primarch's most trusted battlefield commanders. 

At the outbreak of the Horus Heresy, the Salamanders were deployed to the world of Isstvan V alongside the Iron Hands and Raven Guard. When the warriors of Horus Lupercal's Sons of Horus Legion revealed their betrayal in what became known as the Drop Site Massacre on Isstvan V, Obek and the Firedrakes fought in immediate support of Vulkan at the centre of the XVIIIth Legion's formation and created a defensive bastion around their lord. 

Clad in Terminator Armour, they withstood overwhelming fire and engaged in brutal close-quarters combat against their former allies. The scale of the betrayal inflicted catastrophic losses upon the Salamanders, and the Firedrakes themselves suffered grievous casualties while attempting to stabilise the collapsing front and extract surviving elements. 

During the chaos of the battle, Vulkan was struck down by a tactical nuclear strike and subsequently disappeared. Despite the psychological shock of his primarch's seeming death, Obek survived the Traitors' trap and eventually made his way off the scoured planet. He attempted to rally scattered survivors and impose order amid the devastation, exemplifying the discipline expected of the Firedrakes even in defeat. 

In the wake of Isstvan V, the XVIII Legion was left shattered and leaderless. Obek, drawing upon his authority as captain of the elite Firedrakes, played a role in maintaining cohesion among his surviving battle-brothers. Though the Shattered Legions had suffered near-annihilation, he refused to accept that Vulkan was truly dead. Obek's conviction that his primarch endured was rooted in years of service at his side and in the belief that Vulkan embodied the very principle of resilience his gene-sons revered. This belief sustained him and those under his command during the Legion's darkest hours.
