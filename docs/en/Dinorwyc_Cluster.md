The Dinorwyc Cluster was a regional grouping of Imperial Agri-worlds located in the Cadian Sector of the Segmentum Obscurus. Its agricultural output was considered vital to the supply of the nearby worlds. 

It was for this reason that in 956.M41 the Imperium moved swiftly to put down the rebellion on the nearby Fortress World of Kieldar during what became known as the Kieldar Offensive. The Segmentum High Command feared that the Kieldarian rebellion, later proved to have been instigated by Chaos Cultists, might have spread to the cluster.
