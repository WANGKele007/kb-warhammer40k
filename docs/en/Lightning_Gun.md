A Lightning Gun is an electromagnetic direct energy weapon wielded by the military units of the ancient Mechanicum during the Great Crusade and Horus Heresy, most notably the Thallax and the Legio Cybernetica's robotic Battle-automata. The weapon's powerful ionising discharges are particularly effective when used to disable enemy machines and electronics. Variants of the weapon may still be in use by the Adeptus Mechanicus forces of the 41st Millennium.

This aptly named weapon takes the form of a baroquely designed, short-barralled carbine connected to a fusion micro-reactor or power core. It fires a las-beam along with a powerful, phased discharge of ionising subatomic particles that looks to an observer like a bolt of lightning. The ensuing effect interferes with electrical systems and allows the weapon to overload mechanical and electronic targets in addition to the physical damage it unleashes against organic enemies.
