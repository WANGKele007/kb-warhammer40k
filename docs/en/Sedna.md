Sedna was a planetoid or planet-sized object located in the outer reaches of the Sol System that was controlled by an unknown xenos species dedicated to war. It was later described by Imperial scholars as a "false world" and a "nemesis world." 

In one of the first campaigns of the Great Crusade in 803.M30, remembered as the "Sedna Campaign," it was assaulted by an Imperial expeditionary force comprised of eight full Space Marine Legions. 

The Space Marines succeeded in defeating Sedna's xenos rulers through an unknown use of psychic abilities. In the wake of this assault, Sedna fell silent. It has since been lost to Imperial knowledge.
