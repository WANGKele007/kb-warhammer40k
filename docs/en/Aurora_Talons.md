The Aurora Talons is a Loyalist Space Marine Chapter of unknown Founding and origin. Like many unorthodox Chapters, this one utilises unique titles that are products of the Chapter's history and creed. 

The Aurora Talons are known for maintaining no less than five "Master Bombardiers" in its command roster. Almost nothing else is known about this Chapter in current Imperial records.
