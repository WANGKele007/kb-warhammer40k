A Gitfinda is an electronic targeting device employed by Ork Flash Gitz that can actually raise their ranged weapon accuracy close to that of an average Human soldier. 

Gitfindas can take many forms, which are as varied as Ork technology, including as ocular bioniks, binocular goggles, monocular head-sets, oversized external telescopes, or myriad other forms whose variety is known only to Mork.

Targeting Squigs

Some Flash Gitz prefer to use specially-bred Squigs called Targeting Squigs that act as a biomechanical form of Gitfinda, positioned somewhere on the Ork's body and cybernetically linked to their master so that the Squig can direct the Flash Git's fire to likely targets.
