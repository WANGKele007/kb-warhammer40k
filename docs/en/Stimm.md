A Stimm is the generic term for any one of a number of different powerful stimulant drugs used in the Imperium of Man. A stimm is a powerful combat drug that works to mask pain and drive warriors on when their bodies would otherwise give up. 

Stimms of various kinds are used in all the branches of the Imperial military forces. The overuse of stimms can have ultimately deleterious effects upon the body, up to and including death.
