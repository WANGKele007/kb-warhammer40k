An Equalizer is a T'au close combat weapon that is utilised exclusively by members of the Ethereal Caste. Equalizers are carried in pairs.

Similar in appearance to batons, Equalizers serve as badges of an Ethereal's office, and also double as potent self-defence weapons. Each Equalizer contains a powerful disruption field that can shatter armour and bone on impact.
