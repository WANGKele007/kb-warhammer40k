The Novaskyr Adamants are regiments of the Astra Militarum native to the world of Novaskyr in the Elara's Veil sector of the Ultima Segmentum. 

They are staunch members of the alliance of Imperial military forces known as the Adeptus Vaelarii, or the "Sentinels of the Veil," that fight alongside and support the Adeptus Astartes Chapters of the Emperor's Spears and the Celestial Lions and the Adeptus Mechanicus forces of the Forge Moon of Bellona.

Notable Regiments

89th Novaskyr Adamants - The 89th Novaskyr provided support to the Emperor's Spears in their ongoing combat operations against the Exilarchy during the Era Indomitus.
