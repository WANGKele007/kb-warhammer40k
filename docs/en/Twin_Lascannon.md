The Twin Lascannon is an Imperial Laser Weapon that is essentially 2 Lascannons twin-linked to fire as one weapon. A Twin Lascannon is often deployed as part of an alternate weapons load-out of the Primaris Space Marine Repulsor grav-tank, replacing its standard Twin Heavy Bolter.

Sources

Codex Adeptus Astartes - Space Marines (8th Edition), pp. 87, 182
