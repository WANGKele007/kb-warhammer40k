The Kindred of Aârnok is a Kindred of the Leagues of Votann. The Kindred of Aârnok is known to possess Balewind harvester stations. This is an example of the extremophile inclinations of many Leagues of Votann Kindreds.

Codex: Leagues of Votann (9th Edition), pg. 25
