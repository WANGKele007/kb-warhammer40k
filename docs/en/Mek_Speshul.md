A Mek Speshul is a type of powerful Ork automatic ballistic weapon similar to a gatling gun. This cannon lays down howling hails of red-hot projectiles in a constant stream. It is often the primary weapon mounted on the Ork attack vehicle known as the Boomdakka Snazzwagon.
