An Orbital Defence Platform, also called an Orbital Defence Station, is an Imperial space station used by the Navis Imperialis and local planetary defence forces to defend a strategically important world against assault by enemy spacecraft. These void stations are usually deployed in low orbit within a habitable planet or moon's gravity well and are armed with a variety of standard Imperial starship weapons. 

The most sophisticated Orbital Defence Platforms, those used to defend Adeptus Mechanicus Forge Worlds and other key planets, have no crew at all and use complex cogitators and other logic engines to detect and fire at enemy vessels that do not broadcast the correct vox identification codes.

Types of Orbital Defence Platforms

Orbital Defence Laser Platform - Obital Defence Laser Platforms are armed with massive Defence Lasers, large laser cannons capable of penetrating a capital ship's void shields and thick hull armour. Built around a powerful plasma reactor, a laser platform is armed with several of these large cannons, giving it the ability to direct fire them in a full 360-degree arc. Defence laser platforms are particularly effective at destroying fast-moving escort voidcraft sent ahead of a main enemy fleet to clear away any defences and carry out reconnaissance. They contributed greatly to breaking up Chaos attacks on the worlds of Orar and Elysium during the opening stages of the Gothic War.

Orbital Torpedo Launcher - Orbital torpedo and missile batteries are one of the greatest deterrents to an enemy intent on attacking an Imperial world. They can attack from a distance to disrupt an enemy fleet's advance and deliver a lethal blow against vessels that approach too closely to a planet. An Ork Terror Ship, named the Barbaric, was destroyed by combined salvoes of fire from three torpedo batteries during its attack on the mining colony of Platea in the Cyclops Cluster and they have also proved decisive in driving off several Chaos invasion forces.

Orbital Weapons Platform - An Orbital Weapons Platform normally mounts several naval-grade laser batteries batteries capable of striking at the enemy before they get too close to the defended planet. many orbital batteries are manned by Navis Imperialis personnel, although some are built and crewed by the local planetary defence force at the expense of the Imperial Commander. The most sophisticated orbital defence platforms, such as those found protecting Port Maw and Adeptus Mechanicus Forge Worlds, have no crew at all and instead rely on automated defence systems driven by cogitators and Machine Spirit (artificial intelligence) routines.
