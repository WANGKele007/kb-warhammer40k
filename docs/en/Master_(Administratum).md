Master is the title and rank of a senior bureaucrat or civil servant of the Adeptus Administratum of the Imperium. A master is normally assigned to lead an entire office or division of the Administratum, making them among the most politically powerful civilian officials in the Imperium. 

A master of each subordinate division of the Administratum often possesses a unique title. For example, the Estate Imperium is led by a "chancellor" and the Historical Revision Unit by a "historicus." Bureaucratically, however, both titles are equal and both are masters of the Administratum.

The Segmentum Fortresses of the Navis Imperialis are each said to be led by an Administratum bureaucrat with the title of master.
