Lucian Trajan was an officer of the Ultramarines Chapter who served as the captain of the Ultramarines 2nd Company and Master of the Watch before his death in the 41st Millennium on the world of Beta-Arcturus in a conflict with the Craftworld Aeldari of Biel-Tan. He was replaced as commander of the 2nd Company by his Command Squad sergeant, Demetrian Titus.

History

Lucian Trajan served as the senior sergeant of the Ultramarines 2nd Company when it was under the command of Captain Severus Agemman during the First Tyrannic War. However the 2nd Company was engaged elsewhere when the Ultramarines' homeworld of Macragge was invaded by Hive Fleet Behemoth. Though the 2nd Company raced home to aid their Chapter, they arrived after victory had been achieved in the Battle of Macragge and the company instead joined the Imperial reinforcements that scoured the worlds of Ultramar free of all remaining Tyranid infestation. 

As the Ultramarines began to rebuild their devastated Chapter after the heavy losses suffered on Macragge, Agemman was promoted to serve as the captain of the reconstituted 1st Company. Trajan stepped up to replace Agemann as the captain of the 2nd Company and he in turn promoted Demetrian Titus to serve as the sergeant of the 2nd Company's Command Squad. 

For a standard century after the Battle of Macragge, Trajan and Titus fought together as close comrades and flourished under the tutelage they received from First Captain Agemman. Sometime later, the 2nd Company deployed to the world of Beta-Arcturus to defend the planet against an invading force of Craftworld Aeldari Aspect Warriors from Biel-Tan, where the company's captain, Lucian Trajan, slew the Aeldari's autarch. Sergeant Titus led Trajan's Command Squad against the elite Aspect Warrior bodyguard of the xenos general, and accounted for the deaths of three of the lethal sword-fighters, brutally carving through their wraithbone armour with his chainsword. The aliens were defeated, but in their spite they captured one of Titus's battle-brothers as they withdrew, a young warrior named Leandros. 

This prompted Captain Trajan to personally lead a combat squad to recover Leandros, ordering Titus to secure the battlefield and await his return. The deceptive Aeldari, however, had lured Trajan into an ambush. Revealing hidden forces not yet committed to the battle, the vengeful Biel-Tan surrounded Trajan and cruelly cut him down. Titus led the counterattack that recovered Trajan's body and assumed command of the strike force that went on to drive the alien aggressors from Beta-Arcturus for good. 

Upon the 2nd Company's return to Macragge, Trajan's body was placed in a grand sepulchre within the Ultramarines' fortress-monastery, the Fortress of Hera. The Ultramarines Chapter Master, Marneus Calgar, decided to promote Sergeant Titus to serve as the new captain of the 2nd Company. While some of his battle-brothers had thought it was a rank he was well suited for in the wake of his long service and heroic actions on Beta-Arcturus, Titus himself had never sought to rise to such heights within the Chapter. He had been content with his position as sergeant and had only wished to serve his commander Trajan and the Emperor in whatever way was asked of him. Many senior Ultramarines, such as First Captain Severus Agemman and the Chapter Master himself, regarded Titus as a model of the humility expected of their Chapter, while also being an exemplary warrior and strategist.
