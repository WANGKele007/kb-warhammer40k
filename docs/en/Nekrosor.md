Nekrosor is a title used among the Necrons of an unknown origin, though it is said that it could only have been granted by the Triarch themselves. With the original meaning of the title in the Necron Lexicon lost, it has become fused with the personal name of the only being still carrying it, the Destroyer Lord Nekrosor Ammentar, and he is usually referred to by it as if it were a proper name rather than a title.
