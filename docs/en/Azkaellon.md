Azkaellon was a Space Marine of the Blood Angels Legion, renowned as the founder and leader of the Sanguinary Guard, the Legion's elite Veteran warriors and personal praetorians of their angelic primarch Sanguinius. Rising to prominence during the Great Crusade, Azkaellon distinguished himself during joint operations alongside other Space Marine Legions, including the Blood Angels and Luna Wolves, where Azkaellon engaged enemies such as the xenos known as the Nephilim and oversaw the containment of Blood Angels warriors afflicted by the Red Thirst. Azkaellon's loyalty to Sanguinius was absolute, shaping both his decisions and his command style, and he quickly became a trusted advisor and commander within the Legion.

During the Horus Heresy, Azkaellon played a critical role in campaigns across the galaxy, including the Signus Campaign, where he confronted both external threats and internal corruption. He witnessed the perils of sorcery, heretical schemes, and the influence of Warp entities, acting decisively to protect his beloved primarch and maintain the cohesion of the Blood Angels. His leadership during these operations demonstrated a combination of tactical precision, physical courage and unwavering devotion to the primarch, even when it placed him in conflict with other members of the Legion.

Azkaellon also participated in the Siege of Terra, where he defended strategic points such as the Eternity Wall Spaceport and the Lion's Gate, often placing himself in direct combat against the most dangerous foes of the Imperium, including the World Eaters, Death Guard and Night Lords. His actions preserved Imperial lives, safeguarded critical positions, and ensured that Sanguinius's directives were carried out even amid chaos. During the final battles of the Siege of Terra, he coordinated with surviving officers to reorganise the Blood Angels Legion and defend the Inner Palace, exemplifying Imperial leadership under extreme duress.

Following the death of Sanguinius at the hands of his brother Horus on the Sons of Horus's corrupted flagship Vengeful Spirit, Azkaellon, like many Blood Angels, succumbed to the Black Rage, turning his fury upon both friend and foe. He later aided in the funeral rites for the primarch and took part in stabilising the IX Legion in the aftermath of the Heresy. In the subsequent division of the Blood Angels into multiple Successor Chapters of the Sanguinary Brotherhood, Azkaellon oversaw the process alongside First Captain Raldoron, helping to ensure the continuity and integrity of the IX Legion's legacy.

Though his mortal body is long deceased, his legacy lives on in the successors of the Sanguinary Brotherhood he created. Following Sanguinius's final orders, the Sanguinary Guard also managed to survive when the Blood Angels' primarch did not, for Azkaellon made certain that their legacy was maintained, not just in the Blood Angels, but in every Successor Chapter that arose from the dissolution of the Blood Angels Legion during the Second Founding. The Sanguinary Guard believe the spirit of their founder lives on in the form of the golden angel of vengeance known as the Sanguinor, preserved against the withering millennia by the Emperor's grace.

History

Origins

Azkaellon served as a senior warrior of the Blood Angels Legion during the Great Crusade, distinguishing himself in multiple campaigns alongside both his Legion and allied forces. He participated in the joint Blood Angels–Luna Wolves assault on Melchior, where the two Legions confronted the enigmatic xenos known as the Nephilim. In the aftermath, it was discovered that Alotros of the Blood Angels' 111th Company had succumbed to the Red Thirst. As one of the few who understood the nature and implications of the condition and the flaw in the Blood Angels' gene-seed that produced it, Azkaellon assisted First Captain Raldoron in securing the site and maintaining operational control. 

Azkaellon initially opposed involving Sanguinius directly in the situation, arguing that it required discretion, though the primarch insisted upon his presence. Both Blood Angels were stunned to witness their gene-father emerge from the execution of Alotros alongside Horus Lupercal. In the tense aftermath, Azkaellon confronted Raldoron over the readiness of his warriors, while the first captain noted that Sergeant Zuriel had also failed to detect the Warmaster's arrival at the scene of the execution. Azkaellon responded that Zuriel would face disciplinary measures, which Raldoron acknowledged as inevitable.

In later years, Azkaellon was dispatched with Task Force Ignis to investigate reports of a scientific facility on Nartaba Octus, purportedly capable of aiding the Blood Angels in managing the Red Thirst. Upon arrival, the IX Legion discovered that the planet had been devastated by Drukhari raiders, who had destroyed the research installation. 

Though Azkaellon played a limited role in the ensuing planetary engagement at the battle of Nartaba Octus, he personally checked on Apothecary Meros after his battle-brother had been injured by a Drukhari soulseeker. Expecting the Apothecary to have perished, Azkaellon was impressed by Meros's survival and resilience. He subsequently relayed operational orders to Meros, Sergeant Cassiel, and Sarga, instructing that the IX Legion to concentrate their forces at the Signus Cluster, in compliance with directives from the Warmaster Horus.

During Task Force Ignis's preparations to depart, a contingent from the Space Wolves Legion arrived, led by Captain Helik Redknife and accompanied by Rune Priest Jonor Stiel. Azkaellon formally rebuked Redknife for reckless piloting and the presence of the Librarian, citing the Edict of Nikaea's restrictions on the use of psychic powers. Redknife clarified that Stiel's abilities were drawn from the power of Fenris rather than the Warp, an explanation which Azkaellon accepted, though with measured disapproval of what was essentially prohibited superstition.

At an earlier point in the Great Crusade, Azkaellon had engaged Lucius of the Emperor's Children in a duel aboard an oceanic warship. Although Lucius struck first and won by drawing first blood, Azkaellon seized the initiative and tackled his opponent, driving him overboard, which humiliated Lucius in front of his warriors and robbing him of the perfect victory he had sought over the Blood Angel Veteran.

Horus Heresy

Signus Campaign

Azkaellon was present during the Blood Angels' subsequent operations in the Signus Cluster during the Signus Campaign, where a contingent of Word Bearers under Chaplain Tanus Kreed accompanied the fleet. While monitoring communications, Azkaellon detected anomalous background noise in the vox transmissions, which he initially dismissed as routine countermeasures against electronic interference. Upon reaching the bridge alongside Sanguinius, Azkaellon witnessed the destruction of the planet Phorus and participated in the convening of the Council of Angels, where Sanguinius ordered a scout team to investigate Holst, a system occupied by both Blood Angels and Word Bearers forces. 

During the operation, the planet itself began attacking the Space Marines, resulting in the destruction of the Paleknight. Azkaellon observed his primarch enact an unprecedented Exterminatus Extremis on Holst, annihilating the planet's surface and its inhabitants. Following this, Azkaellon debriefed the warriors involved in the operation, including First Captain Raldoron and High Warden Dahka Berus, and expressed deep skepticism regarding the account provided by Apothecary Meros.

During a second mission to the world of Scoltrum, survivors of the Chaos assault on the Signus Cluster were located, and Azkaellon questioned them about the Nephilim, though Captain Dar Nakir confirmed that they did not recognize the entities. As the Blood Angels fleet approached Signus Prime, non-Astartes aboard the ships were assailed by a psychic scream of unknown origin. Azkaellon accompanied Sanguinius and Raldoron when Kyriss the Perverse, a Greater Daemon of Tzeentch claiming dominion over the Signus Cluster, made contact with the fleet.

The engagement escalated when Admiral Athene DuCade, shipmaster of the Blood Angels' flagship Red Tear, succumbed to madness and set the battleship on a collision course with Signus Prime. Azkaellon advocated abandoning the vessel, but Sanguinius refused, electing to lead the fleet's descent alongside his warriors. Subsequently, during another Council of Angels of the IX Legion's senior commanders, Apothecary Meros reported that the Dark Page, a vessel carrying Word Bearers forces, had escaped, leaving the Red Tear exposed -- a fact Azkaellon independently verified. 

Captain Nassir Amit raised concerns that the Signus Cluster operation was a trap, potentially orchestrated by the Warmaster Horus himself to weaken the Blood Angels. Sanguinius reacted with near-lethal anger to this disparagement of his beloved brother primarch, only restrained from killing Amit outright when the Space Wolf Captain Helik Redknife revealed that Magnus the Red had violated the Edict of Nikaea. Azkaellon initially struggled to reconcile himself with the implications of the Imperium turning upon its own, denying that such was possible, but watched as Sanguinius commanded the Blood Angels to swear an oath of moment against the enemy.

During the ensuing climax of the Signus Campaign at the Battle of Signus Prime, Azkaellon fought alongside Sanguinius but was separated from his gene-father when the latter engaged the Bloodthirster Ka'Bandha and was badly injured. In a desperate effort to protect his primarch's life, Azkaellon forcefully moved Apothecary Meros aside as he attempted to stabilise Sanguinius with a wild look in his eyes. Upon teleportation of the primarch to the Red Tear, the Apothecaries were unable to revive Sanguinius, and the Blood Angels as a whole began exhibiting heightened and irrational aggression and fury in response to their primarch's incapacitation. Unable to contact the rest of the Council of Angels, Azkaellon urged Raldoron to halt the campaign to prioritise Sanguinius's recovery.

At this point, Mkani Kano, Raldoron's adjutant and a former Librarian, revealed that the Blood Angels' former Librarius Corps had assembled and was prepared to employ their psychic powers to restore Sanguinius. Although Azkaellon opposed flagrantly violating the Edict of Nikaea in this way, Raldoron authorised a strike team including Meros and Tyllian Niobe, a Human survivor from Scoltrum who was also an anti-psyker Pariah, to neutralise the Warp power causing the Blood Angels' unending rage. Throughout the operation, Azkaellon remained vigilant among the Librarians, cautioning that if he observed any sign of sorcery he would cut off their heads with his Glaive Encarmine.

Despite his unwavering loyalty to Sanguinius, Azkaellon and the Sanguinary Guard were temporarily overtaken by the rage affecting their brethren, joining the broader conflict on the battlefield and abandoning direct protection of the unconscious primarch. Following the intervention of Apothecary Meros, whose sacrifice finally neutralised the malign Warp influence over his brethren, Azkaellon regained full clarity of mind. In the aftermath, he assisted in overseeing the removal of the Blood Angels' operational presence from the Signus Cluster.

During this operation, Warden Dahka Berus presented Azkaellon with the bodies of Captain Helik Redknife and his accompanying Space Wolves, they realised the importance of their death wounds having been caused by Space Marines, and kept the truth of their execution hidden from Sanguinius. Azkaellon recognised the strategic necessity of concealing this fact from Sanguinius. Later on, when Captain Nassir Amit confessed his involvement in the deaths of the Space Wolves to Raldoron, Azkaellon administered an oath of secrecy to both warriors, saying if Sanguinius knew the truth, he would blame himself and would not lie to his brother primarch Leman Russ, which threatened the solidarity of the Legiones Asartes while they were still at war. While Raldoron reluctantly saw the logic of this course, Amit asked how he would quiet the pain in his heart that he felt as a result of his deeds. Azkaellon told him that was now his burden to bear. 

Imperium Secundus and Siege of Terra

After the Signus Campaign, when the Blood Angels reached Macragge, the capital of what was then the Imperium Secundus that had been established by Roboute Guilliman during the period when the galaxy was cut in half by the Ruinstorm, Azkaellon was assigned to guard Sanguinius's throne room, where he served as the sovereign of the Imperium Secundus, when the Night Lords primarch Konrad Curze, having emerged from concealment on the planet, launched a direct assault. The Night Hunter took the unconscious Azkaellon hostage in order to be able to communicate his own precognitive visions of a horrific future to Sanguinius, who also shared the Emperor's ability to foresee the future. 

At the conclusion of this encounter, Curze severed Azkaellon's arm and detonated an explosive trap, mutilating the warrior before hurling him from a high balcony to make his escape. Sanguinius chose to rescue his beloved gene-son rather than pursue his mad brother, and the winged primarch successfully retrieved the injured Sanguinary Guard captain in mid-fall. Despite the grievous wounds he had suffered, Azkaellon survived the encounter and was later observed engaging with allies Drakus Gorod, the Ultramarines who commanded Guilliman's Ultramarines Invictarus Suzerain honour guard and Holguin, the commander of the Dark Angels' Deathwing, with whom he had developed both a friendship as well as a professional rapport.

Throughout the campaign by the Blood Angels, Ultramarines and Dark Angels to penetrate the Ruinstorm and reach Terra, Azkaellon continued to serve at Sanguinius's side. He participated in combat engagements at Pyrrhan and Davin, in the latter acting decisively to rescue the primarch from the clutches of the forces of the Daemon of Chaos Undivided Madail. During the Battle of Beta-Garmon, Azkaellon led a boarding operation against the Traitor-occupied star fort called The Anvil, successfully capturing the installation but narrowly escaped its destruction, recognising the space station had been used as a trap set by the Traitors.

During the Siege of Terra, Azkaellon remained vigilant over Sanguinius, concerned for the primarch's safety during his repeated attempts to leave the relative safety of the Sanctum Imperialis to inspire the Imperial troops defending the Imperial Palace complex. When Traitor forces assaulted the Helios Gate of the Imperial Palace, Azkaellon and the Sanguinary Guard engaged alongside their primarch against waves of Chaos Space Marines, including the Death Guard, World Eaters and Night Lords, demonstrating both strategic discipline and personal valour. Prior to Sanguinius's departure to board the Vengeful Spirit alongside the Emperor and Rogal Dorn to confront the Warmaster Horus, Azkaellon was ordered to remain within the Imperial Palace.

Following the fall of Bhab Bastion, Azkaellon rallied with remaining Loyalist officers, including Fafnir Rann, Namahi, Zephon, Maximus Thane and Satel Aimery, to organise the final defence of the Inner Palace from the Traitors under the command of the captain of the Imperial Fists' Huscarl honour guard, Archamus II. In this capacity, Azkaellon exemplified leadership, resilience and tactical acumen, maintaining order among the surviving Blood Angels and ensuring the continued defence of critical Palace positions despite the overwhelming forces arrayed against them.

However, after sensing the death of Sanguinius at the hands of Horus aboard the Vengeful Spirit, the Blood Angels were overwhelmed by the first onset of the so-called "Black Rage", their genetic flaw manifesting in full fury as they psychically sensed the final moments of their primarch's life. Azkaellon was among those who succumbed to this uncontrollable rage, entering a state of near-feral violence in which he attacked indiscriminately, striking out at both Loyalist and Traitor forces alike. Azkaellon even engaged his close comrade Fafnir Rann in combat. Rann initially hesitated to defend himself against his friend, but quickly realised that Azkaellon's strength and rage were such that he could not have slain the captain of the Sanguinary Guard even if he had tried. The duel between the two was only interrupted by the assault of the World Eaters and Death Guard, against whom Azkaellon turned his unnatural ferocity, attacking the Traitors with lethal efficiency until they were slain and he could feast upon their bloody remains.

Following the death of Horus at the hands of the Emperor and the subsequent end of the Black Rage, Azkaellon returned to his faculties. He participated in the funeral rites for Sanguinius, attending alongside Raldoron and an estimated five hundred surviving Blood Angels, honoring their fallen gene-father and beginning the consolidation of the Legion in the wake of its primarch's death.

Post-Heresy

With the remainder of the Sanguinary Guard lost during the boarding of the Vengeful Spirit, Azkaellon emerged as the sole surviving member of his unit alongside Taerwelt Ikasati. In the aftermath of the Heresy, as the Imperium began the arduous process of reconstruction, Azkaellon assumed a pivotal role in the reorganisation of the Blood Angels Legion in accordance with the new order the Second Founding imposed after the Heresy that transformed the Legiones Astartes into the Adeptus Astartes. 

Working alongside his old friend First Captain Raldoron, who would become the first Chapter Master of the newborn Blood Angels Chapter -- Azkaellon helped oversee the division of the IX Legion into the multiple Successor Chapters that comprised the Sanguinary Brotherhood. This process required careful allocation of the Legion's Veteran officers, gene-seed reserves, war materiel and fleet assets to ensure that each new Chapter was capable of independent operation while still honoring the glorious legacy of Sanguinius.

Personality

Azkaellon was defined by his unwavering and absolute loyalty to his primarch Sanguinius, a devotion so extreme that it at times unsettled even Raldoron. When questioned whether he would place the safety of the primarch above that of his own battle-brothers, Azkaellon unhesitatingly affirmed that he would willingly sacrifice any member of the IX Legion to ensure his gene-father's protection. This single-minded commitment to the Great Angel became a hallmark of his character.

Azkaellon was marked by a rigid and unyielding approach to both strategy and combat. Where other leaders, such as Raldoron, employed adaptability and fluid tactics, Azkaellon relied on discipline, precision and a strict adherence to his chosen methods. This inflexibility often created friction between his Sanguinary Guard and the 1st Company commanded by Raldoron, but it also reinforced Azkaellon's reputation as a steadfast and resolute commander, capable of carrying out orders with relentless determination under extreme conditions.

Wargear

Artificer Armour - Like the rest of the Sanguinary Guard, Azkaellon wore a golden suit of Artificer Armour.

Winged Jump Pack - A modified version of the standard Astartes Jump Pack that is designed to emulate Sanguinius' own angelic wings, these relics were used almost exclusively by the Blood Angels' Sanguinary Guard, the Winged Jump Packs of these formidable warriors an awe-inspiring sight to behold. When leaping down from above, it is as if the battle-brothers have taken flight on broad angelic wings like the avenging angels of legend, thus striking fear into their foes and bringing hope to their allies.

Angelus Pattern Bolter - The Angelus Pattern Bolter is a variation on the ubiquitous Space Marine bolter used exclusively by the Blood Angels and their successors. Its magazine is loaded with very rare and potent Bloodshard Shells, whose razor-filament payloads make a mockery of most armour.

Canon Conflict

Earlier lore stated that Azkaellon was the sole member of the Sanguinary Guard to survive the Siege of Terra during the Horus Heresy, but The End and the Death novels claim that Taerwelt Ikasati also survived.
