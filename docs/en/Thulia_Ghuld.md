Thulia Ghuld, known as the Vox Dei among her cybernetic troops, is an Archmagos Terminus of the Adeptus Mechanicus native to the Forge World of Mars. In the Era Indomitus she sought to defend the battered worlds of the Cadian Gate, particularly the Forge World of Agripinaa, from an assault by the forces of Chaos under the command of the Iron Warriors Warsmith Kravek Morne.

History

All Tech-priests of the Adeptus Mechanicus who rise to the rank of Dominus come to master their particular fields of study, and for Thulia Ghuld that field is the art of war. She is a veteran of the Ordo Reductor -- the subcult of the Adeptus Mechanicus that reveres the Omnissiah as an avatar of destruction -- who has spent hundreds of standard years crafting herself and her forces into the purest expression of His divine wrath, augmenting her aging flesh with blessed steel cybernetics until she towered over her peers.

She believes that the Machine God Himself has made her His instrument for the efficient conduct of war, and scorns those like Archmagos Dominus Belisarius Cawl who waste valuable time and resources on diplomacy and experimentation with new technologies when they could be more productively simply blowing Heretics to pieces instead. In turn, Cawl and those of his faction accuse Ghuld of seeing no value in knowledge if it cannot be applied to the pursuit of war -- and in fairness, they are absolutely right. 

Not that the Archmagos Terminus would even dispute those assertions as aspersions on her character, as she sees her single-minded obsession with warfare as just another optimisation granted by the Omnissiah. If He did not want her to slaughter Traitors and xenos by the thousands, why did he make them so easy to kill? 

While many of her Tech-priest peers find Thulia Ghuld distasteful company at best, she is beloved by the Skitarii Legions of the Mechanicus, who view her as the Vox Dei -- the living vox-caster of the Machine God's word. They adore her for her unceasing pursuit of victory, often seized by unstoppable Skitarii armoured legions crowned by heavy Hastarii infantry.

Thulia Ghuld's talents and forces have now been used to defend the remaining Loyalist worlds of the Cadian Gate in the Era Indomitus, which have been ravaged by the forces of Chaos since the Great Rift's creation at the end of the 13th Black Crusade. The Traitors have had free reign to bring their fleets out of the Eye of Terror and Ghuld has used her unstoppable armoured legions of Skitarii to wage war against them. These are often crowned by legions of Hastarii heavy infantry and the archmagos has also used her resources to shore up the Cadian Gate's defences. However, Ghuld is now focusing on defending the Forge World of Agripinaa, which has become targeted by the forces of the Iron Warriors Warsmith Kravek Morne after his recent emergence from the Warp rift.
