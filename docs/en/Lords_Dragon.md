The Lords Dragon is the name given to a secret order of the Adeptus Mechanicus comprised of Tech-priests with the rank of archmagos. 

This cabal is responsible for monitoring all the members of the Mechanicus for Chaos corruption and tech-heresy and eliminating it where it exists. 

In many ways the Lords Dragon act as an Inquisition-like body just for the Adeptus Mechanicus, without involving that organisation in the Mechanicus' internal affairs.

It is rumoured in the Calixis Sector that the Panopticon Orbital Station in the Lathe System is the headquarters of the Lords Dragon, given to them by order of the fabricator-general of Mars himself.
