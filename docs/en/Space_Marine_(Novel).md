Space Marine is a novel written by Ian Watson. The novel is now generally considered no longer canonical as much of the lore surrounding the Imperial Fists and the recruitment of Space Marines described in this novel has changed since 1993. 

The novel is currently still available from Black Library, but only under the Heretic Tomes brand which is reserved for works no longer considered consistent or canonical with the present-day Warhammer 40,000 universe.

Synopsis

Three boys -- Lexandro d'Arquebus, Yeremi Valance, and Biff Tundrish -- are recruited from Hive Trazior on Necromunda to join the Imperial Fists.
