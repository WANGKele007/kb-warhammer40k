The Caer-class Orbital Defence Station is a type of Imperial space station in use by the Navis Imperialis for a system or planetary defence role.

The Caer-class station is ten kilometres across and can accommodate 50,000 residents. It is capable of serving as a naval headquarters and system defence command centre in the event of an attack by enemy space forces. The Caer-class can also serve as a pre-processing centre for convicted criminals before they are transferred to a nearby penal colony.
