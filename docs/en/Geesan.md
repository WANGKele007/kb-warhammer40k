Geesan was a priest of the Adeptus Ministorum who was chosen to become Roboute Guilliman's first Militant-Apostolic during the Indomitus Crusade in the Era Indomitus.

History

Roboute Guilliman created this new rank of priest to guarantee the political support of the Adeptus Ministorum for the actions and changes he made to the Imperium and its various adepta as the Imperial Regent and Lord Commander of the Imperium. The main duty for those priests who hold the title of Militant-Apostolic is to allay any fears among their peers that Guilliman is a Heretic for believing the Emperor is not a god in accord with the ancient doctrines of the Imperial Truth, and to grant assurances that the primarch's non-belief will not spread across the Imperium. 

Geesan served in this role for some time before dying of old age during the Battle of Raukos at the end of the first phase of the crusade to stabilise the Imperium Sanctus. The Missionaria Galaxia priest Mathieu then succeeded Geesan as the new Militant-Apostolic.
