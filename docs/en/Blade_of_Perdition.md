A Blade of Perdition was a master-crafted, two-handed Power Sword wielded by the elite Astartes units of the Blood Angels Legion during the Great Crusade and Horus Heresy eras. 

It generated a unique form of matter disruptor power field that was known as "Deathfire" that did more damage to its target than that of standard pattern Power Weapons.
