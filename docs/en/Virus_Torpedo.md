A Virus Torpedo is a starship weapon similar to a virus bomb used primarily by the Navis Imperialis to release the horrific engineered Life-eater virus onto enemy void ships to eliminate their biological crew and passengers. When fired at an enemy craft the torpedo first penetrates its hull before releasing the Life-eater virus into the vessel's atmosphere. Virus Torpedoes are most commonly used in situations where an enemy ship needs to be captured intact without regard for the safety of its crew or passengers. 

Virus Torpedoes are also sometimes used by pirates who have either stolen them from Imperial naval stocks or constructed crude versions of their own to more easily capture and plunder merchant vessels.
