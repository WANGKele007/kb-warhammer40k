The Regio Quinquaginta-Unus, more commonly known as the Regio, is the Armoury of the Blood Angels Chapter.

Located beneath the surface of the Blood Angels' homeworld of Baal, the vast subterranean complex holds weapons and relics of the Chapter too holy to be displayed within their fortress-monastery and various unique Standard Template Construct technologies that the Blood Angels have re-discovered, such as the Baal Predator. 

This unusual security measure is intended as a defence against a possible attack on the Blood Angels' fortress-monastery from hostile forces, both outside and from within the Imperium, and the Techmarines of the Blood Angels guard their relics vigilantly.

Regio Quinquaginta Unas Research Station

Regio Quinquaginta Unus was also the name of a secret Adeptus Mechanicus research station on the Forge World of Fecundia where Mechanicus Genetors were studying Genestealers and other Tyranid bioforms with the aid of the closely-allied Reclaimers Chapter of the Adeptus Astartes. The escape of some of their test subjects eventually led to an assault by a splinter of Hive Fleet Kraken that revealed that in certain circumstances, the different Tyranid hive fleets were actually hostile to one another.

Commissar Ciaphas Cain was intrumental in the defence of Regio Quinquaginta Unus and Fecundia from the splinter of Hive Fleet Kraken and the research station's own Genestealer and Tyranid test-subjects.

Trivia

"Regio Quinquaginta-Unus" roughly translates from Latin as "Region 51" or "Area 51," the infamous United States Air Force base in Nevada that has been rumoured to be the site where the U.S. government stores extraterrestrial artefacts and corpses.
