House Th'arkone is a Loyalist Imperial Knight house. Its homeworld is the rad-blasted planet of Cironis.

During the Era Indomitus the Torchbearers fleet called The Mahj of Cironis' Fleet was led by the ruling Noble of House Th'arkone.
