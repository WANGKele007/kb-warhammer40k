A War Dog Brigand is a variant of the Armiger Pattern Knight known as a War Dog that serves the Chaos Knights as a ranged combat specialist. 

Between them, War Dog Brigands and their Fallen Noble pilots have become so enamoured of the skillful, ranged kill that they mostly disdain the chaos of close assaults. Some even swear dark pacts compelling them to slay potent foes with a certain level of elegance, or else to purge war zones of all life with sweeping contempt.

Unit Composition

1-3 War Dog Brigands
