The Sigma-Drakoni Union is a league of the Leagues of Votann that is located in the galactic core. It is a martially-inclined league that prizes honourable combat greatly. Its Kinhosts are held to a strict code of conduct at all times, and take an unusual degree of pride in their heraldry and decorative insignia.
