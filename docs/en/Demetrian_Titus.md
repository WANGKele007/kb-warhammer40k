Demetrian Titus, called the Warden of Ultramar, is a former Firstborn captain and commander of the Ultramarines 2nd Company and later a Primaris Marine lieutenant of the same company who has fought for the Imperium of Man for almost 300 Terran years. His exploits earned him a reputation as one of the greatest heroes of the Ultramarines. Despite his relative youth by the standards of the Adeptus Astartes, he was already a decorated Veteran of several major campaigns waged across the galaxy by his Chapter. 

One of Titus' last known campaigns before his disappearance occurred upon the Forge World of Graia where he helped launch a successful defence from an assault by an Ork WAAAGH! led by the Warboss Grimskull and then a subsequent Chaos invasion commanded by the Chaos Sorcerer Nemeroth and his Chosen of Nemeroth warband of Heretic Astartes.

Showing an unnatural affinity to shrug off attacks made using the powers of the Warp by the forces of Chaos during the Liberation of Graia, Titus was taken into custody on suspicion of heresy by the Inquisition after the end of that campaign. Subsequently, he vanished from all official Imperial records for almost a standard century. 

By the Emperor's will, the wayward Titus was miraculously returned to the Ultramarines in the Era Indomitus and cleared of all charges. He proceeded to cross the Rubicon Primaris and was transformed into a Primaris Space Marine. Reassigned to the 2nd Company, he was unfortunately reduced to the rank of lieutenant so as not to bring discord to the Ultramarines' existing hierarchy. Titus cared not for the reduction in status, he was grateful just to be deemed worthy to serve the Chapter once more. he subsequently believed he redeemed himself in both his Chapter's and his own eyes with the victory he helped achieve for the Ultramarines and the Imperial forces in the Recidious Campaign during the Fourth Tyrannic War. 

Following a mission to the world of Zsah'Uj that was expected to result in his death, Titus was instead promoted to the rank of captain and given command of the Ultramarines 2nd Company for the second time, following the death of his predecessor, Sevastus Acheran, defending the Hive World of Trygg from a Genestealer Cult, who had himself replaced Titus' successor in the command, Cato Sicarius. In addition, Titus was named Master of the Watch, the officer tasked with the defence of the Ultramarines fortress-monastery on Macragge. In addition, as Primarch Roboute Guilliman had ordered the Ultramarines to expand the Realm of Ultramar back to its pre-Heresy size of five hundred worlds, Titus was tasked with carrying out this nearly impossible mission and named the "Warden of Ultramar". As a result of the demands of this duty, Titus was allowed to select a unique, hand-picked Command Squad of both mortal Humans and Astartes that was known as the Wardens of Ultramar.

Demetrian Titus is the primary protagonist of the Warhammer 40,000: Space Marine and Warhammer 40,000: Space Marine II third-person action video games.

History

Origins

The saga of Demetrian Titus begins at his birth approximately in the year 665.M41 according to the old Imperial Calendar in the Realm of Ultramar, home of the Ultramarines Chapter who are hailed by the wider Imperium as one of the greatest Chapters of the Adeptus Astartes and the staunchest defenders of the Imperium.

Titus was born on the Ultramarian Agri-world of Tarentus, some eighty standard years before the Battle of Macragge in 745.M41, and was the only child of a humble family of modest means. Titus was later selected as an aspirant by an Ultramarines selector named Metaurus, who remarked that the child had never known fear. This was because Metarus had discovered Titus as a small child carrying an Astartes Combat Knife through a battlefield. Metaurus worried what a soul like that might be capable of becoming. As such, Metaurus recommended the boy become an Astartes so that his talents could be harnessed for the Emperor. Throughout Titus' subsequent brilliant career as a Space Marine, Metaurus remained a mentor and close confidante.

After his induction as a neophyte, Titus joined the Ultramarines' 10th Company as a Scout Marine and excelled at every trial set before him. After becoming a Space Marine, Titus had trouble at times reigning in his temper during his youth, but eventually became a decorated warrior. However in a campaign against the forces of Chaos he was badly injured slaying a Chaos Sorcerer who wiped out the rest of his squad. The aftermath of that action led some to question how Titus had survived where the rest of his battle-brothers had fallen.

More than once since that incident, Titus has been forced to defend his reputation within his Chapter. He overcame these doubts, though, due to his reputation for decisive action and for being a warrior of incredible skill, who always emerged victorious throughout countless battles, despite facing seemingly impossible odds.

Regardless of all challenges, he overcame many opponents and suffered many wounds, both physical and psychological, and rose through the ranks of the Ultramarines while serving in the 2nd Company. While he followed the rules and guidance of the Codex Astartes, the Ultramarines' most sacred tome of strategy, organisation, and moral behaviour penned by his Chapter's primarch Roboute Guilliman, Titus believed that the most important part of the Codex was its spirit, not its letter, and how one chose to uphold its values. 

Early in his career, Titus befriended fellow Ultramarine and Veteran Sergeant Sidonus. At some point in Titus' service, he was part of an Ultramarines squad who engaged a Chaos Sorcerer, a corrupted psyker corrupted by the Ruinous Powers. Titus' whole squad was killed by the sorcerer's Warp-infused attacks, but Titus somehow survived direct blasts of Warp energy, despite suffering grievous wounds during the course of the battle, and single-handedly killed the sorcerer. 

Much to the surprise of the Chapter's Apothecaries, Titus survived his injuries and made a full recovery. Many within the Chapter questioned how he survived, intimating that he may have been touched by the corruption of Chaos. Titus himself had no explanation for his seeming invincibility to the direct touch of the Warp.

Beta-Arcturus and Command of the 2nd Company

Sometime later, the Ultramarines' 2nd Company deployed to the world of Beta-Arcturus to defend the planet against an invading force of Craftworld Aeldari Aspect Warriors from Biel-Tan, where the company's captain, Lucian Trajan, slew the Aeldari's autarch. Titus led Trajan's Command Squad against the elite Aspect Warrior bodyguard of the xenos general, and accounted for the deaths of three of the lethal sword-fighters, brutally carving through their wraithbone armour with his chainsword. The aliens were defeated, but in their spite they captured one of Titus's battle-brothers as they withdrew, a young warrior named Leandros. 

This prompted Trajan to lead a combat squad personally to recover Leandros, ordering Titus to secure the battlefield and await his return. The deceptive Aeldari, however, had lured Trajan into an ambush. Revealing hidden forces not yet committed to the battle, the vengeful Biel-Tan surrounded Trajan and cruelly cut him down. Titus led the counterattack that recovered Trajan's body and assumed command of the strike force that went on to drive the alien aggressors from Beta-Arcturus for good. 

Titus' brevet captaincy over the 2nd Company was a state of affairs that Chapter Master Marneus Calgar was to make permanent, granting Titus command of the company after they bore Trajan's body back to Macragge to be interred within a grand sepulchre within the Fortress of Hera. Titus went on to lead the 2nd Company to victory after victory against the mutant, the alien, and the Heretic. Many senior Ultramarines, such as First Captain Severus Agemman and the Chapter Master himself, regarded Titus as a model of the humility expected of their Chapter, while also being an exemplary warrior and strategist. 

However, despite his meteoric rise and accolades from his Chapter's high command, doubts still lingered within Titus as he still felt the weight of taking over the mantle of command from his fallen mentor. By late in the 41st Millennium according to the old Imperial Calendar, Titus had served for over 150 standard years, with Leandros and Sidonus at his side in every battle, leading the Ultramarines 2nd Company to victory after victory against all manners of horrific threats.

Liberation of Graia

Ten standard years after taking command of the 2nd Company, Captain Titus led an Ultramarines strike force to relieve the Forge World of Graia, which had been invaded by the 1 million-strong force of Orks of WAAAGH! Grimskull. Warboss Grimskull was intent on stealing advanced Imperial weapons, including a Warlord-class Battle Titan.

The assault was part of an intricate plan unleashed by the Chaos Sorcerer Nemeroth, the Chaos Lord of a warband of Chaos Space Marines known as the Chosen of Nemeroth, to get his hands on an experimental power source on the world that would allow him to ascend to become a Daemon Prince. Accompanied by 2 members of his Command Squad, Sergeant Sidonus and Battle-Brother Leandros, Titus joined with the Astra Militarum's 203rd Cadian Shock Troops Regiment and its only surviving officer, Second Lieutenant Mira, to eventually kill Grimskull and halt the WAAAGH!.

Unfortunately, the Ordo Xenos Inquisitor Drogan, whom Titus and his Astartes relied upon for assistance in retaking the world, was actually an undead pawn of Nemeroth who used a specially-designed weapon called the "Psychic Scourge" powered by the experimental power source Nemeroth wanted to open a portal into the Warp. This allowed the Chaos Sorcerer, a horde of Chaos Daemons and his Chaos Space Marines to come to Graia. 

Curiously, however, Titus once more displayed an unknown immunity to the sorcerer's psychic powers which immediately raised the suspicions of the highly orthodox and Puritan Leandros to his commander's possible corruption.

Following the death of his old friend Sidonus at Nemeroth's hand, Titus hunted down the foul Chaos Sorcerer and slew him just as he was in the throes of the ascent to Daemonhood, banishing his dark soul back to the Warp for punishment by his Dark Gods.

Titus' Captivity

Despite his valiant efforts to liberate Graia, Captain Titus was sequestered by Inquisitor Thrax and his retinue of Black Templars Space Marines on suspicion of heresy, after Brother Leandros reported his captain's display of uncommon resistance to corruptive Warp power unleashed by the Chaos Sorcerer Nemeroth, as well as Titus' multiple deviations from the Codex Astartes, held sacrosanct by all Ultramarines. 

Captain Titus was taken away for interrogation by Inquisitor Thrax, whereupon he disappeared from the Chapter annals for a time. Cato Sicarius, who had felt that he had been overlooked for command following the First Tyrannic War, replaced Titus as captain of the 2nd Company. 

As it turns out, Inquisitor Thrax held an extreme grudge against Astartes, seeing them as a liability to the Imperium and never quite trusting them, especially after the events of the Badab War, an uprising instigated by the Renegade Astral Claws Chapter early in the 10th century of the 41st Millennium. Titus was imprisoned by the Inquisition for over a standard century, continuously tortured and psychically interrogated by Thrax in an attempt to find the truth of Titus' supernatural resilience to Warp energies, keeping Titus in stasis between interrogations. The Space Marine captain was psychically scourged and subjected to brutal mind-trawls, and even though no sign of Chaos corruption was ever discerned, the Inquisitor refused to relinquish his captive. 

During this time, Ultramarines Chapter Master Marneus Calgar made numerous petitions to representatives of the Holy Ordos of the Emperor's Inquisition, demanding that his captain be returned to the brotherhood of the Ultramarines. Whether these requests fell on deaf ears or were lost in the labyrinthine levels of secrecy and self-proclaimed hierarchy that exists within the Inquisition, Calgar never learned. No word reached him of either Inquisitor Thrax or his captive, Demetrian Titus, for many Terran years.

Deathwatch Service

After a standard century of captivity by the Inquisition, Inquisitor Thrax led an operation against the traitorous Grey Slayers Chapter, who had become corrupted by the insidious machinations of the Dark Gods. Leading a large retinue of Grey Knights upon the corrupted Chapter's homeworld, they began to purge the Heretic Astartes in earnest. 

However, during the conflict, Thrax was possessed by a Daemon, and in turn, was killed by his retinue of Grey Knights. In the wake of Thrax's death, forces drawn from the Red Hunters Chapter were dispatched to investigate and secure the Inquisitor's holdings. In a remote Inquisitorial watch station far to the galactic south, a Red Hunters kill-team discovered almost a score of Space Marine interrogation subjects locked in stasis cells, preserved from the passage of time. Titus' story could have come to an end there, had the Inquisitor responsible for overseeing the seizure of Thrax's materials felt inclined to conceal the shame of her fellow Inquisitor. Instead, Titus, and several other warriors of various Chapters who had attracted Thrax's attention, were conveyed to the Deathwatch watch fortress of Watcher Keep to be evaluated.

After being found free of Chaos taint or disloyalty, Titus would eventually enter the service of Watcher Keep as a Deathwatch Black Shield, his former identity unknown to those he fought alongside. Having received no word from his brethren since his incarceration, he came to believe he had somehow brought shame upon his name and that of his beloved Ultramarines and determined that he was not worthy to bear the Chapter's name. Overcome with shock and grief at his discommendation, Titus had all of his former Chapter's heraldry purged from his battle-plate and assumed a self-imposed exile as an outcast Black Shield. 

If he could serve the Imperium and his Emperor as an alien-hunting battle-brother of the Deathwatch until death claimed him, he would do so gladly, having no more desire for personal glory than he did at the start of his life as a Space Marine. Assuming the name "Nullus," Titus honourably served alongside his fellow battle-brothers in the Deathwatch for many standard years. During this point in time, unbeknownst to Titus, the Ultramarines' primarch Roboute Guilliman had been resurrected on Macragge during the Ultramar Campaign of the 13th Black Crusade, and now once again served as lord commander of the Imperium and Imperial Regent. 

Guilliman led his newly declared Indomitus Crusade against the various enemies of Humanity, as the galaxy had literally been split in two by the opening of the Great Rift at the start of the Era Indomitus, a galaxy-spanning Warp rift formed in the wake of the destruction of the Fortress World of Cadia at the hands of the Warmaster of Chaos, Abaddon the Despoiler. However, this crusade left many worlds weakened after committing large numbers of troops to the war effort, leaving them more vulnerable to invasion. 

During this time, the Fourth Tyrannic War began in the Segmentum Pacificus, as swarms of Tyranids, ravenous extragalactic aliens that threatened to consume all life in the Milky Way, invaded the already-beleaguered strongholds of Humanity in the galactic west.

Return to the Ultramarines

Titus, as "Nullus," deployed as part of the Deathwatch's Kill-team Kasaeran, alongside Brother Ulfar of the Space Wolves, Brother Kelstros of the Blood Angels, Brother Beren of the Black Templars, and Brother Darius of the Dark Angels, transiting to the Recidious System via Corvus Blackstar. During their mission to the Recidious System, Nullus and his squad were tasked with reinforcing the Astra Militarum forces of the 8th Cadian based there on the Jungle World of Kadaku, who were already weakened by the toll taken by the Indomitus Crusade muster across the Imperium Sanctus. The kill-team's mission involved firing a virus bomb into the stratosphere using a local orbital launcher at the main swarm of the Tyranids in the hopes of killing enough of the bioforms before they adapted to the pathogen to give the Astra Militarum a chance at eradicating the rest of the swarm.

The kill-team's gunship was brought down by swarming flocks of winged Tyranid bioforms, forcing the warriors to engage with the insectoid aliens on the planet's surface as they struggled to get the virus bomb to the launcher. During this mission, Titus was gravely wounded by a monstrous Tyranid Carnifex after he had fought off swarm after swarm of lesser bioforms. As life bled from his torn body, his fellow Deathwatch battle-brothers having all been slain before him, he prepared to commend his soul to the Emperor. It was then that Titus was reunited with the warriors of Ultramar, as an Ultramarines strike force arrived to relieve the Deathwatch at Kadaku. 

Varro Tigurius, Chief Librarian of the Ultramarines, detected the soul-sign of the warrior once known as Demetrian Titus in the local Immaterium as their strike craft traversed the Warp. Even as the Tyranids' Shadow in the Warp began to cloud Tigurious' witch-sight like cataracts, the Chief Librarian recognised he who had once been the captain of the Ultramarines 2nd Company. 

As fate would have it, Tigurius travelled aboard the Righteous Fury, the same vessel that had borne Titus and Trajan before him to battle many times, and it was this mighty voidcraft of the Chapter that broke from the Warp at the edge of the Recidious System. There, the strike cruiser prepared its Thunderhawk gunships to deploy to the planet where the broken form of the Black Shield known to his comrades as Nullus lay dying. 

The Ultramarines 1st Company arrived on the planet to reinforce the Deathwatch forces, bringing none other than First Captain Severus Agemman and Chief Librarian Tigurius. The Ultramarines fought valiantly, their weapon blasts wreaking devastation upon the Tyranid bioforms with the carefully born discipline of experienced Tyranid hunters, holding back the tide. They arrived too late to save Kadaku from falling to the Great Devourer but the Deathwatch warriors of Watcher Keep were able to recover their dead and wounded and fall back into the Ultramarines' gunships. All except the ravaged Black Shield Nullus, who Tigurius ordered returned to the Apothecarion bays aboard the recently arrived battle barge Resilient. 

Titus' wounds were grave, and it was decreed that he would undergo the "Calgarian Rites," the Ultramarines' name for the Rubicon Primaris, in order to have even a chance of surviving. Titus withstood the procedures necessary to become a Primaris Space Marine as stoically as he had endured any of Inquisitor Thrax's interrogations and was remade stronger than he had ever been. When he awoke, he was surprised to find himself honoured with the rank of lieutenant under the command of Captain Sevastus Acheran of the 2nd Company -- a warrior who had only recently left the Scout Marine reserve when Titus was taken from the Ultramarines almost a standard century before. Once more Titus resolved to do as was bid of him by his Chapter, and serve under a warrior who held the titles he had once held. 

There were those amongst the Ultramarines who were apprehensive about Titus' return to the Chapter. Chief Librarian Tigurius, however, had performed his own examination of the former captain and found him to harbour no taint, before psychically communing with Chapter Master Marneus Calgar who authorised Titus' immediate reinstatement. Their word was as adamantine, and Titus was officially welcomed back into the warrior brotherhood of his gene-sire. Lieutenant Demetrian Titus, once and once more of the Ultramarines 2nd Company, would bring the wrath of his Chapter to the foes of the Imperium anew.

Recidious Campaign

Yet Titus knew none of this as he lay unconscious in the Apothecarion aboard the Ultramarines battle barge Resilient. At his awakening, Titus was greeted by a Chaplain and was informed that the Ultramarines' Chapter Master Marneus Calgar had ordered that Titus undergo the Rubicon Primaris surgery while he was unconscious, a dangerous process that transformed Firstborn Space Marines like Titus into the larger, faster and stronger Primaris Marines. The Rubicon Primaris had to be crossed because the Apothecaries had determined that only the enhanced physiology provided by the additional Primaris gene-seed organs could save Titus' life. 

Titus' first impulse upon awakening was to return to his Long Vigil in the Deathwatch but the Chaplain reminded him that he had been declared free of any Chaos taint by the Inquisition and if he truly yearned for redemption, then he must return to his Chapter and earn back their favour. However, his service in the Deathwatch as a dishonorable Black Shield named "Nullus" who would not reveal his true Chapter or identity would be removed from the Ultramarines' records and much of his past would remain shrouded from his brothers.

After re-inducting him into the Ultramarines and having him outfitted in a new suit of Mark X Tacticus Power Armour, the Chaplain ordered Titus to keep his past a secret and to report to Sevastus Acheran, the new captain of the Ultramarines 2nd Company that Titus had once commanded. Acheran stated that he had reservations about Titus' reinstatement in the Ultramarines, particularly in his old company, but nonetheless provided him with his new rank of lieutenant in the company and command of a truncated squad. 

Joining Titus were Sergeant Valorem Gadriel, the former commander of the squad Titus had been assigned, and Brother Meduras Chairon, both Primaris Marines, who gradually became suspicious about Titus' past as he continually refused to answer their questions and concerns about his long absence from the Chapter. Neither Space Marine knew anything about Titus' past or that he had once been a captain and hero of the Chapter.

Captain Acheran informed Titus that the 2nd Company was currently in the process of a planet-wide evacuation of Kadaku's remaining Human population and ordered him and his squad to aid the Astra Militarum forces on the planet in retaking control of its orbital defence cannons.

Fighting through endless waves of Tyranid bioforms, Titus and his squad managed to retake the orbital cannons, allowing the Imperials to destroy one of the Tyranid Hive Ships and buying the Imperial forces time to continue with the evacuation. Titus was then ordered to escort Archmagos Nozick Beta-12 off-world, but the latter refused to leave until important data related to "Project Aurora" was retrieved from his overrun research facility. 

After reaching Nozick's facility and completing the data transfer, the Thunderhawk gunship transporting Nozick to the Resilient in orbit mysteriously crashed in territory overrun by the Tyranids. Titus and his squad raced to save Nozick but found the archmagos already dead. Surveying the wreckage, the Ultramarines discovered that Nozick had not been slain by Tyranids but by the forces of Chaos. Before they left the area, a squad of Ultramarines transported a mysterious object from the wreckage whose proximity caused Titus to lose consciousness.

After awaking on the battle barge Titus informed Acheran of Nozick's demise and of the presence of Chaos-worshippers on Kadaku despite the concerns of his squad about his health and fitness for service. Acheran ordered Titus to go to the nearby Hive World of Avarax in the Recidious System to retrieve Archmagos Nozick's apprentice, Morias Lueze, who was the only person qualified to continue his master's work on Project Aurora. But first the Ultramarines needed to know where in the vast conurbation of Avarax Lueze was located. 

Titus and his squad reached an Adeptus Mechanicus facility on Avarax and Titus accessed a cogitator within that provided him with Lueze's location. Suspicious of the mysterious object that had earlier been recovered by the Ultramarines from Nozick's crash site and caused him to lose consciousness, Titus also searched through the cogitator's files and discovered to his horror that Project Aurora was actually an attempt to construct a Warp-based weapon powered by the same Chaos artefact Titus had destroyed over a standard century before on Graia, its remains having been retrieved and reconstructed by the Mechanicus. 

Returning to the battle barge, Titus confronted Acheran about the artefact. Having experienced the terrible consequences its use on Graia, which had led to a Warp incursion on that Forge World, he demanded that the project be stopped and the artefact destroyed once more, but Acheran refused as the Mechanicus claimed they were working on the project by order of Roboute Guilliman himself.

Returning to Avarax to find Lueze, Titus and his squad were ambushed by the Heretic Astartes forces of the Thousand Sons Traitor Legion who were also seeking out Lueze. Fighting their way through the Traitor forces, the Ultramarines managed to reach Leuze's hiding place and escorted him to the battle barge. Along the way, Lueze unwittingly revealed to Gadriel and Chairon that Titus had once been the captain of the Ultramarines' 2nd Company over two standard centuries before and was thought dead after he held the Chaos artefact during Liberation of Graia campaign, further inciting Gadriel's suspicions about and resentment of Titus. 

After returning to Acheran, Titus urged the captain to contact Chapter Master Marneus Calgar and warn him about the true nature of Project Aurora. Acheran proved dismissive of the idea, especially since the astropathic relay which would allow them to send an astrotelepathic message to Calgar was currently overrun by the Tyranids and all messages in and out were blocked by the Chaos forces' sorcerous machinations. Titus proposed that he and his squad fight their way to the relay while other squads of the 2nd Company eliminated the Hive Tyrant synapse creature leading the Tyranid swarms and end the forces of Chaos' jamming operation. Acheran agreed to the plan.

As Titus and his squadmates fought their way to the relay, the Tyranids they faced suddenly died or become feral, unconnected to the Hive Mind, as a result of their fellow Ultramarines successfully eliminating the Hive Tyrant and the effort of the forces of Chaos to jam all Imperial astropathic communications. Once within the astropathic relay, Titus mets with Astropath Neoma who offered to send a message to Marneus Calgar. 

As she telepathically sent the message, Neoma suddenly accused Titus of being a Traitor and trying to lure Calgar to the planet to kill him. A suspicious Gadriel attacked Titus as a result but stopped when Chairon suddenly shot Neoma, recognising her as a liar who had been corrupted by Chaos. At her death, her body exploded in a blast of Warp energy that revealed the Thousand Sons Sorcerer Lord Imurah. After a brief duel, Imurah escaped and Titus returned to report to Captain Acheran. Acheran informed Titus that thrall-bands of the Thousand Sons had appeared across the Recidious System and were focusing their assault on Demerium, a local Cemetery World and the new home of Project Aurora, where Lueze was attempting to activate the Warp-based weapon. 

As the Ultramarines prepared to assault Demerium, Titus and Gadriel made amends, with Gadriel accepting that Titus was a loyal servant of the Emperor. After making planetfall and fighting through the Thousand Sons forces present on the Cemetery World, the Ultramarines arrived at the location of Project Aurora where they discovered that Demerium was actually a Necron Tomb World. 

The Astartes discovered Lueze, who revealed that by combining Necron and Adeptus Mechanicus technology with the Chaos artefact, the Project Aurora weapon will allow Imperial forces to negate and banish to the Warp all those touched by the energies of Chaos, first in the Recidious System but eventually the effect will spread across the entire galaxy, providing the Imperium with a final victory over Chaos. 

Sorcerer Lord Imurah attempted to stop the work on the weapon and was seemingly banished into the Immaterium by the weapon. However, this proved a ruse, and as Imurah returned he revealed that it was he who had covertly influenced the Tech-priests of the Adeptus Mechanicus into creating and continuing the project so that he might gain control of the reconstructed artefact. The sorcerer then slew Lueze and stole the artefact for himself.

Returning to the surface of Demerium, Titus' squad discovered a Necron obelisk had erupted that had formed a massive Warp rift on the planet's surface, allowing Imurah to bring more of his Thousand Sons forces and a Lord of Change to the Cemetery World. After the squad reunited with Captain Acheran, Gadriel suggested that they might be able to close the Warp rift by shutting down the lesser obelisks empowering the main one. 

Fighting their way to each of the Necron obelisks, Titus and his squad were nearly overwhelmed by the Thousand Sons before they were saved by the sudden arrival of Marneus Calgar and his Ultramarine reinforcements. Titus and his squad proceed to shut down the lesser obelisks while Calgar and the rest of the Ultramarines distracted Imurah's forces. After shutting down the Warp emanations of the final lesser obelisk, the combined strength of the Ultramarines begin pushing back the forces of Chaos.

During the battle, Imurah and the Lord of Change fled through a portal into the Immaterium, unleashing a spell that froze all of the Ultramarines engaged in the battle save Calgar, Titus, Gadriel, and Chairon. Followed by Calgar, Titus and Titus' squadmates, the Ultramarines managed to reunite with Calgar within the Warp and duel Imurah and the Lord of Change. They managed to drive the Lord of Change back as Imurah attempted to use the Project Aurora's power source to imbue himself with the divine knowledge of the Warp. 

However, midway through that process, Titus claimed the Project Aurora power source while Imurah was distracted battling the Chapter Master and reversed the polarity of the power source as Imurah imbued himself with it, causing Imurah to explode with a massive burst of psychic energy when he attempted to channel an amount of Warp energy that was too great for him to handle without the artefact's assistance. The explosion also drove back the Daemonic tide, granting victory to the Ultramarines and the other Imperial defenders.

Awakening back in realspace, Titus found himself surrounded by the victorious Ultramarines, now free of the sorcerer's spell. Returning to the battle barge Resilience, Marneus Calgar praised Titus for his deeds, presented him with the Laurels of Victory decoration and asked that he join him on a mission elsewhere. Titus said farewell to Gadriel and Chairon but was intercepted by the same Chaplain who had greeted him upon retunring to the Ultramarines, who was also accompanying Calgar on the new mission.

The Chaplain congratulated Titus for having proven himself worthy of rejoining the Ultramarines, but warned him that the risk of corruption by Chaos is ever-present and if Titus were ever to truly falter and betray the Emperor he would not hesitate to carry out his execution. The Chaplain then removed his Skull Helm and revealed himself to be Brother Leandros, Titus' young protege who had served with him over a standard century before during the Liberation of Graia and who had originally reported him to the Inquisition for his inability to be affected by the power of the Warp. Titus' redemption had, in truth, been a belated redemption for them both.

Mission to Zsah'Uj

Sometime later, Lieutenant Titus was reunited with his original recruiter into the Ultramarines, Bladeguard Veteran Sergeant Metaurus, on a mission to locate and destroy a Chaos relic on the planet Zsah'Uj, a former Imperial world that had fallen to a Chaos Cult dedicated to the Chaos God Tzeentch. The elite four-Astartes demi-squad, composed of Titus, Metaurus, and two other Bladeguard Veterans named Icaron and Levantus, were sent to that world with the expectation that their assignment was likely a suicide mission.

The squad's primary objective was to locate and destroy an apostate relic, an idol dedicated to Tzeentch. Their secondary objective was to provide ordinance strike coordinates for the site of the idol that would be transmitted to an orbiting Ultramarines warship. The squad was launched from their strike cruiser via a Drop Pod, which made planetfall near the idol's approximate location. The strong influence of the Warp in that area interfered with the strike cruiser's ordinance targeting arrays and vox communications systems, which had necessitated the deployment of an infantry strike team to identify the exact location of the idol and the Chaos Cult that served it.

Upon landing, Titus was placed in charge of hauling an ornate stasis sarcophagus through hostile terrain, briefly leaving it behind to bulldoze and destroy a group of Chaos Cultists and their vehicles. At the bottom of a dark chasm the team was beset by the Beastmen servants of Tzeentch known as Tzaangors, who were easily dispatched like the cultists.

Upon reaching their destination, a statue of an avian Tzeentchian Greater Daemon known as a Lord of Change located at the end of an enormous hall, the Primaris Marines were ordered to deploy a portable device that functioned similar to a voidship's Gellar Field, in this case the stasis casket that contained an astropath and the portable Gellar Field device, which would dampen the Warp interference and allow the orbiting strike cruiser to get a lock on the idol and destroy it with an orbital strike. 

Titus deployed the astropath who had been contained within the stasis sarcophagus he had been hauling and assaulted the statue with his teammates, unleashing a hailstorm of bolter fire. However, a mutant Chaos Sorcerer, who had hidden herself in the orb of the idol's staff prior to their arrival, emerged from the statue and used her psychic powers to freeze time and paralyse the Ultramarines, after disabling their defences by telepathically casting a knife through the awakened astropath's heart, disrupting the mobile Gellar Field effect and rendering the Space Marines vulnerable to her abilities. The sorcerer proceeded to mentally assault the members of the Ultramarines squad one by one, using their innate, hidden fears against them, which led to the deaths of Icaron and Levantus. 

When the sorcerer entered the mind of Metaurus, the vision she evoked was of Metaurus observing Titus, first as a child, then as a Space Marine, then as what he had most feared might happen -- Titus as a Chaos Space Marine. In this vision, Chaos-corrupted Titus gutted Metarus with his Combat Knife, leaving him badly wounded and on the cusp of slipping into a Sus-an Membrane-mediated coma. 

When the sorcerer next entered the mind of Titus, however, she discovered to her surprise that Metarus had actually been correct, Titus possessed no fear the sorcerer could exploit. He was able to break free of her control, shattering the sorcerer's Force Staff during her failed attack on his mind. The sorcerer attempted to take flight, but the lieutenant turned the tables on the witch and used his Combat Knife to slice the Heretic in two. 

With the sorcerer's death, her control of time was broken and the squad's originally unleashed bolter rounds resumed their intended trajectory and destroyed the statue of the Greater Daemon, which resulted in a beam of light shooting up from the Chaos ruins. This signal was seen from the Ultramarines' vessel in orbit, and was used by its crew to deploy ordnance to the location intended to ensure that nothing of the site remained to spread its corruption. 

With their mission objectives accomplished, the Ultramarines warship voxed a single message to Metaurus and Titus, imploring them to die well. Titus instead dragged the wounded Metaurus from the ruins to safety where the orbital strike's impact would not kill him. Metaurus urged Titus to leave him behind as they had completed their duty to the Emperor, but the lieutenant was not in the mood to die just yet. Titus secured his helmet and prepared to eagerly battle a horde of Chaos Cultists who were rapidly bearing down on their position.

Warden of Ultramar

Sometime after he and Metaurus' unexpected survival on the mission to Zsah'Uj, and the death of Sevastus Acheran, who was heroically slain alongside his entire Ultramarines task force defending the Hive World of Trygg from a Genestealer Cult uprising during the Fourth Tyrannic War, Titus was promoted by Chapter Master Marneus Calgar to the rank of captain once more and offered command of the Ultramarines' 2nd Company and the position of Master of the Watch, the defender of the Ultramarines' fortress-monastery on Macragge. 

He was subsequently tasked with overseeing the defence of Macragge and the entirety of the Realm of Ultramar itself, as well as expanding Ultramar back to its former size of five hundred worlds at the time of the Horus Heresy as Primarch Roboute Guilliman had ordered. As such, Titus was also named the "Warden of Ultramar" and allowed to choose a special command team of both mortals and Space Marines known as the "Wardens of Ultramar" to assist him in his duties.

Personality

Demetrian Titus was an unusual Ultramarine who believed that the Codex Astartes offered an excellent series of guidelines for what it meant to be an Astartes, but that it was not sacred writ to be followed soulessly and automatically. 

He believed that the spirit of the Codex was more important than its letter and that how one chose to live out the directives contained within it truly determined if one was an Astartes or not.

Psychic Resistance

Titus has displayed a potent resistance to the psychic powers unleashed by the servants of Chaos on multiple occasions, the origin of which is unknown but which has laid suspicion upon him of somehow having been corrupted by the Ruinous Powers. 

Despite torturous investigation by Inquisitor Thrax, there was never any sign of such a Chaos taint, nor has Titus been deemed to be a psyker, so the nature and scope of this resistance remains unknown at the present time, though this ability led to Titus' arrest after the campaign on Graia by the Inquisition.

Wargear

At one point or another, Captain Titus wielded almost every piece of wargear in the Space Marine arsenal, including the standard Bolt Pistol and chainsword favoured by many Astartes officers. 

Perhaps Titus' most distinctive wargear when he was still a Firstborn Astartes was a weapon that fired explosive mines that generated a gravitic field and would stick to foes before exploding, which was called the "Vengeance Launcher."

As a Primaris Marine lieutenant and captain, Titus wielded the new, upgraded wargear made available to the Adeptus Astartes during the Era Indomitus by the technological genius of Archmagos Dominus Belisarius Cawl.

Canon Conflict

At the time of its release, the developer of the Space Marine video game claimed on its forums that it took place in a parallel Warhammer 40,000 universe where the Ultramarines 2nd Company is commanded by Demetrian Titus rather than Cato Sicarius.

To deal with this problem to Warhammer 40,000 continuity, in Space Marine II Titus was not only transformed into a Primaris Marine but was also reduced in rank from captain to lieutenant so that he no longer was equal in rank or authority to the current commander of the Ultramarines 2nd Company, Captain Sevastus Acheran. White Dwarf 498 established that the events of the Liberation of Graia in the first game happened many solar decades before Cato Sicarius took command of the 2nd Company.
