The Yamnin Volunteers are regiments of the Astra Militarum. They are known to have collectively saved 103 Imperial planets from the enemies of the Emperor and purified 69,203,669 impenitents.

The Yamnin Volunteers took part in the Haephos Containment during the Era Indomitus and contributed 4 artillery musters to that campaign to aid in the cleansing of the Eletyrio Spaceport.
