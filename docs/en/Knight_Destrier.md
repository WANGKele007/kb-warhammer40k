A Knight Destrier is a Bellatus Pattern Knight prized for its incredible speed and grace, achieved by slimming down the chassis and mounting two large rocket boosters to the back of its carapace. So great is the added thrust of these boosters that large counterweights must be added to the Imperial Knight's legs to help it maintain its balance.

The Knight Destrier's Bellatis Pattern chassis is slightly shorter than the Questoris Pattern, but still significantly larger than the Armigers assigned to lesser bondsmen. Knights Destrier are favoured by aggressive Nobles who long for the cut-and-thrust brutality of melee combat, where they can rapidly seek out enemy behemoths and earn glory with a single rocket-boosted thrust of what is essentially a colossal power sword. 

Knights Destrier make use of their exceptional agility to close with the enemy and engage face-to-face with devastating Thundershock Spears and Bellatus-sized Reaper Chainswords. The former are enormous rocket-assisted power lances capable of punching through the heaviest armour, while the latter are versatile blades that can sweep entire infantry squads aside or grind through single, tougher targets.

The few surviving stragglers can hardly be considered lucky either, as they have only lasted long enough to get locked in the sights of the Knight Destrier's Chastiser Gatling Cannon or Frag Bombard.
