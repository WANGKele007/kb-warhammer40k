The Punisher-class Titan was a rare pattern of Imperial Battle Titan that possessed three-legs deployed in a tripod fashion rather than the standard bipedal, humanoid shape. 

History

A Punisher-class Titan called the Modus Destructor was deployed to the Ork homeworld of Ullanor Prime during the War of the Beast in the mid-32nd Millennium to defend the Adeptus Mechanicus command centre that had been established on that world to support the Imperial invasion in an attempt to kill the Ork warlord known as The Beast.

Armament

The Modus Destructor was armed with twin Tezlan Accelerators that possessed under-slung Plasma Annihilators.

Adeptus Mechanicum Departmento Analyticus Technical Specifications

The Adeptus Mechancus has not released the technical specifications of this Titan.
