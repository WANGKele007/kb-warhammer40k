The Reaper Chain-cleaver is a large Chain Weapon carried by the combat walkers of the Imperial Knights, particularly the smallest class of Knights known as Knights Armiger. The Reaper Chain-cleaver is a saw-toothed weapon similar to but smaller than the similar Reaper Chainsword that mangles metal and gnaws flesh to ruin with each shuddering impact.
