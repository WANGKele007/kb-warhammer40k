A Dominator was a member of a Dominator Cohort Squad, also called the Tyranthikos, that were an elite formation of Terminators exclusively utilised by the Iron Warriors Space Marine Legion during the Great Crusade and Horus Heresy. Once they had been the honour guard of Primarch Perturabo himself, and high in the esteem of their primarch and Legion, but their failure at the Battle of Phall in 007.M31 would see them reduced to the miserable state in which they existed during the Horus Heresy. 

The Dominator cohorts existed in purgatory, stripped of the honours once bestowed upon them by their primarch and forced to serve in the forefront of the most gruelling assaults. The Iron Circle Battle-Automata that had replaced them as Perturabo's honour guard were an ever-present reminder of their failures during the opening salvos of the Horus Heresy and became the focus of a malignant disdain.

History

There existed in the ranks of the Iron Warriors Legion a series of long-standing warrior societies that existed outside of the Legion's standard military order and command structure, and which seldom became known to outsiders. One such warrior society of the IVth Legion was the "Tyranthikos," also known as the "Dominators." It was said the title of Tyranthikos had its origins on the Iron Warriors' Legion homeworld. Here, the twelve lords of Olympia were themselves referred to as the Tyranthikos. The Dominators were a Veteran core of line breakers and assaulters who had each survived a score of desperate and bloody sieges to receive the name and were the foremost proponents of the use of Terminator Armour within the Legion. They were seeded throughout the ranks of the Legion's grand battalions. 

Once proudly honoured as peerless among their brethren and granted the role of retinue and honour guard for their gene-sire, those of the Dominator cohorts were relieved of their most prized duty following the events of the Battle of Phall. 

Perturabo's unveiling of his "Iron Circle" automata displaced the former bodyguard of the primarch and sent the veteran warriors back into the front lines of Warmaster Horus' campaign towards Terra. With their battle-honed experience replaced by the calculating logic engines of the Iron Circle and their honour besmirched, the Dominators became increasingly resentful of their Legion command and within them grew an unrestrained hatred towards the automata that had replaced them.

Unit Composition

5-10 Dominators

Wargear

Cataphractii Pattern Terminator Armour - The Cataphractii Terminator Armour utilised by the Dominator cohorts contained additional, non-standard ablative plating.

Combi-bolter

Thunder Hammer

Land Raider Proteus - Dedicated transport for up to 5 Dominators. 

Optional Wargear

For every five Space Marines in the squad, one Dominator may exchange their Combi-bolter for one of the following: 

Heavy Flamer

Reaper Autocannon

Multi-Melta

Chainfist - Any Dominator in the unit may exchange their Thunder Hammer for a Chainfist.

Minor Combi-Weapon - Any Dominator in the unit may exchange their Combi-bolter for a Minor Combi-Weapon.

Magna Combi-Weapon - Any Dominator in the unit may exchange their Combi-bolter for a Magna Combi-Weapon.

Notable Members

Galan Dion - Galan Dion was a veteran member of the Iron Warriors' Tyranthikos who fought during the Great Crusade and Horus Heresy. He took part in the Battle of Phall, which saw his warrior society lose its role as his primarch's chosen honour guard. During this conflict, the Iron Warriors' hated rivals from the Imperial Fists Legion managed to board Perturabo's flagship, the Iron Blood, during the intense void battle and managed to reach the primarch's inner sanctum. Though the warriors of the VIIth Legion were all killed before they could inflict harm upon their lord, the failure of the Tyranthikos to halt the boarders' advance caused the Iron Lord to replace his organic honour guard with the Iron Circle Battle-Automata. Dion and his fellow outcast Dominator brethren constantly sought a way to regain their status and favour in their primarch's eyes. In the aftermath of the Battle of Phall, Perturabo led the Iron Warriors in a crusade of vengeance against the Imperial Fists. They targeted the world of Hydra Cordatus, which was guarded by the fortress known as the Cadmean Citadel that was manned by a force of Imperial Fists. Temporarily assigned to the 33rd Grand Battalion under the command of Siege Captain Ascari Valkar, the Iron Warriors launched a direct assault against the citadel. Despite his protestations against the siege captain's straightforward tactics, Dion had no choice but to obey his primarch's command and follow the upstart Valkar into battle. Despite their fierce resistance the Iron Warriors managed to breach the citadel's outer gates, however, they were set upon by an Imperial Fists Forge Lord who commanded five maniples of Castellax Battle-Automata, whose brutal firepower took a toll on the encroaching Iron Warriors. Were it not for the intervention of the Dominator cohort, the Iron Warriors would not have been able to regain their footing. However, after sustaining serious losses on both sides, the battle entered a bloody stalemate. With the loss of two-thirds of the 33rd Grand Battalion's Astartes, Dion and the other Tyranthikos could see that the siege captain's assault had failed. To save the remainder of their forces, Dion and his fellow Dominators forcibly removed Valkar from command and brought him before their waiting primarch. Without provocation, upon the primarch's order, his Iron Circle automata were ordered to execute the failed commander. The Iron Circle proceeded to render Valkar into bloody pieces of ravaged meat. Dion hoped that his actions would earn his primarch's approval, but Perturabo had none to give. He immediately ordered the Iron Circle to break the stalemate for the Cadmean Citadel's outer gates. Meanwhile, the Tyranthikos would be sent out once again to the front lines to serve and bleed like every other Legionary of the Iron Warriors. 

Legionary Khorius Rex - Khorius Rex was assigned to the 3rd Grand Company, 7th Grand Battalion during the Great Crusade and Horus Heresy. He was a veteran of the Tyranthikos, having trod a hundred worlds and more in the service of the Great Crusade. Once an ideologue, for Rex the Imperial Truth was long rendered down to but a kernel of cold, hard fact; the galaxy is no place for the weak and only those prepared to endure any hardship have any right to prevail. This moment of utmost clarity came when the 7th Grand Battalion was recalled to the Iron Warriors' homeworld of Olympia and bore witness to the rebellion that had gripped their primarch's world. In the bloodshed that followed, the man Khorius Rex had once been died forever, to be reborn and recast into a warrior whose heart was as cold and as hard as the ceramite skin of his Tactical Dreadnought Armour. Following the events of the Drop Site Massacre on Isstvan V, Khorius Rex took part in the IVth Legion's extermination operations in the aftermath of this campaign. Like many his master considered lacking or unstable, he was abandoned on Isstvan V when the bulk of the Legion was pulled out to pursue Warmaster Horus' war against the Imperium.
