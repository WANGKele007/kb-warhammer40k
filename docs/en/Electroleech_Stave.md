An Electroleech Stave is the primary weapon of a Fulgurite Electro-priest of the Adeptus Mechanicus. The powerful capacitors built into the length of each Electroleech Stave allow them to drink every iota of electric force from those they strike -- be they living foeman or blasphemous machine.

Sources

Codex: Adeptus Mechanicus - Cult Mechanicus (7th Edition) (Digital Edition), "Weapons of Enlightenment"
