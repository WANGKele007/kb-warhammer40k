The Selenarchs is a Loyalist Space Marine Chapter of unknown Founding and origin.

The Selenarchs were reinforced with Primaris Space Marines during the Indomitus Crusade after meeting up with the crusade's Fleet Secundus.

This influx of new blood and technology provided the Selenarchs with the needed strength to retake the Garredes Cluster.
