The Death Knights is a Codex Astartes-compliant Space Marine Chapter of unknown origin and Founding. Nothing else is recorded about this Chapter in Imperial records.

Chapter Appearance

Chapter Colours

The Death Knights primarily wear emerald green Power Armour. The Aquila or Imperialis on the chest plate is silver. The white squad specialty symbol -- Tactical, Assault, Devastator or Veteran -- is indicated on the right shoulder plate. A black Gothic numeral centred on the squad specialty symbol indicates squad number. The shoulder plate trim colour indicates company number in accordance with the Codex -- i.e. White (1st Company), Yellow (2nd Company), Red (3rd Company), Green (4th Company), etc.

Chapter Badge

The Death Knights' Chapter badge is a large, white skull with a black Maltese Cross centred on its forehead, on a field of emerald green.
