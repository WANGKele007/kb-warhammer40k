A Melta Blast-gun is a large and powerful thermal ray Melta Weapon that was developed for use as an alternate main turret armament for the ancient Kratos Heavy Assault Tank of the Space Marine Legions. It was employed primarily as an anti-armour weapon, intended to destroy enemy tanks and armoured transports.
