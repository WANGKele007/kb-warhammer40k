Valador was an Asuryani Wraithlord of Craftworld Biel-Tan in the service of that craftworld's Autarch Kyre who was slain by the Ork Warlord Gorgutz on the world of Acheron that had long been lost in the Warp. Valador's Spirit Stone was destroyed, and his death had been secretly orchestrated by Kyre's spymaster, the Ranger Ronahn, who sought to stop Kyre from claiming the long-lost artefact known as the Spear of Khaine that could serve as a catastrophic weapon.
