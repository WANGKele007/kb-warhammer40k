Sedition's Gate is the second limited release volume in The Horus Heresy series of novels, released as a Horus Heresy Weekender Exclusive in hardback format. Sedition's Gate has yet to be re-released in non-limited format, although the short stories contained within are slowly being released as stand alone e-books, starting with Nick Kyme's Artefacts.

Synopsis

Across the galaxy, the war for the future of humanity rests on a knife-edge. While the greatest heroes of the Imperium battle the Traitor forces, in the shadows, the power of Chaos is in the ascendance.

Contents

Sedition's Gate contains the following:

Artefacts (Short Story) by Nick Kyme
The Harrowing (Short Story) by Rob Sanders
Allegiance (Short Story) by Chris Wraight
The Laurel of Defiance (Short Story) by Guy Haley
Sermon of Exodus (Short Story) by David Annandale
