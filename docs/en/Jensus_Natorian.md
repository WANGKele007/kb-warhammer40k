Jensus Natorian is a Codicier of the Blood Ravens Chapter who has been seconded to the Deathwatch Chapter. He serves as the Deathwatch Librarian in Kill-team Cassius out of watch fortress Talasa Prime.

Codicier Natorian is a potent combat psyker. He first manifested his powers as a youth, slaughtering scores of Orks in a psychic rampage with his bare hands to avenge the death of his parents during a Greenskin invasion of his homeworld. Natorian's abilities enhance his innate prowess, and he destroys xenos with empyric blasts and hate-fuelled sweeps of his Force Sword, Duty's Revelation.
