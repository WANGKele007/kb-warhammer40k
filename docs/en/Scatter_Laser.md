The Scatter Laser, or Sierbahn in the Aeldari Lexicon, is a Laser&nbsp;Weapon that can be mounted on Aeldari Wraithlords, War Walkers, Falcons, Wave Serpents, Aeldari Jetbikes  and Vypers. The Scatter Laser can also be used as a support weapon for Guardian Squads whilst mounted to a Grav Platform.

The weapon itself has six separate laser chambers which allow it to fire in bursts or all six chambers at once, at either the same target or a multitude of different ones. It stores its energy in crystalline power cells.

The Scatter Laser, like most Aeldari weapons, has an Imperial counterpart, the Multi-Laser, though the Aeldari version is far more power efficient and advanced in design.
