An Infiltration Cadre is a type of T'au Empire Fire Caste cadre utilised for reconnaissance in force. It is comprised of Pathfinders, Sniper Drone Teams and Piranhas engaging the foe with guerrilla tactics while calling down strikes by gunships and aircraft on key strategic targets.
