A Magna-Coil Grav Bike, also called a Magna-Coil Bike, is an anti-gravitic skimmer bike used as the primary vehicle of the Hernkyn Pioneers of the Leagues of Votann. The Magna-Coil Bike is fast, maneuverable and capable of navigating the most difficult terrain or bodies of water with ease.
