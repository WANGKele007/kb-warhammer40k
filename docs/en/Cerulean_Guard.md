The Cerulean Guard is a Loyalist Chapter of Space Marines of unknown Founding and origin who fought to defend the Fortress World of Cadia during the climax of the 13th Black Crusade in 999.M41. 

Chapter History

Notable Campaigns

13th Black Crusade (999.M41) - The Cerulean Guard deployed a full three companies of the Chapter to aid the high-orbit offensive led by many Chapters of the Adeptus Astartes against the forces of Chaos during the final battle of Abaddon the Despoiler's 13th Black Crusade just before the fall of Cadia.
