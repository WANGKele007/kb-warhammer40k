The Knights of the Crimson Order is a Loyalist Space Marine Chapter and an Unforgiven genetic successor of the Dark Angels of an unknown Founding. 

Chapter History

Notable Campaigns

Battle of the Caliban System (998.M41) - This was a confrontation between the Dark Angels and several of their allied Unforgiven Chapters, including the Consecrators, the Knights of the Crimson Order, the Angels of Absolution, the Repentant Brotherhood, the Angels of Redemption and the Angels of Vengeance, and a unified Traitor fleet combining a Death Guard Plague Fleet and a warfleet led by the Fallen in the Caliban System. The stage for this battle was set when the Fallen Cypher turned himself in to the Inner Circle of the Dark Angels. He revealed the intent of the Fallen Angel Merir Astelan and Typhus of the Death Guard to use the ancient Tuchulcha Engine and its brother engines to create a time rift in the Caliban System and transport the Fallen from ten thousand standard years previous to the 41st Millennium. The Fallen would then combine with Typhus's own forces in combination with recently captured Loyalist gene-seed to forge a new Space Marine Legion known as the "Death Angels". Horrified by this notion and after being convinced by the Watchers in the Dark, the Dark Angels' Supreme Grand Master Azrael agreed to work with Cypher to foil the Traitors' plans. The entire Dark Angels Chapter aboard The Rock eventually arrived at the Caliban System just as the Plague Fleet of Typhus supported by other Traitor ships broke from the Warp. A massive void battle broke out between the two fleets, and the Traitor forces initiated a boarding operation against The Rock itself. Led by the Terminus Est, the Chaos fleet was large but unfocused, as even Typhus was more concerned about overseeing the ritual to bring the Fallen forward in time than he was on directing the battle. The Traitors were able to open the Warp breach, and it seemed that their plan to rewrite history and doom the future would succeed. However Azrael was eventually able to order the self-aware Tuchulcha Engine to scatter all those near the time portal across the galaxy through the Warp, inadvertently including the Fallen on the other side of the portal ten thousand standard years earlier during the Fall of Caliban. Meanwhile, a second fleet of warships drawn from the fleets of the Unforgiven arrived in the Caliban System to the rear of the remnants of the Chaos armada, forcing its vessels to retreat. The Warp breach created by the Chaos forces was finally closed by the Ravenwing Black Knight Annael who took control of a Ravenwing Dark Talon to fly into the Warp rift and used his Rift Cannon to close the breach at the cost of his own life. While the Dark Angels were victorious and prevented a major victory by the Fallen, a third of the Chapter was wounded or slain in the battle.

Notable Knights of the Crimson Order

Grand Master Dane - Dane is an accomplished naval commander and expert in void warfare who is utterly loyal to the Dark Angels' Supreme Grand Master Azrael. This was mentioned to Azrael by Chief Librarian Ezekiel, when Azrael was surprised to see the Knights of the Crimson Order amongst the Unforgiven successors who came to aid the Dark Angels during the Battle of the Caliban System because the Chapter had suffered high casualties in their most recent campaigns. Dane replied that he had come at the Dark Angels' request because he would willingly lead even the last of the Crimson Order against the Dark Angels' enemies to maintain the honour of the Crimson Order in Azrael's eyes. As a result of Dane's expressed loyalty, Ezekiel told Azrael that there was no need to worry if Dane was told the truth of what had caused the Battle of the Caliban System to occur, namely that the Chaos forces sought to bring the Fallen forward in time to the 41st Millennium from the Fall of Caliban. Ezekiel knew that Dane would follow Azrael's lead and not pry too deeply into the matter. Azrael agreed and later gave Dane command of the entire Unforgiven fleet due to his noted achievements as a naval commander. Dane commanded the fleet from aboard his flagship, the battle barge Flame of Galandros.

Chapter Appearance

Chapter Colours

The Knights of the Crimson Order wear slate grey power armour with crimson robes and red trim on their shoulder plates.

Chapter Badge

This Knights of the Crimson Order's Chapter badge is not listed in current Imperial records.

Chapter Fleet

Flame of Galandros (Battle Barge) - The Flame of Galandros served as the flagship of Grand Master Dane when the Knights of the Crimson Order aided the Dark Angels in the Battle of the Caliban System in 998.M41. The Flame of Galandros then became the flagship of the entire assembled Unforgiven fleet, when Supreme Grand Master Azrael gave command of the fleet to Dane due to his great skill at void warfare.
