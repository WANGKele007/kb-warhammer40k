The Disciples of Destruction are a warband of Chaos Space Marines of unknown origin and allegiance. They were first sighted by Imperial forces in 988.M41.

Warband Appearance

Warband Colours

The Disciples of Destruction wear purple and a dark orange camouflage patterned Power Armour with gold trim on the shoulder guards and greaves. Tattered devotional scrolls dedicated to the Chaos Gods often hang from their armour.

Warband Badge

The Disciples of Destruction have no known badge. Instead they opt to adorn their armour with the usual heretical Chaos symbols and fetishes.
