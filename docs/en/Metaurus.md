Metaurus is a Bladeguard Veteran sergeant of the Ultramarines Chapter who was once a Firstborn Space Marine before crossing the Rubicon Primaris in the Era Indomitus and becoming a Primaris Marine. It was Metaurus who first encountered Demetrian Titus when the future Ultramarines captain was only a boy and recommended that he become an Ultramarines aspirant. 

Metaurus served as a mentor and advisor throughout Titus's subsequent stellar career with the Chapter. Metaurus was recently chosen by Titus to serve on his Command Squad, the "Wardens of Ultramar", after he was promoted to a second tenure as the commander of the Ultramarines 2nd Company. Now, Metaurus is a voice of measured experience among the Wardens of Ultramar, always keeping an eye on his protégé -- lest he get the urge to charge headfirst into the enemy once again.

History

Discovery of Demetrian Titus

Sergeant Metaurus was the Firstborn Ultramarine who discovered and chose a young Demetrian Titus as an aspirant for the Chapter sometime in the seventh century of the 41st Millennium according to the old Imperial Calandar. Metaurus first encountered the young Titus on a battlefield carrying an Astartes Combat Knife as if it were a sword. Metaurus described Titus as having only known rage and pain as a child, but never fear, and worried what a soul like that might be capable of becoming. 

As such, Metaurus recommended the boy become an Astartes so that his talents could be harnessed for the Emperor. Throughout Titus' subsequent brilliant career as a Space Marine, Metaurus remained a mentor and close confidante.

Mission to Zsah'Uj

More than two standard centuries later in the Era Indomitus of the 41st Millennium, Metaurus was assigned to serve alongside Primaris Lieutenant Demetrian Titus after he had returned to the Ultramarines from the Deathwatch during the Recidious Campaign of the Fourth Tyrannic War.

Metaurus was assigned as the commander of a small demi-squad of Bladeguard Veterans, assisted by Titus, on a mission to locate and destroy a Chaos relic on the planet Zsah'Uj, a former Imperial world that had fallen to a Chaos Cult dedicated to the Chaos God Tzeentch. The elite four-Astartes demi-squad, composed of Titus, Metaurus, and two other Bladeguard Veterans named Icaron and Levantus, were sent to that world with the expectation that their assignment was likely a suicide mission.

The squad's primary objective was to locate and destroy an apostate relic, an idol dedicated to Tzeentch. Their secondary objective was to provide ordinance strike coordinates for the site of the idol that would be transmitted to an orbiting Ultramarines warship. The squad was launched from their strike cruiser via a Drop Pod, which made planetfall near the idol's approximate location. The strong influence of the Warp in that area interfered with the strike cruiser's ordinance targeting arrays and vox communications systems, which had necessitated the deployment of an infantry strike team to identify the exact location of the idol and the Chaos Cult that served it.

Upon landing, Titus was placed in charge of hauling an ornate stasis casket through hostile terrain, briefly leaving it behind to bulldoze and destroy a group of Chaos Cultists and their vehicles. At the bottom of a dark chasm the team was beset by the Beastmen servants of Tzeentch known as Tzaangors, who were easily dispatched like the cultists.

After cutting a bloody swath through the cult's forces, the Primaris Marines reached their target, a large statue of a Tzeentchian Greater Daemon known as a Lord of Change. They had been ordered to deploy a portable device that functioned similar to a voidship's Gellar Field, in this case the stasis casket that contained an astropath and the portable Gellar Field device, which would dampen the Warp interference and allow the orbiting strike cruiser to get a lock on the idol and destroy it with an orbital strike.

When the squad finally reached the idol and unleashed a volley of bolter fire at it, they were attacked by the high priestess of the Tzeentchian Chaos Cult, who had hidden herself in the orb of the idol's staff prior to their arrival. The high priestess of the cult was a powerful Chaos Sorcerer who used her psychic powers to freeze time and paralyse the Ultramarines, after disabling their defences by telepathically casting a knife through the awakened astropath's heart, disabling the mobile Gellar Field effect and rendering the Space Marines vulnerable to her abilities. The sorcerer proceeded to mentally assault the members of the Ultramarines squad one by one, using their innate, hidden fears against them, which led to the deaths of Icaron and Levantus. 

When the sorcerer entered the mind of Metaurus, the vision she evoked was that of Titus as a child, the child who had never known fear. The vision was of Metaurus observing Demetrian Titus, first as a child, then as a Space Marine, then as what he had most feared might happen -- Titus as a Chaos Space Marine. In this vision, Chaos-corrupted Titus gutted Metarus with his Combat Knife. Metaurus was left severely wounded by the psychic assault and sustained a series of serious internal injuries, including critical failure of his Larraman's Organ, an unresponsive Belisarian Furnace, an inactive Oolitic Kidney and a Sus-an Membrane primed to place him into a coma.

When the sorcerer next entered the mind of Titus, however, she discovered to her surprise that Metarus had actually been correct, Titus possessed no fear the sorcerer could exploit, and he was able to break free of her control, shattering the sorcerer's Force Staff during her failed attack on his mind. The sorcerer attempted to take flight, but the lieutenant turned the tables on the witch and used his Combat Knife to slice the Heretic in two. 

With the sorcerer's death, her control of time was broken and the squad's originally unleashed bolter rounds resumed their intended trajectory and destroyed the statue of the Lord of Change, which resulted in a beam of light shooting up from the Chaos ruins. This signal was seen from the Ultramarines' vessel in orbit, and was used by its crew to deploy ordnance to the location intended to ensure that nothing of the site remained to spread its corruption.

With their mission objectives accomplished, the Ultramarines warship voxed a single message to Metaurus and Titus, imploring them to die well. Titus instead dragged the wounded Metaurus from the ruins to safety where the orbital strike's impact would not kill him. Metaurus urged Titus to leave him behind as they had completed their duty to the Emperor, but the lieutenant was not in the mood to die just yet. Titus secured his helmet and prepared to eagerly battle a horde of Chaos Cultists who were rapidly bearing down on their position.

Wardens of Ultramar

Metaurus survived the mission to Zsah'Uj alongside Demetrian Titus, though he was badly wounded and took some time to recover. After the death of Sevastus Acheran during his missioin to Trygg, Marneus Calgar restored Titus to command of the Ultramarines 2nd Company. 

Titus immediately selected Metaurus to serve on his unique Command Squad, the "Wardens of Ultramar", which contained both mortal and Astartes officers. The 2nd Company had been tasked with the expansion of the borders of the Realm of Ultramar to their pre-Horus Heresy extent of five hundred worlds. As he always had, Titus values the advice and counsel of his old mentor Metaurus above all others.
