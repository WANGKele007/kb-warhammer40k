Warpflame Gargoyles are an add-on for Chaos Space Marine vehicles. It takes the form of of a gargoyle-shaped muzzle mounted on all of a vehicle's weapons that allows them to attack the foe with unnatural blasts of fire powered by the energy of the Immaterium.

Sources

Codex: Chaos Space Marines (6th Edition), pg. 68
