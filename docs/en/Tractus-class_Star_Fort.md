A Tractus-class Star Fort is a type of star fort employed for planetary and system defence by the Navis Imperialis. It is a large enough void station to allow for the docking of the largest class of void ships, including Imperial battleships.

Notable Tractus-class Star Forts

Faith's Bulwark — The Tractus-class Star Fort Faith's Bulwark was positioned in one of the most secure regions of the galaxy. However, it suffered a terrible combustion accident when one of its sub-plasma drives exploded.
