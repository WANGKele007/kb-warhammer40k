The Emperor's Hands is a Loyalist, Codex Astartes-compliant Space Marine Chapter of unknown Founding and origin. The Emperor's Hands' homeworld is the planet Paragon. Almost nothing else is known about them in current Imperial records.

Chapter Appearance

Chapter Colours

The Emperor's Hands primarily wear dark blue Power Armour. The Aquila or Imperialis on the chest plate is silver. Both gauntlets are white. The right shoulder plate bears a white squad specialty symbol: Tactical, Assault, Devastator or Veteran. A Gothic numeral centred upon the squad specialty symbol, indicates squad number. The colour of the Gothic numeral on the squad specialty symbol indicates company number in accordance with the Codex Astartes -- i.e. White (1st Company), Yellow (2nd Company), Red (3rd Company), Green (4th Company), etc.

Chapter Badge

The Emperor's Hands' Chapter badge is a stylised, armoured, white hand that is held up in a "halt" gesture, on a field of dark blue.
