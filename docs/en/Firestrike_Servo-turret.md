The Firestrike Servo-turret is a Space Marine mobile weapon turret utilised by the forces of the Primaris Space Marines. Primarily utilised as a defensive weapon, the Firestrike Servo-turret lays down withering volleys of ballistic or directed energy fire to secure flanks or the Space Marines' base of operations. 

Mounted on anti-gravitic ventral plates based on the same gravitic technology developed for the Primaris Marines by Archmagos Dominus Belisarius Cawl, they can hover across the battlefield to find the ideal firing positions from which to slaughter attacking enemies.

Firestrike Servo-turrets can be outfitted with two variant weapon load-outs, either twin-linked Las-talons as standard or twin-linked Accelerator Autocannons as an optional choice.

Unit Composition

1-3 Firestrike Servo-turrets

Wargear

Twin-linked Las-talon
Twin-linked Accelerator Autocannons (As optional replacement for twin-linked Las-talons)
