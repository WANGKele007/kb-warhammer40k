A War Dog Karnivore is a variant of the Armiger Pattern Knight known as a War Dog that serves the Chaos Knights as a close combat specialist. 

Aggressive and bloodthirsty, the Fallen Nobles who pilot Karnivores eschew the more potent ranged weapons available to the War Dog chassis. They revel in causing bloody confusion and despair among their foes, seeking the visceral thrill that only close-quarters slaughter provides.

As a result, many of their pilots fall to the open worship of one of the Chaos Gods.

Unit Composition

1-3 War Dog Karnivores

Wargear

Havoc Multi-launcher
Reaper Chaintalon
Slaughterclaw
