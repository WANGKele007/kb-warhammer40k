The EtaCarn Plasma Gun is a man-portable Plasma Weapon used by the military forces of the Leagues of Votann that possesses a long range and potent stopping power. It has the longest range of any of the EtaCarn Plasma Weapons used by the Kin. It is often used as the standard ranged weapon of the Einhyr Hearthguard.

However, Kin weapons like the EtaCarn Plasma Gun are superior in almost every respect to those patterns of Plasma Weapons wielded by the agents and military of the Imperium, since the Kin employ superlative materials and methods in their construction and have no fear of further innovating on an ancient STC design. As a result, the EtaCarn Plasma Gun and its variants often have longer ranges and more stopping power than any of their Imperial equivalents, and are far less prone to the explosive instability that particularly marks Imperial Plasma Weapons.
