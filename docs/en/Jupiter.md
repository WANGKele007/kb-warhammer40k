The great gas giant of Jupiter is a major Forge World of the Adeptus Mechanicus and the fifth world of the Sol System in the Segmentum Solar.

Jupiter in the Era Indomitus of the 41st Millennium is the site of the massive manufactoria and voidship construction facilities of the Adeptus Mechanicus' ancient orbital Jovian Shipyards, which date back at least to the time of Old Night.

These Jovian colonies were powered by the radiation that surged from the massive planet and much of their industry fed on the mineral riches that had been yielded from long standard centuries of exploitation of its system of moons, with its full potential having not been fully exhausted even after millennia. As a result of these resources, Jupiter served as Terra's shipyards and the skies of its settlements and moons were always filled with voidships, spacedocks and manufactoria that worked tirelessly to construct anything from single manned Raven Interceptors to the gargantuan hulls of Emperor-class Battleships.

History

Origins

Jupiter is a gas giant orbited by a complex web of dozens of smaller satellite moons. It is almost a solar system in miniature, with the gas giant at its core rather than the blazing orb of a sun.

The cloud of satellites and Trojan asteroids surrounding it are full of Human colonies, manufactoria and Adeptus Mechanicus forge temples, powered by drinking in the radiation surging from the mammoth planet and feeding on the mineral riches that in centuries of exploitation had yet to be fully exhausted. Jupiter is Terra's shipyard, and its sky was forever filled with vessels.

Centred on Ganymede and a dozen other smaller moons, spacedocks and fabricators worked ceaselessly to construct everything from single-crew Raven interceptors up to the gargantuan hulls of mighty Emperor-class Battleships.

The early history of Jupiter remains largely lost to Imperial historitors. During the Age of Strife, Jupiter's moons were occupied by cruel xenos overlords of an unknown race. Following the Treaty of Mars, when the Red Planet was first incorporated into the burgeoning Imperium, the Great Crusade finally began in earnest.

The rest of the Sol System was the first region of space to be conquered by the Emperor and His newly rearmed and re-equipped Legiones Astartes. Alien invaders were flushed from the moons of Saturn and Jupiter and their wretched Human slaves repatriated to Terra.

The Jovian Shipyards and the Jovian Void Clans were utilised throughout the Great Crusade to build a fleet of vessels for the Armada Imperialis, the precursor of the Navis Imperialis.

Horus Heresy

During the Horus Heresy Jupiter was the centre of Primarch Rogal Dorn's third sphere of defence under the command of the Imperial Fists Legion's Captain Efried. In the later stages of the Solar War that preceded the Siege of Terra in 014.M31, the massive array of moons and orbital habitations of the Jovian system were captured by Iron Warriors forces commanded by Perturabo. 

Instead of reducing every pocket of Loyalist resistance or wasting his time during his drive to Terra to occupy the many habitation zones around Jupiter, Perturabo instead unleashed fleets of pirates, Traitor Imperial Army, Heretic Astartes warbands of the Night Lords and Emperor's Children Legions to terrorise and subdue the region's inhabitants. Jupiter was subsequently reclaimed by the Imperium during the Great Scouring after the Traitors' defeat at the Siege of Terra.

Jovian Purge

In the Era Indomitus of the 41st Millennium, Jupiter's orbital shipyards were subjected to the Jovian Purge after Heretek elements dedicated to the service of an independent Chaos power were discovered there. The Purge began after Inquisitor Xie of the Ordo Malleus warned the Custodians of the Solar Watch of a terrible threat that to the Imperium's defence that existed on the Jovian Platform XI. 

In response to this report, the Solar Watch dispatched one of its Shield Companies to the orbital platform, along with several Sisters of Silence from the Outer Solar Vigil and a Callidus Assassin. This combined force discovered that Platform XI was seething with Hereteks of a Chaos Cult that worshiped the independent Greater Daemon Vashtorr the Arkifane. Their efforts to corrupt the construction of Imperial warships with hidden biomechanoid parasites were brought to an end, and the Heretek cultists were purged by the Shield Company and its allies.

Manufactoria Capabilities

In the 41st Millennium, the Jovian Shipyards still play a vital role in the construction of the fleets of the Imperial Navy in combination with the Ring of Iron of Mars itself. The space docks and orbital workshops that circle Jupiter, like an artificial ring of more moons, are home to the millions of technomat and drone servitors that work under the supervision of the Adeptus Mechanicus Tech-priest specialists like Artisans and Rune Priests to build the Navis Imperialis' warships.

Each vessel is a vast undertaking, often taking solar decades or even centuries to complete for the largest and most powerful warships. Many of those who work on the construction of an Imperial Navy warship will live and die during the process, never seeing the magnificent starship they struggled to bring to life as a mobile cathedral dedicated to the greater glory of the God-Emperor.

Each of the Imperium's warship designs is constructed by only a single Jovian orbital shipyard; for instance, the Emperor-class Battleship, as well as Tyrant and Dominator-class Cruisers, are typical of the Imperial warships produced at the Jovian Shipyards and are each crafted by a completely different orbital manufactoria.

The Jovian Shipyards are also responsible for the manufacture and production of the Plasma Drives that provide power to all of a starship's systems and the construction of a vessel's vital launch bay components and landing bays, which are equipped with dedicated ports for launching attack craft and magnetic guidance coils for steering them safely back aboard after a mission.

To further enhance their vessels, the Jovian Shipyards also possess the capabilities to manufacture their own pattern of Nova Cannon, which replaces the standard explosive shells with Vortex Warheads. The resultant rifts into the Warp have been known to rend target vessels cleanly in half.

Notable Locations

Moons

Ananke - Ananke is a moon of Jupiter known to have had Mechanicum habitats on its surface during the Horus Heresy era.

Callisto - One of the greater Galilean moons of Jupiter that was also known to have had Mechanicum residential habitats in orbit above it by the time the Horus Heresy began.

Europa - One of the greater Galilean moons of Jupiter, this geo-engineered Ocean Moon was known to have had Mechanicum habitats on its surface during the Horus Heresy era.

Ganymede (Aquila Adamant) - One of the greater Galilean moons of Jupiter that was known to have had ancient Mechanicum habitats on its surface during the Great Crusade and Horus Heresy eras and served as the central hub of the massive complex that made up the Jovian Shipyards. Later, it was transformed into a Warp-tainted wasteland created by the malfunction of experiments designed to test the limits of Squat Warp-coil technology. In the Era Indomitus, Ganymede was transformed on the orders of the primarch Roboute Guilliman into a massive hub-fortress of the Officio Logisticarum called Aquila Adamant. Its purpose is to supply, support, and archive the actions of the Indomitus Crusade's fleets and battle groups as they advanced across the galaxy.

Io - One of the greater Galilean moons of Jupiter that was known to have had Mechanicum habitats on its surface during the Horus Heresy era.

Iocaste - One of Jupiter's minor moons known to have had Mechanicum habitats on its surface during the Horus Heresy era.

Lysithea - Lysithea was a moon of Jupiter that during the Age of Strife was occupied by an unknown species of xenos. The moon was liberated by the forces of the early XIX Legion, the later Raven Guard, during the opening years of the Great Crusade.

Thule - At the time of the opening years of the Horus Heresy, the asteroid moon of Thule had already orbited the shipyards of Jupiter for six standard millennia. Suspended high above the gaseous surface of its patron planet, it dwelled innocuously beyond the greater Galilean moons: Callisto, Ganymede, Europa and Io. It was an ugly chunk of rock, its gravity so weak that its form was misshapen and mutated. The Mechanicum had hollowed it out using massive boring machines and filled its interior with energy and life-support-carrying conduits, as well as vast tunnels and chambers. The dead core of Thule had become a giant manufactorum of forge temples and compressors, a massive gravity engine its beating heart. This construction extended from the surface via metal tendrils that supported blister domes, clinging like limpets to the rock, and pneumatic lifter arrays. But Thule was no mere misshapen asteroid. It was an orbital shipyard of Jupiter and the place where the Word Bearers' Apocalypse-class Battleship, the Furious Abyss, was clandestinely built by hidden Dark Mechanicum elements. Following its completion and subsequent launch, these same elements destroyed Thule so that there were no witnesses that could verify the vessel's existence. The ragged clump of its remains still orbited far out at the edges of the planetary system for millennia to come.

Orbitals (Void Stations)

Kadal - Kadal was a satellite munitions fortress and one of the primary reserves of fleet ordnance in the Sol System. A lump of ultra-hard nickel-iron ore, it had taken the ancient Mechanicum two solar decades to excavate, and another decade to graft the fortress structures within. Nova Cannon shells, torpedo warheads, macrocannon shells, propellant and explosive precursors, all lay in vibration and temperature-controlled dark at the core of the rock. When an Imperial warship needed rearming it would approach and hold anchor away from Kadal. Orbital barges would then shuttle the munitions load out to the waiting vessel and return. Nothing and no one approached closer than several thousand kilometres; anything that tried was greeted by Kadal's formidable defences. When dealing with enough destructive material to crack open a moon, there were no acceptable risks. During the later years of the Horus Heresy at the opening phase of the Solar War, the Alpha Legion instigated an anti-Imperial revolt on the moon and Kadal was subsequently destroyed.

Platform XI - In the Era Indomitus, Platform XI was an orbital shipyard of Jupiter that had become infiltrated by Heretek cultists of the Greater Daemon Vashtorr the Arkifane. They sought to use their biomechanoid parasite creations to corrupt newly-constructed Imperial warships to the service of the Master of the Soul Forge. Platform XI was the site of the Jovian Purge carried out by the Adeptus Custodes of the Solar Watch and their allies among the Sisters of Silence, the Ordo Malleus and the Culexus Temple.

Polar Shoal City-Stations - These were vast, void-borne space colony conglomerations that spread across an orbit circling Jupiter's north pole. The Polar Shoal city-stations were home to the ruling class of Jupiter and contained the most advanced amenities in the Jovian system.

Saros Station - Located in a geosychronous orbit above Jupiter's Great Red Spot was a vast void station in the shape of a spindle, which appeared like a massive crystal chandelier bathed in the constant crimson glow of the raging storm far below. Saros was a resort platform where the Jovian elite could find respite and diversion from the work of the shipyards and manufactoria. It was said that only the Venus orbitals could surpass Saros Station for its luxury. It was outfitted with avenues of gold and silver, acres of null-gee gardens and auditoriums; and the finest opera house outside the Imperial Palace complex itself.

The Caul - The Caul is a sphere of debris created by many standard millennia of manufacturing activity that surrounds Jupiter's orbital shipyards and extends deep into space in every direction from the gas giant. This debris field is home to mazes of equatorial breaker yards that gather the scrap and dissemble or retrofit voidships.
