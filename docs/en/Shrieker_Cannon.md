A Shrieker Cannon, known as a Buanna in the Aeldari Lexicon, is an upgraded version of the standard Aeldari Shuriken Cannon with a longer barrel and thus greater range. Utilised exclusively by the macabre Harlequin Death Jesters, these weapons are infinitely more cruel than a typical Shuriken Cannon, as Shrieker Cannons fire projectiles impregnated with virulent genetic toxins known as Margrech, which turn the luckless victim into a walking bomb.

The special Shrieker ammunition is hollow and contains a genetically-engineered enzyme-based serum. As the missile hits its target, the centripetal forces created by the spinning shuriken disc force the toxin through microscopic holes in the shuriken's spines and into the target's flesh. The serum works in a very complex and unpleasant manner, combining with the victim's own genetic material and cellular machinery to twist and distort tissues, causing the victim's organs to malfunction in a spectacular fashion.

Eventually the victim is driven into a violent delirium as the serum reaches their brain and they lose control of their body. Afterwards the pace of the constantly replicating genetic serum reaches a fever pitch as the rapid, unnatural acceleration of the victim's metabolism causes the blood to boil, organs to rupture and flesh to sear from within, before the victim explodes in a shower of gore with sickening violence. 

The characteristic shrieking noise of these shurikens is caused by the rush of air into the hollow missile as the serum is forced into the victim. This noise has prompted Imperial troops who have faced the Harlequins in battle to give the weapon its name "Shrieker".
