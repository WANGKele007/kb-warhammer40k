The Moripatris, known in Low Gothic as the "Mass of Doom," is the name of a ritual prayer uttered by the Chaplains of the Blood Angels and some of their Successor Chapters before a battle. 

When they discover that one of their Astartes has begun to succumb to the genetic curse of the Black Rage, the Chaplain will utter the Moripatris, which marks that Space Marine's formal induction into the Chapter's Death Company.
