The Horus Heresy Chapbook is an anthology book in the Horus Heresy Series. The Horus Heresy Chapbook was first released exclusively at Games Day UK in 2007. The book contains only two short stories, The Dark King by Graham McNeill and The Lightning Tower by Dan Abnett. The stories were later republished separately.

Synopsis

No official synopsis has been released.

Contents

The Horus Heresy Chapbook contains the following short stories:

The Dark King by Graham McNeill
The Lightning Tower by Dan Abnett
