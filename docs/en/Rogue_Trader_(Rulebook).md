Warhammer 40,000: Rogue Trader is the 1st Edition of the Warhammer 40,000 core rulebook of the tabletop miniatures war game published in 1987. It is a highly sought-after collectible among enthusiasts of the hobby. It is also the only edition of Warhammer 40,000 not to have a starter set of miniatures for the game (such as Battle for Macragge or Assault on Black Reach).

The cover features Space Marines of the Crimson Fists Space Marine Chapter combating an horde of Orks.
