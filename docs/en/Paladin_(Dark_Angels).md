A Paladin, also called a "pure-blade," was an intermediate officer rank of the Dark Angels Legion during the Great Crusade and Horus Heresy eras. The Ist Legion followed the general principles of the Principia Belicosa, dividing their Legion's strength into chapters, led by senior officers called Masters, and orders, which in turn were led by Paladins. These formations were further broken down into companies, commanded by captains.

Each Paladin was an elite swordsman and master of close combat. They often served as a Company Champion for the Legion. For instance, the 9th Order of the Ist Legion was known to have at least fifty Paladins during the Great Crusade who also served as the honour guard of Primarch Lion El'Jonson.

The rank of Paladin became defunct after the Second Founding and is no longer in use among the Dark Angels Chapter or its Unforgiven successors.

Notable Paladins

Alajos - Master of the chapter containing the 9th Order of the Ist Legion and a former Paladin.

Inardin - Paladin Commander of the 9th Order of the Ist Legion.

Corswain - Paladin-Captain of the 9th Order and Champion of the Ist Legion.

Xavis - Paladin of the 20th Order of the Ist Legion.

Barzareon - Paladin of the 31st Order of the Ist Legion.

Constantine - A Paladin and former voted-lieutenant of the Dark Angels' Dreadwing formation of the Hexagrammaton.

Ennedreth - Ennedreth took part in the Battle of Perditus during the Horus Heresy under the command of Knight-Preceptor Vendrahel to secure archeotech weapons from the Mechanicum vaults of Umbral-51. He died while facing a force of Death Guard Traitor Legionaries.

Notable Variants

Paladin of the Hekatonystika - Veiled in allegory and obscure symbolism, the orders of the Hekatonystika bore the heavy burden of keeping the most secret and dangerous knowledge acquired by the Dark Angels on the battlefield. Many of the of the Ist Legion's most veteran warriors were initiates of these orders and wore their colours with pride on the field of battle.
