The Murabla Kindred is a Kroot Kindred that serves as mercenaries dedicated to vengeance against the Tyranids for the assault of Hive Fleet Kraken upon their homeworld of Pech. They are currently in the employ of Inquisitor Angmar.

During the Second Tyrannic War, the Murabla Kindred aided their fellow Kroot in repelling the Tyranid invasion of Pech. Once the battle was over, they declared their intent to claim vengeance for the deaths of their fellow Murabla during the invasion and pursued Hive Fleet Kraken wherever it could be found across the galaxy. This ultimately led them to Ichar IV, where the Kroot were encountered by Inquisitor Angmar, who granted the Murabla Imperial sanction and inducted them into his retinue.

Notable Murabla Kindred

Khibala Yusra - Khibala Yusra, called the "First Born Twin of the Spear," was a Kroot mercenary and member of the Murabla Kindred who served Inquisitor Angmar in his battle against the Tyranids of Hive Fleet Kraken on Ichar IV.
