A Pulsar Lance is a starship-grade, directed-energy Lance weapon used by the voidships of the Craftworld Aeldari.

Pulsar Lances have proved to be some of the most deadly weapons employed by Asuryani voidships for ship-to-ship combat. They fire pulsed bursts of multiple high-energy laser beams, hence the name, rather than the single beam produced by Imperial versions of the technology. However, the Aeldari version is far more accurate.
