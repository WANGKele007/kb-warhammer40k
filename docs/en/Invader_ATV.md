The Invader ATV, usually part of an Invader ATV Squad, is an all-terrain vehicle used by the Primaris Space Marines of the Adeptus Astartes in a close support role as a highly-mobile reconnaissance platform.

Armament

The Invader is a highly flexible all-terrain vehicle perfectly adapted to an aggressive reconnaissance-in-force role. Outfitted with either a Multi-Melta or Onslaught Gatling Cannon, it can rapidly deliver punishing fire against vulnerable parts of the enemy line, or swiftly engage and destroy the scouting elements of the opposing forces.

Unit Composition

1-3 Invader ATVs

Wargear

Mark X Tacticus Power Armour
Bolt Pistol (Sidearm for each Primaris Marine)
Onslaught Gatling Cannon (Turret-mount)
Multi-Melta (Replacement for Onslaught Gatling Cannon)
Twin-linked Auto Bolt Rifle
