The Bellicatus Missile Array is a potent Imperial Missile Launcher system deployed on the Primaris Space Marine Impulsor assault grav transport.

It is used on the Impulsor when its squad of accompanying Primaris Marines is in need of greater fire support.

The Bellicatus Missile Array can fire a variety of different missile types as the tactical situation requires. These include Krak Missiles, Frag Missiles and anti-aircraft Icarus Missiles.
