The Penitent Blades are a Loyalist Space Marine Chapter and a successor of the Dark Angels, created during an unknown Founding.

Chapter History

The Penitent Blades hail from a world with a surface that is made up of thousands of small landmasses suspended kilometres in the air above acidic oceans. How this phenomenon occurs is a mystery, but it has ensured the Penitent Blades are aerial combat experts.

The Penitent Blades' 2nd Company has four oversized squads providing gunship pilots, rather than the two in most other Unforgiven Chapters.

Notable Campaigns

Arks of Omen Campaign (Unknown Date.M42) 
Battle of Idolatros - The Penitent Blades participated in the Unforgiven's fight against Abaddon the Despoiler's Arks of Omen during the Battle of Idolatros.

Notable Pentinent Blades

Captain Lytheus - As a genetic son of Lion El'Jonson, Lytheus joined his primarch following his resurrection in the Arks of Omen Campaign. Lytheus was later assigned by the Lion to remain on Baal to oversee the Unforgiven forces detached detachment to Commander Dante's Blood Angels fleet within the Imperium Nihilus.

Codicier Iaoel - Iaoel is a Penitent Blade who has joined the Deathwatch and serves his Vigil at the Eye of Octos watch fortress. Iaoel is currently among the Deathwatch forces deployed from that watch fortress that are fighting a splinter of Hive Fleet Leviathan on the oddly-named world Death of Bianzeer.

Chapter Appearance

Chapter Colours

The Penitent Blades primarily wear orange and blue power armour in a quartered livery scheme. The colour of the Aquila or Imperialis worn on the chestplate indicates company assignment in accordance to the dictates of the Codex Astartes.

The black squad tactical specialty symbol -- battleline, fire support, close support, Veteran or command -- is indicated on the right shoulder pauldron. A white High Gothic numeral prominently displayed upon it indicates a battle-brother's assigned squad.

The left shoulder pauldron displays the Chapter's iconography.

It is unknown how the Chapter indicates a battle-brother's company assignment.

Chapter Badge

The Penitent Blades' Chapter badge consists of three downwards pointing swords centred upon a field of orange.
