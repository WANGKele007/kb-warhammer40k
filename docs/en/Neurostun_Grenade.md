A Neurostun Grenade is an Imperial grenade often used by the Space Marines of the Deathwatch Chapter that detonates in a vapour of metallic dust through which runs a painful electrostatic charge able to disrupt the senses of their targets.

Sources

Codex Supplement: Deathwatch (9th Edition), pg. 48
