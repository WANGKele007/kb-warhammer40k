The Asuryata is an Aeldari text that tells an epic legend about the Phoenix Lords. It is only recited in full once in each generation by the mystical order of Aeldari poets known as the Bards of Twilight.

The tale is composed of many well-known parables intended to pass on important teachings about life and Aeldari culture. The Asuryani of the craftworlds use the text to derive guiding principles for both their day-to-day lives, the war councils and their military campaigns.
