The Order of the Blue Robe is a minor Order Militant of the Adepta Sororitas that serves under the auspices of the Convent Sanctorum of Terra. 

Their most notable recent actions were when the order recently assisted Inquisitor Halen Jentz during the Second Albonian Genocide and helped to defend the Shrine World of Saint's Halt from the forces of the T'au Empire's Fourth Sphere Expansion and Fifth Sphere Expansion in the Era Indomitus.

Order History

Notable Campaigns

Second Abonian Genocide (688-698.M40) - Inquisitor Halan Jentz of the Ordo Hereticus declares the entire population of the Abonian Sub-sector diabolos inculcatus and gathers together an Imperial Crusade army that includes elements of the Order of the Blue Robe and the Order of the Black Sepulchre of the Adepta Sororitas as well as the Red Hunters, War Bearers and Subjugators Chapters of the Adeptus Astartes. The crusade rages for a solar decade before the Inquisitor declares the sub-sector's crimes accounted for.

Defence of Saint's Halt (Unknown Date.M42) - The tides of the Great Rift send the fleets of the T'au Empire's Fourth Sphere Expansion into the Chalnath Expanse, and the xenos attempt to establish a foothold on the Shrine World of Saint's Halt as part of their larger invasion of the region. The Order of the Blue Robe manages to hold the T'au assault long enough to receive Deathwatch Space Marine reinforcements from Watch Fortress Mortguard.

Order Appearance

Order Colours

The Order of the Blue Robe makes use of silver power armour and light blue vestments. Their weaponry is also painted light blue.

Order Badge

The iconography of the Order of the Blue Robe is that of a black-coloured ankh, with an image of a blue-coloured patron saint placed in the centre of its upper portion.
