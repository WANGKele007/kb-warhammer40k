Valesnus was a watch master of the Deathwatch Chapter who served as the watch commander of Fort Starshield in the Kadus Sector until it was destroyed by the arrival of the Great WAAAGH! of the Ork Warlord Ghazghkull Mag Uruk Thraka in the sector in the Era Indomitus. In the battle that ensued, the watch fortress and its entire complement of Astartes was destroyed after they put up a heroic defence.

History

The Watch Companies of Fort Starshield completed several textbook extermination missions against xenos warships that surged into the Kadus Sector from the galactic west. The Deathwatch noted that each battle was made easier by the condition of their foes, whose voidcraft evidenced severe battle damage and whose crews seemed panicked, even frantic. Realising that these invaders must, themselves, be fleeing some other, greater peril, Watch Master Valesnus requested urgent reinforcement. 

Fort Starshield's astropathic relay was soon after annihilated by a titanic eruption of lurid green ectoplasm. Deep-void auspex buoys went dark one by one; and their picters sent back last, blurry images of Ork warships beyond number filling the starfield. Recognising that his forces were impossibly outmatched, Watch Master Valesnus girded his warriors for a selfless strike against whatever mighty Greenskin warlord ruled this space-borne WAAAGH!. He hoped that, by striking off the beast's head, he might make the sacrifice of his and his battle-brothers' lives a worthy one. 

Solar months later, the requested Imperial reinforcements finally reached the location of Fort Starshield. They found nothing but drifting wreckage, that of dozens of Ork warships and of Fort Starshield itself. They located the mangled corpses of Watch Master Valesnus and his closest brothers pinned to the starfort's wreckage with huge scrap-metal spikes. Each warrior's chestplate had been torn away, and on their flesh was branded the personal glyph of Ghazghkull Mag Uruk Thraka...
