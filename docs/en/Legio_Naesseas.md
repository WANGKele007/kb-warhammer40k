The Legio Naesseas, also known as the Legio Naessias (or "Deep Dwellers" in Low Gothic) is a Loyalist Titan Legion of the Collegia Titanica, one of the military arms of the Adeptus Mechanicus. 

Hailing from the Ocean World of Daxxos, they are charged with the defence of their Mechanicus masters' stackforges from deep-sea megafauna such as vast predatory whales and fanged megadectapods.

Indeed it is from one of these beasts, the dread Krakynekin, that the Titan Legion derives its heraldic icon, the hooked tentacle of Daxxos. 

Though relatively small, the Deep Dwellers are a proud Titan Legion, equally capable of waging war in the briny depths of their homeworld as on the battle-ravaged city streets of a hundred worlds.

During the years of the Horus Heresy they remained fiercely loyal to Terra, taking part in the battles of Drussen, Tallarn, and the Solar War, though it would cost the Legio Naesseas dearly. 

This Deep Dwellers are known to be extremely loyal to the Emperor and have close ties to the Imperial Fists Chapter.

Legion History

There is little official information on the exact founding of this particular Titan Legion, however, official Imperial records indicate that the Legio Naesseas, known commonly as the "Deep Dwellers," hail from the Ocean World of Daxxos, whose oceans are home to some of the deadliest creatures in the galaxy. 

As wardens of this world, these god-engines are known to stride the oceans' floor, constantly battling sea leviathans, gnarlwhales and other such terrible creatures of the briny depths that would threaten the monolithic Roam-Stackforges of the Daxxosian Mechanicus that follow in the Deep Dwellers' wake, gathering their raw materials for production from the oceanic crust. 

Due to their constant deployment in the depths of the deep oceans, the outer hulls of the Legio's Titans become tarnished over time, however, their bioluminescent white patterns still stand out in the murky deep. The Deep Dwellers use these markings to identify themselves while fighting in the abyssal darkness of Daxxos' ocean bed.

When called upon to battle off-world to take part in a campaign or Imperial Crusade, the Titans are hoisted to the surface of Daxxos by vast cranes. 

Notable Campaigns

Drussen Atrocity (009-010.M31) 
Battle of Tallarn (010-012.M31) - During the Horus Heresy, Perturabo, primarch of the Iron Warriors, launched an all-out planetary invasion of the verdant Imperial staging world of Tallarn, his IVth Legion bolstered by dozens of allied Traitor Imperial Army regiments, the Titans of the Legio Krytos and the Knights of House Caesarean. Both the Loyalist and Traitor high commands were taken by surprise by Perturabo's actions and the invasion quickly escalated after he ordered an Exterminatus upon the world, scouring its surface of living matter by way of a voracious Life-eater virus bomb barrage. While the population of the once-verdant world was all but wiped out, many defenders survived thanks to extensive subterranean shelters. The ensuing campaign was fought between vast formations of Imperial Army and Solar Auxilia tanks, the Titans of the Legio Naesseas, Legio Gryphonicus, the Knights of House Megron, the indentured Battle-Automata of the household army of the Rogue Trader Sangrea as well as the armoured forces of the Iron Hands, Imperial Fists, White Scars and Ultramarines Legions. No infantry could survive unprotected in the poisoned wastes, and the war quickly drew in other forces from across the Segmentum Tempestus. The Battle of Tallarn is considered the largest armoured engagement in the known history of Humanity, and while ultimately counted as a victory for the Loyalists, millions of warriors and war machines on both sides were left scattered across the lifeless, deadly surface of the planet.
Solar War (010-014.M31) - The Solar War refers to the final stage of the Horus Heresy, where the traitorous Warmaster Horus made one last push on the Segmentum Solar to wage war upon the Imperial Throneworld itself. A series of battles were fought throughout the Sol System from 010-014.M31. With the Emperor preoccupied with the War Within the Webway and Malcador the Sigillite waging the Shadow Wars, it was left to the Imperial Fists Primarch Rogal Dorn, the Praetorian of Terra, to oversee the defences of both Terra and the greater Sol System. The Legio Naesseas fought alongside the Imperial Fists Legion in the defence of Terra, providing their god-engines in defence of the various defensive Spheres organized by Dorn to help stave off the inevitable invasion of the Imperial Throneworld by the Traitor forces.

Legion Traits

Elite Magos - This Titan Legion is well known for the skill of its tech-priests and magi. Even in the heat of battle these individuals can call upon the favour of the Omnissiah to keep a Titan in the fight, though its Machine Spirit might be ready to rebel.
Motive Mastery - The princeps of the Legio have learnt to wear their Titans like a second skin and can coax bursts of speed and devastating charges out of their god-engines via the constant connection of their MIU.

Legion Combat Doctrine

Pragmatic and relentless hunters of the deep-ocean floor, the Legio Naesseas has adapted to war under circumstances alien to many other Titan Legions. 

Training and operating under almost perpetual Zone Abyssia conditions, the Deep Dwellers earned their cognomen in a lightless world defined by its crushing pressure and unseen, constant threat. To survive in such an environment requires a specific regimen of training, strategy, wargear, and culture amongst the scions of this Titan Legion.

Reputed to strike with the remorselessness of the sea in storm, the Legio Naesseas favours the use of energy weaponry, particularly lasers, as the short, sharp release of power boils the ocean with each strike and is not diminished by pressure and water resistance in the same manner as more conventional projectile-based weaponry. For this reason, the Legio ensures that each of its Titans is equipped with Plasma Rifling.

Advancing relentlessly whilst unleashing eye-searing bursts of energy, the Deep Dwellers are also notorious for abandoning ranks when the enemy closes with them to instead stride into the jaws of death with their magna-harpoons raised. 

They score their kills in the fashion of their homeworld, spearing enemies in place while pumping fire into them to ensure they cannot break free to scatter and harm the stackforges. When within range, they will rush the enemy in the hope of winning renown, honour, and additional kill-stripes.

Legion Specific Wargear

Ranging Auspexes - Focusing lens arrays stud the Titan's command canopy, allowing its princeps an unequalled view of the battlefield. Through these telescopic eyes the distance to enemies can be swiftly determined, allowing for the most effective weapon volleys.
Plasma Rifling - Gas channels allow for excess plasma from the Titan's reactor to be funnelled into the firing chambers of its energy weapons, extending their range and increasing their lethality. These mechanisms are not without their dangers, however, and can put extra strain on the Titan's reactor.

Legion Appearance

Legion Colours

The colours of the Legio Naesseas is aquamarine, white with dark gold trim. The Titans of this Legio often decorate their hulls with depictions of the horrific sea creatures that live in the deep fathomless depths of their homeworld's oceans that the Legio Naesseas must fight on a daily basis.

When fighting in the deep oceans of Daxxos, each god-engine is all but isolated in the darkness from its brethren. A damaged Titan has little hope of survival if ranging far along the seafloor, for even a small hull breach can result in a torturous, drowning death. 

In aid of these ranging missions, the Legio Naesseas also pays great heed to the risk of creating firing solutions against friendly god-engines when they are occluded behind swirling eddies of silt. 

To ensure this does not happen, each god-engine is coated in a unique pattern of white bioluminescent paint and equipped with the Ranging Auspexes.

Legion Badge

The badge of the Legio Naesseas takes the form of a large black tentacle, representing their homeworld's deadly apex-predator, the Krakynekin.
