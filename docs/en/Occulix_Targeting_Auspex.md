An Occulix Targeting Auspex is a highly-advanced Imperial sensor and targetting technology dating back to the Great Crusade and Horus Heresy eras originally deployed on suits of Saturnine Terminator Armour.

Incorporating a potent Machine Spirit (artificial intelligence), this advanced auspex sensor system allowed the wearer of Saturnine Terminator Armour to track targets for its multiple heavy weapon systems at once, though this increased firepower was bought at the cost of mobility for the armour.
