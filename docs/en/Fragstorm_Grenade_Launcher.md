A Fragstorm Grenade Launcher is a type of Imperial Grenade Launcher wielded by the Primaris Space Marines. 

It is deployed for use as an anti-personnel fragmentation weapon by squads of Aggressors, and two of them can also be placed within the chest of a Redemptor Dreadnought.
