The Knights of Dorn is a Codex Astartes-compliant Loyalist Space Marine Chapter. 

Though their origin and Founding is unknown, their Chapter name suggests they are successors of the Imperial Fists. 

Chapter Appearance

Chapter Colours

The Knights of Dorn wear a heraldry of dark brown and bone white on their power armour, each colour painted on one half of the battle-plate. 

Individual honour markings are painted on the right knee plate. 

The bone white squad specialty symbol -- battleline, close support, fire support, Veteran or command -- is located on the right shoulder plate. 

A black High Gothic numeral is stenciled in the centre of the squad specialty symbol, indicating squad number. 

The colour of the Aquila or Imperialis on the chest guard indicates company number in accordance with the Codex Astartes -- i.e. white (1st Company), yellow (2nd Company), red (3rd Company), green (4th Company), etc.

Chapter Badge

The Knights of Dorn's Chapter badge is an ebon skull within a white roundel outlined in black. This roundel is centred on a field of bone white.
