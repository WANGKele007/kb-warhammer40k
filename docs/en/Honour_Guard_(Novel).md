Honour Guard is the fourth volume in the Gaunt's Ghosts series of novels by Dan Abnett.

Synopsis

In disgrace after a catastrophic war, Colonel-Commissar Ibram Gaunt and the Tanith First-and-Only are sent to retrieve precious artefacts from a world lost to Chaos. With enemies all around and time against them, Gaunt's Ghosts and their allies must prevent the holy relics of Saint Sabbat from falling into their foes' hands, and as mysterious events begin to occur, Gaunt begins to suspect that the Saint herself might be aiding them from beyond the grave.
