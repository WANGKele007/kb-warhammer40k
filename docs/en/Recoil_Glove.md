A Recoil Glove is a heavy gauntlet used to absorb the recoil of certain Imperial ranged weapons. 

It utilises interlocking plates entwined with memory wire to stiffen the gauntlet into a protective grip around the particular shape and size of a user's hand when a weapon fires, absorbing the force of the recoil. 

This makes it easier for the wearer to fire one-handed ranged weapons.
