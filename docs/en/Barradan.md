Barradan is a watch captain of the Deathwatch Chapter who is currently serving in the Fort Excalibris watch fortress in the Imperium Nihilus region of the Segmentum Obscurus.

Barradan is one of the few Primaris Space Marines to have risen to that rank so far in the Era Indomitus. He has already distinguished himself in many battles with xenos forces. Such promotions
have proved strategically beneficial, for with their intrinsic understanding of Primaris infantry forces and Repulsor tanks, these watch captains have helped to fully integrate these weapons into the Deathwatch's arsenal.
