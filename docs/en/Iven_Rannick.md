Iven Rannick is an interrogator of the Ordo Hereticus of the Inquisition in service to Inquisitor Grendyl. He is the highest-ranking member of Grendyl's retinue and warband of Acolytes and one of the Inquisitor's most trusted servants. 

He has been provided a personal office aboard the Rogue Trader frigate Mourningstar that Grendyl requisitioned to serve as the Inquisition warband's mobile base during its mission to cleanse Atoma Prime's capital hive city of Tertium from the Chaos corruption of the Cult of Admonition and the Traitoris Militarum Moebian 6th Regiment. 

Rannick serves as Grendyl's right hand and the embodiment of the Inquisitor's command authority during the Hive Tertium operation.

History

Rannick is an interrogator of the Ordo Hereticus and a highly trusted member of Inquisitor Grendyl's retinue; while interrogation is certainly one of his most important duties, the title is also indicative of a senior Inquisition Acolyte who is close to being raised to the status of a full Inquisitor. As such, during the events on Atoma Prime, Rannick is subordinate only to his master Grendyl.

As Grendyl's highest ranking Acolyte, Interrogator Rannick has a wide range of responsibilities, which include the identification, interrogation and purging of any Traitors discovered within the warband on the Mourningstar or any prisoners secured by the warband's Acolytes during their missions within Hive Tertium.

Rannick works closely with Explicator Orgustine Zola, a relatively new Acolyte raised up by Grendyl from the population of Hive Tertium itself, who helps to train the new recruits known colloquially as "Rejects," chosen to join the warband as fresh Acolytes. Rannick supervises the overall strategy for retaking Tertium from the forces of Chaos, while Zola serves essentially as his executive officer, a vital source of intelligence on the geography, people and culture of Tertium and sometimes leads particularly important missions into the hive city herself. 

Rannick depends on Sergeant Major Vincent Morrow, a veteran member of the Armageddon Steel Legion who fought in the Second War for Armageddon against the Orks, to serve as the commander of the Astra Militarum units that have been requisitioned into the warband and as a drill sergeant who trains up and integrates new Acolytes into the Inquisition's service in Hive Tertium. As the warband's supply of infantry is steadily dwindling, Morrow often grumbles about the poor quality of the new "Rejects" he is expected to train.

Rannick also works closely with the Aeronautica Imperialis pilot Flight Lieutenant Gillia Masozi who is responsible for transporting teams of Acolytes to their mission zones in the lower levels of the hive city aboard her Valkyrie Storm Raptor.

Personality

Rannick is a cold and calculating man, largely because he has to be to achieve the Ordo Hereticus' and Inquisitor Grendyl's goals on Atoma Prime. For him, all of the other Acolytes of the warband on the Mourningstar are simply tools in the hands of the Emperor. They are to be used to cleanse the Heretics that threaten Hive Tertium and will be promoted or discarded as the situation demands.
