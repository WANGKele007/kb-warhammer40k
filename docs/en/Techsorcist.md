A Techsorcist is a Tech-priest of the Adeptus Mechanicus who specialises in analysing and expunging corrupted Machine Spirits from Chaos-infected technology. 

Techsorcists are very highly sought out by Inquisitors of the Ordo Malleus, who value their abilities to deal with any strange or Warp-related technological discoveries found during the course of an investigation into the activities of Chaos.

Role

Some Forge World Tech-priests develop an interest in analyzing corrupted Machine Spirits. Unusually for Tech-priests, Techsorcists are capable of intuitive thinking, speculation, and improvisation, and are famous (some among the orthodox worshippers of the Machine God might say infamous) for their lateral and often unorthodox process when strict logic and standard procedures fail. This is an invaluable tool for an Inquisitor trying to understand their foe's weaknesses. 

In the service of the Inquisition, a Techsorcist performs all the typical tasks of a standard Tech-priest, but has a deeper understanding of how the energies of Chaos mutates and adapts technology. Created by Magos Eremor in the latter half of the 36th Millennium in response to the reappearance from the Warp of the space hulk Fatum Posterus, the Techsorcists have long been on the front lines whenever heretical technology has been found. 

The success of this branch of the Adeptus Mechanicus within the Calixis Sector has caused their ranks to swell as the demand for trained Daemon-hunting Tech-priests has grown. In fact a quiet, but popular, joke within the Ordos Calixis is that there are actually four ordos of the Inquisition: Xenos, Hereticus, Malleus, and Mechanicus. This is a view which the Adeptus Mechanicus is more than happy to foster, as it allows their representatives to be the first to deal with any strange technological discoveries found during the course of an Inquisitor's investigations.

A trained Techsorcist, if not several, are in the employ of nearly every Ordo Malleus Inquisitor in the sector. On the tidally-locked world of Barsapine the Adeptus Mechanicus has over a dozen highly trained Techsorcists poring over the logs and remains of several technological devices of dubious origin. The Ecclesiarchy keeps a close eye on their research after the Macharian Incident. The call for additional assistance is strong, and many Inquisitors investigating the remains of the devices found there have petitioned for the Tech-priests in their employ to be trained as Techsorcists.

Becoming a Techsorcist

Becoming a Techsorcist is a simple affair for ordained Tech-priests of the Adeptus Mechanicus. First, a petition of transfer must be filed with their superiors, countersigned by the Inquisitor whom they are assigned to. Then, a special mechadendrite is installed that includes hexagrammic data-gates for protection from corrupted data or scrap-code. 

Finally, the potential Techsorcist is given three solar hours to access selected records regarding Chaos-corrupted Machine Spirits, then three more solar hours to complete a 7,000-question test. Assuming they pass, the Tech-priest is now officially recognised as a Techsorcist and is given clearance to request additional texts from the Ordo Malleus.

Wargear

By becoming an expert in Warp-altered technology, the Techsorcist has chosen to alter one of their mechadendrites to assist in their duties. This mechadendrite has special electronic wards put in place to resist the effects of corruption from Chaos-infested Machine Spirits (artificial intelligences). 

It also houses a small cogitator memory unit for storing proscribed documents and is set to automatically wipe itself upon removal of the mechadendrite or the death of the Techsorcist.
