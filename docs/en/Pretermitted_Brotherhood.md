The Pretermitted Brotherhood was a Blackshield Titan Legion active during the Horus Heresy.

Legion History

The Pretermitted Brotherhood was a Blackshield Legio that watched over the burned-out remains of their homeworld, striking down all who dared sully its memory with their blasphemous tread.
