The Silver Phoenixes is a Loyalist Space Marine Chapter of unknown Founding and origin.

The commander of the Torchbearers task force assigned to locate and bring Primaris Space Marine technology to the Silver Phoenixes received an arcane relic sphere called the Orb of Cleansing that is able to push back malignant energies.

In the darkest regions of the Imperium Nihilus beyond the Cicatrix Maledictum, its pulsating waves of purity have been seen to cloud psykers' control of their powers and even burn away the minds of witch-spawn.
