The Stone Gauntlets is a Loyalist Space Marine Chapter of unknown Founding and origin. This Chapter's most notable action occurred during the Imperium's defence of the Medina Corridor from a large invasion by the Forces of Chaos. Almost nothing else is known about this Chapter in Imperial records.
