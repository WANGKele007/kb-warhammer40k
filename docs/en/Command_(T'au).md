A command, or uash'o in the T'au Lexicon, is the term used for all the forces of a single T'au caste located in a given location, usually on a planet or in a star system. For instance, all the Fire Caste members on the world of Nimbosa were part of Fire Caste Command Nimbosa, while all Air Caste formations were part of the Air Caste Command Nimbosa.
