Meduson is the third limited release volume in The Horus Heresy series of novels, released as a Horus Heresy Weekender Exclusive in hardback format. Meduson has yet to be re-released in non-limited format, although two of its short stories have been released as a stand alone e-book and audio drama.

Meduson was later released as Meduson: The Ultimate Edition, which contains the seven short stories from the original anthology, as well Grey Talon and The Either as full-cast audio dramas with complete production scripts. Also included is a brand new story, Hand Elect by Chris Wraight.

Synopsis

Driven almost to the brink of self-destruction by the death of their Primarch, Ferrus Manus, the Iron Hands Legion now seek vengeance for the many horrors of Istvaan V. Gathering survivors from the Raven Guard and Salamanders Legions aboard any vessels capable of Warp travel, they wage a new campaign of annihilation against the Traitor forces across the galaxy -- a campaign masterminded by legendary warleader Shadrak Meduson.

Contents

Meduson contains the following short stories:

Meduson by Dan Abnett
Unforged by Guy Haley
Immortal Duty by Nick Kyme
The Keys of Hel by John French
The Noose by David Annandale
Deeds Endured by Gav Thorpe
Unspoken by Guy Haley

Meduson: The Ultimate Edition includes these extra stories:

Hand Elect by Chris Wraight
Grey Talon* by Chris Wraight
The Either* by Graham McNeill

These stories are presented in script form.
