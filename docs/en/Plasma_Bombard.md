A Plasma Bombard is an advanced form of heavy Plasma Weapon used by Saturnine Terminators during the Unification Wars, the Great Crusade and the Horus Heresy. 

The default weapon of a Saturnine Terminator, this formidable armament is effectively a Plasma Cannon that does not need a line of sight to make an accurate strike against an enemy target. A unit of Saturnine Terminators set up in an obscured position sporting a pair of Plasma Bombards can threaten a vast portion of the battlefield with relative impunity.

Heavy Plasma Bombard

A Heavy Plasma Bombard is a larger variant of a Plasma Bombard that was deployed as a primary weapon on Saturnine Dreadnoughts during the Unification Wars, the Great Crusade and the Horus Heresy.

Like its smaller counterpart, the Heavy Plasma Bombard can serve as a stand-off artillery weapon, unleashing its devastating plasma bolts accurately without requiring a line of sight. The Heavy Plasma Bombard had a longer range and damage capacity than the smaller variant of the weapon.
