Earth-33452 is the designation given in the Marvel Multiverse to the timeline that includes the universe of Warhammer 40,000.

Game History

When Marvel Comics began publishing comics set in the Warhammer 40,000 universe such as Warhammer 40,000: Marneus Calgar and Warhammer 40,000: Sisters of Battle, this was the designation given to this particular grim dark reality in the vast Marvel Multiverse.
