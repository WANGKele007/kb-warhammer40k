The Hammer of the Soul Forges is a Daemon Weapon that serves as the primary melee weapon wielded by Vashtorr the Arkifane, the Greater Daemon of Chaos Undivided who rules the Forge of Souls in the Realm of Chaos. But it is more than just a weapon, for it aids him in all his acts of creation and destruction within the Forge of Souls.
