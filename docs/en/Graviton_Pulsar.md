A Graviton Pulser is an Imperial gravitic weapon deployed on Imperial Navy starships, and in smaller sizes, by certain classes of Imperial Knights, such as the Knight Moirax. 

The crushing force projected by a Graviton Pulsar is capable of crumpling power armour or ripping the treads from a tank. While able to cause great destruction, such weapons are more often employed to disable and impede rather than destroy, fired to halt the advance of war machines so that they might be captured largely intact.
