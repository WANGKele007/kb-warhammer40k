A Heavy Rock Drill is a type of Imperial mining tool that is sometimes used as a melee weapon by Acolyte Hybrids of the Genestealer Cults. The Heavy Rock Drill is modified by the hybrids so it can be carried into
battle as a weapon. Its triple grinders can turn even the stoutest enemy champion to gory gobbets of flesh.
