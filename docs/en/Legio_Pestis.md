The Legio Pestis is a Traitor Titan Legion in the service of the Plague God Nurgle. 

Other than its service in the Plague Wars of the early 42nd Millennium alongside the other forces of Chaos that invaded the Realm of Ultramar, little else is currently known about the Legio Pestis in Imperial records.

Legion History

Notable Campaigns

Plague Wars (ca. 111.M42) - The Legio Pestis was counted among the forces dedicated to Grandfather Nurgle that invaded the Realm of Ultramar during the Plague Wars.
