The Angels of Doom is a Loyalist Space Marine Successor Chapter of the Dark Angels, created during an unknown Founding. The Angels of Doom's variant of Adeptus Astartes monasticism has a distinctly savage edge, a cultural adaptation drawn from the customs of their barbaric homeworld Meldegod. 

Chapter Appearance

Chapter Colours

The Angels of Doom wear green and red power armour, the colours halved vertically. 

Chapter Badge

The Angels of Doom's Chapter badge is a skull in front of a red sword, all on a green background. 

Trivia

The Angels of Doom, as well as their homeworld of Meldegod, were created by a Games Workshop employee identified only as "Cameron".
