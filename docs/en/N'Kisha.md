N'Kisha is a Keeper of Secrets, a Greater Daemon of Slaanesh.

History

In the starless gloom of the Noctis Aeterna, Craftworld Iyanden was invaded for the second time by the forces of Chaos since the birth of the Great Rift during the Era Indomitus.

Hoping to replicate the Masque of Slaanesh's success during the Battle of Biel-Tan, the Slaaneshi Keeper of Secrets N'Kisha used the Warp rift's power to breach the craftworld.

The daemons are repelled at the last, however, as Aeldari reinforcements arrived from all corners of the Webway, including Drukhari, Ynnari, Harlequins and warhosts from every major Asuryani craftworld.
