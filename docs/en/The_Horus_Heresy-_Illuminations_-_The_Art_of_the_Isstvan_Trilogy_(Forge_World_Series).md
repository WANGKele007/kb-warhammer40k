The Horus Heresy: Illuminations - The Art of the Isstvan Trilogy is the first art book in the The Horus Heresy series by Forge World. This 56 page art book contains artwork from the first three books in the The Horus Heresy series by Forge World -- The Isstvan Trilogy. This book was only available to those who purchased the limited print collector's edition of The Horus Heresy: Book Three - Extermination. This limited edition release contained Extermination as well as a collector's edition of The Horus Heresy: Legiones Astartes - Crusade Army List, and The Horus Heresy: Legiones Astartes - Isstvan Campaign Legions, the The Horus Heresy: Illuminations - The Art of the Isstvan Trilogy, and a special hard slip case that included enough room for Books One and Two also. This special edition was only available to the first 1500 pre-orders.
