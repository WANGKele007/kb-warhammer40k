The Phoenix Lord of the Asuryani Eagle Pilots is Amon Harakht, a term in the Aeldari Lexicon that literally translates into Low Gothic as "fighter pilot."

Little else is known about this Phoenix Lord other than that he founded the Eagle Pilots Aspect.
