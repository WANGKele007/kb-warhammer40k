The Kindred Stoic of Nightgulf is a Kindred of the Leagues of Votann. It is an example of a Kindred whose name is a combination of several common naming conventions, in this case a Kindred that has been named for a defining feature of their Hold World and the defining nature of its Kin.

Sources

Codex: Leagues of Votann (9th Edition), pg. 10
