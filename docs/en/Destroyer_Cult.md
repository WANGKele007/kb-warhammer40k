The Destroyer Cult is a sect of Necron society whose members have been overwhelmed by a form of insanity in whch they have come to hate all forms of organic life and seek to unleash "Red Harvests" across the galaxy that will eradicate all life.

History

Those Necrons of the Destroyer Cults are those who have descended into apparent madness and are considered untrustworthy and highly dangerous by their own kind. They are nothing less than agents of annihilation whose sole reason for existence is to service their unshakable yearning to cleanse the universe of all organic life. 

Unlike most other still self-aware Necrons, those of the Destroyer Cult do not wish to reverse biotransference and regain their organic forms; instead they have heavily modified their necrodermis bodies to better kill their foes. What impels them to join the Destroyer Cult is unknown but some suspect a subconscious imperative reprogrammed into their command protocols -- possibly by the C'tan, whose interests such a directive would serve. The Oruscar Dynasty Immortal Revkkah, for example, believed the Nightbringer Aza'gorod was responsible for the creation of the Destroyer Cults and thought the infected were the C'tan's bastard offspring.

Members of the Destroyer Cults can be found on every Tomb World and have been seen among every social class in Necron society. Destroyer Cultists are outcasts in their own society, with most Necrons believing their madness to be infectious. However, many Necron dynasties still find them useful shock troops and so allow them to be deployed alongside their own regular forces.

Such is the Destroyers' hatred of organic life that members of the Destroyer Cults hold that they will relentlessly scour a planet's organisms down to the microscopic level. This includes plants, bacteria and even oceans, which they will boil with their weaponry. Even if it takes solar decades, Destroyers will never cease in their attempt to purge the galaxy of all life. They even welcome their own deaths, for they themselves are amongst the living and in the end will seek total oblivion.
