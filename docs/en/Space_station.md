A space station, also commonly called a void station in the Imperium, is a spacecraft which remains in orbit of a celestial body and hosts Humans and other intelligent species for extended periods of time. It is therefore an artificial satellite featuring habitation facilities. 

A space station is usually (but not always) placed in orbit around a planet, moon, star or other such celestial body. Space stations can be equipped with support facilities for habitation, trade, defence and spacecraft repair and resupply.

A star fort is a particularly large and well-armed Imperial space station, often used as the home base for major Navis Imperialis battlefleets. Some are mobile, outfitted with massive Warp-Drives capable of moving the station through space at FTL speeds, while others possess only a limited mobility and must be moved through realspace or the Warp by Warp-capable voidships which tow them using massive cables.

Notable Space Stations

41 Pry - A neutral merchant space station originally built by the Imperium in the Calixis Sector.	

Aegida - The Imperial fortress-monastery of the Scythes of the Emperor Chapter.

Alcazar Astra - The Imperial	fortress-monastery of the Fists Exemplar Chapter.

Apex Cronus Bastion - The Imperial star fort which serves the Grey Knights and orbits their homeworld of Titan in the Sol System.

Arca Silentus - A massive forge-station dating back to the Dark Age of Technology active during the Horus Heresy. The station was corrupted by Lukas Chrom's scrap-code emitter on Mars, forcing an Adeptus Custodes boarding party and later Dark Angels reinforcements to destroy it after a bloody battle against its corrupted constructs.

Astigma Binary - An Adeptus Mechanicus void station.	

Basilica Aureus - An Imperial void sation belonging to the Ecclesiarchy.

Basilica Mortis- This space station serves as the fortress-monastery of the Mortifactors Chapter of the Adeptus Astartes.

Broadsword Station	- Broadsword Station is a void station of the Grey Knights Chapter that serves as the first line of defence for Titan if it were ever to be attacked, though it primarily serves as a transport hub as well as dockyard for the fleet of warships used by the Grey Knights.

Cage Twenty-Twenty - An infamous Imperial void station in the Ultima Segmentum that is commanded by the Adeptus Arbites and serves as a prison for the segmentum's most corrupt Heretics. The Arbites keep the prisoners there quarantined for interrogation or ritual chastisement before they are executed.	

Carnus Maxima - A derelict Imperial orbital platform converted into a void station providing cheap pleasures for millions of underfed workers, criminals and Astra Militarum soldiers. An army of criminals peddle drugs and recycled food. The station is heavily infiltrated by Heretics and mutants, but Imperial officials still hesitate to shut down the station due to its becalming effect on the masses.

Centrati's Eye - An Imperial orbital defence station that orbits the world of Centrati. At one point the station was attacked by a Drukhari raiding party, who captured and enslaved the station's personnel before turning the station's weapons on the planet below for their amusement. However, a Black Templars battle barge was also in orbit, having been despatched to Centrati to help exterminate an alien infestation. The Black Templars were quick to respond, leading a counterattack that retook the station.

Chimaera - The Chimaera is an Imperial Ramilies-class Star Fort owned by the Dark Angels Chapter. The base of operations for a crusading Dark Angels Chapter under Master Althalos and Captain Ormand, the Chimaera came to the rescue of Space Wolves forces during the Battle of the Alaxxes Nebula during the Horus Heresy.

Chrysalis Shipyards - The Chrysalis Shipyards orbit above Arimaspia in the Gothic Sector. During the Gothic War, these Imperial void stations were fought over eighteen times between 150.M41 and 153.M41. Controlling shipyards which had the ability to repair and replenish fleets became of paramount importance as the Gothic War continued. At least one of the battles for the shipyards included a Chaos Space Marine invasion which was fought against for over three solar weeks by Navis Imperialis personnel.

The Cradle - The Cradle is a large void station that serves the Rogue Trader houses of the Davamir Compact. Its upper levels contain the busts and death masks of past Void Kings and Void Queens who led the Compact, along with the Servo-skulls, Cherubs and stranger constructs that dwell among them. The Cradle's core contains the Bladespire, which leads to the Arches of Victory and the Halls of the Lost. This in turn leads to the Starlit Marches whose great viewports look out across the void station's docking spurs and their relentless voidship activity. The Cradle is currently under the command of the Void King Davos Lamertine.

Damaroth	- Damaroth is a Deathwatch watch fortress. It is a vast artificial ring constructed around a glowing moon. It serves as the headquarters for Deathwatch operations in the Centaurus Arm of the Ultima Segmentum.

Delugen - Delugen is an Imperial gyroscopic void station. It was once infested by the Genestealer Cult Tendricul until the 41st Millennium, when a Craftworld Ulthwe strike force, led by Farseer Anathroelle Starseeker, attacked Delugen. The Asuryani sought to destroy the cult, but to do so they killed any Human the strike force came across. During the attack, however, the void station sent out an astropathic distress signal which reached the Black Templars Chapter. The Space Marines came to Delugen's aid and attacked before Ulthwe's forces could complete their purge. The Asuryani were ultimately drive off and Delugen's surviving population, including the remnants of Cult Tendricul, escaped from the void station to spread their infestation to new Imperial worlds.

Dhulsa - The Dhulsa is an Imperial star fort that lies at the northern entrance to the Nachmund Gauntlet. It is currently besieged by an unknown foe.

Diogenes IV Research Station - The Diogenes IV Research Station is an Imperial void station anchored amongst the infamous Pulsars in the Calixis Sector.

Drystan Construction Yards	- The Drystan Construction Yards is an Imperial void station in The Periphery sub-sector of the Calixis Sector, located on the border with the neighbouring Scarus Sector.

Echo Station - Echo Station is a xenos-made space station that lies within the Imperium Nihilus. It is under the control of The Risen, the former Fallen Angels who now serve the Dark Angels Chapter's resurrected primarch Lion El'Jonson. It consists of five extended arms around a globular core. Echo Station's xenos creators designed the station with a scale and layout that is similar to those used by Humanity. Despite this, its angles look odd to Humans, which causes a subtle wrongness to cling to the space station. Also adding to the uncertainty surrounding the true nature of the station is the question of why Echo Station was placed at the edge of the Cordova Nebula, where it is too far from any of the nearby settled worlds to protect them.

Echonis Augury - Echonis Augury is an ancient space station located in the Askellon Sector, where it drifts along the sector's rim and away from regular voidship traffic. It originally served as a major Adeptus Mechanicus research site and hundreds of Tech-priests and servitors worked there to info-scour the surrounding parsecs in a secretive quest. Several standard centuries ago, however, a supply vessel arrived to find the station empty and barren and no sign of what had happened to the Tech-priests. Since then Echonis Augury has become a popular trading post for Rogue Traders and common merchants alike, where thousands now live; with extended generations working to maintain and expand it. All who live there, though, have occasional concerns over the mysterious fate that befell its previous occupants.

Endeavour of Will - Alongside the Bastion Inviolate, the Endeavour of Will is an Imperial Fists star fort that helps guard the region of Imperial space around the Eye of Terror from the forces of Chaos. The Endeavour of Will was attacked by the Iron Warriors Warsmith Shon'tu in 998.M41, leading to the Battle for the Endeavour of Will. The star fort's Machine Spirit (artificial intelligence) is quasi-sentient, able to communicate actively with its sister star fort the Bastion Inviolate and even expressed regret when the Bastion Inviolate sacrificed itself to save the Endeavour of Will.	

Entropy Omega - Entropy Omega is an Adeptus Mechanicus star fort located in the Lacaon System in the Tenebra Sub-sector of the Caligari Sector in the Segmentum Tempestus. This Cult Mechanicus void station was found deserted after long isolation and was recently repopulated by a contingent of Tech-priests. However, its maze of tunnels hasn't been properly explored, purged and blessed by holy oils and tech-chants yet. The base is under Adeptus Mechanicus supervision in theory, but the Inquisition considers the place unholy ground, which has led to squabbles between the two Imperial agencies.

Eucladus - The Eucaladus is an Imperial star fort located in the Masali System that is part of the defences of the Realm of Ultramar.

Eye of Selene - The Eye of Selene is an Imperial void station that protects the orbit of the Hive World Necromunda. It was built on the orders of Cinderak Helmawr, after she won the Two-Faced War and secured her title as the Hive World's next planetary governor. She tasked the Van Saar with its construction in reward for aiding her in the succession war with her brother, Gothrul Helmawr. When it was completed, Necromunda's orbit was finally secure, as all in-going or out-going vessels and materials needed to pass through the void station. Afterwards, Cinderak further rewarded the Van Saar by making them one of Necromunda's Clan Houses.

Galatan -	The Galatan is an Imperial star fort that serves with the Ultramar Defence Fleet. The greatest of Ultramar's star forts, Galtan is a hundred kilometres in length and also maintains orbital shipyards which rivalled those of Luna. Its weaponry is said to be equal in strength to that which a sector battlefleet of the Navis Imperialis can bring to bear. Its defence garrison consists of tens of thousands of Ultramar Auxilia and hundreds of Space Marines, many of which hail from the Novamarines Chapter. During the Plague Wars, it attempted to provide support for Roboute Guilliman's forces on Parmenio but was boarded by a Nurglish Plague Fleet. Massive casualties ensued as the Imperials defended the station, including the loss of Bardan Dovaro, Chapter Master of the Novamarines.

Mathus Station - Mathus Station was an Imperial void station attacked by a Chaos Cult called the Hounds of the Ruined Sky. The Imperial Fists defending the station were hard-pressed to combat the massive cultist horde. The long siege lasted nearly two hundred standard years and the cultists even built a little civilisation on the station, breeding new generations of themselves to fight with the Space Marines. In the end, there were only four Imperial Fists left out of the original twenty-two who had manned the station. It was only after the Blood Angels responded to the distress signal coming from the station that the cult was finally destroyed.	

Optera Void Station - The Optera Void Station is an Imperial void station that was attacked by the Red Corsairs warband of Heretic Astartes in 979.M41.

Phalanx - The Phalanx serves as the mobile fortress-monastery of the Imperial Fists Chapter of the Adeptus Astartes and is the largest void station in the Imperium. 

Port Rigava - Port Rigava is an Imperial void station and the primary outpost of the Navis Imperialis located in the Tarantir System of the Agartha Sub-sector of the Caligari Sector in the Segmentum Tempestus. Port Rigava was once a proud Navis Imperialis station that hosted cruisers and battleships, but a daring Drukhari raid crippled a large percentage of the once-proud local battlefleet. According to the estimation of the High Admiralty, rebuilding the fleet will take several solar decades, therefore these days the port is mainly used by Rogue Traders.

Port Therion - Port Therion is an Adeptus Mechanicus void station located in the Phaera System of the Agartha Sub-sector of the Caligari Sector in the Segmentum Tempestus. The station is the commercial and xeno-archeological hub of the region, loosely supervised by Rogue Trader houses and Adeptus Mechanicus-appointed enforcers. The port's authorities tend to turn a blind eye on smuggling and lesser illicit activities as long as Adeptus Mechanicus privileges on xenos artefacts are respected. The station is closely monitored by the personnel of the Ordo Xenos for selective purges when the Cold Trade in alien artefacts is deemed to have become particularly dangerous.

Port Wander - Port Wander is a massive void station on the uttermost edge of the Drusus Marches sub-sector of the Calixis Sector in the Rubycon II System, rightly regarded as the last bastion of the rule of the Emperor of Mankind this side of the Koronus Expanse and the last stop before entering that untamed region of the Halo Stars. A place of desperate hopes and vain dreams, Port Wander teems with a transitory population of traders, spies, merchant factors, pilgrims, and missionaries amongst which move Adeptus Administratum functionaries and minions of the Adeptus Mechanicus, all feeding on the riches that flow from the realms beyond the Warp storms in the Koronus Expanse.

Port Wrath - Port Wrath is an Imperial void station that serves as a combination dockyard and naval base for the Navis Imperialis. It serves as the headquarters for Battlefleet Calixis and is the mainstay naval installation in the Calixis Sector. It half protrudes from a metallic planetoid orbiting the white dwarf star Mundus, and bristles with lance batteries matching the firepower of most fleets. In 815.M41 the commander of Port Wrath was Rear Admiral Zoila Kusch, who had served in that capacity for over fifty standard years by that point. At one time the Heretek Malygrisian Tech-priests were purged from the foundry wards of Port Wrath.

Precipice - Precipice is a space station located in the Segmentum Pacificus that has been cobbled together from the wreckage of numerous voidships that have tried to forcibly claim the nearby seventh Blackstone Fortress. It is home to various Human, Abhuman and xenos scavengers and serves as a waypoint for those looking to venture into the Blackstone Fortress and discover its secrets.

Pride of Sol	- The Pride of Sol is an orbital citadel of the Imperium that was once invaded by the forces of the Thousand Sons. However, the Great Company of the Space Wolves Wolf Lord Harald Deathwolf later came to its aid and defeated the Chaos Space Marines. The Great Company's victory on the citadel was later among the many honour and campaign badges that were displayed on Deathwolf's Great Banner.

Ranieli Starfort	- The Ranieli Starfort is an Imperial star fort belonging to the Navigator House Ranieli located in the Malcorum System in the Triglav Sub-sector of the Caligari Sector in the Segmentum Tempestus. The Ranieli Starfort is home to House Ranieli's Sacred Archives, which contain a vast collection of tomes on long-forgotten Warp trading routes, sanctioned Warp-folios and other useful knowledge for Navigators in the Caligari Sector.

Raptorus Rex	- The Raptorus Rex was the vast, mobile pre-Imperial star fort of the Fire Hawks Chapter that served as their primary base of operations and mobile fortress-monastery. During the Badab War this vessel was the largest warship operating within the Badab Sector and is indeed one of the largest warships ever used in the Imperium of Man's service. The formidable firepower of this colossus of war was capable of scouring the surface of a world within mere moments of its emergence from the Warp or challenge an entire squadron of cruisers alone. The Raptorus Rex and the entire Fire Hawks Chapter fleet was officially declared "Lost in the Warp" by the Imperium following an attempted Warp jump from the Piraeus System to the Crow's Word Sub-sector in 963.M41.	

Sabriel - Sabriel is an Adeptus Mechanicus void station, long forgotten and abandoned. It located near the domain of the Severan Dominate and the Stygies Deeps in the Spinward Front region of The Periphery Sub-sector of the Calixis Sector.	

Sinopha Station - Sinopha Station was an Imperial void station located at the edge of the Protean Ebb in the Magulanox Sector. At one point the Alpha Legion led an attack on the station. A unit of Chaos Space Marines was delivered to Sinopha Station via a warship known as The Ninth Eye and proceeded to slaughter most of the inhabitants and personnel, leaving a few alive so as to spread fear through the surrounding regions. Scout Sergeant Kholka of the White Scars later came to Sinopha Station; the survivors' accounts allowed him to pick up the trail of the warband that had attacked. After tracking the Alpha Legion to the fourth planet of the Cernis System, he and his Scouts alerted Kor'sarro Khan, the Chapter's Master of the Hunt, who was hunting the Heretic Astartes' master, the Daemon Prince Kernax Voldorius.

Skyfall - Skyfall is an Imperial planetoid-sized, Ultrus-scale starport, fleet facility and defensive void station, that belongs to the Blood Angels Chapter and is located within the Baal System. Its surface is etched with a star map of the Imperium and the statue of an angel twenty kilometres tall, with a sword another seven kilometres long, held aloft atop the starport. Skyfall is under the command of the Blood Angels's captain of the 8th Company, who is known as the "Lord of Skyfall", and it has undergone a vast reconstruction in the Era Indomitus. This was done to not only repair the damage done to it during the Devastation of Baal, but also to turn it into a hub for voidships in the system. Lord Commander Dante, plans for the Baal System to become the capital of the Imperium Nihilus that he now rules and Skyfall reflects that ambition, as the starport has thousands of jetties projecting from it. This allows Skyfall to service a great many voidships at once and also serve as the base of operations for the Battlefleet Nihilus armada. Thousands of Tech-priests from numerous Adeptus Mechanicus Forge Worlds populate the station as well to repair and refuel the voidships docked with it. Skyfall also possesses the capability to serve as an orbital shipyard and construct new warships. The Blood Angels battle barge Baal's Fury was built in the starport's drydocks.

Solari Anchorage - The Solari Anchorage is an enormous combination of Imperial star fort and deep-void docks which served as a major base for the Navis Imperialis's Battlefleet Charadon. It is located within the Metalica System and played a major part in the system's defence, as it was home to a sizable Navis Imperialis fleet. However when the Charadon Campaign began, Lieutenant-Heraldus Lihua Sheradane departed with most of the fleet to engage the Champion of Nurgle Typhus's Chaos forces. A small fleet, led by the Emperor-class Battleship Mercy of the Blade, remained to guard the anchorage, which was under the command of Rear Admiral Vordkin. This proved to be an inadequate defence, as Typhus surprised the Imperial forces by invading the Metalica System via the The Sore, a tunnel through the Warp opened through the use of Nurglish sorcery. The tunnel had suddenly materialised within the system, and caught its defenders off-guard, as Typhus's forces appeared from within and prepared to invade the Forge World Metalica. First, though, Typhus intended to deal with any defenders that stood in his way and this included the Solari Anchorage. As Typhus's fleet advanced upon it, Rear Admiral Vordkin knew the Mercy of the Blade's fleet could not stop the invaders. Rather than have his fleet destroyed, Vordkin ordered the Mercy of the Blade away, with as as many Imperial warships and support craft as could escape with it in time. Soon after the Imperial fleet departed, Typhus's warships came into range of the Solari Anchorage's weaponry and it opened a relentless barrage upon them. However, Typhus's fleet weathered the star fort's fire and the Heretic Astartes managed to board it. Vordkin and his armsmen fought valiantly to defend the Anchorage, but they were killed by the unending hordes of the Chaos. After the last Imperial defender was slain, the Solari Anchorage was taken by Typhus and became a part of the realm of Grandfather Nurgle.

Suu'suamyth - The Suu'suamyth, previously known as the Glory of Argyre, is a former Imperial orbital shipyard that was seized by the T'au Empire during the Fifth Sphere of Expansion. Located in the Nem'yar Atoll, the shipyard is led by a ruling council containing a member of each of the T'au castes, with Ethereal Aun'song in overall command. After several acts of sabotage aboard the shipyard led to numerous deaths, the council tasked the Gue'vesa Kalice Arkady and the T'au investigator Por'ui Fi'rios Kau'kartyr to look into the matter. Their investigation later led to the discovery of a Genestealer Cult infestation aboard the Suu'suamyth, which was soon successfully destroyed by the Fire Caste.

Talus's Wheel - Talus's Wheel was an Imperial void station that had very low gravity. This led to its inhabitants being thin and too weak to be able to move in normal Terran gravity without using a cybernetic exo-skeleton.

Telstarax - The Telstarax is a massive, pre-Imperial space station that encircled Medusa, the homeworld of the Iron Hands Chapter. Built during the Dark Age of Technology, it is believed the Telstarax was used to convey Medusa's minerals into space though its original purpose is still unknown. Even by the time of Ferrus Manus, the Telstarax was an abandoned ruin, with a great portion of it having plunged onto the planet below. The structure has been studied heavily by the Techmarines of the Iron Hands. These Techmarines claim that they feel something within the Machine Spirits (artificial intelligences) of the relic -- a sense of anticipation, of purpose as yet undiscovered, and of prospects that seem to lie just out of reach. What these may be is still a mystery. During the Horus Heresy, the Iron Hands used a shard of the Telstarax to construct a powerful warship dubbed Memorius.

Triad - The Triad is the name given to the three Imperial star forts that are among the forces defending the Formidyre System during the Fourth Tyrannic War. As Hive Fleet Leviathan's Tyranids invade the system, the Triad are located in the Vyrox Void Warren Quarantine Zone. The star forts are now being assailed on all sides by the Tyranids.

Valiant Mountain	- Valiant Mountain is an Imperial void station in orbit of the planet Phellur III. At one point a Chaos ritual was enacted aboard Valiant Mountain. However, when the ritual was interrupted by Imperial Guardsmen of the Death Korps of Krieg, a Warp rift opened at the heart of the void station which allowed Daemons of Tzeentch to encroach on the Materium. The Death Korps were reinforced by the Tempestus Scions of the 29th Zetic Tygers regiment. They led a series of strikes against the attacking Daemons, breaking through to the Lord of Change commanding them. After defeating the Greater Daemon, the Warp rift sealed itself.

Vigil - The Vigil is an Imperial void station that serves as the fortress-monastery of the Red Scorpions Chapter. It orbits their homeworld of Zaebus Minoris.

Will of Eternity	- The Will of Eternity was one of six dormant xenos Blackstone Fortresses discovered by the Imperium in the Gothic Sector in the late 31st Millennium. It was turned into an Imperial star fort by the Adeptus Mechanicus, and served as the primary Navis Imperialis base for its sub-sector. It was captured during the Gothic War of the early 41st Millennium by the Chaos Lord Abaddon the Despoiler, and activated using the artefacts known as the Hand of Darkness and Eye of Night. Abaddon nonetheless still had difficulty controlling the xenos vessel, but ultimately was able to find a solution by creating an artificial noctilith-infused being known as Dravura Morkath. Together with two other captured Blackstone Fortresses, it was used to destroy the Tarantis star in the Tamahl Sector. Abaddon took the three Blackstone Fortresses to Schindlegeist to try to capture another Imperial Blackstone Fortress there, but while the Will of Eternity and the two other fortresses built up their power to destroy the system's star, Captain Abridal of the Flame of Purity drove his warship into the fortresses' converging energy waves, destroying his warship, but dissipating the energy. As the rest of the Imperial fleet closed in on Abaddon's forces, he was forced to retreat, and managed to escape into the Warp with the Will of Eternity and one other Blackstone Fortress. Abaddon kept the Will of Eternity in the Eye of Terror for many standard centuries, where it became warped and twisted, and so hideously altered that it now looked like an artefact of Chaos. The Will of Eternity was employed by Abaddon during the 13th Black Crusade against the forces of Cadia. During the war, the Space Wolves and the Adeptus Mechanicus were able to prevent the Will of Eternity from destroying the planet and largely disabled it during an assault that saw Wolf Lord Sven Bloodhowl go missing. However, the Will of Eternity was partially reactivated by Morkath, who intended to use it to still conquer Cadia and eventually Terra. Abaddon instead decided to use the Will of Eternity as a different kind of weapon of mass destruction and dropped the massive space station from orbit onto Cadia's surface, destroying the famed Fortress World and the Will of Eternity both, guaranteeing the Fall of Cadia and the successful conclusion of the 13th Black Crusade with a significant if limited Chaos victory.
