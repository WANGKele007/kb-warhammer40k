L.J. Goulding, also known as Laurie Goulding, is an editor and author for Games Workshop's Black Library imprint.

Bibliography

Warhammer 40,000

Deathwatch

Last Watch (Short Story) (2012, collected in Xenos Hunters (Anthology))

The Horus Heresy

The Heart of the Pharos (Short Story) (2016, collected in The Burden of Loyalty (Anthology))

Space Marine Battles

Scythes of the Emperor (2017, ISBN 1-78496-563-4) collects the following:
The Aegidan Oath (Short Story) (also collected in Lords and Tyrants (Anthology))
Slaughter at Giant's Coffin (2017, ISBN 1-78496-523-5)
Heloth (Short Story)
Reclamation (Short Story)
Daedalus (Short Story)
Terminal Velocity (Short Story)

Other

The Word of the Silent King (Short Story) (2014, collected in The Everliving Legion (Anthology), Crusade + Other Stories (Anthology) and The Great Devourer: The Leviathan Omnibus (Anthology))
