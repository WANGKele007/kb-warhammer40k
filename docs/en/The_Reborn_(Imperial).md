The Reborn is a Loyalist Space Marine Chapter of unknown Founding and a genetic successor of the Ultramarines.

The Reborn are currently one of the nine Shield Chapters of Ultramar, 9 Chapters of the Adeptus Astartes (plus the Ultramarines) that the Lord Commander of the Imperium Roboute Guilliman charged with permanently defending the Realm of Ultramar during the Era Indomitus.

In order to perform their duties, each Shield Chapter was given command of a world within Ultramar to use as their base of operations.

Notable Members

Yansar - Yansar is a Primaris Apothecary and member of Tetrarch Decimus Androdinus Felix's Chosen of Vespator Honour Guard. 

Chapter Appearance

Chapter Colours

The Reborn's Chapter colours are blue and white.

Chapter Badge

The Reborn's Chapter badge is a white skull centred within a white omega letter drawn from the ancient Grecian alphabet of Terra, which lies on a blue background. This is an inverted version of the icon of the Reborn's parent Chapter, the Ultramarines.
