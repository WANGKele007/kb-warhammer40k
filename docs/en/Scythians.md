The Scythians are a xenos species whose weapons have found their way into the Armoury of the Grey Knights and the Inquisition.

The Scythians are a race of warrior-monks, dedicated to a peculiar form of armed martial arts. The dagger-like Scythian Venom Talon is one of the more basic close combat weapons they use, able to incapacitate a foe with the slightest scratch.

Quite how the Inquisition and the Grey Knights came to acquire such weapons is perhaps best left to the imagination.
