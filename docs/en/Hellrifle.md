A Hellrifle is a modified ranged weapon intended specifically to attack Daemons. 

A weapon much favoured by Radical Inquisitors, the Hellrifle closely resembles an antique firearm from a much earlier age of Human technical development. 

In reality, it is a highly sophisticated weapon that employs an array of containment shielding and galvanic impellers to fire razor-sharp shards of daemonic matter.
