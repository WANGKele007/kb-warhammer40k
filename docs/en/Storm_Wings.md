The Storm Wings is a Loyalist Codex Astartes-compliant Space Marine Chapter of unknown Founding which is also a rare Successor Chapter of the Raven Guard. The Storm Wings' Chapter homeworld is Eyrie, a planet in the Segmentum Solar.

Chapter History

Notable Campaigns

Encounter with the Tiger Claws (680.M41) - The Storm Wings encountered the long-lost strike cruiser Bakasurra, a known vessel of the Tiger Claws Chapter, which reappeared on the edge of the Segmentum Pacificus after being declared "Lost in the Warp" over a thousand standard years earlier in 280.M40 when the entire Tiger Claws Chapter itself had disappeared. The starship appeared to have been subject to severe temporal distortion in the Warp, and for the 100 battle-brothers aboard the Bakasurra, no more than a few solar months had elapsed since they had set out on their journey. After making contact with the surviving Tiger Claws, the Storm Wings strike force aided them in battle against Drukhari slavers. They resupplied the Tiger Claws, who then departed to make their way towards the location of their fortress-monastery on their homeworld of Krodha.

Chapter Beliefs

The Space Marines of the Storm Wings Chapter believe in embracing the burden of one's failure as a symbol of strength. This belief comes in the form of a chain of thick, dark iron links.

Each link is crafted in the shape of two interlocking talons, like birds of prey bound in combat. The chain serves as a record of the Chapter's failures. The talons are designed to intentionally bite into the skin of the neck so that no battle-brother will forget the failures of the Chapter.

Notable Storm Wings

Tarvarius - Tarvarius was a Space Marine of the Storm Wings seconded to service with the Astra Militarum forces deployed to the planet Ourea, including the 900th Cadian Shock Troops Regiment.

Chapter Appearance

Chapter Colours

The Storm Wings wear dim grey power armour. The Aquila or Imperialis on the chestplate is red. 

The red squad tactical specialty symbol on the right shoulder guard designates operational specialty -- battleline, close support, fire support, Veteran or command. 

A black High Gothic numeral is stenciled in the centre of the squad tactical specialty symbol which indicates squad number. 

The colour of the shoulder plate trim indicates company number in accordance with the dictates of the Codex Astartes -- i.e. white (1st Company), yellow (2nd Company), red (3rd Company), green (4th Company), etc.

Chapter Badge

The Storm Wings' Chapter badge is a red skull, facing forward, with red, lightning-like wings projecting from its sides. This is centred on a field of dim grey.
