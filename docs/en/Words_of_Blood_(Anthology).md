Words of Blood is an anthology of short stories published in 2002 and edited by Marc Gascoigne and Andy Jones. 

Synopsis

In the war-torn 41st Millennium, Humanity is locked in a desperate struggle for survival against a relentless tide of aliens. Read tales of heroism and valour in this action-packed selection of savage science fiction stories ripped from the pages of Inferno! magazine. 

The anthology contains the following short stories:
Words of Blood by Ben Counter
Raptor Down by Gav Thorpe
Chains of Command by Graham McNeill
Loyalty's Reward by Simon Jowett
Deus ex Mechanicus by Andy Chambers
Barathrum by Jonathan Curren
Missing in Action by Dan Abnett
Liberty by Gav Thorpe
Ork Hunter by Dan Abnett
Business as Usual by Graham McNeill
Defixio by Ben Counter
