The Regents of Gheren are a Loyalist Space Marine Chapter of unknown Founding and genetic origin.

The Chapter was sent to aid the Sons of Galathor Chapter homeworld Throne of Galat in the Ultima Segmentum close to the Malfactus Warpstorm after it was invaded, however they were attacked before reaching that destination.
