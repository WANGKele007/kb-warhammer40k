Shang, also spelled Sheng, was a lord commander of the Night Lords Legion and equerry to his primarch Konrad Curze during the Great Crusade and Horus Heresy. He was regarded as one of the few Night Lords that their primarch did not openly despise for being a close confidant and aide. Unlike many within the VIII Legion, Shang's loyalty was directed personally toward his gene-father rather than solely toward the Legion's doctrine of ruling through fear, a distinction that both elevated him in Curze's regard and marked him as vulnerable in the primarch's eyes.

During the Thramas Crusade and the destruction of Nostramo, Shang stood at Curze's side, even as he became one of the first Night Lords to openly acknowledge that his gene-father was descending into madness. After Curze's disappearance following his capture by the Dark Angels, Shang attempted to reforge the fractured Legion and search for its missing primarch, but his efforts led to a bitter power struggle with Gendor Skraivok. The conflict culminated in Shang's death in a duel aboard the Night Lords' flagship Nightfall, which left Skraivok supreme over the Night Lords.

History

After Konrad Curze's took command of his Night Lords Legion on Nostramo following his rediscovery by the Emperor, Shang was among the first transhuman Space Marines created from the Night Hunter's gene-seed on the primarch's homeworld. Raised during the early consolidation of the VIII Legion under Curze's direct authority, Shang developed a reputation for discipline and reliability at a time when the Night Lords were still defining their identity within the Great Crusade.

Over subsequent campaigns, Shang rose steadily through the Legion's hierarchy, eventually attaining the rank of Lord Commander. He became one of the senior officers of the Night Lords and was noted for maintaining a comparatively stable and direct relationship with his gene-father. As a result of this proximity and perceived dependability, Curze appointed Shang as his personal advisor. 

Unlike some of his battle-brothers, including First Captain Jago Sevatarion, Shang's loyalty was directed primarily toward Curze himself rather than solely toward the Legion's doctrine of ruling through fear. However, Curze regarded such personal devotion with ambiguity, often interpreting overt loyalty to his person as a vulnerability rather than an asset.

During the destruction of Nostramo, when Curze ordered the annihilation of his own homeworld in a fit of vengeance over its reversion to its criminal culture, Shang expressed open concern regarding the primarch's actions. He appealed to Savatarion in an attempt to moderate or halt Curze's escalating violence. Sevatarion rejected this appeal and criticised Shang's stance as weakness. Nevertheless, Shang was among the first Night Lords to openly acknowledge that their primarch's mental state appeared to be deteriorating.

During the campaign in the Tramasi System, Shang accompanied Curze and Sevatar to Tsagualsa for negotiations with the Dark Angels and their primarch, Lion El'Jonson. The meeting devolved into armed conflict between the two Legions. During the fighting, Shang took part in the engagement against the Dark Angels and contributed to the death of Captain Alajos. When Curze was wounded in the confrontation, Shang assisted in his extraction from combnat back to the Night Lords' flagship.

After Curze's disappearance when he was captured by the Dark Angels aboard their flagship Invincible Reason, Shang was as affected by their primarch's loss as the other Night Lords, but remained focused on preserving the Legion's hierarchy even as it threatened to collapse. He sought to stabilise the Night Lords and advocated for continued raiding operations on Loyalist strongholds in the galactic east amid efforts to locate their missing primarch. This stance placed him in opposition to Gendor Skraivok, who sought to align the Night Lords more closely with Horus Lupercal and so committ the VIII Legion to the Traitors' assault on Terra.

The rivalry between the two commanders intensified. In an effort to neutralise Skraivok's influence, Shang arranged for his confinement within a maze-like section of the VIII Legion's flagship Nightfall. However, Skraivok escaped, aided by the advantages provided by his Daemon Weapons. The conflict concluded in a direct duel between the two Night Lords commanders. Skraivok slew Shang during their duel, subsequently consolidating his own authority within the VIII Legion as its supreme commander until the later return of Konrad Curze after the Heresy. Skraivok was recognised by Horus as the Night Lords' new supreme commander and he led the VIII Legion into the Siege of Terra in 014.M31.
