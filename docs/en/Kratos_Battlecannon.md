The Kratos Battlecannon is a variant of the standard Battle Cannon created for use millennia ago on the Kratos Heavy Assault Tank. It is a ballistic weapon that can be loaded with high-explosive shells intended to kill large numbers of enemy infantry, armour-piercing shells to eliminate enemy armour or assault well-defended fortifications or flashburn shells that are most effective when used against super-heavy enemy tanks.
