Harlequin is a novel by Ian Watson. It was released in 1994 and is collected in the Inquisition War Omnibus. 

Synopsis

The galaxy of the 41st Millennium is torn by strife. Earth's Inquisition seems at war with itself as Secret Inquisitors and other shadowy figures conspire to inflict mind-slavery on the Human race. Their aim is to create a psychic doomsday weapon but if the weapon backfires the very fabric of reality could be destroyed. 

Amongst all this Jaq Draco is a renegade. Yet might this formerly loyal Inquisitor prove to be a saviour of the human race? 

Mischievous aliens -- Harlequin player-warriors -- are about to stage a Ceremony of Cataclysm. Their vast orbital theatre circles the devastated world of Stalinvast -- the very world to which Jaq inadvertently brought genocide. Meanwhile elite Space Marines muster at the order of the Inquisition, to purge the Harlequins and seize their time-old secrets. 

Jaq is impelled to intervene, but can they act before his Navigator finally loses his sanity to the madness of the Warp? And can Meh'lindi, his lethal assassin-courtesan, survive frightful illegal surgery before she can impersonate an alien life-form?
