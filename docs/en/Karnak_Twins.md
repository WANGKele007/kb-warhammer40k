The Karnak Twins, also called simply The Twins, are Rinda Karnak and her fraternal twin brother Rodin Karnak. The two are native, Hive Tertium-born officers of the Moebian 6th Regiment, a Traitoris Militarum regiment based on the Hive World of Atoma Prime that had fallen to the service of Nurgle. They became instrumental to the plans of the Nurglite Chaos Cult of the Admonition to corrupt the hive city. The Karnak Twins are brutal, cunning, and the de facto leaders of the Moebian 6th in their commander Captain Wolfer's absence. They were always eager to commit extreme acts of violence and depravity. 

The Twins are actually highborn of House Karnak. As nobles born in the upper spires of Hive Tertium, they were granted many privileges in Imperial society. One of the many boons bestowed upon them due to their high birth were the officer commissions they were granted upon joining the Moebian 6th Regiment, the most prestigious of the Moebian Regiments of the Astra Militarum.

While little is known of their time in the 6th, they appear to have caused much trouble for their fellow guardsmen, though they were shielded from all retribution by Captain Wolfer. Rodin Karnak was noted as being a cruel bully who wasn't fit to be an officer, while Rinda Karnak maintained many feuds and rivalries with her comrades. The Twins were present on Nox Alpha when the Moebian 6th were corrupted by Nurgle.

Explicator Orgustine Zola's obsession with bringing Tertium's Traitors to justice led her to start a special, off-the-books mission for Inquisitor Grendyl's warband of Acolytes: the so-called "Orthus Offensive," intended to capture one of the Karnak Twins in the Torrent district of Hive Tertium in order to interrogate them. However, both twins were subsequently killed by the volunteer strike team sent to capture one of them. This was partially due to Interrogator Iven Rannick's intervention mid-mission to prevent the unauthorised operation and attempt to salvage what he could of Zola's overly zealous personal pursuit.

History

Origins

The twins were born into immense wealth and high status in the upper spires of Hive Tertium as highborn of House Karnak, both taking extreme delight in making life hell for those they viewed as their lessers, a tendency that has continued throughout their military careers as guardsmen and subsequently as Traitor guardsmen. At some point the two joined the Astra Militarum, becoming members of the elite Moebian 6th Regiment.

The twins are noted as being at Anchorpoint Numen for a time during the Nox Alpha campaign of the Moebian Domain's Fringe War where the Moebian 6th were corrupted by Nurgle. While at Anchorpoint Numen, Rodin Karnak would beat new recruits to bloody pulps and Rinda Karnak partook in cannibalism behind closed doors. Eventually, Anchorpoint Numen was exposed to the disease known as "Nox Rot", wiping out most if not all of the remaining Loyalists at that position. 

The corruption consuming the regiment from within was unknown to most of its guardsmen as the seeds of treachery festered. Rinda, Rodin and the other corrupted Moebians would all survive due to their willingness to serve the Plague Lord. The Moebian 6th who would remain loyal and were stationed elsewhere saw Rinda's survival of Anchorpoint Numen's fall as a cruel irony. The twins also likely took part in the assault on Anchorpoint Jorholm where the Traitor Moebian 6th's commander, Captain Wolfer, led his troops in the open slaughter of any remaining Moebian 6th Loyalists.

After the Moebian 6th turned Traitor and fell to Chaos, the Karnak Twins joined suit in pledging themselves to Nurgle, always eager to offer sacrifices to Nurgle's cauldron and feast on the flesh of their enemies. Due to their ferocity, cunning, and strength, the Karnak Twins served as the most important lieutenants of Captain Wolfer, the leader of the Moebian 6th's operations in Hive Tertium. They were the attack hounds of Wolfer who did much of his dirty work for him, and were the de facto authority for the regiment in his absence. The two worked together under Wolfer, intending to ultimately conquer Hive Tertium and "liberate" the population from the chains of the Imperium. 

The twins proved to be highly effective combatants, killing countless squads of Loyalist troops and Inquisition Acolytes, butchering all they came across. They maintained control over an entire habzone of the hive city, the so-called "Torrent," overseeing the production and distribution of stimms blighted by Nurgle's Warp-spawned pathogens across the hive city. Eventually, Rodin Karnak established himself as the new "King of the Torrent District."

The two began hunting down the squads of Inquisitorial Acolytes deployed from the warband of the Ordo Hereticus Inquisitor Grendyl to combat the Moebian 6th and their allies among the Admonition Chaos Cultists. The Twins killed countless squads of these "Rejects." Whenever they faced more resistance than they could effectively counter, they would disappear behind a cloak of toxic smoke unleashed by a Blight Grenade and retreat from combat.

Orthus Offensive

After Explicator Orgustine Zola, the executive officer of Grendyl's warband of Acolytes deployed to Atoma Prime, learned that the Karnak Twins were the primary cult leaders in the Torrent District, she sent a strike team of Rejects to hunt them down and capture one or both for interrogation in an off-the-books mission she called the "Orthus Offensive." 

The Karnak Twins ambushed the Acolyte team at several points, but each time they were fended off. The Twins employed various tactics such as preparing ambushes with their Moebian 6th Regiment troops and laying toxic gas traps. Despite this, this strike team of Rejects kept coming, overcoming all obstacles. 

The Twins faced down the squad, eager to slaughter them like all the others, but despite their best efforts, the Karnak Twins both fell in combat to the forces of the Inquisition, largely because Interrogator Iven Rennick learned of the capture operation and intervened to try and stop it, castigating Zola for allowing her zealous hunt for Captain Wolfer to jeopardise their larger goals in Hive Tertium.

Resurrections

Unbeknownst to the Inquisition, who assumed that both twins had died during the Orthus Offensive, Rinda was left barely clinging to life. Rinda Karnak would be revived from the brink of death through intervention by the Cult of Admonition. Through the aid of a mysterious Chaos Cultist, a taxing surgery, much medicae, and the blessings of Nurgle, Rinda's revival was assisted by Urmal, an Admonition "tinkerer" who enhanced her medicae treatments by adding a special "gift" to the mix. The mysterious gift Urmal bestowed is expected to "ripen" with time.

As Rinda recovered from her grievous wounds, she reflected on her second chance at life and her desire to reap a bloody vengeance for the death of her brother Rodin, and she eventually fully embraced the religious tenets of the Admonition. Where before she had viewed the cultists as undisciplined, zealous fools little better than underhive rabble, she now adopted much of their philosophy and language, including their use of Admonition's Dark Tongue dialect. The mysterious cultist who had aided her promised that she would soon have her vengeance for she was now the "Herald of Admonition."

Rinda Karnak proceeded to kill hundreds of the Inquisition warband's Acolytes during this time and increasingly became a cause for concern among the warband's leaders, Zola in particular.

Later, Rinda Karnak, as the Herald of Admonition, would speak to Captain Wolfer as the Admonition prepared to undertake a Nurglish sorcerous ritual to resurrect Rodin Karnak within the Carnival district's Cathedrum of Messelina Vengeant, but this too was foiled by the appearance of the same strike team of Ordo Hereticus Acolytes who had defeated the Twins in the Torrent during the Orthus Offensive.

Appearance

The Karnak Twins dress similarly, with both wearing standard Astra Militarum Flak Armour decorated with various heretical emblems and sigils. Rinda Karnak bears the Mark of Chaos Undivided on her helmet, but has since become a fervent follower of Nurgle as the "Herald of Admonition." 

Rodin Karnak bore the Mark of Nurgle on his helmet, and thus worshipped the Plague Lord just like the rest of the Admonition cult.

Personality

Both Karnak Twins are sadists, a personality trait evident even before their corruption by Chaos, and they take great delight in the extreme suffering and gruesome death of anyone loyal to the Imperium. The two also delight in making life difficult for anyone they view as beneath them in status, a habit they also displayed when they were still spire nobles.

Through dialogue, it is heavily implied that the Karnak Twins also now partake in cannibalism, with both wishing to "tenderise" their opponents and put them in "the cauldron." The twins share a strong sibling bond with one another and are actually quite encouraging to each other. The two also sometimes seem to share a sibling rivalry, as Rodin comments on his annoyance with Rinda "playing with her food" before they enter combat and Rinda calling her brother weak if he should meet his end in combat.

Rodin Karnak was a belligerent brawler who liked to feed his own ego. He seemed to be a blunt opportunist who grasped at power and violence whenever convenient. For instance, Rodin fed his ego by claiming the former throne of the "King of the Torrent" when the Twins were controlling the Torrent District of Hive Tertium. Whether he did this in earnest or jest (or both) matters little, as all he wants is power, control and to feed his overweening pride.

While Rodin is just as much of a sadist as his sister, he may be slightly more compassionate than her. He does seem to care what people think of him, even if it's only in regards to maintaining their fear of him. While Rinda might browbeat or mock Rodin on occasion, he still cares for his sister in his own twisted way.

Rinda Karnak seems more keen and calculated in her approach. During the Nox Alpha campaign of the Fringe War at Anchorpoint Numen, Rinda cannibalised her fellow Moebian 6th lasmen in secret. Sometimes she shared her fresh meat with other Moebians. Whether her fellows knew it was actually Human flesh they were eating is unknown. 

Many of her victims were those in the regiment she had feuded with or were a subordinate she believed to have been slacking in their duties. Even when supplies ran low, Rinda always ate well and those she didn't favour with her gifts of flesh eventually starved or simply disappeared, themselves becoming Rinda and her allies' next meal.

Rinda appears to be far more self-assured than her brother and she has always viewed him as weaker than herself. When Rodin pretended to take up the Ragged King's throne in the Torrent District, she told him, "There is nothing you have that I can't take." Rinda may have had a habit of browbeating Rodin, if not generally keeping his ego in check when necessary. Rinda seemed to be more competent and decisive than her brother, though this may simply be that she was actually more self-assured.

Wargear

The Karnak Twins are incredibly durable combatants, able to take a tremendous beating before finally succumbing to their injuries. Their fortitude is further bolstered by the personal Refractor Field each has equipped. 

Rinda Karnak cleaves through her foes in melee using a Power Sword, quickly closing gaps to cut down her foes. Rodin Karnak prefers to stay at range and disintegrate foes with his Plasma Pistol, while using Nurglish Blight Grenades to sap the strength of any Loyalists in his path.
