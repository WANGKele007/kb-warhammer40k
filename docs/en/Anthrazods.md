The Anthrazods are a sturdy, if dim-witted, xenos species that was annexed to the T'au Empire during its First Sphere Expansion.

Due to the shortage of T'au that affected the empire at the end of this period, the Water Caste assigned the Anthrazods to the arduous toils of asteroid mining, which the Anthrazods proved very well suited for.
