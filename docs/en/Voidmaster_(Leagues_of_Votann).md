Voidmaster is the title given by the Leagues of Votann to their voidship captains. Kin voidmasters are skilled in every aspect of spacefaring and are often augmetically-enhanced to aid them in their duties.

Sources

Codex: Leagues of Votann (9th Edition), pg. 13
