Amelia Minervas, also spelled Aemelia Minervas, is a legatus of the Ultramar Defence Auxilia, the combined planetary defence forces of the Realm of Ultramar. Minervas is a well-respected veteran of the Ultramarines' mortal auxilia, who are far better trained than most PDFs and are equal in combat skill and discipline to the best Astra Militarum regiments. 

She was selected in the Era Indomitus by Captain Demetrian Titus, commander of the Ultramarines 2nd Company, to join his unique mixed Command Squad of mortals and Astartes known as the "Wardens of Ultramar". 

Minervas is expected to act as the 2nd Company command team's liaison to its legions of Human soldiery, who will be crucial assets in the Wardens of Ultramar's efforts to protect the borders of Ultramar and expand them to encompass the realm's pre-Horus Heresy size of five hundred worlds.

When the Ultramarines go to war inside their own realm, they are joined by the vast armies of the Ultramar Defence Auxilia -- a well-trained and well-equipped force of baseline Human soldiers who at times can stand a cut above even the regular regiments of the Astra Militarum. Aemelia Minervas commands the 30th Macragge Defence Auxilia -- a particularly renowned and respected regiment attached to Captain Titus's fleet -- and acts as the Wardens of Ultramar's liaison to their Human soldiery, leading its forces fearlessly from the front.

As a dedicated scion of Macragge, Aemelia can quote the Codex Astartes front to back, and holds it in regard matching the most pious of Ultramarines. Where others see it as a set of immovable rules, however, Legatus Minervas has built upon the work of generations of forebears and adapted many of its guidelines and principles for use in her Auxilia regiment, memorising them with perfect clarity and using them to great effect on the battlefield.
