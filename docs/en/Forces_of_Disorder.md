The Forces of Disorder was an uneasy, and very brief, alliance between a warband of World Eaters Chaos Space Marines led by the Chaos Lord Crull, known as the Blood Legion of Khorne, and the Orks serving Warboss Gorgutz 'Ead 'Unter. 

The two factions joined forces in order to repel an incursion of the Astra Militarum on the Ice World of Lorn V.

Infighting between the Orks and the World Eaters was common, only stopping in order to combat their shared enemy, the Imperial Guard. The alliance was dissolved when Gorgutz killed Crull during the final days of the war on Lorn V.
