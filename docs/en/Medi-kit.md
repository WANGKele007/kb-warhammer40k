Small, compact, and reliable, a medi-kit is essential gear for every Astra Militarum squad. Each has a simple diagnostic cogitator complete with bio-scanner and probes, along with chemicals, drugs, contraseptics, synthetic blood packs and other means to treat a variety of injuries and ailments. 

Advanced models, usually called "medi-packs," that can weigh as much as 3 kilograms include such additional items as tox wands, synth-skin applicators, and other aids usually available only to the wealthiest of the Imperial elite or to the Apothecaries of the Adeptus Astartes.
