A Spore Chimney is a Tyranid biomechanical technology that is used to pollute a prey planet's atmosphere and biosphere in preparation for digestion of its biosphere by releasing billions of microscopic spores which are absorbed by the local flora and fauna with devastating results. 

Imperial fire teams have learned that shooting these structures can cause a large, spontaneous release of spores into the air. 

Role

Spore Chimneys are Tyranid plant-analogue bioconstructs seeded onto a hive fleet's target world in the early stages of a Tyranid infestation by Mycetic Spores or early stage reconnaissance bioforms. After they mature within a matter of solar days, Spore Chimneys pump out billions of microscopic alien spores which not only pollute the atmosphere, altering it to match the climatic conditions preferred by Tyranid bioforms, but also alter the native flora and fauna. 

Local species of vegetation will gradually be replaced by highly aggressive xenos flora that grows from the released spores. As the process of consumption accelerates, the sky darkens from the sheer number of spores entering the atmosphere and the planet's temperature begins to rise as the spores consume biomatter. The surface is soon transformed into an environmental hothouse through an artificially-induced greenhouse effect, accelerating the process of digestion still further and optimising conditions for the next stage of consumption. 

Within solar days of the appearance of Spore Chimneys on an infested world hordes of Rippers will have consumed the vegetation mass and added it to the digestive enzymes of the Reclamation Pools, where all of the world's digested biomatter is broken down into its biochemical molecular components and ready to be collected by the Capillary Towers for transfer to the Hive Ships in low orbit.
