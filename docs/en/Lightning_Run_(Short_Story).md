Lightning Run by Peter McLean is a Warhammer 40,000 short story published in 2018 and collected in the anthologies Nexus & Other Stories and Warriors and Warlords. 

Synopsis

While the Astra Militarum defends holy ground, the Imperial Navy rains down the Emperor's fiery wrath from the skies. 

Pilot Salvatoria Grant has been tasked with transporting a venerable military general on a secret mission. But when the journey goes wrong, Grant finds herself in the greatest danger of her life.
