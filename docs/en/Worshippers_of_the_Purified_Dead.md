The Worshippers of the Purified Dead is one of several Cannibal Cults active on the Hive World of Avarax during the Recidious Campaign of the Fourth Tyrannic War in the Era Indomitus. 

It was formed during the food shortages that enveloped the hive cities of Avarax after it was invaded by Hive Fleet Leviathan and the Thousand Sons Traitor Legion. The Worshippers of the Purified Dead believe Human flesh is purified after the body dies and the cult consumes it to survive as an act of worship towards the Emperor. However, while the members of the cult eat the dead, they will also kill other Imperials in order to survive as well. 

Given that Avarax was under assault by the Great Devourer and the forces of Chaos, it is possible the cult was tied to either of them in some way.
