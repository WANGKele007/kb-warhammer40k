The Excrutia Armatus was a complex array of restraints and excruciation tools that was among the most recognisable symbols of the Sisters of Silence's Chamber of Judgement during the Great Crusade and the Horus Heresy. The sight of its gruesome implements displayed by the Silent Judges is a promise of retribution to the enemies of the Emperor, a slow and terrible end in the pain halls of the Silent Sisterhood. In the face of such a dire promise of retribution lesser foes are struck by terror and hesitate in the face of the Silent Sisterhood's onslaught.
