The Gnostari are a Prospect fleet of the Leagues of Votann that was misidentified by the Imperium as a different xenos species. During the Nachmund Rift War in the Era Indomitus between the Imperium and the forces of Chaos, the Gnostari used the opportunity provided by the conflict to raid Imperial mining outposts on the asteroids known as Giara's Necklace.
