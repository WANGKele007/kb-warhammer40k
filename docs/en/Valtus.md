Valtus is a Redemptor Dreadnought of the Ultramarines' 2nd Company. He fought during the Plague Wars against the Death Guard in the Realm of Ultramar and at the Battle of Demerium during the Recidious Campaign, notably killing a Thousand Sons Helbrute and casting down a Heldrake.

Warhammer 40,000: Space Marine II (Video Game), Campaign Mission 5: Dawn's Descent; Operation 2: Reliquary
