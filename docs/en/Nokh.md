Nôkh is a Hernkyn of the Greater Thurian League's Kindred of Vôrtun. He accompanied the Oathbands under the overall command of Kâhl Ûthar the Destined that was sent to the Kin World of Törg by her home Kindred during the Battle of Örgvayr to rescue its Cthonian Guild miners from an assault launched by the Heretic Astartes of the World Eaters and The Purge who had emerged out of Warp Storm Örgvayr after the birth of the Great Rift. 

Once the battle began, Nôkh's Hernkyn Pioneer bands, supported by light gunships and rugged Sagitaur ATVs, swept through the high passes of the Fumarok Range to attack the World Eaters from the rear. They launched swift hit-and-run strikes, using their pan spectral scanners to maintain a fix on their enemies amidst the toxic murk of Törg's surface. Goaded to action, many wrath-crazed Khornate Berserkers broke off their assault on Harvester Fortress Five and pursued Nôkh's band into the hazardous mountain passes, allowing the other Thurian Oathbands to rescue the Cthonians and their haul of valuable resources from the harvester fortress.

Despite losses, the Thurians ultimately succeeded in rescuing both the miners and much of their valuable harvested raw materials.
