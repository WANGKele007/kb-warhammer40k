The Bloody Crescent are a notorious warband of Chaos Space Marines. In 357.M41, they fought the Space Wolves Great Company under Wolf Lord Asvald Stormwrack. During this particular battle, a young Logan Grimnar saved Asvald's life from one of the warband's Helbrutes.
