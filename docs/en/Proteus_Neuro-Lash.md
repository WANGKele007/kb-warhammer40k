The Proteus Neuro-Lash was a type of non-lethal melee weapon used by the psyker-hunters of the Sisters of Silence. Fashioned in the shape of baroque multi-headed flails or ornate three-sectioned staves, Proteus Neuro-Lashes are relics of the Age of Strife used by the most skilled warriors among the ranks of the Silent Sisterhood to subdue large numbers of assailants at once. 

The neural prongs that adorn the weapons generate a phase-conducted electrical shock field which works to overload a victim's nervous system with bursts of intense agony, so that even the slightest touch leaves them helpless. The weapon was particularly useful for subduing hostile psykers without killing them so that they might be taken for the Great Tithe.
