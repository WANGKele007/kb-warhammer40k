A shimmershield is an advanced Craftworld Aeldari force field generator often wielded as a defensive technology against enemy melee attacks by elite Asuryani warriors. The device is usually worn on the off-weapon arm and its protective energy field is activated once the user enters melee combat.

Shimmershields are primarily used by the Exarchs of the Dire Avenger and Shining Spears Aspect Warriors, but Shimmershields have also been reported to be wielded by Corsair Princes, Voidstorm Squads and Void Dreamers among the myriad fractious crews of the Aeldari Corsairs.
