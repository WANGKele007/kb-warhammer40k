A Krakstorm Grenade Launcher is a type of Imperial Grenade Launcher usually wielded by the Primaris Space Marines. 

It fires a volley of three Krak Grenades at a time upon the enemy. 

It is deployed for use as an anti-armour or anti-fortification weapon by Repulsor grav tanks.
