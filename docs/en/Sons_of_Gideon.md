The Sons of Gideon were a former Loyalist Space Marine Chapter of unknown origin and Founding who surrendered to madness and embraced heresy, becoming a Renegade Chapter.

The Sons of Gideon, alongside the Marines Vigilant, are one of the two Chapters that were exterminated by the Crimson Fists on the orders of the Inquisition and the Adeptus Terra.
