A Knight Abyssal was a senior rank within the order of the Sisters of Silence. The mightiest warriors, Daemon Hunters, and psyker executioners of their order, drawn from the ranks of the Oblivion Knights, each is a vastly experienced fighter and is personally responsible for the execution of some of the most dangerous psykers known to the Imperium, individuals of such terrible power and potency that they could have proven a threat to the very existence of Mankind's empire. 

Knights Abyssal are counted among the most potent psychic Nulls and the mere proximity to the aura of one is enough to cause crippling weakness and terror to psykers, and in some cases even a catastrophic meltdown of the psyker's physical body and an agonising death. In truth, they will even sow fear and disgust in the hearts and minds of those who are without the psyker's gift too, no matter how strong-willed they may be. Such is the curse of those who bear the Pariah Gene.

The Knights Abyssal go to war in the strongest Artificer Armour and Voidscale Cloaks of their order and are armed with the finest weapons which include Charnabal Sabres, a Paragon Blade, or a Proteus Neuro-Lash alongside a Hand Flamer, Needle Pistol, or Plasma Pistol. With these instruments they deliver the Emperor's judgement upon those psykers unworthy of inclusion in the Great Tithe or deemed too dangerous to exist.
