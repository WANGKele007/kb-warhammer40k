The Remnants are a Loyalist Space Marine Chapter and a successor of the Blood Angels of an unknown Founding.

Chapter Appearance

Chapter Colours

The Remnants are known to use a purple and gold quartered colour scheme for their power armour. The Iron Halos worn by the elite of the Chapter bear an icon of a winged Red Grail.

Chapter Badge

The Remnants' Chapter badge is not listed in current Imperial records.

Game History

The Remnants were created from a kit bash by Games Workshop employee John Bracken so that they could be displayed in White Dwarf 395.
