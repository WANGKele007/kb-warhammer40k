The Nostraman Chainglaive was a type of Chain Weapon used by the Heretic Astartes of the Night Lords Legion that resembled a great pole arm with a chainsword blade at its tip crafted in the Nostraman style.

Notable Nostraman Chainglaives

Night's Whisper

Night's Whisper is a master-crafted Nostraman Chainglaive that is one of several weapons the Night Haunter had discarded in his gore-spattered sanctum. First Captain Jago Sevatarion, commander of the Atramentar Terminators of the Legion's 1st Company, appropriated it for his final battles against the Dark Angels during the Thramas Crusade, as their armour had proved resistant to the teeth of his old chainglaive. 

In form Night's Whisper was an elegant Nostraman Chainglaive of archaic make, its archeotech motor tuned to a sibilant purr rather than the discordant roar of more commonplace weapons of the type.
