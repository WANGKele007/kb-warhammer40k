A Command Squad is an elite Space Marine formation of the 41st Millennium that hails back to the Legion Command Squads from the bygone era of the Legiones Astartes of the Great Crusade and the Horus Heresy eras. 

Command Squads are made up of highly disciplined and worthy Veteran Space Marines and Primaris Space Marines who often accompany Chapter Masters, Space Marine captains and other important officers and specialists, serving both as bodyguards and Standard Bearers. 

The Codex Astartes states that the Chapter Master of a Space Marine Chapter should make proper provision for the protection of important heroes on the battlefield, recognising as it does the importance of the Chapter's officers, Standard Bearers, Apothecaries, Librarians and other specialists. Most Codex-compliant Chapters therefore organise Command Squads to act in the role of bodyguard to these important warriors.

Command Squads accompany high-ranking Space Marine officers on the field of battle. The exact nature and title of a Command Squad's members can vary greatly, depending on a Chapter's organisation and the personality of a company's captain. The most common specialists to be part of a Command Squad alongside several Veteran Marines in the period before the Era Indomitus were the Company Champion, Apothecary and Company Standard Bearer, and these could be found in almost all Command Squads of that era. 

Following Primarch Roboute Guilliman's reforms to the Codex Astartes following his resurrection and the integration of the Primaris Space Marines into the Adeptus Astartes in the Era Indomitus, the Command Squad was altered to serve primarily as an honour guard for Chapter Masters and company captains and was comprised of several Veterans, a Veteran sergeant to lead the squad, the company Ancient and the Company Champion.

Service in a Command Squad undoubtedly speeds promotion within the Chapter -- most company captains fill their retinues with Veterans in whom the bloody skills of combat are matched by tactical and strategic brilliance. This being the case, Space Marine captains and their Command Squads can invariably be found in the thickest fighting, battling in the midst of the most gruelling of wars against the most terrible of foes.

Some Chapters maintain other titles and positions in their Command Squads in accordance with their traditions, such as the "Foeseekers" of the Omega Marines, the "Prognosticars" of the Silver Skulls, the "Terrorblades" of the Death Spectres and the "Pyre Wardens" of the Fire Lords.

History

The Space Marine Command Squad is the successor of the Legion Command Squad, a tactical formation of the ancient Space Marine Legions used during the Great Crusade and Horus Heresy eras in the late 30th and early 31st Millennia. 

In the ancient Legiones Astartes it was a common sight to see senior Legiones Astartes commanders and champions accompanied in battle by a squad of elite, Veteran warriors that served both as a bodyguard for their commander and were entrusted to carry one of the Legion's great standards. On the battlefield this made them a rallying point for the Legion's troops. These chosen Space Marines had access to the finest wargear available to the Legion's rank-and-file and embodied the Legion's honour in war.

By the 41st Millennium, Command Squads of the Adeptus Astartes were a common formation of elite specialists who accompanied high-ranking Space Marine officers on the field of battle. The exact nature and title of a Command Squad's members can vary, depending on a Chapter's organisation and the personality of a company's captain. 

The Codex Astartes states that the Chapter Master of a Space Marine Chapter should make proper provision for the protection of important heroes on the battlefield, recognising as it does the importance of the Chapter's officers and other specialists. Most Codex-compliant Chapters therefore organise Command Squads to act in the role of bodyguard to these important warriors.

The Command Squads are not a codified part of the company organisation of the Chapter; rather they are Codex Astartes-approved, yet still ad hoc units. As such they can vary in size and composition but they rarely comprise more than ten Space Marines and normally consist of between 5 and 10 Astartes. In the Battle and Reserve Companies of a Chapter these Astartes are always Veterans drawn from the ranks of the Company's battleline, close support and fire support squads who have not yet been promoted to the elite 1st Company. The number of Astartes in a Command Squad will vary according to the availability of such Veterans and to the tactical situations the Chapter encounters, but in the Era Indomitus averages about 7-10 Space Marines, including Veterans, a Veteran sergeant, the company Ancient, the Company Champion and the officer they serve.

The Command Squad is more flexible than most of the other squad types of the Space Marines because up to two of the Veteran Astartes that comprise it may be designated as special weapons troopers or as heavy weapon troopers. This is in part due to the availability of specifically-trained Astartes and in part due to differing tactical needs. The mix of supreme individual warriors and highly experienced Veteran Space Marine battle-brothers makes Command Squads a particularly powerful fighting force and many a battle has been won by their timely intervention or clever deployment by a Space Marine Force Commander.

The sergeant of a Command Squad may display his back banner. The banner displays the Chapter badge, the sergeant's red skull rank badge and is trimmed in the company colour (if the Chapter follows the Codex Astartes's standard suggested iconography).

Era Indomitus Command Squad Organisation

After the resurrection of Primarch Roboute Guilliman at the start of the Era Indomitus, he reformed the tactical prescriptions of the Codex Astartes to better match the demands of warfare in the era of the Great Rift as well as to take account of the integration of the Primaris Space Marines and their new, more technologically advanced wargear and vehicles, into the Adeptus Astartes' order of battle. One such change was to alter the nature of the Command Squad into a dedicated honour guard for Chapter Masters and company captains made up of each Chapter's recognised heroes.
