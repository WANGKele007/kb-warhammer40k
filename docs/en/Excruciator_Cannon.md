An Excruciator Cannon is a potent Warp-based weapon used by the Chaos Daemon Engines known as Venomcrawlers.

Upon locking onto their target the Venomcrawlers emit an earsplitting screech. Pent-up Warp energy is channelled into their Excruciator Cannons, coalescing into hails of solid shot that rip through entire ranks of armoured foes with ease.

Codex Heretic Astartes - Chaos Space Marines (8th Edition) (Revised Codex), pp. 79, 151
