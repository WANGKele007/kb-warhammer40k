A Wayfarer-class Station is a type of Imperial void station designed to operate on the frontiers of Imperial-controlled space. Some, having been cut-off from regular Imperial communication and commerce due to their isolated locations, have evolved beyond their original specifications into independent trading or refuelling depots for merchant vessels, Rogue Traders and the odd Navis Imperialis warship.

Dimensions

Dimensions: 5 kilometres in diameter, approx.

Mass: 22.1 megatons, approx.

Crew Complement: 10,000 crew, 80,000-100,000 inhabitants (many of whom are also involved in crew activities), approx.
