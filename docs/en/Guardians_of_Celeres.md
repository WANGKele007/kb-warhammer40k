The Guardians of Celeres is a Loyalist Codex Astartes-compliant Space Marine Chapter of unknown Founding and origin. Nothing else is currently known about them in Imperial records.

Chapter Appearance

Chapter Colours

The Guardians of Celeres primarily wear dark green Power Armour with the exception of the shoulder plates which are white. The Aquila or Imperialis on the chest plate is silver. The black squad specialty symbol -- Tactical, Assault, Devastator, or Veteran -- is located on the right shoulder plate. A white Roman numeral is centred on the squad specialty symbol, indicating squad number. The colour of the shoulder plate trim indicates company number in accordance to the Codex Astartes -- i.e. White (1st Company), Yellow (2nd Company), Red (3rd Company), etc.

Chapter Badge

The Guardians of Celeres' Chapter badge is a large black skull, centred between a pair of Greek letters -- "Sigma" -- which are addorsed (facing away from one another), on a field of white.
