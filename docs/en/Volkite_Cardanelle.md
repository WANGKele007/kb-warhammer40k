A Volkite Cardanelle was a large and powerful directed energy Volkite Weapon that was developed for use as a main turret armament on the ancient Kratos Heavy Assault Tank for the Space Marine Legions. It was employed primarily as an anti-infantry weapon. Its potent heat ray could melt the flesh from large formations of enemy foot soldiers.
