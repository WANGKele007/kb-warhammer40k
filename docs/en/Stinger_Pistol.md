The Stinger Pistol is a Drukhari anti-personnel weapon used primarily by the Haemonculi and their Wrack servants. A Stinger Pistol is a lightweight sidearm characterised by a long, syringe-like barrel and a venom reservoir that holds searingly potent toxins. When fired it ejects a long sliver of hollow glass that can pierce a foe's skin and introduce the toxins straight into his bloodstream, invariably with horrific results.
