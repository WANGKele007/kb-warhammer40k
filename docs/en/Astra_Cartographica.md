The Astra Cartographica, also called the Departmento Cartographicae, is a sub-agency of the Imperial Adeptus Administratum which is dedicated to mapping the galaxy. Many Rogue Traders are employed by the organisation to aid it in gathering data on new regions of the galaxy or to update existing data on already explored regions. 

The esoteric cartomancers of the Navis Nobilite sometimes aid the officials of the Astra Cartographica in creating cartographs of stable Warp routes. 

Most interstellar maps used in the Imperium are created by the Astra Cartographica, which is always seeking new information about changing galactic conditions to update its maps.
