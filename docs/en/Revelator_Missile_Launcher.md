The Revelator Missile Launcher is a Titan-grade Missile Launcher deployed on the upper chassis of the Warmaster-class Heavy Battle Titan. The massive Revelator Missiles it unleashes are used as anti-Titan, anti-vehicle and anti-fortification weapons due to their massive explosive punch. The Revelator Missile Launcher is intended to provide the Titan with another source of long-ranged firepower.
