Sa'ra'am, also called "The Daemon Beneath," "The Knife's Edge," and the "Laughter of War," is a Daemon of Chaos Undivided who was bound to the core of the Forge World of Sarum, which had long served as the primary supplier of wargear to the World Eaters Legion during the Great Crusade and Horus Heresy. 

During an interaction with Primarch Perturabo and a contingent of his Iron Warriors, the Daemon created the first Chaos Space Marine Obliterator.

History

Sa'ra'am claimed to have come into existence at the moment the first weapon made by a mortal to take a life was forged. The Daemon only grew stronger in the millennia that followed as the weapons created by mortals had developed, spread and become ever more destructive. Being confined to the heart of the Forge World was a fate the Daemon secretly relished, as this meant it was free from the direct control of the Chaos Gods and no longer had to serve their every whim.

During the Horus Heresy, Primarch Perturabo and a contingent of his Iron Warriors came to Sarum seeking the location of his brother Angron, as he knew the World Eaters had long used Sarum as a primary source of their supply. Perturabo had been tasked by the Warmaster Horus with bringing Angron and the World Eaters to the Traitor Legions' muster on Ullanor in preparation for the start of the Siege of Terra. 

Perturabo threatened to free the Daemon if it did not reveal the whereabouts of Angron. The Daemon acquiesced and revealed that Angron could be found on the world of Deluge. The Daemon then possessed the Iron Warrior Volk who was accompanying Perturabo. Volk was the former commander of the 786th Grand Flight, a Lightning Crow pilot who had most of his body rebuilt and housed within a suit of modified Tactical Dreadnought Armour after being shot down in an engagement. 

The Daemon's touch transformed Volk into the first Obliterator. This new amalgamation of Daemon and Heretic Astartes left the planet with Perturabo. Volk would not be the last of his kind.
