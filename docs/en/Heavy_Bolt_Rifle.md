The Heavy Bolt Rifle is a variant of the Mark II Cawl Pattern Bolt Rifle primarily used by Heavy Intercessors. Its elongated barrel gives it a greater range than the standard Bolt Rifle.

Codex: Space Marines (9th Edition), pg. 141
