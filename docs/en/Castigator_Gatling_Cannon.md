The Castigator Gatling Cannon is a powerful, rotary ballistic weapon deployed as a hull-mounted weapon on the Astra Militarum's Rogal Dorn Battle Tank.

The Castigator Gatling Cannon is the perfect anti-infantry weapon, capable of scything down whole squads of foot soldiers in a single burst.
