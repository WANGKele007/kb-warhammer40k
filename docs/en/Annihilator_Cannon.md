The Annihilator Cannon was a type of starship-borne Vortex Weapon used by the Dark Angels Legion during the Horus Heresy.

These cannons were massive armaments, whose size could run the entire length of an Imperial warship, with kilometre-long vanes like the vertebrae of a saurian extruding from its flanks. The technology of the Annihilator Cannon was known to draw upon the same ancient Terran technical knowledge as the Dark Angels Legion's void munitions, though rumours claimed that the weapon was an artefact of the Unification Wars that had been developed by the Emperor in His laboratories.

When preparing to fire, energy would flare along the length of a vessel outfitted with an Annihilation Cannon, arcs of purple and blue energy leaping from one vane to the next, moving from the bow to the stern in rapid succession. A few solar seconds later another surge of lightning would ripple down the length of the starship, with the pause between surges becoming shorter every few solar seconds. Pulses of cerulean fire enveloped the warship almost constantly. 

While in orbit above a planetary battlefield, it would seem as though a violet star had sprung into life in the skies above. In space, as the weapon prepared to fire, the host vessel would be awash with swirling energy, the void around it buckling and twisting like a warped mirror, the flare of power casting impossible shadows against the vacuum of space itself. When the bolt was finally discharged, in the instant that followed a rift in space extending into the Immaterium would expand around the target, taking the form of a lightning-wreathed oval of discordant energy that filled the space around the enemy, its edge crackling, and a screech like a god's whetstone would split the air, causing any speakers or vox-units in the area to emit their own piercing feedback wails in reply. 

Millions of tonnes of planetary material would sparkle in blue suspension around the growing rift, every grain of rock and mote of ash shimmering with a tiny arc of Warp energy. In the depths of this rift shapes would move almost unseen -- the broadest sweep of a Daemonic face or raking claw. They appeared more like afterimages, rendered in three dimensions against the stretched skin of reality, pressing to pass through a veil only made visible by their presence. An instant later the Warp field would collapse like a storm bubbling down into a single drop of rain. Anything caught within its rift would disappear with a boom that eclipsed all other sounds.

Annihilator Cannons could also be adjusted for precision strikes, such as during the Battle of Zepath, where the fire from the cannon mounted aboard the Dreadwing flagship Intolerant was adjusted to create a precise corridor of fire no more than one kilometre wide, and two kilometres long, to support the Dreadwing's advance upon the Traitor stronghold held by the World Eaters and Word Bearers Legions.

The only known Annihilator Cannon was mounted on the Dreadwing vessel Intolerant, which was outfitted with many other advanced technologies dating from the Age of Strife.

A variant of this technology is used on the anti-ship version of the Adeptus Astartes' Thunderhawk gunship known as a Thunderhawk Annihilator.
