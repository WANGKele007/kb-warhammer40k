A servo-automata is a strange, avian Imperial Robot created and used by the Raven Guard Chapter of the Adeptus Astartes as surveillance devices.

The Raven Guard's servo-automata normally reside in the towers of the Raven Guard's fortress-monastery of Ravenspire alongside vat-grown organic avians and several always attend to Master of Shadows Aethon Shaan. These constructs even follow him into battle, but will find hidden perches or soar unseen overhead while he is engaged in combat. 

These avian servo-automata serve the Chapter in a non-combat role as combat surveillance mechanisms and their constant presence also reassures the Chapter's battle-brothers. These automatons remind the Raven Guard that, wherever the Chapter wages war, the gaze of their lost primarch, Corvus Corax, is still upon them.
