Gudrun is the former capital world of the Helican Sub-sector of the Scarus Sector in the Segmentum Obscurus and had been a home to Humanity for 3,500 standard years by the 41st Millennium.

Gudrun is ruled by a feudal system of noble patronage including such powerful noble houses as the Glaws, the Pariti and the Froigre, whose power extendeds beyond Gudrun into neighbouring star systems. Gudrun is the recruiting ground for the Gudrunite Rifles regiments of the Astra Militarum. A founding of a new regiment is always a cause for a planet-wide celebration.

History

Once the capital world of the Helican Sub-sector, in 241.M41 one of the preeminent noble houses on Gudrun -- House Glaw -- was found to be pervaded by a Chaos Cult.

After a successful Imperial Crusade launched against the Heretics of the world by Battlefleet Scarus and the destruction of House Glaw, Gudrun lost the honour of being the capital world of the sub-sector to Thracian Primaris due to the damage suffered to its reputation for loyalty to the Emperor.

Before its corruption, Gudrun was a relatively idyllic world with stately manors controlling vast estates of rolling green hills studded with small villages. Its culture was not dissimilar to ancient Terran ideas of "Merry Olde England." For a time it was also the home and headquarters of the Inquisitor Gregor Eisenhorn.

Sometime after the Great Rift's creation in the Era Indomitus, Gudrun was invaded by the forces of Chaos and the Imperium now fights to save the world from their corruption. Gudrun was one of the Imperial worlds that the Brazen Claws Chapter helped to defend during this period.

Canon Conflict

White Dwarf 287 (UK) claimed that Gudrun was located in the Thracian Primaris System. But the Battlefleet Gothic: Armada Rulebook, White Dwarf 291 (UK) and the 2003 Eye of Terror Worldwide event all placed the world within the Scarus Sector, but outside that system.
