The Lathe-class Monitor-cruiser is a light cruiser of the Basilikon Astra, the fleet of the Adeptus Mechanicus. 

It is used by the Priesthood of Mars more for exploration than combat as it excels at long voyages and can operate for solar decades without outside assistance.

History

The design was recovered from the data-vaults of the Forge World of Lathe-Het in the Lathe System during the Angevin Crusade to claim the Calyx Expanse for the Imperium and was produced for centuries afterwards by the Mechanicus of the Calixis Sector.

Armament

Lathe-class Monitor-cruisers are bristling with many different starship-grade weapon types on its port, starboard, prow and dorsal sections.

Specifications

Dimensions - 4.2 kilometres long, 0.5 kilometres abeam approx.
Mass - 20.9 megatonnes
Crew - 35,000 + 25,000 servitors approx.
Acceleration - 6 gravities maximum sustainable acceleration
