Saint Argenta was a saint of the Imperial Cult venerated by the Imperial faithful in the Calixis Sector and the neighbouring Koronus Expanse.

Saint Argenta's hagiography states that, when she was a young girl, her home planet was invaded by the forces of the Ruinous Powers. A mysterious silver star fell from the sky into her hands, whose light held back the corrupting powers of Chaos. Argenta inspired her people to rise up against the invaders in the name of the Emperor, freeing her world and beginning a counterattack to liberate the sector before the Imperium could marshal a reaction force. 

Her followers eventually evolved into a full Imperial Crusade, though it was cut short when Saint Argenta's voidship was shot down somewhere in the Koronus Expanse; her final position was lost to Imperial records, and the exact nature of the silver star that came to her remains a mystery. 

Argenta is venerated by the Adepta Sororitas' Order of Our Martyred Lady, which had agents searching the Koronus Expanse for her final resting place when the sudden opening of the Great Rift severed the region from the rest of the Imperium -- among these sisters of the Adepta Soroitas was the saint's namesake Sister Argenta, a Sister of Battle who had been seconded to the Orders Pronatus and had taken passage with the Rogue Trader Theodora von Valancius of House von Valancius.

Sister Argenta's name was chosen in honour of the saint at the Schola Progenium where she was raised as an orphan. It was a replacement for the birth name given to her by her parents who were Astra Militarum officers killed in the line of duty.
