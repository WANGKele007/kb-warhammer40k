The Legio Ferocastrum ("The Holdfasts") is a Loyalist Titan Legion of the Collegia Titanica, one of the military arms of the Adeptus Mechanicus. The Legio is based on the Forge World of Sigma-Ulstari, the only Imperial Warden Planet which is located in the Cordon Impenetra in the Octarius Sector, bordering the Ork Empire of Octarius.

Legion History

Notable Campaigns

Octarius War (Unknown Date.M42) - During the invasion of the Pankallis Sub-sector by the Orks of the Octarius Empire and Hive Fleet Leviathan in the Octarius War, the Legio Ferocastrum helped to defend its home Forge World of Sigma-Ulstari from the innumerable assaults of the Greenskins and Tyranids.

Notable Personnel

Princeps Majoris Batiator Akropolit — At the time of the Octarius War, Batiator Akropolit was a princeps majoris of the Legio Ferocastrum.
