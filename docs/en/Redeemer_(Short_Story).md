Redeemer by Guy Haley is a Warhammer 40,000 short story published in 2019 and collected in the anthologies Nexus & Other Stories and The Hammer & The Eagle: Icons of Warhammer. 

Synopsis

The Blood Angels have long been the most valiant defenders of the Imperium. Yet for all their selfless sacrifice, they suffer from a fatal flaw: the Black Rage. These Space Marines must constantly strive to resist this darkness, lest the madness consume them and monsters be made of men. 

In Redeemer, High Chaplain Astorath travels to the world of Asque, where a brother of the Blood has succumbed to the Black Rage. It falls to the Redeemer of the Lost to hunt down the benighted warrior, and deliver the peace that only death can bring.
