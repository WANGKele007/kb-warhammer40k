The Legio Frostreaver is a Traitor Titan Legion of the Dark Mechanicum that repudiated its oaths to the Emperor of Mankind and followed the Warmaster Horus into the service of the Ruinous Powers during the Horus Heresy.

Legion History

Notable Campaigns

Assault on the Crythe Cluster (Unknown Date.M41) - The Legio Frostreaver participated in an assault of the Black Legion under the command of Abaddon the Despoiler and elements from several Night Lords warbands on the Crythe Cluster. Over two thousand Astartes and more than a million Human soldiers marched at the behest of the Despoiler to conquer these worlds and to create a beachhead on a Forge World of the Adeptus Mechanicus able to outfit and service Titans. The forces of Chaos overwhelmed the orbital defences of several worlds in the cluster but their assault on Crythe Prime was met with an attack from an Imperial fleet led by several companies of the Blood Angels, Flesh Tearers and Sons of Guilliman Chapters.   

Legion Organisation

The Legio Frostreaver numbered a dozen Titans of various classes prior to the campaign in the Crythe Cluster. It is unknown whether any were lost during this campaign.
