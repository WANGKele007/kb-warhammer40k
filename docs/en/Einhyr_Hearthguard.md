Einhyr Hearthguard are the elite assault infantry of the Leagues of Votann. Clad in formidable exo-armour and equipped with a fearsome array of ranged and melee weaponry, Einhyr Hearthguard are a force to be reckoned with.

Whether forming a bodyguard for their Oathband's commanding heroes, striking deep into the heart of enemy territory, or serving as a potent voidship boarding party, the Einhyr are unstoppable on the attack and immovable in defence.

Role

When it comes to protecting their all-important Ancestor Cores, the Leagues of Votann demand no less than the very best weapons and armour around. The honoured warriors who wield them are true elites, wading into battle clad in servo-assisted exo-armour, carrying a name that commands respect from all Kin -- the Einhyr Hearthguard.

Selected from the most accomplished fighters in a Kin hold, such as the theyns, the Einhyr Hearthguard have many roles -- from bodyguards to deadly first-strike troops. Each Hearthguard has proved themselves over many years of front-line combat, ascending purely by the virtue of their deeds, just as the staunch meritocracy of the Leagues requires.

Theyns who truly excel at war may be selected to become members of the elite Einhyr formations, or else to ascend to the rank of "kâhl." Treading one of these paths does not preclude following the other at a later date, and some Kin have even belonged to both elite warrior circles at once. Most commonly, however, the kâhls stand apart as war leaders and generals, while the warriors of the Einhyr fulfil tactical roles as elite strike troops, bodyguards, voidship boarding parties and the like. 

Like Hearthkyn Warriors, Einhyr Hearthguard are dependable fighters, but their experience and inch-thick battle-plate take the Kin's natural durability to a whole new level. Crack units of Einhyr Hearthguard can be trusted to hold the line and inspire their fellows to stand firm even in the face of untold horrors.

Each Einhyr is equipped with a EtaCarn Plasma Gun and a shoulder-mounted Exo-armour Grenade Launcher, laying down a solid curtain of fire as they advance. Once the Einhyr join the melee, they are just as formidable, lashing out with devastating Concussion Gauntlets. 

These marvels use ancient technologies to increase the mass of a warrior's fist as they swing, turning an already burly uppercut into an earth-shattering blow that can lift a hulking Ork right off their feet. 

Those who prefer the deftness of a sword over the raw power of their Concussion Gauntlets can fit a thrumming Plasma Blade Gauntlet to their wrist, perfect for slicing and dicing the galaxy's less damage-resistant dangers.

The exo-armour of the Einhyr Hearthguard is made from ultra-dense materials attached to a complex, servo-assisted frame, which allows the wearer to shrug off strikes even from anti-tank weaponry while retaining full freedom of movement. The armour can even auto-stabilise their aim while they return fire. 

Every squad of Einhyr Hearthguard is led by a veteran warrior who bears the title of "hesyr" and is outfitted with a slightly different wargear loadout from the "standard" Einhyr.

Trivia

The name "Einhyr" is a fictional cognate to the einherjar of Norse mythology, which means literally "army of one" or "those who fight alone" in Old Norse. The einherjar are those who have died in battle and are brought to Odin's hall of Valhalla in Asgard by valkyries. 

In Valhalla, the einherjar eat their fill of the nightly-resurrecting beast Sæhrímnir, and valkyries bring them mead. This usage would be in keeping with the use of Norse themes for much of the culture of the Leagues of Votann.
