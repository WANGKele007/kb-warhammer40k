The Red Widow is a powerful Daemon of Chaos encountered by the Imperial Fists during their assault on the Iron Warriors' Fortress World of Malodrax after Captain Darnath Lysander escaped from a thousand-standard-year-long captivity on the world and returned with reinforcements from his Chapter to put an end to the Traitors. 

The Red Widow lived among the coral-reef like growth that surrounded Malodrax in orbit, and attacked the crews of voidships wrecked on the reefs. The Red Widow had the form of an elongated and emaciated Human woman with lank hair and sharp talons, wearing a dress of rags. Her face was a black void; gazing directly into it exposed the viewer to the baleful energies of the Warp, temporarily incapacitating them. When attacking voidships, the Red Widow was accompanied by a cadre of weaker Daemons known as "Grey Hungers."

The Daemons under the Red Widow's command assaulted the Imperial Fists warship Breaker of Darkness after it arrived at Malodrax and their assault injured an Imperial Fists Space Marine and many of the vessel's crewmen, but was ultimately defeated and banished to the Warp by the actions of Lysander and Brother Halaestus.
