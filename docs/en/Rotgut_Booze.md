Rotgut Booze is a catchall term (especially among interstellar travellers) for the fermented liquid and more basic, cheaper brews of alcohol compared to the relatively more expensive Amasec. 

Indeed, alcohol comes in many shapes and sizes throughout the Imperium, and most human cultures are noted for producing at least one kind of barely digestible rotgut.
