The Formidyre System was an Imperial star system located in the Bastior War Zone of the Segmentum Pacificus of the galactic west that played an early and important role in the Fourth Tyrannic War unleashed by the multiple tendrils of the Tyranid Hive Fleet Leviathan coming up from below the galactic core in the Era Indomitus. 

The system was home to both the well-populated Hive World of Oghram and Sanctum, the Chapter planet of the White Templars Chapter of the Adeptus Astartes.

The system was invaded by the Tyranids of Hive Fleet Leviathan who successfully consumed Oghram and its billions of people despite the valiant efforts of the Space Marines to defend the world from the Great Devourer. The Tyranids next turned to an assault upon Sanctum, which was defended by the White Templars, the Imperial Fists and their mobile fortress-monastery the Phalanx, detachments from several other Space Marine Chapters, a vast Navis Imperialis battlefleet and the Astra Militarum forces under the command of Lord Commander Solar Arcadian Leontus.

System Worlds

The Formidyre System is comprised of seven inhabited Imperial worlds, which orbit from nearest to farthest from the system's primary of Formidyre, a blue giant star, as listed below:

Sanctum (White Templars Chapter planet) - During the Fourth Tyrannic War, the White Templars' own Chapter planet of Sanctum came under assault by Hive Fleet Grendyllus, a tendril of the larger Hive Fleet Leviathan. With the aid of Lord Commander Solar Arcadian Leontus and the forces of the Astra Militarum he commanded who were using Sanctum as base to face the Great Devourer in the galactic west, the White Templars were able to defeat the assault of the Tyranids even as it crashed against the walls of their fortress-monastery Holdfast. The White Templars' Chapter Master Kruxor Stavro served as the co-commander of the Imperial forces tasked with defending Holdfast. The other was Lord Solar Leontus, who acted as the heart and mind of the fortress-monastery's defence. Stavro served as the defence's blade-wielding fist and led each punishing counter-attack that drove the invading Tyranids back from Holdfast and ensured any breaches the xenos made in the fortress-monastery's walls were sealed. The Imperial Fists had also arrived in orbit of Sanctum in their great mobile fortress-monastery Phalanx to aid the defence of the White Templars homeworld along side a great battlefleet of the Navis Imperialis intended to hold the xenos enemy at bay for as long as possible. 

Dornwall (Asteroid Field) - The Dornwall, named after Primarch Rogal Dorn who the White Templars once believed mistakenly to be their primarch, was located in orbit of Sanctum and served as the Chapter planet's first line of defence from void assault. It was defended by servitor-manned orbital gun-shrines and outer defence forts placed amid the orbital debris.

Krematos (Mortuary World) - Krematos was a Mortuary World where large areas of the planet's surface were given over to care for the remains of the honoured Imperial dead. It was administered by the Ecclesiarchy just like Shrine Worlds.

Lembyq (Laboratory World) - Lembyq served as a "Laboratory World", better known as a Research Station, under the control of the tech-priests of the Adeptus Mechanicus.

Oghram (Dead World (Currently)/Hive World (Formerly)) - Oghram is the outermost planet of the Formidyre System. It was in a pivotal position in the path of the oncoming swarm -- its defiance could have become a legendary note of hope, like noble Macragge during the First Tyrannic War, or it could end as just the latest world stripped lifeless by the insatiable hunger of the Tyranids. Home to countless billions of Imperial citizens, this rich, bustling hub of biomass had caught the notice of the Tyranids, and soon suffered beneath a barrage of alien incursions. For vengeance, and to cleanse the world of xenos taint, the Adeptus Astartes launched a vicious counter-offensive. The fate of the world came to rest on a knife-edge. In the first phase of the Tyranid assault, Tyranid spores rained down upon the planet's surface. The chittering hordes of aliens devoured all in their path -- the single-minded will of the Great Devourer made manifest. Even at this early stage, little hope remained for the survival of the world and its people. Yet despite the valiant defence of the Space Marines, their efforts came to nought, and the world ultimately fell to the Leviathan, its people and all of its remaining biomass consumed by the hive fleet until all that remained was barren, lifeless rock. Despite its status as a Hive World, Oghram was still inhabited before the destruction of its biosphere by some strains of Terran wildlife, including mammals like foxes, badgers and pumas, and bird species like the heron.

==Notable System Locations== 
Dust Nebula - An extensive nebula comprised of interstellar dust swept through the orbits of Lembyq and Phraktam. Within it lies hidden the Beacon Station of Vanmyre's Eyrie. Space Marine void squadrons from several Chapters wage hit-and-run war through the Dust Nebula of Formidyre to slow the Tyranid advance upon Vanmyre's Eyrie.

The Triad - The Triad are three Imperial star forts located between the orbits of Jovenghast and Oghram in the Vyrox Void Warren Quarantine Zone which have repeatedly launched all of their attack craft to furiously fight the alien swarms that assault them from all sides.

The Blade - The Blade is another large Imperial star fort located between the orbits of Resolutuon's Ire and Jovenghast which has repeatedly attempted to send reinforcements to Sanctum, but all of its transports have been swiftly boarded and overwhelmed by Tyranid organisms.

Vanmyre's Eyrie (Beacon Station) - Vanmyre's Eyrie is an astropathic Beacon Station located in the Dust Nebula of the Formidyre System that the Imperial defenders seek to protect at all costs from the Tyranids to maintain their communications with the rest of the Imperium.
