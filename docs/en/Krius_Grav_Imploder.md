A Krius Grav Imploder is a Titan-grade, directed energy Graviton Weapon wielded as a primary ranged weapon by a Warmaster Iconoclast-class Heavy Battle Titan. 

A product of the Age of Technology, these weapons were barely understood even at the time of the Horus Heresy by the Tech-priests of the ancient Mechanicum, who sanctioned their use. Through means unknown, a Krius Grav Imploder can concentrate gravity into a beam of particles that can effortlessly turn the mass of an enemy against itself. The use of a Krius Grav Imploder in combat can turn other Titans into a heap of molten slag or crumple an enemy Knight like an empty tin of corpse-starch.

The Krius Grav Imploder is contained within a four-fingered claw that grants the Titan additional ways to rip apart fortified bunkers, walls or other Titans in close combat.
