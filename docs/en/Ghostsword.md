A Ghostsword is a Ghost Weapon, one of the large, melee Power Weapons used by the Craftworld Aeldari's various Ghost Warriors.

The rudimentary sentience within the Spirit Stone core of each Ghost Weapon, whether it takes the form of an axe, glaive or sword, guides the wielder's blows into their target's weak spots.

The Ghostsword is used by Asuryani Wraithblades. They are the smaller version of Ghostglaives. 

Usually wielded in pairs, they leave glowing traces in the air as they are swung.
