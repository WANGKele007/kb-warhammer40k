Watch Station Tryphon is a Deathwatch watch station located in the heart of the Orpheus Salient of the Jericho Reach. 

It has somehow been able to get an occasional astropathic message through the Shadow in the Warp created by the presence of the Tyranid Hive Fleet Dagon in the salient.

It is currently unknown how the station is able to achieve this feat. Some of the more Puritanical members of the Ordo Xenos feel that this is the Emperor's will being manifested through the astropaths on station. 

More Radical Inquisitors feel that the Deathwatch have captured a piece of xenos technology and are keen to acquire this for themselves.

Where the truth actually lies is unknown, as the Deathwatch are too busy fighting off the Tyranids to offer up an explanation, and the Inquisition is hesitant to order a full-scale investigation into the watch station while it remains as one of the only means of contact with the Orpheus Salient.
