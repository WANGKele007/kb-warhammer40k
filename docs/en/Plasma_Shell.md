The Plasma Shell is a type of ammunition used by Navis Imperialis warship Nova Cannons. When detonated, a Plasma Shell creates a fleet-consuming plasma explosion the size of a small planet that burns with the ferocity of a small star for a fraction of a solar second. 

Used primarily in Mars, Ryza and Stygies Pattern Nova Cannons, Plasma Shells utilise a very large Thermonuclear Fusion Warhead such as in the Mars Pattern or a highly unstable Macro Plasma Warhead in the case of Stygies and Ryza Pattern Nova Cannons.
