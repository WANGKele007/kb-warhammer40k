Yênna's Kindred is a Kindred of the Leagues of Votann. It is known to possess Balewind harvester stations. This is an example of the extremophile inclinations of many Leagues of Votann Kindreds.

Sources

Codex: Leagues of Votann (9th Edition), pg. 25
