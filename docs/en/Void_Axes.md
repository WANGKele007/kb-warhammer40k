The Void Axes are a Loyalist Space Marine Chapter of unknown Founding and genetic origin.

Their homeworld is Hasciar which lies in the Segmentum Ultima, in the depths of the Imperium Nihilus.

The Chapter sent aid to the Night Sentinels' Chapter homeworld of Lychnos which was under assault, however contact with the relief force was lost before they reached Lychnos.
