Ahriman - Sorcerer is the second novel in the Ahriman series. The novel was released in January 2015.

Synopsis

Ahriman, the greatest sorcerer of the Thousand Sons and architect of the Rubric of Ahriman that laid his Legion low, continues to walk the path towards salvation, or damnation. Searching for a cure for his Legion, he is forced to consider -- was the great ritual somehow flawed from the very beginning? The answer may lie within the mysterious artefact known as the Athenaeum of Kallimakus, a grimoire of forgotten lore which is reputed to contain the exact words of the lost Book of Magnus... or, perhaps, even a transcription of the primarch's deepest and most secret thoughts.
