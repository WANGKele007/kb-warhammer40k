The Officio Agricultae is a sub-division of the Adeptus Administratum that is tasked with overseeing the methods of production and guaranteeing the agricultural output of the Imperium's many Agri-worlds. The Officio Agricultae's Administratum adepts are present on Agri-worlds across the galaxy to ensure that those world's rulers and population are meeting their harvesting quotas. Without the sometimes tedious but crucial work of these adepts, billions of subjects of the Emperor might go hungry on those planets like Hive Worlds that are wholly dependent on food imported from Agri-worlds for their survival.
