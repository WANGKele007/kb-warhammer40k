The Pulveriser Cannon is a short-range, large-bore ballistic cannon used as a hull-mounted weapon on the Astra Militarum's Rogal Dorn Battle Tank that is the smaller counterpart of the more common Demolisher Cannon.

Like its larger sibling, the Pulveriser Cannon is intended to be used primarily as an anti-armour weapon and can also be used effectively against dug-in enemy positions and fortifications.
