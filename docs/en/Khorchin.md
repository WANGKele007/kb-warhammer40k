Khorchin was the name of the language spoken by the people of the White Scars' homeworld of Chogoris, and thus also of those Space Marines themselves.

Notable Words

Hain - Ork

Minghan - Brotherhood

Trivia

The Khorchin dialect is a variety of Mongolian spoken in the east regions of Inner Mongolia, namely in the Hinggan League, in the north, north-east and east of Hinggan and in all but the south of the Tongliao region.
