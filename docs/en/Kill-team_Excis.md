Kill-team Excis was the tactical code name given to a Deathwatch kill-team split off from Kill-team Cassius by order of Chaplain Ortan Cassius of the Ultramarines and commanded by Thaniel Ectros of the White Consuls Chapter that was ordered in 681.M41 to investigate the Mining World of Ghosar Quintus after the disappearance of Inquisitor Chaegryn and his team of Tempestus Scions on a previous investigation of the world.

Excis followed Chaegryn's trail into the Great Pit, the primary mining complex on the planet yet they too were swallowed by its mysteries, apparently slain to the last Astartes in their first encounter with the Genestealer Cult of the Four-Armed Emperor that lurked within the planet's depths and controlled its ruling Trysst Dynasty.

Upon learning of the loss of Kill-team Ectis, Ortan Cassius led a mission unauthorised by his watch commander to Ghosar Quintus with Kill-team Cassius to discover the horrific mystery of what had slain his battle-brothers.

Roster

Thaniel Ectros - Ectros was the commander of Kill-team Ectis and an Astartes of the White Consuls. He was armed with a Power Maul and Storm Shield.

Luca Thalassi - Thalassi was an Astartes of the Flesh Tearers. He was armed with a Power Sword and a Bolter.

Koenen Siegfric - Siegfric was a Space Marine drawn from the Howling Griffons. He was armed with a Deathwatch Shotgun.

Vondel Omid - Omid was a proud Astartes of the Crimson Fists. He was armed with an Infernus Pattern Heavy Bolter.

Stentor Pranus - Pranus was a Battle-Brother of the Novamarines. He was armed with a Stalker Pattern Bolter.
