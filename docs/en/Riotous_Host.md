The Riotous Host is a Traitor Titan Legion of the Dark Mechanicum that serves the Chaos God Slaanesh.

Its god-engines' upper limbs and carapaces have been equipped with Titan-sized Sonic Weapons.

Legion History

Notable Campaigns

The Fall of Extremis Six (ca. 999.M41) - During the course of the 13th Black Crusade, the Riotous Host allied with a warband of the Emperor's Children and, under the orders of the Chaos Lord Lucius the Eternal, assaulted the mineral-rich world of Extremis Six, famous for its massive "starscrapers." The starscrapers that studded Extermis Six's primary continent were shaken to their foundations by translocator-sized Sonic Weapons attached to the upper limbs and carapaces of the Riotous Host's war machines. The extreme auditory barrage deafened, drove insane or even liquefied those in its path, depending on their proximity. After continuous pulses of mind-killing noise eroded their structural integrity, the starscrapers collapsed in on themselves in swift succession, the billowing hurricane of rock dust making extraction all but impossible even for Power Armoured soldiers. The airborne Astra Militarum regiments that moved to enact vengeance, including the 352nd Elysian Drop Troops, 998th Harakoni Warhawks, 142nd Etharic Seraphs and their Militarum Tempestus allies of the 12th Thetoid Condors and 1st Zetic Harriers, reported heavy resistance from the Heretic Astartes of the Emperor's Children. Ironically, for all the planet-shattering noise that laid it low, the war zone soon went utterly quiet. The alleged sighting of a gigantic, serpent-bodied creature that may have been the Daemon Primarch Fulgrim -- seen outside the Eye of Terror for the first time in over 10,000 standard years -- slaughtering everything the Astra Militarum could send against it was neither confirmed nor denied by Imperial authorities. Ultimately, the world was considered lost to the Forces of Chaos when no further communications were forthcoming.
