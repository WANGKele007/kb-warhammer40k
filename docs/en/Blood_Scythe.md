The Blood Scythe is a powerful scythe-like Necron melee weapon available to the nobles of the Novokh Dynasty which could replace a Necron Overlord's Voidscythe or Warscythe.

It is said that the Necron champion Ultep the Divider fought ten thousand duels and was never once defeated. He is amongst the Novokh Dynasty's greatest heroes, venerated to this day by the dynasty's warrior cults. 

Only the untrammelled power of a rampaging C'tan finally scattered Ultep's metal body to atoms, though his crimson war scythe survived his destruction. 

Forged from sanguiphagic star-metal alloys, a single cut from this blade can draw a torrent of blood from an opponent.
