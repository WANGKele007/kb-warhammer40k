The Collegiate Astrolex is a corps of strategic advisers, cloisters of Ecclesiarchy priests and a personally maintained stable of psychic seers, prophets and Emperor's Tarot readers that accompany the Lord Commander Solar Arcadian Leontus wherever he goes in defence of the Imperium. 

With the aid of the insights and predictions provided by the Collegiate Astrolex and his own hand-picked subordinate officers, the Lord Solar Commander has masterminded hundreds of stunning victories.
