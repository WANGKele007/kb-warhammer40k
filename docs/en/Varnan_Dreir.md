Varnan Dreir is a Lord Marshal of the Death Korps of Krieg, a former Death Rider and Ridemaster of the 143rd Krieg Infantry Regiment. Both he and his cybernetic steed, ES819, have cheated death multiple times, leading charges of the Death Riders in dozens of war zones. Some Krieg soldiers claim the two are unkillable, having been blessed by the Emperor Himself.

History

Varnan Dreir began his career as a lowly trooper in the 143rd Krieg Infantry Regiment. As a Ridemaster in that regiment's Death Rider cavalry contingent he first rode his faithful cybernetic steed ES819. 

Dreir is a steely-eyed combat veteran who has survived a treacherous path, from the trenches of the common trooper right up to the highest echelons of command. In typical Krieg fashion he still leads his troops from as close to the front lines as possible, and he's widely respected for having already done the many horrific things he expects of those under his command -- much to the continued horror of his superiors.

Dreir rose through the ranks of the Astra Militarum to eventually become a Lord Marshal. He has commanded Imperial Guard battle groups in dozens of war zones, demonstrated an uncompromising determination to achieve victory regardless of the Human cost in each. Despite this outlook, he is not accused of being wasteful or irresponsible with his troops. Rather, Dreir is held in great esteem by the greatest Imperial strategists and remains well-liked by even the common soldiers under his command. This is due not only to his string of victories, but also his willingness to personally risk his own safety by always commanding and motivating from the front.

Dreir's steed, ES819, has fallen in battle multiple times and suffered wounds that in normal circumstances would have resulted in termination. Yet on each occasion, the loyal mount appears at the Lord Marshal's side once again fully recovered and repaired. Some Krieg soldiers claim the two are unkillable due to having been blessed by the Emperor Himself. Dreir holds no such belief, and knows one day his time will come.
