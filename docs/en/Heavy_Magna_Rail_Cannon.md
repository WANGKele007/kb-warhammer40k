A Heavy Magna Rail Cannon, also spelled Heavy Magna-rail Cannon, is a potent kinetic Rail Weapon used by the military forces of the Leagues of Votann. The Heavy Magna Rail Cannon is large, vehicle-sized weapon that possesses much greater range and stopping power than its man-portable variant in the Leagues of Votann's arsenal, the Magna Rail Rifle.

The Heavy Magna Rail Cannon is often deployed as a main turret weapon on the Hekaton Land Fortress, the Kin's primary main battle tank and armoured personnel carrier.
