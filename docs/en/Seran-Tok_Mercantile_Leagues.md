The Seran-Tok Mercantile Leagues is a league of the Leagues of Votann that is located in the galactic core. It is a league that has risen to prominence only in recent times, due to its lucrative trading relationship with the T'au Empire.

Demiurg

The T'au Empire in the past has reported that it made a commercial deal with the "SrrykTok Brotherhood" of the Demiurg. The Demiurg are now known to have been a Prospect of the Leagues of Votann that was misidentified by the T'au and Humanity as a minor xenos species. Whether the SrrykTok Brotherhood is actually the Seran-Tok Mercantile Leagues has not yet been clarified.
