A Ta'shiro is a massive deep space fortress of the T'au Empire that is operated and maintained by the T'au Air Caste. 

Positioned deep in the void between Septs, these fortresses possess enough thrust to resist drift and therefore maintain their permanent position. 

The T'au have followed several patterns of development when it came to the construction of the colossal fortresses, with the largest having a population rivaling a continent-sized city.
