Grey Angel is the sixth audio drama for the Horus Heresy Series that was not originally released as part of an anthology or other release. Grey Angel was released as an MP3 format download on August 3, 2012 and was written by John French. It was later re-released as an audio CD alongside the story Burden of Duty, but can still be bought as a stand-alone audio drama MP3. This story was later re-published in prose format in January 2016 as part of the Black Library's "Tales of the Knights Errant" week. The story was also published as part of The Silent War main series anthology novel. The title received updated cover artwork when it was released as an e-book, both the original cover and the updated cover are shown.

Official Synopsis

On a distant world of the Imperium, an agent of Rogal Dorn finds himself the prisoner of a Legion whose loyalties may be divided. Shackled and bound, the former Luna Wolf must fight a battle of wits with his captor, lest the course of the Horus Heresy take an unexpected turn. Will his very presence drive his erstwhile allies into the arms of the Warmaster Horus or will maintaining the status quo prevent another Legion from turning Traitor? And just who is the mysterious Space Marine aiding him from the shadows?
