A Warpsurge is a relatively minor type of Warp storm or other Warp anomaly in space-time that frequently appears in the Caligari Sector of the Segmentum Tempestus. Warpsurges are a phenomenon that seem to be unique to that sector. 

Warpsurges appear mostly without any warning, and they can last for an indeterminable amount of time. These surges are always confined to specific Warp-routes within the sector, and they can cut off entire star systems or in extreme cases even large parts of a sub-sector from both FTL Warp travel and astropathic communication. Warpsurges can also dissipate in mere solar days, but occasionally they linger for solar decades or even longer periods of time.

Specific regions of the Caligari Sector are more prone to the manifestation of Warpsurges while others remain relatively safe. These more volatile regions are referred to by the local Navigators as "storm zones." There also exist stable zones of the sector where the local Warp always remains untainted. Obviously these safe regions became the hubs of the Imperial presence in the sector over the millennia. It also means that a large number of star systems can be cut off from the rest of the Imperium regularly, occasionally for a substantial amount of time, which affects the stability of the affected planets, which in turn frequently become hotbeds for Heretic cults and also attract other forms of unwanted attention such as xenos raids.

It is unknown why Warpsurges are so focused on specific regions of the sector. Despite the fervent research of the Navis Nobilite and the groups of Adeptus Mechanicus tech-priests dedicated to researching the problem in the local systems, the true nature and cause of the Warpsurges remain a mystery.

All in all, the Warpsurges of the Caligari Sector made this region an ideal hunting ground for the Inquisition: there are simply too many backwater worlds with various twisted interpretations of the Imperial Creed, not to mention the cut-off systems left unattended for centuries, ripe for Chaos infestation and heresy -- leaving Inquisitors of the Caligari Conclave with plenty of work to do in the name of the God-Emperor.
