Fennias Maxim is a Techmarine and the Master of the Forge for the Ultramarines Chapter. He is responsible for overseeing the actions of the Chapter's other Techmarines and ensuring that the Chapter Armoury is able to maintain and repair the Ultramarines' stocks of wargear, aircraft and vehicles.

Codex: Space Marines (5th Edition), pg. 17
