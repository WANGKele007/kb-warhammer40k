A Plasma Caster was a small, wrist-mounted Plasma Weapon unique to the Dark Angels Space Marine Legion used by the I Legion's Knights Cenobium during the Great Crusade and Horus Heresy eras. 

The weapon was fitted into each Astartes' vambrace to allow the Knights Cenobium to fight unencumbered in combat.
