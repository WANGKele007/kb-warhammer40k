The Angels Redeemed is a Codex Astartes-compliant Loyalist Space Marine Chapter of unknown Founding and origin. 

Chapter Appearance

Chapter Colours

The Angels Redeemed primarily wear dark orange-red power armour. The power pack and arms are black. The Aquila or Imperialis on the chest is silver. 

The black squad specialty symbol -- battleline, close support, fire support, Veteran or command -- is indicated on the right shoulder plate. 

A white High Gothic numeral centred in the middle of the squad specialty symbol indicates squad number. The white High Gothic numeral on the left knee plate designates company number.

Chapter Badge

The Angels Redeemed's Chapter badge is a white winged sword centred on a field of dark orange-red, reminiscent of the Dark Angels' Chapter icon.
