The Indrajit-class Heavy Cruiser was a cruiser of the Imperial Army's Armada Imperialis that served in the expeditionary fleets of the Legiones Astartes during the Great Crusade and Horus Heresy. A void warship of rare and rugged design, the class was named for the invincible demigod of ancient Indus myth from Old Earth. 

The Indrajit-class was a macro-delivery vessel, built to unload millions of tonnes of ordnance onto a target at ultra-close range, packing the hull armour and the void-shielding necessary to soak up the enemy's punishment while it closed. 

Notable Indrajit-class Heavy Cruisers

Sybarite (Emperor's Children Legion) - The Sybarite was a heavy cruiser of the Emperor's Children Legion. Some apostate of the Dark Mechanicum must have cut into the ancient vessel's transponder, because it screamed its name across the narrowing gulf of the void towards it targets with a feminised voice, intoning "Sybarite! Sybarite! Sybarite!" The Sybarite engaged the Dark Angels' Gloriana-class flagship Invincible Reason at the defence of the Death Guard's homeworld of Barbarus late in the Horus Heresy from the Ist Legion's Exterminatus assault just before the Siege of Terra. The Sybarite was captained by an Emperor's Children Possessed Chaos Space Marine who had transformed into his daemonic form and swallowed the rest of the vessel's bridge crew. The Sybarite closed with the Invincible Reason as it was designed to do, and actually managed to hold its own and present a considerable threat to the already damaged Gloriana-class Battleship. The Sybarite even attempted to ram the much larger Dark Angels flagship before it was destroyed.
