The Protectorate of Unsubdued Steel was a Blackshield Titan Legion active during the Horus Heresy.

Legion History

The Protectorate of Unsubdued Steel was a Blackshield Legio created during the Cataclysm of Iron in the region of the galaxy known as the Belt of Iron. It was composed of Titans from several different Legios, including the Legio Astorum and the Legio Damnatus. 

As the Loyalists and Traitors waged war on each other within the Belt of Iron, the Protectorate of Unsubdued Steel began building a pocket stellar empire of its own. Among the first worlds to fall to the Protectorate was the Forge World of Arl'yeth after it refused to peacefully join the Protectorate's empire. The conquered Forge World then joined a dozen other planets that the Blackshield Legio controlled at the time.
