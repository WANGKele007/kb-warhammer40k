The Emperor's Claws is a Loyalist Space Marine Chapter of unknown Founding and origin. It possesses Primaris Space Marines within its ranks. 

Notable Emperor's Claws

Chapter Master Laethos Akare - Laethos Akare is the current Chapter Master of the Emperor's Claws.
Sergeant Gadiel - Gadiel is a Primaris Space Marine and the Sergeant of a Hellblaster squad.
Brother Sigaf - Sigaf is a Dreadnought of the Emperor's Claws.
