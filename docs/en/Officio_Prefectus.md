The Officio Prefectus, also known as the Commissariat, is a subdivision of the Departmento Munitorum of the Adeptus Administratum, whose task is to ensure the recruitment, training and deployment of the corps of political officers tasked with keeping the personnel of the Astra Militarum and Navis Imperialis on mission and under intense discipline, even in the face of some of the most terrifying threats in the galaxy. 

The Imperial Guard is exposed on a regular basis to terrible threats which can shake the resolve of even the most steel-hearted veteran, from bizarre aliens to the terrifying Daemonic inhabitants of the Warp. To encourage the troops to fight in the most horrendous of situations, political officers called "commissars" are attached to regiments of the Imperial Guard and the vessels of the Imperial Navy. 

The role of a commissar is to fight alongside the regiment's or warship's regular officers and take control when morale is flagging or the troops are not fighting with sufficient zeal. In such situations a commissar can take over command from a regular officer and summarily execute any individual who tries to flee. Even the commanding colonel of a regiment must be sure to display courage and the proper zeal at all times when a commissar is watching him or her.

Commissars are trained from the orphans of parents who were killed serving the Imperium in one of the other Imperial military forces or adepta. They were taken as wards of the state and educated within the Ecclesiarchy's special hybrid orphanages/military schools, the Schola Progenium, and selected from among those who show fearless faith in their Emperor. 

Upon reaching adolescence, they are recruited by the Officio Prefectus and given advanced training in combat tactics and Human psychology. Once ready, they are then assigned by the Commissariat to Astra Militarum regiments and Navis Imperialis vessels whether the regiment's or ship's officers in question like it or not. 

Commissars are generally extremely pious if not fanatical believers in the Imperial Cult, oft-times self-righteous and unswerving in their purpose. They are exceptionally brave and many a seemingly hopeless battle has been turned by a commissar taking command. However, all commissars are universally feared and loathed by the troops of the Astra Militarum and the crews of the Navis Imperialis and are always the first officers against the wall when a regiment or starship crew mutinies.

History

The Imperial Army of the Great Crusade was organised from the fighting troops of the worlds liberated by the Space Marine Legions of the Emperor of Mankind. They were used almost exclusively for garrison and pacification roles on both their home and foreign worlds throughout the early stages of the crusade, but by the latter half, they were fighting offensive battles and supplementing the Space Marine Legions as they began to be stretched thinner by the sheer size of the growing Imperium. 

The Imperial Army was organised with no particular standardisation, and regimental makeups ranged from barbarians wielding Power Axes to advanced mechanised infantry armed with directed energy weapons whose designs have now been lost. During this time, the regiments were left almost exclusively to their own recognisance as long as they served faithfully, and conformed to the few restrictions placed on them.

During the Great Crusade, some Imperial Army regiments utilised specialists known as "Discipline Masters". These stern task masters were responsible for maintaining discipline within the regiment to which they were assigned. They were also responsible for tracking down Imperial Army deserters that repudiated their oaths to the Emperor and shirked their soldierly responsibilities. 

They were known to use hunting eagles to help them track their prey. Their fanatical dedication to the service of the Emperor overrode any feelings of compassion, mercy or Humanity when carrying out the summary executions of those found wanting, for which they employed a ceremonial Electro-scythe. The Discipline Masters served as the pre-cursor and model for the later Commissariat and its merciless commissars.

During the Horus Heresy, an estimated 40-50% of the Imperial Army turned on the Emperor and joined the Warmaster Horus Lupercal to make war on the Imperium. Most of these Traitor Army regiments eventually broke down into feudal and fragmented Chaos Cults, worshiping the various Chaos Gods, and causing great havoc amongst the civilian populations of millions of worlds. 

With the added range of their attached Armada Imperialis regimental cruisers, the Traitor Army units spread quickly and left a bloody swathe of depopulated and dead worlds in their wake during the Heresy, many of which had once been their own responsibilities or even their homeworlds. It is suspected that a number of Traitor Army regiments were used in the Siege of Terra, though the exact extent of the Traitor forces has never been properly calculated, and most Chaos-tainted Imperial Army regiments became indistinguishable from each other.

With Horus's defeat, and the retreat of the forces of Chaos to the Eye of Terror, the remaining Loyalist regiments of the Imperial Army underwent massive changes, and became heavily standardised. The Navis Imperialis or Imperial Navy was bureaucratically separated from the Imperial Army and transformed into a separate service, whilst the Imperial Army's ground forces were renamed the Astra Militarum or "Imperial Guard".

During this time, the Officio Prefectus or Commissariat was formed to be the Imperium's bulwark among the members of the Astra Militarum against the corrupting influence of Chaos. The organisation's primary official, the commissar, was given power to ensure his or her regiment's continuing loyalty, and was charged to do anything necessary to prevent the corruption or spread of Chaos among the guardsmen of that unit. 

Commissars were attached to every regiment and Imperial Navy starship, and were used extensively in the immediate wake of the Traitor forces' retreat into the Eye of Terror during the Great Scouring to ensure that the population of planets that had been ravaged by Chaos during the Horus Heresy were not subverted by Chaos splinter cells or sleeper Chaos Cults. 

Before the Imperium was re-forged in the Time of Rebirth, millions of people would be executed by the Commissariat as Traitors or Heretics. Eventually, this role was shifted to the Imperial Inquisition and its Ordo Hereticus and Ordo Malleus sub-divisions.

Over the last 10,000 standard years, the Officio Prefectus has remained an important facet of the Astra Militarum's and Navis Imperialis command structure, and it is rare not to see at least one commissar attached to the command staff of a regimental officer or Lord Commander's staff. Several commissars have even attained Imperial military commands of their own, including Commissar Sebastian Yarrick, who took command of the Steel Legionnaires of Hades Hive during the Second War for Armageddon, and became overall commander of Imperial forces during the Third and the Fourth War for Armageddon. 

The only known commissars to have held and retained overall command of a full regiment included Commissar Ibram Gaunt, who became the only known colonel-commissar of the orphaned Tanith 1st Regiment, after receiving command of the Tanith 1st, 2nd and 3rd Regiments from Warmaster Slaydo during the Sabbat Worlds Crusade, and losing the 2nd and 3rd when Tanith fell to Chaos. 

Commissar-General Delane Oktar also commanded the Hyrkan 8th Regiment and was Gaunt's personal tutor. 

Also, a ruthless individual named Lady Commissar-General Viktoria Balshin was charged with purging the perceived taint of Chaos from the Second Front of the Sabbat Worlds Crusade.

Local Commissariats

Within the Imperial military, some Planetary Defence Forces and individual sectors or sub-sectors of the Imperium possess their own, semi-autonomous local branch of the Commissariat who maintain authority over the Imperial military forces operating in their region of the galaxy. 

These organisations are empowered with all the usual right and duties of the larger Imperial Commissariat, but are subordinate to the parent organisation, and possess fewer powers over regular troops of the Astra Militarum.

Nevertheless, in the past, this has caused friction between local planetary Commissariats and the official Officio Prefectus due to jurisdictional concerns, and has even been known to result in Imperial commissars executing their local planetary counterparts for overstepping their authority.

Chaliced Commissariat - The Chaliced Commissariat is the local branch of the Commissariat found within the Calixis Sector and based on the sector capital world of Scintilla. This special military unit is a direct political and military extension of Lord Sector Marius Hax's will and was created to maintain direct control over locally-raised regiments and Planetary Defence Forces (PDF). Feared and hated in equal measure across the sector, the mere sight of the laurel-wreathed chalice badge and deep red rank insignia are enough cause for a defence trooper to blanch and consider whether their life has come to an end. This organisation was primarily created to monitor for corruption within local PDF units as well as ferreting out the possibility of an organised and armed secession on the part of a local planetary governor that is supported by PDF units. The Lord Marshal of the Calixian Adeptus Arbites has made no secret of his dislike for Hax's "specially selected scum", seeing them as bordering on being an unlawful agency, and therefore one step away from an instrument of rebellion themselves.

Vervun Primary House Commissariat (VPHC) - The VPHC was a local Commissariat found upon the Hive World of Verghast within the Sabbat Worlds Sector. Verghast did not possess a unified Planetary Defence Force like most Imperial worlds, but instead each of its hive cities raised their own armed force. These forces could operate individually or could combine with those of the other hive cities as a true PDF when circumstances required. Additionally, when the Imperial Guard required Verghast to raise a new regiment, the soldiers who served in these local forces were exempt from this draft. The military of Vervunhive was known as the Vervun Primary; due to the Vervun Primary's exemplary service in the "Trade War" of 679.M41, it was given permanent garrison duty. Soldiers of the Vervun Primary therefore found themselves with a lifetime vocation that offered little of the risk of Imperial Guard service. The Vervun Primary consisted of 500,000 soldiers, plus another 70,000 auxiliaries (including Vervunhive's wall-gunners). Their chief day-to-day role was to man the walls, forts and gatehouses of Vervunhive, but they also possessed the capacity to launch assaults beyond the city thanks to organic armoured formations raised and trained within the force. The Vervun Primary's uniforms were blue, with relatively conventional battle fatigues augmented by jackboots, a double-breasted greatcoat, a leather equipment harness and a spiked helmet. Auto Weapons were the standard issue firearms for the force rather than lasguns. Vervun Primary officer dress uniforms were white. The Vervun Primary also posssessed its own local Commissariat which was empowered with all the usual rights and authority of the official Imperial Commissariat, and was also rumoured to be used by the ruling mercantile nobility of the Verghastian hive city of Vervunhive as a form of secret police force. The VPHC was overseen by a full Imperial commissar, assigned the role of watching over both the Verghastian Imperial Guard forces deployed in defence of Vervunhive and the VPHC. This local Commissariat was theoretically subordinate to the Officio Prefectus and its officials and retained very limited powers over regular Imperial Guardsmen, but conflicts of jurisdiction did arise during the Defence of Vervunhive campaign of the Sabbat Worlds Crusade, resulting in the eruption of a firefight between the VPHC and Imperial Guard forces. The senior officer of the Vervun Primary had the power to enforce martial law and dethrone the civil government of the hive city as its new leader, under section 45jk of the Act of Entitlement. The Vervun Primary had a rigid command structure, which did not place emphasis on personal initiative. This vulnerability proved to be a source of early casualties during the Zoican attack of 769.M41, but the Vervun Primary eventually proved its ability to adapt to the varying threats it came to face during the Sabbat Worlds Crusade. Individual Vervun Primary soldiers went on to display considerable military talent, particularly those that became members of the Scratch Companies. The Vervun Primary was decimated during the Defence of Vervunhive, and effectively ceased to exist at the end of the campaign. When the city was razed, several of the survivors joined one of the various Imperial Guard regiments that had fought in the campaign, under the Act of Consolation, of which the most famed was the Tanith 1st.
