The Nameless is a Loyalist Space Marine Chapter of unknown Founding and genetic origin. Their homeworld is Repentance, an Artificial World located beyond the Eastern Fringe, to the galactic south-east of Macragge.

Chapter Appearance

Chapter Colours

The Nameless' Chapter colours are not listed in current Imperial records.

Chapter Badge

The Nameless' Chapter badge appears to be a bolt of lightning.
