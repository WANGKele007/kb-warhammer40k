An A.N-vyl is a technical workstation employed by the Brôkhyr engineers and artificers of the Leagues of Votann where they fabricate most of the superlative devices and other advanced technology of the Kin. Each A.N-vyl possesses all of the tools and materials an individual Brôkhyr needs to begin work on their next creation.

Codex: Leagues of Votann (9th Edition), pg. 32
