A Gaugamela-class Star Fort is a type of Imperial star fort used by the Navis Imperialis. It is armed with lance batteries.

During the Era Indomitus, two Gaugamela-class Star Forts were stationed in geostationary orbit above the world of Avalus, a part of the Lion's Protectorate in the Imperium Nihilus, and destroyed two Hellbringers during the battle over the planet fought between the Imperium and the Heretic Astartes of the Ten Thousand Eyes warband.
