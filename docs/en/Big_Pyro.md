Big Pyro is an Ork Mek who often rides to battle in a Boomdakka Snazzwagon. The deranged Deathskulls Orks known as the "Pyro-Mekaniaks" are the hangers-on and toadies of Big Pyro. 

In their never-ending quest to attain pole position at the head of their mechanised warband's advance, they have achieved infamy for their apparent willingness to set everything around them -- and occasionally themselves -- on fire. 

Meanwhile, Big Pyro is diligent in keeping his Snazzwagon well stocked with Burna Bottles in preparation for the next fight.
