Karazantor, also called Karazantor the Vile, is an undead Chaos Lord of Tzeentch originally from the world of Chicano, a once wealthy and prosperous Mining World that is now a barren rock inhabited by few.

The planet fell in one local night to Chaos Cultists under the influence of the Chaos God Tzeentch.  Karazantor was the most trusted warrior of the Chicanan army and his betrayal culminated in the death of the planet's great ruler and planetary governor Lord Xian Torus, earning him the title "Traitor of Xian."  

As a reward for his service, Karazantor was given the gift of precognition by his new master. He can now see the threads of potential futures weaving through time like tangled skeins of multicoloured cords.
