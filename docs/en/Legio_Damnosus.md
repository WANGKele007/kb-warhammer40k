The Legio Damnosus ("Lords of Ruin") is a Traitor Titan Legion of the Dark Mechanicum that repudiated its oaths to the Emperor of Mankind and followed Warmaster Horus into the service of the Ruinous Powers during the Horus Heresy.

Notable Campaigns

Horus Heresy (007-014.M31) - The Legio Damnosus took part in numerous engagements during the dark days of the Horus Heresy.
