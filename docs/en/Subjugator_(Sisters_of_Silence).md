A Subjugator, usually a member of a Subjugator Cadre, are Jetbike-riding Sisters of Silence who serve as scouts and reconnaissance-in-force specialists for a larger assault by their order.

Heralded by the ethereal howl of their Erinyes Pattern Jetbike engines, Subjugator cadres are specialists in scouting and harassing their targets ahead of a primary force of Sisters of Silence. Their Jetbike mounts, a refined pattern of antigravitic, individual vehicles, rely less on brute force and power than similar Imperial patterns and are instead a minimalistic, lightweight design. Equipped with debilitating Snare Cannons they allow their riders to disable larger enemies and expose their vulnerabilities to attack before breaking away and preying on the next threat. 

Alternatively outfitted with a variety of esoteric weaponry such as Adrathic Destructors and Needle Cannons capable of inflicting equally bizarre and horrifying deadly effects on their targets, small squads of these units are commonly tasked with disabling specific targets in lightning strikes behind the enemy's lines, where their defences are weakest.
