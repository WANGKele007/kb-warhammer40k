The Void Daggers are a Loyalist Space Marine Chapter of unknown Founding and genetic origin. 

Chapter History

During the Era Indomitus the Void Daggers formed a coalition with the Emperor's Swords to assault the T'au Empire's Fifth Sphere of Expansion in the Imperium Nihilus. However, the Imperium's contact with this force has now been lost.
