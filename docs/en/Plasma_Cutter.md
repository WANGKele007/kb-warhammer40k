A Plasma Cutter is a type of Plasma Weapon used by Firstborn and Primaris Techmarines. While actually intended to be used on the battlefield as a tool to repair damaged war machines, the Plasma Cutter is equally adept at slicing through armour, flesh and bone with its intense, constant beam of superheated plasma.
