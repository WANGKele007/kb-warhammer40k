A Webway Shunt Generator is a highly complex and advanced Craftworld Aeldari technology that is most often equipped aboard a Skathach Wraithknight tasked with defending the labyrinthine corridors of the Webway from all trespassers.

It alllows this solitary Ghost Warrior to enter, exit from and maneuver through the Webway at will.
