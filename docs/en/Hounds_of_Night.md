The Hounds of Night is a Blackshield Titan Legion that was active during the Horus Heresy and chose to maintain its ties to the Emperor when its former Legio turned Traitor.

Legion History

The Hounds of Night were a Loyalist faction of the Traitor Legio Audax, who split off from the rest of their Legio during the Horus Heresy and dedicated themselves to hunting down Traitor Titans at the Battle of Beta-Garmon.
