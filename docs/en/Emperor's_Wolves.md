The Emperor's Wolves is a Loyalist Space Marine Chapter of unknown origin and Founding. The Emperor's Wolves lost their 8th Company in the Warp who reemerged solar decades later as the warband of Chaos Space Marines known as the Blood Disciples. Nothing else is known about them in Imperial records.

Chapter Appearance

Chapter Colours

The Emperor's Wolves primarily wear black Power Armour. The shoulder plates are red, with black trim. The Aquila or Imperialis on the chest plate is silver. The white squad specialty symbol -- Tactical, Assault, Devastator or Veteran -- is located on the right shoulder plate. A black Low Gothic numeral is centred on the squad specialty symbol, indicating squad number. The white Gothic numeral on the left knee guard indicates company number.

Chapter Badge

The Emperor's Wolves' Chapter badge is a large stylised grey wolf's head, centred on a field of red.
