The Nightfire Wars were a conflict fought in the late 41st Millennium between the Imperium of Man and the T'au Farsight Enclaves. The Iron Knights and Hammers of Dorn Space Marine Chapters saw bitter combat in the campaign.

Little else concerning these conflicts is currently recorded in Imperial records.
