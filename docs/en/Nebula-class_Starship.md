The Nebula-class Starship was a colossal voidcraft utilised by the Leagues of Votann. One such vessel was merged into a space hulk that was designated "Rôkh 8-8-V" and was later salvaged by a team of Hearthkyn Salvagers led by Theyn Dêki Ânhad. The Nebula-class was known to maintain large armouries of Kin wargear and vehicles.
