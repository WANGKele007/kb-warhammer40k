Synskin, also spelled syn-skin, is a spray-on, airtight membrane that protects its wearer against all gases, hard vacuum, and blinding lights when dispersed over the facial orifices; if applied over the whole body it forms a specialised form of body-suit which protects the wearer holistically from the external environment and enhances their physical strength. 

Synskin is in common use by many of the military forces of the Imperium, most notably by the terrifying operatives of the Officio Assassinorum. The synskin used by Imperial Assassins can also be reapplied with another spray-layer that then serves as camouflage if the operative needs to hide, such as their default black synskin suits being changed to white by the application of a new spray-layer to blend into a snowy area.

Synskin is bio-reactive and molds itself perfectly to the wearer's physical form. It is also non-reflective and reduces the wearer's infrared profile, facilitating infiltration operations. It provides the wearer with sufficient oxygen to freely breathe for 12 solar hours along with stimulant chemicals increasing the wearer's senses, reaction times, and other physical functions, after which the wearer will collapse and die of suffocation if the material is not removed with synskin solvent. Internal oxygen and carbon dioxide exchangers that are inserted into the wearer's throat increase the usable period of a single application for a further twelve solar hours. Speech and hearing is usually maintained through internal vox communicators also inserted into the throat alongside the receiver at the base of the ear.
