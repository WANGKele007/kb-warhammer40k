The Rusthounds are a Renegade Knight house in service to the Plague God Nurgle who are former members of House Krast.

House History

Notable Campaigns

Battle of Chambakh (Unknown Date.M42) - Amidst the ruins of Chambakh, Imperial Knights of House Krast battle the mechanised infantry of the Death Guard Traitor Legion's Pallid Hand warband. Shrugging off salvo after salvo of Blight Launcher rounds, the Knights trample the Death Guard warriors and claim victory. Yet in doing so their mechanical steeds are infected with the daemonic disease known as the Ferric Blight. What emerges from that festering war zone is a warband of Renegade Knights rusted through in body and soul, limbs shrieking and seized, Thrones Mechanicum tainted beyond recovery. Renaming themselves the "Rusthounds," the Knights set off on the hunt for their former allies' souls.
