Rylus Darkscourge was the Caele (monarch) of Aurum when that planet in the Jericho Reach was discovered by the Achilus Crusade in 787.M41.

Like all of Aurum's leaders, his right to rule did not come through birth, but through victory in combat.

History

Rylus was a charismatic speaker and a strong warrior. At the Imperium's first contact, he realised his people's way of life would never be the same, by the mere fact that they were no longer alone. 

Since that time he has meticulously gathered lore and stories of the planets and politics beyond his world, making him far savvier about the Imperium than most of his people.

He drew quite a grim conclusion from the stories of traders and from reading between the lines of history books. He was certain that if the crusade was allowed to absorb Aurum, the Imperium would ruthlessly exploit both its people and its resources, leaving Aurum far less than it was.

This was not an easy decision for Rylus to reach. As he learned of the galaxy, he came to understand its many threats and now knows that the Imperium may be Humanity's best defence. The message of the watching Emperor of Mankind and a Humanity united rings true with him.

However, he was the proud king of a proud people. Submitting the Golden Planet to rule by a cruel and unworthy dictator was a shame he could not allow. 

To him, the battle-brothers in the Rogue Trader Diaz Lan's entourage presented a unique opportunity, for Rylus had heard legends of the honour and valour of the Adeptus Astartes.

Because their Chapters exist outside the normal strictures of the Imperium, they might be able to provide him an unbiased warrior's view on how to deal with the Imperium's hunger for his world.

They were also the first members of the Imperium he believed might be able to win respect in the eyes of his people. If they could prove themselves the equal of Auran warriors, he would finally have an off-world ally to help him understand and purge the Malissectors.

The Deathwatch

In 817.M41, a Deathwatch Kill-team was sent to both convince Rylus to accept Imperial Compliance for Aurum, and investigate the death of Sister Rachayel of the Order Hospitaller. 

They met within a large, circular chamber of his homestead's main building, one with sand floor and no furniture. It had no external windows and instead several wall sconces provide torchlight and gave off a faint herbal smoke. 

Between the torches hung tapestries so faded that their weaves were nothing but mottled gray outlines of men and beasts.

The Caele of Aurum strode toward these black-armoured warriors with confident grace and his height, unusually for a mortal, was level with most of them. He nodded a greeting but did not smile; his dark eyes remained serious. Knowing that the Adeptus Astartes did not come without cause, he asked their reason in coming to Aurum.

The battle-brothers broached the subject of the suspected xenos presence, and possibly the Imperium's attempts to return Aurum to Humanity's fold. 

Rylus was dismissive of the Achilus Crusade's overtures of alliance, saying that the Imperium had yet to prove they had either the capacity or the worth to govern the Auran people.

Description

Rylus Darkscourge was a tall and fit man in his youth after Imperial forces first made contact with Auran in 787.M41. 

When Deathwatch forces arrived 30 standard years later, he remained tall and powerfully built even for an Auran -- standing almost as tall as a Space Marine.

His long black hair contains several small braids, but is unadorned except for the polished bone circlet on his brow.
