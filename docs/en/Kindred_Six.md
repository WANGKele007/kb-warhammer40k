Kindred Six is a Kindred of the Leagues of Votann. It is an example of the Kin's tendency to sometimes use oddly basic names for their Kindreds.

Notes

It is possible that Kindred Six is simply another name for the Kindred of Six, a Kindred of the Kronus Hegemony.

Sources

Codex: Leagues of Votann (9th Edition), pg. 10
