The Praetors of Ultramar is a Loyalist Space Marine Chapter comprised entirely of Primaris Space Marines, created from the lineage of the Ultramarines and raised during the Ultima Founding of ca. 999.M41. 

The Praetors of Ultramar is one of the nine Adeptus Astartes Chapters (plus the Ultramarines) permanently assigned by Lord Commander of the Imperium Guilliman with defending the Realm of Ultramar in the wake of the Plague Wars known as the Shield Chapters of Ultramar. 

The Praetors were created after the end of the Indomitus Crusade's first phase early in the Era Indomitus and their numbers were originally drawn from the remaining Unnumbered Sons of Ultramarines genetic descent. These were the nine Legion-sized forces which comprised half of the Primaris Space Marines created during the Ultima Founding who were not placed within the normal Adeptus Astartes Chapter structure. 

The Praetors of Ultramar's Chapter homeworld was named Howsbridge.

Chapter History

Notable Campaigns

Guardians of Ultramar (Unknown Date.M42) - The Praetors of Ultramar were originally among the members of the Unnumbered Sons who accompanied Primarch Roboute Guilliman when he went to the aid of the Realm of Ultramar during the Plague Wars. Following the conflict and the formal end of the Indomitus Crusade, Guilliman created the Praetors of Ultramar Chapter from Primaris Space Marines descended from his Ultramarines lineage and charged them with being one of the ten Shield Chapters assigned to the region to permanently defend Ultramar from further attacks by the forces of Chaos.

Chapter Appearance

Chapter Colours

The Praetors of Ultramar's Chapter colours are black, gold and white.

Chapter Badge

The Praetors of Ultramar's Chapter badge is a cream skull placed in the centre of a white Ultramarian inverted omega symbol drawn from the ancient Grecian alphabet of Terra set on a black background centered within a golden sunburst.
