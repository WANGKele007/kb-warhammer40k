The Blood Knights is a Loyalist Space Marine Chapter of unknown genetic origin and Founding.

Chapter Appearance

Chapter Colours

The Blood Knights primarily wear red power armour, including the power pack, upper torso, groin area and legs. The shoulder plates, helmet and Aquila or Imperialis on the chest, are white. The dark red squad tactical specialty symbol -- battleline, close support, fire support, Veteran or command -- is indicated on the right shoulder plate.

A white Low Gothic numeral centred on the squad specialty symbol indicates squad number.

The colour of the left knee plate indicates company number in accordance with the Codex Astartes -- i.e. white (1st Company), yellow (2nd Company), red (3rd Company), etc.

Chapter Badge

The Blood Knights' Chapter badge is a pair of ebon, crossed swords, with the blades pointed downwards, centred on a field of white.
