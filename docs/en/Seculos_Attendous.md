Seculos Attendous is a minor Radical faction of the Imperial Inquisition that maintains that the religious beliefs spread and enforced by the state church of the Ecclesiarchy across the Human-settled galaxy is hampering and holding back Humanity from advancing, and therefore they seek to reduce its political power and influence wherever and whenever possible. The Inquisitors of this sect do not object to the worship of the Emperor as a god, only to the oppressive means wielded by the Ecclesiarchy to spread and maintain absolute loyalty to its doctrines. 

Inquisitors of this sect believe that the hulking dogma and Imperial Creed doctrine of the Adeptus Ministorum is slowly corrupting Mankind and hampering its growth and capacity for real advancement and renewal, and that the light of the Emperor does not need the oppressive hand of the Imperial Cult's bureaucracy to reach those who wish to embrace it. As yet, the faction has remained very quiet about its intentions and goals, no doubt to avoid immediate censure and the risk of excommunication. 

The operations of this faction are twofold. First they seek to twist the Ecclesiarchy's power from within, making small alterations in its operation that will ultimately help to weaken its power over the masses, while still maintaining the populace's firm faith in the God-Emperor and His glory. The second is managing the state church's contact with new worlds and remote frontiers, places where a freer, more natural worship of the Emperor is easier to perform. 

In both cases, the faction must tread very lightly, as the power of the Ecclesiarchy is not to be casually trifled with and should even the barest hint of its subversion come to light retribution would be swift and final.
