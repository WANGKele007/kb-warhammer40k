Lucia Vestha is an Ultramarian diplomat and administrator who was chosen to join the unique Command Squad of Captain Demetrian Titus of the Ultramarines' 2nd Company known as the "Wardens of Ultramar". The squad is comprised of both mortals and Astartes. She is expected to lend her talents to Titus's task of restoring the borders of the Realm of Ultramar to their pre-Horus Heresy size of five hundred worlds. 

Vestha joined Titus's war councils as an already highly-valued plenipotentiary and diplomat whose keen negotiating skills have saved the 2nd Company from needless battle in pursuit of its new objectives on countless occasions.

A plenipotentiary is a diplomat or envoy who acts independently with the full authority and confidence of their master. Just knowing that a Chapter of the Adeptus Astartes is willing to place their affairs in the hands of a Human dignitary says everything one really needs to know about Lucia Vestha, whose incredible negotiating skills and famously silver tongue have brought dozens of worlds back into the fold of Ultramar without a single drop of blood being spilled.

The same abilities she uses in diplomacy are also priceless in the many, many war councils Captain Titus now finds himself holding, as the slightest falsehood or hesitance will be uncovered before it has the chance to cause damage. Her diligent service and exemplary track record have earned Lucia great respect from across the 2nd Company, and with the looming threat of an Ultramarines fleet at her back, she finds the job of cowing rebellious worlds a lot easier.
