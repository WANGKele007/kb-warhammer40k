The Galvanic Rifle, officially the Mark IV Arkhan Galvanic Rifle, modelled after the hunting flintlocks of Mars's distant colonial past, is a precision tool in the hands of a Skitarii Ranger of the Adeptus Mechanicus. 

Its bodywork is that of an antique firearm from Humanity's ancient past, with a polished wooden stock and curlicues that echo the sandy seas of Mars's crimson deserts. Yet the energised, galvanic servitor-bullets inside are incredibly advanced. When such a bullet strikes home, it causes all the potential energy of the target to burn out in a killing blast of coruscating, electric force that leaves its prey a smoking husk.

Notable Patterns

Mark IV Arkhan Pattern Galvanic Rifle - The standard pattern of the Galvanic Rifle used by Skitarii Rangers.

Mark CV Branx Pattern Galvanic Rifle - The Mark CV Branx Galvanic Rifle manufactured on the Forge World of Branx in the Moebian Domain is a specialised pattern with a longer range than the standard pattern that is a marksman's choice. The Mark CV is modelled after ancient Martian colonial hunting rifles, but modified by the Adeptus Mechanicus's peculiar aptitude for devastation. This pattern has been used by the Skitarii Alpha Primus assigned to assist the Ordo Hereticus Inquisitor Grendyl in eliminating the Chaos forces plaguing the hive city of Hive Tertium on the world of Atoma Prime.
