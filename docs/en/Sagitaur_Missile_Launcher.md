The Sagitaur Missile Launcher is a turret-mounted, vehicle-based missile launcher that is used as an optional primary turret weapon on the Leagues of Votann's Sagitaur ATV. Its missiles possess a longer range than those of their Sagitaur's other missile launcher option, the L7 Missile Launcher, and each Sagitaur missile can unleash a more potent explosive blast than its L7 counterpart.

Sources

Codex: Leagues of Votann (9th Edition), pp. 97, 101
