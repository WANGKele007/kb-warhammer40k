A Drilldozer Blade is a type of Imperial mining and construction equipment that has been repurposed into a potent weapon by the hybrids of the Genestealer Cults.

The giant, jutting plough of the Drilldozer Blade makes the Goliath Rockgrinder it is attached to an unstoppable force. Grinding forward with the power of high-octane engines behind it, its whirring cutter arrays can tear apart even the hardest stone, doing the same to its living prey in a squall of spraying blood.
