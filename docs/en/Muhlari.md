The Muhlari were an intelligent alien species whose knowledge was said to span the entirety of the galaxy. They made their home at Zarabek, but were exterminated by the Ordo Xenos and the Space Marines of the Deathwatch Chapter in a series of actions culminating in the Purgation of Zarabek. 

The Deathwatch burnt Zarabek clean, slaying the Muhlari's den mothers and destroying their sacellum of knowledge. Crushing all their data crystals, mixing them with the powdered ash of their people and firing them into the heart of a star, the Muhlari were consigned forever to the Book of Extinctions.
