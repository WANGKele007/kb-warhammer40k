A Multicompass is an ancient human navigation aid for exploring new worlds. When first arriving on a new planet, a device of this ancient tech-pattern is indispensable -- but few in the Imperium have the means or influence to acquire one. 

After a few solar seconds of analyzing planetary data, a multicompass can display directions, show topographical maps, point out compass bearings, indicate altitude, and much more. 

Even the Adeptus Mechanicus do not fully understand all of the capabilities of such a rare and hallowed device.
