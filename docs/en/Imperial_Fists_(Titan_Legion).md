The Imperial Fists are a Loyalist Titan Legion of the Collegia Titanica, one of the military arms of the Adeptus Mechanicus. There is very little information in the Imperial records concerning this obscure Titan Legion, though they are suspected to have been founded before the Age of the Imperium began, during the Age of Strife.

Legion Colours

The colours of this Titan Legion are blue and orange.

Legion Badge

The badge of the Imperial Fists Titan Legion is a clenched fist wearing a steel gauntlet in front of an orange sun, on a quartered-field of blue and white.
