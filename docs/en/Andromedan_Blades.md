The Andromedan Blades are a Loyalist Space Marine Chapter of unknown Founding and genetic origin. 

Chapter History

Notable Campaigns

Indomitus Crusade (Unknown Date.M42) - Six companies of the Andromedan Blades were assigned to serve as part of Battle Group Kallides of the Indomitus Crusade Fleet Primus. Battle Group Kallides fought against the Necrons located in the foreboding region of the galaxy designated the Pariah Nexus and relieved the Necron siege of the city of Exulta on the world of Mesanor in the Zeidos System.

Notable Andromedan Blades

Chapter Master Karyas Kaeso - Kaeso is the current Chapter Master of the Andromedan Blades. He commanded six companies serving in Indomitus Crusade Fleet Primus's Battle Group Kallides during the Indomitus Crusade.
