Fort Pykman is a watch fortress of the Deathwatch Chapter located in the Ghoul Stars, northeast of the Segmentum Ultima beyond the Eastern Fringe in the Imperium Nihilus.

The Ghoul Stars harbour many hidden threats to Humanity, from the emergent Barghesi to the awakening Necron dynasties. The kill-teams of Fort Pykman favour Malleus tactics; they stand ready to demolish ancient sites should there be even a flicker of suspicion they are linked to Tomb Worlds or alien world nests.

The current watch commander of Fort Pykman is Watch Master Utorian Denash. Danash claims his constant vigilance against the Imperium's xenos foes shall never end.
