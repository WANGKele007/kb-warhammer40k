A multikey is a device available in the Imperium of Man that is designed to digitally circumvent most forms of electronic security mechanisms. As it can open most standard Imperial locks, the multikey is not a standard item for most honest Imperial citizens. For the same reason, though, they are widely sought after by criminals and other disreputable elements.
