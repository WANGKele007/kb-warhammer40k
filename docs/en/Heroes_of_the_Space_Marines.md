Heroes of the Space Marines is an anthology of short stories, published by Games Workshop's Black Library imprint in May 2009 and written by a host of recognisable Warhammer 40,000 authors, edited by Nick Kyme and Lindsey Priestley. It features a prequel story to the Salamanders Series by Nick Kyme, a tie-in to the new Imperial Fists series by Chris Robertson, and new Deathwatch and Night Lords stories, and more. The stories are as follows:

"The Skull Harvest" by Graham McNeill
"Gauntlet Run" by Chris Roberson
"Renegades" by Gav Thorpe
"Honour Among Friends" by Dylan Owen
"Fires of War" by Nick Kyme
"The Labyrinth" by Richard Ford
"Headhunted" by Steve Parker
"And They Shall Know No Fear..." by Darren Cox
"Nightfall" by Peter Fehervari
"One Hate" by Aaron Dembski-Bowden
