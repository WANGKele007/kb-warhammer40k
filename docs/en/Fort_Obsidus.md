Fort Obsidus is a watch fortress of the Deathwatch Chapter that is located in the Segmentum Solar. 

Fort Obsidus is currently commanded by Watch Master Astoren Korr. Following the admittance of Primaris Marines into the Deathwatch following the Ultimaris Decree in the Era Indomitus, he has proven especially adept in the deployment of Repulsor battle tanks. This skill has led to the defeat of the Speed Kults of WAAAGH! Wazdakka, as well as several other recent Ork attacks in the region.
