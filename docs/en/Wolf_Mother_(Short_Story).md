Wolf Mother is a short story published in the Horus Heresy Series. Wolf Mother was originally published simultaneously as a part of the anthology novella Blades of the Traitor and as a stand-alone e-book. It was later released as an audiobook as part of the Black Library's Audio Week 2015. It is now also included in the War Without End anthology novel. Wolf Mother is the sequel to Book 29 Vengeful Spirit.

Synopsis

Aboard the Molech's Enlightenment, amongst the countless civilian refugees that fled the Warmaster's invasion of Molech, a serpent makes its nest. The Serpent God Chaos Cult that brought House Devine to its knees lives on, preying upon the weak and the helpless, and it falls to Alivia Sureka to root out the evil that hides in plain sight. Luckily for her, she has an ally who is more than familiar with such tactics: the lone wolf Knight Errant, Severian.
