A HYLas Beam Cannon, more formally known as the High Yield Laser Beam Cannon, is a powerful, vehicle-mounted Laser Weapon often deployed as a main turret weapon on the armoured vehicles of the Leagues of Votann, such as the Sagitaur ATV. The weapon possesses an extremely long range and both a high rate and high yield of laser fire for excellent stopping power.

However, Kin weapons like the HYLas Beam Cannon are superior in almost every respect to those patterns of Laser Weapons wielded by the agents and military of the Imperium, since the Kin employ superlative materials and methods in their construction and have no fear of further innovating on an ancient STC design. As a result, the HYLas Beam Cannon and its variants often have longer ranges and more stopping power than any of their Imperial equivalents.
