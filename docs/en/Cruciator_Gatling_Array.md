A Cruciator Gatling Array is a Titan-grade, ballistic anti-infantry weapon deployed on the Warmaster Iconoclast-class Heavy Battle Titan. It is a gatling weapon whose spinning barrels unleash a withering hail of death upon any infantry or vehicles who are revealed after this close-combat Titan pattern rips through their static fortifications.
