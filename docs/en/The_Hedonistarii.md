The Hedonistarii are a warband of Chaos Space Marines dedicated to the Chaos God Slaanesh. Though the exact origins of this warband are currently unknown, these Slaanesh-worshipping Renegades often raid the extremities of the Eastern Fringe in the Ultima Segmentum.

The Hedonistarii are fleet-based Heretic Astartes pirates that revel in war for its own sake. So ready are they to tear down law and order wherever they find it that they have a long-standing pact with the anarchist Chaos Lord Drathoni, whose Heldrake eyries are famed amongst the Renegade Warpsmiths of the Ultima Segmentum.

Warband Appearance

Warband Colours

The Hedonistarii warband's colours are purple with black or bronze trim and bronze Chaos fetishes, spikes or studs.

Warband Badge

The Hedonistarii warband's badge is a blasphemous white sigil of the Pleasure God Slaanesh centred upon a field of purple.
