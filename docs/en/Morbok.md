Morbok was an Ork warlord who commanded the WAAAGH! Morbok and was the master of the Ork Empire known as the "Empire of Scrap" in the galactic core. He and his entire WAAAGH! were ultimately wiped out by the Kin of the Grand Orion Compact in the Five Hundred Years' War.

History

It was Morbok who triggered the Five Hundred Years' War with the Grand Orion Compact of the Leagues of Votann when his WAAAGH! overran the hold of Oriâkh's Irongate Kindred and tore apart the Ancestor Core within. 

The Grand Orion Compact, the league of which Oriâkh's Irongate was a member, swore the most widespread Grudge in recorded Kin history for this death of their Votann, hurling all their might against Morbok's Empire of Scrap for more than five Terran centuries, until the last Ork was slain and the insult answered.
