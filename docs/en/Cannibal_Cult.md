A Cannibal Cult is not necessarily a true religious organisation, but a group of Humans whose members eat the flesh of their fellows. The formation of a Cannibal Cult can be the result of Chaos corruption, or may simply arise due to environmental factors that bring on starvation conditions in a Human population.

Cannibal Cults can be created during planetary food shortages, which lead the cults' members to resort to cannibalism of their fellow Humans simply in order to survive. However, while Cannibal Cults will resort often at first to eating the dead, they will also murder living Humans in order to survive as well. 

Some cannibals can be driven mad by their hunger and become crazed hordes that constantly need to eat. When such an incident occurred on the Hive World of Thracian Primaris in 071.M41, the hordes of that world's Cannibal Cults scoured Hive Ferax's entire population. Once they were done, the Cannibal Cults then left the hive city in search of more prey to devour.

Several Cannibal Cults, such as the so-called Corpse Grinder Cults of Necromunda, actively worship Chaos as true Chaos Cults and some consume Human flesh as part of a sorcerous ritual intended to summon Daemons into realspace from the Warp. For instance, the Thousand Sons Traitor Legion has created Cannibal Cults in order to summon Daemons as part of a sorcerous ritual that involves mass cannibalistic slaughter. 

Some Cannibal Cults are altered Genestealer Cults whose members act out the all-consuming hunger of the Tyranid hive fleets through their Brood Mind.

Ogryns are also capable of joining into Cannibal Cults as well.

Notable Cannibal Cults

Corpse Grinder Cults - Corpse Grinder Cults are cannibal Chaos Cults devoted to the service of the Chaos God Khorne active in the hive cities of the Hive World of Necromunda.

Cult of the Flesh Immaculate - This cult was one of several Cannibal Cults active on the Hive World of Avarax during the Recidious Campaign of the Fourth Tyrannic War in the Era Indomitus. It was formed during the food shortages that enveloped the hive cities of Avarax after it was invaded by Hive Fleet Leviathan and the Thousand Sons Traitor Legion. The members of the Cult of the Flesh Immaculate believe Human flesh is purified after the body dies and the cult consumes it to survive as an act of worship towards the Emperor. However, while the members of the Flesh Immaculate eat the dead, they will also kill other Imperials in order to survive as well. Given that Avarax was under assault by the Great Devourer and the forces of Chaos, it is possible the cult was tied to either of them in some way. 

Worshippers of the Purified Dead - This cult was one of several Cannibal Cults active on the Hive World of Avarax during the Recidious Campaign of the Fourth Tyrannic War in the Era Indomitus. It was formed during the food shortages that enveloped the hive cities of Avarax after it was invaded by Hive Fleet Leviathan and the Thousand Sons Traitor Legion. It holds to the same beliefs as its ally, the Cult of the Flesh Immaculate.

Saynay Clan - The Saynay Clan are a cannibalistic Chaos Cult operating in the Koronus Expanse. The Saynays are reavers, mostly consisting of Chaos-worshipping mutants and sorcerers, operating a flotilla of voidships on the outskirts of the Koronus Expanse. Some members of the clan have successfully infiltrated the Korunus Expanse's Imperial colonies, voidships and void stations, where they commit wanton slaughter against the innocents unlucky enough to encounter them. The clan's flotilla attacks isolated voidships and colonies and then bring survivors back aboard their flotilla's ships, where they are sacrificed to the Saynay's god in rituals where the victims are bled, butchered and devoured while still alive. The sacrifices serve to summon Daemons, some of which are then bound into the hulls of the voidships themselves by Saynay sorcerers, while others are bound into people to create Daemonhosts. Yet others (particularly the minor Daemons known as "Blade-things") are left unbound and aid in the abduction of fresh victims (or not, as is their whim). An unlucky few captives are kept alive to introduce new blood to the clan. Sorcerers are not allowed to be leaders -- known as "matriarchs" and "patriarchs" -- among the Saynays, only advisors at most, but one cannot become even a Saynay shipmaster without the support of at least one sorcerer, and the more sorcerers supporting an individual, the more successful within the clan that individual will be. Clan Matriarchs and Patriarchs wear the centuries-old finery rescued from the clan's noble origins, now rotted with the dried gore accumulated over that time. The majority of ships in the Saynay flotilla are in poor repair and not very heavily armed or armoured, so picking on seemingly weak targets from positions of surprise is their most commonly used tactic out of sheer necessity. The decks of these ships are highly reminiscent of slaughterhouses, with dismembered human corpses hanging from hooks all over.
