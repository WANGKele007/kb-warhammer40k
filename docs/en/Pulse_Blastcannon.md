The Pulse Blastcannon is one of the largest Pulse Weapons at the T'au's disposal, employing superheated plasma on a scale that leaves only glowing craters to mark where its targets once stood. 

Pioneered by the finest of T'au's Earth Caste weapons scientists, the Pulse Driver Cannon is a weapon so massive in size and power that it can only be borne into battle by a dedicated artillery Ballistic Suit such as the KV128 Stormsurge. 

The Pulse Blastcannon uses Aggressive Reactive Charge technology (A.R.C) to hyper-accelerate plasma energy. A stream of negatively-charged particles are fired from the gun milliseconds before the main plasma charge, accelerating the plasma blast into the target to explosive effect; a shot that disperses over a wider area the further away the target.

Multi-Phase-Locked Pulse ARC Cannon

The Multi-Phase-Locked Pulse ARC Cannon is a more advanced and potent version of the Pulse Blastcannon designed by the Earth Caste scientist Tu'len that is capable of merging its beam with the beams of other Multi-Phase-Locked Pulse ARC Cannons to create a massive ray of destructive directed energy capable of destroying heavy vehicles, including Titan-grade enemies, in a single shot. On the T'au world of Hul'ann, Multi-Phase-Locked Pulse ARC cannons were used in this manner to destroy an Ork Gargant.
