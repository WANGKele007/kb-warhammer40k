The Emanatus Force Field is an advanced defensive technology found on the Onager Dunecrawlers of the Skitarii Legions. Similar in concept to the Refractor Fields common to the lesser order of the Martian Priesthood of the Adeptus Mechanicus, they disperse hostile energies into the atmosphere, each incoming shell transformed into little more than a flash of actinic blue light and a sudden tang of ozone.

These overlapping energetic force fields are marvels of military science, capable of harmlessly dissipating rounds which can even destroy a Leman Russ Battle Tank.
