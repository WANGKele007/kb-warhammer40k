A Techno-virus Injector is a melee weapon used by the Daemon Engine mounts of Lords Discordant known as Helstalkers. 

Some Helstalkers are outfitted with enormous hypo-armour syringes that -- after piercing the outer shell of their prey -- inject viral scrapcode and daemonic dataphages directly into the machine's circuitry.
