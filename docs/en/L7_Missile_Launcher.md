The L7 Missile Launcher is the primary missile launcher used by the military forces of the Leagues of Votann. It exists in both a man-portable form and as a main turret weapon for Kin vehicles. 

The man-portable version is carried as a specialist weapon by certain members of a squad of Hearthkyn Warriors who show sufficient aptitude to be given additional training in order to wield specialist weaponry. This variant of the L7 can fire seven missiles which can unleash all seven in a single focussed blast for a high-yield explosion or multiple smaller detonations for each missile separately.

The vehicle-mounted variant of the L7 Missile Launcher can be deployed even on lighter Kin vehicles such as the Sagitaur ATV, which is unusually heavily armed for its size. Like the man-portable variant, the vehicle-mounted launcher can fire all seven L7 missiles which can unleash a single focussed blast for a high-yield explosion or multiple smaller detonations for each missile separately.
