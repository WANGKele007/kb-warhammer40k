An Inferno Lance is a Craftworld Aeldari Fusion Weapon wielded as a primary armament by the massive Ghost Warriors known as Skathach Wraithknights. 

A refinement of the same type of fusion weaponry wielded by the Fire Dragons Aspect Warriors, the beam of an Inferno Lance is capable of burning through the armour of even the mightiest enemy war machine in a single, sustained burst of superheated subatomic particles and high-band, ionising radiation. 

To make the best use of the Inferno Lance, they are usually equipped on a Skathach Wraithknight as a pair.
