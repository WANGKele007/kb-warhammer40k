The Razorclaw Beetle is a species of insect native to the Death World of Yanth in the Askellon Sector. Each is a half-metre sized flyer with talons so sharp they have been known to take off a person's head without even slowing the creature as it speeds past. 

Often the first sign of a razorclaw attack is the screams of victims and witnesses as detached limbs fall to the ground and are rapidly devoured by carnivorous flora.
