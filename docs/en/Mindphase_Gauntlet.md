The Mindphase Gauntlet is an advanced Drukhari neural controller in the shape of a gauntlet that saps both the target's mental strength and will. It can stop a rampaging foe in his tracks with a single touch. 

Even monstrous Tyranid Carnifexes can be stopped by the psychic power contained within these gauntlets, though against the most powerful alien and daemonic foes they are not as reliable as when used against humanoid minds more similar to those of the Drukhari.
