House Feardraken is a Loyalist Imperial Knight House of the Imperium. There is very little information in Imperial records about this Knight House, or from what Knight World it originally hails from.

The only thing that is known about them, is that they hail from a water-locked world, where in ancient days they were forced to battle giant sea creatures to protect the lives of their people. In an effort to strike fear into the hearts of their foes, the Knights would later adopt the name of the creatures they battled -- thus House Feardraken was born.

House Colours

The colours of House Feardraken are sky blue and white.

House Arms

The arms of House Feardraken is a white Cog Mechanicum, with an ebon sword centered upon it, pointing upwards.
