The Kindred of Six is a Kindred of the Kronus Hegemony of the Leagues of Votann. The brutally direct Kin of the Kindred of Six pride themselves on their mastery of urban warfare. They are known rivals of Sylâkh's Kindred.

Notes

It is possible that the Kindred of Six is the same as the Kindred known simply as Kindred Six.
