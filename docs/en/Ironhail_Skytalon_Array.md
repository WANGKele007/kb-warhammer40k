The Ironhail Skytalon Array is an antiaircraft weapon deployed on the Primaris Space Marine Impulsor grav assault transport. 

It is composed of twin-linked Icarus Ironhail Heavy Stubbers, a pair of ballistic weapons that unleashes a hail of projectiles at incoming enemy aircraft in an attempt to blow them out of the sky.
