Concussion Knuckles are a type of Leagues of Votann Concussion Weapon that take the form of a pair of brass knuckles.

Like all Concussion Weapons, be they in the shape of warhammers, mauls or even just these brass knuckles, Concussion Knuckles mount so-called "mass driver" technology that magnifies the force of its impact to a colossal degree beyond the simple mass of the knuckles themselves. 

Concussion Knuckles are often wielded as melee weapons by Hearthkyn Salvager Dôzrs.
