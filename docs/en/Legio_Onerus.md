The Legio Onerus is a Traitor Titan Legion that fell to the corruption of the Plague God Nurgle. It served among the Nurglite forces of Chaos that invaded the Realm of Ultramar during the Plague Wars of the Era Indomitus.

Legion History

Notable Campaigns

Plague Wars (ca. 012.M42) - The Legio Onerus aided in the Chaos attack on the world of Espandor in Ultramar, which nearly fell to the Nurglite assault, until the Lord Commander of the Imperium Roboute Guilliman arrived in the region following the end of the first phase of the Indomitus Crusade and counter-attacked with a large Imperial strike force. As battle raged anew across Espandor's surface, the Legio Onerus' Titans were destroyed by Guilliman's forces at the Spires of Priandor. It is unknown if the entire complement of the Legio's Titans were destroyed on Espandor.
