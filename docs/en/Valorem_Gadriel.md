Valorem Gadriel is a Primaris Marine sergeant of the Ultramarines Chapter's 2nd Company who fought in the Recidious Campaign of the Fourth Tyrannic War under the command of Lieutenant Demetrian Titus, newly restored to his Chapter after an absence of more than a standard century.

Following the Recidious Campaign, Gadriel continued to fight alongside Titus until the lieutenant was promoted to serve as the captain of the 2nd Company once more following the death of Captain Sevastus Acheran and Gadriel's friend Chairon on the Hive World of Trygg. Gadriel was promoted to become the ancient of the 2nd Company and became a member of Titus' unique Command Squad comprised of both Ultramarines Astartes and Ultramarian mortals called the Wardens of Ultramar. Together, they were all tasked with protecting and expanding the borders of the Realm of Ultramar to its pre-Horus Heresy borders until it once more encompassed five hundred worlds.

Though one of the youngest Ultramarines ever to hold the honour of serving as his company's ancient, Gadriel has shown himself to be every bit as skilled and courageous as his forebears -- valued as much for his plain-spoken advice as his martial might.

History

Origins

Born into a noble family on the Ultramarian world of Talassar, Valorem Gadriel left his family at the age of ten standard years to embark on a solo pilgrimage to the Ultramarines fortress of Castra Tanagra in the desolate Glaudor Valley. Reaching the fortress on his own earned Gadriel a place in the Ultramarines' selection trials, where he won the right to become a neophyte. After completing his training Gadriel was first assigned to the Ultramarines 6th Company. 

Gadriel's growing accomplishments soon caught the attention of the 2nd Company's Captain Sevastus Acheran, who offered him a spot in the company despite Gadriel's young age and inexperience. Rising through the ranks fast, Gadriel eventually was promoted to sergeant and was given command of his own squad.

Recidious Campaign

Gadriel, alongside his squadmate Brother Chairon, were the Ultramarines of the 2nd Company placed under the command of the newly-returned Lieutenant Demetrian Titus by Captain Sevastus Acheran during the Recidious Campaign of the Fourth Tyrannic War. 

As his duty required, Gadriel was obedient to but distrustful of Titus due to his mysterious background as a Deathwatch Black Shield and his seeming connection to Project Aurora and the Inquisition. When Titus proved reluctant to share knowledge of his past and intelligence surrounding the nature of Project Aurora with his squadmates, Gadriel grew increasingly resentful of his new commander. 

These suspicions came to a head while battling the thrall-band of the Thousand Sons Heretic Astartes commanded by the Sorcerer Lord Imurah present on the Hive World of Avarax where Gadriel requested an official transfer to another squad upon the completion of their mission. 

However, while still on Avarax, an astropath named Neoma who was in charge of the world's astropathic relay accused Titus of being a Heretic who planned to kill the Ultramarines' Chapter Master Marneus Calgar. Finally overcome by his growing suspicions, Gadriel attempted to kill Titus in a mistaken attempt to save Calgar and stop what he believed to be a Chaos plot, but a less credulous Chairon killed the astropath, whose death revealed that the psyker had in fact been possessed by Imurah's sorcery to sow dissension among the Ultramarines. Realising his grave mistake, Gadriel ceased his hostility towards Titus.

After witnessing his manipulation by Imurah, Gadriel took a far more apologetic tone with Titus, which the lieutenant gracefully accepted as he understood that based on what the younger Astartes knew, Gadriel's suspicions were wholly reasonable. Together with Titus and Chairon, the trio went on to defeat Imurah and his allied Tzeentchian Lord of Change on the Cemetery World of Demerium after following the Sorcerer Lord into the Immaterium itself.

Following the Ultramarines' victory on Demerium, Chairon was among those in attendance when Titus received the Laurels of Victory before being reassigned to a new mission under the command of Marneus Calgar. Bidding farewell to Titus, Chairon and Gadriel witnessed his departure.

Defence of Hive Mordica

Later, during the defence of Hive Mordica, Gadriel rallied a battered Astra Militarum line with rousing speeches and strengthened their resolve against an overwhelming Tyranid attack, saving a critical gate and earning Titus' personal recommendation that he carry the 2nd Company's battle standard after the loss of their previous ancient.

Wardens of Ultramar

Following further service with Lieutenant Demetrian Titus at the defence of Hive Mordica following the triumph of the Recidious Campaign, Gadriel was raised to become the ancient of the Ultramarines' 2nd Company after Titus was promoted by Marneus Calgar to a second tenure as the captain of the 2nd Company. This promotion brought sadness as well as glory, however, for it had come in the wake of the deaths of Gadriel's comrade Chairon and his former captain Sevastus Acheran on the world of Trygg, defending that Hive World from the assault of a Genestealer Cult. 

His new position led Gadriel to join the "Wardens of Ultramar", the name given to Titus's unique Command Squad that was composed of both his fellow Astartes and skilled mortals. They were tasked by Primarch Roboute Guilliman with protecting the borders of the Realm of Ultramar and expanding them to encompass the five hundred worlds that had once comprised that realm before the Horus Heresy.

Newly promoted at Titus's express recommendation, despite his relative youth among Ultramarines Veterans, Ancient Valorem Gadriel now hefts the massive banner of the 2nd Company and can always be found in the thickest fighting alongside Titus's own mentor, Bladeguard Veteran Sergeant Metaurus. Both have ample experience fighting alongside Titus, and he values their advice and counsel above all others.
