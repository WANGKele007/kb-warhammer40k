Ukûlak's Kindred is a Kindred of the Trans-Hyperian Alliance of the Leagues of Votann. Ukûlak's Kindred have chosen to honour their league's exploratory traditions by having every last one of their Kin serve at least a Terran decade as Hernkyn during their early years.

Codex: Leagues of Votann (9th Edition), pg. 31
