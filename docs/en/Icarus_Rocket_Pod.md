An Icarus Rocket Pod is a cylindrical rocket pod capable of launching up to 7 surface-to-air rockets that is mounted above the head of a Redemptor Dreadnought and on the Storm Speeder Thunderstrike and is used as a form of personal air defence.

Codex Adeptus Astartes - Space Marines (8th Edition), pp. 83, 166
