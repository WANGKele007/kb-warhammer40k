The Organicists are a sect of the Adeptus Mechanicus who believe that biological organisms are just a form of machine, no different and no worse than their artificial counterparts. Obviously, this is a sect that is most popular among the Mechanicus' genetors and magi biologis. However, taken to an extreme, it can sometimes also result in its own forms of tech-heresy.

History

At first, this philosophy may seem most out of place in an organisation that is based upon the use of so much machinery and metal. For most of the Adeptus Mechanicus, flesh is weak and a hindrance in most aspects -- but not so to this sect. The primary tenet of the Organicists sees biological matter as simple machinery in another guise. The brain is stylised as a complex computer and the body's diverse anatomical and physiological workings as a biomechanical apparatus.

This philosophy's followers, as could be expected, are mostly tech-priests who are genetors, magi biologis and other members of the division of the Mechanicus called the "Adeptus Biologis," studying and improving upon organic matter to improve it in all possible ways, growing all sorts of weird and terrible creations in their bio-vats. Where a typical tech-priest may, at various stages of their career, "upgrade" themselves with bionic limbs, data-repositories, and all sorts of other cybernetic enhancements, an Organicist is far more likely to implant into themself various genetically-engineered glands, increase their muscle mass with stimms, and enhance their senses with laboratory-grown enhanced organs.

The majority of Organicists do not reject the ideas of their brethren simply on the basis of their preference for metal instead of flesh. In some cases, Organicists may even prefer to make use of bionics themselves; it is simply that they do not reject biological components out of hand.

It is for their ultimate acceptance of machinery, whether they choose organic substitutues or not, that whilst certainly considered bizarre, the Organicists are not persecuted by the rest of the Adeptus Mechanicus (along with their proficiency where it comes to servicing older magi's remaining biological components).

Organicist Plots

Examples of the plots that the more extreme tech-priests of the Organicist sect who have fallen into tech-heresy have sought to pursue, include the following:

A magos biologis has created what he believes to be the "perfect soldier." A genetically enhanced warrior that takes less time to produce than a Space Marine, whilst possessing the strength and toughness of the Emperor's finest. The only problem with the new "perfect soldier" is that it is mentally unstable, not reacting well to some of its organic implants, and is currently wandering the magos biologis' lab, killing (brutally) everything it comes across after its bloody escape. It must be stopped, at all costs!

Iggedo is a world in ruins, the forces of Chaos having ravaged it in a brutal raid. It has been rumoured that Fabius Bile was present amongst the forces of the Archenemy, and has left on the planet several of his "experiments." Now, an Inquisitorial team is attempting to round them up and destroy the heretical creations, whilst an Organicist magos biologis seeks to capture some for study of Bile's techniques and implants. The "experiments" themselves aren't that keen on encountering either, and need to flee to the sewers, where they can disappear into obscurity.

Tyranids are considered to be the fastest-evolving creatures in the galaxy, and notoriously difficult to capture. An Organicist has learned that a Rogue Trader has gained several vicious, smaller examples of Tyranid bioforms. Unwilling to pay the extortionate amount the Rogue Trader is demanding, the Organicist has concocted a plan whereby he can storm the bridge and seize control of the ship, along with its precious cargo. The Rogue Trader will not like the idea of course, and then there's the matter of the Tyranid bioforms themselves - it would be terrible if they were to get loose amidst all the confusion...
