The Acastus Pattern Knights were a class of super-heavy Imperial Knights first deployed during the Great Crusade and the Horus Heresy. Perhaps the most heavily-armed and armoured chassis of Imperial Knights ever produced by Imperial technology, all the designs of Acastus Pattern Knights essentially sacrificed the mobility and capacity for melee combat valued by most Knights for greater ranged firepower.

Even at the height of the Great Crusade, Acastus Pattern Knights were incredibly rare and only a few Knight houses were able to field Acastus Pattern armours. They were viewed as symbols of the Omnissiah's favour and the physical embodiment of the Machine God's power and authority by the ancient Mechanicum. Their martial strength was such that Acastus Pattern Knights were able to rival even a Warhound-class Scout Titan, the smallest of the Collegia Titanica's Titans, in combat strength. 

In the 41st Millennium, they are even rarer; ancient reminders of the Adeptus Mechanicus's lost power and technological mastery.
