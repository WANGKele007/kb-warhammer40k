The Endurance-class Light Cruiser is an Imperial Endeavour-class Light Cruiser that has had its weapons batteries refitted to carry lances. A common tactic amongst Imperial Navy fleet admirals is to pair up Endeavours and Endurances for mutual protection, and a fearsome combined firepower. Given that the Endurance is produced in fewer numbers than its variant, a lack of Endurances does not always make this possible. 

It is more likely that a mixed squadron of the two classes will contain two or three Endeavours with a single Endurance in support. Operating in this manner, the Endurance-class Light Cruiser Ad Liberis was responsible for the destruction of nine Ork escorts as the Third War for Armageddon began above the high-gravity world of Pelucidar during a savage three-solar-hour engagement, before a withdrawal was ordered by Admiral Parol. 

The Endurance-class is more commonly found within Battlefleet Armageddon, but it is used by Battlefleet Bakka and the Bastion Fleets of the Segmentum Obscurus. It is known to have also been used by the fleets of the Adeptus Mechanicus.
