Warhammer 40,000: Battlesector is a turn-based tactics video game developed by Black Lab Games and published by Slitherine Software for Windows on 15 July 2021 and for the Playstation 4, Xbox One and Xbox X/S on 2 December 2021.

Gameplay

Warhammer 40,000: Battlesector is a turn-based tactics game, played on a grid-based map with armies comprised of with 10-25 units. The initial 20-mission single-player campaign centres on the recently integrated Primaris Marines of the Blood Angels Chapter fighting the remaining Tyranids of Hive Fleet Leviathan on Baal Secundus, the moon of their Chapter planet of Baal, in the wake of the terrible conflict that was the Devastation of Baal during the Era Indomitus. The player helps Sergeant Carleon and his allies purge the Tyranid infestation on Baal Secundus, and preserve the honour of the Blood Angels.

In May 2025, the Deeds of the Fallen story DLC was released, which contained twelve new missions where the player took on the role of the Sisters of Battle's Order of Our Martyred Lady commanded by Canoness Selicia through a story-driven campaign set against the Orks on the world of Ashenfell. The Orders Militant of the Adepta Sororitas received three new units, the Imagifier, the Castigator Battle Tank, and the Zephyrim -- to fight in the name of the Emperor.

There is player versus player multiplayer, either via online, hotseat, or play-by-mail methods.

In addition to the story content and individual skirmishes capable of using all the factions in the game, there is a "Planetary Annihilation" mode where the player can take on randomly-generated campaigns across a map using their favorite faction. 

There is also the rogue-like "Crusade" mode based on the similar-named game type from the tabletop Warhammer 40,000 miniature war game where the player chooses a faction and a starting force. Then, as the player fights the AI, they are able to grow the army of their chosen faction not only in size and abilities, but also by upgrades for specific unit types. If the player gains enough experience during the battles, they will be given the choice of one of three random upgrades, each of which will make a unit type of their faction more expensive to field though also more powerful.

Release

Battlesector was announced on 9 February 2021, for a release in May 2021. Console versions for PlayStation 4 and Xbox One were due for summer 2021. On 11 May 2021, the game was delayed to mid-July 2021. The game was released for Windows on 22 July 2021 with the Blood Angels Elite and Tyranid Elite content combined with the game's soundtrack as Deluxe Edition content. On 15 November 2021, the console versions were delayed to 2 December 2021. 

Two major downloadable content (DLC) packs were released for the game in 2022: Necrons and Sisters of Battle. In 2023, the Orks and Daemons of Khorne DLC were released, with the T'au and Astra Militarum DLCs following in 2024. Each of these DLCs were faction packs, adding those Warhammer 40,000 armies to the game.

In May 2025, the Deeds of the Fallen story DLC was released, representing the first new story content for the game since its initial release.

Reception

Warhammer 40,000: Battlesector received "mixed or average" reviews according to review aggregator Metacritic.

Luke Plunkett of Kotaku gave a positive review and said: "My favourite thing about this entire game is its voice acting, which takes a solid strategy game and turns it into a memorable 40K experience."

Joe Robinson of PCGamesN summarised the game as "[a] solid turn-based strategy experience that explores the Space Marine power fantasy, marred by humdrum mission design."

Jon Bolding of IGN gave a positive review and said: "The momentum-based tactical system and broad customizability of the forces you lead, combined with randomization in the mission setups, gives you a fun campaign with a tasty side dish of multiplayer skirmish."

Matt Bassil of Wargamer reviewed the Necrons expansion and summarized: "The Warhammer 40,000 Battlesector – Necrons DLC is a well-made unit pack, but a unique story or a better realised campaign mode would go a long way to improving its overall value. The toys are all there, but they could do with a better box."

Battlesector was included on Eurogamer's list of "The best Warhammer 40k games to play in 2022". PC Gamer listed the game 10th on its list of best Warhammer 40,000 games.
