A Harlequin's Caress is a wrist-mounted close combat weapon utilised exclusively by the enigmatic Aeldari Harlequins. 

This weapon encases the user's hand in a phase field that allows him to reach through his foe's armour and pluck out their heart as easily as though he were running his fingers through thin air. 

This weapon is commonly employed by the sinister and deadly Solitaires within a Harlequin Masque.
