Ghost Weapons are the large, melee Power Weapons used by the Craftworld Aeldari's various Ghost Warriors.

The rudimentary sentience within the Spirit Stone core of each Ghost Weapon, whether it takes the form of an axe, glaive or sword, guides the wielder's blows into their target's weak spots. 

Types of Ghost Weapons

Ghostsword - The Ghostsword is a large melee Power Weapon used by Asuryani Wraithblades. They are the smaller version of Ghostglaives. Usually wielded in pairs, they leave glowing traces in the air as they are swung. Like all Ghost Weapons, they can help guide the wielder's blows into the most vulnerable areas of a foe.
Ghostaxe - The Ghostaxe is a large melee Power Weapon used by Craftworld Aeldari Wraithblades. They are the axe variant of a Ghostsword. Like all Ghost Weapons, they can help guide the wielder's blows into a foe's most vulnerable areas.
Ghostglaive - The Ghostglaive is a large, heavy, melee Power Weapon wielded by Wraithknights and Wraithlords. These great blades can even wound the most potent of Greater Daemons in combat successfully.
