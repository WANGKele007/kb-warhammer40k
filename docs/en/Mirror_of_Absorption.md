A Mirror of Absorption, also called a Contorted Epitome, is a relic of Slaanesh that is used to provide a powerful protection against psychic or other magical assaults on the battlefield.

Role

Rivalry is common among the Daemons of Slaanesh, but those few that can abide each other's presence are often assigned as stewards of an ornate Mirror of Absorption.

These semi-sentient relics are taken from the Palace of Slaanesh in the Realm of Chaos and used in battle to absorb the magical energies of the Dark Prince's foes, before turning them back with terrifying effectiveness...
