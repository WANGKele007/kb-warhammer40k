The Bastion Serpentis is an Inquisitorial fortress of the Calixian Conclave located on Lachesis, one of the two moons of the Calixis Sector capital world of Scintilla. It serves as the base of the Calixian Conclave's Tyrantine Cabal, which is dedicated to unravelling the mystery of the Tyrant Star that plagues the sector.

The Bastion Serpentis is a bleak Imperial fortress of age-polished black stone jutting from the surface of Lachesis. Few people know of its existence, and those who do not are kept away from the moon by dire warnings about geological instability. Inquisitor Lord Anton Zerbe of the Ordo Hereticus, the leader of the Tyrantine Cabal, can normally be found at the Bastion Serpentis, as can many of the Inquisitors currently serving the interests of the cabal.
