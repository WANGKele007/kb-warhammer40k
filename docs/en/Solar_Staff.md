The Solar Staff is a powerful Necron directed energy and melee weapon that can replace any Necron noble's Staff of Light. It is only available to the nobles of the Nephrekh Dynasty.

Forged within the Heliaconvarium of Aryand, the Solar Staff burns with the light of truth and honour. Set loose, the staff's energies blaze outward in a mighty flare, as though a new sun was born. 

The darkness is driven back by this false dawn, and the foe reels as their eyes are blinded and their deceptions are laid bare.
