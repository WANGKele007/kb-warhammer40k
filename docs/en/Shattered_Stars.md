The Shattered Stars is the name of a region of the galactic core located to the east of the borders of the space of the Urani-Surtr Regulates of the Leagues of Votann. 

Bogg Da Freeboota King is an Ork Freebooter who commands a seemingly endless empire of ragtag Greenskin pirates whose fleets make constant raids into URSR space from the Shattered Stars.
