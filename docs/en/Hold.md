A hold is the term used by the Leagues of Votann for any major, fortified dwelling of the Kin, whether it be a city, void station, voidship, industrial outpost or even an entire planet. All Kin live, train and toil within their home hold when not abroad amongst the stars for trade, scouting, mineral prospecting or war.

The Kin habitually load apparently simple terms such as "hold" with nuanced meaning, being disinclined to even waste words. Thus, while the term is used throughout the Leagues of Votann, it can refer to wildly different structures and locations. Some holds are fusions of fortification, city, industrial complex and strip mine, the largest of which may sprawl across -- or honeycomb beneath -- much of a so-called "Hold World"'s surface. Others may be heavily armed void stations, chains of domes scattered through asteroid belts, nomadic harvesting fleets of voidships, mobile matter-energy syphoning plants riding the fringes of black holes to harvest their accretion discs, or even stranger marvels of the Kin's advanced technology.

Each Kindred -- the basic political unit of one of the Kin's leagues equivalent to a clan or nation-state -- may possess one or more holds in their territory, depending on the population of the Kindred. A Kindred's hold may boast bustling cityscapes, industrial and military powerhouses, and many of the Kin's massive voidships. 

Every hold generally possesses four crucial components -- crucibles where new generations of Kin are cloned and gestated; a Forge where the engineers and artificers of the Kin known as the Brôkhyr craft the weapons, equipment, tools, vehicles and technologies required to sustain them; a Hearth that is the power source that provides energy to the hold; and a Fane, the device or node used by the Grimnyr or "Living Ancestors" to interface with the Votann, the self-aware machine intelligences that govern the Leagues. 

Notable Holds

Broken Triplets - The Broken Triplets is a Hold World of the Trans-Hyperian Alliance. The Broken Triplets are actually a trio of worlds found in the galactic core where the three planets were smashed together during some ancient catastrophe, and the colossal quantities of debris from their demise still whirls and ricochets in a vast cloud to this day, trapped by the gravitic anomaly that caused the disaster. The Broken Triplets is home to the hold of Sunder Stair, a void station that exists precariously yet safely at the heart of the three shattered worlds.

Brôkhfyre - The hold of Brôkhfyre is dug deep into the Ymyr Conglomerate Hold World of Tamâkh's planetary mantle -- insulated against the exotic and deadly energy spectra that pour from the world's nearby star. What violent apotheosis the tortured star is undergoing, none can say, but for three Terran millennia now the Kin of the Orksbane Kindred have captured the energies of its raging solar flares using kilometres-high stellar vanes. These energies power their Forge, which is amongst the greatest in all the Leagues of Votann, renowned for the amazing weapons of war it can produce.

Crimson Gulf - Crimson Gulf is a Hold World of the Greater Thurian League. It is famed for its sheer size and nearly impentrable defences, much like its western Thurian neighbour, Ûrvymm's Bulwark.

Cynder Span - Cynder Span is a Hold World of the Ymyr Conglomerate whose Forge is famous for producing machnes and other advanced technologies of sublime might.

Delvegard - Delvegard is a Hold World of the Ghulo Industrial Complex.

Demmâk's Haven - Demmåk's Haven is a Hold World.

Grandhall - Grandhall is a Hold World of the Greater Thurian League.

Hold Silver Six - Hold Silver Six is a Hold World of the Greater Thurian League.

Hyvôk's Kindred - Hyvôk's Kindred is a Hold World of the Leagues of Votann. Its people were once saved from destruction by the famed Hernkyn adventurer Sîmmka Farstryd and in thanks, a unique void suit called the Wayfarer's Grace was crafted for her.

Jâluk - Jâluk was a Hold World of the Ymyr Conglomerate that was claimed by a Warp Storm. The only remnant of its people is the Ymyr relic armour crest known as the Last Crest of Jâluk.

Obsyd Gate - Obsyd Gate is a Hold World of the Greater Thurian League. 

Sunder Stair - Sunder Stair is the name of the Trans-Hyperian Alliance's hold inhabited by Nôthka's Kindred in the Broken Triplets. Sunder Stair sits at the heart of the Broken Triplets gravitic anomaly behind a breathtaking bulwark of interwoven force fields. These both shelter the immense void station that serves as the hold and also maintain clear space lanes for its voidcraft to take in and out of the system. Not only do the Kin of Sunder Stair enjoy the natural defence of the ruin-belt -- which would swiftly cripple invading voidcraft -- but they have also spent Terran centuries mining the exposed innards of the Broken Triplets.

Tamâkh - Tamâkh is a Hold World of the Ymyr Conglomerate. The hold of Brôkhfyre is dug deep into Tamâkh's planetary mantle -- insulated against the exotic and deadly energy spectra that pour from the world's nearby star. What violent apotheosis the tortured star is undergoing, none can say, but for three Terran millennia now the Kin of the Orksbane Kindred have captured the energies of its raging solar flares using kilometres-high stellar vanes. These energies power their Forge, which is amongst the greatest in all the Leagues of Votann, renowned for the amazing weapons of war it can produce.

Ûrvymm's Bulwark - Ûrvymm's Bulwark is a Hold World of the Greater Thurian League. It is famed for its sheer size and nearly impentrable defences, much like its eastern Thurian neighbour, Crimson Gulf.

Vônyk's Hall of Hammers - Vônyk's Hall of Hammerers is a hold of the Ymyr Conglomerate whose Forge is famous for producing machines and other advanced technologies of sublime might.

Ymus-Solaryn - Ymus-Solaryn is a hold of the Ymyr Conglomerate whose Forge is famous for producing machines and other advanced technologies of sublime might.
