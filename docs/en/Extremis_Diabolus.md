Extremis Diabolus is a High Gothic term used by Inquisitors to level an accusation of consorting with Daemons or being under the corrupting influence of the Daemonic or the Warp. It is the most serious charge that an Inquisitor can bring to bear against an accused and it is most often levelled by a member of the Ordo Malleus. The term can be used to accuse a person, organisation or even an entire planet.

Witchcraft, sorcery, pursuing the development of forbidden Warp-corrupted technologies and heresy against the Emperor by turning to the service of Chaos are all grounds for the pronouncement of an accusation of Extremis Diabolus. If the accusation is proved, the punishment is immediate execution if the accused is an individual or an Exterminatus action if the charge is brought against a whole world.

Notable Instances

Inquisitor Ahenobarbus of the Ordo Hereticus was dispatched to the world of Entymion IV after it suffered from a Daemonic incursion and he declared the entire planet Extremis Diabolis. Afterwards, he conducted an Exterminatus action upon the world with his personal warfleet.

The Puritan Inquisitor Torquemada Coteaz of the Ordo Malleus declared his own mentor, Inquisitor Lord Laredian, to be Extremis Diabolus due to his attempt to convince his conclave to use Chaos-corrupted crystalline relics to assist the Inquisition's fight against the Archenemy, thus treading the path of the Radical. Despite being an Inquisitor Lord, Laredian was not immune to the charge and had to face his accuser, and was ultimately found guilty and executed by Coteaz on the spot. Afterwards the local conclave immediately appointed Coteaz to take his mentor's place as an Inquisitor Lord.
