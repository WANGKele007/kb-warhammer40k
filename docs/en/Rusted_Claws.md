The Rusted Claws is a warband or vectorium of Heretic Astartes of the Death Guard Traitor Legion's 2nd Plague Company.

Warband History

Notable Campaigns

The Smog of War (Unknown Date) - Vectoriums of the Death Guard Legion's 2nd Plague Company, including the Rusted Claws and the Tainted Lung, engaged the Iron Hands of Clan Kaargul and Astra Militarum armoured regiments on Tsarvia II. As the grinding tank war starts to turn against them, the Death Guard set light to the sprawling chem-works of Tsarvia Secundus. Malignant Plaguecasters channelled the resultant fumes into banks of corrosive smog that wreathed the Death Guard tanks and dissolved the hulls of their Imperial foes. Able to withstand the hideous conditions in a way that their enemies cannot, the Death Guard soon crushed Tsarvia II beneath their armoured treads.

Warband Appearance

Warband Colours

The Rusted Claws' power armour is the same as that of the Death Guard, composed of decayed and cracked plates of ceramite. The Rusted Claws Chaos Space Marines, like those of the Death Guard, often wear grotesquely warped suits of antique Mark III Iron Power Armour.

Warband Badge

The Rusted Claws' warband badge is usually a modified version of the standard Mark of Nurgle, similar to that used by the rest of the Death Guard.
