The Estate Imperium is a division of the Adeptus Administratum described as "the million-strong records office of the Administratum." Presumably its purpose is to keep and furnish records of Imperial adepta when they are called for by the relevant arms of the Adeptus Terra. The Estate Imperium is also one of the overseers of the vast Departmento Munitorum. It is an ancient organisation dating back to the Unification Wars. 

The division's head carries the title of Chancellor of the Estate Imperium. The Estate Imperium's importance is such that its leader sometimes holds one of the temporary, rotating seats in the Senatorum Imperialis of the High Lords of Terra. 

In 945.M41, the Chancellor of the Estate Imperium was Seward Rosencranz, while by 999.M41 it was an official named Brach, who died during the 13th Black Crusade. His former seat on the Senatorum Imperialis remained unfilled for a time after until the Adeptus Custodes Captain-General Trajann Valoris claimed it during the fifth standard year of the Indomitus Crusade in the Era Indomitus.
