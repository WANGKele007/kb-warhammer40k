The 5th Betan Tygers are an elite Tempestus Scions regiment of the Militarum Tempestus. Little else is known about them in Imperial records.

Regimental History

The Tyger and the Bull (939.M41) - The Tempestus Scions of the 5th Betan Tygers scramble to defend the Ogryns of Drannon Prime from the massive Ork WAAAGH! that descends upon it.
