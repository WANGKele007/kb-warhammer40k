Utorian Denash is a Deathwatch Chapter watch master who is the current watch commander of the Fort Pykman watch fortress in the Ghoul Stars of the Imperium Nihilus. Denash is a hard-like Puritan who claims his constant vigilance against the Imperium's xenos foes shall never end.
