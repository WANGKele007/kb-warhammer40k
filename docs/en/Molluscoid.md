A Molluscoid is a large, snail-like Daemon of Nurgle that is protected by a hard shell very similar to that of Terran gastropods. A Molluscoid is surrounded by the virulently infectious fluids and rancid smell that defines all the daemonic servants of the Plague Lord. 

Horticulous Slimux, the daemonic Herald of Nurgle who serves as the "Grand Cultivator" of the Garden of Nurgle in the Realm of Chaos, makes use of a Molluscoid he has affectionately named Mulch as his steed. 

Molluscoids leave extraordinarily toxic and infectious trails of slime in their wake wherever they move.
