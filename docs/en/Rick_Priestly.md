Rick Priestley is a game designer for Games Workshop, who has worked for the company since its inception.

Priestley was the original designer and writer of Warhammer for Games Workshop.

He has worked extensively on various Warhammer 40,000 rulebooks and sourcebooks, including the fourth edition of Warhammer 40,000.
