An Inquisitorial Cruiser is a type of Imperial strike cruiser that is employed by the forces of the Inquisition. 

For all the vast power and authority wielded by an Inquisitor, such august personages will typically rely on guile and subtlety to accomplish their missions. It is most common for them to travel in relative obscurity, relying on little more than the services of a Rogue Trader or passage aboard a passing Imperial merchantman to ferry them between worlds. However, when addressing issues of particular urgency, an Inquisitor has the ability to call upon any warship of the Navis Imperialis or the Adeptus Astartes, or even the might of an entire fleet or Space Marine Chapter if absolutely necessary.

On occasion Inquisitors will utilise one of their own organisation's specially-constructed warships. The Inquisition can summon vast resources, and its seat on Terra enables the relatively few vessels they actually possess to call upon all the technological advances and fabrication methods available to the shipwrights and fleets of Mars itself. While a few standard patterns are used by the Inquisition, they are just as often purpose-constructed and optimised for a particular requirement. Inquisitors and their retinues of Acolytes and Throne Agents must be prepared for any contingency, and these ships are faster, better armed and better protected than warships typically utilised by the Navis Imperialis. 

While not specifically or officially Black Ships per se, Inquisitors are not above referring to their Inquisitorial Cruisers as such precisely because of the awe and fear the mere mention of such vessels incur. Inquisitors will typically utilise these ships only as required for their mission, though some may have such vessels and their crews detailed to them for extended periods of time. Inquisitor Lord Torquemada Coteaz is but one of a number of Inquisitor Lords that have an Inquisitorial Cruiser permanently detailed at his or her express disposal.

Inquisitorial Cruisers are armed with port and starboard weapons batteries, a dorsal Bombardment Cannon and attack craft launch bays that can house Thunderhawk gunships also in the prow. Some Inquisitorial Cruisers are heavily modified to meet the specific mission requirements of their ordo, while others replace the launch bays with torpedo tubes and the dorsal Bombardment Cannon with lance arrays.

Notable Imquisitorial Cruisers

Imperator Illuminatio - The Imperator Illuminatio is an adapted Inquisitorial Cruiser constructed in the pattern of the voidships of the Adeptus Mechanicus' Basilikon Astra fleet.

Umbrarius Inquis -- The Umbrarius Inquis is an Inquisitorial Cruiser that has been adapted from the standard pattern of strike cruiser used by the Adeptus Astartes.

Obsession for Integrity 

Paladin's Shadow - The Paladin's Shadow is an Inquisitorial Cruiser commanded by the Ordo Xenos Inquisitor Kyria Draxus, who has equipped it with shields based off the Craftworld Aeldari's Holo-Field technology. During the Indomitus Crusade, it served with Battle Group Kallides of Indomitus Crusade Fleet Primus as they fought the Necrons in the Pariah Nexus.
