The Skull Harvest is a tournament fought between Champions of Chaos that takes place upon the world of New Badab in the Maelstrom Warp rift at unknown intervals. It is presided over by the master of the Red Corsairs Heretic Astartes, the Chaos Lord Huron Blackheart, who also rules the planet. Many alien mercenaries and Human Chaos warbands gather, and the warbands' Chaos Champions (or the leaders' chosen champions) fight to the death, till only the best three remain. 

These warriors fight in a free-for-all space known as the Arena of Thorns, till one triumphs. The failed combatants' skulls are presented to Huron Blackheart, and adorn the spikes before his throne outside the arena. The victor of the Skull Harvest is accepted by all the warbands present as their new Chaos Lord and receives recognition from Blackheart as the champion deserving the honour of temporary command over a Chaos Warfleet provided by the Red Corsairs.

In ca. 999.M41, the Iron Warriors Warsmith Honsou won the tournament in a bid to create a ground and naval large enough to exact his vengeance upon Ultramarines Captain Uriel Ventris. Thanks to his victory, his army swelled from a handful of Iron Warriors Heretic Astartes to more than 17,000 Chaos Space Marines, xenos mercenary and Lost and the Damned mortal warriors, and he was granted command of several powerful Chaos warships drawn from the Red Corsairs' own fleet. Honsou used these forces to unleash his Invasion of Ultramar in 854.999.M41.
