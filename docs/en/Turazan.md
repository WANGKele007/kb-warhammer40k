Turazan was a Chaos Space Marine Warpsmith of the piratical Red Corsairs warband in the Era Indomitus. His abilities earned him a high rank as an officer among those Heretic Astartes.

During the Red Corsairs' search for the Tzeentchian Chaos artefact known as the Ebon Talon, needed to unlock the secrets of the Blackstone Fortress granted them by Abaddon the Despoiler before the Terran Crusade, Turazan sided with the his fellow officer Verngar the Apostate during his power struggle with the Red Corsairs' Chaos Lord Huron Blackheart. However, Turazan was burnt alive by the power of the Ebon Talon when Blackheart's Daemonic Familiar, the Hamadrya, deflected the artefact's power onto the unlucky Warpsmith. 

History

During the Red Corsairs' assault on the Invaders Chapter's homeworld of Ogrys, Verngar ordered Turazan to bring the Ebon Talon to him aboard the captured Ultramarines battle barge Macragge's Honour which served as his command ship. Blackheart had been sure that Verngar had not learned how to access the relic's power and that this was why he had not immediately used the relic to activate the warband's Blackstone Fortress. But thanks to the aid of Turazan and the sorcerer Garlon Souleater, Verngar proved able to unleash the Ebon Talon's fiery destruction. Verngar, driven to anger by their refusal to follow his orders during the assault on Ogrys, moved to loose the artefact's Warpflame upon the Wolf of Fenris, the strike cruiser of the Red Corsairs' sub-warband known as the Red Wolves, but Blackheart intervened to prevent the unnecessary destruction of a potent asset of the Red Corsairs.

Yet Verngar, in a rage further fuelled beyond all reason by the curse of the Ebon Talon, refused to relent even as Blackheart sensed that it was the Ebon Talon that was driving his subordinate's actions. As the Heretic Astartes continued to argue, the Wolf of Fenris suddenly attacked the Macragge's Honour, as the Red Wolves had divined Verngar's plans and had no intention of meekly accepting their fate without a fight.

The Red Wolves' attack distracted Verngar long enough that Blackheart struck his rival with a blast from the built-in flamer of his Tyrant's Claw, ready to openly move to eliminate his rival. Verngar recovered from the blast and prepared to unleash the power of the Ebon Talon at Blackheart. However, Blackheart's connection to his Daemonic Familiar known as the Hamadrya allowed him to psychically deflect the relic's power onto Turazan. As the Warpsmith's body was consumed from the inside out by the Warpflame of Tzeentch, Blackheart ended the fight by seriously wounding Verngar and then lopping off the hand with which he held the Ebon Talon, severing the link between Verngar and the relic.
