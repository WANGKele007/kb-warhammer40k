The Legio Dominatus is a Loyalist Titan Legion of the Collegia Titanica, one of the military arms of the Adeptus Mechanicus. It served as part of the Imperial Crusade fleet led by the Dark Angels Supreme Grand Master Azrael to the Space Wolves Chapter's homeworld of Fenris in ca. 999.M41 to take part in the campaign remembered as the Siege of the Fenris System.
