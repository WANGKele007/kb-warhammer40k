Truesilver Armour is a type of physical protection used by the Grey Knights for their vehicles.

Strands of sanctified silver and iron have been embedded into the vehicle's armour, rendering it unbearable to the touch of daemons and Chaos-tainted psykers.

Sources

Codex: Grey Knights (5th Edition), pg. 61
