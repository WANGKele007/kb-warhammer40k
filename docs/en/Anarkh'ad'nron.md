Anarkh'ad'nron is a Bloodthirster, a Greater Daemon of the Blood God Khorne. His participation in a combined daemonic host, comprised of followers of all four patron Chaos Gods, is briefly mentioned in records of an unknown campaign launched by Chaos against the forces of the Imperium of Man. Leading a cohort of bestial Bloodletters, he followed his master, a Herald of Khorne known as Deathrender, in a campaign of slaughter and mayhem.
