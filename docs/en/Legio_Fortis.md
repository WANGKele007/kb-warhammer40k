The Legio Fortis is a Titan Legion of the Collegia Titanica.

Legion History

Notable Campaigns

Plague Wars (ca. 111.M42) - The Legio Fortis was counted among the Imperial forces dedicated to defending the Realm of Ultramar during the Plague Wars from the forces of Chaos in service to the Plague God Nurgle.
