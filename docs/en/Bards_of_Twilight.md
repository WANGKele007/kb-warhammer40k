The Bards of Twilight are an order of mystical Craftworld Aeldari poets and storytellers. They are tasked with reciting the ancient Aeldari epic poem known as the Asuryata once in each generation.

The tale is composed of many well-known parables intended to pass on important teachings about life and Aeldari culture. The Asuryani use the text to derive guiding principles for both their day-to-day lives and their military campaigns.
