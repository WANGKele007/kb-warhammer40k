Duralium is a metallic substance mined by the Imperium for use in the Plasma Reactors of voidships and void stations as well as to carry out repairs. 

One of the primary worlds where its ore was once mined is the Derelict World of Bairsten Prime where much of the War of the Spider was fought in the Era Indomitus.
