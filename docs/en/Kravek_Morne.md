Kravek Morne is a Warsmith of the Iron Warriors Traitor Legion who took command of an Iron Warriors assault upon the Imperium during the Era Indomitus. 

History

Perturabo, Daemon Primarch of the Iron Warriors, does not really like or respect any of his commanders, but a precious few have at least earned the Daemon Primarch's trust with a consistent record of victories -- and among their rarefied kind, Kravek Morne stands out as a particularly callous and brutal warlord. 

Morne does not possess the arrogance and lethargy of those veterans of the Long War who fought at the gates of the Imperial Palace during the Siege of Terra, having been forced into the Iron Warriors' service in the 39th Millennium, a "mere" 2,000 years before the Great Rift tore across the galaxy, and it took Morne only 500 standard years before his natural gift for devastating head-long assaults secured him a place among the Iron Warriors' commanding Warsmiths.

Long standard years of fighting in the deadliest frontal assaults imaginable has turned Morne's body into a siege weapon as powerful as any tank or Daemon Engine in his arsenal, built around an ancient suit of Cataphractii Terminator Armour from the Horus Heresy that he calls his Iron Mantle. It bristles with weaponised mechadendrites and augmetics slaved to the armour's Mind Impulse Unit, though none are as deadly as Last Argument -- his personally-crafted Thunder Hammer that crackles with infernal power.

Morne is at the forefront of the Iron Warriors' new campaign against the Imperium during the Era Indomitus and he has command of several legions' worth of Iron Warriors and their mortal allies.
