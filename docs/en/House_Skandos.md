House Skandos is a Loyalist Imperial Knight House of the Imperium. There is little information on this knightly house in official Imperial records or what Knight World they originally hailed from. Like most Knight Worlds, House Skandos maintains its own distinctive culture and traditions. By long tradition the nobles of House Skandos are known as Jarls, and their household detachments as Jarlhosts.
