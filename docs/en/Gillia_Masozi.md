Gillia Masozi is a flight lieutenant of the Navis Imperialis' Aeronautica Imperialis, the division of the Imperium's navy that provides air support to the soldiers of the Astra Militarum engaged in ground combat. Flight Lieutenant Masozi has been seconded to the warband assembled by Inquisitor Grendyl of the Ordo Hereticus to combat the Nurglish Chaos infestation of Hive Tertium on the Moebian Hive World of Atoma Prime. 

She is the pilot of a Valkyrie gunship she has named Storm Raptor that is based on the flight deck of the requisitioned Rogue Trader frigate Mourningstar. She serves as the primary means of transport planetside for Acolytes of Grendyl's warband who are tasked with carrying out combat missions against the Cult of Admonition and the Traitoris Militarum of the Moebian 6th Regiment in the lower levels of Hive Tertium. She is the best pilot the warband has at their disposal. Storm Raptor is known and feared among the Scabs of the Moebian 6th. 

Among Masozi's duties are training new Valkyrie pilots to provide transport for the warband. Her officer rank allows her to operate with some autonomy in the field, within reason. Despite the perquisites that come with being an officer of the Navis Imperialis, even a relatively junior one, Masozi has been known to complain about all the paperwork required at her rank.

While flight has always been Gillia Masozi's true passion, her desire for excitement also extends to gambling. During downtime aboard the warband's mobile base, the frigate Mourningstar, Masozi will play cards with the more veteran Acolytes among the warband, and is not above cheating at times. She also enjoys the Calthan brandy she buys from the ship's purser Alice Hallowette. Most of her time is spent either on missions or preparing for them by performing maintenance, tune-ups and instituting improvements to her beloved Valkyrie gunship, which she has named Storm Raptor.

Like many pilots, Masozi values instinct, practicality and improvisation over intensive planning or following strict military rules and procedures. This attitude has at times caused friction with other senior members of Inquisitor Grendyl's retinue, including the tech-priest Hadron Omega-7-7, who is frazzled by her attempts to "improve" the sacred technology of her Valkyrie, and the intelligence analyst Darius Melk who finds her tendency towards improvisation dangerous and is irritated by her lack of respect for his noble pedigree. 

Masozi is also known for giving gifts to those Acolytes of the warband who are zealous believers in the Imperial Creed, including a piece of stone Masozi claimed had been part of a a saint's statue, and a holy canticle of Saint Alicia Dominica's wrath.

During return missions from dropping off an Acolyte strike team in Hive Tertium, Masozi sometimes finds herself being tailed by what Explicator Orgustine Zola has called the "Sky Reavers." The Sky Reavers are a criminal gang of airborne raiders, smugglers, and bandits. They often tail the various Valkyries flying about the hive city on Imperial business, seeking new opportunities to make a major score on a valuable airborne shipment across the hive.
