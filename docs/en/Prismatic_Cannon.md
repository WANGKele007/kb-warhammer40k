A Prismatic Cannon is a heavy Aeldari weapon that uses a scaled-down version of the Prism Cannon technology utilised by Aeldari Fire Prism grav-tanks.

The Prismatic Cannon is utilised only by the mysterious Harlequins, and uses multiple laser arrays set around a fashioned shard of psychocrystal to project searing beams of directed energy into foes. The weapon's apertures can be adjusted in order to focus or broaden its destructive might at will. These weapons are usually mounted upon specialised open-topped, anti-gravitic Harlequin gunships known as Voidweavers.
