ca. M18-M20 The Twilight Cults - Those Aeldari leading the new paradigm of total self-indulgence and the pursuit of hedonism rise in status and power within the Aeldari Empire until they can secede entirely from the Aeldari settlements in realspace. They take up permanent residence in the Webway, from which they can plumb the depths of their decadence undisturbed by the puritans and weaklings among their own kind who warn of the dangers of such a path for the future survival of the Aeldari species. Over time their sovereign estates within the Labyrinth Dimension grow into entire sub-realms of the Webway, each of which is powered by the energy of a sun "stolen" from realspace. The star systems in realspace plunged into darkness by the Aeldari's sun-theft wither and die in the freezing cold of the void, often condemning millions of other species to death, but the self-centred Aeldari care nothing for the damage they cause across the galaxy.

ca. M20 Expansion of Human Colonisation - In this period, Mankind spreads across the stars from the Sol System and its early colonies, becoming widely dispersed and divergent. There is evidence of many wars, but none that threatened the stability of rapidly-expanding Human space. The few still-existing records from this period list intelligent xenos enemies long since extinct, along with more familiar names such as the Aeldari and Orks. Interplanetary trade was established between the Human colonies and great fleets carried goods to and from the ends of the galaxy. As Human-settled planets became overpopulated, the recently invented construction mediums of plasteel, plascrete, ferrocrete and rockcrete were used to build colossal cities and arcologies ironically intended to allow greater population support with less environmental damage: the first proto-hives.
