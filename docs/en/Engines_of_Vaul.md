The Engines of Vaul is the name given by the Craftworld Aeldari in the Aeldari Lexicon to their super-heavy armoured vehicles. The name refers to Vaul, the ancient Aeldari god of the forge and craft. 

The term is generally applied to the following vehicles:

Cobra
Scorpion
Storm Serpent
Void Spinner
Tempest
Deathstalker
