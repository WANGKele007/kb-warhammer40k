The Sardonites are a lizard-like, intelligent alien race of the Milky Way Galaxy. Imperial scholars have calculated their homeworld to be located somewhere in the sparsely-populated southwest of Segmentum Tempestus. The Proton Disruptors used by Sardonite raiding ships are powerful weapons that fire an intense beam of alternating protons and electrons which is designed to do heavy damage to a voidship's electrical systems.
