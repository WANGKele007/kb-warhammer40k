An Equinox Power Blade, also called an Equinox Power-blade Case, was one of a set of twin Power Swords used by the Blood Angels Legion's elite Legion Assault Marines known as Dawnbreakers during the Great Crusade and Horus Heresy. 

The set was divided into a "Sunrise Blade" and a "Sunset Blade," each of which had slightly different combat characteristics. Each blade is fashioned to serve a ritualised form of combat adapted from the battle practices of the barbarian Human tribes of Baal -- the "Path of the Sun."

The pair were wielded together by a single Astartes in melee combat. The Sunrise Blade was effective at shredding flesh, while the Sunset Blade was capable of sundering even the hardest of substances.
