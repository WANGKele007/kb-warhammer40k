The Red Knights are a relatively new Loyalist Chapter of Space Marines that are genetic successors of the Blood Angels, created during an unknown Founding.

Almost nothing is known about this Chapter in Imperial records save that it has clashed with the Night Lords warband of Chaos Lord Cull, the so-called "Prince of Thorns," in orbit above the planet of Quelos.

Like many of their fellow Blood Angels successors of the Sanguinary Brotherhood, the Red Knights tend to display a violent savagery in battle. Created as they were from the Blood Angels' gene-seed, they carry the genetic flaws inherited from Sanguinius.
