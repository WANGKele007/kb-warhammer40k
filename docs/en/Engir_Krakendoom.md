Engir Krakendoom is a Wolf Lord of the Space Wolves Chapter of the Adeptus Astartes, one of twelve legendary warriors who lead the Great Companies into battle. Krakendoom's sigil is that of the Sea Wolf and his Great Company is called the Seawolves.

Coming from a proud line of warlords who rule over the southernmost isles of Fenris, Engir Krakendoom is born of hardy stock indeed. The people of the south, dark of skin and temperament, are expert explorers and oarsmen. They ply the infested oceans of Fenris in search of sea monsters; harpoons and long-axes at the ready. It is said that Engir was given the name "Krakendoom" after he was borne under the waves by a giant, many-limbed sea devil.

When man and monster eventually resurfaced, it was Engir alone who roared in triumph, later claiming to have throttled the beast with its own tentacles.

The Seawolves prefer to go to war in armoured transports, and boast many Swift Claws among their number who act as outriders for the main force. Engir's warriors excel in void warfare, especially ship-to-ship conflicts and boarding actions; when the Space Wolves ply the Sea of Stars it is usually Engir Krakendoom's Great Company that leads the way.

Notable Campaigns

From Out of the Warp it Came (877.M41) - The Seawolves were close at hand when a vast USM-class anomaly breached the yawning Warp Gate of Sloth. Twelve thousand miles of heaving, twisted flesh, the entity defied classification until a female xenobiologist realised that the anomaly was once a void whale. Since swimming the unnatural tides of the Warp it had become something even more terrifying. As the monstrosity prepared to engulf the astral stronghold of Perillia, the Space Wolves launched towards it in drill-tipped boarding torpedoes. They bargained without the fact that the planet-sized predator had its own ecosystem, and a hostile one at that -- Engir and his warriors were soon beset by the piscine Remora, a vampiric race of Warp-slicked bipeds with sucker-like mouths and needle teeth. After a gruesome and desperate war amongst the frond-forests and gill-chasms of the mutated void whale, Engir and his Astartes cut their way into the beast's circulatory system and planted thermal charges deep within the behemoth's vital organs. They escaped with solar seconds to spare before the beast finally came apart in a great cloud of Warp-tainted blood. The Perillian Gas Belt was born from its remains.

A mighty ward against enemy attacks, these powerful girdles incorporate potent gravitic force fields that protect the wearer. Forged by the master Iron Priest Stef Blacksoul after the disappearance of Russ, these belts are important relics of the Chapter.
