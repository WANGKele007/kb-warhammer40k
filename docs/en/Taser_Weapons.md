Taser Weapons are a type of esoteric close combat weapon utilised exclusively by the warriors of the Skitarii Legions of the Adeptus Mechanicus. Powered by hyperdynamo capacitors, Taser Weapons store an incredible amount of potential energy.

A solid impact will cause this energy to be discharged in a scorching blast of electrical energy, only to be harnessed once more by the electrothief prongs at the weapon's tip.

Taser Goad

A long sword crackling with electricity that is commonly used by Skitarii Ranger and Vanguard Alphas, and Sicarian Infiltrators.

Taser Lance

A long, lance-like Taser Weapon wielded by Sydonian Dragoons.
