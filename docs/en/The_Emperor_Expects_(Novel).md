The Emperor Expects is the third volume in The Beast Arises series of novels.

Synopsis

As Ork ships lay waste to world after world, heading inexorably towards Terra, Imperial citizens are in despair. The High Lords become desperate to prove that victory is possible, no matter the cost. A massive Imperial Navy fleet is assembled -- their mission to make a definitive strike against the Orks at Port Sanctus, an area of enemy-held space. But when the Imperial armada arrives, they find themselves outclassed and outmanoeuvred -- can human courage and faith possibly prevail against such terrible odds?
