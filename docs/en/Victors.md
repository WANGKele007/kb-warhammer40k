The Victors is a Loyalist Space Marine Chapter of unknown Founding and origin. Almost nothing is known about this Chapter in Imperial records, except that it was created some time after the Second Founding.

Chapter Appearance

Chapter Colours

The Victors primarily wear orange-red Power Armour. The knee guards and greaves are black. The white squad specialty symbol -- Tactical, Devastator, Assault or Veteran -- is located on the right shoulder plate. A black Roman numeral is stenciled in the centre of the squad specialty symbol, indicating squad number. The colour of the Aquila or Imperialis on the chest guard indicates company number in accordance with the dictates of the Codex Astartes -- i.e. White (1st Company), Yellow (2nd Company), Red (3rd Company), Green (4th Company), etc.

Chapter Badge

The Victors' Chapter badge is a stylised white letter "V", superimposed over a white, open-faced rectangle, on a field of orange-red.
