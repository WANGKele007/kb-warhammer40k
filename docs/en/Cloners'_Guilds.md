The Cloners' Guilds are Guilds of the Leagues of Votann. The Cloners' Guilds oversee the birth of new Kin in their hold's crucibles. They often encourage the inclusion of particularly useful cloneskeins in the genetic makeup of newborn Kin while they are gestating.

Sources

Codex: Leagues of Votann (9th Edition), pg. 12
