A void shield is a special form of energy field employed by the Imperium of Man's various military forces to protect voidships and super-heavy vehicles like Imperial Titans from enemy attacks. Void shields use Imperial Warp-based technology to both power the shields and then displace ranged attacks by subtly distorting the localised space-time around the point of impact. 

It is unclear whether void shields neutralise the incoming projectile or directed energy beam in realspace through the manipulation of gravitic or other physical forces, transport it directly into the Warp, or whether some other method is deployed to displace the damaging force of a physical attack upon the vehicle or vessel. 

Void shields seem to be able to be penetrated in some instances if an incoming projectile is moving below a certain minimum speed. However void shields of a high enough intensity can stop all movement, interfere with radio communications like Vox-casters, and even stop interdimensional travel.

Imperial void shields act in the same manner as Ork Kustom Force Fields, though Ork energy fields, like all Greenskin technology, are far less reliable and tend to be inoperable by other intelligent species without access to the Orks' gestalt WAAAGH! energy once they are deactivated. 

Inversely, Imperial void shields can be re-activated after being collapsed, even during battle. In combat, void shields do not usually protect from close combat assaults or other vehicles moving through them to then attack the shielded vehicle or vessel although even they can be stopped if the void shield is of a high enough intensity. 

Most physical and energy effects can be blocked by void shields, and this includes even weather effects like rain which makes a crackling sound as it hits the shield. 

Vehicle Void Shields

When used to protect ground forces, these void shields are most commonly mounted on Imperial Titans for protection from ranged attacks, but they have also been employed on other super-heavy main battle tanks. On vehicles with multiple void shields, or void shield banks, it has been said that the void shields must be calibrated to overlap perfectly or "weak spots" can occur.

These chinks in the shields appear to be undetectable to anything but direct visuals, and can cause catastrophic void shield failure when shot at by Titan-grade weapons. Void shields are also said to whine audibly when active, and fizzle against one another when touching or overlapping.

While the void shields of Titans can normally be bypassed through the use of teleportation as in the case of Terminator Titanhammer Squads or travel through the Warp in the case of Warp Missiles, they can be configured to block everything including transdimensional travel such as that used by the Necrons by increasing the shield intensity to critical levels. However this increase in power does threaten to potentially cause a catastrophic overload of the affected shields.

Starship Void Shields

Void shields are also used by Imperial naval and civilian voidships to survive the hostile environment that is the vacuum of space. Void shields form an invisible band of energy around the vessel, a variable layer of force that can absorb radiation, interstellar dust and particle showers as well as blocking weapon hits and teleportation attempts. Starships are also capable of diverting power between different shields on different sides of the vessel, allowing it to more effectively block an attack. Although torpedoes and attack craft can normally pass through starship void shields, there have been occasions where void shields have blocked both when they are set at a high enough intensity to stop all physical movement into the protected space.

A change in the frequency of the wave harmonics of a void shield can interfere with the gravitic fields of tractor beam weapons. A special type of commercially available void shields known as "Repulsor Shields" had their frequency adjusted to better protect a vessel from stellar debris like the matter within nebulae, plasma clouds and ice rings.

Void shields have a maximum tolerance and can be overloaded by sustained weapons fire or massive collisions, forcing the void shield generators to shut down temporarily to vent the excess kinetic or directed energy, leaving the ship's unprotected hull open to attack.

Although standard Navis Imperialis doctrine is to maintain void shields at fifty percent power capacity at all times, due to the high energy and maintenance demands that this requires most captains prefer to keep their ships' shields at minimum power in safe conditions and only increase their power when entering a potentially dangerous situation such as approaching a strange port or risking combat.

Notable Void Shield Variants

Triple Void Shield Array - The ancient grand cruisers occasionally still operated by the Navis Imperialis and certain fabulously wealthy Rogue Trader dynasties possess titanic layered banks of antique void shields that fill cavernous field decks the size of small towns. These are hellish compartments, rank with the stench of ozone and prone to unpredictably deadly arcs of lightning from copper discharge points as the onion-layered void shields are pounded by enemy ordnance. Grand cruisers are capable of mounting triple layers of void shields, making them more durable than all but battleships.

Voss "Glimmer" Pattern Void Shield Array - Void shields are an ancient standby of Human defensive technology, an almost un-improvable design. Their only real flaw is the great power drain produced. The Voss Forge World attempted, more than six standard millennia ago, to lessen the power demand of void shield generation by reducing the phased shield pattern output. While more energy-efficient, this reduced the protective value of the shields. No longer manufactured by Voss, some of these designs are still in use in relatively safe regions of the galaxy where little opposition is expected.

Reflex Shield - A Reflex Shield is an ancient variant of Void Shield technology that was used by the starships of the Raven Guard Legion as a cloaking device to protect them from detection by enemy sensors and even the naked eye. The Reflex Shield technology was deployed as a notable feature of the Shadow of the Emperor, the Raven Guard Legion's flagship during the Great Crusade and Horus Heresy.
