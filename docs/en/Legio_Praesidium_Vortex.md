The Legio Praesidium Vortex is a Loyalist Titan Legion of the Collegia Titanica, one of the military arms of the Adeptus Mechanicus. It is based on the Forge World of Agripinaa. 

Legion History

Notable Campaigns

13th Black Crusade (ca. 999.M41) - The Legio Praesidium Vortex aided the defence of its home Forge World of Agripinaa from the forces of Chaos during Abaddon the Despoiler's 13th Black Crusade.
