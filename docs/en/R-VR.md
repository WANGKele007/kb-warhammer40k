The R-VR, an acronym for Rantal-class Venatus Reclamator, is an advanced cybernetic variant of the Cyber-mastiff used by the Adeptus Arbites. It is considered a technological marvel which combines the best attributes of a loyal Terran species of mastiff with the mechanical endurance of a servitor.
