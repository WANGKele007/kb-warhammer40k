A Neural Shredder is a vicious and shockingly effective though little understood weapon used against organic targets by both the Imperium and Aeldari Corsairs. Though short-ranged, the weapon projects a cone of electromagnetic disruption in the direction of fire that tears apart a victim's neural pathways. This wave of energy is of the same frequency as most humanoid species' central nervous system, overloading brain and nerve receptors alike, resulting in rather catastrophic effects on creatures within the area of effect. 

Armour is no protection against this hideous attack, and even the mightiest foes drop to their knees, spasming and convulsing violently, leading to unconsciousness or even death as liquified brain-matter froths from their eyes, ears and mouths. This strange weapon's origin is unknown, although some speculate that it is but one of a range of psychic weapons developed by the Adeptus Astra Telepathica.

As a consequence of its rather specific design, the Neural Shredder is useless against non-living material such as wargear and vehicles, though it passes directly through such objects as if they were not there to affect any living occupants encased within. Therefore even the heaviest personal armour offers no protection against a Neural Shredder, neither are the operators of heavily plated vehicles nor the inhabitants of buildings shielded against its effects.

The Neural Shredder is a rare and exotic weapon rarely seen in the galaxy, but it is a common armament of Officio Assassinorum agents trained by the Callidus Temple, though it can occasionally be seen in the hands of other individuals resourceful enough to acquire one, such as members of the Inquisition.

During the Great Crusade the Dark Angels Legion was known to utilise Neural Shredders manufactured at the rebel forge of Tyrhenius. So terrible were the weapons crafted by the Renegades of Tyrenius, that the Emperor decreed both the Neural Shredders and Tyrhenius were to be destroyed, leaving only a few of its relic weapons in the hands of the I Legion.
