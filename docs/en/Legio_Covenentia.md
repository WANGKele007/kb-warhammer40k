The Legio Covenentia is a Traitor Titan Legion in the service of the Ruinous Powers. 

Legion History

Notable Campaigns

Occlusiad War (550-560.M37) - The Occlusiad War, also known simply as the Occlusiad, was a major conflict that raged from the late 500s.M37 on the northwestern fringe of the Segmentum Obscurus between the Imperium of Man and the rogue Tech-priests known as the Apostles of the Blind King. During this time, the Iron Council of the Iron Hands Chapter made a show of dispatching a huge force to aid in battle against the Renegade disciples of the Blind King. Clan Raukaan and no fewer than four other clan companies took to the field. In a series of bloody battles, their vast strike force blunted a dozen uprisings on as many worlds. At Pelos they even hurled back the turncoat Titans of the Legio Covenentia with a vast armoured phalanx. As the war ground on, the Iron Hands were lauded as heroes by Segmentum Command and the Martian Priesthood alike.
