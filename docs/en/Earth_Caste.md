The Earth Caste of the T'au Empire is primarily made up of farmers, labourers, and technicians responsible for building the machines, cultivating the food, and erecting the dwellings that allow the T'au to survive. The Earth Caste also includes all of the T'au Empire's artisans, scientists, and engineers. The sophisticated machinery and technology that drive the T'au Empire are created by the foremost minds of the Earth Caste, including their legendary battlesuits.

The T'au use the titular prefix of fio to designate someone of the Earth Caste. 

The T'au of the Earth Caste are stouter of build than the normal T'au phenotype, and tend to have calloused hands and a pragmatic outlook on life. Their speech is very literal and matter of fact, often appearing to be quite monotonous to those not of their caste. 

Before the coming of the Ethereals during the Mont'au, or "Time of Terror," the T'au lived in separate tribes, the foundations of today's castes. Those destined to become the Earth Caste built cities and fortresses of stone to defend themselves against the neighboring tribes of the warlike, nomadic plains dwellers, who would become the Fire Caste.

Notable Earth Caste Members

O'Vesa - O'Vesa is a battlesuit pilot and member of The Eight, Commander Farsight's elite team. He is fact not a shas'vre at all, but an old Earth Caste colleague of Farsight kept alive by microdrones of his own invention. He pilots a modified XV104 Riptide Battlesuit. He fought with Farsight during the Arkunasha War. O'Vesa joined with Farsight after the Damocles Crusade and now supports his rule in the Farsight Enclaves.
