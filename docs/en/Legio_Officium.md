The Legio Officium is a Loyalist Titan Legion of the Collegia Titanica, one of the military arms of the Adeptus Mechanicus. 

Legion History

The Legio Officium's last remaining Warlord-class Titan, Fire Bane, was present on the Cemetery World of Dianixis when the Thousand Sons Chaos Sorcerer Ahriman and his forces attacked the world in the battle remembered as the Death of Dianixis, seeking the skull of Lepidus, a hero of the 2nd Black Crusade buried there and the arcane information inscribed upon it.

Notable Titans

Fire Bane (Warlord-class Titan) - Fire Bane was the last Warlord-class Titan of the Legio Officium. During the Death of Dianixis Fire Bane strode to war from its shrine. The cry of its warhorns echoed across the damned world, and its weapons burnt a path towards Ahriman's circle of Chaos Sorcerers. Drawing together the power of his fellow sorcerers, Ahriman forced the massive god-engine to its knees, before pulling the core of its plasma reactor through its carapace.
