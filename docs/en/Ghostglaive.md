A Ghostglaive is a Ghost Weapon, one of the large, melee Power Weapons used by the Craftworld Aeldari's various Ghost Warriors.

The rudimentary sentience within the Spirit Stone core of each Ghost Weapon, whether it takes the form of an axe, glaive or sword, guides the wielder's blows into their target's weak spots.

The Ghostglaive is a massive powered sword wielded by Wraithknights and Wraithlords. These great blades can even wound the most potent of Greater Daemons in combat successfully.
