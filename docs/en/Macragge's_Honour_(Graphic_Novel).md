Macragge's Honour is the first Horus Heresy graphic novel. Macragge's Honours story is written by Black Library author Dan Abnett with stunning full page art drawn by artist Neil Roberts. First released as a limited edition leatherbound hardback, it is now available as both a non-limited hardback and an e-book. The book contains a total of 128 pages, with 100-pages of story and art by Dan Abnett and Neil Roberts, with the remaining pages consisting of behind-the-scenes material, including script pages, sketches and more. Macragge's Honour is a sequel to Book 19, Know No Fear.

Synopsis

In the aftermath of the Word Bearers' attack on Calth, the wounded Kor Phaeron flees aboard his Battle Barge Infidus Imperator, intent on returning to the war that now rages across the entire galaxy. But the Ultramarines' First Chapter Master Marius Gage –- under orders from Roboute Guilliman himself –- has taken the Primarch's mighty flagship the Gloriana-class Battleship Macragge's Honour in pursuit, and the battle to come will surely be remembered for all eternity. Cut off from their Legions and with no hope of reinforcement, the crews of the two vessels stalk one another to the very edge of reality...and beyond.
