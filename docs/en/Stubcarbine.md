A Stubcarbine is a compact, ballistic projectile weapon used exclusively by Sicarian Infiltrators, perhaps the most sinister of the Adeptus Mechanicus' Skitarii cybernetic warrior clades.

The Stubcarbine, despite its smaller size, deals the same damage as the Heavy Stubbers mounted on the tanks of the Astra Militarum. When a squad of Sicarian Infiltrators opens fire with these weapons, the air fills with a storm of solid shot that chews their victims to ruin.
