The Silver Guard is a Loyalist Space Marine Chapter of unknown Founding and origin. 

It fought to liberate the Sabbat Worlds Sector from the control of the forces of Chaos during the Sabbat Worlds Crusade that began in 741.M41.

Chapter History

Notable Campaigns

Sabbat Worlds Crusade (714.M41-Unknown Date.M41) - The Silver Guard was one of the Astartes Chapters that fought to liberate the Sabbat Worlds Sector in the crusade that began in 741.M41. The Chapter was instrumental in achieving several key victories during the numerous hard-fought campaigns of that Imperial Crusade including the important Imperial victory on the world of Balhaut in 765.M41 which was won with the aid of a task force from the Silver Guard. With the aid of the Royal Volpone Bluebloods, Pragar 10th and the Kolstec 477th Light Support Astra Militarum regiments, the Silver Guard Chapter Master Veegum stormed the Monastery of St. Kiodrus. Captain Allentis was also a part of the strike force and led a desperate attack on the Oligarchy Gate but was stymied by Heritor Asphodel's daemonic Woe Machines. When the Hyrkan 8th Regiment broke through the enemy buttress fields, their attack was supported by Captain Allentis, the Kolstec "Hammers" and 4 Astra Militarum armoured divisions.

Notable Silver Guard

Chapter Master Veegum - Veegum is the current Chapter Master of the Silver Guard as of 765.M41.
Captain Allentis - Captain Allentis was the commander of one of the Silver Guard companies currently involved in the Sabbat Worlds Crusade. He is known to have played a major role in the assault on the Oligarchy Gate of the Monastery of St. Kiodrus on the world of Balhaut, and was killed in the fighting there.
Sergeant Eadwine - Eadwine was a sergeant of the Silver Guard attached to the Astra Militarum regiment called the Tanith First and Only along with two other Astartes from the White Scars and Iron Snakes during the Imperial raid on Salvation's Reach.

Chapter Appearance

Chapter Colours

The Silver Guard primarily wears dark purple power armour. Part of their battle plate is metallic silver, including the helm, the Aquila or Imperialis on the chest plate, the shoulder plate trim and arms. 

The white squad tactical specialty symbol -- battleline, close support, fire support, Veteran or command -- is located on the right shoulder plate. 

A black High Gothic numeral is stenciled in the centre of the squad specialty symbol, indicating squad number.

Chapter Badge

The Silver Guard's Chapter badge is a large white skull, surrounded by three white swords in the shape of a triangle, on a field of black.
