The Red Legion is a Loyalist Codex Astartes-compliant Space Marine Chapter of unknown genetic origin and Founding. Almost nothing is known about this Chapter, save that it hails from the world of Oxatan.

Chapter Appearance

Chapter Colours

The Red Legion primarily wears red power armour. The wings of the Aquila or Imperialis on the chest plate are yellow, while the skull is black. A white squad tactical specialty symbol -- battleline, close support, fire support or Veteran -- is located on the right shoulder plate. 

A white High Gothic numeral is located on the inside-corner of the inset of the right shoulder plate, next to the squad specialty symbol, indicating squad number. A white High Gothic numeral on the left poleyn (knee guard) indicates company number. 

The Chapter iconography is displayed on the left shoulder plate.

Chapter Badge

The Red Legion's Chapter badge is a large ebon skull surrounded by yellow flames, centred on a field of red.
