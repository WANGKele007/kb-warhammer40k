The Margin Crusade was an Imperial Crusade and War of Faith launched by the Adeptus Ministorum from the Calixis Sector of the Segmentum Obscurus in 784.M41, under the leadership of High Confessor Corvinus. The crusade sought to bring the nearby Halo Stars into Imperial Compliance. Recruitment of reinforcements and requisition of resources from the Calixis, Scarus, and Ixaniad Sectors continued for solar decades after.

In truth, all contact ceased with Corvinus' forces sometime around the year 788.M41, though this is a secret known only to a very few senior members of the Imperial adepta. The crusade is presumed to have been destroyed, but it is still considered ongoing by the Imperium to serve as a cover for the ongoing Achilus Crusade. Forces raised to fight in the Halo Margins are in fact diverted through the secret Warp Gate in the Maw between the Calixis Sector and the Koronus Expanse to join the Imperial meat grinder in the Jericho Reach -- for the most part having no knowledge that their true destination lies beyond the Eastern Fringe on the opposite end of the Imperium.

History

Origins

The priests of the Adeptus Ministorum ceaselessly wage war against the darkness that seeks to damn the souls of Mankind within the Imperium's borders. Yet, on occasion, the Ecclesiarchy turns its gaze outward, launching grand Imperial Crusades and Wars of Faith, casting forth legions of troops and vast fleets into the void to carve out new realms in the Emperor's holy name through fire and blood. Since its official sanction as the Imperial state church in the mid–32nd Millennium, the Ministorum has sanctioned and initiated hundreds of such crusades -- ranging from minor expeditions to reclaim lost holdings, to colossal endeavors that reshape entire sectors of Imperial space. The greatest and most recent of these is the Margin Crusade.

This monumental undertaking was proclaimed by holy writ of the Sector Synod Obscurus, directing that the crusade unfold upon the distant spinward frontier of the Calixis Sector. The writ entrusted High Confessor Corvinus of the world of Thracian Primaris with supreme authority, empowering him to initiate a War of Faith and calling upon the Calixis, Scarus and Ixaniad Sectors to furnish the troops, arms, and materiel required to stoke the Imperial war machine. Soon, vast tithes of soldiery and resources were levied, and entire musters marched beneath the Ministorum's banners. From the fleets, several capital warships of Battlefleet Calixis --including the mighty Chariot of Wrath -- alongside a formidable contingent of escorts, were diverted to join the Emperor's holy cause.

Failure

The Margin Crusade made only meager gains in the standard years following its inception, its strength bleeding away as it drifted ever farther beyond the borders of Calixian space, past even the distant Frontier World of Kalf. The campaign became a sprawling, blood-soaked morass -- hampered by the fickleness of Warp routes, squandered by the incompetence of its commanders, and sustained only by the ceaseless exhortations of its attendant Ecclesiarchy priests. 

Millions of lives were expended for little lasting gain, and the appearance of the dreaded Tyrant Star, Komus, above the world of Karrik seemed an ill omen foretelling its doom. Calamity soon followed: High Confessor Corvinus fell to xenos hands, and with his death the crusade's armies were shattered amid the lightless gulfs of the Halo Stars, reduced to ruin and slaughter. By 788.M41, all contact with the Margin Crusade had been lost.

Cover for Achilus Crusade

Despite the ignominious collapse of the Margin Crusade, the Adeptus Ministorum has, for solar decades, perpetuated the fiction of its triumph -- proclaiming victories over alien dominions and the reclamation of distant worlds in the Emperor's name. From the pulpit of Ecclesiarchy cathedrals, priests thunder forth calls for fresh recruits, while the scribes and procurators of the Departmento Munitorum divert tens of thousands of Astra Militarum soldiers, supply vessels, and resources from Compliant worlds across the Calixis Sector and beyond. 

For the Ecclesiarchy, the fiction of a living crusade is far too useful to abandon. Beyond the wealth of troops and materiel it commands, the crusade also serves to distract the faithful from local unrest, the disputes of system synods, and the failings of planetary governance. Thus, so long as it serves their purpose, the Margin Crusade endures -- if only on parchment, data-slate and in sermons.

Some of the resources siphoned in its name are quietly absorbed by the Imperial state church itself; the greater portion, however, is funneled to sustain another bloody campaign: the Achilus Crusade. This truth is withheld from the general population, from the soldiers dispatched to fight, and even from the planetary and sub-sector governors, lest the vital flow of flesh and iron into the Achilus war machine falter. Should the deception be revealed, the Ministorum would risk not only the collapse of its elaborate charade but also the loss of invaluable resources -- and, perhaps, the loyalty of dozens of worlds who have already sacrificed their sons and daughters to what they believed was an Emperor-ordained War of Faith.
