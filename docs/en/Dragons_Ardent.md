The Dragons Ardent are a Loyalist Space Marine Chapter and a successor chapter of the Blood Angels originally created during an unknown Founding. The Chapter was destroyed when the World Eaters warband known as the Foresworn assaulted their homeworld of Nautilos. 

Later, during the Ultima Founding of the Era Indomitus, the Chapter was refounded and became a Chapter comprised entirely of Primaris Space Marines. 

Chapter History

Unknown Founding Chapter

The Dragons Ardent Chapter was destroyed when the World Eaters' Foresworn warband assaulted their homeworld of Nautilos sometime after the Great Rift's creation in the Era Indomitus of the 41st Millennium. The Dragons Ardent's Chapter Master had his battle-brothers defend their fortress-monastery the Praecipitium, as it was believed its walls and the adamantium portcullis of the fortress's sea-gate could keep the Heretic Astartes at bay. The Chapter also relied on their homeworld's deadly giant waves to aid in the defence and at least a company's worth of the Foresworn were killed when their landing craft were swamped in this way. However the warband's Khornate Berzerkers numbered in the hundreds and they eventually breached the Praecipitium's walls despite all the defenders could do. This Foresworn swarmed into the fortress-monastery and proceeded to butcher every last one of the remaining Dragons Ardent.

The warband departed Nautilos to seek new blood for the Blood God and only Captain Kurrinon had survived the assault. He vowed revenge against the Foresworn for their destruction of the Dragons Ardent and also for the death of his close friend, Battle-Brother Tanikhor, who died defending the Praecipitium. Kurrinon left Nautilos to seek out the Traitor Legion and killed any World Eaters he encountered. At some point durng his quest for vengeace, the captain's sheer rage led him to become delusional and he forgot that his brother Tanikhor had died. This led Kurrinon to begin seeing and speaking to Tanikhor as if he was still alive and the captain even accepted his counsel on certain matters. 

Kurrinon's ever-growing need for vengeance ultimately led him to fall to heresy solar decades later, and he was declared a Heretic by the Imperium. He became desperate to save himself from the expected Imperial move against him and "Tanikhor" advised him to join the Foresworn for his own safety. Though Kurrinon was deeply reluctant to join the very warband of Chaos Space Marines that had destroyed his Chapter, the imagined Tanikhor convinced him that it was the only way to save himself from the Imperium.

Ultima Founding Chapter

Sometime after Kurrinon left Nautilos, a Torchbearer fleet of the Indomitus Crusade had arrived at the Ocean World. It had been charged with reinforcing the Dragons Ardent Chapter with Primaris Space Marine Greyshields of the Ultima Founding and the ability to create its own Primaris Marine reinforcement, but instead discovered that the Dragons Ardent had been destroyed. 

With no survivors to be found, the Torchbearer fleet used its Blood Angels-lineage Greyshields to refound the Chapter and repaired the damaged Praecipitium fortress-monastery and the other Chapter facilities present on Nautilos as well. While the Chapter's unique gene-seed variant was lost to them, the new Dragons Ardent carried on the legacy and traditions of their forebears. Eventually, though, word reached them that two members of the original Dragons Ardent had survived the Foresworn's attack and in fact had joined the very warband of Heretic Astartes that had destroyed their Chapter.

After learning of this, the refounded Dragons Ardent's Chapter Master ordered a group of the Chapter's forces to hunt down the Traitors and bring their heads back to him. They learned Kurrinon's name and it was the Champion Su'ul Marhen who tracked the Traitor down, just as the Foresworn were assaulting yet another an Imperial world. 

After catching Kurrinon by himself, Marhen struck and disarmed the Traitor before knocking him down. The Champion told the former captain what had occurred after he left Nautilos and about the subsequent rebirth of the Dragons Ardent. Afterwards, Marhen tried to convince Kurrinon to repent his heresy by giving up the name of the other traitorous Dragons Ardent member. Kurrion refused, however, and instead told the Champion he was now an "Eater of Worlds". This led an exasperated Marhen to execute the former captain where he lay and take his head before beginning to look for the other Traitor. Of course, Marhen did not know that the other Traitor was the long-dead Tanikhor, who in his delusions Kurrinon had believed was still alive.

Chapter Beliefs

The Dragons Ardent's homeworld was the sparsely populated and harsh Ocean World Nautilos, whose only landmasses were a few scattered islands. Besides a small scattering of Human settlements, the islands also held the Chapter facilities of the Dragons Ardent which included their fortress-monastery, the Praecipitium. They lived by a philosophy inherited from their Blood Angels progenitors known as the "Angel's Graces", whose primary ethics were mercy, restraint and forgiveness. The Dragons Ardent also followed the calming mantras of the Solus Encarmine and upon leaving the Chapter's 10th Company, it was a tradition for the new initiates of the Chapter to carve a verse of the Moripatris across their foreheads.

This verse was intended to be deep enough to permanently scar the bone and the verse reflected whatever wisdom the initiate had learned in their time with the 10th Company as a Scout Marine. The Chapter also maintained the tradition of eating the flesh of their dead and claiming the heads of their slain battle-brothers. There were some within the Imperium that looked askance at such practices and whispered it spoke of the Chapter's gene-seed degrading or worse, a turn towards heresy. However, despite these concerns, the Dragons Ardent always served the Emperor faithfully and the skulls of their dead were taken as keepsakes and trophies by the living. These were then turned into objects of beauty by the Chapter's battle-brothers, who etched, carved and engraved the skulls. However the verses of the Moripatris would still be visible when the skulls were displayed, the dead's final message to the rest of their Chapter.

Once the skulls were deemed finished, they were adorned in the Praecipitium's grand hall for all to see. It was there that the Dragons Ardent's Reclusiarch led them in meditation on the humility they were to show in victory and the strength the Chapter drew on in defeat. This reflected in their personal lives as well, as the Dragons Ardent lived in cells within their fortress-monastery which contained prayer cabinets. The Chapter's battle-brothers were allowed to keep objects of personal significance to them within these cabinets, as well as on their bedstands. These objects could include the skulls of those brothers they were close to on a personal level and had been slain.

Notable Dragons Ardent

Captain Kurrinon (Khornate Berzerker) - Kurrinon was originally a Dragons Ardent captain who had two hundred and sixty standard years of service to his Chapter and was also close friends with Battle-Brother Tanikhor. However this came to an end in the Era Indomitus when the World Eaters' Foresworn warband emerged from a giant Warp storm and assaulted the Chapter's homeworld Nautilos. During the attack, the Chapter thought the walls of the Praecipitium and their world's massive, deadly waves would allow them to defeat the warband. In the end, the Foresworn's superior numbers won out and its Khornate Berzerkers breached the Praecipitium's walls and butchered the Dragon's Ardent. Only Kurrinon survived and he fell to despair when he discovered the slain body of his friend Tanikhor. In keeping with the Dragon Ardent's traditions of claiming the honoured heads of their dead, the captain began decapitating his battle-brother. As he did so, Kurrinon almost wished he had convinced his Chapter Master to charge out to meet the Foresworn in one last glorious assault, so that at least he could have died with his Chapter. The captain finished cutting off Tanikhor's head and held it close to him in mourning, as he told his friend that this was not the end. It was then that Kurrinon remembered the Human tribal population of Nautilos believed that if you held its seashells to your ear, you could hear the demands of their gods. Kurrinon did the same with Tanikhor's lips and suddenly heard the gods speaking to him. The captain swore revenge on the Foresworn and vowed to kill as many of them as he could. Kurrinon left Nautilos and entered the Great Rift in pursuit of the warband, where he began a solar-decades-long journey that saw him kill an unknown number of the Foresworn. However during the course of his vengeance, he became delusional and forgot that his friend had died. This led Kurrinon to begin seeing and speaking to a hallucination of Tanikhor as if he was still alive and the captain even accepted "his" counsel in certain matters. Kurrinon's ever-growing need for vengeance later led him into heresy and he was formally recohnised and declared as a Heretic by the Imperium. He became desperate to save himself afterwards from the assault he expected Imperial forces to launch against him and Tanikhor advised him to join the Foresworn for his safety. Though Kurrinon was very reluctant to join the very warband that had slaughtered his Chapter, the imaginary Tanikhor convinced him that it was the only way to save himself from the Imperium. In order to be accepted into the warband, Tanikhor told Kurrinon he would need to have the Butcher's Nails cranial implants implanted within his own head. "Tanikhor" then directed the captain to the Chaos Space Marine Apothecary Bredek to have the procedure done. Even as Kurrinon had grave doubts about going through with the surgery, "Tanikhor" continued to prod him to move foreward, warning him that he had little other choice if he wanted to survive. While Bredek was left wondering who the captain was talking to, he successfully implanted the Butcher's Nails. Kurrinon was readily accepted into the Foresworn and soon lost himself to the savage madness precipitated by the cranial implant. Like his new brethren he fell all too easily into the veneration of Khorne and even slowly forgot his own identity driven only by the growing desire for murder and blood as the warband slaughtered every living being they encountered. The former Dragon Ardent even came to believe that he had always been a World Eater and had taken part in the Siege of Terra during the Horus Heresy. Kurrinon even began to "remember" seeing the primarchs Angron and Sanguinius fight within the Imperial Palace complex. Unbeknownst to Kurrinon, however, a Torchbearer fleet had come to Nautilos sometime after he had left his homeworld. It had been sent to reinforce the Dragons Ardent with Primaris Space Marines of the Ultima Founding, but finding the Chapter already destroyed, the fleet used its Blood Angels-lineage Greyshields to refound the Chapter. The restored Dragons Ardent then carried on the legacy and traditions of their forebears as they sought to serve the will of the Emperor. Eventually, word reached them that two members of the original Dragons Ardent had survived the Foresworn's attack and in fact had joined the very warband that had destroyed their Chapter. After learning of this, the refounded Dragons Ardent's Chapter Master ordered a group of the Chapter's forces to hunt down the Traitors and bring their heads back to him. The Dragond Ardent strike force learned Kurrinon's name and it was the Champion Su'ul Marhen who tracked the Traitor down while the Foresworn were in the process of assaulting another Imperial world. After catching Kurrinon by himself, Marhen struck and disarmed the Traitor Marine before knocking him down. The Champion told the former captain what had occurred after he left Nautilos and the subsequent rebirth of the Dragons Ardent. Kurrinon was left dumbstruck as the use of his original name caused his true past back to come back to him in a rush and he remembered everything that had transpired, including that his Brother Tanikhor was long dead. Afterwards, Marhen tried to convince Kurrinon to repent his heresy by giving up the name of the other traitorous Dragons Ardent member. Kurrinon refused, however, as he believed it was far too late for him to earn any redemption. As the Butcher's Nails begin throbbing in his head, Kurrinon instead told the Champion he was an "Eater of Worlds". This led an exasperated Marhen to execute the corrupted former captain. But even as he died, Kurrinon heard "Tanikhor" whisper that this was not the end. Kurrinon went to his end gladly, for as long as there was a need for vengeance Kurrinon would always exist. At the same time, he knew there was no true death for those who pledged their souls wholly to Khorne.

Chapter Champion Su'ul Marhen - Su'ul Marhen was originally among the Primaris Space Marine Greyshields that accompanied the Torchbearers fleet that was sent by the Indomitus Crusade to aid the Dragons Ardent Chapter. However when the fleet reached the Chapter's homeworld Nautilos, the Torchbearers discovered the Dragons Ardent had been destroyed. They had fallen at the hands of the World Eaters' Foresworn warband and no survivors were found in the ruins of the Chapter's fortress-monastery and other facilities. The fleet's commanders decided to refound the Dragons Ardent using their Greyshields of the Ultima Founding and Marhen was chosen to become the restored Chapter's Champion. Eventually, though, word reached the refounded Dragons Ardent that two members of the original Dragons Ardent had survived the Foresworn's attack and in fact had joined the very warband that had destroyed their Chapter. After learning this, the refounded Dragons Ardent's Chapter Master ordered a group of the Chapter's forces to hunt down the Traitors and bring back their heads back to him to end the dishonour. The strike force learned the identity of one of the Traitors was Kurrinon and it was Marhen who tracked the Heretic Astartes down, even as the Foresworn were launching yet another bloody assault on an Imperial world. After catching Kurrinon by himself, Marhen struck and disarmed the Traitor before knocking him down. The Champion told the former Dragons Ardent captain what had occurred after he left Nautilos and the subsequent rebirth of the Dragons Ardent as a Chapter of Primaris Marines. Afterwards, Marhen tried to convince Kurrinon to repent of his heresy by giving up the name of the other traitorous Dragons Ardent member that Marhen knew existed. Kurrion refused, however, and as the Butcher's Nails implants in his head fired, he instead told the Champion that now he was only an "Eater of Worlds". This led an exasperated Marhen to execute the former captain and take his head before beginning to look for the other Traitor. Of course Marhen did not know that the other Traitor was the long-dead Tanikhor, who in his delusions Kurrinon had believed was still alive.
