Vandred Anrathi, called "The Exalted" after his possession, was a former sergeant of the Night Lords Traitor Legion, commanding the 1st Claw (squad) of the 10th Company up until the Siege of Terra in 014.M31.

By the time of the 41st Millennium, Anrathi had allowed himself to be possessed by a Daemon called "The Exalted" and he had mutated into a Possessed Chaos Space Marine dedicated to the service of the Chaos God Tzeentch. 

The Daemon's influence twisted Anrathi's physical form until he only resembled a shadow of his former self.

History

Beginning as a sergeant of the Night Lords Legion, Vandred Anrathi commanded the 1st Claw up until the Traitor assault on the Imperial Palace during the Siege of Terra. Following Captain Malcharion's entombment within a Chaos Dreadnought, Vandred succeeded him as the commander of the Night Lords' 10th Company; under his leadership, the company degenerated into a Chaos Space Marine warband that survived by piracy. 

Anrathi thus disregarded the vision of the Night Lords' primarch Konrad Curze, which proved to be a major point of contention with Talos Valcoran. Allying himself with Abaddon the Despoiler, he allowed himself to be possessed by a Daemon. 

Before accepting the Daemon which would come to dominate him, Vandred was an honored warrior, but his true forte was void combat. A shadow of his former self after the possession, it soon became apparent that "The Exalted" was the title of the Daemon which possessed him, and it used his memories and knowledge to further its pursuit of power. 

After his change into "The Exalted", he became even more focussed on void combat. He was a true master of it, understanding all the nuances of his ship's speed, mass, maneuverability and weaponry, and therefore willing to throw his vessel, the Covenant of Blood, into seemingly suicidal maneuvers.

Allying himself with Abaddon during the Crythe campaign, The Exalted eventually grew tired of Abbadon's behaviour and his use of the 10th Company as fodder for the Despoiler's schemes. Eventually 1st Claw was betrayed upon the Industrial World of Crythe by Abaddon's Black Legion. 

Talos decided that The Exalted was no longer fit to command. Vandred's disgust towards Abaddon and the Black Legion helped him to temporarily overcome the Daemon within him and reassert his personality. Refusing to allow his warriors to fall to the whims of Abaddon, Vandred rescued his forces on Crythe and escaped the vengeful Blood Angels Chapter.

When Vandred had his ship dock at Hell's Iris in order to repair and refit the battle-damaged vessel, 1st Claw sent ambassadors from their warband to meet with the station's commanding Chaos Lord, Huron Blackheart. Blackheart agreed to have the Night Lords' vessel repaired on the condition that The Exalted's warband would owe him a debt to be paid by accompanying the lord of the Red Corsairs on a military campaign on the Imperial world of Vilamus, serving as the Red Corsairs' vanguard force. 

Vandred agreed, but for motives of his own, as the Night Lords recognised that one of the Red Corsairs' warships was the ancient Night Lords strike cruiser Echo of Damnation, the sister-ship to their own vessel, the Covenant of Blood. Following along with Blackheart's plan, The Exalted's warband deployed onto the world of Vilamus in stealth, invading the fortress-monastery of the Marines Errant located there. 1st Claw fought alongside Blackheart for a time, before simply and quietly abandoning the battle, as did all of The Exalted's forces already committed. Managing to take control of the Echo of Damnation, 1st Claw made its escape with its new warship.

The Red Corsairs immediately pursued the stolen warship, their fleet striking out after the Night Lords in overwhelming numbers. Vandred, in his prison of a body, immediately recognised that the fight was unwinnable for the Covenant of Blood, but that the Covenant's sacrifice would allow Talos Valcoran to escape with the Echo of Damnation.

As death seemed imminent for the Night Lords, this threat weakened Vandred's Daemonic possession and he was able to bring his own personality to the fore for first time in many standard years. Once more in full control of himself, he ordered all personnel to abandon the Covenant of Blood and transport themselves to the Echo of Damnation. Keeping a skeleton crew aboard, Vandred used the Covenant of Blood to hold off the Red Corsairs fleet, allowing the Echo of Damnation and all aboard her to escape under the command of Talos Valcoran of 1st Claw.

With a surge of willpower, Vandred took control of his body from the Daemon again, and commanded the Covenant of Blood in a fleet action that destroyed a half-dozen or so vessels of the Red Corsairs fleet with a daring series of suicide maneuvers. Right before the Red Corsairs were about to make the final kill of the Covenant of Blood, Vandred completely relinquished control over his body, letting The Exalted's furious struggles burn Vandred's soul away for good -- intentionally so, because it meant The Exalted, shortly thereafter, experienced the death-pain of Vandred's body alone as it was banished back to the Warp.
