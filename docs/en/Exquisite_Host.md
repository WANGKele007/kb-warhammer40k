The Exquisite Host is a Chaos Space Marine warband in the service of Slaanesh, the Chaos God of pleasure and pain. It is not known with any certainty, but they are most likely a warband originally derived from the Emperor's Children Traitor Legion. The Exquisite Host is known to employ an infamous battle formation known as the so-called Lament of the Damned -- a terrifying onslaught of Chaos Sonic Dreadnoughts bound to the service of Slaanesh -- outfitted with the same destructive and mind-blasting arcane sonic technology as the Noise Marines.

Notable Members of the Exquisite Host

Sothai - A Slaanesh Sonic Dreadnought, Lord Sothai is a fallen Chaos Lord bound to the service of the Exquisite Host. Sothai is known to have taken part in Abaddon the Despoiler's 13th Black Crusade in 999.M41.
