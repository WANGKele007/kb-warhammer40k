The Predator Cannon, also known as a Syrtis Pattern Autocannon, is the primary weapon of the Predator Destructor main battle tank used by the Adeptus Astartes. The Predator Cannon is a long-barreled Autocannon that features an automated ammunition feeder, muzzle flash suppressor, and a discharge extractor.

The weapon is slave-linked to a multi-spectral remote targeting surveyor and accuracy talisman that is controlled by the vehicle's commander, who is also the vehicle's gunner, and is of particular use as an anti-infantry weapon.
