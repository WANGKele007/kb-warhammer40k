The Red Wolves are a warband of the Red Corsairs Heretic Astartes that is composed of former Space Wolves Space Marines who surrendered to the Chaos Lord Huron Blackheart after he successfully boarded their strike cruiser Wolf of Fenris. 

The Red Wolves have now fully turned against the Imperium as Renegade Space Marines under Blackheart's command and use the Wolf of Fenris as their base of operations. However, while they serve the Red Corsairs, the Red Wolves will disobey any order that keeps them from fighting or the chance to take plunder.

Warband History

Capture of the Wolf of Fenris

Realising that they needed warships to carry out their piratical raids on Imperial shipping from their home in the Maelstrom Warp rift, Huron Blackheart, the Chaos Lord of the Red Corsairs, ordered an attack on a Space Marine patrol group of the Space Wolves and Flame Hawks Chapters near the world of Parenxes. In a surprise attack near Parenxes's star, Blackheart's forces crippled the Space Wolves' strike cruiser Wolf of Fenris and damaged several other vessels, though all the others were able to escape the attack. 

Using Boarding Torpedoes, the Red Corsairs first took control of the strike cruiser's enginarium so that the Space Wolves could not self-destruct the vessel, then a second wave was sent into the cargo bays. The third wave, led by Blackheart himself, entered at a point fifty metres from the warship's command deck. Blackheart stormed the bridge and tore apart the ship's captain, Gnyrll Bluetooth, with the Tyrant's Claw that served as his right arm.

As the Red Corsairs' capture of the vessel became inevitable, several Space Wolves turned on their fellows to save their own lives and threw themselves upon the mercy of Blackheart. They renounced their pledges to their Chapter, primarch and the Emperor, and swore allegiance to Blackheart as their new lord. As a reward for their allegiance, Blackheart granted these Renegades command of the Wolf of Fenris, which became part of the Red Corsairs' growing fleet of warships.

Notable Campaigns

Invasion of Ogrys (Unknown Date.M42) - Ogrys, the homeworld of the Invaders Chapter of Loyalist Space Marines, was raided by the Red Corsairs. During the assault, the Red Corsairs captured and removed a Laskar-class Star Fort called the Emperor's Eye from its orbit of the moon of Ogrys and from the system entirely before being driven away by a relief force sent by the Ultramarines. In truth, the Red Corsairs' attack had been part of a scheme by Huron Blackheart to curb the growing power of his ambitious warlord Verngar the Apostate during his quest to claim the Tzeentchian Chaos relic called the Ebon Talon.

War for Chogoris (Unknown Date.M42) - The opening of the Great Rift and the coming of the Noctis Aeterna occurs while the White Scars defend Chogoris from attack. The entire Yasan Sector fell to Huron Blackheart's attack, and when Daemons manifested from the growing darkness, it seems the sons of Jaghatai Khan will be making their last stand. But one more fleet arrives before the stars go black. Arriving late from their war against the T'au in the Eastern Fringe, Kor'sarro Khan leads his 3rd Brotherhood and reinforcements from the White Scars' successors in an unexpected charge that breaks the Red Corsairs' blockade of the Khum Karta mountain range that surrounds Quan Zhou, the fortress-monastery of the White Scars, even as its ramparts are burning. With the return of the Master of the Hunt, the planet's defenders fight back with renewed hope, and the combined efforts of the Chapter's Stormseers drives back the malefic energies surrounding Chogoris, causing Huron Blackheart's daemonic allies to lose their physical form. The Heretics withdraw, falling back to strongholds elsewhere in the Yasan Sector and leaving Chogoris broken but unbowed.

Warband Fleet

Wolf of Fenris (Strike Cruiser) - The Wolf of Fenris was a strike cruiser of the Space Wolves Chapter that was captured by the Red Corsairs at the Battle of Parenxes sometime after the Badab War. It then became the flagship of the Red Wolves warband of Heretic Astartes who joined the larger Red Corsairs warband after surrendering the warship to the Chaos Lord Huron Blackheart at Parenxes.

Warband Appearance

Warband Colours

The Red Wolves now paint their power armour in the red and black colours of the Red Corsairs.

Warband Badge

The Red Wolves' badge, if it differs at all from that of the Red Corsairs, is unknown to Imperial records.
