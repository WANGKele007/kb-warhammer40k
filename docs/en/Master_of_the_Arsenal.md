The Master of the Arsenal is a Space Marine Captain of a Codex Astartes-compliant Chapter who not only leads a company of Space Marines into battle, but is also charged by his Chapter Master with overseeing his Chapter's arsenal of weapons and munitions. This special duty is traditionally given to those officers who serve as the Captains of the Chapter's 3rd Company.

Notable Masters of the Arsenal

Captain Adrax Agatone (Salamanders)
Captain Mikael Fabian (Ultramarines)
Captain Thracian (Scythes of the Emperor)
