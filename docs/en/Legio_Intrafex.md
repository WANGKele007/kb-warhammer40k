The Legio Intrafex is a Loyalist Titan Legion of the Collegia Titanica.

Legion History

Notable Campaigns

War of the Beast (544.M32) - The Legio Intrafex is one of several different Imperial military factions that took part in the defence of the Imperium when it was attacked by the massive Ork WAAAGH! of the infamous Warlord known only as The Beast. When the world of Ullanor was determined to be The Beast's homeworld, the Legio Intrafex took part in the Third Invasion of Ullanor as part of an Imperial strike force. These forces invaded the Orks' capital world and finally succeeded in bringing the greenskin threat to a halt with he death of The Beast.
