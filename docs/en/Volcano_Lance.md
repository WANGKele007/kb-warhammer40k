The Volcano Lance is a massive, Knight-grade Imperial Laser Weapon that serves as one of the primary armaments of the Dominus-class Knight Castellan. Possessing massive range and damage output, this massive Lascannon is the bane of super-heavy war engines and beasts. It can core out a lumbering Squiggoth or behead an enemy Titan, and when several Knights Castellan open fire at once, the effect is commensurate with the Lance batteries of an Imperial Navy warship.
