Meric Voyen was the company Apothecary of the Death Guard Legion's 7th Great Company that was commanded by Battle-Captain Nathaniel Garro during the Great Crusade and the opening days of the Horus Heresy in 005.M31. He later remained true to the Emperor and followed Garro into exile on Terra during the Flight of the Eisenstein. He dedicated the remainder of his life to finding a cure for Nurgle's Rot after the horrors of that journey, but ultimately became infected by the unnatural disease and died. 

His body was later possessed by the Nurglish Daemon known as the Lord of the Flies until it was finally defeated when Garro killed Voyen's body once more and banished the Lord of the Flies to the Warp on the slopes of the White Mountain subterranean command post on Terra just before the start of the Siege of Terra in 014.M31.

History

Meric Voyen was the Apothecary of the 7th Great Company of the Death Guard Legion and the long-time friend and confidante of its commanding officer, Battle-Captain Nathaniel Garro. Garro was furious with Voyen when the Apothecary confessed that he was a member of one of the secret warrior lodges that had begun to spring up in the XIV Legion. Garro let it be known to his warriors that he did not approve, for he felt that these lodges, though they had their worth, were ultimately predicated upon secrecy and the act of concealment which was anathema to the Legiones Astartes, and he would take no part in it. 

Garro later pulled Voyen aside and confronted him in private. Though he formally censored the Apothecary for being a member of the Death Guard's warrior lodge, he made the Apothecary swear to him that he would never keep any secrets from him. Voyen readily agreed, but knew in his heart that it would take much to regain his commander's trust.

Before the Isstvan III Atrocity, Garro's 7th Great Company fought in concert with the Emperor's Children's elite 1st Company against traitorous forces on Isstvan Extremis, the outermost planet within the Isstvan System. Whilst fighting against a powerful Slaaneshi psyker known as a Warsinger, Garro sustained serious injuries that included crushing damage to his torso, arm and the loss of his right leg from the mid-thigh down. He was only saved from certain death by the timely ministrations of an Apothecary from the Emperor's Children Legion -- their Chief Apothecary Fabius -- much to Voyen's displeasure. 

The Legion's Apothecaries had not declared him fully healed and therefore Garro was deemed unfit for battlefield operations. His command also remained on limited duty until Garro was fit to reenter combat. As a result, Garro and his company were not among the Death Guard Loyalists assigned to unleash the speartip assault upon Isstvan III, so they remained aboard the frigate Eisenstein providing standby tactical support for the Drop Pod deployment operation, and remained on alert to perform rapid-reaction interdict duties.

Before the mission began, First-Captain Calas Typhon pulled aside Commander Ignatius Grulgor, the captain of the Death Guard's 2nd Great Company, and spoke to him of their primarch's Mortarion's desire to bring Garro to Horus's banner over the Emperor's, but they both knew that Garro would never betray the Master of Mankind. When the turning point arrived, Typhon informed Grulgor that there were certain duties that Horus wanted him to perform. The commander relished his part in this conspiracy against Garro, whom he had long found insufferable.

Aboard the Eisenstein, suspicious of Commander Grulgor's actions, Apothecary Voyen stealthily made his way to the launch bay to investigate. Voyen saved Garro's housecarl Kalab from narrowly being captured by Grulgor's warriors Kalab shared with Voyen that he had seen Grulgor and his Space Marines loading an unusual cargo aboard the frigate. Digesting what the housecarl told him, Voyen realised with growing horror that the deadly cargo were Life-Eater virus bombs -- biological weapons of mass destruction that contained an engineered viral strain of such complete lethality that it could only be deployed in the most extreme circumstances, usually against the most foul xenos. 

With only Apothecary Voyen, Battle-Brother Sendek and his housecarl, Garro confronted Commander Grulgor and his entire Command Squad and a handful of the frigate's crew. A vicious fight ensued and Garro and Voyen narrowly avoided death. Grulgor and his warriors suffered the lethal effects of the Life-Eater pathogen when it was accidentally released by a stray bolter round that punctured one of the warheads, and they died screaming in agony.

During their flight to Terra to bring warning to the Imperium of the treachery of Horus, Voyen second-guessed his commander's actions multiple times, but ultimately, Garro's desperate gamble of detonating the frigate's Warp-Drive when they became trapped in the Warp and were under assault by the undead Plague Marines of Nurgle, proved successful in bringing Loyalist aid. The crew of the Eisenstein were rescued by the primarch Rogal Dorn and his Imperial Fists Legion. Aboard the Phalanx, they brought word of Horus's treachery to his stern brother. In response, and unsure who to trust, the Imperial Fists brought Garro and his warriors to Luna, where they were imprisoned within the Somnus Citadel of the Silent Sisterhood while the truth of the matter was determined. 

One of Garro's younger warriors, Solun Decius, finally succumbed to the mysterious contagion he had contracted when the Loyalist Death Guard were confronted aboard the Eisenstein by the reanimated corpses of Grulgor and his Traitors -- the first true Plague Marines. A vicious battle ensued within the Somnus Citadel and out on the airless plains of Luna, and Garro was able to destroy the Chaos-tainted Daemon that had possessed Decius's tortured body. Following this final battle, Voyen informed Garro of his intentions to renounce his oaths as a warrior in order to dedicate his life to healing and the discovery of a cure for the mysterious contagion contracted by Decius, which was later identified as the first known case of Nurgle's Rot.

Garro later returned to Meric Voyen upon receiving information from Rogal Dorn that the ex-Apothecary was in possession of Solun Decius's mutated remains for the purposes of research upon the unnatural disease that had claimed him. While in the midst of a heated conversation, the glass casket holding the corpse was revealed to be compromised, and as Nurgle's suddenly released Daemonic insects attacked the pair, Garro shoved Meric to safety outside the compartment. However, upon eliminating the threat, Garro found Meric to have been bitten by one of the insect and infected with Nurgle's Rot himself. While Meric's actual final fate is never revealed, it was implied by Garro's sombre disposition that he was forced to end his comrade's life before the disease could fully claim him.

Shortly before the Siege of Terra, the Lord of the Flies, the same Daemon that had originally possessed Solun Decius on Luna, took possessed Voyen's body. The Daemon was able to organise Chaos Cult activities on Terra to disrupt the Loyalists' preparations for the arrival of Horus's fleet at the Throneworld, which led Malcador the Sigillite's Knights-Errant to face them near White Mountain. Nathaniel Garro was forced to kill Voyen and finally banish the Lord of Flies back to the Warp once and for all.
