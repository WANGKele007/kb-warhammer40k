Cybertheurgy is the name given to the ancient arts of the Age of Technology utilised exclusively by the Magos Dominus of the Legio Cybernetica to fashion and maintain a small, but powerful cadre of Battle-Automata for the Imperium of Man.

This archaic knowledge hails from a time when the un-living phalanxes of Mankind's sentient machine creations shattered forgotten alien empires to carve humanity's first empire from the stars thousands of Terran years before the birth of the Imperium of Man.

Although much of this lore had been lost or become expressly forbidden, most notably through the institution of the Crimson Accords, by the time of the Great Crusade, fragments of this knowledge still existed and were utilised in the formation of the Legio Cybernetica, one of the most feared and powerful of the Legiones Militant of the ancient Mechanicum.

The use of Cybertheurgy allowed for the Legio Cybernetica 's Magi to control those robotic engines designed for warfare and slaughter -- the Imperial Robots. Such is the nature of the dark arcana of Cybertheurgy, however, that it is not without its risks, as what is awakened may slip the leash of its master's control with disastrous consequences.
