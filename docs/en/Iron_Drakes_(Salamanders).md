The Iron Drakes is a Loyalist Space Marine Chapter and a successor of the Salamanders composed entirely of Primaris Space Marines that was raised during the Ultima Founding of ca. 999.M41 by Archmagos Dominus Belisarius Cawl.

It should not be confused with the older Chapter of the same name that was corrupted and became the Grey Death warband of Heretic Astartes.

Chapter History

The Salamanders were kept largely unaware of the new Successor Chapters of Primaris Marines that were created from their gene-seed by Belisarius Cawl during the Ultima Founding. Once they learned of their successors' existence, they were always quick to dispatch a Chaplain to the Chapter to teach them the ways of the Promethean Cult.
