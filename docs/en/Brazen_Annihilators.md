The Brazen Annihilators are a Loyalist Space Marine Chapter of an unknown Founding and the genetic heritage of the Imperial Fists.

Even for genetic scions of the Imperial Fists, the Astartes of the Brazen Annihilators are known for their over-emphasis on the role of heavy ordnance in battle. Because of this, by the time the Annihilators' warriors charge home to finish the fight in close or ranged infantry combat, their enemies' positions have already been reduced to little more than scorched, bloody craters.

As a result, the artillery bombardments unleashed by this Chapter have been legendary among the Adeptus Astartes for standard millennia, whether they be fired from the Chapter fleet in orbit of a target world or from their Thunderfire Cannons and Whirlwinds on the surface.

Chapter Appearance

Chapter Colours

The Brazen Annihilators wear dark copper power armour with white trim and a white Aquila or Imperialis on the chest.

The white squad specialty symbol -- battleline, close support, fire support, Veteran or command -- is displayed on the right shoulder pauldron, while the inset of the left shoulder pauldron is red and proudly displays the Chapter's iconography.

The company number is displayed as a white High Gothic numeral on the left knee plate and the squad number as a black High Gothic numeral placed in the centre of the squad specialty symbol on the right shoulder pauldron.

Chapter Badge

The Brazen Annihilators' Chapter badge is a black skull from which a white burst of energy is emanating.
