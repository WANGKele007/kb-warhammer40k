An Enmitic Obliterator is a massive Necron Enmitic Weapon that serves their military forces as an artillery piece. Enmitic Weapons are directed energy weapons that unleash pulses which cause the victim's atoms to violently repel each other. Enmitic Weapon ammunition is not a physical material or power source, but rather information. They cast quantum-commanded hekatic decrees written in the basal language of reality itself, which re-orders the molecules of their targets in such a way as to dissolve their atomic bonds.
