A Liquifier Gun is a Drukhari weapon which sprays a huge amount of potent acid that melts through flesh and armour alike. 

Minions of the feared Haemonculi like Wracks usually have Liquifier Guns implanted within their bodies from which they can fire their own acid-like blood onto their foes.

Sources

Codex: Dark Eldar (5th Edition), pg. 61
