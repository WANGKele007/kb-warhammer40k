House Agaron is a Loyalist Imperial Knight House of the Imperium. There is very little information in Imperial records about this Knight House. In 784.M34, after more than a century of self-imposed exile as a Freeblade household detachment, the Agaron brothers return to the Knight World of Silverdawn after the death of their father. The siblings are welcomed back as heroes after the disastrous reign of their sire. Basilio, the eldest of the three, re-establishes trade with the nearby Forge Worlds of Estaban III and VII.
