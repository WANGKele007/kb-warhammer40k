The Grav Shell is a type of ammunition used by Imperial warship Nova Cannons. Grav Shells are a relatively new technological development for the Imperium and are very difficult to construct, making them rare and dangerous munitions.

When detonated, a Grav Shell generates an intense gravitational field which implodes into a miniature black hole. This singularity exerts a devastating gravitational force on anything within a radius of several thousand kilometres.
