The Dais of Dominion is a great, anti-gravitic Necron construct that the Silent King and the ruling Necron Triarch sit atop when going to battle.

The dais is accompanied by two Triarchal Menhirs, and a captured C'tan known as the Enchained is bound atop it.

The current Triarch that makes use of the Dais of Dominion in the Age of the Dark Imperium is the last of the Silent Kings, Szarekh, and his two companion phaerons, Mesophet the Shadowed Hand and Hapthatra the Radiant.
