A Judiciar is a Primaris Space Marine who is in training to become a Primaris Chaplain. Sworn to silence after accepting their role, Judiciars do not preach aloud, but instead use their deeds to preach a litany of righteous fury. 

Wielding an arcane, hourglass-shaped device known as a Tempormortis in one hand that can be used to slow the progression of time in a limited area around an enemy unit to easier kill those foes, and an immense Executioner Relic Blade in the other, Judiciars must prove their worth in battle to join their Chapter's Chaplaincy proper, doing so through acts of devotion and the slaying of enemies.

Notable Judiciars

Admonius - Admonius is a Judiciar of the Ultramarines Chapter who served under Captain Aeschelus in the Indomitus Crusade Fleet Quintus' Battle Group Faustus. He was welcomed into the ranks of the Ultramarines' Reclusiam as a Judiciar for the zealotry he displayed during Battle Group Faustus' assault upon and destruction of the Renegade Callosi Station. Afterwards Admonius filled his Tempormortis with grain taken from the destroyed void station and proved even more zealous in his devotion to the Emperor thereafter. Lieutenant Nemetus felt that this increased devotion was due to Admonius not wanting his status as a novice Chaplain to be counted against him, and the lieutenant soon learned it was best not to contradict or challenge anything the Judiciar wanted.
