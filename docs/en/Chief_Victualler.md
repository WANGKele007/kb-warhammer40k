The Chief Victualler is a title traditionally held by the Captain of the 7th Company of a Codex Astartes-compliant Space Marine Chapter. 

The Chief Victualler is responsible for all of the Chapter's supply needs, particularly those pertaining to food, drink and the other personal requirements of the Chapter's Astartes and human support personnel.

Codex: Space Marines (5th Edition), pp. 17, 54
