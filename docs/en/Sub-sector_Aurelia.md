Sub-sector Aurelia is a sub-sector of the Korianis Sector within the Segmentum Ultima, which lies on the frontier of Imperial space. The Human-inhabited worlds within this sector serve as the primary recruiting ground for the Blood Ravens Space Marine Chapter. 

In the 41st Millennium, the entire sub-sector found itself consumed by war as first a splinter fleet of the Tyranid Hive Fleet Leviathan assaulted its inhabited worlds and then the forces of Chaos sought to lay claim to the sub-sector and transform its former capital planet of Aurelia into a new Daemon World dedicated to the Ruinous Powers.

These threats were countered by the Blood Ravens in the First and Second Aurelian Crusades. Finally, during the campaign known as the "Cleansing of Aurelia" or the Third Aurelian Crusade, the Blood Ravens were forced to confront the Chaos corruption that had consumed the leadership of their own Chapter in the person of their Chapter Master and Chief Librarian Azariah Kyras even as the Ordo Hereticus of the Inquisition, led by the Puritan Inquisitor Adrastia and the Astra Militarum forces seconded to her command, sought to unleash an Exterminatus upon the entire sub-sector to end the growing threat it posed to the rest of the Imperium.

Notable Planets

Some key inhabited worlds within the Aurelian Sub-sector include:

Aurelia - Aurelia is now an Ice World that was recently restored to realspace after over 1,000 Terran years in the Warp. The planet was formerly a Hive World and the capital of the sub-sector. It was also once the Chapter homeworld of the Blood Ravens, until the planet was consumed by a Warp storm and transformed into a prison for the Great Unclean One Ulkair.

Meridian - Meridian is an industrial Hive World that serves as the planetary capital of the sub-sector.

Typhon Primaris - Typhon Primaris is now a Dead World, but was formerly a Feral World infested by Feral Orks and Tyranids that was destroyed by an Exterminatus carried out on the order of Inquisitor Adrastia.

Cyrene - Cyrene is a Dead World, formerly a Civilised World that fell to Chaos corruption and was destroyed by a Blood Ravens Exterminatus ordered by Gabriel Angelos, the current Chapter Master of the Blood Ravens.

Calderis - Calderis is a Feudal World and Desert World known for its arid climate and the hardy desert warriors its difficult environment produces. The Blood Ravens have recruited from this world for millennia. An Imperial planetary governor oversees the planet from a space station in orbit.

Other Locations

Judgement of Carrion - The Judgement of Carrion is the official Imperial designation given to a space hulk infested by Tyranids and Orks found in Sub-sector Aurelia.
