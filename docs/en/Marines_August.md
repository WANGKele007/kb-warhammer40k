The Marines August is a Codex Astartes-compliant Space Marine Chapter of unknown origin and Founding. Almost nothing else is known about the Chapter in current Imperial records.

Chapter Appearance

Chapter Colours

The Marines August primarily wear crimson Power Armour. The power pack is black. The Aquila or Imperialis on the chest guard, as well as the helm and shoulder plate trim are golden. The open-faced white squad specialty symbol -- Tactical, Devastator, Assault or Veteran -- is located on the right shoulder plate. A white Gothic numeral is stenciled above the squad specialty symbol, indicating squad number. The iconography on the left knee guard indicates company number in accordance with the Codex Astates.

Chapter Badge

The Marines August's Chapter badge consists of two white Greek "lambda" symbols, one inverted over the other, with a large white skull superimposed in the centre of both, on a field of crimson.
