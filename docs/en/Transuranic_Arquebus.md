A Transuranic Arquebus is a long-barrelled infantry weapon which epitomises the precision and inhuman efficiency that typify the cyborg Skitarii Legions of the Adeptus Mechanicus. 

Firing a shell of depleted transuranium, a transuranic arquebus can puncture a tank from one side to the other, the resultant pressure wave also pulping any biological creatures that may be sheltering inside. 

Both Skitarii Vanguards and Skitarii Rangers typically field Transuranic Arquebuses as a special weapon in their squads.
