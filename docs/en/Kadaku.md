Kadaku is an Imperial Jungle World and Death World that is the third moon of Gas Giant 32.1 in the Recidious System of the Bastior Sub-sector of the Segmentum Pacificus. Kadaku was invaded by a splinter of the Tyranid Hive Fleet Leviathan during the Fourth Tyrannic War of the Era Indomitus. It was known to be home to unusually deadly species of flora and fauna, leading to its further classification as a Death World, though this did not prevent the Imperium from constructing well-defended fortresses and refineries across the planet's surface. 

The neighbouring Hive World of Avarax is entirely dependent on Kadaku's rich agricultural and energy resources, and without their export industry would grind to a halt and the massive population of Avarax would starve. If Kadaku were to fall to the Tyranids it would also inflict a major blow on the Recidious System's own supply of war materiel and logistical contributions to the larger Imperial war effort against the Great Devourer in the Bastior Sub-sector.

Even before the Tyranid invasion, Kadaku was a deadly planet, home to a wide variety of vicious fauna and flora. Thick jungles and deep, treacherous swamps made navigation by land extremely difficult, and the humid atmosphere clogged air filters and spread many pathogens that produced wasting diseases in the Human population. All of the dangers of the planet's ecosystem were only amplified by the Tyranids once they began their process of "tyrannoforming" the moon.

Before the hive fleet's arrival, Kadaku's diverse flora and fauna had also provided the nearby Hive World of Avarax with plentiful supplies of food, but also had given rise to the planet's most important resource: large reserves of subterranean promethium. Isolated promethium refineries were found across the world's surface. These outposts were well-fortified to guard against the encroachment of the Kadakan jungle and its powerful predators, and except for the arrival of sporadic orbital transports, were mostly self-sufficient. 

The moon's population was largely comprised of workers who were engaged in the dangerous work of drilling and siphoning the promethium fields, and refining the crude promethium into essential fuel for the Emperor's machines of war, which were then transported to Avarax, or to the handful of remote Adeptus Mechanicus research outposts spread across the moon. 

The Priesthood of Mars found the natural dangers of the jungle and isolation from the wider Imperium beneficial in keeping prying eyes from their arcane work, and the ready supply of fuel ensured they were able to perform their rites with optimal efficiency. Whole regions of the world were forbidden to aircraft not sanctioned by the Adeptus Mechanicus, leading the few who knew of such things to suspect that the Tech-priests were engaged in unusually secret and important research on Kadaku. They were right.

One of these Mechanicus research outposts was the site of "Project Aurora", a classified attempt by the local tech-priests under the leadership of Archmagos Nozick Beta-12 to build a Warp-based weapon that could banish all Chaos-corrupted foes to the Immaterium using the Chaos relic power source first encountered during the Liberation of Graia over a standard century before.

During the subsequent Recidious Campaign Kadaku was defended from the Great Devourer by the 8th Cadian Regiment of the Astra Militarum as well as the 2nd Company of the Ultramarines Chapter commanded by Captain Sevastus Acheran and assisted by Lieutenant Demetrian Titus. 

History

Demetrian Titus had originally arrived on-planet as a Deathwatch Black Shield named "Nullus" serving with the Deathwatch's Kill-team Kasaeran who had been tasked with unleashing a virus bomb into the world's upper atmosphere in the hopes that it would stop the Tyranid assault in its tracks. He served alongside Brother Ulfar of the Space Wolves, Brother Kelstros of the Blood Angels, Brother Beren of the Black Templars, and Brother Darius of the Dark Angels, all of whom were slain in the course of the mission after their Corvus Blackstar was unexpectedly brought down by a Tyranid air attack. Titus succeeded in launching the virus bomb but was mortally wounded in a fight with a Carnifex.

Titus was recovered from the battlefield by the arrival of the Ultramarines' 2nd Company under the command of Sevastus Acheran, but was forced to cross the Rubicon Primaris, what the Ultramarines called the "Calgarian Rites," as only the enhanced physiology of a Primaris Marine could ensure his survival. Persuaded by Chaplain Quintus to return to the Ultramarines' fold to seek redemption for his dishonour at Graia, Titus was reintegrated into the 2nd Company as a lieutenant and tasked with aiding the Ultramarines in defending Kadaku from the continuing Tyranid assault. 

Later, Titus and his Ultramarine allies were indispensable in stopping a plot by the Thousand Sons Sorcerer Lord Imurah and his thrall-bands of Heretic Astartes to claim the Chaos relic that served as the power source for Project Aurora. With the aid of Chapter Master Marneus Calgar, Titus defeated Imurah within his own construct in the Immaterium, and finally destroyed the power source that had haunted him since the Liberation of Graia over a standard century before. The victory in the Recidious Campaign convinced Titus that he had gained some measure of redemption from the events on Graia and that he was worthy to serve alongside his brothers in the Ultramarines once more.

Progression of Tyranid Invasion

During the Recidious Campaign of the Fourth Tyrannic War in the Era Indomitus, Kadaku was invaded by a splinter fleet of Hive Fleet Leviathan. However, Astra Militarum regiments drawn from the Cadian Shock Troopers and Astartes of the Ultramarines Chapter's 2nd Company came to its defence in order to evacuate high-value Adeptus Mechanicus personnel and the research data and components of Project Aurora. 

The world was inevitably abandoned to the Tyranids once the evacuations were complete, and the Great Devourer began infesting the moon with Capillary Towers within the first solar week of their assault.

Timeline

Early Stage - The first Tyranid spores arrive on Kadaku. Vanguard bioforms such as Lictors and Tyranid Warriors immediately begin culling and devouring the planet's top order predators, while xenophagic viruses and bacteria infect the local plant life to render it down into a biomass-rich slurry. Swarms of Tyranid bioforms attack the most vulnerable promethium refineries on the moon to increase the production of the energy-rich promethium exponentially in an attempt to expand the moon's available biomass.

Day 1 - Tyrannoforming of the moon's ecosystem begins. The Cadian Shock Troopers of the Cadian 8th Regiment are now present planetside. Flora and fauna are culled by the assaulting Tyranid bioforms. The most vulnerable refineries are swarmed by Tyranid bioforms. Promethium production continues to exponentially expand to increase Tyranid biomass reserves. The largest promethium refineries on the moon are transformed into enormous Capillary Towers by the Tyranids so the hive fleet can begin to absorb the refineries' biomass.

Day 2 - The Cadian 8th Regiment continued to defend the moon despite its worsening strategic position throughout this solar day.

Day 2, Local time 09:37:16 - Deathwatch Kill-team Primus deployed from the watch fortress of Watcher Keep, of which Demetrian Titus is a member, deploy a virus bomb that is detonated in Kadaku's stratosphere, the effects of which slow the initial stages of the Tyranids' invasion.

Day 2-4 - The Ultramarines' 1st Company and 2nd Company arrived in system to combat the Tyranid threat. The 1st Company leaves Kadaku at some point between the second and fourth solar day of the invasion.

Day 4 - Kadaku is considered lost to the Tyranids by the Imperial defenders.

Day 4, Local time 05:47:02 - A planetary defence base is recaptured by the Astra Militarum forces and an Ultramarines squad. Orbital Defence Lasers are used to destroy a hive ship in low Kadaku orbit.

Day 4, Local time 09:53:12 - The remaining high-value Adeptus Mechanicus personnel (Archmagos Nozick Beta-12) and research data (including the Project Aurora power source artefact) are retrieved and evacuated a few solar hours later by the forces of the Ultramarines' 2nd Company.

Day 4, Local time 11:28:33 - Tyranid Capillary Towers now loom over the moon's surface while Hierophant Bio-Titans roam the surface defending the towers. The air defence Icarus Lascannons of an abandoned Astra Militarum fortress are repurposed by the forces of the Ultramarines 2nd Company to slay a Bio-Titan and destroy several capillary towers.
