A Ghostaxe is a Ghost Weapon, one of the large, melee Power Weapons used by the Craftworld Aeldari's various Ghost Warriors.

The rudimentary sentience within the Spirit Stone core of each Ghost Weapon, whether it takes the form of an axe, glaive or sword, guides the wielder's blows into their target's weak spots.

The Ghostaxe is used by Craftworld Aeldari Wraithblades. They are the axe variant of a Ghostsword.
