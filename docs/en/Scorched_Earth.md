Scorched Earth is a novella in the Horus Heresy Series of novels, originally released in hardback format. 

The novella has been reprinted in the anthology Born of Flame in 2019.

Synopsis

Nearly a quarter of a million loyal Space Marines lost their lives on Istvaan V -– the Drop Site Massacre lasted only a few hours, and yet the Iron Hands, Raven Guard and Salamanders were slaughtered by those they had once called kin. With the disappearance of their primarch weighing heavily upon their hearts, Ra'stan and Usabius of the XVIII Legion leave behind their fellow survivors and strike out into the Urgall Depression. Their mission: to find what, if anything, remains of mighty Vulkan...
