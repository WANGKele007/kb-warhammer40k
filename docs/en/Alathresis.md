Alathresis is a watch master of the Deathwatch Chapter seconded from the Novamarines who serves as the current watch commander of the Balefortress watch fortress. The Balefortress is perhaps the last Imperial bulwark left that can stop the Ork WAAAGH! Nakkaslash from capsizing the sector.
