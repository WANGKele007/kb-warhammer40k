Mesh Armour is a suit of Imperial personal armour that takes the form of an anti-ballistic bodysuit usually made from carbon-ceramic polyfibres. It can also be constructed in imitation of the much more advanced Aeldari mesh armour from tiny metal rings or thousands of thermoplas cells that are linked together. Phased out of use by the Astra Militarum in the 31st Millennium and replaced by the cheaper and easier to mass-produce Flak Armour, its use is ubiquitous elsewhere in the galaxy among paramilitary, pirate and other criminal groups; it always has been common among the hive gangs on the major Hive Worlds. The gangs preferred to use Mesh Armour for personal protection as all a ganger needed to make even an inferior quality suit was a powered nanoloom, water, and some powdered bone.

Originally designed by the xenos known as the Craftworld Aeldari for their militia forces called Guardians before the Imperium attempted to copy the design using inferior technology, Mesh Armour offers more protection than the standard Flak Armour used by the Astra Militarum, without sacrificing any of the wearer's mobility. 

A fabric-like material, when exposed to heat or sudden impacts, Mesh Armour becomes momentarily rigid, spreading and dissipating the kinetic force or thermal energy of the attack across the whole torso. Mesh Armour can be manufactured into numerous styles of protective garments including gloves, cowls, vests and cloaks. Though typically light weight and comfortable, when Mesh Armour is worn in especially large quantities, it can become extremely heavy. While the finest variants of this armour can only be manufactured by the Aeldari, even the cruder Human-made variants offer excellent protection.
