Wurgen, also known as "Battle-cant", is the name of the short-hand combat language employed by the Space Wolves Chapter of the Adeptus Astartes. 

It is derived from Juvjk, the dialect of Low Gothic spoken by the natives of Fenris, the Space Wolves' Chapter planet, but has been heavily altered so only other Space Wolves can fully understand it. 

Wurgen is used to provide easily understood tactical instructions among Space Wolf packs during combat.
