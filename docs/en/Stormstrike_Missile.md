A Stormstrike Missile is an Imperial missile that detonates with such a thunderous boom that it leaves those caught in the blast radius reeling and disoriented. Stormstrike Missiles have an extremely long range, and are effective against light to medium-armoured vehicles, as well as heavily-armoured infantry targets.

Stormstrike Missiles at present are only carried by the Stormraven gunships of the Adeptus Astartes and the Corvus Blackstars of the Deathwatch .
