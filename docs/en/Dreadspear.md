The Dreadspear is a large Dreadnought Close Combat Weapon that was wielded by Legio Custodes Contemptor-Achillus Dreadnoughts. It was created to emulate the Custodians' favoured melee weapon, the Guardian Spear. Each Dreadspear could also be equipped with a Corvae Las-Pulser to provide ranged fire.
