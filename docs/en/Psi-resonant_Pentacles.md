The Psi-resonant Pentacles were a class of archeotech artefacts in the forms of crowns or other types fo battle armour recovered from the Vrant Cluster among the troves of the Reclaimants.

These armour pieces, including Pentacular Crowns or protective armour pieces such as Pentagrammic Sallets and Five-sided Bevors, were all painstakingly inscribed with sigils and runes of warding.

These markings, through unknown means, projected an aura around themselves which could slow the passage of time or project waves of force.

Least well understood of all, the Pentacles could turn the gaze of the Warp back upon psykers, blinding them and preventing them from using their powers by immersing them within the turmoil of the Immaterium.

History

The Thousand Sons Legionaries who originally discovered these technological treasures promptly petitioned for them to be destroyed, gaining the support of the Navis Nobilite and the choirs of the Astronomican.

However, orders sent by the officers of Malcador the Sigillite dictated that they were instead to be prohibited from use and remanded to the Throneworld for further study. The barge carrying the entire collection to Terra was lost en route and the articles within were later seen on battlefields throughout the galaxy during the Horus Heresy.
