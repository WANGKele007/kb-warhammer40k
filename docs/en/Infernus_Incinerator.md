The Infernus Incinerator is a type of large Flamer weapon employed by the Legio Custodes Contemptor-Galatus Dreadnoughts, usually as a twin-linked, built-in additional weapon on their Galatus Warblades. These incinerator units are designed to swiftly purge a numerically-superior enemy.
