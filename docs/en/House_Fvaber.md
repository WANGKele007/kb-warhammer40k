House Fvaber is a Loyalist Imperial Knight House of the Imperium. There is very little information in Imperial records about this Knight House, or from what Knight World it originally hails from. They have remained steadfast in an alliance with the Legio Gryphonicus Titan Legion.

House History

Notable Campaigns

Battle of Beta-Garmon, "The Titandeath" (006-013.M31) - House Fvaber took part in the long and savage Beta-Garmon campaign, which included hundreds of war zones across dozens of worlds. During this conflict, House Fvaber provided Knight support to the Legio Gryphonicus. Even before the greater armies of the Warmaster Horus reached the star cluster, the battle lines had long since been drawn, and fighting had been going on for many Terran years. It was a cauldron of battle that would consume millions of lives before its end and see the demise of entire Titan Legions, earning this campaign the dire moniker of the "Titandeath." The Beta-Garmon Cluster, also known as the gateway to the throneworld of Terra, was the last hurdle that the Warmaster Horus' forces had to overcome before they reached the Imperium's capital world. Heavily fortified by the Loyalists, Beta-Garmon would become one of the greatest and bloodiest battles of the Horus Heresy, as well as one of the longest-lasting.
