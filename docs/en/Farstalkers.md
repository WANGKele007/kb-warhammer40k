The Farstalkers are a Loyalist Space Marine Chapter of unknown Founding and genetic origin. Their Chapter homeworld is Vagoris, which lies beyond the Segmentum Ultima and Warp Storm Malfactus, to the galactic west of the Ghoul Stars.

Chapter Appearance

Chapter Colours

The Farstalkers' Chapter colours are not currently listed in Imperial records.

Chapter Badge

The Farstalkers' Chapter badge appears to be an open eye.
