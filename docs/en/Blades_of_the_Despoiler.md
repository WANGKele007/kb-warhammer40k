The Blades of the Despoiler were a warband of the Black Legion dedicated to the service of Khorne. 

They were eradicated by the Battle-Sisters of the Order of the Ebon Chalice at some point during the Era Indomitus even as their path of slaughter took them on a course headed directly for Holy Terra.
