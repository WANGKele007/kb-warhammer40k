Shenlong is a former Imperial Forge World located in the Ultima Segmentum that is now a Dead World. It had been a Forge World of the Adeptus Mechanicus for time immemorial, having been originally settled by Humans during the Dark Age of Technology. The world was captured by the Chaos Space Marines of the Word Bearers Traitor Legion in the 41st Millennium and later liberated by the Blood Angels where it played a role in the outbreak of the Blood Angels Civil War in ca. 999.M41. 

However, despite the victory of the Loyalists in that conflict, the Chaos corruption spread across the Forge World by the Word Bearers proved impossible to eradicate, and Chief Librarian Mephiston of the Blood Angels ordered an Exterminatus action against Shenlong that led to the death of its entire population. 

History

The Lantern

The world of Shenlong was originally ruled by a death cult which claimed to worship a dragon-like god based on ancient Terran Asian belief systems. During the Great Crusade, the Emperor Himself arrived on Shenlong with the Principia Imperialis expeditionary fleet and forced its population into Imperial Compliance through the destruction of the death cult. Shortly before his execution, the leader of the cult gave the Emperor a heavy, drum-shaped firearm made for the hands of something bigger than a man, which he referred to as the Lantern. The Emperor took the Lantern to His personal workshop on board His flagship the Bucephelus. 

Many standard years later, after reaching Barbarus, the Emperor decided to give His newly-discovered gene-son Mortarion a token of welcome -- an object of power crafted by His own hands -- as He had done for all the other primarchs. To the Emperor's unwelcome surprise, Mortarion rejected His original gift: a menacing scimitar of broad dimensions and shimmering lethality. Mortarion told the Emperor that he already owned a blade, referring to the war-scythe that had accompanied him since his youth on Barbarus.

Mortarion looked through the Emperor's workshop, and his attention was caught by the Lantern pistol from Shenlong resting in a plasteel cradle. This was not the overwrought rendering of some weapon-artist. It was the weapon of a true killer, industrial and heavyweight. The Emperor explained to Mortarion the weapon's history before gifting it to him, and explained that one token of His affection for His son was as good as any other.

Blood Angels Civil War

In the 990s.M41, Shenlong was captured by the Word Bearers. Not long after the planet was captured and subjected to the horrors of rule by the forces of Chaos, the Blood Angels Chapter of Space Marines launched an assault that liberated the world from the Word Bearers' grip. 

However, the Blood Angels in this assault force were manipulated by the Chaos-corrupted Inquisitor Ramius Stele to believe that their fellow battle-brother Arkio was a reincarnation of the Blood Angels' primarch Sanguinius in the hopes of turning them to the service of the Dark Gods like himself. 

When the liberated populace of Shenlong learned that Arkio might be the reincarnation of a sacred son of the Omnissiah, they rallied to the side of Arkio and his cause. Arkio, increasingly under the influence of Chaos, intended to launch an Imperial Crusade against the forces of Chaos and chose 1,000 Shenlongi citizens to serve as his "Warriors of the Reborn".

When the other Blood Angels eventually defeated and slew both Arkio and Inquisitor Stele during the Blood Angels Civil War, the Loyalist Blood Angels watched as the Shenlongi proved all too willing to revert to their loyalty to the Word Bearers and the Ruinous Powers. 

Deeming the world hopelessly tainted by Chaos, the Blood Angels' Chief Librarian Mephiston ordered an Exterminatus action from orbit that swept the world's tainted surface clean of all life. Today Shenlong is classified by the Imperium as a Dead World.
