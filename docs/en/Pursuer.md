A Pursuer, often part of a Pursuer cadre, was a Null Maiden of the Sisters of Silence who served as the cyber beast-handlers and hunters of that order, called upon to track down a fleeing quarry such as a Rogue Psyker and either flush them out of hiding for capture or destroy them on sight as needed. They served as the masters and operators of Cyber-mastiffs, Steeltalon Hawks, and other cybernetic hunting beasts collectively called "Pursuer Beasts."

As some of the most agile of their order, Pursuers were typically deployed away from the bulk of the Silent Sisterhood's detachments among the streets, hab-blocks and alleyways of the targeted city in order to monitor for potential ambushes, those attempting to escape the tithe or other dangers which might threaten the operations of the detachment.

In this pursuit each Pursuer holds command over a single Cyber-jackal, Cyber-mastiff, Steeltalon Hawks or other cybernetic Pursuer Beast, and through programmed tap-codes broadcast from a wristpiece, the beasts are directed and their sight and hearing relayed back and displayed so the Pursuers can quickly identify potential signs of refuge or ambush and alert their comrades. The cyber-beasts, as part of their creation, are suited to nearly any urban terrain and are often deployed based on the potential utility of their systems.

Cyber-jackals are widely used among Pursuer deployments and are valued both for their endurance and the sheer terror they cause, while Steeltalon Hawks are frequently designated for use in urban environments with tall hab-blocks, narrow streets and wide stretches of terrain impassable by foot, extending the visual range of the Sisterhood's hunting cadres far beyond mere Human ability.
