Sylâkh's Kindred is a Kindred of the Leagues of Votann. The Kin of Sylâkh's Kindred, a known rival of the Kindred of Six of the Kronus Hegemony, favour the use of massed artillery and siege warfare.

Codex: Leagues of Votann (9th Edition), pg. 31
