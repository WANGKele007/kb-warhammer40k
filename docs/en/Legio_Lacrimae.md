The Legio Lacrimae was a Loyalist Titan Legion of the Collegia Titanica. At some point, they became lost during the Great Crusade, though the exact circumstances of their destruction are not known. 

Legion History

Notable Campaigns

Route of Megronia (Unknown Date.M30) While fighting alongside the Thousand Sons Legion during the latter part of the Great Crusade, the Legio Lacrimae were saved by the Thousand Sons when they drove back a force of countless Orks that threatened their crippled Titans during the reversals of the Route of Megorania. In gratitude for their assistance, the Legio Lacrimae swore oaths of perpetual kinship with the Thousand Sons.
