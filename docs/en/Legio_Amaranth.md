The Legio Amaranth ("Night Spiders") is a Loyalist Titan Legion of the Collegia Titanica, one of the military arms of the Adeptus Mechanicus. 

The Night Spiders fought during the Horus Heresy at the Battle of Beta-Garmon for the Loyalists. After the Warmaster Horus' Traitor forces proved victorious in the Beta-Garmon campaign, the remnants of the Night Spiders deployed to Beta-Garmon retreated back to Terra. But with the fall of Beta-Garmon, the last obstacle preventing Horus' march on the Throneworld, the Siege of Terra began not long after. 

The remaining Titans of the Legio Amaranth participated in the defence of the Imperial Palace during that final campaign of the Heresy.
