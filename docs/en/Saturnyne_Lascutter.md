The Saturnyne Lascutter is a Titan-grade Laser Weapon deployed on Imperial Warlord-class Titans. The Lascutter is a close-range weapon capable of both ranged and melee attacks yet whose primary purpose is to serve as a tool used to cut through wreckage and other obstacles blocking the path of the Titan.
