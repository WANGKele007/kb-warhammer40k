The High Lords of Terra, also called the "High Twelve", collectively form the Council of the High Lords of Terra, also known as the Senatorum Imperialis in High Gothic and officially as the Lords Temporal, Martial and Ecclesiarchical of the Most Divine and Righteous Imperium of Mankind. 

The Senatorum is an Imperial governing body comprised of the twelve highest-ranked officials of the most powerful adepta of the Imperium of Man. Throughout most of Imperial history they have ruled the Imperium as a collective oligarchy in the name of the Emperor.

This body carries out the day-to-day decisions required to govern the Imperium according to what they believe to be the divine will of the Emperor of Mankind. The primary task of the High Lords is thus to interpret and enact the will of the Emperor. Accordingly, the rank of High Lord is among the most politically powerful in the Imperium. 

Since the Emperor can no longer govern His empire directly, it is the claim of the High Lords that His immortal will is known to them and the Emperor thus rules through the High Lords of Terra. Though the Imperium is far too vast to be centrally governed in any effective manner, the High Lords ultimately make decisions affecting the lives of trillions of Imperial subjects across the galaxy.

At different times in the Age of the Imperium, the Senatorum Imperialis has been led by a chair who serves as first-among-equals with the titles of "Lord Commander of the Imperium" and "Imperial Regent." The current holder of these titles and thus the de facto ruler of the Imperium is Primarch Roboute Guilliman.

Despite their vital importance to the Imperium, the Adeptus Astartes are not represented among the High Lords. This may have been an intentional decision taken by Roboute Guilliman during his first term as Imperial Regent and Lord Commander of the Imperium when he founded the Senatorum Imperialis, for he fervently believed, like the Emperor Himself, that it was the duty of the Space Marines to protect Mankind, not rule it. This tradition was broken only for a brief period in the 32nd Millennium, during the War of the Beast and the event remembered as The Beheading, when successive Chapter Masters of the Imperial Fists -- Koorland and Maximus Thane -- briefly reigned as Lord Commander of the Imperium before the position was largely unfilled over the subsequent millennia.

History

Unification Wars

The original High Lords of Terra were simply bureaucrats in the service of the Emperor's new regime who followed in the wake of His armies during the Unification Wars in the 30th Millennium to build the foundations of His new empire on a reunited Terra. These earliest bureaucrats were known as the "Magisters Temporal", and as the Emperor's realm expanded were reorganised into the "Lords Civilian". The four greatest Lords Civilian eventually became known as the "High Lords Civilian". The four original High Lords were the Master of the Administratum, Chancellor of the Estate Imperium, Grand Provost Marshal, and the Lord Commander Militant of the Imperial Armies.

Great Crusade

The High Lords of Terra are at present the executive governing body of the Imperium of Man. They are tasked with interpreting the Emperor's will and enacting His rule across the largest empire in the galaxy. On their command, the fleets and armies of the Imperium move, only their edict can approve a Space Marine Founding, and on their orders, the Adeptus Astra Telepathica's Black Fleet sails and their grim tithes are collected. 

The Senatorum Imperialis has, at its roots, many echoes from Human ruling bodies formed before the birth of the Imperium. In the beginning of the Age of the Imperium, the Emperor Himself ruled His domain directly, although He formed about Him a council of advisors, called the War Council, that was comprised of key individuals. 

This top circle contained the Fabricator-General of Mars, the Paternova of the Navigator Houses and Malcador the Sigillite, perhaps the Emperor's greatest ally during the Unification Wars of Terra. As the Emperor left the homeworld of Mankind to lead the Great Crusade, He left in His stead the legendary Malcador, more or less to act as Regent of Terra and head of the Council of Terra in His absence.

Over the course of the Great Crusade which reclaimed the galaxy and freed Humanity from enslavement, the Emperor discovered the lost primarchs and brought them into the fold. As the Great Crusade began to branch off in many new directions, the Emperor grew to rely on His War Council. 

After the decisive victory during the Ullanor Crusade, when Mankind's re-ascension was no longer in doubt, the Emperor decided to leave the Imperial war effort in the hands of His primarchs under the command of the newly-proclaimed Warmaster Horus and return to Terra to carry out the next phase in the creation of the Imperium of Man -- shaping a Human version of the Aeldari's Webway.

At the same time, the Emperor decided that the Imperium could no longer be ruled directly by Him and a council comprised of the primarchs, instead, the ordinary mortals of the Imperium must also be allowed some say in its management. To this end, the Emperor created the Council of Terra to carry out His decisions and to handle many of the lesser administrative and bureaucratic tasks that did not require the Emperor's immediate attention. The primarchs were left in control of the War Council of the Imperium, which was led by Horus and now had true power only over the military direction of the Great Crusade rather than over the Imperium as a whole. 

The decision to create the Council of Terra to oversee civil affairs proved to be exceedingly unpopular with the primarchs, who disliked relinquishing their say over the direction of the Imperium to the control of men and women whom they viewed as far less capable than themselves. This issue would be one of the festering resentments that allowed Chaos to corrupt the Space Marines of the Traitor Legions and turn them against the Emperor.

Horus Heresy

Following the calamities of the Horus Heresy, the Emperor was interred upon the Golden Throne and could no longer rule His realm directly. Several legends tell of the Emperor's last words, spoken as He was attached into the vast machinery that would allow His mind to live on. Full of foresight beyond mortals, the Emperor's final instructions were for the rule of Mankind's galaxy-wide kingdom. 

In that time of great change, Malcador too was now gone, and it had been he who had proven best able to enact the Emperor's vision. Now that role, and the leadership for the coming reformation, fell upon Roboute Guilliman, the great primarch of the Ultramarines. 

It was he who set up the new ruling body, the Senatorum Imperialis, or as it is more commonly known now, the High Lords of Terra. It was their duty to interpret the will of the Emperor and, in His stead, to command the Imperium. 

The number of this ruling council was set at 12, with Roboute Guilliman himself taking a seat under the title of Lord Commander of the Imperium as the supreme commander of the Imperial armed forces and chairman of the Senatorum Imperialis, the old term Warmaster having fallen out of favour for obvious reasons. 

The lord commander title had previously been borne during the Horus Heresy by Primarch Rogal Dorn, the Praetorian of Terra, who surrendered it to Guilliman after the Heresy ended so that his brother could begin the political stabilisation and reform of the Imperium of Man during the Time of Rebirth.

Reformation

Following the Horus Heresy, the temporary Grand Council of Reconstruction ruled the Imperium in the chaotic aftermath of the campaigns of the Great Scouring. Its formation saw the mortal, non-Astartes High Lords, fearful of another rebellion on a scale as grand and terrible as the Horus Heresy, gain much power and act as a political counterweight to the surviving Loyalist primarchs, who had dominated all earlier Imperial politics.

Early in the Time of Rebirth, the Senatorum Imperialis formally succeeded the Council of Terra as the main executive body of the Imperium as a result of the reformation of the Imperial government and military initiated by Primarch Roboute Guilliman, who joined the new Senatorum as Imperial Regent and Lord Commander of the Imperium, commanding the entirety of the Imperial armed forces.

In the early 32nd Millennium, the rise of the Imperial Creed and the Cult of the Saviour Emperor to become the state religion of the Imperium meant that the Ecclesiarch of the Adeptus Ministorum was granted a seat, which soon became permanent. 

In the 36th Millennium, High Lord Goge Vandire, Master of the Administratum, led a coup d'état and also became the Ecclesiarch of the Adeptus Ministorum, holding dual leadership over the two most powerful adepta of the Imperium. During his Reign of Blood in the Age of Apostasy, the High Lords were powerless to act. When Fabricator-General Gastaph Hediatrix demanded that the High Lords finally account for themselves and execute Vandire, Goge dissolved the Senatorum Imperialis and declared the Space Marines and Adeptus Mechanicus to be Traitors to the Imperium. Before long, Goge was slain by his own bodyguards, the Brides of the Emperor (who would become the Adepta Sororitas), after Loyalist forces led by the reforming Ecclesiarchy priest Sebastian Thor launched an invasion of Terra to bring Vandire down. 

The High Lords of Terra returned to power after the Senatorum Imperialis was restored, though major reformations, including the creation of the Ordo Hereticus of the Inquisition, were carried out to prevent any one man from ever again seeking to replace the Emperor as the true ruler of Mankind.

Era Indomitus

In the closing days of the 41st Millennium according to the old Imperial Calendar, the resurrected Primarch Roboute Guilliman returned to Terra during the Terran Crusade and again declared himself Lord Commander of the Imperium and Imperial Regent following a meeting with the Emperor. 

The primarch pledged to deal peacefully with the sitting High Lords and promised to reorganise and rearm the Imperium to face the coming threats caused by the birth of the Great Rift splitting the Imperium in half. Soon after Guilliman returned to power five of the sitting High Lords were removed and replaced due to corruption and incompetence. Guilliman next oversaw a general purge of the Imperial bureaucracy known as the "Primarch's Scourge".

Many of the High Lords continued to oppose Guilliman, longing for a return to the relatively stagnant yet highly stable era of what Terrans called the Imperium Eterna. This culminated in an attempted coup d'etat by the Hexarchy, a group of rebels composed of both current and deposed High Lords who refused to accept the reforms Guilliman imposed upon the Imperium, led by the former Master of the Administratum Irthu Haemotalio. In the end, the Hexarchy's coup failed when it was foiled by the actions of Grand Master of Assassins Fadix. 

The High Twelve

Since the dark days of the Horus Heresy, the number of seats on the Senatorum Imperialis has largely remained the same, fluctuating during various points of crisis before eventually returning to its original number of twelve. Each seat is filled by a leader from one of the most powerful adepta and other organisations of the Imperium. A complex web of tradition, skulduggery, promises of support, threats of retaliation and considerations of mutual interest binds them together and determines who holds office and who does not. 

In practice, some of the Imperium's adepta and institutions are so powerful and vital that it would be unthinkable for their leader to not be granted a seat amongst the High Lords of Terra. Naturally, over the long millennia, the unthinkable has happened many times over, however, and the existing High Lords often put in place an inordinate amount of measures to ensure that their seat is a permanent one -- that upon their deaths their position is automatically filled by the new head of their organisation. 

The following offices are almost invariably represented as High Lords because they form the cornerstones of the Imperium, the most important of its ancient institutions. Although the sources regarding the exact composition of the Senatorum Imperialis are varied, some institutions and organisations within the Imperium are so powerful that their leaders can be considered a de facto High Lord. The following nine offices are almost always represented as High Lords:

The Master of the Adeptus Administratum

The Representative of the Inquisition

The Ecclesiarch of the Adeptus Ministorum

The Fabricator-General of the Adeptus Mechanicus

The Grand Provost Marshal of the Adeptus Arbites

The Paternoval Envoy of the Navigators

The Master of the Astronomican

The Grand Master of the Officio Assassinorum

The Master of the Adeptus Astra Telepathica

These nine posts are virtually sacrosanct, and there are very few times in the history of the Imperium when their seats upon the High Lords of Terra became empty and were not filled with a successor from the same organisation. 

Note that a specific Inquisitor does not typically hold the position of Inquisitorial Representative on their own, but instead, the seat is retained for whichever individual is sent on behalf of the Inquisition during a meeting of the Senatorum. The eldest Grand Master of the Grey Knights is also occasionally present as the Inquisitorial Representative. 

Similarly, the place of the Paternoval Envoy is open to whoever might be the Envoy of the Paternova of the current ruling family of Navigators. The Paternova themselves never leaves the Palace of Navigators, for it is forbidden for them to do so. 

To prevent a single house from initiating a monopoly on power, the strongest Navigator houses usually agree to elect a Paternova from the weakest Navigator houses. If a strong house were to rise to power in the Senatorum Imperialis, that house would gain control of the Navigator's voice in the Imperium and thereby control all but the strongest Navigator families.

The total number of High Lords is said to be twelve, leaving three non-permanent openings. These positions are most often filled from among the following powerful Imperial leaders:

Lord Commander of the Segmentum Solar

Lord Commander Militant of the Astra Militarum

Lord High Admiral of the Navis Imperialis

Cardinal(s) of the Holy Synod of Terra

Abbess Sanctorum of the Adepta Sororitas

Captain-General of the Adeptus Custodes

Chancellor of the Estate Imperium

Speaker for the Chartist Captains (representative of the Imperial merchant fleets and the Chartist Captains)

Lord Constable of the Synopticon

It is an oddity that, throughout its history, very few members of the Adeptus Astartes have served among the High Lords of Terra -- given the importance of Humanity's most elite fighting force and the fact that the first council was initiated by Roboute Guilliman, the primarch of the Ultramarines, Imperial Regent and Lord Commander of the Imperium. 

This lack seems to have been an intentional choice by Guilliman, who knew that at times of great need, Space Marine leaders would have no choice but to step in and help guide the Imperium, but would otherwise remain outside Humanity's ruling structure. Some say the primarch's discouragement of Space Marines serving in the Senatorum Imperialis was based upon the Emperor's original Council of Terra -- which was separate from His War Council, and a ruling body that did not include any Astartes. Guilliman clearly believed, as his great work, the Codex Astartes points out, that it is the Space Marines' duty to serve Mankind, not to rule it, just as the Emperor Himself held.

In its long existence, the Senatorum Imperialis has gone through many changes. The High Lords have been forced to give one of its seats over to a religious leader (the Ecclesiarch, who joined shortly after the Adeptus Ministorum was named the sole state religion of the Imperium in the early 32nd Millennium), wiped out to a man by assassination (on the orders of a slighted Grand Master of the Officio Assassinorum, an event known as "The Beheading"), and dissolved altogether by the ruling Ecclesiarch (during the civil war known as the "Reign of Blood" during the Age of Apostasy in the 36th Millennium). 

Many members have disappeared under suspicious circumstances and the Inquisition has been asked to investigate a number of times (although many have suggested that at least some missing High Lords of Terra have disappeared because of the Inquisition). 

Yet always, despite the many power struggles and strife, the High Lords of Terra have continued to interpret the Will of the Emperor and thereby rule the greatest empire in the galaxy.

Lesser High Lords

The actual institution of the Senatorum Imperialis itself is overseen by an official known as the Chancellor of the Imperial Council. While not among the High Twelve, it is nonetheless a powerful and influential position because of its proximity to power. There are also lesser lords who, while not counted among the High Twelve, were still at various times across Imperial history considered full members of the Senatorum Imperialis.

Commandant of the Schola Progenium

Lord Constable of the Synopticon

Mistress Plenary of the Catacombs

Consul Pre-Eminus of the Navis Nobilite

Chirurgeon-General of the Orders Hospitaller

High Lord of the Imperial Chancellery

At Present

In the wake of the resurrection of Roboute Guilliman during the Ultramar Campaign of the 13th Black Crusade in ca. 999.M41 and the primarch's return to Terra during the Terran Crusade, Guilliman has reestablished himself as the ruling Lord Commander of the Imperium and Imperial Regent for the second time since the end of the Horus Heresy. Upon his resurrection, Guilliman had replaced five of the High Twelve of the Senatorum Imperialis upon his reassumption of the regency of the Imperium, and hundreds of the lesser high lords that comprised the Terran nobility.

The High Lords of Terra now serve as a council for Guilliman and the chief administrators of their organs of Imperial power, but for the first time in many millennia the Imperium is once more functionally controlled by one individual on a day-to-day basis rather than an oligarchy of competing interests. 

The Imperium entered the Era Indomitus and unleashed the Indomitus Crusade more politically united than it had been in many standard centuries. 

This was a necessity; only the organisational skills of a primarch serving as the Imperium's de facto ruler offered Humanity any hope of survival in the wake of the birth of the Great Rift and the fall of the Noctis Aeterna.

Yet even a primarch's return did not completely silence the political tensions that always rippled through the Imperium's upper echelons. Guilliman discovered that there was some resistance among various planetary governors to his dictate that the Realm of Ultramar was to be reconstituted to its original size of five hundred worlds. These governors often outright refused to honour the ancient treaties that had originally bound their worlds to Ultramar or even declared that their worlds had never been a part of that realm. By the time of the Battle of Raukos that ended the first phase of the Indomitus Crusade some twelve standard years after its inception, only three-quarters of the worlds that had originally been part of Ultramar before the Horus Heresy had been restored to the Lord of Macragge's rule.

Additionally, Guilliman allowed the Adeptus Mechanicus to maintain the autonomy it had long practiced under the terms of the ancient Treaty of Mars the original Mechanicum had made with the Emperor, and recognised that he could not run roughshod over the Tech-priests' beliefs without unleashing a potentially diastrous schism in their ranks. 

The primarch repeatedly refused Archmagos Dominus Belisarius Cawl's request to be named fabricator-general of Mars, explaining that he did not have the power to do so, but if he attempted it, it would unleash a second Martian civil war as many of the more orthodox believers in the Cult Mechanicus viewed Cawl and his faction, so dedicated to technological innovation, as Hereteks.

Composition

Era Indomitus

Following the reorganisation of the Imperium upon the resurrection of Primarch Roboute Guilliman at the dawn of the Era Indomitus, the then-current Senatorum Imperialis was dissolved. Following his meeting with the Emperor, Guilliman declared himself Lord Commander of the Imperium, Imperial Regent, and Lord Militant of the Senatorium Imperialis and took up a period of personal rule. 

Sometime later during the Indomitus Crusade, the High Twelve were restored with Guilliman serving as their chair when he determined that he needed to restore some finer control over the bureaucracy. By this point, he had replaced five previous High Lords as well as hundreds of the lesser lords, and founded the new Imperial institutions of the Officio Logisticarum and the Logos Historica Verita. Guilliman's general purge of the Imperial bureaucracy became known as the "Primarch's Scourge".

Many of the High Lords continued to oppose Guilliman, longing for a return to the era of what the Terran nobility had known as the Imperium Eterna. This culminated in an attempted coup d'etat by the Hexarchy, a group of rebels composed of both current and deposed High Lords who refused to accept the reforms Guilliman imposed upon the Imperium, led by the former Master of the Administratum Irthu Haemotalio. In the end, the Hexarchy's coup failed when it was foiled by the actions of Grand Master of Assassins Fadix. 

When Guilliman first reestablished the Senatorum Imperialis during the Indomitus Crusade, before the Hexarchy's attempted coup, it was comprised of the following members:

Roboute Guilliman - Lord Commander of the Imperium, Imperial Regent, Lord Militant of the Senatorum Imperialis and Primarch of the Ultramarines Chapter and its successors.

Trajann Valoris - Captain-General of the Adeptus Custodes

Violeta Roskavler - Master of the Administratum

Lucius Throde - Master of the Astronomican

Zlatad Aph Kerapliades - Master of the Adeptus Astra Telepathica

Eos Ritira - Ecclesiarch of the Adeptus Ministorum

Kleopatra Arx - Inquisitorial Representative of the Inquisition

Kadak Mir - Paternoval Envoy of the Navis Nobilite

Xasandera Valdet - Fabricator-General of the Adeptus Mechanicus and High Priest of the Cult Mechanicus

Aveliza Drachmar — Grand Provost Marshal of the Adeptus Arbites (Later slain in the Hexarchy's coup attempt)

Mar Av Ashariel - Lord Commander Militant of the Astra Militarum (Later slain in the Hexarchy's coup attempt)

Merelda Pereth - Lord High Admiral of the Navis Imperialis (Later slain in the Hexarchy's coup attempt)

Fadix - Grand Master of Assassins

Morvenn Vahl - Abbess of the Adepta Sororitas

Theromestes Xemprye Kleng - Unknown position
