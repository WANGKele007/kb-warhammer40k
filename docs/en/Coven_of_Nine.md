The Coven of Nine is a warband of Chaos Space Marines dedicated to the service of Tzeentch, the Lord of Change. They participated in the assault on the Stygius Sector during the Stygius Crusade that ultimately led to the conquest of that region of the Imperium for the Changer of Ways. The Coven of Nine was led, as the name implies, by nine Exalted Sorcerers of Tzeentch. The Sorcerers lead a vast warhost that includes daemons, Chaos Space Marines, and hordes of Chaos Cultists. Little else is currently known about them in Imperial records.
