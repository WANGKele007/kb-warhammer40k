The Ryza-class Battle Barge is a battle barge class of the Adeptus Astartes. 

Though six Ryza-class Battle Barges were built by the Adeptus Mechanicus in the 37th Millennium in the dockyards of the Forge World of Ryza, by the 41st Millennium only three were still extant, their construction techniques having been lost by the Adeptus Mechanicus in the interim. 

The Ryza-class is renowned for the steadfastness of each vessel's construction, having been designed to run minefields and asteroid blockades and withstand damage that would destroy other voidship types. 

Theoretically, a Ryza-class battle barge should still be intact enough to be capable of deploying its complement of troops even after crashing onto a planetary surface, though it would suffer such great damage that the ship would never again be able to achieve orbit.

Notable Ryza-class Battle Barges

Tempestus (Astral Knights) - In 912.M41, the Astral Knights were among the fifteen Space Marine Chapters who responded to the appearance of the Necron World Engine in the Vidar Sector. The World Engine's void shields were impervious to any weapon the Imperial task force could fire at it, and likewise repelled any attempt to board the vessel with Drop Pods, Boarding Torpedoes or teleporters. In a gamble, the Astral Knights' Chapter Master, Artor Amhrad, piloted the Tempestus on a bow-first collision course, piercing the World Engine's shields with its adamantium-tipped prow, and allowing the entire Chapter to deploy aboard the xenos vessel via Drop Pods once the Astartes vessel was within the World Engine's shield envelope. This gamble allowed the Astral Knights to sabotage the World Engine's shields and render it vulnerable to destruction by the rest of the Imperial fleet, but every last battle-brother of the Astral Knights was slain, including Amhrad. At the insistence of Captain Aphael of the Blood Angels, the Ultramarines salvaged the wreck of the Tempestus and converted it into an Imperial shrine dedicated to the Astral Knights on the world of Safehold, the last Imperial planet to be scoured clean by the World Engine. The shrine contains 772 statues, one for each of the fallen Astral Knights. Though Safehold remains lifeless and deserted except for scavengers, the shrine continues to be guarded by volunteers from all the other Chapters who fought alongside the Astral Knights.
