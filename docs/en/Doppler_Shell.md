The Doppler Shell is a type of ammunition used by Imperial warships' Nova Cannons. When detonated, a Doppler Shell releases a massive burst of gravimetric waves. These act as a form of gravimetric sonar which bounce off the hulls of nearby starships and expose hidden enemies to the auspex of the warship which fired the shell.
