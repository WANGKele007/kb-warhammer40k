The Emperor's Hawks is a Loyalist, Codex Astartes-compliant Space Marines Chapter of unknown origin and Founding date. 

The Chapter specialises in Jump Pack-assisted assaults, followed by squadron-strength attacks by highly-mobile units of Land Speeders.

Chapter Appearance

Chapter Colours

The Emperor's Hawks primarily wear red power armour. The Aquila or Imperialis on the chest plate, as well as the shoulder pauldron trim, are yellow. 

The right knee plate bears a white squad specialty symbol -- battleline, close support, fire support, Veteran or command. 

A black High Gothic numeral is centred upon the squad specialty symbol, indicating squad number. The white High Gothic numeral on the left knee designates company number.

Chapter Badge

The Emperor's Hawks' Chapter badge is a single stylised, white hawk's wing, on a field of red. 

This symbol is used on both the right and left shoulder plates.
