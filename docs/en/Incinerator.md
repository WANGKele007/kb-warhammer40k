An Incinerator is a powerful Flamer weapon specifically created for the use of the Grey Knights Space Marines but also used by the Ordo Malleus' specially-trained Pyroclast Acolytes.

The Incinerator has many of the same characteristics as the standard Imperial Heavy Flamer but is fuelled by a special mix of consecrated promethium and Ecclesiarchy-blessed oils which are especially effective against Daemons and produce what is known as "psyflame."

An Incinerator is a heavily modified variant of the Heavy Flamer, its fire projectors and containment vessels significantly altered in order to
accommodate its unique, psychically-impregnated promethium fuel. When triggered, the weapon gouts forth an incandescent torrent of azure or white flame that burns far hotter than any "normal" combustion ever could with the added power of the pure faith of the billions who believe in the divinity of the Emperor of Mankind.

Heavy Incinerator

A Heavy incinerator is a much larger and more powerful version of the standard Incinerator.

It is only known to be mounted on Nemesis Dreadknights.

Flamestorm Incinerator

A Flamestorm Incinerator is a large, vehicle-mounted version of the Incinerator that is mounted on the Vortimer Pattern Land Raider Redeemer used by the Grey Knights.

Atalan Incinerator

The Atalan Incinerator is a heavy weapon very similar to the Incinerator of the Grey Knights which unleashes blasts of blazing promethium but without the the special consecration or the addition of the oils blessed by the Adeptus Ministorum to produce "psyflame."

The Atalan Incinerator is a favoured weapon of the Atalan Jackal outriders who serve the Genestealer Cults that infest many Imperial worlds.
