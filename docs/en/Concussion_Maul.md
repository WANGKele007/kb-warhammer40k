A Concussion Maul is a type of two-handed Concussion Weapon wielded by the warriors of the Leagues of Votann. 

Like all Concussion Weapons, be they in the shape of warhammers, mauls or even just reinforced armoured gauntlets, the Concussion Maul mounts so-called "mass driver" technology that magnifies the force of its impact to a colossal degree, producing a wave of concussive force far greater than what would be created by the simple mass of the maul itself. 

Concussion Mauls are often wielded as alternative melee weapons by Cthonian Beserks.
