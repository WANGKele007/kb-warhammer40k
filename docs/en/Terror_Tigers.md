The Terror Tigers is a Loyalist Space Marine Chapter of unknown Founding and genetic origin. 

Chapter History

Notable Campaigns

Shame of the Terror Tigers (Unknown Date.M41) - The Terror Tigers lost one quarter of their Chapter's strength, about two and a half companies' worth of Astartes, when a mutant warlord assaulted their homeworld, after which their Chapter Master ordered his own execution as punishment.

Salvation of Hannibal (Unknown Date.M41) - An exploration team of Terror Tigers rediscovered the lost Feral World of Hannibal, intervening in an Craftworld Aeldari attempt to reclaim the world as their own. The team dramatically came to the primitive Human natives' rescue from the hands of the deceitful xenos but was ultimately destroyed. The Terror Tigers were still too undermanned to send forces to defend the world, so it fell to the company of the Imperial Fists Captain Lexandro D'Arquebus to aid Hannibal and avenge the deaths of the Terror Tigers' exploration team.
