The Tempest Guard is a Loyalist Space Marine Chapter of unknown Founding and origin. 

Almost nothing is known about this Chapter in Imperial records.

Chapter Appearance

Chapter Colours

The Tempest Guard primarily wears midnight blue power armour, which includes the power pack, chest plate, shoulder plate trim, torso, groin and upper legs. 

The Aquila or Imperialis on the chest guard, as well as the helm and greaves are white. The shoulder plate insets are bone white. 

The black squad specialty symbol -- battleline, close support, fire support, Veteran or command -- is located on the right shoulder plate. 

A white High Gothic numeral is stenciled in the centre of the squad specialty symbol, indicating squad number.

A black High Gothic numeral on the left poleyn (knee guard) indicates company number.

Chapter Badge

The Tempest Guard's Chapter badge is a black winged-livery shield, with a black Iron Halo crowning it, centred upon a field of bone white.
