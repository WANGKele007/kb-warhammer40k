A Plasma Torch is a tool utilised by the Brôkhyr of the Leagues of Votann and their E-COG robotic assistants to carry out welding repairs and cut through metal in the course of their duties. 

In a pinch, the Plasma Torch's powerful integrated plasma cutting field can be wielded as an impromptu melee Plasma Weapon that can slice through flesh and heavy armour alike.

Sources

Codex: Leagues of Votann (9th Edition), pp. 92, 100
