An Electrocorrosive Whip is a powerful Drukhari weapon that is similar to an Agoniser and is a lashing tongue of venom-soaked polymer with a high-yield electrical dynamo in its hilt. 

Its touch is painful and debilitating in the extreme, sapping the strength and robbing the will of the enemy to fight.
