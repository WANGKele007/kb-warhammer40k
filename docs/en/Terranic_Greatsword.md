The Terranic Greatsword was a large, two-handed Power Sword used by the Knights Cenobium of the Dark Angels Space Marine Legion during the Great Crusade and Horus Heresy eras.

Notable Terannic Greatswords

The Blade - This masterworked Terranic Greatsword was granted to Corswain, the equerry to Primarch Lion El'Jonson by his mentor, Alajos. It is said The Blade was once raised aloft at Advex-Mors by Grand Master Urian Vendraig to incite the Rangdan Xenocides, and was wielded in the violence of every campaign against the foul xenos breed by a champion of the Ist Legion. 

Notable Variants

Advex-Mors Greatsword

A unique pattern of the iconic Ist Legion Terranic Greatswords, the Advex-Mors Pattern was created by the Cenobites of the Order of Broken Claws in the wake of the Rangdan Xenocides, using the valuable experience earned by the sacrifice of their brothers in the wars against the xenos bio-monstrosities of the Rangda.
