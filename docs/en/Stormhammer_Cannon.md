The Stormhammer Cannon is a powerful ballistic artillery weapon that fires explosive shells and serves as the primary armament of the Astra Militarum's Stormhammer Super-Heavy Assault Tank. It is particularly designed for taking on large groups of infantry, such as mobs of Ork Boyz. It is larger and more potent than the Battle Cannon that is used as the standard primary weapon on the Leman Russ Tank.
