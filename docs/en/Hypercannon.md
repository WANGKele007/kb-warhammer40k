A hypercannon was a powerful weapon created by the most skilled Necron Crypteks over sixty million Terran years ago on the orders of Szarekh, the Silent King, during the War in Heaven, that were intended to be used in the Necrons' planned revolt against their C'tan masters. The C'tan arrogantly believed that the weapons were created to honour them and were caught by surprise when they were instead turned against them in the opening stages of the Necron rebellion. 

Hypercannons work by focusing the unimaginable energies of the universe into an attack so powerful that it damages space-time in such a way that causality itself is altered. Not even the C'tan could endure the forces unleashed by these weapons so they were defeated during the revolt in a battle that not even the great Necron Overlords of the crownworlds can remember due to the damage the weapons inflicted on the space-time continuum.

In the aftermath of the Necron revolt, Szarekh removed the knowledge of how to create such dreadful weapons from the Necron species' memory engrams in fear of what might later be done with them.
