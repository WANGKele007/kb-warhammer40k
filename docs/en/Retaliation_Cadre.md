A Retaliation Cadre is a type of T'au Empire Fire Caste cadre that is comprised of multiple teams of battlesuits, particularly stealthsuits, that have been grouped into a single force ideal for rapid insertion drops and counter-assault.

Sources

Codex: Tau Empire (6th Edition), pp. 8-12, 22-24
Codex: Tau Empire (7th Edition), pp. 15-16
