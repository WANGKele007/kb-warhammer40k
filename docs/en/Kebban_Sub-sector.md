The Kebban Sub-sector was a region of Imperial space that became infected with Orks during the events of the Psychic Awakening in the Era Indomitus.

After being infested with Orks, a horde of these xenos began to emerge from the Kebban Sub-sector to assault the surrounding Imperial worlds. The Inquisition discovered that this WAAAGH! was set to collide with a different Ork horde led by the Warboss Bakrash, enhancing the potential destructive power of both. 

To prevent this from happening, the Ordo Xenos tasked the Deathwatch with killing Bakrash, but the operation ended in failure. This caused Bakrash to lead his WAAAGH! into the Kebban Sub-sector and make war upon the Orks already rampaging within it. 

Fearing that a Warlord would soon emerge to unite the Orks, the Ordo Xenos immediately requested the aid of several companies from the Rift Cobras, Templars of the Covenant and the Iron Hounds Chapters of the Adeptus Astartes. 

The Rift Cobras despatched 3 companies, the Templars of the Covenant 4 and the Iron Hounds 2. The Ordo now believes that the forces of these Chapters will successfully destroy the Orks of this region while the xenos are still focussed on combating each other.
