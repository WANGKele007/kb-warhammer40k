The Tempest Hammer is a melee weapon in the form of a great warhammer used by the Space Marines of the Space Wolves Chapter. Through unknown technology, an aura of killing frost crawls across the head of the weapon, ticking and cracking like pack ice on Fenris. 

When the warhammer is swung, this frigid energy discharges in a blast that can freeze the target of the weapon's wrath solid, which the wielded of the Tempest Hammer can then smash apart like glass. This deadly combination means that the wielder can often reduce his foe to shattered shards of crimson ice in but a single blow.
