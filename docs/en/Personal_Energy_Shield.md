A Personal Energy Shield is a personal protective device available to the Imperial military and to the local planetary and personal security forces of nobles on many worlds of the Imperium, including Necromunda. A Personal Energy Shield is a device that can generate a small force field in the shape of a standard infantry shield to provide to its wielder protection from kinetic and directed energy attacks.

Notable Patterns

Hystar Pattern Personal Energy Shield - The Hystar Pattern Personal Energy Shield is used by the forces of House Van Saar, one of the Clan Houses of Necromunda.
