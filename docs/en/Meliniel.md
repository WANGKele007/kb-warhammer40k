Meliniel is an Autarch of Craftworld Biel-Tan and twin brother of the Farseer Lathriel.

During the Battle of Biel-Tan, he commanded the assault on the former Maiden World of Ursulia and later the defences of the craftworld against the daemons of Chaos.

He later chose to join the Ynnari.
