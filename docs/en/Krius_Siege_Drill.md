A Krius Siege Drill is a Titan-grade siege weapon intended to be used by a Warmaster Iconoclast-class Heavy Battle Titan to drill through stubborn enemy static fortifications so that it can destroy the defenders within. 

The Krius Siege Drill is contained within a four-fingered claw that grants the Titan additional ways to rip apart fortified bunkers, walls or other Titans in close combat.
