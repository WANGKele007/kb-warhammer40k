The Traveller is a short story published in The Horus Heresy series that was originally part of an anthology. The Traveller was first published as part of the Mark of Calth main series anthology in April 2013. The cover image used is that of Mark of Calth, as The Traveller has yet to be published separately.

Synopsis

A survivor of the Word Bearers' initial surprise attack on the orbital defences of Calth finds that he may not be alone.
