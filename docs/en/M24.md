ca. M19-M24 The Ailing Aeldari Pantheon - The worship of the traditional Aeldari gods has largely waned among the people of the Aeldari Empire by this time as new sects and secret societies have claimed the majority of the Aeldari's devotion. The Dark Muses, many of whom are synonymous in Aeldari culture with sensual vice and uncontrolled, hedonistic sin, become the unofficial figureheads and figures of worship in the new order.

ca. M24 Kin Homeworlds Isolated - What will eventually become the Kin ("Squat") homeworlds in the galactic core are cut off from the rest of interstellar Human society in the late 24th Millennium as large Warp storms begin near the galactic centre, preventing interstellar trade and communication. These worlds were rich in strategic minerals needed by the interstellar Human civilisation of the Age of Technology which is what had led the Human mining fleets into the core whose employees were later genetically modified by the self-aware machine intelligences called the Votann into the Abhuman Kin to better survive the region's myriad environmental hazards. Cut off for the next 5,000 Terran years from the rest of Mankind, the people of these worlds will adapt through both natural means and genetic engineering to their planets' local environmental conditions, becoming known as the Squats. This period sees the formation of the Leagues of Votann.
