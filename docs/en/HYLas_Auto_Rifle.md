The HYLas Auto Rifle, which is more formally known as the High Yield Laser Auto Rifle, is a powerful, man-portable Laser Weapon used by the military forces of the Leagues of Votann. The weapon possesses an extremely long range and both a high rate and high yield of laser fire for excellent stopping power, making it one of the preferred ranged weapons deployed among Hearthkyn Warriors, the Kin's primary troops of the line. 

However, Kin weapons like the HYLas Auto Rifle are superior in almost every respect to those patterns of Laser Weapons wielded by the agents and military of the Imperium, since the Kin employ superlative materials and methods in their construction and have no fear of further innovating on an ancient STC design. As a result, the HYLas Auto Rifle and its variants often have longer ranges and more stopping power than any of their Imperial equivalents.
