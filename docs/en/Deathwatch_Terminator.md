A Deathwatch Terminator, usually part of a Deathwatch Terminator Squad, is a Veteran Firstborn or Primaris Space Marine who serves in the Deathwatch Chapter as a Terminator or Assault Terminator wearing Tactical Dreadnought Armour.

The indomitable Deathwatch Veterans honoured to wear the hulking Terminator Armour are an inspiring sight to their brethren. Deathwatch Terminators carry the most powerful close combat weapons and the strength and durability of their armour allows them to take the heaviest ranged firepower directly into hidden xenos lairs.
