The High Host, sometimes known as the Archae or the Principatii, was a fraternity of the Blood Angels Legion, one of the lesser Orders of the First Sphere of the Legion, during the Great Crusade and Horus Heresy. The High Host was made up of those warriors who bore the sorrowful duty of delivering Primarch Sanguinius' ultimate sanction to his enemies. 

Unlike their brothers in the Angel's Tears, the High Host did not shrink away from showing their true faces to an uncaring galaxy as they deployed weapons born of Humanity's darkest nightmares that left a trail of murdered worlds in their wake.
