A Mass Gauntlet is a powerful melee weapon wielded by the military forces of the Leagues of Votann that takes the form of a massive, armoured gauntlet. It incorporates "mass driver" technology that allows its wielder to magnify the force of its impact to a colossal degree beyond the simple mass of the gauntlet itself.

A Mass Gauntlet is a favoured alternative melee weapon for Leagues of Votann kâhls.

Sources

Codex: Leagues of Votann (9th Edition), pp. 89, 100
