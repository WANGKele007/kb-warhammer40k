The Founding is the first omnibus in the Gaunt's Ghosts series of novels by Dan Abnett. The Founding gathers together the first three novels and one short story in the Gaunt's Ghosts series.

Synopsis

In the Chaos-infested Sabbat Worlds, the massed ranks of the Imperial Guard stand shoulder to shoulder as they counter an invasion by heretical forces. Amongst the Guard are the troops of the Tanith First-and-Only, a displaced army forced to flee their home planet before it succumbed to the unrelenting assault of Chaos. Nicknamed the "Ghosts," their specialist scouting role has thrown them into the thickest of the fighting, trying to complete whatever desperate mission their uncaring commanders have volunteered them for. Now, their leader Ibram Gaunt must evade the treacherous scheming of rival regiments and the lethal firepower of the enemy if he is to win through and lead his Ghosts to victory.
