The Hellfyre Missile Rack is a Missile Launcher system that is deployed on the Terminator Armour of the Scarab Occult Terminators of the Thousand Sons Traitor Legion. 

Hellfyre Missiles are compact but deadly warheads that explode with the power of the Warp, unleashing detonations of aetheric light.
