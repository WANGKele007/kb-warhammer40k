The Astartes Praeses are a group of twenty Loyalist Space Marine Chapters whose sole purpose is to guard the Eye of Terror in the Segmentum Obscurus. The ancient tome known as the Mythos Angelica Mortis makes a single reference to 20 Space Marine Chapters created from the unique gene-seed of those primarchs who did not fall to Chaos during the Horus Heresy and who were charged with protecting the Imperial sectors surrounding the Eye of Terror and supporting Cadia and the Inquisition as needed. 

Although the brotherhood of Space Marines included in the Astartes Praeses originally included 20 Chapters, one has been destroyed by the forces of Chaos and two other Chapters, the Relictors and the Sons of Malice, were declared Excommunicate Traitoris by the High Lords of Terra. It should be noted, however, that the latter Chapter is only suspected of being one of the original 20 Chapters mentioned in the Mythos Angelica Mortis. 

The Astartes Praeses were evidently founded anew rather than assembled from the remnants of the Space Marine Legions, like many of the other Second Founding Chapters, to reduce the possibility of corruption by Veteran Marines who had been exposed to Chaos during the fighting of the Horus Heresy.
