Blueblood is a short story published in the Gaunt's Ghosts series that was originally part of an anthology. Blueblood was published as part of the Sabbat Worlds anthology in October 2010, and was later re-published as a stand-alone e-book.

Synopsis

As the war in the Sabbat Worlds drags on, the Volpone Bluebloods -- an elite Astra Militarum unit known as much for their aristocratic disdain as their fury in battle -- find themselves encamped with several other regiments. Sabotage and mysterious deaths signal a Chaos taint in the camp, and the Bluebloods investigate. They must overcome their distaste for the primitive warriors around them and band together to find the source of the corruption before it damns them all.
