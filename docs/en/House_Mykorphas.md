House Mykorphas is a Chaos Knight house that serves the Plague God Nurgle. As a result of this allegiance, the Mykorphas Fallen Nobles have seen their Knights Despoiler become slowly coated in living rust.

Many of their more complex weapons have failed over time as a result of this corruption, so that now the house relies on verdigrised Rapid-Fire Battle Cannons and Avenger Gatling Cannons, and plague-riddled Stormspear Rocket Pods, wherever possible.
