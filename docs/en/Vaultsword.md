A Vaultsword is a type of archeotech power sword that is wielded by the elite Blade Champions of the Adeptus Custodes. They can be wielded either as single greatswords, known as "Klaimors," or paired with a slightly smaller "Nemis," depending upon what the Blade Champion's chosen Martial Ka'tah requires.
