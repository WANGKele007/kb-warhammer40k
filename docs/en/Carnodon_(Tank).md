The Carnodon, also called the Saturnyne Pattern Carnodon after the most commonly produced pattern, is an Imperial medium battle tank that was deployed by the forces of the Imperial Army during the Great Crusade and Horus Heresy. It was particularly popular among the elite armoured regiments of the Solar Auxilia. 

The Carnodon operated in strike squadrons of up to three Carnodons at a time. It was a potent armoured vehicle intended to provide heavy fire support for infantry. To this tactical end, it was outfitted with one set of twin-linked Lascannons, Multi-Lasers, Autocannons or a single Volkite Culverin as a main turret weapon. Additionally, it was armed with two sponson-mounted Autocannons, Lascannons, Multi-Lasers, Heavy Bolters, Heavy Flamers, or Volkite Calivers.

Some of these tanks have survived into the Era Indomitus of the 41st Millennium where they are revered relics among those few armoured regiments of the Astra Militarum lucky enough to possess one. Yet these relics will not remain outliers for long. With the hour so dark as the Imperium finds itself assaulted on all sides in the wake of the Great Rift's birth, and the need for trusted war assets greater than ever, the Priesthood of Mars has reinstated the STC template for Carnodon production on several Forge Worlds. New variants of the ancient tank have trundled forth from their manufactoria once more to aid the servants of the Emperor in their most desperate hour.

Role

The Carnodon is a versatile medium battle tank which acted as the lynchpin for many of the storied successes of the Imperial Army during the Great Crusade. As a premier mobile fire platform, it was regularly adapted to combat a wide vareity of foes and respond to any number of battlefield conditions. 

At Gamma-Incunablar, a squadron of the 43rd Veradan Regiment's Carnodons, fitted with las weapons, brought down the first of the great Basemekanic beasts which had subjugated that world, their concentrated firepower surpassing even the Predator tanks of the III Legion to punch through the vast creature's thick metallic hide.

On Hektun, the 3rd Saturnyne Rams earned their reputation as the "Incinerators" of the Solar Auxilia when their Carnodons, fitted entirely with Volkite Weapons, were used to strategically ignite the hydrogen-rich vents of the Phenaem's tunnel structures, eradicating the foul xenos within.

This battle tank was foremost of many initiatives undertaken by the Imperial military to exploit the modular design of the relatively new Aurox chassis by creating variants based on it. During the early years of the Great Crusade, it rapidly became a byword for conquest among the expeditionary fleets, a legacy that led to the production of the dedicated Imperial main battle tanks of later days.

As the Great Crusade moved ever outwards and supply of the Aurox chassis peaked, it is thought that many Carnodon tanks entered a strategic reserve and were distributed to muster worlds behind the front lines, with over 200,000 examples thought to have been stored pending secondary mobilisation in the warrens under Tallarn, where they would bolster the forces of the Loyalists during the Battle of Tallarn of the Horus Heresy.

Variants

Saturnyne Pattern Carnodon

The Saturnyne Pattern Carnodon was the standard variant of the Carnodon medium battle tank exployed by the Solar Auxilia's Carnodon Strike Squadrons during the Great Crusade and Horus Heresy.
