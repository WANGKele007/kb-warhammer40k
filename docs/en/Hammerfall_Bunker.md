A Hammerfall Bunker is a space-to-surface deployable, heavily-fortified automated bastion used by the Space Marines to secure territory during their planetary assault campaigns.

Hammerfall Bunkers are launched from Adeptus Astartes warships in orbit in the same manner as Drop Pods. Automated area-denial assets crewed by hard-wired servitors, they serve all kinds of tactical roles, including securing hard-won beachheads during a planetary assault, hampering enemy offensives and wreaking havoc if dropped behind enemy lines.

The bunker's primary armament is the Hammerfall Missile Launcher. Anti-personnel weapony is supplied by an array of Heavy Bolters or Heavy Flamers.

Wargear

Hammerfall Missile Launcher
Superfrag Missiles - Superfrag Missiles are used as even more explosively powerful anti-personnel weapons than standard Frag Missiles since they explode into thousands of highly lethal fragments of shrapnel that can easily shred even heavily armoured infantry.
Superkrak Missiles - Superkrak Missiles are even more potent anti-armour weapons than standard Krak Missiles that can shred heavy armour plates with their powerful, shaped explosion.
