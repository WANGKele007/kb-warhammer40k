A Heavy Rock Saw is a type of Imperial mining tool that is sometimes used as a melee weapon by Acolyte Hybrids of the Genestealer Cults. The blades of the Heavy Rock Saw are designed to cut through dense boulders of ore -- when the time of war comes, they slice through the hulls of enemy vehicles as easily as a butcher slices through meat.
