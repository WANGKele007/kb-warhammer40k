The Roaring Blades are a Traitor Imperial Army formation dedicated to the Chaos God Slaanesh. They had close ties to the Emperor's Children Traitor Legion, whom they served with before, during, and after the Horus Heresy, in a similar style as that of the Prospero Spireguard that served the Thousand Sons. They were originally known as the "Righteous Blades" before they fell from grace and turned against the Imperium.

History

Long ago, during the era of the Great Crusade, the Righteous Blades were one of the most decorated and respected infantry units in the Imperial Army. Vassals of Primarch Fulgrim, the Righteous Blades provided infantry support for the III Legion. The Righteous Blades fought on countless worlds in the name of the Emperor, and won several victories. But when the Emperor's Children turned Traitor and joined the Warmaster Horus in dedicating themselves to the Dark Gods of Chaos, the Righteous Blades followed suit, pledging their souls to the desires of the Lord of Pleasure. 

The Imperium lost a proud band of warriors that day, but the Righteous Blades lost their souls, becoming pleasure-addicted acolytes of Slaanesh. These Emperor-forsaken Heretics then became forever after known as the "Roaring Blades." 

It is believed by Imperial intelligence that the nervous systems of the traitorous troopers of the Roaring Blades have been altered by the corrupted Apothecaries of the Emperor's Children. As a result, their bodies react to pain as if it were adrenaline, meaning that wounds sustained in battle only drive them further into the throes of ecstatic violence.

Notable Campaigns

Scouring of Vernalis (Unknown Date) - During an unknown point in Imperial history, the Roaring Blades were used by the Emperor's Children Traitor Legion to help invade and conquer the Mining World of Vernalis. This led them into direct conflict with the Imperial Fists Chapter. In due time the Imperial Fists were able to halt the invasion and neutralise the forces of Chaos on Vernalis, including elements of the Roaring Blades.

Organisation

The Roaring Blades have a preference for close combat with bladed weapons of various kinds while screaming out in ecstasy throughout a battle. They also recruit and enslave new members from any and all worlds they encounter, making use of both standard Heretics and mutants. They put little to no effort in maintaining any form of organisational structure in their ranks, as all have become far too selfish and sensation-seeking to adhere to any form of ordered military hierarchy, a common problem among Slaanesh-worshippers.
