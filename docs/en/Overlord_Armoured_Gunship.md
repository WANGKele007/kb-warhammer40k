An Overlord Armoured Gunship is a Squat airship originally developed by the Engineers Guild for the purpose of mining liquid metal from the deep atmospheres of gas giant worlds. Since then, it has been adapted for void warfare with the addition of an armoured gundola and stronger armour. 

Powered by several lighter-than-air gas cells which provide lift, each cell is self-sealing and filled with inert gases to prevent explosions in case of damage. An armoured gondola hangs underneath the hull, equipped with Melta Bomb racks and Battle Cannon turrets. Gravitic thrusters are positioned at the rear of the hull to supply forward power and extra lift. A select crew of Engineers Guild aeronauts operate the craft under the watchful eye of an airship captain, most often the engineer who oversaw the airship's construction.

History

Overlord Armoured Gunships were developed by the Squats' Engineers Guild to harvest liquid metals deep within the dense atmospheres of gas giants. They would cruise through the upper or lower atmosphere of these worlds and were crafted to be able to survive the extreme temperatures and pressures present in these hostile environments. They were later re-evaluated as military assets after the Engineers Guild realised their potential to serve as combat vehicles. The Squats created six prototype airships which could survive and fly in standard void pressure rather than inside the dense atmospheres of gas giants. These Overlord Gunships proved to be a huge success for the Engineers Guild and are now produced in great numbers.

Crew

Overlord Armoured Gunships are typically crewed by a group of Guild Aeronauts who maintain the gravitic engines and operate the gunship's weapons under the command of the airship captain.
