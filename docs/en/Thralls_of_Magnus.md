The Thralls of Magnus are a thrallband of Heretic Astartes of the Thousand Sons Traitor Legion. The Thralls of Magnus are the largest and most powerful sect of the Cult of Scheming, and have been since shortly after the Legion's fall to Chaos. 

Many rival sects have sought to dethrone them, only to succumb to impossibly intricate traps and plots. The same inescapable fate has befallen countless Imperial and xenos worlds.

Perhaps their most notable subsidiary thrallband was known as the Makers of Truth.

Warband Appearance

Warband Colours

The Thralls of Magnus thrallband's colours are azure trimmed in gold. They also wear gold robes and tabbards.

Warband Badge

The Thralls of Magnus thrallband's symbol is a light yellow sigil of the Cult of Scheming centred upon a field of light azure and trimmed in gold.
