Trygg is an Imperial Hive World located in the Pyremoat System of the Bastior Sub-sector in the Segmentum Pacificus. As the Fourth Tyrannic War raged in the segmentum during the Era Indomitus, Trygg acted as a vital stronghold for the Imperium that protected the Pyremoat System from a Tyranid invasion. It was infested by a Genestealer Cult that almost led to the loss of the world until an Ultramarines task force led by Captain Sevastus Acheran of the 2nd Company successfully defeated the cult at the cost of his own life and that of all his battle-brothers, including Meduras Chairon, a hero of the Recidious Campaign. 

History

After a Departmento Munitorum survey vessel and its escort of Mordian Iron Guard Astra Militarum troops disappeared, its crewmen and guardsmen letting out a single psychic scream before vanishing without a trace, Captain Sevastus Acheran led a strike force of the Ultramarines' 2nd Company to investigate, accompanied by his decorated lieutenant, Brother Meduras Chairon.

What the Ultramarines found on Trygg was a powerful Genestealer Cult embedded in Trygg's primary hive city, their influence stretching from the undercity all the way up to the spires of the ruling nobility, poised to crack open the world's defences when the Tyranids arrived. The xenophile cult's uprising had already begun, and threatened to spread across the planet. In order to contain it, Acheran chose to activate the hive's anti-atomic lockdown protocols, sealing his entire force inside the hive city with a nearly endless horde of enraged cultists.

This noble choice, to sacrifice himself and his brothers, kept the Genestealer Cult bottled up, and saved the world -- and an entire vulnerable sector -- from certain doom in the Fourth Tyrannic War. Every last Space Marine that landed on Trygg was slowly worn down in a series of increasingly desperate battles. When Imperial reinforcements arrived to purge the last of the cult, they discovered that Acheran, Chairon and the rest of their brothers had sold their lives to cull untold numbers of xenos Genestealer hybrids.
