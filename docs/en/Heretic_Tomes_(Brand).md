Heretic Tomes is a brand name of the publisher Black Library, itself a subsidiary of Games Workshop. Books printed under the Heretic Tomes label are considered to no longer reflect the state of the current universe of Warhammer 40,000 and are thus no longer canonical. The brand was introduced in January 2010. 

Currently, only three books are known to have been printed under this brand name. 

Space Marine by Ian Watson
Pawns of Chaos by Brian Craig
Farseer by William King
