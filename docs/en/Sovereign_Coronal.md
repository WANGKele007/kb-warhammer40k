The Sovereign Coronal is an unusual crown-like artefact available to high-ranking members of the Necron Szarekhan Dynasty.

A band of living quicksilver, this strange artefact is shot through with artificial networks of command-wave nanocircuitry. This strange pseudo-animate crown is gifted to especially prominent nobles amongst the Szarekhan Dynasty. 

As it is donned it sinks through the metallic, necrodermis skin of its wearer's brow and meshes with their synthetic cortex, enhancing and projecting their dominating will through multiple spectra across a substantial distance.
