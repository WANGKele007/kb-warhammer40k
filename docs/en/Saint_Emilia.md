Saint Emilia, also spelled Saint Emelia, was an Imperial Saint venerated by the Imperial faithful in the Eastern Fringe region of the galaxy. Little is known about Emilia's life or deeds in Imperial records, but she was described by Inquisitor Amberley Vail of the Ordo Xenos as having "a keen appreciation of Human frailty", and was noted for advocating greater compassion and generosity among Imperial Cult believers.

Precepts of Saint Emilia

A scripture of the Imperial Cult called The Precepts of Saint Emilia comprises a significant portion of Emilia's hagiography. Commissar Ciaphas Cain quoted this scripture quite frequently in his official memoir, as well as in the so-called "Cain Archive", the unpublished collection of private journals edited and published by his ally, the Inquisitor Amberley Vail, for her colleagues after his death. Vail noted Cain seemed uncharacteristically fond of the saint, considering his usual distaste for excessively pious Imperial clergy.

Notable Precepts

"A generous heart is the gift of the Emperor."

"The path of duty is often a stony one, made smoother by thought for others."

Canon Conflict

Saint Emilia and the Precepts are first mentioned by Inquisitor Amberley Vail in the novel Death or Glory, spelled "Emilia". Another character misquotes her Precepts in the novel Duty Calls; in this context, Vail's footnote spells her name "Emelia".
