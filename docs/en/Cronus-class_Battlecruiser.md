The Cronus-class Battlecruiser was an aging cruiser design employed by the Imperial Army's Armada Imperialis during the Great Crusade and Horus Heresy. The Cronus was considered an obsolete pattern and had been retired from active service before the Great Crusade ended.

However, a Cronus-class Battlecruiser served as the flagship of the Traitor defence of the Death Guard Legion's homeworld of Barbarus late in the Heresy from the Exterminatus campaign carried out by the Dark Angels Legion against Traitor Legion homeworlds just before the start of the Siege of Terra. This vessel was presumed to be destroyed by the Loyalists during the subsequent fleet action.
