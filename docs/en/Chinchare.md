Chinchare is a vivid blue mineral-rich Imperial Mining World that is the fourth world of the Chinchare System located in the Segmentum Obscurus, orbiting a rogue star that is currently duelling with the star Pymbyle Minor, resulting in the world and its star system being afflicted with clashing gravity wells. The Chinchare System wanders between the Halo Stars on the far edge of the Imperium, and has suffered many devastating cosmological collisions in its wanderings. Once located not far from the Eye of Terror, the Chinchare System now lies almost at the edge of the segmentum close to the Halo Stars.

The rogue star system's strange orbital pattern and its current close proximity to two other star systems (the Pymbyle Major and Minor Systems) creates extremely unpredictable gravitational fluxes, and as a consequence, provided for the formation of extremely rare minerals in Chinchare's crust. These include such ultra-rare strategic metals as ancylitum and phorydnum. Chinchare has been a miner's plunder-haven for as long as it has been known to Mankind.

The only Human settlement on the world is Cinchare Minehead. A mining and port facility founded in 321.M41, it is currently run by a cartel comprised of the megacorporations Imperial Allied Minerals and Ortog Mining.

The citizens of Chinchare are likely slaves or penal workers employed by the corporations, living out miserable lives in arid mines or aboard freighters.

History

Around the time of the Great Crusade in the 30th Millennium, the Chinchare System was comprised of nine planets and an asteroid belt. This system had once been a neighbour of nearby 3458 Dornal near the Eye of Terror, but sometime between the 30th and 40th Millenniums, the Chinchare System had reached the Pymbyle Systems, Major and Minor, and had suffered two serious cosmological collisions, and was now comprised of only six planets and radiating sheets of asteroid belts that were all that remained of the three former worlds.

Chinchare is an irregular, blue mineral-rich asteroid locked in a figure of eight orbit around a binary star. It was once home to a creature of Chaos formed from the coagulated bodies of pilgrims who had descended into the asteroid to worship a corrupted crystal structure known as "the Lith". The creature was destroyed by Inquisitor Gregor Eisenhorn and Magos Geard Bure.

The only reason for any venture of Humanity to this forsaken place is its wealth of ultra-rare metals such as ancylitum and phorydnum. The mining rights for the world officially belonged to the Imperial corporation Imperial Allied Minerals and its partner Ortog Mining, which supported a workforce of almost 3,000, but in such a desolate region of space there was very little to stop the infestation of rogue prospectors.

Chinchare's mines were the site of a massive Hrud infestation in 844.M41 that was defeated by Terminators drawn from the 1st Company of the Dark Hands Chapter with support from the Astra Militarum's 39th Cadian "Xenobane" Regiment. The combat was fought in the close, shadowy confines of the myriad mining tunnels that snaked through the planet's crust like a rat's nest. The Imperial forces successfully rid several worlds in the Chinchare System of the xenos Hrud, halting what was later determined by Ordo Xenos investigators as the beginning of a potentially devastating entropic infestation of the xenos.

Later in 999.M41 the Chinchare System, along with several neighbouring star systems, lost all contact with the Imperium as a direct result of the massive incursion by the forces of Chaos during the 13th Black Crusade.
