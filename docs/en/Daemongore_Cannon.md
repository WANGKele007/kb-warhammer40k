The Daemongore Cannon is a large Daemonic Weapon that is commonly used by the Lord of Skulls Daemon Engine, mounted onto its front torso. 

The Daemongore Cannon unleashes a gout of burning filth at the Lord of Skulls' enemies. The weapon takes the form of a large, multi-barrelled cannon.
