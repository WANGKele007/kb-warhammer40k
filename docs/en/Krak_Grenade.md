The Krak Grenade is a type of anti-armour explosive device used by the military forces of the Imperium. Krak Grenades use a concentrated, inward-shaped implosive charge capable of punching holes in armoured targets such as vehicles or bunkers (thus "cracking" them open), and its charge can even rip armour plating apart. 

Typically Krak Grenades are attached directly onto vehicles or fortifications with magnetic, adhesive or physical clamps, maximising their effect on the target. They can also be used against monstrous creatures (such as the larger Tyranid bioforms), when they are attached to the creature's carapace before detonation. However, a Krak Grenade must be placed more carefully on the target than Frag Grenades, because their explosive radius is only half that of their counterpart.

These munitions are often used for attacking fortified defensive positions and vehicles, and provide Imperial infantry with a means of harming heavy targets that their primary anti-personnel weaponry may not be able to affect. However, the focused detonation lacks a blast radius, therefore making them impractical for use against enemy infantry.

This class of grenades encompass a wide variety of form factors, including shaped charges, plasma detonators, and crude bombs, all intended to disable armoured vehicles or heavily-armoured static defences.

Krak Grenades are used by a wide variety of Human factions and those with access to Imperial technology in the galaxy, including but not limited to the Astra Militarum, the Orders Militant of the Adepta Sororitas, the Adeptus Astartes, the Chaos Space Marines and the Orks.
