A Heavy Lascannon is a Laser Weapon and Lascannon that serves as a man-portable Field Ordnance Battery utilised by the Astra Militarum. Equipped with a potent Lascannon with a greater range and stopping power than the standard Lascannon, the Heavy Lascannon is intended to serve as a direct fire anti-tank and anti-armour weapon. A Heavy Lascannon can also be towed by other vehicles from battlefield to battlefield when more mobility is required.

The batteries' component ordnance teams usually comprise a gunner, loader and gunnery sergeant or junior artillery officer, who together crew the artillery piece, and are each equipped with a standard lasgun for personal defence. In the case of the Heavy Lasgun, the loader replaces the weapon's large power packs when depleted.

Field ordnance batteries are deployed in vast numbers by many Astra Militarum siege regiments, providing a bedrock of long-range and powerful weapons that most of the regiment's infantry are denied. Field Ordnance Batteries may be seconded to other regiments as well. When assigned to light infantry regiments, they usually operate as the heaviest firepower available, while amongst dedicated artillery regiments, their smaller footprint and silhouette enable them to deploy in dense terrain impassable to larger artillery tanks and mechanised artillery pieces.
