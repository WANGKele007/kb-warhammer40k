The Macharian PDF is a highly-devout Planetary Defence Force hailing from the world of Macharia in the Cadian Sector. They are utterly devoted to the famed hero and saint, Lord Solar Macharius, their planet's namesake.

The forces of the Macharian PDF, particularly the 24th Regiment, are notable for their use of Veteran Squads, consisting of decorated heroes.

Although their weapons are very personalised, their uniforms still match the Cadian standard.

The main additions are the Imperial scripture embroideries and Purity Seals that adorn their Flak Armor. These additions show that these troops are the utmost devout followers of the Imperial scripture in the sector, and have proven their faith in battle.
