House Sublico is a lesser Imperial Knight house of the Questor Imperialis that serves the Imperium of Man directly. Very little information is available in Imperial records about this Knight House and their only known battle honour dates back to the 36th Millennium.

House History

Notable Campaigns

Dark Marches Crusade (610-758.M36) - Launched in the wake of the Age of Apostasy after a period of relative calm for the Segmentum Tempestus, the Dark Marches Crusade was an Imperial Crusade launched to expand and fortify the domains of the Imperium. Considered a great victory, the so called Dark Marches-region was brought into the fold of the Imperium and several xenos threats were eliminated, which brought the entire region a standard millennium of stability. By edict of the High Lords of Terra, four new sectors were constituted from these newly conquered worlds: the Haxan, Leyak, Eurydice and Orpheus Sector. House Sublico contributed to the war-effort by sending two fully fledged households to fight as part of the expeditionary forces of the Adeptus Mechanicus, which mainly constituted of a demi-legio of Battle Titans from the Legio Osedax.
