Inferno Weapons are a type of ranged weapons used by the military forces in the service of the Chaos God Tzeentch, especially the Heretic Astartes of the Thousand Sons Traitor Legion. 

The sorcerers of the Thousand Sons and other Tzeentchian warbands imbue these weapons with the power of the Warp so that their ammunition can melt through flesh and even ceramite on impact, making them deadly against even foes outfitted in power armour.

Inferno Bolt Pistol

The Inferno Bolt Pistol is a standard Bolt Pistol that has been imbued with the power of Tzeentch.

Inferno Bolter

The Inferno Bolter is a standard Boltgun that has been imbued with the potent power of Tzeentch.

Inferno Combi-Bolter

An Inferno Combi-Bolter are twin Bolters that have been configured to fire together and imbued with the power of Tzeentch. This weapon dates back to the time of the Great Crusade and the Horus Heresy and predates the Storm Bolter design which replaced it.

Soulreaper Cannon

The Soulreaper Cannon is a heavy, rotary assault ballistic weapon very similar to the standard Adeptus Astartes Assault Cannon that has been imbued with the sorcerous power of Tzeentch.
