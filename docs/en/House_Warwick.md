House Warwick is a Loyalist Imperial Knight House of the Questor Mechanicus in service to the Adeptus Mechanicus. There is very little information in Imperial records about this Knight House, and it is unknown from which Knight World it hails.

None listed in current Imperial records.

House Appearance

House Colours

The colours of House Warwick are purple and green.

House Arms

The arms of House Warwick are not listed in current Imperial records, although the images attached suggest that they might be a green dragon head breathing flames on a field of purple, or some variation thereof.

Canon Conflict

This Knight House appears in the outdated 126th issue of the UK edition of White Dwarf magazine, and the images presented here do not mesh with existing canon. The Knights shown in this article are all labelled as Knight Lancers, although these Knights in their current incarnation do not use any of these chassises.
