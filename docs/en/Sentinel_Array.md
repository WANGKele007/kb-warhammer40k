A Sentinel Array is a man-portable backpack containing a suite of motion trackers and target acquisition systems which provides Eversor Assassins with an electronically-enhanced full awareness of their surroundings. The Sentinel Array can be provided with targeting data by uploading biometric information of the Assassin's intended targets to the device before deployment of the operative. This data is sometimes collected by another operative taking pict captures of the targets before the upload, though other forms of data acquisition are possible.
