The Legio Bombastor is a Loyalist Titan Legion of the Collegia Titanica.

Legion History

Notable Campaigns

The Brazier (Unknown Date; ca. 900s.M41) - A mighty warband of Death Guard and Iron Warriors Heretic Astartes attacked the Fortress World of Shen'tzi Vo. The planet's defensive shrines were corrupted by the Gellerpox, while its vaunted maniples of Legio Bombastor Titans fell prey to a devastating Iron Warriors ambush and were annihilated. Forced back to the Bastion Primus, a combined force of Salamanders and Battle Sisters from the Order of Our Martyred Lady were all that stood between the heretics and a crushingly swift victory. Recognising that their situation was untenable, the defenders slighted their own defences to lure the foe deeper into the Bastion Primus, before unleashing a conflagration of fused munitions, burning promethium and searing holy fire. Many Imperial warriors were consumed along with the attackers, but many more survived, whether by their sheer endurance to heat or by the impenetrable shield of their faith. The attackers were less fortunate; scorched Heretic survivors staged a last stand in the fortress' western gatehouse before being crushed by the combined assault of the Adeptus Astartes and Adepta Sororitas.
