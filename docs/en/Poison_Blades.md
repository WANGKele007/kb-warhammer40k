Poison Blades are needle-like poisoned melee weapons used by Callidus Assassins of the Imperial Officio Assassinorum. A Callidus Assassin frequently has to operate with little or no weaponry while on missions. However, they are never totally unarmed and will always manage to secrete several Poison Blades about their person. The Callidus Assassin can drive these through weak points in enemy armour, such as eyes and other exposed areas.

In sealed pockets across their synskin bodygloves, many Callidus operatives keep an array of envenomed needles, each several inches long and wickedly sharp. The toxins on these miniature blades are enough to drop a full-grown Ogryn in solar seconds, and a Callidus Assassin is an expert at slamming them point-first through eye-slits, armour joints and other weak spots to ensure their victim's swift demise.
