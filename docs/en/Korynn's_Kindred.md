Kôrynn's Kindred is a Kindred of the Kronus Hegemony of the Leagues of Votann. The Fane of its hold achieved self-awareness and became a full Ancestor Core, less than a Terran millennium ago. This rare event allowed the Kindred to become the founder of an aggressive new major league, the Kronus Hegemony.
