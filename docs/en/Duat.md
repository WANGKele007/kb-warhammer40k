Duat was a fief-world of the World Eaters Legion during the Great Crusade and Horus Heresy eras of the late 30th and early 31st Millennia. This Ocean World was used as a mustering and recruitment planet for the myriad campaigns of the XII Legion.

Though Duat had no true landmasses, only areas where local analogues of seaweed had grown thick enough over its planet-wide ocean to support the weight of edifices, it was still inhabited by Humans. The local tide-clans of Duat were ideal candidates for recruitment into the Legiones Astartes, for if these sea-going nomads were not fighting the monsters of the Duat depths, then they were at war with one another.

The local presence of the XII Legion was centred around three "darkwater nodes," artificial islands that floated atop beds of vat-grown sargasm seaweed. Node Primus housed gene-banks and bio-processing units and was used for the recruitment and implantation of new World Eaters Astartes. Node Secundus was an armoury and weapons production facility, responsible for the manufacture of ammunition, weapons and power armour.

Node Tertius was outfitted with refuelling stations and spare parts depots for maintenance and repair of the Legion's armour and other vehicles. These installations were protected by a Tetra Pattern planetary defence grid. Overall command of the Legion presence on Duat fell to Praetor-Apothecary Kalibos, a senior Apothecary of the World Eaters.

It was on Duat that Kalibos experimented on the production of World Eaters gene-seed and was able to reduce the training time for new Astartes from Terran years to only solar months, though at the cost of a dramatic increase in fatalities among the recipients.

During the Horus Heresy, Duat was attacked by a force of Loyalist Blackshields under the command of the former World Eaters Praetor Endryd Haar. Haar used a call for aid from Kalibos to raid the facilities under the guise of being a XII Legion force coming to bolster the defence of the planet. When true World Eaters arrived in-system in response to the distress call and engaged the Blackshield fleet, Haar and his forces made good their escape, but not before Kalibos was slain by Haar's own hand, putting an end to the World Eaters' efforts to rapidly increase their numbers.
