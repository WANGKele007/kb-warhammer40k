The Clementine Knights are a Loyalist Space Marine Chapter of unknown Founding and genetic origin. 

Chapter Appearance

Chapter Colours

The Clementine Knights wear primarily orange power armour with white edging on the right shoulder plate. The left shoulder plate is white and bears the Chapter's iconography. The hemet is black with a white stripe down the centre.

Chapter Badge

The Clementine Knights' Chapter badge is a depiction of a clementine outlined in white halved so that one side represents the exterior of the fruit and the other its internal, segmented structure, all on an orange background.

Trivia

The Clementine Knights were created by a Games Workshop employee identified only as "George".
