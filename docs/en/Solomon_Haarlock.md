Solomon Haarlock was a Rogue Trader and scion of House Haarlock, an infamous and (according to some) damned Rogue Trader dynasty. In ca. 760.M36 he was the first to successfully chart the area of space known as the Calyx Expanse in the Segmentum Obscurus that later became the Calixis Sector of the Imperium after the Angevin Crusade of the 39th Millennium. 

Some rumours that have long made the rounds at Port Wander and Footfall say Haarlock also strayed beyond the Maw into the Koronus Expanse as well, though this is unconfirmed.
