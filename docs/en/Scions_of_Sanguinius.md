The Scions of Sanguinius is a Loyalist Space Marine Chapter and Successor of the Blood Angels, created during an unknown Founding. 

Created as they were from the Blood Angels' gene-seed, the Scions of Sanguinius carry the genetic flaws inherited from the Primarch for which they were named.

Chapter History

Notable Campaigns

Devastation of Baal (999.M41) - The Scions of Sanguinius took part in the Blood Angels' defence of their homeworld of Baal. Of the Chapter's warships that took part in the battle, only a single Strike Cruiser survived.

Notable Scions of Sanguinius

Sardegna — Sardegna was a Scion of Sanguinius who served in the Deathwatch.
