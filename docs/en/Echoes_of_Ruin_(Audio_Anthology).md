Echoes of Ruin is the first audio drama anthology for The Horus Heresy series. Echoes of Ruin was first released as an audio disk in March 2014, and contains seven original audio dramas.

Official Synopsis

As war continues to ravage the galaxy, the new Age of Darkness sees whole systems simply lost and countless battles forgotten. Surely, there can be no hope for peace, forgiveness or respite in such times, and all that will be left are the dying Warp-echoes of supreme galactic ruin...

Contents

Echoes of Ruin contains the following Horus Heresy Series audio dramas:

Veritas Ferrum by David Annandale
Warmaster by John French
Strike and Fade by Guy Haley
Lucius: The Eternal Blade by Graham McNeill
Khârn: The Eightfold Path by Anthony Reynolds
Cypher: Guardian of Order by Gav Thorpe
Wolf's Claw by Chris Wraight
