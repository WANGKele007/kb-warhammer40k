An Electromagnetic Shell is a type of ammunition used by Imperial warships' Nova Cannons. When detonated, an Electromagnetic Shell releases a flood of electromagnetic energy designed to disrupt enemy sensors and communications.

Planet Killer 1, pp. 31-33
