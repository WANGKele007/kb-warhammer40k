The Astral Hounds are a Loyalist Space Marine Chapter of unknown Founding and genetic origin.

Chapter Appearance

Chapter Colours

The Astral Hounds wear primarily purple power armour with a golden Aquila on the chest. The facial plate on the helmet and the shoulder pads are cream and the shoulders have black edging.

Chapter Badge

The Astral Hounds Chapter badge is a depiction of a black canid on a cream background.

Trivia

The Astral Hounds and the Tome Keepers were both created by the staff of White Dwarf in 2020 as candidates to serve as the mascot Chapter of the magazine. They ultimately chose the Tome Keepers, but the Astral Hounds were fully concepted out by the staff and some of the background ideas for the Chapter were re-used for the Tome Keepers.
