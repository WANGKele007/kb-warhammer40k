An APM Launcher, officially an Adaptive Payload Missile Launcher, is a type of Kin-portable missile launcher used by the warriors of the Leagues of Votann, particularly by Hernkyn Yaegir Gunners. 

These advanced weapons can recalibrate the chemical nature of their missile's warhead, enabling widespread anti-infantry destruction with a large shrapnel spread or an armour-piercing capability as required by the tactical situation or the nature of the target.
