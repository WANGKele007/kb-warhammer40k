The Immortal Hearts is a Loyalist Space Marine Chapter of unknown Founding and origin. Almost nothing is known about this Chapter in Imperial records.

Chapter History

Notable Campaigns

Third War for Armageddon (998.M41) - The Immortal Hearts sent one Battle Company in defence of the Hive World of Armageddon against the green tide of the massive Ork WAAAGH! led by the infamous Warlord Ghazghkull Mag Uruk Thraka during the Third War for Armageddon.
