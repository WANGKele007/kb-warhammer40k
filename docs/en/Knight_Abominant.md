A Knight Abominant is a type of Questoris Pattern Chaos Knight that is capable of unleashing psychic attacks. Seen as especially blessed by the Dark Gods, Knights Abominant are suffused with Warp energy. They unleash this power in deadly waves, corrupting and mutating those nearby, and incinerating distant foes with blasts of thermal energy from their Volkite Combustors. 

In melee combat, they thrash coiling, writhing appendages like arm-mounted Electroscourges and Balemace tails wrought by their long exposure to the Warp, crushing anything that remains untainted by the power of Chaos.
