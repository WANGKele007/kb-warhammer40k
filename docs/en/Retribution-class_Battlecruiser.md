The Retribution-class Battlecruiser is an ancient class of Imperial battlecruiser used by the expeditionary fleets of the Legiones Astartes during the Great Crusade and Horus Heresy.

Notable Retribution-class Battlecruisers

Galthalamor (Word Bearers) - Active in the Eastern Fringe during the Great Crusade, the Galthalamor was destroyed in battle with the Orks. The Word Bearer Malforian, who later served as the weapons master aboard the Furious Abyss, was aboard the Galthalamor during the battle; the injuries he sustained were severe enough to require that he receive augmetic replacements for his chin and lower jaw.
