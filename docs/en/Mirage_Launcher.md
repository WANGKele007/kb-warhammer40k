A Mirage Launcher is a one-shot holo-grenade pod mounted upon the hulls of Aeldari Harlequin anti-gravitic vehicles. Mirage Launchers are usually attached to the underside of the vehicle's hull in two clusters of three.

When triggered, they emit an explosion of blinding colours, masking the vehicle from sight for precious seconds. This cunning countermeasure reduces the craft to little more than a technicolour blur when on the move.

Sources

Codex: Harlequins (7th Edition) (Digital Edition), pp. 43, 61, 212
