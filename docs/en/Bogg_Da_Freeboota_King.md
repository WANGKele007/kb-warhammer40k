Bogg Da Freeboota King is an Ork Freebooter whose fleets are located in the Shattered Stars region of the galactic core to the east of the borders of the space of the Urani-Surtr Regulates of the Leagues of Votann. Bogg commands a seemingly endless empire of ragtag Greenskin pirates whose fleets make constant raids into URSR space.
