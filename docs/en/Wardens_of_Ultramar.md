The Wardens of Ultramar is the name of the unique Command Squad of both mortal Human and Space Marine advisers who serve Captain Demetrian Titus after he was once again named the commander of the Ultramarines Chapter's 2nd Company, Master of the Watch and Warden of Ultramar in the Era Indomitus of the 41st Millennium. 

Restored to his old command by Chapter Master Marneus Calgar following Sevastus Acheran's heroic death on Trygg, Titus needed such an unusually enhanced Command Squad as Primarch Roboute Guilliman had tasked the Ultramarines with overseeing the defence of Macragge and the entirety of the Realm of Ultramar itself, as well as expanding Ultramar back to its former, pre-Horus Heresy size of five hundred worlds. 

Even the most exceptional men cannot rule alone, and Titus' many adventures have taught him that mortals and Astartes alike have plenty to offer his command team. 

Newly promoted at Titus's express recommendation, despite his relative youth among Ultramarines Veterans, Ancient Valorem Gadriel hefts the massive banner of the 2nd Company and can always be found in the thickest fighting alongside Titus's own mentor, Bladeguard Veteran Sergeant Metaurus. Both have ample experience fighting alongside Titus, and he values their advice and counsel above all others.

On the shorter side among the Command Squad's mortal members, Commodore Gaius Silva commands a vast fleet of the Ultramar Defence Fleet and is an expert at handling the tensions between an overworked mortal crew and their often-pitiless Astartes masters, making him an essential part of Titus' muster. 

Amelia Minervas is a highly respected legatus of the Ultramar Defence Auxilia and serves as the 2nd Company command team's liaison to its legions of Human Planetary Defence Force soldiery. Lucia Vestha joins Titus's war councils as a valued Ultramarian plenipotentiary and diplomat whose keen negotiating skills have saved the 2nd Company from needless battle on countless occasions, while the sharp-tongued and forthright Dainal Kornelius is a veteran astropath who has survived for over 100 standard years in the Ultramarines' employ.

Notable Wardens of Ultramar

Captain Demetrian Titus - Captain of the Ultramarines 2nd Company, Master of the Watch, Warden of Ultramar

Ancient Valorem Gadriel - The Ultramarines 2nd Company Ancient. Gadriel first fought with Demetrian Titus as part of his squad during the Recidious Campaign.

Bladeguard Veteran Sergeant Metaurus - The Ultramarine who first met Demetrian Titus as a young boy and who offered him the chance to become an aspirant of the Chapter. He has been a mentor to Titus ever since, throughout his long rise through the ranks of the Ultramarines.

Commodore Gaius Silva of the Ultramar Defence Fleet - Commordore Silva is the commander of a large fleet drawn from the warships of the Ultramar Defence Fleet that provides the naval power and transport capacity needed by the 2nd Company to carry out its mission of restoring Ultramar's ancient borders. Silva is a noted expert at calming the tensions that often arise between mortal crews and soldiery and the demands made by their Adeptus Astartes commanders who no longer understand the weaknesses and frailties of unaugmented Humans.

Legatus Amelia Minervas of the Ultramar Defence Auxilia - The liaison between the 2nd Company Command Squad and the mortal PDF soldiers of the Ultramar Defence Auxilia who serve as vital manpower in the campaign to restore the Realm of Ultramar to its former size.

Lucia Vestha - Ultramarian diplomat and administrator whose negotiation skills can lead to the enlargement of Ultramar without the need for the 2nd Company to engage in battle.

Dainal Kornelius - Veteran Ultramarian astropath who serves as the 2nd Company's primary communications link with the rest of Ultramar and the galaxy beyond. Kornelius is over 100 standard years old.
