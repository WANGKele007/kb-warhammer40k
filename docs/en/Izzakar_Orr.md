Izzakar Orr was a Librarian of the Thousand Sons Legion during the Great Crusade. Though his efforts many Humans were freed from the ignorance brought upon them by Old Night. 

Orr was slain by Wolf Lord Bulveye of the Space Wolves' 13th Great Company during the Burning of Prospero at the start of the Horus Heresy, but ironically his disembodied spirit would aid the Space Wolves Lord of Runes Njal Stormcaller ten thousand years later in the 41st Millennium to find Bulveye and the remaining lost members of the 13th Great Company.

History

Izzakar Orr's quest to better Mankind came to an end, however, when Prospero was invaded by the Space Wolves Legion on the order of the Emperor, leading to the Burning of Prospero, in response to the actions of the Thousand Sons' primarch Magnus the Red, who had violated the anti-sorcery precepts of the Edict of Nikaea. Magnus had done so to warn the Emperor of the planned treachery of the Warmaster Horus, but his effort damaged the Emperor's Imperial Webway Project and unleashed the War Within the Webway. The Emperor had ordered the Space Wolves primarch Leman Russ to return Magnus to Terra for trial, but Horus intervened to spur his brother's inherent hatred for sorcery and convinced him to execute Magnus outright instead.

During the the assault on Prospero, Orr's temple was attacked by the Wolf Lord Bulveye's 13th Great Company and the Thousand Sons under the Librarian's command fought to protect the great library of knowledge about the Warp and psychic abilities that filled the temple. Though the XV Legion made the Space Wolves pay in blood for every step they took, many of the Thousand Sons fell in battle and they could not stop the Space Wolves from ravaging Orr's library. Soon only the Librarian and a small group of Thousand Sons were left defending the temple and Orr knew they would die if they stayed there. 

Orr brought his remaining followers to a hidden Webway Gate within the temple to escape, but was confronted by Bulveye before he could activate it. Orr was shocked when the Wolf Lord implored the Thousand Sons to stop fighting and accept the Space Wolves' judgement for their transgression against the will of the Emperor. The incredulous Librarian yelled that the Space Wolves were Traitors and murders, who attacked his Legion without reason and destroyed his library, which was filled with knowledge that could have bettered Mankind's future. His words did not sway Bulveye, however, and as the Space Wolves advanced upon his position, Orr completed the ritual to activate the Webway Gate and escaped within it with his small group of followers.

The Space Wolves of the 13th Great Company pursued them, but while Orr knew the rituals that would allow him to safely navigate the ancient Webway network, the Wolf Lord and his Great Company blundered blindly through them. The Space Wolves were now no longer a threat to the Librarian, as the Space Wolves only succeeded in separating themselves and becoming hopelessly lost in the Webway. However, Orr realised that because the Space Wolves were not using the required rituals to open the various portals they passed through, they were damaging the Webway and threatening to destabilise it, leading to a catastrophic breach into the Warp. 

In order to save the network, Orr used his psychic powers to guide Bulveye and his group of Space Wolves to the Librarian's location within the centre of the Thousand Sons' Webway network and promptly surrendered to the Wolf Lord. The Librarian then explained his reason for doing so and opened a gateway that would take Orr and the Space Wolves back to Prospero.

Bulveye at first seemed to consider Orr's proposal, until he asked the Librarian what would happen to those members of the 13th Great Company that were still lost within the Webway. Orr truthfully admitted that they were likely to eventually be purged by the Webway's own inherent defences. This sealed both his fate and that of his remaining followers. Bulveye was not willing to leave any of his Great Company behind, nor was he willing to disobey his primarch's orders to take no prisoners, and so he calmly split Orr's head with his power axe. As the Librarian died, the Webway Gate back to Prospero closed and Orr's remaining Thousand Sons followers were killed shortly afterwards by the Space Wolves. After they were surrounded by the corpses of their enemies, Bulveye and the Thirteenth Great Company began searching the Webway for their lost kin and to kill any member of the Thousand Sons they came upon who still remained within it.

Ten thousand standard years later, Izzakar Orr's disembodied spirit, still bound to the Materium through his psychic abilities, was found by the Space Wolves' Lord of Runes Njal Stormcaller on Prospero as he sought to find and rescue Bulveye and the members of the long-lost 13th Great Company. Orr acted as a psychic guide and oracle for Njal and his forces on Prospero. By this point Izzakar gladly helped his former enemies, for he had little loyalty left for Magnus the Red, who had transformed the Thousand Sons into puppets of the Chaos God Tzeentch.
