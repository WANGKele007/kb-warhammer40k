The Riatov Grenadiers are regiments of elite, well-armoured Astra Militarum Grenadier infantry raised from the Riatov System in the Chalnath Expanse of the Segmentum Ultima. They fought in the Haephos Containment campaign and contributed a total of 2 infantry regiments to that conflict.
