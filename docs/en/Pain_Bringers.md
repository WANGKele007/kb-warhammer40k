The Pain Bringers is a Loyalist Space Marine Chapter of unknown Founding and origin. Its most notable action occurred in 846.M38, on the third moon of Vida's World, where it engaged and defeated an Eldar army. As a result, the Pain Bringers recovered a nearly-intact Fire Prism grav tank. Almost nothing else is known about this Chapter in current Imperial records.

Chapter History

Notable Campaigns

Battle of Vida's World (263.846.M38) - The Pain Bringers fought against an Eldar army on the third moon of Vida's World. In the battle's aftermath, the Adeptus Mechanicus received word that a near-intact Eldar war machine had been secured -- a Fire Prism grav tank. The Eldar vehicle was found in a crater on the moon's dark side, during the post-battle cleansing operations. Its crew was killed in the crash after being brought down by the stalwart warriors of the Pain Bringers Chapter.

Notable Pain Bringers

Sergeant Gressil - Gressil commanded the Pain Bringers squad that brought down the crew of the Fire Prism grav tank.
