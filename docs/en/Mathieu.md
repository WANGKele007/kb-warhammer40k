Mathieu was a priest of the Adeptus Ministorum who was chosen to become Roboute Guilliman's second Militant-Apostolic during the Indomitus Crusade of the Era Indomitus. He died from the wounds sustained heroically aiding Imperial forces from the assaults of Nurgle's servants during the climax of the Plague Wars on Iax. 

Before his death, the primarch promised to recommend Mathieu for canonisation as an Imperial Saint and revealed that the priest's actions had contributed to his willingness to reconsider the atheism of the Imperial Truth.

History

Before Mathieu was chosen by Roboute Guilliman to replace Geesan as the Militant-Apostolic, he was originally a priest of the Acronite Mendicants, a sub-order of the Missionaria Galaxia. 

After the Terran Crusade Mathieu was among those trapped on the Macragge's Honour following its capture by the Red Corsairs. During the vessel's captivity Mathieu was essentially a thrall of the Chaos Space Marines, but in secret held prayer services to the Emperor for the other Imperial captives at great personal risk. Mathieu displayed apparent psychic ability similar to that of a Living Saint when he channeled the power provided by his potent faith in the Emperor to exorcise a Daemon that had possessed a child aboard the vessel. Mathieu displayed confidence after the episode that the Macragge's Honour would eventually be freed, which proved prescient. 

After his rescue by the Ultramarines when they reclaimed their flagship, Mathieu joined the Indomitus Crusade. During the crusade he gave sermons to its participants and fought alongside the troops of the Astra Militarum. It was these actions that brought him to the attention of Roboute Guilliman when he sought a new Militant-Apostolic after the death of Geesan, the previous holder of the position. Though at first Mathieu was hesitant to take the title as he believed himself unworthy of such a lofty position, Guilliman told him it was those very qualities of humility and a desire to be of service that made him an ideal candidate. After some further consideration, Mathieu accepted and gave a rousing sermon following the Indomitus Crusade's victory at the Battle of Raukos which ended its first phase in stabilising the Imperium Sanctus. 

Afterwards Mathieu was among the Imperial forces Guilliman led to aid the Realm of Ultramar during the Plague Wars. Despite his title, Mathieu took part in the battles of the Plague Wars and wrote a record of Guilliman's exploits against the Chaos God Nurgle's forces and the Daemon Primarch Mortarion. During the campaign on Iax Mathieu commanded an army of the Ecclesiarchy's Frateris Militia, spreading word of both the Emperor and Guilliman's divinity in defiance of the Imperial Regent's wishes to honour the ancient doctrine of the Imperial Truth. 

During the final battle on Iax Mathieu took part in the assault to capture the portion of the metaphysical psychic construct known as the Cauldron of Nurgle held by the Great Unclean One Ku'gath in realspace. Mathieu's intense faith transformed him into a Living Saint for a time, channeling the power of his faith in the Emperor to shield Imperial forces from the corrupting Warp energies of the Plaguelord before destroying the cauldron himself, though at the price of his own life. 

The dying Mathieu was returned to the Imperial fleet in orbit of Iax to meet with Guilliman one last time, during which the Militant-Apostolic explained that the Emperor's consciousness in the Warp was reawakening and that his part in the Emperor's plan was finished. As Mathieu died, Guilliman declared to all that the Imperium would remember his heroic sacrifice and that he should be canonised as an Imperial Saint. 

Personality

While Mathieu had always believed himself to be a modest man, he did have one ambition that he had done his best to hide. This was Mathieu's hope that he could convince Roboute Guilliman to finally accept his inherent divinity as a true son of the God-Emperor. 

In this Mathieu earned a small measure of success at the end, as Guilliman began to question his own materialist belief in the Imperial Truth in the wake of his victory in the Plague Wars and his own resurrection from the effect of Nurgle's supernatural Godblight disease through the power of the Emperor while on Iax.

Gothic War Connection

Though a connection has not been explicitly confirmed, Militant-Apostolic Mathieu shared his name with the Mathieu family who had founded a lineage of Navis Imperialis captains in the Battlefleet Gothic, traditionally commanding the Lunar-class Cruiser Excellent until its destruction in the Battle of Gethsemane during the Gothic War.
