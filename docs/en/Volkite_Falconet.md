A Volkite Falconite is a directed energy thermal beam weapon similar in operation to other Volkite Weapons that could be deployed on the Deredeo Pattern Dreadnought. With the application of extreme heat it causes the flesh of its targets to explosively burst into ash and flame, laying waste to enemy hordes.

When Volkite Falconets were deployed on both weapons arms in a twin-linked format it was known as a Volkite Falconet Battery.
