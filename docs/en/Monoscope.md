Though it can be used to project a beam of light, the Monoscope is primarily intended as a pict-capture device and visual uploader for the Tempestus Scions special operations troops of the Militarum Tempestus. Panning left and right as the Tempestus Scion goes about the prosecution of war, each Monoscope can be tapped into by any Scions' Tempestor or Tempestor Prime officer who wishes to see what his subordinate is witnessing. The act of covering up a Monoscope's lens, whether accidentally or by design, is punishable by a full solar day's electro-whipping and a number of days without rations at the commanding officer's discretion.
