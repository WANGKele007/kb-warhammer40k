Verngar, also known as Verngar the Apostate, was once a Loyalist Space Marine of the Angels Encarmine Chapter until he was exiled for insisting that the Chapter's affliction by the Red Thirst was not the result of a genetic flaw but of the corruption of Chaos. He later became a high-ranking member of the Red Corsairs Heretic Astartes warband and even contested briefly with its Chaos Lord Huron Blackheart for leadership using the Tzeentchian Chaos relic called the Ebon Talon before being defeated and maimed in his bid for mastery.

History

Not long after he joined the Red Corsairs, its Chaos Lord Huron Blackheart came to consider Verngar as a potential threat to his own leadership of the piratical warband and capable of becoming a powerful Chaos Lord in his own right. Verngar was granted command of the Red Corsairs who garrisoned the Blackstone Fortress which had been gifted to the Renegades by Abaddon the Despoiler. In this position, while working alongside the twin-headed Lord of Change Kairos Fateweaver, Verngar oversaw the temporary captivity of Primarch Roboute Guilliman after his capture by Fateweaver during the Terran Crusade, without the knowledge or consent of Blackheart himself.

After Guilliman's escape, Verngar returned to the Red Corsairs' homeworld of New Badab in the Maelstrom Warp rift with the captured Ultramarines flagship Macragge's Honour. Blackheart was highly suspicious of Verngar, believing that the warrior's triumph was an attempt to usurp his leadership of the warband, so he publicly criticised Verngar for allowing Guilliman to escape. 

Yet, during Verngar's tenure on the Blackstone Fortress, Kairos Fateweaver had told him of the existence and power of the Tzeentchian Chaos relic called the Ebon Talon and its location within the Maelstrom on the Daemon World of Kyren. The massive star fort, for all the efforts of Verngar and his Red Corsairs, had remained inert, its powerful, planet-destroying weapon systems inaccessible. Fateweaver explained that with the power of the Ebon Talon the Red Corsairs would be able to fully activate the Blackstone Fortress and assert full control over its vast power. 

Verngar reported all this to Blackheart, and offered to find and claim the Ebon Talon to increase the Red Corsairs' power. Blackheart suspected, though, that Verngar truly wished to claim the relic for himself, in order to raise his esteem among his fellow Red Corsairs. Verngar already had a sizable following and Blackheart believed that with the Ebon Talon in his possession, Verngar would attempt to take command of the warband and become its new lord.

In the end, Blackheart agreed to allow Verngar to search for the relic, but stated he would be accompanying the expedition as well in his battle barge Spectre of Ruin. By doing so, Blackheart sought to claim the Ebon Talon first and put an end to Verngar's threat to his command of the Red Corsairs. When the Heretic Astartes reached Kyren, their two forces fought together against the mutant Human tribes of Tzeentch-worshippers that called the strange crystalline Daemon World home, but it was Verngar who entered the crystal mountain and claimed the Ebon Talon first. He escaped with the relic, but it had been the power of the Ebon Talon and its connection to Tzeentch's Realm of the Sorcerer that had created and sustained Kyren's unnatural existence. Its removal from the planet caused the world's crystalline structure to shatter, and the Red Corsairs barely escaped before Kyren simply exploded into drifting hunks of crystal. 

After the capture of the Ebon Talon, Verngar's standing within the Red Corsairs grew considerably and he now planned the next step in his plan to gain command of the warband. With a group of his followers, Verngar confronted Blackheart and demanded to be given command of a large fleet, so he could join the warband's coming invasion of Chogoris, the homeworld of the White Scars Chapter. 

Blackheart refused, claiming that despite Verngar's success on Kyren, he had never taken full command of a war zone that stretched over an entire star system. Even some of Verngar's followers agreed with this logic, but Blackheart compromised by providing Verngar with another target -- the Invaders Chapter's former homeworld of Ogrys. It was being rebuilt for the Chapter by the Imperium and a Red Corsair assault upon the world would be an opportunity for Verngar to gain the necessary command experience. Blackheart also joined Verngar aboard his warship, the captured Ultramarines battle barge Macragge's Honour, captured from Roboute Guilliman himself by Kairos Fateweaver during the Terran Crusade, to personally monitor his performance.

Blackheart promised Verngar that if his assault proved successful, then he would be allowed to join the invasion of Chogoris with command of the fleet he had demanded. Verngar agreed and the Red Corsairs' attack on Ogrys initially went well, until the Red Wolves, a sub-warband of the Red Corsairs comprised of former Space Wolves who had surrendered to Huron Blackheart after he successfully boarded the strike cruiser Wolf of Fenris, disobeyed Verngar's orders. 

Enraged by this insubordination in front of Blackheart, Verngar ordered the Warpsmith Turazan to bring the Ebon Talon to him. Blackheart had been sure that Verngar had not learned how to access the relic's power and that this was why he had not immediately used the relic to activate the warband's Blackstone Fortress. But thanks to the aid of Turazan and the sorcerer Garlon Souleater, Verngar proved able to unleash the Ebon Talon's fiery destruction. Verngar moved to loose the artefact's Warpflame upon the Red Wolves' Wolf of Fenris, but Blackheart intervened to prevent the unnecessary destruction of a potent asset of the Red Corsairs. 

Yet Verngar, in a rage further fuelled beyond all reason by the curse of the Ebon Talon, refused to relent even as Blackheart sensed that it was the Ebon Talon that was driving his subordinate's actions. As the Heretic Astartes continued to argue, the Wolf of Fenris suddenly attacked the Macragge's Honour, as the Red Wolves had divined Verngar's intentions and had no intention of meekly accepting their fate without a fight. 

The Red Wolves' attack distracted Verngar long enough that Blackheart struck his rival with a blast from the built-in flamer of his Tyrant's Claw, ready to openly move to eliminate his rival. Verngar recovered from the blast and prepared to unleash the power of the Ebon Talon at Blackheart. However, Blackheart's connection to his Daemonic Familiar known as the Hamadrya, allowed Blackheart to psychically deflect the relic's power onto Turazan. As the Warpsmith's body was consumed from the inside out by the Warpflame of Tzeentch, Blackheart ended the fight by seriously wounding Verngar and then lopping off the hand with which he held the Ebon Talon, severing the link between Verngar and the relic. 

With the threat of Verngar to his leadership of the Red Corsairs brought to a conclusive end, Blackheart took the Ebon Talon and returned to his flagship, the Spectre of Ruin. He presented the Ebon Talon to the Red Corsairs' Master of the Forge Armanneus Valthex and ordered him to find a safe way to use the relic without suffering any of its ill effects. While Blackheart fully intended to make use of its power in the future, he had no intention of surrendering all reason and logic to the relic's curse.
