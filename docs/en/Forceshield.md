A Forceshield is a personal protective device utilised by the Craftworld Aeldari. The Forceshield, as its name implies, projects a powerful defensive force field around its user. 

The Forceshield is a wrist-mounted device that contains a miniaturised force shield projector, setting it apart by its sheer elegance from the clunky and restrictive personal armour often used as protection by the members of less technologically-advanced species like Humanity.

Forceshields are most commonly used by Asuryani Autarchs and Ghost Warriors like Wraithblades.
