The Angel's Tears Grenade Launcher is a special type of Grenade Launcher used during the Great Crusade and Horus Heresy by the Blood Angels Legion warriors who were members of the Order of the First Sphere known as the Angel's Tears.

History

A weapon spawned from the Unification Wars and the terror of Old Night, the Rad Grenade fired by the Angels' Tears Grenade Launcher proved well-suited to the Angel's Tears' role as purgers of sin.

For while its deadly blast of radioactively-contaminated fragments eradicates the foes of the Blood Angels, it leaves the worlds they corrupted intact, waiting only for the time that they may be declared pure once again and worthy of hosting true servants of the Emperor once more.
