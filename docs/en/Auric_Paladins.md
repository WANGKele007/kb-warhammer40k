The Auric Paladins are a Loyalist Space Marine Chapter and a successor of the Blood Angels created during an unknown Founding. 

Chapter Appearance

Chapter Colours

The Auric Paladins' Chapter colours are not listed in current Imperial records.

Chapter Badge

The Auric Paladins' Chapter badge is is a golden drop of blood sprouting white angelic wings on a red background.

Trivia

The Auric Paladins were created by a Games Workshop employee identified only as "Mark".
