The Games Day Anthology 2011/12 is an anthology book published by the Black Library. The book was first released in July 2012, for exclusive release at Games Day. The book contains several short stories from different Games Workshop properties. The short stories contained within were later released separately.

Synopsis

No official synopsis has been released.

Contents

The Games Day Anthology 2011/12 contains the following short stories:

Horus Heresy

Death of a Silversmith by Graham McNeill

Warhammer 40,000

The Treasures of Biel-Tanigh by Andy Chambers
The Curse of Shaa-Dom by Gav Thorpe
Emperor's Deliverance by Nick Kyme

Warhammer Fantasy

The March of Doom by Chris Wraight
Plague Priest by C.L. Werner
