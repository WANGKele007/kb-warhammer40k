New Badab is a planet located within the Maelstrom Warp rift in the Ultima Segmentum of the galaxy which became the homeworld and fleet base of the Heretic Astartes of the piratical Red Corsairs warband after the destruction of their former homeworld of Badab Primaris during the Badab War.

The world was originally inhabited by hundreds of thousands of mutants and other Chaos-corrupted Humans, but the Chaos Lord Lufgt Huron, the former Tyrant of Badab who now calls himself "Huron Blackheart" and his surviving 300 Astral Claws made short work of the existing population and quickly took over the world. The Red Corsairs eventually established themselves at the head of a vast Renegade fleet of pirates and raiders that threaten Imperial worlds and merchant and military shipping in the adjacent Maelstrom Zone. 

New Badab is the location of the "Palace of Thorns", a huge fortress named after Lufgt Huron's former palace on Badab Primaris that is surrounded by a frightful battery of orbital defence guns, which is now the residence and command centre of Huron Blackheart and his Red Corsairs. With hundreds of Chaos warships defending its orbit, it is visited by many alien species seeking mercenary work or unrestricted trade, such as the Loxatl or the mortal warriors and pirates of the Lost and the Damned seeking employment amongst one of the major power centres of the forces of Chaos in the eastern region of the galaxy. 

New Badab was also the site of the annual Skull Harvest, a contest hosted by Blackheart between Chaos warbands where Chaos Champions vied with one another for supreme dominance, until only one victor emerged. The reigning Champion than took total control of the losing Champions' warbands. The Skull Harvest took place within the Arena of Thorns, the large venue that hosted the murderous contest; the decapitated heads of the fallen were mounted and displayed upon spikes surrounding Huron Blackheart's throne. The Tyrant of Badab's rule decreed that there could be only one Chaos Champion left standing at the tournament's end. Whoever came out on top as the sole victor earned the patronage of Blackheart himself, who would gift the remaining Champion with additional vessels, warriors and weapons to be used for the express purpose of dealing death to the hated forces of the Corpse Emperor. 

The Skull Harvest was used by the Iron Warriors Warsmith Honsou to dramatically increase the size of his own forces in preparation for his Invasion of Ultramar in ca. 854.999.M41. Thanks to his victory, Honsou's army swelled from a handful of Iron Warriors Heretic Astartes to more than 17,000 Chaos Space Marines, xenos mercenary and Lost and the Damned mortal warriors, and he was granted command of several powerful Chaos warships drawn from the Red Corsairs' own fleet.
