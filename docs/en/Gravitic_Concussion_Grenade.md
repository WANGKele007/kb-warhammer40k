A Gravitic Concussion Grenade is a grenade used by the military forces of the Leagues of Votann that when detonated unleashes a powerful concussive blastwave produced by manipulating the force of gravity in the affected area. Gravitic Concussion Grenades are standard grenade weapons for Kin forces, and are particularly employed by Hearthkyn Warriors and Hernkyn Pioneers.
