Njal Stormcaller, called the "Tempest that Walks", is the Space Wolves Chapter's greatest Rune Priest (Librarian). He has proven to be of unparalleled ability both as an Astartes warrior and a formidable psyker without peer. 

The barbarian tribes of Fenris believe that the most powerful Sky Warriors can bend the savage elements themselves to their will. Njal Stormcaller is the living proof of that belief. The skies are his to command, and he takes fierce delight in summoning ice-toothed blizzards and howling gales with which to scatter and destroy those in his path. 

Njal has manifested a psychic talent to rival any in the Imperium. At the Battle of Goreswirl, Njal showed his true might by blasting apart the dread Bloodthirster that had slain so many of his brothers. He then scattered the Greater Daemon's minions with an ice storm so fierce that it drove them back into the Warp. From that day on, Njal has been known as the Stormcaller. He now holds the revered position of Lord of Runes, the Chapter's High Rune Priest.

History

As a mortal tribesman of Fenris, Njal Stormsson was a vital and fierce warrior, flame-red of hair and forked of beard. He displayed unmatched courage in battle, wading through the blood and corpses of a rival tribe in close-quarter fighting aboard the enemy's longboats. His ferocity was such that he drew the gaze of the Space Wolves' Rune Priest Heimdall, who whisked away his battered and broken body at the end of a terrible battle to begin his induction into the Space Wolves Chapter as a neophyte once he was healed of his mortal injuries.

Under Heimdall's tutelage, Njal learnt to harness the destructive power of nature itself. The old Rune Priest watched with pride as the young Astartes developed a formidable psychic talent that rivalled that of many Librarians within the ranks of other Space Marine Chapters. 

At the Battle of Goreswirl, Njal avenged the death of his mentor Heimdall, blasting apart a dread Bloodthirster Greater Daemon of Khorne with a mighty psychic attack, before summoning forth a terrible ice storm that drove the remainder of the Daemons of Chaos from the field. 

From that day on, Njal has been referred to by the epithet of "Stormcaller" by his battle-brothers. This title has suited him well. In terms of raw psychic power Njal is one of the strongest Human psykers in the galaxy. Njal is also a skilled warrior and master of both tactical and strategic combat, possibly surpassing even the skill of the Space Wolves' owb Great Wolf (Chapter Master) Logan Grimnar.

Around the same time as the 13th Black Crusade erupted, Njal Stormcaller accompanied a fleet of the Adeptus Ministorum to the world of Ras Shakeh, where he rendezvoused with the Space Wolves' Járnhamar Pack under the command of Gunnlaugur. Disdainful of the Ecclesiarchy purges being carried out on Ras Shakeh, Njal instead focused on leading the pack in stopping the space hulk Festerax before it could destroy the Hive World of Kefa Primaris. During the subsequent boarding operation on the hulk, the pack member and nascent psyker Baldr was captured by the Death Guard Sorcerer Mycelite. However Njal was able to destroy Baldr's psychic nullification collar, allowing the Space Wolf to annihilate Mycelite by unleashing his own psychic abilities. Afterwards, Njal still designated Baldr a dangerous witch despite his help, as he had no control over his abilities, and the Lord of Runes condemned Baldr to execution. This action led the the rest of Baldr's Járnhamar Pack to rescue Baldr before he could be slain and flee from Njal, accepting life as Renegade Space Marines.

At the dawn of the Era Indomitus, Njal received a vision of a son of the Allfather returning to usher in the apocalyptic Wolftime and bring salvation to Fenris, while at the same time he foresaw Logan Grimnar apparently meeting his end at the hand of a mighty beast believed to be the Ork Warlord Ghazghkull. Many among the Space Wolves interpreted this vision as the return of their primarch Leman Russ, but in the end the vision was fulfilled by the resurrection of the Ultramarines primarch Roboute Guilliman and the arrival of Primaris Space Marines as reinforcements for the beleaguered Adeptus Astartes. Njal urged caution in his actions to Grimnar, who distrusted the new Space Marines, but Njal himself was mostly accepting of the new warriors, and after scanning the memories of the Unnumbered Son Gaius, later known as Kjarg Iron-oath, he reassured the Great Wolf of his Chapter that these new warriors were true sons of Fenris despite their distant origins in time and space.

Some time after the introduction of the Primaris Space Marines to the Space Wolves, Njal Stormcaller was led to Prospero by the spirit of the Thousand Sons Librarian Izzakar Orr. Izzakar offered to aid Njal in finding the remaining lost members of the Space Wolves 13th Great Company on Prospero through a network of Warp portals called the "Portal Maze" in exchange for restoring his disembodied spirit to his body, which was also located somewhere in the Portal Maze. In the ensuing battle in the Prosperine capital of Tizca and the Portal Maze, Njal was able to rescue most of the Horus Heresy Space Wolf warriors from Magnus the Red and the Thousand Sons and return to his warship in orbit, ordering that Prospero be purged once more from orbit.

Abilities

When Njal's temper is roused, the elements themselves are summoned forth to wage war upon the foe. He can summon the winds, lightning and powerful ice storms that can flay the skin from the bodies of his foes.

Wargear

Runic Terminator Armour - Njal wears the blue-coloured Terminator Armour denoting his specialist position as a Rune Priest of the Chapter. Njal alone has the skill to ensorcel suits of Terminator Armour which are covered in glowing, protective Fenrisian Runes of Power that nullify the affects of enemy psychic powers upon him.

Psychic Hood - An arcane device utilised by Space Marine Librarians to amplify the wearer's psychic powers and protect against an assault by enemy psykers. Most importantly, a Psychic Hood renders the wearer capable of nullifying the effects that other psykers have on the Warp nearby.

Staff of the Stormcaller - Njal's staff is a formidable ancient staff adorned with potent symbols of might, and has absorbed so much power that it has developed a wyrd of its own, which is the ability to nullify enemy psychic powers.

Nightwing - Always at Njal's side is his fierce animal companion Nightwing, a Psyber-raven. At the Battle of Rust World, Njal saved the life of the Iron Priest Ulf Blackbrow from the jaws of Morkai with a deadly accurate axe throw. The great blacksmith, a fierce man who did not like to owe anything to anyone, repaid the debt by forging Njal a Psyber-familiar that has saved Njal's life more than once, pecking out the eyes of those enemies that dare attack his master.
