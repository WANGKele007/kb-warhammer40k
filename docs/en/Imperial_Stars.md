The Imperial Stars is a Loyalist Codex Astartes-compliant Space Marine Chapter of unknown origin and Founding. Nothing else is known about the Chapter in current Imperial records.

Chapter Appearance

Chapter Colours

The Imperial Stars' primarily wear purple Power Armor. The Aquila or Imperialis on the chest plate is golden. The white squad specialty symbol -- Tactical, Assault, Devastator or Veteran -- is indicated on the right shoulder guard. A black Roman numeral centred upon the squad specialty symbol designates squad number. The colour of the shoulder plate trim displays company designation in accordance with the Codex Astartes -- i.e. White (1st Company), Yellow (2nd Company), Red (3rd Company), etc.

Chapter Badge

The Imperial Stars' Chapter badge consists of three white five-pointed stars, each positioned above the intersections of a black, linear equilateral triangle. This is centred on a field of purple.
