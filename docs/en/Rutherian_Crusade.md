The Rutherian Crusade was an Imperial Crusade of the Black Templars Chapter that was launched at some point after the opening of the Great Rift in the Era Indomitus. 

The Gladius Stike Force of this crusade, commanded by Castellan Dramos, assisted by the Knights of House Mortan under the command of Sir Dirkwald, intervened during the Talledus War to defend the Talledus System of the Veritus Sub-sector from an assault by the forces of Chaos.

Dramos and most of his Command Squad were slain when they destroyed the Soul Harvester fortress-factory Scarax Krond with a cyclonic charge.

This sacrifice saved the Fortress World of Ghreddask from being overrun by the troops of the Iron Warriors warband commanded by Warpsmith Etrogar.
