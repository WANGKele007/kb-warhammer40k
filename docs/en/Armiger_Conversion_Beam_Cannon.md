An Armiger Conversion Beam Cannon is a Conversion Beamer weapon large and powerful enough to be utilised by Armiger Pattern Knights. Though among the most devastating of long-range weapons compatible with the Armiger's frame and reactor core, the firing of an Armiger Conversion Beam Cannon is a rare sight on the battlefield as most Knight Scions assigned to Armiger Knights show a distinct preference for close-range engagements.
