The Retributors are a Loyalist Space Marine Chapter of unknown genetic origin and Founding. 

They have faced many threats to the Imperium of Man, including hordes of foes, cunning witchcraft, and terrifying unknown technologies.

Chapter History

Notable Campaigns

Argosa Uprisings (482.M39) - After these rebellions against the Emperor's authority had been checked, the Retributors were tasked with hunting down the leaders of the uprisings.

Chapter Appearance

Chapter Colours

The Retributors have grey power armour with a golden Aquila or Imperialis on the chest.

All Chapter markings are in white.

Chapter Badge

The Retributors' Chapter badge is a golden gauntlet clutching a golden skull. 

The full, formal Chapter badge sometimes includes the wings of the Imperial Aquila spreading from the skull, with golden lightning bolts extending in an arc from beneath the gauntlet.

Trivia

The Retributors were the stars of the fan-made YouTube video series Astartes, created by Syama Pederson. 

In 2021 Games Workshop announced that the series had proven to be so successful and well-crafted that they were adding Astartes to the line-up of official Warhammer 40,000 animations coming to YouTube.

Described on Pederson's Patreon page as relentless, efficient, and distant, the Retributors are known for their stern and austere nature and are more interested in battlefield victories than glory. Their Chapter doctrine requires their forces to be applied as widely as possible across an operating theatre, deploying in small, self-sufficient units tasked with eliminating as many high-risk targets as possible. Their "Impulsor Squads" are instrumental in the application of this strategy and are known to work in a highly independent fashion with other supporting Imperial military elements.

With the transition of the Retributors to official status, more information will be added to this page as those details become canon.
