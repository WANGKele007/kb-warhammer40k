The Jehangir are a savage xenos species that terrorised the Tears of Piruz region of the galaxy. The Cowled Wardens Chapter of the Unforgiven were nominally tasked with the purging of the Jehangir in the Tears of Piruz, but the Chapter's secret purpose was actually the investigation of what the Unforgiven believed to be a large network of the Fallen in the area.

Not long after his awakening from his ten-millennia-long sleep beneath The Rock, Primarch Lion El'Jonson is known to have visited the Cowled Wardens. The nature and purpose of this meeting is unknown, but at its end the Lion led the Cowled Wardens to a final victory over the Jehangir species.
