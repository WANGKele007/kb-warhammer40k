Dreadwing is a novella in the The Horus Heresy series. Dreadwing was released in November 2018 alongside Spear of Ultramar as part of the Black Library's Novella Collection #1, a collection of novellas written by various authors featuring stories from Warhammer: Age of Sigmar, Warhammer 40,000 and The Horus Heresy.

Synopsis

With the Ruinstorm broken and the forces of Roboute Guilliman and Sanguinius making all haste for Terra, the Dark Angels lead a bloody campaign of annihilation against the worlds of the Traitor Legions. But as the Legion fragments and the tally of shattered worlds between them and Terra grows, it falls to the Wing lieutenants to question whether the Lion still wages a war he intends to win -- or one he has already lost.
