House Arundel is a Loyalist Imperial Knight house of the Questor Mechanicus that has sworn fealty to the Adeptus Mechanicus. There is very little information in Imperial records about this Knight house, and it is unknown from which Knight World it hails.

House Colours

The base colour of House Arundel is red. The secondary colour is sadly unknown, as the Knight Warden presented above is the only example of the house's Knights present in current Imperial records. Knight Wardens are painted almost entirely white, with only small elements painted in the house's base colour.
