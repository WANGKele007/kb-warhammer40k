Fleishgate is a lynchpin abattoir Agri-world that provides the meat of grox, grontock and bovian to the Mawdlin System.

Fleishgate has long been taken over by a Genestealer Cult called the Innerwyrm, its primogenitor Purestrain Genestealer organism brought to the planet inside the guts of an immature Void Whale hewn apart for its meat.
