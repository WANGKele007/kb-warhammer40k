Amon Tauromachian was a member of the first circle of the Legio Custodes during the Great Crusade and Horus Heresy in the 30th and early 31st Millennia. During his long career, he was present at many notable events of the Great Crusade and the Horus Heresy, and would be recognised as one of the greatest of the Custodians.

History

Origins

Amon Tauromachian was present at the Triumph of Ullanor in 000.M30, where Horus Lupercal was invested by the Emperor with the title of Imperial Warmaster and command over all the Imperium's armed forces. While he manned a checkpoint alongside a fellow Custodian, Haedo Venator, as well as seven Null-Maidens of the Silent Sisterhood, the Thousand Sons Primarch Magnus the Red was impressed by Amon's stoicism and dedication and predicted that great things lay ahead for him.

Several Terran years before the start of the Horus Heresy, Amon was also present at the Council of Nikaea in 001.M31 to watch over the trial of the Crimson King for his use of sorcery. Whilst protecting Kasper Hawser, a skjald of the Space Wolves, Amon was attacked by a minion of Chaos in the disguise of a Thousand Sons Space Marine, but managed to survive the encounter thanks to the efforts of the Space Wolves Astartes.

Amon was later charged with protecting the Emperor when Terra began the process of fortification to withstand the coming assault on the Throneworld by the Warmaster Horus and his Traitor Legions. After news of Horus' betrayal at Isstvan III reached Terra, Amon was tasked with taking part in the "Blood Games," a rite all Custodians endured and one which set them against the formidable defences of the Imperial Palace in the hope of improving the Custodians' defence of the Emperor through the exposure of flaws in His protection. After ten solar months, and with great difficulty, Amon was able to successfully infiltrate the Imperial Palace and came within striking distance of his target (presumably the Emperor).

Following his success in the Blood Games, Amon Tauromachian and a fellow Custodian Haedo Emankon were sent on a dangerous mission by Captain-General Constantin Valdor, Chief Custodian of the Legio Custodes, to gather incriminating evidence on a member of the government of unified Terra, Pherom Sichar of Hy Brasil, who was suspected of treason against the Emperor. 

When Amon went to confront Sichar, the Imperial Fists' Primarch Rogal Dorn intervened on Sichar's behalf and explained to the Custodians that he was, in fact, an Imperial double-agent. But due to Amon's interference, Sichar's cover was now blown. Audaciously, Amon suggested to the primarch that in the future, all Imperial organisations should share such intelligence to avoid such situations from reoccurring. 

It was later revealed that one of Sichar's bodyguards -- a member of the illustrious Imperial Army regiment known as the Lucifer Blacks -- was an actual agent of the Warmaster Horus. In a suicide attack, this agent detonated a bomb that killed Sichar. He then attempted to set off another bomb so that it would destroy Planalto Central, one of Hy Brasil's largest hive cities, but was stopped by the timely intervention of Amon.

Siege of Terra

During the Siege of Terra Amon was tasked by Constantin Valdor with investigating suspected Daemonic activity within the Imperial Palace. His investigation led him into the refugee camps of the Inner Palace, where he met Euphrati Keeler, the survivor of Isstvan III who had become a living saint to those who believed in the cult of Emperor-worship. 

Amon expressed his distrust concerning the intentions of the future saint and her followers, fearful of the fanaticism that such religious beliefs always portended, but was told by Malcador the Sigillite, the Regent of Terra, to tolerate their presence in order to potentially use their faith as a weapon to combat the powers of the Warp. 

However the Nurglish Daemon Cor'bax Utterblight was ultimately unleashed in the palace through the machinations of those loyal to Horus, and this intrusion into realspace was accompanied by a swarm of Emperor-worshippers who had been possessed by Daemons. With the aid of Keeler and Malcador, Amon was able to defeat Utterblight. For his great deeds and to indicate his future status as a warden of Keeler's cult of Emperor-worshippers, Malcador recommended that Constantin Valdor award Amon a new name as was the custom of the Custodians to mark great events in their lives. Valdor did so, and granted Amon the new name of Longinus.

Later during the Siege of Terra, Amon was assigned to oversee Keeler as she resumed her original occupation as a Remembrancer and interviewed the infamous biomechanical engineer Basilio Fo, an insane criminal captured by the forces of the Emperor during the Unification Wars who used his skills to create monstrosities that he had gleefully unleashed on Terra's population during the Age of Strife. When Fo revealed the existence of a bioengineered contagion capable of killing not only the Traitor primarchs but all Space Marines, Amon agreed with Keeler's assessment that the data should be provided to Rogal Dorn, the primarch who was the Praetorian of Terra and was overseeing the Throneworld's defence. 

Amon and Keeler met with Fo again, and this time recorded all the information he provided concerning the existence and capabilities of this transhuman-killing virus. Amon was then tasked with hunting down Fo after the Renegade escaped from Imperial custody, but the criminal eluded him, which forced Valdor to apprehend him personally. After Valdor's recapture of Fo, the scientist was handed back into Amon's custody. During the final solar hours of the Siege of Terra, as Valdor, Dorn and the Emperor fought Horus personally aboard his flagship the Vengeful Spirit, Amon received a summons to hand Fo over to the Custodian Wardens for execution.

As Amon escorted Fo to what he believed was the engineer's likely death, the madman attacked Amon's confidence in the execution order by questioning its validity. As Amon was about to place Fo in the custody of the Custodian Wardens, Fo forced his would-be executioners to admit they could not confirm the validity of their order to execute him. This caused the two sides to nearly come to blows, and Amon stormed back off with Fo in tow until such time that the execution order could be affirmed by a legitimate authority.

Amon next ran into Zaranchek Xanthus, one of Malcador's Chosen, an agent of Malcador the Sigillite, and Andromeda-17, a potent psyker and Selenar genewitch, who accompanied him to an office of the Sigillite. Amon, Fo, Xanthus, and Andromeda-17 next journeyed to the Sigillite's Retreat, a slender and moldering stone tower within the Imperial Palace's Sanctum Imperialis, to use Malcador's personal laboratory to create a perfected version of Fo's transhuman bio-weapon that had no chance of mutating to also infect non-Astartes.

Soon enough, even the Sigillite's Retreat was overrun by the advancing Chaos forces. Thinking all other options expended, Amon was about to activate Fo's biological weapon to infect the Heretic Astartes until he was interrupted by Ios Raja, a Legio Custodes Sentinel Guard of the Hetaeron Companions, and Khalid Hassan, captain of the 4th Clandestine Orta, a unit of the Imperial Army's special operations forces, who together overruled the order to release the anti-transhuman virus. 

Later, Amon seemingly executed Basillo Fo when he escaped custody once more, but was unaware that Fo had taken on the identity of the cloned Zaranchek Xanthus. Amon was among the Custodians who survived the Siege of Terra and later met with the new master of the Chosen of Malcador following the Sigillite's heroic death, Khalid Hassan, in the depths of the Imperial Palace as he investigated the fate of the Emperor in his fight against Horus.

Name Etymology

Like all members of the Legio Custodes, Amon's name was formally awarded for each successfully completed assignment, deeds of valour earned in combat or earned after contemplation of the mysteries.

As they live far longer than mortal men, Custodians could accumulate a long list of names during their lifetime. Listed below are (as far as can be ascertained) all the known names earned by Amon Tauromachian:

Amon - His forename. Possibly his birth name.

Tauromachian - Not a family name, but one that at least described the occupation of the bloodline that had provided Amon's gene-source.

Xigaze - The site of Amon's organic birth.

Lepron - The house of his formative study.

Cairn Hedrossa - The place where he was first tutored in weapon use.

Eleven More Names

Pyrope - Seventeen words into his nomenclature sequence, this name remembered his first live combat, deployed on an orbital station of Terra of that name.

Several more names

Leng - This name was awarded in the early years of the Horus Heresy to recognise Amon's feat in a Blood Game. Leng was the place on Terra where he attempted to strike his target.

Longinus - Longinus was the name awarded by Malcador the Sigillite during the Siege of Terra, for Amon's efforts in seeking out a Nurglish plague that had manifested among the followers of the Lectitio Divinitatus in the precincts of the Imperial Palace itself.
