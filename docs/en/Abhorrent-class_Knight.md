An Abhorrent-class Knight is a Chaos Knight of any standard class that has been so warped by the energies of the Warp that their very structures have mutated into savage new forms. 

These war engines are often hybrids of the Knight's original mechanical form and daemonic flesh grafted onto its frame by the reality-reshaping powers of Chaos.

Sources

Codex: Chaos Knights (8th Edition), pg. 12
