Talons of the Emperor was the name given during the Great Crusade and the Horus Heresy to the elite and specialised Imperial military forces who drew their commands directly from the Emperor. These forces served the Emperor as emissaries, bodyguards and destroyers and among their number they included the Legio Custodes, the superhuman companions of the Emperor who served as His "right hand" and His sworn protectors, the witchseekers of the Sisters of Silence, who represented His "left hand" and acted as the harvesters of the Great Tithe, and the shadowy, inhuman killers of the temples of the Officio Assassinorum. 

There were other, even stranger and more occluded agencies in direct service to the Master of Mankind, including the tech-arcanists of the Templi-Chronos and the dread Psi-Titans of the Ordo Sinister.

While the Legiones Astartes led the conquest of the galaxy, carrying along the countless ranks of the Imperialis Auxilia in their wake, the Talons of the Emperor were given other, often more mysterious and secret tasks, but were not less vital for that. When the Talons were unleashed they wielded weapons and abilities created by the Emperor or derived from archeotechnological relics of the Dark Age of Technology which were only entrusted to them.
