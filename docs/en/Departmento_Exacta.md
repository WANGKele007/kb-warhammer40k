The Departmento Exacta is the sub-division of the Adeptus Administratum tasked with the recovery of the Exacta, the second most important Imperial tithe after that of the Departmento Munitorum, which determines what civilian strategic resources a world may possess that can benefit the wider economy of the Imperium. 

The Departmento Exacta has a well-deserved reputation for mercilessness and is able to command the invasion of a world by the Imperial military should its criteria for the expected fulfillment of that planet's Exacta not be met to the exact specifications of their data. 

The aspect of the Imperial tithe concerned with the drafting of recruits into the Astra Militarum is not overseen by the Departmento Exacta but instead by the Departmento Munitorum.
