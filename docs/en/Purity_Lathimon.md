Purity Lathimon was a Rogue Trader who first opened the Koronus Expanse of the Segmentum Obscurus up to Imperial exploration in 997.M40.

Purity Lathimon succeeded where dozens of other Rogue Traders had failed and perished, when she discovered and plotted safe passage from the Calixis Sector through what she dubbed "the Maw" to the Koronus Expanse beyond. Fated and cursed in equal measure in some quarters, Purity Lathimon's crew returned largely too insane or troubled in mind to travel the void again, and she bartered her knowledge for vast wealth with the other Rogue Traders posed to exploit her discovery. 

Purity herself is said to have disappeared back within the Imperium after imparting her secrets to those who would follow her. According to some apocryphal stories she is said to have purchased her own paradise world outright and in others to have met a far darker fate.
