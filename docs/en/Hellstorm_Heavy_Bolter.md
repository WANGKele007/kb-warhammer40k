A Hellstorm Heavy Bolter is a variant of Heavy Bolter utilised primarily by Primaris Marine Heavy Intercessors. It is able to fire more rapidly than the standard Astartes pattern Heavy Bolter, hence the name, though it does so at the cost of some range.

Codex: Space Marines (9th Edition), pg. 141
