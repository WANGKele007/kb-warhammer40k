Templar is the twenty-second audio drama for the The Horus Heresy series that was not originally released as part of an anthology or other release. Templar was published in May 2014 by the Black Library. The title was also published as part of The Silent War main series anthology novel.

Official Synopsis

On the doorstep of Holy Terra, the shadow of treachery still lurks. Upon a comet-shrine dedicated to the glory of Unity, a group of Heretic Word Bearers prepare to return to the Sol System after being abandoned by their brethren -- only the forces commanded by Sigismund, First Captain of the Imperial Fists, stand between the Traitors and their goal. But will the legendary Templar's skill at arms be enough to prevail over this new enemy, or will the doubt in his heart prove ultimately to be his undoing?
