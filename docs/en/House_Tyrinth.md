House Tyrinth is a Loyalist Imperial Knight House aligned to the Adeptus Mechanicus. There is very little information in Imperial records about this Knight House, and it is unknown from which Knight World it hails.

House Appearance

House Colours

The deep red of House Tyrinth honours the ancient world of Mars and their formal alliance with the Adeptus Mechanicus. As an Adeptus Mechanicus house the Cog Mechanicum features heavily on the Knight suits of House Tyrinth, along with arrows, warning and binding sigils and other industrial icons that would be meaningless to those not raised in the service of the Machine God. The complex inner workings are also apparent, making it clear that only the Adeptus Mechanicus could have created such a machine.

House Arms

The arms of House Tyrinth is a white Cog Mechanicum with a Lamda letter centered within, surrounded by an Imperialis wreath on a field of black.
