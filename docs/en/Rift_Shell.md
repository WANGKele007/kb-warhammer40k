The Rift Shell is a type of ammunition used by Imperial warships' Nova Cannons. When detonated, a Rift Shell creates a fleet-consuming Warp rift. As such, they are similar to other Vortex Weapons such as Vortex Torpedoes but with a far larger area of effect. Rift Shells are used primarily by the rare Jovian Pattern Nova Cannons.
