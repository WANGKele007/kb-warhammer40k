Mesophet, also called Mesophet the Shadowed Hand, is a Necron phaeron and member of the revived ruling Triarch of the Necron species in the Era Indomitus alongside Hapthatra the Radiant and the Silent King Szarekh himself.

When the Triarch goes to war, they do so together upon the Dias of Domination with the aid of the captured C'tan known as the Enchained.
