A Nostraman Chainblade was an Astartes chainsword crafted in the Nostraman style that was in wide use among the Night Lords Legion's Space Marines during the Great Crusade and Horus Heresy. It was a particular favorite of the Night Lords' feared Contekor Elite Terminator squads.
