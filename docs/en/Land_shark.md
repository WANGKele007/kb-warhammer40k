A land shark is a giant, deadly predatory xenos animal native to the Death World and Jungle World of Miral in the Ultima Segmentum. Land sharks are carnivorous, subterranean-dwelling creatures capable of tearing Human-sized prey to pieces in solar seconds, or even killing a Space Marine. 

The land sharks of Miral went extinct when the world suffered an Exterminatus campaign unleashed by the Scythes of the Emperor in 993.M41 during the Second Tyrannic War when the world was claimed by the Tyranids of Hive Fleet Kraken. Yet, although originally native to Miral, there have been reports of land sharks on Human-settled worlds across the Ultima Segmentum. As such, the destruction of Miral's biosphere has not led to the land shark's extinction galaxy-wide.

To the Human settlers on Miral, the land shark was often the subject of fearful myths and legends. Some regarded it as a cursed creature, a physical embodiment of the planet's lethal nature, while others saw it as a divine trial sent by the Emperor to test the strength and resolve of His people.

History

The Space Marines of the Storm Lords Chapter, who used Miral as one of their recruiting worlds, were familiar with the land shark. Storm Lords initiates recruited from Miral, adapted to the hostile environment of their homeworld, and had faced these beasts during training and battle alike. Surviving an encounter with a land shark was considered a mark of honour and great skill among the Storm Lords.

The Astra Militarum forces stationed on Miral and several other worlds where the creature has been reported to exist have also encountered the land shark, and have suffered high casualties from the creature's attacks when they have ventured into the world's deadly jungles. As a result, Imperial Guard hunting parties or patrol squads are always heavily armed with anti-armour weapons to fend off these behemoths should they surface unexpectedly.

Colonel "Iron Hand" Straken, commander of the Catachan II Regiment, when still a grizzled sergeant, earned the augmetic arm that would become both his trademark and his moniker during an encounter with a land shark. Whilst stalking a Craftworld Aeldari patrol, Straken was savagely attacked by a Miral land shark that tore off his arm. By all rights, Stracken should have died of his injuries, but he is no normal man.

Stracken was born and bred on Catachan and he is one of the toughest breeds of warriors ever to serve in the Astra Militarum. Stracken boasts that instead of crying like some newborn he ripped the land shark's throat out with his own teeth, but some skeptics believe it is more likely that he is referring to his Catachan "Fang" knife.

Appearance

The land shark is a massive, heavily armoured carnivore, resembling a cross between a Terran shark and a subterranean reptile. It can grow up to eight metres in length. Its bullet-shaped maw is filled with rows upon rows of razor-sharp, diamond-hard teeth, capable of tearing through metal and flesh alike, including even materials like ceramite. 

The creature's body is covered in thick, elongated bony plates and fin-like appendages, providing it with armoured protection from both the harsh environment and other predators. It possesses prehensile flippers that the creature uses to propel itself through loose soil and undergrowth. Its muscular, serpentine frame allows it to move swiftly underground and explode upwards to ambush its prey. It has no visible eyes, and it attacks in complete silence.

Anatomy and Physiology

The land shark evolved on Miral, a Death World and Jungle World of the Ultima Segmentum infamous for its lethal ecosystems. Death Worlds like Miral are defined by extreme environments where only the strongest, most vicious animals thrive. The planet itself was a heavily jungled world covered with crags, mesas and ravines, making it the perfect habitat for subterranean predators like the land shark.

The creature is named for its vague resemblance to a Terran shark, though it hunts underground rather than in the sea. The land shark has evolved to dig through soil, rock, and sand, "swimming" beneath the planet's surface in search of prey. With a body designed to burrow quickly and a keen sense of vibration, the Land Shark can detect movement above ground from great distances. The land shark also has a particularly keen sense of smell due to its lack of eyes.

As an ambush predator, the land shark relies on the element of surprise. It often waits patiently underground, listening for any vibrations or signs of movement above. When it detects prey, it bursts from the earth in a violent eruption, attacking with its gaping maw, swallowing victims whole or ripping them apart with its powerful jaws. Its ambush is swift, violent, and usually deadly before the target even knows what hit them.

The land shark primarily fed on large beasts that roamed the surface of Miral before its destruction, but it was known to attack the world's Human settlements and even Astra Militarum soldiers stationed on the planet.

Despite their lethality, land sharks played a crucial role in the balance of Miral's ecosystem. As apex predators, they helped to control the population of other dangerous animals, preventing any one species from dominating the landscape. However, their presence ensured that only the most adaptable and cautious life forms could survive on the planet's surface. 

What ecological role the land shark plays on other worlds where it still exists and how it was brought to them, remains unknown.
