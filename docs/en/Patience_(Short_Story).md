Patience is the twenty-sixth short story published in the Horus Heresy Series that was not originally part of an anthology novel. Patience was first published as a double e-book with the short story Ghosts Speak Not as part of the "Tales of the Knights Errant" week. Both are now included in The Silent War anthology novel.

Synopsis

Amendera Kendel, once a Sister of Silence but more recently in service to Malcador the Sigillite, gives Helig Gallor of the Death Guard a new purpose, and a new duty –- one that will ultimately see him reunited with his former battle captain on the field of war.
