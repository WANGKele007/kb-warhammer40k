The Necropolis Hawks is a Loyalist Space Marine Chapter comprised entirely of Primaris Space Marines. It was created from the rare genetic lineage of the Raven Guard and raised during the Ultima Founding of ca. 999.M41.

Trained extensively in close-quarters warfare and veterans of a dozen close-fought city-fights, the Necropolis Hawks are stoic and efficient in their approach to combat. 

They bear their battlefield tactical role insignia upon their backpacks so that their comrades can identify them even in the choking smoke and dust of urban war.

Although a newer Chapter, the Necropolis Hawks have earned a reputation for pragmatic brutality and relentless, if carefully measured, aggression.

Chapter History

Notable Campaigns

War of Beasts (Unknown Date.M42) - On the vitally important Imperial Hive World of Vigilus straddling the far side of the Nachmund Gauntlet connecting the Imperium Sanctus and the Imperium Nihilus through the Great Rift, it was the seven companies deployed by the Necropolis Hawks that turned the tide against the xenos insurrection and Gellerpox infection that rocked the hive sprawl of Dontoria to its foundations. Taking control of the hive city building by building, they established several safe zones that were expertly defended -- and pitilessly demolished those that they considered lost causes. Several Necropolis Hawks were also captured by a force of Drukhari escaping from Vigilus to be used as pain slaves.

Nachmund Rift War (Unknown Date.M42) - The Necropolis Hawks deployed more than 130 battle-brothers from the 2nd, 4th, and 6th Companies to fight in the Nachmund Rift War during the Era Indomitus.

Fourth Tyrannic War (Unknown Date.M42) - The Necropolis Hawks were among the Chapters of the Adeptus Astartes that deployed to defend the Western Reaches of the Segmentum Pacificus from the latest and greatest assault by the Tyranid Hive Fleet Leviathan.

Notable Necropolis Hawks

Chapter Master Raquilon Zandtus - Raquilon Zandtus is the current Chapter Master of the Necropolis Hawks. Zandtus took part in the War of Beasts on Vigilus. During that campaign, an outbreak of the Gellerpox virus struck the Dontoria Hive, and while a quarantine zone was put around it, Zandtus led his Chapter within Dontoria to purge any Gellerpox Infected mutants they came across.

Captain Miloni Takta - Takta led Necropolis Hawks forces during the Nachmund Rift War. During the evacuation of Dharrovar, he volunteered to stay behind and buy time for his allies to retreat.

Chaplain Lannator - Lannator participated in the War of Beasts on Vigilus.

Chaplain Barbateion Zer - Zer was among the Necropolis Hawks forces that joined the Imperium's invasion of Dharrovar during the Nachmund Rift War.

Epistolary Hozidik Opolio - Opolio was among the Necropolis Hawks forces that joined the Imperium's invasion of Dharrovar during the Nachmund Rift War.

Epistolary Drakht - Drakht is a renowned xenos-hunter and battle-psyker of the Necropolis Hawks. When the Fourth Tyrannic War began, Drakht was among the Space Marines who formed a Solblade task force to attack the newly discovered tendrils of Hive Fleet Leviathan, code-named Hive Fleet Nautilon and Hive Fleet Promethor. During the conflict, Solblade Drakht aided local Imperial forces in stabilising the Iahgo System and afterwards followed the origin point of Tyranid bioships advancing from the neighboring Othlor System. This led Drakht's Solblade to the Othlor System's Hive World Shakar, which the Imperial forces discovered had been conquered by the Tyranids. However, instead of having its entire biosphere consumed which transformed the planet into a Dead World as was the Great Devourer's standard procedure, Shakar had been almost completely covered in an organic, membrane-like structure. Drakht's forces noted vast quantities of non-Tyranid lifeforms being held in biologically-induced stasis beneath the membrane and that numerous bioships were feeding off of Shakar's biosphere. Before the Solblade could discover more about their intentions, the Tyranids became aware of the Imperials' presence and the xenos launched a massive counter-attack that left the Solblade no choice but to retreat. This was the Imperium's first encounter with what its military forces later termed the Tyranids' "Larder Worlds." Drakht is currently rallying the Solblades Pormannon and Chesna, so that he can return to Shakar.

Lieutenant Caelun Durus - Durus was among the Necropolis Hawks forces that joined the Imperium's invasion of Dharrovar during the Nachmund Rift War.

Chapter Appearance

Chapter Colours

The Necropolis Hawks wear blue-grey Mark X Power Armour. The Aquila or Imperialis on the chest is gold. The helm, backpack, pauldrons, pauldron insets and gauntlets are white.

The livery of the Necropolis Hawks is well-chosen, for it enables them to blend into the ruined urban landscapes, cordite-scented smoke and dirt-smog typical of war in a dense cityscape.

The black squad tactical specialty symbol -- battleline, close support, fire support, Veteran or command -- is displayed on the right shoulder pauldron, while the left shoulder pauldron proudly displays the Chapter's iconography. The squad specialty symbol is also displayed on the backpack for easier identification during urban combat.

A white Low Gothic numeral centred in the middle of the squad specialty symbol indicates squad number.

The white Low Gothic numeral centred on the grey, left knee guard designates an individual battle-brother's assigned company.

The colour of the shoulder pauldron trim and top of the helm also designates company colour in accordance to the tenets of the Codex Astartes -- i.e. white (1st Company), yellow (2nd Company), red (3rd Company), etc.

Necropolis Hawk Veteran sergeants are designated instead by a red helm with a white vertical stripe centred upon it, while sergeants wear a solid red helm.

Lieutenants are designated by a white helm bisected by a large, vertical white stripe, with two smaller vertical red stripes to either side.

The top vent of the Necropolis Hawks' standard battle-brother is yellow, while the rest of the helm is white. The faceplate on all helms is white as well.

Chapter Badge

The Necropolis Hawks' Chapter badge is a stylised black raptor's head, reminiscent of the ancient Raptor Imperialis symbol of Terran Unity, centred on a field of white, which represents the sharp wits and visual acuity that make them such formidable urban fighters.
