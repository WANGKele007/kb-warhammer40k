A Magna-Coil Sniper Rifle is a type of Sniper Rifle employed by the warriors of the Leagues of Votann that employs electromagnetic railgun technology to unleash a powerful shot with enormous penetrative and stopping power. It is often employed by Hernkyn Yaegir Riflekyn who are among the best snipers in the galaxy.
