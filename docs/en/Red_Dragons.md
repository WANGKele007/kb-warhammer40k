The Red Dragons are a Loyalist Codex Astartes-compliant Chapter of unknown Founding and genetic origin.

Chapter Appearance

Chapter Colours

The Red Dragons primarily wear red power armour. The Aquila or Imperialis on the chest guard is yellow. 

A white symbol indicating squad tactical specialty -- battleline, close support, fire support, Veteran or command -- is painted on the right shoulder pauldron. 

A black High Gothic numeral is painted on the squad specialty symbol, which indicates squad number. 

In accordance with the Codex Astartes, the colour of the shoulder pauldron trim indicates company number, i.e. white (1st Company), yellow (2nd Company), red (3rd Company), etc.

Chapter Badge

The Red Dragons' Chapter badge is a large white dragon's head breathing fire, centred on a field of red.
