The Firebreathers are a Loyalist Space Marine Chapter of unknown Founding and genetic origin. Their Chapter homeworld is Goritus, which lies in the Segmentum Tempestus.

Chapter History

Notable Campaigns

Octarius War (999.M41-Present) - The Octarius War is a major conflict being waged between the Orks of the Empire of Octarius and the Tyranids' Hive Fleet Leviathan. However, to some extent, the military forces of the Imperium have also clashed with the forces of Chaos during this devastating conflict. The Firebreathers are one of several Chapters of the Adeptus Astartes currently engaged in this ongoing campaign.
