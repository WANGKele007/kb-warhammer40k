The Binary Succession is the forty-second audio drama for The Horus Heresy series that was not originally released as part of an anthology or other release. The Binary Succession was first released as an audio drama in March 2017. The title has since been released as part of The Burden of Loyalty main series anthology.

Official Synopsis

Even as the Imperium faces the Renegade Legions of the Warmaster Horus, the ruling Council of Terra is becoming paralysed from within by increasingly petty bureaucracy. Fabricator-General Zagreus Kane has seen his fellow Mechanicum Adepts dismissed again and again by the High Lords -- with his traitorous predecessor Kelbor-Hal still at large on the Red Planet, the political status of the Tech-priesthood remains ambiguous. New alliances must be forged from the old, if Mars and Terra are to survive the final battle.
