An Omni-visor is an advanced scanning tool used by the Brôkhyr engineers and artificers of the Leagues of Votann. While it was developed to provide aid with civil engineering tasks, it has been adapted by the Brôkhyr Thunderkyn as a weapon of war, allowing them to pick out the telltale signs of enemy units through dense terrain.
