An Injector is an Imperial medical device used to deliver drugs and other chemicals directly to a patient's bloodstream. Most specialised drugs used in the Imperium require the use of Injectors to deliver the substance to the patient correctly.

These can take many forms, from low-tech, disposable syringes up to sophisticated hypo-sprays which simply inject the drug hypodermically and painlessly through cellular osmosis. An Injector can hold one dose of any drug, which a medicae or other medical professional can deliver after being trained in its use.
