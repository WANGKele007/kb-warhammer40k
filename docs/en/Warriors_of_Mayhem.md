The Warriors of Mayhem are a warband of Chaos Space Marines of unknown origin and allegiance. This warband was first sighted by Imperial forces in 925.M41.

Warband Appearance

Warband Colours

The Warriors of Mayhem wear deep purple coloured Power Armour with silver trim.

Warband Badge

The Warriors of Mayhem do not have their own individual badge or iconography. Instead they have been noted to wear the usual blasphemous Chaotic icons and fetishes that identifies them as servants of the Dark Gods.
