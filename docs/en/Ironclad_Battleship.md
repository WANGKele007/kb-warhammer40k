An Ironclad Battleship, also known as an "Admanticlad" due to the nature of its hull armour, is an archaic class of Imperial battleship that in most cases has been retired from service in the Navis Imperialis and been replaced by more advanced capital ships. These vessels are rare, due to their highly archaic design and the lack of Imperial orbital fleet facilities still capable of repairing, never mind manufacturing them.

Ironclad Battleships hail back to a time before the Age of the Imperium, when Humanity's void shield technology was still too primitive to be used for the defence of massive starships of battleship grade. Instead, their hulls were heavily armoured, covered with layer upon layer of thick adamantium plates arranged to deflect and minimise the impact of torpedoes and laser fire. 

However, those few remaining in service have been re-commissioned for a variety of purposes; various pattern Ironclads may be retrofitted with gargantuan ship-, station- and even planet-killer cannons running the entire length of the vessel's keel, linked directly to the stern fusion reactors; others may simply be braced and reinforced for the purpose of ramming into -- and usually through -- enemy vessels. 

Over the many standard centuries of their service, every Ironclad Battleship has fought in countless space battles, and each ship's surface is pitted and rutted with the scars of combat, a glorious history of its long defence of the Imperium. 

Its main weapon is the fusion cannon, a weapon of awesome power that loses energy at long range, and in combat, it must surge forward to deliver its attack at close quarters, braving the enemy fire and trusting to the protection of its massively-armoured hull.

Ironclad Battleships were pressed into service once more during the First Tyrannic War in the 41st Millennium and used to supplement more advanced Imperial warships during the desperate defence of Imperial space against Hive Fleet Behemoth. They were readied to serve again to supplement Imperial voidship fleets in preparation for the arrival of Hive Fleet Kraken in 992.M41.
