Warhammer 40,000: Dawn of War - Soulstorm is the third and final expansion of the Dawn of War series of PC games, featuring two new factions: the Dark Eldar and the Sisters of Battle. Each older faction received new units and air units were incorporated as well for the first time. 

The campaign is much like the Dark Crusade, though the experience has been enhanced with new units and factions. In this installation of the franchise, players must conquer 32 territories across four planets and three moons in the Kaurava System to win. 

The game also features multi-player achievements, including medals, which are earned when certain objectives are accomplished. The game was released for Microsoft Windows on March 4-7, 2008, depending on the geographical region.

Gameplay

Much like its popular predecessor, Dark Crusade, Soulstorm is a stand-alone expansion pack. All nine factions are available for play in the campaign and single player skirmish games, but in order to play as the older factions online, the player must have all of the previous respective games to unlock them. The Platinum Edition of Dawn of War includes the original game and its two previous expansions, Winter Assault and Dark Crusade.

As with its predecessor Dark Crusade, Soulstorm features a "meta-campaign" featuring 31 territories spread over four planets and three moons. One difference however, is that unlike Dark Crusade, there are no persistent bases. Once the player conquers a province, the base structures the player has built up will not be present in future conflicts. This can be remedied by reinforcing provinces with buildings and units in between battles, or by establishing a forward base using the Sisters of Battle's unique army ability.

Plot

Setting

The events of Soulstorm take place in the Kaurava System, an Imperial star system in the Lithesh Sector of the Ultima Segmentum. The system consists of four planets and three moons: the urbanised Kaurava I, the tropical Kaurava II and its two moons Irridene and Orridune, the barren Kaurava III and the rural Kaurava IV with its moon of Lacunae. The system had been the site of battles between the Eldar and Necrons during the War in Heaven, and various Webway Gates that allow travel between the moons and planets through the Eldar Webway are scattered across the system. 

The system's worlds are dominated by Human settlers of the Imperium of Man, who can be found across all four planets. The system is protected by the 252nd Kauravan Conservator Regiment of the Astra Militarum, who have grown complacent and neglectful in their authority. Their newly-appointed lord general, Vance Stubbs, sought to change that. 

There is also a sizeable Feral Ork population on Kaurava II, which is contained within the Rokclaw Mountains in the planet's southern hemisphere. These "Rokclaw Orks" have attempted to take over the system multiple times before but have always been beaten back to their strongholds in the mountains, from which the guardsmen of the 252nd Kauravan have never been able to remove them. Neither the Humans nor the Orks are aware that Kaurava III is a Tomb World, with an army of Necrons sleeping beneath its sands.

Initial Dispositions

The Kaurava Campaign began when an ignorant Imperial Guardsman with latent psyker genes was seduced by the call of the Chaos Gods and performed a ritual on Kaurava IV at their instruction. The ritual created a tear in the fabric of reality, initiating a Warp storm at the outer edge of the system which allowed a warband of the Alpha Legion Chaos Space Marines, under the command of Chaos Lord Firaeveus Carron, to step through the rift and arrive in the Kaurava System on Kaurava IV. 

The sudden and seemingly mysterious appearance of the Warp storm caused havoc within the system. As the Alpha Legion began their assault, the Warp energies unleashed twisted and corrupted the environment, flora, and fauna of Kaurava IV. The Heretic Astartes' attacks on the 252nd Kauravan Conservators, combined with the maddening influence of Chaos that washed over the system, devasted the regiment. In short order, as many as 50% of the Astra Militarum forces in the Kaurava System were either dead or had become Traitoris Militarum. 

The Chaos assault also provided an opening for Kaurava II's Feral Orks, who had already been roused to action by the arrival of Warboss Gorgutz 'Ead 'Unter some time before the Warp storm's emergence. Arming the Feral Orks with advanced technology, and encouraging them to abandon their traditional hit-and-run tactics in favour of prolonged combat, Gorgutz led his new WAAAGH! out of the Rokclaw Mountains to begin claiming the system for himself. 

The Warp storm also caught the attention of various galactic powers, who began mobilising their own forces in response. The first to arrive was a Space Marine army comprised of five companies of the Blood Ravens Chapter led by Force Commander and Captain of the 9th Company Indrick Boreale. The Adeptus Astartes established a base on Kaurava II's northern continent of Solitude to end the danger posed to the Imperium by the xenos and Heretics alike in the system. Boreale also had his suspicions regarding the Astra Militarum, as he suspected that they had played some role in the creation of the Warp storm. When the 252nd Kauravan Conservators resisted the Blood Ravens, a decision backed by adherence to their regimental charter and Stubbs' own ambitions, that confirmed the regiment's heresy in the eyes of the Space Marines. 

The second army to enter the system was a strike force from the Fal'Shia Sept in the T'au Empire. Led by T'au Commander Shas'O Or'es'Ka, and consisting of T'au from the Air, Earth and Fire Castes, as well as Kroot and Vespid auxiliaries, the force landed on Orridune, one of the two moons of Kaurava II. The T'au's primary objective was to contain the Warp storm, but, by doing so, the T'au planned to expand the borders of their empire to encompass the Kaurava System. 

To facilitate these twin goals, the Earth Caste constructed a fortress on Orridune named Nan Yanoi, equipping it with prototype ion lasers that only affected the central nervous systems of advanced organic life forms. The T'au incorporated this technology into their orbital defences, as well as the Ar'Ka Cannon, an artillery piece with interplanetary range. 

From under the sands of Kaurava III's Imossa Province, the Necrons arose from the stasis tombs of the Great Sleep, perhaps due to the effects of the Warp storm. Once enough Necrons had awoken, their Necron Lord began a campaign of extermination of the other intelligent species present on what they viewed as their worlds. By the time the Imperials realised what was happening, the deserts of Imossa had become a death-trap to any who attempted to travel across it. 

In response to the Necrons' awakening, long unused Webway gates reactivated and brought the Eldar of the Ulthwe craftworld, led by Farseer Caerys, to fight against their ancient deathless enemies. They arrived in the Upper Wastes of Kaurava III, a region where they had once battled the Necrons many standard millennia before, and set up a base of operations there, cloaking it from prying eyes. Caught between the Necrons in the south and the Eldar in the north, it was not long before the Imperial forces lost control of Kaurava III entirely. 

While this was occurring, a raiding party from the Kabal of the Black Heart of the Dark Eldar emerged from the shadows of an ancient Webway Gate on the dark side of Lacunae, the moon of Kaurava IV. Led by the one of the kabal's archons, Tahril, they planned to take advantage of the war to harvest spoils, slaves, and souls, striking whenever a weak point or vulnerability emerged over the course of the conflict. 

The Dark Eldar rigged the Webway Gate they had emerged from so it was usuable only by them. Because it provided sadistic xenos with access to every other Webway Gate in the system, the Dark Eldar found they had unparalleled reach. Nevertheless, they began their raids by targeting the uncorrupted areas of Kaurava IV, sandwiching the remaining Imperials between them and the Alpha Legion. 

The last of the forces to arrive were the battle-sisters of the Order of the Sacred Rose, a sect of the Adepta Sororitas' Orders Militant, better known in the Imperium as the "Sisters of Battle". They established a base on Kaurava I under the military command of Canoness Selena Agna and the spiritual leadership of Confessor Treganum March. They had come to Kaurava to cleanse the star system of every last xenos and Heretic, and Confessor March in particular suspected that the system had been corrupted to such an extent that it might need to be cleansed of all life. 

To enable this, Agna ordered both the Blood Ravens and the 252nd Kauravan Conservators to cede authority to her. However, both Captain Boreale, who had orders to neutralise any force that opposed them, and Lord General Stubbs, who knew that submission would not save him or his guardsmen from sisters' zealotry, refused. Taking their rebuttal as evidence of Chaos corruption within the ranks of both the Astra Militarum and Adeptus Astartes forces in the system, the Sisters of Battle engaged the other Imperial forces in the system, determined to wipe out corruption wherever it was found.

Outcomes

The fate of the Kaurava System depends on the actions taken by the various factions fighting over it; it can be returned to the control of the Imperium, become a new frontier Sept of the T'au Empire, become the black heart of the Necrons' next genocidal "red harvest", the base of the greatest Ork WAAAGH! ever launched, or a new Daemon World of the Warp. 

If the Kaurava Campaign is ultimately won by the Imperial Guard's 252nd Conservator Regiment under the command of Governor-General Vance Stubbs, the Kaurava System remains a part of the Imperium of Man. Lord General Stubbs and his troops are then declared heroes by the people of the Kaurava System. The system began to thrive again thanks to vast rebuilding, mining and terraforming projects that were initiated by Governor Stubbs. 

The numerous military academies that were built there following the Imperial Guard's victory turned out superior guardsmen who were second in quality only to the Kasrkin Cadian Shock Trooper regiments of Cadia. 

Only Kaurava III was left largely barren, due to the fact that it had been the Tomb World where the Necrons had risen.

General Stubbs' ability to organise and reform the long-neglected Kauravan Imperial Guard regiment did not go unnoticed by Ultima Segmentum Command, and he was promoted in time from the lesser role of governor-general to full control over a small star system as an Imperial planetary governor.

Canonical Ending

The ultimate fate of the Kaurava System depends on the actions taken by the various factions fighting over it. In Dawn of War II, the follow-on PC game, Scout Sergeant Cyrus reveals that the Blood Ravens under Captain Indrick Boreale had died and suffered a terrible defeat in the Kaurava System, with all 5 of the Blood Raven companies deployed under his command destroyed, essentially costing the Chapter half of its manpower in a single campaign. 

As a result, the Blood Ravens needed to step up their recruiting process in Sub-sector Aurelia, their only remaining recruiting ground for neophytes, who were desperately needed to make good the Chapter's losses from the disastrous Kaurava Campaign. The subsequent invasion of that region by a Tyranid splinter hive fleet during the First Aurelian Crusade soon threatened the very existence of the Blood Ravens Chapter.

However, the plot of Dawn of War III, released in 2017, confirms that the Orks under Warlord Gorgutz 'Ead 'Unter proved to be the victor of the battle for the Kaurava System, making it the heart of his WAAAGH!, though no details were provided explaining how the Greenskins defeated all the other factions.

New Forces

Dark Eldar

The Dark Eldar are the forsaken and corrupt kindred of the Eldar, an ancient and advanced race of elf-like humanoids. Their armies, like their Eldar counterparts, usually have the advantages of mobility and advanced technology, though they are often lacking in resilience and numbers. 

The Dark Eldar revel in piracy, enslavement and torture, and are sadistic in the extreme. Dark Eldar armies make use of various anti-gravity skimmers such as Raiders and Ravagers to launch high speed attacks. They strike with little or no warning, using an interdimensional labyrinth known as the Webway to traverse the galaxy safely and far more quickly than most advanced races are able to with their Warp jumps. 

The Dark Eldar are unique amongst the intelligent races of the Milky Way galaxy because they do not live on a settled world or worlds, but rather in one foul city-state - the Dark City of Commorragh -- that lies within the "ordered" Immaterium of the Eldar Webway. The Dark Eldar are mainly pirates and slavers who prey on targets across the galaxy to feed their unholy appetites for other sentient beings' souls, a terrible desire called the Thirst, though they are sometimes used as mercenaries by other species. 

The Dark Eldar are the living embodiments of all that is wanton and cruel in the Eldar character. Highly intelligent and devious to the point of obsession, this piratical people revel in the physical and emotional pain of others, for feeding upon the psychic residue of suffering is the only way they can stave off the slow consumption by the Chaos God Slaanesh of their own souls. 

The Dark Eldar, particularly their warrior castes, are tall, lithe, white-skinned humanoids. Their alabaster skin is death-like in its pallor, for there is no sun within their dark realm to provide colour. Their athletic bodies are defined by whipcord muscle, shaped and enhanced until they are superior to those of their Eldar counterparts, as the Dark Eldar prize physical and martial prowess highly. 

Yet for all their physical beauty, the Dark Eldar are still repugnant monsters. When viewed with the witch-sight of a psyker, the Dark Eldar's black souls are revealed, for they eternally thirst only for the anguish and torment of other thinking beings in order to fill their own infinite emptiness.

Sisters of Battle

The Adepta Sororitas, also known as the "Sisters of Battle", are an all-female division of the Imperial Cult's ecclesiastical organisation known as the Ecclesiarchy or, more formally, as the Adeptus Ministorum. The Sisterhood's Orders Militant serve as the Ecclesiarchy's fighting arm, mercilessly rooting out corruption and heresy within Humanity and every organisation of the Adeptus Terra. 

There is naturally some overlap between the duties of the Sisterhood and the Imperial Inquisition; for this reason, although the Inquisition and the Sisterhood remain entirely separate organisations, the Orders Militant of the Sisterhood also act as the unofficial Chamber Militant of the Inquisition's Ordo Hereticus. 

The Adepta Sororitas and the Sisters of Battle are commonly regarded as the same organisation, but the latter title technically refers only to the Orders Militant of the Adepta Sororitas, the best-known part of the organisation. 

The Sisterhood serves as the Ministorum's only official military force because the Decree Passive laid down by the reformist Ecclesiarch Sebastian Thor held that in the wake of the Age of Apostasy of the 36th Millennium, the Ecclesiarchy cannot maintain any "men under arms". 

This was supposed to limit the power of the Ecclesiarchy. However, the Ministorum was able to circumvent this decree by using the all-female force of the Sisterhood.
