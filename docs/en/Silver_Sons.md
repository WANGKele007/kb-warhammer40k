The Silver Sons are a Chaos Space Marine warband or "thrallband" that was formed by members of the Thousand Sons Traitor Legion who were exiled by their Primarch Magnus the Red for aiding in the disastrous sorcerous ritual called the Rubric of Ahriman. 

The Silver Sons covet certain priceless metals found within celestial objects, believing them to be links to the past and future. 

Once obtained, these metals are used in the creation of twisted Daemon Engines, and should such substances lie on inhabited worlds, the Silver Sons will incinerate all who stand between them and their prize.

Warband History

Notable Campaigns

Assault on Nephrekh (Unknown Date.M42) - During a battle with the Necron Nephrekh Dynasty, the Silver Sons forged sigils on their Maulerfiends, so the enormous forelimbs and lashing tendrils of these beasts proved so powerful following the sorcerous enhancement that they not only crashed through the Necron battlelines, but also so damaged the Necrons' necrodermis that they were no longer able to enact their self-repair protocols. Due to the powerful Warp influence present on the Maulerfiends, the damaged Necrons instead merged into a mutilated, abnormal conglomeration of mismatched body parts.

Dy'aketh Campaign (Unknown Date.M42) - Amenex Soulrend, Exalted Sorcerer of Tzeentch, unleashed his Silvered Sons against the newly founded T'au Sept World of Dy'aketh. While his Rubric Marines marched through recently constructed conurbations and laboratory-complexes, incinerating all in their path with salvoes of incendiary rounds, Soulrend and his personal retinue headed deep underground. Far beneath the planet's crust lay an Earth Caste research facility, which had spent several solar months studying a strange artefact -- a sceptre crafted from voridium crystals that thrummed with barely suppressed energy. Just as Soulrend neared the priceless relic, he was assailed by experimental battlesuits and Fire Warriors wielding super-charged Pulse Carbines. These weapons fired searing beams of green-white fire that burned through even the Warp-blessed armour of Soulrend's warriors. Battle lines were drawn as Chaos sorcery met cutting-edge technology, and the fires of war consumed Dy'aketh.

Notable Silver Sons

Amaenex Soulrend - Amenex Soulrend is a Thousand Sons Exalted Sorcerer who led a warband called the Silvered Sons to the T'au Sept World of Dy'aketh to claim an artefact sceptre comprised of voridium crystals. His warband has become consumed in warfare with the T'au Fire Warriors who defend that world using experimental weaponry.

Warband Appearance

Warband Colours

The Silver Sons warband's colours are a dark blue with silver trim, not drastically different from their parent Legion. They are known to line their power armour with priceless metals that have been extracted from celestial objects. 

They believe this makes them one with the stars by bestowing upon them a measure of the cosmos' natural power. They wear sky blue tabards and robes.

Warband Badge

The Silver Sons warband's badge is a stylised yellow sigil of the Cult of Time centred on a field of dark blue and trimmed in silver.

Canon Conflict

The 8th Edition T'au Codex refers to a Tzeentchian warband called the "Silvered Sons" led by Amaenex Soulrend who assaulted the T'au Sept World of Dy'aketh to claim a sceptre made of voridium crystals. 

An assumption has been made here that the "Silvered Sons" is the same as the Silver Sons warband due to their near-identical names, allegiance and goals, but it is possible that they are entirely different groups.
