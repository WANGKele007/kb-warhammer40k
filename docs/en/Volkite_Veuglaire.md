A Volkite Veuglaire is an Imperial Volkite Weapon deployed on the Knight Moirax. These ancient weapons are a rare sight even amongst the most venerable of Knight households, for their complex mechanisms are often beyond the ability of simple Sacristans to repair. But for those houses that still operate them, Volkite Veuglaire are prized for their ability to clear light infantry from the battlefield in a blaze of scintillating directed energy beams.
