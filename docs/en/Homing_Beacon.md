A Homing Beacon is a multi-spectrum T'au homing device that, when placed and activated, allows a Hunter Cadre’s T'au Battlesuit reinforcements to deploy with pinpoint precision in the immediate area. 

Homing Beacons are usually only issued to XV25 Stealthsuit pilots with the rank of Shas’vre, Ethereals, and Recon Drones.
