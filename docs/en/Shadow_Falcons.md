The Shadow Falcons is a Loyalist Space Marine Chapter of unknown Founding and origin. 

This Chapter is notable for possessing a high number of Librarians within its ranks. At least one junior Lexicanium serves in each company's Command Squad. 

Notable Shadow Falcons

Chaplain Icarus - Icarus serves as a Chaplain in the Shadow Falcons Chapter. He is currently taking part in the Lentus Crusade in pursuit of his arch-foe, an unnamed Khornate warlord, who has managed to escape his grasp on numerous occasions.
Sergeant Praccimius - Praccimius is an elite Terminator Armoured Veteran of the Chapter's elite 1st Company. Sergeant Praccimius is notable for his actions when he undertook a Space Hulk cleansing operation and survived with a very high kill ratio -- 28 Genestealer kills, 4 from close combat.
