Dusk is an Imperial Feral World on the very edge of the Halo Stars in the Malfian Sub-sector of the Calixis Sector. It is seen as accursed and is known for the extreme hostility of its native fauna. Amongst certain Calixian Rogue Traders and Chartist Captains the saying "A walk on Dusk" has become a byword for a horrible and painful death.

Dusk is a swampy world whose geography is marked by trackless fens, mist-drenched forests, and moss-shrouded, wooded swamps. Theoretically a prime candidate for conversion by the Imperium into an Agri-world capable of undertaking mass agricultural production for the sector, each attempt (going as back as far as the days of the Angevin Crusade which brought the Calixis Sector into the Imperium) has met with disaster, suffering and death, making the world's very name synonymous among the sector's people with ill-omen, sinister mystery and folly.

Dusk was visited by Elana the Unshaken, a Missionary of the Adeptus Ministorum who made it her lifelong goal to bring the word of the Emperor to the dark places of the Imperium. It is said that she bore a scar for each world she visited, and that upon Dusk she was savaged by its vicious predators whilst travelling in the fetid wilderness.

There are no stable Warp routes between Dusk and other planets of the Calixis Sector, though Malfi is the nearest major settled world.

Mara Strain Psychneuein have been encountered on Dusk.

While Inquisitor Erya Nephthys was on Dusk she suffered through a series of terrible events and had to spend a full standard year convalescing after that experience, though she ultimately went Renegade as her sanity cracked under the weight of her terrible burdens.

Dusk Hag

The Dusk Hag is a local Warp-entity that the people of Dusk can bargain with and forge Dark Pacts. The legend says that if anyone should risk the journey into the deepest, most trackless swamps of Dusk with hatred and desire burning in their heart, they might come to the black hollow where the Dusk Hag dwells. It is said that the price of her boons are always paid in flesh, sinew and bone, but her curses can strike death and inflict terror even worlds away, and all the secrets that men whisper in the night are hers. Just a legend perhaps, on a world shrouded in countless dark fables, but still petitioners seek her out and some return, maimed and mutilated, but filled with a dark purpose.

The Rogue Trader Erasmus Haarlock sought out the Dusk Hag to find an answer to how he could return his murdered family to life. After paying an unknown price to the Dusk Hag, she revealed that only by travelling to the heart of Komus, the Tyrant Star anomaly that long plagued the Calixis Sector via his Blind Tesseract's portals could he put an end to his desires. Yet the Dusk Hag warned that he would be utterly destroyed if he did so.

Notable Flora and Fauna

Dusk Stalker - The Dusk Stalker is a common and quite deadly predator found in the swamps of Dusk.

Glo-Slug of Dusk - Glo-slugs are commonly found in the swamps that cover the world of Dusk. In the water they are quite unremarkable, looking like a giant brown or black Terran slug. Like much of Dusk's native fauna, the slugs are carnivorous and spend their lives searching for dead creatures they can cover in their enzymatic slime and digest. When removed from the water the slug's skin immediately gives off a phosphorous white glow.

Nightwing - The nightwing is a predator that produces a potent narcotic dust that they use to subdue their prey, lulling them into a nightmarish stupor, while the haemovoric animal quickly sucks them dry of all blood. Captured or slain nightwings are harvested for this narcotic, which is then concentrated into a powerful and prohibited drug known as "night dust" popular across the Calixis Sector.

Duskan Mist Drake - The mist drake is a carnivorous reptiloid creature that sometimes attacks Humans and whose jaws lock on their prey until either they or their victim are dead.

Boraxis - Boraxis is a bio-toxin produced by the Dusk-native plant of the same name; it is a controlled xenos substance that is not legally allowed to be exported off-world.

Notable Exports

Dusk's greatest export is the hard-bitten feral warriors it raises for the Astra Militarum as part of its tithe to the Imperium. It is also known for the production of insectoid-based protein derived from the creatures that inhabit its swamps that are called "ration grubs" which are sometimes supplied to Imperial military units operating in the Calixis Sector.
