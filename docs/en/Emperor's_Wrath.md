The Emperor's Wrath is a Loyalist Space Marine Chapter of unknown origin and Founding that is based in a star system in the Segmentum Solar. The Chapter Master and the 1st, 2nd and 3rd Companies are currently on an Imperial Crusade against the Forces of Chaos who had occupied a portion of and subsequently destroyed the world of Cadia following Abaddon's 13th Black Crusade against the Imperium.

Chapter History

Very little is known about the Emperor's Wrath Chapter. The Emperor's Wrath's homeworld is in a star system where there are no planets, only asteroids. This system contains three asteroids that have been hollowed out and made into three massive hive cities. It is from this system that the Chapter recruits new members. 

The Emperor's Wrath has a deep hatred for xenos like the Tyranids and the Orks. The Emperor's Wrath has purged many Ork-held star systems using the Exterminatus and has done the same on many worlds that have fallen to the various Tyranid Hive Fleets. 

Unfortunately, some of the worlds purged still had large numbers of Imperial forces on them, which has produced tense relations between the Emperor's Wrath and the Imperial Guard and several other Space Marine Chapters whose Battle-Brothers were killed during such drastic actions.
