The Oriente Sector is a sector of the Imperium of Man located in the Segmentum Tempestus and situated at the very edge of the Astronomican's reach. 

Imperial control over the sector is tenuous and it is constantly beset by hostile xenos forces. 

Genestealer Cult forces have infiltrated the Hive World of Cryobuur and are slowly working to corrupt the sector capital world of Khamrang Prime.
