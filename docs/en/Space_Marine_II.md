Space Marine II, formally Warhammer 40,000: Space Marine II, is a third-person shooter, hack-n-slash video game developed by Saber Interactive and published by Focus Entertainment as a sequel to Warhammer 40,000: Space Marine (2011). 

The game was released for PlayStation 5, Microsoft Windows, and Xbox Series X and Series S on 9 September 2024. The game received generally positive reviews from critics.

The game was released in Standard, Gold and Ultimate Editions. The Gold Edition was the same as the Standard Edition, but came with a "season pass" to provide the first year of post-launch DLC content and 4-days of early access. The Ultimate Edition contained everything in the Gold Edition but released with an Ultramarines Champions Pack that contained more cosmetic choices for the Ultramarines' power armour and bolt rifles. 

There was also a limited release, physical Collector's Edition which included all of the content of the Ultimate Edition as well as a painted 8.25 x 6.7" resin statue of Lieutenant Demetrian Titus, and a Collector's Edition Steelbook entitled The Art and Making of Warhammer 40,000: Space Marine II which was a 172-page art book showcasing the work used to develop the game.

Gameplay

In Warhammer 40,000: Space Marine II, the player engages enemies using a mix of both melee and ranged combat from a third-person perspective. The player controls Demetrian Titus, a Primaris Lieutenant of the Ultramarines Chapter and the returning protagonist from the first game. 

Titus is accompanied by fellow Primaris Space Marines Chairon and Sergeant Gadriel throughout the campaign, who can be controlled by other players in the game's cooperative multiplayer mode or by artificial intelligence (AI) in single player.

The game utilises ranged combat when facing weaker enemies (such as Tyranid Gaunts), while hack and slash melee combat is required when fighting more powerful enemies (such as the Tyranid Warrior) that can block incoming projectile damage. Players can parry an opponent's attack, though some hostile attacks are unblockable and therefore must be dodged. Additionally, the player can fight alongside Astra Militarum foot soldiers controlled by the AI throughout sections of the game. 

Titus is protected by his power armour. When his armour breaks, his health will drop permanently when he is attacked. Health will only replenish when the player recovers medi-packs on the game's battlefield. His armour, however, will regenerate after Titus performs an execution attack on an enemy. If a character's health is depleted the character will drop to the ground and bleed out for 30 seconds during which other characters can revive them before they die.

The game also supports a separate cooperative mode named "Operations," and three 6v6 competitive multiplayer modes (Annihilation, Seize Ground, and Capture and Control). Players can choose from six different Primaris Marine character classes and freely customise their player avatar with various cosmetic items.

Plot

Prelude

The game is set in the Warhammer 40,000 universe's 41st Millennium, during the Era Indomitus, the Age of the Dark Imperium after the birth of the Great Rift has cut the galaxy in two. After the resurrection of Roboute Guilliman, the primarch of the Ultramarines, as lord commander of the Imperium and Imperial Regent, the Imperium of Man has launched a galaxy-wide and a solar-decade-long campaign known as the Indomitus Crusade with the goal of reclaiming lost worlds from its myriad Chaos and xenos enemies and bringing it back from the brink of collapse in the wake of the Great Rift's opening. 

However, just when it seems that the Imperium has finally gained the upper hand, the savage alien species known as the Tyranids of Hive Fleet Leviathan emerges from its defeat in the Battle of Baal to launch a surprise assault from the intergalactic void upon the western Segmentum Pacificus region of the galaxy. This assault of the Great Devourer is comprised of a swarm containing greater numbers than ever seen before, and strikes the Imperium recisely where its defences are weakest in the wake of the Indomitus Crusade's first phase to secure the Imperium Sanctus. This great invasion marks the start of what will be remembered as the Fourth Tyrannic War.

The game's main enemies are the Tyranids (believed to be a splinter fleet of Hive Fleet Leviathan) and the forces of Chaos. The Tyranids are an extragalactic insectoid race whose sole purpose is to consume all forms of biomatter. The forces of Chaos return from the first game, followers of the daemonic Gods of Chaos. Confirmed to feature in the game are the Thousand Sons, one of the nine Traitor Legions of Heretic Astartes who are devoted to Tzeentch, the Chaos God of change, ambition and sorcery.

Following the events of Warhammer 40,000: Space Marine over a standard century before, Demetrian Titus, formerly the captain of the 2nd Company of the Ultramarines Chapter, was imprisoned by Inquisitor Thrax on charges of heresy due to his unexplained resistance to the psychic powers of Chaos during the Liberation of Graia campaign. 

Although he was eventually deemed free of Chaos taint, Inquisitor Thrax continued to imprison Titus for over a standard century and torture him, seeking answers the Space Marine could not provide. Thrax was later killed by the Grey Knights after being possessed during another mission by a Daemon.

After Thrax's demise, other members of the Inquisition freed Titus and the other surviving prisoners of Thrax and allowed him to return to his Chapter if he so chose. Having received no word from his brethren since his incarceration, he came to believe he had somehow brought shame upon his name and that of his beloved Ultramarines and determined that he was not worthy to bear the Chapter's name. He would eventually enter the service of the Deathwatch, the Order Militant of the Ordo Xenos composed of Space Marines from multiple Chapters dedicated to hunting and exterminating all alien threats to the Imperium. watch fortress Watcher Keep as a Deathwatch Black Shield, his former identity unknown to those he fought alongside. 

Overcome with shock and grief at his discommendation, Titus had all of his former Chapter's heraldry purged from his Mark VII Aquila Power Armour's battle-plate and assumed a self-imposed exile as an outcast Black Shield. Titus served with the Deathwatch for several solar decades.

A tendril of the Tyranids' Hive Fleet Leviathan invaded the Imperial Recidious System in the Segmentum Pacificus as part of the Fourth Tyrannic War. Archmagos Nozick Beta-12 of the Adeptus Mechanicus had called for aid to defend the Jungle World of Kadaku in the system and to safeguard the classified "Project Aurora" being developed there. 

Titus, as the Deathwatch Black Shield named "Nullus," deployed as part of the Deathwatch's Kill-team Kasaeran, alongside Brother Ulfar of the Space Wolves, Brother Kelstros of the Blood Angels, Brother Beren of the Black Templars, and Brother Darius of the Dark Angels, transiting to the Recidious System via Corvus Blackstar. During their mission to the Recidious System, Nullus and his squad were tasked with reinforcing the Astra Militarum forces of the 8th Cadian based there on the Jungle World of Kadaku, who were already weakened by the toll taken by the Indomitus Crusade muster across the Imperium Sanctus. The kill-team's mission involved firing a virus bomb into the stratosphere using a local orbital launcher at the main swarm of the Tyranids in the hopes of killing enough of the bioforms before they adapted to the pathogen to give the Astra Militarum a chance at eradicating the rest of the swarm.

The kill-team's gunship was brought down by swarming flocks of winged Tyranid bioforms, forcing the warriors to engage with the insectoid aliens on the planet's surface as they struggled to get the virus bomb to the launcher. During this mission, Titus was gravely wounded by a monstrous Tyranid Carnifex after he had fought off swarm after swarm of lesser bioforms. As life bled from his torn body, his fellow Deathwatch battle-brothers having all been slain before him, he prepared to commend his soul to the Emperor. It was then that Titus was reunited with the warriors of Ultramar, as an Ultramarines strike force arrived to relieve the Deathwatch at Kadaku. 

Varro Tigurius, Chief Librarian of the Ultramarines, detected the soul-sign of the warrior once known as Demetrian Titus in the local Immaterium as their strike craft traversed the Warp. Even as the Tyranids' Shadow in the Warp began to cloud Tigurious' witch-sight like cataracts, the Chief Librarian recognised he who had once been the captain of the Ultramarines 2nd Company. 

As fate would have it, Tigurius travelled aboard the Righteous Fury, the same vessel that had borne Titus and Trajan before him to battle many times, and it was this mighty voidcraft of the Chapter that broke from the Warp at the edge of the Recidious System. There, the strike cruiser prepared its Thunderhawk gunships to deploy to the planet where the broken form of the Black Shield known to his comrades as Nullus lay dying. 

The Ultramarines 1st Company arrived on the planet to reinforce the Deathwatch forces, bringing none other than First Captain Severus Agemman and Chief Librarian Tigurius. The Ultramarines fought valiantly, their weapon blasts wreaking devastation upon the Tyranid bioforms with the carefully born discipline of experienced Tyranid hunters, holding back the tide. They arrived too late to save Kadaku from falling to the Great Devourer but the Deathwatch warriors of Watcher Keep were able to recover their dead and wounded and fall back into the Ultramarines' gunships. All except the ravaged Black Shield Nullus, who Tigurius ordered returned to the Apothecarion bays aboard the battle barge Resilient. 

Titus' wounds were grave, and it was decreed that he would undergo the "Calgarian Rites," the Ultramarines' name for the Rubicon Primaris, in order to have even a chance of surviving. Titus withstood the procedures necessary to become a Primaris Space Marine as stoically as he had endured any of Inquisitor Thrax's interrogations and was remade stronger than he had ever been. When he awoke, he was surprised to find himself honoured with the rank of lieutenant under the command of Captain Sevastus Acheran of the 2nd Company -- a warrior who had only recently left the Scout Marine reserve when Titus was taken from the Ultramarines almost a standard century before. Once more Titus resolved to do as was bid of him by his Chapter, and serve under a warrior who held the titles he had once held. 

There were those amongst the Ultramarines who were apprehensive about Titus' return to the Chapter. Chief Librarian Tigurius, however, had performed his own examination of the former captain and found him to harbour no taint, before psychically communing with Chapter Master Marneus Calgar who authorised Titus' immediate reinstatement. Their word was as adamantine, and Titus was officially welcomed back into the warrior brotherhood of his gene-sire. Lieutenant Demetrian Titus, once and once more of the Ultramarines 2nd Company, would bring the wrath of his Chapter to the foes of the Imperium anew.

Recidious Campaign

Yet Titus knew none of this as he lay unconscious in the Apothecarion aboard the Ultramarines battle barge Resilient. At his awakening, Titus was greeted by a Chaplain and was informed that the Ultramarines' Chapter Master Marneus Calgar had ordered that Titus undergo the Rubicon Primaris surgery while he was unconscious, a dangerous process that transformed Firstborn Space Marines like Titus into the larger, faster and stronger Primaris Marines. The Rubicon Primaris had to be crossed because the Apothecaries had determined that only the enhanced physiology provided by the additional Primaris gene-seed organs could save Titus' life. 

Titus' first impulse upon awakening was to return to his Long Vigil in the Deathwatch but the Chaplain reminded him that he had been declared free of any Chaos taint by the Inquisition and if he truly yearned for redemption, then he must return to his Chapter and earn back their favour. However, his service in the Deathwatch as a dishonorable Black Shield named "Nullus" who would not reveal his true Chapter or identity would be removed from the Ultramarines' records and much of his past would remain shrouded from his brothers.

After re-inducting him into the Ultramarines and having him outfitted in a new suit of Mark X Tacticus Power Armour, the Chaplain ordered Titus to keep his past a secret and to report to Sevastus Acheran, the new captain of the Ultramarines 2nd Company that Titus had once commanded. Acheran stated that he had reservations about Titus' reinstatement in the Ultramarines, particularly in his old company, but nonetheless provided him with his new rank of lieutenant in the company and command of a squad. 

Joining Titus were Sergeant Valorem Gadriel, the former commander of the squad Titus had been assigned and Brother Meduras Chairon, both Primaris Marines, who gradually became suspicious about Titus' past as he continually refused to answer their questions and concerns about his long absence from the Chapter. Neither Space Marine knew anything about Titus' past or that he had once been a captain and hero of the Chapter.

Captain Acheran informed Titus that the 2nd Company was currently in the process of a planet-wide evacuation of Kadaku's remaining Human population and ordered him and his squad to aid the Astra Militarum forces on the planet in retaking control of its orbital defence cannons.

Fighting through endless waves of Tyranid bioforms, Titus and his squad managed to retake the orbital cannons, allowing the Imperials to destroy one of the Tyranid Hive Ships and buying the Imperial forces time to continue with the evacuation. Titus was then ordered to escort Archmagos Nozick Beta-12 off-world, but the latter refused to leave until important data related to Project Aurora was retrieved from his overrun research facility. 

After reaching Nozick's facility and completing the data transfer, the Thunderhawk gunship transporting Nozick to the Resilient in orbit mysteriously crashed in territory overrun by the Tyranids. Titus and his squad raced to save Nozick but found the archmagos already dead. Surveying the wreckage, the Ultramarines discovered to their shock that Nozick had not been slain by Tyranids but by the forces of Chaos. Before they left the area, a squad of Ultramarines transported a mysterious object from the wreckage whose proximity caused Titus to lose consciousness.

After awakening on the battle barge Resilient, Titus informed Acheran of Nozick's demise and of the presence of Chaos-worshippers on Kadaku despite the concerns of his squad about his health and fitness for service. Acheran ordered Titus to go to the nearby Hive World of Avarax in the Recidious System to retrieve Archmagos Nozick's tech-priest apprentice, Morias Lueze, who was the only person qualified to continue his master's work on Project Aurora. But first the Ultramarines needed to know where in the vast conurbation of Avarax's hive city of Fervastium Lueze was located. 

After fighting their way through the Tyranid swarms, Titus and his squad reached an Adeptus Mechanicus facility hidden deep within Fervastium and Titus accessed a cogitator within that provided him with Lueze's location. Suspicious of the mysterious object that had earlier been recovered by the Ultramarines from Nozick's Thunderhawk crash site and had caused him to lose consciousness, Titus also searched through the cogitator's files and discovered to his horror that Project Aurora was actually an attempt to construct a Warp-based weapon powered by the same Chaos artefact Titus had destroyed over a standard century before during the Liberation of Graia, its remains having been retrieved and reconstructed by the Mechanicus. 

Returning to the Resilient, Titus confronted Acheran about the artefact. Having experienced the terrible consequences of its use on Graia, which had led to a Warp incursion on that Forge World, he demanded that the project be stopped and the artefact destroyed once more, but Acheran refused as the Mechanicus claimed they were working on the project by order of Roboute Guilliman himself.

Returning to Avarax to find Lueze, Titus and his squad were ambushed by the Heretic Astartes forces of the Thousand Sons Traitor Legion who were also seeking out Lueze. Fighting their way through the Traitor forces, the Ultramarines managed to reach Leuze's hiding place and escorted him to the battle barge. Along the way, Lueze unwittingly revealed to Gadriel and Chairon that Titus had once been the captain of the Ultramarines' 2nd Company over two standard centuries before and was thought dead after he held the Chaos artefact during the Liberation of Graia campaign, further inciting Gadriel's suspicions about and resentment of Titus. 

After retunring to Acheran, Titus urged the captain to contact Chapter Master Marneus Calgar and warn him about the true nature of Project Aurora. Acheran proved dismissive of the idea, especially since the astropathic relay which would allow them to send an astrotelepathic message to Calgar was currently overrun by the Tyranids and all messages in and out were blocked by the Chaos forces' sorcerous machinations. Titus proposed that he and his squad fight their way to the relay while other squads of the 2nd Company eliminated the Hive Tyrant synapse creature leading the Tyranid swarms and end the forces of Chaos' jamming operation. Acheran agreed to the plan.

As Titus and his squadmates fought their way to the relay, the Tyranids they faced suddenly died or become feral, unconnected to the Hive Mind, as a result of their fellow Ultramarines successfully eliminating the Hive Tyrant and the effort of the forces of Chaos to jam all Imperial astropathic communications. Once within the astropathic relay, Titus met with Astropath Neoma who offered to send a message to Marneus Calgar. 

As she telepathically sent the message, Neoma suddenly accused Titus of being a Traitor and trying to lure Calgar to the planet to kill him. An already suspicious Gadriel attacked Titus as a result but stopped when Chairon suddenly shot Neoma, recognising her as a liar who had been corrupted by Chaos. At her death, her body exploded in a blast of Warp energy that revealed the Thousand Sons Sorcerer Lord Imurah. 

After a brief duel, Imurah escaped and Titus returned to the Resilient to report to Captain Acheran. Acheran informed Titus that thrall-bands of the Thousand Sons had appeared across the Recidious System and were focusing their assault on Demerium, a local Cemetery World that was one of the two moons of Avarax and the new home of Project Aurora, where Lueze was attempting to activate the Warp-based weapon. 

As the Ultramarines prepared to assault Demerium, Titus and Gadriel made amends, with Gadriel accepting that Titus was a loyal servant of the Emperor. After making planetfall and fighting through the Thousand Sons forces present on the Cemetery World, the Ultramarines arrived at the subterranean location of Project Aurora where they discovered from the xenos nature of the surroundings that Demerium was actually a Necron Tomb World and that the Mechanicus had been using the xenos' technology to advance their research and development of Project Aurora. 

The Astartes found Lueze, who revealed that by combining Necron and Adeptus Mechanicus technology with the Chaos artefact as a power source, the Project Aurora weapon will allow Imperial forces to negate and banish to the Warp all those touched by the energies of Chaos, first in the Recidious System but eventually the effect will spread across the entire galaxy, providing the Imperium with a final victory over Chaos. 

Sorcerer Lord Imurah attempted to stop the work on the weapon and was seemingly banished into the Immaterium by Project Aurora. However, this proved a ruse, and as Imurah returned he revealed that it was he who had covertly influenced the Tech-priests of the Adeptus Mechanicus into creating and continuing the project previously begun on Graia so that he might gain control of the reconstructed artefact. The sorcerer then slew Lueze and stole the artefact for himself.

Returning to the surface of Demerium from the Necron tomb, Titus' squad discovered a Necron obelisk had erupted that had formed a massive Warp rift on the planet's surface, allowing Imurah to bring more of his Thousand Sons forces and a Lord of Change to the Cemetery World. After the squad reunited with Captain Acheran, Gadriel suggested that they might be able to close the Warp rift by shutting down the lesser obelisks empowering the main one. 

Fighting their way to each of the Necron obelisks, Titus and his squad were nearly overwhelmed by the Thousand Sons before they were saved by the sudden arrival of Marneus Calgar and his Ultramarine reinforcements. Titus and his squad proceed to shut down the lesser obelisks while Calgar and the rest of the Ultramarines distracted Imurah's forces. After shutting down the Warp emanations of the final lesser obelisk, the combined strength of the Ultramarines begin pushing back the forces of Chaos.

During the battle, Imurah and the Lord of Change fled through a portal into the Immaterium, unleashing a spell that froze all of the Ultramarines engaged in the battle save Calgar, Titus, Gadriel, and Chairon. Followed by Calgar, Titus and Titus' squadmates, the Ultramarines managed to reunite with Calgar within the Warp and duel Imurah and the Lord of Change. They managed to drive the Lord of Change back as Imurah attempted to use Project Aurora's power source to imbue himself with the divine knowledge of the Warp. 

However, midway through that process, Titus claimed the Project Aurora power source while Imurah was distracted battling the Chapter Master and reversed the polarity of the power source as Imurah imbued himself with it, causing Imurah to explode with a massive burst of psychic energy when he attempted to channel an amount of Warp energy that was too great for him to handle without the artefact's assistance. The explosion also drove back the Daemonic tide, granting victory to the Ultramarines and the other Imperial defenders.

Awakening back in realspace, Titus found himself surrounded by the victorious Ultramarines, now free of the sorcerer's spell. Returning to the battle barge Resilience, Marneus Calgar praised Titus for his deeds and asked that he join him on a mission elsewhere. Titus said farewell to Gadriel and Chairon but was intercepted by the Chaplain he had first met upon returning to the Ultramarines, who was also accompanying Calgar on the new mission.

The Chaplain congratulated Titus for having proven himself worthy to rejoin the Ultramarines, but warned him that the risk of corruption by Chaos is ever-present and if Titus were ever to truly falter and betray the Emperor he would not hesitate a moment in carrying out his execution. The Chaplain then removed his Skull Helm and revealed himself to be Brother Leandros, Titus' young protege who had served with him over a standard century before during the Liberation of Graia and who had originally reported him to the Inquisition for his inability to be affected by the power of the Warp. Titus' redemption had, in truth, been a belated redemption for them both.

Development and Release

Warhammer 40,000: Space Marine II was announced on 9 December 2021 with a cinematic trailer at The Game Awards 2021. It was confirmed that Demetrian Titus was making his return and had become a Primaris Space Marine. The game would take place in Games Workshop's Warhammer 40,000 universe and featured the Ultramarines Chapter of Space Marines. 

The Tyranids were announced to be the primary featured enemies. It was also announced that the role of Titus had been recast (he had been voiced by https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mark_Strong Mark Strong in the original game), and that he would now be voiced by Clive Standen. A gameplay reveal was also shown at The Game Awards 2022.

In June 2023, at Summer Game Fest 2023, it was confirmed the campaign would feature three player co-op with either other players or the game's AI controlling the characters and it was additionally revealed that the game was set for a "winter" 2023 release. A gameplay trailer was released at the end of August 2023, as well as confirmation that there would be a beta release.

In September 2023, Games Workshop released a board game based on the upcoming game which included a miniature model of Demetrian Titus. In November 2023, Games Workshop and Wētā Workshop revealed several statuettes in the works, with one of them being a statuette of Titus with interchangeable weapons and heads.

Space Marine II was originally scheduled to release for PlayStation 5, Microsoft Windows, and Xbox Series X and Series S in 2023, before being delayed to 9 September 2024. In June 2024, developer Saber Interactive announced that the planned beta was cancelled in order to focus on getting the game ready for the delayed launch date.

Reception

Warhammer 40,000: Space Marine II received "generally favorable" reviews from critics, according to review aggregator website Metacritic. The game attracted more than 2 million players on its launch day. As of early 2025, the play count surpassed 6 million.
