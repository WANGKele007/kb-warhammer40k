Nemiel was a knight of The Order of Caliban who was later elevated to the rank of Chaplain within the Dark Angels Legion during the Great Crusade. From his youth onward, Nemiel maintained a persistent rivalry with his cousin, Zahariel El'Zurias. 

This rivalry endured until the two were separated following the Dark Angels' actions during the Sarosh Campaign, events that marked a decisive turning point in their relationship and respective paths within the I Legion as the Horus Heresy began.

Nemiel was slain in 008.M31 by his own primarch, Lion El'Jonson, after he insisted that the Edicts of Nikaea against the use of psychic powers within the legiones Astartes must be upheld contravening the Lion's own more pragmatic stance on the issue in the face of the extremes of the Horus Heresy.

History

Origins

Nemiel and Zahariel El'Zurias were among the hundreds of youths who presented themselves before The Order's fortress-monastery on Caliban before first contact with the Imperium who sought to be inducted as knights. As part of the trial, they were required to stand for more than twelve solar hours beneath the frozen snow, keeping watch for the monstrous beasts that prowled the dark forests of Caliban. Nemiel was among the few who endured the ordeal and was accepted, beginning his path as a supplicant, the rank preceding full knighthood.

Under the strict tutelage of Master Remiel, Nemiel underwent intensive physical and mental training as a supplicant of The Order. Throughout this period, he constantly competed with his cousin, whose reputation within the Order steadily grew following his early encounters with the Great Beasts of Caliban. Though neither could clearly surpass the other in training, Zahariel's rising renown increasingly drew the attention of the senior Masters of The Order.

A measure of resentment began to take root in Nemiel when Zahariel survived his first encounter with a Great Beast and later completed a successful Beast Quest, earning elevation to full knighthood and the personal recognition of The Order's leader and their future primarch, Lion El'Jonson. Zahariel's achievement, which marked him as an exceptional figure within The Order, cast a long shadow over Nemiel's own progress.

Determined not to remain eclipsed, Nemiel soon undertook a Beast hunt of his own. Through discipline and resolve rather than spectacle, he succeeded in slaying a Great Beast and was likewise elevated to the rank of a full knight of The Order. Though his ascension lacked the singular renown of Zahariel's deed, it firmly established Nemiel as a proven warrior and reinforced his belief that duty, obedience and adherence to the The Order's strict codes of ethics were the true measures of worth.

Knights of Lupus

Following their elevation to knights, both cousins were assigned to the assault on the fortress-keep of the Knights of Lupus, a rival knightly order that had openly opposed Lion El'Jonson's campaign to finally eradicate the Great Beasts of Caliban. Under the leadership of Lord Sartana, the Knights of Lupus had rejected the rapid changes imposed upon Caliban and, unbeknownst to the wider Order, had secretly captured and trained the Great Beasts rather than destroying them.

During the siege, the assaulting forces of The Order encountered fierce resistance. When the fortress walls were breached, imprisoned Great Beasts were released against the attackers, inflicting heavy casualties. Amid the fighting, Nemiel distinguished himself through his discipline and bravery, becoming the first to force a breach through the outer defences, enabling the attack to press forward.

As the assault reached its conclusion, Nemiel and Zahariel entered the ruined keep of the Knights of Lupus and witnessed the death of Lord Sartana, who took his own life rather than face capture. With Sartana's death, the Knights of Lupus were destroyed as an order of knights, their remnants hunted down within the fortress. The campaign marked Nemiel as a proven warrior of The Order and further shaped his rigid adherence to duty and authority.

Descent of Angels

Soon after the campaign against the Great Beasts of Caliban ended in their complete eradication, the I Legion of Space Marines arrived on Caliban and was reunited with its missing primarch, Lion El'Jonson. Within solar months of the Imperial arrival, the I Legion was conducting rigorous trials to evaluate the skill, endurance and character of potential Astartes aspirants drawn from The Order and the other knightly orders of the Death World. Nemiel committed himself fully to these martial and spiritual tests, enduring grueling physical challenges and demonstrating unwavering discipline and resilience.

Through his performance, Nemiel distinguished himself among the aspirants, showing both courage and steadfast loyalty, qualities highly prized by the Legiones Astartes. After completing the trials, he was formally accepted into the ranks of the Legion, becoming one of the newly transformed battle-brothers of the Chapter, forged through the application of Imperial science and the primarch's guidance. His success marked him as a proven warrior of the I Legion, now renamed the "Dark Angels" by the will of Lion El'Jonson after an ancient Calibanite legend, and solidified his place among the Lion's gene-sons.

The one matter Nemiel and Zahariel never openly discussed was their involvement in the conspiracy to assassinate the Emperor of Mankind. Shortly before the Emperor's arrival on Caliban, Nemiel brought Zahariel to a secret gathering attended by several high-ranking members of the recently disbanded Calibanite knightly orders. 

While some proposed staging a false atrocity, the conspirators ultimately resolved to attempt the Emperor's assassination in the hopes of preventing whatever changes the Imperials would bring to Caliban's tradition-bound society. Upon hearing this, Zahariel stormed from the meeting in anger, with Nemiel following soon after. Nemiel swore that he would attempt to dissuade the others from acting. Whether he truly did so remains unknown, but the assassination attempt ultimately went forward regardless, though it failed.

Sarosh Campaign

During the Great Crusade, the Dark Angels were dispatched to the world of Sarosh, a planet that had persistently delayed its acceptance of Imperial Compliance, though its leadership claimed to be ready to aceept entry into the Imperium willingly. The I Legion was tasked with relieving the White Scars, who had previously been assigned to oversee the planet's compliance with little success. 

During a formal reception held by the Saroshi ruling elite, the planet's Human hosts suddenly turned hostile. Nemiel remained at the side of Lion El'Jonson throughout the incident, maintaining his position as the primarch swiftly slew the emissary sent to assassinate him. Nemiel's unwavering presence during the betrayal further demonstrated his loyalty and discipline under sudden threat. 

Shortly thereafter, Nemiel once again accompanied his gene-father on a mission to investigate and seal a powerful Warp energy surge uncovered within a mining complex on Sarosh. The native population had unwittingly unleashed a Daemon into realspace through the enaction of forbidden rites tied to the site. Nemiel fought valiantly alongside his gene-sire throughout the engagement, and together they succeeded in banishing the Warp entity and containing the breach.

In contrast to Zahariel, Nemiel was permitted to remain with his battle-brothers after the Sarosh Compliance was complete. In recognition of his conduct and spiritual authority, he was elevated to the relatively new rank of Chaplain and appointed as a "Redeemer-Brother", while Luther, Zahariel, and others received orders to return to Caliban and oversee the recruitment of new Dark Angels for the rest of the Great Crusade.

Death

After the Horus Heresy began, during the Thramas Crusade against the Night Lords Traitor Legion in 008.M31, Lion El'Jonson dispatched a portion of his forces to the world of Perditus in search of an ancient and powerful artefact known as the Tuchulcha. This device possessed the ability to enable travel through the Ruinstorm, the vast Warp storm conjured by the Word Bearers during the Battle of Calth that had cut much of the Imperium in Half. At the time, the artefact was under the control of the ancient Mechanicum and had become a point of contention between surviving elements of the Iron Hands and a force of the Death Guard, led by their corrupted first captain, Calas Typhon.

During the Warp transit to Perditus, the Invincible Reason, flagship of the Dark Angels Legion, was assaulted by Daemons. The sons of the Lion engaged the Warp entities with conventional weaponry, but their attacks proved largely ineffective against the immaterial foes. Only the use of flamers, the focused gaze of the warship's Navigators' Third Eye, and the unleashed psychic powers of the Librarian Asmodeus succeeded in destroying the invading Daemons. Until that moment, Asmodeus had deliberately suppressed his abilities in strict adherence to the Edicts of Nikaea, which forbade the use of psychic powers within the Legiones Astartes.

Witnessing this breach of Imperial Law, Chaplain Nemiel accused Asmodeus of treachery for defying the Edicts of Nikaea and attempted to carry out his execution. Lion El'Jonson intervened, halting the act and formally abolishing the prohibition on psychic powers within his Legion, now that it had become clear to him that the Emperor had been wrong and some measure of psychic might would be needed to defeat the Traitors' Chaos allies. Nemiel protested once more, declaring that a decree issued by the supreme authority of the Imperium, the Emperor of Mankind, could not be revoked, even by a primarch.

In response, Lion El'Jonson slew his gene-son with a single blow, decapitating him without hesitation. Following Nemiel's death, the Lion placed Asmodeus in command of one of the Invincible Reason 's purge squads dedicated to eradicating all manifestations of Daemonic activity, marking a decisive shift in the Dark Angels' conduct during the Horus Heresy and reaffirming the primarch's absolute authority over his Legion.
