The Loki-class Q-ship is simply an umbrella designation for any heavily armed starships disguised as merchant vessels and designed by the Imperial Navy to lure in marauding pirates or other unsuspecting foes.

Armament

Q-ships are often armed with macrobatteries and lances with both dorsal and prow emplacements.

Specifications

Dimensions: 2 kilometres long, 0.4 kilometres abeam approx.
Mass: 8 megatons
Crew: 18,000 approx.
Acceleration: 3 gravities maximum sustainable acceleration
