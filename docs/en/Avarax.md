Avarax is an Imperial Hive World of the Recidious System that was invaded by a splinter of the Tyranid Hive Fleet Leviathan during the Fourth Tyrannic War of the Era Indomitus. The Ultramarines 2nd Company and the 8th Cadian Regiment of the Astra Militarum came to its aid during the Recidious Campaign.

Avarax was a major centre of industrial production in the region and the output of its manufactoria produced by its billions of workers was crucial to the continued defence of the Imperium. It is home to over 90% of the Recidious System's population.

Avarax has 2 moons, the closest being the Cemetery World of Demerium and the furthest designated X_0020. 

One of the primary hive cities on Avarax was called Fervastium and another was Thassean, a smaller hive city of the hive cluster that comprised the capital hive's local region. The world is also the home of the system's primary Astropathic Relay.

Avarax had an aestimare (estimated planetary value) to the Imperium of C750.

History

Origins

The world of Avarax is a juggernaut of industry that is home to myriad hive cities and logistic hubs which span entire continents. Almost the entirety of Avarax's surface is devoted to manufacturing and transport, which has left the Hive World mostly covered in towering conurbations of ferrocrete and plasteel.

As is common on Hive Worlds, the biosphere of Avarax has collapsed into a desert wasteland afflicted by frequent sandstorms due to the hive cities' excessive and unregulated industrial pollution and exponential over-exploitation of the planet's natural resources. Because of this environmental degradation, Avarax is forced to rely on food shipments from the neighbouring Jungle World of Kadaku to keep its population fed and industry supplied with promethium so the Hive World can continue functioning. 

This trade became a liability in the Era Indomitus, when the Recidious System was invaded by Hive Fleet Leviathan and subsequently the Thousand Sons Legion of Heretic Astartes under the command of the Sorcerer Lord Imurah during the Fourth Tyrannic War. Both worlds were attacked by the Tyranids and the Traitor Legion, but Avarax began suffering from food shortages, as the invasion disrupted Kadaku's crucial food and energy shipments to the Hive World.

The resulting starvation caused the formation of a series of Cannibal Cults on the world as the Ultramarines 2nd Company and the Cadian 8th Regiment of the Astra Militarum defended Avarax. Even as its population struggled to survive, the Adeptus Mechanicus shifted as many of the Hive World's manufactoria as possible to full military production in order to keep the Ultramarines and Cadian guardsmen well-supplied. 

Adding to the Hive World's woes was the strange inaction of Avarax's planetary governor and its ruling nobility during the invasion. This lassitude in their world's defence outraged the Cadian 8th's commanders and caused them to suspect that a Genestealer Cult was present on the world or that the Tyranids' Lictors had carried out a decapitation strike against the planetary government. 

For its part, the Inquisition suspected the Thousand Sons were responsible for the planetary government's strange lack of resistance. This led retinues of Inquisitorial Acolytes to be sent to Avarax, in order to seek out any possible Chaos corruption that was caused by the presence of the Traitor Legion.

Recidious Campaign

During the Recidious Campaign of the Fourth Tyrannic War, Captain Sevastus Acheran of the Ultramarines 2nd Company ordered Lieutenant Demetrian Titus to go to the nearby Hive World of Avarax in the Recidious System to retrieve Archmagos Nozick Beta-12's apprentice, the Tech-priest Morias Lueze, who was the only person qualified to continue his master's work on the development of the anti-Chaos weapon known as Project Aurora. But first the Ultramarines needed to know where in the vast conurbation of Avarax's hive city of Fervastium Lueze was located.

After fighting their way through the Tyranid swarms assaulting the world, Titus and his squad reached an Adeptus Mechanicus research facility hidden deep within Fervastium and Titus accessed a cogitator within that provided him with Lueze's location. Suspicious of the mysterious object that had earlier been recovered by the Ultramarines from Archmagos Nozick's crash site on the neighboring Jungle World of Kadaku whose presence had caused him to lose consciousness, Titus also searched through the cogitator's files and discovered to his horror that Project Aurora was actually an attempt to construct a Warp-based weapon powered by the same Chaos artefact Titus had destroyed over a standard century before during the Liberation of Graia. That weapon's remains had been retrieved and reconstructed by the Mechanicus.

Returning to the Ultramarines battle barge Resilient, Titus confronted Captain Acheran about the artefact. Having experienced the terrible consequences of its use on Graia, which had led to a Warp incursion on that Forge World, he demanded that the project be stopped and the artefact destroyed once more, but Acheran refused as the Mechanicus claimed they were working on the project by order of Roboute Guilliman himself.

Returning to Avarax to find Lueze, Titus and his squad were ambushed by the Heretic Astartes forces of the Thousand Sons Traitor Legion who were also seeking out Lueze and his knowledge of Project Aurora. Fighting their way through the Traitor forces, the Ultramarines managed to reach Leuze's hiding place and escorted him to the battle barge through constant assault by the Thousand Sons. 

Along the way, Lueze unwittingly revealed to Titus's squadmates Sergeant Valorem Gadriel and Brother Meduras Chairon that Titus had once been the captain of the Ultramarines' 2nd Company over two standard centuries before and was thought dead after he held the Chaos artefact during the Liberation of Graia campaign, further inciting Gadriel's suspicions about and resentment of Titus.

Avarax was plagued by at least two Cannibal Cults during the Recidious Campaign -- the Cult of the Flesh Immaculate and the Worshippers of the Purified Dead -- that emerged due to the food shortages and the resulting starvation inflicted on the world's hive cities by the conflict. Given that Avarax was under assault by the Great Devourer and the forces of Chaos, it is possible these cults were tied to either of them in some way.
