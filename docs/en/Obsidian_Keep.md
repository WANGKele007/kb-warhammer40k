The Obsidian Keep is a great, black-walled fortress that serves as the headquarters of the Adeptus Astra Telepathica in the 41st Millennium. 

It stands in the precinct of the Imperial Palace on Terra called the Outer Palace. 

It may exist in the portion of the palace that was once known as the City of Sight in the days of the Great Crusade and the Horus Heresy.

Psykers taken by the League of Blackships as part of the Imperial Tithe are brought here for training by the Astra Telepathica as astropaths or Sanctioned Psykers.
