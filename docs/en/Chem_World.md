A Chem World is an Imperial planetological classification used as a sub-classification for certain Industrial Worlds whose atmospheres have been poisoned by the industrial waste of billions of people that has been pumped out of their manufactoria for standard millennia.

Described as being the Imperium's oubliettes and the filthiest of its worlds, Chem Worlds' populations must wear helmets or respirators at all times when outside in order to safely breathe their atmospheres. Needless to say, the life spans of the inhabitants of Chem Worlds are some of the shortest in the Human-settled galaxy.
