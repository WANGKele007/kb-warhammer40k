A bloodstone is a type of blood-red gemstone native to the world of Baal that was used by the Blood Angels to decorate their wargear and other equipment. The gems are carefully extracted by hand from the deep caves of the Cruor Mountain on Baal.

Sources

Codex Supplement Blood Angels (9th Edition), pp. 7, 58
