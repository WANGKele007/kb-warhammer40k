A Laskar-class Star Fort is a type of star fort in use by the Navis Imperialis for planetary and system defence. 

The star fort's weapons are operated by servitors as well as Human naval gunnery crews and their targeting system is guided by Machine Spirits (artificial intelligences). The targeting spirit needs to detect and select its targets before firing. If the Laskar-class Star Fort's targeting systems become inoperable as a result of enemy activity, it can switch to manual, direct-fire protocols. While lacking a Warp-Drive of its own, the Laskar-class can be towed through the Warp by two frigates, connected to the void station via cabling.

Notable Laskar-class Star Forts

Emperor's Eye - The Laskar-class Star Fort Emperor's Eye was stationed in orbit of the moon of Ogrys, the Chapter homeworld of the Invaders Chapter of the Adeptus Astartes. The void station came under assault during a Red Corsairs raid on the system as part of the power struggle for control of that Heretic Astartes warband between its Chaos Lord Huron Blackheart and his lieutenant Verngar the Apostate following the latter's claiming of the Tzeentchian Chaos relic known as the Ebon Talon. The attack on Ogrys was intended to prove Verngar's capacity to command a large-scale fleet action so he could assume a greater role within his warband. When the Emperor's Eye proved unable to defend itself after the Red Corsairs unleashed Chaos sorcery to bring down its defences, it was subsequently captured by the Chaos Space Marines and towed away through the Warp to join their growing Renegade armada in the Maelstrom Warp rift.
