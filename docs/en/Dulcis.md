Dulcis was an Imperial Swamp World that was ravaged by a plague of Enslavers during the Era Indomitus after the birth of the Great Rift. Its population was ultimately exterminated by the combined forces of the Blood Angels and Red Wings Chapters.

History

Dulcis is an Imperial Swamp World located near the Cicatrix Maledictum in the Imperium Nihilus. Its surface was primarily comprised of marshlands with the few regions of dry ground serving as the sites of Human settlement. The primary export of the world was meat from its native species of large and aggressive mudeels and other crustaceans.

At the time of the Indomitus Crusade, Dulcis was invaded by Enslavers from the Warp and the world's population of forty million people ultimately had to be exterminated by the Space Marines of the Red Wings and the Blood Angels. During the campaign, the Red Wings' captain, Ares, the only Firstborn among a Red Wings complement comprised solely of Primaris Marines, was killed when Lieutenant Jadriel unexpectedly fell victim to the Black Rage. 

Without a Firstborn to explain the genetic flaw that afflicted all of the scions of Sanguinius, the Red Wings Primaris Marines had no idea what was happening to them until the arrival of a Blood Angels force under the command of High Chaplain Astorath the Grim, who soon taught the Red Wings the nature of their curse and how it was to be contained. After the elimination of the Enslaver plague, Dulcis itself was left intact for Imperial resettlement at a later date.
