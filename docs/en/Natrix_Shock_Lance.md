The Natrix Shock Lance is a Titan-grade ranged weapon deployed by the Warhound-class Scout Titans of the Legio Audax. The Natrix Shock Lance is an upgraded version of the Titan-grade Ursus Claw that delivers a potent energy burst to a grappled Titan struck by its harpoon-like lance, potentially shutting down its reactor for a brief period on a successful strike. 

The Natrix Shock Lance has a slightly shorter range than the Ursus Claw, but if it gets a direct or critical hit upon an enemy Titan, that god-engine must immediately shut down its reactor.
