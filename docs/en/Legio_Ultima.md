The Legio Ultima is a Loyalist Titan Legion of the Collegia Titanica, one of the military arms of the Adeptus Mechanicus. Their heraldry and colour scheme proclaims a joint allegiance to the Adeptus Mechanicus and the Ultramarines Chapter of the Adeptus Astartes. 

There is a distinct possibility that this Legion may be one of two classified Titan Legions that personally serve the fabricator-general of the Adeptus Mechanicus on Mars. The Legion's current status remains unknown.

Legion History

Notable Campaigns

War of the Beast (544.M32) - The Legio Ultima is one of several different Imperial military factions that took part in the defence of the Imperium when it was attacked by the massive Ork WAAAGH! of the infamous warlord known only as The Beast. When the world of Ullanor was determined to be where The Beast's forces originated from, the Legio Ultima took part in the Second Invasion of Ullanor as part of the Imperial strike force in order to slay The Beast, but the mission ultimately proved unsuccessful.

Legion Badge

The badge of the Legio Ultima is a clenched armoured fist with two criss-crossing lightning bolts behind it. Behind the central symbol is the Cog Mechanicum symbol of the Adeptus Mechanicus.

Trivia

The Legio Ultima was created by Games Workshop employee Matt Murphy-Kane and was featured in White Dwarf 401 (UK) and in the The Beast Must Die novel by Gav Thorpe.
