The Terror Lords are a Renegade Chapter of Heretic Astartes of unknown origins and Founding. Nothing is known about them except for their recent infiltration of the Hive World of Fulcrum.

History

Notable Campaigns

The Fulcrum Conspiracy (195.998.M41) - Inquisitor Lowry of the Ordo Hereticus reported encountering three members of the Terror Lords while investigating seditious activity on the Hive World of Fulcrum in the Pandraxx Sub-sector of the Acteron Sector in the Segmentum Ultima. The Renegades slew the better part of a company of Planetary Defence Forces troopers before they were overwhelmed and killed. Inquisitor Lowry herself was severely wounded in the battle.
