The Huskovite Grenadiers is a Militarum Regimentum of the Astra Militarum.

Notable Huskovite Grenadiers

Colonel Cox - Cox was a senior officer deployed to the Vogen campaign on Khai-Zan in 968.M41 in response to the Khai-Zan Uprising. During the fight, Cox's soldiers had to reach Angel Square in Vogen, but the only straight marching path was blocked by several buildings. In response, Cox ordered his soldiers to launch Krak Grenades at the buildings' supports to clear a path.
