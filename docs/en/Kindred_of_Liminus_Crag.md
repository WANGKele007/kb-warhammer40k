The Kindred of Liminus Crag is a Kindred of the Trans-Hyperian Alliance of the Leagues of Votann. The folk of the Kindred of Liminus Crag are famed as shipwrights of the swiftest voidcraft and atmospheric gunships among the Leagues.

Codex: Leagues of Votann (9th Edition), pg. 10
