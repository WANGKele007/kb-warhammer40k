Evander Garrius, also known as the "Tyrant of Cthonia" and the "Butcher of Larissan", was the lord-castellan and the venerable captain of the 456th Company of the Imperial Fists Legion, a force largely composed of newbloods, who was most notable for his conquest of the Mining World of Cthonia, the homeworld of the Sons of Horus Legion, during the Horus Heresy.

Garius's aggression during combat, which often led to more casualties among the forces at his command than were necessary to secure his objectives, was rare for an Imperial Fist, and coupled with his Legion's famed skill at fortification, he presented a dire foe for the would-be liberators of the Sons of Horus to overcome, though Garrius's ruthless approach to warfare eventually caused him to fall from favour with his primarch, Rogal Dorn. Despite his long service to the Legion, he never had the opportunity to fight alongside Dorn in the field, which always remained among his deepest regrets. Throughout the brutal campaigns of the Horus Heresy, Garrius continued to hope that one final victory might restore Dorn's favour and earn him the honour of standing beside his gene-sire in battle.

Garrius commanded the successful First Siege of Cthonia in 006.M31 and defended the world from an attempt by the Sons of Horus to retake their homeworld during the Second Siege of Cthonia in 013.M31 before the planet's ultimate destruction by the Dark Angels Legion in 014.M31 following the conclusion of the Siege of Terra.

History

Origins

Evander Garrius was a Space Marine veteran of the Unification Wars, during which he earned renown that continued to grow throughout the early standard years of the Great Crusade. He was deployed in numerous battles alongside the Luna Wolves Legion, with whom he developed a close friendship while serving as a Breacher Lieutenant on the world of Lactrical. His methods of warfare closely resembled those of Horus's gene-sons and earned the praise of their primarch himself. Garrius stopped at nothing to achieve victory, regardless of the lives lost, including his own, as demonstrated by the grievous wounds he suffered on Larissan.

However, this casual brutality earned Garrius the disfavour of Rogal Dorn. After their primarch's reunification with the VII Legion, Garrius was consumed by a single-minded desire to prove himself worthy of serving among Dorn's inner circle, though his ruthless methods stood in stark contrast with the ideals Dorn sought to instill in his gene-sons. As a result of his actions and reputation, Garrius was ordered to remain sequestered on garrison duties for much of the Great Crusade far from the side of his gene-father. 

When Warmaster Horus Lupercal openly declared his rebellion against the Emperor, Garrius immediately opposed him. To him, the betrayal of the Imperium by a man he had once respected was an unforgivable crime. When Dorn called on him to retake Cthonia -- the homeworld of the Luna Wolves, now called the Sons of Horus -- for the Imperium, Garrius rejoiced and vowed before his brother captains of the Imperial Fists to bring the Arch-traitor's homeworld to heel, swearing that only his primarch would move him from that forsaken world.

Siege of Cthonia

Garrius led the First Siege of Cthonia in 006.M31, commanding a combined Imperial planetary assault force comprising elements taken from the Imperial Fists, Space Wolves, the Imperial Army and the Legio Custodes. The lord-castellan personally led the assault on the hive city of Lupercal's Gate, seeking to seize control of the vault above the hive's spire, where the Black Throne of Cthonia stood.

Despite being granted the title of "Protector of Cthonia" by the Imperium, few among the Imperial Fists regarded the appointment as anything more than another censure added to Evander Garrius's record, and he was soon known unflatteringly as the "Tyrant of Cthonia". His conduct during the battle had resulted in unnecessary casualties among the huscarls and varagyr among the attacking force. Rogal Dorn had given him a heavy burden: a final opportunity to prove his worth, but also a bitter punishment for his past failures. So long as Garrius remained bound to Cthonia, he could not be transferred to any other major front of the Horus Heresy.

When the Second Siege of Cthonia finally began in 013.M31 when the forces of the Sons of Horus attempted to retake the planet after almost seven standard years of a continuing guerilla campaign against Imperial rule, Garrius initially focused on directing his forces from the rear, coordinating his Imperial Fists troops of the 456th Company directly with his allies, which were chiefly remnants of the Shattered Legions and Loyalist elements of the Thousand Sons. It was not until a solar month into the second siege, once again focused on Lupercal's Gate, renamed "Traitor's Gate" by the Loyalist garrison, that he personally joined his troops in battle, leading an ambush of the Traitor forces and confronting Vheren Ashurhaddon, the commander of all Sons of Horus forces on the planet. However, the battle left Garrius's forces too depleted to risk another open engagement, forcing him to devote his efforts to reinforcing the final defensive line.

During this period, Garrius came into contact with Kovarny, a chieftain of the Sons of Horus' Reaver Attack Squads who commanded the Heathens Reaver Squad with whom Garrius had sworn bonds of brotherhood during the Lactrical campaign of the Great Crusade when the Sons of Horus were still the Luna Wolves. Garrius's efforts ultimately prevented Ashurhaddon's forces on the hive world's surface from consolidating into a more dangerous offensive and led the Sons of Horus captain to target Garrius for death. Ashurhaddon charged the Kovarny with this task, but Garrius's old comrade secretly refused to do so. Instead, Kovarny sought to capture the Imperial Fists captain alive and convert him into worshiping the Chaos gods. Garrius would then willingly join Kovarny's Heathens Squad and their brotherhood would be renewed as they fought the Imperium together. Garrius, however, had no intention of betraying everything he stood for. He instead lured Kovarny and his entire squad into an ambush, killing them without remorse despite their former oaths of brotherhood.

The siege continued for almost a standard year until both sides received news from the Throneworld in 014.M31 that Horus had initiated the Siege of Terra. Determined to reclaim Cthonia while their master conquered Terra, the Traitors launched a final assault, resulting in a bloody battle between the two forces. Garrius and Ashurhaddon fought within what had once been Horus's throne room, where the Son of Horus commander nearly slew his Imperial Fist counterpart. Their duel was interrupted by the arrival of the Dark Angels, who subjected Cthonia to a devastating orbital bombardment that ultimately destroyed the planet.

With the aid of his Thousand Sons allies, Garrius escaped what would otherwise have been his place of death and was healed through biomancy. Resentful over the loss of Cthonia and still enraged at the loyalists, he did not return to Terra following the defeat of the traitors. Instead, he set out to hunt the remaining traitors at the head of a force composed of Thousand Sons and Imperial Fists Inductii recruited on Cthonia.

Later Life

Garrius recovered his health after the end of the First Siege of Cthonia, and during the Bitter War that the Imperium unleashed against the Word Bearers who defended their homeworld of Colchis during the Great Scouring, took part in the assault on that corrupted Legion homeworld.

Wargear

Subjugator - A relic power fist once wielded by a gene-wright tyrant of Old Earth, this ugly tool of war held little subtlety or grace but was brutally effective on the field of war. Garrius had claimed it as his own in the early years of the Great Crusade, and many claimed it was the taint of the ancient tyrant that had once wielded the same weapon against the Emperor that stained Garrius's own soul and prompted him to such bitter rage on the field of battle.

Incineratus - A unique variant of the Volkite Charger directly connected to the extensive bionic augmentations that were installed in Garrius's body after his near-fatal wounding on the world of Larissan, Incineratus can fire deadly arcing rays of exotic energy that eat away at the flesh and armour of the foe.

Tyrant's Regalia - An artificer-made suit of Cataphractii Pattern Terminator Armour that offers its wearer some of the best protection available to any lord of the Legiones Astartes, the Tyrant's Regalia was crafted to specifically fit around Evander Garrius's ruined body and its internal systems were designed to pour pain suppressants directly into his bloodstream and numb the constant agony caused by his old wounds.
