A Blight Launcher is a heavy Grenade Launcher used by the Blightlord Terminators and Plague Marines of the Death Guard Traitor Legion. It is particularly effective at firing Blight Grenades at the foe, unleashing innumerable daemonic pathogens and diseases.

Sources

Codex Heretic Astartes - Death Guard (8th Edition), pg. 90
