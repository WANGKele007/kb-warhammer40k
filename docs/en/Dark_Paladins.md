The Dark Paladins was a Loyalist Space Marine Chapter of the 3rd Founding and unknown genetic origin. Little else is known about it in Imperial records save that the Chapter was destroyed while fighting xenos who were most likely Necrons on the world of Jarrman Primus in the 41st Millennium.

Chapter History

Notable Campaigns

Fall of the Dark Paladins (Unknown Date.M41) - The last report of the Chapter of the Adeptus Astartes known as the Dark Paladins has filled many secret councils of the Imperium with cold silence and furious debate in equal measure. The words, taken from a desperate communication with the Chapter's primary Battle Barge, tell of the Space Marines landing upon the planet Jarrman Primus to investigate the sudden loss of all communication with that manufacturing world. It was not long after the first Thunderhawk set down that the reports of unliving warriors of steel assaulting them from all sides came in with furious speed. The Dark Paladins' Chapter Master made the fateful decision to deploy the entirety of his Chapter's forces against the unknown xenos threat, and within a solar hour Drop Pods were descending from the skies bringing the full might of the Imperium's finest warriors to bear. However, reports from the battlefield soon became confused and uncertain. The Dark Paladins fought what quickly became a valiant rearguard action against the massed ranks of the inhuman creatures, destroying many towering vehicles and bringing down fiendish steel beasts that seemed to slip from their grasp, becoming almost intangible in battle. The most worrying were the final garbled reports of the Dark Paladins' Chapter Master, who explained that whilst the combat raged and his men died, the Dark Paladins' Techmarines stood rigid, their eyes frozen in awe at the mechanical monstrosities they faced. Final uncertain pict-views, sifted from the transmissions of the Dark Paladins' fleet, show the destruction of their great Battle Barge by midnight black, blade-shaped ships that appeared as if from nowhere, their beams of viridian energy tearing the vast Battleship apart in a solar second. Of the Dark Paladins, nought else is known, save not one of them left Jarrman alive.
