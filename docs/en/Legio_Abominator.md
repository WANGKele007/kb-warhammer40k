The Legio Abominator is a Traitor Titan Legion in the service of the Dark Mechanicum. 

Legion History

Notable Campaigns

13th Black Crusade (999.M41) - The Knights of Tanika march to the aid of the Iron Hands Chapter on Medusa, which has been besieged by the forces of Chaos spilling forth from the Eye of Terror. Led by the matriarchal huntresses of House Thorne, they battled against the Renegade Titans of the Legio Abominator and buy time for the Iron Hands' clan companies to rally and fight back. Forging steely chains of allegiance and loyalty, the Iron Hands and Tanikan Knights drive the Heretic invaders back.
