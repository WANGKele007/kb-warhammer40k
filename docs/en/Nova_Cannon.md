The Nova Cannon is one of the most exceptionally powerful starship weapons employed by the military forces of the Imperium of Man. A Nova Cannon fires massive, gravitically-impelled warheads that can unleash fleet-consuming plasma explosions, gravitational disruptions or even Warp rifts. 

Mounted below the heavily-armoured prows of Navis Imperialis cruisers and battleships, Nova Cannons have few equals in terms of their range or destructive power.

Very few weapons are capable of creating a blast effect that can encompass multiple Warp-capable starships. These rare and massive weapons are distinguished by that capacity.

History

The Nova Cannon is an ancient technology, whose origins are lost to Imperial history but stretch back into the Dark Age of Technology.

During the Great Crusade Nova Cannons were considered very experimental, and were unavailable to most captains of the Armada Imperialis.

At the start of the Horus Heresy in the Isstvan System, at least one grand cruiser of the Iron Warriors Legion utilised a Nova Cannon against the escaping remnants of the Raven Guard following the Drop Site Massacre, which surprised the primarch Corvus Corax.

During the Reclamation of Kathur in the 13th Black Crusade in ca. 999.M41. the Dominator-class Cruiser Depth of Fury magnetically fired a Grav Shell from its Nova Cannon at the infamous Death Guard Traitor battleship Terminus Est. Hitting the diseased ship's prow, a sizeable chunk of it simply ceased to exist, wrenched out of physicality and into nothingness. After this the Depth of Fury was badly damaged by the Chaos forces. However, a few solar minutes later it fired another Grav Shell into the Plague Ship's underbelly, which gouged a wicked, bleeding hole in the enemy vessel. Just before the Depth of Fury fired its Nova Cannon a third time, it was destroyed by the wounded Terminus Est and its support cruisers, though the battle had been a near-run thing for the Nurglish vessel. 

At the battle of Paradyce II during the Pariah Crusade in the Era Indomitus, Nova Cannons were among the few Imperial starship weapons that were capable of significantly damaging Necron warships. Unfortunately they were unable to destroy the Necron Pylon Ship.

Role

The Nova Cannon is a weapon of ancient providence dating back to the Age of Technology, though its exact origins remain unknown. But what is a well-established fact is that Nova Cannons are a classification of exceptionally powerful starship weaponry, easily able to wreak vast destruction upon enemy vessels at great range. 

However, a Nova Cannon is inaccurate and requires a stable vessel to serve as a platform from which it can fire effectively. This platform is most often the prow of Imperial cruisers and battleships as only they have powerful enough plasma engines to counteract the Nova Cannons' immense recoil when fired. 

While variation exists, a typical Nova Cannon consists of an array of potent gravimetric impellers designed to accelerate a projectile to a fraction of the speed of light. The nature of these projectiles vary, ranging from sophisticated plasma warheads which burn with the ferocity of a small star for a fraction of a solar second, to implosive devices which exert destructive gravitational forces upon all those caught within several thousand kilometres of the detonation. 

In any case, a well-used Nova Cannon is a terrifying thing to face, as much a psychological tool as a weapon. Despite this, these weapons are often ill-favoured by the Navis Imperialis, with most starship captains preferring to utilise the more traditional torpedoes. Those few who favour the weapon understand that it is difficult to use and often rendered useless at close ranges.

The Nova Cannon itself is massive; the barrel is the length of all but the largest Imperial vessels. The projectiles have a diameter of at least 50 metres and are fired at close to light speed after being accelerated by gravimetric coils. When the projectile has travelled the predetermined distance, the warhead implodes with a force equivalent to several plasma bombs. This creates a blast zone the size of a small planet, powerful enough to destroy a light cruiser in a single hit or cripple even a battleship.

While the exact working principle of the weapon remains unknown, there have been several arguments about the kinetic energy of the impact. Unfortunately, while the kinetic energy can be estimated with some accuracy, the weapon, primarily due to the extreme range and unreliable aiming, is unlikely to directly hit anything smaller than a planetoid. 

The primary effect of the Nova Cannon is derived from the explosive force of its projectile, which is produced by an unknown method, though it is presumably a very large thermonuclear fusion warhead in the case of plasma explosions. Due to the lethal nature of their warheads, Nova Cannon shells are not armed for a fraction of a second after firing, allowing them to travel many tens of thousands of kilometres through the void before they become truly deadly. For these reasons, a Nova Cannon is only fired from an Imperial warship according to specific rules which do not apply to other naval weapons.

Notable Patterns

Although each pattern of Nova Cannon is generally most efficient when it fires the particular type of ammunition it was purpose-built to use, each is also capable of using all of the other types produced as well.

Mars Pattern Nova Cannon - Though Nova Cannons are quite rare, even by the standards of Imperial starship construction, the Mars Pattern is the most common construction template. These massive cannons -- hundreds of metres in length -- fire an enormous shell that echoes a traditional explosive warhead, though on a much larger scale. These shells are accelerated to near-relativistic velocities, causing an explosion that detonates with more force than dozens of plasma warheads.

Ryza Pattern Nova Cannon - This variant of the Nova Cannon replaces the massive explosive shells with a highly unstable plasma-based macro-bomb. These weapons are even more power-hungry than a Mars Pattern Nova Cannon, but are believed to be far more effective as well, capable of shattering battleships with full shielding upon a successful strike. With this additional power does come some additional questions of stability, as weaponising such a huge quantity of plasma carries its own inherent dangers.

Stygies Pattern Nova Cannon - A Stygies Pattern Nova Cannon is similar to the Ryza Pattern Nova Cannon in using a plasma-based macro-bomb, except it requires less power and is less damaging overall.

Jovian Pattern Nova Cannon - The Jovian Pattern Nova Cannon is one of the rarest and the most feared of its kind. Most commonly found mounted on cruisers, the Jovian Pattern replaces the traditional explosive warhead with Vortex Warheads; the discharge of which results in a rift being opened in realspace to the Warp. These rifts have been known to rend vessels cleanly in half. As powerful as it is, the ammunition utilised by the Jovian Pattern Nova Cannon is quite unstable, and damage that would normally render another pattern of Nova Cannon simply unusable will outright destroy the Jovian Pattern instead.

Nova Cannon Ammunition

Rift Shells - Used primarily by Jovian Pattern Nova Cannons, Rift shells are Vortex Weapons that produce an effect similar to a Vortex Torpedo, except the area of effect when detonated is much larger.

Electromagnetic Shells - Electromagnetic shells unleash a storm of electromagnetic energy designed to interfere with enemy sensors and communications.

Grav Shells - A newer design, Grav Shells are extremely difficult to manufacture and as such are extremely rare and dangerous. When detonated, a Grav Shell implodes in on itself under the effects of an intense artificially-generated gravitational field and creates a miniature black hole that sucks in anything within a given radius stretching across thousands of kilometres near the point of detonation.

Doppler Shells - A Doppler Shell is intended to serve as a way of quickly locating hidden enemy warships. When detonated it releases a massive burst of gravimetric waves that bounce off the hulls of nearby starships and expose hidden enemies as a form of gravimetric sonar.

Plasma Shells - Used primarily in Mars Pattern, Stygies Pattern, and Ryza Pattern Nova Cannons, Plasma Shells utilise either a very large Thermonuclear Fusion Warhead as in the Mars Pattern or a highly unstable Macro Plasma Warhead in Stygies and Ryza Patterns. When detonated, Plasma Shells unleash an explosion the size of a small planet that burns with the ferocity of a small star for a fraction of a solar second.

Nova Cannon-Equipped Warship Classes

Navis Imperialis

The Navis Imperialis uses Nova Cannon technology on battleships, battlecruisers, and cruisers, including the following classes of vessels:

Victory-class Battleship - The Victory-class is similar to the Retribution-class Battleship, except that it has lance batteries and a Nova Cannon in place of macrocannons and torpedo launchers.

Apocalypse-class Battleship - The Apocalypse-class Battleship uses its Nova Cannon as it advances on the enemy which is capable of destroying entire squadrons of escorts in one shot.

Mars-class Battlecruiser - Equipped with weapon batteries, lances, attack craft, and usually a Jovian Pattern Nova Cannon, the Mars-class Battlecruiser can engage the enemy out of range of their main weapons or move in closer to make lightning raids.

Mercury-class Battlecruiser - The Mercury-class Battlecruiser often uses the Jovian Pattern Nova Cannon.

Dominator-class Cruiser - Built using a Lunar-class Cruiser hull and equipped with the Jovian or Stygies Pattern Nova Cannon, the Dominator-class Cruiser uses its Nova Cannon to bombard the enemy at extreme ranges as it advances, before unleashing its weapon batteries to destroy what's left of the target at brutally close ranges.

Adeptus Mechanicus

The Adeptus Mechanicus makes somewhat different use of the Nova Cannon technology within its own fleet, the Basilikon Astra, than the strategists of the Navis Imperialis. 

Other than the battleship-equivalent Ark Mechanicus, the Tech-priests prefer to mount the Nova Cannon on select vessels of their line cruiser classes, including the following types of vessels.

Ark Mechanicus

Tyrant-class Cruiser

Gothic-class Cruiser

Dictator-class Cruiser

Lunar-class Cruiser

Adeptus Astartes

Although the Adeptus Astartes do not normally equip their starships with Nova Cannons, there are some rare exceptions. In 544.M32 the battle barge Alcazar Remembered of the Fists Exemplar Chapter underwent a retrofit that saw the ship installed with a Nova Cannon. When fired, the Nova Cannon's need for immense amounts of energy caused a brief power failure throughout the vessel.

Other Platforms

In addition to starships, Nova Cannons were also mounted on Superorbital Plates and the walls of the Imperial Palace during the Horus Heresy. 

There are also smaller, ground-based versions of the Nova Cannon. One such weapon is mounted on the Ordinatus Oberon, also known as the Ordinatus Armageddon, on the Hive World of Armageddon.
