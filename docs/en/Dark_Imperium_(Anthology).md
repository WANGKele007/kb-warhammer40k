Dark Imperium is an anthology of short stories published in 2001 and edited by Marc Gascoigne and Andy Jones. 

Synopsis

In the war-torn 41st millennium, Humanity stands on the shores of damnation. Its only saviour is the immortal God-Emperor and the massed armies of the Imperium, in this searing anthology of Warhammer 40,000 stories torn from the pages of Inferno! magazine. 

The anthology contains the following short stories:
Apothecary's Honour by Simon Jowett
Daemonblood by Ben Counter
Nightmare by Gav Thorpe
The Lives of Ferag Lion-Wolf by Barrington J. Bailey
Small Cogs by Neil Rutledge
Angels by Robert Earl
Hellbreak by Ben Counter
Battle of the Archaeosaurs by Barrington J. Bayley
Know Thine Enemy by Gav Thorpe
The Wrath of Khârn by William King
Ancient History by Andy Chambers
Snares & Delusions by Matthew Farrer
Hive Fleet Horror by Barrington J. Bayley
