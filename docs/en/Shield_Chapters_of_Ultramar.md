The Shield Chapters of Ultramar were nine Chapters of the Adeptus Astartes, all successors of the Ultramarines Legion, charged by Lord Commander of the Imperium and Imperial Regent Roboute Guilliman with permanently defending and expanding the Realm of Ultramar to its pre-Horus Heresy size of five hundred worlds in the Era Indomitus.

Each Shield Chapter was granted full command and political control of a world within Ultramar. This planet served as their base of operations and area of responsibility in defending that portion of the realm. The Ultramarines are considered the tenth member of the Shield Chapters of Ultramar and serve as the leader of the group and the coordinator of the Shield Chapters' actions in the defence and recent expansion of Ultramar to its pre-Horus Heresy size of five hundred worlds.

Source Notes

In Chapter 1 of the novel Dark Imperium: Godblight Tetrarch Decimus Felix explained that each member of his Chosen of Vespator Honour Guard came from one of the ten Shield Chapters of Ultramar.

In Chapter 5 of the novel Belisarius Cawl: The Great Work, Felix listed the names of each of his Chosen of Vespator and to which Chapter they originally belonged.

In Chapter 20 of the 2021 revised edition of the novel Dark Imperium Roboute Guilliman named several of the worlds in Ultramar that the Shield Chapters were granted to serve as their bases of operation.
