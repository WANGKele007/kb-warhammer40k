The Battle of the Caliban System was a confrontation between the Dark Angels and several of their allied Unforgiven Chapters including the Consecrators, the Knights of the Crimson Order, the Angels of Absolution, the Repentant Brotherhood, the Angels of Redemption and the Angels of Vengeance and a unified Traitor fleet combining a Death Guard Plague Fleet and a warfleet led by the Fallen in the Caliban System. 

The stage for this battle was set when the Fallen Cypher turned himself in to the Inner Circle of the Dark Angels. He revealed the intent of the Fallen Angel Merir Astelan and Typhus of the Death Guard to use the ancient Tuchulcha Engine and its brother engines to create a time rift in the Caliban System and transport the Fallen from ten thousand standard years previous to the 41st Millennium. The Fallen would then combine with Typhus' own forces in combination with recently captured Loyalist gene-seed to forge a new Space Marine Legion known as the "Death Angels". 

Horrified by this notion and after being convinced by the Watchers in the Dark to set aside his grave reservations at such an alliance, the Dark Angels' Supreme Grand Master Azrael agreed to work with Cypher to foil the Traitors' plans. The entire Dark Angels Chapter aboard The Rock eventually arrived at the Caliban System just as the Plague Fleet of Typhus supported by other Traitor ships broke from the Warp. A massive void battle broke out between the two fleets, and the Traitor forces initiated a boarding operation against The Rock itself. 

Led by the battleship Terminus Est, the Chaos fleet was large but unfocused, as even Typhus was more concerned about overseeing the ritual to bring the Fallen forward in time than he was on directing the battle. The Traitors were able to open the Warp rift, and it seemed that their plan to rewrite history and doom the future would succeed. However Azrael was eventually able to order the self-aware Tuchulcha Engine to scatter all those near the time portal across the galaxy through the Warp, inadvertently including the Fallen on the other side of the portal ten thousand standard years earlier during the Fall of Caliban. 

Meanwhile, a second fleet of warships drawn from the fleets of the Unforgiven arrived in the Caliban System to the rear of the remnants of the Chaos armada, forcing its vessels to retreat. The Warp breach created by the Chaos forces was finally closed by the Ravenwing Black Knight Annael who took control of a Ravenwing Dark Talon to fly into the Warp rift and used his Rift Cannon to close the breach at the cost of his own life. While the Dark Angels were victorious and prevented a major victory by the Fallen, a third of the Chapter was wounded or slain in the battle.
