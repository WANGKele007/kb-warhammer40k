Delvegard is a Hold World of the Ghulo Industrial Complex of the Leagues of Votann located in the galactic core. It is an important world for the Ghulo, and sits at the heart of a rich centre of trade routes and mining centres that serve the needs of its commerce-minded league.

Codex: Leagues of Votann (9th Edition), pp. 22, 31
