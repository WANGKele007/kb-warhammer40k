A Plasma Projector is a type of large Imperial plasma weapon deployed on Imperial warships capable of firing massive bolts of superheated plasma at enemy vessels during ship-to-ship fleet actions.

Proteus Plasma Projector

A Proteus Plasma Projector is a miniaturised, man-portable version of a Plasma Projector of which two are mounted on the Telemon Caestus of the Adeptus Custodes Telemon Heavy Dreadnoughts.
