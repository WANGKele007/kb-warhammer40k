The Shadow Haunters is a Loyalist Space Marine Chapter of unknown Founding that is a successor of the Raven Guard.

A strike force of the Chapter participated in the pacification of the Fenris System in 999.M41 as part of the Imperial Crusade fleet led by Supreme Grand Master Azrael of the Dark Angels, when Fenris was caught up in a Daemonic incursion led by the Thousand Sons Traitor Legion.

Notable Shadow Haunters

Captain Slyth - Slyth is a captain of the Shadow Haunters.

Sergeant Arro - Arro is a Scout Marine sergeant of the Shadow Haunters' 10th Company.

Chapter Appearance

Chapter Colours

The Shadow Haunters' Chapter colours are grey.
