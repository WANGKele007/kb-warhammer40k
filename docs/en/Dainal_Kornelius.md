Dainal Kornelius is a veteran Ultramarian astropath who serves as the Ultramarines 2nd Company's primary communications link with the rest of Ultramar and the galaxy beyond. Kornelius is over 100 standard years old.

Kornelius was chosen to join the unique Command Squad of Captain Demetrian Titus of the 2nd Company known as the "Wardens of Ultramar". The squad is comprised of both mortals and Astartes. Kornelius is expected to lend his psychic talents to Titus' task of restoring the borders of the Realm of Ultramar to their pre-Horus Heresy size of five hundred worlds.

Having served as an astropath in the Ultramarines' fortress-monastery on Macragge for over a standard century before coming under the command of Captain Titus, Dainal has developed a mastery over his order's unique psychic skills that is second to none. He is able to send and receive interstellar messages with the utmost clarity and never shies from danger when the tides of the Warp are rougher than usual -- which is great courage indeed, given how a spontaneous head explosion is one of the kindest outcomes one could expect from a mishap with the power of the Immaterium.

It will come as no surprise that this constant proximity to horrible death has given Dainal something of an abrasive personality, and there are few even among his own kind who can stand to be around him, despite the undeniable value of his skills. Luckily, Titus has had to put up with more than his fair share of uncooperative grumblers, and can forge past the astropath's prickly exterior when the defence of Ultramar requires the very best of his Chapter's personnel, Astartes or mortal alike.
