A HYLas Rotary Cannon, more formally known as the High Yield Laser Rotary Cannon, is a powerful, vehicle-mounted Laser Weapon often deployed as a turret weapon on the vehicles of the Leagues of Votann, such as the Magna-Coil Grav Bike that is a favoured transport of Hernkyn Pioneers. The weapon possesses an extremely long range and a high rate of laser fire due to its revolving, gatling gun-like rotary laser barrels which provide excellent stopping power.

However, Kin weapons like the HYLas Rotary Cannon are superior in almost every respect to those patterns of Laser Weapons wielded by the agents and military of the Imperium, since the Kin employ superlative materials and methods in their construction and have no fear of further innovating on an ancient STC design. As a result, the HYLas Beam Cannon and its variants often have longer ranges and more stopping power than any of their Imperial equivalents.
