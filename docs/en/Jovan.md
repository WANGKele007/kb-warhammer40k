Jovan is a former Cardinal of the Ecclesiarchy within the Jericho Reach who has become a Heretic and an apostate. He styles himself the "High Cardinal" and "Lord of the Righteous Lost," a heretical cult of the Imperial Creed.

In the Jericho Reach he is thought to be leading his subversive and insurgent body of cultists on several Fortress Worlds within the Achilus Crusade's Acheros Salient.

Recent intelligence by the Deathwatch suggests that Jovan's followers have turned a number of high-ranking Astra Militarum commanders to their beliefs, which have striking similarities to those of the Radical Horusian faction active within the Inquisition.
