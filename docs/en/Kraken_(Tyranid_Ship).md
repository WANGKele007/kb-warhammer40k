A Kraken is an escort-analogue bio-ship found in Tyranid hive fleets. 

Kraken, which come in several different variants based on the starship-sized biomorphs they are grown with, act as scout vessels for a hive fleet and escort vessels for the much more massive Tyranid Hive Ships. 

The species is named after Hive Fleet Kraken, the hive fleet in which their existence was first encountered and documented by the Imperium of Man.

Kraken are often encountered in giant swarms accompanying Hive Ships, and Hive Ships have been known to give birth to more Kraken when engaged in battle. 

Kraken pose no direct threat to planets as this species of bio-ship is entirely space born. However they will viciously attack space stations, shuttles, military vessels, and other spacecraft that cross their path.
