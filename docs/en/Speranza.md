The Speranza is an Ark Mechanicus of the Adeptus Mechanicus that served as the flagship and mobile forge of Archmagos Lexell Kotov's Explorator fleet which travelled into the region of space known as the Halo Zone during the Kotov Crusade of the 41st Millennium. Its mission was to find the Breath of the Gods, known to the Craftworld Aeldari as the Caoineag. This was an ancient C'tan device of incredible power, capable of syphoning the energies of stars simultaneously in the past, present and future, originally providing the C'tan with an essentially unlimited source of power. The technology was also capable of creating new solar systems through its manipulation of time. 

However, the relic's temporal capabilities were extremely dangerous and if used improperly could collapse the entire timeline, potentially destroying all existence. The artefact had originally been sought by an Adeptus Mechanicus expedition led by the Explorator Vettius Telok, but his fleet had been declared lost. Archmagos Kotov was determined to follow in his footsteps and recover the Breath of the Gods, hoping that such an extraordinary technological find would recover his own fortunes in the wake of a myriad of mishaps.

The size of a continent, the Speranza has a gravitational field equivalent in strength to that of an unstable moon. It also has an industrial capacity comparable to that of an entire Imperial hive city with kilometre upon kilometre of manufactoria, refineries, crackling plasma reactors, laboratories, test ranges, chemical vats and gene-bays. Its engines are larger in mass then most starships and it has hundreds of Gellar Field and void shield generators, each strong enough to protect a whole frigate by themself. 

The Speranza also contains exotic weaponry dating to the Age of Technology that included graviton-based Beam Weapons, Chronometric Cannons, Anti-matter Projectors, and Hypometric Weapons as well as a complete and still updating STC database containing all the knowledge of the Men of Gold and their ancient ancestors. The vessel possessed an ancient Machine Spirit (artificial general intelligence) that was actually a forbidden, self-aware Silica Animus far more advanced than any others possessed by the Imperium; a tech-priest who linked with the vessel's Machine Spirit risked their mind being absorbed by the thinking machine.

History

The Speranza was first discovered by the Adeptus Mechanicus on the Forge World Palomar by accident. It was in fact a half-completed Human vessel dating back to the Age of Technology. Led by Archmagos Lexell Kotov the Adeptus Mechanicus worked to complete the vessel's construction. When the starship was finished and its Machine Spirit (an actual forbidden artificial general intelligence or "Silica Animus") absorbed the other Machine Spirts present on Palomar, becoming a gestalt entity whose birth scream resulted in the destruction of the surface of Palomar in a radioactive assault. 

The Speranza was later used as Kotov's flagship for his expedition into the Halo Zone with Kotov's second, Fabricator-Locum Tarkis Blaylock, serving as the vessel's master, accompanied by a complement of Skitarii and the Legio Sirius Titan Legion. Before arriving at the abandoned Mechanicus research station of Valette Manifold, a devastating training accident between the Titans occurred on the Speranza. 

During the Kotov Crusade to discover the remains of Vettius Telok's original expedition to claim the Breath of the Gods, the fleet was attacked by Eldar forces from Craftworld Biel-Tan under the command of Farseer Bielanna Faerelle who had foreseen that the expedition could bring about disaster for the galaxy if it proved successful. 

In response to the xenos attack, Archmagos Kotov linked with the Speranza's Machine Spirit. Through the link, Kotov discovered that the ship contained a complete and still updating STC database containing all the knowledge of the Men of Gold and their ancestors. This included but was not limited to technological echoes of machines surely predating the Imperium, power generation capable of using galactic background radiation to accelerate starships beyond light speed without the need for the Warp, planet-cracking weapons, and event horizon machines capable of consuming entire star systems within artificially-generated black holes.

Kotov realised that the data contained within the Speranza was represented the ultimate goal of the Mechanicus' Quest for Knowledge and theorised that all Ark Mechanicus ships contained such a database, but that the Priesthood of Mars had been unable to access their files. Unfortunately upon disconnecting from the Speranza's Machine Spirit Kotov forgot all he had learned while linked to it.

The Speranza's Machine Spirt responded to the archmagos' request for help by unleashing its exotic weaponry on the Eldar forces. First a a great gun barrel built into the vessel's superstructure rose into place on heavy-duty rails. The weapon system then combined streams of antimatter and graviton pumps in a reactor powered by dark matter. Targeting the Eldar vessel Starblade without any command from the bridge, the weapon released a pulse of dark energy at lightspeed which the nimble Starblade dodged. The dark energy pulse coalesced a hundred kilometres off the Eldar warship's stern and unleashed a miniature black hole that absorbed all matter and light within its reach.

While the highly-maneuverable Starblade was able to avoid this fate, its solar sail and mast were destroyed by the weapon's secondary attack, which utilised waves of chronometric energy to shift its target a nanosecond into the past, forcing identical neutrons into the same quantum space. As this was an untenable state of being for matter on a fundamental level, all objects caught within the blast detonated. The loss of its sail sent the Starblade reeling and disabled its Holo-Field, allowing the captains of the Mechanicus fleet to fire salvo after salvo of torpedoes, destroying it. 

Later, aboard the Speranza, the tech-priest Galatea, who had been rescued from the Valette Manifold, was a former subordinate of Vettius Telok and had previously dabbled in tech-heresy, betrayed Kotov by absorbing the mind of Tech-priest Linya Tychon. He then sought to meet with Telok, who was still alive and had grown quite insane, determined to use the Breath of the Gods to return to the Sol System, scour Terra and Mars clean of all life by siphoning away the power of the sun and declare himself the new emperor. Galatea was in fact just an extension of Telok's whose mission had always been to seize control of the Speranza after he was "rescued" from the Valette Manifold. 

As Telok and his crystal constructs boarded the ship, Galatea sought to take control of its systems. Kotov and his other surviving allies were able to return to the Speranza were they engaged Telok in a psycho-techno battle within the cyberspace of the ship's systems. The Speranza's Machine Spirit ultimately sided with Kotov and rejected Telok's control, allowing Kotov to delete Telok's data-core. This caused the Breath of the Gods to tear itself once Telok was no longerable to control it through his data-core.

To ensure no one would ever rebuild the Breath of the Gods and again pose a threat to the entire space-time continuum, the Speranza fired all manner of arcane weaponry into the debris of the C'tan device. This included chronometric cannons, anti-matter projectors and hypometric weapons which possessed the power to delete entire regions of spacetime from existence.

In the aftermath, the Speranza was one of the few surviving Imperial vessels of the ill-fated Kotov Crusade and returned to Imperial space with the expedition's survivors.

Armament

In addition to hundreds of Gellar Field and void shield generators and the standard weaponry of an Ark Mechanicus vessel, the Speranza also posed several different archeotech weapon systems dating to the Age of Technology.

Graviton Beam Weapons - These weapons fired pulse of dark energy at light speed which coalesced into miniature black holes that absorbed all matter and energy within the effective diameter of their event horizon.

Chrono-weaponry - Also known as Chronometric Cannons, these weapons used waves of chronometric energy to send their target nanoseconds into the past, which forced identical neutrons into the same quantum space. This was an untenable state for any form of matter, and resulted in the catastrophic detonation of a target. Chrono-weaponry was used alongside the Speranza's Graviton Beam Weapons as part of a single weapons system.

Anti-matter projectors - Little is known about these weapons other than that they fired the same pulsing streams of purple-hued antimatter at their target used to power the reactor of the Graviton Beam Weapons.

Hypometric Weapons - These weapons used an unknown technology so powerful that they could extinguish whole regions of spacetime from existence.
