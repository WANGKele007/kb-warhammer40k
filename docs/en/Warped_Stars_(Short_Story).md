Warped Stars is a short story published in the Inquisition War series by Ian Watson. It is included in The Inquisition War (Omnibus) and Deathwing (Anthology). 

Synopsis

Jomi Jabal has an imaginary friend who helps him make sense of the universe. Except Jomi's friend isn't imaginary, and he's coming to visit very, very soon...
