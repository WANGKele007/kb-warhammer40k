The Legio Absolutium is a Loyalist Titan Legion of the Collegia Titanica.

Legion History

Notable Campaigns

War Zone Damocles (999.M41) - After the T'au drove the Planetary Defence Forces of Agrellan from their homeworld, Segmentum Command escalated operations in War Zone Damocles. Waves of reinforcements began a desperate rearguard action to slow the T'au advance. Meanwhile, a mighty armada of Imperial warships departed for Agrellan, bearing over one thousand regiments of Cadian, Catachan, Elysian, Tallarn and Cthonol Imperial Guardsmen, several full Battle Companies of Raven Guard and White Scars Space Marines, and the Titans of the Legio Absolutium. The hammer of Imperial retribution bore inexorably down upon the attacking T'au, its wielders determined to crush the upstart xenos once and for all during the Second Agrellan Campaign.
