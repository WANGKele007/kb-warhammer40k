The Eye of Damocles is a watch fortress of the Deathwatch Chapter located in the Damocles Gulf region of the Eastern Fringe of the galaxy.

The Eye of Damocles is a vast watch fortress that monitors the borders between Imperial space in the Lithesh Sector and that of the usurper T'au Empire. Its kill-teams specialise in vertical, airborne assault from attack craft. As a result, this watch fortress' Dominatus Kill-teams will often strike from Corvus Blackstars to turn one-sided firefights into bloody melees.
