The Emperor's Shadows is a Loyalist Space Marine Chapter of unknown Founding and genetic origin. 

Chapter Appearance

Chapter Colours

The Emperor's Shadows' power armour colour scheme is loosely based off of that of the Black Templars, with contrasting details in red and turquoise. 

Many Chapter historical references are subtly incorporated into the Chapter's squad iconography which allude to the ancient Japanese samurai of Old Earth.

Emperor's Shadow officers may wear fully red power armour and helmets that have been modified to present a fierce, Daemon-like face.

Chapter Badge

The Emperor's Shadows' Chapter badge is stenciled onto a white leather papyrus material -- a black bonsai tree silhouette with a red rising sun behind it, surrounded by vertical Mongolian script often misidentified as Japanese Kanji characters. This leather is strapped over a field of turquoise, secured by small cords.

Trivia

The Emperor's Shadows were originally created by Victoria Lamb using the colour scheme of the Black Templars as a starting point for the "Creating a Chapter" article in White Dwarf 299. The characters on the Chapter badge were officially identified as Japanese Kanji characters, but in fact Lamb used vertical Mongolian characters to provide more of a mystical "Far Eastern" feel. The characters are decorative only and do not actually spell out any message.
