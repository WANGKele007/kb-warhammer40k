The Flame Eagles is a Loyalist, Codex Astartes-compliant Space Marine Chapter. It is suspected that it is one of the rare Successor Chapters of the Raven Guard. Its exact Founding date is unknown. 

Chapter Appearance

Chapter Colours

The Flame Eagles primarily wear golden power armour. The Aquila or Imperialis on the chest plate is red. The red squad specialty symbol -- battleline, close support, fire support, Veteran or command -- is indicated on the right shoulder plate. 

A white High Gothic numeral is stenciled on the centre of the squad specialty symbol which indicates squad number. 

The colour of the shoulder plate trim indicates company number in accordance with the dictates of the Codex Astartes -- i.e. white (1st Company), yellow (2nd Company), red (3rd Company), green (4th Company), etc.

Chapter Badge

The Flame Eagles' Chapter badge is a red eagle with small flames emanating from its wings, which are displayed and inverted, centred on a field of yellow.
