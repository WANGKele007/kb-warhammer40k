The Samnokh Dynasty is a Necron Dynasty located in the southern reaches of the galactic core. One of their borders faces the Urani-Surtr Regulates, a league of the Leagues of Votann. The Samnokh Dynasty's phaeron is a single-minded Necron who appears intent upon claiming the Urani-Surtr Hold Worlds.
