The Riatov Ordnancers are artillery regiments of the Astra Militarum raised from the Riatov System in the Chalnath Expanse of the Segmentum Ultima. They fought in the defence of the Riatov System from the T'au Empire's Fifth Sphere Expansion and an uprising by a Genestealer Cult. They contributed a total of 28 artillery regiments to the defence of their home system.
