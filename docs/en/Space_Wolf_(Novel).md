Space Wolf by William King is the first volume in the Space Wolf series of novels and is collected in The Space Wolf Omnibus. 

Synopsis

In the grim future of Warhammer 40,000, the Space Marines of the Adeptus Astartes are Humanity's last hope. On the planet Fenris, young Ragnar Blackmane is chosen to be inducted into the noble yet savage Space Wolves Chapter. But with his ancient primal instincts unleashed by the implanting of the sacred Canis Helix, Ragnar must learn to control the beast within and fight for the greater good of the wolf pack.
