The SiNR Handbow is a type of projectile weapon used by the Kin warriors of the Leagues of Votann, particularly Hernkyn Yaegir Trackers.

These weapons fire specialised explosive bolts outfitted with advanced force field technology that suppresses the noise of their detonation. Their impacts result in a contained and intensified rupture of the target's internal organs that allows the wielder to eliminate their targets silently.
