Nephalor is the current Grand Master of the Fleet for the Dark Angels Chapter of Space Marines, and bears the title of "High Huntsman of the Void."

Nephalor is the overall commander of the Dark Angels Chapter fleet and seems to share these duties with the Master of the 4th Company, who bears the lesser title of Master of the Fleet.

The division of responsibility or difference between the positions is not clear.
