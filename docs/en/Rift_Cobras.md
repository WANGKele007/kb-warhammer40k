The Rift Cobras are a Loyalist Space Marine Chapter of the Ultima Founding composed entirely of Primaris Space Marines of an unknown genetic lineage.

Chapter History

Notable Campaigns

Psychic Awakening - Assault on the Kebban Sub-sector (Unknown Date.M42) - The Rift Cobras despatched 3 companies to deal with the greenskins of the Kebban Sub-sector during the Psychic Awakening. The Kebban Sub-sector is a region of Imperial space that was infested with Orks and a horde of these xenos began to emerge from the sub-sector to assault the surrounding Imperial worlds. The Inquisition discovered that this WAAAGH! was set to collide with a different Ork horde led by the Warboss Bakrash, enhancing the potential destructive power of both. To prevent this from happening, the Ordo Xenos tasked the Deathwatch with killing Bakrash, but the operation ended in failure. This caused Bakrash to lead his WAAAGH! into the Kebban Sub-sector and make war upon the Orks already rampaging within it. Fearing that a Warlord would soon emerge to unite the Orks, the Ordo Xenos immediately requested the aid of several companies from the Rift Cobras, Templars of the Covenant and the Iron Hounds Chapters. The Ordo now believes that the forces of these Chapters will successfully destroy the Orks of this region while the xenos are still focussed on combating each other.
Octarius War (Unknown Date.M42) - The Rift Cobras were among the Chapters that participated in the Octarius War against the Orks and the Tyranids of Hive Fleet Leviathan.

Chapter Appearance

Chapter Colours

The Rift Cobras wear black power armour with the exception of the helmet, elbow guards, vambraces (forearm armour) and gauntlets, which are light green. Both the shoulder pauldron trim and knee guards are dark green.

The Aquila or Imperialis worn upon their chest guard indicates company assignment in accordance with the dictates of the Codex Astartes -- i.e. 1st Company (white), 2nd Company (red), 3rd Company (yellow), 4th Company (green), etc.

The Chapter badge is worn on the left shoulder plate, and the white squad tactical specialty symbol -- battleline, close support, fire support, Veteran or command -- is displayed on the right pauldron.

A large, black Low Gothic numeral centred upon the squad specialty symbol indicates a battle-brother's squad assignment.

Chapter Badge

The Rift Cobras' Chapter badge is a white viper's head in profile, mouth open, centred on a field of black.
