A Titan Power Saw is a Titan-grade, melee Power Weapon in the form of a massive, Titan-sized, motorised buzz saw that is capable of ripping through the toughest armour plating of its Titan-scale opponents. The Titan Power Saw is a simple variant on the standard Titan-grade chainfist used by many Titan Orders. Its whirling, adamantium-tipped blades can slice through armour, weapons and power cables to cripple opposing Titans.
