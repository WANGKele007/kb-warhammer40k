The Tempest-class Strike Frigate is a specialised frigate produced in the Calixis Sector and surrounding sectors for the local battlefleets of the Navis Imperialis. 

It trades long-ranged firepower for heavy, short-ranged broadsides from weapons batteries designed to devastate enemies at "knife-fight" distances.

To get to those distances, Tempests have triple-armoured prows and boosted plasma drives, and often carry Assault Boats and larger complements of naval ratings than is strictly necessary for participation in boarding actions. 

These larger crew quarters and hanger bays have been found very useful for other, more commercial purposes as well when these vessels are acquired by Rogue Traders.

Notable Tempest-class Strike Frigates

Advent Imperatus - The Advent Imperatus is a Navis Imperialis Tempest-class Strike Frigate that became separated from its fleet during the Psychic Awakening. It was later found silently drifting by Imperial forces and a squadron of the 105th Falkenberg Wardrakes Regiment of the Astra Militarum was sent to investigate what happened to the frigate's crew. Led by Sergeant Gerrikan Orzan, the squadron found no trace of anyone and decided to separate into two groups in order to cover more ground. The sergeant took his guardsmen to the bridge while his sister Izzren Orzan led her guardsmen to the frigate's crew decks. Each found that the crew and and the ship's servitors had been stricken with a disease that left them unable to move. While the crew had managed to save some of their number by putting them into life-support systems on the crew decks, others fell where they stood and died of starvation. Sergeant Orzan's group of guardsmen used a data extractor on the frigate's cogitators to determine what happened and discovered that the Necrons had unleashed the disease. However, before the guardsmen could escape with this information, the Necrons appeared aboard the vessel and killed them all.

Highness Ser Armaduke - The Highness Ser Armaduke was a Tempest-class Strike Frigate that fought during the Sabbat Worlds Crusade. It was originally built to fight in the Khulan Wars and was considered old and past its prime once the Sabbat Worlds Crusade began.
