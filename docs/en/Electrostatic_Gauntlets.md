Electrostatic Gauntlets are the powerful electromagnetic weapons wielded by the Corpuscarii Electro-priests of the Adeptus Mechanicus. 

Corpuscarii wear metal apparatus around their wrists that channels tremendous electrostatic potential from dorsal generators. This can be discharged in a cascade of killing lightning.

Sources

Codex: Adeptus Mechanicus (7th Edition) (Digital Edition), "Weapons of Enlightenment"
