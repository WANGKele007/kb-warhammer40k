A Phosphex Incinerator Cannon was an Imperial Phosphex Weapon wielded by the troops of the Dark Angels Legion during the Great Crusade and the Horus Heresy. Phosphex Incinerators were a favoured weapon of the Dark Angels Dreadwing's Naufragia Terminators. The Phospex Incinerator Cannon fired phospex shells that were each capable of reducing entire enemy infantry squads to ash.
