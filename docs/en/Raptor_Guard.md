The Raptor Guard, also known as a Raptora Cadre, was an elite formation of the Oblivion Knights of the Sisters of Silence that existed during the Great Crusade and the Horus Heresy. Raptora often served as the honour guard for the highest-ranking sisters of their order, including the Knight-Commander.

Role

The Silent Sisters of the Raptora Cadre stand as the foremost warrior knights of the Chamber of Oblivion. Each of them is a sublime fighter of superlative skill and they often serve as the
personal bodyguard for the high command of their order, for which purpose they are issued with the finest weapons and armour the Silent Sisterhood can provide.

Incorruptible and possessed of an untarnished spirit, the Oblivion Knights of the Raptora Cadre are only ever deployed against the most dangerous of foes such as rogue alpha-level psykers or major Daemonic incursions, where they form the spearhead of the Silent Sisterhood's attack.

The Raptora Cadre is known to have formed the honour guard of Knight-Commander Jenetia Krole herself during the First Battle of Prospero and, though their order suffered greatly upon the battlefield of Tizca, it is certain that they were instrumental in the near destruction of Primarch Magnus the Red's traitorous Thousand Sons Legion.

Notable Raptora

Aphone - Aphone was an Oblivion Knight of the Sisters of Silence during the Horus Heresy. By the time of the Siege of Terra in 014.M31, Aphone was the overall commander of the Raptor Guard and served as Knight-Commander Jenetia Krole's second-in-command. When Krole went to assist in the defence of the Imperial Palace complex's Eternity Wall Spaceport from Traitor forces, she left Aphone behind despite her reluctance to be parted from her mistress. Krole expected that upon her death, Aphone would take up the reins as the new knight-commander of the Silent Sisterhood.
