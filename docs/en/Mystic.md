A Mystic is a minor Imperial Sanctioned Psyker, chosen for his or her unyielding willpower and utter reliability in the face of daemonic temptation. 

Though a mystic lacks the mental fortitude to summon the destructive fury wielded by other psykers, they can nonetheless conjure a steady psychic signal that can be broadcast through the Immaterium. 

Though far weaker, this signal is not dissimilar in nature to that projected by the Astronomican, and can be used as a psychic beacon for the Navigator of an Imperial starship carrying troops through the Warp to answer an Inquisitor's summons. 

Many Inquisitors maintain whole choirs of mystics on the Imperium's far-flung worlds, the better to coordinate the completion of their plans and reinforcement of their armies. So have the mystics proven crucial to many an Inquisitor's designs.
