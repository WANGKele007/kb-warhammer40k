The Legio Manus Flameaus is a Loyalist Titan Legion of the Collegia Titanica, one of the military arms of the Adeptus Mechanicus. 

There is very little information in Imperial records about this obscure Titan Legion, and it is unknown when it was founded and from what Forge World it hails.

Legion History

Notable Campaigns

Jarix V Campaign (Unknown Date) - The Legio Manus Flameaus is credited with taking part in this little-known Imperial campaign, where "Indomitus" Battle Group of the Legion's war engines battled against the Aeldari, destroying a number of that enigmatic race's graceful xenos Titans and -- presumably -- successfully driving them off world. Armed with an array of weaponry the Battle Group fired off its Vortex and Barrage missiles as soon as they could, damaging the Holo-Fields on some of the Phantom Titans. The Vortex field forced many of the Eldar grav vehicles to leave the safety of the ruins or be destroyed, denying them the cover they needed to make pop-up attacks. Once the Eldar were exposed, the Titans unleashed volleys from their other weapons. The Indomitus Ambitus was equipped to attack the Eldar Phantom Titans. The barrages from its Multiple Rocket Launcher were able to destroy their Holo-Field generators before it dealt killing blows with its Turbo-Laser Destructor and Gatling Blaster.
