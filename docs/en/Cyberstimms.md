Cyberstimms are neurological stimulants artificially produced by the cranial augmetic implants of the Leagues of Votann's Cthonian Beserks. Cthonian Beserks are workers of the Cthonian Mining Guilds of the Leagues who can also serve as powerful assault infantry and melee combatants in many Kin Oathbands. 

The courageous workers and part-time warriors of the Cthonian Mining Guilds represent some of the most heavily augmented Kin -- unreasonably brave souls who have enhanced the physical capabilities of their cloneskeins with cybernetic implants and upgrades, all the better to extract precious minerals from radiation-plagued nebulae, explosive asteroids, and fathomless ocean depths. 

Boosted by the cyberstimms that flood their neurological and metabolic systems from their implants, the Beserks can keep battling through life-or-death situations.
