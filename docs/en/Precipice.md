Precipice is the name of a ramshackle void station cobbled together from the hulls of wrecked starships that was located in the galactic west in the Segmentum Pacificus' own frontier Western Reaches. 

Precipice was constructed in the midst of a massive starship graveyard that extended across a million miles. 

Its purpose was to serve as a base of operations for various Human, Abhuman and xenos explorers seeking to claim the Seventh Blackstone Fortress that lay within the heart of the debris field.
