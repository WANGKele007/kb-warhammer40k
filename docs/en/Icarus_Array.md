An Icarus Array is an anti-aircraft weapon utilised exclusively by Onager Dunecrawlers of the Skitarii Legions of the Adeptus Mechanicus. When fired, an Icarus Array fills the sky with inescapable death. 

Comprised of twin-linked Icarus Autocannons with Skyscryer Lenses, a Gatling Rocket Launcher that fires whole salvos of flak, and an armour-piercing Missile Launcher, its combined firepower can shred entire squadrons of enemy flyers.
