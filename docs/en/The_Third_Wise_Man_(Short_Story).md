The Third Wise Man is a short story in the Iron Snakes series of novels by Dan Abnett and is written by Nik Vincent. The Third Wise Man was first published online during the Black Library's 2012 Holiday Advent Calendar, on day 13.

Synopsis

The senior officers of the Iron Snakes unite in judgement of one of their number. As his victories and failures are weighed up, his fate will be decided.
