The Pyremoat System is an Imperial star system located in the Segmentum Pacificus' Bastior Sub-sector. As the Fourth Tyrannic War raged during the Era Indomitus, the system's primary population centre, the Hive World Trygg, acted as a vital stronghold for the Imperium that protected the Pyremoat System from a Tyranid invasion by Hive Fleet Leviathan and its sub-fleets. 

Notable Worlds

The worlds of the Pyremoat System, in order of their distance from the primary star, include the following:

Blohtt (Scriptorum World)

Eleidee (Industrial Moon)

Graehl (Death World)

His Will (Imperial Star Fort)

Resthur (Death World)

Trygg (Hive World) - The primary population centre of the Pyremoat System, one of the hive cities of this world was infested by a Genestealer Cult. Ultramarines Captain Sevastus Acheran, his heroic subordinate Brother Meduras Chairon and a task force drawn from Acheran's 2nd Company sacrificed their own lives to eliminate the cult and end its threat to the world and the Pyremoat System. Demetrian Titus was subsequently promoted for the second time to serve as captain of the 2nd Company.
