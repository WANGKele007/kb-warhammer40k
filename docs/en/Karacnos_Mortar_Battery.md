A Karacnos Mortar Battery is an Imperial mortar battery that fires rad-phage shells and was usually deployed on the carapace of the massive Acastus Knight Asterius combat walker. 

The Karacnos was designed to saturate fortress ramparts with high-impact, rad-phage explosives and is a crucial component of the Asterius' long-ranged fortification-busting firepower.
