The Synopticon is an agency of the Adeptus Terra whose name indicates that it is dedicated to surveillance and intelligence analysis. The Synopticon acts as the primary signals intelligence and internal security surveillance agency for the Imperium.

The official who leads the Synopticon is known as the "lord constable" and is sometimes granted one of the three temporary seats among the High Lords of Terra.
