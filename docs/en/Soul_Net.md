A Soul Net is one of the Thorians' crude attempts to replicate the technology embodied within the Aeldari Spirit Stones. It is a latticework of psycho-conductive material that is designed to activate upon death to trap the escaping soul of the wearer. 

Unfortunately, it interferes with the psychic aura that surrounds the user, reducing him or her to a comatose state or even inducing death. This side effect has seen use as a weapon against daemons and psykers.
