A Plasma Eradicator is a type of heavy Imperial Plasma Weapon deployed by the Astraeus Super-Heavy Grav Tank favoured by the Primaris Space Marines. Plasma Eradicators are deployed as sponson weapons on Astraeus grav tanks and are often used to replace the Las-Rippers that are the standard anti-personnel weapons on that vehicle.
