The Goliath-class Star Fort is a type of Imperial star fort that was in use by the fleets of the Navis Imperialis in ca. 556.M32, some one hundred standard years after The Beheading, when Drakan Vangorich, the Grand Master of Assassins, who had become a tyrant and the de facto leader of the Senatorum Imperialis following a coup against them, was overthrown by an alliance of the Adeptus Astartes.

Notable Goliath-class Star Forts

High Wall — During the Fall of Vangorich in ca. 556.M32, the Goliath-class Star Fort High Wall had been positioned in Terran orbit directly above the Imperial Palace complex. It had been newly commissioned some one hundred standard years after The Beheading.
