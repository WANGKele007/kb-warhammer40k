This is a list of official Astra Militarum (Imperial Guard) regiments created by Games Workshop. 

It does not include any fan-created regiments, though it does include those Traitoris Militarum regiments considered Excommunicate Traitoris by the High Lords of Terra whether they serve the Dark Gods or not. 

This is not a complete list as not every Astra Militarum regiment in existence in the Imperium of Man is known.

Please Note: Fan-created regiments must be posted at the Warhammer 40,000 Homebrew Wiki and not on this page.

Numbered Regiments (0-9)

1st Combine Replenishment Battalion

The 1st Combine Replenishment Battalion is most likely an ad hoc regiment comprised of multiple badly mauled regiments that were merged into one battle-effective, combat-ready regiment. The 1st Combine Replenishment Battalion took part in the defence of Cadia and its surrounding systems during the 13th Black Crusade against the forces of Chaos, commanded by Abaddon the Despoiler in 999.M41.

3rd Devash

The 3rd Devash is a regiment of the Imperial Guard that hails from the Imperial world of Devash. The 3rd Devash were sent in support of the Sabbat Worlds Crusade, a massive Imperial Crusade fought over the course of more than 23 standard years between 755.M41 and 778.M41, to help retake the Sabbat Worlds Sector located at the rimward edge of the Segmentum Pacificus from the heretical forces of Chaos.

4th Grey Phantoms

The 4thGrey Phantoms is a regiment of the Imperial Guard raised on an unspecified world. In 999.M41, the Phantoms took part in the valiant defence of Cadia and its surrounding systems during the 13th Black Crusade, against the forces of Chaos, commanded by Abaddon the Despoiler.

5th Columnus

The 5th Columnus is a Traitor Imperial Guard regiment that fought with the forces of Chaos under the command of Abaddon the Despoiler during the 13th Black Crusade in 999.M41. Commanded by the demagogue Colonel Jobe, the 5th Columnus made a push towards the Belis Corona System, but had first to secure a jungle moon that was the sight of an Astropathic way station. Chapter Master Absolon led the Astartes Marines Exemplar Space Marine Chapter in an attempt to retake the way station, but his force was greatly outnumbered by the Traitor Guardsmen. Leading an attempted breakout, the majority of his company managed to escape, but the Chapter Master's Thunderhawk gunship was shot down, exploding as it failed to clear the jungle canopy.

6th Canadal

The 6th Canadal is an Imperial Guard regiment that hails from the Imperial world of Canadal. They are known to have participated in the Defence of Rogsburg, a city on the world of Tallarn III, during a massive Ork invasion.

7th Cartigian

The 7th Cartigian is an Imperial Guard regiment that hails from the Imperial world of Cartigia. They took part in an Imperial campaign on the world of Bath Sanctuary alongside the Catachan XVII "the Screaming Devils" Regiment.

8th Cadian

The 8th Cadian or Cadian VIIIth, known as "The Lord Castellan's Own," is amongst the most illustrious of the Cadian Shock Troops regiments that hail from the Fortress World of Cadia, located in the Segmentum Obscurus. They are famed throughout the Imperium for their skill and discipline, standing on the front line of Cadia's defence against the predations of the forces of Chaos that pour out of the Eye of Terror. The 8th Cadian serves as the personal regiment of Ursarkar E. Creed, the current Lord Castellan of Cadia.

9th Cardosian

The 9th Cardosian are a regiment of the Imperial Guard that hails from the Imperial world of Cardosia. The regiment was annihilated during Colonel Zurinev's aborted "Grand All-Conquering Raid." The remnants of the 9th Cardosian now a part of an Imperial garrison charged with guarding the Xenthorp System.

9th Regiment

The 9th Regiment, also known as "The Traitor 9th," is a Traitor Imperial Guard regiment that fought with the forces of Chaos under the command of Abaddon the Despoiler during the 13th Black Crusade in 999.M41.

13th Penal Legion

The 13th Penal Legion, also known as the "Last Chancers," is a special regiment composed of Imperial criminals and convicts. All the men and women of the Last Chancers are hand-picked by the fiercely uncompromising Colonel Schaeffer for the performance of suicide missions on behalf of the Imperium of Mankind throughout the galaxy. The convicts are given a "last chance" by Schaeffer to be forgiven of their crimes in the eyes of the Emperor of Mankind and this is achieved by receiving an Imperial pardon from the Colonel. These pardons are awarded only to those members of the regiment who survive a number of military operations determined by Schaeffer. Colonel Schaeffer personally pardons all those who die during an operation posthumously.

13th/5th Imperial Army

The 13th/5th Imperial Army, also known as "Hylgar's Hellraisers," was an Imperial Army formation according to obscure but extensive records. Although recorded in Imperial records as an Imperial Guard regiment of the late 41st Millennium active between 975.M41 and 988.M41, many of its elements are in fact highly archaic and more reminiscent of an Imperial Army regiment of the Great Crusade and Horus Heresy periods. What is known is that its regimental name designates it as the 13th Imperial Army of the 5th Quadrant, and that it was commanded by Julius Hylgar, a senior member of the Administratum as well as the holder of several other Imperial titles, such as "Cardinal of Kolarne" and "Grand Marnier Militaris." This latter title was one used by the Administratus Militarium, the Administratum's martial branch (a possible antecedent of the current Departmento Munitorum).

13th/23rd Tiger Lizards

The 13th/23rd, also known as the "Tiger Lizards," was a regiment of the Imperial Guard. They were known to have fought against the Tyranids under the regimental command of Lieutenant Colonel Uskra, who commented on his regiment's ability to attack in perfect coordination.

17th/21st Tiger Lizards

The 17th/21st, also known as the "Tiger Lizards," was an Imperial Army formation that existed as early as the 30th Millennium, possibly during the Unification Wars era. Their presence was recorded in the Codicium Arkathalor, a fragmentary record of the Imperial campaigns conducted in the Pandora Sector. The Tiger Lizards were recorded as one of at least two regiments made up of "split" formations, where two wholly individual, but badly mauled regiments were merged into one battle-effective fighting regiment (in this case they were merged with the 61st/320th Orenian).

19th Grey Lancers

The 19th Grey Lancers is a regiment of the Imperial Guard raised on an unspecified world. In 999.M41, the Lancers took part in the valiant defence of Cadia and its surrounding systems during the 13th Black Crusade, against the forces of Chaos, commanded by Abaddon the Despoiler.

The 24th Grenadiers are an Imperial Guard regiment in the late 41st Millennium that also serves as the house guard of the Imperial nobleman, Lord Jerome A. Vicore.

43rd/65th Heidrun

The 43rd/65th Heidrun is a regiment of the Imperial Guard raised on the world of Heidrun. The regimental name designates it as the 43rd Imperial Army Regiment of the 65th Quadrant, indicating that this particular regiment's origins might lay in the bygone era of the Great Crusade. The 43rd/65th Heidrun took part in the valiant defence of Cadia in 999.M41, during the 13th Black Crusade against the forces of Chaos, commanded by Abaddon the Despoiler.

81st Malfi Light Infantry

The 81st Malfi Light Infantry hail from the fractious Hive World of Malfi in the Calixis Sector, making up the bulk of Imperial Guard forces stationed at Fort Saint Annard. Commanded by Major Barl Felthan, the 81st Malfi is a light, highly mobile infantry force specialising in skirmishing and urban combat.

85th Vendoland

The 85th Vendoland is an Imperial Guard regiment raised from the Imperial world of Vendoland, located in Sub-sector Aurelia during the events surrounding the invasion of a Tyranid splinter Hive Fleet and an incusion by the forces of Chaos aimed at destroying the Blood Ravens Space Marine Chapter. The 85th is based on the sub-sector capital Hive World Meridian, where they often have to put down food riots. The 85th Vendoland is never seen deploying tanks in support of its units, but the men of the regiment are hardy and the 85th has many Storm Trooper companies, who assisted the Blood Ravens during the battles at Angel Gate and Typhon. The 85th Vendoland emphasises use of the bayonet in close-quarters fighting, and the troops of this regiment are equipped in much the same way as regiments of the Cadian Shock Troops.

87th Bluebloods

The 87th "Bluebloods" was an Imperial Guard regiment that faced an unfortunate end. Fighting under the command of Captain Anton Mossman, the Bluebloods were extinguished to a man in in 967.M38, when they fought against the Dark Eldar Kabal of the Flayed Skull led by the cruel female Archon Kruellagh. The only survivor was Captain Mossman, who later died at the hands of the Inquisition while undergoing questioning.

122nd Borlean

Prior to the Gothic War in 139.M41, following the scout frigate Ascendance receiving a garbled plea for help from the ageing astropath of the Arx watch station, the 122nd Borlean Imperial Guard Regiment was sent to reinforce the Imperial Guard skeleton garrison on the planet. Arriving four solar months later, the 122nd found the Imperial garrison wiped out, and their investigation, described by Captain Thetis, failed to identify the attackers. The experienced Inquisitor Horst was sent to find those responsible.

Aegina Prestige

The Aegina Prestige are the elite heavy infantry regiments of the Imperial Guard raised up from the Imperial world of Aegina, located in the Medina Corridor, in the Medina Sub-sector. The Prestige regiments appear resplendent in their armour of wire and carbon-diamond. These resolute and fearless Guardsmen are fiercely methodical, utilising Mortars and heavy weapons fire-teams to funnel the enemy into "murder lanes" in which they march towards the enemy laying down a fan of Lascannon fire that forces the foe to scatter into concentrated groups even easier to pick off. The Aegina Prestige are notable for utilising a small number of Guardsmen who lay down enough sustained fire to convince the enemy that there are actually a higher number of Imperial troops holding the lines.

At the Techpriest's behest a courageous defence of the planet's spaceport ensured. Elements of the Agathon, Catachan, Mordian, Kanak and Molov regiments die to buy enough time for the Adeptus Mechanicus to flee with the invaluable technological relic.

Arcadian Regiments

The Arcadian Regiments are the regiments of the Imperial Guard that hail from the Imperial Hive World of Arcadia. This world is the origin world of the Cult of the Spider; its arachnid symbol can often be seen tattooed on the faces of the Guardsmen that serve in its regiments. Due to its large population size, Arcadia has produced hundreds of regiments throughout the history of the Imperial Guard. Once an Arcadian regiment is disbanded or destroyed, a successor Arcadian regiment will often assume its number in order to keep alive the traditions and history of that regiment.

Archite Palatines

The Archite Palatines were an Imperial Army regiment that served during the Great Crusade in the late 30th and early 31st Millennia. The Palatines served with the 28th Expeditionary Fleet of the Emperor's Children Space Marine Legion, commanded by Primarch Fulgrim. When the Emperor's Children followed the Chaos-corrupted Warmaster Horus into rebellion against the Emperor, the Archite Palatines willingly followed their Space Marine overlords. They are known to have taken part in numerous notable campaigns, including the destruction of the xenos Laer and the infamous Drop Site Massacre of Istvaan V.

They are specialists in fighting in the most inhospitable landscapes and conditions, such as the Mannheim Gap and the Blackfire Death Pits, as they did defending their world during the multiple Wars for Armageddon.

Armageddon Ork Hunters

The Armageddon Ork Hunters are a specialised unit of Imperial Guard soldiers formed in the wake of the Third War for Armageddon in 998.M41. They were created to conduct xenocidal campaigns throughout the equatorial jungles of the Hive World of Armageddon in an attempt to rid them of the Ork presence that had taken root there. Specialising in guerrilla tactics and jungle warfare, the Ork Hunters fight their feral enemy on their own terms, and have become extremely adept at their savage purpose. Their feral appearance and coarse manner has led to the Ork Hunters being regarded as little better than the Greenskin scum that they hunt and have earned them the enmity of many of the Imperial Guard regiments stationed on Armageddon.

Armageddon Steel Legion

The Armageddon Steel Legion are the regiments of the Imperial Guard that fight as mechanised infantry alongside Chimera armoured personnel carriers in defence of the strategically-located Imperial Hive World of Armageddon and its surrounding star systems. Armageddon, located in the Segmentum Solar, was a planet blighted and poisoned by millennia of heavy industrial output, though it was also a major manufacturing centre. In the late 41st Millennium, Armageddon became a constant battleground between the Imperial Guard's Steel Legion Regiments and the countless Ork hordes of the Warlord Ghazghkull Mag Uruk Thraka.

Arminiun Honus Squads

In 999.M41, a Demi-legion of these troops was deployed in support of the defence of Armageddon after the First Season of Fire, reinforcing the other Imperial forces locked in battle with the Orks in the aftermath of the Third War for Armageddon.

Asgardian Rangers

The Asgardian Rangers are the elite cavalry-based regiments of the Imperial Guard that specialise in scouting, camouflage and infiltration. Hailing from the Feudal World of Asgard, the Asgardian Rangers are often viewed as primitive, almost feral, and the Rangers' feudal background does little to alter this perception. It has been observed that the Asgardian Rangers have many advantages in their use throughout a warzone above and beyond the Scouts of the Space Marines. When facing a tactical situation that is very fluid, the Asgardians take advantage by utilising the sheer speed of their biologically enhanced mounts, bred for generations by their world's contingent of Tech-priests. The mounts' incredible endurance and tenacity, coupled with their engineered resistance to wound and toxins, can carry the Rangers through the worst of enemy-held territory as they follow armies, staying out of the reach of opposing scouts and outriders

The Avellornians performed admirably during the Siege of Cadia, and were the only non-Cadian regiment recommended for the "Ward of Cadia."

Cantican Colonial

The Cantican Colonial, also known as the CantiCol, are regiments of the Imperial Guard raised up from the Imperial world of Cantica, located in the Medina Corridor, in the Medina Sub-sector. Cantica was the defensive buttress of the Medina Corridor. Although a little-known regiment, the CantiCol were the Medina Corridor's primary troop formation. According to an Imperial census, 400,000 Guardsmen were garrisoned thinly across the Medina System alongside indigenous Planetary Defence Force elements. The CantiCol regiments had been raised in the 35th Millennium from the Cantican Loyalists of the Reclamation Wars. They had been one of the few warring dust tribes to aid the Imperium during the Reclamation and were thus the only regiment granted the right to defend the entire region. As a poorly equipped regiment, the CantiCol Guardsmen learned to compensate for equipment shortfalls. The Canticans did not possess many armoured vehicles, but the Cantican infantry squadrons were able to power safely across the battlefield in mostly ancient, obsolete tanks known as Siegfried Siege Tanks and Centaurs. The Siegefried tank was a modified variant of the Land Crawler utility vehicle commonly found by the billions all over the Imperium, while the Centaur is a small armoured utility vehicle of the Imperial Guard that is used in a wide variety of battlefield roles. The Canticans were also known to utilise elite Rough Rider mounted cavalry squadrons known as Cantican Horse Cavalry. Also called the Cantican Lancers, these Rough Riders were considered fighting men of no small repute. Much like the Kasrkin of Cadia or the Commandos of Kurass, the Lancers were an elite formation whose membership was limited and highly selective. As a poorly equipped force, Cantican Guardsmen were defined by the quality of their men rather than the superiority of their wargear, and the Lancers represented the apogee of this philosophy. Candidates could be drawn from any unit within the Cantican Colonial Regiments and thus selection was egalitarian in a rough, though uncompromising way. The Cantican Lancers are considered a hard bunch and the rank-and-file of the company, not the officers, decide who is permitted to wear the Lancer pin. The regular Cantican Guardsmen lacked the heavy armour prevalent amongst other Imperial Guard formations, and the Lancers bridged the gap between infantry and vehicle. While the primary role of the Cantican light horse was to escort the few precious tanks in the Cantican arsenal as mounted infantry, the Lancers were utilised as shock troops. Wearing chest bandoleers clustered with Fuse Bombs, the Lancers would charge upon their destriers headlong into enemy formations, leaving a trail of explosive Fuse Bombs in their wake.

Praetorian Guard

The Praetorian Guard are regiments of the Imperial Guard raised from the world of Praetoria. Praetoria is a heavily populated Hive World that lies in a star system close to the Imperial naval base at Bakka in the Segmentum Tempestus. The overpopulated hive cities of Praetoria are renowned for their squalor and degradation, and produce some of the toughest hive gangers on any of the Hive Worlds in the Imperium. This vast population works as little more than slave-labour in the huge manufactoria complexes on the world, producing the goods that have made the planet (and its rulers) amongst the richest in the Imperium of Man. Praetoria possesses a huge fleet of merchant craft that trade all over the Segmentum and beyond. Imperial Guard regiments raised on Praetoria are renowned for their iron discipline and bravery, even in the face of the most overwhelming odds. They detest slovenliness and disorder and even in the midst of the fiercest battle will ensure their ranks are correctly dressed in parade ground fashion and have their uniforms smartly fastened. The planetary lords of Praetoria hold it is only through fearsome training and draconian punishments for the most minor infringements that the hive-toughened inhabitants of their planet can be made to follow orders and do as they are told as part of a highly-regimented military unit. The Praetorian Guard are easily recognisable in their distinctive uniforms which typically consists of their trademark scarlet tunic with epaulettes and dark blue trousers with a vertical scarlet stripe on the outside leg. They are also easily recognised by their infamous Praetorian Pith Helmet, which is worn as headgear by both the common troops and officers alike.

The regiment is rather poorly equipped - a result of being far down the Departmento Munitorum's list of priorities for supply. This fact no doubt explains the large number of expert "scroungers" amongst their ranks. The Deepers are a light infantry regiment, much like Gaunt's Ghosts themselves, but without the same capacity for stealth. They suffered greatly in the defence of Vervunhive, where they fought in mustard drab fatigues and netted helmets.

Vitrian Dragoons

The Vitrian Dragoons are regiments of the Imperial Guard raised on the vast arid Desert World of Vitria. The Dragoons stem from a martial culture formed entirely around the Vitrian Honour Code, the Byhata, an ancient treatise on warfare. Every Vitrian Trooper carries his own copy of the Byhata on his person at all times, even into combat. Tradition states that the Byhata should be carried in a special pocket under the trooper's armour, directly over his heart. Every copy of the Byhata is fitted with a lock that is gene-coded to the owner, so that only he may open the book. In the Vitrian culture, it is a capital offence for anyone other than a Vitrian to gain access to or read the Byhata. The Dragoons are heavy infantry regiments and have a Storm Trooper-mentality, constantly firing their laser weapons on the highest setting, as it states in the Byhata that one should always strive to make both the first strike and the last one. While this means that each shot is more likely to penetrate armour and inflict greater tissue damage on the target, it also means they deplete their las-clips far more quickly than other regiments with a more measured approach. Vitrian Dragoons wear specially fitted armor formed from the metallic glass that their homeworld is famous for. This glass armour gives the Vitrian Dragoons extraordinary protection, especially against energy weapons. At the flip of a switch on the waist, the glass pieces of the armour will rotate in place to present the much darker underside, providing the Vitrians with effective nighttime camoflauge. Regiments of Vitrian Dragoons have fought in many warzones across the Imperium. Vitria sent multiple regiments, including the 2nd Vitrian (Mobile), 10th Vitrian (Armoured Brigade), and the 161st Vitrian (Heavy Support), in support of the Sabbat Worlds Crusade, a massive Imperial Crusade fought over the course of more than 23 standard years between 755.M41 and 778.M41, to help retake the Sabbat Worlds Sector located at the rimward edge of the Segmentum Pacificus from the heretical forces of Chaos.

Vostroyan Firstborn

The Vostroyan Firstborn are the regiments of the Imperial Guard that originate from the industrial Hive World of Vostroya located near the Halo Stars in the Segmentum Obscurus. The Vostroyan Regiments have served the Emperor for countless centuries, though in truth they fight to absolve themselves of a terrible shame incurred by their ancestors over ten millennia ago when, during the dark days of the Horus Heresy, Vostroya failed in its duty to the Emperor of Mankind. When the galaxy was consumed in war and anarchy, the Emperor commanded that worlds still loyal to the Imperium send forth fighting men to help defend it against the Warmaster Horus and his Traitor Legions. Vostroya refused, arguing that it could better serve the Imperium by continuing to devote all its efforts to the manufacture of weapons for the Emperor's armies, and that to give up so much of its populace to become warriors for the Imperial Army would render it incapable of producing those weapons in sufficient quantities. The Vostroyans' refusal to provide soldiers was met with uncommon mercy by the Imperium once the Heresy had ended, and the people of Vostroya have forever after sought to atone for their mistake, promising to provide the first-born son of every Vostroyan family to the service of the Imperial Guard.

Once drafted into the Astra Militarum this same armour is given regimental markings, though many of the lurid gang emblems of their former lives are retained on helmets and breastplates.
