The Claws of Ursus are a Loyalist Space Marine Chapter of unknown Founding and genetic origin. They are known to be best suited to operations in cold and arctic environments, which their power armour is coloured to match.

Chapter Appearance

Chapter Colours

The Claws of Ursus' power armour is painted white and blue.

Chapter Badge

The Claws of Ursus' Chapter badge is not listed in current Imperial records.

Trivia

The Claws of Ursus were created by a Games Workshop employee named Aidan Mee.
