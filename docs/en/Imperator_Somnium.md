The Imperator Somnium was a flagship utilised by the Emperor of Mankind during the latter years of the Great Crusade in the late 30th Millennium. It served as the command ship of the 1st Expeditionary Fleet, the Principia Imperialis, that the Emperor personally directed.

This immense vessel was a unique, golden battleship of unknown class, and was described as dwarfing even the largest Imperial battleships of that era. The fate of this vessel following the events of the Horus Heresy is unknown.

Trivia

The name Imperator Somnium roughly translates from Latin as "Emperor's Dream."
