The 7th Founding of Space Marine Chapters occurred at an unknown date at some point before the mid-34th Millennium and after the mid-32nd Millennium. 

Only a single Chapter is currently known to have been raised in this Founding. This is the Iron Shades.

Select 7th Founding Chapters

Iron Shades - The Iron Shades are a Loyalist Space Marine Chapter of the 7th Founding and unknown genetic origin. They recruit aspirants from the Death World of Shoba. The philosophy of the Iron Shades holds that perfection is achieved through conflict. Hailing from a perpetual world of darkness and preferring to work in the shadows with little fanfare, they nonetheless are unusually well-disposed towards average Imperial citizens compared to most Adeptus Astartes.
