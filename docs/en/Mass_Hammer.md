A Mass Hammer is a powerful melee weapon wielded by the military forces of the Leagues of Votann that takes the form of a massive, single-handed warhammer. It incorporates "mass driver" technology that allows its wielder to magnify the force of its impact to a colossal degree beyond the simple mass of the weapon itself.

A Mass Hammer is a favoured primary melee weapon for Leagues of Votann Einhyr Champions.
