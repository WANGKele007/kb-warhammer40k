A Clearance Incinerator is a massive, multi-chambered Imperial Heavy Flamer that is able to turn a landslide of rock to molten slurry. Though originally designed to be used to assist mining guild operations, the Clearance Incinerator has been repurposed as a potent weapon by the hybrids of the Genestealer Cults. It is usually deployed as a primary weapon on Goliath Rockgrinders.
