The Zeist Sector is a sector of the Imperium of Man located in the Ultima Segmentum which was the site of the Zeist Campaign fought by the forces of the Imperium against the Third Sphere Expansion of the T'au Empire in 303.999.M41.

Notable Worlds

Praetonis (Forge World)

Augura - Augura was a staging post for the T'au Empire's expansion into Imperial space in the Zeist Sector during the Third Sphere Expansion. By the end of the Zeist Campaign, forces from over a dozen Chapters of the Adeptus Astartes had fought against the T'au, forcing them to evacuate the planet. By that time, all of Augura's military and defensive infrastructure lay in ruins.
