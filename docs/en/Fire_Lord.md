The Fire Lord is a super-heavy assault aircraft and Daemon Engine dedicated to the service of Tzeentch, the Chaos God of change and deceit. 

The Fire Lord resembles a Daemonically-possessed aircraft in the shape of a bird of prey. The craft's jewel-encrusted hull is studded with Lascannons mounted on swiveling turret-mounts and the pinions of its large, majestic wings bear a pair of deadly Flame Cannons.

The aircraft is capable of tremendous speeds, allowing it to unleash devastating attack runs, opening fire with its Flame Cannons or Lascannons and dropping its deadly cargo of Firestorm Bombs on targets below. The Fire Lord is the larger twin to the Doom Wing, another Daemon Engine aircraft of Tzeentch. The Doom Wing operates as an interceptor and fighter craft.

The Fire Lord is protected and escorted while in flight by flocks of Doom Wings. The Fire Lord can be used as both an atmospheric fighter and as a void-based attack craft. These machines are carried into combat in the hangers of Chaos starships, such as the Devastation-class Cruiser.
