A Guildmaster is one of the high-ranking officials who leads the Guilds of the Leagues of Votann, the primary economic organisations of the civilian society of the Kin, which are similar in function to Imperial corporations. Every Guild is ruled over by its Guildmasters, who set the standards for Guild accreditation, the expected levels and quality of workmanship and the tithes their members levy. The most prominent Guildmasters also attend their Kindred's governing Hearthspake to give voices to the civilian Kin in their Guilds and may even serve on the Kindred's military Votannic Council in times of great crisis.
