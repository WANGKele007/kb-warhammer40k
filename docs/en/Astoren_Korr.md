Astoren Korr is a watch master of the Deathwatch Chapter who serves as the watch commander of Fort Obsidus, a watch fortress located in the Segmentum Solar.

Following the admittance of Primaris Marines into the Deathwatch following the Ultimaris Decree in the Era Indomitus, he has proven especially adept in the deployment of Repulsor battle tanks. This skill has led to the defeat of the Speed Kults of WAAAGH! Wazdakka, as well as several other recent Ork attacks in the region.
