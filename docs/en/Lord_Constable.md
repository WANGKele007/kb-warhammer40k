The lord constable is the Imperial official who is the leader of the Synopticon, an agency of the Adeptus Terra whose name indicates that it is dedicated to surveillance and intelligence analysis. The Synopticon acts as the primary signals intelligence and internal security surveillance agency for the Imperium. The lord constable is sometimes granted one of the three temporary seats among the High Lords of Terra.
