The Legio Proditor is a Traitor Titan Legion in the service of the Ruinous Powers. 

The Legio Proditor was once a Loyalist Titan Legion of the Collegia Titanica who spat on their oaths of fealty and instead pledged their undying loyalty to the ever-changing Chaos God Tzeentch. 

Legion History

Notable Campaigns

Invasion of the Stygius Sector (Unknown Date.M42) - In the wake of the destruction of the Fortress World of Cadia and the formation of the Great Rift, Tzeentch looked upon his fellow Dark Gods, and grew envious. When he saw Nurgle corrupt the whole Realm of Ultramar, he grew jealous. And so, he sought as ever to conspire against him and to seize systems for his own. So did the Architect of Fate put a million plans in motion. He would lay claim to the Stygius Sector, located in the Segmentum Obscurus, near the Eye of Terror first. The Legio Proditor were a part of this large Tzeentchian war host, charged by their manipulative god, with invading the Stygius Sector. Comprised of eleven different army groups, the Chaos forces wove a pattern of attacks and advances that was well-coordinated and seamless. Guided by mighty Sorcerers and oracles, it was not long before a new realm in realspace was carved out for Tzeentch.
