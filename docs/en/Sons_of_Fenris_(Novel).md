Sons of Fenris by Lee Lightner is the fifth volume in the Space Wolf series of novels and is collected in Space Wolf The Second Omnibus. 

Synopsis

Few Space Wolves are as famous as Ragnar Blackmane -- his exploits are legendary and his courage is limitless. When Ragnar and his Wolfblade battle-brothers are sent to investigate reports of Chaos attacks on the planet Hyades, he encounters his Chapter's most bitter rivals - the Dark Angel Space Marines. As old feuds come to the fore, both sides call in reinforcements and the situation quickly escalates out of control. Can these two ancient rivals call a truce and work together or will savage forces of Chaos exploit the infighting and complete their nefarious plans?
