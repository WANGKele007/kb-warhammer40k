The Malfactus is a semi-permanent Warp storm of the Milky Way Galaxy that raged during the Era Indomitus within the Imperium Nihilus portion of the Ultima Segmentum. The Chapter homeworlds of the Grey Giants, Farstalkers, Night Sentinels and Sons of Galathor Chapters of Space Marines all were located near this Warp storm.

Several of these worlds came under assault by enemy forces.
