The Ebon Talon is a potent Chaos relic of Tzeentch capable of destroying any foe, though as with most artefacts of Chaos, its use carries a potentially high personal cost. The Ebon Talon is a curved black spike that gives off a radiant light and is the length of a Space Marine's forearm. When the Ebon Talon is activated, by holding it and speaking the words of a sorcerous ritual, the relic begins to glow more brightly with arcane Warpflame. Its wielder can then unleash the artefact's destructive Warp energies by burning their foes from the inside out until they disintegrate into dust. 

Yet this boon comes at a great cost, as those who touch the Ebon Talon fall under the intelligent relic's sway and become enraged at others by the slightest provocation. This makes them all the more eager to use the Ebon Talon's powers on those who anger them, regardless of whether the new target is their closest comrade or their most hated enemy.

The Ebon Talon is currently in the possession of Huron Blackheart, the Chaos Lord of the piratical Red Corsairs warband of Heretic Astartes.

History

The Ebon Talon once resided on the former Daemon World of Kyren in the Maelstrom Warp rift, which was made entirely of crystals. Tzeentch, the Chaos God of change and sorcery, held the world in his grip and despite its unnatural makeup, Kyren was home to tribes of mutant Human Tzeentch-worshippers that both revered and coveted the Ebon Talon. They constantly warred with each other around the crystal mountain that held the relic, and sought to reach the cave it rested within. But any who tried to claim it were killed by the heavily mutated and corrupted guardians that protected the relic.

However, despite the Ebon Talon's power and the incessant warfare among Kyren's people to claim it, the existence of the relic seemed to be unknown outside of the Daemon World. This changed in the Era Indomitus of the 41st Millennium, when Verngar, a high-ranking Chaos Space Marine of the Red Corsairs warband sought to claim the relic in order to take command of the warband. Verngar, a former Loyalist Astartes of the Angels Encarmine exiled for insisting that the Red Thirst that afflicted his Chapter was not a genetic flaw but a manifestation of Chaos corruption, had become a potent Heretic Astartes and was considered a potential threat to the rule of the Chaos Lord Huron Blackheart over the Red Corsairs. 

The twin-headed Lord of Change Kairos Fateweaver had told Verngar of the existence and power of the Ebon Talon and its location within the Maelstrom when the Red Corsair held watch over the Blackstone Fortress that the warband had been given possession of by Abaddon the Despoiler before the Terran Crusade. Yet the massive star fort, for all the efforts of Verngar and his Red Corsairs, had remained inert, its powerful, planet-destroying weapon systems inaccessible. Fateweaver explained that with the power of the Ebon Talon the Red Corsairs would be able to fully activate the Blackstone Fortress and assert full control over its vast power. 

Verngar oversaw the temporary captivity of Primarch Roboute Guilliman after his capture by Fateweaver during the Terran Crusade, without the knowledge or consent of Blackheart himself. After Guilliman's escape, Verngar returned to the Red Corsairs' homeworld of New Badab in the Maelstrom with the captured Ultramarines flagship Macragge's Honour. Blackheart was highly suspicious of Verngar, believing that the warrior's triumph was an attempt to usurp his leadership of the warband, so he publicly criticised Verngar for allowing Guilliman to escape.

Yet Verngar reported all that he had learned about the Ebon Talon to Blackheart, and offered to find and claim the Ebon Talon to increase the Red Corsair's power. Blackheart suspected, though, that Verngar truly wished to claim the relic for himself, in order to raise his esteem among his fellow Red Corsairs. Verngar already had a sizable following and Blackheart believed that with the Ebon Talon in his possession, Verngar would attempt to take command of the warband and become its new lord.

In the end, Blackheart agreed to allow Verngar to search for the relic, but stated he would be accompanying the expedition as well in his own battle barge Spectre of Ruin. By doing so, Blackheart sought to claim the Ebon Talon first and put an end to Verngar's threat to his command of the Red Corsairs. When the Heretic Astartes reached Kyren, their two forces fought together against the mutant tribes, but it was Verngar who entered the crystal mountain and claimed the Ebon Talon first. He escaped with the relic, but it had been the power of the Ebon Talon and its connection to Tzeentch's Realm of the Sorcerer that had created and sustained Kyren's unnatural existence. Its removal from the planet caused the world's crystalline structure to shatter, and the Red Corsairs barely escaped before Kyren simply exploded into drifting hunks of crystal. 

After the capture of the Ebon Talon, Verngar's standing within the Red Corsairs grew considerably and he now planned the next step in his plan to gain command of the warband. With a group of his followers, Verngar confronted Blackheart and demanded to be given command of a large fleet, so he could join the warband's coming invasion of Chogoris, the homeworld of the White Scars Chapter. 

Blackheart refused, claiming that despite Verngar's success on Kyren, he had never taken full command of a war zone that stretched over an entire star system. Even some of Verngar's followers agreed with this logic, but Blackheart compromised by providing Verngar with another target -- the Invaders Chapter's former homeworld of Ogrys. It was being rebuilt for the Chapter by the Imperium and a Red Corsair assault upon the world would be an opportunity for Verngar to gain the necessary command experience. Blackheart also joined Verngar aboard his warship, the former Ultramarines battle barge Macragge's Honour, captured from Roboute Guilliman himself by Kairos Fateweaver during the Terran Crusade, to personally monitor his performance.

Blackheart promised Verngar that if his assault proved successful, then he would be allowed to join the invasion of Chogoris with command of the fleet he had demanded. Verngar agreed and the Red Corsairs' attack on Ogrys initially went well, until the Red Wolves, a sub-warband of the Red Corsairs comprised of former Space Wolves who had surrendered to Huron Blackheart after he successfully boarded the strike cruiser Wolf of Fenris, disobeyed Verngar's orders. 

Enraged by this insubordination in front of Blackheart, Verngar ordered the Warpsmith Turazan to bring the Ebon Talon to him. Blackheart had been sure that Verngar had not learned how to access the relic's power and that this was why he had not immediately used the relic to activate the warband's Blackstone Fortress. But thanks to the aid of Turazan and the sorcerer Garlon Souleater, Verngar proved able to unleash the Ebon Talon's fiery destruction. Verngar moved to loose the artefact's Warpflame upon the Red Wolves' Wolf of Fenris, but Blackheart intervened to prevent the unnecessary destruction of a potent asset of the Red Corsairs. 

Yet Verngar, in a rage further fuelled beyond all reason by the curse of the Ebon Talon, refused to relent even as Blackheart sensed that it was the Ebon Talon that was driving his subordinate's actions. As the Heretic Astartes continued to argue, the Wolf of Fenris suddenly attacked the Macragge's Honour, as the Red Wolves had divined Verngar's purpose and had no intention of meekly accepting their fate without a fight. 

The Red Wolves' attack distracted Verngar long enough that Blackheart struck his rival with a blast from the built-in flamer of his Tyrant's Claw, ready to openly move to eliminate his rival. Verngar recovered from the blast and prepared to unleash the power of the Ebon Talon at Blackheart. However, Blackheart's connection to his Daemonic Familiar, known as the Hamadrya, enabled him to psychically deflect the relic's power onto Turazan. As the unlucky Warpsmith's body was consumed from the inside out by the Warpflame of Tzeentch, Blackheart ended the fight by seriously wounding Verngar and then lopping off the hand with which he held the Ebon Talon, severing the link between Verngar and the relic. 

With the threat of Verngar to his leadership of the Red Corsairs brought to a conclusive end, Blackheart took the Ebon Talon and returned to his flagship, the Spectre of Ruin. He presented the Ebon Talon to the Red Corsairs' Master of the Forge Armanneus Valthex and ordered him to find a safe way to use the relic without suffering any of its ill effects. While Blackheart fully intended to make use of its power in the future, he had no desire to surrender all reason and logic to the relic's curse.
