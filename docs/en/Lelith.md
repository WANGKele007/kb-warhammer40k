The Lelith were an aggressive, intelligent xenos species that assaulted an entire Imperial sub-sector in the Halo Stars of the Segmentum Pacificus. At some time in the 41st Millennium, the Guardians of the Covenant Chapter of the Unforgiven engaged them in battle and despite heavy losses were able to drive this species back into the void and reclaim the sub-sector for Mankind. The exact date when this event occurred has been lost to Imperial records.
