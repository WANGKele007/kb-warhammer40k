House Winterscale is a powerful Rogue Trader dynasty that is one of the three largest Rogue Trader houses operating within the Koronus Expanse of the Segmentum Obscurus. It was founded by Sebastian Winterscale, who went on to establish Winterscale's Realm in the Koronus Expanse. The house still rules that region of the Expanse to this day. The current head of the dynasty is Calligos Winterscale.

Notable Members

Sebastian Winterscale - Sebastian Winterscale was the founder of House Winterscale.

Elbor Winterscale - Elbor Winterscale was the first to make contact with the Asuryani ghostship Whisper of Anaris, the only survivor of the Chaos-cursed craftworld Lu'Nasad.

Arturos Winterscale - Arturos Winterscale was Calligos Winterscale's predecessor as head of House Winterscale.

Calligos Winterscale - Calligos Winterscale is the current head of the dynasty, though some claim he seized the house's Warrant of Trade after Arturos Winterscale's death with no actual hereditary right to it.
