Nihl Zee is the name of a Vindicare Temple Assassin who serves as the protector of the Inquisitor Adrielle Quist of the Ordo Xenos.

Before joining Quist, Nihl left Hive Malfi for the training temples on Terra at so young an age that he remembers nothing else. 

He is a lithe, quiet man who speaks to everyone -- serf, Space Marine, and Inquisitor -- with eloquent but brief honesty. He does not seem particularly pleased to have been tithed to the Ordo Xenos, but speaks no ill of the Inquisitor or her cadre.
