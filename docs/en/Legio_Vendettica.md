The Legio Vendettica is an obscure Titan Legion of the Collegia Titanica. 

The only known reference to the Legio Vendettica dates back to the 31st Millennium and the dark days of the Horus Heresy. 

History

Notable Campaigns

Schism of Mars (007.M31) - When open civil war erupted all over the Red Planet as the civil war within the Mechanicum known as the Schism of Mars began, the Legio Vendettica had already pledged their support to the Fabricator-General Kelbor-Hal and his co-conspirators of the Dark Mechanicum. At the time of the Schism of Mars and the subsequent blockade of the Red Planet by the Imperial Fists, the Legio Vendettica maintained an extensive armoury depot in the eastern part of the Mare Hadriacum. This facility was used by the Traitors of the Dark Mechanicum who were aligned with the Traitor fabricator-general. It was raided by the Loyalist Mechanicum resistance force known as the Omnissian Faithful.
