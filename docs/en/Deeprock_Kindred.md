The Deeprock Kindred is a Kindred of the Greater Thurian League of the Leagues of Votann located in the galactic core. It was one of many Thurian Kindreds whose Hearthspakes voted to take up defensive positions and face the onslaught of the forces of Chaos pouring out of Warp Storm Örgvayr after it was created in northeastern Thurian space with the birth of the Great Rift.
