An Astartes Grenade Launcher is an Imperial Grenade Launcher utilised by the Chapters of the Adeptus Astartes. Astartes Scout Bikers can employ Astartes Grenade Launchers outfitted with adaptive targeters to compensate for the high speeds at which they hurl themselves at the enemy.

The Astartes Grenade Launcher is usually armed with Frag and Krak Grenades, depending on the biker's tactical need, though any pattern of Imperial grenade can be fired from the launcher.
