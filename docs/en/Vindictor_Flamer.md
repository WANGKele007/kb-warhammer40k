A Vindictor Flamer, also called a Zealot's Vindictor, is a two-handed Imperial melee weapon that combines a chainsword with a flamer. It is a favorite weapon used by those preachers of the Adeptus Ministorum who go into battle against the enemies of the Emperor alongside the various military forces of the Imperium, including the Adepta Sororitas, the Astra Militarum, the Inquisition and various Rogue Traders.
