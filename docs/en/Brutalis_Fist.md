A Brutalis Fist is a Dreadnought Close Combat Weapon employed by the melee-focused Brutalis Dreadnought. These Primaris Marine Dreadnoughts can be alternatively outfitted with Brutalis Fists rather than Brutalis Talons which hit with the force of a thunderbolt and come with built-in, wrist-mounted, twin-linked Bolt Rifles for added anti-infantry firepower.
