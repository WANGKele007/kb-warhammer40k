Glasscrete is a type of substance commonly used as a building material by the more technologically advanced worlds of the Imperium of Man. 

Though transparent like glass, glasscrete is far stronger and more resilient capable of safely containing even organisms as powerful as Purestrain Genestealers.

It is often used in constructions that require transparency but also a degree of protection, such as a form of bullet-proof glass.
