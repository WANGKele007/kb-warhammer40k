An Immaterium Surge Station is a type of Imperial space station that is capable of minimising the effects of a nearby active Warp storm on navigable voidship routes.

The technology employed in Immaterium Surge Stations to minimise Warp energies are older than the Imperium itself and can keep the tides of nearby Warp storms in check. If a surge station active in such an unstable region of space is sabotaged or destroyed, the Warp can breach the border with the Materium to spill forth and swallow nearby star systems.

Notable Immaterium Surge Stations

Choralis Immaterium Surge Station - Located in the Nachmund Gauntlet, one of the few stable realspace corridors that bridges the Great Rift between the Imperium Sanctus and the Imperium Nihilus in an attempt to keep the power of the rift at bay, the Choralis was sabotaged by the Thousand Sons during the Nachmund Rift War to drown the nearby world of Kherik III in the Warp.
