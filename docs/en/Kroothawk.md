The Kroothawk (Krootis accipiter), a raptor-like avian native to the Kroot homeworld of Pech, is thought by Imperial xenographers to be the original evolutionary ancestor of all Kroot. Trained Kroothawks are used by the military forces of the Kroot as a fast-hitting avian skirmisher. Kroothawks can fly, giving them an advantage over their grounded brethren, although they are more animal than humanoid. Kroothawks, like Kroothounds, can only participate in melee combat.

A whole family of Kroothawk species exist, and every species is now found on all the known Kroot homeworlds in the T'au Empire. They are powerful, fast-moving hunters that are also known to act as scavengers.

The Kroothawk is an animal sacred to the Kroot, perhaps because they recognise it as the progenitor of their species, and the Kroothawk Totem is a sacred fetish of Kroot Shapers, used during their ancestor worship rituals to provide the bearer with mystic foresight and wisdom, allowing them to alter their actions to produce a better outcome based on the vision of the future shown them by the totem spirit.
