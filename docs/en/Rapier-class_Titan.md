The Rapier-class Titan is a Titan combat walker that was most commonly used during the Great Crusade and Horus Heresy by both the Loyalist Titan Legions and the Traitor Titan Legions. It is a Scout Titan, and is the smallest known class of Imperial or Chaos Titan, being even lighter armed and swifter than a Warhound-class Scout Titan. 

During the final years of the Horus Heresy, it was noted that the Rapier was becoming an ever rarer sight among the orders of battle of the Titan Legions of both Traitors and Loyalists.

Notable Rapier-class Titan

Alacrity of Thought - The Alacrity of Thought was a Rapier-class Scout Titan of the Legio Defensor. It was destroyed in combat on Beta-Garmon III during the Battle of Beta-Garmon of the Horus Heresy.
