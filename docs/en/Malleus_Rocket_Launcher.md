A Malleus Rocket Launcher is a man-portable Field Ordnance Battery utilised by the Astra Militarum. Equipped with multi-launch rocket systems, the Malleus is designed to rain a hail of explosives and shrapnel down on a battlefield to devastate enemy formations.

Role

The Malleus Rocket Launcher is distinguished from other Astra Militarum artillery pieces through its twin, six-barreled rocket launchers, enabling the weapon to disgorge a dozen direct-fire rockets to saturate the battlefield, and then be quickly adjusted to fire again. The Malleus is one of the largest man-portable artillery pieces in the Astra Militarum's arsenal, giving it a high degree of flexibility and mobility, but it can also be towed to battlefields a great distance away by other vehicles.

The Malleus is most commonly operated in combat by a crew of three: a commander, a gunner and a loader. The commander, who also functions as a spotter, directs his launcher's fire to best rain destruction upon the enemy. The gunner makes appropriate adjustments so each shot strikes true, and the loader prepares the next volley. Additionally, guardsmen may sometimes be deployed as forward spotters for the launcher.
