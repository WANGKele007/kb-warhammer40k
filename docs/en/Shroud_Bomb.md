A Shroud Bomb was an explosive device used to conceal Space Marine movements by the Legiones Astartes of the Great Crusade and Horus Heresy. These bombs were canisters that when detonated unleashed a grey fog of smoke and a multi-spectrum electromagnetic charge that were intended to foil passive, active and visible light scanning devices.
