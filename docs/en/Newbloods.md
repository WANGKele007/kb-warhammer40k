Newbloods were a type of Space Marines rapidly created and trained during the Horus Heresy in order to reinforce the losses sustained by the Legiones Astartes with additional troops as attrition took hold over the course of the arduous campaigns of the galactic civil war. Where once the gene-forging of a new Space Marine had been a methodical, near-sacred process stretching a solar decade or more, the necessity of war birthed a grim innovation. From the gene-vaults and bio-fanes of both Loyalist and Traitor Space Marine Legions alike came this hastily-forged breed of Space Marine, tempered not in the crucible of their Legions' cultures and traditions but in the furnace of desperation. 

Newblood Legionaries were the product of accelerated gene-seed implantation programs developed in the civil war's most desperate theatres -- campaigns where entire Legions had been hollowed out by attrition, where reinforcement was no longer a matter of honour, but of survival. Utilising abbreviated training regimens, chemically-augmented indoctrination, and streamlined gene-seed implantation, the Newbloods were often made fully battle-ready in as little as four standard years -- a fraction of the time taken during the Great Crusade. Entire proto-Legionary classes would be birthed, trained, modified, and hurled into combat before their minds had fully internalised the totality of what it meant to be Astartes.

History

During the darkest days of the Horus Heresy, as entire worlds burned and the Legiones Astartes bled themselves dry in the Emperor's name or in rebellion against it, a desperate solution was forged in blood and haste -- the so-called "Newbloods". These were Astartes wrought not with the careful genetic artifice of the Great Crusade, but in the crucible of need: warriors created from Human adolescents in a fraction of the time, honed for battle with brutal expedience, and cast into the fire before their scars had time to heal. These warriors were rushed through both creation and training, given none of the immersion and cultivation that the Imperium had once lavished on its paramount living weapons. It was well-known among their brothers and other Imperial forces that their training was suboptimal. A few had even counselled against the process entirely, acutely aware of the consequences when a Space Marine entered service with a flawed background. 

Despite these drawbacks, Newbloods were still proud, the soul of their primarchs smouldering no less within their own blood than that of their properly trained brothers. They burned to fight, to demonstrate what they could do, both to themselves and to the established members of their Legion. Though viewed with suspicion or contempt by their elder brethren, the Newbloods were the grim answer to a galaxy set aflame -- shock-assault killers, forged in shattered bio-vaults and trained to die before ever knowing the stars. In them, the original ideals of the Space Marine Legions and the Great Crusade itself were all but abandoned -- replaced by the savage reality of a war that cared nothing for legacy, only for slaughter.

This process was not without great cost. Mortality rates during gene-seed implantation among adolescent aspirants were catastrophically high, even by the already grim standards of the Legions. Those who survived often emerged changed in more than just flesh. The hypno-indoctrination rituals of the Legiones Astartes, once painstaking and gradual, were now performed en masse via psycho-sonic immersion chambers and mind-scourge scripts that left lasting psychic scars. These warriors were not the paragon knights of the Emperor's original vision, nor the solemn Imperial templars of the earlier Legion days. They were a breed of engineered fury -- haunted, half-formed, and barely bound to reason.

Though the Newbloods lacked the refinement and cohesion of their elder brethren, none could question their ferocity. What they lacked in standard centuries of experience or meditative discipline, they made up for with sheer violence. Unclouded by remorse, and often driven by rage both chemical and psychic in nature, Newblood companies among the Space Marine Legions gained a reputation for brutality and shock assault tactics. In Loyalist Legions like the Iron Hands, White Scars, and Blood Angels, they were deployed in massed spearhead formations -- expendable hammers of gene-wrought steel. Among the Traitor Legions, particularly within the World Eaters and Word Bearers, they became the raw flesh of the Horus Heresy, molded into berserker cadres, Daemon-hosted line breakers, or sacrificial warriors in the Dark Apostles' blood-rites to honour the Dark Gods and gain their power.

Among their elder peers, Newbloods were treated with a strange mix of disdain and pity. Yet in the crucible of battle, many rose above their forged birth to etch their names into the war-torn scrolls of Heresy-era legend. The Siege of Terra would see entire Newblood battalions raised on multiple worlds, and even Terra itself -- fighting tooth and nail atop the ruined walls of the Imperial Palace. For them, there was no future beyond this war. They were not made for peace. They were not made to endure.

Few Newbloods would survive the Horus Heresy's end. Of those that did, most were quietly reabsorbed, disbanded, or disposed of in the dark centuries that followed. The Codex Astartes, penned by Ultramarines Primarch Roboute Guilliman in the Heresy's aftermath, outlawed the barbaric gene-seed cultivation and training acceleration techniques that birthed them. Yet, in forbidden vaults beneath Terra and the gene-labs of the more secretive Chapters of the Adeptus Astartes, records remain -- scars upon parchment and flesh alike -- telling of the warrior breed made for a war without mercy.

Sons of Horus Newborns

Amongst the ranks of the Sons of Horus Legion during the Horus Heresy, Newbloods were referred to as "Newborn". Created in vast and hurried batches, each cycle harvested tens of thousands of aspirants from conquered and Dark Compliant worlds across the Isstvan and Maeleum clusters. These brutal trials of transhuman alchemy lasted scarcely half a Terran year, during which thousands perished in the gene-laboratories and hypno-chambers of the XVIth Legion. What few survived the brutal accelerated gene-seed implantation process and the convulsions of accelerated hypno-indoctrination were cast in ceramite and war-chant, their names entered into the rolls of the Warmaster Horus' war hosts before they had even taken their first breath outside the birthing crypts. 

Bereft of a homeworld or shared cultural identity, these hastily wrought Legionaries clung to the battle-cant, Cthonian sigils and grim gang culture of their older brethren, painting their power armour in the same metallic sea-green hues and adorning themselves with mirror-coins and kill talismans. It was a hollow mimicry of their elder brethren, but a necessary one -- for the Newborn were outsiders even within their own Legion, resented by the veterans of the Ullanor Crusade and the early battles of the Heresy in the Isstvan System, who saw them as pale reflections of the true Sons of Horus. Trained not through drill or war-saga, but through neural induction and hypno-rigged memory implants, the Newborns' understanding of war was more instinct than experience, more reflex than honed skill.

The difference was fatal. On the blood-soaked battlefields of the Horus Heresy, where Loyalist Legions fought with the cold discipline of ancient campaigns, the Newborn were often found wanting. Their courage was undeniable, their fury unrestrained, but they lacked the refined cohesion and tactical cunning of true Legionary brotherhoods. Thrown into the crucible alongside their betters, the Newborn were used by their commanders as shock troops and sacrificial blades -- expendable assets in a war that had long since stopped caring for honour or lineage. Very few survived more than a handful of deployments. Fewer still rose in rank or reputation within the XVIth Legion. 

And yet, for all their shortcomings, the Newborn endured. Each one was a living indictment of the Warmaster's descent from the shining ideal of the Great Crusade to the ruthless calculus of betrayal and expedience. In their howling charges and short, brutal lives, the Newborn served as the Heresy's bitter truth made flesh: that in the end, all things sacred would be broken, and all that remained was the will to kill, or be killed.

Notable Newblood Recruiting Worlds

Cylor - Cylor was a world conquered by the Warmaster Horus Lupercal's Traitor Legion forces during the Horus Heresy. It then served as a source of aspirants for the Sons of Horus Legion, as they began to quickly increase their numbers with the Inductii known as Newborns.

Manhansu - Mahansu was a world similar to Cylor that was conquered by the Sons of Horus and from which large numbers of Inductii were raised to serve as Newborns during the Siege of Terra.

Neo-geddon - Neo-geddon was a world similar to Cylor that was conquered by the Sons of Horus and from which large numbers of Inductii were raised to serve as Newborns during the Siege of Terra.
