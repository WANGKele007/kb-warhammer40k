An Enmitic Annihilator is a double-barreled, heavy Enmitic Weapon often employed by Necron Skorpekh Lords. Enmitic Weapons are directed energy weapons that unleash pulses which cause the victim's atoms to violently repel each other. Enmitic Weapon ammunition is not a physical material or power source, but rather information. They cast quantum-commanded hekatic decrees written in the basal language of reality itself, which re-orders the molecules of their targets in such a way as to dissolve their atomic bonds.
