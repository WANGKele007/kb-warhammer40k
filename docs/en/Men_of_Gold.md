The Men of Gold, also called the Golden Races, are a mysterious sub-sect or sub-species of Humanity that guided the species during the spread of interstellar civilisation in some way from the Age of Terra up until the 21st Millennium during the Age of Technology. 

After that period, their influence over Human development waned and was taken by their own creations, the Human sect or sub-species they had created called the "Men of Stone" in later legends who were designed to rapidly advance Human exploration, colonisation and exploitation of interstellar space.

History

According to ancient, fragmentary sources predating the Age of Strife, the so-called "Men of Gold" appeared at some unknown point during the Age of Terra, joining the psychic Perpetual later known as the Emperor as He watched and nurtured Humanity's survival, growth and technological advancement. 

To watch and learn from Mankind, these "Golden Races" spread across the face of Old Earth, multiplying and establishing order and civilisation over the anarchy of nature. Though physically and emotionally superior to baseline Humans, the Men of Gold eventually created a new augmented faction of Humanity called the "Men of Stone" in the ancient legends for the express purpose of exploring and colonising the galaxy. 

In time, the Men of Stone supplanted the Men of Gold and by the 21st Millennium during the Age of Technology, their influence over Human society had waned. It is ultimately unknown what happened to the Men of Gold.
