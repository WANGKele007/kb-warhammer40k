A Balesword is a Daemon Weapon of Nurgle. These plague-infected blades are favoured melee weapons of the Heralds of Nurgle called Poxbringers and the Blightlord Terminators of the Death Guard Traitor Legion.

Sources

Codex Heretic Astartes - Death Guard (8th Edition), pp. 68-69, 81
Codex: Chaos Daemons (8th Edition), pg. 98
