House Ju'll was a formerly Loyalist Imperial Knight household that repudiated its oath of loyalty to the Imperium of Man during the Horus Heresy. There is little information on this formerly loyal Knightly House in official Imperial records or from what world they originally hailed from. All that is known is that they were allied to the traitorous Legio Mortis Titan Legion.
