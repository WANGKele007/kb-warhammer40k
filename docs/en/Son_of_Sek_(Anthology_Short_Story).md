Son of Sek is a short story published in the Gaunt's Ghosts series that was originally part of an anthology. Son of Sek was published as part of the Sabbat Worlds anthology in November 2014, and has yet to be released as a stand-alone e-book.

Synopsis

A newly christened warrior of the Sons of Sek prepares to take his final vows while a newly inducted Imperial Guardsman blunders his way through his first engagement, in an unexpectedly heroic manner.
