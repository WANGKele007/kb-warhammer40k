A Cluster Rocket System is a T'au weapon system consisting of a pair of massive rocket pods programmed to fire dozens of Drone-assisted warheads in optimised saturation patterns, maintaining a rate and density of fire sufficient to destroy entire mobs of Orks or swarms of Tyranid weapon beasts. 

The explosions it generates carpet a target zone, hurling bodies into the air and atomising screaming foes by the score. It is currently only deployed on the KV128 Stormsurge Ballistic Suit.
