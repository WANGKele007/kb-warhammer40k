The Legio Atrox is a Loyalist Titan Legion of the Collegia Titanica, one of the military arms of the Adeptus Mechanicus. It was based on the Forge World of Gulgorahd, the capital of the Gulgorahd Protectorate sector on the edge of the Ultima Segmentum.

The Legio Atrox fought during the Horus Heresy in the Thramas Crusade, where they aided the Loyalist forces in resisting the advance of the Night Lords Traitor Legion.
