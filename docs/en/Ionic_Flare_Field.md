An Ionic Flare Field is a less power-hungry variant of the directional Ion Shield used by larger Mechanicum-indentured Imperial Knight variants that can be deployed on the lighter Armiger Pattern Knights. The Ionic Flare Field projects a wider effect, ensconcing the entire war machine within lesser, but still formidable, protection from oncoming projectiles.
