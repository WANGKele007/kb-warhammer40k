Decimus is the "Prophet" of the Night Lords Traitor Legion, created from the gene-seed of Talos Valcoran and heir to the prophetic legacy of the "martyred" primarch Konrad Curze, and the commander of a Night Lords warband which was formerly the Night Lords' 10th Company, including its infamous 1st Claw. 

Decimus is known for the accuracy and strength of his precognitive visions and for foretelling the triumphant outcome of the 13th Black Crusade, predicting that Abaddon the Despoiler, Horus's greatest gene-son and heir as the Warmaster of Chaos, would triumph over the Imperium even as many of the Night Lords' own warband leaders would fall.

History

Decimus is a Space Marine of the Night Lords Legion, notable for manifesting the precognitive abilities long associated with the gene-seed of Primarch Konrad Curze, the VIII Legion's gene-father. Curze's foresight, originally described by the Emperor as a gift, persisted in a select number of his gene-sons after their primarch's death, and proved nearly as much of a curse to them as it had been to Curze.

Talos Valcoran, known as the "Soul Hunter", was one such bearer of this curse and came to be regarded as a prophetic figure among his gene-brothers. Following Valcoran's death, the Night Lords Apothecary Variel the Flayer recovered his gene-seed in accordance with Legion practice, ensuring the continuation of Curze's bloodline. From this largely uncorrupted gene-seed was eventually produced a new Night Lords warrior of the 41st Millennium, later known as Decimus.

Decimus may have been the child of two of Talos Valcoran's Human servants: Septimus, Talos Valcoran's former Legion serf, and Octavia, a Navigator enslaved by the Night Lords. According to surviving accounts, Talos had warned them that Variel would one day retrieve their child should he survive the events on Tsagualsa, a prediction that proved accurate.

Upon his induction into the Night Lords, Decimus began to suffer from precognitive visions similar to those experienced by his primarch and his direct gene-sire Talos Valcoran. He also came into possession of several relics tied to the legacy of the 1st Claw, including the power sword Aurum and the twin-linked boltgun of the Warsage Malcharion. His power armour incorporated traditional elements used by the deceased battle-brothers of 1st Claw: a ritualistically broken Aquila once worn by Yalcoran, a cloak of flayed skin that had been the pride and joy of Uzas, and a studded helm combining a skull, Talos's Soul Hunter rune, the ceremonial winged crest of Xarl, and the lightning-tear motifs of Cyrion.

Shortly before the 13th Black Crusade erupted, Decimus addressed a gathering of Night Lords warbands, a rare event for a Traitor Legion that rarely ever gathered together after the assassination of their primarch. He predicted the victory of Abaddon the Despoiler over the Imperium at Cadia, while predicting the deaths of several Night Lords warband leaders in the coming campaign, including Zar Tavik, Kalex, Darjyr and Toriel the White-Handed.
