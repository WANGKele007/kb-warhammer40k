The Marines Vigilant were a Space Marine Chapter of unknown origin and Founding.

It is, alongside the Sons of Gideon, one of the two Chapters that were exterminated by the Crimson Fists on orders of the Inquisition and the Adeptus Terra.

Chapter History

At some point in the Marines Vigilant's history, this troubled Chapter suddenly and inexplicably became unwilling to fight even xenos forces. It is believed their minds were altered in some way by exposure to an unknown, alien phenomenon.

For this reason, the Adeptus Terra and Ordo Malleus ordered the Crimson Fists to destroy the Marines Vigilant, an attack in which the Crimson Fists' Chapter Master Pedro Kantor took part.

Even as the Crimson Fists rained down death and destruction in growing grief and misery, the Marines Vigilant did not raise a single hand in their own defence. The entire Chapter was destroyed.

This action was remembered as the most distasteful in the Crimson Fists' history, but they were proud that their loyalty to the Emperor was held in such esteem by the Inquisition as to send them time and again on missions as terrible as these.
