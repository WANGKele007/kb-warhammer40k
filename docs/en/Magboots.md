Magboots are a type of magnetised boots used with Void Suits in the Imperium of Man for extravehicular activities in microgravity environments. 

Heavy and bulky, these oversized boots contain electromagnets that allow the wearer to adhere to metallic surfaces such as exterior hull plating and are often found in void ship emergency lockers.

Magboots substantially reduce the user's agility and dexterity when used, but otherwise allow normal movement in low- or zero-gravity areas, provided there is a suitable surface to walk upon.
