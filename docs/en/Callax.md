Callax is a Daemon World named after the Daemon Prince of Slaanesh Fulgrim's home city of Callax on his lost homeworld of Chemos that he ruled in his youth. Callax was granted to Fulgrim to be his personal playground by Slaanesh as a reward for his mortal wounding of the Ultramarines' primarch Roboute Guilliman at the Battle of Thessala in 121.M31.

The location of Callax is unknown, though it seems to lie within the Warp, perhaps within the Realm of Chaos itself, or inside a Warp rift like the Eye of Terror due to the large numbers of Daemons that dwell there without issue or the need for a mortal host body. Many among the Emperor's Children Legion and other Slaaneshi Chaos Cultists desperately seek out the world as the ultimate embodiment of their faith in the Dark Prince.

Callax is a world of unending pleasure described as the "Gardens of Hell" and "not a true planet," but instead a shadow of one being cast upon the light of a dying sun in what could be called the Callax System. 

The world is in a constant state of physical flux, with its oceans comprised of Human blood one day and pomegranate juice the next and its forests rising and falling like waves. Slaaneshi pilgrims flock to the world in search of Fulgrim, but instead those who make planetfall find that they are forced to dance endlessly. If they cease their dancing they are torn apart by waiting Daemonettes.

History

Perhaps the greatest mystery for the increasingly fractured Emperor's Children Legion following the end of the Horus Heresy was the fate of their primarch Fulgrim, for it appeared that he disappeared entirely. The Dark Prince of Chaos had granted him apotheosis during the events of the Heresy and he assumed the mantle of a Daemon Primarch -- his mortal shape transformed into a serpentine form with four arms and wings very similar to the appearance of the Laer xenos that Fulgrim and his Legion had exterminated when he began his fall to Chaos.

Bored by the pedestrian excesses of his Legion in the wake of the Horus Heresy during the Legion Wars, Fulgrim, now a Daemon Prince of Slaanesh, isolated himself on a Slaaneshi Daemon World in the Eye of Terror, which he renamed Callax, after the capital city of his lost homeworld of Chemos. He spent Terran centuries transforming Callax into a reflection of his homeworld, and then recreated the circumstances of his arrival on that world and his drive to eventually rule over it and improve its fortunes. 

Fulgrim now spends most of his time recreating the old battles that unified his homeworld under his leadership. He fights them repeatedly, attempting different strategies in search of the perfect victory to rewrite his history. Sometimes, when truly bored, he even allows the enemy to win.

To this day, many of the scattered warbands of the fractured Emperor's Children Legion and the agents of the Inquisition's Ordo Malleus seek the location of this world, and only a handful of individuals have succeeded in finding it, sometimes invited by its foul master, sometimes not. There are those among the Dark Prince's devotees that claim that Fulgrim oversees such debased extremes of sensation and experience on Callax as no mortal can imagine. Some of those who revere Slaanesh regard this mythical place as the holy of holies, and spend entire lifetimes obsessively questing after it. 

Abaddon the Despoiler was also able to somehow find Fulgrim on Callax and was able to gain the favour of Slaanesh for his plans by trading an unblemished psyker from the world of Pythos taken during the Pandorax Campaign with the Emperor's Children Daemon Primarch.
