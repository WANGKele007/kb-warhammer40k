The Forsaken Reavers is a Traitor Titan Legion in the service of the Ruinous Powers. 

This Traitor Titan Legion has long plagued the Calixis Sector and those sectors that surround it. 

They are also known to operate alongside elements of the dreaded Iron Warriors Heretic Astartes.
