A Gamma Pistol is a weapon often utilised by the military forces of the Adeptus Mechanicus, particularly its various Tech-priests. The Gamma Pistol is entrusted only to the truly blessed. The beam of ionizing radiation that leaps from its muzzle can reduce a man to a blackened shadow in a solar second, but this is a waste of its true strength -- those able to tame its savage Machine Spirit can cut holes in a heavily-fortified Aquila Stongpoint if necessary.
