The Cthonian Beserk Mole Grenade Launcher, also just called a Mole Grenade Launcher, is a long-ranged Mole Launcher that fires grenades rather than burrowing torpedoes that is employed by the Cthonian Beserks of the Leagues of Votann. This repurposed mining equipment delivers subterranean explosives into the midst of enemy units, even if they are cowering out of sight. 

Every Cthonain Beserk who opts to wield a Mole Grenade Launcher is assisted in combat by an L-COG, a robotic Kin labour drone, who carries his primary melee weapon that the Beserk can switch to once close combat begins.
