The Marauder Bomber is the primary atmospheric bomber aircraft used by the Navis Imperialis. The Marauder was previously used by the Navis Imperialis as their primary bomber for operations in vacuum, but has since been replaced by the more powerful Starhawk bomber. The aircraft is usually based on an orbiting starship, and can operate in the vacuum of space as well as in a planet's atmosphere. 

During a prolonged Imperial campaign, Navis Imperialis Aeronautica Imperialis airbases can be established on the ground, but due to the Marauder's extreme range they are located safely behind the front, sometimes even on another continent from the frontlines. During ground assault missions, the Astra Militarum forces on the ground will mark the enemy's positions with coloured flares. Sometimes experienced Aeronautica Imperialis pilots will accompany the ground forces to better coordinate air strikes.

Role

The Marauder Bomber is the workhorse of the Navis Imperialis's Aeronautica Imperialis, mostly due to its extreme range, high speed and large payload for an aircraft of its modest size. The Marauder is used in several common tactical roles such as:

Strategic Bombings - The Marauder will fly long-range missions to attack enemy cities, spaceports, industrial centres and supply routes. The Marauder can operate at extremely high altitudes that are usually out of the range of enemy anti-air defence weapons. During these missions the bomber is usually accompanied by Thunderbolt Fighters to provide anti-fighter support.

Free Range Interdiction - Marauder Bomber squadrons will fly deep into enemy territory without ground support and seek targets of opportunity. The bombers will attack targets that may have already been identified by aerial or orbital reconnaissance. These targets can include enemy supply convoys, fuel dumps, troops and vehicles in the open, airfields, and others as they present themselves. The Marauder is most effective in this role.

Heavy Air Support for Ground Forces - Marauder Bomber squadrons will fly in direct support of ground forces, striking targets along the front line and engaging enemy armour. These are generally the most dangerous, as the bombers have to fly at lower altitudes, allowing enemy anti-air weapons to target them and can sometimes cause friendly causalities.

Void Assault - The Marauder Bomber has a variant that is used as an attack craft aboard Navis Imperialis warships in ship-to-ship void combat.

Troop Transport - By reducing its crew to two and removing every weapon system except those in the nose turret, slaving it to the pilot, a Marauder can also serve as a transport for drop-troopers outfitted with grav-chutes, carrying them in the empty bomb bay. The drop-troopers deploy throgh the aircraft's side hatch.

Maritime Antiship Assault - On worlds with extensive oceans, the Marauder may replace its standard bombs with a single aquatic torpedo to serve in a maritime anti-ship role.

Strategic Nuclear Bomber - The Marauder can also serve as a strategic nuclear bomber by carrying a single Atlas bomb. 

Anti-Armour Gunship - To deal with concentrations of enemy tanks and other enemy armour, the Marauder can carry missile racks under its wings and a stripped-down version of the Battle Cannon as its main armament, which then occupies most of the forward fuselage area.

Armament

The Marauder is armed with a large amount of weapon systems, including a nose-mounted, movable turret with Twin-linked Lascannons, rear turret-mounted Twin-linked Heavy Bolters, dorsal-mounted Twin-linked Heavy Bolters, twelve 200 kilogram High Explosive Bombs in an internal bomb bay, and up to six 400 kilogram Heavy Bombs on an external bomb rack. 

Instead of regular bombs the internal bomb bay may carry two Hellstorm Bombs, large incendiary devices which convert an area into a destructive inferno. Alternatively, the Marauder may deploy chaff comprised of feathery nickel filaments used to blind enemy sensor equipment, illumination-mines or fuel-air explosives.

Marauder06.png|A Marauder Bomber, front view

The Marauder is equipped with a terrain-scanning auspex with a range of a 100 kilometres, a vox that is able to transmit between individual crew-members aboard the aircraft, to other aircraft in its squadron and to flight command, a bombardier foresight reticule that uses a viewscreen to project a digitally-constructed target image based on data collected from the bomber's forward auspex sensors and cameras, chaff and heat-flare launchers, afterburners, an enemy target-lock alarm warning, and a modar. It also has vector-nozzles, allowing the bomber to switch from forward flight to vertical in order to land.

Marauders can also be equipped with fuel drop tanks to extend range and, when operating over oceans, with a life raft, that has two paddles, life vests, emergency rations for 10 solar days, water rations for 2 solar days, 5 flares and an emergency transponder with batteries that allow 24 solar hours of operation.

Like all Imperial aircraft, Marauder bombers can also receive further upgrades.

The Marauder Destroyer variant replaces the Lascannons with 3 Twin-linked Autocannons and the rear-mounted Twin-linked Heavy Bolters with Twin-linked Assault Cannons.

The Marauder Colossus and Marauder Vigilant replace the bomb storage with a single heavy bomb and surveillance equipment, respectively. They also replace the nose weapons with Twin-linked Heavy Bolters. All variants of the Marauder can be outfitted with ejector seats, flare or chaff launchers, extra armour for the cockpit, infra-red targeting equipment, illumination flares and distinctive paint schemes and decals.

Notable Users of the Marauder Bomber

Navis Imperialis Aeronautica Imperialis - The Marauder is the primary atmospheric bomber of the Imperial Navy and it can be found on nearly all warships that support ground operations and on airfields throughout the Imperium.

Imperial Navy 23rd Bomber Wing - The 23rd Bomber Wing made use of the Marauder Bomber during the battles fought on the Death World of Denkari-Prime.

Imperial Navy 133rd Bomber Wing - The 133rd Bomber Wing made extensive use of the Marauder Bomber during the Betalis III Campaign.

Imperial Navy 501st Bomber Wing - Marauder Bombers were used to great effect during the Taros Campaign against the T'au by the 501st Bomber Wing.

Imperial Navy 1099th Bomber Wing - The seven squadrons of the 1099th Bomber Wing were deployed in support of the 88th Siege Army during the second stage of the Siege of Vraks. The long range of the Marauder was the sole means the Loyalists had to bombard Vraks' Citadel at that point in the conflict.

Imperial Navy 1167th Bomber Wing

Imperial Navy 2424th Bomber Wing - The 2424th Bomber Wing used the Marauder Bomber during the Taros Campaign against the Tau and later in the Third War for Armageddon against the Orks.

Imperial Navy 4521st Bomber Wing - The 4521st Bomber Wing deployed the Marauder Bomber exclusively during the Third War for Armageddon against the Orks. It was during this conflict that the Marauder Destroyer variant of the aircraft was first conceived.

XXI Phantine Air Corps, "Halo" Flight - The Phantine Air Corps, one of the many affiliates of the Navis Imperialis, also use the Marauder Bomber. As part of the Sabbat Worlds Crusade, the Phantine XXI "Halo" Flight fought both in the Phantine Liberation campaign and the Enothian War. It was led by one of the most esteemed officers of the entire Phantine Air Corps, Wing Commander Oskar Viltry.

Variants

There are several known variants of the standard Marauder Bomber in use by the Navis Imperialis. These variants allow the Marauder to engage in different tactical roles, or to be more effective in a single role. Known variants of the Marauder Bomber include the following:

Marauder Destroyer - By far the most widespread variant, the Destroyer variant replaces a portion of the standard Marauder's bomb capacity with missiles to engage enemy armoured units. It also replaces the nose-mounted turret Lascannons with 3 Twin-linked Autocannons to better engage enemy infantry. This variant was first developed during the Second War for Armageddon, to fight the Orks who had air superiority.

Marauder Colossus - The Colossus variant of the Marauder Bomber replaces both the internal bomb storage and top-mounted turret of the original with room for a single warhead. The warhead carried by the Colossus is a single 10,000 kg Guided Bomb. This weapon allows the Marauder to destroy heavily fortified structures and positions. This variant also replaces the nose weapons with a single set of twin-linked Heavy Bolters.

Marauder Vigilant - The Vigilant variant of the Marauder replaces the internal bomb storage and top-mounted turret of the original with sophisticated surveillance equipment. This variant is used to gather intelligence on enemy positions and bases, flying at a high altitude allows the craft to remain relatively safe. This variant also replaces the nose weapons with a single set of twin-linked Heavy Bolters.

Marauder Nightstalker - The Marauder Nightstalker is a variant of the Marauder Vigilant, not yet fully approved by the Adeptus Mechanicus for regular service by the time of the Sabbat Worlds Crusade. Operating as a dedicated nightfighter, the Marauder Nighstalker is stripped of nearly all its weapon systems, boasting only light armour and a nose equipped with whisker antennae. It is unpressurised and is operated by a two-person crew in full flight-armour, a pilot and auspex operator who is responsible for the aircraft's high-gain auspex equipment. During combat operations, the Marauder Nightstalker drops behind an enemy bomber swarm, approaching until it achieves hard target-lock. Its only armament, a modified Volcano Cannon mounted on the nose between the antennae array, has enough power for six shots and can down a Chaos Tormentor, Harbinger or Apocalyptor aircraft in a single hit.

Marauder Pathfinder - The Marauder Pathfinder is an electronic warfare and squadron support variant of the Marauder Bomber. Equipped with an array of sensory probes, the Pathfinder is used to scout ahead of a squadron and keep an eye on enemy activity. Replacing the bomb bay and dorsal turret, the Marauder Pathfinder features an array of advanced augurs and tracking systems, supplemented by auspex banks, cogitators, multi-spectrum surveyors and high-powered vox-arrays. It functions as a high-altitude, long-range reconnaissance aircraft, operating both before and after a bombing or attack run by Marauder squadrons has taken place. An experienced augur operator is able to relay the data stream to an entire squadron in rapid fashion, allowing for quick tactical changes to counter enemy movements while the squadron is still en route to its target.

Voidborne Marauder - The Marauder Bomber has a variant that is used in ship-to-ship void-combat, and is stationed full time as an attack craft on Navis Imperialis voidships. This variant is far larger than its Aeronautica Imperialis ground support counterpart, being 260 feet long, with a wingspan of 360 feet and weighing 190 tons unladen. In planetary operations mode, it has a maximum range of 4000 kilometres, extended to 6500 kilometres with a provided auxiliary fuel supply, and a maximum atmospheric thrust of 286.752 km/s. During void operations, this Marauder variant has a maximum interstellar thrust of 502.73 km/s and a flight time of 9.3 solar hours, extended to 14 solar hours with auxiliary atmospheric tanks. It is armed with two nose-mounted Vulcan Pattern Lascannons, two dorsal-mounted twin-linked Autocannons and two tail-mounted Heavy Bolters. It carries a payload of six wing-slung Flail missiles and 14,500 lbs. of explosives or incendiaries. Its crew consists of six persons: a flight commander, a bombardier, an Adeptus Mechanicus tech-priest and three gunners, one first-class and two second-class. The Marauder void combat variant operates in squadrons of five aircraft, with another as a standby back-up in case of mechanical failure. The squadron consists of thirty crew members, with ten more as standby reserves, and is serviced by a ground crew of fifty, including tech-priests and servitors.

Hephasestus - Hephaestus was a unique Marauder Bomber belonging to the Aeronautica Imperialis's 2208th Expeditionary Air Wing. During the air war on the world of Bacchus it was modified by Flight Sergeant Petre Plient to carry a housing for a Seer-skull, to allow Propagandist Kile Simlex to create picts of an active warzone for dissemination across the Imperium. The modifications were technically sanctioned, though Plient doubted they would pass inspection if an Enginseer was to properly inspect them. The Seer-skull Mizar was integrated into the bomber's electronic systems, and thus filmed the combat zone from both its own mount's perspective as well as those of all the gunners' sights.
