The Blades of Rage are a host of Khorne Daemonkin of unknown origin. Their most notable actions occurred in 444.M41, when they joined their fellow Daemonkin host, The Wrath, and aided the Daemon Primarch Angron's attack upon the Imperial Hive World of Armageddon in the First War for Armageddon.
