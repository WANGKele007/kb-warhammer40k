The Collegium Analyticus is an educational and research organisation of the Navis Imperialis located at the Segmentum Fortress of the Segmentum Obscurus on the Forge World of Cypra Mundi. The members of the collegium publish texts describing and analysing Imperial military developments and who seek to educate Imperial naval strategists.

Notable Publications

Old Foe, New Threat - An Assessment of Orkoid Military Developments in the Late 41st Millennium - This work of military science was written by Praeceptor Jakahn of the Collegium Analyticus and an excerpt from the text focuses on Imperial underestimation of the tactical and strategic skills of Ork Warlord Ghazghkull Mag Uruk Thraka as exemplified by the mishandling of the Palmeros Incident.
