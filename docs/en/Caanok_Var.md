Caanok Var is an Iron Captain of the Iron Hands Chapter and commander of the Veterans who comprise its Clan Avernii, also known as the 1st Company.

History

Caanok Var has served as Iron Captain of the Iron Hands Chapter's elite Clan Avernii since before the eruption of the Great Rift and the start of the Era Indomitus. One of the Iron Hands' most seasoned and formidable warriors, the true span of his years is impossible to determine. He has led his battle-brothers in many bitter conflicts, demonstrating a strategic genius to rival any of the galaxy's Chapter Masters. 

His flesh has long since yielded to the perfection of steel -- his augmetic hearts and arms are relic cybernetic technology dating back to the Horus Heresy itself, while his left leg was reclaimed from the fallen Iron Father Karax Gaarman after that hero's death at the Siege of Tessar. Only one of the Iron Captain's bionic components -- his right eye -- was crafted specifically for him as a commendation for his crucial role in the Varakon Decimation, a rare honour indeed, and one reserved only for the Chapter's finest and most respected champions. Of his original organic form, only his left eye remains his own, to serve as both a reminder of his prior weakness and a punishment.

No battle-brother better encapsulates the dichotomy of the Iron Hands than he. Though largely shorn of his own flesh and outwardly as dispassionate and calculating as any machine construct, Var still harbours a burning fury that he finds to be a shameful trace of his Humanity, one that no amount of surgical excision has been able to vanquish. He knows this flaw all too well, yet cannot still it, and so his anger has become both his greatest strength and his deepest imperfection.

His battleplate is a heavily modified suit of Terminator Armour, its ancient systems integrated with a relic logic-engine known as the Cerebrex. This unique device, salvaged during some long-forgotten conflict, interfaces directly with Var's neural augmetics, granting him access to real-time battlefield data at speeds beyond even the enhanced transhuman cognition of an Astartes. Accompanying him at all times are two Servo-skulls, named Dextrum and Sinistrus (High Gothic for "Right" and "Left"), which provide a constant stream of atmospheric readings, tactical analysis, and threat evaluations. His primary weapon is the Clan Avernii relic Power Maul Axiom, a master-crafted symbol of both his authority and his wrath.

Under Caanok Var's steely leadership, the Veterans of Clan Avernii -- also known as the Iron Hands' elite 1st Company -- have played a crucial part in a number of bitter wars. The Invasion of the Stygius Sector, undertaken by a combined force of the Night Lords and Thousand Sons, saw Var lead his battle-brothers to war on multiple occasions. Though Imperial forces suffered a grave strategic defeat in the wider conflict, Var's strike forces won several tactical victories, most notably routing the Chaos Lord Ahkrek Deathshriek's Night Lords from Mordian. Indeed, Var's strategic genius saw the Iron Hands outfight and defeat the numerically superior Night Lords force, liberating that crucial Imperial planet from the grip of Chaos. 

The Noctis Aeterna has seen Clan Avernii's warriors thrust into yet more heavy fighting, and the Iron Hands' homeworld of Medusa's proximity to the Eye of Terror ensures a constant stream of requests for aid flow to the Chapter planet. The Chapter's ruling Iron Council can scarcely respond to every desperate petition for reinforcement. Thus, the clan companies of Medusa have been directed only to those war zones considered of the utmost priority. Belis Corona, Agripinaa and Thracian Primaris are amongst those worlds and systems to have benefited from Clan Avernii's aid in defiance of the threat of Chaos emerging from the ruins of Cadia. Agripinaa in particular has been identified as exceptionally important to continued Imperial war efforts in the region, and Caanok Var himself has been dispatched in response to the Forge World's requests for reinforcement. Whether he and his battle-brothers arrive in time to help shield the beleaguered Forge World remains to be seen.

Wargear

Storm Bolter

Modified Indomitus Pattern Terminator Armour - Var wears an ancient, heavily modified suit of Indomitus Pattern Terminator Armour outfitted with an advanced logic-engine, the Cerebrex, recovered by the Iron Hands during some unknown ancient conflict. This logic-engine's Machine Spirit (aritifical intelligence), slaved directly to Var's cerebral augmetics, enables him to process battlefield information at preternatural speed, granting near-precognitive awareness of enemy maneuvers. 

Servo-skulls Dextrum and Sinistrus - Two ancient Servo-skulls attend Caanok Var at all times -- Dextrum and Sinistrus, meaning "Right" and "Left" in High Gothic. They serve as both his familiars and tactical adjutants, feeding atmospheric, environmental, and strategic data directly into Var's augmetic neural combat interface. 

Axiom - The Power Maul Axiom is a relic weapon revered among the Astartes of Clan Avernii. Believed to be based on weapon designs from ancient Terra, Axiom is capable of crushing most infantry and can even pose a threat to heavily armoured foes, such is the potency of its disruption field. In Var's hands, Axiom becomes the embodiment of his wrath -- a symbol of the Iron Hands’ enduring defiance against weakness.
