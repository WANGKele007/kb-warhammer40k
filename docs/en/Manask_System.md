The Manask System was a star system in the Segmentum Tempestus settled by the Ebon Sentinels Space Marine Chapter on the world of the same name. Pressed by extreme losses, the Ebon Sentinels ultimately turned to the service of Chaos and became a Renegade Chapter of Chaos Space Marines. 

They were purged from existence by the action of the Grey Knights during the Battle of Manask who secretly came to the Manask System in the wake of the Torchbearer Fleet Conqueror's Forge.

System Primary

Sereid's Lantern - Sereid's Lantern is the name of the Manask System's star.

Worlds

The following worlds orbit Sereid's Lantern as their primary, listed from the closest orbit to the furthest.

Manask (Chapter planet) - Manask was the homeworld of the Ebon Sentinels Chapter.
Ta'rond - Ta'rond was an Imperial orbital weapons platform that orbited Manask.
Forghost - Forghost was Manask's orbital dockyard, where the Ebon Sentinels maintained their Chapter fleet.

Vengla (Dead World) - Vengla had served as the prison for a potent Greater Daemon of Chaos for at least a million standard years. It was the influence of this entity that pushed the Ebon Sentinels over the edge in their despair at their declining numbers to embrace the dark power of the Warp. The daemonic presence here was purged by the Grey Knights during a banishment ritual enacted at the Battle of Manask.

Ffion's Chorus - Ffion's Chorus was the name given to the void station that circled in the outer regions of the system that served as its astropathic relay.
