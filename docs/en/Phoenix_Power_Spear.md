A Phoenix Power Spear is a Power Weapon in the form of a great, elegant spear or glaive that was once wielded by the Phoenix Guard of the Emperor's Children Legion during the Great Crusade and the Horus Heresy. 

They were patterned in imitation of the deadly power halberds known as Guardian Spears wielded by the warriors of the ancient Legio Custodes; though merely pale imitations of those legendary polearms, Phoenix Power Spears were still hugely lethal weapons in their own right.

In the 41st Millennium Phoenix Power Spears are still used by the Lords Exultant of the Emperor's Children.
