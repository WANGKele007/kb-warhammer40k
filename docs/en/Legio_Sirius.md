The Legio Sirius was a Loyalist Titan Legion of the Collegia Titanica that hailed from the Forge World of Lokabrenna. The Legio was part of Magos Lexell Kotov's Explorator fleet that entered the Halo Scar during the Kotov Crusade in search of the ancient C'tan artefact known as the Breath of the Gods.

Legion History

Notable Campaigns

Kotov Crusade (Unknown Date.M41) - The Legio Sirius was a part of the Explorator fleet of Magos Lexell Kotov, and his expedition into the region of space known as the Halo Scar located beyond the Halo Worlds. The magos was intent on following the footsteps of the Radical Magos Telok, who had previously made the foolhardy expedition himself, but never returned. Thousands of Terran years later, the ambitious Kotov was bound and determined to seek out the so-called "Breath of the Gods" -- a mysterious arcane device said to have the power to unmake and reshape the very stars themselves. With the might of the god-engines of the Legio Serius, the Astra Militarum and the Space Marines of the zealous Black Templars Chapter augmenting his own forces, he searched for hidden clues which would lead him to the greatest power that the galaxy had ever known. The Breath of the Gods, known to the Aeldari as the Caoineag, was actually an ancient technology created by the C'tan. It was capable of syphoning the energies of stars in a chronologically non-linear way across the past, present and future, giving the ancient C'tan an essentially unlimited source of power to feed upon. The Breath of the Gods was also capable of creating new solar systems through its time manipulation. However its temporal capabilities were extremely unstable and dangerous and if used improperly could erase the entire timeline of the universe. Magos Explorator Vettius Telok had led the first expedition in search of the Breath of the Gods, but he had ultimately been declared lost. Unknown to the Imperium, Telok had indeed discovered the artefact but had gone quite insane in the process, intending to use it to scour Terra and Mars of all life and ruling as the new emperor. Through he originally intended to use the Necron technologies present within the Noctis Labyrinth on Mars to allow him full control over the Breath of the Gods, he had discovered the entropic abilities of the Hrud xenos could produce the same desired effect. To this end he experimented on a captive Hrud colony on his personal Forge World of Exniliho. Telok's ambitions were ultimately thwarted by Archmagos Lexell Kotov and the Kotov Crusade as well as the Craftworld Aeldari of Biel-Tan commanded by Bielanna Faerelle. When Telok was slain by Lexell Kotov aboard the Ark Mechanicus Speranza, the semi-active Breath of the Gods artefact was torn apart without its leader in place to control it and its compliment of enslaved Hrud were freed to wreak havoc. In the aftermath of the battle over Exnihilo nearly all of the Humans and Aeldari were slain but the Speranza and its survivors managed to limp back to Imperial space. Though the threat of the Breath of the Gods to the Imperium had been eliminated, it was possible that Telok's psychic essence may have survived within the mind of Tarkis Blaylock, Kotov's Fabricator-Locum and second in command who had served as the captain of the Speranza.

Legion Appearance

Legion Colours

The colours of the Legio Sirius were described as grey, gold and blue on the carapaces.

Legion Badge

The badge of the Legio Sirius is said to be a canidae, a member of the genus containing Terran dogs.
