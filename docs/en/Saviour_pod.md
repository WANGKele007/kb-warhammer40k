A saviour pod is a small, coffin-like Imperial escape pod intended for use by voidships crews in emergencies to escape from their vessel. They can be found on almost all Imperial voidships, Marauder Bombers and submersibles. They are designed to make planetary landings in case of an emergency. 

Each saviour pod is outfitted with an automated cogitator system intended to navigate towards the cloest safe planetary mass, and is fitted with retro-thrusters intended to slow its descent, but they do not contain the facilities needed to keep their occupants alive for an extended period of time in the void or in another hostile environment.

Marauder Bomber Saviour Pods

Imperial Navy Marauder Bombers each possess one armored Saviour Pod, which is placed behind a sealed door. It has enough belted seats for the bomber's entire crew to escape the vessel. When the pod's release button is pressed in its small cockpit, explosive bolts ignite around its base. This blast hurls the saviour pod away from the Marauder. It the saviour pod is released while the bomber is in a planetary atmosphere, the saviour pod will start to tumble until its retro jets activate and stabilise its descent. 

Submersible Saviour Pods

Several saviour pods are placed next to each other within Imperial submersibles and each has room for at least two people. They are accessed through high set, quick-slam hatches and are equipped with buoys and compression-change valves. 

Once activated the residual power they stored from the submersible is used to flush water from the pod's submersible compartments and turn on their onboard flotation and propulsion systems. These will propel the saviour pod to the surface of the body of water where the submersible is located so that its occupants can be rescued. 

Submersible saviour pods have only limited charge in their onboard batteries, so they cannot remain powered for long. Their oxygen tanks also provide only a limited duration of breathable atmosphere and are attached to the pod's exterior flanks.
