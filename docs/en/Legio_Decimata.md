The Legio Decimata is a Loyalist Titan Legion of the Collegia Titanica.

Legion History

Notable Campaigns

War of the Beast (544-546.M32) - The Legio Decimata is known to have taken part in the War of the Beast, a massive Ork WAAAGH! of extraordinary size that threatened Terra itself. A greenskin invasion on this scale had not been seen since the Ullanor Crusade during the latter years of the Great Crusade. The Legio Decimata played a vital role in aiding Imperial forces during the third invasion of The Beast's homeworld of Ullanor.
