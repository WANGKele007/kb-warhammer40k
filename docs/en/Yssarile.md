Yssarile was Daemon Prince of Tzeentch, so powerful among Changer of Way's servants he was later acclaimed as a "Daemon-king". He eventually attempted to usurp the power of the Chaos God Tzeentch but failed and received a True Death at the hands of his former master. 

History

According to the Malus Codicium, Yssarile was either a lieutenant, warlord, or prince among Chaos Daemons. This was at a time when there were only three major Ruinous Powers, before the birth of Slaanesh. Yssarile was a servant of Tzeentch, but grew disillusioned with his master and attempted to destroy him, taking Tzeentch's place as the new Chaos God of change and sorcery. Yssarile struck out and initiated a war said to have lasted for a billion Terran years before his eventual defeat, suffering a permanent True Death at the hands of his former master. 

However, Yssarile's destruction was not to be his end, for a powerful legacy lived on. Many Daemons less powerful than Yssarile were in service to him and fled into the Materium when he was destroyed, finding a new home on the world of Ghül, where they built their capital city, the greatest of seven worlds raised in honour of the daemon-king, and six hundred additional colonies. This Daemonic empire was established many millions of standard years before even the Aeldari began to move among the stars. It is said that the capital of Ghül is built upon the mausoleum of the Daemon-king.
