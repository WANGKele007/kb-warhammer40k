The Haephosian Tritons are the primary regiments of Astra Militarum infantry raised from the Mining World of Haephos in the Segmentum Ultima. They fought in the Haephos Containment campaign and contributed a total of 44 infantry regiments to that conflict.

The Haephosian Tritons also contributed 23 companies to the Argovon Campaign of the Pariah Crusade in the Pariah Nexus.
