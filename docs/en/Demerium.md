Demerium is one of the two moons of the planet Avarax in the Recidious System of the Bastior Sub-sector in the Segmentum Pacificus and an Imperial Cemetery World, called a "Burial World". It was a crucial strategic location fought over between the Ultramarines' 2nd Company and the forces of the Thousand Sons Traitor Legion during the Recidious Campaign of the Fourth Tyrannic War in the Era Indomitus.

By the time of the Era Indomitus in the 41st Millennium, Demerium's Imperial records stated that the number of deceased currently interred on the moon exceeded 91,000,000,601,000,014.

During the Recidious Campaign, Demerium was invaded by the Thousand Sons, though hints of their manipulation of the secret Imperial Project Aurora were discovered on the Hive World of Avarax by the Ultramarines. 

However, unknown to the Imperials, Demerium was also a Necron Tomb World. The Adeptus Mechanicus had originally excavated an ancient, possibly Human civilisation's ruins with carvings similar to those of Necron hieroglyphics, while deeper beneath those ruins, monolithic stasis-tomb structures of actual Necron origin were discovered. The Mechanicus used these ruins to initiate research into the properties and uses of blackstone within the xenos complex, while Project Aurora's research facility was also later established somewhere deep within the Necron stasis-tomb. 

History

Early Conflicts

Four thousand standard years prior to the 41st Millennium, a clash between the Ultramarines and unknown Heretics occurred in which the Sword of Atreus, a battle barge, was destroyed and its remains laid to rest on Demerium with full military honours. The hull of the warship had been ruined in the fighting, but the internal electronics and engines still functioned even as the vessel was interred beneath the moon's surface. 

According to diagnostics run on the vessel by Adeptus Mechanicus Artisan Balthazar and Enginseer Sigma-Eight during the Recidious Campaign, the battle barge's engines no longer had sufficient capability to escape the planet's gravity, though the Mechanicus had begun to restore the vessel to full functionality.

At some point in the past, an Imperator-class Titan was also laid to rest upon Demerium.

Project Aurora

The Adeptus Mechanicus's excavation and research on the ruins hidden beneath the Cemetery World discovered two separate layers of ruins. The first consisted of monolithic complexes built of stone, populated by arrays of obelisks and hieroglyphic carvings inscribed on most of the architecture. The Mechanicus researchers believed that the ancient underground complex, if built by Humans, might predate the Great Crusade, though all that was certain about this layer of the ruins was that it echoed in architectural style and form the identifiably Necron blackstone stasis-tomb structures found below.

Magos Ekaterina Mal-Volos was one of the Mechanicus researchers examining the excavated data from these ancient ruins beneath the surface of Demerium. During the Thousand Sons' assault on the moon, she discovered very old Lingua-technis cogitator code that had been deprecated millennia before. Within the data metaset the magos analysed, a simple subroutine was found, a single word deciphered to mean "AWAKENING". Following a set of coordinates that led down into the Necron layer of the world's ruins, undisturbed for 60,000 standard years by her estimation, Magos Mal-Volos connected to a residue covered uplink at the base a sarcophagus. 

The connection took only solar seconds to establish but the only signal received from it was an endless repetition of the "AWAKENING" code. This particular sarcophagus revealed itself to be that of a Canoptek Tomb Stalker, a defensive robotic construct common on Necron Tomb Worlds. It awakened and eventually slew Mal-Volos after she attempted to flee. While being hunted by the centipede-like construct, Mal-Volos again ran the anomalous code but now its translation had changed to "AWOKE".

At some point, the Mechanicus's research efforts led them to penetrate even deeper into the Necron stasis-tomb complex. Several more Tomb Stalkers apparently later awoke and were defeated by the expedition's forces. This was made clear to the Ultramarines when they discovered Tomb Stalker remains with Mechanicus diagnostic equipment attached to their insectoid heads and their disassembled limbs seen throughout expedition sites. 

A trail of Mechanicus wiring, scaffolding, tents and other expeditionary equipment ultimately led to the location where the Project Aurora device lay, at the base of a prominent Necron obelisk. It was the largest obelisk within the layer of the Necron stasis-tomb complex and stood several times taller than any other structure within sight.

Recidious Campaign

During the Recidious Campaign's final stages, Captain Sevastus Acheran of the Ultramarines' 2nd Company informed Lieutenant Demetrian Titus that thrall-bands of the Thousand Sons had appeared across the Recidious System and were focusing their assault on Demerium, a local Cemetery World that was one of the two moons of Avarax and the new home of Project Aurora, where Lueze was attempting to activate the Warp-based weapon intended to specifically target the servants of Chaos. 

After making planetfall and fighting through the Thousand Sons forces present on the Cemetery World, the Ultramarines arrived at the subterranean location of Project Aurora where they discovered from the xenos nature of the surroundings that Demerium was actually a Necron Tomb World and that the Mechanicus had been using the xenos' technology to advance their research and development of Project Aurora. 

The Astartes found Lueze, who revealed that by combining Necron and Adeptus Mechanicus technology with the Chaos artefact as a power source, the Project Aurora weapon will allow Imperial forces to negate and banish to the Warp all those touched by the energies of Chaos, first in the Recidious System but eventually the effect will spread across the entire galaxy, providing the Imperium with a final victory over Chaos. 

The Thousand Sons Sorcerer Lord Imurah attempted to stop the work on the weapon and was seemingly banished into the Immaterium by Project Aurora. However, this proved a ruse, and as Imurah returned he revealed that it was he who had covertly influenced the Tech-priests of the Adeptus Mechanicus into creating and continuing the project previously begun on Graia so that he might gain control of the reconstructed artefact. The sorcerer then slew Lueze and stole the artefact for himself.

Returning to the surface of Demerium from the Necron tomb, Titus' squad discovered a Necron obelisk had erupted that had formed a massive Warp rift on the planet's surface, allowing Imurah to bring more of his Thousand Sons forces and a Lord of Change to the Cemetery World. After the squad reunited with Captain Acheran, Gadriel suggested that they might be able to close the Warp rift by shutting down the lesser obelisks empowering the main one. 

Fighting their way to each of the Necron obelisks, Titus and his squad were nearly overwhelmed by the Thousand Sons before they were saved by the sudden arrival of Marneus Calgar and his Ultramarine reinforcements. Titus and his squad proceed to shut down the lesser obelisks while Calgar and the rest of the Ultramarines distracted Imurah's forces. After shutting down the Warp emanations of the final lesser obelisk, the combined strength of the Ultramarines begin pushing back the forces of Chaos.

During the battle, Imurah and the Lord of Change fled through a portal into the Immaterium, unleashing a spell that froze all of the Ultramarines engaged in the battle save Calgar, Titus, Gadriel, and Chairon. Followed by Calgar, Titus and Titus' squadmates, the Ultramarines managed to reunite with Calgar within the Warp and duel Imurah and the Lord of Change. They managed to drive the Lord of Change back as Imurah attempted to use Project Aurora's power source to imbue himself with the divine knowledge of the Warp. 

However, midway through that process, Titus claimed the Project Aurora power source while Imurah was distracted battling the Chapter Master and reversed the polarity of the power source as Imurah imbued himself with it, causing Imurah to explode with a massive burst of psychic energy when he attempted to channel an amount of Warp energy that was too great for him to handle without the artefact's assistance. The explosion also drove back the Daemonic tide, granting victory to the Ultramarines and the other Imperial defenders.

Awakening back in realspace, Titus found himself surrounded by the victorious Ultramarines, now free of the sorcerer's spell. Returning to the battle barge Resilience, Marneus Calgar praised Titus for his deeds and asked that he join him on a mission elsewhere. Titus said farewell to Gadriel and Chairon but was intercepted by the Chaplain he had first met upon returning to the Ultramarines, who was also accompanying Calgar on the new mission.
