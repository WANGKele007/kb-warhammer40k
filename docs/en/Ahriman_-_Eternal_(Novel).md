Ahriman - Eternal is the fourth novel in the Ahriman series. The novel was released in June 2022.

Synopsis

Doom has come upon the Thousand Sons. Born from the Rubric of Ahriman, a curse of fire and dust stalks them across time and space. The spirits of the Rubric Marines are vanishing from the prisons of their power armour, while living sorcerers take their place one-by-one. Driven by the need to save his Legion and find redemption, Ahzek Ahriman seeks the time-altering technology of the forgotten Necrons to overwrite the past. Shadowed by Aeldari Harlequins and with secrets and division spreading through his forces, he must find a new path to salvation before all becomes dust.
