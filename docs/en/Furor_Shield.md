Furor Shield is a watch fortress of the Deathwatch Chapter located in the Ork-occupied Octarius Sector of the Ultima Segmentum.

The kill-teams of Furor Shield monitor the Ork Empire of Octarius in the Octarius Sector, into which Inquisitor Fidus Kryptman of the Ordo Xenos misdirected a tendril of Hive Fleet Leviathan early in the Third Tyrannic War to buy time for the Imperium to face this largest of the Tyranid hive fleets. Both Tyranids and Orks adapt under duress -- when the victor of the ever-escalating Octarius War emerges, the Deathwatch of Furor Shield stands ready to help slay them.

In the Era Indomitus, Vermillion-level astropathic alerts reached the Doombreak, Eye of Octos and Furor Shield watch fortresses as the unthinkable finally happened. Triggered by empyric shock waves from the opening of the Great Rift, swarms of enormous Tyranids and hordes of hulking Ork Skarboyz finally broke away from the Octarius War to attack neighbouring star systems. The watch masters of all three fortresses immediately requested urgent reinforcement from the wider Imperium, before surging into battle.
