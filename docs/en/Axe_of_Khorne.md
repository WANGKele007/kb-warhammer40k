An Axe of Khorne is a type of Daemon Weapon imbued with the power of the Chaos God Khorne. It is wielded by Bloodthirsters of Unfettered Fury and Wraith of Khorne Bloodthirsters, capable of splitting a battle tank in two, though it is also rarely wielded by the mortal followers of the Blood God.

It is not a true Daemon Weapon in a strict sense, as it lacks sentience and is incapable of rebelling against its wielder, but the bloodlust contained within the weapon drives the user to commit ever-greater acts of destructive carnage.

Great Axe of Khorne

A Great Axe of Khorne is a much larger, two-handed, great weapon variant of the standard Axe of Khorne Daemon Weapon. Great Axes of Khorne are described as being as large as the gates of fortresses. They are constructed of blood, bone and brass, and their grips are composed of the skins of all those who have challenged the forces of Khorne and lost. The Great Axe of Khorne is a true Daemon Weapon, for within its physical form is bound the essence of a Greater Daemon of Khorne.
