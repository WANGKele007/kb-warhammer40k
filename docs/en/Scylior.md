The Scylior is a warship of the Carcharodons Chapter known to appear, albeit infrequently, within the Jericho Reach. The ship itself is the rough size and make of a frigate, similar in design to the standard Imperial Sword-class Frigate.

However, unlike a standard vessel of its class, the Scylior possesses powerful, even oversized, Plasma Drives capable of propelling it at impressive speeds, and it appears to trade some of its weapon batteries for expanded hangar bays capable of holding Thunderhawk gunships.

The remaining macrobatteries appear to be a medley of types, ranging from the traditional laser-based weapons found aboard a Sword frigate to archaic macrobeamers and magno-carronades.

Characteristically, the Scylior has never allowed any non-Carcharodons to serve aboard the vessel, and only identifies itself by name, never by Chapter. That the vessel belongs at all to the Carcharodons is simply conjecture.

Nevertheless, the warship has been sighted in the Reach at least twice since the beginning of the Achilus Crusade. Long range Imperial Navy patrols reported a vessel matching its description off the Black Reef, engaged with two T'au Defender-class escorts.

Though outnumbered, the Scylior managed to cripple one with macrobattery fire and ram and board the other, with the results one would expect from a Space Marine boarding action. Even when its task is transport, it seems the Scylior is unwilling to pass up a chance for blood.

Recently, Inquisitor Adrielle Quist unearthed some information from Watch Fortress Erioch's data-vaults pertaining to a possible origin of the Scylior. A ship matching its description named the Prowler was reported to have hunted the space lanes of the Jericho Sector many millennia ago as a pirate vessel.

According to the crumbling data-tape, the Prowler vanished with a number of other pirate vessels during a solar decade of "mysterious disappearances" amongst reavers plaguing Imperial shipping.
