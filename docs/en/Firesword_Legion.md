The Firesword Legion is a Titan Legion of the Collegia Titanica.

Legion History

Notable Campaigns

Vania Excoriation (Unknown Date) - The Firesword Legion fought alongside their allies from the unforgiving Iron Hands Space Marine Chapter, when they assaulted the Black Fortress upon the world of Vania, when the god-engines of the Firesword Legion brought down its walls, making a larger breach in the enemy's defences. This allowed the Iron Hands the opportunity to exploit the breach and strike deep into the heart of the enemy fortification and bring the battle to a swift conclusion.
