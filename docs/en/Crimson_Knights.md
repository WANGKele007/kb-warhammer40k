The Crimson Knights is a Loyalist Space Marine Chapter and a successor of the Blood Angels. They were created during an unknown Founding in the 35th Millennium as a standing force intended to bolster the defences of a region of Imperial space against the predations of the Craftworld Aeldari; specifically, this led the Chapter to fight a series of battles against Craftworld Kaelor. 

Their 3rd Company captain is regarded as a hero for successfully leading a raid against this craftworld, earning both the captain and the Crimson Knights the eternal enmity of the Asuryani.

The Chapter is currently rebuilding its numbers after having suffered severe losses in battle. During one notable incident, the Chapter's forces were saved due to the timely intervention of an Imperial Navy battlefleet. Due to this event, the Crimson Knights now count the Imperial Navy as one of their closest allies.

The Chapter's homeworld is the distant world of Tembron, where the Chapter's leadership is also regarded as the world's ultimate authority.

Chapter Homeworld

The Crimson Knights hail from the distant planet of Tembron, which is classified as a Feudal World by Imperial cartographers. The world's predominant terrain is a wasteland. The wind-scoured and polluted wastes are dominated by massive, looming keeps where the feudal lords of this planet control and protect the peasants of their households. The Crimson Knights have taken to directly ruling the barren planet, meaning the Chapter's leaders are figures of ultimate authority on this world.

Chapter Gene-Seed

The Crimson Knights' gene-seed originates from their progenitors, the Blood Angels. As such, they too, suffer from the effects of the inherited genetic Flaws of the Red Thirst and Black Rage.

Chapter Appearance

Chapter Colours

The Crimson Knights' Chapter colours are primarily red with golden greaves.

Chapter Badge

The Crimson Knights' Chapter badge is a sword flanked by multiple blood drops.

Trivia

The Crimson Knights and Tembron were created by Fantasy Flight Games, long a licensee of Games Workshop, who created many of the Warhammer 40,000 board games and roleplaying games like the two editions of Dark Heresy, Rogue Trader and Deathwatch. The Crimson Knights were used as an example of how to create a Space Marine Chapter using the rules in the Deathwatch RPG sourcebook Deathwatch: Rites of Battle.
