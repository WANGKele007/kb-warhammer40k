The Korianis Sector was a sector of Imperial space located in the Ultima Segmentum of the galaxy. It was the location of Sub-sector Aurelia, the most important recruiting region for the Blood Ravens Chapter of the Adeptus Astartes and was once the site of its lost Chapter planet, Aurelia.

The Korianis Sector was where the Aurelian Crusades of the Blood Angels and their Imperial allies against the Tyranids, Orks and the forces of Chaos were fought in the 41st Millennium before the Era Indomitus began.
