The Master of the Rites is the title traditionally given in Codex Astartes-compliant Space Marine Chapters to the captain of the 6th Company. In addition to his company command duties, the Master of the Rites, as the name implies, oversees all the rites and important rituals of the Chapter cult.

Notable Masters of the Rites

Captain Ferren Areios - Ferren Areios is the current captain of the Ultramarines Chapter's 6th Company and its Master of the Rites. Areios was one of the first wave of recruits to be transformed into a Primaris Space Marine during the Ultima Founding. Formally awakened after over ten thousand standard years in stasis during the earliest days of the Indomitus Crusade, he has since forged a reputation as an incredibly effective battlefield commander.

Captain Maximus Epathus (Ultramarines) - Epathus is the former captain of the Ultramarines' 6th Company and Master of the Rites. He led the 6th Company alongside the Dark Angels and their allies during the Siege of the Fenris System in 999.M41. After arriving at Fenris, Epathus and Terrek of the Iron Hands set out for Frostheim, and en route attended a briefing led by Elezar of the Dark Angels. During the Bloodborn's Invasion of Ultramar, Epathus led the 6th Company on the planet Quintarn in its defence alongside the 5th Company, Chronus, Cassius, Telion and some of his Scout Marines. Some time after the Great Rift's creation, Epathus underwent the surgical procedure known as the Rubicon Primaris and ascended to become a Primaris Space Marine. The captain understood well the value of displaying unquestionable martial might and went to battle in the masterwork Mark X Gravis Artificer Armour called the Armour Gloriastus. He commanded an Ultramarines strike force during the Charadon Campaign. During the Battle of Garrovire, a battle of the Indomitus Crusade, Epathus was killed by a possessed member of his 6th Company. On Garrovire, the Ultramarines had discovered a Chaos-tainted statue. Deciding to destroy the corrupted idol, the Ultramarines were attacked by Chaos Cultists and one of their number was possessed by a Daemon and proceeded to kill Epathus. In the aftermath of the battle, Ferren Areios assumed de facto command of the 6th Company and was later officially confirmed as the new Master of the Rites and captain of the 6th.

Shadow Captain Syras Colfaen (Raven Guard) - Syras Colfaen is a Shadow Captain of the Raven Guard. He is the commander of the 6th Company and is the Raven Guard's Master of the Rites.

Master Araphil (Dark Angels) - Araphil is the current Master of the Dark Angels' 6th Company and the Chapter's Master of the Rites.
