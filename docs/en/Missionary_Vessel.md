A missionary vessel is a type of Imperial voidship operated by the Missionarius Galaxia of the Adeptus Ministorum which carries Ecclesiarchy missionaries on their quests to convert newly discovered Human-settled worlds to the Imperial Creed.

A missionary vessel of the Adeptus Ministorum serves as a mobile cathedrum of the Imperial Cult, complete with stained armaglass windows and infrawave Laud Hailers. Its hallowed decks contain all manner of implements that its resident missionaries use to ply their craft of conversion, allowing them to spread faith in the Imperial Cult through persuasion when possible and through fear and excruciation if unfortunately necessary.

Missionary vessels are usually also well-armed and equipped in order to better survive long enough to carry out their duties to the Emperor.

Notable Missionary vessels

Clarion - The Clarion was a missionary vessel whose crew was dedicated to exploring the Seventh Blackstone Fortress under the authority of the overzealous Ecclesiarchy priest Taddeus the Purifier.
