A Questor Scout Titan, known also simply as a Questor, is a scout-grade Chaos Titan and Daemon Engine dedicated to the service of Slaanesh, the Chaos God of pleasure and excess. 

It is a variant of the Subjugator Scout Titan, but the Questor possesses a higher stature and slightly thicker armour than its counterpart. However, despite its greater bulk, its powerful legs can still drive forward at a considerable pace.

Armament

Graceful arms spread from the Questor's carapace, each tipped with a rapid-firing, Warp-based directed energy weapon called a Tormentor Cannon. The Questor also carries a pair of powerful Lascannons, one mounted in the head, the other slung between the legs. 

A Questor is also armed with a pair of bolters to serve as an anti-infantry deterrent.

Powers and Abilities

Like the Daemon Knights of Slaanesh, the Questor and Subjugator are surrounded by a glittering wall of energy known as a "Glamour of Slaanesh." This is a result of the Warp-interface which keeps the daemonic spirit within them imprisoned in the material universe. This shifting, swirling aura makes these Daemon Engines very hard to accurately target. As the Glamour of Slaanesh is an interface with the immaterial universe of Warpspace it also provides a certain amount of protection from attacks that use the power of the Warp, such as Vortex Missiles and psychic powers.
