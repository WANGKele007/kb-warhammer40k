Laser Weapons, or Las Weapons as they are known in the Imperium of Man, are a family of directed energy weapons that produce beams of high-intensity, amplified light that induce heat damage when they strike a target. Laser Weapons are relatively easy to produce and maintain.

No solid ammunition is ever used -- only power cells which can be recharged and quickly swapped out when depleted with another fully-charged power cell. This makes Laser Weapons ideal for use by large troop formations like the Astra Militarum.

While the accuracy of the las beam cannot be distorted by wind or other weather effects like ballistic ammunition, it can be quickly diffused by heavy particles in the air such as smoke and hence lose its power over long distances.

Certain types of Lasgun have specific uses, most notably the more powerful Imperial Long-Las which is the catch-all term for sniper variants because they bear longer barrels.

Laser Weapons are used in many shapes and sizes. Some are big and powerful enough to be used in stellar engagements between voidships, others are used on vehicles like Titans, tanks or Dreadnoughts, whilst the smallest ones can be operated individually as man-portable firearms by infantry. Depending on their size, they are powered by on-board generators, power cells or portable power supplies.

Laser Weapons are used by many intelligent species in the galaxy, and as such, Laser Weapons are a crucial part of many xenos arsenals, including those of the Orks, Aeldari, Leagues of Votann and T'au, as well as the arsenals of the Chaos-corrupted Humans that make up the forces of Chaos among the Lost and the Damned and the Chaos Space Marines.
