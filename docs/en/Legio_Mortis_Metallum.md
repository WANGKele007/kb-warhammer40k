The Legio Mortis Metallum is a Loyalist Titan Legion of the Collegia Titanica. 

Legion History

Notable Campaigns

Defence of Fornax Orbis Majoris (999.M41) - As the 41st Millennium drew to a close, a massive invasion by the Chaos, led by the Warmaster of Chaos Abaddon the Despoiler, invaded the realm of Mankind. Heralding the 13th Black Crusade, the forces of Chaos proceeded to simultaneously attack several Imperial systems. During this invasion the Legio Mortis Metallum valiantly defended the hive city of Godspire Secundus when a large Chaos invasion, spearheaded by the vile Death Guard Traitor Legion, invaded the Forge World Fornax Orbis Majoris.
