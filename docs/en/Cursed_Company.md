The Cursed Company is a little known warband of Chaos Space Marines of unknown origin and Founding. The only information of note is that they took part in the 13th Black Crusade.

Warband History

Notable Campaigns

13th Black Crusade (999.M41) - The 13th Black Crusade was a massive invasion of Imperial space by the Forces of Chaos led by the Warmaster Abaddon the Despoiler against the Fortress World of Cadia and the surrounding sectors. This was the largest mobilisation of both Imperial and Chaotic forces seen since the bygone days of the Horus Heresy.
