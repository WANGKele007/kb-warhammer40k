A Comet-class Scout Ship is a specialised class of Leagues of Votann voidship often utilised by Hernkyn Pioneers and Hearthkyn Salvagers on far-reaching missions of exploration, salvaging and resource acquisition. The Comet-class is equipped with voidship-sized HYLas banks powerful enough to carve entire sections off of a space hulk. 

Notable Comet-class Scout Ships

Reachfar - The Reachfar was commanded by Voidmaster Hegêm and served as a base of operations of Theyn Dêki Ânhad and her band of Hearthkyn Salvagers.
