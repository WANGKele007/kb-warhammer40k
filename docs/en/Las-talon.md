The Las-talon is a specialised type of Laser Weapon that can be found on the Stormhawk Interceptor air superiority fighter used by the Adeptus Astartes and the Primaris Space Marine Repulsor armoured transport, Storm Speeder Thunderstrike and Gladiator Valiant grav-tank.

The Las-talon fires two potent blasts of laser energy in rapid succession, ensuring a clean kill against even the heaviest armoured targets. Unlike other laser weapons, the Las-talon is relatively short-ranged, with its damage heavily reduced at great range.
