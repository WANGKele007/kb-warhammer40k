The Helican Sub-sector is a sub-sector of the Scarus Sector, which is situated in the Segmentum Obscurus. Jeromya Faurlitz IV is currently the sub-sector's Imperial Governor and Lord Commander of the Astra Militarum forces in Helican.

He has established strong political ties to the Imperial Warmaster Honorius present in the sector. The Helican Sub-sector was the site of the Helican Schism, a wave of rebellion, terrorism and sabotage that swept the area in the early 41st Millennium.

Notable Worlds

Thracian Primaris (Hive World) - Planetary capital of the Helican Sub-Sector, Thracian Primaris glows like an oozing, cataracted eye. The planet has no moons and its populace lives primarily in hive cities scattered across seven-tenths of the surface. Guarded by five Ramilies-class Starforts and with a Planetary Defence Force of 8 million men, the planet is as well-defended as any Imperial world.

Bonaventure - A world where ancient wheel-like tombs were discovered, a possible remnant of the pre-Imperial xenos civilisation known as the Saruthi. This world was the birthplace of the Untouchables Alizebeth Bequin and the Imperial agent and scholar Kara Swole, both of whom served the infamous Inquisitor Gregor Eisenhorn.
