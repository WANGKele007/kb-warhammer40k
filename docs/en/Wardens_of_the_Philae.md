The Wardens of the Philae is a Loyalist Space Marine Chapter of unknown Founding and origin.

During the Era Indomitus, the Torchbearers' Gladius Ad Umbra Fleet could not locate the Chapter they had been designated to reinforce with Primaris Space Marines, the Lions Azure, in the Afqal Sub-sector.

But they did unexpectedly contact the Wardens of the Philae. The Wardens' history over the last three standard millennia so closely matched the fleet's records of the Lions Azure that the commander could not be certain if they were one and the same or not.
