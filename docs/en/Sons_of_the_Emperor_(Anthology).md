Sons of the Emperor is the first Primarchs anthology novel and contains 8 short stories by various authors. Sons of the Emperor was first made available as an event-only release at the 2018 Horus Heresy Weekender.

Synopsis

No synopsis for Sons of the Emperor is available.

Contents

Sons of the Emperor contains the following 8 short stories:

The Passing of Angels by John French
The Abyssal Edge by Aaron Dembski-Bowden
Mercy of the Dragon by Nick Kyme
Shadow of the Past by Gav Thorpe
The Emperor's Architect by Guy Haley
Prince of Blood by John French
The Ancient Awaits by Graham McNeill
Misbegotten by Dan Abnett
