The Mor Deythan, whiuch meant the "Shadowmasters" in the dialect of Low Gothic spoken on Kiavahr, were a small elite cadre of infiltration squads within the Raven Guard Legion during the era of the Great Crusade and Horus Heresy in the late 30th and early 31st Millennia. In a Legion already well known for its nigh-unsurpassed skill in infiltration tactics, the Mor Deythan were, however, far more than specialists in the arts of stealth, for each warrior was a veteran of the Lycaen Uprising who had fought at the side of their primarch Corvus Corax himself. 

When the Emperor arrived on Lycaeus and the Raven Lord learned the truth of his creation, some of the rebels sought to remain at his side. The strongest of the young freedom fighters were judged worthy of ascension to the ranks of the XIXth Legion, providing a constant reminder of the primarch's roots. These elite Legionaries were rumoured to have inherited through their gene-seed a modicum of the unique psychic trait of their genetic father that allowed them to "shadow-walk", enabling them to deny their presence to anything with a mind, making them effectively invisible. 

The Mor Deythan utilised chainswords and Bolt Pistols as their primary weapons but often deployed Sniper Rifles like the Nemesis Bolter, Shotguns or other rare weaponry such as Volkite Chargers. In battle, they utilised a specially developed battle-cant called "Stalk Argot", that consisted of a series of sighs and whispers that were nigh-indistinguishable from wind and other environmental sounds. Their squad leaders were known as "shades".

The Mor Deythan was reestablished by the Raven Guard's then-Chapter Master Kayvaan Shrike in the Era Indomitus from among those few Astartes present in the Raven Guard and its Successor Chapters like the Knights of the Raven who still developed these latent psychic abilities to cloud their opponents' minds. By that time, the emergence of these abilities had become extremely rare among those who bore the genetic lineage of Corvus Corax.

History

Origins

Corvus Corax was separated from his father the Emperor of Mankind while still an infant in the Emperor's gene-laboratory beneath the Himalazian (Himalayan) Mountains on Terra by the machinations of the Ruinous Powers of Chaos. The Chaos Gods snatched the infant primarch's gestation chamber and flung it through the Warp half-way across the galaxy. Corax was eventually discovered on the world of Lycaeus, the desolate but inhabitable moon of the planet Kiavahr. At this time, Kiavahr was a technologically advanced Forge World, providing its city-size manufactoria with minerals extracted from the non-atmospheric moon by legions of mine slaves, and was home to a guild of Tech-masters who were open in their hatred towards the Mechanicum of Mars. 

Under the iron watch of heavily-armed guards, the enslaved inhabitants of Lycaeus, who included criminals, political opponents of the Tech-masters and workers who had failed to meet their quotas, had long provided the rulers of Kiavahr with a free and unlimited source of manpower. When they discovered the pale-skinned child who became Corax, "The Deliverer" in the Kiavahran tongue, they kept him from their masters, training him in the various skills they believed a leader and warrior needed. 

Educated to be a leader of men as well as a rebel guerrilla, Corax began his task by organising the Lycaean workers into fight teams, promoting the best to squad leaders. The first Mor Deythan were formed from the best warriors who followed Corax during his shadow crusade to free the moon of Lyceaus from the rule of the Kiavahran Tech-masters. Corax fought a hard and bloody guerrilla war against these tyrants, and afterwards, when the Emperor came to the newly renamed moon of Deliverance, those young enough to withstand the genetic augmentations became the first Space Marines of the XIXth Legion.

Amongst the ranks of the first Deliverance-born Astartes were those who displayed the same unique ability as their primarch, enabling them to "Shadow-Walk". Through some genetic quirk of their gene-seed, a small percentage of these Astartes had inherited more of their primarch's genome than any of their Terran-born brethren. Forming a specialised unit known as the Mor Deythan in the Kiavahran language, Corax utilised his formidable knowledge of stealth and sabotage to the fullest extent, training these specially selected Legionaries to be called upon to perform highly dangerous reconnaissance and guerrilla missions deep behind enemy lines. 

Nicknamed "Shadow Masters", this elite unit was not known even amongst the members of their own Legion. Only the primarch himself and a select group of Legion Apothecaries were aware of the existence of the Mor Deythan at the time of the Horus Heresy. Often times Corax would call upon the Mor Deythan to test highly temperamental thermal technology to help further their goals and objectives against their enemies.

By the time of the Drop Site Massacre on Isstvan V, the Mor Deythan were few in number, having fought countless campaigns over eight solar decades of the Great Crusade at their master's side and they rarely admitted new recruits into their ranks. During this time, many of their number were too spread out across the galaxy performing their missions when the deadly events of Isstvan V transpired. 

After carrying out countless missions on behalf of their primarch, attrition by this time had begun to take a serious toll on their numbers. Most who remained were grizzled veterans, supremely confident in their abilities. They were adept at moving silent and unseen through the shadows and attacking as and where they willed. They utilised a range of weapons and specialised equipment, but there were some that whispered that the Mor Deythan were possessed of a portion of the preternatural skills of their gene-father, the primarch Corax, including his ability to walk unseen even in full view of the foe. This ability had somehow been passed on to them through the unknowable processes of gene-seed implantation. 

Following the bloody events of Isstvan V, in which a large proportion of the Raven Guard Legion was lost, only a few dozen Mor Deythan were left alive. These few specialised elite warriors continued to fight on against the treachery of the Warmaster Horus and his Traitor Legions until the last of their numbers finally fell in service to the Emperor.

Era Indomitus Restoration

By the time of the Era Indomitus, the latent genetic ability to cloud the minds of their opponents so that they were rendered effectively invisible had become extremely rare among the Astartes of the Raven Guard. Nevertheless, some still displayed this rare genetic inheritance of their primarch.

To confront a series of difficult missions the Chapter was forced to undertake after the birth of the Great Rift left the Imperium struggling against a multitude of threats, Kayvaan Shrike, then still the Master of Shadows of the Raven Guard, re-established the Mor Deythan. Yet at its new inception, the Mor Deythan comprised only four new members.

Notable Era Indomitus Mor Deythan

Shadow Captain Illith - Illith is a shadow captain of the Raven Guard who developed the ancient psychic ability to make himself invisible to his foes that led former Chapter Master Kayvaan Shrike to select him as a member of the reborn Mor Deythan in the Era Indomitus. Before this appointment, Illith nearly met his end at the hands of a Genestealer when the Raven Guard were clearing out an infestation of those xenos within a mine. The Genestealer surprised Illith, disemboweled him and tore out one eye, but the Space Marine was able to kill the creature with his Combat Knife. Illith's injuries led to his assumed death by an Apothecary when his body was discovered next to that of the xenos. It was only when that Apothecary was about to extract Illith's gene-seed that it was discovered that Illith still lived. Illith eventually recovered from his critical injuries, but the attack left Illith with a scar that caused the left side of his mouth to curl into a permanent snarl. His missing eye was replaced by a flickering, red relic bionic orb that according to the Chapter's legends was created on Mars by the ancient Mechanicum during the Great Crusade. According to its legend, the bionic was worn by a legendary Raven Guard Legion shadow captain before it was lost on a battlefield. The bionic was later found by the Adeptus Mechanicus and eventually returned to the Raven Guard. Illith refused to confirm if this legend was true. After his ability to wield the power of the ancient Mor Deythan was discovered, then-Chapter Master Kayvaan Shrike selected Illith, Sergeant Artarix, Shadow Captain Aevar Qeld and the Knights of the Raven Captain Mordren, to be members of the newly restored order. The birth of the Great Rift had caused the Imperium to be beset by many new foes and Shrike intended to use the resurrected Mor Deythan's abilities to carry out targeted attacks against these threats wherever possible. The four new members of the Mor Deythan would be gathered together by him only to carry out such missions under Illith's command, and their first undertaking was eliminating the Alpha Legion Chaos Lord Mazik. Their second mission was to board a mag-train on the world of Delephron to assassinate an Ordo Hereticus Interrogator who had learned disastrous information that would draw the Inquisition's searching gaze to the Raven Guard. However, the Mor Deythan were not the only ones seeking the Interrogator, and the squad clashed with Nurglish Raptors who attacked the mag-train. The Mor Deythan were able to kill many of the Raptors and they eventually discovered a maddened astropath aboard the mag-train. From the psyker's repeated ramblings, they learned that the the Iron Ravens a Successor Chapter of the Raven Guard, which had been drawn into the Warp a thousand years before, had now returned. The astropath claimed, however, that their time in the Warp had corrupted the Iron Ravens to the service of Nurgle and the Mor Deythan realised that if the Inquisition learned this, it would throw suspicion upon the purity of the Raven Guard and all their other Successor Chapters. Comprehending that this was the information damaging to the Raven Guard the Interrogator had found they decided to take the maddened astropath to Kayvaan Shrike. However the Mor Deythan noticed that the Nurglish Raptors had strangely passed by the carriage and left the astropath alive. Illith worried that the Raptors were seeking something else on the mag-train, and ordered Artarix to stay with the astropath while the remaining Mor Deythan hunted down the Traitor Marines. When they caught up to the Raptors, the Mor Deythan saw the Chaos Space Marines were pursuing a fleeing figure. Illith charged Qeld with capturing that person before they escaped, while he and Mordren dealt with the Raptors. However Qeld was captured by the Raptors and borne away from the mag-train, while the fleeing figure escaped into another carriage. At that point Illith believed the survivors on the mag-train had been corrupted to Chaos by contact with the Nurglish Raptors and ordered them to be purged. As the Mor Deythan eliminated the corrupted travellers on the mag-train, the fleeing figure escaped from it. After the purgation of the mag-train was complete, Artarix and Mordren determined to find the captured Qeld, but Illith refused. He claimed their first priority was to warn Chapter Master Shrike of the Iron Raptors return and then they would begin the search for Qeld. The fleeing figure from the mag-train, however, turned out to be their original target, Interrogator Bledheim, who soon informed Inquisitor Sabbathiel of the nature of the maddened astropath's rantings.

Shadow Captain Aevar Qeld - Aevar Qeld is the current shadow captain of the Raven Guard Chapter's 5th Company and is known as the "Master of Truth". He was formally shadow captain of the Raven Guard's 7th Company as well as the Chapter's former "Master of Lies". Qeld was one of the few Raven Guard Astartes in the 41st Millennium to develop the ancient psychic ability shared by his primarch Corvus Corax to make himself invisible to his enemies. As a result, he was selected by then-Chapter Master Kayvaan Shrike to join the reborn Mor Deythan. During a mission with the Mor Deythan to a mag-train on the world of Delephron he was captured by Raptors in service to the Nurglish Chaos Space Marines of the Iron Ravens. His fellow Mor Deythan then sought his rescue at the next opportune moment.

Captain Mordren - Mordren is a Knights of the Raven captain and a member of the newly refounded Mor Deythan, the elite Raven Guard infantry that once existed during the Great Crusade and Horus Heresy, that the former Raven Guard Chapter Master Kayvaan Shrike assembled in the Era Indomitus. He served beside Sergeant Artarix and the Shadow Captains Aevar Qeld and Illith, in the newly restored Mor Daythan. Qeld, however, was incensed at Mordren's inclusion in the new unit, as the Knight of the Raven was not a member of the Raven Guard Chapter like all the others. The shadow captain felt Mordren was unworthy of being a part of the unit because of this, but Chapter Master Shrike ended such talk immediately. The enraged Chapter Master chastened Qeld by saying the Knights of the Raven were the Raven Guard's brothers and he would not allow any decisiveness between them to bloom in such dark times. The birth of the Great Rift had caused the Imperium to be beset by foes on every side and Shrike would use the Mor Deythan's psychic ability to avoid detection in targeted attacks that would topple those new threats. The four would only be gathered together by him for such missions, with Shadow Captain Illith normally in command, and their first undertaking was to eliminate the Alpha Legion Chaos Lord Mazik. It was during that mission that Qeld ended the misgivings he had about Mordren, after they each saved each others' lives while they fought the Chaos warlord. The Mor Deythan's second mission was boarding a mag-train on Delephron, in order to kill an Ordo Hereticus Interrogator who had learned disastrous information that would draw the Inquisition's gaze to the Raven Guard. They were not the only ones seeking the Interrogator though, and the Mor Deythan clashed with Nurglish Chaos Space Marine Raptors who attacked the mag-train. The Mor Deythan were able to kill many of the Raptors and Mordren's life was saved by Shadow Captain Qeld, who killed a Raptor that had borne the Knight of the Raven away from the mag-train. Mordren in turn saved Artarix's life by killing a Raptor that had impaled the sergeant. As the Mor Deythan searched for the Interrogator, they found a maddened astropath within one of the mag-train's carriages. From the psyker's repeated ramblings, they learned that a missing Raven Guard Second Founding Successor Chapter, the Iron Ravens, whose homeworld of Taltos had been drawn into the Warp a thousand standard years before, had now returned. The astropath claimed, however, that their time in the Warp had corrupted the Iron Ravens, turning them to the worship of the Chaos God Nurgle, which he claimed was something the Knights of the Raven knew about. Mordren denied these accusations, but they were not taken seriously by the others. The Mor Deythan knew, however, that the Inquisition's discovery of the Iron Ravens' Chaos corruption would turn their suspicions towards the Raven Guard. They realised that this was the information the Interrogator had found and they planned to immediately take the astropath to Kayvaan Shrike. However the Mor Deythan noticed that the Nurglish Raptors had passed by the carriage and yet the Traitors had left the astropath alive. Illith worried the Raptors, who might be the returned Iron Ravens, could be seeking something else on the mag-train, and ordered Artarix to stay with the astropath, while the remaining Mor Deythan advanced after the Traitors. When they caught up to the Raptors, the Mor Deythan could see the Traitors were pursuing a fleeing figure. Illith charged Qeld with capturing the person before they escaped, while he and Mordren dealt with the Raptors. However Qeld was captured by the Raptors and borne away from the mag-train, while the fleeing figure escaped into another carriage. By that point, Illith believed the survivors on the mag-train had been corrupted by the Nurglish Raptors and ordered them to be purged. While they did so, however, the fleeing figure escaped from the mag-train, without the Mor Deythan realising it. After the mag-train was purged, Artarix and Mordren wished to find the captured Qeld, but Illith refused. He claimed their first priority was to warn Chapter Master Shrike of the Iron Raptors' return and then they would begin the search for Qeld. The fleeing figure from the mag-train, however, turned out to be their original target, Interrogator Bledheim, who soon informed Inquisitor Sabbathiel of the maddened astropath's rantings.

Sergeant Artarix - Artarix was a sergeant of the Raven Guard who developed the ancient Mor Deythan's abilities and was assigned to serve with the reborn Mor Deythan of the Era Indomitus by then-Chapter Master Kayvaan Shrike.
