A Venatari Lance is a two-handed Power Weapon wielded by the elite Venatari Custodians of the Adeptus Custodes that also contains a single-barrel archeotech ballistic repeater known as a "Kinetic Destroyer" that is identical to an Archaeotech Pistol for ranged combat.

The Venatari Lance incorporates a lever-action example of the archaeotech Kinetic Destroyer. This ingenious design, borrowed from the ancient Enri Compendium gifted to Constantin Valdor by the Emperor of Mankind, allows the Kinetic Destroyer to be fired stably in quick succession without significant additional recoil. 

The lance allows the Venatari Custodian to maintain their keen marksmanship while moving against the enemies of the Emperor both at a great height and at great speeds.
