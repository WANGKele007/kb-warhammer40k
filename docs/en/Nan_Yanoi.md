The Nan Yanoi or "Sword Moon" is one of the two moons of Kaurava II, in the Kaurava System. It is the base of the T'au led by the Fire Caste commander, Shas'O Or'es'Ka. It was named after the mighty T'au Ar'Ka Cannon, which was built there. The moon's previous name was Orridune.
