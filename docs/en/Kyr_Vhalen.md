Kyr Vhalen, also called "Shatterblade" by his battle-brothers, was the commanding Warsmith of the Iron Warriors Legion's 77th Grand Battalion during the Great Crusade. He and his Legionaries were among the few Iron Warriors to remain loyal to the Emperor during the Horus Heresy and were crucial combatants during the Invasion of Paramar V in 006.M31. 

History

Origins

Like many of those caught up in the cataclysm of the Horus Heresy who were to earn fame or infamy during its dark passages of Human history, Kyr Vhalen's was a name of relative obscurity before the galactic civil war was to thrust upon him the mantle of greatness. He was neither Olympian nor Terran by birth, having been recruited as an adolescent from the formerly xenos-enslaved world of Meru at the edge of the Yetzirah Abyss. Initiated into the 77th Grand Battalion of the Iron Warriors Legion, he fought his way up through the ranks of the Legion by dint of excellence and sheer bloody will to survive, gaining the epithet of "Shatterblade" after fighting through a nine-solar-hour-long battle with the broken remains of a Xenarch sabre impaled through his chest. 

By the time the 77th Grand Battalion was deployed as part of the Therikon Suppression Taskforce, Vhalen had become the captain of its 2nd Company, and over eleven standard years of gruelling war during the Great Crusade ascended to eventually take its command as Warsmith. The 77th, like a number of Iron Warriors detachments dispersed across the Imperium and all but forgotten by their Legion's command, had become almost completely-self-sustaining by the end of the Great Crusade, and when the Horus Heresy erupted, he and his forces were utterly ignorant of their Legion's betrayal. At Paramar V, Vhalen and his Legionaries took bitter pride in their stubborn loyalty to the ideals of the Emperor's Great Crusade as brother turned against brother. 

Invasion of Paramar V

At the start of the Horus Heresy, the 77th Grand Battalion had been operating under their own aegis for more than four solar decades, conducting a seemingly unending campaign of suppression of the Therikon Wastes region of the galaxy where they had been left to garrison against the numerous xenos species that had made the trackless nebula of the wastes their home. Kyr Vhalen had returned with his troops to Paramar V for resupply as he had done many times before. Utterly ignorant that his Legion had by this time turned against the Emperor, he was determined that he would not be denied his rights to resupply. 

Thus, the attack of the Traitor Alpha Legion upon his forces at Paramar V in 006.M31 , including the battleship Tyche's Lament, came as a complete and unwelcome surprise. Extant records of the battle show that though his battleship's auspexes had been blinded and the bridge flamed about him in the wake of the Alpha Legion fleet's unexpected attack, it was Kyr Vhalen himself who threw aside his steersman and triggered the battleship's main drives at maximum power, shouting black and profane oaths aloud as the seven-kilometre-long warship hurtled like a burning spear at the Alpha Legion heavy assault cruiser Anax-Rho. 

Locked into firing solutions against the surface of Paramar V and the wounded Iron Warriors escorts, the Anax-Rho could not recover itself in the short moments that the Tyche's Lament devoured the distance between the two huge warships, and when its own main drives triggered to manoeuvre, it was already too late. The blunt-bladed prow of the Tyche's Lament struck the Anax-Rho amidships from directly below, shattering its void shields and smashing it aside in a storm of broken debris and venting atmosphere. The heavy assault cruiser -- itself forged to endure terrible damage -- had survived the devastating impact, barely, but now spun all but out of control, its own engine-thrust carrying it out of orbit in order to escape the scarred Iron Warriors monster which was already beginning to turn again for another attack run. However, before the Iron Warriors fleet could attack the wounded Alpha Legion flagship, Paramar V's own ground-based Defence Lasers fired, destroying the heavy cruiser and ending the Traitors' first assault upon the planet. 

Unfortunately, despite the defenders' best efforts, the Invasion of Paramar V ultimately ended in a Traitor victory. Vhalen was badly wounded during the conflict, but was saved from death by his battalion's Apothecaries, still cursing his enemies as he was dragged to safety within Paramar V's central Panopticon complex. However, when Dark Mechanicum forces entered the complex, they found no trace of Vhalen, his surviving Iron Warriors, or the Loyalist Mechanicum commander Suyria Nihhon. Oddly, the Alpha Legion declined to search further for the missing Loyalists, and left the planet. The forces of the Imperium later returned to Paramar V in an unsuccessful attempt to recapture it in 011.M31 during the Second Battle of Paramar V.
