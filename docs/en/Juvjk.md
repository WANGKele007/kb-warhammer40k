Juvjk, also called "Hearth-cant", is the name of the dialect of Low Gothic spoken on the Death World of Fenris, the homeworld of the Space Wolves Chapter of the Adeptus Astartes. The language is the everyday tongue spoken by the Human tribes of Fenris and the Space Wolves Astartes raised up from among them.

In Juvjk, the Space Wolves are known as the Vlka Fenryka or "Wolves of Fenris."
