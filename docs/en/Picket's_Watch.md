Picket's Watch is a Deathwatch watch station and star fort located close to the Damocles Gulf in the Eastern Fringe. Its forces are held in the fortress of Westkeep and are currently led by Watch Commander Jotunn. 

History

Though the Imperium's records do not detail the identity of the "Picket" that the fortress was named after, there is evidence that it was originally constructed in the Age of Strife and was discovered by the Imperial forces during the Great Crusade. At that time, the Imperial Fists Legion claimed ownership of the star fortress and built the fortress of Westkeep there atop the ancient ruins. The VII Legion used the fortress as an outpost to stand guard over the Damocles Gulf, but their reasons for doing so at that time have been lost to the ages. 

For millennia afterwards, the fortress was maintained only by servitors until the rise of the nearby T'au Empire. The T'au's rapid expansion in the 41st Milllennium soon led them into conflict with the Imperium and the outpost found itself on the front lines of the battles between the rival stellar empires. This led the Deathwatch to lay claim to what became its newest watch station, and they proceeded to heavily modify Westkeep's defences and weaponry. 

Despite these additions, Picket's Watch's chief defence remains the secrecy of its location. The fortress is readily capable of sustaining no more than three kill-teams at a time. Nonetheless, it is the current base of operations for the Second Chamber of Watch Fortress Talasa Prime. Three Astartes strike cruisers are known to operate out of Picket's Watch. These include the Nemesis, Robidoux and Troilus.

Naval Assets

Nemesis (Strike Cruiser) - The Nemesis is seconded to Picket's Watch and is commanded by Shipmaster Loni Ferral.

Robidoux (Strike Cruiser) - In 999.M41 the Robidoux was commanded by Kill-team Orion when they were charged with raiding one of the T'au Empire's travelling emissary craft. However, while they successfully boarded the T'au spacecraft, the mission ended in failure when the kill-team were trapped aboard it by the T'au's Fire Warriors. With no other Deathwatch Astartes aboard the Robidoux to reinforce, the strike cruiser's mortal crew sent an astropathic message back to Picket's Watch, detailing the kill-team's predicament. The watch station was also informed that the Robidoux would now be travelling to the nearest Mandeville Point, but Picket's Watch had no further contact with the strike cruiser after that last message.

Troilus (Strike Cruiser) - The Troilus was a six hundred-standard-year-old Deathwatch strike cruiser that had been heavily modified with extra ablative armour on its flanks, a suite of plasma generators and three dorsal lance batteries. It was seconded to Watch Station Picket's Watch and commanded by Kill-team Primus when they were ordered to destroy a suspected T'au Empire communication relay in the Sexton System in 999.M41. However, when they arrived at the relay's location, on the moon QX-937, the kill-team instead found a hidden T'au base, filled with experimental T'au Battlesuits. The T'au spotted Kill-team Primus and killed them before they could escape or warn the Troilus of what they had found. Left unaware of the kill-team's deaths, the Troilus waited for word from the kill-team, and was later destroyed by T'au naval forces before the ancient strike cruiser could fire a single shot. Sometime later, Watch Captain Nergui of Picket's Watch came searching Kill-team Primus and discovered their fate as well as the wreckage of the Troilus. He made a note of the wreck's location and when Nergui returned to Picket's Watch with news of the kill-team's death, the watch captain sent a reclamation craft to tow the Troilus's remains back to the watch station. What happened to the wreck of the strike cruiser afterwards is unknown.
