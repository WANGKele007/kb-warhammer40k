The Accipiters is a Codex Astartes-compliant Loyalist Space Marine Chapter of unknown Founding and genetic origin.

Chapter Appearance

Chapter Colours

The Accipiters Chapter primarily wears black power armour with a black backpack. The arms, shoulder plates and helmet are blue-grey.

The Aquila or Imperialis on the chest armour is dark red.

The white squad tactical specialty symbol -- battleline, close support, fire support, Veteran or command -- is indicated on the right shoulder plate.

The squad iconography on the right knee guard indicates squad number. Company number is indicated by a white High Gothic numeral centred on the left knee guard.

Chapter Badge

The Accipiters' Chapter badge is a white Imperial Aquila icon with its wings elevated on a field of blue-grey.

Trivia

Accipiter is a genus of birds of prey in the family Accipitridae. Most species in the genus are called goshawks or sparrowhawks, although all New World species of the genus are simply known as "hawks".
