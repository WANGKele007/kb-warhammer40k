The Shatter Corps is a Chaos Space Marine warband of the Iron Warriors Traitor Legion. Its Heretic Astartes are led by the Warsmith Madrydon Drados. The Shatter Corps is known to be active in the Warp rift known as the Screaming Vortex.

History

The Shatter Corps contains numerous warriors, as well as a great many powerful war machines that were recently liberated from the nearby Spinward Front and have since been corrupted into Daemon Engines due to the efforts of the warband's Warpsmith, Gracix. Under the command of the Warsmith Madrydon Drados, the Shatter Corps seeks to destroy the Imperium's many fortifications and fortresses throughout the sectors that lay near the Screaming Vortex. By doing so, Drados believes the Imperium will soon topple beneath its own weight and leave itself open to be attacked by the Forces of&nbsp;Chaos.
