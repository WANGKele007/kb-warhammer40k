Vortex Weapons are some of the most deadly weapons ever created by the technologically advanced intelligent species of the Milky Way galaxy. While they come in many different shapes, sizes and delivery vehicles, all Vortex Weapons operate on the same basic principle of ripping open a gaping Warp rift into the Immaterium within a given volume of realspace. 

As with a naturally-occurring black hole, anything that comes in contact with this rift, whether matter or energy, is effectively wiped from existence as it is sucked into the nightmare dimension known as the Immaterium and transformed into the psychic energy that comprises the Warp. 

Along with their destructive potential, Vortex Weapons are also unpredictable, as the Warp rift created by a detonation can travel randomly across a battlefield or a given volume of space until it eventually vanishes when there is not enough ambient psychic energy present to maintain it. As a result of this unpredictability, the employment of Vortex Weapons is always risky, as likely to end up damaging their users as the targeted foe.

Imperial Vortex Weapons

Vortex Bolts - Very uncommon, Vortex Bolts are Vortex Weapons that have been constructed in the form of bolter ammunition. Crafted long ago in forgotten times, these immensely rare bolts create miniature Warp vortexes within the target upon detonation. Such an event causes catastrophic damage to even the largest enemies and psykers who miraculously survive are driven mad by the creatures of the Warp that pour from the tear in reality.

Vortex Grenade - Taking the form of a weighty sphere the size of a fist and often shaped like a Human skull, Vortex Gernades are most commonly used by Inquisitors, agents of the Officio Assassinorum and senior officers of Space Marine Chapters. When detonated, a Vortex Grenade opens a Warp rift on the battlefield in a small volume of space, usually instantly annihilating its targets. Like all Vortex Weapons, these very rare grenades are extremely unpredictable, and the rift they open can easily begin moving in unexpected directions, consuming friends as well as the foe.

Vortex Warhead - Consisting of a Vortex Weapon attached to an intercontinental ballistic missile, Vortex Warheads are equipped by Titans, Deathstrike Missile Launchers, and various fortress hardpoints.

Vortex Torpedo - Mounted on Imperial warships, Vortex Torpedoes can unleash voidship-shredding Warp rifts in a small radius upon detonation.

Rift Shell - Used by the Nova Cannons of Imperial warships, Rift Shells create fleet-consuming Warp rifts. As such, they are similar to Vortex Torpedoes but possess a far larger area of effect.

Annihilator Cannon - Mounted on the warships of the Dark Angels Legion during the Horus Heresy, the Annihilator Cannon discharged a bolt of Warp energy which created a momentary Warp rift around the target before collapsing and causing anything caught within it to disappear into the Immaterium.

Void Munitions - Taking the form of special tank and artillery shells used by the Dreadwing of the Dark Angels Legion during the Great Crusade and Horus Heresy, these Vortex Weapons created destructive interdimensional vortexes before detonating in an implosion that dragged anything nearby into the Warp.

Aeldari Vortex Weapons

D-Cannon - Also known as Distortion Cannons, D-Cannons use the same technology employed by the Warp-Drive to create a hole in space-time leading into the Immaterium. The target of the D-Cannon will either be torn apart by gravitational forces from the weapon, sucked into the Warp, or transported to a random location like inside a star or the vacuum of space.

D-scythe and Heavy D-scythe - Also known as a Distortion Scythe, this weapon is a form of Aeldari Distortion Weapon, and a variant of a Vortex Weapon. Considered to be a taboo weapon among the Craftworld Aeldari, a D-scythe can force the soul of its target from their physical body and banish it into the Warp, a truly frightful proposition for the Aeldari, who so fear the consumption of their souls by Slaanesh. When fired, a D-scythe emits no visible blast and leaves behind no damage, save the suddenly lifeless corpse of its target. The Aeldari call the act of separating the spirit from its body the "Cutting of the Crone's Cord," for in Aeldari Mythology a strand ties the soul of a being to its material form in realspace. The Heavy D-scythe is simply a more potent version of the standard D-scythe commonly mounted under the wings of Hemlock Wraithfighters. Originally designed to sever the psychic link between Tyranids and the Hive Mind, these powerful weapons can even scramble the electronic engrams of purely mechanical beings such as the Necrons.

Wraithcannon - A Wraithcannon is similar to the D-Cannon except smaller, more portable, more tightly controlled and more accurate. 

Vortex Torpedo - Like the Imperium, the warships of both the Craftworld Aeldari and Drukhari utilise Vortex Torpedoes.

Ork Vortex Weapons

Vortex Torpedo - The Orks will sometimes utilise Vortex Torpedoes which they have looted from other starfaring species that possess them.
