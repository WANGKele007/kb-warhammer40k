A Plague Spewer is a type of plague weapon favoured by the servants of the Plague God Nurgle, particularly the Blightlord Terminators of the Death Guard Traitor Legion. The Plague Spewer can unleash a noxious brew of Nurgle-crafted daemonic pathogens and toxins upon any foes who come within range.
