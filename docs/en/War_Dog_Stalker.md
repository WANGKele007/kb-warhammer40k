A War Dog Stalker is a variant of the Armiger Pattern Knight known as a War Dog that serves the Chaos Knights as multi-role skirmishers. Only the most strong-willed of minor Fallen Nobles have the mental fortitude to pilot a War Dog Stalker. 

Like pack alphas, they often lead other War Dogs in carefully planned attacks, harrying the foe's flanks, butchering reinforcements and hunting down lone threats. They unleash salvoes of fire at range or in the heart of combat with deadly claw strikes.
