The Executor Bolt Rifle is a variant of the Mark II Cawl Pattern Bolt Rifle primarily used by Heavy Intercessors. It has a longer range, does more damage and is capable of greater armour penetration than the standard pattern Bolt Rifle.

It is similar in appearance to the Heavy Bolt Rifle and Hellstorm Bolt Rifle variants of the Cawl Pattern Bolt Rifle, save for its elongated, box-shaped magazine in which the bolt ammunition is exposed and which is embossed with a skull and crossbones.
