HY-Pex Mines are a type of high-yield penetrative explosive mine used by the warriors of the Leagues of Votann that are perfect for ripping open fortress walls and vehicle armour alike. They are often used by Hernkyn Yaegir Ironbraek sappers and explosives specialists.
