The Silver Stars was once a Loyalist Space Marine Chapter of unknown Founding and origin. Almost nothing is known about this Chapter in current Imperial records except for the dire fate that it succumbed to. Unable to recover enough of the precious Progenoid Glands of their fallen Battle-Brothers, the Silver Stars could not cultivate further Astartes organ implants to create future generations of Space Marines for the Chapter. The Silver Stars soon died off from attrition in combat, unable to replace their losses. Like its counterpart, the Sons of Gorgax, this Chapter is now no more than a memory, the only evidence of its existence a dry footnote within the Index Astartes.
