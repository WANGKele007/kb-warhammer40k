Psyk-out Munitions were Psi-Reactive ammunition and anti-personnel explosive devices commonly found within the ranks of Legion Destroyer Squads during the Great Crusade and Horus Heresy eras.

Originally an anti-psyker weapon solely produced and utilised by the Orders Militant of the Great Astronomican, the use of these munitions, whose payload was akin to psychically-charged ash, quickly spread to the more specialised echelons of the Space Marine Legions.

By the later years of the Horus Heresy, the use of Psyk-out Munitions by Legion Destroyers was almost commonplace in war zones plagued by esoteric, Warp-related phenomenon or the rogue psykers sometimes deployed by Traitor forces in a manner similar to unguided bombs.
