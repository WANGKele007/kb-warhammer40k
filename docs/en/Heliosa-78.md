Heliosa-78 was the high matriarch of the remaining members of the Selenar gene-cults that still existed on Luna at the time of the Solar War and the Siege of Terra in 014.M31. 

This version of the original Selenar high matriarch was arrogant and corrupt, determined to maintain her power on Luna through continued control of the gene-cults' vital role in the creation of new Space Marines for the Legiones Astartes.

After the start of the Solar War for control of the Sol System during the Horus Heresy, the Primarch Rogal Dorn, the Praetorian of Terra, ordered Heliosa-78 to destroy the technology the Selenar possessed for the creation of new Astartes gene-seed, lest it fall into the hands of the Warmaster Horus' Traitor Legions.

However, once the Sons of Horus under the command of First Captain Ezekyle Abaddon launched their conquest of Luna, Heliosa-78 hesitated and ultimately refused to carry out the primarch's order.

Instead, to maintain her own position, Heliosa-78 swore loyalty to the Warmaster and pledged to use the Selenar's expertise and technology to create new Heretic Astartes for his Space Marine Legions. It was this betrayal of Selenar ideals that led the fugitive gene-witch Ta'lab Vita-37, who had secretly been protecting the ancient Selenar artefact known as the Magna Mater since the First Pacification of Luna two hundred standard years before, to seek the aid of the Loyalists in keeping the device out of Horus' hands.
