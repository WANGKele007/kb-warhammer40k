Crimson Gulf is a Hold World of the Greater Thurian League of the Leagues of Votann located in the galactic core. It is situated in the central region of Thurian space and is a major trade hub for the Leagues. Crimson Gulf is famed among the Kin alongside its fellow Thurian Hold World Ûrvymm's Bulwark for its sheer size and nearly impenetrable defences.
