The Blood Dragons are a Loyalist Space Marine Chapter and a successor of the Blood Angels, created during an unknown Founding.

Chapter History

The Blood Dragons' name is one feared rather than celebrated by many Imperial worlds.

The silhouettes of their many gunships are not a sign of the Emperor's deliverance, but of imminent doom, for the Blood Dragons care little for the citizens of the Imperium.

Instead, they desire nothing less than the total extermination of the Emperor's foes, believing that He will save the souls of the innocent and destroy those of the guilty.

Chapter Appearance

Chapter Colours

The Blood Dragons primarily wear dark red power armour with black shoulder pauldron trim. 

The Aquila or Imperialis on the chest is golden.

Like their progenitors, the Blood Dragons also utilise the same unique blood drop heraldry to designate company number, which is indicated on the right shoulder pauldron.

The left shoulder pauldron proudly displays the Chapter's iconography.

The heraldry on the left knee plate indicates squad designation.

Chapter Badge

The Blood Dragons' Chapter badge is a large black dragon's head with a single, bright red droplet of blood falling from its maw, centred on a field of dark red.
