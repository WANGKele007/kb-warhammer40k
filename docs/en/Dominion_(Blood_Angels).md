Dominion was an officer rank used in the ancient Blood Angels Legion during the Great Crusade and the Horus Heresy to designate an officer who commanded a company, esentially equivalent to the rank of captain in the other Space Marine Legions. 

The Blood Angels, as with many of the other Legiones Astartes, had their own titles and names for authority and a host of lesser divisions to suit their style. Dominions ranked just below officers known as "Archeins," who were the Blood Angels' equivalent to the other Legions' praetors, more senior officers who usually commanded chapter-strength units of approximately 1,000 Astartes, known instead as a "Host" among the ranks of the IX Legion.
