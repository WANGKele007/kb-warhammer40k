The Inquisition War is an omnibus novel containing the full Inquisition War series of novels by Ian Watson. 

Synopsis

The Inquisition War is the classic story of Inquisitor Jaq Draco and his desperate mission to uncover a plot that will bring Mankind to its knees before the Dark Gods. 

Written by award-winning author Ian Watson, The Inquisition War explores areas of the Warhammer 40,000 mythology that few others authors have dared to tackle! Collected together for the very first time, the seminal trilogy of Draco, Harlequin, Chaos Child and two linking short stories make the legend of the Inquisition War complete!

Contents

The Inquisition War includes the following novels and short stories:
The Alien Beast Within (Short Story)
Draco
Warped Stars (Short Story)
Harlequin
Chaos Child
