Feeder Tendrils are special organs possessed by some Tyranid bioforms such as Lictors and Ymgarl Genestealers that enable them to drain their dead victims' brain matter and cerebrospinal fluid, absorbing all of their memories and learned knowledge in the process. 

This provides the Tyranids with crucial information about an enemy's strengths and weaknesses, along with invaluable intelligence and reconaissance information about a world whose biosphere has been targeted for absorption by a Tyranid hive fleet.
