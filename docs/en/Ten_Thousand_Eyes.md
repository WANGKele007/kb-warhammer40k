The Ten Thousand Eyes is a great warhost comprised of multiple Chaos Space Marine warbands active in the Imperium Nihilus region of the Ultima Segmentum during the Era Indomitus. They were led by the Sorcerer Lord Seraphax, one of the Fallen Angels.

Seraphax and his warhost became the first major opponents of the Dark Angels Primarch Lion El'Jonson after his reawakening beneath The Rock in the 41st Millennium and the beginning of his travels to new worlds through the sub-realm of the Immaterium known as Mirror-Caliban.

Warhost History

Origins

Seraphax was originally a member of the garrison of the Dark Angels Legion left behind under the command of Luther on Caliban during the Horus Heresy to oversee the Legion's homeworld. When Caliban was destroyed at the end of the Heresy, Seraphax was one of the Fallen who was cast through time and space in the Immaterium as Caliban was destroyed by a great Warp storm.

Seraphax reemerged into history as a formidable warrior and charismatic leader who led a brutal insurrection against the Imperial planetary government of the world of Bast and made himself its heretical lord governor. He ruled over Bast for over a standard century, terrorising its people and gathering other Heretics to his rule. 

However, eventually the Imperium received word of the fall of Bast to Renegade forces. When the Dark Angels learned of what had happened on Bast, the Chapter recognised Seraphax as one of their Fallen and dispatched a strike force commanded by Interrogator-Chaplain Razyl to capture the sorcerer. As the Dark Angels began what proved to be the ultimately successful invasion and liberation of Bast, Seraphax fled off-world rather than be brought back in chains to the dungeons of The Rock to face the tender mercies of the Interrogator-Chaplains.

By the time of the birth of the Great Rift in the Era Indomitus, Seraphax had become the Sorcerer Lord of a great warhost of Chaos Space Marines known as the Ten Thousand Eyes, alongside his lieutenants and fellow Chaos Lords Markog and the Fallen Baelor. In the chaos of the Imperium Nihilus, the Ten Thousand Eyes were soon able to seize control of an interstellar pocket empire that encompassed multiple Imperial worlds. Among these planets was the Jungle World of Camarth, where the Dark Angels Primarch Lion El'Jonson first reappeared after awakening from his long sleep beneath The Rock and beginning his journeys through the sub-realm of the Warp he knew as Mirror-Caliban.

Bloodrage Project

The Ruby Crescents Chapter, genetic successors of the Blood Angels, had long maintained a fortified outpost known as Redmoon Keep on Camarth. The garrison of the keep was left there to watch over the people of that world, who greatly respected and honored the Adeptus Astartes who walked among them. But when the Great Rift was born in the Era Indomitus, the Ruby Crescents of Carmath found themselves cut off from the rest of their Chapter as Carmath was located in the Imperium Nihilus. During this time, Camarth was assaulted by the Ten Thousand Eyes, the warhost of Heretic Astartes commanded by Seraphax. The Chaos forces overwhelmed the now-isolated garrison at Redmoon Keep and claimed the world for their own as part of their pocket empire.

In truth, Seraphax had deliberately targetted the Ruby Crescents because they were so isolated and easily overcome. The Sorcerer Lord wished to use Space Marines of the Blood Angels' genetic lineage as fodder for a research endeavour he termed the "Bloodrage Project." This was an attempt to turn the Flaw in the gene-seed of all the sons of Sanguinius against them so that the mindless ferocity of the Black Rage and the Red Thirst would become all-consuming, transforming all the Astartes of the Sanguinary Brotherhood into weapons to be used against the Imperium.

The Ten Thousand Eyes took control of Redmoon Keep and imprisoned nine of the surviving Ruby Crescents who had been captured at high cost to the warband in one of the fortress' cells. The Captives' blood was drained from them into a giant vat placed before them, where it was transformed by Seraphax's Chaos sorcery into a glowing liquid that he hoped could be used to initiate the Flaw in any of Sanguinius' scions. 

The imprisoned Ruby Crescents were later discovered by the Dark Angels Primarch Lion El'Jonson, who had found his way to Camarth through the Immaterium's sub-realm of Mirror-Caliban after his awakening beneath The Rock, and the redeemed Fallen Zabriel, now one of The Risen who had been forgiven by their primarch after being encountered on Camarth. The two sought to redeem Camarth for the Imperium, and had sought to expel the Ten Thousand Eyes from Redmoon Keep when they discovered the secret research being conducted in its dungeons.

The Lion and Zabriel tried to free the Ruby Crescents, but each of the Space Marines had fallen to the Red Thirst and tried to attack their rescuers. Unaware of the nature of the Red Thirst which afflicted his brother Sanguinius' gene-sons, the Lion simply assumed they had already been corrupted by Chaos and granted them the Emperor's Peace.

Then the Lion and Zabriel sabotaged the generatorium of the Redmoon Keep and destroyed it in a great explosion, which brought to an end the Bloodrage Project.

Later, the Lion and Zabriel helped unite and lead the people of Camarth against the remaining Chaos forces to reclaim their world. Afterwards, its people joined the Lion's Protectorate in the Imperium Nihilus and provided contingents of the mortal paramilitary militia under the primarch's command known as the Lion Guard.

Defeat by the Lion

Yet, rather than fear at the sudden emergence of a primarch to oppose his schemes, Seraphax welcomed Lion El'Jonson's return, for he saw it as an opportunity to carry out a plan to ensure the Emperor's ultimate victory over Chaos. Seraphax lured the Lion to the world of Sable in the Imperium Nihilus, not far from Camarth, by having his Ten Thousand Eyes warhost butcher much of that world's population, knowing that this massacre of the innocent would eventually draw the primarch to oppose him. 

When the primarch arrived at Sable through the Mirror-Caliban sub-realm of the Immaterium as expected, Seraphax was able to capture him by binding the Lion in psychic chains summoned by his sorcery. Seraphax revealed to the captive El'Jonson his grand plan to save Humanity -- he would force the Lion's soul from his body and then use his sorcerous powers to take control of the primarch's corpse as his undead puppet. Using the Lion's return as a pretext for the second returned Loyalist primarch to gain an audience with the Emperor on Terra, Seraphax would use that audience to have his puppet strike down the Master of Mankind upon the Golden Throne, which would finally free Him from His chains of flesh to fully ascend as a new god in the Warp. Fully ascended, Seraphax believed that the Emperor would then possess the power to both destroy the Chaos Gods and all of the remaining xenos threats to Humanity in the galaxy, such as the Tyranids and Orks.

However, once the ritual of soul-banishment began, the Lion was able to resist its effects long enough for The Risen, the Fallen Angels he had met on his journeys and whose crimes he had forgiven, to arrive and assault the Ten Thousand Eyes on Sable, eventually disrupting the ritual and allowing the primarch to free himself. 

During the combat between the Lion and the crazed Sorcerer Lord that ensued, Seraphax called upon the Warp to transform himself into a bestial mutant form, but was slain by his own lieutenant Baelor who stabbed him from behind. Baelor was a Fallen Angel himself, who saw the return of his primarch as a chance to gain his own redemption and become one of The Risen. He also saw Seraphax's mutation in his fight with the primarch as the final proof he needed that his friend had fallen too far to the corruption of Chaos to be allowed to survive.

After Seraphax's death, Baelor asked for and received the forgiveness of the Lion, joining the ranks of The Risen for a short time. However, Baelor's body was slowly degrading under the influence of the Daemonsword Seraphax had gifted him long before, so he requested and the Lion granted him the quick and noble release of the Emperor's Peace. 

Sable was restored to its surviving people and became part of the Lion's Protectorate in the Imperium Nihilus. The warhost of the Ten Thousand Eyes was scattered with the loss of its leaders, its survivors fleeing off-world.

Notable Ten Thousand Eyes

Sorcerer Lord Seraphax - Seraphax was a Fallen Angel of the Dark Angels Legion and the Sorcerer Lord of the Ten Thousand Eyes warhost of Heretic Astartes that operated in the Imperium Nihilus region of the Ultima Segmentum. Like many of the Fallen, Seraphax did not believe himself to be a Traitor to the Emperor, but instead sought to ultimately use the power of Chaos as a means to better the lot of Humanity. Seraphax was originally a member of the garrison of the Dark Angels Legion left behind under the command of Luther on Caliban during the Horus Heresy to oversee the Legion's homeworld. When Caliban was destroyed at the end of the Heresy, Seraphax was one of the Fallen who was cast through time and space in the Immaterium as Caliban was destroyed by a great Warp storm. Seraphax reemerged into history as a formidable warrior and charismatic leader who led a brutal insurrection against the Imperial planetary government of the world of Bast and made himself its heretical lord governor. He ruled over Bast for over a standard century, terrorising its people and gathering other Heretics to his rule. However, eventually the Imperium received word of the fall of Bast to Renegade forces. In the chaos of the Imperium Nihilus, the Ten Thousand Eyes were soon able to seize control of an interstellar pocket empire that encompassed multiple Imperial worlds. Seraphax was slain on the world of Sable by Lion El'Jonson after the Dark Angels primarch's awakening in the Era Indomitus. Drawn to Sable through the sub-realm of the Immaterium known as Mirror-Caliban by the Sorcerer Lord's machinations as part of an insane plan to use the primarch's undead body to overthrow the Chaos Gods, Seraphax was ultimately slain during his duel with the primarch as a mutated beast by his own lieutenant Baelor, who stabbed him through the back. Baelor, also one of the Fallen, believed that Seraphax had finally been too corrupted by Chaos to save and also sought his primarch's forgiveness before the Lion granted him the Emperor's Peace. During his youth on Caliban as a Dark Angel Seraphax possessed red hair the colour of hungry flames. After his fall to Chaos, his hair mutated into actual bright, yellow flames that covered the entire left half of his face as well as his head. These flames caused him no pain nor did they harm any other part of his body. 

Baelor - Baelor, also known as "Baelor the Imposter," is a Fallen Angel and Chaos Lord. While hunting for him unsuccessfully in the late 40th Millennium in the Nephilim Sector, the Dark Angels discovered the lost STC template for the advanced "Lionheart Engine" which allowed the Chapter to create the Nephilim Jetfighter, named for the sector where the discovery was made. The new technology was used to modify older Imperial fighter designs by the Chapter to create the Dark Angels' signature interceptor. By the time of the Great Rift's birth during the Era Indomitus, Baelor had come to serve as the second-in-command of the Heretic Astartes warhost known as the Ten Thousand Eyes under the command of his fellow Fallen, the Sorcerer Lord Seraphax. Seraphax used his sorcerous abilities to keep Baelor free of mutation, and both still saw themselves as working for the betterment of Humanity through the use of the power of Chaos. When the Primarch Lion El'Jonson reemerged on Camarth, Baelor was disturbed by Seraphax's plan to capture the primarch and use him as a means to kill the Emperor and elevate the Master of Mankind through His death to the status of a new god in the Warp who could defeat the Chaos Gods and all of Humanity's xenos enemies. While Seraphax succeeded in capturing The Lion, those of the Fallen he had met on his journeys and forgiven, now known as The Risen, arrived to interfere with Seraphax's plans. Seraphax called upon the Warp to transform himself into a bestial mutant form, but was slain by Baelor who stabbed him from behind. Baelor saw the return of his primarch as a chance to gain his own redemption and become one of The Risen. He also saw Seraphax's mutation in his fight with the primarch as the final proof he needed that his friend had fallen too far to the corruption of Chaos to be allowed to survive. After Seraphax's death, Baelor asked for and received the forgiveness of the Lion, joining the ranks of The Risen for a short time. However, Baelor's body was slowly degrading under the influence of the Daemonsword Seraphax had gifted him long before, so he requested and the Lion granted him the quick and noble release of the Emperor's Peace. 

Chaos Lord Markog - Markog was the Chaos Lord of the Dolorous Guard warband of Chaos Space Marines who served as the honour guard of Seraphax after they joined the Sorcerer Lord's warhost of the Ten Thousand Eyes. Markog was originally an officer of the Ashen Blades Chapter of the Adeptus Astartes known for his intense pride. When the Ashen Blades' Chapter fleet was trapped in the Immaterium by the sudden onset of a Warp storm, the Chapter's Space Marines were consumed by infighting as Chaos slowly claimed them. When the survivors finally reemerged into realspace aboard their battle barge Blade of Truth after an unknown subjective amount of time trapped in the Warp, they had become Heretic Astartes commanded by Markog, who was now their Chaos Lord and a Chaos Champion of Slaanesh who had been drawn to the Dark Prince by his pride and ego. It was Markog who renamed the survivors the Dolorous Guard and eventually brought them to join the warhost of the Ten Thousand Eyes commanded by the Sorcerer Lord Seraphax. Seraphax welcomed the Dolorous Guard, making the Blade of Truth the flagship of his Chaos fleet and naming Markog's warband his honour guard. Markog greatly respected the power of the Sorcerer Lord and gladly followed his every command. The Chaos Lord was suited to his new role, for as a Slaaneshi Champion he had been gifted the Daemon Axe Heartdrinker by his divine patron. As long as he held the weapon, Markog was able to heal any wound dealt to him and also teleport the Dolorous Guard through the Warp to wherever they needed to be. However, Markog's corruption by the Prince of Chaos had left him with intense cravings to feel the pleasure engendered by consuming Human flesh, especially those of his fellow Astartes. During the battle between the Ten Thousand Eyes and the returned Lion El'Jonson and his redeemed Fallen Angels known as The Risen on the world of Sable, Markog was slain by a group of The Risen that included the former Fallen Zabriel, Guain, Borz, Galad, and Launciel.

Dark Apostle Jai'tana - Jai'tana was once a Chaplain of the Ashen Blades Chapter and was attached to the command of the officer Markog on the battle barge Blade of Truth when the Chapter's fleet was trapped in the Immaterium by a Warp storm. During the infighting among the Ashen Blades that erupted as they slowly fell to Chaos, the Chapter's Master of Sanctity was killed by Jai'tana for opposing the service of the Ruinous Powers. After the surviving Ashen Blades returned to realspace under the command of Markog, who had become a Chaos Champion of Slaanesh during their time in the Warp, he formed the Ashen Blades' survivors into the warband of Heretic Astartes he renamed the Dolorous Guard. Jai'tan now served as their Dark Apostle, for he had wholly accepted the worship of the Chaos Gods during his time in the Warp. After the Dolorous Guard joined Seraphax's Ten Thousand Eyes warhost as the Sorcerer Lord's honour guard, Jai'tana rose to become a powerful leader among the warhost. He has largely cast off the authority of Markog and heeds only the commands of Seraphax and what he believes to be the will of the Dark Gods.

Warhost Fleet

Blade of Truth (Battle Barge) - The Blade of Truth was the flagship of the Ten Thousand Eyes warhost. The battle barge was once part of the fleet of the Ashen Blades, until that entire Chapter was corrupted by Chaos after its fleet was caught in a Warp storm. Once the Ashen Blades escaped back into realspace, the former Ashen Blades officer Markog, now a Chaos Lord, remained in command of the Blade of Truth and its surviving Astartes. It was Markog who led the remaining Ashen Blades into becoming the Heretic Astartes warband known as the Dolorous Guard. Under Markog's command, the Dolorous Guard eventually joined the Ten Thousand Eyes warhost as the honour guard of its Sorcerer Lord Seraphax, who made the Blade of Truth the flagship of his own Chaos fleet.

Eye of Malevolence (Class Unknown) - The Eye of Malevolence was a warship of the Ten Thousand Eyes' Chaos fleet.

Dread Sentinel (Strike Cruiser) - The Dread Sentinel was a warship of the Ten Thousand Eyes' Chaos fleet that had been captured from the Angels of Vigilance.
