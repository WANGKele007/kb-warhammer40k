House Althalos is a Loyalist Imperial Knight House of the Questor Imperialis and thus serves the Imperium directly. There is very little information in Imperial records about this Knight House, and it is unknown from which Knight World it hails.

House History

Notable Campaigns

Defence of Hexis Alpha (Unknown Date.M41) - Along with the Knights of House Mortan and House Thalmus, House Althalos came to the aid of the Hive World Hexis Alpha, when a Warp rift spawned a tide of Daemons across its surface. More than 100 billion lives were saved, due to their successful efforts in defending the Hive World from the Daemons.
