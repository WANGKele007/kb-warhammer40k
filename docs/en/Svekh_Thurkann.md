Svêkh Thûrkann is a kâhl of the Greater Thurian League's Kindred of Vôrtun. She led one of the Oathbands under the overall command of Kâhl Ûthar the Destined that was sent to the Kin World of Törg by her home Kindred during the Battle of Örgvayr to rescue its Cthonian Guild miners from an assault launched by the Heretic Astartes of the World Eaters and The Purge who had emerged out of Warp Storm Örgvayr after the birth of the Great Rift. 

The Kin launched a massive drop offensive into the world's Shatterfields. Ûthar himself led an armoured spearhead against the World Eaters commanded by the World Eater Chaos Lord Hakatar. Kâhl Thûrkann and her Oathband met The Purge head-on. As orbital fire from both sides rained down onto the planet's surface, the two swirling battles erupted into a storm of close-range firefights and brutal offensives. Despite losses, the Thurians ultimately succeeded in rescuing both the miners and much of their valuable harvested raw materials.
