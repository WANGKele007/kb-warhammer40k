The Eye of Wrath is a unique Aeldari monocular device worn over one eye, that can unleash a tempest of lightning upon those under its gaze.

This ingenious directed energy weapon is utilised by Prince Yriel, the brilliant, if somewhat headstrong, scion of House Ulthanash and Autarch and High Admiral of Craftworld Iyanden.
