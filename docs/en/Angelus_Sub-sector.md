The Angelus Sub-sector is a sub-sector of the Scarus Sector, which is in its turn part of the Segmentum Obscurus. The sub-sector's capital is the Imperial world of Eustis Majoris, and it governs the planets Lenk, Encage, Rigyion, Quenthus XIII, Fedru, Malinter, Ledspar, Flint, Bostol, Gaxton, Tancred, Sancour, Mirepoix, Heveron, Hasarna, Lamsarotte and Ingeran.
