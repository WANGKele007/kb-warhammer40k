A Leviathan Grav-Flux Bombard is a heavy ranged weapon usable by Imperial Leviathan Pattern Siege Dreadnoughts. A Leviathan Grav-flux Bombard creates a spiralling vortex of gravitation-torsion force, and is a fortress-breaching weapon which can reduce the toughest armour to twisted scrap and crush anything living beyond recognition.
