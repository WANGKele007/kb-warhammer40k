The Feast of the Emperor's Ascenion is one of the only galaxy-wide holidays celebrated on most worlds of the Imperium of Man with a strong Ecclesiarchy presence. It is a religious holiday that celebrates the moment, ten thousand standard years ago, when the Emperor of Mankind was interred within the Golden Throne and shed His mortal existence to return to His full divinity, watching over and protecting Humanity from all threats, spiritual and physical. 

On many worlds this is a solar-week-long event, marked by feasting, games and spectacles to honour the Emperor and the great sacrifice He made for His people. 

History

The Feast of the Emperor's Ascension commemorates the final day of the Siege of Terra during the Horus Heresy when the Emperor defeated and slew His traitorous gene-son, the primarch Horus, aboard his flagship the Vengeful Spirit in orbit of Terra. 

Though in fact the duel left the Emperor mortally wounded, which required His shattered body to be placed within the life support mechanisms of the Golden Throne and ended His ability to directly communicate with Humanity, the theology of the Imperial Cult tells a different story. The priests of the Ecclesiarchy teach that the Emperor offered up His mortal life as a great sacrifice to save Humanity from the horrors perpetuated by Horus and his Traitors. In this view, the Emperor's internment within the Golden Throne allowed His soul to ascend back to His proper place in the divine heavens as the god of Mankind where He now guides and protects His people from all threats to the lives and souls, including Heretics, mutants, witches and xenos.

In the 41st Millennium, the Feast of the Emperor's Ascension is a popular holiday celebrated on many Imperial worlds with feasting, gift-giving, athletic competitions and a variety of religious services and ceremonies. On some planets, symbolic sacrifices are often made in the Emperor's name, such as the burning of books to offer their precious knowledge up to Him. It is also an auspicious time to join people in wedlock or conceive children, leading to mass gatherings of citizens intent on one or both of these undertakings. It is also said that during the Emperor's Ascension, no false judgements can be made, making it impossible to overturn a legal ruling made during this time.

Not all worlds celebrate the Emperor's Ascension with feasts and festivals. On some, especially primitive planets like Feral Worlds and Feudal Worlds, this holiday marks a time when the Emperor looks down from His divine realm and remembers His mortal followers. It is at this time that those brave and bold enough can earn a place at His side. Thus the Ascension becomes a bloody time when men and women fight and die in an effort to please the Emperor, each trying to outdo the courage and skill of the others.

However it is celebrated, the Feast of the Emperor's Ascension is the most notable and widespread Imperial holiday and the one that the majority of Imperial civilians observe, even if they do so only to gain a brief respite from their daily drudgery.
