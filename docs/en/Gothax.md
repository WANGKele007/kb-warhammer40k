Gothax, also called Gothax the Morose, is a Chaos Lord of the Death Guard Traitor Legion and the commander of its Tollguard warband. He is famed within the Legion for going into battle with a retinue of seven Noxious Blightbringers. On the Plague Planet, he also oversees the fortress-factory known as the Peal Yard, which creates the tocsins of misery used by the Death Guard's Blightbringers.
