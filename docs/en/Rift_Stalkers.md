The Rift Stalkers is a Loyalist Space Marine Chapter comprised entirely of Primaris Space Marines, created from the rare genetic lineage of the Raven Guard and raised during the Ultima Founding of ca. 999.M41.

The Rift Stalkers is a fleet-based Chapter, and its task is to patrol the Cicatrix Maledictum, as many foul abominations emerge out of that great rent in reality.

In addition to slaying the Emperor's enemies, the Rift Stalkers diligently seek a method of reconnecting with the hundreds of thousands of worlds cut off from Terra in the galactic north of the Imperium Nihilus, which has led them into the continuing conflicts surrounding the Nachmund Rift during the Era Indomitus.

Chapter History

Notable Campaigns

Bargheist Stars Campaign (Unknown Date.M42) - After seven Terran years of brutal warfare, and thanks partly to the arrival of several companies of the Rift Stalkers Chapter, the forces of the Imperium finally defeated their Chaos foes in the Bargheist Stars. A grand triumph was held, with parades and martial celebrations spreading glory across a dozen Imperial worlds. Yet at the celebrations' height, Warboss Grukk Face-rippa appeared at the head of a brand new WAAAGH!. His Greenskins flooded the planets of Tremendix and Aposia before the horrified Imperial defenders could rally their forces, and plunged the beleaguered Bargheist Stars back into bloody war.

Fall of the Vision of Torment (Unknown Date.M42) - During a solar-month-long naval engagement within the depths of the Spyral Nebula, several Space Marine boarding parties from the Rift Stalkers Chapter were lost trying to navigate the madness of the immense Thousand Sons flagship Vision of Torment in an attempt to disable its enormous crystal Warp-lance. It was only when a Kill-team led by Captain Therrin of the 2nd Company breached the vessel's Void Shields and fought their way through the maze of illusions that guarded its weapons decks that the planet-immolating weapon was finally shattered, allowing the Rift Stalkers' battle barges to close in and destroy the Vision of Torment in a series of devastating broadsides.

Nachmund Rift War (Unknown Date.M42) - The Rift Stalkers participated in the Nachmund Rift War during the Era Indomitus. The Rift Stalkers were one of the Chapters of the Adeptus Astartes to respond to the Castellans of the Rift's psychic call for aid to defend the Nachmund Gauntlet, joining the other responding Chapters in forming the alliance known as the Wardens of the Gauntlet. The Rift Stalkers task in this war zone was to seek out new routes through the region, striking swiftly against any Chaos forces blocking Warp channels and launching boarding assaults against enemy voidships.

Chapter Combat Doctrine

The Rift Stalkers' Apothecaries often operate in enemy territory and far from Imperial lines where they have little access to standard medicae resources. As such, they are expert improvisers when it comes to handling their battle-brothers' wounds and injuries.

Notable Rift Stalkers

Captain Therrin - Therrin was the captain of the Rift Stalkers' 2nd Company. Therrin led the assault on the Thousand Sons flagship Vision of Torment and managed to destroy its world-shattering crystal Warp-lance. This allowed the Rift Stalkers fleet to then move in and destroy the great Chaos warship.

Epistolary Fornost - Fornost is currently part of the Rift Stalkers' contribution to the Wardens of the Gauntlet alliance of Adeptus Astartes Chapters fighting the Nachmund Rift War. Part of his duties include the dissemination of highly sensitive strategic intelligence to the various member groups of the alliance of Imperial military forces spread throughout the Nachmund Gauntlet war zone.

Donatello - Donatello is a battle-brother who serves in the Rift Stalkers's 2nd Company, 3rd Squad (battleline). It is currently unknown if he participated in the boarding actions against the Thousand Sons flagship Vision of Torment alongside other elements of the company that were led by Captain Therrin.

Chapter Appearance

Chapter Colours

The Rift Stalkers wear white Mark X Power Armour, the polar opposite of their progenitors the Raven Guard who prefer midnight black. The Aquila or Imperialis on the chest is black.

Squad tactical specialty is designated by the black marking on the right shoulder pauldron -- battleline, close support, fire support, Veteran or command.

The colour of the trim on the right shoulder pauldron indicates company number in accordance with the tenets of the Codex Astartes -- i.e. white (1st Company), yellow (2nd Company), red (3rd Company), etc.

The left shoulder pauldron proudly displays the Chapter's iconography -- the same black raven heraldry as the Raven Guard.

A black Low Gothic numeral stenciled on the left knee guard indicates squad number.

Chapter Badge

The Rift Stalkers' Chapter badge is the same stylised black raven centred on a field of white utilised by their parent Chapter, the Raven Guard.
