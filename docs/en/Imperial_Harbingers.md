The Imperial Harbingers is a Loyalist Space Marine Chapter of unknown genetic origin that was raised during the 23rd Founding in the 37th Millennium. The Harbingers spend much of their time pursuing one Imperial Crusade after another.

Chapter History

The Imperial Harbingers Chapter was created during the 23rd Founding, the so-called "Sentinel Founding", to help replace the considerable losses suffered by the Adeptus Astartes in the preceding millennia. Along with several other Chapters, the Imperial Harbingers was from the outset conceived as a replacement Chapter that would spend its existence on continuous Crusade and was to be created from the most stable gene-seed stocks available to the Adeptus Mechanicus. Like many of the Space Marine Chapters created during this tumultuous period in Imperial history, its exact progenitor Chapter remains unknown. The Harbingers consider themselves sworn enemies of the Death Guard Traitor Legion.

Chapter Appearance

Chapter Colours

The Imperial Harbingers primarily wear light grey power armour. The Aquila or Imperialis on the chest guard is silver. 

The black squad tactical specialty symbol -- battleline, close support, fire support, Veteran or command -- is designated on the right shoulder plate.

A white High Gothic numeral centred on the squad specialty symbol designates squad number. 

The colour of the helm indicates company number in accordance with the Codex Astartes -- i.e. white (1st Company), yellow (2nd Company), red (3rd Company), green (4th Company), etc.

Chapter Badge

The Imperial Harbingers' Chapter badge is a large white skull in profile, engulfed by flames, on a field of light grey.
