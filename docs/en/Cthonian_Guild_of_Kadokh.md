The Cthonian Guild of Kâdokh is a Cthonian Mining Guild of the Leagues of Votann that operates in the Greater Thurian League. Like all Cthonian Guilds, the Guild of Kâdokh carries out most of the Greater Thurian League's mining and resource extraction activities, and embodies the belligerence and acquisitiveness of the Kin. These Kin miners are fearless in the cause of locating, securing and harvesting resources for their league, and think nothing of braving environments so extreme that even other Kin would baulk at their hazards.
