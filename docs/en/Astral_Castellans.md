The Astral Castellans are a Loyalist Space Marine Chapter of unknown Founding and genetic origin. 

Notable Astral Castellans

Arkitos - A Venerable Dreadnought serving with the Deathwatch at the Eye of Octos watch fortress. He is currently serving with the Deathwatch forces that are combating a splinter of Hive Fleet Leviathan on the world called Death of Bianzeer.

Chapter Appearance

Chapter Colours

The Astral Castellans' Chapter colours are not listed in current Imperial records.

Chapter Badge

The Astral Castellans' Chapter badge consists of two white swords crossed diagonally with four white octagrams positioned between them at the cardinal points, all centred on a medium blue background.
