Griffayn, known as "The Spear-Cast" among his battle-brothers in the Dark Angels Legion, served as a voted-lieutenant of the Firewing during the Great Crusade and the Horus Heresy. He began his career as a sergeant-at-arms and was promoted to lieutenant during the Muspel Campaign, demonstrating his competence and leadership qualities. 

Griffayn accompanied his Chapter Master Belath to the Dark Angels' Legion homeworld of Caliban to solicit reinforcements for the Thramas Crusade, unaware that Luther, Lion El'Jonson's foster father and mentor, had already rebelled against the primarch. Over time, Griffayn came under Luther's influence and was pardoned during the purges that claimed the lives of many of Belath's warriors. Following these events, Griffayn joined the forces of Merir Astelan after the assassination of the Loyalist Dark Angels Librarian Asmodeus, becoming a figure associated with the Renegade Dark Angels faction known as the Fallen.

In Griffayn's absence, Vastael was elected the new voted-lieutenant of the Firewing.

History

When Lion El'Jonson was reunited with his Dark Angels after the Emperor's arrival on Caliban, Griffayn was one of the first youths of that world chosen to join the Ist Legion during the Great Crusade. Originally a knight of The Order, raised in the feudal military traditions of his homeworld, he brought to the Legion the strict codes of oath, hierarchy and military honour that had defined the chivalric knightly orders of Caliban long before contact with the Imperium.

Solidifying his place among the Lion's gene-sons, Griffayn was assigned to the Firewing, a formation of the Hexagrammaton that specialised in intelligence-gathering, internal security, counterinsurgency, and precision target destruction. Its warriors operated in secret and carried out delicate missions that required discipline, discretion and unwavering loyalty. Griffayn's placement in this Order suggests that he was considered reliable, controllable and capable of carrying out missions that required both subtlety and determination.

When Lion El'Johnson led most of the Legion out into the wider galaxy during the Great Crusade, he ordered a small garrison led by Luther to remain on Caliban to oversee the introduction of new recruits into the Legion. Over time, the Dark Angels stationed on Caliban over the course of the Great Crusade developed a distinct identity, influenced by both remnants of Calibanite traditions and Imperial doctrine.

As the Great Crusade gave way to the Horus Heresy, isolation and resentment began to affect the Caliban garrison. Lion El'Jonson's perceived neglect of his gene-sons on his homeworld and Luther's growing autonomy created ideological divisions within the Legion's garrison. Despite his origins in the Firewing -- a branch accustomed to suppressing rebellion against the Imperium on conquered worlds -- Griffayn joined Luther's faction after he arrived on Caliban with Master Belath to seek reinforcement for the Heresy's upcoming Thramas Crusade. Whether it was out of personal loyalty, faith in Caliban's sovereignty, or conviction in Luther's righteousness, Griffayn's choice placed him among those who would later rise up against their primarch.

When Lion El'Jonson returned to Caliban at the end of the Horus Heresy, tensions between the returning Dark Angels and the Calibanite garrison they had left behind escalated into open civil war. Griffayn fought on Luther's side during the ensuing conflict. The battle escalated into planetary devastation, culminating in an orbital bombardment by the Lion's orbiting fleet and the unleashing of a catastrophic Warp rift that destroyed Caliban. In the ensuing cataclysm, many of Luther's followers were caught up in the Warp storm unleashed during the final battle between the Lion and Luther. Griffayn was counted among the Fallen, the Dark Angels who rebelled against their gene-father during the Fall of Caliban and were subsequently scattered throughout time and space by the will of the Ruinous Powers.

As with many of his Fallen brethren, Griffayn's fate remains unknown. His name survives only in fragmentary Imperial records and in the hidden archives of the Dark Angels, who continue their silent and relentless hunt for those who once betrayed the Emperor. Legion Devastator Vastael was elected as the new voted-lieutenant of the Firewing.
