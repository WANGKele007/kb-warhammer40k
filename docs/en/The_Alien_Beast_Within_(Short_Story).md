The Alien Beast Within is a short story published in the Inquisition War series by Ian Watson. It is included in The Inquisition War (Omnibus) and Deathwing (Anthology). 

Synopsis

Meh'Lindi, the foremost assassin of the Callidus Temple, is tasked with the most demanding mission of her career -- to infiltrate a Genestealer Cult disguised as one of the monstrous four-armed xenos hybrids and destroy them from within. As she adopts the mindset of a alien-influenced cultist, Meh'Lindi starts to worry that she may lose herself and, even worse, fail in her mission and doom a world to destruction...
