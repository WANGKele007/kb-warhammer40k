A stasis-crypt is a form of personal stasis technology employed by the Necrons. Originally constructed in preparation for the Great Sleep, stasis-crypts allowed the Necrons to safely enter a state of suspended animation for tens of millions of Terran years until the time of designated reawakening was at hand. It is said that trillions of Necrons are currently sleeping in such devices in the vast subterranean tomb complexes which are hidden beneath the surface of their Tomb Worlds.
