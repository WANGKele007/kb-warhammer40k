House Miranor is a Loyalist Imperial Knight House of the Imperium. There is little information on this knightly house in official Imperial records. The Freeblade known as the Hour of Reckoning was once a Noble of House Miranor.

This Knight bore the Icon Incognitus, the stylised "X" that represents anonymity, upon his faceplate to show his hatred for House Miranor. Rumours say that rivals within his own house had killed the Noble's family. Choosing the path of the Freeblade, he cast off the distinctive golden faceplate of Miranor and replaced it with a sable Icon Incognitus.
