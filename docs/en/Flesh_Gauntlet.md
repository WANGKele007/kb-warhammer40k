The Flesh Gauntlet is a Drukhari melee weapon in the form of a claw-glove crammed with syringe-like protrusions and vials that can inject potent electrosteroids that force rapid and unnatural growth. Its victim will literally outgrow his own skin, suffering a truly unpleasant death as he bursts apart in a welter of steaming, heaving gobs of fleshy matter. The Flesh Gauntlet is most often used by the Haemonculi, and their Wrack and Grotesque servants.
