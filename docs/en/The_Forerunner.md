The Forerunner is the name given to the Zoat whose spacecraft was discovered aboard the Seventh Blackstone Fortress in the churning region of chaotic matter known as "the Seethe." 

Another Zoat named The Archivist later duped a group of explorers into recovering the spacecraft's remains and the archeotech within for it, but what had originally become of the Forerunner to stand its vessel aboard the Blackstone Fortress is not known.
