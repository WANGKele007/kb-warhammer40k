The Silent Furies are Jetbike-mounted warriors of the Sisters of Silence who operate alongside the Silent Judges of the Chamber of Judgement's Excruciatus order as pursuit and strike experts.

The Silent Furies aid in the aid of the capture of Rogue Psykers that try to escape the demands of the Great Tithe. They take to the field piloting sophisticated Erinyes Pattern Jetbikes that enable them to cover ground far quicker than infantry and go where heavier vehicles cannot. Silent Furies often accompany their sisters of the Subjugator cadres in their hunt and acquisition missions, aiding in the capture or destruction of their fleeing quarries with the wide array of their esoteric weaponry.

Their duty is not to simply confront the enemy in a frontal assault, but to direct the Subjugator cadres and other forces of the Silent Sisterhood precisely where and when they are needed the most.

The Erinyes Pattern Jetbike used by the Silent Furies was equipped with a Snare Cannon, a Needle Cannon, or an Adrathic Destructor.
