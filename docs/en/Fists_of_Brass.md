The Fists of Brass were a host of Khorne Daemonkin of unknown origin. Almost nothing is known about them aside from their involvement in a fatal clash with the Ork WAAAGH! Badstomp in 301.M40 which destroyed the warband.

Warband History

Notable Campaigns

Martyrs' Glory - In 301.M40 the Fists of Brass hurled themselves into the teeth of WAAAGH! Badstomp across the seventeen moons of the Madrakae. Explosive carnage broke out as the two war-hungry hordes collided head on, but the Chaos warband was massively outnumbered and wiped out within solar days. Despite their deaths, however, the oceans of blood the Fists of Brass had spilled during the battle drew eight legions of Khornate Daemons into realspace, which soon fell upon the Orks and started the battle anew.
