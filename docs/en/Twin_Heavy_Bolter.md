The Twin Heavy Bolter is an Imperial weapon that is essentially 2 Heavy Bolters twin-linked to fire as one weapon. A Twin Heavy Bolter is often deployed as part of the standard weapons load-out of the Primaris Space Marine Repulsor grav-tank.

Sources

Codex Adeptus Astartes - Space Marines (8th Edition), pp. 87, 182
