Destroyer Blades are an upgrade that can be made to the hulls of Chaos Space Marine vehicles. These vehicles have hideously sharp spikes, whining blades and jagged spars added to their chassis to cause murderous damage with even the slightest touch against enemy infantry and vehicles.

Sources

Codex: Chaos Space Marines (6th Edition), pg. 68
