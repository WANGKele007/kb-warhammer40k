The Corvae Las-Pulser is a type of Laser Weapon used by the forces of the Legio Custodes during the Great Crusade and the Horus Heresy. 

It was equipped to the Dreadspear of the Custodes Contemptor-Achillus Dreadnought and could be used as an optional main armament on the Gyrfalcon Jetbike.
