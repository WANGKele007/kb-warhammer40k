The Great Beasts of Caliban, also simply called the Great Beasts, were highly aggressive and dangerous fauna native to the Dark Angels Legion's homeworld of Caliban during the Age of Strife and according to some indications, even before.

These monstrous beasts, which were of variable shapes, sizes and physical power and of a wide variety of different species, were eventually discovered to have been created by the corrupting mutant taint of the Warp, possibly as a result of the world's proximity to the Eye of Terror or by the deliberate actions of the self-aware artefact created by the Old Ones that was known as the Ouroboros that had long been present on Caliban. According to Luther, the Great Beasts possessed a malicious intelligence and actively hated the Human settlers of their world.

The Great Beasts terrorised the population of Caliban for many Terran centuries, until a knightly brotherhood known as The Order arose to combat them. Under the eventual leadership of the primarch Lion El'Jonson after he arrived on Caliban, the Great Beasts were finally purged from Caliban during a great crusade led by the Lion and his knights. This crusade fundamentally transformed the future for the people of Caliban, offering them hope of a better life where they were no longer restricted to huddling around stongholds in fear of Caliban's many forests.

However, as the purge of the Great Beasts ended only solar months before the Emperor came to Caliban to reunite with El'Jonson in 846.M30, the Calibanites soon were forced to confront a very different future from what they had imagined, as the rapid changes brought by Imperial Compliance and rapid industrialisation soon created difficulties and strains on Calibanite society nearly as great as those produced by the rampages of the Great Beasts.

Notable Great Beasts of Caliban

Horn of Ruin

The Horn of Ruin was a massive, multi-eyed creature with a large horn sprouting from above its uppermost left eye. It was one of the most ferocious known Great Beasts, and some 40 knights of Caliban were slain killing the beast. A young Luther took part in the battle to slay the monster.

Cragshadow

The Cragshadow was a massive winged, avian creature that slew Luther's parents in battle.

Calibanite Lion

The Calibanite Lion was the most fierce and deadly of all the Great Beasts of Caliban and the creature for which Lion El'Jonson was named by Luther when the young primarch was discovered as a feral savage in the forests of the Death World. Of all the heroes of their time, only Lion El'Jonson and Zahariel El'Zurias ever killed one.

The Calibanite Lions' name came from a mane of razor-sharp spines that encircled their neck. Aside from being quadrupeds with paws, this was the only feature that likened them to their Terran namesakes. Each paw's claws were as sharp as daggers and incredibly deadly.

Calibanite Lions also had two lethally sharp, enlarged teeth protruding from their top jaw as well as a natural armoured skin that was able to heal itself and shift where needed during combat. Calibanite Lions had orange eyes with reptile-like, slitted black pupils. 

As with all the Great Beasts of Caliban, the Calibanite Lions possesses an almost unworldly intelligence and seemed to enjoy causing suffering and pain. They could also shrug off their own pain and wounds without second thought, and could be seen to visibly heal their wounds even as they continued to fight, making them extraordinarily deadly opponents.
