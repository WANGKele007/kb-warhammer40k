The Greyva are an intelligent alien species that are currently engaged with Imperial military forces in multiple war zones.

Due to the reputation of the Indigan Praefects regiments of the Astra Militarum in combat against powerful xenos megafauna, they have been requested as reinforcements in multiple Imperial war zones assailed by the Greyva and other unknown beasts. Where these requests have been honoured, the Praefects set about eradicating the xenos fiends with practised efficiency.
