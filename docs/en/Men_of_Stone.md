The Men of Stone are an unknown faction or sub-species of Humanity created through artificial means by the equally legendary Human sub-group or sub-species known as the Men of Gold at some point in the 21st Millennium during the Dark Age of Technology. The Men of Stone were specifically created to aid Humanity's exploration and settlement of the galaxy and were also responsible in turn for the creation of the self-aware humanoid robots remembered in legend as the "Men of Iron".

History

According to dimly remembered legends and the few scraps of actual historical record that survived the Age of Strife, the Men of Stone were an artificially-created or augmented faction or sub-species of Humanity who had been engineered by the equally legendary and poorly understood Men of Gold. They were created at some point during the 21st Millennium in the Dark Age of Technology for the specific purpose of aiding Humanity in exploring and settling new colony worlds in the furthest regions of the galaxy. Their appearance marked the decline of the Men of Gold's influence in Human society and their eventual disappearance from the historical record.

According to the legends, the Men of Stone were said to exist in a state of only "half-life", which made them far less susceptible to the Chaos corruption of the Warp or possession by Daemons, making superluminal travel through the Immaterium far easier for them. 

It was the Men of Stone who began the development of humanoid robots who were fully self-aware known as the "Men of Iron". The Men of Stone were eventually destroyed during the Cybernetic Revolt of the Dark Age of Technology by their thinking machine creations, likely some time in the late 23rd Millennium.
