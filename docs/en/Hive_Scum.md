Hive scum, also called "scummers", are masterless or itinerant residents of an Imperial hive city's lowest socioeconomic class often highly skilled in the martial arts needed to survive their miserable native environment. They will fight for anyone who offers them coin or a way out of their abject poverty. 

Hive Scum are ultimately violent fixers who specialise as gunslingers and bladesmiths, either dual-wielding autopistols, stub pistols, and razor-sharp shivs, or cobbling together brutal makeshift weaponry like crowbars and saws to overwhelm their foes in a savage display that will make others think twice about upsetting the syndicates of their hive's criminal underworld who they usually serve.

Hive scum's unlawful connections provide them access to a wide range of illicit substances. With a rudimentary knowledge of chemistry and pharmacology they can enhance their abilities with combat stimms, coat their weapons in vile toxins, and fashion devastating chem bombs.

Role

Some hive scum are mercenaries who travel from zone to zone of a hive city, earning whatever easy money is around before moving on. Many, however, are drunkards, addicts and down-and-outs who, despite their appearances, are more than capable of holding their own in a fight. Regardless of their background, hive scum are too wild and independent to submit to the leadership of anyone for very long and will only work when they feel like it -- as their services are always in demand.

In the underhive of Necromunda's Hive Primus, most hive scum end up working for the Merchants Guild, though a few are always willing to aid one of that Hive World's innumerable gangs. They are especially valuable to newly-created gangs, who hire hive scum to supplement their low numbers or inexperienced gang members. For the more established gangs, however, hive scum are either seen as not worth hiring or nothing more than cannon fodder to use against the gang's enemies.

On Atoma Prime, in Hive Tertium, hive scum often serve as dangerous fixers and mercenaries for the organised crime cartels that some say truly run Tertium. In the Era Indomitus, the Ordo Hereticus warband of Inquisitor Grendyl operating in that hive city to cleanse it of the Nurglite Admonition Chaos Cult and its ally, the Traitoris Militarum Moebian 6th Regiment, has begun accepting the local Loyalist hive scum as Acolytes. They provide needed manpower in the fight against Chaos corruption, even if their loyalties still lie primarily with their original criminal employers who have seen their profits impacted by the Chaos assault rather than the Emperor.

Growing up among the worst of the worst in Imperial society, hive scum are skilled survivalists and masters of many types of weapons, including most Imperial ranged weaponry and many melee weapons. Some hive scum, especially those native to Atoma Prime, are known for being able to dual wield ranged and/or melee weapons with seemingly unnatural skill. Hive scum are known for their use of combat stimms to enhance their combat abilities beyond the unaugmented Human norm.
