Vangash'hagash the Ever-Bloody is a Bloodthirster, a Greater Daemon of the Blood God Khorne. Within the benighted realms of the Eye of Terror this daemon lord holds sway over the Daemon World of Kathalon. His nightmare planet is a great burning lake criss-crossed with arching bridges of brass and bone, where the daemonic legions of Khorne and Tzeentch have fought each other for aeons.
