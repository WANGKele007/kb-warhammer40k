Typhon Primaris was a jungle-covered Imperial Feral World found in the Sub-sector Aurelia of the Korianis Sector, in the Ultima Segmentum, and was one of the primary recruiting grounds for the Blood Ravens Space Marines Chapter. Its population primarily consisted of a small number of Human settlements and several klanz of Feral Orks.

During the events of Dawn of War II in the 41st Millennium a Craftworld Aeldari Warp Spider Exarch was provoking the Orks to attack Imperial outposts and villages, and the Space Marines of the Blood Ravens Chapter intervened. However, unknown to them, Typhon Primaris had been infested with Tyranid Genestealers and some plants and animals became mutated by the Tyranid organisms, killing the human settlers' livestock.

Soon after, a Tyranid splinter fleet of Hive Fleet Leviathan appeared over the lush, biomatter-rich planet, drawn by the Genestealers' psychic emanations, threatening to engulf the entire star system, though this hive fleet was defeated by the Blood Ravens in the course of the conflict known to that Chapter as the First Aurelian Crusade.

Ultimately, to deal with the ongoing Tyranid and Chaos threat approximately a standard decade later, an Exterminatus action was carried out upon the world by an Imperial Retribution Fleet under the command of the Inquisition's Ordo Malleus on the orders of the Puritan Inquisitor Adrastia during the Third Aurelian Crusade, leaving it a barren, blasted Dead World.

History

Typhon Primaris was first settled by Humans during the Dark Age of Technology. At the time of the Fall of the Aeldari in the 30th Millennium, a fatally damaged Aeldari craftworld crashed on the planet; it eventually became buried under the earth and rapidly growing tropical vegetation. Meanwhile, the Human population technologically regressed to a state comparable to the Bronze Age during the isolation of the Age of Strife, while Orks soon arrived to continuously battle the Humans. A small number of Aeldari Exodites also settled on the world, unwittingly attracted to the call of the Infinity Circuit of the dead craftworld.

Typhon Primaris eventually became a recruitment world for the Blood Ravens Chapter, but this practice ended at some point in the 41st Millennium, when the Orks of Typhon Primaris united long enough to wipe out the small population of native Humans. Eventually, however, the Imperium assaulted the world, reduced the triumphant Orks to a population of primitive Feral Orks, and began to resettle the planet. 

By the time of the First Aurelian Crusade, barely 30,000 Humans lived scattered across the world, living in scientific stations, resource extraction sites and military bases. Despite their small number, Typhon's population was able to sustain itself, and the Blood Ravens once again began to draw aspirants from Typhon Primaris. However, the Feral Orks remained the predominant force on the world, with most of them loyal to Warlord Grakka da Skurj of the Evil Sunz klan at the time. Some Adeptus Mechanicus Tech-priests also followed in the wake of the Imperial resettlement, intent on researching the local Human ruins dating to the Dark Age of Technology.

The Tyranids of a splinter fleet of Hive Fleet Leviathan invaded Typhon Primaris during the events of the First Aurelian Crusade. The Blood Ravens were able to blunt the attack and prevent the Jungle World from being consumed by the Hive Mind, but scattered Tyranid forces remained on the world in the wake of that campaign. In the ten standard years that followed the failed Tyranid invasion, the Imperium deployed several Astra Militarum regiments to secure Typhon Primaris, but their campaigns ultimately bogged down. Frustrated at the failure of the Imperial high command to support them, one Astra Militarum regiment -- the 39th Cadian Shock Troops -- even turned Traitor and became Traitoris Militarum dedicated to the Dark Gods.

Despite the attempts by Aeldari from Craftworld Alaitoc to save Typhon Primaris in an attempt to continue to salvage the craftworld buried there, the planet was subjected to an Exterminatus on the order of the Puritan Inquisitor Adrastia as a result of the xenos and Chaos incursions in Sub-sector Aurelia some ten standard years after the Second Aurelian Crusade during the start of the Third. The buried craftworld's Infinity Circuit and any remaining spirit stones were destroyed along with every living being on the planet with the impact of the Atmospheric Incinerator Torpedo variant of Cyclonic Torpedoes.
