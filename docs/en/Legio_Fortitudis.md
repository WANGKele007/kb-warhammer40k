The Legio Fortitudis is a Loyalist Titan Legion of the Collegia Titanica, one of the military arms of the Adeptus Mechanicus. 

Legion History

The Legio Fortitudis was deployed as part of the Imperial force led by the Ultramarines Master of Sanctity Ortan Cassius and the Cadian General Arka of the Astra Militarum that defended the Agri-world of Styxia Prime in the Eastern Fringe from an assault by the Tyranid Hive Fleet Gorgon in the late 41st Millennium.

Notable Titans

Dominatus Rex (Warlord-class Titan) - The Dominatus Rex, commanded by Princeps Perthion, fought during the defence of Styxia Prime from Hive Fleet Gorgon.
Victorix (Warlord-class Titan) - The Victorix, commanded by Princeps Perthion, fought during the defence of Styxia Prime from Hive Fleet Gorgon. 

Notable Personnel

Princeps Perthion - Perthion commanded the Warlord-class Titan Dominatus Rex during the defence of Styxia Prime from Hive Fleet Gorgon.
Princeps Jasyn - Jasyn commanded the Warlord-class Titan Victorix during the defence of Styxia Prime from Hive Fleet Gorgon.
