A T'au Kor'shuto is a massive orbital city, which are often found above well-established T'au colony worlds. Many Septs have constructed these colossal orbital cities which are inhabited by the Air Caste. 

The T'au can move these space stations to give them a "jumping off" point for a major colonisation effort, a military campaign, or a large research endeavour.
