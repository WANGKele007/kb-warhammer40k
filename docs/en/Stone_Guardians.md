The Stone Guardians were massive stone statues that were present on the Dark Angels' lost homeworld of Caliban. They were steeped in mystery and eldritch power and after Caliban was destroyed, the Dark Angels managed to save several as relics. The Chapter is known to have used fragments of the Stone Guardians in order to power a number of potent Chapter artefacts, including the Spiritshield Helm worn by Master Lazarus and the Arbiter's Gaze, a relic bionic eye with enhanced tactical capabilities sometimes employed by Dark Angels warriors allowed to implant the augmetic.
